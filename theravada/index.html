Theravāda

The Pāli Canon's own commentarial tradition — beginning with Buddhaghosa's 5th-century Visuddhimagga, translated here directly from the Pāli, question by question, chapter by chapter.

Why a new translation

Every existing English translation of a Pāli text this old is either in copyright or, where it is old enough to be free, shows its age. This section exists to translate directly from the Pāli original — with the source text alongside our English on every page, and reading notes grounded only in the text's own internal logic and the general, uncontested conventions of Pāli commentarial literature, not in any single modern scholar's introduction or footnotes.
