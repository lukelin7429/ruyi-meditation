Visuddhimagga

Buddhaghosa's 5th-century systematic treatise on virtue (sīla), concentration (samādhi), and understanding (paññā) — the most complete surviving manual of Theravāda practice. Ru-Yi's translation works directly from the Pāli, chapter by chapter, with the original alongside our English and a reading note for the passages that repay a second look.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Author: Bhadantācariya Buddhaghosa, 5th c. CE Source: VRI Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana (romanized), cross-checked against PTS/GRETIL

Translation Method

The Pāli source is the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana (Sixth Council) romanized edition maintained by the Vipassana Research Institute (VRI, tipitaka.org), cross-checked against the Pali Text Society's Rhys Davids edition (1920–21) as digitized on GRETIL. The VRI text is used under its own terms — free for non-commercial use, with attribution — while the GRETIL/PTS digitization, which carries its own CC BY-SA licence, is used only to verify readings, never copied.

The English translation is Ru-Yi's own, made directly from the Pāli. No existing English translation — including Bhikkhu Ñāṇamoli's copyrighted The Path of Purification — is consulted, paraphrased, or used as a guide for wording or structure. Reading notes are grounded only in the text's own internal citations (it quotes the Paṭisambhidāmagga, the four main Nikāyas, and the Abhidhamma Piṭaka constantly) and in the general, uncontested conventions of the Pāli commentarial genre — never in a modern scholar's specific interpretation.
