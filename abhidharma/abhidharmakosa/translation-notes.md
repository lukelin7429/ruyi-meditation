# Abhidharmakośa Translation Notes

These notes record recurring translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Abhidharmakośa* (阿毘達磨俱舍論, Taishō T1558, no. 1558, vol. 29). They exist so that terminology stays consistent across all thirty fascicles.

## Source and independence from prior translations

- Base text: Taishō T1558, via the CBETA digital edition (public domain). We translate from this Chinese text directly.
- The only complete modern English translation is Leo M. Pruden's rendering of Louis de La Vallée Poussin's French *Abhidharmakośabhāṣyam* (Asian Humanities Press, 1988–91), still under copyright. We may consult it privately to check difficult passages, but the English wording, sentence structure, and section framing on this site must be our own. Do not paraphrase it sentence-by-sentence.
- Chinese commentaries such as Yen-pei's (演培) *俱舍論頌講記* are likewise consulted for context only, not quoted at length.
- Unlike the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra and the Cheng Weishi Lun on this site, this treatise does not (yet) follow a single named teacher's lecture transcript. Reading notes are Ru-Yi's own, grounded in standard Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma scholarship, and are labeled "Reading note — Ru-Yi" rather than attributed to an external commentator. If a suitable source becomes available later, note this explicitly when adopting it, rather than silently changing the attribution convention partway through.

## Core terminology

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 阿毘達磨 | Abhidharma | Left untranslated as the proper term; glossed on first use as "facing the dharma." |
| 對法 | Abhidharma | The two-character Chinese rendering of the same term; translate as "Abhidharma," not literally as "facing dharma," except when the text is explicitly explaining the etymology. |
| 法 | dharma(s) | Keep as "dharma," not "phenomenon" or "thing," except in loose English framing sentences. Defined by the treatise itself as "that which holds its own particular mark" (能持自相). |
| 有漏 | contaminated | āsrava-present; opposite of 無漏. |
| 無漏 | uncontaminated | anāsrava; the pure, liberating kind of wisdom, dharma, or aggregate. |
| 蘊 | aggregate(s) | skandha. |
| 界 | element(s) | dhātu — the eighteen elements analyzed in Chapter 1. |
| 處 | sense field(s) | āyatana — the twelve sense fields. |
| 隨眠 | latent affliction(s) | anuśaya — Chapter 5's subject. |
| 資糧 | provisions | saṃbhāra; the preparatory requisites for the path. |
| 擇法 | discernment of dharmas | the technical definition of 慧 (wisdom/prajñā) given in fascicle 1. |
| 世親 | Vasubandhu | author. |
| 玄奘 | Xuánzàng | translator into Chinese, 651 CE. |

Keep this table growing as new recurring terms appear in later fascicles — add a row rather than improvising a fresh translation of a term that already has an entry.

## Structure

Follow the nine traditional chapters (品) and the fascicle divisions given by the mnemonic verse 界二根五世間五，業六隨三賢聖四，智二定二破我一 — see the [Abhidharmakośa overview page]({{ '/abhidharma/abhidharmakosa/' | relative_url }}) for the full chapter-to-fascicle map. Within a fascicle, structure sections around the root verses (頌) and their prose commentary (長行), matching how the Chinese text itself alternates between the two.

## Register

- Plain English, close to the Chinese, not a loose paraphrase — matching the method already used for the Mahāprajñāpāramitā Śāstra and the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra on this site.
- Where the Chinese compresses a technical argument into very few characters, it is fine for the English to expand slightly for clarity, but do not add explanation that belongs in a reading note instead of the translation itself.
- Mark every fascicle's status honestly (e.g. "opening section only") rather than implying more is finished than actually is.
