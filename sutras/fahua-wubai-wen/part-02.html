The Portents of the Introduction and the Opening of the "Skillful Means" Chapter

This part continues Zhanran's questions on Ji's Profound Praise of the Lotus through the rest of the "Introduction" and into the "Skillful Means" chapter. It begins with the title of the Immeasurable Meanings, which the Buddha teaches before the Lotus, and with Ji's attempts to divide the two sūtras as substance and function, principle and practice, or bodhisattvas and śrāvakas; Zhanran answers that the Immeasurable Meanings produces many from one, while the Lotus returns the many to the one. Ji's glosses on the portents (the samādhi, the flowers, the earthquake, the light, the six destinies, the jewels, the carts) are found accurate but silent on what makes the Lotus different. In the "Skillful Means" chapter the dispute sharpens. Ji classifies skillful-means wisdom in Yogācāra terms, takes the chapter's substance from subsequently attained wisdom, reads "only a buddha together with a buddha" through Vasubandhu's Treatise, and holds that only bodhisattvas of the first, seventh, or eighth ground can believe or know this teaching. Zhanran replies that the Lotus reaches every being, that buddha-nature cannot exclude those of "fixed nature," and that a treatise written to crush the "rotten seeds" of the two vehicles cannot be used to read a sūtra that revives them. The part ends with Ji's first gate on the substance of the one vehicle.

The Meaning of "Great" and the Names of the Immeasurable Meanings

Chinese Source

問：大是何義？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the meaning of "great"?

Chinese Source

答：十二門論六種釋大：一、出二乘；二、佛果最大，此乘能至；三、佛所乘；四、能滅大苦而與大樂；五、觀音等大人所乘；六、盡諸法源底。今謂過二乘獨在佛息大苦盡法底，諸經共有，然須簡出，豈無量義耶？又曰：攝大乘對法有七大：一、境大，緣百千教法故；二、行大，具二利故；三、智大，了二無我故；四、進大，三僧祇故；五、方便大，具悲智離二邊過故；六、證得大，成就十力無畏故；七、業大，窮生死邊際建佛事故。今謂須簡，意亦同前。又曰：勝鬘一乘，一乘即大乘，大乘即佛性，佛性即涅槃。今謂勝鬘是權一乘，今經是實一乘，何得以權來釋於實？權實既殊，涅槃豈等（云云）？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: The Twelve Gates Treatise explains "great" in six ways: first, it goes beyond the two vehicles; second, the buddha-fruit is the greatest, and this vehicle can reach it; third, it is what the buddhas ride; fourth, it can extinguish great suffering and give great joy; fifth, it is what great persons such as Avalokiteśvara ride; sixth, it exhausts the source and bottom of all dharmas. Now I say: going beyond the two vehicles, being only in the buddha, stopping great [suffering], and exhausting the bottom of dharmas — all sūtras share these; still one must single out [what is unique]. Is it not [so for] the Immeasurable Meanings? It further says: "The Mahāyānasaṃgraha and the Abhidharma[samuccaya] have seven greatnesses: first, greatness of object, because it takes as object hundreds of thousands of teachings; second, greatness of practice, because it possesses the two benefits; third, greatness of wisdom, because it understands the two kinds of no-self; fourth, greatness of diligence, because of the three asaṃkhyeyas; fifth, greatness of skillful means, because it possesses compassion and wisdom and is free of the faults of the two extremes; sixth, greatness of attainment, because it accomplishes the ten powers and the fearlessnesses; seventh, greatness of activity, because it does the Buddha's work to the limit of birth-and-death." Now I say: one must single out [what is unique]; the point is the same as before. It further says: "The one vehicle of the Śrīmālā: the one vehicle is the Great Vehicle, the Great Vehicle is buddha-nature, buddha-nature is nirvāṇa." Now I say: the Śrīmālā is the provisional one vehicle, and the present sūtra is the real one vehicle; how can one explain the real by the provisional? Since the provisional and the real differ, how could the nirvāṇa be equal (and so on)?

Chinese Source

問：諸經如淨名等並有異名，此無量義有異名乎？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The various sūtras, such as the Vimalakīrti, all have alternative names; does this Immeasurable Meanings have alternative names?

Chinese Source

答曰：論中具列一十七名，初名無量義，乃至第十六名妙法蓮華，第十七名最上法門。今問：對前三異，則名異義異，本不相關。若其相關，名異義同，此經亦應名無量義耶？如其亦得名無量義，乃是此經與彼同體、同法、同人、異名而已。若異名而已，已聞無量義竟，何須三請更說法華？若也不同，與論妨不？況論但云是法華異名，不云無量義也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: The Treatise lists seventeen names in full: the first is named Immeasurable Meanings, up to the sixteenth, named Wondrous Dharma Lotus Flower, and the seventeenth, named Supreme Dharma-Gate. Now I ask: compared with the three different [cases] earlier, then names and meanings differ and are originally unrelated. If they are related, the names differ and the meaning is the same; should this sūtra then also be called Immeasurable Meanings? If it may also be called Immeasurable Meanings, then this sūtra and that one are the same substance, the same Dharma, and the same persons, differing only in name. If they differ only in name, having already heard the Immeasurable Meanings, why need three requests to teach the Lotus again? If they are not the same, does this conflict with the Treatise or not? Moreover, the Treatise says only that these are alternative names of the Lotus; it does not say they are of the Immeasurable Meanings.

Chinese Source

又曰：無量義云：無量義者，從一法生。其一法者，即是無相。如是無相，無相不相，無相不相，名為實相。菩薩安住如是實相，發大悲心。故知如是安住，能開實相，發菩提心，則與今經能會之實，其相永別。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "The Immeasurable Meanings says: 'Immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma; that single dharma is the markless. That which is markless in this way — markless and not a mark, markless and not a mark — is called the true mark. The bodhisattva, abiding securely in such a true mark, gives rise to the mind of great compassion.' Hence we know that such abiding securely can open the true mark and give rise to the resolve for bodhi, and that its mark is forever distinct from the real merged by the present sūtra."

Chinese Source

又曰：無量義云：善男子！菩薩若能修一法門無量義者，必得疾成無上菩提。善男子！如一種子生百，百生千，千生萬，萬中一一復生百千萬數，乃至無量。此經亦爾，從一法生，乃至無量，故名無量義。彼釋曰：此經不唯取所出教理，無量義體即真智境體。今謂彼經正明從一出多為宗，何以棄所出之無量，取能出之正體？體雖極要，經意不然。若然者，從一出多，何異此經會多歸一？同異屬目，而無解分辨，異則以三義取體，爾乃同為一類。此事可見，何以固違？

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "The Immeasurable Meanings says: 'Good son! If a bodhisattva can cultivate the one dharma-gate of immeasurable meanings, he will surely quickly attain unsurpassed bodhi. Good son! As from a single seed a hundred are born, from the hundred a thousand, from the thousand ten thousand, and from each of the ten thousand again hundreds of thousands of myriads are born, up to the immeasurable — this sūtra is likewise: born from a single dharma, up to the immeasurable; therefore it is called immeasurable meanings.'" His explanation says: "This sūtra does not take only the teaching and principle that are produced; the substance of immeasurable meanings is the substance of the object of true wisdom." Now I say: that sūtra properly takes producing many from one as its cardinal doctrine; why discard the immeasurable that is produced and take the substance proper that produces it? Though the substance is most essential, the intent of the sūtra is not so. If it were so, how would producing many from one differ from this sūtra's merging the many to return to the one? Sameness and difference are plain to see, yet you have no understanding to distinguish them: where they differ, you take the substance by three meanings, and then make them the same kind. This matter can be seen; why insist on going against it?

Chinese Source

問：既知無量義名同異，若是餘名不能盡理，追逐第十六名名法華者，前雖略釋，因茲更問其義何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Since the sameness and difference of the name Immeasurable Meanings are known — if the other names cannot exhaust the principle, and so one pursues the sixteenth name, Lotus — though it was briefly explained before, on this account I ask again: what is its meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：一出水義，以所詮理名華；二華開義，以能詮教名華。今問：經詮因果及以權實，以故蓮華譬彼二雙，何以但語教理棄蓮存華？開與出水華實無缺，安得二時輒分能所？況復與前四法義違，然亦與向行果文背。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: First, the meaning of emerging from the water: the principle expressed is called the flower; second, the meaning of the flower opening: the teaching that expresses is called the flower. Now I ask: the sūtra expresses cause and fruit and the provisional and the real, and so the lotus flower symbolizes those two pairs; why speak only of teaching and principle, discarding the lotus and keeping the flower? In opening and in emerging from the water, flower and fruit are not lacking; how can the two times be rashly divided into what expresses and what is expressed? Moreover, this goes against the meaning of the four dharmas earlier, and also against the passage on practice and fruit just now.

Chinese Source

又曰：無量義者，又有三義故：一、名無量義，體用勝故；二、教菩薩法，化根熟故；三、佛所護念，依佛有故。今問：何故前對法華但有體而無用，今獨釋無量義乃云體用勝故？又勝何經名之為勝？若勝小乘，未足為勝；若勝大乘，法華亦在大乘之根。若當體名勝，小乘亦然，何獨此耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "Immeasurable Meanings further has three meanings: first, it is named Immeasurable Meanings because its substance and function are superior; second, it is a teaching for bodhisattvas, because it transforms those whose faculties are ripe; third, it is guarded and held in mind by the Buddha, because it depends on the Buddha for its existence." Now I ask: why, earlier, relative to the Lotus, did it have only substance and no function, while now, explaining the Immeasurable Meanings alone, you say its substance and function are superior? Moreover, superior to what sūtra is it called superior? If superior to the Lesser Vehicle, that is not enough to be superior; if superior to the Great Vehicle, the Lotus too is among the roots of the Great Vehicle. If it is named superior in itself, the Lesser Vehicle is also so; why only this?

Chinese Source

問曰：曾聞他問，法華亦與無量義同名，何故此三昧起方說此經？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I have heard others ask: the Lotus also has the same name as the Immeasurable Meanings; why is this sūtra taught only after rising from this samādhi?

Chinese Source

答：有五解：一者、蓮華二時得名，彼如未出水時性能出故，故未化二乘令趣一乘，此經如出水正化二乘。二、彼正化菩薩傍化二乘，此正化二乘傍化菩薩，故彼正名無量義傍名法華，此正名法華傍名無量義，時異體同，然將教理以對行果。三、彼據智體名無量義，此約智用名為法華，會二歸一故。四、彼以理教名無量義，菩薩已修一乘因、趣一乘果，故不為說行果一乘名蓮華，由但不知應病與藥之教理，但說教理名為蓮華。此聲聞未能應病與藥，故不為說教理蓮華，但說行果蓮華令趣入故。故下經云：乘此寶乘直至道場，因行蓮華也。唯以佛之知見示悟眾生，果蓮華也。論云：開者無上義，示者同義，悟者不知義，入者令至不退轉地義。釋曰：前三是果，後一是因。五者、彼以教理二種無量義，此經對彼二乘教理行果並名蓮華。前之三義彼此體同，後之二義此寬彼狹。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: There are five explanations. First, the lotus flower gets its name at two times: that [sūtra] is like [the time] before emerging from the water, when by nature it can emerge; so, before transforming the two vehicles, it makes them head for the one vehicle. This sūtra is like emerging from the water, properly transforming the two vehicles. Second, that one properly transforms bodhisattvas and incidentally the two vehicles; this one properly transforms the two vehicles and incidentally bodhisattvas. Hence that one is properly named Immeasurable Meanings and incidentally Lotus, and this one properly Lotus and incidentally Immeasurable Meanings; the times differ and the substance is the same; thus teaching and principle are matched against practice and fruit. Third, that one is named Immeasurable Meanings in terms of the substance of wisdom, and this one Lotus in terms of the function of wisdom, because it merges the two to return to the one. Fourth, that one names teaching and principle Immeasurable Meanings: the bodhisattvas have already cultivated the cause of the one vehicle and are heading for the fruit of the one vehicle, so it does not teach them the one vehicle of practice and fruit named lotus flower; because they only do not know the teaching and principle of giving medicine according to the illness, it teaches only teaching and principle, named lotus flower. These śrāvakas cannot yet give medicine according to the illness, so it does not teach them the lotus flower of teaching and principle, but teaches only the lotus flower of practice and fruit, to make them enter. Hence the sūtra below says: "Riding this jeweled vehicle straight to the seat of enlightenment" — the lotus flower of causal practice; "only by the buddha's knowledge and vision they show and awaken beings" — the lotus flower of the fruit. The Treatise says: "Opening is the meaning of unsurpassed; showing is the meaning of sameness; awakening is the meaning of not knowing; entering is the meaning of bringing them to the ground of nonretrogression." The explanation says: "The first three are fruit, the last one is cause." Fifth, that one [names] the two kinds, teaching and principle, Immeasurable Meanings; this sūtra, relative to that, names the teaching, principle, practice, and fruit of the two vehicles all lotus flower. Of the first three meanings, this and that are the same in substance; of the latter two meanings, this is broad and that narrow.

Chinese Source

又曰：實理經文上下亦通教理行果，今謂五義釋異，自覺不當，乃伏云理實上下具於四法，當知上來二二異釋，其義自壞，何須更難？

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "The real principle and the text of the sūtra, above and below, also extend to teaching, principle, practice, and fruit." Now I say: the five meanings explain differently, and [you] yourself realize they are not apt, and so concede that principle and reality above and below possess the four dharmas. You should know that the paired different explanations above collapse in meaning of themselves; why need further objection?

Chinese Source

且如第一義立彼如未出水時，若以聲聞未迴心時名未出水，則爾前諸教一切皆然，何獨無量義耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Take, for example, the first meaning, which establishes that that [sūtra] is like [the time] before emerging from the water: if the time before the śrāvakas turned their minds is called "before emerging from the water," then all the teachings before that are so; why only the Immeasurable Meanings?

Chinese Source

二、傍正者，彼經亦應少許聲聞於彼得記，若全無者，何名為傍？只應有無相對，何名傍正相對？若言此經正化二乘，即應聲聞得益多於菩薩，何以菩薩得益倍於餘經？若言彼經正化菩薩，菩薩得益應多於此，如何少耶？若云但是名傍正者，論中直列一十七名，名既並施，傍正何在？正即俱正，傍亦並傍。

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, incidental and proper: that sūtra too should have a few śrāvakas receiving predictions in it; if there are none at all, why call it incidental? It should simply be a contrast of having and lacking; why call it a contrast of incidental and proper? If you say this sūtra properly transforms the two vehicles, then the śrāvakas should gain more benefit than the bodhisattvas; why do the bodhisattvas' benefits double those of the other sūtras? If you say that sūtra properly transforms bodhisattvas, the bodhisattvas' benefit should be greater than here; why is it less? If you say it is incidental and proper only in name, the Treatise simply lists seventeen names; since the names are all set out alike, where is incidental and proper? If proper, all are proper; if incidental, all are equally incidental.

Chinese Source

三、體用者，已如前難。四、教理等其義已壞，但開示等。四、準論文中不云因果，然論釋開只云如來能證令眾生知，次釋示云法身平等示眾生知，釋悟中云唯佛一乘令眾生悟，並無果名。準不退地，其地却成直至道場，道場是果，何名為因？其第五義亦隨前破，言菩薩不知應病與藥之教理，何異聲聞？只緣聲聞之人未知正應授與，故知聲聞自行義強，唯稀化物；菩薩化他久著，唯應進行至果。若果不須至，分別功德何須進位？諸義既廢，何論寬狹？

Ru-Yi Translation

Third, substance and function: already objected to above. Fourth, teaching and so on: its meaning already collapses; but [as for] opening, showing, and so on: fourth, according to the Treatise's text, it does not speak of cause and fruit. The Treatise, explaining opening, says only that the Tathāgata can realize it and makes beings know it; next, explaining showing, it says that the Dharma-body is equal and is shown to beings to know; in explaining awakening it says that only the one buddha vehicle makes beings awaken; none has the name "fruit." According to [the explanation of entering as reaching] the ground of nonretrogression, that ground instead becomes "straight to the seat of enlightenment"; the seat of enlightenment is fruit; how is it called cause? The fifth meaning likewise collapses with the foregoing. To say that bodhisattvas do not know the teaching and principle of giving medicine according to the illness — how do they then differ from śrāvakas? It is only because the śrāvakas do not yet know how to respond properly and give; hence we know that the śrāvakas are strong in the meaning of self-practice and rarely transform beings, while bodhisattvas have long been notable in transforming others, and should only advance in practice to the fruit. If the fruit need not be reached, why does the "Distinguishing Merits" need advancing stages? Since all the meanings are abolished, why discuss broad and narrow?

The Samādhi and the Portents

Chinese Source

問：從佛說此經已至不動者，其義如何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: From "When the Buddha had finished teaching this sūtra" to "unmoving" — what is the meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：表智處得理，方可說法。今謂此為入於何等三昧？得何等理？若入無量義三昧，還得無量義理，只應正為菩薩而說教理。若入法華三昧，經文但云入於無量義處三昧，況復得理？應記二乘。又曰：若不入者，則有分別動搖，說不自在。今入三昧，身心不動，離於分別動搖，於法自在。又離一切障，謂入三昧，離諸定障。若無定者，於障及說不自在故。今問：何以將婆沙之義用釋法華三身？吾今此身即是法身，不謀而應，無運而作。佛不入定，何障何縛而不自在？但為示化儀，現入定相，亦令見者得四悉益，得種等益，得顯密益。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: It symbolizes that in the place of wisdom one attains principle, and only then can one teach the Dharma. Now I say: what samādhi is entered here? What principle is attained? If one entered the samādhi of immeasurable meanings and so attained the principle of immeasurable meanings, he should simply teach teaching and principle, properly for bodhisattvas. If one entered the Lotus samādhi — yet the sūtra text says only that he entered the samādhi of the Station of Immeasurable Meanings — how much less is there attaining principle? It should predict the two vehicles. It further says: "If he did not enter, there would be discrimination and agitation, and his teaching would not be free. Now, entering samādhi, his body and mind unmoving, he is free of discrimination and agitation and free in the Dharma. Moreover, he is free of all obstructions; that is, entering samādhi, he is free of the obstructions to concentration. Without concentration one is not free with regard to obstructions and to teaching." Now I ask: why use the meaning of the Vibhāṣā to explain the three bodies of the Lotus? "My present body is the Dharma-body": it responds without planning and acts without effort. The Buddha does not enter concentration; what obstruction, what bond, is he not free of? It is only to show the method of transformation that he manifests the appearance of entering concentration, also causing those who see it to gain the benefit of the four siddhāntas, the benefit of planting [the seed] and so on, and the benefit of the manifest and the secret.

Chinese Source

問：曾有人問：佛常在定，何須云入？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Someone once asked: the Buddha is always in concentration; why say "entered"?

Chinese Source

答曰：有十義故：一者隨緣動靜利物故；二者為放光若不入定則非佛瑞；三凡欲說法須先審機；四顯法殊妙。今謂此四既然，餘六請止，與前釋佛氣類欲同。若言隨緣動靜，如須菩提亦不違物情。若言放光不入則非佛瑞，如般若中支節放光，及涅槃中面門放光，乃至諸經凡有放光，豈必入定？若言審機，與婆沙中日夜三時審機何別？若顯法妙，華嚴、般若及以大集、涅槃直爾說之，豈不妙耶？故知此四並附小乘，非法華意。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: For ten reasons: first, following conditions in movement and stillness to benefit beings; second, for emitting light, since if he did not enter concentration it would not be a buddha's portent; third, whenever he is about to teach the Dharma, he must first examine the capacities; fourth, to reveal the Dharma as especially wondrous. Now I say: these four being so, please stop the remaining six; they are of a kind with the earlier explanation of "the Buddha." If you say "following conditions in movement and stillness," even Subhūti does not go against beings' feelings. If you say "if he emitted light without entering, it would not be a buddha's portent" — as in the Prajñā, light emitted from the limbs and joints, and in the Nirvāṇa, light emitted from the face, and in all the sūtras wherever light is emitted — must it always involve entering concentration? If you say "examining capacities," how does that differ from the Vibhāṣā's examining capacities three times by day and three times by night? If "revealing the Dharma as wondrous," the Avataṃsaka, the Prajñā, the Great Collection, and the Nirvāṇa are taught directly; are they not wondrous? Hence we know these four all attach to the Lesser Vehicle and are not the intent of the Lotus.

Chinese Source

問：天雨四華，其義如何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The heavens rained four [kinds of] flowers; what is the meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：華名柔軟，表見者離於剛強麤鑛三業。今謂華表顯實之徵，預呈開權之兆，但除麤鑛三業，欲定亦可治之。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: Flowers are called soft; this symbolizes that those who see them are free of hard, stubborn, coarse, and rough threefold action. Now I say: the flowers symbolize the sign of revealing the real and present in advance the omen of opening the provisional. How can one be above and named prajñā, while the other two below still have separate names? Above and below, before and after, being at odds, cause and fruit, agent and object, are forever distinct. The Larger [Prajñā] says: "The bodhisattva constantly contemplates nirvāṇa while practicing the path." Hence we know that prajñā illuminates and nirvāṇa is what is contemplated. Nirvāṇa possesses the three, and what contemplates is likewise. Agent and object wholly one, each interfusing with the other — only so is it the wondrous object of the Lotus. The wondrous object requires wisdom, and wisdom is taken as the gate. Entering the gate and seeing the principle requires practice, and practice is the pāramitā of knowledge and vision. Entering the stages and realizing the fruit, giving rise to function to benefit others, agent and object all fully possessed — only so is it called the "Skillful Means" chapter. Hence understanding skillful means is understanding the later chapters.

Leaving the Burning House

Chinese Source

問：嘗聞人問：無學果位，亦居分段，應在門中。如小菩薩，應非出宅。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I once heard someone ask: the fruit-stage of those beyond learning still dwells in the segmented [birth-and-death], and so should be within the gate; like the lesser bodhisattvas, it should not have left the house.

Chinese Source

答曰：果位二乘從因名出，諸小菩薩惑未斷故，故不名出。今謂會二歸此，不出菩薩為何益耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: The two vehicles at the fruit-stage are called "having left," named from the cause; the lesser bodhisattvas have not yet severed delusion, and so are not called "having left." Now I say: merging the two to return to this — of what benefit is it to the bodhisattvas who have not left?

Chinese Source

又曰：問：何以二子趣於羊鹿故稱出宅，大子牛車未名出宅？答：二二乘惑盡故出宅，菩薩初劫煩惱猶行如未出宅。今謂婆沙菩薩三祇百劫惑尚猶行，豈況初祇具同凡品，會二歸此何足為奇？故知全迷瓔珞華嚴菩薩位行。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "Question: Why are the two sons, heading for the goat and the deer, called having left the house, while the eldest son with the ox cart is not yet called having left the house? Answer: The two, the two vehicles, have exhausted delusion and so have left the house; the bodhisattva in the first eon still acts with afflictions, like one who has not yet left the house." Now I say: the bodhisattvas of the Vibhāṣā, through three asaṃkhyeyas and a hundred eons, still act with delusion; how much more in the first asaṃkhyeya, wholly the same as the ordinary? Merging the two to return to this — what is remarkable about that? Hence we know you are entirely confused about the bodhisattva stages and practices of the Jeweled Necklace and the Avataṃsaka.

Chinese Source

又曰：七地分段盡此一生亦名出宅，二乘同故。已前即非，猶多生故，如有學類。故出宅已方乃上車。今謂七地同於二乘，乃是大品三乘共位，何得先引三祇後援共地？兩無分判，故二並非。若以得有餘果名為上車，有餘未出分段，何以出宅索車？菩薩三十四心爾前猶來，未上菩提樹下，已號極尊佛更索車，都無是理。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "At the seventh ground, the segmented [existence] ending in this one life, it is also called having left the house, because it is the same as the two vehicles. Before that it is not, because there are still many lives, like the class of learners. Hence only after leaving the house does one board the cart." Now I say: the seventh ground being the same as the two vehicles is the stage shared by the three vehicles in the Larger [Prajñā]; how can you first cite the three asaṃkhyeyas and afterward draw on the shared ground? Neither has a clear division; hence both are wrong. If attaining the fruit with remainder is called boarding the cart, [those] with remainder have not left the segmented; why [say they] leave the house and ask for the carts? The bodhisattva before the thirty-four minds is still coming [and going]; not yet having ascended [to the seat] beneath the bodhi tree, and already called the utmost honored buddha, yet still asking for a cart — there is no such principle at all.

The Substance of the One Vehicle

Chinese Source

問：一乘之義，正是經宗，云何分別？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The meaning of the one vehicle is precisely the cardinal doctrine of the sūtra; how is it distinguished?

Chinese Source

答：五門分別：一、出體性；二、釋名；三、明說意；四、彰差別；五、問答料簡。初體性者，略為三：一、總含體，謂無漏若種若現，有為無為，若因若果，根本方便，能成佛德，皆名一乘。一乘者，即大乘也，亦名無上乘。今謂若別論宗體，理合甄分，通而言之，應兼能所、自他、因果，豈以能成獨立乘耶？況因果等不判深淺，又通種現，大成混和，以種現共論，其理天隔。若其別說，種屬凡愚，若凡亦名乘，事應未可。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: It is distinguished in five gates: first, revealing the substance and nature; second, explaining the name; third, clarifying the intent of the teaching; fourth, displaying the distinctions; fifth, sorting out by question and answer. First, the substance and nature, briefly threefold. First, the all-containing substance: undefiled seeds or manifest activity, conditioned or unconditioned, cause or fruit, fundamental or skillful means — whatever can accomplish the buddha's virtues is called the one vehicle. The one vehicle is the Great Vehicle, also called the unsurpassed vehicle. Now I say: if one discusses the cardinal substance separately, in principle it should be sifted; speaking generally, it should include agent and object, self and other, cause and fruit; how can "what can accomplish" alone establish the vehicle? Moreover, cause and fruit and so on are not judged as deep or shallow, and also extend to seeds and manifest activity, making a great confusion; discussing seeds and manifest activity together, their principles are as far apart as heaven [from earth]. If they are spoken of separately, seeds belong to ordinary fools; if the ordinary too are called the vehicle, that matter is not yet acceptable.

Chinese Source

又曰：中邊分別論云：無上乘有三品：一、正行體，謂十度；二、所緣，謂十二緣；三、修證。今謂若準此論，不取種也。雖不取種，未了所緣。既修十度以為行相，故所緣如何取異緣覺？故依古釋，別有差分。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "The Madhyāntavibhāga says: The unsurpassed vehicle has three grades: first, the substance of right practice, namely the ten perfections; second, what is taken as object, namely the twelve [links of] conditions; third, cultivation and realization." Now I say: according to this treatise, seeds are not taken. Though seeds are not taken, what is taken as object is not yet understood: since one cultivates the ten perfections as the mark of practice, how then does what is taken as object differ from the pratyekabuddhas? Hence, according to the ancient explanations, there is separately a distinction.

Chinese Source

二、隨勝體，有六：一、攝事歸理，如勝鬘等，多以法身、真理、法性為一乘。二、攬餘歸智體，亦以真智為一乘，如上文云：方便、知見、不退、不知等。三、隱劣隨勝體，下文云：諸佛唯以大事故，出現於世。四、二運廣用體，唯以因智名為一乘，如火宅中，與諸菩薩及聲聞眾，乘此寶乘，直至道場。五、勝出分段體，通取因果，出分段死所有理性，名一乘體。六、引攝殊勝體，以詮旨一乘教門，名一乘體。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Second, the substance that follows the superior, of six kinds. First, gathering phenomena into principle, as in the Śrīmālā and so on, which mostly take the Dharma-body, true principle, and the Dharma-nature as the one vehicle. Second, the substance that gathers the rest into wisdom, also taking true wisdom as the one vehicle, as the text above says: skillful means, knowledge and vision, nonretrogression, not knowing, and so on. Third, the substance that hides the inferior and follows the superior; the text below says: the buddhas only for the great matter appear in the world. Fourth, the substance of the two carryings and broad function, taking only causal wisdom as the one vehicle, as in the burning house: together with the bodhisattvas and the assembly of śrāvakas, riding this jeweled vehicle straight to the seat of enlightenment. Fifth, the substance of superior emergence from the segmented, taking cause and fruit together: the nature of principle that emerges from segmented death is called the substance of the one vehicle. Sixth, the substance of superior drawing-in: the teaching-gate of the one vehicle that expresses the purport is called the substance of the one vehicle."

Chinese Source

今謂隨六種勝，體義不成，如人名位、事業、功用，可多可異。若論其體，不可分張。然六中初理依希可爾，言依希者，事理之名通涉大小，直爾言之不成一乘，若攬餘歸智智不名體，理智既別故簡是非，從勝乃是佛出世意不名乘體，廣用二運亦非車體，自運容可從因，運他理須在果，況自利利物皆須因果，不獨用故故至道場，道場是果也。勝出分段者，應云二死不獨分段，況因果非出教體永乖，故知雖云有體而實無體，並是莊挍，但可以當體為名耳。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now I say: following the six kinds of superiority, the meaning of substance does not hold. A person's names and positions, deeds and works, can be many and different; but if one discusses his substance, it cannot be divided up. Yet of the six, the first, principle, may roughly pass. I say "roughly" because the names of phenomena and principle extend to the great and the small; stated simply, they do not make the one vehicle. As for gathering the rest into wisdom, wisdom is not called the substance, since principle and wisdom are distinct; hence one sifts right and wrong. Following the superior is the Buddha's intent in appearing in the world and is not called the substance of the vehicle. The broad function of the two carryings is also not the substance of the cart: carrying oneself may proceed from the cause, but carrying others must in principle lie in the fruit; moreover, benefiting oneself and benefiting beings both require cause and fruit, not function alone; hence "straight to the seat of enlightenment" — the seat of enlightenment is the fruit. As for emerging from the segmented, one should say the two deaths, not the segmented alone; moreover, cause and fruit are not emergence, and [as for] the teaching as substance, it is forever at odds. Hence we know that though it is said to have a substance, it really has no substance; all are ornamentation, and one can only take the substance itself as the name.

Chinese Source

三真實體者，根本大乘理教行果，及能入大乘方便四法，皆名一乘咸能運載，故品初云其智慧門等，本論云阿含言教。今謂真實之中理教行果，與隨事中理等何別？第一是理，豈可不攝事耶？第二是智，智即是行；第三即是四法因緣；第四是因，因亦屬行；第五亦行，行通因果又屬此果；第六在教，徒分四異粟柄禾莖，況復四法共釋一乘，與前法華對無量義自相違也。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Third, the true and real substance: the principle, teaching, practice, and fruit of the fundamental Great Vehicle, and the four dharmas of skillful means that can enter the Great Vehicle, are all called the one vehicle, all being able to carry. Hence the beginning of the chapter says 'the gateway to their wisdom' and so on, and the original treatise says 'the verbal teaching of the āgama.'" Now I say: within the true and real, how do principle, teaching, practice, and fruit differ from principle and the rest within [the substance that] follows the superior? The first is principle; how can it not include phenomena? The second is wisdom, and wisdom is practice. The third is the causes and conditions of the four dharmas. The fourth is cause, and cause also belongs to practice. The fifth too is practice; practice extends to cause and fruit, and also belongs to this fruit. The sixth lies in the teaching. Uselessly dividing them into four differences — millet stalks and grain stems! Moreover, having the four dharmas together explain the one vehicle contradicts your own earlier matching of the Lotus against the Immeasurable Meanings.

Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

Voices. As in Part 1: "Answer" reports Ji's Profound Praise (T1723), "Now I say / Now I ask" is Zhanran, and "It further says" introduces more of Ji. Where Ji's text runs on between Zhanran's objections without a marker (units 71, 79, 89), Ji's words are put in quotation marks so that the speakers stay distinct. In units 61 and 89, "the other" (他) is Ji. In unit 45, "the Commentary and Record" (疏記) probably refers to Zhiyi's Textual Commentary and Zhanran's own Record on it (Fahua wenju ji, T1719).

Immeasurable Meanings quotations. "Immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma… is called the true mark" and the seed simile (unit 3) follow the round-two draft of the Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings (`wuliangyi-jing`), adjusted where our text differs: it reads 無相不相，無相不相 for the sūtra's 無相不相，不相無相, and abridges the seed simile.

Lotus quotations. "The Station of Immeasurable Meanings," "body and mind unmoving" (The Assembly and the Six Signs); "Sun of wisdom, great and holy World-Honored One…," "the husks and chaff of the assembly," "the bodhisattvas whose power of faith is firm," "alarmed and doubtful," "the single meaning of liberation," "the one great cause," "the pāramitā of skillful means," "his words gentle and pleasing to every mind," "only a buddha together with a buddha… the true mark of all phenomena" (Skillful Means: The Buddha's Wisdom, and The Buddha's Hesitation and the Turning of the Wheel); "was you yourself" (The Assembly and the Six Signs). Wording follows the site; the sūtra's 一大事因緣 keeps the site's "the one great cause," while Ji's and Zhanran's own 大事 is "the great matter."

Treatise sources. The six meanings of "great" are from the opening of the Twelve Gates Treatise (T1568). "The latter five pāramitās are all subsequently attained wisdom" and the ādāna verse are in the Vijñaptimātratā-siddhi (T1585); the verse is originally from the Saṃdhinirmocana (T676). The seven greatnesses are found in the Mahāyānasaṃgraha and the Abhidharmasamuccaya; the seven factors that make giving a perfection are the Vijñaptimātratā-siddhi's list.

The six destinies (unit 24). The glosses on the names are compressed in the source; "the Miscellaneous Heart" (雜心) is the Saṃyuktābhidharmahṛdaya. The punctuation 涅槃云：多思雜心。云：意靜名人 is read as 涅槃云多思，雜心云意靜，名人 ("much thinking," "the mind calm" — [these are] called human). "From the root of non-abiding [all dharmas are established]" alludes to the Vimalakīrti; "the world abides eternally" alludes to the Lotus "Skillful Means" verse 世間相常住.

Four kinds of birth-and-death (unit 25). 方便、因緣、有有、無有 ("skillful means, causes and conditions, with further existence, without further existence") are the four of the Śrīmālā tradition; Ji's Profound Praise (T1723) contains 方便生死、因緣生死.

Pronunciation (unit 61). The fanqie notes are translated as content, since the dispute is about how to read 便; the transcriptions fang-lian, bang-lian are modern approximations, not reconstructions.

fang and bian. In Ji's glosses on 方便 (unit 61), 有則曰方 is rendered "having a norm is called fang." Alternatively: "where something exists, it is called fang."

The letter i (units 110–111). The three dots of the Siddhaṃ letter i are the standard image for the three virtues as neither vertical nor horizontal. Ji (T1723) says the letter is "like an inverted 品" (one dot above, two below); Zhanran counters that the ancient form is like the dots "under 艸" (two above, one below), like the three eyes of Maheśvara ("the heavenly eyes").

The four storehouses (unit 106). Ji's fourfold 藏 (containing, giving rise, covering, revealing) is rendered "storehouse" throughout, with 如來藏 kept as tathāgatagarbha, following the round-two Mahāyāna Calming-and-Contemplation notes. "The Vimalakīrti's seven consciousnesses" is read as the seven abodes of consciousness (七識處) of the "Buddha-Path" chapter.

Broken text (unit 22). The unit ends mid-sentence (此等亦何); the English stops with a dash.

Probable copying errors, translated as corrected. Unit 16: 四生勤 read as 四正勤. Unit 33: the blank before 沙 read as "its sand." Unit 37: 酉方 read as 西方, 遮熟 as 遮熱. Unit 71: 答曰：有二智慧。及門論云 read as 有二：智慧及門。論云. Unit 79: 掖苦 read as 拔苦. Unit 99: 驚悕 read as 驚怖. Unit 121: 隨事中理 read as 隨勝中理 (Ji's "substance that follows the superior"). Unit 53: the stop in 自古。章疏 is ignored.
