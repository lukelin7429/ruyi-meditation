The Burning House and the Destitute Son

This part follows Ji's commentary through the parable of the burning house and into the "Belief and Understanding" chapter, where the four great disciples tell the parable of the destitute son. Almost every exchange turns on one question: were the three carts that the sons received on leaving the house already the great vehicle, or were all three provisional? Ji holds that the ox-cart outside the gate is the bodhisattva vehicle, so that only the two lesser vehicles need to be merged; Zhanran argues from the wording of the sūtra ("please give us the three kinds of jeweled carts," "he gave each a great cart alike") that all three belong to the past teaching and that the great white ox cart is given only afterward. The same issue shapes the discussion of the gate of the house, the open ground, the phantom city, the substance of the great cart, and the stage at which the cart is obtained. In the "Belief and Understanding" chapter, Zhanran contests Ji's definitions of belief and understanding and his mapping of the destitute son's wanderings onto stages of practice, showing that Ji's account of a son who "has not yet retreated from the great" cannot fit the story of a son who ran away.

The True and Counterfeit Dharma in a Pure Land

Chinese Source

問：云何名為正像等耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is meant by "true [Dharma]," "counterfeit [Dharma]," and so on?

Chinese Source

答曰：正法時教行證三皆悉具有，像法無證、末法無行，唯有教在。今謂約此三法以判正像，唯此穢土其理必然。唯今華光及諸聲聞並是淨土，何以無行但有教耶？未審淨土至末法時，即生慳貪破戒等耶？若有此等不名淨土。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "In the true Dharma, teaching, practice, and realization are all present; in the counterfeit Dharma there is no realization; in the final Dharma there is no practice, and only the teaching remains." Now I say: judging true and counterfeit by these three dharmas — the principle surely holds only for this defiled land. But now Flower Light and the śrāvakas are all in pure lands; how could there be no practice and only the teaching? I do not understand: when pure lands reach the final Dharma, do stinginess, greed, breaking the precepts, and so on arise? If there are such things, it is not called a pure land.

The Round of Parable and the Burning House

Chinese Source

問：初周既訖，譬周如何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The first round is finished; how about the round of parable?

Chinese Source

答曰：從此已去譬喻說周，文分為四：初佛喻化，二中周領，三藥艸述，四佛記。今問：前後依論，此中何不依論以立對治平等等名，仍不分判不依之意？故知論判本在於同。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "From here on is the round of teaching by parable. The text is divided into four: first, the Buddha's parable of conversion; second, the understanding of the middling [disciples]; third, the relating in the Herbs; fourth, the Buddha's prediction." Now I ask: before and after [this] you rely on the Treatise; why here not rely on the Treatise to set up names such as "remedy" and "equality," nor explain your intent in not relying on it? Hence we know the Treatise's divisions basically lie in sameness.

Chinese Source

問：經從是諸千二百至疑惑者，其義何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The sūtra from "these twelve hundred" to "doubt and confusion" — what is its meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：已得決定，復聞佛說捨權就實。今謂捨權就實，與前教何殊？而經但云一切皆成佛道，及以一切皆為佛乘，何以將於取捨之言，用釋即權而實之教？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "Having already obtained certainty, they again hear the Buddha teach abandoning the provisional and going to the real." Now I say: how does abandoning the provisional and going to the real differ from the former teaching? The sūtra says only "all will accomplish the buddha-path" and "all are for the buddha vehicle"; why use words of taking and abandoning to explain the teaching that is real while identical to the provisional?

Chinese Source

問：何名國邑聚落？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is meant by "town or village"?

Chinese Source

答曰：化佛但化三千國土，國如大千、邑如中千、聚如小千。廣雅云：聚落者，居也、聚也。人所聚居落，亦庭院太窄。長者既大，大於垢衣。大既三千，為所居土。垢衣所居為寬狹耶？此與信解門內長者為同異耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The transformation buddha converts only the trichiliocosm: the country is like the great chiliocosm, the town like the middle chiliocosm, the village like the small chiliocosm. The Guangya says: 'Village means dwelling, gathering.'" A settlement where people gather to dwell is also [only] a courtyard, too narrow. Since the elder is great, he is greater than [the one in] soiled clothing. Since [his] greatness is the trichiliocosm, it is the land where he dwells; is the dwelling of [the one in] soiled clothing wide or narrow? Is this the same as or different from the elder within the gate in the "Belief and Understanding"?

Chinese Source

問：長者之義有差別不？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Are there distinctions in the meaning of "elder"?

Chinese Source

答曰：長者有三：一可承長者，喻慈普慧圓妙決疑網；二者耆敬長者，喻佛道羣聖神通莫測；三可親輔長者，喻佛智滿。今謂雖有慧圓智滿之言，若滿若圓何教無之？決疑等言仍須教判。曾見古德以十德消帖經文甚為周悉，於十德中仍為三釋，謂世出世及以觀心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "There are three [kinds of] elders: first, the elder who can be relied on, symbolizing kindness universal and wisdom perfect and wondrous, resolving the net of doubt; second, the elder revered for age, symbolizing the Buddha's path and the host of sages, with spiritual powers beyond measure; third, the elder who may be drawn near as a helper, symbolizing the fulfillment of the Buddha's wisdom." Now I say: though there are the words "wisdom perfect" and "wisdom fulfilled," which teaching lacks fulfillment or perfection? Words such as "resolving doubt" must still be judged by the teaching. I have seen the ancient worthies explain and apply the sūtra text by the ten virtues very thoroughly; within the ten virtues there are still three explanations, namely worldly, transworldly, and contemplating the mind.

Chinese Source

又云：以異熟識為宅，佛處其中，故宅中火起。今問：為佛居眾生異熟識中？為佛自居佛異熟識？為是種子變為三界？佛入眾生所變識中，為是誰宅大火起耶？若屬眾生，何故云今此三界皆是我有？

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "The maturation consciousness is taken as the house, and the Buddha dwells within it; hence the fire arises within the house." Now I ask: does the Buddha dwell in the maturation consciousness of beings? Does the Buddha dwell in the Buddha's own maturation consciousness? Is it the seeds that transform into the three realms? When the Buddha enters the consciousness transformed by beings, whose house is it in which the great fire arises? If it belongs to beings, why does it say "Now these three realms are entirely my own"?

The Gate of the House

Chinese Source

問：門有幾耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: How many gates are there?

Chinese Source

答曰：有五，唯有一門，火宅門狹小，門在門外，佛教門。今問唯一，只是火宅之門狹小，還指火並喻教，教即是法，何須云五？豈以分五處即出五門耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "There are five: [the house has] only a single gate; the gate of the burning house is narrow and small; the gate is outside the gate; the gate of the Buddha's teaching." Now I ask: "only a single" means only that the gate of the burning house is narrow and small; [you] turn back to point to the fire and liken it to the teaching; the teaching is the Dharma; why say five? Would dividing it into five places make five gates?

Chinese Source

問：其火宅門，其數只然更有別途。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Is the number of gates of the burning house only this, or is there another way?

Chinese Source

答曰：又有二門：一者、大門，即一乘教；二、小側門，即二乘教。今問：出宅只有一門而已，何以輒立大小兩門？初以三車出火宅竟，諸子方乃從父來索車，索三既無父唯賜大，故知大車於宅外得，如何大小俱是宅門？若爾，宅門即長者之門。若即是者，出宅之時應當已得大乘教法，何故得小四十餘秊方受大化？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "There are also two gates: first, the great gate, namely the teaching of the one vehicle; second, the small side gate, namely the teaching of the two vehicles." Now I ask: for leaving the house there is only a single gate; why arbitrarily set up two gates, great and small? First, by the three carts, they leave the burning house; only then do the sons go to the father to ask for the carts; asking for the three, there are none, and the father gives only the great. Hence we know the great cart is obtained outside the house; how can the great and the small both be gates of the house? If so, the gate of the house is the elder's gate. If they are the same, then at the time of leaving the house they should already have obtained the teaching of the Great Vehicle; why did they obtain the small for more than forty years and only then receive the great conversion?

Chinese Source

問：曾聞人問：經云安穩得出，出在子前，何故下文云若不時出，必為所焚？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I once heard someone ask: the sūtra says "[I myself am able to] escape safely" — the leaving comes before the sons; why does the text below say "if [my sons and I] do not come out in time, we shall surely be burned"?

Chinese Source

答曰：下文譬云聞已驚入，合譬中云為度眾生而生三界。大經云：譬如長者其家失火從舍而出，為諸子故還旋赴救不顧其難，佛菩薩亦爾。今謂若消却入，其理則爾，忽不時出，佛豈焚耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The parable below says 'hearing this, rushed in alarm into [the burning house]'; the matching of the parable says 'he is born into … the three realms in order to deliver beings.' The Great Sūtra says: 'It is like an elder whose house catches fire; he goes out of the house, and for the sake of his sons turns back to rescue them, not regarding the danger; buddhas and bodhisattvas are likewise.'" Now I say: if one explains it as going back in, the principle is so; [but] if suddenly they did not come out in time, would the Buddha be burned?

Chinese Source

問：經從舍利弗至從舍出之等，其義何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The sūtra from "Śāriputra" to "carry them out of the house" and so on — what is its meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：分為四段：此初、從此至出之為大乘果化。今謂此始思惟辭用於大，如何即云用果化耶？況判下文方屬果段。二、復更思惟至而復狹小為大乘因化。今謂此是思惟欲息大化，何名因化？三、從諸子至為火所燒為大乘行化。今謂此乃諸子無有大機，故云幼稚未有所識，如何名為大乘行化？又行只是因，如何因行以分兩化？四、從我當為說至所燒害為無悕取大乘之化。今謂此是長者為說五濁怖畏之文，不名無悕取文也。作此消文不順經語，故不用之，況對四比與前違反。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "It is divided into four sections. This is the first: from here to 'carry them out' is the fruit-conversion of the Great Vehicle." Now I say: these are the words in which he first thinks of using the great; how can you call it using the fruit-conversion? Moreover, [you] judge that the text below belongs to the fruit section. "Second, from 'Then he thought again' to 'and it besides is narrow and small' is the cause-conversion of the Great Vehicle." Now I say: this is his thinking of desisting from the great conversion; how can it be called the cause-conversion? "Third, from 'My sons' to 'be burned by the fire' is the practice-conversion of the Great Vehicle." Now I say: this means the sons have no capacity for the great, hence it says "young and small, without understanding"; how can it be called the practice-conversion of the Great Vehicle? Again, practice is just cause; how can cause and practice be divided into two conversions? "Fourth, from 'I should tell them' to 'harmed and burned' is the conversion for those with no aspiration to take the Great Vehicle." Now I say: this is the text in which the elder tells them of the fearful dangers of the five turbidities; it is not called a text of no aspiration to take. Explaining the text in this way does not accord with the sūtra's words; hence I do not use it. Moreover, the matching with the four contradicts what came before.

The Three Carts and the Sons' Liking

Chinese Source

問：經從父知諸子至情必樂著，為何義耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The sūtra from "The father, knowing that his sons" to "their hearts would surely be drawn" — what is its meaning?

Chinese Source

答曰：正明為說三乘喻。此初，知機也。三乘種性各別，故今謂既受大化，何以為說三乘？故知此三俱非大也。若不爾者，何故不云為說二耶？而次子乃云三車也。翫者，弄也，亦戲也，亦翫習也，好也。當知不受於一，唯翫方便之三。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "It properly makes clear the parable of teaching the three vehicles. This is the first: knowing their capacities. The lineages of the three vehicles are distinct; hence —" Now I say: since they had received the great conversion, why teach them the three vehicles? Hence we know these three are all not the great. If it were not so, why does it not say he taught two? And next the sons speak of "three carts." 翫 (playthings) means to play, also to sport, also to practice repeatedly, to like. You should know that they did not accept the one, but only played with the three of skillful means.

Chinese Source

問：曾聞人問：何故與城之中不云先好，與車乃言好？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I once heard someone ask: why, in giving the city, is "liked before" not mentioned, while in giving the carts "liked" is said?

Chinese Source

答曰：車是遍趣行，機學可言好；城但是息苦，非生不名好。車是修生，城是修顯，故車說好，城不說之。今問：車苦應不息，城應無趣行，車既至宅外，豈可不息苦？城始勸令入，何得不論行？故知說城時，有好故能入；聞說三車時，息苦故方好。豈可無好言，即云無好義？見車有好字，却置息苦理。況復三乘俱為息苦，故俱翫好，故息苦乘非究竟乘。而告之言去，知即見是昔日賜三車也。三既在昔，故非今日等賜一大。故婆沙中四階成道同在小部，人不思之，故令意悞。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The cart is a practice that goes everywhere; for those capable of learning one can speak of liking. The city is only resting from suffering; it is not produced, so it is not called liked. The cart is produced by cultivation; the city is revealed by cultivation; hence for the cart 'liked' is said, and not for the city." Now I ask: [then] the cart should not stop suffering, and the city should have no practice of going forward. Since the carts reach outside the house, how could they not stop suffering? Since the city is first urged for entering, how could practice not be discussed? Hence we know that when the city is taught, there is liking, and so they can enter; when the three carts are heard of, they stop suffering, and so they are liked. How can one, because the word "liked" is absent, say there is no meaning of liking? Seeing the character "liked" for the carts, you set aside the principle of resting from suffering. Moreover, the three vehicles are all for resting from suffering; hence all are played with and liked; hence the vehicle of resting from suffering is not the ultimate vehicle. "And said to them" — know then that what they see is the three carts given in the past. Since the three are in the past, they are not the one great given equally today. Hence in the Vibhāṣā, the attainment of the path of the four stages is likewise in the small division; people do not reflect on this, and so their understanding is mistaken.

Why the Three Rounds Differ

Chinese Source

問：曾有人問：何故方便品權實理智因果雙彰，下之二周理智因果各互偏說？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Someone once asked: why in the "Skillful Means" chapter are the provisional and the real, principle and wisdom, cause and fruit, all displayed together, while in the two rounds below principle and wisdom, cause and fruit, are each taught partially?

Chinese Source

答曰：初周根利雙盡玄宗，中根一聞微知權實，取捨雖辨未識取因，故說三車令識一乘之行，隱果中之覺寂，說因內之智車，祕因位之理乘，遣修生於種智，理微解故智未閑故。下根雖聞權實及與智果，果理猶迷，更彰理有假真，說本令其取捨，隱果中之智品，藏因位之雙嚴，應機不同三周少異。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "In the first round, those of sharp faculties exhaust the profound doctrine on both [sides]; those of middling faculties, hearing once, slightly know the provisional and the real; though they distinguish taking and abandoning, they do not yet recognize the cause to be taken. Hence the three carts are taught to make them recognize the practice of the one vehicle: concealing awakening and quiescence within the fruit, teaching the cart of wisdom within the cause, keeping secret the vehicle of principle in the causal stage, and sending what is produced by cultivation to the wisdom of the modes, because their understanding of principle is subtle and their wisdom not yet practiced. Those of lower faculties, though they hear of the provisional and the real and of the wisdom-fruit, are still confused about the fruit and principle; further revealing that principle has the provisional and the true, [the Buddha] tells of the origin to make them take and abandon, concealing the class of wisdom within the fruit and hiding the twofold adornment of the causal stage. Since capacities are not the same, the three rounds differ slightly."

Chinese Source

今謂文辭至美於理未彰，譬中初敘三車權也，後與一大實也。三車敘昔一大在今，豈非譬中俱說三一各賜諸子？諸子各乘遊於四方因也，直至道場果也，其車高廣理也，有大白牛智也，長者為說教也，各得遊戲行也，具遊四方位也。果即覺寂何隱之有？車從理立安祕之耶？中根具聞此之四法，下根三聽四法彌精，豈以言之廣略便以增減判之？事似稍殊於理不異，不爾則不名三周，若前後增減乃成一周。何謂為三周？謂周迊即三迊，說入於人理等。況復三周俱說於教，不可巳於教下所詮，故知四文一不可廣，故請後學開眼思之。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now I say: the wording is most beautiful, but the principle is not displayed. In the parable, first the three carts are related: the provisional; afterward the one great is given: the real. The three carts relate the past; the one great is in the present; are not both the three and the one taught in the parable, each given to the sons? "The sons each ride," roaming the four directions: cause. "Straight to the seat of enlightenment": fruit. "The cart high and broad": principle. "There is a great white ox": wisdom. The elder teaches them: teaching. Each obtains [it] and plays: practice. Fully roaming the four directions: stage. The fruit is awakening and quiescence; what is hidden? The cart is set up from principle; how is it kept secret? Those of middling faculties fully hear these four dharmas; for those of lower faculties, hearing a third time, the four dharmas are even finer; how can one judge increase and decrease by the breadth or brevity of words? The matter seems slightly different, but the principle is not different; otherwise it would not be called three rounds; if before and after increased and decreased, they would become one round. What is meant by three rounds? A round is a circuit, that is, three circuits, teaching entry into persons, principle, and so on. Moreover, the three rounds all teach the teaching; one cannot differ in what is expressed under the teaching. Hence we know that not one of the four texts can be dispensed with. Therefore I ask later students to open their eyes and reflect on this.

Chinese Source

問：曾聞有人於此立五問。云何為五？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I once heard that someone set up five questions here. What are the five?

Chinese Source

答：一問云：智中有虗實，三種並名車。理中有假真，城名遍三種。何故大涅槃，不名為城名寶所？二問：二乘學智果，智果分羊鹿。二乘學滅果，滅果城應二。三問：何故城嶮，而車在門外？四問：何故車則云遊戲，而城云安穩？五問：何故化城勸前進，今車云速出？彼亦具有答，答還不盡理，是故不能述。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The first question says: 'In wisdom there are the empty and the real; all three kinds are called carts. In principle there are the provisional and the true; the name "city" extends to all three kinds. Why is the great nirvāṇa not named a city but named the place of treasure?' The second question: 'Those of the two vehicles study the wisdom-fruit; the wisdom-fruit is divided into goat and deer. Those of the two vehicles study the fruit of cessation; [then] the city of the fruit of cessation should be two.' The third question: 'Why is the city in danger, while the carts are outside the gate?' The fourth question: 'Why is it said of the carts "playing," while of the city it is said "peaceful and secure"?' The fifth question: 'Why does the phantom city urge them to advance, while now of the carts it says "come out quickly"?' They also have answers, but the answers do not exhaust the principle; therefore I cannot relate them."

Chinese Source

今謂凡設問者，必須使答能破大疑，方可置問。若不爾者，虗增費辭。且如初問，智中虗實，為從何處得虗實名耶？良由不分菩薩殊四階，不名為實。理中有假真，真名無簡判。聲聞證真實，一切教皆然。如何說無真，而獨稱為假？城名遍三種，三種並在昔。是故大涅槃，須別名寶渚。不分大小殊，故有如斯問。智果從人分，滅果從理一。道理必須然，何勞設此問？從人以論理，理亦隨人異。智果從義同，智果亦無二。城未出二死，二死未安穩。車從分段出，是故須云外。城從分段說，城亦三百外。車無二死說，車亦名在嶮。不了相望殊，徒勞設此問。車能運諸子，出苦名遊戲。城從息勞苦，故得名安穩。城既息苦已，城中亦遊戲。車已出流轉，車亦名安穩。守名迷於義，是故生此問。城從已入邊，故勸更前進。車從未上義，是故勸速出。速出望離苦，前進投寶所。所望永不同，何須作此問？

Ru-Yi Translation

Now I say: whenever one poses questions, one must make the answers able to break great doubts; only then may questions be posed. If not, one adds empty words and wastes expressions. Take the first question: wisdom has the empty and the real — from where does one get the names "empty" and "real"? It is simply from not distinguishing that bodhisattvas differ [from those of] the four stages, [who] are not called real. In principle there are the provisional and the true; the true is named without distinguishing judgment. The śrāvakas realize the true and real; all teachings are so. How can one say there is no true, and call it only provisional? The name "city" extends to the three kinds; the three kinds are all in the past. Therefore the great nirvāṇa must be named separately the jeweled isle. Not distinguishing the difference of great and small, therefore there is such a question. The wisdom-fruit is divided by persons; the fruit of cessation is one by principle. The principle of the path must be so; why trouble to pose this question? If principle is discussed by persons, principle too differs with persons; if the wisdom-fruit is the same in meaning, the wisdom-fruit too is not two. In the first text, 'ill' [means] that after twelve years beings' delusions increased, so the Buddha too was ill. Translator's notes

Voices. As before, "Answer" is Ji (T1723) and "Now I say / Now I ask" is Zhanran. In units 7, 43, 74, and 121 a comment follows Ji's words without a marker; only the words that are clearly Ji's are put in quotation marks. In unit 28, 子 ("you") is Zhanran's address to Ji.

Lotus quotations. The wording follows the site's Lotus Sūtra: The Parable of the Burning House (prose: "town or village," "only a single gate," "carry them out," "their hearts would surely be drawn," "sitting in the open ground at the crossroads," "swift as the wind," "one single mark, one single kind," "beyond what they had originally hoped for," "I now guarantee this to you myself"); The Burning House in Verse ("That house had grown old," "fallen into sudden ruin," "heard someone say," "I shall make these carts for you," "reached the open ground," the retributions for slander, and "If there is a monk…"); The Parable of the Destitute Son. Site "eon" is changed to "eon."

"So hard to bear and raise" (unit 82). The site renders 生育甚難 "so hard to raise." Because Zhanran discusses 生 (bearing) and 育 (raising) separately, the English adds "bear and."

"Lending and trade" (units 102–103). The site renders 出入息利 "profits from lending and trade." Zhanran's question turns on the literal words 出入 "going out and coming in," which the English gives in the answer.

"One nature, one kind" (unit 47). The sūtra has 一相一種 (site: "one single mark, one single kind"); Zhanran writes 一性一種, translated as written.

The four wisdoms (unit 36). Ji's "wondrous observing wisdom" and "wisdom of equality" are two of the four Yogācāra wisdoms (with the great mirror wisdom and the wisdom that accomplishes what is to be done).

Solitary buddhas and the condition-awakened (unit 62). Ji distinguishes 獨覺 ("solitary buddhas," who attain without a buddha) from 緣覺 ("the condition-awakened," who contemplate the twelve conditions). Both are pratyekabuddhas; the English keeps the two Chinese terms apart.

The Gongyang gloss (unit 68). The quotation explaining 頓 as "exhausted" was not located in the received Gongyang Commentary; it is translated literally, and its sense is uncertain.

Glosses on characters (units 70, 90, 147, 155). Fanqie readings are given as "(pronunciation note)." "Dharma Master [Xuan]ying's Sounds of the Sūtras" is Xuanying's Yiqiejing yinyi. The Picang is written 俾倉 here; it is presumably the Picang dictionary written 埤倉.

Slandering the sūtra (unit 92). Ji's numbering of the retributions and of the six perfections is given in full as he gives it. Good Precepts Sūtra (善戒經) is the Bodhisattva Good Precepts Sūtra (T1582); the Brahmā's Net is the Fanwang jing (T1484).

The destitute son (units 104–145). Ji reads the son's wanderings as stages of a bodhisattva who "has not yet retreated from the great" (未退大); Zhanran's objections repeatedly test that reading against the story. "Rocana's quiescent seat" (unit 135) is the site of the Flower Ornament teaching.

The Vimalakīrti couch (unit 157). Ji quotes Xuanzang's version (Wugoucheng jing, T476); Zhanran answers from Kumārajīva's wording ("the four dhyānas are the couch and seat, born from pure livelihood"). "Golden Grain" is Vimalakīrti's identity as a past tathāgata.
