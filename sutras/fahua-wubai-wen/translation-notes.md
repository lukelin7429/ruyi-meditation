# Five Hundred Questions on the Lotus — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Fahua wubai wen lun* (法華五百問論, CBETA X56, no. 939 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** CBETA X0939, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 一乘 / 三乘 | the one vehicle / the three vehicles | Whether the three are expedient or real is the core of the debate. |
| 五性各別 | the five distinct lineages | Ji's Yogācāra doctrine that some beings can never become buddhas. |
| 開權顯實 | opening the provisional to reveal the real | Zhanran's reading of the Lotus. |
