From the Sewn Jewel to the Dragon Girl

Part 6 closes Fascicle Two and opens Fascicle Three. It runs from the "Prediction for the Five Hundred Disciples" chapter through the "Upholding" chapter. With the parable of the jewel sewn in a sleeping drunkard's robe, Zhanran objects to Ji's account of the "unsettled persons," his reading of the drunkenness as a kind of ignorance, and his five kinds of "peace and happiness." The longest dispute concerns how the Lotus is divided. Ji makes the "Teacher of the Dharma" chapter the start of a nineteen-chapter dissemination section with no further teaching of the one vehicle. Zhanran answers with the Tiantai division into trace gate and origin gate, and argues that the revealing of the Buddha's distant lifespan cannot be dissemination. In the "Teacher of the Dharma" and "Seeing the Jeweled Stūpa" chapters he examines Ji's explanations of the Tathāgata's emissary, the relic of the Dharma-body, the parable of digging for water on a high plateau, the three purifications of the land, the seating of the emanation buddhas, and the moving of the assemblies. In the "Devadatta" chapter, a run of questions ending "Please answer" presses Ji to explain how the dragon girl's swift buddhahood can follow from his own view that a woman's body cannot become a buddha. The part ends early in the "Upholding" chapter.

The "Prediction for the Five Hundred Disciples" Chapter

The Jewel Sewn in the Robe

Chinese Source

問：五百弟子受記品，其義如何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the meaning of the "Prediction for the Five Hundred Disciples" chapter?

Chinese Source

答：論七喻治七增上慢中，第五無定人於下劣心生虗妄解增上慢，為說繫寶珠喻。釋曰：謂彼散亂心實無有定者，宿有大善，不自覺知，不求於大，於下劣心生虗妄解，為第一乘。對治此故，說繫珠喻。今謂論意指前入城以為其人，然不得以餘意消文，以如前說。故論文云：第五人者，顯示過去善根，令憶念故，教入三昧。故他釋云：此是凡夫有學未得定心，執二乘道以為究竟，不知過去大乘善根。今令入三昧，憶念彼時，如醉臥繫珠。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "Among the Treatise's seven parables that remedy the seven [kinds of] overweening pride, the fifth is the overweening pride of the unsettled persons, who give rise to a false understanding in an inferior mind; for them the parable of the jewel sewn [in the robe] is told." The explanation says: "That is, those with scattered minds who in reality have no concentration had great good [roots] in the past but are not aware of it themselves and do not seek the great; in an inferior mind they give rise to a false understanding, taking it as the foremost vehicle. To remedy this, the parable of the sewn jewel is told." Now I say: the Treatise's intent points to those who earlier entered the city as these persons; yet one may not explain the text by other meanings, as said before. Hence the Treatise text says: "As for the fifth person, it shows the past good roots to make them remember, teaching them to enter samādhi." Hence the other's explanation says: "These are ordinary beings and learners who have not yet attained a concentrated mind, who cling to the path of the two vehicles as ultimate and do not know their past Great Vehicle good roots. Now they are made to enter samādhi and remember that time, like [the man] lying drunk with the jewel sewn [in his robe]."

Chinese Source

今問：二乘之人既以小定能知宿種，得小果已，即應自知。若不爾者，今入何定？若有大乘定可入者，乃是已入大門已，應證大理，小乘法中無此定故。況今只是為應得記者說於往緣，即是令其憶念，何須加足入定之言？若云佛加，令入令見，方有是理。論文不云凡夫及以學人入定，何用加之？論第四無上云：令解無上，說繫珠喻。謂說往緣，令生覺解。今謂義同佛加，則能領解。言令入三昧者，令知彼時也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now I ask: since those of the two vehicles can know their past seeds by small concentration, once they have attained the small fruit they should know it themselves. If not, what concentration do they now enter? If there is a Great Vehicle concentration that can be entered, they have already entered the great gate and should realize the great principle, because there is no such concentration in the Dharma of the Lesser Vehicle. Moreover, now [the Buddha] is only telling the past affinity to those who should receive predictions, which is making them remember; why add the words "entering concentration"? If you say that by the Buddha's empowerment they are made to enter and made to see, only then is there such a principle. The Treatise text does not say that ordinary beings and learners enter concentration; why add it? The Treatise's fourth unsurpassed [aspect] says: "To make them understand the unsurpassed, the parable of the sewn jewel is told," that is, telling the past affinity to make them awaken and understand. Now I say: the meaning is the same as the Buddha's empowerment; then they can understand. "Making them enter samādhi" means making them know that time.

Chinese Source

又曰：十無上及七喻中，但云繫珠不云五百。授記者，根熟即記後方說喻，從初為名故名授記。今謂不然，但由文狹入此品中，豈可未記而不領耶？領謂領法，非專領記。若此中領記但領法者，即應有過，故知不要如是通難。若領記者，何以上中領前記後？當知此意不須更通。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "In the ten unsurpassed [aspects] and the seven parables it speaks only of the sewn jewel, not of the five hundred. As for 'receiving predictions': when their faculties were ripe they were predicted, and only afterward was the parable told; it is named from the first part, hence it is named receiving predictions." Now I say: this is not so. It is only because the text is narrow [in scope] that it enters this chapter; how could they not give their understanding before being predicted? Understanding means understanding the Dharma, not solely understanding the prediction. If here understanding the prediction were only understanding the Dharma, there would be a fault; hence we know there is no need to resolve objections in this way. If it is understanding the prediction, why in the upper and middle [rounds] is the understanding before and the prediction after? You should know that this intent needs no further resolution.

又曰：下根滿慈前已領解，五百已下記訖更陳，重明本意解悟分明。今謂滿慈內懷大行外現小迹，故今世尊為發實本，非領解也。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "Among those of lower faculties, Pūrṇa had already given his understanding earlier; after the five hundred and the rest had been predicted, he set it forth again, making clear once more that his original intent and his understanding and awakening were clear." Now I say: Pūrṇa inwardly harbors great practice and outwardly shows small traces; hence the World-Honored One now discloses his real origin; it is not giving understanding.

Pūrṇa is one person, few; hence he is not [counted in the title]." Now I say: the first meaning is not needed.

問：經云法食等者，何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the sūtra's saying about "the joy of the Dharma as food" and so on?

Chinese Source

答曰：攝論四食，一不清淨、二清淨、三淨不淨、四示現依止住食。餘諸經論四食者，謂觸、思、識段。增一阿含九種食，四是世間、五是出世間。世間謂前四，出世謂一禪食、二願食、三念食、四解脫食、五喜食。常共當除世食得出世，五世食資出世食。破裂有漏，於有漏不名為食。又佛地經二食，謂廣大味，喜樂所持可然。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The Mahāyānasaṃgraha's four kinds of food: first, impure; second, pure; third, pure-and-impure; fourth, the food that shows the support of abiding. As for the four foods in the other sūtras and treatises: contact, volition, consciousness, and morsels. The Ekottarika Āgama has nine kinds of food: four are worldly and five transworldly. The worldly are the former four; the transworldly are: first, the food of dhyāna; second, the food of vows; third, the food of mindfulness; fourth, the food of liberation; fifth, the food of joy. Always together, one should remove worldly food and obtain the transworldly; the five worldly foods nourish the transworldly food. [The transworldly] breaks up the defiled; with respect to the defiled it is not called food. Further, the Buddhabhūmi Sūtra has two foods, namely vast flavor, upheld by joy and bliss." That may be so.

問：云何名無價寶珠？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is meant by "a priceless jewel"?

Chinese Source

答曰：寶珠有三：一水精、二如意、三吐金。吐金有三：一下品吐一得千、二中品吐一得萬、三上品吐一得無量喻。於一佛乘分別說三，從小至大如次配之。今謂能吐所吐既殊於一，出三復定，本乘不動不與三同。又問：此珠吐已更能收不？収入一時，能入所入同耶別耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "There are three [kinds of] jewels: first, crystal; second, wish-fulfilling; third, gold-producing. The gold-producing has three: first, the lower grade produces a thousand for one; second, the middle grade produces ten thousand for one; third, the upper grade produces an immeasurable [amount] for one, symbolizing [that] within the one buddha vehicle three are distinguished and taught; they are matched in order from small to great." Now I say: since what produces and what is produced differ from the one, and the three that come out are again fixed, the original vehicle does not move and is not the same as the three. Again I ask: after this jewel has produced, can it take [them] back in? When they are taken in at once, are what takes in and what is taken in the same or different?

Chinese Source

又曰：彼以末那相應名為醉臥，今以第六不共無明名為醉臥之體。今謂若以不共無明名為醉者，若共無明應是醉醒。若取通途迷真起妄而為醉者，以異生位一切無明皆為醉體。又醉與內衣其相何別？既以意識為衣，正是醉時，意識與彼不共無明何別？若其不別，應當繫醉，何名繫衣？若醉即是衣，醉醒應脫。若醉非衣，不共之時如何取別？

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "They take [the ignorance] associated with manas as 'lying drunk'; now we take the unshared ignorance of the sixth [consciousness] as the substance of lying drunk." Now I say: if unshared ignorance is called drunkenness, then shared ignorance should be sobering up from drunkenness. If, generally, being deluded about the true and giving rise to the false is taken as drunkenness, then at the stage of ordinary beings all ignorance is the substance of drunkenness. Further, how do the marks of the drunkenness and the inner robe differ? Since you take the mind-consciousness as the robe, precisely at the time of drunkenness, how does the mind-consciousness differ from that unshared ignorance? If they do not differ, it should be "sewn into the drunkenness"; why "sewn into the robe"? If the drunkenness is the robe, on sobering up it should be taken off. If the drunkenness is not the robe, at the time of the unshared [ignorance], how do you distinguish them?

問：經云：我昔欲令汝，得安樂五欲。五欲者何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The sūtra says: "In the past I wished to bring you peace and happiness [and] the five desires." What are the five desires?

Chinese Source

答曰：即五安樂：一、自性安樂；二、因安樂；三、斷受安樂；四、苦對安樂；五、無惱安樂。或云：佛果五法，清淨法界及以四智。今謂不爾，若等以五欲合五，應云色清淨故般若清淨，餘四亦然。五法清淨即五解脫，解脫即涅槃，涅槃即是安樂。五種解脫悕向慕樂，義當於欲。五義及欲復是安樂，三義俱成。故知五種安樂非圓極義，不得作於法華因果。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "They are the five kinds of peace and happiness: first, the peace and happiness of self-nature; second, the peace and happiness of the cause; third, the peace and happiness of the cutting off of feeling; fourth, the peace and happiness of counteracting suffering; fifth, the peace and happiness free of vexation. Or it is said: the five dharmas of the buddha-fruit, [namely] the pure Dharma-realm and the four wisdoms." Now I say: it is not so. If you equally match the five desires with five [things], you should say: because form is pure, prajñā is pure, and the other four likewise. The purity of the five dharmas is the five liberations; liberation is nirvāṇa; nirvāṇa is peace and happiness. Hoping and longing for the five kinds of liberation corresponds in meaning to desire. The five meanings and desire are again peace and happiness; the three meanings are all accomplished. Hence we know that the five kinds of peace and happiness are not the meaning of the perfect ultimate and cannot be made the cause and fruit of the Lotus.

The "Prediction for Learners and Those Beyond Learning" Chapter

Learners, Ānanda, and the First Cause

Chinese Source

問：來意云何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the intent of [its] coming?

Chinese Source

答：下根之徒略有二類：名高之輩，前品與記；名非高輩，此品與記。今謂不然，亦高亦名或在前，高而不名多在此中，名而不高非記所攝，何況前耶？然諸聖本豈有高卑？迹示前後安將判記？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "Those of lower faculties are of roughly two kinds: those renowned and eminent were given predictions in the previous chapter; those whose renown is not eminent are given predictions in this chapter." Now I say: this is not so. Those both eminent and renowned are perhaps in the former; those eminent but not renowned are mostly in this one; those renowned but not eminent are not included in the predictions — how much less in the former? Yet in the origin of the sages, how could there be high and low? The traces show earlier and later; how can these be used to judge the predictions?

Chinese Source

問：經從爾時至三菩提者，其義何耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the meaning of the sūtra from "At that time" to "awakening"?

Chinese Source

答曰：初說始因也。俱舍云：於三無數劫，逆次逢勝觀，燃燈寶髻佛，初釋迦牟尼。今說中途隨一所逢，非最初也。今謂若釋俱舍得作此說，今釋法華應如壽量，壽量之外應皆云迹，既其迷空王之遠，乍可指寶藏之近。今一代教中行有多種，如舍那因即釋迦因也。迷權之說與理相乖，佛化尚自存權，弟子如何執實？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "This first tells the initial cause. The Abhidharmakośa says: 'In the three incalculable eons, in reverse order, he met Vipaśyin, Dīpaṃkara, and the Buddha Ratnaśikhin; the first was Śākyamuni.' Here [the sūtra] speaks of one he met somewhere along the way, not the very first." Now I say: if explaining the Kośa, one may say this; now, explaining the Lotus, it should be as in the Lifespan: everything outside the Lifespan should be called trace. Since you are deluded about the distance of [the Buddha] King of Emptiness, you might as well point to the nearness of Ratnagarbha. Now in the teaching of the whole lifetime there are many kinds of practice; for instance, the cause of Rocana is the cause of Śākyamuni. A teaching deluded about the provisional runs counter to principle; if even the Buddha's conversion keeps the provisional, how can a disciple cling to [it as] real?

問：經從阿難常樂多聞至斯記，文相如何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What are the textual marks of the sūtra from "Ānanda always delighted in much learning" to "this prediction"?

Chinese Source

答：此陳後行也。華嚴云：譬如貧窮人等故，不唯多聞而能超越，要行助故。彼經又云：若欲求除滅，無量諸過患，應當一切時，發勇猛精進，即能得菩提，要假聞為導。又云：多聞能引樂，多聞攝眾善，多聞捨無義，多聞得涅槃。故知此文且據一相。又云：亦同眾生意樂意趣。今謂若以阿難為非而引證者，只應引初譬如貧窮人、譬如聾聵人等，何事須引讚多聞偈及聞行相假文耶？夫引文證義，先定義宗，次定所引大小、偏圓、同異，況佛引意亦非全毀，只云難與多聞成之前後。阿難雖後持我法藏，故次授記。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "This sets forth the later practice. The Flower Ornament says: 'Like a poor person,' and so on; hence it is not only by much learning that one can go beyond; practice is needed as an aid. That sūtra also says: 'If you wish to seek to remove immeasurable faults and troubles, you should at all times arouse valiant diligence; then you can attain bodhi' — [yet] hearing is needed as a guide. It also says: 'Much learning can bring happiness; much learning gathers the many goods; much learning abandons the meaningless; much learning attains nirvāṇa.' Hence we know that this text speaks for now of one aspect." It also says: "It is also the same as the intention of beings' inclinations." Now I say: if Ānanda is to be shown wrong by citing proofs, you should only cite the first, "like a poor person," "like a deaf person," and so on; why need you cite the verses praising much learning and the texts on hearing and practice depending on each other? In citing texts to prove a meaning, one first settles the tenet of the meaning, and next settles whether what is cited is great or small, partial or perfect, the same or different. Moreover, the Buddha's intent in citing it is not entire disparagement either; it only speaks of the earlier and later accomplishment of Ānanda, with much learning, [and the Buddha]. Though Ānanda [comes] later, he upholds my Dharma treasury; hence he is predicted next.

The "Teacher of the Dharma" Chapter

The Main Section and the Dissemination

Chinese Source

問：來意云何？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is the intent of [its] coming?

Chinese Source

答：初一品序述因由，次八品是正宗。如前信解品云：今此經中唯說一乘。此品又云：開方便門，顯真實相。即以一乘正為經宗，逗不定性，并與授記。後十九品名為流通，非正逗二乘退大心者，更開權顯實，正說一乘之處。雖此品及持微有一乘八部四眾授記，其因言總記略，非更說一乘，逗令修學而與授記，故皆流通。今謂若不正逗，即應傍逗，傍正則應俱是正宗。若是流通，但通於正，何論一乘有之與無？若言八品唯為退大藥艸，何故却更施三而為真實，反以法華而為權耶？故知此品正開三乘之方便，以顯一乘之真實；又是雙開三一之方便，顯非三一之真實。故五佛章及以譬說宿世因緣，只是別明逗彼三根，今此總明重顯別妙。若總非顯實，別亦安是？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The first chapter is the introduction, setting out the causes; the next eight chapters are the main section. As the earlier 'Belief and Understanding' chapter says: 'Now in this sūtra only the one vehicle is taught.' This chapter also says: 'It opens the gate of skillful means and reveals the mark of true reality.' That is, the one vehicle is properly the cardinal purport of the sūtra, adapted to those of indeterminate nature, along with the giving of predictions. The last nineteen chapters are called the dissemination; they are not where [the sūtra] is properly adapted to those of the two vehicles who retreated from the great mind, further opening the provisional to reveal the real and properly teaching the one vehicle. Though this chapter and the 'Upholding' slightly [mention] the one vehicle and predictions for the eight groups and the fourfold assembly, their causes are spoken of generally and the predictions briefly; it is not further teaching the one vehicle, adapting it to make them study and giving them predictions; hence all are dissemination." Now I say: if it is not properly adapted, it should be adapted auxiliarily; [whether] auxiliary or principal, both should be the main section. Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What about the chapter title?

Chinese Source

答：論云：持力有三，謂法師、安樂、行、勸持。釋曰：法師通能、所，安樂唯所持，此品唯能持。今謂論文但云持力有三，故一持字通於能、所，故知三品皆通能、所。如法師是人，師從故法；安樂是行，藉能行人；勸持灼然，義兼能、所。法師無法，非法師故。安樂是果，行是能趣，望果行因，有人方達。勸持是人，所勸是法，如何偏判各云能、所？

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "The Treatise says: the power of upholding has three [chapters], namely the Teacher of the Dharma, the Peaceful Practices, and the Encouragement to Uphold." The explanation says: "The Teacher of the Dharma covers both what upholds and what is upheld; the Peaceful [Practices] is only what is upheld; this chapter is only what upholds." Now I say: the Treatise text says only that the power of upholding has three; hence the single word "uphold" covers both what upholds and what is upheld; hence we know that all three chapters cover both what upholds and what is upheld. As the Teacher of the Dharma is a person: the teacher follows, hence there is the Dharma; the Peaceful [Practices] is practice, depending on a person who can practice; the Encouragement to Uphold clearly includes in meaning both what upholds and what is upheld. A Teacher of the Dharma without the Dharma is not a Teacher of the Dharma. Peace [and ease] is the fruit, practice is what goes toward it; practice as the cause directed toward the fruit is achieved only when there is a person. The one who encourages and upholds is a person; what is encouraged is the Dharma; how can you judge one-sidedly, calling each [only] what upholds or what is upheld?

Reading Notes for Part 6

Translator's notes

Voices. "Answer" is Ji (Fahua xuanzan, T1723, fascicle 8); "Now I say / Now I ask" is Zhanran. Several short comments follow Ji's words without a marker ("This may be so" in units 12 and 15, "The citation is very apt…" in unit 44, "To match it with Mañjuśrī's excellent virtue…" in unit 132). They are left outside the quotation marks as Zhanran's. In unit 91 (as in Part 5, unit 77), 又云 introduces Zhanran's own summary or question and is rendered "Again:". From unit 130 on, Zhanran closes his objections with "Please answer" (請答).

Fascicle division. Units 0–2 are the end title of Fascicle Two and the heading of Fascicle Three ("One Hundred and Twenty-Five Questions").

Lotus quotations. The wording follows the site's Lotus Sūtra: The Jeweled Robe and the Priceless Pearl ("official business," "priceless jewel," "Universal Brightness," "the joy of the Dharma"); The Teacher of the Dharma ("an emissary of the Tathāgata, sent by the Tathāgata himself, doing the Tathāgata's own work," "the hardest to believe and hardest to understand," "the Tathāgata's own hand rests upon his head," the high plateau and dry earth, "profound, firm, and deep," the room, robe, and seat); The Jeweled Stūpa ("Whoever is able to uphold it thereby upholds the Buddha's own body"); Devadatta (Prajñākūṭa, "this is hard to believe"). Where Zhanran's wording differs from the received sūtra (unit 21 "peace and happiness [and] the five desires"; unit 74 "profound, firm, deep, and far"), his words are translated.

Main section and dissemination (units 31–38). Ji divides the sūtra into one introductory chapter, eight chapters of main section ("Skillful Means" to "Learners and Those Beyond Learning"), and nineteen of dissemination. Zhanran answers with the Tiantai division: in the trace gate, "Teacher of the Dharma" to "Peaceful Practices" is dissemination, while the origin gate has its own main section ("Welling Up" second half, "Lifespan," first half of "Distinguishing Merits"). "The age of one's parents cannot but be known" (unit 33) is from the Analects (4.21). "The three rules" (unit 37) are the room, robe, and seat of the "Teacher of the Dharma" chapter.

Sources located. The Sūtra of the Samādhi Surpassing the Sun's Brightness (unit 136) is T638; there the monk is named Shangdu (上度), while T1723 reads 上座 ("a senior monk"). Zhanran's spelling is kept. The killing-of-sages comparison (unit 48) is in the Pravara-devarāja-pariprcchā (勝天王般若波羅蜜經, T231), where the offense of slandering the sūtra is said to exceed killing countless sages; Zhanran (and Ji) say "buddhas." The Daśabhūmikavibhāṣā passage (unit 77) and the Abhidharmakośa verse on the three asaṃkhyeyas (unit 27) are quoted as in T1723. "Walking the great straight path, because there are no hindrances" (unit 60) is the Immeasurable Meanings (T276) per Tiantai usage; the phrase was not located in the vault copy. The Great Collection passages (units 48, 53) and the Flower Ornament verses (units 29, 114) were not checked against the sūtras.

King of Emptiness and Ratnagarbha (unit 27). 空王 is the Buddha King of Emptiness, under whom Śākyamuni and Ānanda aroused the mind (sūtra, "Learners" chapter); 寶藏 is presumably the Buddha Ratnagarbha of the Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka, before whom Śākyamuni made his vows.

Unshared ignorance (unit 20). 不共無明 is the Yogācāra "unshared" (independent) ignorance associated with the sixth consciousness, as against ignorance that arises together with other afflictions.

Sumeru-Lamp-King (unit 100). The site's Vimalakīrti name for 燈王.

The dragon palace (units 127–128). Unit 127 breaks off after "at the beginning of the sūtra and," and unit 128 begins with 答 ("Answer") before what is clearly the rest of the question. The first 答 is put in parentheses; the question is that Mañjuśrī is listed in the assembly at the start of the sūtra yet here comes out of the dragon palace.

Glosses (units 10, 104, 124). Fanqie readings are given as "(pronunciation note)"; characters discussed as characters are kept in Chinese.

Probable copying errors, translated as corrected. Unit 103: 羯却新來 is read as 揭 ("lifted off"). Unit 126: 待者請還 is read as 侍者 ("the attendant"). Unit 151: 起諭諂 is read as 諛諂 (T1723 has 諂諛). Unit 128: the misplaced 答 (see above).
