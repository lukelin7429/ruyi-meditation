The One Vehicle, the Wheel of the Dharma, and Śāriputra's Prediction

This part finishes the first fascicle of the Five Hundred Questions and opens the second. It continues Zhanran's reading of Ji's discussion of the one vehicle in the "Skillful Means" chapter: the five gates of its substance, why the fruit is still called a "vehicle," the three senses of "one," Ji's four kinds of merging, his ten purposes for teaching the one vehicle, and how the Lotus differs from the Śrīmālā, the Nirvāṇa, and the Immeasurable Meanings. Zhanran's recurring objection is that Ji reads the Lotus through Yogācāra treatises that were written to rebuke the two vehicles, and so keeps a class of beings permanently outside the one vehicle. Shorter exchanges follow on the five turbidities, the three natures in the verse "the seed of buddhahood arises from conditions," the wheel of the Dharma, and the rarity of buddhas. After the fascicle break, the "Parable" chapter begins: Śāriputra's joy and his statement of understanding, the six phrases defining a true child of the Buddha, the seven parables of Vasubandhu's Treatise, and Ji's six gates on prediction. The longest objection concerns Ji's claim that predicted śrāvakas reach buddhahood by prolonging their present bodies through a special concentration; Zhanran answers that the treatises Ji cites speak of a transformation body, not the present body.

The Five Gates of the Substance and the Name "Vehicle"

Chinese Source

又云：此真實體，五門不同：一、詮旨，謂理教。今謂若以能詮為教，所詮為理，故不應以二共為體。二、因果，三、福智，四、覺寂，五、二利。今謂因果二利，亦與前同。福智在因，覺寂有果，何須更以四法而為真實變化？五門之名，望前都無別義。又曰：三六如所引之法身，即是乘體。佛性含三乘語通，無足以顯一乘之體。今謂皆是乘果及具度耳。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "This true and real substance differs in five gates: first, the expressing and the purport, namely principle and teaching." Now I say: if what expresses is taken as the teaching and what is expressed as principle, then the two should not jointly be taken as the substance. "Second, cause and fruit; third, merit and wisdom; fourth, awakening and quiescence; fifth, the two benefits." Now I say: cause and fruit and the two benefits are also the same as before. Merit and wisdom lie in the cause, awakening and quiescence exist in the fruit; why must the four dharmas further be taken as the true and real transformation? The names of the five gates, compared with the earlier ones, have no distinct meaning at all. It further says: "The Dharma-body cited in [items] three and six is the substance of the vehicle. 'Buddha-nature' contains the three vehicles; the word is general and is not enough to reveal the substance of the one vehicle." Now I say: these are all merely the fruit of the vehicle and its fittings and measures.

Chinese Source

又曰：釋名者，乘運載。今謂運至何處？所載者何？從何處來？有何資具互為大小？車主是誰？駕者幾人？經歷幾處（云云）。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "Explaining the name: 'vehicle' means carrying." Now I say: carried to what place? What is carried? From what place does it come? What equipment does it have, that [vehicles] are great or small relative to one another? Who is the owner of the cart? How many draw it? Through how many places does it pass? (and so on).

Chinese Source

問：曾聞人問：因行名乘，果運既息，何得名乘？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: I once heard someone ask: practice in the cause is called a vehicle; since carrying ceases at the fruit, how can [the fruit] be called a vehicle?

Chinese Source

答曰：五義名乘：一者、因通二運，果廣運他。今謂此正答所問。二者、能體通二運，能體並名乘；果中體有能，非無亦乘攝。今謂此不答所問，何須列之本問因果？不應更以有無足之。三者、二乘唯自運，小尚得名乘，況佛廣運他，何理非乘攝？今謂此亦不答所聞本問因果所得之名，徒將大小以相對。又下二句其言不成，應云：況佛廣利他，豈得非乘攝？四者、因中理智能雙運，二用勝故得名乘；果中理智因修成，因乘種類亦乘攝。今謂種類之名非正收，故知不如從初答。五者、因中二運常增進，作用勝故得名乘；果中自運窮未來，令他增進得乘攝。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "It is called a vehicle for five meanings. First, in the cause it extends to both kinds of carrying; in the fruit it broadly carries others." Now I say: this correctly answers what was asked. "Second, substance and capacity both extend to the two kinds of carrying, and capacity and substance are both called the vehicle; in the fruit the substance has the capacity, and not lacking it, it too is included in the vehicle." Now I say: this does not answer what was asked; why list it? The original question was about cause and fruit; one should not further pad it out with having and lacking. "Third, the two vehicles carry only themselves; if even the small gets the name 'vehicle,' how much more the Buddha, who broadly carries others — by what principle is he not included in the vehicle?" Now I say: this too does not answer the original question that was asked, about the names obtained by cause and fruit; it merely sets great and small against each other. Moreover, the last two lines do not make sense; it should say: "How much more the Buddha, who broadly benefits others — how could he not be included in the vehicle?" "Fourth, in the cause principle and wisdom can carry both ways; because the two functions are superior it is named a vehicle. In the fruit, principle and wisdom are accomplished by cultivation in the cause; being of the same kind as the vehicle of the cause, it too is included in the vehicle." Now I say: the name "of the same kind" is not a proper inclusion; hence we know it is not as good as answering from the first. "Fifth, in the cause the two kinds of carrying constantly increase and advance; because the activity is superior it is named a vehicle. In the fruit, carrying oneself extends to the end of the future, and causing others to increase and advance, it is included in the vehicle."

Chinese Source

又云：初四隨他不正答，後一隨自正義答。今謂初一乃可正義答，後四相從因起答。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "The first four answer by following others and are not correct answers; the last one answers with the correct meaning, following [our] own [view]." Now I say: the first one may be the correct answer; the last four are answers that arise by following from the cause.

The Meaning of "One"

Chinese Source

問：何名為一？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: What is meant by "one"?

Chinese Source

答曰：有三義名一：一者、簡別故名一；二者、破別故名一；三者、會別故名一。言簡別者，昔三、今一，一實、二權，至佛極界方名為一，非簡別三。論云：無二乘者，無二涅槃體。又云：我設是方便，令得入佛慧。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "It is called one for three meanings: first, it is called one because it distinguishes [itself] from the separate; second, it is called one because it breaks the separate; third, it is called one because it merges the separate. As for distinguishing from the separate: formerly three, now one; the one is real and the two are provisional; only at the ultimate realm of the Buddha is it called one; it does not distinguish [itself] from three. The Treatise says: 'No two vehicles means there are no two substances of nirvāṇa.' It also says: 'I devised these skillful means to cause them to enter the Buddha's wisdom.'"

Chinese Source

又云：如火宅之許，以三車出宅門已，但與牛車。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "As in the promise of the burning house: after they have gone out of the gate of the house by the three carts, he gives only the ox cart."

Chinese Source

又云：二十秊中常令除糞。今謂自昔至今二乘難破，逐難為語，如向所引。若以此文即定法華一部文者，何不引序中諸求三乘人，若有疑悔者，佛當為除斷，令盡無有餘？菩薩聞是法，疑網皆已除，未聞未解，不名善行菩薩之道。願賜我等三種寶車，許三索三。佛以方便力示以三乘教，三皆方便、皆權、皆索，故知白牛非三中一。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "For twenty years he constantly had him clear away dung." Now I say: from the past until now the two vehicles have been hard to break, and [the sūtra] speaks according to the difficulty, as in the passages just cited. If you use these passages to settle the text of the whole Lotus, why not cite from the Introduction: "For all who seek the three vehicles, if any harbor doubt or regret, the Buddha shall remove and cut it off for them, until none remains at all"? [Or] "Hearing this Dharma, the bodhisattvas have had every net of doubt removed"; "those who have not yet heard and not yet understood are not called practicing the bodhisattva path well"; "Please give us the three kinds of jeweled carts" — three were promised and three were asked for. "By the power of skillful means he showed the teaching of the three vehicles" — all three are skillful means, all are provisional, all are asked for; hence we know the white ox is not one of the three.

Chinese Source

又曰：一理簡教，二果簡因。今謂若以一理簡於三教，簡二歸於何處？菩薩四階位耶？共地位耶？五十位耶？故佛度五人名六羅漢，三乘同坐所證無殊，簡因因本不殊，至果果無別，簡教教本不同，但理無異趣，故證法皆同。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "First, principle distinguishes [itself] from the teaching; second, the fruit distinguishes [itself] from the cause." Now I say: if the one principle distinguishes [itself] from the three teachings, to what place do the two that are distinguished return? To the four stages of the bodhisattva? To the stages of the shared ground? To the fifty stages? Hence when the Buddha delivered the five men, they were called the six arhats; the three vehicles sat together, and what they realized was no different. Distinguishing the cause: the causes are fundamentally not different; arriving at the fruit: the fruits are not distinct; distinguishing the teaching: the teachings are fundamentally not the same, but the principle has no differing direction; hence the dharma realized is the same for all.

Chinese Source

又云三乘疑佛為除斷者，身子除疑耳。經文三乘竝除，釋義但歸身子，違經背義當奈之何？雖引論文但云無二，但不見論釋開等中三乘同得，若但為二不應云三。淨名菩薩尚自被彈，於法華中豈得不會？彈本在會，教旨可知。若不會者，婆沙四階、大品共地、瓔珞賢聖、華嚴初住，而此四者所證法身同耶？別耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says that the doubts of the three vehicles which the Buddha removes and cuts off are only Śāriputra's doubts. The sūtra text removes [the doubts of] all three vehicles alike, yet the explanation assigns it only to Śāriputra; going against the sūtra and turning its back on the meaning, what can be done about it? Though it cites the Treatise's text, [the Treatise] only says "no two"; but one does not see in the Treatise's explanation of "opening" and the rest that the three vehicles attain together; if it were only for the two, it should not say three. If even the bodhisattvas of the Vimalakīrti were rebuked, how could they not be merged in the Lotus? The rebuke is at root for the sake of merging; the purport of the teaching can be known. If they are not merged, then the four stages of the Vibhāṣā, the shared ground of the Larger [Prajñā], the worthies and sages of the Jeweled Necklace, and the first abiding of the Flower Ornament — is the Dharma-body realized by these four the same, or different?

Chinese Source

又曰：勘梵本云無第二第三，此自與前難會義同，豈能合於願賜我等三種寶車，亦云願賜我等第二第三耶？次破二者，準望可知。

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "Checking the Sanskrit text, it says 'no second and no third.'" This is in itself the same as the meaning of merging objected to above; how can it accord with "Please give us the three kinds of jeweled carts"? Does it also say "Please give us the second and the third"? Next, as for breaking the two, it can be known by comparison.

Ji's Four Kinds of Merging

Chinese Source

又釋會云：有四義故：一者、會教，如無垢稱一音說法。今謂彼經若有一音，即是彼經已會，何須更至法華方會？若彼已會，何故彼經仍云種敗？二者、會理，寶性論云：三獸度河，河性無差，得水淺深。今謂釋法華理，應引今教，今教無文，方求外部，況部有會，方乃求之？求於本無，何名求耶？若諸經無文，何論獨有？論無經有，理乃可然；論有經無，終無此理。得水名同，應無三獸，獸三仍別，會義不成。若以寶性釋法華，則二乘至於法華，猶住聲聞、緣覺。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also explains merging, saying: "There are four meanings. First, merging the teaching, as in the Vimalakīrti's teaching of the Dharma with a single voice." Now I say: if that sūtra has the single voice, then that sūtra has already merged; why need one wait until the Lotus to merge? If it has already merged there, why does that sūtra still speak of rotten seeds? "Second, merging the principle: the Ratnagotra[vibhāga] Treatise says: 'Three animals cross a river; the nature of the river is not different, but they reach the water at different depths.'" Now I say: to explain the principle of the Lotus one should cite the present teaching; only when the present teaching has no text does one look to other works — how much more should one look there only when [another] work has merging? Looking for what is fundamentally absent, how can it be called looking? If the sūtras have no text, how could a treatise alone have it? That a treatise lacks it while the sūtra has it is reasonable; that a treatise has it while the sūtra lacks it — there is no such principle. If the name "reaching the water" is the same, there should be no three animals; if the animals remain three and distinct, the meaning of merging does not hold. If one explains the Lotus by the Ratnagotra, then the two vehicles, on reaching the Lotus, still abide as śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas.

Chinese Source

又云：下文高原得水，今謂此與寶性同耶？別耶？寶性尚住二乘，此經同記作佛，況高原對水？以昔異今，不得引證會教文也。三、會行者，鶖子云：為是究竟法？為是所行道？今謂幸有汝等所行之文，如何引昔迴遑之教，以證今經會行語？

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "The text below [speaks of] reaching water on the high plateau." Now I say: is this the same as the Ratnagotra, or different? The Ratnagotra still abides in the two vehicles, while this sūtra predicts that all alike will become buddhas — how much less can the high plateau be matched with the water [of the river]? Since the former differs from the present, it cannot be cited as proof of the text on merging the teaching. "Third, merging practice: Śāriputra says: 'Is this the ultimate Dharma? Or is this the path one is to practice?'" Now I say: there is fortunately the text "What you are practicing [is the bodhisattva path]"; how can one cite a teaching of former hesitation to prove the words about merging practice in the present sūtra?

Chinese Source

又曰：我設是方便，令得入佛慧。今謂亦不應引佛施權智用證二乘會行之文。四、會乘者，下經文云：為求牛車出於火宅。今謂若二乘至此應求行果，何以證乘？況出宅之牛車非入大之寶乘，若出宅車是等賜車，已乘出宅何須更求？則昔教已會，何須至今？

Ru-Yi Translation

It further says: "I devised these skillful means to cause them to enter the Buddha's wisdom." Now I say: one also should not cite the Buddha's use of provisional wisdom in establishing the provisional to prove the text on the two vehicles' merging of practice. "Fourth, merging the vehicle: the sūtra text below says: 'to seek the ox-cart, they came out of the burning house.'" Now I say: if the two vehicles at this point should seek practice and fruit, how does this prove the vehicle? Moreover, the ox-cart of leaving the house is not the jeweled vehicle of entering the great. If the cart of leaving the house is the cart given equally, then having already ridden it out of the house, why need they seek further? Then the former teaching had already merged; why wait until now?

Chinese Source

又云：雖依諸教，而法華中顯會教理，密會行果。今謂若顯密二文同在今教，今教之中何處是顯？何文是密？聲聞於此專求行果，應行果是顯，聞教得記，二顯不虗。夫云密者，唯自己知而眾不知，或眾皆知而己不知，於此何人不知得記，而以四法分顯密耶？若準前以無量義經而望法華，則云法華正明行果，則應行果名之為顯，何以教理却為顯耶？若是密者，法華仍是覆相明一，教仍在權，何名入實？若以微密為密教者，此經教理非麤非權，故皆不可。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "Though it relies on the various teachings, within the Lotus it merges teaching and principle explicitly and merges practice and fruit in a hidden manner." Now I say: if the two texts, explicit and hidden, are both in the present teaching, where in the present teaching is the explicit? Which text is the hidden? The śrāvakas here single-mindedly seek practice and fruit, so practice and fruit should be explicit; hearing the teaching they receive predictions, so the two being explicit is not empty. What is called "hidden" means either that only oneself knows and the assembly does not, or that the whole assembly knows and oneself does not; here, who does not know of receiving the prediction, that one should divide explicit and hidden by the four dharmas? If, as before, one compares the Lotus with the Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, then one says the Lotus properly clarifies practice and fruit; then practice and fruit should be called explicit; why are teaching and principle instead explicit? If they are hidden, then the Lotus still clarifies the one with its marks covered, and the teaching remains in the provisional; how can it be called entering the real? If one takes the subtle and hidden as the secret teaching, the teaching and principle of this sūtra are neither coarse nor provisional; hence none of this is acceptable.

The True and the Skillful in the Present Sūtra

Chinese Source

問：若真實方便相對，今經此二，何者是耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: If the true and real and skillful means are paired, which of these two is the present sūtra?

Chinese Source

答曰：此經正以一乘因果以為真體，教為方便。今問：若以實教對實理等而為此二，則應教為行因，行為證因。若棄行唯教，有言無行，何可師之？若以方便教，而真實之方便彌不可也。況因果二法不名一體，具如前問。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "This sūtra properly takes the cause and fruit of the one vehicle as the true substance, and the teaching as skillful means." Now I ask: if the real teaching is paired with the real principle and so on to make these two, then the teaching should be the cause of practice, and practice the cause of realization. If one discards practice and keeps only the teaching, there are words without practice; how could one take it as a teacher? If one takes the teaching as skillful means, then the skillful means of the true and real is even less acceptable. Moreover, the two dharmas of cause and fruit are not called one substance, as fully set out in the earlier questions.

Chinese Source

又云：今此小義雖未動於智海，如愚所知頗亦絕於今古，博識深智當自詳之。今謂自媒自衒士女惡聞，然此法華一乘為主，攢一代教總括諸乘，大事因緣於茲適遂，降為小義寧免自傷？如以祖考為後生、以大王為小吏，有耳識者莫不寒心，唯佛與佛乃能究盡，豈有愚見能絕古今？衡岳台崖雙弘禪慧，靈山親稟妙悟斯宗，尚云：佛法不思議，唯教相難解，二乘及菩薩，尚所不能測，何況諸凡夫，而欲判此事？譬如生盲人，分別日輪相，欲判虗空界，一切諸色像，而言了達者，畢竟無是事。

Ru-Yi Translation

It also says: "Now this small meaning of mine, though it has not stirred the ocean of wisdom, as far as my foolishness knows, is rather unmatched past and present; those of broad learning and deep wisdom should examine it for themselves." Now I say: a gentleman or lady who arranges their own match and promotes themselves is loathed by those who hear of it. Yet this Lotus takes the one vehicle as its master, gathers the teachings of a whole lifetime, and includes all the vehicles; the great matter of [the one great] cause is here brought to fulfillment. To lower it to a "small meaning" — how can one avoid harming oneself? It is like taking one's ancestors for latecomers, or a great king for a petty official; of those with ears and understanding, none would not shudder. "Only a buddha, together with another buddha, can fathom [it] to its very limit"; how could a foolish view be unmatched past and present? Hengyue and Taiya both spread meditation and wisdom, and on Vulture Peak personally received and wondrously awakened to this school; yet even they said: "The Buddha's Dharma is inconceivable; only the marks of the teaching are hard to understand. Those of the two vehicles and the bodhisattvas still cannot fathom it; how much less can ordinary beings wish to judge this matter? It is like a person blind from birth discerning the marks of the sun's disk, or wishing to judge the realm of space and all the forms and images within it, and claiming to have understood them thoroughly — there is ultimately no such thing."

Ji's Ten Purposes for Teaching the One Vehicle

Chinese Source

問：說此一乘為有義意？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Is there a purpose in teaching this one vehicle?

Chinese Source

答曰：凡有十意：一、為不定性二乘。今問：何不辨於此土他土、我於餘國作佛等文，彼土所說非法華耶？若非法華，何以經云得聞是經？二、任持所餘不定種性，謂六住菩薩退依二乘涅槃，故云菩薩聞是法，疑網皆已除。今謂向不定性二乘即此六住等退者耶？況退大已依小涅槃即名二乘，何以復引證菩薩耶？若此屬退大，尚應名決定，況退大者不必皆從六住退也。經稱六者，據其退位耳。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "There are ten purposes in all. First, for those of the two vehicles of indeterminate nature." Now I ask: why not discuss this land and other lands, and texts such as "I shall become a buddha in another land" — is what is taught in that land not the Lotus? If it is not the Lotus, why does the sūtra say "will come to hear this very sūtra"? "Second, to sustain the rest of indeterminate lineage, namely bodhisattvas of the sixth abiding who retreat and rely on the nirvāṇa of the two vehicles; hence it says, 'Hearing this Dharma, the bodhisattvas have had every net of doubt removed.'" Now I say: are those of the two vehicles of indeterminate nature just mentioned the same as these who retreat from the sixth abiding and so on? Moreover, those who have retreated from the great and already rely on the small nirvāṇa are called the two vehicles; why then cite them as proof [of what is said of] bodhisattvas? If they belong to those who retreated from the great, they should still be called determined; moreover, those who retreated from the great did not necessarily all retreat from the sixth abiding. The sūtra speaks of the sixth with reference to the stage of retreat.

Chinese Source

三者、法等故，行乘雖異，真如無差。今問：若理性真如名無差者，三途四趣皆悉無差，豈三乘耶？若已得真如名無差者，地前菩薩尚自未得，何關二乘？又無差者，差定性不？若差定性，則非無差。又定性差，為待性定方乃差之？為未定前即便差耶？若未定差者，法界眾生幾許應當入定性耶？故不可也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now I say: this too is not familiar with texts of rebuke, so that what is cited contradicts itself before and after, and citing again contradicts the meaning just set up. Saying those bent on quiescence are not ignorant of the Dharma also contradicts the Treatise's words "not yet ripe." Hence one should not take the teaching of rebuke to prove the true and real teaching of the Lotus. Hence before the Lotus all bodhisattvas, in contrast with those of the two vehicles, had to say [the latter] extinguished their seeds.

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Is there further teaching?

Chinese Source

答曰：亦如涅槃八六四二。今謂此亦正當趣寂，能發何事？節節前後迴惑。若云八六四二，亦云不解我意，諍論之事已如前問。又曰：故知趣寂定不作佛。今謂定說正當諍論之文，此乃不知佛意兩遮遮於雙諍。若得佛意乃名雙是，趣寂不發施權故是，若言能發顯實故是，若引諍論之文還成諍論之事，如此豈成體佛意耶？故一家判一切實等四句不同，成前逗後彌復可依，知論弘權方了教旨。

Ru-Yi Translation

Answer: "Also like the eight, six, four, and two of the Nirvāṇa." Now I say: this too applies exactly to those bent on quiescence; what can they give rise to? At each step, before and after, [you] turn about in confusion. If [you cite] the eight, six, four, and two, it also says "did not understand my intent"; the matter of disputes is as questioned above. It further says: "Hence we know those bent on quiescence definitely do not become buddhas." Now I say: "definitely" belongs exactly to the texts of dispute; this is not knowing the Buddha's intent, which negates both, negating the twofold dispute. If one gets the Buddha's intent, it is called "both right": those bent on quiescence not giving rise is right as establishing the provisional; saying they can give rise is right as revealing the real. If one cites the texts of dispute, it again becomes a matter of dispute; how can this be embodying the Buddha's intent? Hence our school judges the four phrases — "all real" and so on — as different, completing the former and leading on to the latter, all the more to be relied upon; knowing that the treatise spreads the provisional, one then understands the purport of the teaching.

Chinese Source

答曰：有人云：身子及四大弟子為上根，五百為中根，學無學為下根。今解不然。華嚴經初說身子是化，入法界品云將六千弟子從自房出，文殊為說十種之法發菩提心，即此經中授記者是，故非應化。今問：華嚴是佛初成道說，身子於彼已能發心，何故初見頞鞞方除邪見，淨名訶斥宴坐非真，至般若中被加方說，法華之始疑悔欲除，聞方便會三乃稱歎領解？若彼文是化，豈此說却非？亦由不了華嚴長遠，復由不見聾啞之文。聾啞望法界品文，當部乃自相翻，曉相違之大旨，不會法亦自通，未達八教之宗，同亦久成永別。

If in that text he is a transformation, how is he instead not one in this teaching? This also comes from not understanding the long [span] of the Flower Ornament, and again from not seeing the text about the deaf and mute. The deaf and mute, compared with the text of the chapter on Entering the Dharma-Realm, contradict each other within the same work; understand the great purport of the contradiction, and even without merging, the Dharma penetrates of itself. Not reaching the tenets of the eight teachings, even what is the same becomes, after long, forever separate.

Reading Notes for Part 3

Translator's notes

Voices. As in Parts 1–2: "Answer" reports Ji's Profound Praise (T1723), "Now I say / Now I ask" is Zhanran, and "It further says / It also says" introduces more of Ji. Most of Ji's wording can be checked in T1723 (the one-vehicle section, the gloss on "the twelve hundred arhats," the six phrases on the true child of the Buddha). In units 83–86, Zhanran's objection continues across several short CBETA lines without a new marker.

Lotus quotations. Wording follows the site's Lotus Sūtra: "For all who seek the three vehicles…" (The Assembly and the Six Signs); "I devised these skillful means…," "no second, and no third," "The buddhas, honored among the two-footed…," "Knowing the foremost quiescent cessation…," "uttered even once, 'Homage to the Buddha,'" "the twelve hundred arhats, all of them too, shall become buddhas," "That buddhas arise and appear in the world is a thing remote and hard to encounter" (The Buddha's Hesitation and the Turning of the Wheel); "if such a person encounters another buddha" (Skillful Means: The Buddha's Wisdom); "I shall become a buddha in another land" (Sixteen Buddhas from One Teacher); the Śāriputra passages, the Flower Light prediction, and "eight intersecting roads" (The Parable of the Burning House); "For twenty years…" (The Parable of the Destitute Son). The site's "eon" is changed to "eon."

"Only the marks of the teaching are hard to understand" (unit 18). The verse attributed to the Tiantai masters is quoted in The Vajra Scalpel (T1932) as words of "the Great Master at the end of his classification of the teachings," that is, Zhiyi; its place in Zhiyi's own works was not located. "Hengyue and Taiya" are Huisi of Mount Heng and Zhiyi of Mount Tiantai.

Ji's ten purposes (unit 20). They come from the Mahāyānasaṃgraha verse that Ji quotes in T1723 ("to draw in one class, and to sustain the rest…"). "Bodhisattvas of the sixth abiding who retreat" refers to the Jeweled Necklace account of Śāriputra's retreat at the sixth abiding.

Five flavors (units 26–27). "Paramārthasamudgata" (勝義生) is the questioner of the Saṃdhinirmocana chapter that sets up the three periods. Zhanran's point is that the Saṃdhinirmocana's "third period" can only rank sūtras taught before it.

"The twenty-three paired disputes" (unit 66). Rendered literally. The Nirvāṇa Sūtra (T374) contains the passages Ji and Zhanran cite ("my disciples did not understand my intent"; "the lion's roar is called the definitive teaching," T374 fascicle 27); how Zhanran counts twenty-three pairs was not traced.

The rarity of buddhas (unit 68). Ji's own text (T1723) reads "after twelve eons the Constellation Eon (星宿劫) begins, and its first buddha…"; our text has "Adornment Eon" (莊嚴劫), which is the past eon. Translated as written. 鶻盧支 is rendered Rucika, the last buddha of the Fortunate Eon.

Subhūti's words (unit 85). "The wisdom-eye I have obtained…" is from the Diamond Sūtra (T235), not the Lotus.

The seven parables of the Treatise (unit 78). Vasubandhu's Treatise on the Lotus (T1519) pairs seven parables with seven kinds of pride. 有定人／無定人 is rendered "settled / unsettled persons," because Zhanran plays on 定 as both "concentration" and "fixed [nature]." Alternatively: "persons with concentration / without concentration."

The true child of the Buddha (unit 98). The six phrases are from the Yogācārabhūmi fascicle 84, quoted in T1723. Where our text has 妾 ("concubine"), Ji's text (T1723) has 婢 ("slave girl"); the Mahāyānasaṃgraha line likewise reads 婢子 in T1723.

Birth-and-death of change (unit 106). Ji cites the Xianyang shengjiao lun (T1602) and the Vijñaptimātratā-siddhi (T1585) on the "concentration of the farthest limit" (邊際定) by which a predicted arhat prolongs his body. Zhanran counters with the Aṅgulimāla Sūtra and the Śrīmālā's "three kinds of mind-made bodies." "Three hundred yojanas … two hundred more" alludes to the Phantom City parable.

Response-transformation śrāvakas (units 115–118). "The deaf and mute" refers to the Flower Ornament's statement that the great śrāvakas at its assembly were as if deaf and mute.

Glosses on names. Namo, vandana (伴談), vandi (伴題), and the older henan (和南) follow Ji's transcriptions; Sarvārthasiddha is Ji's form of Siddhārtha. 達磨多羅 (unit 48) is read as Dharmatrāta; the identification is uncertain.

Probable copying errors, translated as corrected. Unit 3: 所聞 read as 所問. Unit 65: the opening 同 read as 問 ("Question"). Unit 70: 𤘆 read as 牾. Unit 106: 無邊際定 read as 無[漏]邊際定 ("undefiled concentration of the farthest limit"). Units 22 and 58 end mid-sentence in CBETA; the English stops with a dash or colon.
