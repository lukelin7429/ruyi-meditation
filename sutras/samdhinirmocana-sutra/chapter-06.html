Chapter 6 · 分別瑜伽品第六

Analyzing Yogic Practice

Maitreya Bodhisattva asks how bodhisattvas cultivate śamatha and vipaśyanā in the Great Vehicle. The Buddha answers through the objects of meditation, consciousness-only images, the knowledge of Dharma and meaning, the removals accomplished by emptiness, and the path by which yoga becomes a definitive teaching.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: chapter 6 complete Source: Taishō T16, no. 676 Chinese translator: Xuanzang

The Basis for Śamatha and Vipaśyanā 止觀所依

Chinese Source

爾時慈氏菩薩摩訶薩白佛言：「世尊！菩薩何依何住，於大乘中修奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！當知菩薩法假安立，及不捨阿耨多羅三藐三菩提願，為依、為住，於大乘中修奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「如世尊說四種所緣境事：一者、有分別影像所緣境事；二者、無分別影像所緣境事；三者、事邊際所緣境事；四者、所作成辦所緣境事。於此四中，幾是奢摩他所緣境事？幾是毘鉢舍那所緣境事？幾是俱所緣境事？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！一是奢摩他所緣境事，謂無分別影像；一是毘鉢舍那所緣境事，謂有分別影像；二是俱所緣境事，謂事邊際、所作成辦。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Maitreya said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, on what do bodhisattvas rely, and in what do they abide, when they cultivate śamatha and vipaśyanā in the Great Vehicle?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, you should understand that bodhisattvas rely on and abide in the provisional establishment of Dharma, and in not abandoning the vow for unsurpassed, complete, perfect awakening, when they cultivate śamatha and vipaśyanā in the Great Vehicle."

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "As the World-Honored One has taught, there are four kinds of objective supports: the objective support of an image accompanied by discrimination; the objective support of an image free from discrimination; the objective support that is the limit of the matter; and the objective support that is the accomplishment of what is to be done. Among these four, how many are objective supports of śamatha? How many are objective supports of vipaśyanā? How many are objective supports of both?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, one is an objective support of śamatha: the image free from discrimination. One is an objective support of vipaśyanā: the image accompanied by discrimination. Two are objective supports of both: the limit of the matter and the accomplishment of what is to be done."

How They Are Cultivated

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！云何菩薩依是四種奢摩他、毘鉢舍那所緣境事，能求奢摩他、能善毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！如我為諸菩薩所說法假安立，所謂契經、應誦、記別、諷誦、自說、因緣、譬喻、本事、本生、方廣、希法、論議。菩薩於此善聽、善受、言善通利、意善尋思、見善通達，即於如所善思惟法，獨處空閑作意思惟。復即於此能思惟心，內心相續，作意思惟。如是正行多安住故，起身輕安及心輕安，是名奢摩他。如是菩薩，能求奢摩他。彼由獲得身心輕安為所依故，即於如所善思惟法，內三摩地所行影像，觀察勝解捨離心相。即於如是三摩地影像所知義中，能正思擇、最極思擇，周遍尋思、周遍伺察，若忍、若樂、若慧、若見、若觀，是名毘鉢舍那。如是菩薩，能善毘鉢舍那。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, how do bodhisattvas, relying on these four objective supports of śamatha and vipaśyanā, seek śamatha and become skilled in vipaśyanā?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, there are the provisional establishments of Dharma that I have taught for bodhisattvas: sūtras, chants, predictions, verses, spontaneous teachings, conditions, parables, past events, past births, expanded teachings, marvelous dharmas, and treatises. Bodhisattvas listen to these well, receive them well, become fluent in their words, think them through well in mind, and penetrate them well in view. Then, with the Dharma they have thought through well, they dwell alone in an empty, quiet place and attend to it in thought. They further attend inwardly to the continuity of the very mind that is able to think. Because they often abide in this right practice, pliancy of body and pliancy of mind arise. This is called śamatha. In this way bodhisattvas are able to seek śamatha.

"Having obtained pliancy of body and mind as their basis, then, with regard to the Dharma they have thought through well, they observe the image that moves within inner samādhi, form a resolute understanding of it, and relinquish the characteristic of mind. Within the meaning known through such a samādhi image, they are able to examine correctly, examine to the utmost, search throughout, and investigate throughout. Whether this is acceptance, delight, wisdom, view, or observation, it is called vipaśyanā. In this way bodhisattvas are able to become skilled in vipaśyanā."

Before Pliancy and the Two Paths 輕安前與二道

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！若諸菩薩緣心為境，內思惟心，乃至未得身心輕安，所有作意，當名何等？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！非奢摩他作意，是隨順奢摩他勝解相應作意。」

「世尊！若諸菩薩乃至未得身心輕安，於如所思所有諸法內三摩地所緣影像作意思惟，如是作意，當名何等？」

「善男子！非毘鉢舍那作意，是隨順毘鉢舍那勝解相應作意。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！奢摩他道與毘鉢舍那道，當言有異？當言無異？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！當言非有異、非無異。何故非有異？以毘鉢舍那所緣境心為所緣故。何故非無異？有分別影像非所緣故。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, if bodhisattvas take mind as their object and inwardly contemplate mind, but have not yet obtained pliancy of body and mind, what should that attention be called?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, it is not śamatha-attention. It is attention associated with resolute understanding that accords with śamatha."

"World-Honored One, if bodhisattvas, before obtaining pliancy of body and mind, attend to the image that is the object of inner samādhi within all dharmas as they have contemplated them, what should such attention be called?"

"Good son, it is not vipaśyanā-attention. It is attention associated with resolute understanding that accords with vipaśyanā."

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, should the path of śamatha and the path of vipaśyanā be said to be different, or not different?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, they should be said to be neither different nor not different. Why are they not different? Because the mind that is the object of vipaśyanā becomes the object. Why are they not not different? Because the image accompanied by discrimination is not the object."

The Samādhi Image Is Consciousness-Only

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！諸毘鉢舍那三摩地所行影像，彼與此心，當言有異？當言無異？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！當言無異。何以故？由彼影像唯是識故。善男子！我說識所緣，唯識所現故。」

「世尊！若彼所行影像，即與此心無有異者，云何此心還見此心？」

「善男子！此中無有少法能見少法；然即此心如是生時，即有如是影像顯現。善男子！如依善瑩清淨鏡面，以質為緣還見本質，而謂我今見於影像，及謂離質別有所行影像顯現。如是此心生時，相似有異三摩地所行影像顯現。」

「世尊！若諸有情自性而住，緣色等心所行影像，彼與此心亦無異耶？」

「善男子！亦無有異！而諸愚夫由顛倒覺，於諸影像，不能如實知唯是識，作顛倒解。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, are the images that move within vipaśyanā samādhi different from this mind, or not different?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, they should be said to be not different. Why? Because those images are consciousness-only. Good son, I say that what consciousness takes as object appears from consciousness-only."

"World-Honored One, if the image that moves there is not different from this mind, how can this mind see this mind?"

"Good son, here there is not even the slightest dharma that can see even the slightest dharma. Yet when this very mind arises in this way, just such an image appears. Good son, it is like relying on the well-polished surface of a clean mirror: with the original object as condition, one sees the original object again, yet thinks, 'Now I am seeing an image,' and also thinks that apart from the object there is a separate image appearing as what is experienced. In the same way, when this mind arises, an apparently different image that moves within samādhi appears."

"World-Honored One, when sentient beings abide according to their ordinary nature and the images moved through by mind take form and other objects as their condition, are those images also not different from this mind?"

"Good son, they also are not different. But ordinary foolish beings, because of inverted awareness, cannot know those images exactly as they are, as consciousness-only, and they understand them in an inverted way."

When Practice Is One-Sided or Combined 止觀單修雙運

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！齊何當言菩薩一向修毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！若相續作意唯思惟心相。」

「世尊！齊何當言菩薩一向修奢摩他？」

「善男子！若相續作意唯思惟無間心。」

「世尊！齊何當言菩薩奢摩他、毘鉢舍那和合俱轉？」

「善男子！若正思惟心一境性。」

「世尊！云何心相？」

「善男子！謂三摩地所行有分別影像，毘鉢舍那所緣。」

「世尊！云何無間心？」

「善男子！謂緣彼影像心，奢摩他所緣。」

「世尊！云何心一境性？」

「善男子！謂通達三摩地所行影像，唯是其識；或通達此已，復思惟如性。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, at what point should a bodhisattva be said to cultivate only vipaśyanā?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, when, in continuous attention, one contemplates only the characteristic of mind."

"World-Honored One, at what point should a bodhisattva be said to cultivate only śamatha?"

"Good son, when, in continuous attention, one contemplates only the uninterrupted mind."

"World-Honored One, at what point should a bodhisattva's śamatha and vipaśyanā be said to be harmonized and turning together?"

"Good son, when one rightly contemplates the mind's one-pointedness toward its object."

"World-Honored One, what is the characteristic of mind?"

"Good son, it is the image accompanied by discrimination that moves within samādhi, the object of vipaśyanā."

"World-Honored One, what is the uninterrupted mind?"

"Good son, it is the mind that takes that image as its object, the object of śamatha."

"World-Honored One, what is the mind's one-pointedness toward its object?"

"Good son, it is penetrating that the image moving within samādhi is only that consciousness; or, after penetrating this, again contemplating suchness."

Kinds of Vipaśyanā and Śamatha

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！毘鉢舍那凡有幾種？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！略有三種：一者、有相毘鉢舍那；二者、尋求毘鉢舍那；三者、伺察毘鉢舍那。云何有相毘鉢舍那？謂純思惟三摩地所行有分別影像毘鉢舍那。云何尋求毘鉢舍那？謂由慧故，遍於彼彼未善解了一切法中，為善了故，作意思惟毘鉢舍那。云何伺察毘鉢舍那？謂由慧故，遍於彼彼已善解了一切法中，為善證得極解脫故，作意思惟毘鉢舍那。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！是奢摩他凡有幾種？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！即由隨彼無間心故，當知此中亦有三種。復有八種，謂初靜慮乃至非想非非想處，各有一種奢摩他故。復有四種，謂慈、悲、喜、捨四無量中，各有一種奢摩他故。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, how many kinds of vipaśyanā are there?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, in brief there are three kinds. First is vipaśyanā with characteristics. Second is searching vipaśyanā. Third is investigating vipaśyanā. What is vipaśyanā with characteristics? It is vipaśyanā that purely contemplates the image accompanied by discrimination that moves within samādhi. What is searching vipaśyanā? Through wisdom, throughout all those dharmas that have not yet been well understood, one attends to them in contemplation in order to understand them well. What is investigating vipaśyanā? Through wisdom, throughout all those dharmas that have already been well understood, one attends to them in contemplation in order to realize the highest liberation well."

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, how many kinds of śamatha are there?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, because it follows that uninterrupted mind, you should understand that here too there are three kinds. There are also eight kinds, because from the first dhyāna through the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception, each has one kind of śamatha. There are also four kinds, because within the four immeasurables of kindness, compassion, joy, and equanimity, each has one kind of śamatha."

Dharma-Based and Summary-Based Practice 依法與總法

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！如說依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，復說不依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。云何名依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？云何復名不依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！若諸菩薩隨先所受所思法相，而於其義得奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，名依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。若諸菩薩不待所受所思法相，但依於他教誡教授，而於其義得奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，謂觀青瘀及膿爛等，或一切行皆是無常、或諸行苦、或一切法皆無有我、或復涅槃畢竟寂靜。如是等類奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，名不依法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。由依止法得奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，故我施設隨法行菩薩，是利根性；由不依法得奢摩他、毘鉢舍那故，我施設隨信行菩薩，是鈍根性。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, you speak of śamatha and vipaśyanā that rely on Dharma, and also of śamatha and vipaśyanā that do not rely on Dharma. What are called śamatha and vipaśyanā that rely on Dharma? And what are called śamatha and vipaśyanā that do not rely on Dharma?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, when bodhisattvas follow the characteristics of Dharma they have previously received and contemplated, and within their meaning obtain śamatha and vipaśyanā, this is called śamatha and vipaśyanā that rely on Dharma. When bodhisattvas do not wait for characteristics of Dharma they have received and contemplated, but simply rely on another's admonition and instruction, and within that meaning obtain śamatha and vipaśyanā, such as by contemplating bluish discoloration, pus and decay, and so on, or that all conditioned practices are impermanent, or that all conditioned practices are suffering, or that all dharmas have no self, or that nirvāṇa is finally quiescent, these and similar kinds of śamatha and vipaśyanā are called śamatha and vipaśyanā that do not rely on Dharma. Because śamatha and vipaśyanā are obtained by relying on Dharma, I establish a bodhisattva who follows Dharma as having sharp faculties. Because śamatha and vipaśyanā are obtained without relying on Dharma, I establish a bodhisattva who follows faith as having dull faculties."

Particular Dharma, General Dharma, and Boundless Dharma

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！如說緣別法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，復說緣總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。云何名為緣別法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？云何復名緣總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！若諸菩薩，緣於各別契經等法，於如所受所思惟法，修奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，是名緣別法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。若諸菩薩，即緣一切契經等法，集為一團、一積、一分、一聚作意思惟：此一切法，隨順真如，趣向真如，臨入真如；隨順菩提，隨順涅槃，隨順轉依，及趣向彼，若臨入彼。此一切法，宣說無量無數善法。如是思惟修奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，是名緣總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！如說緣小總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，復說緣大總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，又說緣無量總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。云何名緣小總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？云何名緣大總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？云何復名緣無量總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！若緣各別契經，乃至各別論義，為一團等作意思惟，當知是名緣小總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。若緣乃至所受所思契經等法，為一團等作意思惟，非緣各別，當知是名緣大總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。若緣無量如來法教，無量法句文字，無量後後慧所照了，為一團等作意思惟，非緣乃至所受所思，當知是名緣無量總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！菩薩齊何名得緣總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！由五緣故當知名得：一者、於思惟時，剎那剎那融銷一切麁重所依；二者、離種種想得樂法樂；三者、解了十方無差別相無量法光；四者、所作成滿相應淨分無分別相，恒現在前；五者、為令法身得成滿故，攝受後後轉勝妙因。」

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！此緣總法奢摩他、毘鉢舍那，當知從何名為通達？從何名得？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！從初極喜地名為通達；從第三發光地乃名為得。善男子！初業菩薩亦於是中隨學作意，雖未可歎，不應懈廢。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, you speak of śamatha and vipaśyanā that take particular dharmas as their object, and also of śamatha and vipaśyanā that take Dharma as a whole as their object. What are śamatha and vipaśyanā that take particular dharmas as their object? And what are śamatha and vipaśyanā that take Dharma as a whole as their object?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, when bodhisattvas take individual sūtras and other dharmas as their object, and cultivate śamatha and vipaśyanā with regard to the Dharma they have received and contemplated, this is called śamatha and vipaśyanā that take particular dharmas as their object. When bodhisattvas take all sūtras and other dharmas as their object, gather them into one cluster, one accumulation, one portion, one collection, and attend to them in contemplation, thinking: 'All these dharmas accord with suchness, tend toward suchness, approach entry into suchness; they accord with bodhi, accord with nirvāṇa, accord with the transformation of the basis, and tend toward and approach entry into them; all these dharmas proclaim immeasurable, countless wholesome dharmas,' and cultivate śamatha and vipaśyanā while contemplating in this way, this is called śamatha and vipaśyanā that take Dharma as a whole as their object."

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, you speak of śamatha and vipaśyanā that take a small whole of Dharma as their object, that take a great whole of Dharma as their object, and that take a boundless whole of Dharma as their object. What are these?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, if one takes individual sūtras through individual treatise meanings and attends to them as one cluster and so on, you should understand this as śamatha and vipaśyanā that take a small whole of Dharma as their object. If one takes sūtras and other dharmas up to those one has received and contemplated, attends to them as one cluster and so on, and does not take them individually, you should understand this as śamatha and vipaśyanā that take a great whole of Dharma as their object. If one takes boundless Dharma teachings of the Tathāgatas, boundless Dharma phrases and words, and what is illuminated by boundless later and still later wisdoms, attends to them as one cluster and so on, and does not merely take up to what one has received and contemplated, you should understand this as śamatha and vipaśyanā that take a boundless whole of Dharma as their object."

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, at what point is a bodhisattva said to have obtained śamatha and vipaśyanā that take Dharma as a whole as their object?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, you should understand that it is called obtained because of five conditions. First, while contemplating, moment by moment one melts away every basis of coarseness and heaviness. Second, separated from various perceptions, one obtains delight in Dharma and joy in Dharma. Third, one understands the boundless light of Dharma in the ten directions, whose characteristic is without difference. 「云何善知心住？謂如實知了別真如。

「云何善知心出？謂如實知出二種縛：所謂相縛及麁重縛。此能善知，應令其心從如是出。

「云何善知心增？謂如實知能治相縛、麁重縛心，彼增長時，彼積集時，亦得增長，亦得積集，名善知增。

「云何善知心減？謂如實知彼所對治相，及麁重所雜染心，彼衰退時，彼損減時，此亦衰退，此亦損減，名善知減。

「云何善知方便？謂如實知解脫、勝處、及與遍處，或修或遣。

「善男子！如是菩薩，於諸菩薩廣大威德，或已引發、或當引發、或現引發。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, how does cultivation bring forth the vast majestic virtue of bodhisattvas?"

"Good son, if bodhisattvas know six points well, they are able to bring forth all the vast majestic virtue of bodhisattvas. First, they know well the arising of mind. Second, they know well the abiding of mind. Third, they know well the exit of mind. Fourth, they know well the increase of mind. Fifth, they know well the decrease of mind. Sixth, they know well skillful means.

"What is knowing well the arising of mind? It is to know exactly as they are the differences in the arising of mind through sixteen modes. This is called knowing well the arising of mind. The sixteen differences in the arising of mind are these. First, the arising of a consciousness that cannot be consciously known and that firmly abides as the container: the ādāna-consciousness. Second, the arising of consciousness whose object has various modes: the discriminating mental consciousness that suddenly grasps all object-realms such as forms, the awareness and feeling that suddenly grasp inner and outer object-realms, or the discriminating mental consciousness that, suddenly in a single moment, instant, or brief interval, presently enters many concentrations, sees many buddha-lands, and sees many Tathāgatas. Third, the arising of consciousness whose object has small characteristics: consciousness bound to the desire realm. Fourth, the arising of consciousness whose object has great characteristics: consciousness bound to the form realm. Fifth, the arising of consciousness whose object has boundless characteristics: consciousness bound to the spheres of boundless space and boundless consciousness. Sixth, the arising of consciousness whose object has subtle characteristics: consciousness bound to the sphere of nothing whatsoever. Seventh, the arising of consciousness whose object has the characteristic of the limit: consciousness bound to the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception. Eighth, the arising of signless consciousness: world-transcending consciousness and consciousness that takes cessation as object.

"Ninth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by suffering: hell consciousness. Tenth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by mixed feelings: consciousness moving in the desire realm. Eleventh, the arising of consciousness accompanied by joy: consciousness of the first and second dhyānas. Twelfth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by bliss: consciousness of the third dhyāna. Thirteenth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by neither suffering nor bliss: consciousness from the fourth dhyāna through the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception. Fourteenth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by defilement: consciousness associated with afflictions and secondary afflictions. Fifteenth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by the wholesome: consciousness associated with faith and so on. Sixteenth, the arising of consciousness accompanied by the indeterminate: consciousness not associated with either of those two.

"What is knowing well the abiding of mind? It is knowing exactly as it is the suchness of cognition. What is knowing well the exit of mind? It is knowing exactly as it is how to exit from the two bondages, namely the bondage to characteristics and the bondage of coarseness and heaviness. Knowing this well, one should cause the mind to exit from them. What is knowing well the increase of mind? It is knowing exactly as it is that when the mind that counteracts bondage to characteristics and bondage of coarseness and heaviness increases and accumulates, those counteractives also increase and accumulate. This is called knowing well increase. What is knowing well the decrease of mind? It is knowing exactly as it is that when the characteristics counteracted by those, and the mind defiled by coarseness and heaviness, decline and diminish, those too decline and diminish. This is called knowing well decrease. What is knowing well skillful means? It is knowing exactly as they are liberations, bases of mastery, and totalities, whether to cultivate them or remove them.

"Good son, in this way, with regard to the vast majestic virtue of bodhisattvas, such bodhisattvas either have already brought it forth, will bring it forth, or are presently bringing it forth."

Nirvāṇa Without Remainder and the Chapter's Name 無餘依涅槃與教名

Chinese Source

慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！如世尊說：『於無餘依涅槃界中，一切諸受無餘永滅。』何等諸受於此永滅？」

「善男子！以要言之，有二種受無餘永滅。何等為二？一者、所依麁重受；二者、彼果境界受。所依麁重受，當知有四種：一者、有色所依受；二者、無色所依受；三者、果已成滿麁重受；四者、果未成滿麁重受。果已成滿受者，謂現在受。果未成滿受者，謂未來因受。彼果境界受，亦有四種：一者、依持受；二者、資具受；三者、受用受；四者、顧戀受。於有餘依涅槃界中，果未成滿受一切已滅，領彼對治明觸生受，領受共有。或復彼果已成滿受。又二種受，一切已滅。唯現領受，明觸生受。於無餘依涅槃界中般涅槃時，此亦永滅。是故說言於無餘依涅槃界中，一切諸受無餘永滅。」

爾時，世尊說是語已，復告慈氏菩薩曰：「善哉，善哉！善男子！汝今善能依止圓滿最極清淨妙瑜伽道，請問如來。汝於瑜伽，已得決定，最極善巧。吾已為汝宣說圓滿最極清淨妙瑜伽道，所有一切過去、未來正等覺者，已說、當說皆亦如是。諸善男子、若善女人，皆應依此勇猛精進，當正修學！」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, as the World-Honored One has said, 'In the nirvāṇa element without remainder, all feelings permanently cease without remainder.' Which feelings permanently cease there?"

"Good son, in brief, there are two kinds of feeling that permanently cease without remainder. What are the two? First, feelings of coarseness and heaviness in the support. Second, feelings of the object-realms that are their fruits. You should understand that feelings of coarseness and heaviness in the support are of four kinds: feelings with a material support, feelings with a formless support, feelings of coarseness and heaviness whose fruits have already been completed, and feelings of coarseness and heaviness whose fruits have not yet been completed. Feelings whose fruits have already been completed are present feelings. Feelings whose fruits have not yet been completed are future causal feelings. Feelings of their fruit-object-realms are also of four kinds: feelings of dependence and support, feelings of supplies, feelings of use and enjoyment, and feelings of clinging.

"In the nirvāṇa element with remainder, all feelings whose fruits have not yet been completed have already ceased, while the feelings born from bright contact that counteracts them are received and are shared in experience. Or again, the feelings whose fruits have already been completed may remain. Also, all two kinds of feeling have already ceased, and only the presently received feelings born from bright contact remain. At the time of final nirvāṇa in the nirvāṇa element without remainder, these too permanently cease. Therefore it is said that in the nirvāṇa element without remainder, all feelings permanently cease without remainder."

At that time, after the World-Honored One had spoken these words, he again told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good, good, good son. You are now well able to rely on the complete, most utterly pure, wondrous path of yoga in asking the Tathāgata. With regard to yoga, you have attained certainty and the highest skill. I have already proclaimed for you the complete, most utterly pure, wondrous path of yoga. All perfectly awakened ones of the past and future have taught it and will teach it in this same way. All good sons and good daughters should rely on this, practice with courageous diligence, and rightly train in it."

Then the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke these verses:

Within the yoga provisionally established through Dharma,

if one practices carelessly, the great meaning is lost.

If, relying on this Dharma and this yoga,

one rightly cultivates practice, one attains great awakening.

Seeing something to be obtained, seeking escape,

and thinking this view is attainment of Dharma:

Maitreya, that one is far from yoga,

as far as the great earth is from empty space.

Firm in benefiting beings, yet without contrivance,

having awakened, diligently cultivating benefit for sentient beings,

the wise who do this through the measure of eons

obtain the highest joy free from defilement.

If someone teaches Dharma for the sake of desire,

that is called abandoning desire and then grasping desire again.

A fool obtains Dharma, a priceless treasure,

yet instead wanders about begging.

should be abandoned; supreme diligence should be aroused.

To liberate devas and the world,

The Teaching's Title

Chinese Source

爾時，慈氏菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！於是解深密法門中，當何名此教？我當云何奉持？」

佛告慈氏菩薩曰：「善男子！此名瑜伽了義之教，於此瑜伽了義之教汝當奉持。」

說此瑜伽了義教時，於大會中，有六百千眾生，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心；三百千聲聞，遠塵離垢，於諸法中，得法眼淨；一百五十千聲聞，諸漏永盡，心得解脫；七十五千菩薩，獲得廣大瑜伽作意。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, within this Dharma-gateway of the Saṃdhinirmocana, what should this teaching be called? How should I uphold it?"

The Buddha told Maitreya Bodhisattva, "Good son, this is called the definitive teaching of yoga. You should uphold it as the definitive teaching of yoga."

When this definitive teaching of yoga was spoken, within the great assembly, six hundred thousand sentient beings gave rise to the mind of unsurpassed, complete, perfect awakening; three hundred thousand śrāvakas, distant from dust and separated from defilement, obtained the purity of the Dharma-eye with regard to dharmas; one hundred fifty thousand śrāvakas, with all outflows permanently ended, were liberated in mind; and seventy-five thousand bodhisattvas obtained vast yoga-attention.

Reading note: This chapter uses "yoga" in the classical Yogācāra sense of disciplined meditative integration. The sūtra does not present śamatha and vipaśyanā as separate techniques only; it shows how they mature into a complete path, from studying Dharma to directly knowing that meditative images are consciousness-only, then using emptiness to remove even the characteristic of emptiness.
