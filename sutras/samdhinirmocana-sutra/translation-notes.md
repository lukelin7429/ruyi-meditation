# Saṃdhinirmocana Sūtra Translation Notes

These notes record recurring translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Saṃdhinirmocana Sūtra* (解深密經, Taishō T16, no. 676), translated into Chinese by Xuanzang. They exist so that the vocabulary of this sūtra stays consistent with the site's larger Yogācāra corpus, especially the *Cheng Weishi Lun* and the *Yogācārabhūmi Śāstra*.

## Source and independence from prior translations

- Base text: Taishō T676, via the CBETA XML P5 edition (2025.R1), based on Xuanzang's Chinese translation.
- This page translates directly from Xuanzang's Chinese. Existing English translations and scholarly studies may be consulted privately to check difficult passages, but the English wording, sentence structure, and section framing on this site must be Ru-Yi's own.
- The title is given as *Saṃdhinirmocana Sūtra*, "The Sūtra Unraveling the Hidden Intent." In headings, the site may also use the shorter English title "Unraveling the Hidden Intent."

## Core terminology

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 解深密經 | *Saṃdhinirmocana Sūtra* | Standard scholarly Sanskrit title; use the full title on first appearance. |
| 薄伽梵 | Bhagavat | Left transliterated in the text's opening, where the Chinese keeps the Indic honorific rather than simply saying 佛. |
| 勝義諦 | the ultimate truth | Keep fixed. In explanatory notes, "ultimate truth" may be glossed as the reality directly known by noble wisdom. |
| 離言法性 | the dharma-nature apart from language | A central phrase in chapter 2. Keep close and slightly formal; do not reduce to "wordless reality" in translated prose. |
| 一切法無二 | all dharmas are nondual | The opening question of chapter 2. |
| 有為 / 無為 | conditioned / unconditioned | Use "conditioned" and "unconditioned" as adjectives and nouns according to context. |
| 假施設 / 假立 | provisional designation / provisionally establish | Use when the text says a term is laid down for teaching purposes, not as an ultimately fixed entity. |
| 遍計所集 | gathered by universal discrimination | Closely related to the imagined/discriminated layer of Yogācāra analysis. |
| 言辭所說 | spoken by language | Keep the language emphasis explicit. |
| 現等正覺 / 現等覺 | directly and perfectly awaken / bring to direct awakening | Translate according to subject: the noble ones awaken; the Buddha establishes names to help others awaken. |
| 解甚深義密意菩薩 | Bodhisattva Revealing the Hidden Intent of the Profound Meaning | Functional English name for readability; revisit if a better standard name is adopted. |
| 如理請問菩薩 | Bodhisattva Who Questions According to the Principle | Functional English name; preserve the role of "questioning according to the principle." |
| 法涌菩薩 | Bodhisattva Dharmodgata | Use the Sanskrit-style form where standard. |
| 善清淨慧菩薩 | Bodhisattva Suviśuddhamati | Use the Sanskrit-style form where standard. |
| 善現 | Subhūti | Render as "Elder Subhūti" when 長老 is present. |
| 廣慧菩薩 | Bodhisattva Vast Wisdom | Functional English name until a fixed Sanskrit form is needed. |
| 德本菩薩 | Bodhisattva Root of Virtue | Functional English name. |
| 勝義生菩薩 | Bodhisattva Born of the Ultimate | Functional English name. |
| 觀自在菩薩 | Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva | Follow the site's Heart Sūtra convention. |
| 慈氏菩薩 | Maitreya Bodhisattva | |
| 曼殊室利菩薩 | Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva | |
| 尋思 | ordinary thought | The conceptual, inferential activity the sūtra contrasts with direct noble realization of ultimate truth. |
| 一異性相 | the characteristic of being one or different | Use in chapter 2 for the question of whether ultimate truth and conditioned phenomena are the same or different. |
| 相縛 | bondage to characteristics | The bondage produced by grasping characteristics as finally real. |
| 麁重縛 | the bondage of coarseness and heaviness | The deeper burden that remains when characteristics are not relinquished. |
| 增上慢 | overweening pride | The pride that declares realization on the basis of something still grasped as attained. |
| 一味相 | the characteristic of one taste | In chapter 2, the ultimate truth is one taste throughout all dharmas and path categories. |
| 清淨所緣 | pure objective support | Keep "objective support" consistent with the site's Yogācāra usage for 所緣. |

## Relationship to the Cheng Weishi Lun glossary

Where this sūtra later teaches the Three Natures, the three naturelessnesses, mind/intellect/consciousness, the ādāna-consciousness, Yogācāra meditation, the bodhisattva grounds, the ten perfections, and buddhahood, follow the established terminology in `sutras/cheng-weishi-lun/translation-notes.md` unless this sūtra's own wording requires a special note.

## Register

- Plain English, close to the Chinese, not a loose paraphrase.
- Dialogue should remain dialogue. The sūtra unfolds as a sequence of bodhisattvas asking questions, so the reader should feel the progression of the conversation.
- Where Xuanzang's Chinese compresses a technical point, expand only enough for intelligible English. Longer explanation belongs in a reading note.
- Mark the translation status honestly on each page.
