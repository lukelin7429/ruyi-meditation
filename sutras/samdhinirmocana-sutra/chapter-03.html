Chapter 3 · 心意識相品第三

The Secret of Mind, Intellect, and Consciousness

This short chapter gives one of the Yogācāra tradition's classic scriptural sources for the ādāna-consciousness and ālayavijñāna: the seed-bearing current on which the six active consciousnesses arise like waves.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: chapter 3 complete Source: Taishō T16, no. 676 Chinese translator: Xuanzang

Vast Wisdom's Question 廣慧菩薩問

Chinese Source

爾時，廣慧菩薩摩訶薩白佛言：「世尊！如世尊說：『於心意識祕密善巧菩薩。』於心意識祕密善巧菩薩者，齊何名為於心意識祕密善巧菩薩？如來齊何施設彼為於心意識祕密善巧菩薩？」說是語已。

爾時，世尊告廣慧菩薩摩訶薩曰：「善哉，善哉！廣慧！汝今乃能請問如來如是深義；汝今為欲利益安樂無量眾生，哀愍世間、及諸天、人、阿素洛等；為令獲得義利安樂，故發斯問。汝應諦聽，吾當為汝說心意識祕密之義。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Vast Wisdom said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, the World-Honored One has spoken of 'bodhisattvas skilled in the secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness.' What makes someone a bodhisattva skilled in the secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness? On what basis does the Tathāgata designate someone as a bodhisattva skilled in the secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness?"

When he had said this, the World-Honored One told Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Vast Wisdom, "Good, good, Vast Wisdom. You are now able to ask the Tathāgata about such a profound meaning. You have raised this question wishing to benefit and bring happiness to immeasurable sentient beings, out of compassion for the world and for devas, humans, asuras, and others, so that they may obtain benefit, welfare, and happiness. Listen carefully. I will explain to you the meaning of the secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness."

The Seed-Bearing Consciousness 一切種子心識

Chinese Source

「廣慧當知，於六趣生死彼彼有情，墮彼彼有情眾中，或在卵生、或在胎生、或在濕生、或在化生，身分生起，於中最初一切種子心識成熟、展轉、和合、增長、廣大。依二執受：一者、有色諸根及所依執受；二者、相名分別言說戲論習氣執受。有色界中具二執受，無色界中不具二種。

「廣慧！此識亦名阿陀那識。何以故？由此識於身隨逐執持故。亦名阿賴耶識。何以故？由此識於身攝受、藏隱、同安危義故。亦名為心。何以故？由此識色聲香味觸等積集滋長故。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Vast Wisdom, you should understand this. Among the various sentient beings in birth and death across the six destinies, beings fall into their particular communities of sentient beings. Whether born from eggs, born from wombs, born from moisture, or born by transformation, when the parts of the body arise, there, from the very beginning, the mind-consciousness containing all seeds matures, turns onward, combines, increases, and expands.

"It depends on two appropriations. First, it appropriates the material faculties and the supports on which they depend. Second, it appropriates the habitual tendencies of characteristics, names, discrimination, language, and conceptual proliferation. In the form realm, both appropriations are present. In the formless realm, the two are not both present.

"Vast Wisdom, this consciousness is also called the ādāna-consciousness. Why? Because this consciousness follows along with the body and grasps and holds it. It is also called the ālayavijñāna. Why? Because this consciousness takes up the body, is hidden and stored within it, and shares its safety and danger. It is also called mind. Why? Because through this consciousness, form, sound, smell, taste, touch, and the rest are accumulated and nourished."

Reading note: This chapter gives three names for the same deep current of consciousness: ādāna-consciousness when emphasizing appropriation and holding, ālayavijñāna when emphasizing storage and hidden support, and mind when emphasizing accumulation and growth. This matches the terminology already used in Ru-Yi's Cheng Weishi Lun translation.

The Six Active Consciousnesses 六識身轉

Chinese Source

「廣慧！阿陀那識為依止、為建立故，六識身轉，謂眼識、耳、鼻、舌、身、意識。此中有識：眼及色為緣生眼識，與眼識俱隨行，同時、同境，有分別意識轉。有識：耳、鼻、舌、身，及聲、香、味、觸為緣，生耳、鼻、舌、身識，與耳、鼻、舌、身識俱隨行，同時、同境，有分別意識轉。廣慧！若於爾時一眼識轉，即於此時唯有一分別意識，與眼識同所行轉。若於爾時二、三、四、五諸識身轉，即於此時唯有一分別意識，與五識身同所行轉。

「廣慧！譬如大瀑水流，若有一浪生緣現前，唯一浪轉；若二、若多浪生緣現前，有多浪轉。然此瀑水自類恒流無斷無盡。又如善淨鏡面，若有一影生緣現前，唯一影起；若二、若多影生緣現前，有多影起。非此鏡面轉變為影，亦無受用滅盡可得。

「如是，廣慧！由似瀑流阿陀那識為依止、為建立故，若於爾時有一眼識生緣現前，即於此時一眼識轉；若於爾時乃至有五識身生緣現前，即於此時五識身轉。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Vast Wisdom, because the ādāna-consciousness serves as support and establishment, the six bodies of consciousness turn: eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, and mental consciousness.

"Here, there is consciousness: with the eye and form as conditions, eye-consciousness arises. Together with eye-consciousness, following along with it, at the same time and with the same object, a discriminating mental consciousness turns. There is consciousness: with ear, nose, tongue, and body, together with sound, smell, taste, and touch as conditions, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, and body-consciousness arise. Together with those consciousnesses, following along with them, at the same time and with the same object, a discriminating mental consciousness turns.

"Vast Wisdom, if at that time one eye-consciousness turns, then at that time there is only one discriminating mental consciousness turning with the same field of activity as eye-consciousness. If at that time two, three, four, or five bodies of consciousness turn, then at that time there is only one discriminating mental consciousness turning with the same field of activity as the five bodies of consciousness.

"Vast Wisdom, it is like the flow of a great torrent. If the conditions for one wave to arise are present, only one wave turns. If the conditions for two waves, or many waves, to arise are present, many waves turn. Yet this torrent itself, as its own kind, constantly flows without interruption and without end.

"Again, it is like the surface of a well-polished mirror. If the conditions for one reflection to arise are present, only one reflection appears. If the conditions for two reflections, or many reflections, to arise are present, many reflections appear. It is not that this mirror surface transforms into the reflections, nor can any exhaustion of its capacity for use be found.

"In this way, Vast Wisdom, because the ādāna-consciousness, like a torrent, serves as support and establishment, if at that time the conditions for one eye-consciousness to arise are present, then at that time one eye-consciousness turns. If at that time the conditions for up to five bodies of consciousness to arise are present, then at that time the five bodies of consciousness turn."

Skill in the Ultimate Meaning 勝義善巧

Chinese Source

「廣慧！如是菩薩雖由法住智為依止、為建立故，於心意識祕密善巧。然諸如來不齊於此，施設彼為於心意識一切祕密善巧菩薩。廣慧！若諸菩薩於內各別：如實不見阿陀那、不見阿陀那識；不見阿賴耶、不見阿賴耶識；不見積集、不見心；不見眼色及眼識；不見耳聲及耳識；不見鼻香及鼻識；不見舌味及舌識；不見身觸及身識；不見意法及意識；是名勝義善巧菩薩；如來施設彼為勝義善巧菩薩。廣慧！齊此名為於心意識一切祕密善巧菩薩；如來齊此施設彼為於心意識一切祕密善巧菩薩。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Vast Wisdom, in this way a bodhisattva, relying on and established by wisdom that abides in Dharma, is skilled in the secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness. Yet the Tathāgatas do not, merely on this basis, designate such a one as a bodhisattva skilled in every secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness.

"Vast Wisdom, if bodhisattvas, inwardly and individually, do not truly see ādāna and do not see the ādāna-consciousness; do not see ālaya and do not see the ālayavijñāna; do not see accumulation and do not see mind; do not see eye, form, and eye-consciousness; do not see ear, sound, and ear-consciousness; do not see nose, smell, and nose-consciousness; do not see tongue, taste, and tongue-consciousness; do not see body, touch, and body-consciousness; and do not see mind, dharmas, and mental consciousness, this is called a bodhisattva skilled in the ultimate meaning. The Tathāgata designates such a one as a bodhisattva skilled in the ultimate meaning.

"Vast Wisdom, only this is called being a bodhisattva skilled in every secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness. Only on this basis does the Tathāgata designate such a one as a bodhisattva skilled in every secret of mind, intellect, and consciousness."

Reading note: The sūtra first explains the functioning of consciousness, then immediately prevents reifying that explanation. To be fully skilled is not merely to know the system, but to see that none of its terms can be found as an ultimately graspable thing.

Verse Summary

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊欲重宣此義，而說頌曰：

阿陀那識甚深細，一切種子如瀑流，

我於凡愚不開演，恐彼分別執為我。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke these verses:

The ādāna-consciousness is extremely profound and subtle;

all its seeds flow like a torrent.

I do not reveal this to the ordinary and the foolish,

fearing they would discriminate and cling to it as a self.
