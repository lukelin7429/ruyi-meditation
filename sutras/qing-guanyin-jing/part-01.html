The Sūtra of Invoking Avalokiteśvara

The Sūtra of Invoking Avalokiteśvara (T1043), translated by the Indian layman Nandi in the early fifth century, is a short dhāraṇī scripture that became the basis of one of Zhiyi's repentance rites (Part 2). A plague strikes Vaiśālī; at the Buddha's word the elder Candracchattra and the citizens invite Amitāyus Buddha and the bodhisattvas Avalokiteśvara and Mahāsthāmaprāpta, offer them a willow branch and pure water, call on the Three Jewels and Avalokiteśvara, and recite a four-stanza verse. Avalokiteśvara then gives three dhāraṇīs: the mantra that dispels and subdues poison and harm, the mantra that breaks the hindrances of evil karma, and the "six-syllable phrases" that rescue from suffering, followed by a fourth "consecration" dhāraṇī given by the Buddha. The sūtra lists the dangers from which these mantras rescue, describes a method of counting the breath and guiding it through the channels of the body, and tells how the monk Upasena, instructed by Śāriputra to see the four elements and five aggregates as empty, became an arhat. Calming and Contemplation (fascicle 2) uses this sūtra as its example of the samādhi of "following one's own intent." The dhāraṇī syllables are not transliterated; their interlinear glosses, mostly names of ghosts, are translated.

Ru-Yi Translation

Chinese Source

一時佛住毘舍離菴羅樹園大林精舍重閣講堂，與千二百五十比丘，皆阿羅漢，諸漏已盡、不受後有，如鍊真金、身心澄靜，六通無礙，其名曰：大智舍利弗、摩訶目揵連、摩訶迦葉、摩訶迦旃延、須菩提、阿㝹樓馱、劫賓那、橋梵波提、畢陵伽婆蹉、薄拘羅、難陀、阿難陀、羅睺羅，如是等眾所知識，常為天龍八部所敬。復有菩薩摩訶薩二萬人俱，大智本行皆悉成就，調伏諸根滿足六度，具佛威儀心大如海，其名曰：文殊師利童子、寶月童子、月光童子、寶積童子、曰藏童子、跋陀婆羅菩薩與其同類十六人俱、彌勒菩薩，如是等菩薩摩訶薩二萬人。爾時世尊與四眾、天龍八部、人非人等恭敬圍遶。

Ru-Yi Translation

At one time the Buddha was staying at Vaiśālī, in the Kūṭāgāra lecture hall of the Great Forest Monastery in Āmrapālī's Grove, together with twelve hundred fifty bhikṣus, all of them arhats whose outflows were exhausted, who would not receive a further existence, like refined true gold, body and mind clear and still, unobstructed in the six powers. Their names were: Śāriputra of great wisdom, Mahāmaudgalyāyana, Mahākāśyapa, Mahākātyāyana, Subhūti, Aniruddha, Kapphiṇa, Gavāṃpati, Pilindavatsa, Vakkula, Nanda, Ānanda, and Rāhula — such as these, known to the assembly, always revered by the gods, nāgas, and the eight classes. There were also twenty thousand bodhisattva-mahāsattvas, all accomplished in great wisdom and its fundamental practice, who had tamed their faculties and fulfilled the six perfections, possessed the Buddha's dignified deportment, and had minds as great as the ocean. Their names were: the youth Mañjuśrī, the youth Jewel Moon, the youth Moonlight, the youth Jewel Heap, the youth Sun Treasury, Bhadrapāla Bodhisattva together with his sixteen companions, and Maitreya Bodhisattva — bodhisattva-mahāsattvas such as these, twenty thousand. At that time the World-Honored One was reverently surrounded by the fourfold assembly, the gods, nāgas, and the eight classes, humans and nonhumans, and others.

The Sickness at Vaiśālī and the Invocation

Chinese Source

時毘舍離國一切人民遇大惡病，一者眼赤如血、二者兩耳出膿、三者鼻中流血、四者舌噤無聲、五者所食之物化為麁澁、六識閉塞猶如醉人。有五夜叉名訖拏迦羅，面黑如墨而有五眼，狗牙上出，吸人精氣。時毘舍離大城之中有一長者名曰月蓋，與其同類五百長者，俱詣佛所。到佛所已，頭面作禮，却住一面，白言：「世尊！此國人民遇大惡病，良醫耆婆盡其道術所不能救。唯願天尊慈愍一切，救濟病苦令得無患。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time all the people of the country of Vaiśālī met with a great and terrible sickness: first, their eyes were red as blood; second, pus came out of both ears; third, blood flowed from the nose; fourth, the tongue was locked and voiceless; fifth, the food they ate turned coarse and rough; the six consciousnesses were closed off, as in a drunkard. There were five yakṣas named Qinajialuo, with faces black as ink and five eyes, their dog-like fangs protruding upward, who sucked people's vital essence. At that time in the great city of Vaiśālī there was an elder named Candracchattra, who with five hundred elders of his kind went together to where the Buddha was. Having arrived where the Buddha was, they bowed with head and face, withdrew to stand to one side, and said: "World-Honored One! The people of this country have met with a great and terrible sickness, which the good physician Jīvaka, exhausting his arts, cannot cure. We pray only that the Heavenly Honored One, in kindness and pity for all, rescue them from the suffering of sickness and let them be free of trouble."

Chinese Source

爾時世尊告長者言：「去此不遠正主西方，有佛世尊名無量壽，彼有菩薩名觀世音及大勢至，恒以大悲憐愍一切救濟苦厄。汝今應當五體投地向彼作禮，燒香散華、繫念數息，令心不散經十念頃，為眾生故當請彼佛及二菩薩。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the World-Honored One said to the elder: "Not far from here, due west, there is a Buddha, a World-Honored One, named Amitāyus; there are bodhisattvas there named Avalokiteśvara and Mahāsthāmaprāpta, who always, with great compassion, pity all and rescue them from suffering and distress. You should now prostrate with the five limbs on the ground and bow toward them, burn incense and scatter flowers, bind your thought and count the breath, keeping the mind from scattering for the space of ten thoughts, and for the sake of beings invite that Buddha and the two bodhisattvas."

Chinese Source

說是語時，於佛光中得見西方無量壽佛并二菩薩。如來神力，佛及菩薩俱到此國，往毘舍離，住城門閫。佛二菩薩與諸大眾放大光明，照毘舍離皆作金色。爾時毘舍離人即具楊枝淨水，授與觀世音菩薩。大悲觀世音憐愍救護一切眾生故，而說呪曰：「普教一切眾生而作是言。汝等今者應當一心稱南無佛、南無法、南無僧、南無觀世音菩薩摩訶薩大悲大名稱救護苦厄者。如此三稱三寶、三稱觀世音菩薩名，燒眾名香，五體投地向於西方，一心一意令氣息定，為免苦厄請觀世音，合十指掌而說偈言：

Ru-Yi Translation

When these words were spoken, within the Buddha's light the Buddha Amitāyus of the west and the two bodhisattvas were seen. By the Tathāgata's spiritual power, the Buddha and the bodhisattvas came together to this country; they went to Vaiśālī and stood at the threshold of the city gate. The Buddha, the two bodhisattvas, and the great assembly emitted great light, illuminating Vaiśālī and making it all golden in color. Then the people of Vaiśālī at once prepared a willow branch and pure water and offered them to Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva. The greatly compassionate Avalokiteśvara, out of pity and to rescue and protect all beings, spoke a mantra, saying: "He universally taught all beings, speaking thus: You should now, with a single mind, call: Homage to the Buddha, homage to the Dharma, homage to the Saṅgha, homage to Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, of great compassion and great renown, who rescues and protects from suffering and distress. In this way, call the Three Jewels three times and call the name of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva three times, burn many famous incenses, prostrate with the five limbs on the ground toward the west, with a single mind and a single intent let the breath become settled, and, to be freed from suffering and distress, invoke Avalokiteśvara; join the palms of the ten fingers and speak this verse:

I now bow my head in homage / to the one who rescues from distress those who hear [his] name; / I now take refuge in / the compassionate father of the world.

Ru-Yi Translation

The First Dhāraṇī

Chinese Source

白佛言：「世尊！如是神呪必定吉祥，乃是過去現在未來十方諸佛大慈大悲陀羅尼印，聞此呪者眾苦永盡，常得安樂遠離八難，得念佛定現前見佛。我今當說十方諸佛救護眾生神呪：

Ru-Yi Translation

[He] said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! Such a spiritual mantra is surely auspicious; it is the dhāraṇī seal of great kindness and great compassion of all buddhas of the ten directions, past, present, and future. Those who hear this mantra will have their many sufferings ended forever, will always attain ease and happiness, will be far from the eight difficulties, and will attain the samādhi of mindfulness of the Buddha, seeing the Buddha before them. I shall now speak the spiritual mantra of all buddhas of the ten directions for rescuing and protecting beings:

「多耶咃（強鵝反）嗚呼膩（名好喉鬼）摸呼膩（名為癡鬼）𨵁婆膩（名怕人鬼）耽婆膩（名叛人鬼）安茶詈（名不白鬼）般茶詈（名為白鬼）首埤帝（名為青鬼）般般茶茶囉囉（名鬼母前鬼母）婆私膩多姪咃（如是）伊梨寐梨（名為去鬼）提梨首梨（名叛人鬼）加波梨（名戴髑髏鬼及縛著兩頰）佉鞮端耆（名食兒鬼）旃陀梨（名就人鬼）摩蹬耆（名師子頭鬼魔王鬼師子頭）勒叉勒叉（守護一切眾生）薩婆薩埵薩婆婆[＊]耶埤（云一切可鬼）𦀟訶（云㥯去）多茶咃伽帝伽帝膩伽帝（云已去也）修留毘修留毘（去莫來莫來也）勒叉勒叉（云守護）薩婆婆[＊]耶埤𦀟訶（云㥯去）」

Chinese Source

白佛言：「世尊！如此神呪，乃是十方三世無量諸佛之所宣說。誦持此呪者，常為諸佛諸大菩薩之所護持，免離怖畏刀杖毒害，及與疾病令得無患。」說是語時，毘舍離人平復如本。

Ru-Yi Translation

[He] said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! This spiritual mantra is what the immeasurable buddhas of the ten directions and the three times proclaim. Those who recite and keep this mantra will always be protected and kept by all buddhas and great bodhisattvas, freed from fear, knives and staves, poison and harm, and sickness, so that they are without trouble." When these words were spoken, the people of Vaiśālī were restored to health as before.

The Dhāraṇī That Breaks the Hindrances of Evil Karma

Chinese Source

爾時世尊憐愍眾生覆護一切，重請觀世音菩薩說消伏毒害陀羅尼呪。爾時觀世音菩薩大悲熏心，承佛神力而說破惡業障消伏毒害陀羅尼呪：

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the World-Honored One, pitying beings and sheltering all, again asked Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva to speak the dhāraṇī mantra that dispels and subdues poison and harm. Then Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva, his mind permeated with great compassion, receiving the Buddha's spiritual power, spoke the dhāraṇī mantra that breaks the hindrances of evil karma and dispels and subdues poison and harm:

Chinese Source

「南無佛陀南無達摩南無僧伽南無觀世音菩提薩埵摩訶薩埵，大慈大悲，唯願愍我救護苦惱，亦救一切怖畏眾生令得大護。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Homage to the Buddha, homage to the Dharma, homage to the Saṅgha, homage to Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, of great kindness and great compassion! I pray only that you pity me and rescue and protect me from suffering and distress, and rescue also all frightened beings, so that they gain great protection.

Chinese Source

「多姪咃陀呼膩（名大鬼）摸呼膩（名水鬼）閻婆膩躭婆膩阿婆凞（虛抵反，名人嗔鬼）摸呼膩（此鬼口出火名曰光鬼）安茶梨（名曰花鬼）般茶梨（此鬼身白名曰白鬼）輸鞞帝（名極白鬼）般茶囉婆私膩休休樓樓（三頭鬼）安茶梨兜兜樓樓（名三頭兒鬼）般茶梨（名母黑兒白鬼）周周樓樓（入山中去不殺，周周樓樓名好偷人小兒山中住鬼）膩槃茶梨（名出白鬼）豆豆富富（豆豆名欲便去鬼，富富名不更來鬼）般茶囉婆私膩矧墀（殯資反，名億鬼）跈墀（乃軫反，名[＊]不億鬼）膩跈墀（名最憶鬼）薩婆阿婆耶羯多薩婆涅婆婆陀伽（莫著疑人）阿婆耶（莫作鬼）卑離陀（云餓鬼）閉殿娑訶（莫來㥯去）

Ru-Yi Translation

"[Dhāraṇī: the syllables are given in the Chinese column.] (named the great ghost) (named the water ghost) (pronunciation note; named the people-angering ghost) (this ghost emits fire from its mouth; it is named the light ghost) (named the flower ghost) (this ghost's body is white; it is named the white ghost) (named the extremely white ghost) (three-headed ghost) (named the three-headed child ghost) (named the ghost whose mother is black and child white) (go into the mountains and do not kill; these syllables name the mountain-dwelling ghost that likes to steal people's small children) (named the ghost that emits white) (the first two syllables name the ghost about to leave; the next two name the ghost that does not come again) (pronunciation note; named the remembering ghost) (pronunciation note; named the not-remembering ghost) (named the most-remembering ghost) (do not cling to doubting people) (do not act as ghosts) (meaning "hungry ghosts") (do not come; go away quietly)

Chinese Source

「一切怖畏、一切毒害、一切惡鬼虎狼師子，聞此呪時口即閉塞不能為害。破梵行人作十惡業，聞此呪時蕩除糞穢還得清淨。設有業障濁惡不善，稱觀世音菩薩誦持此呪，即破業障，現前見佛。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"All fears, all poisons and harms, all evil ghosts, tigers, wolves, and lions — on hearing this mantra their mouths are at once shut and they cannot do harm. Those who have broken pure conduct and done the ten evil deeds, on hearing this mantra, are cleansed of that filth and made pure again. If there are karmic hindrances, turbid, evil, and not good, [one who] calls on Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva and recites and keeps this mantra at once breaks the karmic hindrances and sees the Buddha before him."

The Benefits of the Mantra

Chinese Source

佛告阿難：「若有四部弟子，受持觀世音菩薩名，誦念消伏毒害陀羅尼。行此呪者身常無患心亦無病，設使大火從四面來焚燒己身，誦持此呪故龍王降雨即得解脫。設火焚身節節疼痛，一心稱觀世音菩薩名號，三誦此呪即得除愈。設復糓貴飢饉、王難、惡獸盜賊、迷於道路、牢獄繫閉杻械枷鎖被五繫縛、入於大海黑風迴波水色之山夜叉羅剎之難、毒藥刀劍臨當刑戮、過去業緣現造眾惡，以是因緣受一切苦極大怖畏。應當一心稱觀世音菩薩名號，并誦此呪一遍至七遍，消伏毒害，惡業惡行不善惡聚如火焚薪永盡無餘。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha told Ānanda: "If there are disciples of the four groups who receive and keep the name of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva and recite the dhāraṇī that dispels and subdues poison and harm — those who practice this mantra will always be free of trouble in body and free of illness in mind. If a great fire comes from the four directions to burn one's body, because one recites and keeps this mantra, the nāga kings send down rain and one is at once freed. If fire burns the body and every joint aches, call the name of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva with a single mind and recite this mantra three times, and one is at once healed. Or if there are dearness of grain and famine, the hardships of kings, evil beasts and thieves, losing one's way on the road, imprisonment and confinement in fetters, cangues, and chains, being bound with the five bonds, entering the great sea with black winds, whirling waves, and water-colored mountains, the hardships of yakṣas and rākṣasas, poisons, knives, and swords, being about to be executed, karmic conditions from the past and many evils done in the present — because of these causes and conditions one undergoes all suffering and the greatest terror. One should with a single mind call the name of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva and recite this mantra once up to seven times; poison and harm are dispelled and subdued, and evil karma, evil conduct, and the heaps of what is not good and evil are ended forever without remainder, like firewood burned by fire.

Chinese Source

以是因緣，此觀世音菩薩所說神呪，名施一切眾生甘露妙藥，得無病畏、不橫死畏、不被繫縛畏、貪欲嗔恚愚癡三毒等畏，是故此娑婆世界皆號觀世音菩薩為施無畏者。此陀羅尼灌頂章句無上梵行，畢定吉祥大功德海，眾生聞者獲大安樂，應當闇誦。若欲誦之，應當持齋，不飲酒不噉肉，以灰塗身澡浴清淨，不食興渠五辛，能熏之物悉不食之，婦女穢污皆悉不往，常念十方佛及七佛世尊，一心稱觀世音菩薩誦持此呪。現身得見觀音菩薩，一切善願皆得成就。後生佛前，長與苦別。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"For these reasons, this spiritual mantra spoken by Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva is named 'the Wondrous Medicine of Nectar Given to All Beings'; one attains freedom from the fear of illness, the fear of untimely death, the fear of being bound, and the fears of the three poisons, greed, anger, and delusion. Therefore in this Sahā world all call Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva the Giver of Fearlessness. This dhāraṇī, these consecration phrases, the unsurpassed pure conduct, surely auspicious, an ocean of great merit — beings who hear it obtain great ease and happiness; one should recite it from memory. If one wishes to recite it, one should keep the fast, not drink wine nor eat meat, smear the body with ash and bathe until clean, not eat asafetida or the five pungent [plants], eat none of the things that give off strong smells, and not go [near] women's impurities; always be mindful of the buddhas of the ten directions and the seven buddhas, World-Honored Ones, call the name of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva with a single mind, and recite and keep this mantra. The Sūtra of the Dhāraṇī Mantra for Invoking Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva to Dispel and Subdue Poison and Harm

Reading Notes for Part 1

Translator's notes

Title — 消伏毒害 is rendered "dispel and subdue poison and harm" throughout. The site's Mohe Zhiguan cites the sūtra as the Sūtra of Invoking Avalokiteśvara (fascicle 2) and the Sūtra of Petitioning Avalokiteśvara (fascicle 9), and names the first dhāraṇī "The Dhāraṇī That Dissolves and Subdues Poisonous Harm."

Translator (unit 1) — 竺難提, "in the language of Jin, Faxi (Dharma Joy)," i.e., Nandi.

Setting (unit 3) — the opening follows the site's renderings: "Āmrapālī's Grove" (Vimalakīrti Sūtra), disciple names as in the site's Lotus and Amitābha sūtras (Kapphiṇa, Gavāṃpati, Vakkula, Aniruddha). 重閣講堂 is the Kūṭāgāra hall of the Mahāvana monastery. Bodhisattva names 寶月, 月光, 寶積, 曰藏 are translated (Jewel Moon, Moonlight, Jewel Heap, Sun Treasury); 曰藏 is read as 日藏.

六識閉塞 (unit 3) — follows five numbered symptoms; translated "the six consciousnesses were closed off." Alternatively: "sixth, consciousness was closed off."

訖拏迦羅 (unit 3) — the name of the five yakṣas, given in pinyin (Qinajialuo); no Sanskrit form has been checked.

"Spoke a mantra, saying" (unit 3) — 而說呪曰 introduces instructions and the verse, not a dhāraṇī; the dhāraṇī comes only in unit 9. Translated as written.

Willow branch and pure water — 楊枝淨水, as in the site's Mohe Zhiguan, fascicle 2 ("prepare a willow branch and pure water").

Dhāraṇī glosses (units 9, 13, 15, 40) — the interlinear notes name the ghosts addressed; they are translated in order. 億鬼／不億鬼 in unit 13 are read as 憶 "remembering," following 最憶鬼. Where a gloss quotes the syllables themselves (周周樓樓, 豆豆, 富富), the English says "these syllables." 㥯去 is read as 隱去 "go away quietly." [＊] marks in the Chinese are CBETA collation marks.

Quotations in Mohe Zhiguan — wording reused from the site's Mohe Zhiguan: "restored to health as before" (unit 10, fascicle 2), "cleansed of that filth and made pure again" (unit 14), "the earth has no solidity … water's nature does not abide … wind's nature is unobstructed" and "enter into the brink of true reality" (unit 23), the questions on gathering in the faculties (unit 23), "the body like lapis lazuli, seeing the Buddha through every pore" (unit 23), and "purifying the roots of the three poisons … beyond doubt" (unit 38).

Six-syllable phrases — Mohe Zhiguan, fascicle 2, interprets the "six syllables" as six forms of Avalokiteśvara for the six paths; this is Zhiyi's reading, not stated in the sūtra.

旃陀利 (unit 14) — the woman's name, rendered Caṇḍālī; it may also mean "a caṇḍāla woman."

Breath method (unit 23) — 心端 "tip of the heart," 中脈 "central channel," 正長八寸 "exactly eight inches long." 正受 is rendered "right absorption" (samāpatti). The sentence 觀世音菩薩以此數息心定力故 is kept with its subject unclear. Alternatively: "Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva, by the power of this counting of the breath…".

野馬 (unit 23) — literally "wild horses," a common image for heat shimmer; kept literal beside 熱時焰 "a mirage in the heat."

Verse names (units 24–28) — Dhṛtarāṣṭra and Virūḍhaka are two of the four heavenly kings; 伊羅鉢 is the nāga Elāpattra; 閻婆 (pinyin Yanpo) and 優波陀 (pinyin Youbotuo) are not identified.

This Ocean of the Samādhi of Contemplating the Buddha (unit 41) — Śāriputra names the sūtra together with 觀佛三昧海, which suggests it was once treated as part of a larger collection; translated as written.
