Zhiyi's Rite of Invoking Avalokiteśvara

This short text is the fourth item in fascicle 1 of the Guoqing Bailu (T1934), the collection of Zhiyi's rules, rites, and letters compiled by Guanding. Its heading says that it records only the outward procedure, while the contemplative wisdom that goes with it is given elsewhere — in practice, in the "following one's own intent" section of Calming and Contemplation, fascicle 2, which uses the same sūtra. The rite lasts three or seven weeks. The place of practice has the Buddha image facing south and Avalokiteśvara facing east, with daily offerings of a willow branch and pure water; up to ten practitioners face west. A leader chants ten homages to the Three Jewels, including the sūtra's three dhāraṇīs; the assembly makes the incense-and-flower offering, counts the breath for ten thoughts, and is mindful of the buddhas and of all beings; they invoke the Three Jewels three times and offer the willow branch and water to Avalokiteśvara. Then they recite, in the sūtra's own order, the invocation, the verse, and the three mantras, repent, make vows, bow, circumambulate, take the three refuges, and hear the sūtra chanted. The full sequence is performed before noon and in the first watch of the night.

Preparing the Place of Practice

Chinese Source

請觀世音懺法第四（直錄其事觀慧別出餘文）

Ru-Yi Translation

Fourth: The Rite of Repentance Invoking Avalokiteśvara (recording only the procedure; the contemplative wisdom is given separately in other texts)

Chinese Source

經云。三七日、七七日，悉應六齋建首，當嚴飾道場，香泥塗地懸諸幡蓋，安佛像南向，觀世音像別東向，日別楊枝淨水、燒香散華。行者十人已還，當西向席地，地若卑濕置低脚床。當脫淨衣，左右出入，洗浴竟著淨服。當日日盡力供養，若不能辦，初日不可無施。安畢各執香罏一心一意，向彼西方五體投地，使明了音聲者唱云：

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: [For] three sets of seven days or seven sets of seven days, all should begin at the head of the six fast days. One should adorn the place of practice, smear the ground with fragrant paste, and hang banners and canopies; set up the Buddha image facing south, and the image of Avalokiteśvara separately, facing east; each day [offer] a willow branch and pure water, burn incense, and scatter flowers. The practitioners, ten people or fewer, should face west and sit on mats on the ground; if the ground is low and damp, set out low-legged couches. One should take off the clean garments when going out to relieve oneself, and after bathing put on clean clothes. One should make offerings each day to the utmost of one's means; if one cannot provide them, on the first day there must not fail to be an offering. When the arrangements are complete, each holds a censer, and with a single mind and a single intent prostrates with the five limbs on the ground toward the west; one who has a clear voice is made to chant, saying:

Bowing to the Three Jewels

Chinese Source

一心頂禮本師釋迦牟尼世尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to my original teacher, the World-Honored Śākyamuni.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮西方無量壽世尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to the World-Honored Amitāyus of the west.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮七佛世尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to the seven buddhas, World-Honored Ones.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮十方一切諸佛世尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to all the buddhas, World-Honored Ones, of the ten directions.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮消伏毒害陀羅尼破惡業障陀羅尼六字章句陀羅尼。

With a single mind I bow in homage to the dhāraṇī that dispels and subdues poison and harm, the dhāraṇī that breaks the hindrances of evil karma, and the dhāraṇī of the six-syllable phrases.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮十方一切尊法。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to all the honored Dharma of the ten directions.

Chinese Source

一心頂禮觀世音菩薩摩訶薩。

Chinese Source

Chinese Source

一心頂禮十方一切諸菩薩摩訶薩。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I bow in homage to all the bodhisattva-mahāsattvas of the ten directions.

Offering, Counting the Breath, and Invocation

Chinese Source

禮竟，燒香散華而作是言：「是諸眾等各各互跪，嚴持香華如法供養，供養十方法界三寶。」念想竟口發誠言：願此香華雲，遍滿十方界，供養一切佛，尊法諸菩薩，無量聲聞眾，以起光明臺，過於無邊界，無邊佛土中，受用作佛事，普熏諸眾生，皆發菩提心。供養訖當向於西方，結跏趺坐繫念數息，令心不散，勿數風喘氣。為眾生故，經十念頃成十念已，次念十方佛及七佛世尊，色身實相妙身猶如虛空。又當慈念念一切眾生。作此念時如一上禪，久運念已安詳徐覺。一人裝香火，各各互跪召請。

Ru-Yi Translation

When the bowing is finished, burn incense and scatter flowers and say: "Let this whole assembly, each and every one, kneel with one knee raised, holding incense and flowers reverently, and make offering as the Dharma directs, offering to the Three Jewels of the dharma-realm of the ten directions." When the visualization is finished, speak sincere words aloud: May this cloud of incense and flowers / fill the realms of the ten directions, / making offering to all the buddhas, / the honored Dharma, and the bodhisattvas, / and the immeasurable assembly of śrāvakas; / may it raise a terrace of light / passing beyond boundless realms, / and in boundless buddha-lands / be received and used to do the work of the Buddha, / perfuming all beings everywhere, / so that all arouse the bodhi mind. When the offering is finished, one should face west, sit in the cross-legged posture, bind one's thought and count the breath, keeping the mind from scattering; do not count the windy, panting breath. For the sake of beings, [count] for the space of ten thoughts; when the ten thoughts are accomplished, next be mindful of the buddhas of the ten directions and the seven buddhas, World-Honored Ones: their form bodies, the true mark, the wondrous body like empty space. Also one should be mindful with kindness of all beings. When making this mindfulness, it is like a superior dhyāna; having kept up the mindfulness for a long time, calmly and slowly come out of it. One person sets out the incense and fire, and each, kneeling with one knee raised, invokes:

Chinese Source

一心奉請南無本師釋迦文佛（三遍奉請前所禮三寶）。

Ru-Yi Translation

With a single mind I respectfully invite: Homage to my original teacher, Śākyamuni Buddha (three times respectfully invite the Three Jewels bowed to above).

Chinese Source

召請竟云：我今已具楊枝淨水，唯願大悲哀憐攝受（三說）。

Ru-Yi Translation

When the invocation is finished, say: I have now prepared a willow branch and pure water; I pray only that Great Compassion have pity and receive them (said three times).

The Order of Recitation, Repentance, and Circumambulation

Chinese Source

次三稱三寶名、觀世音名。次合掌說偈，願救我苦厄去訖偈後四長行經文。次誦消伏毒害呪，說呪後七行經文（或三遍或七遍）。次更稱三寶名，誦破惡業障陀羅尼呪。次更稱三寶名，誦六字章句呪竟。自以智力披陳懺悔，破梵行人作十惡業，蕩除糞穢還得清淨。次當發願懺願竟，一心作禮，禮上來所請三寶。禮竟如法行道，或三或七旋竟三自歸，自歸竟令一人登高座，唱誦《請觀音經》。午前初夜施上方法，餘時坐禪禮佛依常法，是為一日一夜規矩。至第二乃至第七七日，亦復如是。

Ru-Yi Translation

Next, call the names of the Three Jewels and the name of Avalokiteśvara three times. Next, join the palms and speak the verse, from "May you rescue me from suffering and distress" to the end, and after the verse the four lines of prose sūtra text. Next, recite the mantra that dispels and subdues poison and harm, and after the mantra the seven lines of sūtra text (either three times or seven times). Next, again call the names of the Three Jewels and recite the dhāraṇī mantra that breaks the hindrances of evil karma. Next, again call the names of the Three Jewels and recite the mantra of the six-syllable phrases to the end. Then, by the power of one's own wisdom, lay open [one's offenses] and repent: those who have broken pure conduct and done the ten evil deeds are cleansed of that filth and made pure again. Next one should make vows; when the repentance and vows are finished, bow with a single mind, bowing to the Three Jewels invited above. When the bowing is finished, circumambulate according to the Dharma, three or seven circuits; when the circling is finished, [take] the three refuges; when the refuges are finished, have one person ascend the high seat and chant the Sūtra of Invoking Avalokiteśvara. Before noon and in the first watch of the night, apply the method above; at other times, sit in meditation and bow to the buddhas according to the usual method. This is the rule for one day and one night. Through the second, up to the seventh seven days, it is likewise.

Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

Source — Guoqing Bailu (T1934), fascicle 1, item 4, 請觀世音懺法第四 (checked in the vault's T1934). The interlinear heading note 直錄其事觀慧別出餘文 is Guanding's.

"The sūtra says" (unit 1) — the sūtra itself (Part 1) mentions only 七七日 (unit 16) and gives none of the room arrangements; the arrangements here are Zhiyi's. The site's Mohe Zhiguan, fascicle 2, gives a parallel outline (images in the west, willow branch and pure water, ten breaths, three dhāraṇīs, one person on the high seat chanting the sūtra).

六齋建首 (unit 1) — the six monthly fast days (8th, 14th, 15th, 23rd, 29th, 30th); the rite begins on one of them. The site's Mohe Zhiguan has "on the fast-day, at the outset."

左右出入 (unit 1) — 左右 means relieving oneself, as in Mohe Zhiguan 便利左右.

一心頂禮 (units 2–11) — "With a single mind I bow in homage to," following the BRIEF's 一心 "with a single mind" and the site's Great Compassion Repentance for 頂禮 "bow in homage" (the site has "With sincere mind, we bow in homage to").

互跪 (units 12, 13) — a variant writing of 胡跪, "kneel with one knee raised."

Offering formula (unit 12) — the opening sentence follows the site's Great Compassion Repentance ("Let this whole assembly, each and every one, kneel with one knee raised…"). The verse 願此香華雲 is the standard offering verse; its first lines follow the round-two draft of the Lotus Samādhi Repentance (fahua-sanmei-chanyi, Part 1).

Sūtra wording reused from Part 1 — "bind one's thought and count the breath, keeping the mind from scattering … for the space of ten thoughts" (sūtra unit 3); "a willow branch and pure water"; the verse "May you rescue me from suffering and distress" (sūtra unit 4); "the dhāraṇī that breaks the hindrances of evil karma"; "those who have broken pure conduct and done the ten evil deeds are cleansed of that filth and made pure again" (sūtra unit 14); "the seven buddhas, World-Honored Ones" (sūtra unit 14).

"Four lines" and "seven lines" (unit 15) — 四長行經文 seems to mean the prose passage after the verse (sūtra unit 8, 白佛言…); 說呪後七行經文 the passage after the first mantra (sūtra unit 10) or after the second (start of unit 14). Which passage is meant is not certain.

(或三遍或七遍) (unit 15) — the note probably applies to the number of recitations of the mantra.

至第二乃至第七七日 (unit 15) — translated "through the second, up to the seventh seven days." Alternatively: "from the second day up to the forty-ninth day."
