# The Sūtra of Invoking Avalokiteśvara — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Qing Guanshiyin jing* (請觀世音菩薩消伏毒害陀羅尼呪經, Taishō T20, no. 1043 (tr. Nandi); T46, no. 1934 (Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1043 and T1934 (fascicle 1, section 4), via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). The sūtra dates from the fourth or fifth century, the rite from the Sui; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 消伏毒害 | dispelling and subduing poison and harm | The sūtra's purpose. |
| 楊枝淨水 | willow branch and pure water | The offering of the invocation. |
| 六字章句 | the six-syllable phrases | The dhāraṇī Tiantai interprets in its commentary. |
