Fascicle 8

From the asuras and the demon kings to the whole of the human destiny《慈悲道場懺法》卷第八・為六道禮佛第二（續）

This fascicle completes the homage owed to the non-human destinies — the asuras, the dragon kings, and, most strikingly, the demon kings themselves — before turning to the human destiny and moving through it rank by rank: the sovereign, the crown prince and the princes, parents living and deceased, teachers and masters, and finally the monastic and lay community, living and deceased. Ten rounds of homage in all, each following the same rhythm of invocation, a roll call of Buddhas and bodhisattvas, refuge, and dedication.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Source: Taishō T1909 Chapters: 為六道禮佛第二（續） Status: complete
Chinese
《慈悲道場懺法》卷第八 — the eighth of ten fascicles, continuing the chapter Homage to the Buddhas on Behalf of the Six Destinies (為六道禮佛第二) begun in Fascicle 7. Completes the homage to the asuras, dragon kings, and demon kings, then opens and completes the seven-part homage to the human destiny (為人道禮佛第七): the sovereign, the princes, parents, past-life parents, teachers, and the monastic community living and deceased.
Covers
Ten rounds of homage in total, each built from the same liturgical unit — an invocation, twenty-seven buddha-names and two bodhisattva-names, a refuge formula, and a dedication of merit: on behalf of the asura king and his court; every dragon king, organized by direction, element, and calendar; the Great Demon King and every demon king, prayed for in the same terms as every other being; the reigning sovereign; the crown prince and the enfeoffed princes; one's own parents; one's parents across past lives whose present destiny is unknown; one's teachers and preceptors; and the monastic and lay community of the ten directions, living and deceased.
Why it matters
The homage to the demon kings is the most theologically pointed moment in the fascicle: Māra, the traditional adversary of every buddha, receives the identical liturgical treatment given to every other being, with a closing vow that he himself might "deliver living beings first and only then become a buddha." Two small textual notes preserved in the Taishō edition — alternate readings naming "the Emperor of the Great Liang" and "the Kings of Linchuān" — are rare, direct traces of this liturgy's long life being adapted, court by court, for use well beyond the reign it was first composed for.
Difficulty
★★☆☆☆ — ten short, formulaic rounds of homage in close succession; individually simple, but repetitive to read straight through, and best approached one section at a time.

1. Homage on Behalf of the Asuras奉為阿脩羅道一切善神禮佛第四

Chinese Source

今日道場同業大眾！重復至誠五體投地，奉為十方盡虛空界一切阿脩羅王、一切阿脩羅各及眷屬；又奉為十方盡虛空界一切聰明正直天地虛空主善罰惡守護持呪八部神王八部神將；乃至若內若外若近若遠、東西南北四維上下遍空法界，有大神足力、有大威德力，如是十方八部神王、八部神將各及眷屬，歸命敬禮一切世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無寶名佛　南無眾清淨佛　南無無邊名佛　南無不虛光佛　南無聖天佛　南無智王佛　南無金剛眾佛　南無善障佛　南無建慈佛　南無華國佛　南無法意佛　南無風行佛　南無善思名佛　南無多明佛　南無密眾佛　南無功德守佛　南無利意佛　南無無懼佛　南無堅觀佛　南無住法佛　南無珠足佛　南無解脫德佛　南無妙身佛　南無善高佛　南無普德佛　南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸命如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。 願以慈悲力同加覆護，願阿脩羅王、一切阿脩羅各及眷屬；又願聰明正直天地虛空主善罰惡守護持呪八部神王、八部神將各及眷屬，解脫客塵清淨緣障，發起大乘修無礙道，四無量心、六波羅蜜常得現前，四辯、六通如意自在，恒以慈悲救護眾生，行菩薩道入佛智慧，度金剛心成等正覺。

Ru-Yi Translation

Today, the assembly gathered together in this common practice — once again, with utmost sincerity, let us prostrate ourselves fully to the ground, on behalf of every asura king and every asura throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space, each together with their retinue; and further on behalf of every wise and upright spirit of heaven, earth, and empty space throughout the ten directions who rewards good and punishes evil and upholds spells, every spirit-king and spirit-general of the eightfold division; extending to every such being, whether within or without, whether near or far, in the four cardinal and four intermediate directions, above and below, throughout the whole Dharma-realm of empty space, possessed of great spiritual power and great majestic virtue — every spirit-king and spirit-general of the eightfold division throughout the ten directions, each together with their retinue, take refuge, offering up our very lives in reverent homage to the great and compassionate father of every world. Homage to the Buddha, Guardian of Merit. Homage to the Buddha of Beneficial Intent. Homage to the Buddha Without Fear. Homage to the Buddha of Firm Contemplation. Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Asuras occupy an ambiguous place in Buddhist cosmology — powerful, often warlike beings, sometimes counted among the six destinies and sometimes folded into the heavens, defined less by suffering than by a corrosive envy of the gods. This section gives them exactly the same liturgical treatment as every other realm: the same twenty-seven buddhas, the same vow of an unhindered path to awakening. Nothing here singles out the asuras' particular fault for correction; the rite's universality is doing the argument's work instead.

2. Homage on Behalf of the Dragon Kings奉為龍王禮佛第五

Chinese Source

今日道場同業大眾！重復至誠五體投地，奉為十方盡虛空界一切不可思議龍王，妙化龍王、頭化提龍王、五方龍王、天龍王、地龍王、山龍王、海龍王、日宮龍王、月宮龍王、星宮龍王、歲時龍王、青海龍王、護形命龍王、護眾生龍王，乃至十方若內若外若近若遠、東西南北四維上下遍空法界，有大神足力、有大威德力，如是一切龍王、一切龍神各及眷屬，歸命敬禮一切世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無妙智佛　南無梵財佛　南無實音佛　南無正智佛　南無力得佛　南無師子意佛　南無華相佛　南無積智佛　南無華齒佛　南無功德藏佛　南無名實佛　南無希有名佛　南無上戒佛　南無無畏佛　南無日明佛　南無梵壽佛　南無一切天佛　南無樂智佛　南無寶天佛　南無珠藏佛　南無德流布佛　南無智王佛　南無無縛佛　南無堅法佛　南無天德佛 南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸命如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。 願以慈悲力同加攝受，願諸龍王各及眷屬，增輝光明神力自在，以無相解斷除緣障，永離惡趣常生淨土，四無量心、六波羅蜜常得現前，四無礙辯、六神通力隨心自在，以慈悲力拯接一切，妙行莊嚴過法雲地，入金剛心成等正覺。

Homage to the Buddha of Rare Renown. Homage to the Buddha of the Supreme Precept. Homage to the Buddha Fearless. Homage to the Buddha of the Sun's Brightness. Homage to the Buddha of Brahmā's Life. Homage to the Buddha of Every Heaven. Homage to the Buddha, Delight in Wisdom. Homage to the Buddha of the Jeweled Heaven. Homage to the Buddha, Treasury of Pearls. Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The invocation here is effectively a small cosmological census: dragon kings sorted by the five directions, by realm (heaven, earth, mountain, sea), by celestial body (sun, moon, the constellations), and by the calendar itself ("the dragon king of the years and seasons"). Dragons in Chinese religion were already bound up with weather, water, and the turning of the year long before Buddhism arrived, and this list reads as the liturgy absorbing that entire indigenous system wholesale rather than replacing it — every dragon a Chinese audience of the sixth century might already have honored is folded into a single Buddhist rite of homage.

3. Homage on Behalf of the Demon Kings奉為魔王禮佛第六

Chinese Source

今日道場同業大眾！重復至誠，奉為大魔王、五帝大魔，乃至東西南北四維上下盡虛空界一切魔王各及眷屬，歸命敬禮一切世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無梵牟尼佛　南無安詳行佛　南無勤精進佛　南無炎肩佛　南無大威德佛　南無薝蔔華佛　南無歡喜佛　南無善眾佛　南無帝幢佛　南無大愛佛　南無須蔓色佛　南無眾妙佛　南無可樂佛　南無善定義佛　南無牛王佛　南無妙臂佛　南無大車佛　南無滿願佛　南無德光佛　南無寶音佛　南無金剛軍佛　南無富貴佛　南無勢力行佛　南無師子力佛　南無淨目佛 南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸命如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。 願以慈悲力同加覆護，願大魔王、五帝大魔、一切魔王各及眷屬，無始已來至于今日，一切緣障皆得清淨，一切罪業皆得銷滅，一切眾苦皆得解脫，四無量心、六波羅蜜常得現前，四無礙智、六神通力如意自在，行菩薩道不休不息，先度眾生然後作佛。

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Of everything in this fascicle, the closing line here is the most theologically pointed: that the demon kings themselves — the very figures traditionally cast as the Buddha's adversary, the tempter who assailed him beneath the bodhi tree — might one day "deliver living beings first and only then become a buddha," the classical formula for a bodhisattva's own vow. The liturgy does not soften Māra's role or explain it away; it simply extends the same possibility of awakening to him that it extends to everyone else, without exception, treating even cosmic opposition to the Dharma as a temporary condition rather than a fixed nature.

4. The Human Destiny, Opening with the Sovereign為人道禮佛第七・奉為國王禮佛第一

Chinese Source

今日道場同業大眾！相與已得奉為諸天諸仙、龍神八部禮佛竟，次應奉為人道一切人王禮佛報恩，又為父母師長、一切人民。何以故爾？若無國王，一切眾生無所依附。由大王故一切得住，行國王地、飲國王水，諸餘利益不可具說。大眾宜各起報恩心。經言：若能一日一夜六時忍苦為欲利益奉報恩者，應當發起如是等心習行慈悲。以是願力，報國王恩、報施主恩。又當念行道報父母慈育之恩，又當行道報師長慈訓之恩，次復運心念如來恩。若能至心常念不絕者，如是等人得入道疾。 今日道場同業大眾！諸佛大聖慈恩開誘慇懃如此令知恩報恩，我等今日既仰賴國王，於末世中興顯佛法，種種供養不惜財寶，率土臣民望風歸附。又令出家之人安身行道，行住坐臥初無留難，凡百不預唯獎以善，皆願我等速出生死，闡無量法門、開人天正路。而國王有如此恩德，豈得不人人禮拜奉報其恩？相與至心等一痛切，奉為國王歸依世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無迦葉佛　南無淨意佛　南無知次第佛　南無猛威德佛　南無大光明佛　南無日光曜佛　南無淨藏佛　南無分別威佛　南無無損佛　南無密日佛　南無月光佛　南無持明佛　南無善寂行佛　南無不動佛　南無大請佛　南無德法佛　南無莊嚴王佛　南無高出佛　南無炎熾佛　南無華德佛　南無寶嚴佛　南無上善佛　南無寶上佛　南無利慧佛　南無嚴土佛 南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸命如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。願以慈悲力同加攝受，願 當今皇帝〔舊云大梁皇帝〕聖體康御、天威振遠，帝基永固、慧命無窮，慈霑無際、有識歸心，菩薩盛化、天人讚仰，四等、六度日夜增明，四無礙辯樂說無盡，得八自在、具六神通，三昧總持應念現前，慈悲即世、恩遍六道，萬行早圓速登正覺。

Ru-Yi Translation

Today, the assembly gathered together in this common practice — together we have now finished paying homage to the gods, the immortals, and the dragon spirits of the eightfold division; next we should pay homage to every human sovereign of the human destiny, to repay their kindness, and also to our parents and teachers and every one of the people. Why is this so? Without a sovereign, living beings would have nothing to rely upon at all. It is because of the great sovereign that all are able to dwell in peace, walking the sovereign's own land and drinking the sovereign's own water — every other benefit besides these cannot all be told. The assembly should each arouse the mind that repays kindness. The sūtra says: whoever is able, for a single day and night, through all six periods, to endure hardship for the sake of benefiting others and repaying kindness, should arouse a mind such as this and practice compassion. By the power of this vow, let us repay the kindness of the sovereign and the kindness of our benefactors; let us further keep in mind the practice of the Way to repay our parents' kindness in raising us with love, and further practice the Way to repay our teachers' kindness in instructing us with compassion; and next, let us turn our minds to the kindness of the Tathāgata himself. Whoever is able to hold this in mind sincerely and without interruption will be one who enters the Way swiftly. Today, the assembly gathered together in this common practice — the Buddhas and great sages, in their compassionate kindness, guide and instruct us so earnestly, teaching us to know kindness and to repay kindness. We ourselves, today, rely upon the sovereign, through whom the Buddha-dharma is revived and made manifest even in this latter age, offerings of every kind made without begrudging any treasure, so that the ministers and subjects of the whole realm look to his example and turn toward him in allegiance. It is he, too, who allows those who have left the household life to settle their bodies and practice the Way, walking, standing, sitting, and lying down without hindrance from the start, interfering in nothing but encouraging only what is good, wishing only that we swiftly escape birth and death, opening up boundless gateways to the Dharma and the true path for gods and humans alike. And since the sovereign holds kindness and virtue such as this, how could each of us fail to bow down and repay this very kindness? Together, with sincere mind, in this same anguish of resolve, on behalf of the sovereign, let us take refuge in the great and compassionate father of the world. Homage to the Buddha Maitreya. Homage to the Buddha, Adorned King. Homage to the Buddha Rising Above. Homage to the Buddha of the Blazing Flame. Homage to the Buddha of Flower Virtue. Homage to the Buddha of Jeweled Adornment. 今日道場同業大眾！以此禮拜之次，重復增到五體投地，普為十方盡虛空界現在未來一切比丘、比丘尼、式叉摩那、沙彌、沙彌尼各及眷屬，又為十方盡虛空界一切優婆塞、優婆夷各及眷屬，又為從來信施檀越、善惡知識、有緣無緣各及眷屬，如是人道一切人類各及眷屬，某甲等今日以慈悲心，普為歸依世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無燈王佛　南無智頂佛　南無上天佛　南無地王佛　南無至解脫佛　南無金髻佛　南無羅睺日佛　南無莫能勝佛　南無牟尼淨佛　南無善光佛　南無金齊佛　南無眾德天王佛　南無法益佛　南無德臂佛　南無鴦伽陀佛　南無美妙惠佛　南無微意佛　南無諸威德佛　南無師子髮佛　南無解脫相佛　南無威相佛　南無斷流佛　南無慧藏佛　南無智聚佛　南無無礙讚佛 南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸依如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。 願以慈悲力同加覆護，願十方盡虛空界一切比丘、比丘尼、式叉摩那、沙彌、沙彌尼各及眷屬，又願十方一切優婆塞、優婆夷各及眷屬，又願從來信施檀越、善惡知識、有緣無緣各及眷屬，乃至一切人道、一切人類，無始已來至于今日，一切煩惱皆得斷除，一切緣障皆得清淨，一切罪業皆得消滅，一切眾苦皆得解脫，離三障業、無五怖畏，四無量心、六波羅蜜常得現前，四無礙智、六神通力如意自在，行菩薩行入一乘道，度脫無邊一切眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

Today, the assembly gathered together in this common practice — following upon this same bowing, let us once more with heightened devotion prostrate ourselves fully to the ground, universally on behalf of every monk, nun, probationary nun, novice monk, and novice nun, present and future, throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space, each together with their retinue; and further on behalf of every layman and laywoman throughout the ten directions, each together with their retinue; and further on behalf of every benefactor and donor who has ever given, every good and evil companion, whether bound to us by karmic affinity or not, each together with their retinue; and on behalf of every member of humankind in the human destiny, each together with their retinue — we disciples, today, with a compassionate mind, universally take refuge, on their behalf, in the great and compassionate father of the world. Homage to the Buddha Maitreya. Homage to the Buddha Śākyamuni. Homage to the Buddha, King of Lamps. Homage to the Buddha of the Summit of Wisdom. Homage to the Buddha of the Supreme Heaven. Homage to the Buddha, King of the Earth. Homage to the Buddha of Utmost Liberation. Homage to the Buddha of the Golden Topknot. Homage to the Buddha of Rāhu's Sun. Homage to the Buddha Whom None Can Surpass. Homage to the Buddha, Pure Muni. Homage to the Buddha of Excellent Light. Homage to the Buddha of Golden Equanimity. Homage to the Buddha, Heavenly King of Manifold Virtue. Homage to the Buddha of the Dharma's Benefit. Homage to the Buddha of the Virtuous Arm. Homage to the Buddha Aṅgada. Homage to the Buddha of Beautiful and Wondrous Wisdom. Homage to the Buddha of Subtle Intent. Homage to the Buddha of Every Majesty and Virtue. Homage to the Buddha of the Lion's Mane. Homage to the Buddha of the Mark of Liberation. Homage to the Buddha of the Majestic Mark. Homage to the Buddha Who Cuts Off the Stream. Homage to the Buddha, Treasury of Wisdom. Homage to the Buddha, Gathering of Wisdom. Homage to the Buddha Praised as Unhindered. Homage to the Bodhisattva of Boundless Body. Homage to the Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. We further take refuge in all the Three Jewels throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space in this way. We pray that, by the power of compassion, they together extend their shelter, and that every monk, nun, probationary nun, novice monk, and novice nun throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space, each together with their retinue; and further, every layman and laywoman throughout the ten directions, each together with their retinue; and further, every benefactor and donor who has ever given, every good and evil companion, whether bound to us by karmic affinity or not, each together with their retinue; extending to every being of the human destiny and every member of humankind, from beginningless time until this very day, may have every affliction cut off, every obstruction of conditioned fault made pure, every karmic fault dissolved, and every suffering liberated, free of the karma of the three obstructions and the five fears, the four immeasurable minds and six perfections constantly manifest before them, the four unhindered wisdoms and six supernormal powers at ease as they wish, practicing bodhisattva conduct and entering the path of the single vehicle, delivering every boundless living being.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This section widens the human destiny's homage out from named relationships — sovereign, prince, parent, teacher — to the entire remaining population the liturgy has not yet named: monastics, lay disciples, benefactors, and, in its final phrase, "every good and evil companion, whether bound to us by karmic affinity or not." That last clause is doing real work: it extends the homage even to strangers and to those the confessor may actively dislike, closing off any reading of this liturgy's universal scope as applying only to people already connected to the practitioner by relationship or gratitude.

10. Homage on Behalf of the Deceased Monks and Nuns of the Ten Directions為十方過去比丘比丘尼禮佛第七

Chinese Source

今日道場同業大眾，重復至誠五體投地，代為十方盡虛空界一切過去比丘、比丘尼、式叉摩那、沙彌、沙彌尼，過去優婆塞、優婆夷，廣及十方一切人道、一切人類，有命過者各及眷屬，某甲等今日以慈悲心，等諸佛心、同諸佛願，普為歸依世間大慈悲父。 南無彌勒佛　南無釋迦牟尼佛　南無寶聚佛　南無善高佛　南無山王相佛　南無法頂佛　南無解脫德佛　南無善端嚴佛　南無吉身佛　南無愛語佛　南無師子利佛　南無和樓那佛　南無師子法佛　南無法力佛　南無愛樂佛　南無讚不動佛　南無眾明王佛　南無覺悟眾生佛　南無妙眼佛　南無意住義佛　南無光照佛　南無香德佛　南無令喜佛　南無不虛行佛　南無滅恚佛　南無上色佛　南無善步佛 南無無邊身菩薩　南無觀世音菩薩 又復歸依如是十方盡虛空界一切三寶。 願以慈悲力救護拯接，願過去一切比丘、比丘尼、式叉摩那、沙彌、沙彌尼各及眷屬，又願過去一切優婆塞、優婆夷各及眷屬，若有地獄道苦，今日即得解脫；若有畜生道苦，今日即得解脫；若有鬼神道苦，今日即得解脫；離八難地受八福生，永捨惡道長生淨土，財施無盡、法施無盡，福德無盡、安樂無盡，壽命無盡、智慧無盡，四無量心、六波羅蜜常得現前，四無礙智、六神通力如意自在，常得見佛聞法，行菩薩道勇猛精進不休不息，乃至進修成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，廣能度脫一切眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

Today, the assembly gathered together in this common practice — once again, with utmost sincerity, let us prostrate ourselves fully to the ground, standing in on behalf of every deceased monk, nun, probationary nun, novice monk, and novice nun throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space, every deceased layman and laywoman, and, extending broadly to every being of the human destiny and every member of humankind throughout the ten directions who has passed from this life, each together with their retinue — we disciples, today, with a compassionate mind, equal to the mind of all the Buddhas and one with the vow of all the Buddhas, universally take refuge, on their behalf, in the great and compassionate father of the world. Homage to the Buddha Maitreya. Homage to the Buddha Śākyamuni. Homage to the Buddha, Gathering of Jewels. Homage to the Buddha of Excellent Loftiness. Homage to the Buddha of the Mark of the Mountain King. Homage to the Buddha of the Summit of the Dharma. Homage to the Buddha of the Virtue of Liberation. Homage to the Buddha of Excellent Dignified Adornment. Homage to the Buddha of the Auspicious Body. Homage to the Buddha of Loving Speech. Homage to the Buddha of the Lion's Benefit. Homage to the Buddha Varuṇa. Homage to the Buddha of the Lion's Dharma. Homage to the Buddha of the Power of the Dharma. Homage to the Buddha of Loving Delight. Homage to the Buddha Praised as Unmoving. Homage to the Buddha, King of Manifold Brightness. Homage to the Buddha, Awakener of Living Beings. Homage to the Buddha of the Wondrous Eye. Homage to the Buddha of Meaning Abiding in Intent. Homage to the Buddha of Illuminating Light. Homage to the Buddha of Fragrant Virtue. Homage to the Buddha, Bringer of Joy. Homage to the Buddha Whose Conduct Is Never in Vain. Homage to the Buddha Who Extinguishes Hatred. Homage to the Buddha of Supreme Form. Homage to the Buddha of Excellent Stride. Homage to the Bodhisattva of Boundless Body. Homage to the Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. We further take refuge in all the Three Jewels throughout the ten directions and the whole reach of empty space in this way. We pray that, by the power of compassion, they rescue and protect us, and that every deceased monk, nun, probationary nun, novice monk, and novice nun, each together with their retinue, and further, every deceased layman and laywoman, each together with their retinue — should they bear the suffering of the hell-destiny, may they be liberated from it this very day; should they bear the suffering of the animal-destiny, may they be liberated from it this very day; should they bear the suffering of the ghost-and-spirit destiny, may they be liberated from it this very day — leaving behind the ground of the eight adversities to receive birth amid the eight blessings, forever giving up the evil destinies to dwell long in the pure land, their giving of wealth inexhaustible, their giving of the Dharma inexhaustible, their blessed virtue inexhaustible, their peace and joy inexhaustible, their lifespan inexhaustible, their wisdom inexhaustible, the four immeasurable minds and six perfections constantly manifest before them, the four unhindered wisdoms and six supernormal powers at ease as they wish, constantly seeing the Buddhas and hearing the Dharma, walking the bodhisattva path with courage and diligence without rest, advancing in cultivation until accomplishing unsurpassed, perfect awakening, and broadly delivering every living being.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This closing section completes a deliberate pairing that has now run through the whole of the human-destiny sequence: parents living, then parents deceased; teachers by implication both; and now the wider community, living, then deceased. The refrain repeated three times in the dedication — "should they bear the suffering of the hell-destiny... the animal-destiny... the ghost-and-spirit destiny, may they be liberated from it this very day" — is the most urgent, unconditional language used anywhere in this fascicle, asking not for gradual improvement but immediate release, regardless of which of the three evil paths a deceased stranger might currently occupy.
