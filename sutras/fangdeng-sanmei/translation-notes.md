# The Practice of the Vaipulya Samādhi — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Fangdeng sanmei xingfa* (方等三昧行法, Taishō T46, no. 1940 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1940, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 方等 | Vaipulya | The class of sūtras the practice is drawn from. |
| 十二夢王 | the twelve dream kings | Dream signs that permit the practice. |
| 半行半坐 | half walking and half sitting | Its place among the four samādhis. |
