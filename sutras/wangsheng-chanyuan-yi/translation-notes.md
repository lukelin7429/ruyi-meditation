# The Rite of Repentance and Vows for Rebirth in the Pure Land — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Wangsheng jingtu chanyuan yi* (往生淨土懺願儀, Taishō T47, no. 1984 (Ciyun Zunshi)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1984, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zunshi died in 1032; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 懺願 | repentance and vows | The two movements of the rite. |
| 極樂 | Sukhāvatī | The land of Amitābha. |
| 坐禪 | seated meditation | The tenth section, contemplating the Buddha and his land. |
