Bowing to the Buddhas, Repentance and Vows, Circumambulation, and Seated Meditation

Part 2 contains the last four sections of the rite and Zunshi's postface. Section Seven is the bowing: to Śākyamuni, the fifty-three ancient buddhas and Lokeśvararāja, the buddhas of the ten directions named in Xuanzang's Sūtra in Praise of the Pure Land, Amitābha, the Dharma of the Pure Land and of this world, the bodhisattvas, and the saṅgha, each bow accompanied by a short visualization verse in which practitioner and buddha appear as images in each other, like the jewels of Indra's net. Section Eight is the fivefold repentance — repentance, requesting, rejoicing, dedication, and making vows — whose first and last members give the rite its name. Repentance is to be done both in phenomena (with the whole body, tears, and full disclosure) and in principle (contemplating that offense is empty), and it is framed by the ten compliant minds that lead into birth and death and the ten contrary minds that reverse them. The requesting, rejoicing, and dedication texts are taken from the verses of Samantabhadra's vows; the final vow asks for a clear death and rebirth in a lotus in Sukhāvatī. Section Nine is circumambulation with recitation of the Amitābha or Contemplation Sūtra, the three refuges, and a passage from the Immeasurable Life Sūtra. Section Ten gives two seated visualizations: picturing one's own rebirth (the Contemplation Sūtra's general contemplation) and fixing the mind on Amitābha's white tuft, then seeing it as empty, provisional, and the middle.

Seventh: The Method of Bowing to the Buddhas

Chinese Source

（讚歎竟，應禮諸佛。當禮佛時，須想一切諸佛是我慈父，能令我生諸佛淨土故。）

Ru-Yi Translation

(When the praise is finished, one should bow to the buddhas. When bowing to the buddhas, one must visualize all the buddhas as one's compassionate father, because they can cause one to be born in the pure lands of the buddhas.)

Chinese Source

一心敬禮本師釋迦牟尼佛。（唱竟，一禮。想云：「能禮、所禮性空寂，感應道交難思議，我此道場如帝珠，釋迦如來影現中，我身影現釋迦前，頭面接足歸命禮。」下去同用。）

Ru-Yi Translation

"With a single mind I bow in reverence to our original teacher, Śākyamuni Buddha." (Having chanted, bow once. Visualize, saying: "The nature of the one who bows and the one bowed to is empty and quiescent; / the mutual resonance between response and appeal is inconceivable. / This place of practice of mine is like Indra's jewel: / the Tathāgata Śākyamuni appears as an image within it, / and my body appears as an image before Śākyamuni, / bowing head and face to his feet, I take refuge and bow." The same is used from here on.)

The verse is as above, only changed to "the fifty-three buddhas appear as images within it, / and my body appears as an image before the buddhas"; those below follow this.)

Bowing to the Dharma, the Bodhisattvas, and the Saṅgha

Chinese Source

一心敬禮極樂世界佛菩薩等所說經法，乃至水鳥、樂樹、一切法音清淨法藏。（想彼淨土法寶，顯現道場，偈云：「真空法性如虛空，常住法寶難思議，我身影現法寶前，一一皆悉歸命禮。」）

Ru-Yi Translation

"With a single mind I bow in reverence to the sūtras and teachings spoken by the buddhas, bodhisattvas, and others of the world of Sukhāvatī, down to the water, birds, musical trees, and all the sounds of Dharma — the pure Dharma-treasury." (Visualize the Dharma-jewel of that Pure Land appearing in the place of practice. The verse says: "The dharma-nature of true emptiness is like empty space; / the eternally abiding Dharma-jewel is inconceivable; / my body appears as an image before the Dharma-jewel, / and to each and every one I take refuge and bow.")

Ru-Yi Translation

"With a single mind I bow in reverence to the Mahāyāna Sūtra of Immeasurable Life of the forty-eight vows, the Sūtra in Praise and the others, all the honored sūtras of the ten directions, the true and pure Dharma-treasury of the twelve divisions." (The Dharma-jewel of this land; the verse is as before.)

一、明懺悔法

Ru-Yi Translation

1. Clarifying the Method of Repentance

Chinese Source

（悔有事、理，應須並運。事則竭其三業，不惜身命，流血雨淚，披露罪根，不敢覆諱。理則觀罪實相，能懺、所懺皆悉寂滅，如餘文廣說。知事、理已，當須等心普為一切懺悔，想云：「我及眾生，無始常為三業、六根重罪所障，不見諸佛，不知出要，但順生死，不知妙理。我今雖知，猶與一切眾生同為一切重罪所障。今對彌陀十方佛前，普為眾生歸命懺悔，唯願加護，令障消滅。」想已，唱云：）

Ru-Yi Translation

(Repentance has phenomena and principle; both must be carried out together. In phenomena, one exhausts the three kinds of action, not sparing body or life, shedding blood and raining tears, laying bare the roots of offense, not daring to cover or hide them. In principle, one contemplates the true mark of offense: the one who repents and what is repented are all quiescent and extinguished, as explained at length in other texts. Having known phenomena and principle, one must, with an impartial mind, repent universally for all. Visualize, saying: "I and sentient beings, from beginningless time, have always been obstructed by the grave offenses of the three kinds of action and the six faculties; we have not seen the buddhas, have not known the essential way out, have only followed birth and death, and have not known the wondrous principle. Though I now know it, I am still, together with all sentient beings, obstructed by all grave offenses. Now before Amitābha and the buddhas of the ten directions, I take refuge and repent universally for sentient beings; I pray only that they add their protection, so that the obstructions may be extinguished." Having visualized, chant:)

The Ten Compliant and Ten Contrary Minds

Chinese Source

普為法界一切眾生，悉願斷除三障，至誠懺悔。（唱已，一禮，各執香爐，先運逆順十心。始則背真逐妄，名順十心；今則背妄向真，名逆十心。有事有理，應細思之，從初至撥無因果是順，故於今日深信下去是逆，至正陳懺悔時，亦須扶此逆順之意，

Ru-Yi Translation

"Universally, on behalf of all sentient beings of the dharma-realm, I vow that all may cut off the three obstructions, and with utmost sincerity I repent." (Having chanted, bow once; each holds a censer and first directs the ten contrary and compliant minds. At the beginning, turning one's back on the real and pursuing the false is called the ten compliant minds; now, turning one's back on the false and facing the real is called the ten contrary minds. They have phenomena and principle; one should reflect on them carefully. From the first up to "denying cause and effect" is compliant; from "therefore today I deeply believe" on is contrary. When one comes to the actual statement of repentance, one must also uphold this meaning of contrary and compliant.

Chinese Source

想云：「我與眾生無始來今，由愛見故，內計我人，一；外加惡友，二；不隨喜他一毫之善，三；唯遍三業，廣造眾罪，四；事雖不廣，惡心遍布，五；晝夜相續，無有間斷，六；覆諱過失，不欲人知，七；不畏惡道，八；無慚無愧，九；撥無因果，十。故於今日深信因果，一；生重慚愧，二；生大怖畏，三；發露懺悔，四；斷相續心，發菩提心，斷惡修善，六；勤策三業，翻昔重過，七；隨喜凡聖一毫之善，八；念十方佛，有大福慧能救拔我及諸眾生，從二死海置三德岸，九；從無始來不知諸法本性空寂，廣造眾惡，今知空寂，為求菩提，為眾生故，廣修諸善，遍斷眾惡，十。」唯願十方諸佛、彌陀世尊，慈悲攝受，聽我懺悔。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Visualize, saying: "I and sentient beings, from beginningless time until now, because of craving and views, have inwardly reckoned self and others — one; outwardly added evil friends — two; not rejoiced in even a hair's breadth of another's good — three; only throughout the three kinds of action broadly committed many offenses — four; though the deeds were not broad, the evil mind spread everywhere — five; continued day and night without interruption — six; concealed and hidden our faults, unwilling to let others know — seven; not feared the evil paths — eight; been without shame and without conscience — nine; denied cause and effect — ten. Therefore today I deeply believe in cause and effect — one; give rise to heavy shame — two; give rise to great dread — three; reveal and repent — four; cut off the mind of continuation, arouse the bodhi-mind, cut off evil and cultivate good — six; diligently spur on the three kinds of action to overturn past grave faults — seven; rejoice in even a hair's breadth of the good of ordinary beings and sages — eight; keep in mind that the buddhas of the ten directions have great merit and wisdom and can rescue me and all sentient beings, [carrying us] from the ocean of the two kinds of death to the shore of the three virtues — nine; from beginningless time, not knowing that the original nature of all dharmas is empty and quiescent, I broadly created many evils; now, knowing they are empty and quiescent, to seek bodhi and for the sake of sentient beings, I broadly cultivate all goods and cut off all evils everywhere — ten." I pray only that the buddhas of the ten directions and the World-Honored Amitābha receive me with compassion and hear my repentance.)

The Repentance Text

Chinese Source

我比丘（某甲）至心懺悔，十方諸佛真實見知，我及眾生本性清淨，諸佛住處名常寂光，遍在剎那及一切法。而我不了，妄計我人，於平等法中而起分別，於清淨心中而生染著，以是顛倒五欲因緣，生死循環，經歷三界，坐此相續，不念出期。而復於中，造極惡業，四重、五逆及一闡提，非毀大乘，謗破三寶，謗無諸佛，斷學般若，用十方僧物、用佛塔物，污梵行人，習近惡法，於破戒者更相讚護，三乘道人種種毀罵，內覆過失，外現威儀，常以五邪招納四事，不淨說法，非律教人，因佛出家，反破佛法，違逆師長如法教誨，恣行貪恚，無慚恥心，以是因緣，諸惡業力，命終當墮阿鼻地獄，猛火熾然，受無量苦，千萬億劫，無解脫期。

Ru-Yi Translation

"I, the bhikṣu (so-and-so), with utmost mind repent. The buddhas of the ten directions truly see and know that I and sentient beings are originally pure in nature, and that the place where the buddhas abide is called the eternal tranquil light, present everywhere in every instant and in all dharmas. But I did not understand this: I falsely reckoned self and others, gave rise to discrimination within the dharma of equality, and gave rise to defiled attachment within the pure mind. Through these inverted causes and conditions of the five desires, birth and death have revolved in a cycle, passing through the three realms; sitting in this continuation, I have not thought of a time of escape. And within it, moreover, I have committed the most evil karma: the four grave [offenses], the five heinous [offenses], and [the deeds of] the icchantika; disparaging the Mahāyāna, slandering and destroying the Three Jewels, slandering [by saying] there are no buddhas, cutting off the study of prajñā; using the property of the saṅgha of the ten directions and the property of the buddhas' stūpas; defiling those of pure conduct; practicing and drawing near to evil dharmas; praising and protecting one another with those who break the precepts; reviling in every way the followers of the three vehicles; covering faults within while displaying dignified deportment without; always using the five wrong [livelihoods] to obtain the four requisites; teaching the Dharma impurely; instructing others contrary to the Vinaya; having left home through the Buddha, yet on the contrary destroying the Buddhadharma; defying the instruction of teachers and elders given according to the Dharma; indulging freely in greed and anger, without a sense of shame. By these causes and conditions and the power of all this evil karma, at the end of life I shall fall into Avīci hell, with fierce fire blazing, undergoing immeasurable suffering for thousands of myriads of koṭis of eons, with no time of liberation.

Chinese Source

今始覺知，生大慚愧，生大怖畏。十方世尊、阿彌陀佛，久已於我生大悲心，無數劫來，為度我故，修菩提道，不惜身命，今已得佛，大悲滿足，真實能為一切救護。我今造惡，必墮三塗，願起哀憐，受我懺悔，重罪得滅，諸惡消除，乃至娑婆生因永盡，諸佛淨土如願往生，當命終時悉無障疑。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now for the first time I awaken to it and know it; I give rise to great shame and give rise to great dread. The World-Honored Ones of the ten directions and Amitābha Buddha have long given rise to the mind of great compassion toward me; for countless eons, in order to deliver me, they cultivated the path of bodhi, not sparing body or life; now they have attained buddhahood, their great compassion is complete, and they can truly be the rescue and protection of all. Now that I have committed evil, I shall surely fall into the three [evil] paths; may they give rise to pity and accept my repentance, so that grave offenses are extinguished and all evils removed, until the causes of birth in the Sahā [world] are forever exhausted, and I am reborn as I wish in the pure lands of the buddhas, with no obstruction or doubt when my life ends."

Chinese Source

（懺悔已，歸命禮阿彌陀佛及一切三寶。禮已，以頭三叩於地，表三處作禮：一、謂彌陀世尊；二、淨土三寶；三、十方三寶。應三說懺悔等文，自看時早晚，若時促，略云：「第二、第三亦如是說。」下四悔準此。）

Ru-Yi Translation

(Having repented, take refuge and bow to Amitābha Buddha and all the Three Jewels. Having bowed, knock the head on the ground three times, signifying bowing to three places: first, the World-Honored Amitābha; second, the Three Jewels of the Pure Land; third, the Three Jewels of the ten directions. The repentance text and the others should be said three times; judge for yourself whether the time is early or late; if time is short, abbreviate, saying: "The second and third [times] are said likewise." The four repentances below follow this.)

The Four Further Repentances

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

Tenth: The Method of Seated Meditation

Chinese Source

如上事畢，當於一處繩床西向，易觀想故，表正向故。跏趺端坐，項脊相對，不昂不傴，調和氣息，定住其心。然所修觀門，經論甚多，初心凡夫那曾遍習？今從要易想，略示二種。於二種中仍逐所宜，未必併用。其有於餘觀想，熟者任便，但得不離淨土法門，皆應修習。所言二種：

Ru-Yi Translation

When the above matters are finished, one should sit on a rope seat in one place, facing west, because it makes contemplation and visualization easy and because it signifies facing [the Pure Land] directly. Sit upright in the cross-legged posture, nape and spine aligned, neither raised nor bent; harmonize the breath and settle and fix the mind. The gates of contemplation to be cultivated are very many in the sūtras and treatises; how could an ordinary being with a beginner's mind ever have practiced them all? Now, taking what is essential and easy to visualize, I briefly show two kinds. Of the two kinds, one still follows what suits oneself; it is not necessary to use both together. Those who are familiar with other contemplations and visualizations may do as is convenient; as long as they do not depart from the Dharma-gate of the Pure Land, all of them should be cultivated. The two kinds are as follows:

Chinese Source

一者、扶普觀意。坐已，自想即時所修計功，合生極樂世界，當便起心生於彼想。於蓮華中，結跏趺坐，作蓮華合想，作華開想。當華開時，有五百色光來照身想，作眼目開想，見佛菩薩及國土想。即於佛前坐聽妙法，及聞一切音聲皆說所樂聞法，所聞要與十二部經合。作此想時，大須堅固，令心不散，心想明了，如眼所見。經久乃起。

Ru-Yi Translation

First, relying on the intent of the general contemplation. Having sat down, think of oneself as reckoning the merit of what one has just cultivated as fit for birth in the world of Sukhāvatī, and at once raise the thought of being born there. [Picture yourself] seated cross-legged within a lotus flower; picture the lotus closing, then picture the flower opening. As the flower opens, picture five hundred colors of light coming to shine upon your body; picture your eyes opening, and picture seeing the buddhas, the bodhisattvas, and the land. At once sit before the Buddha listening to the wondrous Dharma, and hear all the sounds, all teaching the Dharma one delights to hear; what is heard must accord with the twelve divisions of the scriptures. When making this visualization, it must be very firm, so that the mind does not scatter and the visualization is clear, as if seen with the eyes. Rise only after a long time.

Chinese Source

二者、直想阿彌陀佛，丈六金軀坐於華上，專繫眉間白毫一相，其毫長一丈五尺，周圍五寸，外有八稜，其毫中空，右旋宛轉，在眉中間，瑩淨明徹，不可具說，顯映金顏，分齊分明。作此想時，停心注想，堅固勿移。

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, directly visualize Amitābha Buddha, a golden body sixteen feet tall seated on a flower, and bind [the mind] exclusively to the single mark of the white tuft between the brows. The tuft is fifteen feet long and five inches around, with eight edges on the outside; the tuft is hollow, coiling to the right, between the brows, lustrous, pure, bright, and clear beyond full description, shining on the golden face, its outline distinct. When making this visualization, settle the mind and fix the visualization, firm and unmoving.

Chinese Source

然復應觀想念所見，若成、未成皆想念，因緣無實性相，所有皆空，一如鏡中面像，如水現月影，如夢如幻。雖空而亦可見，二皆心性所現。所有者即是自心，心不自知心，心不自見心。心有想即癡，無想即泥洹。心有心無，皆名有想，盡名為癡，不見法性，三因緣生法，即空、假、中，不一不異，非縱非橫，不可思議。心想寂靜，則能成就念佛三昧。久而乃起。（廣如別說。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Then one should also contemplate what is seen in the visualization: whether it is accomplished or not yet accomplished, all of it is visualization and thought; causes and conditions have no real nature or mark; everything there is is empty, just like the image of a face in a mirror, like the reflection of the moon appearing in water, like a dream, like an illusion. Though empty, it can still be seen; both are manifestations of the nature of mind. Whatever there is, is one's own mind; mind does not know mind itself, and mind does not see mind itself. Mind possessed of conceptual thought is delusion; mind free of conceptual thought is nirvāṇa. Whether mind exists or mind does not exist, both are called conceptual thought and all are called delusion, not seeing the dharma-nature. Dharmas born of the three causes and conditions are empty, provisional, and the middle, neither one nor different, neither vertical nor horizontal, inconceivable. When the mind's visualization is quiet and still, one can accomplish the samādhi of mindfulness of the Buddha. Rise only after a long time. (In full, as explained elsewhere.)

Chinese Source

問：「念佛三昧久習方成，十日、七日修懺之者，云何卒學？」答：「緣有生熟，習有久近。若過去曾習及今生預修，至行懺時，薄修即得。若宿未經懷，近懺方學，此必難成。然雖不成，亦須依此繫心，為坐禪觀境。經云：『若成、不成，皆滅無量生死之罪，生諸佛前。』又云：『但聞白毫名字，滅無量罪。』何況繫念！凡欲修者，勿生疑怖，自謂無分，彼佛有宿願力，令修此三昧者，皆得成就般舟。依三力成就：一、佛威力；二、三昧力；三、己功德力。《觀經》：『但聞無量壽佛、二菩薩名，能滅無量生死之罪，況憶念者乎！』若有樂修餘觀，當自隨情。」坐已即起，隨意佛事。或要修觀，更坐無妨。若不慣習坐，乃行道稱念亦得，於夜夢中見彌陀佛，具如經說。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: "The samādhi of mindfulness of the Buddha is accomplished only after long practice; how can those who cultivate repentance for ten days or seven days learn it all of a sudden?" Answer: "Conditions may be ripe or unripe, and practice long or recent. If one practiced it in the past, or has prepared in this life, then when one carries out the repentance, slight cultivation will attain it. If one has never held it in mind before and learns it only now at the repentance, this will certainly be hard to accomplish. Yet even if it is not accomplished, one must still bind the mind according to this, taking it as the object of seated meditation and contemplation. The sūtra says: 'Whether it is accomplished or not, all extinguish the offenses of immeasurable birth and death and are born before the buddhas.' It also says: 'Merely hearing the name of the white tuft extinguishes immeasurable offenses' — how much more binding thought to it! Whoever wishes to cultivate it should not give rise to doubt or fear, thinking oneself to have no share. That buddha has the power of his past vows, enabling all who cultivate this samādhi to accomplish the pratyutpanna [samādhi]. It is accomplished by relying on three powers: first, the Buddha's majestic power; second, the power of samādhi; third, the power of one's own merit. The Contemplation Sūtra [says]: 'Merely hearing the names of Amitāyus Buddha and the two bodhisattvas can extinguish the offenses of immeasurable birth and death — how much more so one who holds them in mind!' If anyone delights in cultivating other contemplations, let him follow his own inclination." Having sat, rise at once and do the Buddha's work as one wishes. Or, if one wishes to cultivate contemplation, there is no harm in sitting again. If one is not accustomed to sitting, it is also acceptable to circumambulate and call [the name] mindfully; in dreams at night one will see Amitābha Buddha, fully as the sūtra describes.

Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

Bowing verse (unit 2). 能禮所禮性空寂… is also in the site's Great Compassion Repentance. 感應道交 follows the fixed rendering "the mutual resonance between response and appeal"; 帝珠 "Indra's jewel."

Ten-direction buddhas (units 5–14). The names come from the Sūtra in Praise of the Pure Land (T0367, Xuanzang); they are translated by meaning, since the site's Amitābha Sūtra (Kumārajīva) gives different lists. Unit 14 lacks 盡 ("throughout") and is translated as it stands.

Five repentances (unit 28). 懺悔／勸請／隨喜／迴向／發願 = repentance / requesting / rejoicing / dedication / making vows, as fixed in the round-2 repentance drafts; 至心懺悔 "with utmost mind I repent" and "I, the bhikṣu (so-and-so)" follow Fahua Sanmei Chanyi Part 2.

Phenomena and principle in repentance (unit 30). The visualization text 我及眾生，無始常為三業、六根重罪所障… and the chant 普為…悉願斷除三障 are shared with the site's Great Compassion Repentance; the wording here keeps "repent" for 懺悔.

Ten compliant and contrary minds (unit 31). Wording follows the round-2 Fahua Sanmei Chanyi Part 3. In the contrary series the text's number 五 is missing after 斷相續心; the translation does not supply it.

Eternal tranquil light (unit 32). 諸佛住處名常寂光 has no 土, so it is "the eternal tranquil light" rather than "the land of eternal tranquil light."

Offenses listed (unit 32). "Five wrong [livelihoods]" (五邪) and "four requisites" (四事: robes, food, bedding, medicine). 斷學般若 is read as "cutting off the study of prajñā" (one's own or others').

Samantabhadra's verses (units 35, 38, 41). The requesting, rejoicing, and dedication texts are from the verses of the Samantabhadra vows in the forty-fascicle Avataṃsaka (T0293). The rejoicing and dedication texts here are shortened from the four-line stanzas. Not on the site; translated here.

Three refuges (units 48–50). Wording as in the round-2 Fahua Sanmei Chanyi Part 2 and the site's Great Compassion Repentance.

Immeasurable Life Sūtra verse (unit 51). Quoted from the Immeasurable Life Sūtra (T0360), fascicle 2, with two lines omitted and small variants (五惡道 for 五惡趣; 各得及長生，壽樂無窮極). The site's English is reused, adjusted to these variants, and with "Peaceful Nourishment" for 安養 (the site has "Peace and Bliss").

Stands still for a moment (unit 46). 定立少願 is read as 定立少頃; 願 is probably a copying error.

General contemplation and white tuft (units 54–55). The lotus-closing and lotus-opening visualization follows the site's Contemplation Sūtra (twelfth contemplation). The description of the white tuft (fifteen feet, eight edges, five inches around, hollow, coiling right) matches the Contemplation Sūtra commentary tradition (see the round-2 Guanjing Shu draft).

Mind with conceptual thought is delusion (unit 55). 心有想即癡，無想即泥洹 is from the Pratyutpanna Samādhi Sūtra (T0417/T0418); wording as in the site's Mohe Zhiguan. Here 想 is "conceptual thought," while elsewhere in the rite 想 as an instruction is "visualize."

Quotations in unit 56. 若成、不成，皆滅無量生死之罪，生諸佛前 and 但聞白毫名字，滅無量罪: source not located (possibly the Ocean-Seal Samādhi of Buddha-Contemplation Sūtra, T0643, not in the local files). The three powers (佛威力、三昧力、本功德力) are from the Pratyutpanna Samādhi Sūtra. The Contemplation Sūtra line follows the site's wording ("Merely hears … how much more so … hold them in mind"), adjusted to Zunshi's shorter text.

Postface (units 59–60). Dazhong Xiangfu 8 (yimao) is 1015. Qining (契凝) of Yue printed the first version; that version's postface is not preserved here.
