# The Gist of Calming and Contemplation — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Zhiguan dayi* (止觀大意, Taishō T46, no. 1914 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1914, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 十乘觀法 | the ten modes of contemplation | As in this site's Mohe Zhiguan. |
| 二十五方便 | the twenty-five expedients | Preparations for practice. |
| 十境 | the ten objects | The objects to which the ten modes are applied. |
| 四種三昧 | the four samādhis | Constantly sitting, constantly walking, half walking and half sitting, neither walking nor sitting. |
