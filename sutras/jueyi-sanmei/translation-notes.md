# The Samādhi of Awakening Attention — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jueyi sanmei* (覺意三昧, Taishō T46, no. 1922 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1922, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 覺意 | awakening attention | Being aware of whatever the mind turns to. |
| 隨自意三昧 | the samādhi of following one's own intention | Another name for it in the Mohe Zhiguan. |
| 四運 | the four phases of thought | Not yet thinking, about to think, thinking, having thought. |
