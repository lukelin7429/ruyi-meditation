# The Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Wuliangyi jing* (無量義經, Taishō T9, no. 276 (tr. Dharmāgatayaśas)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T0276, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). The translation dates from the fifth century; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 無量義者從一法生 | immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma | The sūtra's teaching. |
| 無相 | markless | The single dharma. |
| 四十餘年未曾顯實 | for more than forty years I have not yet revealed the real | The line Tiantai cites for the Five Periods. |
