The Ten Merits

This Part is the sūtra's third and last chapter, "Ten Merits." Great Adornment Bodhisattva praises the sūtra and asks where it comes from, where it goes, and where it abides. The Buddha answers that it comes from the dwelling of the buddhas, goes to the arousing of the bodhi mind in all beings, and abides where bodhisattvas practice. He then sets out its ten inconceivable powers of merit. Each is a short section, and most rest on a simile: a single seed that multiplies without limit, which explains the name "Immeasurable Meanings"; a sick ship-master who cannot cross himself but supplies a sturdy ship for others; a newborn prince already honored by the court; a dragon seven days old that can already bring rain; a young prince ruling as regent; a strong man rewarded with half the kingdom. The common point is that a person still bound by afflictions, on the ground of ordinary beings, can already carry others across by upholding and teaching this sūtra. The chapter ends with signs in the heavens, the Buddha's charge to the bodhisattvas to spread the sūtra in Jambudvīpa after his passing, their vow to do so, and his praise of them.

Chapter Title

Chinese Source

無量義經十功德品第三

Ru-Yi Translation

The Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, Chapter Three: Ten Merits

Where Does This Sūtra Come From?

Chinese Source

爾時，大莊嚴菩薩摩訶薩復白佛言：「世尊！世尊說是微妙甚深無上大乘《無量義經》，真實甚深！甚深甚深！所以者何？於此眾中，諸菩薩摩訶薩及諸四眾，天、龍、鬼、神、國王、臣、民諸有眾生，聞是甚深無上大乘《無量義經》，無不獲得陀羅尼門、三法、四果、菩提之心。當知此經，文理真正，尊無過上，三世諸佛之所守護，無有眾魔群道得入，不為一切邪見生死之所壞敗。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Great Adornment Bodhisattva-mahāsattva again said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! The subtle, wondrous, very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings that the World-Honored One has taught is true, real, and very profound — very profound, very profound! Why? In this assembly, the bodhisattva-mahāsattvas and the four assemblies, gods, nāgas, ghosts and spirits, kings, ministers, people, and all beings, having heard the very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, without exception have obtained dhāraṇī gates, the three dharmas, the four fruits, and the bodhi mind. Know that this sūtra's wording and principle are true and correct, it is honored with none above it, it is guarded by the buddhas of the three times, no host of māras or crowd of [other] paths can enter it, and it cannot be ruined by any wrong views or by birth-and-death.

Chinese Source

所以者何？一聞能持一切法故。若有眾生得聞是經，則為大利。所以者何？若能修行，必得疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。其有眾生不得聞者，當知是等為失大利，過無量無邊不可思議阿僧祇劫，終不得成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。所以者何？不知菩提大道直故，行於險徑，多留難故。世尊！是經典者不可思議。唯願世尊廣為大眾慈哀，敷演是經甚深不思議事。世尊！是經典者，從何所來？去何所至？住何所住？乃有如是無量功德不思議力，令眾疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提？」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Why? Because with one hearing one can uphold all dharmas. If there are beings who get to hear this sūtra, it is a great benefit for them. Why? If they can practice it, they will certainly attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening swiftly. As for beings who do not get to hear it, know that they have lost a great benefit: though immeasurable, boundless, inconceivable asaṃkhyeya kalpas pass, they will never attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening. Why? Because they do not know that the great path of bodhi is direct, and they travel on perilous byways with many hindrances and difficulties. World-Honored One! This scripture is inconceivable. We pray only that the World-Honored One, in kindness and pity for the great assembly, expound at length the very profound and inconceivable matters of this sūtra. World-Honored One! This scripture — from where does it come? To where does it go? Where does it abide? That it should have such immeasurable merit and inconceivable power, enabling the multitude swiftly to attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening?"

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊告大莊嚴菩薩摩訶薩言：「善哉！善哉！善男子！如是，如是，如汝所言。善男子！我說是經，甚深甚深！真實甚深！所以者何？令眾疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提故，一聞能持一切法故，於諸眾生大利益故，行大直道，無留難故。善男子！汝問是經從何所來、去至何所、住何所住者，當善諦聽。善男子！是經本從諸佛宮宅中來，去至一切眾生發菩提心，住諸菩薩所行之處。善男子！是經如是來、如是去、如是住。是故，此經能有如是無量功德不思議力，令眾疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。善男子！汝寧欲聞，是經復有十不思議功德力不？」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the World-Honored One said to Great Adornment Bodhisattva-mahāsattva: "Excellent, excellent, good son! It is so, it is so, as you say. Good son! I teach this sūtra as very profound, very profound, true, real, and very profound. Why? Because it enables the multitude swiftly to attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening; because with one hearing one can uphold all dharmas; because it is of great benefit to beings; because it travels the great direct path, without hindrances and difficulties. Good son! As for your question, from where this sūtra comes, to where it goes, and where it abides, listen well and attentively. Good son! This sūtra comes originally from the palace-dwelling of the buddhas; it goes to the arousing of the bodhi mind in all beings; it abides in the place where bodhisattvas practice. Good son! This sūtra comes in this way, goes in this way, abides in this way. Therefore this sūtra can have such immeasurable merit and inconceivable power, enabling the multitude swiftly to attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening. Good son! Would you like to hear that this sūtra further has ten inconceivable powers of merit?"

Chinese Source

大莊嚴言：「願樂欲聞。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Great Adornment said: "I would gladly hear it."

The First Merit: Arousing Every Good Mind

Chinese Source

佛言：「善男子！第一，是經能令菩薩未發心者，發菩提心；無慈仁者，起於慈心；好殺戮者，起大悲心；生嫉妬者，起隨喜心；有愛著者，起能捨心；諸慳貪者，起布施心；多憍慢者，起持戒心；瞋恚盛者，起忍辱心；生懈怠者，起精進心；諸散亂者，起禪定心；於愚癡者，起智慧心；未能度彼者，起度彼心；行十惡者，起十善心；樂有為者，志無為心；有退心者，作不退心；為有漏者，起無漏心；多煩惱者，起除滅心。善男子！是名是經第一功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said: "Good son! First: this sūtra can cause bodhisattvas who have not yet aroused the mind to arouse the bodhi mind; those without kindness or humaneness to give rise to a mind of kindness; those fond of killing to give rise to a mind of great compassion; those who give rise to jealousy to give rise to a mind of rejoicing; those with craving and attachment to give rise to a mind able to give up; the stingy and greedy to give rise to a mind of giving; the very arrogant to give rise to a mind of keeping the precepts; those strong in anger to give rise to a mind of patience; those who give rise to laziness to give rise to a mind of vigor; the scattered and distracted to give rise to a mind of meditative concentration; the foolish to give rise to a mind of wisdom; those not yet able to carry others across to give rise to a mind of carrying others across; those who do the ten evils to give rise to a mind of the ten goods; those who delight in the conditioned to set their minds on the unconditioned; those with a retreating mind to make a mind that does not retreat; those with outflows to give rise to a mind without outflows; those with many afflictions to give rise to a mind that removes and extinguishes them. Good son! This is called the first inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Second Merit: From One Seed, Immeasurable Meanings

Chinese Source

「善男子！第二，是經不可思議功德力者，若有眾生得是經者，若一轉、若一偈、乃至一句，則能通達百千億義、無量數劫不能演說所受持法。所以者何？以其是法，義無量故。善男子！是經，譬如從一種子生百千萬，百千萬中一一復生百千萬數，如是展轉乃至無量；是經典者，亦復如是，從於一法生百千義，百千義中一一復生百千萬數，如是展轉，乃至無量無邊之義。是故，此經名無量義。善男子！是名是經第二功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Second, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if there are beings who obtain this sūtra, whether one reading of it, one verse, or even one phrase, they will penetrate hundreds of thousands of koṭis of meanings, and for immeasurable numbers of kalpas they could not finish expounding the Dharma they have received and upheld. Why? Because this Dharma's meanings are immeasurable. Good son! This sūtra is like a single seed from which hundreds of thousands of myriads are born, and from each of the hundreds of thousands of myriads again hundreds of thousands of myriads are born, and so on in turn up to the immeasurable. This scripture is likewise: from a single dharma hundreds of thousands of meanings are born, and from each of the hundreds of thousands of meanings again hundreds of thousands of myriads are born, and so on in turn up to immeasurable, boundless meanings. Therefore this sūtra is called Immeasurable Meanings. Good son! This is called the second inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Third Merit: The Sick Ship-Master

Chinese Source

「善男子！第三，是經不可思議功德力者，若有眾生得聞是經，若一轉、若一偈、乃至一句，通達百千萬億義已，雖有煩惱，如無煩惱，出生入死，無怖畏想，於諸眾生，生憐愍心，於一切法，得勇健想。如壯力士能擔、能持諸有重者；是持經人，亦復如是，能荷無上菩提重寶，擔負眾生出生死道，未能自度，已能度彼。猶如船師身嬰重病，四體不御，安止此岸，有好堅牢船舟，常辦諸度彼者之具，給與而去；是持經者，亦復如是，雖嬰五道諸有之身，百八重病常恒相纏，安止無明老死此岸，而有堅牢此大乘經無量義，辦能度眾生。能如說行者，得度生死。善男子！是名是經第三功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Third, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if there are beings who get to hear this sūtra, whether one reading of it, one verse, or even one phrase, and penetrate hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of meanings, then though they have afflictions it is as if they had no afflictions; going out of birth and entering death, they have no thought of fear; toward beings they give rise to a mind of pity; toward all dharmas they obtain the thought of courage. Like a strong, powerful man who can carry and hold every heavy thing, one who upholds this sūtra is likewise: he can bear the heavy treasure of unsurpassed bodhi and carry beings out of the path of birth-and-death; though not yet able to carry himself across, he is already able to carry others across. It is like a ship-master who, though his body is bound by grave illness and his four limbs do not obey him, stays on this shore and has a good, sturdy ship, always supplied with all that is needed to carry others across, and gives it to them so that they go. One who upholds the sūtra is likewise: though bound to a body in the five paths and all existences, with the hundred and eight grave illnesses constantly entangling him, staying on this shore of ignorance, old age, and death, he has this sturdy Great Vehicle sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, which provides what can carry beings across. Those who can practice as taught will be carried across birth-and-death. Good son! This is called the third inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Fourth Merit: The Newborn Prince

Chinese Source

「善男子！第四，是經不可思議功德力者，若有眾生得聞是經，若一轉、若一偈、乃至一句，得勇健想，雖未自度而能度他，與諸菩薩以為眷屬，諸佛如來常向是人而演說法。是人聞已，悉能受持，隨順不逆，轉復為人隨宜廣說。善男子！是人譬如國王夫人新生王子，若一日、若二日、若至七日，若一月、若二月、若至七月，若一歲、若二歲、若至七歲，雖復不能領理國事，已為臣民之所宗敬，諸大王子以為伴侶，王及夫人愛心偏重，常與共語。所以者何？以稚小故。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Fourth, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if there are beings who get to hear this sūtra, whether one reading of it, one verse, or even one phrase, they obtain the thought of courage; though not yet carrying themselves across, they can carry others across; they have the bodhisattvas as their retinue, and the buddhas, the tathāgatas, constantly teach the Dharma to this person. Having heard it, this person can receive and uphold all of it, follow it without going against it, and in turn teach it broadly to others as fits. Good son! This person is like a newborn prince of a king and his queen: whether one day, two days, or up to seven days old; whether one month, two months, or up to seven months; whether one year, two years, or up to seven years — though he cannot yet manage the affairs of state, he is already honored and revered by ministers and people, the great princes are his companions, and the king and queen love him especially and always speak with him. Why? Because he is young and small.

Chinese Source

善男子！是持經者，亦復如是，諸佛國王，是經夫人，和合共生是菩薩子。若是菩薩得聞是經，若一句、若一偈，若一轉、若二轉，若十、若百、若千、若萬、若億萬億、若恒河沙無量無數轉，雖復不能體真理極，雖復不能震動三千大千國土，雷震梵音，轉大法輪，已為一切四眾、八部之所宗仰，諸大菩薩以為眷屬，深入諸佛祕密之法，所可演說無違無失，常為諸佛之所護念，慈愛偏覆，以新學故。善男子！是名是經第四功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! One who upholds the sūtra is likewise. The buddhas are the king and this sūtra is the queen; in union they together give birth to this bodhisattva child. If this bodhisattva gets to hear this sūtra, whether one phrase or one verse, whether one reading or two readings, whether ten, a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand, myriads of koṭis, or Ganges-sands of immeasurable, countless readings — though he cannot yet embody the ultimate of true principle, though he cannot yet shake the lands of the three-thousand-great-thousandfold world, thunder with the Brahmā voice, and turn the great Dharma wheel — he is already honored and looked up to by all the four assemblies and the eightfold host, the great bodhisattvas are his retinue, he enters deeply into the secret Dharma of the buddhas, and what he expounds is without contradiction or error; he is constantly held in mind and protected by the buddhas, who cover him especially with their loving kindness because he is newly learning. Good son! This is called the fourth inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Fifth Merit: The Young Dragon

Chinese Source

「善男子！第五，是經不可思議功德力者，若善男子、善女人，若佛在世、若滅度後，其有受持、讀誦、書寫如是甚深無上大乘《無量義經》，是人雖復具縛煩惱，未能遠離諸凡夫事，而能示現大菩提道，延於一日以為百劫，百劫亦能促為一日，令彼眾生歡喜信伏。善男子！是善男子、善女人，譬如龍子始生七日，即能興雲，亦能降雨。善男子！是名是經第五功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Fifth, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if good sons or good daughters, whether while the Buddha is in the world or after his passing, receive and uphold, read and recite, and copy such a very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, then though this person is still fully bound by afflictions and not yet able to leave far behind the affairs of ordinary people, he can display the great path of bodhi, stretching one day into a hundred kalpas, and also able to shorten a hundred kalpas into one day, causing those beings to rejoice, believe, and submit. Good son! This good son or good daughter is like a young dragon only seven days born, already able to raise clouds and also able to bring down rain. Good son! This is called the fifth inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Sixth Merit: The Prince as Regent

Chinese Source

「善男子！第六，是經不可思議功德力者，若善男子、善女人，若佛在世、若滅度後，受持、讀誦是經典者，雖具煩惱而為眾生說法，令得遠離煩惱、生死，斷一切苦。眾生聞已修行，得法、得果、得道，與佛如來等無差別。譬如王子雖復稚小，若王遊巡及以疾病，委是王子領理國事。王子是時，依大王命，如法教令群寮百官，宣流正化，國土人民各隨其安，如大王治等無有異。持經善男子、善女人，亦復如是，若佛在世、若滅度後，是善男子雖未得住初不動地，依佛如是用說教法而敷演之，眾生聞已，一心修行，斷除煩惱，得法、得果、乃至得道。善男子！是名是經第六功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Sixth, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if good sons or good daughters, whether while the Buddha is in the world or after his passing, receive and uphold, read and recite this scripture, then though still possessed of afflictions they teach the Dharma for beings, enabling them to leave afflictions and birth-and-death far behind and to cut off all suffering. When beings hear it and practice, they attain the Dharma, attain the fruit, and attain the path, equal to and no different from the Buddha, the Tathāgata. It is like a prince who, though young and small, is entrusted with managing the affairs of state when the king goes on a tour of inspection or falls ill. At that time the prince, relying on the great king's command, instructs the officials and the hundred ministers according to the law and spreads right government; the land and people are each at peace as they should be, no different from the great king's rule. The good sons and good daughters who uphold the sūtra are likewise: whether while the Buddha is in the world or after his passing, these good sons, though they have not yet come to abide in the first immovable ground, rely on the Buddha's use of teaching in this way and expound it; when beings hear it, they practice with one mind, cut off afflictions, and attain the Dharma, attain the fruit, and even attain the path. Good son! This is called the sixth inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Seventh Merit: Half the Kingdom

Chinese Source

「善男子！第七，是經不可思議功德力者，若善男子、善女人，於佛在世、若滅度後，得聞是經，歡喜信樂，生希有心，受持、讀誦、書寫、解說，如法修行，發菩提心，起諸善根，興大悲意，欲度一切苦惱眾生，雖未修行六波羅蜜，六波羅蜜自然在前，即於是身得無生忍，生死煩惱一時斷壞，即昇第七地與大菩薩位。譬如健人為王除怨，怨既滅已，王大歡喜，賞賜半國之封，皆悉與之。持經男子女人，亦復如是，於諸行人最為勇健，六度法寶不求自至，生死怨敵自然散壞，證無生忍，半佛國寶封賞安樂。善男子！是名是經第七功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Seventh, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if good sons or good daughters, while the Buddha is in the world or after his passing, get to hear this sūtra, rejoice, believe, and delight in it, give rise to a mind that it is rare, receive and uphold, read and recite, copy, explain it, and practice according to the Dharma, arouse the bodhi mind, give rise to good roots, and set forth the intention of great compassion, wishing to carry all suffering beings across — then though they have not yet practiced the six pāramitās, the six pāramitās appear before them of themselves; in this very body they obtain the acceptance of non-arising, birth-and-death and afflictions are broken at one stroke, and they at once ascend to the seventh ground and the rank of great bodhisattva. It is like a strong man who removes the king's enemy; when the enemy has been destroyed, the king rejoices greatly and rewards him with a fief of half the kingdom, giving him all of it. The men and women who uphold the sūtra are likewise: among all practitioners they are the most courageous; the Dharma-treasure of the six perfections comes without being sought; the enemy, birth-and-death, scatters and is destroyed of itself; they realize the acceptance of non-arising, and are rewarded with the treasure of half the buddha-kingdom as a fief, at peace and happy. Good son! This is called the seventh inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra.

The Eighth Merit: Turning Unbelievers to Faith

Chinese Source

「善男子！第八，是經不可思議功德力者，若善男子、善女人，於佛在世、若滅度後，有人能得是經典者，敬信如視佛身，令等無異，愛樂是經，受持、讀誦、書寫、頂戴，如法奉行，堅固戒忍，兼行檀度，深發慈悲，以此無上大乘《無量義經》廣為人說。若人先來都不信有罪福者，以是經示之，設種種方便，強化令信，以經威力故，發其人心，欻然得迴；信心既發，勇猛精進故，能得是經威德勢力，得道、得果。是故，善男子、善女人即於是身，得無生法忍，得至上地，與諸菩薩以為眷屬，速能成就眾生淨佛國土，不久得成無上菩提。善男子！是名是經第八功德不思議力。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Eighth, the inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra: if good sons or good daughters, while the Buddha is in the world or after his passing, are able to obtain this scripture, revere and believe in it as if seeing the Buddha's body, making it equal and no different; love and delight in this sūtra, receive and uphold, read and recite, copy, and carry it on their heads, practice it with reverence according to the Dharma, are firm in precepts and patience, practice giving as well, and deeply arouse kindness and compassion, and teach this unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings broadly to others; [then,] if there are people who from the first have not believed at all that there is offense and merit, they show them this sūtra and set up various skillful means to transform them forcefully and make them believe; because of the sūtra's majestic power, those people's minds are stirred and suddenly turn around. Because the mind of faith has been aroused and they are courageous and vigorous, they can obtain the majestic virtue and power of this sūtra and attain the path and attain the fruit. Ru-Yi Translation

The Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings

Reading Notes for Part 3

Translator's notes

Ten inconceivable powers of merit. 十不思議功德力 is rendered "ten inconceivable powers of merit," and each closing formula 是名是經第N功德不思議力 as "This is called the Nth inconceivable power of merit of this sūtra." The chapter title 十功德品 is "Ten Merits."

一轉 (units 5–7). Literally "one turning," i.e. one reading or recitation through of the sūtra; rendered "one reading."

Where the sūtra comes from (unit 2). 諸佛宮宅 "the palace-dwelling of the buddhas."

The second merit (unit 5). The seed simile is the sūtra's own explanation of its name, matching Chapter 2: 從於一法生百千義 "from a single dharma hundreds of thousands of meanings are born." "A single dharma" and "Immeasurable Meanings" keep the English used in Part 2.

The third merit (unit 6). 百八重病 "the hundred and eight grave illnesses," i.e. the hundred and eight afflictions; 五道 the five paths (destinies). The sentence 而有堅牢此大乘經無量義，辦能度眾生 is read with 無量義 in apposition to 此大乘經: "this sturdy Great Vehicle sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings."

The fourth merit (unit 7). 諸佛國王，是經夫人 is read as a metaphor ("the buddhas are the king and this sūtra is the queen"). 體真理極 "embody the ultimate of true principle."

The sixth merit (unit 9). 初不動地 "the first immovable ground"; the eighth ground is usually called the immovable ground, so 初 may mean "the first [stage of the] immovable ground." Alternatively: "the first ground [or] the immovable ground." The literal reading is kept.

The seventh merit (unit 10). 即昇第七地與大菩薩位 "they at once ascend to the seventh ground and the rank of great bodhisattva"; 無生忍 "the acceptance of non-arising," 無生法忍 (units 11, Part 2 unit 16) "the forbearance of the non-arising of dharmas."

The ninth merit (unit 12). 首楞嚴三昧 the śūraṃgama samādhi; 大總持門 "the gate of the great total retention" (總持 = dhāraṇī as "total retention"); 二十五有 "the twenty-five [kinds of] existence," as in the Samantabhadra sūtra draft.

The tenth merit (unit 13). 法雲地 "the ground of the Dharma cloud," the tenth bodhisattva ground. 漸見超登 "gradually they are seen to rise beyond."

Fixed formula (units 1, 15). 文理真正，尊無過上… has the same English as in Part 2 (units 8, 15). In unit 1 it reads 三世諸佛之所守護 (without 共), rendered "guarded by the buddhas of the three times."

Spiritual penetration (unit 17). 神通願力 "the power of the vows of spiritual penetration"; 神通 follows the site's Lotus Sūtra, "Samantabhadra's Encouragement," where Samantabhadra makes a similar vow to guard the Lotus in Jambudvīpa.
