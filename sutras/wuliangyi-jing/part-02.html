Teaching the Dharma

This Part is the sūtra's second chapter, "Teaching the Dharma," the doctrinal center of the text. Great Adornment Bodhisattva asks which single Dharma gate lets a bodhisattva swiftly reach unsurpassed awakening. The Buddha names it "Immeasurable Meanings": because beings' natures and desires are immeasurable, the teaching is immeasurable, and its meanings are immeasurable; yet "immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma," and that single dharma is the markless, which is the true mark. Great Adornment then presses the question: for more than forty years the Buddha has taught emptiness, impermanence, and no-self, and people have reached every stage from warmth to the tenth ground — what is new now? The Buddha answers that "for more than forty years I have not yet revealed the real." He compares the Dharma to water, one in nature but different as well, pond, river, and ocean, and says that his first, middle, and present teachings use the same words but carry different meanings. Tiantai writers quote this passage, in the form 「四十餘年未顯真實」, as scriptural support for dividing the Buddha's teaching into periods, with the Lotus as the place where the real is finally revealed. The chapter closes with earthquakes, rains of flowers, and the attainments of the assembly.

Chapter Title

Chinese Source

無量義經說法品第二

Ru-Yi Translation

The Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, Chapter Two: Teaching the Dharma

Great Adornment Asks for the One Dharma Gate

Chinese Source

爾時，大莊嚴菩薩摩訶薩與八萬菩薩摩訶薩說是偈讚佛已，俱白佛言：「世尊！我等八萬菩薩之眾，今者欲於如來法中有所諮問，不審世尊垂愍聽不？」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Great Adornment Bodhisattva-mahāsattva and the eighty thousand bodhisattva-mahāsattvas, having spoken these verses in praise of the Buddha, together said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! We, the assembly of eighty thousand bodhisattvas, now wish to ask about something within the Tathāgata's Dharma. Will the World-Honored One, in his pity, hear us or not?"

Chinese Source

佛告大莊嚴菩薩及八萬菩薩言：「善哉！善哉！善男子！善知是時，恣汝所問。如來不久當般涅槃，涅槃之後，普令一切無復餘疑。欲何所問，便可說也。」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Great Adornment Bodhisattva and the eighty thousand bodhisattvas: "Excellent, excellent, good sons! You well know that this is the time; ask whatever you wish. Before long the Tathāgata will enter parinirvāṇa; [I wish] that after my nirvāṇa all everywhere should have no remaining doubts. Whatever you wish to ask, you may say it."

Chinese Source

於是，大莊嚴菩薩與八萬菩薩即共同聲，白佛言：「世尊！菩薩摩訶薩欲得疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，應當修行何等法門？何等法門能令菩薩摩訶薩疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提？」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then Great Adornment Bodhisattva and the eighty thousand bodhisattvas together in the same voice said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! If bodhisattva-mahāsattvas wish swiftly to attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening, what Dharma gate should they practice? What Dharma gate can enable bodhisattva-mahāsattvas swiftly to attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening?"

Chinese Source

佛告大莊嚴菩薩及八萬菩薩言：「善男子！有一法門能令菩薩疾得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。若有菩薩學是法門者，則能疾得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Great Adornment Bodhisattva and the eighty thousand bodhisattvas: "Good sons! There is one Dharma gate that can enable bodhisattvas swiftly to obtain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening. If there are bodhisattvas who learn this Dharma gate, they will be able swiftly to obtain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening."

Chinese Source

「世尊！是法門者，號字何等？其義云何？菩薩云何修行？」

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! What is this Dharma gate called? What is its meaning? How do bodhisattvas practice it?"

Immeasurable Meanings Arise from a Single Dharma

Chinese Source

佛言：「善男子！是一法門，名為無量義。菩薩欲得修學無量義者，應當觀察一切諸法自本來今性相空寂，無大、無小，無生、無滅，非住、非動，不進、不退。猶如虛空無有二法，而諸眾生虛妄橫計，是此、是彼、是得、是失，起不善念，造眾惡業，輪迴六趣，備諸苦毒，無量億劫不能自出。菩薩摩訶薩如是諦觀，生憐愍心，發大慈悲，將欲救拔。又復深入一切諸法——法相如是，生如是法，法相如是，住如是法，法相如是，異如是法，法相如是，滅如是法；法相如是，能生惡法，法相如是，能生善法，住、異、滅者亦復如是。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said: "Good sons! This one Dharma gate is called Immeasurable Meanings. Bodhisattvas who wish to cultivate and learn Immeasurable Meanings should observe that all dharmas, from their origin until now, are empty and tranquil in nature and marks: without great, without small, without birth, without extinction, neither abiding nor moving, neither advancing nor retreating — like empty space, without two dharmas. Yet beings falsely and perversely reckon, 'This is this, that is that; this is gain, that is loss,' give rise to not-good thoughts, commit many evil actions, revolve in the six destinies, undergo every suffering and poison, and for immeasurable koṭis of kalpas cannot get themselves out. Bodhisattva-mahāsattvas, attentively contemplating in this way, give rise to a mind of pity, arouse great kindness and compassion, and set about rescuing them. Further, they enter deeply into all dharmas: such is the mark of dharmas that it gives birth to such dharmas; such is the mark of dharmas that it makes such dharmas abide; such is the mark of dharmas that it makes such dharmas change; such is the mark of dharmas that it makes such dharmas cease; such is the mark of dharmas that it can give birth to evil dharmas; such is the mark of dharmas that it can give birth to good dharmas; and so likewise with abiding, changing, and ceasing.

Chinese Source

「菩薩如是觀察四相始末，悉遍知已，次復諦觀一切諸法，念念不住，新新生滅。復觀即時生、住、異、滅，如是觀已，而入眾生諸根性欲。性欲無量故，說法無量；說法無量，義亦無量。無量義者，從一法生；其一法者，即無相也。如是無相，無相不相，不相無相，名為實相。菩薩摩訶薩安住如是真實相已，所發慈悲明諦不虛，於眾生所，真能拔苦。苦既拔已，復為說法，令諸眾生受於快樂。

Ru-Yi Translation

"When bodhisattvas have observed in this way the beginning and end of the four marks and know them all thoroughly, next they attentively contemplate that all dharmas do not abide from thought to thought, but arise and cease anew and anew. Further, they observe that they arise, abide, change, and cease at the same moment. Having observed in this way, they enter into the faculties, natures, and desires of beings. Because natures and desires are immeasurable, the teaching of the Dharma is immeasurable; because the teaching of the Dharma is immeasurable, the meanings too are immeasurable. Immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma; that single dharma is the markless. That which is markless in this way — markless and not a mark, not a mark and markless — is called the true mark. When bodhisattva-mahāsattvas abide securely in such a true and real mark, the kindness and compassion they arouse are clear, sure, and not empty; toward beings they are truly able to remove suffering. When suffering has been removed, they further teach them the Dharma and cause beings to receive happiness.

Chinese Source

「善男子！菩薩若能如是修一法門無量義者，必得疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。善男子！如是甚深無上大乘《無量義經》，文理真正，尊無過上，三世諸佛所共守護，無有眾魔群道得入，不為一切邪見生死之所壞敗。是故，善男子！菩薩摩訶薩若欲疾成無上菩提，應當修學如是甚深無上大乘《無量義經》。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! If bodhisattvas are able in this way to cultivate the one Dharma gate of Immeasurable Meanings, they will certainly attain unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening swiftly. Good sons! Such a very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings — its wording and principle are true and correct, it is honored with none above it, it is guarded in common by the buddhas of the three times, no host of māras or crowd of [other] paths can enter it, and it cannot be ruined by any wrong views or by birth-and-death. Therefore, good sons, bodhisattva-mahāsattvas who wish swiftly to attain unsurpassed bodhi should cultivate and learn such a very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings."

More Than Forty Years: What Was Taught Before

Chinese Source

爾時，大莊嚴菩薩復白佛言：「世尊！世尊說法不可思議，眾生根性亦不可思議，法門解脫亦不可思議。我等於佛所說諸法，無復疑惑，而諸眾生生迷惑心，故重諮問。世尊！自從如來得道已來，四十餘年，常為眾生演說諸法四相之義，苦義、空義、無常、無我，無大、無小、無生、無滅，一切無相，法性、法相本來空寂，不來、不去、不出、不沒。若有聞者，或得煖法、頂法、世第一法；須陀洹果、斯陀含果、阿那含果、阿羅漢果；辟支佛道；發菩提心；登第一地、第二、第三、至第十地。往日所說諸法之義，與今所說有何等異，而言甚深無上大乘無量義經，菩薩修行必得疾成無上菩提？是事云何？唯願世尊慈愍一切，廣為眾生而分別之，普令現在及未來世有聞法者，無餘疑網。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Great Adornment Bodhisattva again said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! The World-Honored One's teaching of the Dharma is inconceivable; the faculties and natures of beings are also inconceivable; the Dharma gates and liberation are also inconceivable. We have no further doubt or perplexity about the dharmas the Buddha has taught, but because beings give rise to confused minds, we ask again. World-Honored One! Since the Tathāgata attained the path, for more than forty years he has constantly expounded for beings the meaning of the four marks of dharmas: the meaning of suffering, the meaning of emptiness, impermanence, no-self, without great, without small, without birth, without extinction, all markless, dharma-nature and the marks of dharmas from their origin empty and tranquil, neither coming nor going, neither appearing nor disappearing. Of those who heard, some attained the dharma of warmth, the dharma of the summit, and the supreme worldly dharma; the fruit of srotaāpanna, the fruit of sakṛdāgāmin, the fruit of anāgāmin, the fruit of arhat; the path of the pratyekabuddha; aroused the bodhi mind; ascended the first ground, the second, the third, up to the tenth ground. What difference is there between the meaning of the dharmas taught in former days and what is taught now, that you say that by the very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings bodhisattvas who practice will certainly attain unsurpassed bodhi swiftly? How is this so? We pray only that the World-Honored One, in pity for all, set it out broadly for beings, so that all who hear the Dharma, now and in the future age, may have no remaining net of doubt."

Chinese Source

於是，佛告大莊嚴菩薩：「善哉！善哉！大善男子！能問如來如是甚深無上大乘微妙之義，當知汝能多所利益安樂人天，拔苦眾生，真大慈悲，信實不虛。以是因緣，必得疾成無上菩提，亦令一切今世、來世諸有眾生得成無上菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the Buddha said to Great Adornment Bodhisattva: "Excellent, excellent, great good son! You are able to ask the Tathāgata about such a very profound, unsurpassed, subtle and wondrous meaning of the Great Vehicle. Know that you are able to benefit and bring happiness to many humans and gods, and to remove the suffering of beings; [this is] truly great kindness and compassion, trustworthy, real, and not empty. For these causes and conditions you will certainly attain unsurpassed bodhi swiftly, and will also enable all beings of this age and of ages to come to attain unsurpassed bodhi.

Chinese Source

「善男子！自我道場菩提樹下，端坐六年，得成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，以佛眼觀一切諸法不可宣說。所以者何？以諸眾生性欲不同。性欲不同，種種說法；種種說法，以方便力，四十餘年未曾顯實。是故，眾生得道差別，不得疾成無上菩提。善男子！法譬如水，能洗垢穢，若井、若池、若江，若河、溪、渠、大海，皆悉能洗諸有垢穢。其法水者，亦復如是，能洗眾生諸煩惱垢。善男子！水性是一，江、河、井、池、溪、渠、大海各各別異。其法性者，亦復如是，洗除塵勞等無差別，三法、四果、二道不一。善男子！水雖俱洗，而井非池，池非江、河，溪、渠非海，而如來世雄於法自在，所說諸法亦復如是。初、中、後說，皆能洗除眾生煩惱，而初非中，而中非後，初、中、後說，文辭雖一，而義各異。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! Since I sat upright for six years beneath the bodhi tree at the site of awakening and attained unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening, observing all dharmas with the buddha-eye, [I saw that they] could not be proclaimed. Why? Because the natures and desires of beings are not the same. Because their natures and desires are not the same, I taught the Dharma in various ways; teaching the Dharma in various ways, by the power of skillful means, for more than forty years I have not yet revealed the real. Therefore beings' attainments of the path differ, and they do not swiftly attain unsurpassed bodhi. Good sons! The Dharma is like water, which can wash away dirt and filth. Whether a well, a pond, or a river, whether a stream, a brook, a channel, or the great ocean, all can wash away every kind of dirt and filth. The water of the Dharma is likewise: it can wash away the dirt of beings' afflictions. Good sons! The nature of water is one, but rivers, streams, wells, ponds, brooks, channels, and the great ocean are each different. Dharma-nature is likewise: in washing away the dust and toil it is equal and without difference, yet the three dharmas, the four fruits, and the two paths are not one. Good sons! Though the waters all wash, yet a well is not a pond, a pond is not a river or stream, and a brook or channel is not the ocean. The Tathāgata, hero of the world, is sovereign over the Dharma, and the dharmas he teaches are likewise. The teachings at the beginning, the middle, and the end can all wash away beings' afflictions, yet the beginning is not the middle, and the middle is not the end. The teachings at the beginning, the middle, and the end, though their wording is one, differ each in meaning.

The Same Words, Different Meanings

Chinese Source

「善男子！我起樹王，詣波羅奈鹿野園中，為阿若拘隣等五人轉四諦法輪時，亦說諸法本來空寂，代謝不住，念念生滅；中間於此及以處處，為諸比丘并眾菩薩辯演宣說十二因緣、六波羅蜜，亦說諸法本來空寂，代謝不住，念念生滅。今復於此，演說大乘《無量義經》，亦說諸法本來空寂，代謝不住，念念生滅。善男子！是故，初說、中說、今說，文辭是一，而義差異。義異故，眾生解異；解異故，得法、得果、得道亦異。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! When I rose from beneath the king of trees and went to the Deer Park at Vārāṇasī, and turned the Dharma wheel of the four truths for Ājñāta-Kauṇḍinya and the others, five persons in all, I also taught that all dharmas are from their origin empty and tranquil, changing and not abiding, arising and ceasing from thought to thought. In the middle period, here and in place after place, for the monks and the assembly of bodhisattvas I explained and proclaimed the twelve links of dependent origination and the six pāramitās, and also taught that all dharmas are from their origin empty and tranquil, changing and not abiding, arising and ceasing from thought to thought. Now again here I expound the Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, and also teach that all dharmas are from their origin empty and tranquil, changing and not abiding, arising and ceasing from thought to thought. Good sons! Therefore in the first teaching, the middle teaching, and the present teaching the wording is one, but the meaning differs. Because the meaning differs, beings' understanding differs; because their understanding differs, their attainment of the Dharma, attainment of the fruit, and attainment of the path also differ.

Chinese Source

「善男子！初說四諦，為求聲聞人，而八億諸天來下聽法，發菩提心。中於處處演說甚深十二因緣，為求辟支佛人，而無量眾生發菩提心，或住聲聞。次說方等十二部經、摩訶般若、華嚴海雲，演說菩薩歷劫修行，而百千比丘、萬億人天、無量眾生得須陀洹、得斯陀含、得阿那含、得阿羅漢、住辟支佛因緣法中。善男子！以是義故，故知說同，而義別異。義異故，眾生解異；解異故，得法、得果、得道亦異。是故，善男子！自我得道，初起說法，至于今日演說大乘《無量義經》，未曾不說苦、空、無常、無我，非真、非假、非大、非小，本來不然，今亦不滅，一切無相，法相、法性不來不去，而眾生四相所遷。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! At first I taught the four truths for those seeking to be śrāvakas, yet eight hundred million gods came down to hear the Dharma and aroused the bodhi mind. In the middle, in place after place, I expounded the very profound twelve links of dependent origination for those seeking to be pratyekabuddhas, yet immeasurable beings aroused the bodhi mind or abode as śrāvakas. Next I taught the Vaipulya twelve divisions of scripture, the Mahāprajñā, and the ocean-clouds of the Avataṃsaka, expounding the bodhisattvas' practice through kalpas, yet hundreds of thousands of monks, myriads of koṭis of humans and gods, and immeasurable beings attained srotaāpanna, attained sakṛdāgāmin, attained anāgāmin, attained arhat, or abode in the Dharma of the causes and conditions of the pratyekabuddha. Good sons! For this reason, know that the teaching is the same but the meaning differs. Because the meaning differs, beings' understanding differs; because their understanding differs, their attainment of the Dharma, attainment of the fruit, and attainment of the path also differ. Therefore, good sons, from the time I attained the path and first arose to teach the Dharma until today, when I expound the Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings, I have never failed to teach suffering, emptiness, impermanence, no-self, neither true nor provisional, neither great nor small, from the origin not burning, now also not extinguished, all markless, the marks of dharmas and dharma-nature neither coming nor going; yet beings are moved along by the four marks.

The Inconceivable Realm of the Buddhas

Chinese Source

「善男子！以是義故，諸佛無有二言，能以一音普應眾聲；能以一身，示百千萬億那由他無量無數恒河沙身；一一身中，又示若干百千萬億那由他、阿僧祇、恒河沙種種類形；一一形中，又示若干百千萬億那由他、阿僧祇、恒河沙形。善男子！是則諸佛不可思議甚深境界，非二乘所知，亦非十住菩薩所及，唯佛與佛乃能究了。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! For this reason the buddhas have no two words; with one sound they can respond everywhere to the many voices. With one body they can show bodies as many as hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of nayutas, immeasurable, countless, [as] the sands of the Ganges; within each body they further show some hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of nayutas of asaṃkhyeyas of Ganges-sands of various kinds and forms; within each form they further show some hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of nayutas of asaṃkhyeyas of Ganges-sands of forms. Good sons! This is the inconceivable, very profound realm of the buddhas. It is not known by the two vehicles, nor reached by bodhisattvas of the ten abidings; only a buddha, together with another buddha, can fathom it fully.

Chinese Source

「善男子！是故，我說微妙甚深無上大乘《無量義經》，文理真正，尊無過上，三世諸佛所共守護，無有眾魔外道得入，不為一切邪見生死之所壞敗。菩薩摩訶薩若欲疾成無上菩提，應當修學如是甚深無上大乘《無量義經》。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good sons! Therefore I teach the subtle, wondrous, very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings: its wording and principle are true and correct, it is honored with none above it, it is guarded in common by the buddhas of the three times, no host of māras or outside paths can enter it, and it cannot be ruined by any wrong views or by birth-and-death. Bodhisattva-mahāsattvas who wish swiftly to attain unsurpassed bodhi should cultivate and learn such a very profound, unsurpassed Great Vehicle Sūtra of Immeasurable Meanings."

Signs and Attainments in the Assembly

Chinese Source

佛說是已，於是三千大千世界六種震動，自然空中雨種種花：天憂鉢羅華、鉢曇摩華、拘物頭華、分陀利華；又雨無數種種天香、天衣、天瓔珞、天無價寶，於上空中旋轉來下，供養於佛，及諸菩薩、聲聞大眾；天厨、天鉢器、天百味充滿盈溢；天幢、天幡、天幰蓋、天妙樂具處處安置，作天伎樂，歌歎於佛。又復六種震動，東方恒河沙等諸佛世界，亦雨天華、天香、天衣、天瓔珞、天無價寶，天厨、天鉢器、天百味、天幢、天幡、天幰蓋、天妙樂具，作天伎樂，歌歎彼佛，及彼菩薩、聲聞大眾；南、西、北方，四維上下，亦復如是。

Ru-Yi Translation

When the Buddha had spoken this, the three-thousand-great-thousandfold world shook in six ways, and of themselves various flowers rained from the sky: heavenly utpala flowers, padma flowers, kumuda flowers, and puṇḍarīka flowers. Countless kinds of heavenly incense, heavenly garments, heavenly necklaces, and heavenly priceless jewels also rained down, whirling from the sky above, in offering to the Buddha and to the great assembly of bodhisattvas and śrāvakas. Heavenly kitchens and heavenly bowls were filled to overflowing with heavenly food of a hundred flavors; heavenly banners, heavenly pennants, heavenly canopies, and wondrous heavenly musical instruments were set up everywhere, and heavenly music was made in song and praise of the Buddha. Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

"For more than forty years I have not yet revealed the real" (unit 11). The sūtra's own wording is 以方便力，四十餘年未曾顯實, translated exactly. Tiantai works cite it as 「四十餘年，未顯真實」 (e.g. Fahua Xuanyi T1716, twice; Sijiaoyi T1929; Weimo jing xuanshu T1777). The site's Profound Meaning renders the cited form "for more than forty years, the truth and the real have not yet been revealed" and, elsewhere, "the real had not yet been revealed"; here 實 is "the real" and 真實 (Part 1, Liu Qiu's preface) "the true and real."

"Immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma" (unit 7). Quoted in the site's Profound Meaning as "Immeasurable meanings arise from a single dharma. That single dharma is what is called the real mark." The Profound Meaning reads 其一法者所謂實相, whereas the sūtra has 其一法者，即無相也 and only then names the true mark. The wording here follows the site, except that 實相 is "the true mark" (per the BRIEF).

Key terms, one English each. 無量義 immeasurable meanings; 一法 a single dharma (一法門 one Dharma gate); 無相 markless; 實相 the true mark (真實相 the true and real mark); 四相 the four marks (arising, abiding, changing, ceasing); 性欲 natures and desires; 三法、四果、二道 the three dharmas, the four fruits, the two paths.

三法、四果、二道 (unit 11). Not explained in the sūtra. The Profound Meaning takes the two paths as the sudden and the gradual, the three dharmas as the three vehicles, and the four fruits as the four stages (site translation, Profound Meaning); that is commentary, kept out of the text.

First, middle, and present teachings (units 12–13). The sūtra lists: the four truths at the Deer Park; the twelve links and six pāramitās "in the middle"; and "next" the Vaipulya twelve divisions, the Mahāprajñā, and the Avataṃsaka "ocean-clouds." Tiantai's Five Periods arrange these differently (the Avataṃsaka first); the sūtra's order is kept as it stands.

本來不然，今亦不滅 (unit 13). 然 is read as 燃 "burn," paired with 滅 "be extinguished": "from the origin not burning, now also not extinguished." Alternatively, "originally not so."

煖法、頂法、世第一法 (units 9, 16). Warmth / summit / the supreme worldly dharma, per the glossary; 世間第一法 in unit 16 is the same stage.

"Only a buddha together with a buddha" (unit 14). 唯佛與佛乃能究了 echoes the Lotus "Skillful Means" chapter (唯佛與佛乃能究盡); the site's Lotus page has "Only a buddha, together with another buddha, can fathom," followed here.

無量義三昧 (unit 16). "The samādhi of immeasurable meanings"; the Lotus (Chapter 1) names the samādhi the Buddha enters after teaching this sūtra 無量義處三昧, "the samādhi of the Station of Immeasurable Meanings" on the site.

Fixed formula (units 8, 15). 文理真正，尊無過上，三世諸佛所共守護，無有眾魔群道得入，不為一切邪見生死之所壞敗 is rendered the same way at each occurrence (also in Part 3); unit 15 has 外道 "outside paths" for 群道 "crowd of [other] paths."
