# Notes on the Profound Meaning of Guanyin — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guanyin xuanyi ji* (觀音玄義記, Taishō T34, no. 1727 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1727, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 性善／性惡 | inherent good / inherent evil | Not cut off by the icchantika or by the buddha. |
| 修善／修惡 | cultivated good / cultivated evil | What practice produces and removes. |
| 性具 | inherent inclusion | The nature includes all dharmas, evil as well as good. |
| 十普 | the ten universal [gates] | The ten senses of 'universal' in the chapter's title. |
