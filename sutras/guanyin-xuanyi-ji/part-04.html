The Ten Universal Gates, Essence, Cardinal Doctrine, Function, and Teaching

The fourth and last fascicle of Zhili's Notes covers the rest of Zhiyi's Profound Meaning of Guanyin. It begins with the first of the ten "universal gates," loving-kindness and compassion. Here Zhili reads the hell realm as containing the ten suchnesses of every other realm, including the Buddha's, and draws on the stories of King Xianyu and of Vasu. A bodhisattva who sees that good and evil, suffering and happiness, are inherently included in the nature can give and remove without object, and only that loving-kindness is "universal." The same test runs through the great vows, which Zhang'an judges by whether good and evil are seen as inherent or only cultivated. It then covers practice, severing delusion, entering dharma-gates, spiritual powers, skillful means, teaching the Dharma, offerings, and bringing beings to maturity. Zhili then explains the last four of the five profound meanings. The essence is numinous wisdom joined with the Dharma-body. The cardinal doctrine is appeal and response, chosen because the chapter does not describe Avalokiteśvara's own cultivation. Here a series of questions tests how ordinary beings and a sage can resonate at all. The function is loving-kindness and compassion benefiting others. The teaching is the ghee of the Lotus, which this single chapter propagates.

Ru-Yi Translation

King Xianyu, wishing to transform non-Buddhists, made offerings to an assembly of five hundred brahmins for twelve years, and afterward had them take refuge in and believe the Mahāyāna vaipulya [teachings]. Not taking refuge or believing, they slandered them, saying they did not exist. Hearing the slander, Xianyu killed the five hundred. The five hundred fell into hell and at once gave rise to three thoughts. The first thought: "What place is this?" — and they knew it was hell. The second thought: "From what place did we come?" — and they knew it was the human path. The third thought: "For what cause did we fall into hell?" — and they knew it was for slandering the vaipulya. Because of this they repented their faults, were then born in a buddha-land, and in the end obtained the body of a buddha. This is because Xianyu knew that the people of hell have buddha-nature; he killed them and let them fall, and [their buddha-nature] was brought forth in the three thoughts.

Lemmas from the Profound Meaning. Every phrase Zhili quotes uses the site's English of the Profound Meaning of Guanyin (Part 4). Zhili's 「上獻佛」 (unit 17) is not in the site's Chinese of the Avataṃsaka quotation; it is translated directly.
