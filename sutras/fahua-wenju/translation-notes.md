# Fahua Wenju Translation Notes

Zhiyi's 《妙法蓮華經文句》(Taishō T1718) is his line-by-line commentary on the *Lotus Sūtra*, delivered as oral lectures and recorded by his disciple Guanding. Terminology in this project is kept consistent with this site's three other Zhiyi/Tiantai translations — the *Mohe Zhiguan*, the *Shi Chan Boluomi Cidi Famen*, and the *Xiao Zhiguan Jiangyi* — and with the *Jiaoguan Gangzong*, which quotes the *Wenju* itself as an authority. Sūtra-quote terminology (chapter titles, proper names, recurring phrases) follows this site's own *Lotus Sūtra* translation wherever the *Wenju* cites it.

## The text and its author

Zhiyi (智顗, 538–597 CE) is the founder of the Tiantai school. The *Fahua Wenju*, together with the *Fahua Xuanyi* (妙法蓮華經玄義, setting out the sūtra's doctrinal architecture) and the *Mohe Zhiguan* (building a system of practice from the sūtra's principles), forms the "Tiantai Three Great Works on the Lotus" — though the *Wenju* alone works through the sūtra's actual words, phrase by phrase.

## Source and copyright

Translated directly from the CBETA edition of the Chinese source text, which is in the public domain (Zhiyi died in 597 CE). Made independently, without reference to any published English translation.

## Structure

Ten fascicles (卷), each split by the Taishō edition into "upper" and "lower" halves (之上/之下) for print-volume reasons; this site treats each fascicle's two halves as one continuous page, matching the convention already used for the *Mohe Zhiguan*.

## Zhiyi's fourfold interpretive method

The method that structures nearly every passage in the *Wenju* — kept visible in the translation exactly as Zhiyi presents it, not smoothed over:

| Chinese | Established English |
|---|---|
| 因緣 | causes and conditions (the "causal-condition" reading) |
| 約教 | in terms of the teachings (the "differentiating the teachings" reading) |
| 本迹 | trace and origin |
| 觀心 | the contemplation of mind |

## Fascicle 1 — Title exegesis and the opening assembly

Confirmed from the source text: the exegesis of "序" (prologue) and "品" (chapter); a survey of five-to-sixth-century masters' competing schemes for dividing the sūtra (Feng of Hexi, Yao of Jiangdong, Guangzhai [Fayun], Elder Long of Mount Lu, Xuanchang, and others); Zhiyi's own threefold (序/正/流通) and twofold trace-gate/origin-gate divisions; the fourfold exegesis applied to "如是我聞...一時...佛...王舍城...耆闍崛山中"; and the start of the disciple roll call — Ājñāta-Kauṇḍinya's fourfold "non-arising" treatment (used as the template for every subsequent disciple), Mahākāśyapa and the three Kāśyapa brothers, Śāriputra, Mahāmaudgalyāyana, Mahākātyāyana, and Aniruddha and Kapphiṇa.
