Meritorious, Demeritorious, and Unshakable Karma

The treatise turns to the classical threefold division of all karma by realm, resolves an apparent puzzle in the twelve links of dependent origination about how ignorance can give rise to wholesome action at all, and closes by pairing each of the ten karma-paths with the specific affliction that serves as its means versus the one that actually completes it.

1. Three Kinds of Karma by Realm

Chinese Source

如契經言：有三種業，謂福業、非福業、不動業。何等福業？謂欲界繫善業。何等非福業？謂不善業。何等不動業？謂色無色界繫善業。

Ru-Yi Translation

As the sūtra says: there are three kinds of karma — meritorious karma, demeritorious karma, and unshakable karma. What is meritorious karma? Wholesome karma bound to the desire realm. What is demeritorious karma? Unwholesome karma. What is unshakable karma? Wholesome karma bound to the form and formless realms.

Reading note — after T1606

T1606 explains why form- and formless-realm wholesome karma specifically earns the name "unshakable": desire-realm wholesome or unwholesome karma, once its own destiny is fulfilled, can still be redirected by intervening conditions into ripening in some other, unexpected destiny — but karma bound to the form and formless realms has no such flexibility, since the specific realm and level its ripened result will occur in is already fixed, immovably, from the very start. It is this fixed, non-transferable quality — not any special moral superiority — that "unshakable" actually names.

2. Two Kinds of Ignorance Behind Every Kind of Karma

Chinese Source

如契經說：無明緣行，若福、非福及與不動。云何福及不動行緣無明生？有二種愚：一異熟果愚、二真實義愚，由異熟果愚故發非福行，由真實義愚故發福及不動行。

Ru-Yi Translation

As the sūtra says: ignorance conditions formations — meritorious, demeritorious, and unshakable alike. How can even meritorious and unshakable formations arise conditioned by ignorance? There are two kinds of ignorance: ignorance of the ripened result, and ignorance of true reality. Ignorance of the ripened result gives rise to demeritorious formations. Ignorance of true reality gives rise to meritorious and unshakable formations alike.

Reading note — after T1606

This passage resolves a genuine puzzle in the classical twelve links of dependent origination, already introduced in Part 21: if the very first link is ignorance, and ignorance conditions the second link, formations, how can a wholesome, meritorious act — generosity, moral discipline, meditative concentration — possibly be conditioned by something as negative-sounding as ignorance at all? The treatise's answer distinguishes two quite different kinds of not-knowing. Ignorance of the ripened result is a coarse, straightforward blindness to karma's own cause-and-effect workings — not believing that unwholesome action actually produces suffering — and this specific ignorance is what generates demeritorious action. Ignorance of true reality, by contrast, is a subtler failure: even someone diligently practicing generosity or cultivating deep meditative concentration, and thereby generating genuinely meritorious or unshakable karma, is still doing so without yet directly realizing selflessness and the ultimate nature of reality — their virtuous action, however commendable, is still performed from within an unenlightened, ignorant standpoint, which is precisely why it still counts as conditioned by ignorance, in this specific, more refined sense. Both kinds of ignorance drive the wheel of rebirth forward; they simply drive it toward different destinations.

3. Means and Completion, Paired for Each Karma-Path

Chinese Source

殺生業道，貪瞋癡為方便，由瞋究竟。如殺生，麁惡語、瞋恚業道亦爾。不與取業道，貪瞋癡為方便，由貪究竟。如不與取，欲邪行、貪欲業道亦爾。虛誑語業道，貪瞋癡為方便，於三種中隨由一究竟。如虛誑語，離間語、雜穢語業道亦爾。邪見業道，貪瞋癡為方便，由癡究竟。

Ru-Yi Translation

The karma-path of killing may employ craving, hatred, or delusion as its means, but is always completed specifically by hatred. As with killing, so too harsh speech and the karma-path of ill will. The karma-path of taking what is not given may employ craving, hatred, or delusion as its means, but is always completed specifically by craving. As with taking what is not given, so too sexual misconduct and the karma-path of covetousness. The karma-path of false speech may employ craving, hatred, or delusion as its means, and may be completed by any one of these three, as fitting. As with false speech, so too divisive speech and frivolous speech. The karma-path of wrong view may employ craving, hatred, or delusion as its means, but is always completed specifically by delusion.

Reading note — after T1606

T1606 explains why killing can be approached through any of the three poisons as its preparatory means, yet always requires hatred specifically at the moment of completion: one might set out to kill motivated by craving (wanting the animal's flesh or hide), by hatred (wanting to eliminate an enemy), or by delusion (as in a misguided ritual sacrifice) — but the actual killing blow itself, the moment the act is truly finished, can only ever occur through a mind genuinely lacking compassion at that instant, which is what hatred specifically supplies; no amount of craving or delusion alone, without at least a momentary lapse into hatred's own absence of care for the victim, could actually complete the deed. Taking what is not given mirrors this exactly in reverse: whatever the initial motive, the act is only truly completed by the specific moment of decisively claiming the object as one's own — an act of appropriation that is, by its very nature, an expression of craving. Wrong view's completion by delusion specifically follows the same logic: whatever emotional coloring accompanies the process, a view only becomes wrong view proper at the precise moment of firm, mistaken conviction — and firm, mistaken conviction is delusion's own defining function. False speech and its two companions are the interesting exception, needing no single fixed poison at completion, since their defining moment — the listener's actual, successful misunderstanding — can be achieved with equal finality whether the speaker's underlying motive was greed, malice, or simple confusion.
