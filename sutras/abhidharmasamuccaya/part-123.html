論議品 Begins: The Six Meanings

Having completed 得品, the treatise turns to its fourth and final chapter, 論議品, on formal discourse and debate — opening with a sevenfold overview and then, first among the seven, determination of meaning: six lenses through which any dharma can be analyzed — its own-nature, its causes, its fruits, its karmic function, how it associates with other dharmas, and the five distinct modes in which it actually operates.

1. The Seven Kinds of Determination Through Discourse

Chinese Source

云何論議決擇？略說有七種：謂義決擇、釋決擇、分別顯示決擇、等論決擇、攝決擇、論軌決擇、祕密決擇。

Ru-Yi Translation

What is determination through discourse? In brief, there are seven kinds: determination of meaning, determination of explanation, determination through differentiated exposition, determination through disputation, determination through inclusion, determination through the rules of debate, and determination through hidden meaning.

Reading note — after T1606

These seven together form the treatise's own closing methodology chapter — not further doctrine to be learned, but the actual toolkit for correctly interpreting scripture and conducting formal discourse about it, the very skills a teacher of this Abhidharma would need to put everything already covered in this treatise to productive use in debate and exposition.

2. Determination of Meaning: The Six Meanings

Chinese Source

何等義決擇？謂依六義而起決擇。何等六義？謂自性義、因義、果義、業義、相應義、轉義。自性義者，謂三自性。因義者，謂三因：一生因、二轉因、三成因。果義者，謂五果：一異熟果、二等流果、三增上果、四事用果、五離繫果。業義者，謂五業：一取受業、二作用業、三加行業、四轉變業、五證得業。相應義者，謂五相應：一聚結相應、二隨逐相應、三連綴相應、四分位相應、五轉變相應。轉義者，謂五轉：一相轉、二安住轉、三顛倒轉、四不顛倒轉、五差別轉。

Ru-Yi Translation

What is determination of meaning? It is determination arising in dependence on six meanings. What are the six? Own-nature, cause, fruit, karmic function, association, and mode of operation.

The meaning of own-nature is the three natures. The meaning of cause is threefold: the cause of generation, the cause of continuation, and the cause of accomplishment. The meaning of fruit is fivefold: the ripened fruit, the fruit of the same kind flowing forth, the dominant fruit, the fruit of active exertion, and the fruit of freedom from bondage. The meaning of karmic function is fivefold: the karma of appropriation, the karma of functioning, the karma of application, the karma of transformation, and the karma of realization. The meaning of association is fivefold: association through aggregation, association through underlying persistence, association through interconnection, association through a given phase, and association through transformation. And the meaning of mode of operation is fivefold: the mode of characteristic marks, the mode of abiding, the mode of distortion, the mode of non-distortion, and the mode of differentiation.

Reading note — after T1606

T1606 briefly glosses each fivefold or threefold set. Among the causes: the cause of generation is the four conditions already examined earlier in this treatise, which actually produce conditioned phenomena; the cause of continuation is what determines the specific sequence in which one dharma gives way to the next, as ignorance conditions formations in the twelve links; the cause of accomplishment is whatever, through direct perception or valid inference, establishes some previously unclear meaning as now understood.

Among the fruits: the ripened fruit is exemplified by the storehouse consciousness itself; the fruit of the same kind flowing forth is a later wholesome state arising from an earlier wholesome state of the same continuum; the dominant fruit is the physical world produced by the combined power of all sentient beings' shared karma; the fruit of active exertion is an ordinary product of labor, like a harvested crop; and the fruit of freedom from bondage is the permanent cessation of an underlying tendency through the power of the noble path.

Among the modes of association: association through aggregation is exemplified by wood and stone gathered together in a house; association through underlying persistence is exemplified by a dormant tendency, which is said to be "associated" with its corresponding affliction even while that affliction is not currently active, simply because the tendency remains present as its cause; association through interconnection is exemplified by relatives connected to one another across a lineage; association through a given phase is exemplified by a specific stretch of experience, such as pleasant feeling, described as "associated" with pleasure for as long as that phase continues; and association through transformation is exemplified by an adventitious affliction actually becoming active, at which point it is said to be "associated" with the corresponding root affliction it manifests.

And among the modes of operation: the mode of characteristic marks is the three conditioned characteristics of arising and the rest, each operating according to its own distinct mark; the mode of abiding is a supported dharma continuing to operate within its own supporting basis; the mode of distortion is a defiled dharma failing to operate in accordance with reality; the mode of non-distortion is a purified dharma operating exactly in accordance with reality; and the mode of differentiation is every conditioned phenomenon operating differently according to whether it belongs to the past, future, or present, and to the internal or the external.
