The Compendium of Abhidharma.

The Abhidharmasamuccaya — "Compendium of Abhidharma" — is Asaṅga's own systematic re-working of Abhidharma scholasticism through a Yogācāra lens: the five aggregates, elements, and sense fields analyzed in exhaustive detail, the four noble truths worked out at treatise-length, and the treatise's own closing chapters on the Dharma, on attainment, and on the rules of formal debate. This page is the entrance for reading it part by part, following the treatise's own two-part structure — 本事分, the foundations, and 決擇分, their detailed determination.

How to read this collection

This is the largest single treatise on this site — Asaṅga's own attempt to gather the entire field of Mahāyāna Abhidharma into one systematic work. We keep the treatise's own structure rather than smoothing it away: the foundations chapter (本事分) working through the aggregates, elements, and sense fields in full technical detail, followed by the determination chapter (決擇分), which takes up the four noble truths, the Dharma, attainment, and formal debate each in turn.

Our commentary guide is the Abhidharmasamuccayavyākhyā (大乘阿毘達磨雜集論), attributed to Sthiramati and completed by Jinaputra, translated into Chinese by the same Xuánzàng, so terminology stays aligned with the root text throughout.

The Treatise's Own Structure

Asaṅga divides the whole work into two parts: 本事分, laying out the foundations, and 決擇分, determining them in far greater technical detail. The determination chapter is itself four further chapters long — 諦品 on the four noble truths, 法品 on the Dharma, 得品 on attainment, and 論議品 on formal debate.

本事分 · Foundations
The Five Aggregates, Elements, and Sense Fields
Form, feeling, perception, volition and the fifty-one mental factors, the ālayavijñāna, the eighteen elements and twelve sense fields, the hundred-gate analysis, wholesome/unwholesome/neutral dharmas, dependent origination, and the eight kinds of suffering.
Parts 1–31
決擇分 · 諦品
The Four Noble Truths
Suffering, origination, cessation, and the path, worked out at treatise length — the afflictions and karma in exhaustive detail, the many names of nirvāṇa, and the entire structure of the path from the path of seeing through the culminating path.
Parts 32–69
決擇分 · 法品
The Chapter on the Dharma
The twelve genres of scripture, the three baskets, the objects and principles of investigating the Dharma, and the vast teachings' own correct reading of emptiness against twenty-eight wrong views.
Parts 70–82
決擇分 · 得品
The Chapter on Attainment
The sevenfold classification of persons from ordinary being to bodhisattva, and the tenfold classification of direct realization with its full catalogue of eighteen categories of buddha-qualities.
Parts 83–122
決擇分 · 論議品
The Chapter on Formal Debate
The treatise's own closing methodology: seven kinds of determination through discourse, the full rules of formal debate down to its eight-part logical structure, and the treatise's own explanation of its own title.
Parts 123–131
