The Sūtra on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra

This is the whole of the Sūtra on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra (T0277), translated by Dharmamitra in the Yuanjia era (424–453) of the Liu Song. Tiantai reads it as the "closing sūtra" of the Lotus trilogy, and it is the scriptural basis of the Lotus repentance that Zhiyi set out in his Lotus Samādhi Rite. The Buddha, three months before his passing, answers Ānanda, Mahākāśyapa, and Maitreya: how can people after his death keep the Great Vehicle and purify the six faculties without first cutting off the afflictions? The answer is a sequence of visions and repentances. Samantabhadra appears on his six-tusked white elephant; the practitioner sees the buddhas of the ten directions, the emanation buddhas of Śākyamuni, and the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna; and at each stage he repents in turn the faults of the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind. The rite turns at the point where the buddhas teach "the great repentance": contemplating mind, there is no mind, and offense and merit have no master. The Buddha then sums up in verses on the six faculties, ending with "the many offenses are like frost and dew; the sun of wisdom can melt them away." The last section gives a way to receive the bodhisattva precepts without a formal karman, and five repentances for rulers and householders.

The Three Great Beings Ask Their Questions

Thus have I heard:

Ru-Yi Translation

At one time the Buddha was in the Lecture Hall of the Multistoried Pavilion at the Great Forest Monastery in the country of Vaiśālī, and he said to the monks: "Three months from now I shall enter parinirvāṇa."

Chinese Source

尊者阿難即從座起，整衣服，叉手合掌，遶佛三匝為佛作禮，胡跪合掌，諦觀如來，目不暫捨。長老摩訶迦葉、彌勒菩薩摩訶薩亦從座起，合掌作禮，瞻仰尊顏。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Venerable Ānanda at once rose from his seat, arranged his robes, crossed his hands and joined his palms, circled the Buddha three times, and bowed to the Buddha; kneeling with one knee raised and palms joined, he gazed attentively at the Tathāgata without taking his eyes away even for a moment. The Elder Mahākāśyapa and Maitreya Bodhisattva-mahāsattva also rose from their seats, joined their palms and bowed, and gazed up at his honored face.

Chinese Source

時三大士異口同音而白佛言：「世尊！如來滅後，云何眾生起菩薩心、修行大乘方等經典、正念思惟一實境界？云何不失無上菩提之心？云何復當不斷煩惱、不離五欲、得淨諸根、滅除諸罪？父母所生清淨常眼，不斷五欲而能得見諸障外事？」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the three great beings, with different mouths but the same voice, said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! After the Tathāgata's passing, how shall beings give rise to the bodhisattva mind, practice the Great Vehicle Vaipulya scriptures, and with right mindfulness reflect on the realm of the one reality? How shall they not lose the mind of unsurpassed bodhi? How, further, without severing the afflictions and without leaving the five desires, shall they purify the faculties and remove and extinguish their offenses? And how, with the pure ordinary eyes born of father and mother, without severing the five desires, shall they be able to see things beyond every obstruction?"

Chinese Source

佛告阿難：「諦聽諦聽，善思念之。如來昔在耆闍崛山及餘住處，已廣分別一實之道；今於此處，為未來世諸眾生等，欲行大乘無上法者，欲學普賢行。普賢行者，我今當說其憶念法。若見普賢及不見者除却罪數，今為汝等當廣分別。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Ānanda: "Listen attentively, listen attentively, and reflect on it well. Formerly, on Mount Gṛdhrakūṭa and in other dwelling places, the Tathāgata has already set out at length the way of the one reality. Now, in this place, for the sake of beings of the future age who wish to practice the unsurpassed Dharma of the Great Vehicle and wish to learn the practice of Samantabhadra — the practice of Samantabhadra — I shall now teach the method of keeping it in mind. For those who see Samantabhadra and those who do not, [and] the number of offenses removed, I shall now set it out at length for you.

Chinese Source

「阿難！普賢菩薩乃生東方淨妙國土，其國土相，《法華經》中已廣分別，我今於此略而解說。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Ānanda! Samantabhadra Bodhisattva was born in a pure and wondrous land in the east. The marks of that land have already been set out at length in the Lotus Sūtra; here I shall explain them briefly.

Who Should Practice, and How Soon One Sees

Chinese Source

「阿難！若比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷、天龍八部、一切眾生誦大乘經者、修大乘者、發大乘意者、樂見普賢菩薩色身者、樂見多寶佛塔者、樂見釋迦牟尼佛及分身諸佛者、樂得六根清淨者，當學是觀。此觀功德，除諸障礙、見上妙色，不入三昧但誦持故，專心修習，心心相次，不離大乘，一日至三七日得見普賢；有重障者，七七日盡然後得見；復有重者一生得見、復有重者二生得見、復有重者三生得見；如是種種業報不同，是故異說。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Ānanda! Monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen, gods, nāgas and the eightfold host, and all beings — those who recite the Great Vehicle sūtras, those who cultivate the Great Vehicle, those who have aroused the Great Vehicle intention, those who delight to see the form-body of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, those who delight to see the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna Buddha, those who delight to see Śākyamuni Buddha and the emanation buddhas, those who delight to attain the purity of the six faculties — should learn this contemplation. The merit of this contemplation removes every obstruction and lets one see supremely wondrous forms. Even without entering samādhi, simply through reciting and upholding, cultivating it single-mindedly, thought following upon thought without departing from the Great Vehicle, in one day up to three sets of seven days one will see Samantabhadra. Those with heavy obstructions will see him after seven times seven days are complete; those heavier still will see him in one lifetime; those heavier still will see him in two lifetimes; those heavier still will see him in three lifetimes. Retributions of action differ in these various ways; therefore it is taught differently.

The Six-Tusked White Elephant

Chinese Source

「普賢菩薩身量無邊、音聲無邊、色像無邊，欲來此國，入自在神通促身令小，閻浮提人三障重故。以智慧力化乘白象，其象六牙，七支跓地，其七支下生七蓮華。象色鮮白，白中上者頗梨、雪山不得為比。身長四百五十由旬、高四百由旬。於六牙端有六浴池，一一浴池中生十四蓮華，與池正等，其華開敷如天樹王。一一華上有一玉女，顏色紅輝有過天女，手中自然化五箜篌，一一箜篌有五百樂器以為眷屬。有五百飛鳥，鳧、鴈、鴛鴦……，皆眾寶色，生花葉間。象鼻有華，其莖譬如赤真珠色，其華金色，含而未敷。

Ru-Yi Translation

"The body of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva is boundless in size, his voice is boundless, his form and image are boundless. Wishing to come to this land, he enters his sovereign spiritual penetration and contracts his body to make it small, because the people of Jambudvīpa have heavy threefold obstructions. By the power of wisdom he transforms himself to ride a white elephant. The elephant has six tusks, and its seven limbs rest on the ground; beneath the seven limbs grow seven lotus flowers. The elephant's color is fresh white, whiter than the finest white; crystal and the Snow Mountains cannot compare with it. Its body is four hundred fifty yojanas long and four hundred yojanas high. At the tips of the six tusks are six bathing pools, and in each bathing pool grow fourteen lotus flowers, exactly as large as the pools; the flowers open out like the king of heavenly trees. On each flower is a jade maiden, her face a radiant red surpassing that of the goddesses; in her hands five konghou harps appear of themselves, and each harp has five hundred musical instruments as its retinue. There are five hundred flying birds — wild ducks, wild geese, mandarin ducks … — all of the colors of the many jewels, born among the flowers and leaves. On the elephant's trunk is a flower whose stalk is like the color of red pearl; the flower is golden, still closed and not yet opened.

Chinese Source

「見是事已，復更懺悔，至心諦觀，思惟大乘，心不休廢。見華即敷，金色金光，其蓮華臺是甄叔迦寶，妙梵摩尼以為華鬘、金剛寶珠以為華鬚。見有化佛坐蓮華臺，眾多菩薩坐蓮華鬚。化佛眉間亦出金光入象鼻中，從象鼻出入象眼中，從象眼出入象耳中，從象耳出照象頂上，化作金臺。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Having seen this, one repents again, contemplates attentively with utmost mind, and reflects on the Great Vehicle without the mind ever ceasing. Then one sees the flower open, golden in color with golden light. Its lotus dais is of kiṃśuka jewel, wondrous brahma-maṇi forms its garlands, and vajra jewel-pearls form its stamens. One sees a transformation buddha sitting on the lotus dais, and many bodhisattvas sitting on the lotus stamens. From between the brows of the transformation buddha golden light also comes forth and enters the elephant's trunk; coming out of the elephant's trunk, it enters the elephant's eyes; coming out of the elephant's eyes, it enters the elephant's ears; coming out of the elephant's ears, it shines on the top of the elephant's head and is transformed into a golden dais.

Chinese Source

「其象頭上有三化人：一捉金輪、一持摩尼珠、一執金剛杵。舉杵擬象，象即能行，腳不履地，躡虛而遊，離地七尺。地有印文，於印文中千輻轂輞皆悉具足，一一輞間生一大蓮華，此蓮華上生一化象，亦有七支，隨大象行。舉足下足生七千象以為眷屬，隨從大象。

Ru-Yi Translation

"On the elephant's head are three transformation persons: one grasps a golden wheel, one holds a maṇi pearl, and one holds a vajra pestle. When the pestle is raised toward the elephant, the elephant is at once able to move; its feet do not tread the ground, but it travels stepping on the air, seven feet above the earth. On the ground there are imprints, and in the imprints the thousand spokes, hub, and rim are all complete. Between each of the rims grows one great lotus flower, and on this lotus flower is born a transformation elephant, which also has seven limbs and follows along behind the great elephant. As it lifts and lowers its feet, seven thousand elephants are born to form its retinue, following the great elephant.

Chinese Source

「象鼻紅蓮華色，上有化佛放眉間光，其光金色，如前入象鼻中，於象鼻中出入象眼中，從象眼出還入象耳，從象耳出至象頸上，漸漸上至象背，化成金鞍，七寶校具。於鞍四面有七寶柱，眾寶校飾以成寶臺。

Ru-Yi Translation

"The elephant's trunk is the color of a red lotus flower. On it is a transformation buddha emitting light from between his brows; the light is golden. As before, it enters the elephant's trunk; from within the elephant's trunk it comes out and enters the elephant's eyes; coming out of the elephant's eyes, it enters the elephant's ears again; coming out of the elephant's ears, it reaches the elephant's neck, and gradually climbs up to the elephant's back, where it is transformed into a golden saddle with fittings of the seven jewels. On the four sides of the saddle are pillars of the seven jewels, adorned with the many jewels to form a jeweled dais.

Chinese Source

「臺中有一七寶蓮華，其蓮華鬚百寶共成，其蓮華臺是大摩尼，有一菩薩結加趺坐，名曰普賢。身白玉色五十種光，光五十種色以為項光，身諸毛孔流出金光，其金光端無量化佛。諸化菩薩以為眷屬，安庠徐步，雨大寶華至行者前。其象開口，於象牙上諸池玉女鼓樂、絃歌，其聲微妙，讚歎大乘一實之道。

Ru-Yi Translation

"In the dais is a lotus flower of the seven jewels. Its stamens are formed of a hundred jewels together, and its lotus dais is a great maṇi. On it a bodhisattva sits with legs crossed, named Samantabhadra. His body is the color of white jade, with fifty kinds of light; the light, of fifty kinds of colors, forms the light around his head. From the pores of his body flows golden light, and at the tips of the golden light are immeasurable transformation buddhas. Transformation bodhisattvas form his retinue. Walking in a calm and unhurried way, he rains great jeweled flowers and arrives before the practitioner. The elephant opens its mouth, and on the elephant's tusks the jade maidens in the pools play music and sing to stringed instruments; their sound is subtle and wondrous, praising the way of the one reality of the Great Vehicle.

Samantabhadra Appears to the Practitioner

Chinese Source

「行者見已，歡喜敬禮。復更誦讀甚深經典、遍禮十方無量諸佛、禮多寶塔及釋迦牟尼、并禮普賢……諸大菩薩，發是誓言：『若我宿福應見普賢，願尊遍吉示我色身。』作是願已，晝夜六時禮十方佛、行懺悔法、誦大乘經、讀大乘經、思大乘義、念大乘事、恭敬供養持大乘者、視一切人猶如佛想、於諸眾生如父母想。

Ru-Yi Translation

"When the practitioner has seen this, he rejoices and bows in reverence. Again he recites and reads the very profound scriptures, bows everywhere to the immeasurable buddhas of the ten directions, bows to the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna and to Śākyamuni, and also bows to Samantabhadra … and the great bodhisattvas, and makes this vow: 'If by merit from former lives I ought to see Samantabhadra, may the honored Universally Auspicious One show me his form-body.' Having made this vow, in the six periods of day and night he bows to the buddhas of the ten directions, carries out the method of repentance, recites the Great Vehicle sūtras, reads the Great Vehicle sūtras, reflects on the meaning of the Great Vehicle, keeps the matters of the Great Vehicle in mind, reverently makes offerings to those who uphold the Great Vehicle, regards all people with the thought that they are buddhas, and regards all beings with the thought that they are his father and mother.

Chinese Source

「作是念已，普賢菩薩即於眉間放大人相白毫光明。此光現時，普賢菩薩身相端嚴——如紫金山端正微妙——三十二相皆悉備有。身諸毛孔放大光明，照其大象令作金色、一切化象亦作金色、諸化菩薩亦作金色。其金色光，照于東方無量世界皆同金色，南、西、北方、四維、上、下亦復如是。

Ru-Yi Translation

"When he has had these thoughts, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva at once emits from between his brows the light of the white curl of hair, the mark of a great being. When this light appears, the bodily marks of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva are upright and dignified — like a mountain of purple gold, upright, subtle and wondrous — and the thirty-two marks are all complete. From the pores of his body he emits great light, illuminating his great elephant so that it becomes golden in color; all the transformation elephants also become golden in color, and the transformation bodhisattvas also become golden in color. That golden light illuminates the immeasurable worlds of the east, so that all become the same golden color; and so it is likewise for the south, west, and north, the four intermediate directions, above and below.

Chinese Source

「爾時，十方面一一方有一菩薩乘六牙白象王，亦如普賢等無有異，如是十方無量無邊滿中化象。普賢菩薩神通力故，令持經者皆悉得見。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time, in each of the ten directions there is a bodhisattva riding a six-tusked white elephant king, no different from Samantabhadra; in this way the immeasurable, boundless [worlds] of the ten directions are filled with transformation elephants. By the power of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's spiritual penetration, those who uphold the sūtra are all enabled to see them.

Chinese Source

「是時，行者見諸菩薩身心歡喜，為其作禮，白言：『大慈大悲者！愍念我故，為我說法。』說是語時，諸菩薩等異口同音各說清淨大乘經法，作諸偈頌讚歎行者，是名始觀普賢菩薩最初境界。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At this time the practitioner, seeing the bodhisattvas, rejoices in body and mind, bows to them, and says: 'O you of great kindness and great compassion! Out of pity for me, teach me the Dharma.' When he has said these words, the bodhisattvas, with different mouths but the same voice, each teach the pure Dharma of the Great Vehicle sūtras and make verses praising the practitioner. This is called the first realm in beginning to contemplate Samantabhadra Bodhisattva.

Chinese Source

「爾時，行者見是事已，心念大乘，晝夜不捨。於睡眠中夢見普賢為其說法，如覺無異，安慰其心而作是言：『汝所誦持，忘失是句、忘失是偈。』爾時，行者聞普賢菩薩所說，深解義趣，憶持不忘；日日如是，其心漸利。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the practitioner, having seen this, keeps the Great Vehicle in mind and does not let it go day or night. In sleep he dreams that Samantabhadra teaches him the Dharma, no different from when he is awake, setting his mind at ease and saying: 'In what you recite and uphold, you have forgotten this phrase; you have forgotten this verse.' At that time the practitioner, hearing what Samantabhadra Bodhisattva says, deeply understands its meaning and purport and remembers it without forgetting; day after day it is so, and his mind gradually grows sharp.

Seeing the Buddhas of the Ten Directions

Chinese Source

「普賢菩薩教其憶念十方諸佛，隨普賢教正心正意，漸以心眼見東方佛，身黃金色，端嚴微妙。見一佛已，復見一佛，如是漸漸遍見東方一切諸佛；心相利故，遍見十方一切諸佛。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Samantabhadra Bodhisattva teaches him to keep in mind the buddhas of the ten directions. Following Samantabhadra's teaching, with right mind and right intention, gradually with the eye of the mind he sees the buddhas of the east, their bodies the color of yellow gold, upright, dignified, subtle and wondrous. Having seen one buddha, he sees another buddha; in this way he gradually sees all the buddhas of the east; because the marks of his mind are sharp, he sees all the buddhas of the ten directions.

Chinese Source

「見諸佛已，心生歡喜而作是言：『因大乘故得見大士，因大士力故得見諸佛，雖見諸佛猶未了了，閉目則見，開目則失。』作是語已，五體投地遍禮十方佛。禮諸佛已，胡跪合掌而作是言：『諸佛世尊十力無畏、十八不共、大慈大悲三念處、常在世間色中上色，我有何罪而不得見？』說是語已，復更懺悔。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Having seen the buddhas, joy arises in his mind and he says: 'Because of the Great Vehicle I have been able to see the great being; through the great being's power I have been able to see the buddhas. Yet though I see the buddhas, I do not yet see them clearly: when I close my eyes I see them, and when I open my eyes I lose them.' Having said these words, he casts the five limbs to the ground and bows everywhere to the buddhas of the ten directions. Having bowed to the buddhas, he kneels with one knee raised, joins his palms, and says: 'The buddhas, the World-Honored Ones, with the ten powers, the fearlessnesses, the eighteen unshared [qualities], great kindness and great compassion and the three foundations of mindfulness, are always present in the world, the highest form among forms. What offense have I, that I do not get to see them?' Having said these words, he repents again.

Chinese Source

「懺悔清淨已，普賢菩薩復更現前，行、住、坐、臥不離其側，乃至夢中常為說法；此人覺已，得法喜樂。如是晝夜經三七日，然後方得旋陀羅尼；得陀羅尼故，諸佛菩薩所說妙法憶持不失，亦常夢見過去七佛，唯釋迦牟尼佛為其說法，是諸世尊各各稱讚大乘經典。

Ru-Yi Translation

"When his repentance is pure, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva again appears before him, and whether he is walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, never leaves his side; even in dreams he constantly teaches him the Dharma, and when this person wakes he obtains the joy of the Dharma. Passing three sets of seven days in this way, day and night, only then does he obtain the revolving dhāraṇī. Because he obtains dhāraṇī, he remembers without losing the wondrous Dharma taught by the buddhas and bodhisattvas. He also constantly sees in dreams the seven buddhas of the past, of whom only Śākyamuni Buddha teaches him the Dharma; these World-Honored Ones each praise the Great Vehicle scriptures.

Chinese Source

「爾時，行者復更懺悔，遍禮十方佛。禮十方佛已，普賢菩薩住其人前，教說宿世一切業緣、發露黑惡一切罪事。向諸世尊，口自發露；既發露已，尋時即得諸佛現前三昧。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the practitioner repents again and bows everywhere to the buddhas of the ten directions. When he has bowed to the buddhas of the ten directions, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva stands before him and teaches him to tell all the karmic conditions of his former lives and to lay bare all his black and evil offenses. Facing the World-Honored Ones, he lays them bare with his own mouth; and once he has laid them bare, he at once obtains the samādhi of the presence of all buddhas.

Chinese Source

「得是三昧已，見東方阿閦佛及妙喜國，了了分明。如是，十方各見諸佛上妙國土，了了分明。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Having obtained this samādhi, he sees Akṣobhya Buddha of the east and the Land of Wondrous Joy, clearly and distinctly. In this way, in each of the ten directions he sees the buddhas and their supremely wondrous lands, clearly and distinctly.

The Purity of the Six Faculties

Chinese Source

「既見十方佛已，夢象頭上有一金剛人，以金剛杵遍擬六根；擬六根已，普賢菩薩為於行者說六根清淨懺悔之法。如是懺悔一日至七日，以諸佛現前三昧力故、普賢菩薩說法莊嚴故，耳漸漸聞障外聲、眼漸漸見障外事、鼻漸漸聞障外香。廣說如妙法華經得是六根清淨已，身心歡喜無諸惡相，心純是法，與法相應。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Having seen the buddhas of the ten directions, he dreams that on the elephant's head there is a vajra person who with a vajra pestle points at each of the six faculties. When the six faculties have been pointed at, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva teaches the practitioner the method of repentance for the purity of the six faculties. Repenting in this way for one day up to seven days — by the power of the samādhi of the presence of all buddhas, and through the adornment of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's teaching of the Dharma — his ears gradually hear sounds beyond the obstructions, his eyes gradually see things beyond the obstructions, and his nose gradually smells fragrances beyond the obstructions. As taught at length in the Wondrous Dharma Lotus Sūtra. Having obtained this purity of the six faculties, he rejoices in body and mind and is free of evil marks; his mind is purely Dharma and accords with the Dharma.

You who have aroused the greatly adorned mind! You who keep the Great Vehicle in mind! When we formerly aroused the bodhi mind, we too were just like you, earnest and never losing it. Repentance of the Eye Faculty

Chinese Source

「時諸菩薩異口同音教於行者清淨六根，或有說言：『汝當念佛。』或有說言：『汝當念法。』或有說言：『汝當念僧。』或有說言：『汝當念戒。』或有說言：『汝當念施。』或有說言：『汝當念天。』『如此六法是菩提心、生菩薩法。汝今應當於諸佛前發露先罪，至誠懺悔於無量世眼根因緣貪著諸色。以著色故，貪愛諸塵；以愛塵故，受女人身，世世生處惑著諸色。色壞汝眼，為恩愛奴；色使使汝經歷三界，為此弊使，盲無所見。今誦大乘方等經典，此經中說：「十方諸佛色身不滅。」汝今得見，審實爾不？眼根不善，傷害汝多，隨順我語歸向諸佛。釋迦牟尼說汝眼根所有罪咎，諸佛菩薩慧明法水願以洗除，令我清淨。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"Then the bodhisattvas, with different mouths but the same voice, teach the practitioner to purify the six faculties. Some say: 'You should be mindful of the Buddha.' Some say: 'You should be mindful of the Dharma.' Some say: 'You should be mindful of the Saṅgha.' Some say: 'You should be mindful of the precepts.' Some say: 'You should be mindful of giving.' Some say: 'You should be mindful of the gods.' 'These six dharmas are the bodhi mind, the dharmas that give birth to bodhisattvas. Now, before the buddhas, you should lay bare your former offenses and repent with utmost sincerity: through countless lives, because of the eye faculty, you have been greedily attached to forms. Because of attachment to forms, you craved the objects; because of craving the objects, you received a woman's body, and in every life, wherever you were born, you were deluded and attached to forms. Forms have ruined your eyes, making you a slave of affection and love; forms have driven you to pass through the three realms; because of this wretched driver you have been blind and seen nothing. Now you recite the Great Vehicle Vaipulya scriptures, and this sūtra says: "The form-bodies of the buddhas of the ten directions do not perish." Now that you are able to see them, is it really so or not? The eye faculty is not good and has harmed you greatly. Follow my words and turn to the buddhas. [Say:] "Śākyamuni teaches the offenses and faults of my eye faculty; may the buddhas and bodhisattvas, with the Dharma-water of their bright wisdom, wash them away and make me pure."'

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

This is called the method of repenting the offenses of the eye faculty.

I pray only that Śākyamuni, of right and universal knowledge, the World-Honored One, be my preceptor; that Mañjuśrī, possessed of great wisdom, with his wisdom confer on me the pure dharmas of the bodhisattvas; that Maitreya Bodhisattva, supreme sun of great kindness, out of pity for me, also permit me to receive the bodhisattva dharmas; that the buddhas of the ten directions appear to bear witness for me; that the great bodhisattvas, each called by name — these supreme great beings who shelter and protect beings — help and protect us. Today I receive and uphold the Vaipulya scriptures; even if I lose my life, even if I fall into hell and suffer immeasurable pain, I will never revile the true Dharma of the buddhas. By the power of the merit of these causes and conditions, may Śākyamuni Buddha now be my preceptor, Mañjuśrī my ācārya; may Maitreya, who is to come, confer the Dharma on me; may the buddhas of the ten directions witness and know me; may the greatly virtuous bodhisattvas be my companions. Relying now on the very profound and wondrous meaning of the Great Vehicle sūtras, I take refuge in the Buddha, take refuge in the Dharma, take refuge in the Saṅgha.' He says this three times.

The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva

Reading Notes for Part 1

Translator's notes

Text and title. T0277, in one fascicle. 行法 in the title is given as "Practice"; 普賢行 in the body is "the practice of Samantabhadra." The site's Lotus Sūtra chapter "Samantabhadra's Encouragement" has "Samantabhadra's own conduct" for 普賢行 (see Decisions).

Lotus wording reused. From the site's Lotus Sūtra ("Samantabhadra's Encouragement" and "The Jeweled Stūpa"): six-tusked white elephant king (六牙白象王), spiritual penetration (神通), revolving dhāraṇī (旋陀羅尼), emanation buddhas (分身諸佛), light of the white curl of hair between the brows, the mark of a great being (眉間白毫大人相光), Prabhūtaratna, Mount Gṛdhrakūṭa (the opening chapter's form; the Samantabhadra chapter itself says "Vulture Peak"), and "unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening" (阿耨多羅三藐三菩提).

Quoted on the site already. The site's Mohe Zhiguan (Fascicle 2, on the half-walking half-sitting samādhi) quotes this sūtra at length, and the site's Profound Meaning quotes 「我心自空，罪福無主」「觀心無心，法不住法」「心純是法」. The sentences are translated here to agree with that wording: "My mind is itself empty; offense and merit have no master," "Contemplating mind, there is no mind; dharmas do not abide in dharmas," "his mind is purely Dharma." The verse 「若欲懺悔者，端坐念實相；眾罪如霜露，慧日能消除」 follows the round-2 Sijiaoyi draft (Part 2): "If you wish to repent, sit upright and be mindful of the true mark; the many offenses are like frost and dew; the sun of wisdom can melt them away."

遍吉 (unit 14). Another name of Samantabhadra ("universally auspicious"); translated "the Universally Auspicious One."

"Broadly as in the Wondrous Dharma Lotus Sūtra" (unit 24). The CBETA text runs 廣說如妙法華經得是六根清淨已 without a break; it is read as two sentences, the first a cross-reference to the Lotus chapters on the merits of the Dharma teacher (the site's "The Purified Six Senses").

"The form-bodies of the buddhas of the ten directions do not perish" (unit 34). Given as a quotation from "this sūtra"; no exact source located in the Taishō files in the vault (the phrase 色身不滅 occurs only here and in a Dunhuang commentary, T2805).

Ellipses (units 9, 14, 45, 50, 76, 83). The CBETA text itself has "……" at these places, marking abbreviated lists; they are kept as "…".

Vairocana and Eternal Tranquil Light (unit 52). 毘盧遮那，遍一切處 is read as name plus gloss ("Vairocana, Pervading All Places"). 常寂光 is the name of that buddha's dwelling; Tiantai later takes it as the fourth land, "the land of eternal tranquil light." The four pāramitās are permanence, self, purity, bliss (常我淨樂). Alternatively, 是色常住法故 may mean "because this form is a dharma that abides eternally."

The great repentance (unit 53). 結使 "fetters and envoys" (使 = the latent afflictions as "envoys"); 使海 "ocean of envoys." 破壞心識 is translated literally, "the destruction of mind and consciousness."

Six grave rules and eight grave rules (unit 73). 六重法 and 八重法 are not defined in the sūtra. Later Chinese commentators link them with the six grave precepts of the Upāsaka Precepts Sūtra and the eight (or ten) major bodhisattva precepts; this is commentary, not the text.

Without a karman (units 71, 73). 羯磨 is the formal act of the saṅgha in ordination; 不須羯磨 "without a karman," 不白羯磨 "no announcement of a karman."

剎利居士 (unit 77). Literally "kṣatriyas and householders"; the passage is addressed to the kings and ministers of unit 76.

Six days of abstinence (unit 80). 六齋日: the 8th, 14th, 15th, 23rd, 29th, and 30th of the lunar month.

Repentance vocabulary. 懺悔 repent / repentance; 悔過 regret one's faults (kept distinct); 發露 lay bare; 五體投地 cast the five limbs to the ground; 胡跪 kneel with one knee raised; 長跪 kneel upright; 晝夜六時 the six periods of day and night — as in the round-2 Xiuchan yaozhi, Jinguangming chanyi, and Sijiaoyi drafts and the site's Great Compassion Repentance.
