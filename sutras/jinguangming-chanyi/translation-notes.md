# The Golden Light Repentance Rite — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming zuisheng chanyi* (金光明最勝懺儀, Taishō T46, no. 1946 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1946, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 功德天 | the goddess Śrī | Who promises blessings to those who uphold the sūtra. |
| 四方四佛 | the buddhas of the four directions | Invited with Śākyamuni. |
| 懺悔 | repentance | The central act of the rite. |
