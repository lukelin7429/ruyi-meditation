The Golden Light Repentance Rite

The Most Excellent Golden Light Repentance Rite (Jinguangming zuisheng chanyi, T1946) was compiled by Zhili (960–1028), the Song-dynasty Tiantai master of Siming, as a liturgy for practice based on the Golden Light Sūtra. It draws on both Chinese versions of the sūtra: the buddha and bodhisattva names and the goddess Śrī come from Dharmakṣema's Golden Light Sūtra (T663), while the confession, the verse of praise, and the dhāraṇī come from Yijing's Sūtra of the Most Excellent King of Golden Light (T665), which gives the rite its title. The order is the usual Tiantai one: homage and the offering of incense and flowers; invitations to the buddhas, the sūtra, the bodhisattvas, the protecting gods, and finally the goddess Śrī, whose dhāraṇī is recited seven times; a verse in praise of the Buddha's body; an offering of food, part of which is carried outside and scattered for the spirits; prostrations to the buddhas of the sūtra; and the fivefold repentance — confession, entreating the buddhas, rejoicing, dedication, and vows — each closed by a bow to the Three Jewels. The rite ends with the three refuges. The small-type instructions say who chants, how many times, and when to bow or kneel; they are translated in parentheses where they stand.

Praise of the Buddha

Offering and the Feeding of Spirits

(Having praised, one should state the intention. Joining palms reverently, have the assembly chant three times in an even tone, saying:)

Ru-Yi Translation

"Homage to Jewel-Flower Lapis Buddha, Homage to the Great Vehicle Golden Light Sūtra, Homage to the great goddess Śrī, foremost in majestic virtue, who accomplishes all affairs." Chinese Source

「今我道場敷設供養，然種種燈，燒種種香，奉種種飲食，淨潔如法，恭持奉供諸佛世尊，大乘經典，菩薩賢聖一切三寶；又復別具香華飲食，奉獻功德大天大辯，四王梵釋，天龍八部聖眾；復持飲食散擲餘方，施諸神等。惟願三寶天仙，憐愍於我及諸眾生，受此供養，以金光明力及諸佛威神，於一念間顯現十方一切佛剎，如雲遍滿、如雨普洽，廣作佛事等熏眾生，發菩提心同圓種智。」（此亦應隨意所陳，未必專誦此語。作是語已，當持飲食至道場外淨處，布散四方，先作是言：）

Ru-Yi Translation

"Now in our place of practice we have set out offerings: lighting various lamps, burning various incense, presenting various foods, pure and in accord with the Dharma, we respectfully hold them up as offerings to the buddhas, World-Honored Ones, the scriptures of the Great Vehicle, the bodhisattvas, worthies and sages — all the Three Jewels. Further, we have separately prepared incense, flowers, and food to present to the great goddess Śrī and the [goddess of] Great Eloquence, the four kings, Brahmā and Śakra, and the holy assembly of gods, dragons, and the eight classes. Further, we take food and scatter it in the remaining directions, giving it to the spirits. We pray only that the Three Jewels and the gods and immortals have compassion on us and on all sentient beings and accept this offering; and that, by the power of the Golden Light and the majestic spiritual power of the buddhas, it may appear in a single thought in all the buddha-lands of the ten directions, filling them like clouds and soaking them everywhere like rain, broadly doing the work of the Buddha and perfuming sentient beings alike, so that they arouse the bodhi-mind and together perfect the wisdom knowing all modes." (This too may be stated as one wishes; one need not recite exactly these words. Having said this, take the food to a clean place outside the place of practice to spread it in the four directions, first saying these words:)

Chinese Source

「我今依教供養大乘三寶及吉祥大天，持此種種飲食，散灑諸方遍施諸神，願諸神明威權自在一念普集，各受法食充足無乏，身力勇銳守護堅強，知我所求願當相與，迴此福利普潤含生，果報自然常受勝樂。」（作是呪願竟，即便以食散擲四方，想無量鬼神悉來受食。爾時或誦前呪，或但云「南無室利摩訶天女」，以食盡為度。散灑畢，迴道場禮佛。）

Ru-Yi Translation

"We now, in accordance with the teaching, make offering to the Three Jewels of the Great Vehicle and to the great goddess Auspicious [Śrī], and take these various foods and scatter and sprinkle them in all directions, giving them to all the spirits. May the spirits, sovereign in majestic authority, gather everywhere in a single thought, each receive the Dharma-food and be filled without lack, strong and keen in bodily strength, firm in guarding and protecting; knowing what I seek, may they grant what I wish. May this blessing and benefit be turned to moisten all living beings universally, so that as their reward they naturally receive excellent happiness forever." (Having completed this incantatory wish, at once scatter the food in the four directions, visualizing countless ghosts and spirits all coming to receive the food. At this time either recite the preceding spell or say only "Homage to Śrī, the great [mahā] goddess," continuing until the food is gone. When the scattering is finished, return to the place of practice and bow to the buddhas.)

Repentance

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

"Universally, on behalf of all sentient beings of the dharma-realm, I vow that all may cut off the three obstructions, and I take refuge and repent." (Bow once, and visualize the ten contrary and compliant minds.)

Ru-Yi Translation

"I, the bhikṣu (so-and-so), take refuge and bow in homage to all the buddhas now present in the ten directions, who have already attained anuttarā samyaksaṃbodhi, who turn the wondrous Dharma-wheel, uphold the illuminating Dharma-wheel, rain the great Dharma-rain, beat the great Dharma-drum, blow the great Dharma-conch, raise the great Dharma-banner, and hold up the great Dharma-torch; who, wishing to benefit and give peace and happiness to all sentient beings, constantly practice the giving of the Dharma, drawing the confused multitudes onward so that they attain the great fruit and realize the eternal and blissful. "From beginningless birth-and-death until now, I have flowed along with evil, and together with all sentient beings have created the offenses of karmic obstruction. Bound by greed, anger, and delusion, when I did not yet know the Buddha, did not yet know the Dharma, did not yet know the Saṅgha, did not yet know good and evil, I have through body, speech, and mind created offenses [leading to] the uninterrupted [hell]: with evil mind drawing blood from a buddha's body, slandering the true Dharma, breaking the harmonious saṅgha, killing arhats, killing and harming father and mother; through the three kinds of conduct of body, the four of speech, and the three of mind, creating the ten evil actions, doing them myself and teaching others, seeing them done and rejoicing; wantonly slandering good people; cheating with measures and scales, passing off the false as true; giving impure food and drink to all; tormenting one another among the fathers and mothers of the six destinies; or stealing the property of stūpas, the property of the saṅgha of the four directions, and the property of the saṅgha present, and using it at will; not delighting in practicing the law and discipline of the World-Honored One, and not following the instructions of teachers and elders; on seeing those who practice the śrāvaka, solitary-awakened, or Great Vehicle [paths], delighting in reviling and insulting them, causing those practitioners to feel regret and vexation; on seeing those better than myself, harboring jealousy; always stingy in the giving of Dharma and the giving of wealth; covered by ignorance, my mind deluded by wrong views, not cultivating good causes and letting evil grow; slandering the buddhas, calling the Dharma not Dharma and what is not Dharma the Dharma.

The Most Excellent Golden Light Repentance Rite

Reading Notes for Part 1

Translator's notes

Title. 最勝 ("most excellent") points to Yijing's version, the Jinguangming zuisheng wang jing (T665). The short English title The Golden Light Repentance Rite drops it; the full title in unit 0 keeps it.

Sources in the sūtra. The list of buddhas in units 19–32 (from Jewel-Flower Lapis to Jewel Mark) and the title of Śrī, "foremost in majestic virtue, who accomplishes all affairs" (第一威德成就眾事大功德天), are from Dharmakṣema's Golden Light Sūtra (T663), chapters on the goddess of merit and on Saṃjñeya. The confession of unit 45 is Yijing's chapter "Eliminating Karmic Obstructions" (滅業障品, T665); the verse of praise in units 12–14 (佛面猶如淨滿月…) is in Yijing's chapter on the Four Heavenly Kings protecting the country. The Golden Light Sūtra is not yet on the site.

Names. The four buddhas of the four directions (units 20–23) are Akṣobhya (east), Jewel Mark (寶相; Skt. Ratnaketu) (south), Amitāyus (west; 無量壽, as on the site's Contemplation Sūtra), and Wondrous Voice (微妙聲; Skt. Dundubhisvara) (north). Other buddha names are translated, not reconstructed in Sanskrit. 寶相 in unit 32 is the same Chinese as the southern buddha and is given the same English. Faith-Mark Bodhisattva (信相) is Ruciraketu, to whom the sūtra is addressed in Dharmakṣema's version.

Gods. 功德天 is the goddess Śrī (Mahāśrī); 吉祥大天 in unit 18 is the same goddess under her other Chinese name ("Auspicious"); 大辯天神 is the goddess of Great Eloquence (Sarasvatī); 堅牢地神 is Dṛḍhā the earth goddess; 散脂大將 is the great general Saṃjñeya; 金剛密跡 is the vajra-bearing guardian Guhyapāda ("Secret-Traces"); 韋馱 (Weituo) is usually identified with Skanda; 訶梨帝喃鬼子母 is Hārītī, Mother of Demon-Children.

"The Thirty-three Heavens, the four world-protecting kings" (unit 8). The Chinese 三十三天護世四王 can also be read as one item: "the four world-protecting kings of the Thirty-three Heavens." The English lists them separately.

Dhāraṇī (unit 11). The spell is that of the goddess Śrī in Yijing's version. It opens with the invocation 南無室利莫訶天女 ("Homage to Śrī, the great goddess"), which unit 18 allows to be said alone in place of the whole spell. The eleven interlinear notes are fǎnqiè readings and tone marks, rendered "(pronunciation note)."

Repentance formulas. "Let all be reverent," "With sincere mind, we bow in homage to …," "kneel with one knee raised," "Universally, on behalf of … I vow that all may cut off the three obstructions," the three refuges, and "We bow in reverence to the whole assembly of sages" follow the site's Great Compassion Repentance. 懺悔 is "repent" (BRIEF), where the site's Great Compassion Repentance has "confess"; 自歸於佛 "taking refuge in the Buddha of my own accord" follows that page, but its first wish here differs (體解大道 "embody and understand the great path," not 紹隆佛種).

The ten contrary and compliant minds (unit 44). Zhili's own Essential Purport of Repentance Practice (T1937) explains them: ten minds that go against nirvāṇa and along with birth-and-death, and ten that reverse them. "Contrary / compliant" follows the glossary's 逆修／順修.

The wisdom knowing all modes (units 16–17). 種智 is the short form of 一切種智 and takes the glossary rendering.

Rejoicing (unit 47). 十方諸佛證妙菩提法，施一切所有功德 follows CBETA's punctuation. Alternatively: 證妙菩提，法施一切, "realizing wondrous bodhi and giving the Dharma to all."

The inexhaustible lamp (unit 49). 無盡燈 is the Vimalakīrti's image of one lamp lighting countless others (site Vimalakīrti Sūtra, "The Bodhisattvas Decline").
