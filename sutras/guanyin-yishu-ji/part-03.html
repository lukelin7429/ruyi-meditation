Seeking a Son or a Daughter, and the Buddha Body in the Lands

Part 3 is the third fascicle of Siming Zhili's Notes on the Commentary on the Meaning of Guanyin (T1729). It follows Zhiyi's Commentary from the capacity of bodily action — the woman who bows to Avalokiteśvara seeking a son or a daughter — into the second question of the Universal Gate chapter, how Avalokiteśvara journeys and teaches. Zhili first defends Zhang'an's claim that the sage can give merit and wisdom and not merely "bring the parents together," and then follows the Commentary as son and daughter come to stand for wisdom and concentration through the precepts, the dhyānas, the two vehicles, and the four teachings, ending in the "twin birth" at the first ground or the first abode. On the comparison with sixty-two koṭis of bodhisattvas he cites Vasubandhu's Treatise on the Lotus with Jingxi's explanation. On the second question he sets out three sets of four propositions (capacity and response, persons and dharmas, cause and fruit) and then places the responsive buddha in the land of true reward, the land of expedients (with the five kinds of mind-made bodies), and the land of dwelling together, pure or defiled. The fascicle closes on why the Buddha showed a superior and an inferior body from the moment he entered the womb.
