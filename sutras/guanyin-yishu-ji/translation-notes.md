# Notes on the Commentary on the Meaning of Guanyin — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guanyin yishu ji* (觀音義疏記, Taishō T34, no. 1729 (Zhili; with Zunshi's Explaining the Verses)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1729, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028, Zunshi in 1032; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 七難 | the seven calamities | As in the Commentary. |
| 二求 | the two wishes | For a son or a daughter. |
| 重頌 | the verses | Explained by Zunshi in the appendix. |
