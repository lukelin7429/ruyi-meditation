# The Letter of Ten Meanings — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Siming shiyi shu* (四明十義書, Taishō T46, no. 1936 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1936, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 觀心 | contemplating the mind | Zhili's subject throughout. |
| 性具 | inherent inclusion | The nature includes all dharmas. |
| 理具 / 事造 | inclusion in principle / creation in phenomena | The two aspects of the three thousand. |
| 山外 | the off-mountain teachers | Zhili's opponents, Wu'en, Qingzhao, and Zhiyuan. |
