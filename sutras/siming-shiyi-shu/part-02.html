Meanings Four to Six: The Two Creations, the Efficacy of Contemplation, and the Mind-Dharma

This part covers Meanings Four to Six of Zhili's Letter of Ten Meanings (1007) and the fascicle break between them. The Fourth Meaning takes up the Fuxing's two kinds of creation: creation in principle, where creating simply means containing, and creation in phenomena, where the deluded mind transforms and produces the ten realms. Zhili argues that contemplation of principle "simply contemplates inclusion in principle" in the mind that creates, and does not range over the outer objects that are created; he charges Qingzhao with blurring the two and then changing his wording from letter to letter. The Fifth Meaning defends the contemplations "relying on phenomena" and "attached to dharmas" as real methods of practice: in Zhiyi's day hearers cultivated them at once, later students can do so under a teacher who supplies the intent of Calming and Contemplation, and some awaken through brief methods alone. The Sixth Meaning turns to the mind-dharma: mind, buddhas, and beings are equally phenomena and principle, the object of contemplation is the deluded mind of the six consciousnesses, and "mind-only" in the Vajra Scalpel refers to the afflicted mind, not to a true mind. Zhili quotes Qingzhao's letters and his own earlier ones throughout; these quotations are marked.

第四不辨事理二造

Ru-Yi Translation

Fourth: Not Discerning the Two Creations, in Phenomena and in Principle

The Two Creations in the Fuxing

Chinese Source

然若解內外境觀之意，不假復論二造之義也。猶恐上人執迷難悟，故不獲已再復言之。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now, if one understood the intent of the objects and contemplations, inner and outer, there would be no need to discuss again the meaning of the two creations. Still, fearing that the Venerable, clinging to his delusion, will find it hard to awaken, I have no choice but to speak of it once more.

Chinese Source

《輔行》云：造有二義。一者約理，造即是具；二者約事，乃論過造於現、過現造當、現造於現、聖人變化所造（云云）。復結云：皆由理具方有事用。今欲修觀但觀理具，俱破俱立俱是法界，任運攝得權實所現（上皆《輔行》文也。但於事中取意而說，略彼廣文）。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Fuxing says: "Creation has two meanings. First, in terms of principle, creating is containing. Second, in terms of phenomena, one discusses the past creating the present, the past and present creating the future, the present creating the present, and what is created by the transformations of sages (and so on)." It further concludes: "Only because of inclusion in principle is there phenomenal function. Now, wishing to cultivate contemplation, one simply contemplates inclusion in principle: all refuted, all established, all the dharma-realm; spontaneously it gathers in what is manifested by the provisional and the real." (The above is all the text of the Fuxing. But within the [section on] phenomena it speaks taking the intent, abbreviating that extensive text.)

Chinese Source

此之二造，各論三千。理則本具三千，性善性惡也，事則變造三千，修善修惡也。

Ru-Yi Translation

These two creations each discuss the three thousand. In principle, [the mind] originally contains the three thousand: inherent good and inherent evil. In phenomena, it transforms and creates the three thousand: cultivated good and cultivated evil.

Chinese Source

論事造，乃取無明識陰為能造，十界依正為所造。若論理造，造即是具。既能造所造一一即理，乃一一當體皆具性德三千，故十二入各具千如也。

Ru-Yi Translation

In discussing creation in phenomena, one takes the aggregate of consciousness, which is ignorance, as what creates, and the dependent and principal of the ten realms as what is created. If one discusses creation in principle, creating is containing. Since what creates and what is created are each identical to principle, each in its very substance contains the three thousand of inherent virtue; therefore each of the twelve entrances contains the thousand suchnesses.

Chinese Source

能造所造、內境外境，皆可當處觀於理具。但《止觀》揀繁從要、捨難取易，去其所造，取於能造觀具三千。能造所造若未觀具，且名凡夫世諦隔歷不融。故揀去界入，專取陰識為所觀境也。即《輔行》先重明境科意也。

Ru-Yi Translation

What creates and what is created, inner objects and outer objects — all can be contemplated, right where they are, as inclusion in principle. But Calming and Contemplation sifts out the complex to follow the essential, and discards the difficult to take up the easy: it sets aside what is created and takes up what creates, contemplating it as containing the three thousand. As long as what creates and what is created have not been contemplated as containing [the three thousand], they are for the time being called the conventional truth of ordinary people, separated and not fused. Therefore it sifts out the realms and entrances and solely takes aggregate-consciousness as the object contemplated. This is the intent of the Fuxing's earlier section "clarifying the object of the aggregates again."

Chinese Source

至十乘中，用於妙觀，觀此能造一念陰心本具三千。既一念即三千、三千即一念，言慮不及，故轉名不思議境也。

Ru-Yi Translation

When it comes to the ten modes, the wondrous contemplation is used to contemplate this single thought of the mind of the aggregates that creates as originally containing the three thousand. Since a single thought is the three thousand and the three thousand are a single thought, words and thoughts cannot reach it; therefore it is renamed the inconceivable object.

Chinese Source

若觀之不已，觀成理顯、或伏或斷，名為心淨。故未淨時，名無明識陰。若已淨時，無明轉故即變為明。能造既明，所造任運自然清淨（若內觀成，以理攝事，則外境事造皆趣內心，名內心淨。若歷外境，一一事境皆為諸法所趣，名任運泯合故也）上人不許唯觀理具三千，須執觀於事造，外境歸心名修實相觀。又歷外事咸歸內心，名修唯識觀。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one contemplates it without ceasing, contemplation is accomplished and principle is revealed; [delusion] is either subdued or severed; this is called the mind being pure. Therefore, while not yet pure, it is called the aggregate of consciousness, which is ignorance. Once it is pure, because ignorance is transformed, it turns into understanding. Since what creates is understanding, what is created is spontaneously and naturally pure. (If inner contemplation is accomplished, principle gathers in phenomena, so outer objects, created in phenomena, all tend toward the inner mind; this is called the inner mind being pure. If one passes through outer objects, each phenomenal object is what all dharmas tend toward; this is why it is called spontaneously dissolving and merging.) The Venerable does not allow contemplating only the three thousand included in principle; he insists on contemplating creation in phenomena, and calls returning outer objects to the mind "cultivating the contemplation of the true mark." Further, he calls passing through outer phenomena and returning them all to the inner mind "cultivating the contemplation of consciousness-only."

Chinese Source

予實不敢輒信此說，有多乖失故。一違《輔行》現文故；二兩觀不分故；三錯認唯識為外觀故；四全不識理具三千故。

Ru-Yi Translation

I truly dare not lightly believe this account, because it has many errors: first, because it goes against the actual text of the Fuxing; second, because it does not distinguish the two contemplations; third, because it mistakes consciousness-only for outer contemplation; fourth, because it does not recognize the three thousand included in principle at all.

Simply Contemplating Inclusion in Principle

Chinese Source

且《輔行》云：皆由理具方有事用。今欲修觀，但觀理具，俱破俱立，俱是法界，任運攝得權實所現。上人不善銷此文故，不識二造也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now, the Fuxing says: "Only because of inclusion in principle is there phenomenal function. Now, wishing to cultivate contemplation, one simply contemplates inclusion in principle: all refuted, all established, all the dharma-realm; spontaneously it gathers in what is manifested by the provisional and the real." Because the Venerable is not skilled at interpreting this text, he does not recognize the two creations.

Chinese Source

既云但觀理具，俱破俱立，俱是法界。豈非令行人於能造心，唯觀理具三千俱空假中耶？若便以所造外事為境，何名但觀理具俱破等耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Since it says "simply contemplates inclusion in principle: all refuted, all established, all the dharma-realm," does it not have the practitioner, in the mind that creates, contemplate only the three thousand included in principle as all empty, provisional, and middle? If one then took the outer phenomena that are created as the object, how could it be called "simply contemplates inclusion in principle: all refuted" and so on?

Chinese Source

良由灸病得穴，故百病自差。伐樹得根，故千枝自枯。故云任運攝得權實所現，以皆由理具方有事用故。只觀理具三千俱空假中，故事用所造自然皆空假中，故云任運攝得權實所現。豈須千枝遍斬、六分全燒方名伐樹灸病耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

It is truly because, in treating an illness with moxa, one finds the point, that the hundred illnesses heal of themselves; because, in felling a tree, one gets at the root, that the thousand branches wither of themselves. Therefore it says, "spontaneously it gathers in what is manifested by the provisional and the real," because "only because of inclusion in principle is there phenomenal function." One only contemplates the three thousand included in principle as all empty, provisional, and middle; therefore what is created as phenomenal function is naturally all empty, provisional, and middle. Therefore it says, "spontaneously it gathers in what is manifested by the provisional and the real." Must one cut every one of the thousand branches, or burn all six portions, before it is called felling the tree or treating the illness with moxa?

Chinese Source

《輔行》明明先分二造，特令行人但觀理造俱空假中。上人剛使遍觀事造，何抑教順情之甚乎。若內心理觀便緣外境事造，唯識觀於何法？唯色觀於何法？斯由上人始從聽講、已至為師，全未曾知事理二造及內外二境，遂錯將事造外境便為內心理具。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Fuxing plainly first divides the two creations and specifically has the practitioner simply contemplate creation in principle as all empty, provisional, and middle. The Venerable stubbornly makes [one] contemplate creation in phenomena pervasively — how extreme is this suppressing of the teaching to follow your own feelings! If the contemplation of principle in the inner mind took outer objects created in phenomena as its object, on what dharma would the contemplation of consciousness-only be practiced? On what dharma the contemplation of form-only? This is because the Venerable, from first attending lectures until becoming a teacher, has never known the two creations, in phenomena and in principle, or the two objects, inner and outer; and so he mistakenly takes outer objects created in phenomena as the inner mind's inclusion in principle.

Chinese Source

且《輔行》云：眾生心中，皆有如來結跏趺坐。豈事造如來耶？又云：下地雖具因果，但是理具。故知理造未論變作。故修理觀者，既云但觀理具，則知唯照本理性德俱空假中，任運攝得事造諸法。作觀之際，實不可便緣事造為境。

Ru-Yi Translation

Moreover, the Fuxing says: "In the minds of sentient beings there are all tathāgatas sitting with legs crossed." Are these tathāgatas created in phenomena? It further says: "Although the lower grounds contain cause and fruit, it is only inclusion in principle." Therefore we know that creation in principle does not yet discuss transformation and making. Therefore, for one who cultivates the contemplation of principle, since it says "simply contemplates inclusion in principle," we know that one only illuminates the original principle, inherent virtue, as all empty, provisional, and middle, and spontaneously gathers in the dharmas created in phenomena. At the time of making contemplation, one truly may not then take creation in phenomena as the object.

Chinese Source

以上人素不分二造故，致將理事散漫而觀，便為深明止觀妙境三千也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Because the Venerable has never distinguished the two creations, he comes to contemplate principle and phenomena diffusely, and takes this to be a deep clarification of the wondrous object, the three thousand, of Calming and Contemplation.

What Discerning Errors Said

Chinese Source

故《辨訛》云：彼《止觀》不思議境初，本欲觀十界依正之法。所以唯觀心者，心為諸法之本故也。伐樹除根、灸病得穴，由是即觀一念識心具造三千之法。何得云非初心作觀便觀諸法。所造三千豈非諸法等耶？又破予揀色觀心，恐心外向之義，乃引內外不二門，託彼依正色心為難。又云：《止觀》初心遍觀十界依正三千之法，三千之內豈無色耶？況圓解者塵塵法界處處遮那。又舉色香中道、諸法趣色等文為難。

Ru-Yi Translation

Therefore Discerning Errors says: "At the beginning of the inconceivable object in that Calming and Contemplation, the original wish was to contemplate the dharmas of the dependent and principal of the ten realms. The reason it contemplates only the mind is that mind is the root of all dharmas. Felling a tree by removing the root, treating an illness with moxa by finding the point — thus it contemplates a single thought of the consciousness-mind as containing and creating the dharmas of the three thousand. How can you say that the initial mind, in making contemplation, does not then contemplate all dharmas? Are the three thousand that are created not all dharmas?" and so on. Further, refuting my meaning of sifting out form and contemplating the mind for fear the mind turns outward, he cited the gate of the nonduality of inner and outer, and raised the difficulty in terms of "relying on the dependent and principal, form and mind." He further said: "In Calming and Contemplation the initial mind pervasively contemplates the dharmas of the dependent and principal of the ten realms, the three thousand; within the three thousand, how could there be no form? Moreover, for one with perfect understanding, every mote of dust is the dharma-realm, everywhere is Vairocana." He also raised such texts as "form and scent are the middle way" and "all dharmas tend toward form" as difficulties.

Chinese Source

此豈非素來全不諳理具事造兩境之義，遂將所造諸法便為性德本具。

Ru-Yi Translation

Does this not show that from the beginning he has been wholly unfamiliar with the meaning of the two objects, inclusion in principle and creation in phenomena, and so takes the dharmas that are created to be what inherent virtue originally contains?

Chinese Source

又將外境難於內觀，此則與一家境觀頓爾相違。

Ru-Yi Translation

Further, he uses outer objects to raise difficulties against inner contemplation; this flatly contradicts the object and contemplation of our school.

Chinese Source

何者？《輔行》令但觀理具俱空假中，上人自觀事造。《大意》令於能造觀具界如，上人便將所造為三千。《止觀》令唯觀識心，上人自遍觀外境。況將所造為三千，此則變造方有非任運具，又須從心而生，安與一念非前非後？物之八相之喻便為徒設。

Ru-Yi Translation

Why? The Fuxing has one simply contemplate inclusion in principle as all empty, provisional, and middle; the Venerable on his own contemplates creation in phenomena. The Gist has one contemplate what creates as containing the realms and suchnesses; the Venerable then takes what is created as the three thousand. Calming and Contemplation has one contemplate only the consciousness-mind; the Venerable on his own pervasively contemplates outer objects. Moreover, if what is created is taken as the three thousand, then these exist only once transformed and created, and are not spontaneously contained; further, they must be produced from the mind — how then are they neither before nor after the single thought? The simile of the eight marks of a thing would be set out in vain.

Chinese Source

故知約識陰所造為三千妙境者，於止觀遠矣。

Ru-Yi Translation

Therefore we know that taking what is created by the aggregate of consciousness as the wondrous object of the three thousand is far from calming-and-contemplation.

Turning the Text to Hide the Fault

Chinese Source

蓋由自昔不知理具與事造，不分而分故也。及被問疑書，以二造徵之。上人迷情似改，略知理具與事造約義須分，又偶得造字通於具義，遂於答疑書內翻文諱罪。乃云：問疑書抑是揚非，枉於《辨訛》將心具便是外境。故特註所造三千云理具名造，實非事造。次文又云：言所造三千者，即是所具三千名造，實非外境事造也（上皆答疑書明文，收掌見在）。

Ru-Yi Translation

This is because from the past you did not know that inclusion in principle and creation in phenomena are divided without being divided. When you were met by the Letter Questioning Doubts, which challenged you with the two creations, the Venerable's deluded view seemed to change: you came to know roughly that inclusion in principle and creation in phenomena must be distinguished in meaning, and by chance you found that the character "create" extends to the meaning of "contain." So in the Letter Answering Doubts you turned the text around to conceal your fault, saying: "The Letter Questioning Doubts suppresses the right and praises the wrong, falsely [claiming] that Discerning Errors takes the mind's containing to be outer objects." Therefore you specially annotated "the three thousand that are created" as "inclusion in principle is called creating; it is truly not creation in phenomena." The next passage further says: "When I speak of the three thousand that are created, it is the three thousand that are contained that are called created; they are truly not outer objects created in phenomena." (The above is all the plain text of the Letter Answering Doubts; it is kept and is still extant.)

Chinese Source

豈非此時因難醒悟，遂欲攀附正義，轉其事造擬為理具，故云所具三千名造，實非事造外境等。

Ru-Yi Translation

Was it not that at this time, awakened by the difficulty, you wished to cling to the correct meaning, and turned your creation in phenomena around to pass it off as inclusion in principle? Therefore you said, "the three thousand that are contained are called created; they are truly not outer objects created in phenomena" and so on.

Chinese Source

及被詰難書取《辨訛》前後明文，驗其自前不知理具三千非外境事造，實不能分內外二境事理二造。故被難曰：若分二境，何故將外依正難內心觀法？若分二造，何故將內心具色破事造報色耶（以《扶宗》云：若不觀色，恐心外向，乃是未歷事造報色，非不觀內心理具妙色也）。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the Letter Pressing Difficulties took the plain texts of Discerning Errors, earlier and later, and verified that from the first you did not know that the three thousand included in principle are not outer objects created in phenomena, and truly could not distinguish the two objects, inner and outer, or the two creations, in phenomena and in principle. Therefore you were challenged: "If you distinguish the two objects, why do you use the outer dependent and principal to raise difficulties against the method of contemplating the inner mind? If you distinguish the two creations, why do you use the form contained by the inner mind to refute the form of retribution created in phenomena?" (Because Supporting the School says, "If one did not contemplate form, one fears the mind turns outward"; that is, one does not yet pass through the form of retribution created in phenomena — it is not that one does not contemplate the wondrous form included in principle in the inner mind.)

Chinese Source

上人既被詰難書將前後文驗之，顯是素來不知二造之義。灼然前書攀附正義，云理具三千實非事造。其過既大。又恐若順《輔行》分於二造，若順不二門分於二境，則《示珠指》及《辨訛》前後之文全然不當。故於今來還拋正義、却復邪宗，仍將事造便為理具，遂不分二造及以二境，乃令事理內外混沌而觀。此說全無所以，特違教文。

Ru-Yi Translation

Since the Venerable has been verified by the Letter Pressing Difficulties with his earlier and later texts, it is evident that from the beginning he did not know the meaning of the two creations. Clearly the earlier letter clung to the correct meaning, saying that the three thousand included in principle are truly not creation in phenomena. That fault was already great. Further, you feared that if you followed the Fuxing in distinguishing the two creations, or followed the Gates of Nonduality in distinguishing the two objects, then the earlier and later texts of Pointing to the Pearl and Discerning Errors would be wholly untenable. Therefore in what you have now sent you again throw away the correct meaning and return to the deviant thesis, once more taking creation in phenomena as inclusion in principle; so you do not distinguish the two creations or the two objects, and have one contemplate phenomena and principle, inner and outer, in a muddle. This account has no basis at all and specially goes against the texts of the teaching.

Chinese Source

若也事理內外混沌而觀，何故《輔行》令但觀理具？何故《四念處》專於內心觀一切法？何故《義例》約理觀心，唯達法性更無餘途；事觀則專照起心，四運推撿。《義例》事觀尚令專照起心，信是未涉外境，唯色之觀方歷外境。唯識之觀但歷能造十界心耳。事觀尚專內心，理觀因何便觀外境？若二造不分為正義者，何故答疑書特云：理具名造，實非事造。良為心無的解，隨時改轉。斯之邪說，壞亂本宗，迷瞑初學，其過莫大。當須忖量，無縱奸諂唯事改轉也。

Ru-Yi Translation

If phenomena and principle, inner and outer, are to be contemplated in a muddle, why does the Fuxing have one simply contemplate inclusion in principle? Why do the Four Foundations of Mindfulness contemplate all dharmas solely in the inner mind? Why do Guiding Principles of Calming and Contemplation [say that] contemplating the mind in terms of principle "only penetrates the dharma-nature, with no other route," while the contemplation of phenomena "solely illuminates the arising mind, examining it by the four movements"? Since Guiding Principles of Calming and Contemplation has even the contemplation of phenomena solely illuminate the arising mind, it truly does not yet involve outer objects; only the contemplation of form-only passes through outer objects. The contemplation of consciousness-only only passes through the mind that creates the ten realms. Since even the contemplation of phenomena is solely on the inner mind, why would the contemplation of principle then contemplate outer objects? If not distinguishing the two creations were the correct meaning, why did the Letter Answering Doubts specially say, "Inclusion in principle is called creating; it is truly not creation in phenomena"? It is truly because your mind has no definite understanding, and changes and shifts with the occasion. This deviant account destroys and disorders our own school and blinds beginning students; no fault is greater. You must weigh it, and not indulge in treachery and flattery, doing nothing but changing and shifting.

Chinese Source

云何將此無憑之解，欲廢觀心教文？如何將此偽妄之心，欲修止觀耶？事理二造既其不分，事理二觀因何而起？堅執須尋《止觀》，如此尋之有何所益？故知簡示陰識，觀具三千俱空假中方名理觀。既無此文，安得云純談理觀？既無理觀，附法觀心如何可廢？無在執迷自損，必須捨暗向明。速示報章，要知進否。

Ru-Yi Translation

How can you wish to abolish the teaching text on contemplating the mind with this baseless understanding? How can you wish to cultivate calming-and-contemplation with this false and deluded mind? Since you do not distinguish the two creations, in phenomena and in principle, from where do the two contemplations, of phenomena and of principle, arise? You firmly insist that one must look into Calming and Contemplation; looking into it like this, what benefit is there? Therefore we know that only sifting and showing aggregate-consciousness, and contemplating it as containing the three thousand, all empty, provisional, and middle, is called the contemplation of principle. Since [the ten sets of three dharmas] lack this text, how can they be said to purely discuss the contemplation of principle? Since they lack the contemplation of principle, how can the contemplation of the mind attached to dharmas be abolished? Do not persist in delusion and harm yourself; you must abandon darkness and turn toward light. Send your reply quickly; I wish to know whether you advance or not.

Ru-Yi Translation

Fifth: Not Grasping the Efficacy of the Contemplative Method

All Three Methods Can Be Cultivated

Within all being deluded, because mind is what creates, one is mostly made to contemplate the mind; because the mind-dharma is near and essential, one is mostly made to contemplate the mind; because, to refute the heavy attachment to form of the lower realms, one is mostly made to contemplate the mind — truly it is not because mind alone is principle that one is made to contemplate the mind. If one knows this intent, one will not insist that speaking of true principle is contemplating the mind.

The Principle Revealed by Contemplation in Terms of Practice?

Chinese Source

既知心法與佛法俱約於事，有高有下，說於佛法不是觀心。上人素不知此意，故累得說佛說理便為理觀。今因詰難，略知此意，故即便轉計：果佛之理是觀妄心所顯。故云：玄文所談，正是《止觀》約行觀心所顯之理。作此說者，意欲救於答疑書內一切三法若橫若竪並我一念罔不照知之義。又是欲救十種三法純談理觀。以上人今知理觀的是約行，故作此救也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Know, then, that the mind-dharma and the Buddha-dharma are both in terms of phenomena, high and low, and that speaking of the Buddha-dharma is not contemplating the mind. The Venerable never knew this intent, and so has repeatedly taken speaking of the Buddha and speaking of principle as the contemplation of principle. Now, because of the Letter Pressing Difficulties, you know this intent a little, and so at once shift your reckoning: the principle of the buddha at the fruit is what is revealed by contemplating the deluded mind. Therefore you say: "What the Profound text discusses is precisely the principle revealed by the contemplation of the mind in terms of practice of Calming and Contemplation." In saying this, you intend to rescue the meaning in the Letter Answering Doubts that all three dharmas, horizontal or vertical, together with my single thought, are all illuminated and known. You further intend to rescue the ten sets of three dharmas as purely discussing the contemplation of principle. Because the Venerable now knows that the contemplation of principle is indeed in terms of practice, you make this rescue.

Chinese Source

不思此說招過極多。何者？若將果佛十種法相入心修觀，自是附法觀心，何得却云是約行所顯？況復此玄正釋料揀十法，唯有所附之法，全無能附之觀。尚略無附法觀義，那有約行觀義？

Ru-Yi Translation

You do not consider that this account incurs very many faults. Why? If one takes the ten dharma-marks of the buddha at the fruit into the mind to cultivate contemplation, that is of itself the contemplation of the mind attached to dharmas; how can you on the contrary say it is revealed in terms of practice? Moreover, the proper explanation and the sorting and sifting of the ten dharmas in this Profound [Meaning] have only the dharmas attached to, and no contemplation that attaches at all. If there is not even a trace of the meaning of contemplation attached to dharmas, how could there be the meaning of contemplation in terms of practice?

Chinese Source

上人今來既省正釋料揀十法之文，殊無附法、約行兩觀之義，乃曲巧自立，令久修、始習二種行人自作兩種觀心，乃云：文中所談備該因果兼示能觀。誰當讀之不思己行，欲以自思己行便乃自修兩種觀心也。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Venerable, now having examined the texts of the proper explanation and the sorting and sifting of the ten dharmas, [found that they] have no meaning of either contemplation, attached to dharmas or in terms of practice, and so crookedly and cleverly set up on your own [the idea of] having two kinds of practitioners, those who have cultivated long and those beginning to practice, make two kinds of contemplation of the mind on their own, saying: "What is discussed in the text encompasses cause and fruit completely and also shows what contemplates; who, reading it, would not consider his own practice?" You wish, by "considering his own practice," to have [them] cultivate the two kinds of contemplation of the mind on their own.

Chinese Source

且自思己行之說，乃是策勸之語。大凡宗師解義，若遇諸聖行證之法，便須誡勸令見賢思齊。豈得此語便可救得純談理觀之失耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Now, the saying "considering one's own practice" is a word of exhortation. Generally, when masters of the school explain a meaning and encounter the methods of practice and realization of the sages, they must admonish and exhort, making [readers] see the worthy and think of becoming equal to them. How could these words rescue the error of "purely discussing the contemplation of principle"?

Chinese Source

若云十法該於因果，及示能觀便是行人自修兩觀，不用別立觀心一科者。

Ru-Yi Translation

Suppose you say: "The ten dharmas encompass cause and fruit and show what contemplates, so the practitioner cultivates the two contemplations on his own, and there is no need to set up separately the one section on contemplating the mind."

Chinese Source

只如四諦五行之文，還該因果？還示能觀否？且世出世因果豈逾四諦，修證體用豈過五行？況從偏小簡至圓實，又以二妙判開已訖，則已彰境觀圓融修證。《妙玄》此等法相，尚須攝入一念別示觀心一科。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then do the texts on the four truths and the five practices encompass cause and fruit, and show what contemplates, or not? Now, the causes and fruits of the worldly and the world-transcending — do they go beyond the four truths? The essence and function of cultivation and realization — do they go beyond the five practices? Moreover, [those texts] sift from the partial and small up to the perfect and real, and have already been judged and opened by the two wonders, which shows the perfect fusion of object and contemplation, cultivation and realization. Yet these dharma-marks of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] still had to be gathered into a single thought and given a separate section on contemplating the mind.

Chinese Source

若此玄從果至因而說，又自是釋迦能觀尚過菩薩修證，豈是初心境觀？何得方談果法即廢附法觀心耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Further, this Profound [Meaning] speaks from the fruit down to the cause, and moreover is Śākyamuni's own contemplation, which surpasses even the cultivation and realization of bodhisattvas; how could it be the object and contemplation of the initial mind? How can you, merely because it discusses the dharmas of the fruit, abolish the contemplation of the mind attached to dharmas?

Chinese Source

《妙玄》十種三法，一一三法皆從凡心一念辨至分真極果。上人尚云：須立觀心一科觀茲十法（答疑書明明說云：觀於十法，不可轉為觀於三教三軌等也）。彼之十法還該因果否？還示能觀否？因何更須觀心一科？此玄十法因何須廢觀心一科？

Ru-Yi Translation

The ten sets of three dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] each discern the three dharmas from a single thought of the ordinary mind up to a portion of the truth and the ultimate fruit. Yet the Venerable still says: "One must set up a section on contemplating the mind to contemplate these ten dharmas" (the Letter Answering Doubts plainly says: "Contemplate the ten dharmas; it may not be turned into contemplating the three teachings, the three tracks, and so on"). Do those ten dharmas encompass cause and fruit or not? Do they show what contemplates or not? Why do they further need a section on contemplating the mind? And why must the section on contemplating the mind be abolished for the ten dharmas of this Profound [Meaning]?

Chinese Source

況復兩種行人，自攝十種三法入心橫竪而照。寔是上人妄語，翻轉前言也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Moreover, [the claim that] the two kinds of practitioners on their own gather the ten sets of three dharmas into the mind and illuminate them horizontally and vertically is truly the Venerable's false speech, turning around his earlier words.

Chinese Source

何者？答疑書自云：以由玄文直顯心性故，論一切三法入心而照。乃結云：此玄所談非但法相圓融，亦乃理觀明白。既云玄文直顯，又云此玄所談，何得今來却是行人自顯、行人自觀？況復前書云純談理觀（若兼附法不名純談），次書云非但法相圓融亦乃理觀明白，豈非翻作正談附法、兼明理觀？則純談理觀自茲永破也。況復自立純談理觀，則正是約行觀心。被難無於陰境十乘，却云託事附法不以陰入為境。

Ru-Yi Translation

Why? The Letter Answering Doubts itself says: "Because the Profound text directly reveals the nature of mind, it discusses all three dharmas entering the mind and being illuminated." It then concludes: "What this Profound [Meaning] discusses is not only the perfect fusion of dharma-marks, but also the clarity of the contemplation of principle." Since it says "the Profound text directly reveals" and further says "what this Profound [Meaning] discusses," how can it now on the contrary be the practitioner revealing on his own and the practitioner contemplating on his own? Moreover, the earlier letter said "purely discusses the contemplation of principle" (if it also involved [contemplation] attached to dharmas, it would not be called "purely discusses"), and the next letter said "not only the perfect fusion of dharma-marks, but also the clarity of the contemplation of principle." Is this not turning it into properly discussing [contemplation] attached to dharmas while also clarifying the contemplation of principle? Then "purely discusses the contemplation of principle" is from this point forever refuted. Moreover, since you yourself set up "purely discusses the contemplation of principle," that is precisely the contemplation of the mind in terms of practice; when challenged that it lacks the object of the aggregates and the ten modes, you say on the contrary that relying on phenomena and attaching to dharmas do not take the aggregates and entrances as the object.

Chinese Source

如此之說，言無準繩，那堪評論教觀？極是不惜人身之者，亦避人嫌笑，未肯作此顛倒言談。況是傳教之人，那得至於斯耶？若欲盡書上人違心負口之過，直恐空費紙墨也。故且止之。幸請省己責躳，捨邪歸正，知過能改亦稱君子也。不可更延歲月，必須速降回音。

Ru-Yi Translation

Such talk has no plumb line; how can it bear to assess teaching and contemplation? Even one who cares nothing for his human body would still avoid people's scorn and ridicule and be unwilling to speak so topsy-turvily. How much more someone who transmits the teaching — how can it come to this? If I wished to write out fully the Venerable's faults of going against his own mind and betraying his own words, I fear I would only waste paper and ink; therefore I stop here for now. I hope you will examine yourself and reproach yourself, abandon the deviant and return to the correct; knowing one's fault and being able to reform is also called being a noble person. You must not prolong it further for years and months; you must quickly send down a reply.

Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

The two creations (units 2–13): "Creation has two meanings… what is created by the transformations of sages" and its conclusion "only because of inclusion in principle is there phenomenal function… spontaneously it gathers in what is manifested by the provisional and the real" are given as the Fuxing (T1912); Zhili notes in unit 2 that he summarizes the phenomenal half. The opening sentence was not found word for word in T1912, but "in the minds of sentient beings there are all tathāgatas sitting with legs crossed," "although the lower grounds contain cause and fruit, it is only inclusion in principle," and "what is created by the transformations of sages also makes sentient beings see with transformed minds" (units 13, 118; Part 3, unit 18) are in T1912. 理具 / 事造 / 理造 follow Part 1: "inclusion in principle / creation in phenomena / creation in principle."

The eight marks of a thing (unit 18): Refers to the simile in Calming and Contemplation, fascicle five, on the inconceivable object: the mind and the three thousand are like a thing and the eight marks that move it, neither before nor after the other. Zhili's point is that if the three thousand were only what the mind creates, they would come after the mind and the simile would no longer apply.

"Burn all six portions" (unit 11, 六分全燒): The phrase pairs with "cut every one of the thousand branches" and seems to mean cauterizing the whole body instead of the right point; the exact sense of 六分 is not clear. Rendered literally.

Quotations reused from the site and earlier drafts: "The difficulty of a light and restless mind" (unit 39) is a line of the confession in the Golden Light Sūtra (T663), worded as in the round-two draft of that sūtra; Zhili applies it to practitioners who ignore the method in front of them. Fasui's jibe "Your fan has fallen to the ground — what will you use to hide your shame?" (unit 81) is from the Separate Biography of Zhiyi (T2050), worded as in the round-two draft; there it mocks Huirong after he lost a debate with Zhiyi. "The kin of afflictions are the seed of the Tathāgata" (unit 96) follows Part 1 (the site's Fahua Xuanyi has "the very company of afflictions"). The Vajra Scalpel passages (units 64, 86, 106) follow the vault draft, with 真如 as "suchness," as in Part 1.

The Lotus Samādhi passages (unit 45): "If a person has not originally practiced sitting…" is in the Lotus Samādhi Repentance Rite (T1941). "If one cultivates according to this method and does not awaken…" was not found word for word in T1941. The parenthesis in unit 45 is Zhili's own note.

Pointing to the Pearl and Pointing to the Essentials (units 67, 88–90): Yuanqing's Pointing to the Pearl is lost; the quotations ("the single thought, numinous knowing…," "Buddha means true awakening…") survive only in Zhili's letters. "The meaning of the mind creating is still lacking, and the text 'without any difference' is lost forever" is in Zhili's Pointing to the Essentials (T1928).
