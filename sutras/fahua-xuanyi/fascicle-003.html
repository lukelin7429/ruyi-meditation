How the Six Objects Combine

Fascicle 3 opens by cross-referencing all six objects of contemplation from Fascicle 2 against one another — the ten suchnesses, the fourfold dependent origination, the fourfold Four Noble Truths, the sevenfold two truths, the fivefold three truths, and the one truth — showing that each is only a different name for the same underlying structure, closing the wondrous of the object. The fascicle then turns to the second of the ten wondrous aspects, the wondrous of wisdom: a catalogue of twenty kinds of wisdom, sorted, defined, and matched back against every one of the six objects in turn.

Status: in progress Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Source: Taishō T33, no. 1716 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)

Opening and Combining the Six Objects: The Twelve Links 諸境開合

Chinese Source

天台智者大師說

二、諸境開合者，先用十如為首。何者？此經命章絕言稱歎十如。今更說五境，云何同異耶？十二因緣與十如開合者，名異故言開，義同故言合。無明支合如是性。行支合如是相。識、名色、六入、觸、受合如是體。愛合如是緣。取合如是力、作。有合如是因。生、老死合如是果、報等(云云)。

Ru-Yi Translation

Expounded by the Great Master Zhiyi of Tiantai.

Second, the opening and combining of the various objects: first, taking the ten suchnesses as the head. Why is this? Because this present sūtra, in naming this section, singles out the ten suchnesses alone for its unqualified praise. Now, having gone on to teach five further objects, how do they agree and differ with the ten suchnesses? As for the opening and combining of the twelve links and the ten suchnesses: since their names differ, they are said to be "opened"; since their sense is the same, they are said to be "combined." The link of ignorance combines with "such a nature." The link of volitional formation combines with "such a mark." Consciousness, name-and-form, the six sense-fields, contact, and sensation combine with "such an essence." Craving combines with "such a condition." Grasping combines with "such a power" and "such an activity." Becoming combines with "such a cause." Birth, and old age and death, combine with "such a fruit" and "such a retribution," and the rest, and so on.

又總合者，如是相合行、有兩支。如是性合無明、愛、取三支。如是體合識、名色乃至老死七支。如是力還是煩惱道三支，無明、愛、取能生業力。如是作，還是行、有二支，能為苦作業也。如是因還是行、有二支，為七苦作因也。如是緣還是無明、愛、取三支，能潤業取苦也。如是果還是行、有之習果也。如是報還是行、有之業，招名色等報。此兩番通用思議十二因緣。合六道十如是。

Again, combining them differently: "such a mark" combines with the two links of volitional formation and becoming. "Such a nature" combines with the three links of ignorance, craving, and grasping. "Such an essence" combines with the seven links of consciousness, name-and-form, down through old age and death. "Such a power" is, again, the three links of the path of affliction — ignorance, craving, and grasping, able to generate the power of karma. "Such an activity" is, again, the two links of volitional formation and becoming, able to construct the karma that produces suffering. "Such a cause" is, again, the two links of volitional formation and becoming, serving as the cause of the seven kinds of suffering. "Such a condition" is, again, the three links of ignorance, craving, and grasping, able to moisten karma and draw forth suffering. "Such a fruit" is, again, the habituated fruit of volitional formation and becoming. "Such a retribution" is, again, the karma of volitional formation and becoming, summoning the retribution of name-and-form and the rest. These two rounds together, in general, apply to the conceivable twelve links, combined with the ten suchnesses of the six destinies.

次用不思議十二因緣合四聖十如者，無明支轉即變為明，明即了因，成聖人如是性。惡行支轉即變為善行，善行即緣因，成聖人如是相。識、名色等苦道轉，即法身，成聖人如是體。愛、取二支轉成聖人菩提心，即是如是力。有支含果，變成六度行，即成聖人如是作，亦轉成聖人如是因。此有支轉，有二種：正道轉成如是因，助道轉成聖人如是緣。老死支轉成法性常住，成聖人如是果、報(云云)。又，總作者，體、力、作三法秖是煩惱、業、苦，變成法身、菩提心、六度行等。勤習三法，在內成性，在外成相，正意成體，誓願深遠成力，立行成作，牽果成因，相助成緣，剋發成果、報(云云)。若細分四聖，節節有異，今取大槩，故通釋耳。經云：「一切智願，猶在不失。」二乘亦得作通釋也。

Next, using the inconceivable twelve links, combined with the ten suchnesses of the four kinds of sages: the link of ignorance, transformed, becomes clarity — clarity is precisely the revealing cause, forming the sage's own "such a nature." The link of evil action, transformed, becomes good action — good action is precisely the conditioning cause, forming the sage's own "such a mark." Consciousness, name-and-form, and the rest, the path of suffering transformed, become the Dharma-body, forming the sage's own "such an essence." The two links of craving and grasping, transformed, become the sage's own bodhi-mind — this is precisely "such a power." The link of becoming, containing its own fruit, transformed, becomes the practice of the six pāramitās, forming the sage's own "such an activity," and likewise transformed into the sage's own "such a cause." This transformation of the link of becoming is of two kinds: the correct path transformed becomes "such a cause," and the assisting path transformed becomes the sage's own "such a condition." The link of old age and death, transformed, becomes the eternal abiding of the dharma-nature, forming the sage's own "such a fruit" and "such a retribution," and so on. Again, taken all together: the three dharmas of essence, power, and activity are simply affliction, karma, and suffering, transformed into the Dharma-body, the bodhi-mind, the practice of the six pāramitās, and the rest. Diligently cultivating these three dharmas: internally, this forms the nature; externally, this forms the mark; correct intention forms the essence; the vow, deep and far-reaching, forms the power; establishing practice forms the activity; drawing forth the fruit forms the cause; mutual assistance forms the condition; and the eventual manifestation forms the fruit and the retribution, and so on. If the four kinds of sages were finely differentiated, each step would show its own particular differences — here, taking only the broad outline, a general explanation alone is given. The sūtra says: "The vow of omniscience remains present, never lost" — this general explanation may be applied to the two vehicles as well.

Reading note

This opens a long, densely combinatorial stretch (roughly the whole of the first section of Fascicle 3) cross-referencing all six objects introduced in Fascicle 2's "broad exposition of the object" against one another, two at a time, to show they all describe the same underlying reality under different names — "opened" where their names differ, "combined" where their sense is the same. Each subsequent pairing (dependent origination against the Four Noble Truths, the Four Noble Truths against the two truths, and so on) will follow this same pattern.

The Four Noble Truths Combined with the Ten Suchnesses and the Twelve Links

Chinese Source

四種四諦合十如者，生滅、無生兩種苦、集，是六道十如。如是相、如是性是集。如是體是苦。如是作、力、因、緣又是集。如是果、報又是苦(云云)。生滅、無生兩種道、滅，是析、體二乘及通菩薩十如。如是相、性即是道。如是體即是滅。如是力、作、因、緣皆是道。如是果、報又是滅。無量、無作兩種苦、集，即是四聖界外果報十如。集諦即是界外如是相、性、力、作、因、緣也。苦諦即是界外如是體、果、報等(云云)。無量、無作兩種道、滅，即是四聖界外涅槃十如。道諦即是涅槃性、相、力、作、因、緣等，亦是般若、解脫也。滅諦即是涅槃體、果、報等，亦成常住法身也(云云)。

Ru-Yi Translation

As for the four kinds of Four Noble Truths combined with the ten suchnesses: the arising-and-ceasing and non-arising kinds of suffering and its arising are the ten suchnesses of the six destinies. "Such a mark" and "such a nature" are the arising. "Such an essence" is suffering. "Such an activity," "such a power," "such a cause," and "such a condition" are, again, the arising. "Such a fruit" and "such a retribution" are, again, suffering, and so on. The arising-and-ceasing and non-arising kinds of the path and cessation are the ten suchnesses of the analytical and embodying two vehicles and the shared teaching's bodhisattva. "Such a mark" and "such a nature" are precisely the path. "Such an essence" is precisely cessation. "Such a power," "such an activity," "such a cause," and "such a condition" are all the path. "Such a fruit" and "such a retribution" are, again, cessation. The immeasurable and uncreated kinds of suffering and its arising are precisely the ten suchnesses of the four kinds of sages' fruit-retribution beyond the three realms. The truth of arising is precisely the mark, nature, power, activity, cause, and condition beyond the three realms. The truth of suffering is precisely the essence, fruit, and retribution beyond the three realms, and so on. The immeasurable and uncreated kinds of the path and cessation are precisely the ten suchnesses of the four kinds of sages' nirvāṇa beyond the three realms. The truth of the path is precisely nirvāṇa's own nature, mark, power, activity, cause, and condition, and the rest — this is also prajñā and liberation. The truth of cessation is precisely nirvāṇa's own essence, fruit, and retribution, and the rest — this also forms the eternally abiding Dharma-body, and so on.

四種四諦合四種十二因緣者，生滅、無生兩苦集，即是兩種思議十二因緣。生滅、無生兩種道滅，即是兩種思議十二因緣，無明滅乃至老死滅也。無量、無作兩苦集，即是兩不思議十二因緣也。無量、無作兩道滅，即是兩不思議十二因緣，無明滅乃至老死滅，此可解。

As for the four kinds of Four Noble Truths combined with the four kinds of twelve links: the arising-and-ceasing and non-arising kinds of suffering and its arising are precisely the two conceivable kinds of the twelve links. The arising-and-ceasing and non-arising kinds of the path and cessation are precisely the two conceivable kinds of the twelve links read as the extinguishing of ignorance down through the extinguishing of old age and death. The immeasurable and uncreated kinds of suffering and its arising are precisely the two inconceivable kinds of the twelve links. The immeasurable and uncreated kinds of the path and cessation are precisely the two inconceivable kinds of the twelve links read as the extinguishing of ignorance down through the extinguishing of old age and death — this may be understood accordingly.

The Seven Kinds of Two Truths Combined with the Ten Suchnesses, Twelve Links, and Four Noble Truths

Chinese Source

七種二諦合十如者，藏、通、別、圓入通凡四俗，皆是六道十如也。藏、通兩真是二乘十如也。別、圓入別兩俗：有邊是六道十如；無邊是二乘十如。圓俗此通九法界十如。別入通、圓入通、別、圓入別、圓，凡五種真，皆是佛法界十如也。

Ru-Yi Translation

As for the seven kinds of two truths combined with the ten suchnesses: the Tripiṭaka, shared, distinct, and perfect-entering-shared teachings' four conventional truths are all, alike, the ten suchnesses of the six destinies. The Tripiṭaka and shared teachings' two real truths are the ten suchnesses of the two vehicles. The distinct and perfect-entering-distinct teachings' two conventional truths: their "existent" side is the ten suchnesses of the six destinies; their "nonexistent" side is the ten suchnesses of the two vehicles. The perfect teaching's own conventional truth here extends generally through all nine dharma-realms. The distinct-entering-shared, perfect-entering-shared, distinct, perfect-entering-distinct, and perfect teachings' five real truths are all, alike, the ten suchnesses of the buddha-dharma-realm.

七種二諦合四種十二因緣者，藏、通、別、圓入通凡四俗，即是思議兩種十二因緣。藏、通兩真即是思議十二因緣，無明滅乃至老死滅也。別、圓入別兩俗：有邊是思議十二因緣；無邊是思議無明滅乃至老死滅。圓俗即通界內、外四種十二因緣也。別入通、圓入通、別、圓入別、圓，凡五種真，即是界外不思議十二因緣，無明滅乃至老死滅也。

As for the seven kinds of two truths combined with the four kinds of twelve links: the Tripiṭaka, shared, distinct, and perfect-entering-shared teachings' four conventional truths are precisely the two conceivable kinds of the twelve links. The Tripiṭaka and shared teachings' two real truths are precisely the conceivable twelve links read as the extinguishing of ignorance down through the extinguishing of old age and death. The distinct and perfect-entering-distinct teachings' two conventional truths: their "existent" side is the conceivable twelve links; their "nonexistent" side is the conceivable twelve links read as extinguishing. The perfect teaching's own conventional truth extends generally through all four kinds of the twelve links, within and beyond the three realms alike. The distinct-entering-shared, perfect-entering-shared, distinct, perfect-entering-distinct, and perfect teachings' five real truths are precisely the inconceivable twelve links beyond the three realms, read as the extinguishing of ignorance down through the extinguishing of old age and death.

七種二諦合四種四諦者，實有二諦，即生滅四諦也。幻有二諦，即無生四諦也。別入通、圓入通兩俗，還是無生苦集也。別入通真，是無量道滅也。圓入通真，是無作道滅也。別俗、圓入別俗，此是無量苦集。圓俗是無作苦集。別真是無量道滅。圓入別真、圓真，是無作道滅也。

As for the seven kinds of two truths combined with the four kinds of Four Noble Truths: the really-existent two truths are precisely the arising-and-ceasing Four Noble Truths. The illusorily-existent two truths are precisely the non-arising Four Noble Truths. The distinct-entering-shared and perfect-entering-shared teachings' two conventional truths are, again, the non-arising suffering and its arising. The distinct-entering-shared's real truth is the immeasurable path and cessation. The perfect-entering-shared's real truth is the uncreated path and cessation. The distinct teaching's conventional truth, and the perfect-entering-distinct's conventional truth, are the immeasurable suffering and its arising. The perfect teaching's own conventional truth is the uncreated suffering and its arising. The distinct teaching's real truth is the immeasurable path and cessation. The perfect-entering-distinct's real truth, and the perfect teaching's own real truth, are the uncreated path and cessation.

Reading note

This dense combinatorial stretch is easier to follow as a lattice than as prose: the seven kinds of two truths, the four kinds of Four Noble Truths, the four kinds of twelve links, and the five kinds of three truths (following below) all trace out the identical Tripiṭaka → shared → distinct → perfect progression, just measured by a different set of terms each time. Zhiyi's point in walking through every pairing explicitly, rather than asserting the equivalence once, is that a reader trained on any one of these six schemes should be able to locate their own understanding precisely within any of the others.

The Five Kinds of Three Truths Combined with the Other Objects

Chinese Source

五種三諦合十如者，別入通、圓入通兩俗，是六道十如。別俗、圓入別俗，有邊是六道十如；無邊是二乘十如。圓俗，意通九界(云云)。五種真諦皆是二乘、菩薩等十如；五種中諦皆是佛界十如也。

Ru-Yi Translation

As for the five kinds of three truths combined with the ten suchnesses: the distinct-entering-shared and perfect-entering-shared teachings' two conventional truths are the ten suchnesses of the six destinies. The distinct teaching's conventional truth and the perfect-entering-distinct's conventional truth: their "existent" side is the ten suchnesses of the six destinies; their "nonexistent" side is the ten suchnesses of the two vehicles. The perfect teaching's own conventional truth, in sense, extends generally through all nine realms, and so on. The five real truths are all, alike, the ten suchnesses of the two vehicles and bodhisattvas; the five middle truths are all, alike, the ten suchnesses of the buddha-realm.

五種三諦合四種十二因緣者，別入通、圓入通兩俗，是六道思議十二因緣。別、圓入別兩俗：有邊是思議六道十二因緣生；無邊是思議十二因緣滅。圓俗，義通(云云)。今且用是四種十二因緣，五種真諦即是思議十二因緣滅，亦即是不思議十二因緣生。五種中諦即是不思議十二因緣滅。

As for the five kinds of three truths combined with the four kinds of twelve links: the distinct-entering-shared and perfect-entering-shared teachings' two conventional truths are the conceivable twelve links of the six destinies. The distinct and perfect-entering-distinct teachings' two conventional truths: their "existent" side is the conceivable arising of the twelve links of the six destinies; their "nonexistent" side is the conceivable extinguishing of the twelve links. The perfect teaching's own conventional truth, in sense, extends generally, and so on. Here, taking up these four kinds of twelve links: the five real truths are precisely the conceivable extinguishing of the twelve links, and are likewise precisely the inconceivable arising of the twelve links. The five middle truths are precisely the inconceivable extinguishing of the twelve links.

五種三諦合四種四諦者，別入通、圓入通兩俗，即無生苦集也。別俗、圓入別俗、圓俗，通是無生之苦集，亦是無生之道滅，亦是無量之苦集也。別入通、圓入通兩真，本取但空邊，是無生道滅也。別真、圓入別真，即是無生之道滅，於無量是苦集。圓真，於無生是道滅，於無量、無作是苦集。別入通中，是無量道滅。圓入通中，是無作道滅。別中，是無量道滅。圓入別中，是無作道滅。圓中，正是無作道滅。

As for the five kinds of three truths combined with the four kinds of Four Noble Truths: the distinct-entering-shared and perfect-entering-shared teachings' two conventional truths are precisely the non-arising suffering and its arising. The distinct teaching's conventional truth, the perfect-entering-distinct's conventional truth, and the perfect teaching's own conventional truth are, in general, the non-arising suffering and its arising, and are also the non-arising path and cessation, and are also the immeasurable suffering and its arising. The distinct-entering-shared and perfect-entering-shared teachings' two real truths, taken originally from their bare-emptiness side, are the non-arising path and cessation. The distinct teaching's real truth, and the perfect-entering-distinct's real truth, are precisely the non-arising path and cessation, while, in terms of the immeasurable, they are suffering and its arising. The perfect teaching's own real truth, in terms of the non-arising, is the path and cessation, while, in terms of the immeasurable and the uncreated, it is suffering and its arising. The distinct-entering-shared's middle truth is the immeasurable path and cessation. The perfect-entering-shared's middle truth is the uncreated path and cessation. The distinct teaching's middle truth is the immeasurable path and cessation. The perfect-entering-distinct's middle truth is the uncreated path and cessation. The perfect teaching's own middle truth is precisely the uncreated path and cessation.

五種三諦合七種二諦者，簡前兩二諦不被合也。次、二種二諦，二俗，即是五種三諦家五種俗。二真，空邊即是五種三諦家真；不空邊即是五種三諦家中。後三種二諦，三俗，空邊即是五種三諦家真；有邊即是五種三諦家俗。三真，即是五種三諦家中。又作一種說，如後簡前二諦不被合，後五俗，有真、有俗；後五真，有真、有中。

As for the five kinds of three truths combined with the seven kinds of two truths: the first two kinds of two truths are set aside, not combined here. As for the next two kinds of two truths: their two conventional truths are precisely the five kinds of three truths' own five conventional truths. Of their two real truths, the bare-emptiness side is precisely the five kinds of three truths' own real truth; the not-empty side is precisely the five kinds of three truths' own middle truth. As for the last three kinds of two truths: their three conventional truths, on the empty side, are precisely the five kinds of three truths' own real truth; on the existent side, they are precisely the five kinds of three truths' own conventional truth. Their three real truths are precisely the five kinds of three truths' own middle truth. Stated in yet another way: just as the first two kinds of two truths are set aside and not combined, the latter five conventional truths contain both a real and a conventional aspect within them, and the latter five real truths contain both a real and a middle aspect within them.

The One Real Truth Combined with Every Other Object

Chinese Source

一實諦合十如者，一一法界皆具十界，簡却九界，但與佛法界同也。簡三種十二因緣，但與一種十二因緣滅同。簡三種四諦，但與一實四諦同。簡七種二諦，但與五真諦有同、不同。簡五種三諦，但與五中諦同(云云)。言無諦不可說者，合十如，如名不異，即是空寂。言辭相寂滅，不可說示，即是十種皆如義也。諸無明滅乃至老死滅，其義甚深，甚深即無諦同也。生生不可說，乃至不生不生不可說，即與無諦同也。七種真諦皆不可說。最初真諦不可說者，如身子云：「吾聞解脫之中，無有言說。」況後六耶？非生死、非涅槃，既非二邊亦無中道，即五種中諦與無諦同也。一實名虛空，虛空無一，云何有實？即無諦同也。無諦自無所存，平等大慧無若干也。雖無若干，若干無量，舒之充滿法界，不知從何而來。無量無若干，收之莫知所有，不知從何而去。不來不去，即是法佛(云云)。

Ru-Yi Translation

As for the one real truth combined with the ten suchnesses: each single dharma-realm already possesses all ten realms within it; setting aside the other nine, it is identical only with the buddha-dharma-realm. Setting aside the three kinds of twelve links, it is identical only with the single kind of the twelve links read as extinguishing. Setting aside the three kinds of Four Noble Truths, it is identical only with the one real truth's own Four Noble Truths. Setting aside the seven kinds of two truths, it is both identical and not identical with the five real truths. Setting aside the five kinds of three truths, it is identical only with the five middle truths, and so on. As for saying that the truth of no-truth cannot be spoken at all: combined with the ten suchnesses, since the name "thus" admits no distinction, this is itself empty quiescence; that "the marks of speech are themselves quiescent and extinct," unable to be shown or spoken of, is precisely the sense that all ten are, alike, "thus." That ignorance is extinguished, down through old age and death being extinguished — this sense is exceedingly deep, and this very depth is identical with the truth of no-truth. "Arising-arising cannot be spoken," down to "non-arising-non-arising cannot be spoken," is likewise identical with the truth of no-truth. All seven kinds of real truth cannot be spoken at all. That the very first real truth cannot be spoken is as Śāriputra says: "I have heard that, within liberation, there is nothing that can be spoken" — how much more so, then, the remaining six! Neither birth-and-death nor nirvāṇa, being neither of the two extremes, and likewise without any middle way either — this is precisely the five middle truths being identical with the truth of no-truth. The one real truth is named empty space; empty space has no "one" within it — how, then, could there be any "real" at all? This, too, is identical with the truth of no-truth. The truth of no-truth has, of itself, nothing that abides within it — the equal, great wisdom has nothing manifold about it at all. Yet, though without anything manifold, its manifold immeasurability, unfolded, fills the entire dharma-realm, and one cannot know from where it comes. Immeasurable and without anything manifold, gathered in, none can know what it possesses, and one cannot know to where it goes. Neither coming nor going — this is precisely the Dharma-buddha, and so on.

復次，七種二諦，赴緣開合，轉轉相入，一一又各有隨情、隨情智、隨智等。餘五義例亦應有，今不具載。何者？佛以一音演說法，眾生隨類各得解。自思之！

Moreover, the seven kinds of two truths, responding to conditions, opening and combining, turn and enter into one another in succession, and each single one, in turn, likewise carries its own senses of according with feeling, feeling-and-wisdom, and wisdom alone. The remaining five objects, by the same pattern, ought likewise to carry these senses — I do not set them all down here in full. Why is this? Because the Buddha, with a single voice, expounds the Dharma, and sentient beings, each according to their own kind, attain their own understanding accordingly. Reflect on this for yourself!

Reading note

This closes the entire opening-and-combining exercise across all six objects, ending, fittingly, with the one truth and the "no-truth" that both cancel and complete it — every scheme, pushed far enough, collapses into the same wordless equal wisdom "neither coming nor going." Zhiyi's closing line is a deliberate invitation rather than an evasion: having modeled the method exhaustively for the two truths, he leaves the reader to complete the same cross-referencing for the remaining five objects independently.

Why the Same Fused Principle Still Requires Many Teachings

Chinese Source

問：諸境理既融會，何意紛葩，更相拘入耶？答：如來觀知十界性相，有成熟者、未成熟者。大機未熟不令起謗；小機若熟不令失時。隨其所宜，應單應複，偏圓相入而成熟之，聞即得益。華嚴雖具鑒十界，兩界熟故，別、圓二種而成熟之。三藏亦鑒十界，二乘性相熟故，用生滅而成熟之。方等亦鑒十界，四界熟故，用四種相入而成熟之。般若亦鑒十界，亦四界熟故，用三種相入而成熟之。法華亦鑒十界，一性相熟，但一圓諦而成熟之。若無善巧方便，出沒調熟，云何境智而得融妙耶？譬如畫師，尚能淡入五彩作種種像，況佛法王於法自在，而不能種種間入調伏眾生耶！

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: Since the principle of all these objects is already fused together as one, why does it still appear so multifariously varied, each further restricting and entering into the others? Answer: The Tathāgata observes and knows the nature and mark of the ten realms, and finds among them those already ripened and those not yet ripened. For a great capacity not yet ripe, he does not let slander arise; for a small capacity, once ripe, he does not let the moment be lost. According with what is fitting for each, whether a single teaching or a compound one is called for, the partial and the perfect enter into one another and bring each capacity to ripeness — hearing it, the hearer at once gains benefit. The Avataṃsaka, though it, too, surveys all ten realms, ripens beings through the distinct and perfect teachings alone, since only these two realms are ripe at that time. The Tripiṭaka teaching, too, surveys all ten realms, ripening beings through the arising-and-ceasing scheme, since the two vehicles' own nature and mark are ripe. The Vaipulya period, too, surveys all ten realms, ripening beings through four kinds of mutual entering, since four realms are ripe. The Prajñā period, too, surveys all ten realms, ripening beings through three kinds of mutual entering, likewise since four realms are ripe. The Lotus, too, surveys all ten realms, ripening beings through the single, perfect truth alone, since a single nature and mark is ripe. If there were no skillful means, no appearing and vanishing to bring beings to ripeness, how could object and wisdom ever come to be fused into the wondrous? It is like a painter, who is able to blend the five colors together to produce every kind of image — how much more so, then, the Buddha, the Dharma-king, sovereign and at ease with respect to the Dharma, should be unable to interweave his teaching in every variety of way to tame and ripen sentient beings!

Reading note

This answers a question the whole preceding combinatorial exercise might naturally raise: if every scheme of objects ultimately says the same thing, why did the Buddha teach so many different, seemingly incompatible versions? Zhiyi's answer is pedagogical rather than metaphysical — the multiplicity tracks which of the ten dharma-realms are ripe for a given assembly at a given moment, not any real plurality in the underlying principle itself.

Where the Six Objects Appear in This Sūtra's Own Text

Chinese Source

問：上明六境等，此經聽可無名，有其義不？答：十如名義已備於前。四種十二因緣者，〈化城品〉明生滅十二緣。〈譬喻品〉但離虛妄，是不生十二緣。〈方便品〉云：「佛種從緣起」，是界外無量、無作兩種十二緣。四四諦者，〈譬喻品〉諸苦所因，貪欲為本，是生滅四諦。〈藥草喻品〉了達空法，是無生四諦。又云無上道，及〈方便品〉但說無上道，如來滅度等，是界外無量、無作兩種四諦也。十如差別是世諦。唯佛與佛乃能究盡諸法實相，即真諦也。〈安樂行〉云：「亦不分別有為、無為，實、不實法。」有是俗諦；無是真諦；「亦不分別」是遮二邊、顯中道。〈壽量〉云：「非如、非異」，「非異」非俗，「非如」非真，三諦義也。〈方便品〉云：「更以異方便，助顯第一義。」是一實諦也。又云：「唯此一事實」也。若言「說無分別法」，又「諸法寂滅相，不可以言宣」，是無諦義也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: As explained above, these six objects — might it be that this present sūtra permits their names to be absent, while their sense is still present? Answer: The name and sense of the ten suchnesses have already been fully treated above. As for the four kinds of the twelve links: the "Phantom City" chapter clarifies the arising-and-ceasing twelve links. "Parable," which says only to leave falsehood behind, is the non-arising twelve links. "Skillful Means" says: "The buddha-seed arises through conditions" — this is the two kinds of twelve links beyond the three realms, the immeasurable and the uncreated. As for the four kinds of the Four Noble Truths: "Parable," where all suffering's own cause has desire as its root, is the arising-and-ceasing Four Noble Truths. "Medicinal Herbs," fully understanding the dharma of emptiness, is the non-arising Four Noble Truths. Again, where it speaks of the unsurpassed path, and where "Skillful Means" speaks only of the unsurpassed path, of the Tathāgata's own extinction, and the rest, this is the two kinds of Four Noble Truths beyond the three realms, the immeasurable and the uncreated. The differentiation among the ten suchnesses is the conventional truth. "Only buddha together with buddha is able to fathom the true mark of all dharmas fully" is precisely the real truth. "Peaceful Practices" says: "Nor does he differentiate the conditioned and the unconditioned, the real and the unreal dharmas" — existence is the conventional truth; nonexistence is the real truth; "nor does he differentiate" screens out the two extremes and reveals the middle way. The "Lifespan" chapter says: "Neither identical nor different" — "not different" is not the conventional; "not identical" is not the real — this is the sense of the three truths. "Skillful Means" says: "Further employing other skillful means, I help to reveal the truth of ultimate meaning" — this is the one real truth. And again it says: "Only this one matter is real." Where it is said: "Teaching a dharma beyond all differentiation," and again: "All dharmas bear the mark of quiescent extinction, and cannot be proclaimed in words" — this is the sense of the truth of no-truth.

Reading note

This closes the wondrous of the object of contemplation (境妙) in its entirety — the very first, and by far the longest, of the ten wondrous aspects of the trace gate, spanning the whole of Fascicle 2's Lower Half plus this opening stretch of Fascicle 3. Zhiyi's method throughout this closing passage is consistent with the treatise's opening chapters: technical vocabulary that this sūtra itself never uses (the twelve links, the Four Noble Truths, the two/three truths by name) is shown to be already fully present in its own language, just under different words — the same move made for "wondrous" itself back in Fascicle 1.

The Wondrous of Wisdom: Twenty Kinds, Enumerated 智妙

Chinese Source

○第二、智妙者，至理玄微，非智莫顯；智能知所，非境不融。境既融妙，智亦稱之，其猶影響矣，故次境說智。智即為二：初、總論諸智，二、對境論智。總智為六：一、數；二、類；三、相；四、照；五、判；六、開。

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, the wondrous of wisdom: the ultimate principle is profound and subtle — without wisdom, it cannot be revealed; wisdom is able to know what it knows only in relation to its object — without the object, it cannot be fused into the wondrous. Since the object has already been fused into the wondrous, wisdom, too, corresponds to it, the two being like a shadow and its echo — hence, following the object, wisdom is discussed next. Wisdom itself has two parts: first, a general discussion of the various kinds of wisdom; second, discussing wisdom in relation to its object. The general discussion of wisdom has six parts: first, enumeration; second, classification; third, characterization; fourth, illumination; fifth, judgment; sixth, opening.

數者：一、世智。二、五停心四念處智。三、四善根智。四、四果智。五、支佛智。六、六度智。七、體法聲聞智。八、體法支佛智。九、體法菩薩入真方便智。十、體法菩薩出假智。十一、別教十信智。十二、三十心智。十三、十地智。十四、三藏佛智。十五、通教佛智。十六、別教佛智。十七、圓教五品弟子智。十八、六根清淨智。十九、初住至等覺智。二十、妙覺智。

As for enumeration: first, worldly wisdom. Second, the wisdom of the five methods for stilling the mind and the four applications of mindfulness. Third, the wisdom of the four good roots. Fourth, the wisdom of the four fruits. Fifth, the pratyekabuddha's wisdom. Sixth, the wisdom of the six pāramitās. Seventh, the śrāvaka's wisdom of embodying dharmas. Eighth, the pratyekabuddha's wisdom of embodying dharmas. Ninth, the bodhisattva's wisdom of embodying dharmas as a skillful means for entering the real. Tenth, the bodhisattva's wisdom of embodying dharmas in emerging into the provisional. Eleventh, the distinct teaching's wisdom of the ten stages of faith. Twelfth, the wisdom of the thirty preliminary minds. Thirteenth, the wisdom of the ten grounds. Fourteenth, the Tripiṭaka teaching's buddha-wisdom. Fifteenth, the shared teaching's buddha-wisdom. Sixteenth, the distinct teaching's buddha-wisdom. Seventeenth, the perfect teaching's wisdom of the disciples of the five ranks. Eighteenth, the wisdom of the purification of the six sense-faculties. Nineteenth, the wisdom from the initial abiding through to the stage of virtual enlightenment. Twentieth, the wisdom of subtle awakening.

二、類者，世智無道，邪計妄執，心行理外，不信不入，故為一。五停心、四念處，已入初賢佛法氣分，俱是外凡，故為一。四善根同是內凡，故為一。四果同見真，故為一。支佛別相觀能侵習，故為一。六度緣理智弱、緣事智強，故為一。通教方便聲聞體法智勝，故為一。支佛又小勝，故為一。通教菩薩入真方便智，四門遍學，故為一。通教出假菩薩智，正緣俗，故為一。別教十信智，先知中道，勝前劣後，故為一。別教三十心俱是內凡，故為一。十地同是聖智，故為一。三藏佛是師位名，勝三乘弟子，故為一。通教佛智，斷惑照機勝，故為一。別教佛智又勝，故為一。圓教五品弟子同具煩惱性，能知如來祕密之藏，故為一。六根清淨智隣真，故為一。初住至等覺同破無明，故為一。妙覺佛智無上最尊，故為一。如是等隨其類分相似者，或離、或合，判為二十(云云)。

Second, as for classification: worldly wisdom is without the path, false calculation and deluded attachment, mental activity operating entirely outside principle, without faith and without entry — hence it forms one class by itself. The five methods for stilling the mind and the four applications of mindfulness have already entered the initial worthy stage's own flavor of the buddha-dharma, and are both still outer ordinary beings — hence they form one class together. The four good roots are likewise both inner ordinary beings — hence they form one class. The four fruits alike see the real — hence they form one class. The pratyekabuddha's own particular-mark contemplation is able to encroach upon habituated tendencies — hence it forms one class by itself. The six pāramitās' wisdom regarding principle is weak, while its wisdom regarding phenomena is strong — hence it forms one class by itself. The shared teaching's skillful-means śrāvaka has a superior wisdom of embodying dharmas — hence it forms one class by itself. The pratyekabuddha, again, is slightly superior still — hence it forms one class by itself. The shared teaching's bodhisattva's wisdom, as a skillful means for entering the real, studies universally through all four gates — hence it forms one class by itself. The shared teaching's bodhisattva's wisdom in emerging into the provisional takes the conventional as its direct object — hence it forms one class by itself. The distinct teaching's wisdom of the ten stages of faith already knows the middle way beforehand, superior to what precedes it and inferior to what follows — hence it forms one class by itself. The distinct teaching's thirty preliminary minds are all, alike, inner ordinary beings — hence they form one class together. The ten grounds are alike sagely wisdom — hence they form one class together. The Tripiṭaka teaching's buddha-wisdom is the name for the teacher's own stage, superior to the three vehicles' disciples — hence it forms one class by itself. The shared teaching's buddha-wisdom, its severance of affliction and illumination of capacities superior — hence it forms one class by itself. The distinct teaching's buddha-wisdom, again superior still — hence it forms one class by itself. The perfect teaching's disciples of the five ranks alike still possess the nature of affliction, yet are able to know the Tathāgata's own secret treasury — hence they form one class together. The wisdom of the purification of the six sense-faculties draws near to the real — hence it forms one class by itself. From the initial abiding through to virtual enlightenment, all alike break through ignorance — hence they form one class together. The subtle-awakening buddha-wisdom is unsurpassed, the most venerable of all — hence it forms one class by itself. Grouping them in this way, according to their similarity of kind, some separated and some combined, they are judged, in total, as twenty, and so on.

Reading note

This twenty-item catalogue tracks the entire Buddhist path from ordinary worldly reasoning through the four teachings' own graded stages up to the buddha-wisdom of subtle awakening — the second of the ten wondrous aspects opens, like the first, with a full enumeration before any single item is characterized in depth below. "The four good roots" (warmth, summit, forbearance, the supreme worldly dharma) and the sequence of stages within each teaching were previously introduced across Fascicles 1–2's discussions of the six identities and the siddhāntas' attainment and use.
