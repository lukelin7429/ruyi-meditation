# Notes on the Textual Commentary on the Golden Light Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming jing wenju ji* (金光明經文句記, Taishō T39, no. 1786 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1786, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 觀心 | contemplation of mind | Zhili's fourth way of reading each passage, defended in Part 1. |
| 三身 | the three bodies | Dharma-, reward-, and response-bodies; treated at length in Parts 4, 6, and 9. |
| 懺悔 | repentance | The repentance chapter, Parts 5–6. |
| 二空 | the twofold emptiness | Of beings and of dharmas; Parts 8 and 12. |
