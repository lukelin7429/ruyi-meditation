# The Separate Biography of Great Master Zhizhe of Tiantai — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Sui Tiantai Zhizhe dashi biezhuan* (隋天台智者大師別傳, Taishō T50, no. 2050 (Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T2050, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Guanding died in 632; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 智者 | Zhizhe, "the Wise One" | The title given to Zhiyi by the Prince of Jin in 591. |
| 華頂 | Huading | The summit of Mount Tiantai where Zhiyi spent a night in solitary practice. |
| 放生池 | the pond for releasing life | Founded by Zhiyi on the coast near Mount Tiantai. |
