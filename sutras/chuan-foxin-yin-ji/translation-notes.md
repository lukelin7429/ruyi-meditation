# Notes on the Tiantai Transmission of the Buddha-Mind Seal — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Tiantai chuan foxin yin ji* (天台傳佛心印記, Taishō T46, no. 1938 (Huaize)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1938, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Huaize lived in the Yuan dynasty; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 性具 | inherent inclusion | The nature includes all dharmas, evil as well as good. |
| 性惡 | inherent evil | The Tiantai teaching that the nature includes evil as well as good; buddhas cut off cultivated evil, never inherent evil. |
| 理具 / 事造 | inclusion in principle / creation in phenomena | The three thousand as included in the nature and as created in actual events. |
| 三因佛性 | the three causes of buddha-nature | The correct cause, the conditioning cause, and the revealing cause. |
