Root of Joy and Superior Intent

Mañjuśrī now tells the story of his own past offense. In the time after the buddha Lion's Roar Drum-Sound King, two monks taught the Dharma. Root of Joy (喜根), plain and upright, did not praise few desires, contentment, or solitude, but taught people only the real mark of dharmas: that the nature of all dharmas is the nature of greed, anger, and delusion. Superior Intent (勝意) kept the precepts strictly, mastered the four dhyānas and four formless concentrations, and practiced the twelve ascetic observances; his disciples liked to find fault in others. Visiting a layman who was one of Root of Joy's disciples, Superior Intent praised austerity and criticized Root of Joy, only to be questioned and confounded by the layman. In anger, he denounced Root of Joy before the whole assembly. Root of Joy answered with a long series of verses — among them the famous lines "Greed is nirvāṇa; / anger and delusion are likewise" — and as they were spoken, the earth split and Superior Intent fell into the great hells, suffering for countless eons afterward. Superior Intent, Mañjuśrī reveals, was he himself. The sūtra closes with the merit of hearing it, its entrustment for the latter age, and its name.

Root of Joy

Chinese Source

「爾時有菩薩比丘名曰喜根，時為法師，質直端正，不壞威儀不捨世法。爾時眾生普皆利根樂聞深論。其喜根法師於眾人前，不稱讚少欲知足細行獨處，但教眾人諸法實相，所謂一切法性即貪欲之性，貪欲性即是諸法性，瞋恚性即是諸法性，愚癡性即是諸法性。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time there was a bodhisattva monk named Root of Joy, who was then a Dharma teacher, plain, upright, and correct; he did not break deportment and did not abandon worldly ways. At that time beings all had sharp faculties and liked to hear profound discussion. The Dharma teacher Root of Joy, before the people, did not praise few desires, contentment, refined conduct, or dwelling alone; he only taught the people the real mark of dharmas, namely: the nature of all dharmas is the nature of greed; the nature of greed is the nature of all dharmas; the nature of anger is the nature of all dharmas; the nature of delusion is the nature of all dharmas.

Chinese Source

其喜根法師以是方便教化眾生，眾生所行皆是一相各不相是非，所行之道心無瞋癡，以無瞋礙因緣故疾得法忍，於佛法中決定不壞。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Dharma teacher Root of Joy transformed beings by this skillful means. What beings practiced was all one mark; they did not judge one another right or wrong; on the path they practiced, their minds were without anger or delusion; and because of being without anger or obstruction, they quickly attained the patient acceptance of the Dharma and were settled and unshakable in the Buddha-dharma.

Superior Intent Visits a Layman

Chinese Source

「世尊！爾時復有比丘法師行菩薩道，名曰勝意。其勝意比丘護持禁戒，得四禪、四無色定，行十二頭陀。世尊！是勝意比丘有諸弟子，其心輕動樂見他過。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One, at that time there was also a monk, a Dharma teacher practicing the bodhisattva path, named Superior Intent. The monk Superior Intent guarded and kept the precepts, attained the four dhyānas and the four formless concentrations, and practiced the twelve ascetic observances. World-Honored One, this monk Superior Intent had disciples whose minds were frivolous and restless and who liked to see the faults of others.

Chinese Source

世尊！後於一時，勝意菩薩入聚落乞食，誤到喜根弟子家，見舍主居士子，即到其所敷座而坐，為居士子稱讚少欲知足細行，說無利語過，讚歎遠眾樂獨行者，又於居士子前說喜根法師過失：『是比丘不實，以邪見道教化眾生，是雜行者，說婬欲無障礙、瞋恚無障礙、愚癡無障礙，一切諸法皆無障礙。』

Ru-Yi Translation

World-Honored One, later, at one time, the bodhisattva Superior Intent entered a village to beg for food and by mistake came to the house of a disciple of Root of Joy. Seeing the householder's son, the master of the house, he went to him, had a seat spread, and sat down. For the householder's son he praised few desires, contentment, and refined conduct, spoke of the faults of useless talk, and extolled those who stay far from crowds and delight in practicing alone. And before the householder's son he spoke of the faults of the Dharma teacher Root of Joy: 'This monk is not genuine. He transforms beings with a path of wrong views. He is one of mixed practice. He says that sexual desire is no obstruction, anger is no obstruction, delusion is no obstruction, that all dharmas are without obstruction.'

The householder's son said: 'Venerable one,

Ru-Yi Translation

Chinese Source

若貪欲不在內不在外，不在東西南北四維上下十方者，即是無生；若無生者，云何言若垢若淨？』

Superior Intent Denounces Root of Joy

Chinese Source

「爾時勝意比丘瞋恚不喜，從座起去作如是言：『是喜根比丘以妄語法多惑眾人。』是人以不學入音聲法門故，聞佛音聲則喜，聞外道音聲則瞋；於梵行音聲則喜，於非梵行音聲則瞋。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Then the monk Superior Intent, angry and displeased, rose from his seat and left, saying: 'This monk Root of Joy deludes many people with a dharma of false speech.' Because this man had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, when he heard the sound of the Buddha he was glad, and when he heard the sound of non-Buddhists he was angry; at the sound of pure conduct he was glad, and at the sound of impure conduct he was angry.

Chinese Source

以不學入音聲法門故，於淨音聲則喜，於垢音聲則瞋。以不學入音聲法門故，於聖道音聲則喜，於凡夫音聲則礙。以不學入音聲法門故，於樂音聲則喜，於苦音聲則礙。

Ru-Yi Translation

Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, at pure sounds he was glad and at defiled sounds he was angry. Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, at the sound of the noble path he was glad and by the sound of ordinary people he was obstructed. Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, at the sound of pleasure he was glad and by the sound of suffering he was obstructed.

Chinese Source

以不學入音聲法門故，於出家音聲則喜，於在家音聲則礙。以不學入音聲法門故，於出世間音聲則喜，於世間音聲則礙。以不學入音聲法門故，於布施則生利想，於慳則生礙想。以不學佛法故。於持戒則生利想，於毀戒則生礙想。以不學佛法故。

Ru-Yi Translation

Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, at the sound of leaving home he was glad and by the sound of staying at home he was obstructed. Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, at world-transcending sounds he was glad and by worldly sounds he was obstructed. Because he had not learned the dharma-gate of entering sounds, toward giving he gave rise to the notion of benefit and toward stinginess the notion of obstruction — because he had not learned the Buddha-dharma. Toward keeping precepts he gave rise to the notion of benefit and toward breaking precepts the notion of obstruction — because he had not learned the Buddha-dharma.

Chinese Source

是時勝意比丘，出其舍已還到所止，眾僧中見喜根菩薩，語眾人言：『是比丘多以虛妄邪見教化眾生，所謂婬欲非障礙、瞋恚非障礙、愚癡非障礙，一切法非障礙。』

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the monk Superior Intent, having left that house, returned to where he was staying. Seeing the bodhisattva Root of Joy among the assembly of monks, he said to everyone: 'This monk transforms beings mostly with false and wrong views, namely, that sexual desire is not an obstruction, anger is not an obstruction, delusion is not an obstruction, all dharmas are not obstructions.'

Chinese Source

「爾時喜根菩薩作是念：『是比丘今者必當起於障礙罪業，我今當為說如是深法，乃至令作修助菩提道法因緣。』爾時喜根菩薩於眾僧前，說是諸偈：

Ru-Yi Translation

"Then the bodhisattva Root of Joy thought: 'This monk will certainly now commit the offense of obstruction. I will now teach him this profound Dharma, so that it may at least become a cause and condition for cultivating the dharmas that aid the path of bodhi.' Then, before the assembly of monks, the bodhisattva Root of Joy spoke these verses:

The Verses of Root of Joy

Chinese Source

「『貪欲是涅槃，　恚癡亦如是，如此三事中，　有無量佛道。

Ru-Yi Translation

"'Greed is nirvāṇa; / anger and delusion are likewise. / Within these three things / are measureless Buddha-paths.

Chinese Source

若有人分別，　貪欲瞋恚癡，是人去佛遠，　譬如天與地。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone discriminates / greed, anger, and delusion, / that person is far from the Buddha, / as far as heaven from earth.

Chinese Source

菩提與貪欲，　是一而非二，皆入一法門，　平等無有異。

Ru-Yi Translation

Bodhi and greed / are one and not two; / both enter one dharma-gate, / equal, without difference.

Chinese Source

凡夫聞怖畏，　去佛道甚遠，貪欲不生滅，　不能令心惱。

Ru-Yi Translation

Ordinary people hear this and fear; / they are very far from the Buddha-path. / Greed neither arises nor ceases; / it cannot distress the mind.

Chinese Source

若人有我心，　及有得見者，是人為貪欲，　將入於地獄。

Ru-Yi Translation

Whoever has a mind of self / and a view that there is attainment — / that person, by greed, / will be led into hell.

Chinese Source

貪欲之實性，　即是佛法性，佛法之實性，　亦是貪欲性，是二法一相，　所謂是無相，若能如是知，　則為世間導。

Ru-Yi Translation

The real nature of greed / is the nature of the Buddha-dharma; / the real nature of the Buddha-dharma / is also the nature of greed. / These two dharmas are one mark, / namely, the markless. / If one can know it thus, / one is a guide for the world.

Chinese Source

若有人分別，　是持戒毀戒，以持戒狂故，　輕蔑於他人，是人無菩提，　亦無有佛法，但自安住立，　有所得見中。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone discriminates / "this is keeping precepts, this is breaking them," / and, mad with keeping precepts, / looks down on others, / that person has no bodhi / and has no Buddha-dharma; / he only settles and stands / within the view that there is attainment.

Chinese Source

若住空閑處，　自貴而賤人，尚不得生天，　何況於菩提？

Ru-Yi Translation

If one dwells in an empty, quiet place, / prizing oneself and despising others, / one cannot even be born in heaven — / how much less attain bodhi?

Chinese Source

皆由著空閑，　住於邪見故，邪見與菩提，　皆等無有異。

Ru-Yi Translation

It is all from clinging to empty quiet / and abiding in wrong views. / Wrong views and bodhi / are equal, without difference.

Chinese Source

但以名字數，　語言故別異，若人通達此，　則為近菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

Only by names and numbers, / by words, are they different. / If someone penetrates this, / he is near to bodhi.

Chinese Source

分別煩惱垢，　即是著淨見，無菩提佛法，　住有得見中。

Ru-Yi Translation

To discriminate the defilement of afflictions / is to cling to a view of purity; / it has no bodhi or Buddha-dharma; / it abides in the view that there is attainment.

Chinese Source

若貪著佛法，　是則遠佛法，貪無礙法故，　則還受苦惱。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one clings greedily to the Buddha-dharma, / one is far from the Buddha-dharma; / greedy for the dharma of non-obstruction, / one again receives suffering and distress.

Chinese Source

若人無分別，　貪欲瞋恚癡，入三毒性故，　則為見菩提，是人近佛道，　疾得無生忍。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone does not discriminate / greed, anger, and delusion, / because he enters the nature of the three poisons, / he sees bodhi. / That person is near the Buddha-path / and quickly attains the patient acceptance of the unarisen.

Chinese Source

若見有為法，　與無為法異，是人終不得，　脫於有為法，若知二性同，　必為人中尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one sees conditioned dharmas / as different from the unconditioned, / that person will never / be freed from conditioned dharmas. / If one knows the two natures are the same, / he will surely be honored among humans.

Chinese Source

佛不見菩提，　亦不見佛法，不著諸法故，　降魔成佛道。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha does not see bodhi, / nor does he see the Buddha-dharma; / because he does not cling to dharmas, / he subdues Māra and attains the Buddha-path.

Chinese Source

若欲度眾生，　勿分別其性，一切諸眾生，　皆同於涅槃，若能如是見，　是則得成佛。

Ru-Yi Translation

If you wish to deliver beings, / do not discriminate their nature. / All beings / are the same as nirvāṇa. / If one can see it thus, / one attains buddhahood.

Chinese Source

其心不閑寂，　而現閑靜相，是於天人中，　則為是大賊，是人無菩提，　亦無有佛法。

Ru-Yi Translation

One whose mind is not quiet and still / yet displays the appearance of quiet stillness / is, among gods and humans, / a great thief. / That person has no bodhi / and has no Buddha-dharma.

Chinese Source

若作如是願，　我當得作佛，如是之凡夫，　無明力所牽。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one makes such a vow: / "I shall become a buddha," / such an ordinary person / is dragged by the power of ignorance.

Chinese Source

佛法湛清淨，　其喻如虛空，此中無可取，　亦無有可捨。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha-dharma is clear and pure; / its simile is empty space. / Within it there is nothing to grasp / and nothing to let go.

Chinese Source

佛不得佛道，　亦不度眾生，凡夫強分別，　作佛度眾生，是人於佛法，　則為甚大遠。

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha does not attain the Buddha-path, / nor does he deliver beings. / Ordinary people forcibly discriminate, / "becoming a buddha, delivering beings"; / such a person, from the Buddha-dharma, / is very far indeed.

Chinese Source

若見眾生苦，　則是受苦者，眾生無眾生，　而說有眾生，住眾生相中，　則無有菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one sees the suffering of beings, / one is oneself a sufferer. / Beings are without beings, / yet one says there are beings; / abiding in the mark of beings, / one has no bodhi.

Chinese Source

若人見眾生，　是畢竟解脫，無有婬恚癡，　知是為世將。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone sees beings / as ultimately liberated, / without lust, anger, or delusion, / know that he is a leader of the world.

Chinese Source

若人見眾生，　不見非眾生，不得佛法實，　佛同眾生性，若能如是知，　則為世間將。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone sees beings / and does not see non-beings, / does not find the reality of the Buddha-dharma — / the Buddha is the same in nature as beings — / if one can know it thus, / one is a leader of the world.

Chinese Source

若人欲成佛，　莫壞貪欲性，貪欲性即是，　諸佛之功德。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone wishes to become a buddha, / let him not destroy the nature of greed. / The nature of greed is itself / the merit of the buddhas.

Chinese Source

若人欲發心，　隨順菩提道，莫自有分別，　心異於菩提，發心即菩提，　知是為世將。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone wishes to give rise to the resolve / and accord with the path of bodhi, / let him not discriminate for himself / the mind as different from bodhi. / Giving rise to the resolve is itself bodhi; / know that he is a leader of the world.

Chinese Source

若說外道惡，　稱佛世中尊，是二說非異，　知是為世將。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one speaks of non-Buddhists as evil / and calls the Buddha honored in the world, / these two statements are not different — / know [who knows this] as a leader of the world.

Chinese Source

若人求菩提，　是人無菩提，若見菩提相，　是則遠菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone seeks bodhi, / that person has no bodhi; / if one sees a mark of bodhi, / one is far from bodhi.

Chinese Source

菩提非菩提，　佛以及非佛，若知是一相，　是為世間導。

Ru-Yi Translation

Bodhi and non-bodhi, / buddha and non-buddha — / if one knows these are one mark, / one is a guide for the world.

Chinese Source

若人作是念，　我當度眾生，即著眾生相，　是人無菩提，亦無有佛法，　住於有見中。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone thinks, / "I shall deliver beings," / he clings to the mark of beings; / that person has no bodhi / and has no Buddha-dharma; / he abides in the view of existence.

Chinese Source

貪欲無內外，　亦不在諸方，分別是空法，　凡夫為所燒，如幻如焰響，　如夢石女兒，諸煩惱如是，　決定不可得。

Ru-Yi Translation

Greed has no inside or outside, / nor is it in any direction. / Discriminating this empty dharma, / ordinary people are burned by it. / Like an illusion, a mirage, an echo, / a dream, a barren woman's child — / all afflictions are like this: / certainly they cannot be found.

Chinese Source

不知是空故，　凡夫為狂惑，若求煩惱性，　煩惱即是道。

Ru-Yi Translation

Because they do not know this is empty, / ordinary people are crazed and deluded. / If one seeks the nature of afflictions, / afflictions are themselves the path.

Chinese Source

若有人分別，　是道是非道，是人終不得，　無分別菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone discriminates / "this is the path, this is not the path," / that person will never attain / bodhi without discrimination.

Chinese Source

凡夫畏佛法，　去佛法甚遠，若不疑空法，　是人得菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

Ordinary people fear the Buddha-dharma / and are very far from the Buddha-dharma. / If one does not doubt the dharma of emptiness, / that person attains bodhi.

Chinese Source

一切有為法，　即是無為法，是數不可得，　無數故無為。

Ru-Yi Translation

All conditioned dharmas / are themselves unconditioned dharmas. / These numbers cannot be found; / because they are without number, they are unconditioned.

Chinese Source

若以菩提心，　自高無所畏，自念當作佛，　是人無菩提，亦無有佛法，　離菩提寶印。

Ru-Yi Translation

If, with the mind of bodhi, / one exalts oneself and fears nothing, / thinking, "I shall become a buddha," / that person has no bodhi / and has no Buddha-dharma; / he is apart from the jewel seal of bodhi.

Chinese Source

若有但誦經，　憶想作分別，不深思義趣，　但為貪名利，自念當作佛，　必成無有疑，唯貪於名利，　讀經住閑靜，分別少欲行，　還為貪心牽。

Ru-Yi Translation

If someone merely recites sūtras, / making discriminations with recollective thought, / not reflecting deeply on their meaning, / only greedy for fame and gain, / thinking, "I shall become a buddha; / I will surely succeed, without doubt" — / only greedy for fame and gain, / he reads sūtras and dwells in quiet stillness, / discriminating the practice of few desires, / and is dragged back by the greedy mind.

Chinese Source

若欲捨遠貪，　不得遠於貪，若達貪實法，　是人能離貪。

Ru-Yi Translation

If one wishes to give up and distance oneself from greed, / one cannot get away from greed. / If one penetrates the real dharma of greed, / that person can be free of greed.

Chinese Source

不得法實際，　雖長夜持戒，得諸無礙禪，　不入佛法味。

Ru-Yi Translation

Not attaining the limit of reality of dharmas, / even if one keeps the precepts through the long night / and attains the unobstructed dhyānas, / one does not enter the flavor of the Buddha-dharma.

Chinese Source

知法無有性，　不壞一切法，不言戒非戒，　得脫有見中。

Ru-Yi Translation

Knowing that dharmas have no nature, / not destroying any dharma, / not speaking of precepts or non-precepts, / one is freed from the view of existence.

Chinese Source

以無持戒性，　知於持戒法，如是知戒相，　終不毀於戒。

Ru-Yi Translation

By the absence of a nature of keeping precepts / one knows the dharma of keeping precepts; / knowing the mark of the precepts in this way, / one never breaks the precepts.

Chinese Source

諸佛之法王，　法藏叵思議，無量方便力，　引導諸眾生，以一相法門，　令入寂滅道。

Ru-Yi Translation

The buddhas, kings of the Dharma, / have a Dharma-treasury beyond conception; / with the power of measureless skillful means / they lead all beings, / and by the dharma-gate of one mark / they bring them into the path of quiescence.

Chinese Source

凡夫聞佛說，　無我無有法，一相自性空，　不信墮深坑。

Ru-Yi Translation

Ordinary people hear the Buddha say / there is no self and there are no dharmas, / one mark, empty in self-nature; / not believing, they fall into a deep pit.

Chinese Source

雖白衣受欲，　聞是法不畏，勝於頭陀者，　住在有見中。

Ru-Yi Translation

Even a white-robed layperson who indulges in desires, / if he hears this Dharma and is not afraid, / surpasses an ascetic / who abides in the view of existence.

Chinese Source

現在十方佛，　利益諸世間，知法如虛空，　皆以得菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

The present buddhas of the ten directions / benefit all worlds; / knowing dharmas as like empty space, / they have all thereby attained bodhi.

Chinese Source

若有無智者，　樂於分別法，聞是實法者，　則生疑怖畏，是人無量劫，　備受諸苦分。』

Ru-Yi Translation

If there are those without wisdom / who delight in discriminating dharmas, / when they hear this real Dharma / they give rise to doubt and fear; / such people, for measureless eons, / undergo every share of suffering.'

Superior Intent Falls into Hell

Chinese Source

「說是諸偈法時，三萬諸天子得無生法忍，萬八千人漏盡解脫。即時地裂，勝意比丘墮大地獄。以是業障罪因緣故，百千億那由他劫，於大地獄受諸苦毒；

Ru-Yi Translation

"When these verses of the Dharma were spoken, thirty thousand gods attained the patient acceptance of the unarisen dharma, and eighteen thousand people exhausted their outflows and were liberated. At that moment the earth split open, and the monk Superior Intent fell into the great hell. Because of the causes and conditions of this offense of karmic obstruction, for hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of eons he suffered every torment in the great hell.

Chinese Source

從地獄出，七十四萬世常被誹謗，若干百千劫乃至不聞佛之名字；自是已後還得值佛，出家學道而無志樂，於六十三萬世常反道入俗；亦以業障餘罪故，於若干百千世諸根闇鈍。

Ru-Yi Translation

After coming out of hell, for seven hundred forty thousand lives he was always slandered; for so many hundreds of thousands of eons he did not even hear the name of a buddha. After that he again met buddhas, left home, and studied the path, but without resolve or delight; for six hundred thirty thousand lives he always abandoned the path and returned to lay life. And because of the remaining offense of karmic obstruction, for so many hundreds of thousands of lives his faculties were dull and dim.

Chinese Source

世尊！爾時喜根法師，於今東方過十萬億佛土，有國名寶莊嚴，於中得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，號勝日光明威德王如來、應供、正遍知，於今現在。其勝意比丘，今我身是。

Ru-Yi Translation

World-Honored One, the Dharma teacher Root of Joy of that time has now, beyond a hundred thousand koṭis of buddha-lands to the east, in a land named Jewel Adornment, attained anuttarā samyaksaṃbodhi; he is named King of Majestic Virtue of Superior Sunlight, the Tathāgata, the Worthy of Offerings, the Perfectly Enlightened, and is present there now. The monk Superior Intent was I myself.

Chinese Source

世尊！我未入如是法相門時，受如是苦分別苦顛倒苦，是故若發菩提心者、若發小乘心者，不欲起如是業障罪、不欲受如是苦惱者，不應拒逆佛法，無有處所可生瞋礙。」

Ru-Yi Translation

World-Honored One, before I entered such a gate of the marks of dharmas, I underwent such suffering — the suffering of discrimination, the suffering of inversion. Therefore whoever has given rise to the mind of bodhi, or to the mind of the lesser vehicle, and does not wish to commit such an offense of karmic obstruction or to undergo such suffering and distress, should not resist or oppose the Buddha-dharma; there is no place where anger and obstruction can properly arise."

The Merit of Hearing This Sūtra

Chinese Source

佛告文殊師利：「汝聞是諸偈得何等利？」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Mañjuśrī: "What benefit did you obtain from hearing these verses?"

Chinese Source

「世尊！我畢是業障罪已，聞是偈因緣故，在所生處利根智慧，得深法忍、得決定忍，巧說深法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One, after I had finished [paying for] this offense of karmic obstruction, because of the causes and conditions of having heard these verses, wherever I was born I had sharp faculties and wisdom; I attained the profound patient acceptance of the Dharma, attained settled patience, and skillfully taught the profound Dharma."

Chinese Source

「文殊師利！為誰力故能憶如是無量阿僧祇劫罪業因緣？」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Mañjuśrī, by whose power are you able to remember the causes and conditions of offenses and karma over such countless asaṃkhyeya eons?"

Chinese Source

「世尊！諸菩薩有所念、有所說、有所思惟，皆是佛之神力。所以者何？一切諸法皆從佛出。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One, whatever bodhisattvas recollect, say, or reflect on is all the spiritual power of the Buddha. Why? All dharmas come forth from the Buddha."

Chinese Source

佛告文殊師利：「若得佛十力，若有聞是經者，等無有異；若得無生法忍，聞是經者亦等無異。」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Mañjuśrī: "Attaining the Buddha's ten powers and hearing this sūtra are equal, without difference; attaining the patient acceptance of the unarisen dharma and hearing this sūtra are also equal, without difference."

Chinese Source

文殊師利言：「如我知佛所說義，聞此經者得無量不可思議功德之利。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Mañjuśrī said: "As I understand the meaning of what the Buddha has said, those who hear this sūtra obtain the benefit of measureless, inconceivable merit."

Chinese Source

「文殊師利！如是如是！聞是經得無量不可思議功德之利，但佛不廣說。何以故？不修道不精進者，如是惡人聞說是利則不能信受。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Mañjuśrī, so it is, so it is! Hearing this sūtra one obtains the benefit of measureless, inconceivable merit, but the Buddha does not explain it at length. Why? Those who do not cultivate the path and are not diligent — such bad people, hearing this benefit described, could not believe and accept it."

Entrustment and Name

Chinese Source

爾時文殊師利法王子及彌勒菩薩白佛言：「世尊！護念是經於未來世後五百歲，當令此經普宣流布皆得受持，魔若魔天不得其便。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the Dharma-prince Mañjuśrī and the bodhisattva Maitreya said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One, protect and keep this sūtra in mind in the future age, in the last five hundred years, so that this sūtra may be proclaimed and spread everywhere and all may receive and keep it, and Māra and the gods of Māra may find no opening."

Chinese Source

爾時佛欲護念是法故左右顧視，即時十方恒河沙無量國土六種震動，如是則為護念是經，及十方恒河沙諸佛亦護念是經。說是經時十方國土中恒河沙等無量眾生得無生法忍，何況得聲聞無學者，何況住學地者。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then, wishing to protect and keep this Dharma in mind, the Buddha looked to the left and right; at once measureless lands, as many as the sands of the Ganges in the ten directions, shook in six ways. In this way he protected and kept this sūtra in mind, and the buddhas of the ten directions, as many as the sands of the Ganges, also protected and kept this sūtra in mind. When this sūtra was spoken, measureless beings as many as the sands of the Ganges in the lands of the ten directions attained the patient acceptance of the unarisen dharma — how much more those who attained the hearers' stage beyond training, how much more those who abide at the stage of training.

Chinese Source

爾時阿難即從坐起，偏袒右肩白佛言：「世尊！當以何名此經，云何奉持？」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Ānanda rose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, and said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One, what should this sūtra be named, and how should we receive and keep it?"

Chinese Source

佛告阿難：「是經名為『諸法無行』。」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said to Ānanda: "This sūtra is named The Non-Activity of All Dharmas."

Chinese Source

說是經已，文殊師利法王子、彌勒菩薩摩訶薩、師子遊步菩薩摩訶薩、華戲慧天子等一切菩薩眾，及阿難、天、人、阿修羅等，聞佛所說，皆大歡喜。

Ru-Yi Translation

When this sūtra had been spoken, the Dharma-prince Mañjuśrī, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva Maitreya, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva Lion's Stride, the divine son Flower-Play Wisdom, and the whole assembly of bodhisattvas, together with Ānanda, gods, humans, asuras, and others, hearing what the Buddha had said, all greatly rejoiced.

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

Reading Notes for Fascicle 3

Translator's notes

Root of Joy (喜根) and Superior Intent (勝意). This story is cited throughout later Chinese Buddhism, especially in Tiantai, as a warning against judging other practitioners by the standards of austerity. The Mohe Zhiguan quotes the opening verse as 「貪欲即是道，恚癡亦如是」 ("greed is itself the path …"), a wording that differs from the received T0650 「貪欲是涅槃」 ("greed is nirvāṇa"); it also warns against those who seize on the line one-sidedly to justify unrestrained conduct.

"Greed is nirvāṇa; / anger and delusion are likewise" (貪欲是涅槃，恚癡亦如是). The opening verse is translated as directly as the Chinese, with no softening. The verses throughout argue from emptiness: because the nature of greed cannot be found, it is the same as the nature of the Buddha-dharma.

"Did not abandon worldly ways" (不捨世法). Root of Joy kept ordinary social conduct; the contrast is with Superior Intent's austerity.

The twelve ascetic observances (十二頭陀). Dhūtaguṇa: wearing rag robes, eating only alms food, dwelling in the forest, and so on.

"Householder's son" (居士子). The Chinese may mean a young householder or the son of a householder; the text also calls him "the master of the house" (舍主) and addresses him as "householder."

The list of reactions to sounds. The Chinese varies between 瞋 ("angry") and 礙 ("obstructed"); the English keeps the variation.

"Know [who knows this] as a leader of the world" (知是為世將). The refrain 知是為世將 can be read "know that he is a leader of the world" or "one who knows this is a leader of the world"; the bracketed words in one stanza show the second reading, which seems required there.

"A barren woman's child" (石女兒). A stock example of something that cannot exist.

"These numbers cannot be found; / because they are without number, they are unconditioned" (是數不可得，無數故無為). 數 can mean "number" or "category" (as in "falling into the categories"); the verse plays on the conditioned (有為) as what falls into number.

"Jewel seal of bodhi" (菩提寶印). The seal that certifies true teaching.

The numbers of lives. Seven hundred forty thousand (七十四萬) and six hundred thirty thousand (六十三萬) lives, as the Chinese gives them.

King of Majestic Virtue of Superior Sunlight (勝日光明威德王). The present name of Root of Joy as a buddha.

"After I had finished [paying for] this offense" (我畢是業障罪已). The bracketed words supply the sense of 畢, "finish."

The last five hundred years (後五百歲). The final period of the Dharma's decline.

The divine son Flower-Play Wisdom (華戲慧天子). Called a bodhisattva earlier; here a "divine son." See the Part 2 Reading Notes.
