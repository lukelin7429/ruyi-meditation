# The Sūtra on the Non-Activity of All Dharmas — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Zhufa wuxing jing* (諸法無行經, Taishō T15, no. 650 (tr. Kumārajīva)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T650, via the CBETA digital edition; in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 貪欲即是道 | greed is itself the path | The sūtra's most quoted line. |
| 無行 | non-activity | All dharmas neither arise nor act. |
| 不動相 | the unmoving mark | Mañjuśrī's teaching in fascicle 2. |
| 音聲法門 | the Dharma-gate of sounds | Every sound as a gate to the Dharma. |
