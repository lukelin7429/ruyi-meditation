# Pointing Out the Essentials of the Ten Gates of Nonduality — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Shi buer men zhiyao chao* (十不二門指要鈔, Taishō T46, no. 1928 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1928, via the CBETA digital edition. Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Quoted base text:** phrases quoted from Zhanran follow this site's translation of the *Ten Gates of Nonduality*, adjusted where Zhili reads them differently.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 性惡 | inherent evil | Evil as inherent in the nature itself, not only produced by cultivation; Zhili's central claim. |
| 別理隨緣 | the distinct principle following conditions | Whether the distinct teaching's principle can follow conditions; a point of dispute with the off-mountain masters. |
| 山家 / 山外 | home-mountain / off-mountain | The two factions of Song-dynasty Tiantai. |
| 一念 | a single thought | For Zhili, the ordinary deluded moment of mind. |
| 正因 / 緣因 / 了因 | the correct cause / the conditioning cause / the revealing cause | The three causes of buddha-nature, matched to this site's Fahua Xuanyi and Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra. |
