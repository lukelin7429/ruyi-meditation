Notes on the Textual Commentary on the Lotus.

Zhanran (湛然, 711–782) wrote these notes on the Fahua Wenju, Zhiyi's line-by-line commentary on the Lotus Sūtra as recorded by Guanding. Going through the sūtra chapter by chapter, he explains Zhiyi's phrases, supplies the sources behind them, defends the fourfold reading — by causes and conditions, by the teachings, by origin and trace, and by contemplating the mind — and answers rival commentators, above all Ji's Yogācāra reading. With his notes on the Fahua Xuanyi and on Mohe Zhiguan, it completes the commentaries that made Zhiyi's three great works the core of the Tiantai school.

How to read this collection

Read it with the Fahua Wenju: every phrase Zhanran quotes from it uses this site's English, so the two pages can be read side by side. Where he reads a phrase differently, the quotation keeps our wording and the note explains his reading.

Lotus quotations use this site's Lotus Sūtra.

Translation Notes

Key terms, kept consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations. See the full translation notes, and also this site's note on translation method.

四釋 — the four modes of explanationCauses and conditions, the teachings, origin and trace, contemplating the mind.
本／迹 — origin / traceThe two gates of the Lotus.
開權顯實 — opening the provisional to reveal the realThe sūtra's purpose, in Zhanran's reading.
四眾 — the four assembliesThe initiating, the matching, the echoing, and those forming karmic affinity.
