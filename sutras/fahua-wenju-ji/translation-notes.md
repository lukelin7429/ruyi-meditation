# Notes on the Textual Commentary on the Lotus — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Fahua wenju ji* (法華文句記, Taishō T34, no. 1719 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1719, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 四釋 | the four modes of explanation | Causes and conditions, the teachings, origin and trace, contemplating the mind. |
| 本／迹 | origin / trace | The two gates of the Lotus. |
| 開權顯實 | opening the provisional to reveal the real | The sūtra's purpose, in Zhanran's reading. |
| 四眾 | the four assemblies | The initiating, the matching, the echoing, and those forming karmic affinity. |
