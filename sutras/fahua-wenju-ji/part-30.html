The Entrustment and the Last Six Chapters

This is the last part of Zhanran's Notes, the lower section of the tenth fascicle. It comments on the last seven chapters of the Lotus, from the Entrustment to Samantabhadra's Encouragement; on the site these are all in fascicle-010 of the Textual Commentary. Almost half of the part is a defense of the Entrustment chapter's place in Kumārajīva's text. Ci'en (Kuiji) and others wanted to move it to the end of the sūtra, as in Dharmarakṣa's and Jñānagupta's versions. Zhanran answers their eight objections and two more from Dharma Master She, point by point, from the narrative of the stūpa, the emanation buddhas, and the pure and defiled land. He then reads the chapter title and the three rubbings of the crown through the four siddhāntas. On Bhaiṣajyarāja he explains the burning of the body as contemplation of the true mark, and weighs the Vinaya's ban on burning against the Brahmā's Net. On Wondrous Sound and the Universal Gate he treats the light from the east, the admonition, the Lotus Treatise's sixty-two koṭis of buddhas, and the verses Kumārajīva did not translate. Shorter notes follow on the Dhāraṇī, King Wondrous Adornment, and Samantabhadra chapters. The part ends with a story from Mount Wutai about Indian interest in the Tiantai teaching, and Zhanran's closing dedication.

Where the Entrustment Chapter Belongs: The General Defense

Chinese Source

釋囑累品

Ru-Yi Translation

Explaining the Entrustment Chapter

Chinese Source

釋此品，先辯他人判品前後，次今文正釋。初文者，慈恩、安國並令移之於〈勸發〉後，若在此中有八相違、十不可也。余雖管見，頗有稟承，每於聽筵忝蒙慈訓，垂示救旨深有所憑，近見秀公《法華圓鏡》，廣立難勢不越先規，今攢舊聞兼資後見，總別救之亦八不可。

Ru-Yi Translation

In explaining this chapter, first I discuss how others judge the chapter's position, before or after; next comes the explanation proper of the present text. As for the first: Ci'en and Anguo both would move it to after the "Encouragement" [chapter], [saying that] if it stays here there are eight contradictions and ten impossibilities. Although my view is like looking through a tube, I have received a good deal by transmission. At lecture gatherings I have often been honored with compassionate instruction, and the defense shown to me has a firm basis. Recently I saw Master Xiu's Perfect Mirror of the Lotus, which sets out objections at length but does not go beyond the earlier pattern. Now, gathering what I heard before and drawing also on what I have seen since, I defend it in general and in particular, again with eight impossibilities.

Chinese Source

初總救者，〈出塔〉已後凡述多寶，皆云「塔中」不云「見佛」，若移在後無出塔處，一不可也。分身散後凡有所述，唯論佛塔不涉分身，若移在後佛無散處，二不可也。〈囑累〉文中佛散土穢，已下經文言不涉淨，若移在後無復穢處，三不可也。會本居地因塔升空，佛散出塔後文在地，若移在後無還地處，四不可也。〈囑累品後〉經既未盡，但述眾喜不云「而去」，若移在後須加「而去」，五不可也。〈勸發品〉後無復餘文，經既已終則云「而去」，若移在後須除而去，六不可也。本迹二門佛事既畢，須有所付，是有囑累，若移在後法無所歸，七不可也。囑累已後明乘乘人，弘經本事事須囑累，若移在後師弟參雜，八不可也。

Ru-Yi Translation

First, the general defense. After "leaving the stūpa," wherever Prabhūtaratna is mentioned it always says "in the stūpa" and does not say "seeing the buddha." If [the chapter] were moved to the end, there would be no point at which [Śākyamuni] leaves the stūpa: this is the first impossibility. After the emanation buddhas disperse, whatever is narrated speaks only of the buddha's stūpa and does not involve the emanation buddhas. If it were moved to the end, there would be no point at which the buddhas disperse: this is the second impossibility. In the text of the "Entrustment," the buddhas disperse and the land is defiled, and in the sūtra text from there on the words do not touch on purity. If it were moved to the end, there would be no point at which [the land] becomes defiled again: this is the third impossibility. The assembly was originally on the ground and rose into the sky because of the stūpa; when the buddhas disperse and [Śākyamuni] leaves the stūpa, the later text is on the ground. If it were moved to the end, there would be no point of returning to the ground: this is the fourth impossibility. After the "Entrustment Chapter," since the sūtra is not yet finished, it only narrates the assembly's joy and does not say "and departed." If it were moved to the end, "and departed" would have to be added: this is the fifth impossibility. After the "Encouragement Chapter" there is no further text; since the sūtra has ended, it says "and departed." If [the Entrustment] were moved after it, "and departed" would have to be removed: this is the sixth impossibility. Once the buddha-work of the two gates of origin and trace is finished, there must be something entrusted; hence there is the Entrustment. If it were moved to the end, the Dharma would have nowhere to go: this is the seventh impossibility. After the Entrustment, [the sūtra] makes clear the persons who ride the vehicle; the former deeds of those who propagate the sūtra need the entrustment [first]. If it were moved to the end, teacher and disciples would be jumbled together: this is the eighth impossibility.

The Particular Defense: Versions, Treatise, and the Other Sūtras

Chinese Source

次別救者，具述元破一一救之。一云眾本相違者，準《正法華》及以隋朝崛多三藏《添品法華》中，此品並皆在於經末。救曰：《正》、《妙》二本譯人既異，所見各別，若令盡同此不可也。言《添品》者，準南山《內典錄》云：「崛多擅移〈囑累〉著後」，崛多既其擅改，法護未可為憑，《正》本既其居先，《添品》不名擅改，南山既斥添品，義當二本俱非。何者？羅什生雖龜茲，遍遊五竺，豈獨不見梵本《法華》？久居長安，豈不曾見法護所譯？而再譯不用者，當知法護非堪指南；若堪指南，何不文義咸依《正》本，若嫌後譯文義澆薄，則隋朝所譯彌薄於前，今依什譯理可準憑。故叡公云：「梵音錯者正之以天竺，秦言謬者正之以字義，不可譯者即而書之。」豈什公與四子頓爾無識，輒移一品安置經中？若不測旨歸，應仰之而已，何得以凡見斗尺量度大海虛空耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Next, the particular defense: I set out the original refutations in full and defend against each one. The first says it contradicts the various versions: in the Correct Dharma Flower and in the Supplemented Lotus of the Sui-dynasty Tripiṭaka [Master] Jueduo, this chapter is in both cases at the end of the sūtra. Defense: The translators of the two versions, the Correct and the Wondrous, were different persons, and what they saw differed; to make them entirely the same is not possible. As for the Supplemented version: according to Nanshan's Catalog of the Inner Canon, "Jueduo arbitrarily moved the Entrustment to the end." Since Jueduo altered it arbitrarily, Dharmarakṣa cannot be relied on either: if the Correct version had stood as the earlier [authority], the Supplemented would not be called an arbitrary alteration. Since Nanshan rejects the Supplemented, the meaning is that both versions are wrong. Why? Although Kumārajīva was born in Kucha, he traveled throughout the five Indias; could he alone have failed to see the Sanskrit Lotus? Having lived long in Chang'an, could he never have seen Dharmarakṣa's translation? That he translated it again without using it shows that Dharmarakṣa is not fit to be a guide. If he were fit to be a guide, why did [Kumārajīva] not follow the Correct version in both wording and meaning? If one dislikes the later translation as thin in wording and meaning, then the Sui translation is thinner still than the earlier one. Now, following Kumārajīva's translation, the principle can be relied on. Hence Master [Seng]rui says: "Where the Sanskrit sounds were wrong, he corrected them by [the speech of] India; where the Qin words were mistaken, he corrected them by the meaning of the characters; what could not be translated he wrote down as it was." Could Master [Kumāra]jīva and his four disciples suddenly have been so ignorant as to move a chapter at will and place it in the middle of the sūtra? If one cannot fathom its purport, one should simply look up to it. How can one take ordinary views as a peck-measure and a foot-rule to measure the great ocean and empty space?

Chinese Source

二云經論相違者，《法華論》云：「修行力有五」，第五護法力中云「如〈普賢勸發品〉及後品」，後品即指〈囑累品〉也。救曰：《論》亦人譯，擅指何疑？況《論》雖西來，譯時既在《正法華》後，秖是譯者順《正法華》故，《正經》在西晉時譯，《論》在後魏時譯，如隋笈多見《正法華．藥草喻》後有一長行偈頌，及〈囑累〉在後，便以《正經》添於品內，及移〈囑累〉在〈勸發〉後，餘無所云，遂使後人云「添品法華」。故知譯者不妨隨見，《妙》、《正》二本同一梵文，乍可信羅什而寢於法護，安得釋《妙》本專以《論》為憑？秀云：言「後品」者，是《普賢觀經》，以同是普賢發起令依經修觀。又言「後品」者，既其不出名目，則似經度不盡，若不爾者，何不云「及囑累品」但言後耶？此亦是一見。然準天台判為結經，不云「在勸發品後」。

Ru-Yi Translation

The second says it contradicts the treatise: the Lotus Treatise says, "The power of practice is of five [kinds]," and under the fifth, the power of protecting the Dharma, it says, "as in the Samantabhadra's Encouragement chapter and the later chapter"; "the later chapter" refers to the Entrustment chapter. Defense: The Treatise too was translated by people; why doubt that they pointed [to it] arbitrarily? Moreover, although the Treatise came from the West, the time of its translation was after the Correct Dharma Flower, so it is simply that the translator followed the Correct Dharma Flower. The Correct Sūtra was translated in the Western Jin, the Treatise in the Later Wei. Just so, Jueduo of the Sui saw that in the Correct Dharma Flower there was a passage of prose and verse after the "Medicinal Herbs Parable," and that the Entrustment was at the end; he then added [that passage] from the Correct Sūtra into the chapter and moved the Entrustment after the Encouragement, saying nothing else about it, and so later people called it the "Supplemented Lotus." Hence one knows that translators were free to follow what they saw. The two versions, the Wondrous and the Correct, are [from] one Sanskrit text; one may well trust Kumārajīva and set Dharmarakṣa aside. How could one, explaining the Wondrous version, take the Treatise alone as the basis? Xiu says: "the later chapter" is the Samantabhadra Contemplation Sūtra, since it too is occasioned by Samantabhadra and has one cultivate contemplation according to the sūtra. Again, as to "the later chapter": since it does not give a title, it seems the text was not fully conveyed; otherwise why does it not say "and the Entrustment chapter," but only say "later"? This too is one view. But Tiantai judges [the Samantabhadra Contemplation] to be the concluding sūtra, and does not say it is "after the Encouragement chapter."

Chinese Source

三、諸教相違，云：一切諸經並在經末，如何此經獨在於斯？救曰：例同諸經違妨更甚，即如《大品》中間有〈累教品〉，經末復有〈囑累品〉，不可一切皆兩處安。如《大寶積》四十九會，會會皆有付囑之文，豈令諸經一切皆爾？如《金剛經》問名問持乃在經中，不可一切悉令居中。《法華》開權顯本授聲聞記，豈令一切悉皆顯實？不類之例其數不一。

Ru-Yi Translation

The third, the contradiction with all the teachings, says: all sūtras place [the entrustment] at the end; how is it that this sūtra alone places it here? Defense: Making it conform to the other sūtras would bring even greater contradictions and obstacles. For example, the Larger [Prajñā] has a chapter "Entrusting the Teaching" in the middle and again an Entrustment chapter at the end of the sūtra; one cannot make all sūtras place it in two places. The Great Ratnakūṭa has forty-nine assemblies, and every assembly has a passage of entrustment; could one make all sūtras like that? In the Diamond Sūtra the questions about the name and about upholding come in the middle of the sūtra; one cannot make all [sūtras] place them in the middle. The Lotus opens the provisional, reveals the origin, and confers predictions on the śrāvakas; could one make all [sūtras] reveal the real? Examples of things that are not alike are not few.

The Two Matters, the Two Commands, and the Stūpa

Chinese Source

四云二事乖角者，分身若還此土復穢，準妙音被誡故知土穢，〈囑累〉中亦令多寶還歸，觀音不應施寶分二，分二即多寶未還，遣去既同不應乖角。

Ru-Yi Translation

The fourth says the two matters are at odds: if the emanation buddhas had returned, this land was defiled again (judging by Wondrous Sound's being admonished, we know the land was defiled). In the "Entrustment" [the Buddha] also had Prabhūtaratna return; [then] Avalokiteśvara ought not to have divided his gift of jewels in two, since dividing it in two means Prabhūtaratna had not returned. Since the sending away was the same, they ought not to be at odds.

Chinese Source

救曰：秖由未還故寶分二，是故但云「一分奉多寶佛塔」，而不云「奉多寶佛」，故知事畢者去、有緣者住，於理何傷？若多寶在分身不合散者，經文但云「塔可如故」，如故秖是依初還閉；令初身散，即云「各還本土」，故知雖即令其塔還，聽餘經故塔閉而在，如塔未開時，但云多寶「於寶塔中出大音聲」。又云四眾聞寶塔中所出音聲，大樂說云「於其塔中發是音聲」。又佛告大樂說「是寶塔中有如來」等，故塔未開，大眾但云「多寶如來於寶塔中」；次塔開已，大眾皆云「見二如來在寶塔中」。若塔閉後，如〈藥王品〉末云「多寶如來於寶塔中」，妙音至此但上釋迦瓔珞以申問訊，次方問云「此久滅度多寶如來，在寶塔中來聽法不？」仍對釋迦申彼佛問云「安隱堪忍久住不？」當知塔閉住而未去。又云「唯願世尊示我令見」，豈二佛並坐乃云請見？又多寶佛於寶塔中告妙音言，秖云「汝為供養釋迦」等，不云「分身」，豈分身矚目而不供養？又妙音欲還但云「供養釋迦及多寶塔已」，不云「見佛」；到彼見淨華宿王，但云「供養釋迦及多寶塔」，亦不云「見多寶如來及以分身」。又文殊來時即云「頭面敬禮二世尊足」，囑累之後全無此文，故塔已閉、分身已散。若分身在，觀音施寶理應供養，不應但二。

Ru-Yi Translation

Defense: It is precisely because he had not returned that the jewels were divided in two. Therefore it says only "offering one part to Prabhūtaratna Buddha's own stūpa," and does not say "offered to Prabhūtaratna Buddha." Hence one knows that those whose task was done departed and the one with a remaining connection stayed; what harm is there to principle? [If one objects that] while Prabhūtaratna remained the emanation buddhas should not have dispersed: the sūtra text says only "let the stūpa return to just as it was before"; "as before" just means it closes again as at first. In making the emanation bodies disperse, it says "each return to their own lands." Hence one knows that although it had the stūpa return, the stūpa stays closed in order to hear the rest of the sūtra, just as before the stūpa was opened it says only that Prabhūtaratna "within the jeweled stūpa sent forth a great voice." Again, it says that the four assemblies heard the voice coming from within the jeweled stūpa; Mahāpratibhāna says, "this voice issues from within the stūpa"; again the Buddha tells Mahāpratibhāna, "within this jeweled stūpa there is a Tathāgata," and so on. So while the stūpa had not yet been opened, the great assembly says only "Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata within the jeweled stūpa"; next, once the stūpa was opened, the great assembly all say "seeing the two Tathāgatas within the jeweled stūpa." After the stūpa was closed, as at the end of the Bhaiṣajyarāja chapter it says, "Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata within the jeweled stūpa." When Wondrous Sound arrived here, he only presented the necklace to Śākyamuni to convey greetings, and only then asked, "Does Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata, extinguished so long ago, come to hear the Dharma from within his stūpa?" and, still facing Śākyamuni, conveyed that buddha's inquiry: "Is he at peace, able to bear remaining long?" One should know that the stūpa was closed and remained without departing. Again he says, "I beg only that the World-Honored One show him to me so that I may see him"; if the two buddhas were sitting side by side, would he ask to see him? Again, Prabhūtaratna Buddha, from within the jeweled stūpa, tells Wondrous Sound only, "that you should come to make offerings to Śākyamuni," and so on, and does not say "the emanation buddhas"; if the emanation buddhas were before his eyes, would he not make offerings to them? Again, when Wondrous Sound wishes to return, it says only "having made offerings to Śākyamuni and to Prabhūtaratna's stūpa," and does not say "seeing the buddha"; arriving there and seeing [the buddha] Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña, he says only "made offerings to Śākyamuni and to Prabhūtaratna's stūpa," and again does not say "saw Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata and the emanation buddhas." Again, when Mañjuśrī came, it says, "bowed with head and face at the feet of the two World-Honored Ones"; after the Entrustment there is no such text at all. Hence the stūpa had been closed and the emanation buddhas had dispersed. If the emanation buddhas were there, Avalokiteśvara's gift of jewels should properly have been offered to them as well, not only in two.

五、二命不齊者，何故？分身、多寶二俱唱散，去留不等，若云「但令塔閉云如故者」，何故《正本》云「還本土」？救曰：二命縱同，所緣各別。寶塔為聽經故來，分身為開塔故集，塔既已閉分身須散，經尚未畢故塔未還，分身既散土合復常，故前文云「為諸佛當來坐故，各淨八方」，況《正法華》云「可還本土」，自是法護所譯不正，豈判什本令從《正經》？若《正經》為正不應重譯，但云「如故」。若欲依《正經》，何不講《正》本？遍觀正本處處相違，及以妄誤前後非一，故不須以《正經》為準。

Ru-Yi Translation

The fifth: the two commands are not uniform. Why? For the emanation buddhas and for Prabhūtaratna alike a dispersal was proclaimed, yet their going and staying are not equal. If one says it "only had the stūpa close, saying 'as before,'" why does the Correct Version say "return to his own land"? Defense: Even if the two commands are the same, what each is connected with differs. The jeweled stūpa came in order to hear the sūtra; the emanation buddhas assembled in order to open the stūpa. Once the stūpa was closed, the emanation buddhas had to disperse; since the sūtra was not yet finished, the stūpa did not yet return. Once the emanation buddhas had dispersed, the land had to revert to its usual state; hence the earlier text says, "for the buddhas who were to come and sit, he purified each of the eight directions." Moreover, that the Correct Dharma Flower says "may return to his own land" is simply Dharmarakṣa's incorrect translation; how could one judge Kumārajīva's version by making it follow the Correct Sūtra? If the Correct Sūtra were correct, there would have been no need to translate it again and say only "as before." If you wish to follow the Correct Sūtra, why not lecture on the Correct version? Looking through the Correct version, it differs [from this one] everywhere, and its errors, front and back, are more than one; therefore there is no need to take the Correct Sūtra as the standard.

Chinese Source

多寶本願但云「以塔聽經」，若以我身示四眾者，令集分身，當知分身開塔故集，釋迦亦為開塔住空，住空故開塔，塔開故命坐，囑累故出塔，出塔故塔閉，塔閉故分身事訖，事訖故須散，故塔開閉分身聚散各有因緣，何須難言二命不齊？

Ru-Yi Translation

Prabhūtaratna's original vow says only that [he comes] "with his stūpa to hear the sūtra"; [as for] "if [that buddha] wishes to show my body to the fourfold assembly," [that buddha] must gather his emanation buddhas. One should know that the emanation buddhas assembled in order to open the stūpa. Śākyamuni too abided in the sky in order to open the stūpa. Because he abided in the sky, the stūpa was opened; because the stūpa was opened, he was invited to sit; because of the entrustment, he left the stūpa; because he left the stūpa, the stūpa closed; because the stūpa closed, the emanation buddhas' task was done; because their task was done, they had to disperse. Reading Notes for Part 30

Translator's notes

Fascicle and site pages. All the lemmas in this part are from the site's fascicle-010 of the Textual Commentary, from "Explaining the chapter Entrustment" to the end. The site renders 實相 in these chapters as "true reality"; that wording is kept inside lemmas, while Zhanran's own sentences use "the true mark." In Zhanran's own sentences the chapter names follow the Lotus list used elsewhere in this translation (Wondrous Sound, the Universal Gate, Dhāraṇī, King [Wondrous] Adornment).

The debate on the Entrustment chapter (units 3–19). "Ci'en" is Kuiji. "Anguo" is probably the same person as "Dharma Master She" of unit 17, i.e., Lishe of Anguo Temple, a Tang monk of Kuiji's school; this identification is not certain. Master Xiu's Perfect Mirror of the Lotus (法華圓鏡) is not extant, and Xiu has not been identified. Daoxuan's Catalog of the Inner Canon (T2149) says of Jñānagupta's version only that it "moved the Entrustment chapter to the end"; the word "arbitrarily" (擅) in Zhanran's quotation is not in the catalog. Sengrui's words on Kumārajīva's method of translation are quoted in Jizang's Mahāyāna Profound Treatise (T1853) with slightly different wording ("定之以字義," "不可變者").

The Lotus Treatise. The "five powers of practice" and "the Samantabhadra chapter and the later chapter" are in both versions of Vasubandhu's Treatise (T1519, T1520). The passage on Avalokiteśvara's name and the names of sixty-two koṭis of buddhas quoted in unit 59 matches T1520 closely.

Sūtra wording. The Lotus lines follow the site's Lotus pages where Zhanran quotes them closely (the Entrustment, the Bhaiṣajyarāja, the Wondrous Sound, the Universal Gate, the King Wondrous Adornment, and the Samantabhadra pages). Several of his "quotations" in units 7, 19, and 21 are paraphrases, for example "for the sake of entrusting this sūtra, he entrusted the Tathāgata's four dharmas to Viśiṣṭacāritra" and "You are the great benefactors of beings" (the sūtra says the Tathāgata is the great benefactor); these are translated as he gives them. The words "I have already attained the dhāraṇī of understanding the language of every being" (unit 47) are close to, but not the same as, the site's Bhaiṣajyarāja page ("I attained the dhāraṇī of understanding the language of every being").

Stinginess (unit 21). The "someone else" who cites the Satyasiddhi on five kinds of stinginess is Kuiji, Profound Praise (T1723), which lists stinginess about dwelling, families, giving, praise, and the Dharma. Zhanran's list in this unit is shorter and its middle items are unclear; the seven retributions of stinginess about the Dharma were not located in the Satyasiddhi. The line "if I converted with the Lesser Vehicle . I would fall into stinginess and greed" is from the Skillful Means chapter.

Burning the body (unit 24). 蘭 and 吉 are abbreviations of 偷蘭遮 (sthūlātyaya) and 突吉羅 (duṣkṛta). The Brahmā's Net rule is the minor precept on not burning the body, arms, or fingers as an offering. Jayoṣmāyatana (勝熱) is the brahmin in the Avataṃsaka who climbs a mountain of knives and leaps into fire before Sudhana. "The nirgrantha and King Yanchi (嚴熾)" probably refers to the Satyaka Nirgrantha Sūtra (T272), in which the nirgrantha Satyaka teaches King Yanchi; this is not certain. In unit 24, 惑斯惑果 is read as 感斯惑果 ("brings about this fruit of delusion").

Sources found. The Abhidharmakośa on the seven gold mountains and Sumeru of four treasures (unit 34) is T1558; Zhanran's wording is a summary. The Avataṃsaka verse on the bodhisattva as craftsman (unit 49) is a paraphrase of the verse in T278/T279 ("若見世界始成立 … 是時菩薩為工匠"). The seven fires (unit 55) are in the Humane Kings (Prajñā) (T245), which has "heavenly fire" where Zhanran has "lightning fire." The ten dharmas of the earth samādhi (unit 68) are in the Baoyun Sūtra (T658), where the samādhi is called 得地三昧. The sentence attributed to the Continued Biographies of Eminent Monks (unit 68), that the verses of the Universal Gate were translated by Jñānagupta, was not found in that form in T2060.

Sources not located. The story that Samantabhadra taught a practitioner the "phrasing and pauses" of the Lotus (unit 17); the Nirvāṇa passage on disciples and donors cited by "someone" (unit 21); the five and six methods of practice cited by "someone" in unit 82 (they resemble the Samantabhadra Contemplation Sūtra); Daosheng's line on spells and the "commentary" (注) set against it (unit 70); "the Avataṃsaka's" list "parents, a guide, a physician, a boat" (unit 74). The phrase "the two rounds . say 'its four'" in unit 70 refers to the site's notes "(this is the first sense)" … "(this is the fourth sense)."

Uncertain readings. Unit 5, 經度不盡, is taken as "the text was not fully conveyed." Unit 17, 表現表當, is taken as "signifies the present and signifies what is to come"; and 以三界八獄為大科形斯為小 as "take the three realms and the eight hells as a major division so that by this shape [the sūtra] is made small" (Zhanran is criticizing Kuiji's outline of the sūtra). Unit 19, 自在天子以大梵冠首 … 迴梵文大梵字不盡, is translated literally; the comparison with Dharmarakṣa's list of names is not clear. In unit 19, 勸持 after Never Disparaging may mean "the exhortation to uphold" or the Upholding chapter (勸持品), which the next sentence names. In unit 55, the last sentence on where to put the three characters "Avalokiteśvara" is translated literally. In unit 73, 華德復為妙音所將及以此土發起之眾 is unclear; it is translated literally.

Four readings. In unit 44 Zhanran reads the Commentary's 當卑其地，自壅其流 as a warning to the hearer: one who looks down on the ground where the bodhisattva appears blocks the flow of the Dharma himself. In unit 73 he reads 喀喀 as the sound of retching, food that will not go down. In unit 24 he takes 燒 as the fire that burns and 然 as the body that is set alight. In unit 34 he takes the "one or two" of the ten treasure mountains as one or two treasures.

Mount Wutai (unit 82). Hanguang was a disciple of Amoghavajra who went with him to India and later worked on Mount Wutai. The "central land losing the Dharma and seeking it in the four borderlands" turns a common saying around: here India is the central land and China the borderland. "Like the people of Lu" alludes to the people of Lu who did not recognize Confucius as a sage. Huiren of Anguo Temple in Luoyang has not been identified further.

Closing title. The Taishō closing line of this fascicle reads 法華文句記卷第十（下）; it is translated in the standard pattern. There is no further colophon in T1719 after it.
