Bodhisattvas Come from the Ten Directions

Fascicle 10 continues the account of bodhisattvas coming from the ten directions, exploring the Buddha's teaching, inconceivable power, and mastery of samadhi.

Jewel Accumulation Explains Śākyamuni's Spiritual Power — Part Fifteen, Continued

Chinese Source

〖經〗寶積佛報普明言：「善男子！西方度如恒河沙等世界，有世界名娑婆，是中有佛，號釋迦牟尼，今現在欲為諸菩薩摩訶薩說般若波羅蜜，是其神力。」

English Translation

Sutra: Jewel Accumulation Buddha replied to Universal Radiance, 'Good man! To the west, beyond worlds as numerous as the sands of the Ganges, there is a world called Sahā. In it is a Buddha named Śākyamuni, who is now about to teach Prajñāpāramitā to the bodhisattva mahāsattvas. This is his spiritual power.'

〖論〗問曰：佛譬如須彌山，不為大海水波所動，今何以答普明？是則動相。攝心則無語，散心則有說。說法從覺觀生，覺觀麁事，佛不應有此麁事！

Treatise: Question: The Buddha is like Mount Sumeru, unmoved by the waves of the great ocean. Why, then, does he answer Universal Radiance? This would be a sign of movement. A collected mind does not speak; a scattered mind speaks. Teaching arises from initial and sustained mental application, which are coarse activities. The Buddha should not have such coarse activities!

答曰：佛雖入深禪定，不為世事所動，今以大慈悲心憐愍眾生，為之說法斷疑。如須彌山王，小風則不能動，若隨藍風至則大動散；佛亦如是，有大慈悲風來，憐愍心動，散身無數，入五道教化眾生，或作天身乃至畜生。

Answer: Although the Buddha enters profound meditative concentration and remains unmoved by worldly affairs, out of great loving-kindness and compassion he cares for beings and teaches them the Dharma to remove their doubts. Small winds cannot move Sumeru, king of mountains, but when the destructive vairambha wind arrives, it shakes and scatters it. Likewise, when the wind of great loving-kindness and compassion arises, the Buddha's compassionate heart is stirred. He manifests countless bodies and enters the five destinies to teach beings, appearing as devas and in other forms, even as animals.

復次，佛實不動常入禪定，先世福德因緣故，身邊出聲，應物如響；如天伎樂，自然發聲。又如摩尼珠，隨人所欲，種種與之，若欲衣被、飲食、音樂，自恣所須，自然皆得；佛亦如是，從其身邊諸毛孔中自然有聲，隨心說法，是中佛無憶想，亦無分別。

Furthermore, the Buddha is actually unmoving and constantly in meditative concentration. Through the causes and conditions of merit accumulated in former lives, sounds issue from around his body, responding to beings like an echo, just as heavenly musical instruments sound spontaneously. A maṇi jewel supplies whatever people desire—clothing, bedding, food, drink, or music. Whatever they need is freely and spontaneously obtained. Likewise, sounds arise spontaneously from the pores all around the Buddha's body, teaching the Dharma according to beings' minds. In this, the Buddha engages in neither deliberate imagining nor discrimination.

The Inconceivable Body and Speech of a Buddha

Chinese Source

如說《密迹金剛經》中，佛有三密：身密、語密、意密。一切諸天人皆不解、不知。有一會眾生，或見佛身黃金色，白銀色，諸雜寶色；有人見佛身一丈六尺，或見一里、十里、百千萬億，乃至無邊無量遍虛空中，如是等名身密。

English Translation

As the Scripture of the Vajra Bearer of Secret Traces (《密迹金剛經》) explains, the Buddha has three mysteries: the mystery of body, the mystery of speech, and the mystery of mind. None of the devas or humans can understand or know them. Within a single assembly, some beings see the Buddha's body as golden, others as silver, and others as the colors of various jewels. Some see his body as one zhang and six chi tall; others see it as one li, ten li, or countless vast distances in height, even as boundless and immeasurable, filling space. Such appearances are called the mystery of body.

語密者，有人聞佛聲一里，有聞十里、百千萬億無數無量遍虛空中；有一會中，或聞說布施，或有聞說持戒，或聞說忍辱、精進、禪定、智慧，如是乃至十二部經、八萬法聚，各各隨心所聞，是名語密。

As for the mystery of speech, some hear the Buddha's voice over one li, others over ten li, and others over immeasurable distances, filling space without limit. Within one assembly, some hear teachings on giving; others hear teachings on keeping the precepts, patience, diligence, meditative concentration, or wisdom. This extends to the twelve divisions of scripture and eighty thousand collections of Dharma. Each hears according to their own mind. This is called the mystery of speech.

是時目連心念：「欲知佛聲近遠。」即時以己神足力，至無量千萬億佛世界而息，聞佛音聲如近不異。

At that time, Maudgalyāyana thought, 'I wish to know how far the Buddha's voice reaches.' Using his own supernatural power, he immediately traveled across immeasurable myriads of Buddha worlds and stopped. He heard the Buddha's voice just as clearly as when nearby.

所息世界，其佛與大眾方食。彼土人大，目連立其鉢緣，彼佛弟子問其佛言：「此人頭虫從何所來？著沙門被服而行！」

In the world where he stopped, its Buddha and his great assembly were eating. The people there were enormous, and Maudgalyāyana stood on the rim of a bowl. The Buddha's disciples asked him, 'Where has this insect with a human head come from? It is walking around dressed in the robes of an ascetic!'

其佛報言：「勿輕此人！此是東方過無量佛土，有佛名釋迦牟尼，此是彼佛神足弟子。」彼佛問：「目度伽略子！汝何以來此？」目連答言：「我尋佛音聲故來至此。」彼佛告目連：「汝尋佛聲，過無量億劫，不能得其邊際。」

That Buddha replied, 'Do not look down on him! To the east, beyond immeasurable Buddha lands, there is a Buddha named Śākyamuni. This is his disciple distinguished in supernatural powers.' The Buddha asked, 'Maudgalyāyana! Why have you come here?' He replied, 'I came here while tracing the Buddha's voice.' That Buddha told him, 'Even if you followed the Buddha's voice for immeasurable koṭis of kalpas, you could not reach its limit.'

復次，佛出世，為斷眾生疑故為說法，此不應難！如不應問曰：「何以除闇？」佛亦如是。不應問佛何以故答！

Furthermore, a Buddha appears in the world and teaches the Dharma to remove beings' doubts. This should not be challenged! Just as one should not ask, 'Why dispel darkness?', one should not ask why the Buddha answers.

Equal Awakening and the Truth of Causes and Conditions

Chinese Source

問曰：諸佛等故，名為等覺，今何以稱言「是彼神力」？

English Translation

Question: Since Buddhas are equal, they are called equally awakened. Why, then, does he say, 'This is his spiritual power'?

答曰：示無吾我，彼此滅嫉慢故。

Answer: To show freedom from ego and possessiveness, and to remove jealousy and pride between oneself and others.

復次，世界有天常求尊勝，憍慢法故，自言天地、人物，是我化作。如梵天王謂諸梵言：「我作汝等。」毘紐天言：「世間有大富貴名聞人，皆是我身威德力分，我能成就世間，亦能破壞世間，世間成壞皆是我作。」有如是天，破因緣法相；諸佛實語不破因緣法相，故言「是彼佛神力」。

Furthermore, some devas in the world constantly seek supremacy. Out of pride, they claim, 'I created heaven and earth, people and all things.' For example, the Brahmā king tells the Brahmās, 'I created you.' Viṣṇu says, 'All the very wealthy, powerful, and famous people in the world possess a share of my majestic power. I can bring the world into being and destroy it. The world's formation and destruction are all my doing.' Such devas deny the nature of causes and conditions. Buddhas speak truthfully and do not deny it. Therefore, Jewel Accumulation says, 'This is that Buddha's spiritual power.'

Never Satisfied with Seeing Buddhas and Hearing the Dharma

Chinese Source

〖經〗是時，普明菩薩白寶積佛言：「世尊！我今當往見釋迦牟尼佛，禮拜、供養；及見彼諸菩薩摩訶薩紹尊位者，皆得陀羅尼及諸三昧，於諸三昧而得自在。」

English Translation

Sutra: Universal Radiance Bodhisattva then said to Jewel Accumulation Buddha, 'World-Honored One! I will now go to see Śākyamuni Buddha, bow to him, and make offerings. I will also see the bodhisattva mahāsattvas there who will succeed to the honored position, who have all attained dhāraṇīs and samādhis and gained mastery over the samādhis.'

〖論〗問曰：若諸佛持戒、禪定、智慧、度人皆等，是普明菩薩何以欲來見釋迦牟尼佛？

Treatise: Question: If all Buddhas are equal in keeping the precepts, meditative concentration, wisdom, and liberating others, why does Universal Radiance wish to come and see Śākyamuni Buddha?

答曰：諸菩薩常欲見佛無厭足，聽法無厭足，見諸菩薩僧無厭足；諸菩薩於世間法皆以厭患，於上三事心無厭足。

Answer: Bodhisattvas are never satisfied with seeing Buddhas, hearing the Dharma, or seeing the community of bodhisattvas. They are disenchanted with worldly things, but their hearts never tire of these three activities.

如手居士從淨居天來欲見佛，其身微細沒失，譬如消蘇，不得立地。佛語手居士：「汝化作麁身，觀此地相！」居士即如佛言化作麁身，觀念此地相，頭面禮佛足，一面立。

For example, the householder Hasta came from the Pure Abodes wishing to see the Buddha. His body was so subtle that it sank down like melting butter, and he could not stand on the ground. The Buddha told him, 'Manifest a coarser body and attend to the characteristics of this ground!' Following the Buddha's words, he manifested a coarser body, directed his attention to the ground, bowed with his head at the Buddha's feet, and stood to one side.

佛問居士：「汝幾事無厭生淨居天？」答言：「我三事無厭生淨居天：一、見諸佛供養無厭，二、聽法無厭，三、供給僧無厭。如佛在閻浮提，四部眾常隨逐佛，聽法、問法；是我淨居諸天，亦常從我聽法、問法。」

The Buddha asked, 'In how many things were you insatiable, leading to your birth in the Pure Abodes?' He replied, 'I was insatiable in three things and was born in the Pure Abodes: first, seeing Buddhas and making offerings; second, hearing the Dharma; and third, providing for the Saṅgha. Just as the fourfold community in Jambudvīpa constantly follows the Buddha to hear the Dharma and ask questions about it, the devas of the Pure Abodes constantly come to me to hear the Dharma and ask questions about it.'

聲聞猶尚聽法無厭足，何況法性身菩薩？以是故，普明菩薩來見釋迦牟尼佛。

Even a śrāvaka never tires of hearing the Dharma. How much more is this true of a bodhisattva with a body of Dharma nature! Therefore, Universal Radiance comes to see Śākyamuni Buddha.

「及見此間諸菩薩摩訶薩紹尊位者，皆得陀羅尼及諸三昧」，如先「讚菩薩品」中說。「於諸三昧而得自在」者。

The words 'and see the bodhisattva mahāsattvas here who will succeed to the honored position, who have all attained dhāraṇīs and samādhis' have been explained in the earlier section praising bodhisattvas. Next comes the explanation of 'gained mastery over the samādhis.'

Mastery of Bodhisattva Samādhis and the Power of Past Connections

Chinese Source

問曰：如佛一人，一切三昧中得自在，何以言「菩薩亦一切三昧中得自在」？

English Translation

Question: Since only a Buddha has mastery over all samādhis, why say that bodhisattvas also have mastery over all samādhis?

答曰：有二種三昧：一者佛三昧，二者菩薩三昧。是諸菩薩於菩薩三昧中得自在，非佛三昧中。

Answer: There are two kinds of samādhi: those of Buddhas and those of bodhisattvas. These bodhisattvas have mastery over bodhisattva samādhis, not over Buddha samādhis.

如說《諸佛要集經》中：文殊尸利欲見佛集，不能得到。諸佛各還本處，文殊尸利到諸佛集處，有一女人近彼佛坐入三昧，文殊尸利入，禮佛足已，白佛言：「云何此女人得近佛坐而我不得？」

As the Scripture of the Essential Assembly of Buddhas (《諸佛要集經》) relates, Mañjuśrī wished to attend an assembly of Buddhas but could not reach it. After the Buddhas had returned to their own abodes, Mañjuśrī reached the meeting place. A woman was sitting near the Buddha, absorbed in samādhi. Mañjuśrī entered, bowed at the Buddha's feet, and asked, 'Why is this woman able to sit near the Buddha, while I am not?'

佛告文殊尸利：「汝覺此女人，令從三昧起，汝自問之。」文殊尸利即彈指覺之而不可覺，以大聲喚亦不可覺，捉手牽亦不可覺，又以神足動三千大千世界猶亦不覺。文殊尸利白佛言：「世尊！我不能令覺。」

The Buddha told Mañjuśrī, 'Rouse this woman from samādhi and ask her yourself.' Mañjuśrī snapped his fingers to rouse her, but could not. Calling loudly did not rouse her; taking her hand and pulling did not rouse her. Even using his supernatural power to shake the great trichiliocosm did not rouse her. Mañjuśrī said, 'World-Honored One! I cannot rouse her.'

是時，佛放大光明照下方世界，是中有一菩薩名棄諸蓋，即時從下方出，來到佛所，頭面禮佛足，一面立。佛告棄諸蓋菩薩：「汝覺此女人！」即時彈指，此女從三昧起。

The Buddha then emitted a great light illuminating the worlds below. A bodhisattva there named Abandoning All Hindrances immediately rose from below, came to the Buddha, bowed with his head at the Buddha's feet, and stood to one side. The Buddha told him, 'Rouse this woman!' He snapped his fingers, and she immediately emerged from samādhi.

文殊尸利白佛言：「以何因緣，我動三千大千世界不能令此女起？棄諸蓋菩薩一彈指，便從三昧起？」

Mañjuśrī asked, 'Why could I not rouse this woman even by shaking the great trichiliocosm, while Abandoning All Hindrances brought her out of samādhi with a single snap of his fingers?'

佛告文殊尸利：「汝因此女人初發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提意，是女人因棄諸蓋菩薩初發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提意。以是故，汝不能令覺。汝於諸佛三昧中功德未滿。是諸菩薩三昧中得自在，佛三昧中少多入，而未得自在故耳。」

The Buddha told Mañjuśrī, 'It was through this woman that you first aroused the aspiration for unsurpassed complete awakening. It was through Abandoning All Hindrances Bodhisattva that she first aroused that aspiration. Therefore, you could not rouse her. Your merit in the samādhis of the Buddhas is not yet complete. These bodhisattvas have mastery over bodhisattva samādhis. They enter Buddha samādhis to some extent, but have not yet gained mastery over them.'
