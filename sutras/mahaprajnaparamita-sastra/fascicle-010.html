Jewel Accumulation Explains Śākyamuni's Spiritual Power — Part Fifteen, Continued

Chinese Source

〖經〗寶積佛報普明言：「善男子！西方度如恒河沙等世界，有世界名娑婆，是中有佛，號釋迦牟尼，今現在欲為諸菩薩摩訶薩說般若波羅蜜，是其神力。」

English Translation

Sutra: Jewel Accumulation Buddha replied to Universal Radiance, 'Good man! To the west, beyond worlds as numerous as the sands of the Ganges, there is a world called Sahā. In it is a Buddha named Śākyamuni, who is now about to teach Prajñāpāramitā to the bodhisattva mahāsattvas. This is his spiritual power.'

〖論〗問曰：佛譬如須彌山，不為大海水波所動，今何以答普明？是則動相。攝心則無語，散心則有說。說法從覺觀生，覺觀麁事，佛不應有此麁事！

Treatise: Question: The Buddha is like Mount Sumeru, unmoved by the waves of the great ocean. Why, then, does he answer Universal Radiance? This would be a sign of movement. A collected mind does not speak; a scattered mind speaks. Teaching arises from initial and sustained mental application, which are coarse activities. The Buddha should not have such coarse activities!

答曰：佛雖入深禪定，不為世事所動，今以大慈悲心憐愍眾生，為之說法斷疑。如須彌山王，小風則不能動，若隨藍風至則大動散；佛亦如是，有大慈悲風來，憐愍心動，散身無數，入五道教化眾生，或作天身乃至畜生。

Answer: Although the Buddha enters profound meditative concentration and remains unmoved by worldly affairs, out of great loving-kindness and compassion he cares for beings and teaches them the Dharma to remove their doubts. Small winds cannot move Sumeru, king of mountains, but when the destructive vairambha wind arrives, it shakes and scatters it. Likewise, when the wind of great loving-kindness and compassion arises, the Buddha's compassionate heart is stirred. He manifests countless bodies and enters the five destinies to teach beings, appearing as devas and in other forms, even as animals.

復次，佛實不動常入禪定，先世福德因緣故，身邊出聲，應物如響；如天伎樂，自然發聲。又如摩尼珠，隨人所欲，種種與之，若欲衣被、飲食、音樂，自恣所須，自然皆得；佛亦如是，從其身邊諸毛孔中自然有聲，隨心說法，是中佛無憶想，亦無分別。

Furthermore, the Buddha is actually unmoving and constantly in meditative concentration. Through the causes and conditions of merit accumulated in former lives, sounds issue from around his body, responding to beings like an echo, just as heavenly musical instruments sound spontaneously. A maṇi jewel supplies whatever people desire—clothing, bedding, food, drink, or music. Whatever they need is freely and spontaneously obtained. Likewise, sounds arise spontaneously from the pores all around the Buddha's body, teaching the Dharma according to beings' minds. In this, the Buddha engages in neither deliberate imagining nor discrimination.

The Inconceivable Body and Speech of a Buddha

Chinese Source

如說《密迹金剛經》中，佛有三密：身密、語密、意密。一切諸天人皆不解、不知。有一會眾生，或見佛身黃金色，白銀色，諸雜寶色；有人見佛身一丈六尺，或見一里、十里、百千萬億，乃至無邊無量遍虛空中，如是等名身密。

English Translation

As the Scripture of the Vajra Bearer of Secret Traces (《密迹金剛經》) explains, the Buddha has three mysteries: the mystery of body, the mystery of speech, and the mystery of mind. None of the devas or humans can understand or know them. Within a single assembly, some beings see the Buddha's body as golden, others as silver, and others as the colors of various jewels. Some see his body as one zhang and six chi tall; others see it as one li, ten li, or countless vast distances in height, even as boundless and immeasurable, filling space. Such appearances are called the mystery of body.

語密者，有人聞佛聲一里，有聞十里、百千萬億無數無量遍虛空中；有一會中，或聞說布施，或有聞說持戒，或聞說忍辱、精進、禪定、智慧，如是乃至十二部經、八萬法聚，各各隨心所聞，是名語密。

As for the mystery of speech, some hear the Buddha's voice over one li, others over ten li, and others over immeasurable distances, filling space without limit. Within one assembly, some hear teachings on giving; others hear teachings on keeping the precepts, patience, diligence, meditative concentration, or wisdom. This extends to the twelve divisions of scripture and eighty thousand collections of Dharma. Each hears according to their own mind. This is called the mystery of speech.

是時目連心念：「欲知佛聲近遠。」即時以己神足力，至無量千萬億佛世界而息，聞佛音聲如近不異。

At that time, Maudgalyāyana thought, 'I wish to know how far the Buddha's voice reaches.' Using his own supernatural power, he immediately traveled across immeasurable myriads of Buddha worlds and stopped. He heard the Buddha's voice just as clearly as when nearby.

所息世界，其佛與大眾方食。彼土人大，目連立其鉢緣，彼佛弟子問其佛言：「此人頭虫從何所來？著沙門被服而行！」

In the world where he stopped, its Buddha and his great assembly were eating. The people there were enormous, and Maudgalyāyana stood on the rim of a bowl. The Buddha's disciples asked him, 'Where has this insect with a human head come from? It is walking around dressed in the robes of an ascetic!'

其佛報言：「勿輕此人！此是東方過無量佛土，有佛名釋迦牟尼，此是彼佛神足弟子。」彼佛問：「目度伽略子！汝何以來此？」目連答言：「我尋佛音聲故來至此。」彼佛告目連：「汝尋佛聲，過無量億劫，不能得其邊際。」

That Buddha replied, 'Do not look down on him! To the east, beyond immeasurable Buddha lands, there is a Buddha named Śākyamuni. This is his disciple distinguished in supernatural powers.' The Buddha asked, 'Maudgalyāyana! Why have you come here?' He replied, 'I came here while tracing the Buddha's voice.' That Buddha told him, 'Even if you followed the Buddha's voice for immeasurable koṭis of kalpas, you could not reach its limit.'

復次，佛出世，為斷眾生疑故為說法，此不應難！如不應問曰：「何以除闇？」佛亦如是。不應問佛何以故答！

Furthermore, a Buddha appears in the world and teaches the Dharma to remove beings' doubts. This should not be challenged! Just as one should not ask, 'Why dispel darkness?', one should not ask why the Buddha answers.

Equal Awakening and the Truth of Causes and Conditions

Chinese Source

問曰：諸佛等故，名為等覺，今何以稱言「是彼神力」？

English Translation

Question: Since Buddhas are equal, they are called equally awakened. Why, then, does he say, 'This is his spiritual power'?

答曰：示無吾我，彼此滅嫉慢故。

Answer: To show freedom from ego and possessiveness, and to remove jealousy and pride between oneself and others.

復次，世界有天常求尊勝，憍慢法故，自言天地、人物，是我化作。如梵天王謂諸梵言：「我作汝等。」毘紐天言：「世間有大富貴名聞人，皆是我身威德力分，我能成就世間，亦能破壞世間，世間成壞皆是我作。」有如是天，破因緣法相；諸佛實語不破因緣法相，故言「是彼佛神力」。

Furthermore, some devas in the world constantly seek supremacy. Out of pride, they claim, 'I created heaven and earth, people and all things.' For example, the Brahmā king tells the Brahmās, 'I created you.' Viṣṇu says, 'All the very wealthy, powerful, and famous people in the world possess a share of my majestic power. I can bring the world into being and destroy it. The world's formation and destruction are all my doing.' Such devas deny the nature of causes and conditions. Buddhas speak truthfully and do not deny it. Therefore, Jewel Accumulation says, 'This is that Buddha's spiritual power.'

Never Satisfied with Seeing Buddhas and Hearing the Dharma

Chinese Source

〖經〗是時，普明菩薩白寶積佛言：「世尊！我今當往見釋迦牟尼佛，禮拜、供養；及見彼諸菩薩摩訶薩紹尊位者，皆得陀羅尼及諸三昧，於諸三昧而得自在。」

English Translation

Sutra: Universal Radiance Bodhisattva then said to Jewel Accumulation Buddha, 'World-Honored One! I will now go to see Śākyamuni Buddha, bow to him, and make offerings. I will also see the bodhisattva mahāsattvas there who will succeed to the honored position, who have all attained dhāraṇīs and samādhis and gained mastery over the samādhis.'

〖論〗問曰：若諸佛持戒、禪定、智慧、度人皆等，是普明菩薩何以欲來見釋迦牟尼佛？

Treatise: Question: If all Buddhas are equal in keeping the precepts, meditative concentration, wisdom, and liberating others, why does Universal Radiance wish to come and see Śākyamuni Buddha?

答曰：諸菩薩常欲見佛無厭足，聽法無厭足，見諸菩薩僧無厭足；諸菩薩於世間法皆以厭患，於上三事心無厭足。

Answer: Bodhisattvas are never satisfied with seeing Buddhas, hearing the Dharma, or seeing the community of bodhisattvas. They are disenchanted with worldly things, but their hearts never tire of these three activities.

如手居士從淨居天來欲見佛，其身微細沒失，譬如消蘇，不得立地。佛語手居士：「汝化作麁身，觀此地相！」居士即如佛言化作麁身，觀念此地相，頭面禮佛足，一面立。

For example, the householder Hasta came from the Pure Abodes wishing to see the Buddha. His body was so subtle that it sank down like melting butter, and he could not stand on the ground. The Buddha told him, 'Manifest a coarser body and attend to the characteristics of this ground!' Following the Buddha's words, he manifested a coarser body, directed his attention to the ground, bowed with his head at the Buddha's feet, and stood to one side.

佛問居士：「汝幾事無厭生淨居天？」答言：「我三事無厭生淨居天：一、見諸佛供養無厭，二、聽法無厭，三、供給僧無厭。如佛在閻浮提，四部眾常隨逐佛，聽法、問法；是我淨居諸天，亦常從我聽法、問法。」

The Buddha asked, 'In how many things were you insatiable, leading to your birth in the Pure Abodes?' He replied, 'I was insatiable in three things and was born in the Pure Abodes: first, seeing Buddhas and making offerings; second, hearing the Dharma; and third, providing for the Saṅgha. Just as the fourfold community in Jambudvīpa constantly follows the Buddha to hear the Dharma and ask questions about it, the devas of the Pure Abodes constantly come to me to hear the Dharma and ask questions about it.'

聲聞猶尚聽法無厭足，何況法性身菩薩？以是故，普明菩薩來見釋迦牟尼佛。

Even a śrāvaka never tires of hearing the Dharma. How much more is this true of a bodhisattva with a body of Dharma nature! Therefore, Universal Radiance comes to see Śākyamuni Buddha.

「及見此間諸菩薩摩訶薩紹尊位者，皆得陀羅尼及諸三昧」，如先「讚菩薩品」中說。「於諸三昧而得自在」者。

The words 'and see the bodhisattva mahāsattvas here who will succeed to the honored position, who have all attained dhāraṇīs and samādhis' have been explained in the earlier section praising bodhisattvas. Next comes the explanation of 'gained mastery over the samādhis.'

Mastery of Bodhisattva Samādhis and the Power of Past Connections

Chinese Source

問曰：如佛一人，一切三昧中得自在，何以言「菩薩亦一切三昧中得自在」？

English Translation

Question: Since only a Buddha has mastery over all samādhis, why say that bodhisattvas also have mastery over all samādhis?

答曰：有二種三昧：一者佛三昧，二者菩薩三昧。是諸菩薩於菩薩三昧中得自在，非佛三昧中。

Answer: There are two kinds of samādhi: those of Buddhas and those of bodhisattvas. These bodhisattvas have mastery over bodhisattva samādhis, not over Buddha samādhis.

如說《諸佛要集經》中：文殊尸利欲見佛集，不能得到。諸佛各還本處，文殊尸利到諸佛集處，有一女人近彼佛坐入三昧，文殊尸利入，禮佛足已，白佛言：「云何此女人得近佛坐而我不得？」

As the Scripture of the Essential Assembly of Buddhas (《諸佛要集經》) relates, Mañjuśrī wished to attend an assembly of Buddhas but could not reach it. After the Buddhas had returned to their own abodes, Mañjuśrī reached the meeting place. A woman was sitting near the Buddha, absorbed in samādhi. Mañjuśrī entered, bowed at the Buddha's feet, and asked, 'Why is this woman able to sit near the Buddha, while I am not?'

佛告文殊尸利：「汝覺此女人，令從三昧起，汝自問之。」文殊尸利即彈指覺之而不可覺，以大聲喚亦不可覺，捉手牽亦不可覺，又以神足動三千大千世界猶亦不覺。文殊尸利白佛言：「世尊！我不能令覺。」

The Buddha told Mañjuśrī, 'Rouse this woman from samādhi and ask her yourself.' Mañjuśrī snapped his fingers to rouse her, but could not. Calling loudly did not rouse her; taking her hand and pulling did not rouse her. Even using his supernatural power to shake the great trichiliocosm did not rouse her. Mañjuśrī said, 'World-Honored One! I cannot rouse her.'

是時，佛放大光明照下方世界，是中有一菩薩名棄諸蓋，即時從下方出，來到佛所，頭面禮佛足，一面立。佛告棄諸蓋菩薩：「汝覺此女人！」即時彈指，此女從三昧起。

The Buddha then emitted a great light illuminating the worlds below. A bodhisattva there named Abandoning All Hindrances immediately rose from below, came to the Buddha, bowed with his head at the Buddha's feet, and stood to one side. The Buddha told him, 'Rouse this woman!' He snapped his fingers, and she immediately emerged from samādhi.

文殊尸利白佛言：「以何因緣，我動三千大千世界不能令此女起？棄諸蓋菩薩一彈指，便從三昧起？」

Mañjuśrī asked, 'Why could I not rouse this woman even by shaking the great trichiliocosm, while Abandoning All Hindrances brought her out of samādhi with a single snap of his fingers?'

佛告文殊尸利：「汝因此女人初發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提意，是女人因棄諸蓋菩薩初發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提意。以是故，汝不能令覺。汝於諸佛三昧中功德未滿。是諸菩薩三昧中得自在，佛三昧中少多入，而未得自在故耳。」

The Buddha told Mañjuśrī, 'It was through this woman that you first aroused the aspiration for unsurpassed complete awakening. It was through Abandoning All Hindrances Bodhisattva that she first aroused that aspiration. Therefore, you could not rouse her. Your merit in the samādhis of the Buddhas is not yet complete. These bodhisattvas have mastery over bodhisattva samādhis. They enter Buddha samādhis to some extent, but have not yet gained mastery over them.'

A Golden Lotus and Permission to Depart

Chinese Source

〖經〗佛告普明：「欲往隨意，宜知是時。」爾時，寶積佛以千葉金色蓮華與普明菩薩而告之曰：「善男子！汝以此華散釋迦牟尼佛上。生彼娑婆世界諸菩薩，難勝難及，汝當一心遊彼世界！」

English Translation

Sutra: The Buddha told Universal Radiance, 'Go if you wish. Know that this is the proper time.' Jewel Accumulation Buddha then gave him a golden lotus with a thousand petals and said, 'Good man! Scatter this flower over Śākyamuni Buddha. The bodhisattvas born in that Sahā world are difficult to surpass or equal. Travel in that world with an undivided mind!'

〖論〗問曰：佛何以言「欲往隨意，宜知是時」？

Treatise: Question: Why does the Buddha say, 'Go if you wish. Know that this is the proper time'?

答曰：佛於弟子愛斷故，於弟子中心不著故。

Answer: The Buddha has cut off possessive affection toward his disciples, and his mind does not cling to them.

復次，是菩薩未得一切智，未得佛眼故，心中少多有疑，謂釋迦牟尼佛功德大，所益或勝，是故語言：「欲往隨意。」

Furthermore, this bodhisattva has not yet attained all-knowledge or the Buddha eye. Some doubt remains in his mind: he thinks that Śākyamuni Buddha's merit is great and that the benefit he offers may be greater. Therefore, the Buddha tells him, 'Go if you wish.'

復次，是菩薩遙見釋迦牟尼佛身小，心生小慢，言彼佛不如。是故佛語：「汝往莫觀佛身，勿念世界，但聽佛說法！」

Furthermore, seeing Śākyamuni Buddha's small body from afar, this bodhisattva feels a little pride and thinks that Buddha inferior. Therefore, the Buddha tells him, 'When you go, do not judge by the Buddha's body or dwell on the world. Just listen to the Buddha teach the Dharma!'

復次，是世界離娑婆世界極遠，最在東邊。是諸菩薩聞釋迦牟尼佛所說諸法相，與寶積佛說諸法相正同，便言：「世界雖遠，法相不異！」增益大信，心轉堅固。

Furthermore, this world is extremely far from Sahā, at the farthest eastern edge. When these bodhisattvas hear Śākyamuni explain the characteristics of dharmas exactly as Jewel Accumulation Buddha does, they will say, 'Though the worlds are far apart, the characteristics of dharmas do not differ!' Their great faith will increase, and their minds will become more steadfast.

復次，先世因緣故，雖遠處生，應來聽法；譬如繩繫雀腳，雖復遠飛，攝之則還。

Furthermore, because of connections formed in past lives, they are meant to come and hear the Dharma even though born far away. It is like a bird with a cord tied to its foot: however far it flies, drawing in the cord brings it back.

復次，是娑婆世界中菩薩，見普明遠來聽法，便作是念：「彼從遠來，況我生此世界中而不聽法？」如是種種因緣，是故佛言：「欲往隨意，宜知是時。」

Furthermore, when bodhisattvas in the Sahā world see Universal Radiance coming from afar to hear the Dharma, they will think, 'He has come from so far away. How could I, who was born in this world, fail to hear the Dharma?' For these various reasons, the Buddha says, 'Go if you wish. Know that this is the proper time.'

Why Buddhas Send Offerings

Chinese Source

問曰：諸佛力等，更不求福，何故以華為信？

English Translation

Question: Since Buddhas are equal in power and no longer seek merit, why send a flower as a token?

答曰：隨世間法行故。如二國王力勢雖同，亦相贈遺。

Answer: They follow worldly conventions. Two kings may be equal in power and influence, yet still exchange gifts.

復次，示善軟心故，以華為信。世間法中，使從遠來，必應有信；佛隨世法，是故致信。

Furthermore, a flower is sent as a token to express kindness and gentleness. In worldly practice, a messenger arriving from afar should bring a token. Following this convention, the Buddha sends one.

復次，諸佛恭敬法故，供養於法，以法為師。何以故？三世諸佛，皆以諸法實相為師。

Furthermore, Buddhas honor the Dharma, make offerings to it, and take it as their teacher. Why? All Buddhas of the three times take the true nature of all dharmas as their teacher.

問曰：何以不自供養身中法，而供養他法？

Question: Why not make offerings to the Dharma within himself, rather than to the Dharma in another?

答曰：隨世間法，如比丘欲供養法寶，不自供養身中法，而供養餘持法、知法、解法者；佛亦如是，雖身中有法，而供養餘佛法。

Answer: This follows worldly practice. A monk wishing to honor the Dharma jewel does not make offerings to the Dharma within himself, but to others who uphold, know, and understand it. Likewise, although the Dharma is present within a Buddha, he makes offerings to the Dharma of another Buddha.

問曰：如佛不求福德，何以故供養？

Question: If a Buddha does not seek merit, why does he make offerings?

答曰：佛從無量阿僧祇劫中修諸功德，常行諸善，不但求報，敬功德故而作供養。

Answer: Through immeasurable asaṃkhyeya kalpas, the Buddha has cultivated merit and constantly practiced goodness. He does not act merely to obtain a reward. He makes offerings out of respect for merit.

The Buddha Threads a Blind Monk's Needle

Chinese Source

如佛在時，有一盲比丘，眼無所見而以手縫衣，時針絍脫，便言：「誰愛福德？為我絍針！」

English Translation

When the Buddha was alive, a blind monk was sewing his robe by touch. The thread slipped out of the needle, and he called, 'Who delights in merit? Please thread my needle!'

是時佛到其所，語比丘：「我是愛福德人，為汝絍針來！」是比丘識佛聲，疾起著衣禮佛足，白佛言：「佛功德已滿，云何言愛福德？」

The Buddha came to him and said, 'I delight in merit. I have come to thread your needle!' Recognizing the Buddha's voice, the monk quickly rose, put on his robe, and bowed at the Buddha's feet. He asked, 'Your merit is already complete. Why do you say that you delight in merit?'

佛報言：「我雖功德已滿，我深知功德恩，功德果報，功德力，令我於一切眾生中得最第一。由此功德，是故我愛。」佛為此比丘讚功德已，次為隨意說法。是比丘得法眼淨，肉眼更明。

The Buddha replied, 'Although my merit is complete, I deeply understand its kindness to me, its results, and its power. It has made me foremost among all beings. Because of what this merit has done, I delight in it.' After praising merit to the monk, the Buddha taught him the Dharma in a way suited to him. The monk gained the pure Dharma eye, and his physical sight was restored.

復次，佛雖功德已滿，更無所須，為教化弟子故，語之言：「我尚作功德，汝云何不作？」

Furthermore, although the Buddha's merit is complete and he needs nothing more, he teaches his disciples by saying, 'Even I still perform meritorious deeds. How can you fail to do so?'

如伎家百歲老翁而舞，有人呵之言：「老翁年已百歲，何用是舞？」翁答：「我不須舞，但欲教子孫故耳！」佛亦如是，功德雖滿，為教弟子作功德故，而作供養。

It is like a hundred-year-old performer who dances. Someone rebukes him: 'You are already a hundred years old. What use is this dancing?' He replies, 'I do not need to dance; I only wish to teach my children and grandchildren!' Likewise, although the Buddha's merit is complete, he makes offerings to teach his disciples to cultivate merit.

A large human lotus has more than ten petals; a heavenly lotus has a hundred; a bodhisattva lotus has a thousand. That world abounds in radiant golden lotuses with a thousand petals. In Sahā, thousand-petaled lotuses exist only as magical manifestations, not as flowers growing in water. Therefore, this lotus is sent. Its thousand petals and golden color were explained above in the discussion of the Buddha's tongue.

答曰：手自供養是身業，軟言問訊是口業，能起身、口業是意業。是三業得功德牢固，與佛道作因緣。

Answer: Making the offering with one's own hands is bodily action. Inquiring after someone with gentle words is verbal action. What initiates these bodily and verbal actions is mental action. Together, these three actions establish firm merit and become causes and conditions for the Buddha path.

Why Bodhisattvas of Sahā Are Difficult to Surpass

Chinese Source

English Translation

Furthermore, why say, 'Be single-minded, respectful, and careful: these bodhisattvas are difficult to surpass, equal, defeat, or approach'? A great lion king is difficult to overcome or defeat. A white elephant king, a dragon king, and a great blaze are also difficult to approach. Through the power of their great merit and wisdom, these bodhisattvas cannot be overcome or defeated. Anyone trying would only defeat himself. Therefore, they are called difficult to approach.

問曰：一切大菩薩皆大功德，智慧利根，一切難近，何以獨言「娑婆世界中菩薩難近」？

答曰：實如所言。但以多寶世界中菩薩遠來，見此世界不如，石沙穢惡，菩薩身小，一切眾事皆亦不如，必生輕慢，是故佛言：「一心敬慎，彼諸菩薩難近。」

Answer: What you say is true. But bodhisattvas coming from the world Many Jewels see this world as inferior: its stones, sand, and impurities; the small bodies of its bodhisattvas; and all its other circumstances seem inferior. They are bound to feel contempt. Therefore, the Buddha says, 'Be single-minded, respectful, and careful. Those bodhisattvas are difficult to approach.'

復次，樂處生人多不勇猛，不聰明，少智慧。如欝怛羅衛人，以大樂故，無出家、無受戒；諸天中亦爾。是娑婆世界中，是樂因緣少，有三惡道、老、病、死，土地自活法難，以是故，易得厭心。見老、病、死至，心大厭患；見貧窮人，知先世因緣所致，心生大厭。以是故智慧根利。

Furthermore, people born in pleasant places often lack vigor, discernment, and wisdom. For example, because the people of Uttarakuru enjoy such great happiness, they neither go forth nor undertake the precepts. The same applies among the devas. In Sahā, conditions for pleasure are few. There are the three bad destinies, aging, sickness, and death, and making a living from the land is difficult. Therefore, disenchantment arises readily. Seeing aging, sickness, and death approach, people become deeply disenchanted. Seeing poverty and knowing that it results from causes and conditions in past lives also brings deep disenchantment. Thus their faculties of wisdom are sharp.

彼間菩薩，七寶世界，種種寶樹，心念飲食，應意即得，如是生厭心難，是故智慧不能大利。譬如利刀，著好飲食中，刀便生垢，飲食雖好而與刀不相宜；若以石磨之，脂灰瑩治，垢除刀利。是菩薩亦如是，生雜世界中，利智難近。

By contrast, those bodhisattvas live in a world of seven jewels with many kinds of jeweled trees. Whatever food and drink they think of appears as they wish. Under such conditions, disenchantment is difficult to arouse, so wisdom cannot become especially sharp. A sharp knife left among fine foods becomes coated with grime. Though the food is good, it does not benefit the knife. Sharpening it on a stone and polishing it with grease and ash removes the grime and restores its edge. Likewise, bodhisattvas born in a world of mixed conditions have sharp wisdom and are difficult to approach.

Furthermore, bodhisattvas in Sahā are difficult to approach because they possess many skillful means. This is not the case elsewhere.

如佛說：「我自憶念宿世，一日施人千命。度眾生故，雖諸功德、六波羅蜜一切佛事具足，而不作佛，恒以方便度脫眾生。」以是事故，是娑婆世界中菩薩難近。

As the Buddha says, 'I recall former lives when, in a single day, I gave my life a thousand times for others. To liberate beings, although I had fulfilled all merit, the six pāramitās, and all the work of a Buddha, I did not become a Buddha, but constantly used skillful means to liberate beings.' For these reasons, bodhisattvas in Sahā are difficult to approach.

問曰：一切聖人不受報果，後更不生，云何言是施福最大？

Question: Noble ones do not receive karmic rewards and will not be born again. How can this gift be said to produce the greatest merit?

答曰：是福雖無人受，其相自大；若有人受者，其報無量。諸聖人知有為法皆無常、空故，捨入涅槃，是福亦捨；譬如燒金丸，雖眼見其好，不可以手觸，燒人手故。

Answer: Although no one receives its reward, the merit is great in its own character. If someone were to receive its result, that result would be immeasurable. Knowing all conditioned dharmas to be impermanent and empty, noble ones relinquish them and enter nirvāṇa, relinquishing this merit too. It is like a heated gold ball: although beautiful to the eye, it cannot be touched because it would burn the hand.

復次，如人有瘡，則須藥塗；若無瘡者，藥無所施。人有身亦如是，常為飢、渴、寒、熱所逼，亦如瘡發；以衣被、飲食，溫煖將適，如藥塗瘡。

Furthermore, someone with a sore needs medicine applied to it. Without a sore, the medicine has nowhere to be applied. Likewise, having a body means being constantly afflicted by hunger, thirst, cold, and heat, as if a sore had broken out. Clothing, bedding, food, drink, warmth, and care are like medicine applied to that sore.

如愚癡人，為貪藥故，不用除瘡；若其無瘡，藥亦無用。諸佛以身為瘡，捨放身瘡故，亦不受報藥。以是故，雖有大福，亦不受報。

A foolish person who craves the medicine may refuse to heal the sore; but without the sore, the medicine is no longer needed. Buddhas regard the body as a sore. Having relinquished that bodily sore, they no longer receive the medicine of karmic rewards. Therefore, although the merit is great, they do not receive its reward.

Are Directions Independently Real?

Chinese Source

〖論〗問曰：如佛法中，實無諸方名，何以故？諸五眾、十二入、十八界中所不攝，四法藏中亦無。說方是實法，因緣求亦不可得，今何以故此中說十方諸佛、十方菩薩來？

English Translation

Treatise: Question: In the Buddha Dharma, the directions do not truly exist as the names suggest. Why? They are not included in the five aggregates, twelve sense bases, or eighteen elements, nor in the four collections of dharmas. Even if one calls direction a real dharma, it cannot be found by examining causes and conditions. Why, then, does this passage speak of Buddhas of the ten directions and bodhisattvas arriving from them?

答曰：隨世俗法所傳故說「方」，求「方」實不可得。

Answer: We speak of directions according to inherited worldly convention. When their reality is sought, it cannot be found.

問曰：何以言無方？汝四法藏中不說，我六法藏中說。汝眾、入、界中不攝，我陀羅驃中攝。是「方」法，常相故，有相故，亦有亦常。

Question: Why say that directions do not exist? Your four collections do not mention them, but our six categories do. Your aggregates, sense bases, and elements do not include them, but our category of substance, dravya, does. Direction has the character of permanence and the character of existence. Therefore, it exists and is permanent.

As our scripture says, 'Where the sun rises is east; where it sets is west; where it travels is south; where it does not travel is north.' The sun has three phases of conjunction: previous, present, and subsequent. In relation to the sun's position, the first conjunction marks east; south and west are determined likewise. Where the sun does not travel has no such division.

復次，有處日不合，是為非「方」，無「方相」故。

Furthermore, a place with which the sun does not come into conjunction would then not be a direction, since it would lack the defining mark of direction.

答曰：若一國中，日與東方合是為有邊，有邊故無常，無常故是不遍。以是故，「方」但有名而無實。

Answer: If, within one country, the sun's conjunction with the east defines a boundary, then direction is bounded. Being bounded, it is impermanent; being impermanent, it is not all-pervading. Therefore, direction is only a name and has no independent reality.

That is why it is used as a comparison. An analogy can use something lesser to illustrate something greater, as when a beautiful face is likened to the full moon.

問曰：更有十方諸清淨世界，如阿彌陀佛安樂世界等，何以故但以普華世界為喻？

Question: There are other pure worlds throughout the ten directions, such as Amitābha Buddha’s World of Bliss. Why use only the world Universal Flowers as a comparison?

答曰：阿彌陀佛世界，不如華積世界。何以故？法積比丘，佛雖將至十方觀清淨世界，功德力薄，不能得見上妙清淨世界，以是故，世界不如。復次，當佛變化此世界時，正與華積世界相似。以是故言「譬如華積世界」。

Answer: Amitābha Buddha’s world does not equal Flower Accumulation. Why? Although a Buddha took the monk Dharma Accumulation to see pure worlds in the ten directions, his power of merit was limited, and he could not see the finest and most wondrous pure worlds. Therefore, his world does not equal it. Furthermore, when the Buddha transformed this world, it closely resembled Flower Accumulation. That is why the text says, ‘It was like the world Flower Accumulation.’

問曰：更有餘大菩薩，如毘摩羅詰、觀世音、遍吉菩薩等，何以不言此諸菩薩在彼住，而但言文殊尸利、善住意菩薩？

Question: There are other great bodhisattvas, such as Vimalakīrti, Avalokiteśvara, and Samantabhadra. Why does the text name only Mañjuśrī and Steadfast Mind as dwelling there?

答曰：是遍吉菩薩，一一毛孔常出諸佛世界及諸佛菩薩，遍滿十方以化眾生，無適住處。

Answer: From each pore, Samantabhadra constantly brings forth Buddha worlds, Buddhas, and bodhisattvas, filling the ten directions to teach beings. He has no fixed dwelling.

文殊尸利分身變化入五道中，或作聲聞，或作緣覺，或作佛身，如《首楞嚴三昧經》中說「文殊師利菩薩，過去世作龍種尊佛」，「七十二億世作辟支迦佛」，是可言可說。

Mañjuśrī manifests multiple bodies and enters the five destinies, appearing as a śrāvaka, a pratyekabuddha, or a Buddha. The Śūraṅgama Samādhi Sūtra says that Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva was once the Buddha Honored of the Dragon Lineage and that he was a pratyekabuddha for seventy-two koṭis of lives. Such things can be put into words.

遍吉菩薩不可量、不可說，住處不可知；若住，應在一切世界中住，是故不說。復次，「及諸大威神菩薩」者，亦應總說遍吉等諸大菩薩。

Samantabhadra, however, cannot be measured or described, and his dwelling cannot be known. If he dwells anywhere, he must dwell in all worlds. That is why he is not named. Moreover, ‘other bodhisattvas of great spiritual power’ should be understood to include Samantabhadra and other great bodhisattvas.

Why the Assembly Gathered

Chinese Source

〖經〗爾時，佛知一切世界，若天世界，若魔世界，若梵世界；若沙門、若婆羅門及天，若揵闥婆、人、阿修羅等，及諸菩薩摩訶薩紹尊位者一切皆集。

English Translation

Sutra: At that time, the Buddha knew that all had assembled: those of the worlds of devas, Māra, and Brahmā; ascetics, brahmins, devas, gandharvas, humans, asuras, and others; and the bodhisattva mahāsattvas who were to succeed to the honored position.

〖論〗問曰：佛神力無量，一切十方眾生，若盡來在會者，一切世界應空；若不來者，佛無量神力有所不能！

Treatise: Question: The Buddha’s spiritual power is immeasurable. If all beings in the ten directions came to the assembly, all the worlds should be empty. If they did not come, there would be something his immeasurable spiritual power could not accomplish!

答曰：不應盡來，何以故？諸佛世界無邊無量，若盡來者，便為有邊。又復十方各各有佛，亦說般若波羅蜜，如彼《般若波羅蜜》四十三品中，「十方面各千佛現，皆說般若波羅蜜」。以是故不應盡來。

Answer: They need not all come. Why? Buddha worlds are boundless and immeasurable; if all their beings came, there would be a limit. Furthermore, each direction has Buddhas who also teach Prajñāpāramitā. As chapter forty-three of the Prajñāpāramitā says, ‘A thousand Buddhas appear in each of the ten directions, and all teach Prajñāpāramitā.’ Thus, there is no need for all beings to come.

問曰：若有十方諸佛，皆說般若波羅蜜，十方諸菩薩何以故來？答曰：如「普明菩薩來章」中已說，與釋迦牟尼佛因緣故來。

Question: If Buddhas in all ten directions teach Prajñāpāramitā, why do bodhisattvas from all ten directions come here? Answer: As explained in the section on Universal Radiance’s arrival, they come because of their connections with Śākyamuni Buddha.

復次，是諸菩薩本願故，若有說般若波羅蜜處，我當聽受、供養，是以遠來。欲以身力積功德故，亦以示諸眾生：「我從遠來供養法故，云何汝在此世界而不供養？」

Furthermore, these bodhisattvas originally vowed, ‘Wherever Prajñāpāramitā is taught, I will listen, receive it, and make offerings.’ That is why they come from afar. They wish to accumulate merit through their own efforts and to show other beings: ‘We have come from far away to honor the Dharma. How can you live in this very world and fail to honor it?’

問曰：佛於法不著，何以故七現神力而令眾生大集？答曰：是般若波羅蜜甚深，難知難解，不可思議，是故廣集諸大菩薩，令新發意者心得信樂。譬如小人所語不為人信，貴重大人人必信受。

Question: Since the Buddha does not cling to dharmas, why does he display spiritual power seven times and bring together a great assembly? Answer: Prajñāpāramitā is profound, difficult to know or understand, and beyond conception. He therefore gathers many great bodhisattvas so that those who have newly aroused the aspiration may gain faith and delight. People may not trust what someone of little standing says, but they readily accept the words of someone they hold in high regard.

The Worlds of Devas, Māra, and Brahmā

Chinese Source

問曰：何以故言「若天世界，若魔世界，若梵世界」？但應言「天世界、人世界」則足，何以故？十號中言「天、人師」，以是故應言「天、人」而已。答曰：諸天有天眼、天耳，利根智慧多，自知來，以是故言「天世界」。

English Translation

Question: Why name the worlds of devas, Māra, and Brahmā? It should suffice to say the worlds of devas and humans, since one of the Buddha’s ten titles is Teacher of Devas and Humans. Answer: Devas have heavenly eyes and ears, keen faculties, and much wisdom. They know on their own to come, so the text names the world of devas.

問曰：若天世界已攝魔、梵，何以故別說「若魔、若梵」？答曰：天中有三大主：釋提婆那民，二處天主；魔王，六欲天主；梵世界中，大梵天王為主。

Question: If the world of devas already includes Māra and Brahmā, why name them separately? Answer: There are three great rulers among the devas. Śakra rules two heavenly realms; Māra rules the six heavens of the desire realm; and Great Brahmā rules the Brahmā world.

問曰：如夜魔天、兜率陀天、化樂天皆有主，何以但有三主？答曰：釋提婆那民依地住，佛亦在地住，常來佛所，大有名稱，人多識故。魔王常來嬈佛，又是一切欲界中主；夜摩天、兜率陀天、化樂天，皆屬魔王。復次，天世界，則三界天皆攝是天中；一切欲界，魔為主，是故別說。復次，魔常嬈佛，今來聽般若波羅蜜，餘人增益信故。

Question: The Yāma, Tuṣita, and Delight in Creation heavens each have a ruler. Why speak of only three rulers? Answer: Śakra dwells on the earth, as does the Buddha. He often visits the Buddha, is renowned, and is widely known. Māra often harasses the Buddha and rules the whole desire realm. The Yāma, Tuṣita, and Delight in Creation heavens are all subject to Māra. Furthermore, ‘world of devas’ includes the devas of all three realms. Māra rules the entire desire realm, so he is named separately. Though he often harasses the Buddha, he now comes to hear Prajñāpāramitā, strengthening the faith of others.

問曰：色界中大有天，何以但言「梵世界集」？答曰：上諸天無覺觀，不喜散心，又難聞故。梵世界有四識，易聞故。又梵世界近故。復次，梵名離欲清淨，今言梵世界，已總說色界諸天。

Question: There are many devas in the form realm. Why speak only of the Brahmā world assembling? Answer: The higher devas are without initial and sustained mental application; they do not favor a dispersed mind and have difficulty hearing. The Brahmā world has four kinds of consciousness, so hearing is easier there. It is also near. Furthermore, ‘Brahmā’ means purity free of desire; naming the Brahmā world already includes the devas of the form realm.

復次，餘天未有人民，劫初生時，梵天王獨在梵宮寂漠無人，其心不悅而自生念：「此間何以不生人民？」是時光音天命盡者，應念來生。梵王便自生念：「此諸天先無，隨我念故生，我能生此諸天。」諸天是時亦各自念：「我從梵王生，梵王是我父也。」以是故，但說「梵世界」。

Furthermore, at the beginning of a kalpa, when the other heavens were not yet populated, Brahmā dwelt alone in his palace. Unhappy in its solitude, he thought, ‘Why are no inhabitants born here?’ Just then, devas whose lives in the Heaven of Streaming Radiance had ended were reborn there in accordance with his thought. Brahmā thought, ‘These devas were not here before. They were born in response to my thought; I can bring these devas into being.’ They each thought, ‘I was born from Brahmā; Brahmā is my father.’ For this reason, the text speaks only of the Brahmā world.

復次，二禪、三禪、四禪天，於欲界見佛聽法，若勸助菩薩，眼識、耳識、身識皆在梵世界中取。以是故，別說「梵世界」。

Furthermore, when devas of the second, third, and fourth dhyānas see a Buddha and hear the Dharma in the desire realm, or encourage bodhisattvas, they draw on visual, auditory, and bodily consciousness from the Brahmā world. Therefore, the Brahmā world is named separately.

Ascetics, Brahmins, Gandharvas, and Asuras

Chinese Source

問曰：何以故獨說「諸沙門、婆羅門」，不說國王及長者諸餘人眾？答曰：智慧人有二分：沙門、婆羅門。出家名沙門，在家名婆羅門。餘人心存世樂，是故不說。婆羅門多學智慧求福，出家人一切求道，是故但說沙門、婆羅門。在家中七世清淨，生滿六歲皆受戒，名婆羅門。是沙門、婆羅門中，有道德智慧，以是故說。

English Translation

Question: Why name ascetics and brahmins in particular, omitting kings, household elders, and others? Answer: The wise are of two groups: ascetics and brahmins. Those who have gone forth are called ascetics, and those who live at home are called brahmins. Others set their minds on worldly pleasures, so they are not named. Brahmins often pursue learning and wisdom and seek merit, while those who have gone forth all seek the path. Therefore, only ascetics and brahmins are mentioned. Among householders, those whose lineage is pure for seven generations and who receive precepts at the age of six are called brahmins. Among ascetics and brahmins are people of virtue and wisdom, which is why they are mentioned.

問曰：先已說天世界，今何以復說「天」？答曰：天世界是四天王、忉利天，魔是他化自在天，梵是色界。今說「天」，是欲界中夜摩、兜率陀、化樂、愛身天等；愛身在六天上，形色絕妙，故言愛身。

Question: The world of devas was already mentioned. Why mention devas again? Answer: Here, ‘world of devas’ refers to the Four Heavenly Kings and the Thirty-Three; Māra refers to Mastery over Others’ Creations; and Brahmā refers to the form realm. ‘Devas’ here includes those of Yāma, Tuṣita, Delight in Creation, and the Cherished Bodies heaven in the desire realm. The Cherished Bodies dwell above the six heavens. Their forms are extraordinarily beautiful, hence their name.

問曰：何以但說「揵闥婆」，不說諸餘鬼神及龍王？答曰：是揵闥婆是諸天伎人，隨逐諸天，其心柔軟，福德力小減諸天。諸鬼神，鬼神道中攝；龍王，畜生道中攝。

Question: Why mention only gandharvas and omit other spirits and dragon kings? Answer: Gandharvas are musicians of the devas and follow them. Their minds are gentle, and their merit is slightly less than that of devas. Other spirits belong to the spirit destiny, while dragon kings belong to the animal destiny.

甄陀羅亦是天伎，皆屬天，與天同住共坐，飲食伎樂皆與天同。是揵闥婆王名童籠磨（秦言樹）。是揵闥婆、甄陀羅，恒在二處住，常所居止在十寶山間；有時天上為諸天作樂，此二種常番休上下。

Kiṃnaras are also heavenly musicians. They belong to the devas, dwell and sit with them, and share their food, drink, and music. The gandharva king is named Tonglongmo (童籠磨), whose name means ‘tree.’ Gandharvas and kiṃnaras stay in two places. They usually dwell among the ten jeweled mountains, but sometimes go up to the heavens to play music for the devas. The two groups take turns going up and coming down.

人在四天下生，生有四種，極長壽乃至無量歲，極短壽乃至十歲。阿修羅惡心鬪諍而不破戒，大修施福，生在大海邊住，亦有城郭宮殿。是阿修羅王名毘摩質多、婆梨、羅睺羅，如是等名阿修羅王。

Humans are born on the four continents, and there are four kinds of birth. The longest lives last immeasurable years; the shortest last only ten. Asuras are hostile and quarrelsome but do not break the precepts, and they cultivate great merit through giving. They are born along the great ocean, where they have cities and palaces. Among the asura kings are Vemacitra, Bali, and Rāhu.

Rāhu Releases the Moon

Chinese Source

如說：一時，羅睺羅阿修羅王欲噉月，月天子怖疾到佛所，說偈言：

English Translation

For example, when the asura king Rāhu was about to swallow the moon, the moon deva hurried to the Buddha in fear and spoke this verse:

「大智成就佛世尊，我今歸命稽首禮；是羅睺羅惱亂我，願佛憐愍見救護！」

‘World-Honored Buddha, perfect in great wisdom, I now take refuge and bow before you. Rāhu is harassing me. Please have compassion and come to my aid!’

佛與羅睺羅而說偈言：

The Buddha spoke this verse to Rāhu:

「月能照闇而清涼，是虛空中大燈明，其色白淨有千光，汝莫吞月疾放去！」

‘The cool moon lights the darkness, a great lamp in the sky. Pure white, it shines with a thousand rays. Do not swallow the moon; let it go at once!’

是時，羅睺羅怖懅流汗，即疾放月。婆梨阿修羅王見羅睺羅惶怖放月，說偈問曰：

Rāhu was terrified and broke out in a sweat. He quickly released the moon. Seeing him release it in fear, the asura king Bali asked in verse:

「汝羅睺羅何以故，惶怖戰慄疾放月？汝身流汗如病人，心怖不安乃如是！」

‘Rāhu, why do you tremble in terror and release the moon so quickly? You sweat like someone who is ill. Why is your mind so frightened and uneasy?’

羅睺羅爾時說偈答曰：

Rāhu replied in verse:

「世尊以偈而勅我，我不放月頭七分；設得生活不安隱，以故我今放此月！」

‘The World-Honored One commanded me in verse. If I held the moon, my head would split into seven pieces. Even if I lived, I would have no peace. That is why I release the moon.’

婆梨阿修羅王說偈：

The asura king Bali then spoke this verse:

「諸佛甚難值，久遠乃出世；說此清淨偈，羅睺即放月。」

‘Buddhas are exceedingly hard to encounter, appearing only after long ages. When the Buddha spoke this pure verse, Rāhu immediately released the moon.’

Who Can Receive the Dharma?

Chinese Source

問曰：何以不說地獄、畜生、餓鬼？答曰：地獄大苦心亂，不能受法；畜生愚癡覆心，不能受化；餓鬼為飢渴火燒身故，不得受法。復次，畜生、餓鬼中，少多有來聽法者，生福德心而已，不堪受道，是故不說。

English Translation

Question: Why are beings in hell, animals, and hungry ghosts not mentioned? Answer: The intense suffering of hell disturbs the mind, so those beings cannot receive the Dharma. Animals’ minds are obscured by delusion, so they cannot receive instruction. Hungry ghosts burn with hunger and thirst, so they cannot receive the Dharma. Furthermore, a few animals and hungry ghosts do come to listen and arouse thoughts of merit, but they cannot receive the path. Therefore, they are not mentioned.

問曰：若爾者，揵闥婆、阿修羅亦不應說，何以故？鬼神道中已攝故。答曰：佛不說攝，今何以言攝？此是迦旃延子等說。如阿修羅力與天等，或時戰鬪勝天。揵闥婆是諸天伎，與天同受福樂，有智慧能別好醜，何以不得受道法？

Question: If so, gandharvas and asuras should also be omitted, since they are already included in the spirit destiny. Answer: The Buddha did not classify them this way. Why say that he did? That is the teaching of Kātyāyanīputra and others. Asuras equal devas in power and sometimes defeat them in battle. Gandharvas are heavenly musicians who share the devas’ enjoyments and have the discernment to distinguish what is good from what is bad. Why should they be unable to receive the teaching of the path?

如《雜阿含．天品》中說：「富那婆藪鬼神母，佛遊行宿其處。爾時，世尊說上妙法甘露，女男二人啼泣，母為說偈止之：

The Deva section of the Saṃyukta Āgama relates that, while traveling, the Buddha lodged at the dwelling of the spirit mother of Punarvasu. As the World-Honored One taught the sublime nectar of Dharma, her daughter and son cried. She quieted them with a verse:

「『汝欝怛羅勿作聲，富那婆藪亦莫啼！我今聞法得道證，汝亦當得必如我。』」

‘Uttara, be quiet; Punarvasu, do not cry! Hearing the Dharma now, I have realized the path. You too will surely attain what I have attained.’

以是事故，知鬼神中有得道者。

From this we know that some spirits attain the path.

復次，摩訶衍中密迹金剛力士，於諸菩薩中勝，何況餘人！如屯崙摩甄陀羅王、揵闥婆王，至佛所，彈琴讚佛，三千世界皆為震動，乃至摩訶迦葉不安其坐。如此人等，云何不能得道？

Furthermore, in the Mahāyāna, Vajrapāṇi of the Secret Traces excels among bodhisattvas, so why rule out others? When the kiṃnara king Tunlunmo and the gandharva king came before the Buddha and praised him to the sound of stringed instruments, the great world system shook, and even Mahākāśyapa could not sit still. How could such beings be unable to attain the path?

如諸阿修羅王、龍王，皆到佛所，問佛深法，佛隨其問而答深義，何以言不能得道？

Asura kings and dragon kings also visit the Buddha and ask about profound Dharma. The Buddha answers their questions with profound explanations. Why say they cannot attain the path?

問曰：於五道眾生中，佛是天人師，不說三惡道，以其無福、無受道分故。是諸龍、鬼，皆墮惡道中。

Question: Among beings of the five destinies, the Buddha is the Teacher of Devas and Humans. He does not name the three bad destinies because their beings lack merit and are not in a position to receive the path. Dragons and spirits have all fallen into bad destinies.

答曰：佛亦不分明說五道，說五道者，是一切有部僧所說；婆蹉弗妬路部僧說有六道。復次，應有六道。何以故？三惡道一向是罪處；若福多罪少，是名阿修羅、揵闥婆等，生處應別。以是故，應言六道。

Answer: The Buddha did not explicitly teach five destinies. The Sarvāstivāda community teaches five; the Vātsīputrīya community teaches six. Furthermore, there should be six destinies. Why? The three bad destinies are places of wrongdoing. Beings with much merit and little wrongdoing, such as asuras and gandharvas, should have a distinct place of rebirth. Therefore, one should speak of six destinies.

復次，三惡道亦有受道福，少故言無。及諸菩薩紹尊位者，如先說。

Furthermore, even in the three bad destinies, some have the merit to receive the path. Because they are few, the text speaks as though there were none. The bodhisattvas who are to succeed to the honored position have been explained above.
