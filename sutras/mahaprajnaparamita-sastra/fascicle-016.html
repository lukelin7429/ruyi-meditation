The Perfection of Diligence

Fascicle 16 explains the characteristics of diligence and compassionate resolve on the bodhisattva path.

Translation Method

Each Chinese paragraph appears beside its English counterpart.

Part Twenty-Seven: The Characteristics of Diligence

Chinese Source

問曰：云何名精進相？

English Translation

Question: What are the characteristics of diligence?

答曰：於事必能，起發無難，志意堅強，心無疲惓，所作究竟，以此五事為精進相。

Answer: Being capable of carrying out an undertaking, initiating it without difficulty, remaining firm in aspiration, keeping the mind free of weariness, and bringing the work to completion: these five are the characteristics of diligence.

復次，如佛所說：「精進相者，身、心不息故。」

Furthermore, as the Buddha said, “Diligence is characterized by body and mind not ceasing their effort.”

譬如釋迦牟尼佛，先世曾作賈客主，將諸賈人入嶮難處；是中有羅剎鬼，以手遮之言：「汝住莫動，不聽汝去！」賈客主即以右拳擊之，拳即著鬼，挽不可離；復以左拳擊之，亦不可離；以右足蹴之，足復粘著；復以左足蹴之，亦復如是；以頭衝之，頭即復著。

For example, in a former life Śākyamuni Buddha was the leader of a merchant caravan. Leading the merchants through a dangerous place, he encountered a rākṣasa who blocked the way with its hand and said, “Stop! Do not move. I shall not let you leave!” The merchant leader struck it with his right fist, which stuck to the demon and could not be pulled free. He struck with his left fist, which also could not be freed. He kicked with his right foot, which stuck as well; the same happened with his left foot. He rammed it with his head, which also stuck.

鬼問言：「汝今如是，欲作何等？心休息未？」答言：「雖復五事被繫，我心終不為汝息也。當以精進力與汝相擊，要不懈退！」鬼時歡喜，心念此人膽力極大，即語人言：「汝精進力大，必不休息，放汝令去！」

The demon asked, “Now that you are like this, what will you do? Has your mind given up?” He replied, “Although these five parts are bound, my mind will never cease striving against you. I shall fight you with the power of diligence and will certainly not slacken or retreat!” Delighted, the demon thought, “This person's courage is immense,” and said, “Your diligence is great and will surely not cease. I shall let you go!”

行者如是，於善法中，初夜、中夜、後夜，誦經、坐禪，求諸法實相，不為諸結使所覆，身心不懈，是名精進相。

A practitioner is similar: throughout the first, middle, and last watches of the night, one recites scriptures, meditates, and seeks the true character of phenomena among wholesome practices. Uncovered by the fetters and without slackening in body or mind, one displays the characteristics of diligence.

是精進名心數法，懃行不住相；隨心行，共心生；或有覺有觀，或無覺有觀，或無覺無觀。如阿毘曇法廣說。於一切善法中懃修不懈，是名精進相。

Diligence is a mental factor characterized by sustained, unceasing practice. It follows the activity of mind and arises together with mind. It may occur with both initial and sustained application of thought, with sustained application alone, or with neither, as extensively explained in the Abhidharma. Tireless cultivation of all wholesome qualities is called the characteristic of diligence.

於五根中，名精進根；根增長名精進力；心能開悟，名精進覺；能到佛道涅槃城，是名正精進；四念處中，能懃繫心，是精進分；四正懃，是精進門；四如意足中，欲、精進即是精進；六波羅蜜中，名精進波羅蜜。

Among the five faculties, it is the faculty of diligence; when that faculty grows, it is the power of diligence. When it enables the mind to awaken, it is the awakening factor of diligence. Leading to Buddhahood and the city of nirvāṇa, it is right diligence. In the four foundations of mindfulness, earnestly sustaining attention is the aspect of diligence. The four right efforts are gateways of diligence. Among the four bases of spiritual power, aspiration and diligence constitute this effort. Among the six perfections, it is called the perfection of diligence.

Diligence and Its Perfection

Chinese Source

問曰：汝先讚精進，今說精進相，是名何精進？

English Translation

Question: You previously praised diligence and now explain its characteristics. What kind of diligence is this?

答曰：是一切善法中精進相。

Answer: These are the characteristics of diligence in all wholesome qualities.

問曰：今說摩訶般若波羅蜜論議中，應說精進波羅蜜，何以故說一切善法中精進？

Question: In this discussion of the great perfection of wisdom, you should explain the perfection of diligence. Why discuss diligence in all wholesome qualities?

答曰：初發心菩薩，於一切善法中精進，漸漸次第得精進波羅蜜。

Answer: A bodhisattva who has newly aroused the aspiration practices diligence in all wholesome qualities and gradually, in sequence, attains the perfection of diligence.

問曰：一切善法中精進多，今說精進波羅蜜，已入一切善法精進中。

Question: Diligence in all wholesome qualities is extensive. The perfection of diligence now being discussed is already included within it.

答曰：為佛道精進，名為波羅蜜；諸餘善法中精進，但名精進，不名波羅蜜。

Answer: Diligence for Buddhahood is called a perfection. Diligence in other wholesome qualities is simply called diligence, not a perfection.

問曰：一切善法中懃，何以不名精進波羅蜜；而獨名菩薩精進為波羅蜜？

Question: Why is diligence in all wholesome qualities not called the perfection of diligence, while a bodhisattva's diligence alone is called a perfection?

答曰：波羅蜜名到彼岸。世間人及聲聞、辟支佛，不能具足行諸波羅蜜，是故不名為精進波羅蜜。

Answer: Pāramitā means reaching the far shore. Worldly people, śrāvakas, and pratyekabuddhas cannot fully practice the perfections. Thus their effort is not called the perfection of diligence.

復次，是人無大慈、大悲，棄捨眾生；不求十力、四無所畏、十八不共法、一切智，及無礙解脫、無量身、無量光明、無量音聲、無量持戒、禪定、智慧。以是故，是人精進不名波羅蜜。

Furthermore, these people lack great loving-kindness and great compassion and abandon living beings. They do not seek the ten powers, four fearlessnesses, eighteen unique qualities, all-knowing wisdom, unobstructed liberation, immeasurable bodies, light, voices, ethical discipline, concentration, and wisdom. Thus their diligence is not called a perfection.

復次，菩薩精進，不休、不息，一心求佛道，如是行者，名為精進波羅蜜。如好施菩薩求如意珠，抒大海水，正使筋骨枯盡，終不懈廢；得如意珠，以給眾生，濟其身苦。菩薩如是難為能為，是為菩薩精進波羅蜜。

Furthermore, a bodhisattva is diligent without resting or ceasing, wholeheartedly seeking Buddhahood. Such practice is called the perfection of diligence. For example, the bodhisattva Delight in Giving sought a wish-fulfilling jewel by emptying the great ocean. Even if sinews and bones withered away, he would never slacken or abandon the task. Having obtained the jewel, he supplied living beings with what they needed and relieved their bodily suffering. Accomplishing such difficult deeds is a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence.

復次，菩薩以精進力為首，行五波羅蜜，是時，名為菩薩精進波羅蜜。譬如眾藥和合，能治重病；菩薩精進亦如是，但行精進，不能行五波羅蜜，是不名菩薩精進波羅蜜。

Furthermore, when a bodhisattva takes the power of diligence as the lead in practicing the other five perfections, this is called a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence. It is like medicines combined to treat a grave illness. A bodhisattva's diligence is similar: merely practicing diligence without the other five perfections is not called a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence.

復次，菩薩精進，不為財利、富貴、力勢，亦不為身，不為生天、轉輪王、梵、釋、天王，亦不自為以求涅槃，但為佛道，利益眾生。如是相，名為菩薩精進波羅蜜。

Furthermore, a bodhisattva's diligence is not for wealth, profit, rank, power, or the body; not for birth in heaven or becoming a wheel-turning king, Brahmā, Śakra, or another heavenly king; nor for one's own nirvāṇa. It is solely for Buddhahood and the benefit of living beings. Such characteristics are called a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence.

復次，菩薩精進，修行一切善法，大悲為首。如慈父愛子，唯有一子而得重病，一心求藥，救療其病；菩薩精進，以慈為首，亦復如是，救療一切，心無暫捨。

Furthermore, a bodhisattva diligently cultivates all wholesome qualities with great compassion as the guide. It is like a loving father whose only child becomes gravely ill: he wholeheartedly seeks medicine to cure the child. A bodhisattva's diligence, guided by loving-kindness, is the same, healing and rescuing all without abandoning them in the mind for even a moment.

復次，菩薩精進，以實相智慧為首，行六波羅蜜，是名菩薩精進波羅蜜。

Furthermore, a bodhisattva practices the six perfections with diligence guided by wisdom concerning the true character of phenomena. This is called a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence.

問曰：諸法實相無為無作；精進有為有作相，云何以實相為首？

Question: The true character of phenomena is unconditioned and without activity. Diligence has the characteristics of conditioning and activity. How can the true character guide it?

答曰：雖知諸法實相無為無作，以本願大悲欲度眾生故，於無作中，以精進力度脫一切。復次，若諸法實相，無為無作、如涅槃相、無一無二，汝云何言「實相與精進相異」耶？汝即不解諸法相。

Answer: Although one knows the true character of phenomena to be unconditioned and without activity, through one's original vows and great compassion one wishes to liberate beings. Thus, within nonactivity, one liberates all through the power of diligence. Furthermore, if the true character is unconditioned and without activity, like nirvāṇa, neither one nor two, how can you say that its characteristics differ from those of diligence? You do not understand the characteristics of phenomena.

Contemplating Beings in the Five Destinies

Chinese Source

復次，爾時菩薩觀三界、五道眾生，各失所樂。無色界天，樂定心著，不覺命盡，墮在欲界中，受禽獸形。色界諸天，亦復如是，從清淨處墮，還受婬欲，在不淨中。欲界六天，樂著五欲，還墮地獄，受諸苦痛。見人道中，以十善福貿得人身，人身多苦少樂，壽盡多墮惡趣中。

English Translation

Furthermore, a bodhisattva then observes beings in the three realms and five destinies, each losing the happiness they cherish. Devas of the formless realm cling to the pleasure of concentration, unaware that their lives are ending, and fall into the desire realm to take animal forms. Devas of the form realm are similar: falling from pure places, they again experience sexual desire amid impurity. Devas of the six desire heavens cling to the five sensual pleasures and fall again into hell to suffer. In the human destiny, beings gain a human body through the merit of the ten wholesome actions, but human life has much suffering and little pleasure. At life's end, many fall into unfortunate destinies.

見諸畜生，受諸苦惱，鞭杖驅馳，負重涉遠，項領穿壞，熱鐵燒爍。此人宿行因緣，以繫縛眾生，鞭杖苦惱；如是等種種因緣故，受象、馬、牛、羊、麞、鹿畜獸之形。

One sees animals suffering: driven by whips and sticks, carrying heavy loads over long distances, their necks and shoulders torn and their bodies seared with hot iron. Through past actions of binding beings and tormenting them with whips and sticks, and through various similar conditions, they take the forms of elephants, horses, cattle, sheep, deer, and other animals.

婬欲情重，無明偏多，受鵝、鴨、孔雀、鴛鴦、鳩、鴿、鷄、鶩、鸚鵡，百舌之屬，受此眾鳥，種類百千。婬行罪故，身生毛羽，隔諸細滑，嘴𭉍麁䩕，不別觸味。

Through strong sexual desire and abundant ignorance, beings become geese, ducks, peacocks, mandarin ducks, doves, pigeons, chickens, wild ducks, parrots, mockingbirds, and hundreds of thousands of other kinds of birds. Because of sexual wrongdoing, their bodies grow fur or feathers, separating them from delicate, smooth contact. Their beaks are coarse and hard, unable to distinguish touch and taste.

瞋恚偏多，受毒蛇、蝮蝎、蚑蜂、百足，含毒之虫。愚癡多故，受蚓蛾、蜣蜋、蟻螻、鵂鷖、角鵄之屬，諸騃虫鳥。憍慢、瞋恚多故，受師子、虎、豹諸猛獸身。

Through predominant anger, they become venomous snakes, vipers, scorpions, wasps, centipedes, and other poisonous creatures. Through abundant delusion, they become worms, moths, dung beetles, ants, and various owls—foolish insects and birds. Through abundant pride and anger, they take the bodies of lions, tigers, leopards, and other fierce beasts.

邪慢緣故，受生驢、猪、駱駝之中。慳貪、嫉妬、輕躁、短促故，受獼猴、𤠙玃、熊、羆之形。邪貪、憎嫉業因緣故，受猫、狸、土虎諸獸之身。無愧、無慚、饕餮因緣故，受烏鵲、鵄鷲諸鳥之形。輕慢善人故，受鷄、狗、野干等身。大作布施，瞋恚、曲心，以此因緣故，受諸龍身。大修布施，心高陵瘧，苦惱眾生，受金翅鳥形。如是等種種結使業因緣故，受諸畜生禽獸之苦。

Through the conditions of perverse conceit, they are born as donkeys, pigs, and camels. Through stinginess, greed, jealousy, restlessness, and a hasty temper, they become monkeys, apes, and bears. Through wrongful greed, hatred, and envy, they become cats, wildcats, and beasts such as earth-tigers. Through shamelessness, lack of moral concern, and gluttony, they become crows, magpies, kites, and vultures. Through contempt for good people, they become chickens, dogs, jackals, and similar animals. Through great giving combined with anger and a crooked mind, they become dragons. Through extensive giving combined with arrogance, oppression, and tormenting beings, they become garuḍas. Through these and various other karmic conditions involving the fetters, they suffer as animals and birds.

The Continuous Turning of Rebirth

Chinese Source

菩薩得天眼，觀眾生輪轉五道，迴旋其中：天中死，人中生；人中死，天中生；天中死，生地獄中；地獄中死，生天上；天上死，生餓鬼中；餓鬼中死，還生天上；天上死，生畜生中；畜生中死，生天上；天上死，還生天上。地獄、餓鬼、畜生亦如是。

English Translation

Having attained the heavenly eye, a bodhisattva observes beings revolving through the five destinies: dying among devas and being born human, dying human and being born among devas, dying among devas and being born in hell, dying in hell and being born among devas, dying among devas and being born as hungry ghosts, dying as hungry ghosts and returning to the devas, dying among devas and being born as animals, dying as animals and being born among devas, or dying among devas and again being born among devas. The same applies to hell beings, hungry ghosts, and animals.

欲界中死，色界中生；色界中死，欲界中生；欲界中死，無色界中生；無色界中死，欲界中生；欲界中死，欲界中生。色界、無色界亦如是。

Dying in the desire realm, beings are born in the form realm; dying in the form realm, they are born in the desire realm. Dying in the desire realm, they are born in the formless realm; dying in the formless realm, they are born in the desire realm. Dying in the desire realm, they are again born there. The same applies to the form and formless realms.

活地獄中死，黑繩地獄中生；黑繩地獄中死，活地獄中生；活地獄中死，還生活地獄中；合會地獄乃至阿鼻地獄，亦如是。炭坑地獄中死，沸𡱁地獄中生；沸屎地獄中死，炭坑地獄中生；炭坑地獄中死，還生炭坑地獄中。燒林地獄乃至摩訶波頭摩地獄，亦如是展轉生其中。

Dying in the Reviving Hell, beings are born in the Black Rope Hell; dying in the Black Rope Hell, they are born in the Reviving Hell; or dying in the Reviving Hell, they are again born there. The same applies to the Crushing Hell and the others up to Avīci Hell. Dying in the Charcoal Pit Hell, beings are born in the Boiling Excrement Hell; dying there, they are born in the Charcoal Pit Hell; or dying in the Charcoal Pit Hell, they are again born there. Likewise, they are born successively among the Burning Forest Hell and the others up to the Great Lotus Hell.

卵生中死，胎生中生；胎生中死，卵生中生；卵生中死，還生卵生中。胎生、濕生、化生亦如是。

Dying among egg-born beings, they are born from wombs; dying among womb-born beings, they are born from eggs; or dying among egg-born beings, they are again born from eggs. The same applies to birth from wombs, moisture, and spontaneous transformation.

閻浮提中死，弗婆提中生；弗婆提中死，閻浮提中生；閻浮提中死，還生閻浮提中。劬陀尼、欝怛羅越亦如是。

Dying in Jambudvīpa, they are born in Pūrvavideha; dying in Pūrvavideha, they are born in Jambudvīpa; or dying in Jambudvīpa, they are again born there. The same applies to Godānīya and Uttarakuru.

四天處死，三十三天中生；三十三天中死，四天處生；四天處死，還生四天處。三十三天乃至他化自在天亦如是。

Dying in the Heaven of the Four Heavenly Kings, they are born in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three; dying in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three, they are born among the Four Heavenly Kings; or dying among the Four Heavenly Kings, they are again born there. The same applies to the Heaven of the Thirty-Three and the others up to the Heaven of Mastery over Others' Creations.

梵眾天中死，梵輔天中生；梵輔天中死，梵眾天中生；梵眾天中死，還生梵眾天中。梵輔天，少光天、無量光、光音，少淨、無量淨、遍淨，阿那跋羅伽、得生、大果，虛空處、識處、無所有處、非有想非無想處亦如是。非有想非無想天中死，阿鼻地獄中生。如是展轉生五道中。

Dying among Brahmā's Retinue, they are born among Brahmā's Ministers; dying among Brahmā's Ministers, they are born among Brahmā's Retinue; or dying among Brahmā's Retinue, they are again born there. The same applies to Brahmā's Ministers; Limited Light, Immeasurable Light, and Streaming Light; Limited Purity, Immeasurable Purity, and Pervasive Purity; Anabhraka, Blessed Birth, and Great Fruition; and the spheres of space, consciousness, nothingness, and neither perception nor nonperception. Dying in the heaven of neither perception nor nonperception, they are born in Avīci Hell. In this way they revolve through the five destinies.

菩薩見是已，生大悲心：「我於眾生為無所益，雖與世樂，樂極則苦；當以佛道涅槃常樂益於一切。云何而益？當懃大精進，乃得實智慧；得實智慧，知諸法實相，以餘波羅蜜助成，以益眾生。」是為菩薩精進波羅蜜。

Having seen this, a bodhisattva arouses great compassion: “I have brought living beings no real benefit. Even if I give them worldly happiness, happiness at its peak becomes suffering. I should benefit everyone through the enduring happiness of Buddhahood and nirvāṇa. How can I do so? I must practice great diligence to attain true wisdom. With true wisdom, I shall know the true character of phenomena and, supported by the other perfections, benefit living beings.” This is a bodhisattva's perfection of diligence.

The Suffering of Hungry Ghosts

Chinese Source

見餓鬼中飢渴故，兩眼陷，毛髮長，東西馳走。若欲趣水，護水諸鬼，以鐵杖逆打；設無守鬼，水自然竭；或時天雨，雨化為炭。

English Translation

One sees hungry ghosts with eyes sunken from hunger and thirst, hair grown long, running about in every direction. If they approach water, its guardian spirits strike them with iron staffs. Even without guardians, the water dries up of itself. Sometimes rain falls but turns into charcoal.

或有餓鬼常被火燒，如劫盡時，諸山火出。或有餓鬼羸瘦狂走，毛髮蓬亂，以覆其身。或有餓鬼常食𡱁尿，涕唾歐吐，盪滌餘汁；或時至廁溷邊立，伺求不淨汁。

Some hungry ghosts are constantly burned by fire, like mountains issuing flames at the end of a kalpa. Some, emaciated, run wildly, their disheveled hair covering their bodies. Others constantly eat excrement, urine, mucus, saliva, vomit, and the liquid left from washing; sometimes they stand beside latrines waiting for filthy liquid.

或有餓鬼常求產婦藏血飲之，形如燒樹，咽如針孔；若與其水，千歲不足。或有餓鬼自破其頭，以手取腦而舐。或有餓鬼形如黑山，鐵鎖鎖頸，叩頭求哀，歸命獄卒。

Some hungry ghosts constantly seek the blood of women in childbirth to drink. Their bodies are like burned trees and their throats like needle-holes; even if given water for a thousand years, it would not suffice. Some split open their own heads and take out their brains with their hands to lick them. Some are shaped like black mountains, with iron chains around their necks, bowing their heads and begging the wardens for mercy.

或有餓鬼先世惡口，好以麁語加彼眾生，眾生憎惡，見之如讎，以此罪故，墮餓鬼中。如是等種種罪故，墮餓鬼趣中，受無量苦痛。

Some hungry ghosts spoke abusively in previous lives, habitually directing harsh words at other beings, who hated them and regarded them as enemies. Through this offense they fell among hungry ghosts. These and various other offenses bring birth in the hungry ghost destiny and immeasurable suffering.

The Reviving Hell and the Black Rope Hell

Chinese Source

見八大地獄，苦毒萬端。活大地獄中諸受罪人，各各共鬪，惡心瞋諍，手捉利刀，互相割剝，以槊相刺，鐵叉相叉，鐵棒相棒，鐵杖相捶，鐵鏟相貫，而以利刀互相切膾，又以鐵爪而相爴裂，各以身血而相塗漫。痛毒逼切，悶無所覺；宿業因緣，冷風來吹，獄卒喚之：「咄！」諸罪人還活，以是故名活地獄；即時平復，復受苦毒。

English Translation

One sees the eight great hells, with their myriad agonies. In the great Reviving Hell, those undergoing punishment fight one another with hatred and anger. Holding sharp blades, they cut and flay one another, stab with spears and iron forks, beat with iron clubs and staffs, pierce with iron spades, slice each other to pieces with knives, and tear one another apart with iron claws, smearing each other with blood. Overwhelmed by pain, they lose consciousness. Through the conditions of past karma, a cold wind blows and the wardens shout, “Hey!” The sufferers revive, and thus it is called the Reviving Hell. Immediately restored, they again undergo torment.

此中眾生，以宿行因緣，好殺物命，牛羊禽獸；為田業、舍宅、奴婢、妻子、國土、錢財故，而相殺害。如是等種種殺業報故，受此劇罪。

Through their past actions, these beings delighted in taking the lives of cattle, sheep, birds, and other animals, or killed one another for farmland, houses, servants, wives, children, territory, or wealth. Through the results of these various killing actions, they undergo this severe punishment.

見黑繩大地獄中罪人，為惡羅剎、獄卒、鬼匠，常以黑熱鐵繩，拼度罪人。以獄中鐵斧，煞之斫之：長者令短，短者令長；方者使圓，圓者使方。斬截四肢，却其耳鼻，落其手足。以大鐵鋸，解析揣截，破其肉分，臠臠稱之。此人宿行因緣，讒賊忠良，妄語、惡口、兩舌、無義語，枉殺無辜，或作姧吏，酷暴侵害。如是等種種惡口讒賊，故受此罪。

One sees those undergoing punishment in the great Black Rope Hell. Evil rākṣasas, wardens, and spirit-craftsmen constantly measure them with black, burning iron cords. With iron axes they kill and chop them, making the long short, the short long, the square round, and the round square. They sever limbs, remove ears and noses, and cut off hands and feet. With great iron saws they split and chop them apart, divide their flesh, and weigh it piece by piece. Through past actions, these people slandered and injured the loyal and good, spoke falsely, harshly, divisively, and meaninglessly, unjustly killed the innocent, or served as corrupt officials who cruelly oppressed others. Through these various acts of abusive speech, slander, and injury, they undergo this punishment.
