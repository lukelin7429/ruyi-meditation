Fascicle 13 · 大智度論卷第十三

The Perfection of Morality

Fascicle 13 explains the perfection of morality and the character of the precepts.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: translation in progress Source base: Taishō T25, no. 1509 Morality and the Buddha path

Translation Method

The Perfection of Morality — Part Twenty-One

Chinese Source

〖經〗「罪、不罪不可得故，應具足尸羅波羅蜜。」〖論〗尸羅（秦言性善），好行善道，不自放逸，是名尸羅。或受戒行善，或不受戒行善，皆名尸羅。

English Translation

Sutra: ‘Because wrongdoing and non-wrongdoing cannot be apprehended, one should fulfill the perfection of morality.’ Treatise: Śīla is glossed as goodness of character: delighting in wholesome conduct without heedlessness. Practicing goodness with or without formally receiving precepts is called śīla.

尸羅者，略說身、口律儀有八種：不惱害、不劫盜、不邪婬，不妄語、不兩舌、不惡口、不綺語，不飲酒，及淨命，是名戒相。

Briefly, śīla comprises eight forms of bodily and verbal restraint: refraining from harming, stealing, sexual misconduct, false speech, divisive speech, harsh speech, frivolous speech, and alcohol, together with pure livelihood. These constitute the character of morality.

若不護、放捨，是名破戒。破此戒者，墮三惡道中。若下持戒生人中，中持戒生六欲天中，上持戒又行四禪、四空定，生色、無色界清淨天中。上持戒有三種：下清淨持戒得阿羅漢，中清淨持戒得辟支佛，上清淨持戒得佛道。不著、不猗，不破、不缺，聖所讚愛，如是名為上清淨持戒。若慈愍眾生故，為度眾生故，亦知戒實相故，心不猗著；如此持戒，將來令人至佛道，如是名為得無上佛道戒。

Failing to guard and maintain these is breaking precepts, which leads to the three bad destinies. Lower observance brings human birth; intermediate observance brings birth in the six desire heavens; higher observance, together with the four dhyānas and four formless attainments, brings birth in the pure heavens of the form and formless realms. Higher observance itself has three degrees: lower pure morality leads to arhatship, intermediate to pratyekabuddhahood, and highest to Buddhahood. Morality without attachment or dependence, unbroken and intact, praised and cherished by noble ones, is highest pure morality. Observing precepts out of compassion to liberate beings, while knowing their true reality and not clinging to them, leads to Buddhahood and is called morality attaining unsurpassed awakening.

若人求大善利，當堅持戒，如惜重寶，如護身命。何以故？譬如大地，一切萬物有形之類，皆依地而住；戒亦如是，戒為一切善法住處。

Anyone seeking great wholesome benefit should firmly guard precepts like precious treasure or life itself. Why? Just as all material things rest upon the earth, morality supports all wholesome dharmas.

復次，譬如無足欲行，無翅欲飛，無船欲渡，是不可得；若無戒欲得好果，亦復如是。若人棄捨此戒，雖山居苦行，食果服藥，與禽獸無異。或有人但服水為戒，或服乳，或服氣；或剃髮，或長髮，或頂上留少許髮；或著袈裟，或著白衣，或著草衣，或木皮衣；或冬入水，或夏火炙；若自墜高巖，若於恒河中洗；若日三浴，再供養火；種種祠祀，種種呪願，受行苦行。以無此戒，空無所得。若有人雖處高堂大殿，好衣美食，而能行此戒者，得生好處及得道果。若貴若賤、若小若大，能行此淨戒，皆得大利。若破此戒，無貴無賤、無大無小，皆不得隨意生善處。

Walking without feet, flying without wings, or crossing without a boat is impossible. Likewise, good results cannot be obtained without morality. Someone who abandons it and practices mountain austerities, eating fruit and medicinal plants, differs little from animals. Some live only on water, milk, or air; shave their heads, grow long hair, or retain a topknot; wear dyed robes, white garments, grass, or bark; enter water in winter or expose themselves to fire in summer; leap from cliffs, wash in the Ganges, bathe three times daily, tend fire twice, or perform sacrifices, invocations, and austerities. Without morality, these gain nothing. Conversely, someone living in a lofty palace with fine clothes and food who observes precepts gains good rebirth and the fruits of the path. High or low, young or old, all benefit from pure morality; those who break it cannot obtain the good rebirth they wish.

復次，破戒之人，譬如清涼池而有毒蛇，不中澡浴；亦如好華果樹，而多逆刺。若人雖在貴家生，身體端政，廣學多聞，而不樂持戒，無慈愍心，亦復如是。如偈說：「貴而無智則為衰，智而憍慢亦為衰，持戒之人而毀戒，今世後世一切衰！」

A person who breaks precepts is like a cool pond containing poisonous snakes, unsuitable for bathing, or a fine fruit tree covered in sharp thorns. Noble birth, beauty, and extensive learning without delight in morality or compassion are similar. As a verse says: ‘Noble rank without wisdom is decline; wisdom with pride is decline. For one who has undertaken precepts, breaking them brings decline in this and future lives.’

人雖貧賤而能持戒，勝於富貴而破戒者。華香、木香不能遠聞；持戒之香，周遍十方。持戒之人，具足安樂，名聲遠聞，天、人敬愛，現世常得種種快樂。若欲天上、人中、富貴、長壽，取之不難；持戒清淨，所願皆得。

A poor, lowly person who keeps precepts surpasses a wealthy, honored person who breaks them. Flower and wood fragrances do not travel far, but the fragrance of morality pervades the ten directions. Those who keep precepts enjoy well-being, widespread good reputation, and the respect and affection of devas and humans. If they wish for heavenly or human birth, wealth, status, or long life, these are easily obtained. Pure morality fulfills their wishes.

復次，持戒之人，見破戒人刑獄考掠，種種苦惱，自知永離此事，以為欣慶。若持戒之人，見善人得譽，名聞快樂，心自念言：如彼得譽，我亦有分。持戒之人，壽終之時，刀風解身，筋脈斷絕，自知持戒清淨，心不怖畏。如偈說：「大惡病中，戒為良藥；大恐怖中，戒為守護；死闇冥中，戒為明燈；於惡道中，戒為橋樑；死海水中，戒為大船。」

Seeing offenders imprisoned and tortured, a precept-holder rejoices in being free of such conditions. Seeing good people praised and happy, one thinks, ‘I too share in such praise.’ At death, as knife-like winds break up the body and sever its sinews, knowing one’s precepts pure removes fear. As a verse says: ‘In grave illness, morality is good medicine; in great fear, a guardian; in death’s darkness, a bright lamp; among bad destinies, a bridge; in the ocean of death, a great ship.’

復次，持戒之人，常得今世人所敬養，心樂不悔，衣食無乏，死得生天，後得佛道。持戒之人，無事不得；破戒之人，一切皆失。

Furthermore, precept-holders receive respect and support, enjoy a mind without regret, lack no clothing or food, are born in heaven after death, and eventually attain Buddhahood. Nothing is beyond their attainment, while those who break precepts lose everything.

譬如有人常供養天，其人貧窮，一心供養，滿十二歲，求索富貴。天愍此人，自現其身而問之曰：「汝求何等？」答言：「我求富貴，欲令心之所願，一切皆得！」天與一器，名曰德瓶，而語之言：「所須之物，從此瓶出。」其人得已，應意所欲，無所不得。得如意已，具作好舍，象馬、車乘、七寶具足，供給賓客，事事無乏。客問之言：「汝先貧窮，今日所由得如此富？」答言：「我得天瓶，瓶能出此種種眾物，故富如是。」客言：「出瓶見示，并所出物！」即為出瓶，瓶中引出種種眾物。其人憍泆，立瓶上舞，瓶即破壞，一切眾物亦一時滅。持戒之人，亦復如是，種種妙樂，無願不得；若人破戒，憍泆自恣，亦如彼人破瓶失物。

A poor man once worshiped a deva single-mindedly for twelve years, seeking wealth. Taking pity, the deva appeared and asked his wish. He wanted prosperity and everything he desired. The deva gave him a ‘vessel of virtue,’ saying it would produce whatever he needed. He obtained fine dwellings, elephants, horses, vehicles, and seven treasures, and supplied guests without lack. Asked how he had become wealthy, he explained the heavenly vessel and displayed it, drawing out various objects. Becoming proud and reckless, he stood on it and danced. It shattered, and all his possessions disappeared at once. Likewise, precept-holders obtain wondrous happiness and fulfilled wishes; breaking precepts through arrogance and indulgence resembles breaking the vessel and losing its contents.

復次，持戒之人，名稱之香，今世、後世周滿天上及在人中。復次，持戒之人，人所樂施，不惜財物，不修世利而無所乏；得生天上、十方佛前，入三乘道而得解脫。唯種種邪見持戒，後無所得。復次，若人雖不出家，但能修行戒法，亦得生天。若人持戒清淨，行禪、智慧，欲求度脫老、病、死苦，此願必得。持戒之人，雖無兵仗，眾惡不加；持戒之財，無能奪者；持戒親親，雖死不離；持戒莊嚴，勝於七寶。以是之故，當護於戒，如護身命，如愛寶物。破戒之人，受苦萬端，如向貧人破瓶失物，以是之故應持淨戒。

Furthermore, the good reputation of precept-holders spreads throughout heavens and human worlds, now and later. People gladly give them possessions; without pursuing worldly gain, they lack nothing. They may be born in heaven or before Buddhas of the ten directions and enter the three vehicles to liberation. Only observance based on wrong views fails to yield the intended attainment. Even householders who practice precepts may attain heavenly birth. Pure morality combined with meditation and wisdom certainly fulfills the wish to escape aging, sickness, and death. Though unarmed, precept-holders are protected from evils. Their wealth of morality cannot be stolen; its kinship remains beyond death; its adornment surpasses seven jewels. Guard precepts as life and treasure. Breaking them brings manifold suffering, like the poor man breaking his vessel. Therefore, maintain pure precepts.

復次，持戒之人，觀破戒人罪，應自勉勵，一心持戒。

Furthermore, observing the faults of breaking precepts, one should encourage oneself and keep precepts single-mindedly.
