The Vow for Buddha Lands

Fascicle 7 opens the explanation of the bodhisattva's immeasurable vows, beginning with the vow to receive and adorn immeasurable Buddha worlds.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: translation in progress Source base: Taishō T25, no. 1509 Buddha-land vows

The Vow for Buddha Lands

Chinese Source

大智度初品中佛土願釋論第十三（第七卷）

Ru-Yi Translation

Explanation of the Vow for Buddha Lands in the First Chapter of the Mahāprajñāpāramitā Śāstra, Part Thirteen, Fascicle Seven

菩薩見已，自發願言：「我作佛時，世界無眾苦，乃至無三惡之名，亦當如是。」

有佛世界七寶莊嚴，晝夜常有清淨光明，無有日月；便發願言：「我作佛時，世界常有嚴淨光明，亦當如是。」

After bodhisattvas see this, they make a vow for themselves, saying, "When I become a Buddha, may my world have no forms of suffering, not even the names of the three bad destinies. May it be like this."

There are Buddha worlds adorned with the seven treasures, where pure light is always present day and night and there is no sun or moon. Seeing this, bodhisattvas make a vow, saying, "When I become a Buddha, may my world always have majestic, pure light. May it be like this."

有佛世界一切眾生皆行十善，有大智慧，衣被飲食，應念而至；便發願言：「我作佛時，世界中眾生，衣被飲食，亦當如是。」

There are Buddha worlds where all beings practice the ten wholesome courses of action, possess great wisdom, and receive clothing, bedding, food, and drink as soon as they think of them. Seeing this, bodhisattvas make a vow, saying, "When I become a Buddha, may the beings in my world have clothing, bedding, food, and drink in this same way."

有佛世界純諸菩薩，如佛色身三十二相，光明徹照，乃至無有聲聞、辟支佛名，亦無女人；一切皆行深妙佛道，遊至十方，教化一切；便發願言：「我作佛時，世界中眾生，亦當如是。」

如是等無量佛世界種種嚴淨，願皆得之；以是故，名願受無量諸佛世界。

There are Buddha worlds made purely of bodhisattvas. Like the Buddha's form body, they have the thirty-two marks, and their light shines through everything. There are not even the names of śrāvakas or pratyekabuddhas, and there are no women. All practice the deep and wondrous Buddha path, travel to the ten directions, and teach and transform all beings. Seeing this, bodhisattvas make a vow, saying, "When I become a Buddha, may the beings in my world also be like this."

In this way, among immeasurable Buddha worlds with many kinds of majestic purity, they vow to attain them all. For this reason, this is called the vow to receive immeasurable Buddha worlds.

Why Vows Are Necessary

問曰：諸菩薩行業清淨，自得淨報，何以要須立願然後得之？譬如田家得穀，豈復待願？

答曰：作福無願，無所摽立，願為導御，能有所成。譬如銷金，隨師所作，金無定也。如佛所說：

Question: Since bodhisattvas' actions and karma are pure, they naturally receive pure recompense. Why must they first establish vows and only then obtain it? It is like a farming household obtaining grain. Does it still have to wait for a vow?

Answer: When one creates merit without vows, there is nothing clearly established. A vow serves as guide and driver, and so something can be accomplished. It is like smelted gold: it follows what the master craftsman makes, and the gold itself has no fixed form. As the Buddha said:

有人修少施福，修少戒福，不知禪法；聞人中有富樂人，心常念著，願樂不捨，命終之後，生富樂人中。

復有人修少施福，修少戒福，不知禪法；聞有四天王天處、三十三天，夜摩天、兜率陀天、化樂天、他化自在天，心常願樂，命終之後，各生其中；此皆願力所得。

Some people cultivate a little merit from giving and a little merit from morality, but do not know the Dharma of meditation. Hearing that among human beings there are people who are wealthy and happy, their minds constantly think of this, they vow for that happiness and do not let it go. After their lives end, they are born among wealthy and happy people.

Again, some people cultivate a little merit from giving and a little merit from morality, but do not know the Dharma of meditation. Hearing of the realm of the Four Heavenly Kings, the Heaven of the Thirty-Three, the Yāma Heaven, the Tuṣita Heaven, the Nirmāṇarati Heaven, and the Paranirmitavaśavartin Heaven, their minds constantly vow for and delight in them. After their lives end, each is born in one of those places. All of this is obtained through the power of vows.

菩薩亦如是，修淨世界願，然後得之。以是故知，因願受勝果。

復次，莊嚴佛世界事大，獨行功德不能成故，要須願力。譬如牛力雖能挽車，要須御者能有所至；淨世界願，亦復如是，福德如牛，願如御者。

Bodhisattvas are also like this. They cultivate the vow for a pure world, and only then do they attain it. For this reason, one should know that because of vows they receive a supreme result.

Furthermore, adorning a Buddha world is a great undertaking. It cannot be accomplished by practicing merit alone, and therefore the power of vows is necessary. For example, although the strength of an ox can pull a cart, there must be a driver for it to reach a destination. The vow for a pure world is also like this: merit is like the ox, and the vow is like the driver.

問曰：若不作願，不得福耶？

答曰：雖得不如有願，願能助福，常念所行，福德增長。

Question: If one does not make vows, does one not obtain merit?

Answer: One does obtain it, but it is not equal to having vows. Vows can assist merit. By constantly remembering what one practices, one's merit grows.

問曰：若作願得報，如人作十惡，不願地獄，亦不應得地獄報？

答曰：罪福雖有定報，但作願者修少福，有願力故得大果報。如先說罪中報苦，一切眾生皆願得樂，無願苦者，是故不願地獄。以是故，福有無量報，罪報有量。

Question: If one obtains a result by making a vow, then when people commit the ten evils, since they do not vow for hell, they also should not receive the result of hell.

Answer: Offenses and merits do have fixed results. But those who make vows cultivate only a little merit, and because of the power of vows they obtain a great result. As was said earlier, among offenses there is painful retribution. All beings wish to obtain happiness; no one wishes for suffering. Therefore no one vows for hell. For this reason, merit has immeasurable results, while the result of offense is limited.

有人言：最大罪在阿鼻地獄，一劫受報；最大福在非有想非無想處，受八萬大劫報。諸菩薩淨世界願，亦無量劫入道得涅槃，是為常樂。

問曰：如〈泥黎品〉中謗般若波羅蜜罪，此間劫盡，復至他方泥黎中，何以言最大罪受地獄中一劫報？

答曰：佛法為眾生故，有二道教化：一者佛道；二者聲聞道。聲聞道中作五逆罪人，佛說受地獄一劫；菩薩道中破佛法人，說此間劫盡，復至他方受無量罪。

Some say: the greatest offense results in retribution in Avīci Hell for one kalpa, while the greatest merit results in retribution in the sphere of neither perception nor non-perception for eighty thousand great kalpas. The bodhisattvas' vow for a pure world also lasts through immeasurable kalpas, until they enter the path and attain nirvāṇa. This is constant bliss.

Question: In the chapter on hells, it says that the offense of slandering the Prajñāpāramitā is such that when this kalpa here is exhausted, one again goes to another place's hell. Why then say that the greatest offense receives only one kalpa of retribution in hell?

Answer: For the sake of beings, the Buddha Dharma has two paths of teaching and transformation: first, the Buddha path; second, the śrāvaka path. Within the śrāvaka path, for a person who commits the five heinous offenses, the Buddha says that one receives one kalpa in hell. Within the bodhisattva path, for a person who destroys the Buddha Dharma, it is said that when this kalpa here is exhausted, one again goes elsewhere and receives immeasurable offenses.

聲聞法最第一福，受八萬劫；菩薩道中大福，受無量阿僧祇劫。

以是故福德要須願，是名願受無量諸佛世界。

In the śrāvaka teaching, the foremost merit is received for eighty thousand kalpas. In the bodhisattva path, great merit is received for immeasurable asaṃkhyeya kalpas.

For this reason, merit must have vows. This is called the vow to receive immeasurable Buddha worlds.

The Recollection-of-the-Buddha Samādhi

念佛三昧，名十方三世諸佛，常以心眼見，如現在前。

問曰：云何為念佛三昧？

答曰：念佛三昧有二種：一者聲聞法中，於一佛身，心眼見滿十方；二者菩薩道於無量佛土中，念三世十方諸佛。以是故言，念無量佛土，諸佛三昧常現在前。

The recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi means that one constantly sees, with the mind's eye, the Buddhas of the ten directions and the three times, as if they were present directly before one.

Question: What is the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi?

Answer: The recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi is of two kinds. First, in the śrāvaka teaching, with regard to the body of one Buddha, the mind's eye sees it filling the ten directions. Second, in the bodhisattva path, amid immeasurable Buddha lands, one recollects the Buddhas of the three times and the ten directions. For this reason, the text says that, while recollecting immeasurable Buddha lands, the samādhi of the Buddhas is constantly present before one.

問曰：如菩薩三昧種種無量，何以故但讚是菩薩念佛三昧常現在前？

答曰：是菩薩念佛故，得入佛道中，以是故，念佛三昧常現在前。

復次，念佛三昧能除種種煩惱及先世罪；餘諸三昧，有能除婬不能除瞋，有能除瞋不能除婬，有能除癡不能除婬、恚，有能除三毒不能除先世罪。是念佛三昧，能除種種煩惱、種種罪。

Question: Since bodhisattvas' samādhis are of many kinds and immeasurable, why does the text praise only this bodhisattva's recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi as constantly present before one?

Answer: Because this bodhisattva recollects the Buddha, the bodhisattva is able to enter the Buddha path. For this reason, the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi is constantly present before one.

Furthermore, the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi can remove many kinds of afflictions and offenses from former lives. Of the other samādhis, some can remove lust but cannot remove anger; some can remove anger but cannot remove lust; some can remove delusion but cannot remove lust and anger; and some can remove the three poisons but cannot remove offenses from former lives. This recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi can remove many kinds of afflictions and many kinds of offenses.

復次，念佛三昧有大福德，能度眾生；是諸菩薩欲度眾生，諸餘三昧無如此念佛三昧福德，能速滅諸罪者。

如說昔有五百估客，入海採寶；值摩伽羅魚王開口，海水入中，船去駃疾。船師問樓上人：汝見何等？答言：見三日出，白山羅列，水流奔趣，如入大坑。

Furthermore, the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi has great merit and can bring beings across. These bodhisattvas wish to bring beings across. Among all other samādhis, none has merit like this recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi, which can swiftly extinguish all offenses.

As it is said, in the past there were five hundred merchants who entered the ocean to gather jewels. They encountered the Makara Fish King opening its mouth; the seawater flowed into it, and their ship rushed along very swiftly. The shipmaster asked the person up in the lookout: "What do you see?" The person answered: "I see three suns rising, white mountains lined up, and water rushing along as if entering a great pit."

船師言：是摩伽羅魚王開口，一是實日，兩日是魚眼，白山是魚齒，水流奔趣是入其口。我曹了矣！各各求諸天神以自救濟！

是時諸人各各求其所事，都無所益。中有五戒優婆塞語眾人言：吾等當共稱南無佛，佛為無上，能救苦厄！眾人一心同聲稱南無佛。

The shipmaster said: "This is the Makara Fish King opening its mouth. One is the real sun; the other two are the fish's eyes. The white mountains are the fish's teeth. The rushing water is entering its mouth. We are finished! Each of you should seek the heavenly deities you serve and save yourselves!"

At that time, each person sought the one he served, but none of it was of any benefit. Among them was an upāsaka who kept the five precepts. He said to the group: "We should all together recite Namo Buddha. The Buddha is unsurpassed and can save us from suffering and danger." With one mind and one voice, the people recited Namo Buddha.

是魚先世是佛破戒弟子，得宿命智，聞稱佛聲心自悔悟，即便合口，船人得脫。

以念佛故，能除重罪，濟諸苦厄，何況念佛三昧。

復次，佛為法王，菩薩為法將，所尊所重唯佛世尊，是故應常念佛。

In a former life, this fish had been a disciple of the Buddha who had broken the precepts. It had gained knowledge of former lives, and when it heard the sound of people reciting the Buddha's name, its mind repented and awakened by itself. It then closed its mouth, and the people on the ship escaped.

Because of recollecting the Buddha, one can remove grave offenses and rescue beings from suffering and danger. How much more is this true of the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi?

Furthermore, the Buddha is the Dharma king, and bodhisattvas are Dharma generals. The one they honor and revere is only the Buddha, the World-Honored One. Therefore they should constantly recollect the Buddha.

復次，常念佛得種種功德利。譬如大臣特蒙恩寵常念其主；菩薩亦如是，知種種功德、無量智慧皆從佛得，知恩重故常念佛。

汝言云何常念佛，不行餘三昧者，今言常念，亦不言不行餘三昧，行念佛三昧多故言常念。

復次，先雖說空、無相、無作三昧，未說念佛三昧，是故今說。

Furthermore, by constantly recollecting the Buddha, one obtains many kinds of meritorious benefits. For example, a great minister who receives special favor and kindness constantly remembers his lord. Bodhisattvas are also like this. Knowing that many kinds of merit and immeasurable wisdom all come from the Buddha, and knowing the Buddha's kindness to be weighty, they constantly recollect the Buddha.

You asked why they constantly recollect the Buddha and do not practice other samādhis. Now, when it says they "constantly recollect," this also does not mean that they do not practice other samādhis. Because they practice the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi extensively, it says that they constantly recollect.

Furthermore, although the samādhis of emptiness, signlessness, and wishlessness have already been explained, the recollection-of-the-Buddha samādhi has not yet been explained. Therefore it is explained here.
