The Buddha's Incomparable Splendor

Chinese Source

〖經〗爾時，世尊在師子座上坐，於三千大千世界中，其德特尊，光明色像威德巍巍，遍至十方如恒河沙等諸佛世界。譬如須彌山王光色殊特，眾山無能及者。

English Translation

Sutra: At that time, the World-Honored One sat on the lion seat. Throughout the great trichiliocosm, his virtue was supremely honored, and the majestic splendor of his light, appearance, and presence extended to Buddha worlds in the ten directions as numerous as the sands of the Ganges. He was like Sumeru, king of mountains, whose exceptional radiance and color no other mountain can equal.

〖論〗問曰：佛以何力故於一切眾生中其德特尊，光明、威德巍巍乃如是耶？如轉輪聖王、諸天、聖人亦有大力、光明、威德，何以獨言佛德特尊？

Treatise: Question: Through what power is the Buddha's virtue supremely honored among all beings, with such majestic light and presence? Wheel-turning sage kings, devas, and noble ones also possess great power, radiance, and majesty. Why say that the Buddha's virtue alone is supremely honored?

答曰：此諸賢聖雖有光明、威德，有量有限。譬如眾星，日光既出，則沒不現。佛從無量阿僧祇劫集大功德，一切具足，因緣大故果報亦大；餘人無此。

Answer: Although these worthy and noble ones possess radiance and majesty, theirs have measure and limits. They are like stars, which disappear from sight when the sun rises. Through immeasurable asaṃkhyeya kalpas, the Buddha has accumulated great merit and brought everything to completion. Because the causes and conditions are great, their result is also great. Others do not possess this.

復次，佛世世修諸苦行，無量無數頭、目、髓、腦常施眾生，豈唯國、財、妻、子而已！一切種種戒、種種忍、種種精進、種種禪定，及無比清淨不可壞不可盡智慧，世世修行，已具足滿。此果力故，得不可稱量殊特威神，以是故言「因緣大故果報亦大」。

Furthermore, life after life the Buddha cultivated arduous practices, constantly giving beings immeasurable and countless heads, eyes, marrow, and brains—not merely kingdoms, wealth, wives, and children! Every kind of morality, patience, diligence, and meditative concentration, together with incomparable wisdom that is pure, indestructible, and inexhaustible, he cultivated life after life and has now fulfilled completely. Through the power of these results, he obtains extraordinary spiritual majesty beyond measure. Therefore, it is said, 'Because the causes and conditions are great, their result is also great.'

Why Does the Buddha Appear to Undergo Suffering?

Chinese Source

問曰：若佛神力無量，威德巍巍，不可稱說，何以故受九罪報？一者、梵志女孫陀利謗，五百阿羅漢亦被謗；二者、旃遮婆羅門女繫木盂作腹謗佛；三者、提婆達推山壓佛，傷足大指；四者、迸木刺腳；五者、毘樓璃王興兵殺諸釋子，佛時頭痛；六者、受阿耆達多婆羅門請而食馬麥；七者、冷風動故脊痛；八者、六年苦行；九者、入婆羅門聚落，乞食不得，空鉢而還。

English Translation

Question: If the Buddha's spiritual power is immeasurable and his majestic virtue beyond description, why does he undergo nine painful karmic results? First, he was slandered in the affair of the female ascetic Sundarī, and five hundred arhats were also slandered. Second, the brahmin woman Ciñcā tied a wooden bowl over her abdomen and slandered him. Third, Devadatta rolled down a rock to crush him, injuring his big toe. Fourth, a splinter of wood pierced his foot. Fifth, when King Virūḍhaka raised an army and killed the Śākyas, the Buddha suffered a headache. Sixth, after accepting the invitation of the brahmin Agnidatta, he ate barley meant for horses. Seventh, a disturbance of cold wind caused back pain. Eighth, he practiced austerities for six years. Ninth, he entered a brahmin village seeking alms but obtained nothing and returned with an empty bowl.

復有冬至前後八夜，寒風破竹，索三衣禦寒。又復患熱，阿難在後扇佛。如是等世界小事，佛皆受之。若佛神力無量，三千大千世界乃至東方恒河沙等諸佛世界，南西北方、四維、上下，光明色像威德巍巍，何以故受諸罪報？

Furthermore, during eight nights around the winter solstice, when the cold wind split bamboo, he asked for the three robes to keep out the cold. He also suffered from heat, and Ānanda stood behind him fanning him. The Buddha experienced all these minor worldly troubles. If his spiritual power is immeasurable, and the majestic splendor of his light and appearance extends throughout the great trichiliocosm and Buddha worlds as numerous as the sands of the Ganges in the east, south, west, north, intermediate directions, above, and below, why does he undergo these painful karmic results?

答曰：佛在人中生，人父母，受人身力，一指節力勝千萬億那由他白象力，神通力無量無數、不可思議。是淨飯王子，厭老、病、死苦，出家得佛道——是人豈受罪報，為寒熱等所困！如佛神力不可思議，不可思議法中，何有寒熱諸患？

Answer: The Buddha was born among humans, of human parents. Even the strength of his human body, in a single finger joint, surpasses that of countless myriads of nayutas of white elephants. His supernormal power is immeasurable, countless, and inconceivable. This son of King Śuddhodana became disenchanted with the suffering of aging, sickness, and death, went forth, and attained the Buddha path. How could such a person be subject to punitive karmic results or troubled by cold and heat? The Buddha's spiritual power is inconceivable. Within inconceivable dharmas, how could there be afflictions of cold, heat, and the like?

Two Kinds of Buddha Body

Chinese Source

復次，佛有二種身：一者法性身，二者父母生身。是法性身滿十方虛空，無量無邊，色像端正，相好莊嚴，無量光明，無量音聲，聽法眾亦滿虛空；常出種種身、種種名號、種種生處、種種方便度眾生，常度一切，無須臾息時。如是法性身佛，能度十方世界眾生。受諸罪報者是生身佛，生身佛次第說法如人法。以有二種佛故，受諸罪無咎。

English Translation

Furthermore, the Buddha has two kinds of body: the body of Dharma nature and the body born of parents. The body of Dharma nature fills space throughout the ten directions, immeasurable and boundless. Its appearance is beautiful, adorned with the major and minor marks, with immeasurable light and immeasurable voice. The assembly hearing its Dharma also fills space. It constantly manifests various bodies, names, places of birth, and skillful means to liberate beings. It continually liberates all, without resting for even a moment. Such a Buddha with the body of Dharma nature can liberate beings in the worlds of the ten directions. The Buddha who undergoes painful karmic results is the Buddha with a body born of parents. This embodied Buddha teaches the Dharma step by step, in accordance with human ways. Because there are these two kinds of Buddha, there is no contradiction in his undergoing such suffering.
