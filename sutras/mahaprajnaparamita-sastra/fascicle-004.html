Why the Bodhisattvas Are Named Separately

Chinese Source

大智度初品中菩薩釋論第八(卷第四)

Ru-Yi Translation

Explanation of the Bodhisattvas in the First Chapter of the Mahāprajñāpāramitā Śāstra, Section Eight, Fascicle Four

〖經〗

復有菩薩摩訶薩。

Sutra:

There were also bodhisattva mahāsattvas.

〖論〗

問曰：

若從上數，應先菩薩，次第比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷，菩薩次佛故；若從下數，應先優婆夷，次第優婆塞、比丘尼、比丘、菩薩。今何以先說比丘，次三眾，後說菩薩？

Treatise:

Question:

If counted from above, the bodhisattvas should be named first, followed in order by the bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās, because bodhisattvas are next to the Buddha. If counted from below, the upāsikās should be named first, followed in order by the upāsakas, bhikṣuṇīs, bhikṣus, and bodhisattvas. Why, then, does the sutra now first mention the bhikṣus, then the three other assemblies, and only afterward the bodhisattvas?

答曰：

菩薩雖應次佛，以諸煩惱未盡故，先說阿羅漢。諸阿羅漢智慧雖少而已成熟，諸菩薩智慧雖多而煩惱未盡，是故先說阿羅漢。

佛法有二種：一、祕密，二、現示。

現示中，佛、辟支佛、阿羅漢皆是福田，以其煩惱盡無餘故。

祕密中，說諸菩薩得無生法忍，煩惱已斷，具六神通，利益眾生。

以現示法故，前說阿羅漢，後說菩薩。

復次，菩薩以方便力現入五道，受五欲，引導眾生；若在阿羅漢上，諸天、世人當生疑怪，是故後說。

Answer:

Although bodhisattvas should be next to the Buddha, because they have not yet exhausted all afflictions, the arhats are mentioned first. The arhats' wisdom may be lesser, yet it is already mature. The bodhisattvas' wisdom may be greater, yet their afflictions are not yet exhausted. For this reason, the arhats are mentioned first.

In the Buddha Dharma there are two modes: first, the hidden; second, the openly displayed.

In the openly displayed mode, Buddhas, pratyekabuddhas, and arhats are all fields of merit, because their afflictions have been exhausted without remainder.

In the hidden mode, it is said that bodhisattvas attain the patience with the non-arising of dharmas, have already cut off the afflictions, possess the six superknowledges, and benefit beings.

Because the sutra follows the openly displayed Dharma, it first mentions the arhats and afterward mentions the bodhisattvas.

Furthermore, by the power of skillful means, bodhisattvas appear to enter the five destinies, experience the five desires, and guide beings. If they were placed above the arhats, devas and people of the world would give rise to doubt and surprise. For this reason, they are mentioned afterward.

問曰：

在阿羅漢後可爾，何以乃在優婆塞、優婆夷後？

Question:

It may be acceptable for them to come after the arhats, but why are they placed even after the upāsakas and upāsikās?

答曰：

四眾雖漏未盡，盡在不久故，通名聲聞眾。

若於四眾中間說菩薩者，則不便。如比丘尼得無量律儀故，應次比丘後，在沙彌前；佛以儀法不便故，在沙彌後。此諸菩薩亦如是，雖應在學人三眾上，以不便故，在後說。

復次，有人言：「菩薩功德、智慧超殊阿羅漢、辟支佛，是故別說。」

Answer:

Although the fourfold assembly has not yet exhausted the outflows, that exhaustion is not far off; therefore they are all generally called the śrāvaka assembly.

If bodhisattvas were mentioned in the middle of the fourfold assembly, it would not be appropriate. For example, because bhikṣuṇīs receive immeasurable disciplinary precepts, they should come right after the bhikṣus and before the śrāmaṇeras. Yet because this would not be appropriate according to the forms of discipline, the Buddha places them after the śrāmaṇeras. These bodhisattvas are also like this. Although they should be above the three assemblies of people in training, because it would not be appropriate, they are mentioned afterward.

Furthermore, some say, “The merit and wisdom of bodhisattvas surpass those of arhats and pratyekabuddhas. Therefore they are mentioned separately.”

問曰：

聲聞經中但說四眾，此中何以別說菩薩眾？

Question:

In the śrāvaka sutras only the fourfold assembly is mentioned. Why does this sutra separately mention the bodhisattva assembly?

答曰：

有二種道：一、聲聞道，二、菩提薩埵道。

比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷四眾是聲聞道，菩薩摩訶薩是菩提薩埵道。

以是故聲聞法中，經初無「佛在某處某處住，爾所菩薩俱」，但言「佛某處某處住，與爾所比丘俱」。如說：「佛在波羅柰，與五比丘俱；佛在伽耶國中，與千比丘俱；佛在舍婆提，與五百比丘俱。」如是種種經初，不說與菩薩若干人俱。

Answer:

There are two kinds of path: first, the śrāvaka path; second, the bodhisattva path.

The fourfold assembly of bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās belongs to the śrāvaka path. Bodhisattva mahāsattvas belong to the bodhisattva path.

For this reason, in the śrāvaka Dharma, the openings of sutras do not say, “The Buddha was dwelling at such and such a place together with so many bodhisattvas.” They only say, “The Buddha was dwelling at such and such a place together with so many bhikṣus.” For example, “The Buddha was in Vārāṇasī together with five bhikṣus”; “The Buddha was in the country of Gayā together with one thousand bhikṣus”; and “The Buddha was in Śrāvastī together with five hundred bhikṣus.” In the openings of these various sutras, it is not said that he was together with any number of bodhisattvas.

問曰：

諸菩薩二種：若出家，若在家。在家菩薩，總說在優婆塞、優婆夷中；出家菩薩，總在比丘、比丘尼中。今何以故別說？

Question:

There are two kinds of bodhisattvas: those who have gone forth and those who remain at home. Bodhisattvas who remain at home are generally included among the upāsakas and upāsikās. Bodhisattvas who have gone forth are generally included among the bhikṣus and bhikṣuṇīs. Why are they now mentioned separately?

答曰：

雖總在四眾中，應當別說。何以故？是菩薩必墮四眾中，有四眾不墮菩薩中。

何者是？有聲聞人、辟支佛人、有求生天人、有求樂自活人，此四種人不墮菩薩中。何以故？是人不發心言「我當作佛」故。

復次，菩薩得無生法忍故，一切名字、生死相斷，出三界，不墮眾生數中。何以故？聲聞人得阿羅漢道，滅度已，尚不墮眾生數中，何況菩薩？

如《波羅延》優波尸難中偈說：

「已滅無處更出不？若已永滅不出不？既入涅槃常住不？惟願大智說其實！」

佛答曰：

「滅者即是不可量，破壞因緣及名相，一切言語道已過，一時都盡如火滅。」

如阿羅漢一切名字尚斷，何況菩薩能破一切諸法、知實相、得法身而不斷耶！

以是故，摩訶衍四眾中別說菩薩。

Answer:

Although they are generally included within the fourfold assembly, they should still be mentioned separately. Why? These bodhisattvas necessarily fall within the fourfold assembly, but there are members of the fourfold assembly who do not fall within the bodhisattvas.

Who are they? There are people of the śrāvaka path, people of the pratyekabuddha path, people who seek rebirth in the heavens, and people who seek only pleasure and self-support. These four kinds of people do not fall within the bodhisattvas. Why? Because they do not give rise to the thought, “I shall become a Buddha.”

Furthermore, because bodhisattvas attain the patience with the non-arising of dharmas, all names and the marks of birth and death are cut off. They go beyond the three realms and do not fall within the number of beings. Why? When people of the śrāvaka path attain arhatship and enter extinction, they no longer fall within the number of beings. How much more so the bodhisattvas?

As a verse in the Questions of Upasīva in the Pārāyana says:

“One who is extinguished, is there nowhere from which he emerges again? If he has been forever extinguished, does he not emerge? Having entered nirvāṇa, does he abide forever? I only pray that the greatly wise one will speak the truth of this.”

The Buddha answered:

“One who is extinguished is beyond measure. Causes and conditions, names and marks, are destroyed. The path of all speech has already been passed beyond; all at once, everything is ended, like a fire gone out.”

If even all names of an arhat are cut off, how much more so for bodhisattvas, who can break through all dharmas, know their true mark, and attain the Dharma body. How could they not be cut off from such naming?

For this reason, in the fourfold assembly of the Mahāyāna, bodhisattvas are mentioned separately.

Why Both Assemblies Are Mentioned

Chinese Source

問曰：

何以故大乘經初，菩薩眾、聲聞眾兩說，聲聞經獨說比丘眾，不說菩薩眾？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

Why is it that at the beginning of Mahāyāna sutras both the bodhisattva assembly and the śrāvaka assembly are mentioned, while in śrāvaka sutras only the bhikṣu assembly is mentioned and the bodhisattva assembly is not?

答曰：

欲辯二乘義故，佛乘及聲聞乘。聲聞乘狹小，佛乘廣大；聲聞乘自利自為，佛乘益一切。

復次，聲聞乘多說眾生空，佛乘說眾生空、法空。如是等種種分別三乘，分別說是二道故，摩訶衍經聲聞眾、菩薩眾兩說。如讚摩訶衍偈中說：

「得此大乘人，能與一切樂，利益以實法，令得無上道！

得此大乘人，慈悲一切故，頭目以布施，捨之如草木！

得此大乘人，護持清淨戒，如犛牛愛尾，不惜身壽命！

得此大乘人，能得無上忍，若有割截身，視之如斷草！

得此大乘人，精進無厭惓，力行不休息，如抒大海者！

得此大乘人，廣修無量定，神通聖道力，清淨得自在！

得此大乘人，分別諸法相，無壞實智慧，是中已具足！

不可思議智，無量悲心力，不入二法中，等觀一切法。

驢馬駝象乘，雖同不相匹；菩薩及聲聞，大小亦如是。

大慈悲為軸，智慧為兩輪，精進為快馬，戒定以為銜，

忍辱心為鎧，總持為轡勒，摩訶衍人乘，能度於一切！」

Answer:

It is because the meaning of the two vehicles is to be distinguished: the Buddha Vehicle and the śrāvaka vehicle. The śrāvaka vehicle is narrow and small; the Buddha Vehicle is vast and great. The śrāvaka vehicle benefits oneself and acts for oneself; the Buddha Vehicle benefits all.

Furthermore, the śrāvaka vehicle mostly teaches the emptiness of beings, while the Buddha Vehicle teaches both the emptiness of beings and the emptiness of dharmas. In these and other ways, the three vehicles are variously distinguished. Because these two paths are explained separately, Mahāyāna sutras mention both the śrāvaka assembly and the bodhisattva assembly. As a verse praising the Mahāyāna says:

“Those who attain this Great Vehicle can give joy to all. They benefit beings with the true Dharma and lead them to the unsurpassed path.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle, because they have compassion for all, give their heads and eyes in charity, abandoning them like grass or wood.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle protect and uphold pure precepts, like a yak that loves its tail, not sparing body or life.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle can attain unsurpassed patience. If someone cuts apart their body, they regard it as cutting grass.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle are diligent without weariness, exerting themselves without rest, like someone trying to empty the great ocean.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle broadly cultivate immeasurable concentration. Through the power of superknowledge and the noble path, they become pure and attain freedom.

Those who attain this Great Vehicle distinguish the marks of all dharmas. Unbreakable true wisdom is already complete within them.

With inconceivable wisdom and the power of immeasurable compassion, they do not enter into duality and equally contemplate all dharmas.

Donkeys, horses, camels, and elephants are all called vehicles, yet they cannot be matched with one another. Bodhisattvas and śrāvakas, the great and the small, are also like this.

Great compassion is the axle; wisdom is the two wheels; diligence is the swift horse; precepts and concentration are the bit.

Patience is the armor of the mind; dhāraṇī is the reins and bridle. Those who ride the Mahāyāna vehicle can carry all beings across.”

問曰：

如聲聞經初但說比丘眾，摩訶衍經初何以不但說菩薩眾？

Question:

If at the beginning of śrāvaka sutras only the bhikṣu assembly is mentioned, why do Mahāyāna sutras not mention only the bodhisattva assembly at the beginning?

答曰：

摩訶衍廣大，諸乘諸道皆入摩訶衍；聲聞乘狹小，不受摩訶衍。譬如恒河不受大海，以其狹小故；大海能受眾流，以其廣大故。摩訶衍法亦如是，如偈說：

「摩訶衍如海，小乘牛跡水；小故不受大，其喻亦如是。」

以是故，小乘眾不受菩薩。

Answer:

The Mahāyāna is vast and great; all vehicles and all paths enter into the Mahāyāna. The śrāvaka vehicle is narrow and small and does not receive the Mahāyāna. It is like the Ganges, which does not receive the great ocean because it is narrow and small; the great ocean can receive all streams because it is vast and great. The Mahāyāna Dharma is also like this. As a verse says:

“The Mahāyāna is like the ocean; the small vehicle is water in an ox's hoofprint. Because the small cannot receive the great, this comparison is also like that.”

For this reason, the small-vehicle assembly does not receive the bodhisattvas.

The Meaning of Bodhi and Sattva

Chinese Source

問曰：

何等名菩提？何等名薩埵？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

What is called bodhi? What is called sattva?

答曰：

菩提名諸佛道，薩埵名或眾生，或大心。是人諸佛道功德盡欲得，其心不可斷不可破，如金剛山，是名大心。如偈說：

「一切諸佛法，智慧及戒定，能利益一切，是名為菩提。

其心不可動，能忍成道事，不斷亦不破，是心名薩埵。」

復次，稱讚好法名為薩，好法體相名為埵。菩薩心自利利他故，度一切眾生故，知一切法實性故，行阿耨多羅三藐三菩提道故，為一切賢聖之所稱讚故，是名菩提薩埵。所以者何？一切諸法中，佛法最第一；是人欲取是法故，為賢聖所讚歎。

復次，如是人為一切眾生脫生、老、死故索佛道，是名菩提薩埵。

復次，三種道皆是菩提：一者、佛道，二者、聲聞道，三者、辟支佛道。辟支佛道、聲聞道雖得菩提，而不稱為菩提；佛功德中菩提稱為菩提，是名菩提薩埵。

Answer:

Bodhi means the path of the Buddhas. Sattva means either a being or a great mind. This person wishes to obtain all the qualities and merits of the path of the Buddhas. That mind cannot be cut off and cannot be broken, like a vajra mountain. This is called a great mind. As a verse says:

“All the dharmas of the Buddhas, wisdom, precepts, and concentration, can benefit all beings. This is called bodhi.

That mind cannot be moved. It can endure the work of accomplishing the path. It is not cut off and not broken. This mind is called sattva.”

Furthermore, praising good dharmas is called sa; the essential mark of good dharmas is called tva. Because the bodhisattva's mind benefits oneself and benefits others, because it carries all beings across, because it knows the true nature of all dharmas, because it practices the path of anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi, and because it is praised by all noble and worthy ones, it is called bodhi-sattva. Why? Among all dharmas, the Buddha Dharma is foremost. Because this person wishes to take up this Dharma, the noble and worthy ones praise them.

Furthermore, such a person seeks the Buddha path in order to free all beings from birth, aging, and death. This is called bodhi-sattva.

Furthermore, three kinds of path are all bodhi: first, the Buddha path; second, the śrāvaka path; third, the pratyekabuddha path. Although the pratyekabuddha path and śrāvaka path attain bodhi, they are not called bodhi. In the qualities and merits of the Buddha, bodhi is called bodhi. This is called bodhi-sattva.

問曰：

齊何來名菩提薩埵？

Question:

From what point onward is someone called a bodhi-sattva?

答曰：

有大誓願，心不可動，精進不退；以是三事，名為菩提薩埵。

復次，有人言：初發心作願，我當作佛度一切眾生；從是已來，名菩提薩埵。如偈說：

「若初發心時，誓願當作佛，已過諸世間，應受世供養。」

從初發心到第九無礙，入金剛三昧中，是中間名為菩提薩埵。是菩提薩埵有兩種：有鞞跋致，有阿鞞跋致；如退法、不退法阿羅漢。阿鞞跋致菩提薩埵，是名實菩薩；以是實菩薩故，諸餘退轉菩薩皆名菩薩。譬如得四道人，是名實僧：以實僧故，諸未得道者皆得名僧。

Answer:

When one has a great vow, an unmoving mind, and diligence that does not retreat, because of these three things one is called a bodhi-sattva.

Furthermore, some say: when one first gives rise to the thought and makes the vow, “I shall become a Buddha and carry all beings across,” from that point onward one is called a bodhi-sattva. As a verse says:

“When one first gives rise to the thought and vows to become a Buddha, one has already gone beyond the worlds and should receive offerings from the world.”

From the first arising of the thought up to the ninth unhindered stage, before entering the vajra samādhi, the period in between is called bodhi-sattva. There are two kinds of bodhi-sattva: vaivartika, those who turn back, and avaivartika, those who do not turn back, just as there are arhats subject to retreat and arhats not subject to retreat. The avaivartika bodhi-sattva is called a true bodhisattva. Because there are these true bodhisattvas, the other bodhisattvas who may still turn back are all also called bodhisattvas. It is like those who have attained the four paths: they are called the true Sangha. Because there is the true Sangha, those who have not yet attained the path may all also be called Sangha.

Marks of Non-Retrogression

Chinese Source

問曰：

云何知是菩薩鞞跋致、阿鞞跋致？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

How does one know that a bodhisattva is vaivartika, one who turns back, or avaivartika, one who does not turn back?

答曰：

若觀阿鞞跋致品中，佛自說阿鞞跋致相，如是相是退轉，如是相是不退轉。

復次，若菩薩一法得好修好念，是名阿鞞跋致菩薩。何等一法？常一心集諸善法。如說：諸佛一心集諸善法故，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

復次，若菩薩得一法，是阿鞞跋致相。何等一法？正直精進。如佛問阿難：「阿難！精進能得道不？」「如是，世尊！阿難！精進能得道。」「阿難！實爾！阿難！常念、常修、常行精進，乃至令人得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」如經廣說。

復次，若得二法，是時是阿鞞跋致相。何等二法？一切法實知空；亦念不捨一切眾生；如是以上是阿鞞跋致菩薩。

復次，得三法：一者、若一心作願欲成佛道，如金剛不可動不可破；二者、於一切眾生，慈心徹入骨髓；三者、得般舟三昧，能見現在諸佛，是時名阿鞞跋致。

Answer:

If one looks in the Chapter on Avaivartika, the Buddha himself explains the marks of avaivartika: such marks are turning back; such marks are not turning back.

Furthermore, if a bodhisattva has one dharma that is well cultivated and well kept in mind, this is called an avaivartika bodhisattva. What is that one dharma? Constantly gathering wholesome dharmas with one mind. As it is said, because the Buddhas gather wholesome dharmas with one mind, they attain anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi.

Furthermore, if a bodhisattva attains one dharma, this is a mark of avaivartika. What is that one dharma? Upright and sincere diligence. As the Buddha asked Ānanda, “Ānanda, can diligence attain the path?” “Yes, Blessed One. Ānanda, diligence can attain the path.” “Ānanda, it is truly so. Ānanda, by constantly keeping diligence in mind, constantly cultivating it, and constantly practicing it, one can even attain anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi.” This is explained broadly in the sutra.

Furthermore, if one attains two dharmas, at that time this is a mark of avaivartika. What are the two dharmas? One truly knows that all dharmas are empty, and one also keeps all beings in mind and does not abandon them. From this and above, such a person is an avaivartika bodhisattva.

Furthermore, one attains three dharmas: first, one makes a single-minded vow, wishing to accomplish the Buddha path, like vajra, unmoving and unbreakable; second, toward all beings, one's loving-kindness penetrates through to the bones and marrow; third, one attains the pratyutpanna samādhi and is able to see the present Buddhas. At that time one is called avaivartika.

復次，阿毘曇中，迦旃延尼子弟子輩說：何名菩薩？自覺復能覺他，是名菩薩。必當作佛，是名菩薩。菩提名漏盡人智慧，是人從智慧生，智慧人所護，智慧人所養故，是名菩薩。

又言：發阿鞞跋致心，從是已後名菩薩。

又言：若離五法得五法，是名菩薩。何謂五法？離三惡道，常生天上、人中；離貧窮下賤，常得尊貴；離非男法，常得男身；離諸形殘缺陋，諸根具足；離捨喜忘，常憶宿命，得是宿命智慧，常離一切惡法，遠離惡人，常求道法，攝取弟子；如是名為菩薩。

又言：從種三十二相業已來，是名菩薩。

Furthermore, in the Abhidharma, the disciples of Kātyāyanīputra say: What is called a bodhisattva? One who awakens oneself and is also able to awaken others is called a bodhisattva. One who will certainly become a Buddha is called a bodhisattva. Bodhi means the wisdom of a person whose outflows are exhausted. Because this person is born from wisdom, protected by people of wisdom, and nourished by people of wisdom, this person is called a bodhisattva.

They also say: after one gives rise to the avaivartika mind, from that point onward one is called a bodhisattva.

They also say: if one leaves five dharmas and attains five dharmas, one is called a bodhisattva. What are the five? Leaving the three bad destinies, one is always born in the heavens or among humans. Leaving poverty and low status, one always obtains honor and nobility. Leaving the state of not being male, one always obtains a male body. Leaving all deformity, deficiency, and ugliness of form, one's faculties are complete. Leaving forgetfulness that abandons joy, one always remembers former lives and attains the wisdom of recollecting former lives; one always leaves all evil dharmas, keeps far away from evil people, always seeks the Dharma of the path, and gathers disciples. In this way one is called a bodhisattva.

They also say: from the time one plants the karma for the thirty-two marks onward, one is called a bodhisattva.

Planting the Karma for the Thirty-Two Marks

Chinese Source

問曰：

何時種三十二相業因緣？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

At what time does one plant the karmic causes and conditions for the thirty-two marks?

答曰：

過三阿僧祇劫，然後種三十二相業因緣。

Answer:

After passing through three asaṃkhyeya kalpas, one then plants the karmic causes and conditions for the thirty-two marks.

問曰：

幾時名阿僧祇？

Question:

How much time is called an asaṃkhyeya?

答曰：

天人中能知算數法；極數不能知，是名一阿僧祇。如一一名二，二二名四，三三名九，十十名百，十百名千，十千名萬，千萬名億，千萬億名那由他，千萬那由他名頻婆，千萬頻婆名迦他，過迦他名阿僧祇。如是數三阿僧祇：若行一阿僧祇滿，行第二阿僧祇；第二阿僧祇滿，行第三阿僧祇。譬如算數法，算一乃至算百，百算竟，還至一。如是菩薩一阿僧祇過，還從一起。

初阿僧祇中，心不自知我當作佛不作佛；二阿僧祇中，心雖能知我必作佛，而口不稱我當作佛；三阿僧祇中，心了了自知得作佛，口自發言，無所畏難，我於來世當作佛。

釋迦文佛從過去釋迦文佛，到罽那尸棄佛，為初阿僧祇；是中菩薩永離女人身。從罽那尸棄佛至然燈佛，為二阿僧祇；是中菩薩七枚青蓮華供養然燈佛，敷鹿皮衣，布髮掩泥，是時然燈佛便授其記，汝當來世作佛名釋迦牟尼。從然燈佛至毗婆尸佛，為第三阿僧祇；若過三阿僧祇劫，是時菩薩種三十二相業因緣。

Answer:

Among devas and humans, those who know the methods of calculation can count, but when the number reaches the extreme, they can no longer know it. This is called one asaṃkhyeya. Thus, one times one is called two; two times two is called four; three times three is called nine; ten times ten is called one hundred; ten hundreds are called one thousand; ten thousands are called ten thousand; ten million are called one koṭi; ten million koṭis are called one nayuta; ten million nayutas are called one bimba; ten million bimbas are called one katha; beyond katha is called asaṃkhyeya. In this way one counts three asaṃkhyeyas: when one has completed practicing for one asaṃkhyeya, one practices the second asaṃkhyeya; when the second asaṃkhyeya is complete, one practices the third asaṃkhyeya. It is like the method of calculation: one counts from one up to one hundred, and when the count of one hundred is finished, one returns to one. In this way, after a bodhisattva passes through one asaṃkhyeya, the count returns again to one.

In the first asaṃkhyeya, the mind does not yet know for itself, “Will I become a Buddha or not?” In the second asaṃkhyeya, although the mind can know, “I will certainly become a Buddha,” the mouth does not yet declare, “I will become a Buddha.” In the third asaṃkhyeya, the mind clearly knows for itself that it will become a Buddha, and the mouth speaks forth without fear or difficulty: “In a future life, I will become a Buddha.”

For Śākyamuni Buddha, the period from the past Śākyamuni Buddha up to Ratnaśikhin Buddha was the first asaṃkhyeya; during this period, the bodhisattva forever left behind a woman's body. From Ratnaśikhin Buddha to Dīpaṃkara Buddha was the second asaṃkhyeya; during this period, the bodhisattva offered seven blue lotus flowers to Dīpaṃkara Buddha, spread out a deerskin robe, and laid down his hair to cover the mud. At that time, Dīpaṃkara Buddha gave him the prediction: “In a future life, you will become a Buddha named Śākyamuni.” From Dīpaṃkara Buddha to Vipaśyin Buddha was the third asaṃkhyeya. After passing through three asaṃkhyeya kalpas, at that time the bodhisattva plants the karmic causes and conditions for the thirty-two marks.

問曰：

三十二相業，何處可種？

Question:

Where can the karma for the thirty-two marks be planted?

答曰：

欲界中，非色、無色界；於欲界五道，在人道中種；於四天下，閻浮提中種，非拘耶尼、鬱怛羅越、弗婆提。唯在閻浮提，於男子身種，非女人；佛出世時種，佛不出世不得種；緣佛身得種，緣餘不得種。

Answer:

It is planted in the desire realm, not in the form realm or formless realm. Among the five destinies of the desire realm, it is planted among humans. Among the four continents, it is planted in Jambudvīpa, not in Godānīya, Uttarakuru, or Pūrvavideha. It is planted only in Jambudvīpa, in a male body, not in a woman. It is planted when a Buddha appears in the world; if no Buddha appears in the world, it cannot be planted. It is planted in dependence on the Buddha's body; in dependence on anything else, it cannot be planted.

問曰：

是三十二相業，於身業、口業、意業，何業種？

Question:

As for the karma for the thirty-two marks, among bodily karma, verbal karma, and mental karma, by which karma is it planted?

答曰：

意業種，非身、口業。何以故？是意業利故。

Answer:

It is planted by mental karma, not by bodily or verbal karma. Why? Because mental karma is sharp.

問曰：

意業有六識，是三十二相業，為是意識種？是五識種？

Question:

Mental karma includes the six consciousnesses. Is the karma for the thirty-two marks planted by mental consciousness, or is it planted by the five consciousnesses?

答曰：

是意識，非五識。何以故？五識不能分別，以是故意識種。

Answer:

It is mental consciousness, not the five consciousnesses. Why? The five consciousnesses cannot discriminate. For this reason, it is planted by mental consciousness.

問曰：

何相初種？

Question:

Which mark is planted first?

答曰：

有人言：足安立相先種，何以故？先安立，然後能種餘相。有人言：紺青眼相初種，得此眼相大慈觀眾生。雖有是語，不必爾也。若相因緣和合時，便是初種。

Answer:

Some say: the mark of firmly planted feet is planted first. Why? One is first firmly planted, and only afterward can the other marks be planted. Some say: the mark of deep blue eyes is planted first; after attaining this eye-mark, one contemplates beings with great loving-kindness. Although these sayings exist, this is not necessarily so. When the causes and conditions for a mark come together, that is the first planting.

One Hundred Merits for Each Mark

Chinese Source

問曰：

一思種？為多思種？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

Are they planted by one thought, or are they planted by many thoughts?

答曰：

三十二思種三十二相，一一思種一一相，一一相百福德莊嚴。

Answer:

Thirty-two thoughts plant the thirty-two marks. Each thought plants one mark, and each mark is adorned by one hundred merits.

問曰：

幾許名一福德？

Question:

How much is called one merit?

答曰：

有人言：業報轉輪聖王，於四天下受福樂得自在，是名一福德。如是百福成一相。

復有人言：作釋提桓因，於二天中得自在，是名一福。

復有人言：作他化自在天王，於欲界中得自在，是名一福。

復有人言：除補處菩薩，餘一切眾生所得福報，是名一福。

復有人言：天地劫盡，一切眾生共福德故，三千大千世界報立，是名一福。

復有人言：是福不可量，（不）可以譬喻知：如三千大千世界一切眾生，皆盲無目，有一人能治令差，是為一福。一切人皆被毒藥，一人能治令差；一切人應死，一人能救之令脫；一切人破戒，破正見，一人能教令得淨戒、正見；如是等為一福。

復有人言：是福不可量，不可譬喻。是菩薩入第三阿僧祇中，心思大行，種是三十二相因緣。以是故，是福無能量，唯佛能知。

Answer:

Some say: the karmic reward of becoming a wheel-turning sage king, receiving blessed happiness and attaining sovereignty over the four continents, is called one merit. In this way, one hundred merits complete one mark.

Others say: becoming Śakra Devānām Indra and attaining sovereignty among the two heavens is called one merit.

Others say: becoming the heavenly king of Paranirmitavaśavartin and attaining sovereignty in the desire realm is called one merit.

Others say: apart from a bodhisattva in the position of one more birth, all the blessed rewards attained by all other beings are called one merit.

Others say: when heaven and earth are exhausted at the end of a kalpa, because of the shared merit of all beings, the retribution-world of the three-thousand-great-thousand world system is established. This is called one merit.

Others say: this merit is immeasurable, but it can be known by analogy. Suppose all beings in a three-thousand-great-thousand world system were blind and without eyes, and one person could heal them and make them recover. This would be one merit. If all people were poisoned, and one person could heal them and make them recover; if all people were about to die, and one person could save them and make them escape; if all people had broken precepts and broken right view, and one person could teach them and enable them to attain pure precepts and right view, such things would be one merit.

Others say: this merit is immeasurable and cannot be expressed by analogy. When this bodhisattva enters the third asaṃkhyeya, with the mind thinking on great practice, one plants the causes and conditions for these thirty-two marks. For this reason, this merit cannot be measured. Only the Buddha can know it.

Praising Puṣya Buddha for Seven Days

Chinese Source

問曰：

菩薩幾時能種三十二相？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

How long does it take for a bodhisattva to plant the thirty-two marks?

答曰：

極遲百劫，極疾九十一劫。釋迦牟尼菩薩九十一大劫行辦三十二相。如經中言：過去久遠，有佛名弗沙。時有二菩薩：一名釋迦牟尼，一名彌勒。弗沙佛欲觀釋迦牟尼菩薩心純淑未？即觀見之，知其心未純淑，而諸弟子心皆純淑。又彌勒菩薩心已純淑，而弟子未純淑。

是時弗沙佛如是思惟：一人之心易可速化，眾人之心，難可疾治。如是思惟竟，弗沙佛欲使釋迦牟尼菩薩疾得成佛，上雪山上，於寶窟中入火定。是時，釋迦牟尼菩薩作外道仙人，上山採藥。見弗沙佛坐寶窟中，入火定，放光明。見已，心歡喜信敬，翹一腳立，叉手向佛，一心而觀，目未曾眴，七日七夜，以一偈讚佛：

天上天下無如佛，十方世界亦無比，世界所有我盡見，一切無有如佛者！

七日七夜諦觀世尊，目未曾眴，超越九劫，於九十一劫中得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Answer:

At the slowest, it takes one hundred kalpas; at the fastest, ninety-one kalpas. Śākyamuni Bodhisattva practiced and completed the thirty-two marks over ninety-one great kalpas. As the sutra says: in the distant past, there was a Buddha named Puṣya. At that time there were two bodhisattvas: one named Śākyamuni and one named Maitreya. Puṣya Buddha wished to observe whether Śākyamuni Bodhisattva's mind had become pure and mature. On observing, he saw that Śākyamuni's own mind was not yet pure and mature, but that all his disciples' minds were pure and mature. He also saw that Maitreya Bodhisattva's own mind was already pure and mature, but that his disciples were not yet pure and mature.

At that time Puṣya Buddha thought in this way: “The mind of one person can easily be transformed quickly; the minds of many people are difficult to cure quickly.” Having thought in this way, Puṣya Buddha wished to cause Śākyamuni Bodhisattva to attain Buddhahood quickly. He went up onto Snow Mountain and, in a jeweled cave, entered the fire concentration. At that time, Śākyamuni Bodhisattva was an ascetic of an outside path who had gone up the mountain to gather herbs. He saw Puṣya Buddha sitting in the jeweled cave, entering the fire concentration and emitting light. Having seen this, his mind became joyful, faithful, and reverent. He stood on one leg, joined his palms toward the Buddha, and contemplated him with one mind. His eyes never blinked. For seven days and seven nights, he praised the Buddha with one verse:

In heaven and under heaven, none is like the Buddha;
In the worlds of the ten directions, none compares with him.
All that exists in the world, I have fully seen;
Among them all, there is none like the Buddha.

For seven days and seven nights, he carefully contemplated the World-Honored One, and his eyes never blinked. He surpassed nine kalpas and attained anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi in ninety-one kalpas.

問曰：

若釋迦牟尼菩薩聰明多識，能作種種好偈，何以故七日七夜一偈讚佛？

Question:

If Śākyamuni Bodhisattva was intelligent and learned, able to compose many kinds of fine verses, why did he praise the Buddha with only one verse for seven days and seven nights?

答曰：

釋迦牟尼菩薩貴其心思，不貴多言。若更以餘偈讚佛，心或散亂，是故七日七夜以一偈讚佛。

Answer:

Śākyamuni Bodhisattva valued the thought of his mind; he did not value many words. If he had praised the Buddha with other verses, his mind might have become scattered. For this reason, for seven days and seven nights he praised the Buddha with one verse.

問曰：

釋迦牟尼菩薩，何以心未純淑而弟子純淑？彌勒菩薩心純淑，而弟子未純淑？

Question:

Why was Śākyamuni Bodhisattva's own mind not yet pure and mature while his disciples were pure and mature, and why was Maitreya Bodhisattva's own mind pure and mature while his disciples were not yet pure and mature?

答曰：

釋迦牟尼菩薩，饒益眾生心多，自為身少故；彌勒菩薩多為己身，少為眾生故。

Answer:

It was because Śākyamuni Bodhisattva had much thought for benefiting beings and little for himself, while Maitreya Bodhisattva had much thought for himself and little for beings.

The Perfection of Giving

Chinese Source

從鞞婆尸佛至迦葉佛，於其中間九十一大劫，種三十二相業因緣竟，六波羅蜜滿。何等六？檀波羅蜜，尸羅波羅蜜，羼提波羅蜜，毗梨耶波羅蜜，禪波羅蜜，般若波羅蜜。

Ru-Yi Translation

From Vipaśyin Buddha to Kāśyapa Buddha, during the interval of ninety-one great kalpas, he finished planting the karmic causes and conditions for the thirty-two marks, and the six pāramitās were fulfilled. What are the six? The perfection of giving, the perfection of morality, the perfection of patience, the perfection of energy, the perfection of meditation, and the perfection of wisdom.

問曰：

檀波羅蜜云何滿？

Question:

How is the perfection of giving fulfilled?

答曰：

一切能施，無所遮礙，乃至以身施時，心無所惜。譬如尸毗王以身施鴿。釋迦牟尼佛本身作王，名尸毗。是王得歸命救護陀羅尼，大精進，有慈悲心，視一切眾生，如母愛子。時世無佛，釋提桓因命欲終，自念言：何處有佛一切智人？處處問難，不能斷疑，知盡非佛，即還天上，愁憂而坐。巧變化師毗首羯磨天問曰：

Answer:

One is able to give everything, without any obstruction. Even when giving one's own body, the mind has no reluctance. It is like King Śibi giving his body for the sake of a dove. In a former life, Śākyamuni Buddha was a king named Śibi. This king had attained the dhāraṇī of taking refuge and giving protection. He had great energy and a mind of loving-kindness and compassion. He regarded all beings as a mother loves her child. At that time there was no Buddha in the world. Śakra Devānām Indra's life was about to end, and he thought to himself, “Where is there a Buddha, an omniscient person?” He went everywhere posing questions, but no one could cut off his doubts. Knowing that none of them was a Buddha, he returned to heaven and sat in sorrow. There was then the skillful transformation-master, the deva Viśvakarman, who asked:

問曰：

天主何以愁憂？

Why was the lord of devas sorrowful?

答曰：

我求一切智人不可得，以是故愁憂。毗首羯磨言：有大菩薩，布施、持戒、禪定、智慧具足，不久當作佛。帝釋以偈答曰：

菩薩發大心，魚子菴樹華，三事因時多，成果時甚少！

毗首羯磨答曰：是優尸那種尸毗王，持戒、精進、大慈、大悲，禪定、智慧，不久作佛。釋提桓因語毗首羯磨：當往試之，知有菩薩相不？汝作鴿，我作鷹，汝便佯怖入王腋下，我當逐汝。毗首羯磨言：此大菩薩，云何以此事惱！釋提桓因說偈言：

我亦非惡心，如真金應試；以此試菩薩，知其心定不。

He answered, “I seek an omniscient person but cannot find one; for this reason I am sorrowful.” Viśvakarman said, “There is a great bodhisattva who is complete in giving, morality, meditation, and wisdom. Before long he will become a Buddha.” Indra answered with a verse:

When bodhisattvas give rise to the great mind,
they are like fish eggs, mango blossoms, and flowering udumbara trees:
these three are many at the time of cause,
but very few at the time of fruit.

Viśvakarman answered, “This King Śibi of the Uśīnara clan has morality, energy, great loving-kindness, great compassion, meditation, and wisdom. Before long he will become a Buddha.” Śakra Devānām Indra said to Viśvakarman, “We should go test him and see whether he has the marks of a bodhisattva. You become a dove, and I will become a hawk. You pretend to be frightened and enter beneath the king's arm, and I will chase you.” Viśvakarman said, “How can we trouble this great bodhisattva with such a matter?” Śakra Devānām Indra spoke a verse:

I too do not have an evil mind;
as true gold should be tested,
so by this we test the bodhisattva
to know whether his mind is firm.

說此偈竟，毗首羯磨即自變身作一赤眼赤足鴿，釋提桓因自變身作一鷹；急飛逐鴿，鴿直來入王腋底，舉身戰怖，動眼促聲。

是時眾多人，相與而語曰：是王大慈仁，一切宜保護。

如是鴿小鳥，歸之如入舍；菩薩相如是，作佛必不久。

When he had spoken this verse, Viśvakarman immediately transformed his own body into a dove with red eyes and red feet, and Śakra Devānām Indra transformed himself into a hawk. The hawk flew swiftly in pursuit of the dove. The dove came straight in beneath the king's arm, its whole body trembling in fear, its eyes moving and its voice urgent.

At that time many people spoke with one another, saying, “This king has great loving-kindness and humanity. He ought to protect all.”

This dove, a small bird,
returns to him as though entering a home.
Such is the mark of a bodhisattva;
his Buddhahood will surely not be long delayed.

是時鷹在近樹上，語尸毗王；還與我鴿，此我所受！王時語鷹：我前受此，非是汝受；我初發意時受此，一切眾生皆欲度之。鷹言：王欲度一切眾生，我非一切耶？何以獨不見愍而奪我今日食？王答言：汝須何食？我作誓願，其有眾生來歸我者，必救護之。汝須何食，亦當相給。鷹言：我須新殺熱肉。王念言：如此難得，自非殺生，何由得也！我當云何殺一與一！思惟心定，即自說偈：

是我此身肉，恆屬老病死，不久當臭爛，彼須我當與。

At that time the hawk was in a nearby tree and said to King Śibi, “Give the dove back to me. This is what I have received.” The king then said to the hawk, “I received this one first; it is not what you received. When I first gave rise to the resolve, I accepted all beings and wished to liberate them all.” The hawk said, “The king wishes to liberate all beings. Am I not among all beings? Why do you alone show me no compassion and take away my food for today?” The king answered, “What food do you need? I have made a vow: any being who comes to me for refuge, I will surely rescue and protect. Whatever food you need, I will also provide for you.” The hawk said, “I need freshly killed warm flesh.” The king thought, “This is hard to obtain. Unless one kills a living being, how could it be obtained? How could I kill one being to give to another?” Having reflected and settled his mind, he spoke this verse:

The flesh of this body of mine
always belongs to aging, sickness, and death.
Before long it will stink and decay;
if he needs it, I should give it.

如是思惟已，呼人持刀，自割股肉與鷹。鷹語王言：王雖以熱肉與我，當用道理，令肉輕重得與鴿等，勿見欺也！王言持稱來！以肉對鴿，鴿身轉重，王肉轉輕。王令人割二股，亦輕不足；次割兩踹、兩臗、兩乳、頸、脊、舉身肉盡，鴿身猶重，王肉故輕。是時近臣內戚，安施帳幔，卻諸看人；王今如此，無可觀也！尸毗王言：勿遮諸人，聽令入看！而說偈言：

天人阿脩羅，一切來觀我！大心無上志，以求成佛道。

若有求佛道，當忍此大苦；不能堅固心，則當息其意。

Having reflected in this way, he called for someone to bring a knife and cut flesh from his own thigh to give to the hawk. The hawk said to the king, “Although the king gives me warm flesh, this should be done according to reason. Let the weight of the flesh be equal to that of the dove. Do not deceive me.” The king said, “Bring a scale.” They weighed the flesh against the dove, but the dove's body grew heavier and the king's flesh grew lighter. The king had them cut flesh from both thighs, but it was still too light and insufficient. Next they cut flesh from both calves, both hips, both breasts, his neck, and his back. When the flesh of his whole body was exhausted, the dove's body was still heavy and the king's flesh was still light. At that time his close ministers and relatives set up curtains and sent away the people who were watching, saying, “The king is now like this; there is nothing to see.” King Śibi said, “Do not block the people. Let them come in and see.” Then he spoke these verses:

Devas, humans, and asuras,
all come and look at me.
With a great mind and unsurpassed aspiration,
I seek to accomplish the Buddha path.

If anyone seeks the Buddha path,
one should endure this great suffering.
If one's mind cannot be firm,
then one should put that intention to rest.

是時，菩薩以血塗手，攀稱欲上，定心以身盡以對鴿。鷹言：大王！此事難辦，何用如此？以鴿還我！王言：鴿來歸我，終不與汝。我喪身無量，於物無益，今欲以身求易佛道。以手攀稱，爾時，菩薩肉盡筋斷，不能自制，欲上而墮。自責心言：汝當自堅，勿得迷悶！一切眾生墮憂苦大海，汝一人立誓欲渡一切，何以怠悶！此苦甚少，地獄苦多，以此相比，於十六分猶不及一，我今有智慧、精進、持戒、禪定，猶患此苦，何況地獄中人無智慧者？是時菩薩一心欲上，復更攀稱，語人扶我，是時菩薩心定無悔。諸天、龍王、阿脩羅、鬼、神、人民皆大讚言：為小鳥乃爾，是事希有！即時天地為六種震動，大海波揚，枯樹生華，天降香雨及散名華，天女歌讚必得成佛。是時四方神仙皆來讚言：是真菩薩，必早成佛！鷹語鴿言：眾試如此，不惜身命，是真菩薩！即說偈言：

慈悲地中生，一切智樹牙，我曹當供養，不應施憂惱！

At that time the bodhisattva smeared his hands with blood and grasped the scale, wishing to climb onto it. With firm mind, he intended to place his whole body against the dove. The hawk said, “Great king, this matter is difficult to accomplish. Why go this far? Give the dove back to me.” The king said, “The dove has come to me for refuge. I will never give it to you. I have lost my body countless times with no benefit to anyone. Now I wish to use this body to seek and exchange for the Buddha path.” With his hands he grasped the scale. At that time, the bodhisattva's flesh was gone and his sinews were cut. Unable to control himself, he tried to climb up but fell down. He reproached his own mind, saying, “You should make yourself firm. Do not become confused or faint. All beings have fallen into the great ocean of sorrow and suffering. You alone have made a vow to carry them all across. Why are you weary and faint? This suffering is very little; the suffering of the hells is much greater. Compared with that, this does not reach even one sixteenth part. I now have wisdom, energy, morality, and meditation, yet I still suffer from this pain. How much more so people in the hells who have no wisdom?” At that time the bodhisattva single-mindedly wished to climb up. Again he grasped the scale and said to the people, “Support me.” At that time the bodhisattva's mind was firm and without regret. The devas, nāga kings, asuras, ghosts, spirits, and people all greatly praised him, saying, “For the sake of a small bird he goes this far. This is rare indeed.” Immediately heaven and earth shook in six ways, the great ocean's waves rose, withered trees produced flowers, the heavens rained fragrance and scattered famous flowers, and heavenly women sang praises, saying that he would certainly become a Buddha. At that time sages from the four directions all came and praised him, saying, “This is a true bodhisattva. He will surely become a Buddha soon.” The hawk said to the dove, “After being tested in so many ways, he still does not cherish body or life. This is a true bodhisattva.” He then spoke this verse:

Born in the ground of loving-kindness and compassion,
he is the sprout of the tree of omniscience.
We should make offerings to him;
we should not cause him sorrow or distress.

毗首羯磨語釋提桓因言：天主！汝有神力，可令此王身得平復。釋提桓因言：不須我也。此王自作誓願，大心歡喜，不惜身命，感發一切令求佛道。帝釋語人王言：汝割肉辛苦，心不惱沒耶？王言：我心歡喜，不惱不沒。帝釋言：誰當信汝心不沒者？是時菩薩作實誓願：我割肉血流，不瞋不惱，一心不悶以求佛道者，我身當即平復如故。即出語時，身復如本。人天見之，皆大悲喜，歎未曾有。此大菩薩必當作佛，我曹應當盡一心供養。願令早成佛道，當念我等！是時釋提桓因、毗首羯磨，各還天上。如是等種種相，是檀波羅蜜滿。

Viśvakarman said to Śakra Devānām Indra, “Lord of devas, you have spiritual power. You can cause this king's body to be restored.” Śakra Devānām Indra said, “There is no need for me. This king has himself made a true vow. With a great mind, he is joyful and does not cherish body or life. He has moved and inspired everyone to seek the Buddha path.” Indra said to the human king, “You have suffered hardship in cutting your flesh. Has your mind become afflicted and sunk?” The king said, “My mind is joyful. It is neither afflicted nor sunk.” Indra said, “Who will believe that your mind has not sunk?” At that time the bodhisattva made a vow of truth: “If, when I cut my flesh and my blood flowed, I did not become angry or afflicted, and if with one mind, without faintness, I sought the Buddha path, then let my body immediately be restored as before.” As soon as he spoke these words, his body was restored as before. When humans and devas saw this, they were all moved with great compassion and joy, and they praised what had never been before: “This great bodhisattva will certainly become a Buddha. We should all make offerings to him with our whole mind. May he quickly accomplish the Buddha path and remember us.” At that time Śakra Devānām Indra and Viśvakarman each returned to heaven. Various marks such as these show that the perfection of giving was fulfilled.
