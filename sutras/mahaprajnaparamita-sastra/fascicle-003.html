Dwelling in Rājagṛha

Fascicle 3 begins the explanation of where the Buddha dwells: Rājagṛha, Vulture Peak, and the reason the sutra gives a place before entering the teaching itself.

Chinese source shown beside English Translation in progress Started August 2026

Opening the Place of the Teaching

Chinese Source

〖經〗

住王舍城。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sutra:

Dwelling in the city of Rājagṛha.

〖論〗

今當說。

問曰：何以不直說般若波羅蜜法，而說「佛住王舍城」？

答曰：說方、時、人，令人心生信故。

Treatise:

Now this will be explained.

Question: Why does the text not directly teach the Dharma of the Perfection of Wisdom, but instead say, “The Buddha dwelt in the city of Rājagṛha”?

Answer: It states the place, time, and person in order to cause people’s minds to give rise to faith.

云何名「住」？四種身儀：坐、臥、行、住，是名住。

又以怖魔軍眾、自令弟子歡喜入種種諸禪定故，在是中住。

What is meant by “dwelling”? There are four bodily postures: sitting, lying down, walking, and standing. This is called dwelling.

Also, in order to frighten the armies of Māra and to cause his disciples to joyfully enter many kinds of meditative concentration, the Buddha dwells there.

復次，三種住：天住、梵住、聖住。六種欲天住法，是為天住。梵天等乃至非有想非無想天住法，是名梵住。諸佛、辟支佛、阿羅漢住法，是名聖住。於是三住法中，住聖住法；憐愍眾生故，住王舍城。

Furthermore, there are three kinds of dwelling: deva dwelling, Brahmā dwelling, and noble dwelling. The dharmas by which the six classes of desire-realm devas dwell are called deva dwelling. The dharmas by which Brahmā devas and others up to the devas of neither perception nor nonperception dwell are called Brahmā dwelling. The dharmas by which buddhas, pratyekabuddhas, and arhats dwell are called noble dwelling. Among these three kinds of dwelling, the Buddha dwells in the dharma of noble dwelling. Because he has compassion for beings, he dwells in the city of Rājagṛha.

復次，布施、持戒、善心三事，故名天住。慈、悲、喜、捨四無量心，故名梵住。空、無相、無作，是三三昧名聖住。聖住法，佛於中住。

Furthermore, generosity, moral discipline, and a wholesome mind are the three matters called deva dwelling. Kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity, the four immeasurable minds, are called Brahmā dwelling. Emptiness, signlessness, and wishlessness, these three samādhis, are called noble dwelling. The Buddha dwells in the dharma of noble dwelling.

復次，四種住：天住、梵住、聖住、佛住。三住，如前說。佛住者，首楞嚴等諸佛無量三昧、十力、四無所畏、十八不共法、一切智等種種諸慧，及八萬四千法藏度人門。如是等種種諸佛功德是佛所住處，佛於中住。

略說「住」竟。

Furthermore, there are four kinds of dwelling: deva dwelling, Brahmā dwelling, noble dwelling, and Buddha dwelling. The first three kinds of dwelling are as explained above. Buddha dwelling means the immeasurable samādhis of the buddhas, such as the Śūraṅgama Samādhi; the ten powers; the four fearlessnesses; the eighteen unshared dharmas; all-wisdom and many other kinds of wisdom; and the eighty-four thousand Dharma treasuries, the gateways for liberating people. Many kinds of buddha virtues such as these are the places where the Buddha dwells, and the Buddha dwells within them.

This completes the brief explanation of “dwelling.”

Why the City Is Called Rājagṛha

Chinese Source

「王舍城」者。

問曰：如舍婆提、迦毘羅婆、波羅[木*柰]大城皆有諸王舍，何以故獨名此城為王舍？

Ru-Yi Translation

Now, “the city of Rājagṛha.”

Question: Great cities such as Śrāvastī, Kapilavastu, and Bārāṇasī all have royal residences. Why is this city alone called Rājagṛha, the King’s Residence?

答曰：有人言：「是摩伽陀國王有子，一頭，兩面，四臂。時人以為不祥，王即裂其身首，棄之曠野。羅剎女鬼名梨羅，還合其身而乳養之。後大成人，力能并兼諸國，王有天下，取諸國王萬八千人置此五山中。以大力勢治閻浮提，閻浮提人因名此山為王舍城。」

Answer: Some say that a king of Magadha had a son with one head, two faces, and four arms. People at the time considered him inauspicious, so the king tore apart his body and head and abandoned him in the wilderness. A female rākṣasa named Līlā joined his body back together and nursed him. Later, when he grew to adulthood, his strength was able to annex other countries, and he ruled the whole realm. He took eighteen thousand kings from other countries and placed them among these five mountains. Because he governed Jambudvīpa with great power and force, the people of Jambudvīpa called this mountain area Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

復次，有人言：「摩伽陀王先所住城，城中失火，一燒一作，如是至七。國人疲役，王大憂怖，集諸智人問其意故。有言：『宜應易處。』王即更求住處，見此五山周匝如城，即作宮殿於中止住，以是故名王舍城。」

Furthermore, some say that in the city where the king of Magadha first lived, fires broke out. Each time the city burned, it was rebuilt, and this happened seven times. The people of the country were exhausted by the labor, and the king was greatly worried and afraid. He gathered wise people and asked the meaning of this. Some said, “It would be best to move to another place.” The king then searched for another place to live. Seeing that these five mountains surrounded the area like a city wall, he built palaces there and dwelt there. For this reason, it was called Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

復次，往古世時，此國有王名婆藪，心厭世法，出家作仙人。

是時居家婆羅門與諸出家仙人共論議。

居家婆羅門言：「經書云：『天祀中，應殺生噉肉。』」

諸出家仙人言：「不應天祀中殺生噉肉。」共諍云云。

Furthermore, in ancient times, this country had a king named Vasu. His mind became weary of worldly dharmas, so he left home and became a seer.

At that time, household brahmins and renunciant seers debated with one another.

The household brahmins said, “The scriptures say, ‘In sacrifices to the devas, one should kill living beings and eat meat.’”

The renunciant seers said, “In sacrifices to the devas, one should not kill living beings and eat meat.” In this way, they argued back and forth.

諸出家婆羅門言：「此有大王出家作仙人，汝等信不？」

諸居家婆羅門言：「信。」

諸出家仙人言：「我以此人為證，後日當問。」

諸居家婆羅門即以其夜先到婆藪仙人所，種種問已，語婆藪仙人：「明日論議，汝當助我。」

The renunciant brahmins said, “There is a great king here who has left home and become a seer. Do you trust him?”

The household brahmins said, “We trust him.”

The renunciant seers said, “We will take him as our witness and ask him another day.”

That very night, the household brahmins went first to the seer Vasu. After asking him many things, they said to him, “In tomorrow’s debate, you should help us.”

如是明旦論時，諸出家仙人問婆藪仙人：「天祀中應殺生噉肉不？」

婆藪仙人言：「婆羅門法，天祀中應殺生噉肉。」

諸出家仙人言：「於汝實心云何？應殺生噉肉不？」

婆藪仙人言：「為天祀故，應殺生噉肉。此生在天祀中死故，得生天上。」

The next morning, during the debate, the renunciant seers asked the seer Vasu, “In sacrifices to the devas, should one kill living beings and eat meat?”

The seer Vasu said, “According to brahmin law, in sacrifices to the devas one should kill living beings and eat meat.”

The renunciant seers said, “What is in your true mind? Should one kill living beings and eat meat?”

The seer Vasu said, “For the sake of sacrifice to the devas, one should kill living beings and eat meat. Because this being dies within a sacrifice to the devas, it will be reborn in heaven.”

諸出家仙人言：「汝大不是！汝大妄語！」即唾之言：「罪人滅去。」是時，婆藪仙人尋陷入地沒踝，是初開大罪門故。

諸出家仙人言：「汝應實語，若故妄語者，汝身當陷入地中。」

婆藪仙人言：「我知為天故殺羊、噉肉無罪。」即復陷入地至膝，如是漸漸稍沒至腰，至頸。

The renunciant seers said, “You are greatly wrong. You have spoken a great falsehood.” They spat at him and said, “Sinful person, disappear.” At that time, the seer Vasu immediately sank into the earth up to his ankles, because he had first opened the gate to a great offense.

The renunciant seers said, “You should speak truthfully. If you deliberately speak falsely, your body will sink into the earth.”

The seer Vasu said, “I know that killing sheep and eating meat for the sake of the devas is without fault.” He then sank into the earth up to his knees. In this way, little by little, he sank to his waist, and then to his neck.

諸出家仙人言：「汝今妄語得現世報，更以實語者，雖入地下，我能出汝，令得免罪。」

爾時，婆藪仙人自思惟言：「我貴重人，不應兩種語。又婆羅門四圍陀法中，種種因緣讚祀天法，我一人死，當何足計！」一心言：「應天祀中殺生、噉肉無罪。」

The renunciant seers said, “Now your false speech has received its result in this present life. If you now speak truthfully, even though you have entered the ground, we can bring you out and enable you to avoid this offense.”

At that time, the seer Vasu reflected to himself, “I am a person of high standing and should not speak in two different ways. Moreover, in the brahmin teaching of the four Vedas, there are many reasons praising sacrifice to the devas. If I alone die, what does that matter?” With single-minded resolve, he said, “In sacrifices to the devas, killing living beings and eating meat are without fault.”

諸出家仙人言：「汝重罪人！催去！不用見汝！」於是舉身沒地中。從是以來乃至今日，常用婆藪仙人王法，於天祀中殺羊，當下刀時言：「婆藪殺汝。」

The renunciant seers said, “You person of grave offense, go quickly. We do not want to see you.” Thereupon his whole body sank into the earth. From that time down to the present day, people have constantly followed the royal law of the seer Vasu: when killing sheep in sacrifice to the devas, at the moment the knife is lowered, they say, “Vasu kills you.”

婆藪之子，名曰廣車，嗣位為王，後亦厭世法，而復不能出家。如是思惟：「我父先王出家，生入地中，若治天下，復作大罪，我今當何以自處？」如是思惟時，聞空中聲言：「汝若行見難值希有處，汝應是中作舍住。」作是語已，便不復聞聲。未經幾時，王出田獵，見有一鹿走疾如風，王便逐之，而不可及，遂逐不止，百官侍從無能及者。轉前見有五山，周匝峻固，其地平正，生草細軟，好華遍地，種種林木、華果茂盛，溫泉、涼池皆悉清淨。其地莊嚴，處處有散天華、天香，聞天伎樂。

Vasu’s son was named Broad Chariot. He succeeded to the throne and became king. Later, he too became weary of worldly dharmas, yet he could not leave home. He reflected, “My father, the former king, left home and sank alive into the earth. If I govern the world, I too will commit great offenses. How should I now conduct myself?” As he was reflecting in this way, he heard a voice in the sky say, “If as you travel you see a rare and hard-to-find place, you should build a residence and dwell there.” After saying this, the voice was not heard again. Not long afterward, the king went hunting in the fields and saw a deer running as swiftly as the wind. The king chased it, but could not catch it, and continued pursuing it without stopping. None of his officials and attendants could keep up. Going farther ahead, he saw five mountains encircling a place, steep and solid like walls. The ground was level and proper, with fine soft grasses growing there, beautiful flowers spread everywhere, many kinds of trees, flowers, and fruits flourishing, and warm springs and cool ponds all pure and clear. The place was adorned, with heavenly flowers and heavenly fragrances scattered everywhere, and the music of heavenly performers could be heard.

爾時，乾闥婆伎適見王來，各自還去。「是處希有，未曾所見，今我正當在是中作舍住。」如是思惟已，群臣百官尋跡而到。

王告諸臣：「我前所聞空中聲言：『汝行若見希有難值之處，汝應是中作舍住。』我今見此希有之處，我應是中作舍住。」即捨本城，於此山中住。是王初始在是中住，從是已後次第止住。是王元起造立宮舍，故名王舍城。

略說王舍城本起竟。

At that time, the gandharva musicians happened to see the king coming and each returned to his own place. The king thought, “This place is rare, something I have never seen before. Now I should build a residence and dwell here.” After he had reflected in this way, his ministers, officials, and attendants followed his tracks and arrived.

The king told the ministers, “Earlier I heard a voice in the sky say, ‘If as you travel you see a rare and hard-to-find place, you should build a residence and dwell there.’ Now I have seen this rare place. I should build a residence and dwell here.” He then abandoned his original city and dwelt among these mountains. This king was the first to dwell there, and after him others dwelt there in succession. Because this king originally established and built palaces and residences there, it was called Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

This completes the brief explanation of the origin of Rājagṛha.

At Gṛdhrakūṭa, Vulture Peak

Chinese Source

〖經〗

耆闍崛山中。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sutra:

On Gṛdhrakūṭa Mountain, Vulture Peak.

〖論〗

「耆闍」名鷲，「崛」名頭。

問曰：何以名鷲頭山？

答曰：是山頂似鷲，王舍城人見其似鷲故，共傳言鷲頭山，因名之為鷲頭山。

Treatise:

“Gṛdhra” means vulture, and “kūṭa” means peak.

Question: Why is it called Vulture Peak Mountain?

Answer: The summit of this mountain resembles a vulture. When the people of Rājagṛha saw that it resembled a vulture, they passed down the name Vulture Peak Mountain, and for this reason it was called Vulture Peak Mountain.

復次，王舍城南屍陀林中，多諸死人，諸鷲常來噉之，還在山頭，時人遂名鷲頭山。

是山於五山中最高大，多好林水，聖人住處。

Furthermore, in the Śītavana charnel ground south of Rājagṛha, there were many dead bodies. Vultures often came to eat them and then returned to the mountain peak. People of that time therefore named it Vulture Peak Mountain.

Among the five mountains, this mountain is the highest and greatest. It has many fine groves and waters and is a dwelling place of noble ones.

Why the Buddha Often Dwells in Rājagṛha

Chinese Source

問曰：已知耆闍崛山義，佛何以故住王舍城？

諸佛法普慈一切，如日照萬物，無不蒙明。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The meaning of Gṛdhrakūṭa Mountain is now understood. Why did the Buddha dwell in Rājagṛha?

The Dharma of the buddhas extends universal compassion to all, like the sun shining on the ten thousand things, so that none fail to receive its light.

如漚祇尼大城、富樓那跋檀大城、阿藍車多羅大城、弗迦羅婆多大城，如是等大城，多人豐樂而不住；何故多住王舍城、舍婆提大城？

波羅柰、迦毘羅婆、瞻婆、婆翅多、拘睒鞞、鳩樓城等，雖有住時，而多住王舍城、舍婆提。云何知多住二處？見佛諸經多在二城說，少在餘城。

Great cities such as Ujjayinī, Pūrṇavardhana, Ahicchatra, and Puṣkarāvatī had many people and abundant happiness, yet the Buddha did not dwell there. Why did he often dwell in the great cities of Rājagṛha and Śrāvastī?

Although he did stay at times in Bārāṇasī, Kapilavastu, Campā, Sāketa, Kauśāmbī, Kuru City, and other places, he mostly stayed in Rājagṛha and Śrāvastī. How do we know that he often stayed in these two places? We see that in the Buddha’s sutras, most teachings were spoken in these two cities, and only a few in other cities.

答曰：佛雖大慈等及，以漚祇尼等諸大城是邊國故不住。

又彌離車弊惡人多，善根未熟故。如偈說：

「如日光等照， 華熟則時開； 若華未應敷， 則亦不強開。 佛亦復如是， 等心而說法， 善根熟則敷， 未熟則不開。 以是故世尊， 住三種人中， 利智善根熟， 結使煩惱薄。」

Answer: Although the Buddha’s great compassion reaches all equally, he did not dwell in great cities such as Ujjayinī because they were border regions.

Also, among the Mlecchas there were many rough and harmful people, and their wholesome roots had not yet matured. As a verse says:

“Sunlight shines equally on all, yet flowers open only when they are ripe. If a flower is not yet ready to blossom, it is not forced open. The Buddha is also like this: with an equal mind he teaches the Dharma. When wholesome roots have matured, they open; when they have not matured, they do not open. For this reason, the World-Honored One dwells among three kinds of people: those of keen wisdom, those whose wholesome roots have matured, and those whose fetters and afflictions are thin.”
