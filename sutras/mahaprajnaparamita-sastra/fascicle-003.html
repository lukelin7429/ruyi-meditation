Dwelling in Rājagṛha

Fascicle 3 begins the explanation of where the Buddha dwells: Rājagṛha, Vulture Peak, and the reason the sutra gives a place before entering the teaching itself.

Opening the Place of the Teaching

Chinese Source

〖經〗

住王舍城。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sutra:

Dwelling in the city of Rājagṛha.

〖論〗

今當說。

問曰：何以不直說般若波羅蜜法，而說「佛住王舍城」？

答曰：說方、時、人，令人心生信故。

Treatise:

Now this will be explained.

Question: Why does the text not directly teach the Dharma of the Perfection of Wisdom, but instead say, “The Buddha dwelt in the city of Rājagṛha”?

Answer: It states the place, time, and person in order to cause people’s minds to give rise to faith.

云何名「住」？四種身儀：坐、臥、行、住，是名住。

又以怖魔軍眾、自令弟子歡喜入種種諸禪定故，在是中住。

What is meant by “dwelling”? There are four bodily postures: sitting, lying down, walking, and standing. This is called dwelling.

Also, in order to frighten the armies of Māra and to cause his disciples to joyfully enter many kinds of meditative concentration, the Buddha dwells there.

復次，三種住：天住、梵住、聖住。六種欲天住法，是為天住。梵天等乃至非有想非無想天住法，是名梵住。諸佛、辟支佛、阿羅漢住法，是名聖住。於是三住法中，住聖住法；憐愍眾生故，住王舍城。

Furthermore, there are three kinds of dwelling: deva dwelling, Brahmā dwelling, and noble dwelling. The dharmas by which the six classes of desire-realm devas dwell are called deva dwelling. The dharmas by which Brahmā devas and others up to the devas of neither perception nor nonperception dwell are called Brahmā dwelling. The dharmas by which buddhas, pratyekabuddhas, and arhats dwell are called noble dwelling. Among these three kinds of dwelling, the Buddha dwells in the dharma of noble dwelling. Because he has compassion for beings, he dwells in the city of Rājagṛha.

復次，布施、持戒、善心三事，故名天住。慈、悲、喜、捨四無量心，故名梵住。空、無相、無作，是三三昧名聖住。聖住法，佛於中住。

Furthermore, generosity, moral discipline, and a wholesome mind are the three matters called deva dwelling. Kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity, the four immeasurable minds, are called Brahmā dwelling. Emptiness, signlessness, and wishlessness, these three samādhis, are called noble dwelling. The Buddha dwells in the dharma of noble dwelling.

復次，四種住：天住、梵住、聖住、佛住。三住，如前說。佛住者，首楞嚴等諸佛無量三昧、十力、四無所畏、十八不共法、一切智等種種諸慧，及八萬四千法藏度人門。如是等種種諸佛功德是佛所住處，佛於中住。

略說「住」竟。

Furthermore, there are four kinds of dwelling: deva dwelling, Brahmā dwelling, noble dwelling, and Buddha dwelling. The first three kinds of dwelling are as explained above. Buddha dwelling means the immeasurable samādhis of the buddhas, such as the Śūraṅgama Samādhi; the ten powers; the four fearlessnesses; the eighteen unshared dharmas; all-wisdom and many other kinds of wisdom; and the eighty-four thousand Dharma treasuries, the gateways for liberating people. Many kinds of buddha virtues such as these are the places where the Buddha dwells, and the Buddha dwells within them.

This completes the brief explanation of “dwelling.”

Why the City Is Called Rājagṛha

Chinese Source

「王舍城」者。

問曰：如舍婆提、迦毘羅婆、波羅[木*柰]大城皆有諸王舍，何以故獨名此城為王舍？

Ru-Yi Translation

Now, “the city of Rājagṛha.”

Question: Great cities such as Śrāvastī, Kapilavastu, and Bārāṇasī all have royal residences. Why is this city alone called Rājagṛha, the King’s Residence?

答曰：有人言：「是摩伽陀國王有子，一頭，兩面，四臂。時人以為不祥，王即裂其身首，棄之曠野。羅剎女鬼名梨羅，還合其身而乳養之。後大成人，力能并兼諸國，王有天下，取諸國王萬八千人置此五山中。以大力勢治閻浮提，閻浮提人因名此山為王舍城。」

Answer: Some say that a king of Magadha had a son with one head, two faces, and four arms. People at the time considered him inauspicious, so the king tore apart his body and head and abandoned him in the wilderness. A female rākṣasa named Līlā joined his body back together and nursed him. Later, when he grew to adulthood, his strength was able to annex other countries, and he ruled the whole realm. He took eighteen thousand kings from other countries and placed them among these five mountains. Because he governed Jambudvīpa with great power and force, the people of Jambudvīpa called this mountain area Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

復次，有人言：「摩伽陀王先所住城，城中失火，一燒一作，如是至七。國人疲役，王大憂怖，集諸智人問其意故。有言：『宜應易處。』王即更求住處，見此五山周匝如城，即作宮殿於中止住，以是故名王舍城。」

Furthermore, some say that in the city where the king of Magadha first lived, fires broke out. Each time the city burned, it was rebuilt, and this happened seven times. The people of the country were exhausted by the labor, and the king was greatly worried and afraid. He gathered wise people and asked the meaning of this. Some said, “It would be best to move to another place.” The king then searched for another place to live. Seeing that these five mountains surrounded the area like a city wall, he built palaces there and dwelt there. For this reason, it was called Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

復次，往古世時，此國有王名婆藪，心厭世法，出家作仙人。

是時居家婆羅門與諸出家仙人共論議。

居家婆羅門言：「經書云：『天祀中，應殺生噉肉。』」

諸出家仙人言：「不應天祀中殺生噉肉。」共諍云云。

Furthermore, in ancient times, this country had a king named Vasu. His mind became weary of worldly dharmas, so he left home and became a seer.

At that time, household brahmins and renunciant seers debated with one another.

The household brahmins said, “The scriptures say, ‘In sacrifices to the devas, one should kill living beings and eat meat.’”

The renunciant seers said, “In sacrifices to the devas, one should not kill living beings and eat meat.” In this way, they argued back and forth.

諸出家婆羅門言：「此有大王出家作仙人，汝等信不？」

諸居家婆羅門言：「信。」

諸出家仙人言：「我以此人為證，後日當問。」

諸居家婆羅門即以其夜先到婆藪仙人所，種種問已，語婆藪仙人：「明日論議，汝當助我。」

The renunciant brahmins said, “There is a great king here who has left home and become a seer. Do you trust him?”

The household brahmins said, “We trust him.”

The renunciant seers said, “We will take him as our witness and ask him another day.”

That very night, the household brahmins went first to the seer Vasu. After asking him many things, they said to him, “In tomorrow’s debate, you should help us.”

如是明旦論時，諸出家仙人問婆藪仙人：「天祀中應殺生噉肉不？」

婆藪仙人言：「婆羅門法，天祀中應殺生噉肉。」

諸出家仙人言：「於汝實心云何？應殺生噉肉不？」

婆藪仙人言：「為天祀故，應殺生噉肉。此生在天祀中死故，得生天上。」

The next morning, during the debate, the renunciant seers asked the seer Vasu, “In sacrifices to the devas, should one kill living beings and eat meat?”

The seer Vasu said, “According to brahmin law, in sacrifices to the devas one should kill living beings and eat meat.”

The renunciant seers said, “What is in your true mind? Should one kill living beings and eat meat?”

The seer Vasu said, “For the sake of sacrifice to the devas, one should kill living beings and eat meat. Because this being dies within a sacrifice to the devas, it will be reborn in heaven.”

諸出家仙人言：「汝大不是！汝大妄語！」即唾之言：「罪人滅去。」是時，婆藪仙人尋陷入地沒踝，是初開大罪門故。

諸出家仙人言：「汝應實語，若故妄語者，汝身當陷入地中。」

婆藪仙人言：「我知為天故殺羊、噉肉無罪。」即復陷入地至膝，如是漸漸稍沒至腰，至頸。

The renunciant seers said, “You are greatly wrong. You have spoken a great falsehood.” They spat at him and said, “Sinful person, disappear.” At that time, the seer Vasu immediately sank into the earth up to his ankles, because he had first opened the gate to a great offense.

The renunciant seers said, “You should speak truthfully. If you deliberately speak falsely, your body will sink into the earth.”

The seer Vasu said, “I know that killing sheep and eating meat for the sake of the devas is without fault.” He then sank into the earth up to his knees. In this way, little by little, he sank to his waist, and then to his neck.

諸出家仙人言：「汝今妄語得現世報，更以實語者，雖入地下，我能出汝，令得免罪。」

爾時，婆藪仙人自思惟言：「我貴重人，不應兩種語。又婆羅門四圍陀法中，種種因緣讚祀天法，我一人死，當何足計！」一心言：「應天祀中殺生、噉肉無罪。」

The renunciant seers said, “Now your false speech has received its result in this present life. If you now speak truthfully, even though you have entered the ground, we can bring you out and enable you to avoid this offense.”

At that time, the seer Vasu reflected to himself, “I am a person of high standing and should not speak in two different ways. Moreover, in the brahmin teaching of the four Vedas, there are many reasons praising sacrifice to the devas. If I alone die, what does that matter?” With single-minded resolve, he said, “In sacrifices to the devas, killing living beings and eating meat are without fault.”

諸出家仙人言：「汝重罪人！催去！不用見汝！」於是舉身沒地中。從是以來乃至今日，常用婆藪仙人王法，於天祀中殺羊，當下刀時言：「婆藪殺汝。」

The renunciant seers said, “You person of grave offense, go quickly. We do not want to see you.” Thereupon his whole body sank into the earth. From that time down to the present day, people have constantly followed the royal law of the seer Vasu: when killing sheep in sacrifice to the devas, at the moment the knife is lowered, they say, “Vasu kills you.”

婆藪之子，名曰廣車，嗣位為王，後亦厭世法，而復不能出家。如是思惟：「我父先王出家，生入地中，若治天下，復作大罪，我今當何以自處？」如是思惟時，聞空中聲言：「汝若行見難值希有處，汝應是中作舍住。」作是語已，便不復聞聲。未經幾時，王出田獵，見有一鹿走疾如風，王便逐之，而不可及，遂逐不止，百官侍從無能及者。轉前見有五山，周匝峻固，其地平正，生草細軟，好華遍地，種種林木、華果茂盛，溫泉、涼池皆悉清淨。其地莊嚴，處處有散天華、天香，聞天伎樂。

Vasu’s son was named Broad Chariot. He succeeded to the throne and became king. Later, he too became weary of worldly dharmas, yet he could not leave home. He reflected, “My father, the former king, left home and sank alive into the earth. If I govern the world, I too will commit great offenses. How should I now conduct myself?” As he was reflecting in this way, he heard a voice in the sky say, “If as you travel you see a rare and hard-to-find place, you should build a residence and dwell there.” After saying this, the voice was not heard again. Not long afterward, the king went hunting in the fields and saw a deer running as swiftly as the wind. The king chased it, but could not catch it, and continued pursuing it without stopping. None of his officials and attendants could keep up. Going farther ahead, he saw five mountains encircling a place, steep and solid like walls. The ground was level and proper, with fine soft grasses growing there, beautiful flowers spread everywhere, many kinds of trees, flowers, and fruits flourishing, and warm springs and cool ponds all pure and clear. The place was adorned, with heavenly flowers and heavenly fragrances scattered everywhere, and the music of heavenly performers could be heard.

爾時，乾闥婆伎適見王來，各自還去。「是處希有，未曾所見，今我正當在是中作舍住。」如是思惟已，群臣百官尋跡而到。

王告諸臣：「我前所聞空中聲言：『汝行若見希有難值之處，汝應是中作舍住。』我今見此希有之處，我應是中作舍住。」即捨本城，於此山中住。是王初始在是中住，從是已後次第止住。是王元起造立宮舍，故名王舍城。

略說王舍城本起竟。

At that time, the gandharva musicians happened to see the king coming and each returned to his own place. The king thought, “This place is rare, something I have never seen before. Now I should build a residence and dwell here.” After he had reflected in this way, his ministers, officials, and attendants followed his tracks and arrived.

The king told the ministers, “Earlier I heard a voice in the sky say, ‘If as you travel you see a rare and hard-to-find place, you should build a residence and dwell there.’ Now I have seen this rare place. I should build a residence and dwell here.” He then abandoned his original city and dwelt among these mountains. This king was the first to dwell there, and after him others dwelt there in succession. Because this king originally established and built palaces and residences there, it was called Rājagṛha, the City of the King’s Residence.

This completes the brief explanation of the origin of Rājagṛha.

At Gṛdhrakūṭa, Vulture Peak

Chinese Source

〖經〗

耆闍崛山中。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sutra:

On Gṛdhrakūṭa Mountain, Vulture Peak.

〖論〗

「耆闍」名鷲，「崛」名頭。

問曰：何以名鷲頭山？

答曰：是山頂似鷲，王舍城人見其似鷲故，共傳言鷲頭山，因名之為鷲頭山。

Treatise:

“Gṛdhra” means vulture, and “kūṭa” means peak.

Question: Why is it called Vulture Peak Mountain?

Answer: The summit of this mountain resembles a vulture. When the people of Rājagṛha saw that it resembled a vulture, they passed down the name Vulture Peak Mountain, and for this reason it was called Vulture Peak Mountain.

復次，王舍城南屍陀林中，多諸死人，諸鷲常來噉之，還在山頭，時人遂名鷲頭山。

是山於五山中最高大，多好林水，聖人住處。

Furthermore, in the Śītavana charnel ground south of Rājagṛha, there were many dead bodies. Vultures often came to eat them and then returned to the mountain peak. People of that time therefore named it Vulture Peak Mountain.

Among the five mountains, this mountain is the highest and greatest. It has many fine groves and waters and is a dwelling place of noble ones.

Why the Buddha Often Dwells in Rājagṛha

Chinese Source

問曰：已知耆闍崛山義，佛何以故住王舍城？

諸佛法普慈一切，如日照萬物，無不蒙明。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The meaning of Gṛdhrakūṭa Mountain is now understood. Why did the Buddha dwell in Rājagṛha?

The Dharma of the buddhas extends universal compassion to all, like the sun shining on the ten thousand things, so that none fail to receive its light.

如漚祇尼大城、富樓那跋檀大城、阿藍車多羅大城、弗迦羅婆多大城，如是等大城，多人豐樂而不住；何故多住王舍城、舍婆提大城？

波羅柰、迦毘羅婆、瞻婆、婆翅多、拘睒鞞、鳩樓城等，雖有住時，而多住王舍城、舍婆提。云何知多住二處？見佛諸經多在二城說，少在餘城。

Great cities such as Ujjayinī, Pūrṇavardhana, Ahicchatra, and Puṣkarāvatī had many people and abundant happiness, yet the Buddha did not dwell there. Why did he often dwell in the great cities of Rājagṛha and Śrāvastī?

Although he did stay at times in Bārāṇasī, Kapilavastu, Campā, Sāketa, Kauśāmbī, Kuru City, and other places, he mostly stayed in Rājagṛha and Śrāvastī. How do we know that he often stayed in these two places? We see that in the Buddha’s sutras, most teachings were spoken in these two cities, and only a few in other cities.

答曰：佛雖大慈等及，以漚祇尼等諸大城是邊國故不住。

又彌離車弊惡人多，善根未熟故。如偈說：

「如日光等照， 華熟則時開； 若華未應敷， 則亦不強開。 佛亦復如是， 等心而說法， 善根熟則敷， 未熟則不開。 以是故世尊， 住三種人中， 利智善根熟， 結使煩惱薄。」

Answer: Although the Buddha’s great compassion reaches all equally, he did not dwell in great cities such as Ujjayinī because they were border regions.

Also, among the Mlecchas there were many rough and harmful people, and their wholesome roots had not yet matured. As a verse says:

“Sunlight shines equally on all, yet flowers open only when they are ripe. If a flower is not yet ready to blossom, it is not forced open. The Buddha is also like this: with an equal mind he teaches the Dharma. When wholesome roots have matured, they open; when they have not matured, they do not open. For this reason, the World-Honored One dwells among three kinds of people: those of keen wisdom, those whose wholesome roots have matured, and those whose fetters and afflictions are thin.”

Gratitude for Birthplace and Awakening Place

Chinese Source

復次，知恩故，多住王舍城、舍婆提城。

問曰：云何知恩故多住二城？

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, because the Buddha knew gratitude, he often dwelt in Rājagṛha and Śrāvastī.

Question: How do we know that it was because he knew gratitude that he often dwelt in these two cities?

答曰：憍薩羅國是佛所生地。如佛答頻婆娑羅王偈說：

「有好妙國土， 在於雪山邊， 豐樂多異寶， 名曰憍薩羅。 日種諸釋子， 我在是中生， 心厭老病死， 出家求佛道。」

Answer: The country of Kosala was the place where the Buddha was born. As the Buddha said in a verse when answering King Bimbisāra:

“There is a fine and wondrous country beside the Snow Mountains, rich, happy, and filled with many rare treasures. It is called Kosala. Among the Śākyas of the solar lineage, I was born there. My mind grew weary of aging, sickness, and death, so I left home to seek the Buddha path.”

又是憍薩羅國主波斯匿王，住舍婆提大城中；佛為法王，亦住此城，二主應住一處故。

復次，是憍薩羅國，佛生身地，知恩故，多住舍婆提。

Moreover, King Prasenajit, the ruler of Kosala, lived in the great city of Śrāvastī. Since the Buddha was the Dharma King, he also lived in that city, because the two rulers should dwell in one place.

Furthermore, the country of Kosala was the land of the Buddha’s birth body. Because he knew gratitude, he often dwelt in Śrāvastī.

問曰：若知恩故多住舍婆提者，迦毘羅婆城近佛生處，何不多住？

答曰：佛諸結盡無復餘習，近諸親屬亦無異想；然釋種弟子多未離欲，若近親屬則染著心生。

Question: If he often dwelt in Śrāvastī because he knew gratitude, Kapilavastu was close to the Buddha’s birthplace. Why did he not dwell there often?

Answer: The Buddha had ended all fetters and had no remaining habits. Even when he was near his relatives, he had no different thought. Yet many Śākya disciples had not yet left desire behind. If they were near their relatives, thoughts of attachment would arise.

問曰：何以不護舍婆提弟子，而多住舍婆提？

答曰：迦毘羅婆弟子多。佛初還國，迦葉兄弟千比丘，本修婆羅門法，苦行山間，形容憔悴。父王見之，以此諸比丘不足光飾世尊，即選諸釋貴人子弟，兼人、少壯，戶遣一人，強令出家。其中有善心樂道，有不樂者，此諸釋比丘不應令還本生處。舍婆提弟子輩不爾，以是故佛多住舍婆提，不多住迦毘羅婆。

Question: Why did he not protect the disciples of Śrāvastī, and instead often dwell in Śrāvastī?

Answer: There were many disciples from Kapilavastu. When the Buddha first returned to his homeland, the thousand bhikṣus who were the Kāśyapa brothers and their followers had originally practiced brahmin dharmas and austerities in the mountains, so their appearance was gaunt and withered. When the Buddha’s father, the king, saw them, he thought that these bhikṣus were not enough to adorn the World-Honored One with honor. He then selected noble young Śākya men, strong and in the prime of youth, and sent one person from each household, compelling them to leave home. Among them, some had wholesome minds and delighted in the path, while others did not delight in it. These Śākya bhikṣus should not be made to return to the place of their birth. The disciples of Śrāvastī were not like this. For this reason, the Buddha often dwelt in Śrāvastī and did not often dwell in Kapilavastu.

復次，出家法應不近親屬，親屬心著，如火、如蛇。居家婆羅門子為學問故，尚不應在生處，何況出家沙門！

復次，如舍婆提城大，迦毘羅婆不爾。舍婆提城九億家，是中若少時住者，不得度多人，以是故多住。

Furthermore, according to the Dharma of leaving home, one should not stay near relatives. The mind’s attachment to relatives is like fire and like a snake. Even a brahmin son living at home, for the sake of study, should not remain in the place of his birth; how much less a śramaṇa who has left home.

Furthermore, Śrāvastī was a great city, while Kapilavastu was not like that. Śrāvastī had nine hundred million households. If the Buddha stayed there only briefly, he would not be able to liberate many people. For this reason he often stayed there.

復次，迦毘羅婆城中佛生處，是中人已久習行，善根熟，利智慧，是中佛少時住說法，不須久住，度已而去。

Furthermore, Kapilavastu was the city where the Buddha was born. The people there had long cultivated practice; their wholesome roots were mature and their wisdom was sharp. There the Buddha stayed only briefly to teach the Dharma. He did not need to remain long; after liberating them, he went away.

The people of Śrāvastī included some who were just beginning practice and some who had practiced for a long time; some whose wholesome roots had matured and some whose wholesome roots had not yet matured; some with sharp faculties and some without sharp faculties. Because they studied many kinds of scriptures and texts, their minds had been sharpened, but they had also entered into many nets of wrong views, served many kinds of teachers, and belonged to many kinds of devas. Answer: In order to repay the kindness of the place of his birth, he often dwelt in Śrāvastī. All beings remember the place of their birth. As a verse says:

“All debaters cherish the dharmas they know, just as people remember their birthplace; even after leaving home, they still argue.”

以報法身地恩故，多住王舍城，諸佛皆愛法身故。如偈說：

「過去、未來， 現在諸佛， 供養法身， 師敬尊重。」

法身於生身勝故，二城中多住王舍城。

In order to repay the kindness of the place of the Dharma body, he often dwelt in Rājagṛha, because all buddhas cherish the Dharma body. As a verse says:

“The buddhas of the past, future, and present make offerings to the Dharma body, honoring and respecting it as teacher.”

Because the Dharma body is superior to the birth body, between the two cities he dwelt more often in Rājagṛha.

Because of these three causes and conditions, we know that in the country of Magadha alms-food was easy to obtain.

Great physicians possess all medicines and can cure many illnesses.

〖經〗

盡諸有結。

〖論〗

There are three kinds of existence: desire existence, form existence, and formless existence.

諸阿羅漢結使應永盡，得一切煩惱離故。有不應盡。何以故？阿羅漢未滅度時，眼根等五眾、十二入、十八持諸有成就故。

The fetters and underlying tendencies of the arhats should be permanently exhausted, because they have become free from all afflictions. But existence should not be exhausted. Why? Before arhats pass into extinction, the five aggregates, such as the eye faculty, the twelve sense entrances, and the eighteen elements still exist.

There is no problem. Here, the cause is being spoken of in terms of the result.

如偈說：

「斷食死無疑，食者死未定；以是故佛說，施食得五事。」

As the Buddha said, “When a donor gives food, he gives five things: life, complexion, strength, happiness, and clear sight.” Food does not necessarily give these five things. Some people die after receiving much food and drink, while others live after receiving only a little food. Food is the cause of these five things. Therefore the Buddha said that by giving food one obtains five things.

As a verse says:

“One who stops eating certainly dies; one who eats does not necessarily die. For this reason the Buddha said that giving food obtains five things.”

亦如人食百斤金，金不可食，金是食因，故言「食金」。佛言「女人為戒垢」，女人非戒垢，是戒垢因故，言「女人為戒垢」。如人從高處墮未至地，言此人死，雖未死，知必死故，言「此人死」。如是諸阿羅漢結使已盡，知有必當盡故，言「有結盡」。

It is also like saying that a person eats one hundred catties of gold. Gold cannot be eaten, but gold is the cause of food, and so one says “eating gold.” The Buddha said, “Women are a defilement of moral discipline.” Women are not themselves a defilement of moral discipline, but they can be a cause for the defilement of moral discipline, and so he said, “Women are a defilement of moral discipline.” It is like a person falling from a high place: before he reaches the ground, people say, “This person is dead.” Although he is not yet dead, they know he will certainly die, and so they say, “This person is dead.” In the same way, the arhats' fetters and underlying tendencies have already been exhausted. Knowing that their existence will certainly be exhausted, it is said that they have “exhausted the fetters of existence.”

諸阿羅漢所作已辦，更不求進，何以故常在佛邊，不餘處度眾生？

答曰：

一切十方眾生，雖盡應供養佛，阿羅漢受恩重故，應倍供養。所以者何？是阿羅漢從佛得成，受無量功德，知結使斷，信心轉多，是故諸大德阿羅漢，佛邊受功德樂味，供養恭敬，報佛恩故，在佛邊住。

Question:

The arhats had already done what had to be done and sought no further progress. Why, then, did they always stay near the Buddha instead of going elsewhere to liberate beings?

Answer:

Although all beings throughout the ten directions should make offerings to the Buddha, the arhats have received especially weighty kindness from him, and therefore should make offerings twice as much. Why? These arhats were accomplished through the Buddha. They received immeasurable merit, knew that the fetters and underlying tendencies had been cut off, and their faith grew even more. Therefore, these great and virtuous arhats stayed near the Buddha to receive the joyful taste of merit, to make offerings and show reverence, and to repay the Buddha's kindness.

諸阿羅漢圍繞佛故，佛德益尊。如梵天人遶梵天王，如三十三天遶釋提桓因，如諸鬼神遶毘沙門王，如諸小王遶轉輪聖王，如病人病癒住大醫邊；如是諸阿羅漢住在佛邊，諸阿羅漢圍繞供養故，佛德益尊。

Because the arhats surrounded the Buddha, the Buddha's virtue became even more honored. It is like the beings of Brahmā Heaven surrounding King Brahmā, like the devas of the Thirty-Three surrounding Śakra Devānām Indra, like the spirits surrounding King Vaiśravaṇa, like lesser kings surrounding a wheel-turning sage king, or like a person whose illness has been cured staying beside a great physician. In this way, the arhats stayed near the Buddha. Because the arhats surrounded him and made offerings, the Buddha's virtue became even more honored.

Why the Arhats Remained Near the Buddha

Chinese Source

問曰：

若諸阿羅漢所作已辦，逮得己利，不須聽法，何以故說般若波羅蜜時，共五千阿羅漢？

答曰：

諸阿羅漢雖所作已辦，佛欲以甚深智慧法試。如佛問舍利弗，如《波羅延經》阿耆陀難中偈說：

「種種諸學人，及諸數法人，是人所行法，願為如實說！」

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

If the arhats had already done what had to be done and had attained their own benefit, they did not need to listen to the Dharma. Why, then, when the Buddha taught the Prajñāpāramitā, were there five thousand arhats with him?

Answer:

Although the arhats had already done what had to be done, the Buddha wished to test them with the Dharma of extremely profound wisdom. It is like when the Buddha questioned Śāriputra, as in the verse from Ajita's Questions in the Pārāyaṇa Sūtra:

“Various people in training, and those counted among Dharma practitioners: please explain truthfully the Dharma they practice.”

「是中，云何學人？云何數法人？」爾時，舍利弗默然。如是三問，三默。佛示義端，告舍利弗：「有生不？」舍利弗答：「世尊！有生。有生者，欲為滅有為生法故，名學人；以智慧得無生法故，名數法人。」是經，此中應廣說。

復次，若有漏、若無漏，諸禪定未得故欲得，已得欲令堅深故，諸阿羅漢佛邊聽法。

“Among these, who are people in training? Who are those counted among Dharma practitioners?” At that time, Śāriputra remained silent. In this way he was asked three times and remained silent three times. The Buddha showed the starting point of the meaning and said to Śāriputra, “Is there birth?” Śāriputra answered, “World-Honored One, there is birth. Those who have birth and wish to extinguish conditioned, birth-producing dharmas are called people in training. Those who, through wisdom, have attained the dharma of nonarising are called those counted among Dharma practitioners.” This sutra should be explained extensively here.

Furthermore, whether with outflows or without outflows, there are meditative concentrations that the arhats have not yet attained and therefore wish to attain; and those already attained, they wish to make firm and deep. For this reason, the arhats listen to the Dharma beside the Buddha.

復次，現前樂故，如《難陀迦經》中說：「以今世樂故聽法。」

復次，諸阿羅漢在佛邊聽法，心無厭足，如《蜫盧提迦經》中說：「舍利弗語蜫盧提迦：『我法中聽法無厭。』」

復次，如佛大師，自一心從弟子邊聽法，不應難言阿羅漢所作已辦，何以聽法？譬如飽滿人得好食，猶尚更食，云何飢渴人而言不應食！以是故，諸阿羅漢雖所作已辦，常在佛邊聽法。

Furthermore, they listen because of present happiness. As the Nandaka Sūtra says, “They listen to the Dharma because of happiness in this very life.”

Furthermore, when the arhats listen to the Dharma near the Buddha, their minds are never satisfied. As the Kundadhānika Sūtra says, “Śāriputra said to Kundadhānika, ‘In my Dharma, one never grows weary of listening to the Dharma.’”

Furthermore, even the Buddha, the great teacher, single-mindedly listened to the Dharma from the side of his disciples. Therefore one should not object, saying, “The arhats had done what had to be done, so why did they listen to the Dharma?” It is like a person who is already full but obtains fine food and still eats again; how much more so a hungry and thirsty person? One could not say that such a person should not eat. For this reason, although the arhats had done what had to be done, they constantly listened to the Dharma beside the Buddha.

The Assembly as Adornment

Chinese Source

復次，佛住解脫法中，諸阿羅漢亦住解脫法中，住法相應，眷屬莊嚴。如《栴檀譬喻經》中言：「有栴檀林，伊蘭圍之；有伊蘭林，栴檀圍之。有栴檀，栴檀以為叢林；有伊蘭，伊蘭自相圍繞。」佛、諸阿羅漢，亦復如是。佛住善法解脫中，諸阿羅漢亦住善法解脫中，住法相應，眷屬莊嚴。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, the Buddha abides in the Dharma of liberation, and the arhats also abide in the Dharma of liberation. Their abiding accords with the Dharma, and the surrounding assembly is an adornment. As the Sandalwood Simile Sūtra says, “There may be a sandalwood grove surrounded by eranda trees, or an eranda grove surrounded by sandalwood trees. Where there is sandalwood, sandalwood forms the grove; where there is eranda, eranda surrounds itself.” The Buddha and the arhats are also like this. The Buddha abides in wholesome dharmas and liberation, and the arhats also abide in wholesome dharmas and liberation. Their abiding accords with the Dharma, and the surrounding assembly is an adornment.

佛以大眾圍繞，如須彌山王，十寶山圍繞；如白香象王，白香象圍繞；如師子王，師子眾圍遶。佛亦如是，佛為世間無上福田，與諸弟子圍繞共住。

The Buddha is surrounded by the great assembly, like King Mount Sumeru surrounded by the ten jeweled mountains; like a white fragrant elephant king surrounded by white fragrant elephants; like a lion king surrounded by a pride of lions. The Buddha is also like this. The Buddha is the unsurpassed field of merit for the world, and he dwells together with his disciples surrounding him.

Why Ānanda Is Excepted

Chinese Source

唯除阿難在學地，得須陀洹。

問曰：

何以言「唯除阿難」？

答曰：

上所讚諸阿羅漢，阿難不在其數。何以故？以在學地，未離欲故。

Ru-Yi Translation

Only Ānanda was excepted: he was still on the level of training and had attained stream-entry.

Question:

Why does it say, “Only Ānanda was excepted”?

Answer:

Ānanda was not included among the arhats praised above. Why? Because he was still on the level of training and had not yet left desire behind.

問曰：

大德阿難第三師，大眾法將，種涅槃種已無量劫，常近佛，持法藏。

大德利根，何以至今未離欲作學人？

答曰：

大德阿難本願如是：「我於多聞眾中最第一。」亦以諸佛法，阿羅漢所作已辦，不應作供給供養人，以其於佛法中能辦大事，煩惱賊破，共佛在解脫床上坐故。

Question:

The venerable Ānanda is the third teacher, the Dharma general of the great assembly. He planted the seed of nirvāṇa for immeasurable kalpas, constantly stayed near the Buddha, and upheld the treasury of Dharma.

The venerable one has sharp faculties. Why, even now, has he not left desire behind and remains a person in training?

Answer:

The venerable Ānanda's original vow was this: “Among those who have heard much, may I be foremost.” Also, according to the Dharma of all Buddhas, arhats have already done what had to be done and should not serve as attendants or providers. This is because, within the Buddha Dharma, they are able to accomplish great matters, have broken the thieves of affliction, and sit together with the Buddha on the seat of liberation.

復次，長老阿難種種諸經，聽、持、誦利、觀故智慧多，攝心少；二功德等者，可得漏盡道。以是故，長老阿難是學人須陀洹。

復次，貪供給世尊故，是阿難為佛作供給人；如是念：「若我早取漏盡道，便遠世尊，不得作供給人。」以是故，阿難雖能得阿羅漢道，自制不取。

Furthermore, Elder Ānanda listened to, retained, recited fluently, and contemplated many different sutras; for this reason, his wisdom was abundant, but his collectedness of mind was limited. When these two merits are equal, one can attain the path that exhausts the outflows. For this reason, Elder Ānanda was a person in training, a stream-enterer.

Furthermore, because he longed to attend upon the World-Honored One, Ānanda served as the Buddha's attendant. He thought, “If I quickly attain the path that exhausts the outflows, I will be far from the World-Honored One and will not be able to serve as his attendant.” For this reason, although Ānanda was able to attain the path of arhatship, he restrained himself and did not take it.

復次，處、時、人未合故。何等處？能集法千阿羅漢未在耆闍崛山，是為處；世尊過去時未到；長老婆耆子不在。以是故，長老阿難漏不盡。要在世尊過去，集法眾合，婆耆子說法勸諫——三事合故，得漏盡道。

復次，大德阿難厭世法少，不如餘人。是阿難世世王者種，端正無比，福德無量；世尊近親，常侍從佛，必有此念：「我佛近侍，知法寶藏，漏盡道法，我不畏失。」以是事故，不大慇懃。

Furthermore, place, time, and person had not yet come together. What was the place? The thousand arhats able to compile the Dharma had not yet gathered on Gṛdhrakūṭa Mountain: this is the place. The time after the World-Honored One's passing had not yet arrived. Elder Vajjiputta was not present. For this reason, Elder Ānanda's outflows were not yet exhausted. It had to be after the World-Honored One's passing, when the assembly for compiling the Dharma had gathered and Vajjiputta taught the Dharma and exhorted him. Because these three matters came together, he attained the path that exhausts the outflows.

Furthermore, the venerable Ānanda had less disenchantment with worldly dharmas than others. Ānanda was born, life after life, in royal lineages; he was incomparably handsome and had immeasurable merit. He was a close relative of the World-Honored One and constantly served the Buddha. He must have had this thought: “I am the Buddha's close attendant and know the treasury of Dharma. As for the path that exhausts the outflows, I am not afraid of losing it.” Because of this, he was not greatly diligent.

The Causes and Conditions for Ānanda's Name

Chinese Source

問曰：

大德阿難名，以何因緣？是先世因緣？是父母作字？是依因緣立名？

答曰：

是先世因緣，亦父母作名，亦依因緣立字。

問曰：

云何先世因緣？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question:

For what causes and conditions was the venerable one named Ānanda? Was it due to causes and conditions from former lives? Was it a name given by his parents? Or was the name established according to circumstances?

Answer:

It was due to causes and conditions from former lives; it was also a name given by his parents; and it was also established according to circumstances.

Question:

What were the causes and conditions from former lives?

答曰：

釋迦文佛先世作瓦師，名大光明。爾時，有佛名釋迦文，弟子名舍利弗、目乾連、阿難，佛與弟子俱到瓦師舍一宿。爾時，瓦師布施草坐、燈明、石蜜漿，三事供養佛及比丘僧，便發願言：「我於當來老、病、死、惱五惡之世作佛，如今佛名釋迦文；我佛弟子名，亦如今佛弟子名。」以佛願故，得字阿難。

Answer:

In a former life, Śākyamuni Buddha was a potter named Great Radiance. At that time, there was a Buddha named Śākyamuni, whose disciples were named Śāriputra, Maudgalyāyana, and Ānanda. The Buddha and his disciples went together to the potter's house and stayed there for one night. At that time, the potter offered seats of grass, lamps, and rock-honey syrup. With these three things, he made offerings to the Buddha and the community of monks. He then made this vow: “In the future, in an evil age of aging, sickness, death, and affliction, may I become a Buddha, just as the present Buddha is named Śākyamuni. May the names of my Buddha-disciples also be just like the names of this Buddha's disciples.” Because of that Buddha-vow, he received the name Ānanda.

復次，阿難世世立願：「我在釋迦文佛弟子多聞眾中，願最第一，字阿難。」

復次，阿難世世忍辱除瞋，以是因緣故，生便端正。父母以其端正，見者皆歡喜故，字阿難(阿難者，秦言歡喜)。

是為先世因緣字。

Furthermore, life after life, Ānanda made this vow: “Among the disciples of Śākyamuni Buddha who have heard much, may I be foremost, and may I be named Ānanda.”

Furthermore, life after life, Ānanda practiced patience and removed anger. Because of these causes and conditions, he was handsome as soon as he was born. Since his parents saw that he was handsome and that all who saw him felt joy, they named him Ānanda. Ānanda means Joy.

This is the naming due to causes and conditions from former lives.

云何父母作字？

昔有日種王名師子頰，其王有四子：第一名淨飯，二名白飯，三名斛飯，四名甘露飯。有一女，名甘露味。

淨飯王有二子：佛、難陀。

白飯王有二子：跋提、提沙。

斛飯王有二子：提婆達多、阿難。

甘露味女有一子，名施婆羅。

Ru-Yi Translation

How was the name given by his parents?

In the past, there was a king of the Solar Lineage named Siṃhahanu. This king had four sons. The first was named Śuddhodana, the second Śuklodana, the third Droṇodana, and the fourth Amṛtodana. He also had one daughter named Amṛtā.

King Śuddhodana had two sons: the Buddha and Nanda.

King Śuklodana had two sons: Bhaddiya and Tissa.

King Droṇodana had two sons: Devadatta and Ānanda.

King Amṛtodana had two sons: Mahānāma and Aniruddha.

The woman Amṛtā had one son named Śivala.

是中悉達陀菩薩漸漸長大，棄轉輪聖王位，夜半出家，至漚樓鞞羅國中尼連禪河邊，六年苦行。是時，淨飯王愛念子故，常遣使問訊，欲知消息：「我子得道不？若病？若死？」

使來白王：「菩薩唯有皮骨筋相連持耳，命甚微弱！若今日、若明日，不復久也。」王聞其言，甚大愁念，沒憂惱海。「我子既不作轉輪王，又不得作佛，一何衰苦，無所得而死！」如是憂惱，荒迷憒塞。

Among these, Bodhisattva Siddhārtha gradually grew up. He renounced the position of wheel-turning sage king, left home at midnight, went to the bank of the Nairañjanā River in the country of Uruvilvā, and practiced austerities for six years. At that time, because King Śuddhodana loved and thought of his son, he constantly sent messengers to ask after him, wishing to know the news: “Has my son attained the path? Is he ill? Has he died?”

The messengers returned and reported to the king: “The Bodhisattva has only skin, bones, and sinews holding together. His life is extremely weak. Whether today or tomorrow, he will not last long.” When the king heard these words, he was deeply saddened and sank into an ocean of grief and distress. “My son has neither become a wheel-turning king nor attained Buddhahood. How miserable and painful it is that he will die having attained nothing!” In this way, overcome by grief and distress, he became confused, bewildered, and blocked in mind.

是時，菩薩棄苦行處，食百味乳糜，身體充滿；於尼連禪水中洗浴已，至菩提樹下，坐金剛座而自誓言：「要不破此結加趺坐，成一切智；不得一切智，終不起也。」是時，魔王將十八億眾到菩薩所，敢與菩薩決其得失。菩薩智慧力故，大破魔軍。魔不如而退，自念：「菩薩叵勝，當惱其父。」至淨飯王所，詭言：「汝子今日後夜已了。」王聞此語，驚怖墮床，如熱沙中魚。

At that time, the Bodhisattva left the place of austerities, ate milk-rice of a hundred flavors, and his body became full and strong. After bathing in the waters of the Nairañjanā, he went beneath the bodhi tree, sat on the vajra seat, and made this vow: “Until this cross-legged seat is broken, I must accomplish all-knowledge. If I do not attain all-knowledge, I will never rise.” At that time, Māra the King led eighteen koṭis of followers to the Bodhisattva's place and dared to decide victory or defeat with him. Because of the power of the Bodhisattva's wisdom, he greatly defeated Māra's army. Māra, unable to prevail, withdrew and thought, “The Bodhisattva cannot be defeated. I should distress his father.” He went to King Śuddhodana and falsely said, “Your son came to an end in the latter part of the night today.” When the king heard these words, he was terrified and fell from his bed, like a fish on hot sand.

王時悲哭而說偈言：

「阿夷陀虛言，瑞應亦無驗，得利之吉名，一切無所獲！」

是時，菩提樹神大歡喜，持天曼陀羅華，至淨飯王所，說偈言：

「汝子已得道，魔眾已破散；光明如日出，普照十方土！」

At that time, the king wept in sorrow and spoke this verse:

“Asita spoke empty words; the auspicious signs also had no proof. The fortunate name ‘One Who Gains the Benefit’ has brought no attainment at all.”

At that time, the spirit of the bodhi tree was greatly joyful. Holding heavenly māndārava flowers, the spirit came to King Śuddhodana and spoke this verse:

“Your son has attained the path; Māra's host has been broken and scattered. His radiance is like the rising sun, universally illuminating lands in the ten directions.”

王言：「前有天來言『汝子已了』，汝今來言『壞魔得道』；二語相違，誰可信者？」

樹神又言：「實不妄語。前來天者詭言已了，是魔懷嫉，故來相惱。今日諸天、龍神華香供養，空中懸繒；汝子身出光明，遍照天地。」王聞其言，於一切苦惱心得解脫。

王言：「我子雖捨轉輪聖王，今得法轉輪王，定得大利，無所失也。」王心大歡喜。是時，斛飯王家使來，白淨飯王言：「貴弟生男。」王心歡喜言：「今日大吉，是歡喜日！」語來使言：「是兒當字為阿難。」

是為父母作字。

The king said, “Earlier a deva came and said, ‘Your son has ended.’ Now you come and say, ‘He has destroyed Māra and attained the path.’ These two statements contradict each other. Whom should I believe?”

The tree spirit again said, “This is true, not false speech. The deva who came before falsely said that he had ended. That was Māra, who harbored jealousy and therefore came to trouble you. Today devas, dragons, and spirits make offerings of flowers and fragrances, and banners hang in the sky. Light issues from your son's body and shines throughout heaven and earth.” When the king heard these words, his mind was freed from all suffering and distress.

The king said, “Although my son has abandoned the wheel-turning sage kingship, he has now attained the kingship of turning the Dharma wheel. He has certainly gained great benefit and has lost nothing.” The king's mind was greatly joyful. At that time, a messenger from King Droṇodana's household came and reported to King Śuddhodana, “Your noble younger brother has had a son.” The king rejoiced and said, “Today is greatly auspicious. It is a day of joy!” He said to the messenger, “This child should be named Ānanda.”

This is the name given by his parents.
