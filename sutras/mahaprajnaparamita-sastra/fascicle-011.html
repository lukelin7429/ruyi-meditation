Why the Buddha Addresses Śāriputra — Part Sixteen

Chinese Source

〖經〗佛告舍利弗。

English Translation

Sutra: The Buddha addressed Śāriputra.

〖論〗問曰：般若波羅蜜是菩薩摩訶薩法，佛何以故告舍利弗而不告菩薩？

Treatise: Question: Prajñāpāramitā is the teaching for bodhisattva mahāsattvas. Why does the Buddha address Śāriputra rather than a bodhisattva?

答曰：舍利弗於一切弟子中，智慧最第一，如佛偈說：

Answer: Śāriputra is foremost in wisdom among all the disciples. As the Buddha says in verse:

「一切眾生智，唯除佛世尊；欲比舍利弗，智慧及多聞，於十六分中，猶尚不及一。」

‘Of all beings, except the World-Honored Buddha, none can compare with Śāriputra in wisdom and learning. Their wisdom and learning do not reach even one sixteenth of his.’

Śāriputra’s Learning and Early Renown

Chinese Source

復次，舍利弗智慧多聞，有大功德。年始八歲，誦十八部經，通解一切經書義理。是時，摩伽陀國有龍王兄弟：一名姞利，二名阿伽羅。降雨以時，國無荒年，人民感之，常以仲春之月，一切大集至龍住處，為設大會，作樂談義，終此一日。

English Translation

Furthermore, Śāriputra possessed wisdom, extensive learning, and great merit. At only eight years old, he could recite eighteen divisions of scripture and thoroughly understand their teachings. At that time, Magadha had two dragon kings who were brothers, named Jili and Agaluo. They brought timely rain, and the country suffered no years of famine. In gratitude, every year in the second month of spring the people gathered at the dragons’ dwelling for a great festival, playing music and discussing teachings throughout the day.

自古及今，斯集未替，遂以龍名以名此會。此日常法，敷四高座：一為國王，二為太子，三為大臣，四為論士。

This gathering had continued from ancient times and was named after the dragons. By custom, four high seats were set out: one for the king, one for the crown prince, one for the chief minister, and one for a debater.

爾時，舍利弗以八歲之身，問眾人言：「此四高座，為誰敷之？」眾人答言：「為國王、太子、大臣、論士。」是時，舍利弗觀察時人婆羅門等，神情瞻向，無勝己者，便昇論床，結跏趺坐。

Eight-year-old Śāriputra asked, ‘For whom are these four high seats prepared?’ The people answered, ‘For the king, crown prince, chief minister, and debater.’ Observing the brahmins and others present, he saw no one whose bearing suggested an intellect greater than his own. He climbed onto the debater’s seat and sat in full lotus posture.

眾人疑怪，或謂愚小無知，或謂智量過人。雖復嘉其神異，而猶各懷自矜，恥其年小，不自與語，皆遣年少弟子傳言問之。其答酬旨趣，辭理超絕！時諸論師歎未曾有，愚智大小一切皆伏。

The crowd was astonished. Some thought him a foolish, ignorant child; others thought his intelligence extraordinary. Although impressed by his unusual ability, the scholars remained proud. Embarrassed to debate someone so young, they sent their younger pupils to question him instead. His replies were exceptional in meaning, expression, and reasoning. The debaters marveled at this unprecedented ability, and everyone, wise or foolish, young or old, acknowledged his superiority.

王大歡喜，即命有司封一聚落，常以給之。王乘象輿，振鈴告告宣示一切，十六大國，六大城中無不慶悅。

The king rejoiced and ordered officials to grant him a village for his continuing support. Riding an elephant carriage, the king had bells rung and the news proclaimed everywhere. Throughout the sixteen great countries and six great cities, everyone celebrated.

Two Friends Seek the Deathless

Chinese Source

是時，告占師子，名拘律陀，姓大目揵連，舍利弗友而親之。舍利弗才明見重，目揵連豪爽最貴。此二人者，才智相比，德行互同；行則俱遊，住則同止；少長繾綣，結要終始。

English Translation

At that time, Śāriputra befriended Kolita, son of a diviner and a member of the Mahāmaudgalyāyana clan. Śāriputra was respected for his brilliance; Maudgalyāyana was esteemed for his noble, generous character. They were comparable in intelligence and alike in conduct. They traveled and stayed together, remaining close as they grew up and pledging lifelong friendship.

後俱厭世，出家學道，作梵志弟子。情求道門，久而無徵，以問於師。師名刪闍耶，而答之言：「自我求道，彌歷年歲，不知為有道果無耶？我非其人耶？而亦不得！」

Later, both became disenchanted with the world, went forth to seek the path, and became pupils of a brahmin. After a long search without any evidence of attainment, they questioned their teacher, Sañjaya. He replied, ‘I have sought the path for many years, yet I do not know whether its fruit exists. Perhaps I am not suited to it, for I too have not attained it!’

他日其師寢疾，舍利弗在頭邊立，大目連在足邊立，喘喘然其命將終，乃愍爾而笑。二人同心，俱問笑意。師答之言：「世俗無眼，為恩愛所侵。我見金地國王死，其大夫人自投火𧂐，求同一處，而此二人行報各異，生處殊絕。」

One day their teacher fell ill. Śāriputra stood by his head and Maudgalyāyana by his feet. As he lay gasping, near death, he smiled with pity. Both asked why. He replied, ‘Worldly people lack vision and are overcome by attachment. I see that the king of the Land of Gold has died. His chief queen has thrown herself onto his funeral pyre, hoping to be with him. But their actions bring different results, and their places of rebirth are far apart.’

是時二人筆受師語，欲以驗其虛實。後有金地商人，遠來摩伽陀國，二人以䟽驗之，果如師語，乃憮然歎曰：「我等非其人耶？為是師隱我耶？」二人相與誓曰：「若先得甘露，要畢同味。」

They wrote down his words to test their truth. Later, merchants from the Land of Gold came to Magadha. Checking their record against what the merchants reported, they found that their teacher’s words were true. Disheartened, they sighed, ‘Are we unsuited to the path, or did our teacher conceal it from us?’ They pledged to each other, ‘Whoever first obtains the nectar of the deathless must share its taste with the other.’

Hearing the Verse on Causes and Conditions

Chinese Source

是時，佛度迦葉兄弟千人，次遊諸國，到王舍城，頓止竹園。二梵志師聞佛出世，俱入王舍城，欲知消息。

English Translation

The Buddha had by then liberated the Kāśyapa brothers and their thousand followers. Traveling onward through various countries, he reached Rājagṛha and stayed in the Bamboo Grove. Hearing that a Buddha had appeared, the two brahmin teachers entered the city together to learn more.

爾時，有一比丘，名阿說示（五人之一），著衣持鉢，入城乞食。舍利弗見其儀服異容，諸根靜默，就而問言：「汝誰弟子？師是何人？」

A monk named Aśvajit (one of the original five monks) put on his robe, took his bowl, and entered the city for alms. Seeing his distinctive bearing and dress, and the calm restraint of his faculties, Śāriputra approached and asked, ‘Whose disciple are you? Who is your teacher?’

答言：「釋種太子厭老、病、死苦，出家學道，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，是我師也。」

He answered, ‘The prince of the Śākya clan became disenchanted with the suffering of aging, sickness, and death, went forth to seek the path, and attained unsurpassed complete awakening. He is my teacher.’

舍利弗言：「汝師教授為我說之！」即答偈曰：「我年既幼稚，學日又初淺，豈能宣至真，廣說如來義！」

Śāriputra asked, ‘Please tell me your teacher’s instruction!’ Aśvajit answered in verse: ‘I am young, and my learning is recent and limited. How could I proclaim the highest truth and explain the Tathāgata’s meaning at length?’

舍利弗言：「略說其要！」爾時，阿說示比丘說此偈言：「諸法因緣生，是法說因緣，是法因緣盡，大師如是說。」

Śāriputra said, ‘Briefly state its essence.’ Aśvajit then spoke this verse: ‘Dharmas arise through causes and conditions. Their causes and conditions are taught, as is the ending of those causes and conditions. Thus the great teacher teaches.’

舍利弗聞此偈已，即得初道，還報目連。目連見其顏色和悅，迎謂之言：「汝得甘露味耶？為我說之！」舍利弗即為其說向所聞偈。目連言：「更為重說！」即復為說，亦得初道。

Hearing this verse, Śāriputra immediately attained the first stage of the path and returned to tell Maudgalyāyana. Seeing his peaceful, joyful expression, Maudgalyāyana greeted him: ‘Have you tasted the nectar of the deathless? Tell me!’ Śāriputra repeated the verse. Maudgalyāyana asked to hear it again. After hearing it once more, he too attained the first stage of the path.

Becoming the Buddha’s Foremost Disciples

Chinese Source

二師與二百五十弟子，俱到佛所。佛遙見二人與弟子俱來，告諸比丘：「汝等見此二人，在諸梵志前者不？」諸比丘言：「已見！」佛言：「是二人者，是我弟子中，智慧第一、神足第一弟子。」

English Translation

The two teachers and their two hundred and fifty disciples went together to the Buddha. Seeing them approach, the Buddha asked the monks, ‘Do you see those two leading the brahmins?’ They answered, ‘We do.’ He said, ‘These two will be foremost among my disciples in wisdom and supernatural power.’

大眾俱來，以漸近佛，既到稽首，在一面立，俱白佛言：「世尊！我等於佛法中欲出家受戒！」佛言：「善來比丘！」即時鬚髮自落，法服著身，衣鉢具足，受成就戒。

The group drew near, bowed their heads in homage, and stood to one side. They said, ‘World-Honored One! We wish to go forth and receive ordination in the Buddha Dharma.’ The Buddha said, ‘Come, monks!’ Their hair and beards immediately fell away, Dharma robes covered them, and their robes and bowls were complete. They received full ordination.

過半月後，佛為長爪梵志說法時，舍利弗得阿羅漢道。所以半月後得道者，是人當作逐佛轉法輪師，應在學地，現前自入諸法，種種具知，是故半月後得阿羅漢道。如是等種種功德甚多，是故舍利弗雖是阿羅漢，佛以是般若波羅蜜甚深法，為舍利弗說。

Half a month later, while the Buddha was teaching the brahmin Long Nails, Śāriputra attained arhatship. Why did it take half a month? He was to become a teacher who followed the Buddha in turning the Dharma wheel. While still a trainee, he needed to enter the dharmas directly and know their many aspects completely. Therefore, he attained arhatship after half a month. His many virtues were like these. Thus, although Śāriputra was an arhat, the Buddha taught him the profound Dharma of Prajñāpāramitā.

Why Subhūti Also Teaches Prajñāpāramitā

Chinese Source

問曰：若爾者，何以初少為舍利弗說，後多為須菩提說？若以智慧第一故應為多說，復何以為須菩提說？

English Translation

Question: If so, why is only a little taught to Śāriputra at the beginning, while much more is later taught to Subhūti? If Śāriputra’s foremost wisdom is the reason, he should receive more of the teaching. Why address Subhūti?

答曰：舍利弗佛弟子中智慧第一；須菩提於弟子中，得無諍三昧最第一。無諍三昧相，常觀眾生不令心惱，多行憐愍。諸菩薩者，弘大誓願以度眾生，憐愍相同，是故命說。

Answer: Śāriputra is foremost in wisdom among the Buddha’s disciples. Subhūti is foremost in attaining the samādhi of non-contention. Its character is constantly to consider beings without causing distress and to practice much compassion. Bodhisattvas make vast vows to liberate beings. Because their compassion is alike, the Buddha instructs Subhūti to speak.

復次，是須菩提好行空三昧，如佛在忉利天，夏安居受歲已，還下閻浮提。爾時，須菩提於石窟中住，自思惟：「佛從忉利天來下，我當至佛所耶？不至佛所耶？」又念言：「佛常說：若人以智慧眼觀佛法身，則為見佛中最。」

Furthermore, Subhūti delights in practicing the samādhi of emptiness. After spending the rains retreat in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three and completing its closing observance, the Buddha returned to Jambudvīpa. Subhūti was staying in a rock cave and wondered, ‘The Buddha is descending from the Heaven of the Thirty-Three. Should I go to him or not?’ He then reflected, ‘The Buddha always says that seeing his Dharma body with the eye of wisdom is the highest way of seeing him.’

是時，以佛從忉利天下故，閻浮提中四部眾集，諸天見人，人亦見天。座中有佛，及轉輪聖王、諸天大眾，眾會莊嚴，先未曾有！

As the Buddha descended, the fourfold community gathered in Jambudvīpa. Devas saw humans, and humans saw devas. The Buddha, a wheel-turning sage king, and great assemblies of devas were present. The assembly’s splendor was unprecedented.

須菩提心念：「今此大眾，雖復殊特，勢不久停，磨滅之法，皆歸無常。」因此無常觀之初門，悉知諸法空無有實；作是觀時，即得道證。

Subhūti thought, ‘Though extraordinary, this great assembly will not last. Subject to dissolution, all of it is impermanent.’ Entering through the contemplation of impermanence, he knew all dharmas to be empty and without substantial reality. Through this contemplation he realized the path.

爾時，一切眾人皆欲求先見佛，禮敬供養。有華色比丘尼，欲除女名之惡，便化為轉輪聖王及七寶千子，眾人見之，皆避坐起去。化王到佛所已，還復本身，為比丘尼最初禮佛。

Everyone wished to be first to see the Buddha, bow, and make offerings. The nun Flower Color wished to remove the disadvantage attached to being called a woman. She manifested as a wheel-turning sage king with the seven treasures and a thousand sons. Seeing this, people rose and made way. On reaching the Buddha, she resumed her form as a nun and was the first to bow.

是時，佛告比丘尼：「非汝初禮，須菩提最初禮我。所以者何？須菩提觀諸法空，是為見佛法身，得真供養，供養中最，非以致敬生身為供養也。」以是故言須菩提常行空三昧，與般若波羅蜜空相相應。以是故佛命令說般若波羅蜜。

The Buddha told her, ‘You were not the first to bow. Subhūti bowed to me first. Why? By contemplating dharmas as empty, he saw the Buddha’s Dharma body and made a true offering, the highest offering. Merely paying respects to the body born of parents is not that offering.’ Thus, Subhūti constantly practices the samādhi of emptiness, which accords with the empty character of Prajñāpāramitā. Therefore, the Buddha instructs him to teach it.

復次，佛以眾生信敬阿羅漢諸漏已盡，命之為說，眾得淨信故。諸菩薩漏未盡，若以為證，諸人不信。以是故，與舍利弗、須菩提，共說般若波羅蜜。

Furthermore, beings trust and honor arhats whose influxes have ended. By instructing them to teach, the Buddha enables the assembly to gain pure faith. Bodhisattvas have not yet ended their influxes; if they were presented as witnesses, people would not believe them. Therefore, he teaches Prajñāpāramitā together with Śāriputra and Subhūti.

Śāriputra’s Family and His Father’s Debate

Chinese Source

問曰：何以名舍利弗？為是父母所作字？為是依行功德立名？

English Translation

Question: Why is he called Śāriputra? Did his parents give him this name, or was it established on the basis of his conduct and virtues?

答曰：是父母所作名字。於閻浮提中第一安樂，有摩伽陀國，是中有大城名王舍，王名頻婆娑羅，有婆羅門論議師，名摩陀羅，王以其人善能論故，賜封一邑，去城不遠。是摩陀羅遂有居家，婦生一女，眼似舍利鳥眼，即名此女為舍利。次生一男，膝骨麁大，名拘郗羅。

Answer: It is a name given by his parents. In Magadha, a country of exceptional well-being in Jambudvīpa, lies the great city Rājagṛha. Its king was Bimbisāra. A brahmin debater named Matuoluo (摩陀羅) was so skilled that the king granted him a village near the city. He married and had a daughter whose eyes resembled those of a śāri bird, so he named her Śāri. A son born afterward had large kneecaps and was named Kauṣṭhila.

拘郗羅（秦言大膝也）是婆羅門，既有居家，畜養男女，所學經書，皆已廢忘，又不業新。是時，南天竺有一婆羅門大論議師，字提舍，於十八種大經，皆悉通利。

(Kauṣṭhila means ‘Great Knees.’) Busy with family life and raising his children, the brahmin forgot the scriptures he had studied and learned nothing new. At that time, a great brahmin debater from southern India named Tiṣya had mastered all eighteen great scriptures.

是人入王舍城，頭上戴火，以銅鍱腹。人問其故，便言：「我所學經書甚多，恐腹破裂，是故鍱之。」又問：「頭上何以戴火？」答言：「以大闇故。」眾人言：「日出照明，何以言闇？」答言：「闇有二種：一者、日光不照，二者、愚癡闇蔽。今雖有日明，而愚癡猶黑。」

He entered Rājagṛha carrying a flame on his head and copper plates around his abdomen. Asked why, he said, ‘I have learned so many scriptures that I fear my belly will burst; therefore I bind it.’ Asked about the flame, he replied, ‘Because the darkness is great.’ People said, ‘The sun is shining. Why speak of darkness?’ He answered, ‘There are two kinds: the absence of sunlight and the obscurity of ignorance. Though the sun shines, ignorance remains dark.’

眾人言：「汝但未見婆羅門摩陀羅，汝若見者，腹當縮，明當闇。」是婆羅門逕至鼓邊，打論議鼓。國王聞之，問是何人？眾臣答言：「南天竺有一婆羅門，名提舍，大論議師，欲求論處，故打論鼓。」王大歡喜，即集眾人而告之曰：「有能難者，與之論議！」

They told him, ‘You have simply not met the brahmin Matuoluo. If you did, your belly would shrink and your light would dim.’ He went straight to the debating drum and struck it. Hearing it, the king asked who had come. His ministers said, ‘A great brahmin debater from southern India named Tiṣya seeks a debate.’ Delighted, the king assembled the people and announced, ‘Whoever can challenge him should debate him!’

摩陀羅聞之自疑，「我以廢忘，又不業新，不知我今能與論不？」僶俛而來。於道中見二特牛，方相觝觸，心中作想：「此牛是我，彼牛是彼，以此為占，知誰得勝？」此牛不如，便大愁憂而自念言：「如此相者，我將不如。」欲入眾時，見有母人，挾一瓶水，正在其前，躄地破瓶。復作是念：「是亦不吉。」甚大不樂。

Matuoluo doubted himself: ‘I have forgotten what I studied and learned nothing new. Can I still debate him?’ Reluctantly he set out. On the way he saw two bulls fighting and thought, ‘Let this bull represent me and that one him. This omen will show who wins.’ His bull lost, and he became deeply anxious, thinking it foretold his defeat. Near the assembly, a woman carrying a water jar fell in front of him and broke it. He regarded this too as inauspicious and felt deeply unhappy.

既入眾中，見彼論師，顏貌意色，勝相具足，自知不如。事不獲已，與共論議。論議既交，便墮負處。王大歡喜：「大智明人，遠入我國！」復欲為之封一聚落。

On entering, he saw that his opponent’s face, bearing, and confidence all displayed the signs of victory, and he knew himself outmatched. Having no choice, he debated and was defeated. The king rejoiced: ‘A man of great wisdom and brilliance has come from afar to my country!’ He wished to grant him a village.

諸臣議言：「一聰明人來，便封一邑，功臣不賞，但寵語論，恐非安國全家之道！今摩陀羅論議不如，應奪其封以與勝者；若更有勝人，復以與之。」王用其言，即奪與後人。

The ministers objected: ‘If every clever newcomer receives a village while meritorious ministers go unrewarded, we honor mere argument rather than secure the state and its households. Since Matuoluo has lost, his grant should go to the victor. If another defeats him, let it pass to that person.’ The king accepted their advice and transferred the grant.

是時摩陀羅語提舍言：「汝是聰明人，我以女妻汝，男兒相累；今欲遠出他國，以求本志。」提舍納其女為婦。

Matuoluo told Tiṣya, ‘You are an intelligent man. I give you my daughter in marriage and entrust my son to your care. I now wish to travel abroad to pursue my original aim.’ Tiṣya married his daughter.

The Names Upatiṣya and Śāriputra

Chinese Source

其婦懷妊，夢見一人，身被甲冑，手執金剛，摧破諸山，而在大山邊立。覺已白其夫言，我夢如是。提舍言：「汝當生男，摧伏一切諸論議師，唯不勝一人，當與作弟子。」

English Translation

During her pregnancy, she dreamed of a man wearing armor and holding a vajra, who shattered mountains and stood beside a great mountain. On awakening she told her husband. Tiṣya said, ‘You will bear a son who will defeat all debaters except one. He will become that person’s disciple.’

舍利懷妊，以其子故，母亦聰明，大能論議。其弟拘郗羅，與姊談論，每屈不如；知所懷子，必大智慧，未生如是，何況出生！即捨家學問，至南天竺，不剪指爪，讀十八種經書，皆令通利，是故時人號為長爪梵志。

Because of the child she carried, Śāri herself became brilliant and skilled in debate. Her brother Kauṣṭhila repeatedly lost arguments with her. Realizing that the unborn child must possess great wisdom—already so powerful before birth, and surely more so afterward—he left home to study in southern India. He did not trim his nails while mastering the eighteen scriptures, so people called him the brahmin Long Nails.

姊子既生，七日之後，裹以白疊，以示其父。其父思惟：「我名提舍，逐我名字，字為憂波提舍。」（憂波，秦言逐；提舍，星名）是為父母作字。

Seven days after his sister’s son was born, the baby was wrapped in white cloth and shown to his father. His father thought, ‘My name is Tiṣya. Following my name, I shall call him Upatiṣya.’ (Upa means ‘following’; Tiṣya is the name of a star.) This was the name given by his parents.

眾人以其舍利所生，皆共名之為舍利弗（弗秦言子）。

Because he was born of Śāri, people called him Śāriputra. (Putra means ‘son.’)

復次，舍利弗世世本願，於釋迦文尼佛所作智慧第一弟子，字舍利弗。是為本願因緣名字，以是故名舍利弗。

Furthermore, life after life he had vowed to become Śākyamuni Buddha’s foremost disciple in wisdom, named Śāriputra. His name thus also arose through the causes and conditions of his original vow.

問曰：若爾者，何以不言「憂波提舍」，而但言「舍利弗」？答曰：時人貴重其母，於眾女人中聰明第一，以是因緣故稱舍利弗。

Question: If so, why call him Śāriputra rather than Upatiṣya? Answer: People held his mother in high regard as the most intelligent of women. Therefore, they called him Śāriputra.

Knowing All Dharmas in Every Aspect

Chinese Source

〖經〗「菩薩摩訶薩欲以一切種知一切法，當習行般若波羅蜜。」

English Translation

Sutra: ‘A bodhisattva mahāsattva who wishes to know all dharmas in every aspect should train in and practice Prajñāpāramitā.’

〖論〗菩薩摩訶薩義，如先〈讚菩薩品〉中說。問曰：云何名「一切種」？云何名「一切法」？

Treatise: The meaning of bodhisattva mahāsattva was explained earlier in the section praising bodhisattvas. Question: What is meant by ‘every aspect’? What is meant by ‘all dharmas’?

Every Aspect, All Dharmas, and the Limits of Śāriputra’s Knowledge

Chinese Source

答曰：智慧門名為「種」。有人以一智慧門觀，有以二、三、十、百、千、萬乃至恒河沙等阿僧祇智慧門觀諸法。今以一切智慧門入一切種，觀一切法，是名「一切種」。

English Translation

Answer: An ‘aspect’ is a gateway of wisdom. Some contemplate dharmas through one gateway, others through two, three, ten, a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand, or asaṃkhyeyas as numerous as the sands of the Ganges. Entering every aspect through all gateways of wisdom and contemplating all dharmas is called ‘every aspect.’

如凡夫人三種觀，欲求離欲、離色故：觀欲、色界麁惡、誑惑、濁重。

Ordinary people wishing to leave sensual desire and form employ three contemplations: they regard the desire and form realms as coarse and harmful, deceptive, and impure and burdensome.

佛弟子八種觀：無常、苦、空、無我、如病、如癰、如箭入體、惱患。是八種觀，入四聖諦中，為十六行之四。十六者，觀苦四種：無常、苦、空、無我；觀苦因四種：集、因、緣、生；觀苦盡四種：盡、滅、妙、出；觀道四種：道、正、行、跡。

The Buddha’s disciples employ eight contemplations: impermanence, suffering, emptiness, non-self, illness, a sore, an arrow lodged in the body, and affliction. These enter the four noble truths and include four of the sixteen aspects. The sixteen are: contemplating suffering as impermanent, suffering, empty, and without self; its origin as accumulation, cause, condition, and arising; its cessation as exhaustion, cessation, excellence, and release; and the path as path, rightness, practice, and the way.

出入息中復有十六行：一、觀入息，二、觀出息，三、觀息長息短，四、觀息遍身，五、除諸身行，六、受喜，七、受樂，八者、受諸心行，九、無作喜，十、心作攝，十一、心作解脫，十二、觀無常，十三、觀散壞，十四、觀離欲，十五、觀滅，十六、觀棄捨。

There are also sixteen aspects of breathing practice: observing inhalation; observing exhalation; observing long and short breaths; observing breath throughout the body; calming bodily activities; experiencing joy; experiencing pleasure; experiencing mental activities; joy without contrivance; collecting the mind; liberating the mind; contemplating impermanence; contemplating dissolution; contemplating dispassion; contemplating cessation; and contemplating relinquishment.

復有六種念：念佛者，佛是多陀阿伽陀、阿羅呵、三藐三佛陀，如是等十號。五念如後說。世智、出世智，阿羅漢、辟支佛、菩薩、佛智，如是等智慧知諸法，名為「一切種」。

There are also six recollections. Recollection of the Buddha recalls his ten titles, beginning with Tathāgata, Arhat, and Fully and Perfectly Awakened One. The other five are explained later. Worldly and supramundane wisdom, and the wisdom of arhats, pratyekabuddhas, bodhisattvas, and Buddhas—knowing dharmas through these kinds of wisdom is called knowing ‘every aspect.’

「一切法」者，識所緣法，是一切法。所謂眼識緣色，耳識緣聲，鼻識緣香，舌識緣味，身識緣觸，意識緣法——緣眼、緣色、緣眼識，耳聲、鼻香、舌味、身觸亦如是，乃至緣意、緣法、緣意識。是名「一切法」，是為識所緣法。

‘All dharmas’ means everything that consciousness takes as its object. Visual consciousness takes forms as objects; auditory consciousness sounds; olfactory consciousness odors; gustatory consciousness tastes; bodily consciousness tangible objects; and mental consciousness dharmas. Mental consciousness can take the eye, forms, and visual consciousness as objects, and likewise the ear and sounds, nose and odors, tongue and tastes, body and tangible objects, through to mind, dharmas, and mental consciousness. These are all dharmas as objects of consciousness.

復次，智所緣法，是一切法。所謂苦智知苦，集智知集，盡智知盡，道智知道，世智知苦、集、盡、道及虛空、非數緣滅，是為智所緣法。

Furthermore, all dharmas are the objects of knowledge. Knowledge of suffering knows suffering; knowledge of its origin knows its origin; knowledge of cessation knows cessation; and knowledge of the path knows the path. Worldly knowledge knows suffering, origin, cessation, and path, as well as space and cessation not obtained through discernment. These are the objects of knowledge.

復次，二法攝一切法：色法、無色法，可見法、不可見法，有對法、無對法，有漏、無漏，有為、無為，心相應、心不相應，業相應、業不相應（丹注云：心法中除思，餘盡相應，業即是思，故除），近法、遠法等。如是種種二法，攝一切法（丹注云：現在及無為是名近法，未來、過去是名遠法）。

Furthermore, paired categories encompass all dharmas: material and immaterial; visible and invisible; resistant and non-resistant; with and without influxes; conditioned and unconditioned; associated and not associated with mind; associated and not associated with karma. (A Dan annotation says: among mental dharmas, volition is excluded; the others are associated. Karma is volition itself, so volition is excluded.) There are also near and distant dharmas. Such pairs encompass all dharmas. (A Dan annotation says: present and unconditioned dharmas are near; future and past dharmas are distant.)

復次，三種法攝一切法：善、不善、無記；學、無學、非學非無學；見諦斷、思惟斷、不斷。復有三種法：五眾、十二入、十八界。持如是等種種三法，盡攝一切法。

Furthermore, threefold classifications encompass all dharmas: wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate; belonging to trainees, those beyond training, and neither; abandoned through seeing the truths, through cultivation, and not abandoned. There are also the three schemes of five aggregates, twelve sense bases, and eighteen elements. These various threefold classifications encompass all dharmas.

復有四種法：過去、未來、現在法、非過去未來現在法；欲界繫法、色界繫法、無色界繫法、不繫法；因善法、因不善法、因無記法、非因善不善無記法；緣緣法、緣不緣法、緣緣不緣緣法、亦非緣緣非不緣緣法。如是等四種法，攝一切法。有五種法：色、心、心相應、心不相應、無為法。如是等種種五法，攝一切法。有六種法：見苦斷法、見習、盡、道斷法、思惟斷法、不斷法。如是等種種六法。乃至無量法攝一切法。是為一切法。

Fourfold classifications also encompass all dharmas: past, future, present, and outside these three times; bound to the desire realm, form realm, formless realm, and unbound; having wholesome, unwholesome, indeterminate causes, and none of these; taking conditioned objects, unconditioned objects, both, and neither. Such fourfold classifications encompass all dharmas. Fivefold classifications include form, mind, dharmas associated with mind, those dissociated from mind, and the unconditioned. Sixfold classifications include what is abandoned through seeing suffering, its origin, cessation, and path, through cultivation, and what is not abandoned. These and innumerable other classifications encompass all dharmas. This is what ‘all dharmas’ means.

問曰：諸法甚深微妙不可思議，若一切眾生尚不能得知，何況一人欲盡知一切法？譬如有人欲量大地，及數大海水渧，欲稱須彌山，欲知虛空邊際，如是等事皆不可知，云何欲以一切種知一切法？

Question: Dharmas are profound, subtle, and inconceivable. Even all beings together cannot know them. How could one person wish to know all dharmas completely? It is like measuring the earth, counting the ocean’s drops, weighing Mount Sumeru, or discovering the limits of space. Since such things cannot be known, how can one wish to know all dharmas in every aspect?

答曰：愚癡闇蔽甚大苦，智慧光明最為樂！一切眾生皆不用苦，但欲求樂。是故菩薩求一切第一大智慧，一切種觀，欲知一切法。是菩薩發大心，普為一切眾生求大智慧，是故欲以一切種知一切法。如醫為一人、二人，用一種、二種藥則足；若欲治一切眾生病者，當須一切種藥。菩薩亦如是，欲度一切眾生故，欲知一切種一切法。如諸法甚深微妙無量，菩薩智慧亦甚深微妙無量。先答破一切智人中已廣說，如函大蓋亦大。

Answer: The darkness of ignorance is great suffering; the light of wisdom is the greatest happiness. Beings reject suffering and seek happiness. Therefore, bodhisattvas seek supreme, great wisdom, contemplating every aspect and wishing to know all dharmas. They arouse expansive intentions and seek wisdom for all beings. A physician treating one or two people may need only one or two medicines; to treat every being’s illnesses requires every kind of medicine. Likewise, to liberate all beings, bodhisattvas wish to know all aspects of all dharmas. Just as dharmas are profound, subtle, and immeasurable, so is bodhisattva wisdom. This was explained fully earlier in answering objections to an all-knowing person: a large box has a correspondingly large lid.

復次，若不以理求一切法，則不可得；若以理求之，則無不得。譬如鑽火以木，則火可得；析薪求火，火不可得。如大地有邊際，自非一切智人，無大神力，則不能知；若神通力大，則知此三千大千世界地邊際。今此大地在金剛上，三千大千世界四邊則虛空，是為知地邊際。欲稱須彌山，亦如是。欲量虛空，非不能量，虛空無法，故不可量。

Furthermore, seeking dharmas without a proper method cannot succeed, whereas seeking them properly can. Fire can be obtained by drilling wood, but not by splitting firewood in search of it. The earth has limits, but someone lacking all-knowledge and great spiritual power cannot know them. With great spiritual power, one knows the boundaries of the land of this great trichiliocosm. This land rests upon vajra, with space on its four sides. That is knowing its boundaries. Weighing Mount Sumeru is similar. As for measuring space, it is not a failure of ability: space has no measurable substance, so it cannot be measured.

〖經〗舍利弗白佛言：「世尊！菩薩摩訶薩云何欲以一切種知一切法，當習行般若波羅蜜？」

Sutra: Śāriputra asked the Buddha, ‘World-Honored One! How should a bodhisattva mahāsattva who wishes to know all dharmas in every aspect train in and practice Prajñāpāramitā?’

〖論〗問曰：佛欲說般若波羅蜜故，種種現神變，現已即應便說，何以故令舍利弗問而後說？答曰：問而後說，佛法應爾。復次，舍利弗知般若波羅蜜甚深，微妙無相之法，難解難知。自以智力種種思惟：「若觀諸法無常，是般若波羅蜜耶？不是耶？」不能自了，以是故問。

Treatise: Question: The Buddha displayed various miracles in preparation for teaching Prajñāpāramitā. Why not teach immediately afterward, instead of waiting for Śāriputra’s question? Answer: Teaching in response to a question is the way of the Buddha Dharma. Furthermore, Śāriputra knew that Prajñāpāramitā is profound, subtle, without marks, and difficult to understand. He reflected in various ways: ‘Is contemplating dharmas as impermanent Prajñāpāramitā, or is it not?’ Unable to resolve this himself, he asked.

復次，舍利弗非一切智，於佛智慧中譬如小兒。如說《阿婆檀那經》中：佛在祇洹住，晡時經行，舍利弗從佛經行。是時有鷹逐鴿，鴿飛來佛邊住，佛經行過之，影覆鴿上，鴿身安隱，怖畏即除，不復作聲。後舍利弗影到，鴿便作聲，戰怖如初。

Furthermore, Śāriputra is not all-knowing. Compared with the Buddha’s wisdom, his is like a child’s. An Avadāna scripture relates that, while staying at Jetavana, the Buddha was walking in meditation in the late afternoon with Śāriputra following him. A pigeon fleeing a hawk came beside the Buddha. As he walked past and his shadow covered it, the pigeon became calm, its fear disappeared, and it stopped crying. When Śāriputra’s shadow reached it, it cried out and trembled as before.

舍利弗白佛言：「佛及我身，俱無三毒，以何因緣佛影覆鴿，鴿便無聲，不復恐怖？我影覆上，鴿便作聲，戰慄如故？」佛言：「汝三毒習氣未盡，以是故，汝影覆時恐怖不除。汝觀此鴿宿世因緣，幾世作鴿？」

Śāriputra asked, ‘Both the Buddha and I are free of the three poisons. Why does your shadow calm the pigeon, while mine leaves it crying and trembling?’ The Buddha replied, ‘Your residual tendencies of the three poisons have not ended. Therefore, your shadow does not remove its fear. Examine its past causes and conditions: for how many lives has it been a pigeon?’

舍利弗即時入宿命智三昧，觀見此鴿從鴿中來，如是一、二、三世，乃至八萬大劫，常作鴿身；過是已往，不能復見。舍利弗從三昧起，白佛言：「是鴿八萬大劫中，常作鴿身；過是已前，不能復知。」

Śāriputra entered the samādhi of knowledge of former lives. He saw that the pigeon had come from another pigeon life, and another, back through eighty thousand great kalpas, always as a pigeon. Beyond that he could not see. Emerging, he told the Buddha, ‘For eighty thousand great kalpas it has always been a pigeon. Earlier than that, I cannot know.’

佛言：「汝若不能盡知過去世，試觀未來世，此鴿何時當脫？」

The Buddha said, ‘If you cannot fully know its past, examine its future. When will it escape this condition?’

舍利弗即入願智三昧，觀見此鴿，一、二、三世，乃至八萬大劫，未脫鴿身；過是已往，亦不能知。從三昧起，白佛言：「我見此鴿從一世、二世、乃至八萬大劫，未免鴿身；過此已往，不復能知！我不知過去、未來齊限，不審此鴿何時當脫？」

Śāriputra entered the samādhi of knowledge through aspiration and saw one, two, three lives, through eighty thousand great kalpas, without escape from a pigeon’s body. Beyond that he could not know. Emerging, he said, ‘I see no escape from being a pigeon for eighty thousand great kalpas. Beyond that I cannot know. I do not know the limits of its past or future. When will it be freed?’

佛告舍利弗：「此鴿除諸聲聞、辟支佛所知齊限，復於恒河沙等大劫中常作鴿身，罪訖得出。輪轉五道中，後得為人，經五百世中，乃得利根。是時有佛，度無量阿僧祇眾生，然後入無餘涅槃。遺法在世，是人作五戒優婆塞，從比丘聞讚佛功德，於是初發心，願欲作佛。然後於三阿僧祇劫，行六波羅蜜，十地具足，得作佛，度無量眾生已而入無餘涅槃。」

The Buddha told him, ‘Beyond the range known to śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas, this pigeon will remain a pigeon for great kalpas as numerous as the sands of the Ganges. When its wrongdoing is exhausted, it will escape. Wandering through the five destinies, it will eventually become human. After five hundred human lives, its faculties will become sharp. A Buddha will then liberate immeasurable asaṃkhyeyas of beings and enter nirvāṇa without remainder. During the period of his surviving Dharma, this person will become a lay follower observing the five precepts. Hearing a monk praise the Buddha’s virtues, he will first aspire to Buddhahood. He will then practice the six pāramitās for three asaṃkhyeya kalpas, fulfill the ten stages, become a Buddha, liberate immeasurable beings, and enter nirvāṇa without remainder.’

是時，舍利弗向佛懺悔，白佛言：「我於一鳥尚不能知其本末，何況諸法！我若知佛智慧如是者，為佛智慧故，寧入阿鼻地獄受無量劫苦，不以為難。」如是等，於諸法中不了故問。

Śāriputra then repented before the Buddha: ‘I cannot know the whole story of even one bird, much less all dharmas! Had I known your wisdom was like this, I would willingly have entered Avīci hell and endured immeasurable kalpas of suffering for the sake of that wisdom, without considering it difficult.’ Thus, he asked because his understanding of dharmas was incomplete.

The Perfection of Giving and Explanations of Prajñāpāramitā — Part Seventeen

Chinese Source

〖經〗佛告舍利弗：「菩薩摩訶薩以不住法住般若波羅蜜中，以無所捨法具足檀波羅蜜，施者、受者及財物不可得故。」

English Translation

Sutra: The Buddha told Śāriputra, ‘A bodhisattva mahāsattva abides in Prajñāpāramitā by not abiding in any dharma, and fulfills the perfection of giving without any dharma being relinquished, because giver, recipient, and material gift cannot be apprehended.’

〖論〗問曰：「般若波羅蜜」是何等法？答曰：有人言：無漏慧根是般若波羅蜜相。何以故？一切慧中第一慧是名般若波羅蜜；無漏慧根是第一，以是故，無漏慧根名般若波羅蜜。

Treatise: Question: What dharma is Prajñāpāramitā? Answer: Some say its character is the undefiled faculty of wisdom. Why? Prajñāpāramitā is the foremost wisdom, and the undefiled faculty of wisdom is foremost. Therefore it is called Prajñāpāramitā.

問曰：若菩薩未斷結，云何得行無漏慧？答曰：菩薩雖未斷結，行相似無漏般若波羅蜜，是故得名行無漏般若波羅蜜。譬如聲聞人行暖法、頂法、忍法、世間第一法，先行相似無漏法，後易得生苦法智忍。

Question: If bodhisattvas have not severed their fetters, how can they practice undefiled wisdom? Answer: Though their fetters are not severed, they practice Prajñāpāramitā resembling undefiled wisdom, and are therefore said to practice it. Likewise, śrāvakas practicing warmth, summit, acceptance, and the highest worldly dharma first practice dharmas resembling the undefiled, making it easier afterward to arouse acceptance based on knowledge of suffering as Dharma.

復有人言：菩薩有二種：有斷結使清淨，有未斷結使不清淨。斷結使清淨菩薩，能行無漏般若波羅蜜。

Others say there are two kinds of bodhisattvas: pure ones who have severed the fetters, and impure ones who have not. Those who have severed them can practice undefiled Prajñāpāramitā.

問曰：若菩薩斷結清淨，復何以行般若波羅蜜？答曰：雖斷結使，十地未滿，未莊嚴佛土，未教化眾生，是故行般若波羅蜜。

Question: If a bodhisattva is already purified of fetters, why continue practicing Prajñāpāramitā? Answer: Although the fetters have been severed, the ten stages are not fulfilled, Buddha lands are not adorned, and beings have not been taught. Therefore, the bodhisattva continues practicing.

復次，斷結有二種：一者、斷三毒心，不著人天中五欲；二者、雖不著人天中五欲，於菩薩功德果報五欲未能捨離——如是菩薩，應行般若波羅蜜。

Furthermore, severing fetters has two aspects: first, cutting off the mind of the three poisons and attachment to human and heavenly sense pleasures; second, relinquishing the sense pleasures that result from bodhisattva merit. Even without attachment to human and heavenly pleasures, a bodhisattva may not yet have relinquished the latter and should therefore practice Prajñāpāramitā.

譬如長老阿泥盧豆，在林中坐禪時，淨愛天女等，以淨妙之身來試阿泥盧豆。阿泥盧豆言：「諸姊作青色來，不用雜色。」欲觀不淨，不能得觀；黃、赤、白色，亦復如是。時阿泥盧豆閉目不視，語言：「諸姊遠去！」是時天女即滅不現。

For example, while the elder Aniruddha meditated in a forest, goddesses including Pure Love came in exquisitely beautiful forms to test him. He told them, ‘Sisters, appear in blue, not mixed colors.’ He wished to contemplate impurity but could not. The same happened with yellow, red, and white. He closed his eyes and said, ‘Sisters, go away!’ They disappeared.

天福報形，猶尚如是，何況菩薩無量功德果報五欲？

If forms produced by heavenly merit are like this, how much more so are the sense pleasures resulting from the immeasurable merit of bodhisattvas?

又如甄陀羅王與八萬四千甄陀羅來到佛所，彈琴歌頌，以供養佛。爾時，須彌山王及諸山樹木、人民、禽獸一切皆舞。佛邊大眾，乃至大迦葉，皆於座上不能自安。是時，天須菩薩問長老大迦葉：「耆年舊宿，行十二頭陀法之第一，何以在座不能自安？」

Likewise, a kiṃnara king and eighty-four thousand kiṃnaras came to the Buddha, playing strings and singing praises as offerings. Mount Sumeru, the other mountains, trees, people, birds, and beasts all danced. Even Mahākāśyapa and the assembly beside the Buddha could not sit still. Bodhisattva Heavenly Subhūti asked him, ‘Venerable elder, foremost in the twelve ascetic practices, why can you not remain still in your seat?’

大迦葉言：「三界五欲不能動我；是菩薩神通功德果報力故，令我如是，非我有心不能自安也。譬如須彌山，四邊風起，不能令動；至大劫盡時，毘藍風起，如吹爛草。」以是事故，知二種結中一種未斷；如是菩薩等，應行般若波羅蜜。

Mahākāśyapa replied, ‘The sense pleasures of the three realms cannot move me. It is the power of this bodhisattva’s supernatural abilities and meritorious results that makes me like this, not a restless intention of my own. Winds on all four sides cannot move Mount Sumeru, but at the end of a great kalpa the vairambha wind blows it away like rotten grass.’ Thus, one of these two kinds of fetter may remain unsevered, and such bodhisattvas should practice Prajñāpāramitā.

是阿毘曇中如是說，復有人言：般若波羅蜜是有漏慧。何以故？菩薩至道樹下乃斷結；先雖有大智慧，有無量功德，而諸煩惱未斷。是故言菩薩般若波羅蜜是有漏智慧。

This is explained in the Abhidharma. Others say Prajñāpāramitā is wisdom with influxes, because bodhisattvas sever their fetters only beneath the tree of awakening. Although they previously possess great wisdom and immeasurable merit, their afflictions have not been severed. Therefore their Prajñāpāramitā is called wisdom with influxes.

復有人言：從初發意乃至道樹下，於其中間所有智慧，是名般若波羅蜜。成佛時，是般若波羅蜜轉名薩婆若。

Others call all wisdom from the initial aspiration until sitting beneath the tree of awakening Prajñāpāramitā. On becoming a Buddha, it is renamed all-knowledge.

復有人言：菩薩有漏、無漏智慧，總名般若波羅蜜。何以故？菩薩觀涅槃，行佛道，以是事故，菩薩智慧應是無漏；以未斷結使，事未成辦故，應名有漏。

Others say a bodhisattva’s wisdom, both with and without influxes, is collectively called Prajñāpāramitā. Contemplating nirvāṇa and practicing the Buddha path make it undefiled; yet because fetters remain and the task is unfinished, it should also be called wisdom with influxes.

復有人言：菩薩般若波羅蜜，無漏無為，不可見無對。

Others say a bodhisattva’s Prajñāpāramitā is undefiled, unconditioned, invisible, and non-resistant.

復有人言：是般若波羅蜜，不可得相，若有若無，若常若無常，若空若實。是般若波羅蜜，非陰界入所攝，非有為、非無為，非法、非非法，無取無捨，不生不滅，出有無四句，適無所著。譬如火焰，四邊不可觸，以燒手故；般若波羅蜜相，亦如是不可觸，以邪見火燒故。

Others say its character cannot be apprehended as existing or not existing, permanent or impermanent, empty or substantial. It is not included in aggregates, elements, or sense bases; it is neither conditioned nor unconditioned, neither dharma nor non-dharma. It involves no grasping or relinquishing, arising or ceasing. Beyond the four alternatives concerning existence and nonexistence, it clings nowhere. Like a flame that cannot be touched from any side without burning the hand, Prajñāpāramitā cannot be grasped without being burned by the fire of wrong views.

問曰：上種種人說般若波羅蜜，何者為實？答曰：有人言：各各有理，皆是實。如經說：五百比丘各各說二邊及中道義，佛言皆有道理。

Question: Which of these various explanations is true? Answer: Some say each has its reasoning and all are true. A scripture relates that five hundred monks each explained the two extremes and the middle way, and the Buddha said all their explanations had sound reasoning.

有人言：末後答者為實。所以者何？不可破、不可壞故。若有法如毫氂許有者，皆有過失可破，若言無亦可破。此般若中，有亦無，無亦無，非有非無亦無，如是言說亦無，是名寂滅、無量、無戲論法。是故不可破、不可壞，是名真實般若波羅蜜，最勝無過者。

Others say the last answer is true because it cannot be refuted or destroyed. Asserting even a hair’s breadth of an existent dharma involves faults that can be refuted; asserting nonexistence can also be refuted. In this prajñā, existence is absent, nonexistence is absent, and neither-existence-nor-nonexistence is absent. Even such formulations are absent. This is the peaceful, immeasurable Dharma free of conceptual proliferation. Therefore it cannot be refuted or destroyed and is called true Prajñāpāramitā, supreme and unsurpassed.
