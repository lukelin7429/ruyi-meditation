Why the Buddha Addresses Śāriputra — Part Sixteen

Chinese Source

〖經〗佛告舍利弗。

English Translation

Sutra: The Buddha addressed Śāriputra.

〖論〗問曰：般若波羅蜜是菩薩摩訶薩法，佛何以故告舍利弗而不告菩薩？

Treatise: Question: Prajñāpāramitā is the teaching for bodhisattva mahāsattvas. Why does the Buddha address Śāriputra rather than a bodhisattva?

答曰：舍利弗於一切弟子中，智慧最第一，如佛偈說：

Answer: Śāriputra is foremost in wisdom among all the disciples. As the Buddha says in verse:

「一切眾生智，唯除佛世尊；欲比舍利弗，智慧及多聞，於十六分中，猶尚不及一。」

‘Of all beings, except the World-Honored Buddha, none can compare with Śāriputra in wisdom and learning. Their wisdom and learning do not reach even one sixteenth of his.’

Śāriputra’s Learning and Early Renown

Chinese Source

復次，舍利弗智慧多聞，有大功德。年始八歲，誦十八部經，通解一切經書義理。是時，摩伽陀國有龍王兄弟：一名姞利，二名阿伽羅。降雨以時，國無荒年，人民感之，常以仲春之月，一切大集至龍住處，為設大會，作樂談義，終此一日。

English Translation

Furthermore, Śāriputra possessed wisdom, extensive learning, and great merit. At only eight years old, he could recite eighteen divisions of scripture and thoroughly understand their teachings. At that time, Magadha had two dragon kings who were brothers, named Jili and Agaluo. They brought timely rain, and the country suffered no years of famine. In gratitude, every year in the second month of spring the people gathered at the dragons’ dwelling for a great festival, playing music and discussing teachings throughout the day.

自古及今，斯集未替，遂以龍名以名此會。此日常法，敷四高座：一為國王，二為太子，三為大臣，四為論士。

This gathering had continued from ancient times and was named after the dragons. By custom, four high seats were set out: one for the king, one for the crown prince, one for the chief minister, and one for a debater.

爾時，舍利弗以八歲之身，問眾人言：「此四高座，為誰敷之？」眾人答言：「為國王、太子、大臣、論士。」是時，舍利弗觀察時人婆羅門等，神情瞻向，無勝己者，便昇論床，結跏趺坐。

Eight-year-old Śāriputra asked, ‘For whom are these four high seats prepared?’ The people answered, ‘For the king, crown prince, chief minister, and debater.’ Observing the brahmins and others present, he saw no one whose bearing suggested an intellect greater than his own. He climbed onto the debater’s seat and sat in full lotus posture.

眾人疑怪，或謂愚小無知，或謂智量過人。雖復嘉其神異，而猶各懷自矜，恥其年小，不自與語，皆遣年少弟子傳言問之。其答酬旨趣，辭理超絕！時諸論師歎未曾有，愚智大小一切皆伏。

The crowd was astonished. Some thought him a foolish, ignorant child; others thought his intelligence extraordinary. Although impressed by his unusual ability, the scholars remained proud. Embarrassed to debate someone so young, they sent their younger pupils to question him instead. His replies were exceptional in meaning, expression, and reasoning. The debaters marveled at this unprecedented ability, and everyone, wise or foolish, young or old, acknowledged his superiority.

王大歡喜，即命有司封一聚落，常以給之。王乘象輿，振鈴告告宣示一切，十六大國，六大城中無不慶悅。

The king rejoiced and ordered officials to grant him a village for his continuing support. Riding an elephant carriage, the king had bells rung and the news proclaimed everywhere. Throughout the sixteen great countries and six great cities, everyone celebrated.

Two Friends Seek the Deathless

Chinese Source

是時，告占師子，名拘律陀，姓大目揵連，舍利弗友而親之。舍利弗才明見重，目揵連豪爽最貴。此二人者，才智相比，德行互同；行則俱遊，住則同止；少長繾綣，結要終始。

English Translation

At that time, Śāriputra befriended Kolita, son of a diviner and a member of the Mahāmaudgalyāyana clan. Śāriputra was respected for his brilliance; Maudgalyāyana was esteemed for his noble, generous character. They were comparable in intelligence and alike in conduct. They traveled and stayed together, remaining close as they grew up and pledging lifelong friendship.

後俱厭世，出家學道，作梵志弟子。情求道門，久而無徵，以問於師。師名刪闍耶，而答之言：「自我求道，彌歷年歲，不知為有道果無耶？我非其人耶？而亦不得！」

Later, both became disenchanted with the world, went forth to seek the path, and became pupils of a brahmin. After a long search without any evidence of attainment, they questioned their teacher, Sañjaya. He replied, ‘I have sought the path for many years, yet I do not know whether its fruit exists. Perhaps I am not suited to it, for I too have not attained it!’

他日其師寢疾，舍利弗在頭邊立，大目連在足邊立，喘喘然其命將終，乃愍爾而笑。二人同心，俱問笑意。師答之言：「世俗無眼，為恩愛所侵。我見金地國王死，其大夫人自投火𧂐，求同一處，而此二人行報各異，生處殊絕。」

One day their teacher fell ill. Śāriputra stood by his head and Maudgalyāyana by his feet. As he lay gasping, near death, he smiled with pity. Both asked why. He replied, ‘Worldly people lack vision and are overcome by attachment. I see that the king of the Land of Gold has died. His chief queen has thrown herself onto his funeral pyre, hoping to be with him. But their actions bring different results, and their places of rebirth are far apart.’

是時二人筆受師語，欲以驗其虛實。後有金地商人，遠來摩伽陀國，二人以䟽驗之，果如師語，乃憮然歎曰：「我等非其人耶？為是師隱我耶？」二人相與誓曰：「若先得甘露，要畢同味。」

They wrote down his words to test their truth. Later, merchants from the Land of Gold came to Magadha. Checking their record against what the merchants reported, they found that their teacher’s words were true. Disheartened, they sighed, ‘Are we unsuited to the path, or did our teacher conceal it from us?’ They pledged to each other, ‘Whoever first obtains the nectar of the deathless must share its taste with the other.’

Hearing the Verse on Causes and Conditions

Chinese Source

是時，佛度迦葉兄弟千人，次遊諸國，到王舍城，頓止竹園。二梵志師聞佛出世，俱入王舍城，欲知消息。

English Translation

The Buddha had by then liberated the Kāśyapa brothers and their thousand followers. Traveling onward through various countries, he reached Rājagṛha and stayed in the Bamboo Grove. Hearing that a Buddha had appeared, the two brahmin teachers entered the city together to learn more.

爾時，有一比丘，名阿說示（五人之一），著衣持鉢，入城乞食。舍利弗見其儀服異容，諸根靜默，就而問言：「汝誰弟子？師是何人？」

A monk named Aśvajit (one of the original five monks) put on his robe, took his bowl, and entered the city for alms. Seeing his distinctive bearing and dress, and the calm restraint of his faculties, Śāriputra approached and asked, ‘Whose disciple are you? Who is your teacher?’

答言：「釋種太子厭老、病、死苦，出家學道，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，是我師也。」

He answered, ‘The prince of the Śākya clan became disenchanted with the suffering of aging, sickness, and death, went forth to seek the path, and attained unsurpassed complete awakening. He is my teacher.’

舍利弗言：「汝師教授為我說之！」即答偈曰：「我年既幼稚，學日又初淺，豈能宣至真，廣說如來義！」

Śāriputra asked, ‘Please tell me your teacher’s instruction!’ Aśvajit answered in verse: ‘I am young, and my learning is recent and limited. How could I proclaim the highest truth and explain the Tathāgata’s meaning at length?’

舍利弗言：「略說其要！」爾時，阿說示比丘說此偈言：「諸法因緣生，是法說因緣，是法因緣盡，大師如是說。」

Śāriputra said, ‘Briefly state its essence.’ Aśvajit then spoke this verse: ‘Dharmas arise through causes and conditions. Their causes and conditions are taught, as is the ending of those causes and conditions. Thus the great teacher teaches.’

舍利弗聞此偈已，即得初道，還報目連。目連見其顏色和悅，迎謂之言：「汝得甘露味耶？為我說之！」舍利弗即為其說向所聞偈。目連言：「更為重說！」即復為說，亦得初道。

Hearing this verse, Śāriputra immediately attained the first stage of the path and returned to tell Maudgalyāyana. Seeing his peaceful, joyful expression, Maudgalyāyana greeted him: ‘Have you tasted the nectar of the deathless? Tell me!’ Śāriputra repeated the verse. Maudgalyāyana asked to hear it again. After hearing it once more, he too attained the first stage of the path.

Becoming the Buddha’s Foremost Disciples

Chinese Source

二師與二百五十弟子，俱到佛所。佛遙見二人與弟子俱來，告諸比丘：「汝等見此二人，在諸梵志前者不？」諸比丘言：「已見！」佛言：「是二人者，是我弟子中，智慧第一、神足第一弟子。」

English Translation

The two teachers and their two hundred and fifty disciples went together to the Buddha. Seeing them approach, the Buddha asked the monks, ‘Do you see those two leading the brahmins?’ They answered, ‘We do.’ He said, ‘These two will be foremost among my disciples in wisdom and supernatural power.’

大眾俱來，以漸近佛，既到稽首，在一面立，俱白佛言：「世尊！我等於佛法中欲出家受戒！」佛言：「善來比丘！」即時鬚髮自落，法服著身，衣鉢具足，受成就戒。

The group drew near, bowed their heads in homage, and stood to one side. They said, ‘World-Honored One! We wish to go forth and receive ordination in the Buddha Dharma.’ The Buddha said, ‘Come, monks!’ Their hair and beards immediately fell away, Dharma robes covered them, and their robes and bowls were complete. They received full ordination.

過半月後，佛為長爪梵志說法時，舍利弗得阿羅漢道。所以半月後得道者，是人當作逐佛轉法輪師，應在學地，現前自入諸法，種種具知，是故半月後得阿羅漢道。如是等種種功德甚多，是故舍利弗雖是阿羅漢，佛以是般若波羅蜜甚深法，為舍利弗說。

Half a month later, while the Buddha was teaching the brahmin Long Nails, Śāriputra attained arhatship. Why did it take half a month? He was to become a teacher who followed the Buddha in turning the Dharma wheel. While still a trainee, he needed to enter the dharmas directly and know their many aspects completely. Therefore, he attained arhatship after half a month. His many virtues were like these. Thus, although Śāriputra was an arhat, the Buddha taught him the profound Dharma of Prajñāpāramitā.

Why Subhūti Also Teaches Prajñāpāramitā

Chinese Source

問曰：若爾者，何以初少為舍利弗說，後多為須菩提說？若以智慧第一故應為多說，復何以為須菩提說？

English Translation

Question: If so, why is only a little taught to Śāriputra at the beginning, while much more is later taught to Subhūti? If Śāriputra’s foremost wisdom is the reason, he should receive more of the teaching. Why address Subhūti?

答曰：舍利弗佛弟子中智慧第一；須菩提於弟子中，得無諍三昧最第一。無諍三昧相，常觀眾生不令心惱，多行憐愍。諸菩薩者，弘大誓願以度眾生，憐愍相同，是故命說。

Answer: Śāriputra is foremost in wisdom among the Buddha’s disciples. Subhūti is foremost in attaining the samādhi of non-contention. Its character is constantly to consider beings without causing distress and to practice much compassion. Bodhisattvas make vast vows to liberate beings. Because their compassion is alike, the Buddha instructs Subhūti to speak.

復次，是須菩提好行空三昧，如佛在忉利天，夏安居受歲已，還下閻浮提。爾時，須菩提於石窟中住，自思惟：「佛從忉利天來下，我當至佛所耶？不至佛所耶？」又念言：「佛常說：若人以智慧眼觀佛法身，則為見佛中最。」

Furthermore, Subhūti delights in practicing the samādhi of emptiness. After spending the rains retreat in the Heaven of the Thirty-Three and completing its closing observance, the Buddha returned to Jambudvīpa. Subhūti was staying in a rock cave and wondered, ‘The Buddha is descending from the Heaven of the Thirty-Three. Should I go to him or not?’ He then reflected, ‘The Buddha always says that seeing his Dharma body with the eye of wisdom is the highest way of seeing him.’

是時，以佛從忉利天下故，閻浮提中四部眾集，諸天見人，人亦見天。座中有佛，及轉輪聖王、諸天大眾，眾會莊嚴，先未曾有！

As the Buddha descended, the fourfold community gathered in Jambudvīpa. Devas saw humans, and humans saw devas. The Buddha, a wheel-turning sage king, and great assemblies of devas were present. The assembly’s splendor was unprecedented.

須菩提心念：「今此大眾，雖復殊特，勢不久停，磨滅之法，皆歸無常。」因此無常觀之初門，悉知諸法空無有實；作是觀時，即得道證。

Subhūti thought, ‘Though extraordinary, this great assembly will not last. Subject to dissolution, all of it is impermanent.’ Entering through the contemplation of impermanence, he knew all dharmas to be empty and without substantial reality. Through this contemplation he realized the path.

爾時，一切眾人皆欲求先見佛，禮敬供養。有華色比丘尼，欲除女名之惡，便化為轉輪聖王及七寶千子，眾人見之，皆避坐起去。化王到佛所已，還復本身，為比丘尼最初禮佛。

Everyone wished to be first to see the Buddha, bow, and make offerings. The nun Flower Color wished to remove the disadvantage attached to being called a woman. She manifested as a wheel-turning sage king with the seven treasures and a thousand sons. Seeing this, people rose and made way. On reaching the Buddha, she resumed her form as a nun and was the first to bow.

是時，佛告比丘尼：「非汝初禮，須菩提最初禮我。所以者何？須菩提觀諸法空，是為見佛法身，得真供養，供養中最，非以致敬生身為供養也。」以是故言須菩提常行空三昧，與般若波羅蜜空相相應。以是故佛命令說般若波羅蜜。

The Buddha told her, ‘You were not the first to bow. Subhūti bowed to me first. Why? By contemplating dharmas as empty, he saw the Buddha’s Dharma body and made a true offering, the highest offering. Merely paying respects to the body born of parents is not that offering.’ Thus, Subhūti constantly practices the samādhi of emptiness, which accords with the empty character of Prajñāpāramitā. Therefore, the Buddha instructs him to teach it.

復次，佛以眾生信敬阿羅漢諸漏已盡，命之為說，眾得淨信故。諸菩薩漏未盡，若以為證，諸人不信。以是故，與舍利弗、須菩提，共說般若波羅蜜。

Furthermore, beings trust and honor arhats whose influxes have ended. By instructing them to teach, the Buddha enables the assembly to gain pure faith. Bodhisattvas have not yet ended their influxes; if they were presented as witnesses, people would not believe them. Therefore, he teaches Prajñāpāramitā together with Śāriputra and Subhūti.

Śāriputra’s Family and His Father’s Debate

Chinese Source

問曰：何以名舍利弗？為是父母所作字？為是依行功德立名？

English Translation

Question: Why is he called Śāriputra? Did his parents give him this name, or was it established on the basis of his conduct and virtues?

答曰：是父母所作名字。於閻浮提中第一安樂，有摩伽陀國，是中有大城名王舍，王名頻婆娑羅，有婆羅門論議師，名摩陀羅，王以其人善能論故，賜封一邑，去城不遠。是摩陀羅遂有居家，婦生一女，眼似舍利鳥眼，即名此女為舍利。次生一男，膝骨麁大，名拘郗羅。

Answer: It is a name given by his parents. In Magadha, a country of exceptional well-being in Jambudvīpa, lies the great city Rājagṛha. Its king was Bimbisāra. A brahmin debater named Matuoluo (摩陀羅) was so skilled that the king granted him a village near the city. He married and had a daughter whose eyes resembled those of a śāri bird, so he named her Śāri. A son born afterward had large kneecaps and was named Kauṣṭhila.

拘郗羅（秦言大膝也）是婆羅門，既有居家，畜養男女，所學經書，皆已廢忘，又不業新。是時，南天竺有一婆羅門大論議師，字提舍，於十八種大經，皆悉通利。

(Kauṣṭhila means ‘Great Knees.’) Busy with family life and raising his children, the brahmin forgot the scriptures he had studied and learned nothing new. At that time, a great brahmin debater from southern India named Tiṣya had mastered all eighteen great scriptures.

是人入王舍城，頭上戴火，以銅鍱腹。人問其故，便言：「我所學經書甚多，恐腹破裂，是故鍱之。」又問：「頭上何以戴火？」答言：「以大闇故。」眾人言：「日出照明，何以言闇？」答言：「闇有二種：一者、日光不照，二者、愚癡闇蔽。今雖有日明，而愚癡猶黑。」

He entered Rājagṛha carrying a flame on his head and copper plates around his abdomen. Asked why, he said, ‘I have learned so many scriptures that I fear my belly will burst; therefore I bind it.’ Asked about the flame, he replied, ‘Because the darkness is great.’ People said, ‘The sun is shining. Why speak of darkness?’ He answered, ‘There are two kinds: the absence of sunlight and the obscurity of ignorance. Though the sun shines, ignorance remains dark.’

眾人言：「汝但未見婆羅門摩陀羅，汝若見者，腹當縮，明當闇。」是婆羅門逕至鼓邊，打論議鼓。國王聞之，問是何人？眾臣答言：「南天竺有一婆羅門，名提舍，大論議師，欲求論處，故打論鼓。」王大歡喜，即集眾人而告之曰：「有能難者，與之論議！」

They told him, ‘You have simply not met the brahmin Matuoluo. If you did, your belly would shrink and your light would dim.’ He went straight to the debating drum and struck it. Hearing it, the king asked who had come. His ministers said, ‘A great brahmin debater from southern India named Tiṣya seeks a debate.’ Delighted, the king assembled the people and announced, ‘Whoever can challenge him should debate him!’

摩陀羅聞之自疑，「我以廢忘，又不業新，不知我今能與論不？」僶俛而來。於道中見二特牛，方相觝觸，心中作想：「此牛是我，彼牛是彼，以此為占，知誰得勝？」此牛不如，便大愁憂而自念言：「如此相者，我將不如。」欲入眾時，見有母人，挾一瓶水，正在其前，躄地破瓶。復作是念：「是亦不吉。」甚大不樂。

Matuoluo doubted himself: ‘I have forgotten what I studied and learned nothing new. Can I still debate him?’ Reluctantly he set out. On the way he saw two bulls fighting and thought, ‘Let this bull represent me and that one him. This omen will show who wins.’ His bull lost, and he became deeply anxious, thinking it foretold his defeat. Near the assembly, a woman carrying a water jar fell in front of him and broke it. He regarded this too as inauspicious and felt deeply unhappy.

既入眾中，見彼論師，顏貌意色，勝相具足，自知不如。事不獲已，與共論議。論議既交，便墮負處。王大歡喜：「大智明人，遠入我國！」復欲為之封一聚落。

On entering, he saw that his opponent’s face, bearing, and confidence all displayed the signs of victory, and he knew himself outmatched. Having no choice, he debated and was defeated. The king rejoiced: ‘A man of great wisdom and brilliance has come from afar to my country!’ He wished to grant him a village.

諸臣議言：「一聰明人來，便封一邑，功臣不賞，但寵語論，恐非安國全家之道！今摩陀羅論議不如，應奪其封以與勝者；若更有勝人，復以與之。」王用其言，即奪與後人。

The ministers objected: ‘If every clever newcomer receives a village while meritorious ministers go unrewarded, we honor mere argument rather than secure the state and its households. Since Matuoluo has lost, his grant should go to the victor. If another defeats him, let it pass to that person.’ The king accepted their advice and transferred the grant.

是時摩陀羅語提舍言：「汝是聰明人，我以女妻汝，男兒相累；今欲遠出他國，以求本志。」提舍納其女為婦。

Matuoluo told Tiṣya, ‘You are an intelligent man. I give you my daughter in marriage and entrust my son to your care. I now wish to travel abroad to pursue my original aim.’ Tiṣya married his daughter.

The Names Upatiṣya and Śāriputra

Chinese Source

其婦懷妊，夢見一人，身被甲冑，手執金剛，摧破諸山，而在大山邊立。覺已白其夫言，我夢如是。提舍言：「汝當生男，摧伏一切諸論議師，唯不勝一人，當與作弟子。」

English Translation

During her pregnancy, she dreamed of a man wearing armor and holding a vajra, who shattered mountains and stood beside a great mountain. On awakening she told her husband. Tiṣya said, ‘You will bear a son who will defeat all debaters except one. He will become that person’s disciple.’

舍利懷妊，以其子故，母亦聰明，大能論議。其弟拘郗羅，與姊談論，每屈不如；知所懷子，必大智慧，未生如是，何況出生！即捨家學問，至南天竺，不剪指爪，讀十八種經書，皆令通利，是故時人號為長爪梵志。

Because of the child she carried, Śāri herself became brilliant and skilled in debate. Her brother Kauṣṭhila repeatedly lost arguments with her. Realizing that the unborn child must possess great wisdom—already so powerful before birth, and surely more so afterward—he left home to study in southern India. He did not trim his nails while mastering the eighteen scriptures, so people called him the brahmin Long Nails.

姊子既生，七日之後，裹以白疊，以示其父。其父思惟：「我名提舍，逐我名字，字為憂波提舍。」（憂波，秦言逐；提舍，星名）是為父母作字。

Seven days after his sister’s son was born, the baby was wrapped in white cloth and shown to his father. His father thought, ‘My name is Tiṣya. Following my name, I shall call him Upatiṣya.’ (Upa means ‘following’; Tiṣya is the name of a star.) This was the name given by his parents.

眾人以其舍利所生，皆共名之為舍利弗（弗秦言子）。

Because he was born of Śāri, people called him Śāriputra. (Putra means ‘son.’)

復次，舍利弗世世本願，於釋迦文尼佛所作智慧第一弟子，字舍利弗。是為本願因緣名字，以是故名舍利弗。

Furthermore, life after life he had vowed to become Śākyamuni Buddha’s foremost disciple in wisdom, named Śāriputra. His name thus also arose through the causes and conditions of his original vow.

問曰：若爾者，何以不言「憂波提舍」，而但言「舍利弗」？答曰：時人貴重其母，於眾女人中聰明第一，以是因緣故稱舍利弗。

Question: If so, why call him Śāriputra rather than Upatiṣya? Answer: People held his mother in high regard as the most intelligent of women. Therefore, they called him Śāriputra.

Knowing All Dharmas in Every Aspect

Chinese Source

〖經〗「菩薩摩訶薩欲以一切種知一切法，當習行般若波羅蜜。」

English Translation

Sutra: ‘A bodhisattva mahāsattva who wishes to know all dharmas in every aspect should train in and practice Prajñāpāramitā.’

〖論〗菩薩摩訶薩義，如先〈讚菩薩品〉中說。問曰：云何名「一切種」？云何名「一切法」？

Treatise: The meaning of bodhisattva mahāsattva was explained earlier in the section praising bodhisattvas. Question: What is meant by ‘every aspect’? What is meant by ‘all dharmas’?
