# Mahaprajnaparamita Sastra Translation Notes

These notes record recurring translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the Mahaprajnaparamita Sastra.

## 秦言

Do not translate 「秦言」 literally as "in Qin." In Kumarajiva's Chinese translation context, this phrase usually introduces the Chinese meaning of an Indic name, term, or expression. For English readers, the important point is the meaning, not the historical label "Qin."

Guidelines:

- For personal names, use `whose name means ...`.
- For technical terms, objects, or general expressions, use `which means ...` or `meaning ...`.
- When the English sentence reads more naturally without an explicit marker, integrate the meaning directly and omit a literal rendering of 「秦言」.

Example:

Chinese source:

> 憍梵波提秦言牛呞

Preferred translation:

> Gavampati, whose name means "Ox-Chewing"
