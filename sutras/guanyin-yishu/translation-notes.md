# The Commentary on the Meaning of Guanyin — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guanyin yishu* (觀音義疏, Taishō T34, no. 1728 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1728, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 感應 | appeal and response | The capacity of beings and the bodhisattva's answer. |
| 七難 | the seven calamities | Fire, water, rākṣasas, swords, ghosts, shackles, and bandits. |
| 三十三身 | the thirty-three bodies | The forms in which Avalokiteśvara teaches. |
| 觀解 | explanation by contemplation | Reading each phrase as the practitioner's own mind. |
