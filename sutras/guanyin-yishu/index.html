The Commentary on the Meaning of Guanyin.

Where the Profound Meaning of Guanyin explains the Universal Gate chapter of the Lotus Sūtra through five profound meanings, this companion work by Zhiyi (智顗, 538–597), recorded by Guanding (灌頂), goes through the chapter phrase by phrase: Akṣayamati's question, the seven calamities, the three poisons, the two wishes, the thirty-three bodies, and the gift of the necklace. Most phrases are read twice — once as the text says, once as a contemplation of the practitioner's own mind.

The Commentary on the Meaning of Guanyin (T1728) records Zhiyi's phrase-by-phrase lectures on the Universal Gate chapter of the Lotus Sūtra, written down by his disciple Guanding; it is the companion of The Profound Meaning of Guanyin, which treats the same chapter through the five profound meanings.
