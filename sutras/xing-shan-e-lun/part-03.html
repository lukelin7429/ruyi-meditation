The Fruition Ground and the Dharma-Gate of Inherent Evil

This part opens Fascicle Three with the sixth of Chuandeng's eight gates, "the fruition ground perfectly realized, with not one thing altered." When a buddha attains the fruit, the buddha-realm manifestly arises and the nine other realms lie hidden, but nothing included in the nature is removed. Chuandeng proves this in five points, citing the Śūraṅgama Sūtra: the tathāgatagarbha is identical to every realm from the six ordinary realms up to buddhahood; it is empty, existent, and both, as the three virtues; within it one and many contain each other; and Avalokiteśvara's thirty-two responding bodies come forth from the ten realms included in what he has realized. The fifth point turns to the "dharma-gates" of inherent good and inherent evil: good is the constant way by which buddhas teach, and evil is the rare variation used when good is not enough. Chuandeng then begins a series of accounts arranged by the six identities. Six accounts of the Buddha himself (subduing Māra, the courtesan Miaoyi, the Wilderness spirit, the drunken elephant, King Xianyu, the guide Great Compassion) illustrate the ultimate; Devadatta, Vimalakīrti, and Mañjuśrī illustrate "a portion of the truth." The part ends inside a long passage of the Jeweled Casket Sūtra on what is poison and what is the Buddha-dharma.

Ru-Yi Translation

Composed by Chuandeng, a śramaṇa of Youxi on Mount Tiantai

Chinese Source

客曰：因心本具，毫無虧欠，既領旨矣。果地圓證，一無所改，其義云何？余對曰：前門約眾生心法，在因本具，明毫無虧欠。雖與真如之體對論無虧，正欲與如來果位對論無差。今此門中，亦雖與真如不變對論不改，正欲與心生對論無差。蓋眾生心法，根塵念起，既具百界千如，三千性相，即真俗中。是故圓修行人之所修者，照此不思議境也。至於果位圓證者，證此不思議境也。故荊谿大師云：三千未顯，同名無明。三千既顯，同稱常樂。四明尊者云：又復應知，六即之義，不專在佛。一切假實，三乘人天，下至蛣𧏙，地獄色心，皆須六即，辨其初後。所謂理蛣𧏙名字，乃至究竟蛣𧏙。以論十界，皆理性故，無非法界，一不可改。故名字去，不惟顯佛，九亦同彰。至於果成，十皆究竟。

Ru-Yi Translation

The guest said: I have received the purport of "the causal mind originally including [all], lacking not a hair." What is the meaning of "the fruition ground perfectly realized, with not one thing altered"? I replied: The previous gate, in terms of the mind-dharma of beings, made clear that in the cause it originally includes [all], lacking not a hair. Although it was discussed in relation to the essence of true suchness as lacking nothing, the intention was precisely to discuss it in relation to the Tathāgata's stage of fruition as without difference. In this present gate, too, although it is discussed in relation to unchanging true suchness as unaltered, the intention is precisely to discuss it in relation to the mind and beings as without difference. For the mind-dharma of beings, when faculty and object [meet] and a thought arises, already includes the hundred realms and thousand suchnesses, the three thousand realms of nature-and-characteristics, which are identical to the real, the conventional, and the middle. Therefore what a practitioner of perfect cultivation cultivates is the illumination of this inconceivable object; and perfect realization at the stage of fruition is the realization of this inconceivable object. Hence the Great Master Jingxi said: "When the three thousand are not yet manifest, they are alike called ignorance; when the three thousand are manifest, they are alike called permanence and bliss." The Venerable Siming said: "Further, you should know that the meaning of the six identities does not lie exclusively in the Buddha. All provisional and real [beings], the three vehicles, humans and gods, down to the form and mind of the dung beetle and the hell [being], must all be distinguished by the six identities from first to last — namely, the dung beetle in principle, in name, up to the ultimate dung beetle. Because the ten realms are all discussed as the nature of principle, none is not the dharma-realm, and not one can be changed; hence from [identity in] name onward, not only is the Buddha revealed, but the nine are equally manifested. When it comes to the accomplishment of the fruit, all ten are ultimate."

Chinese Source

正以十界真如不變之體，而能隨緣。故使隨緣，造成十界，其體不變。今論佛果，則隨淨緣，造成佛法界。而真如性中，所具十種法界，與夫三諦，悉皆不變。但佛界現起，九界冥伏，謂之為佛。其實所具九界冥伏者，毫無所改。今為廣引經文，明證非虗。凡為五意：一證成果地圓證十界之性，二證成果地圓證三諦之體，三證成果地圓起一多之用，四證成果地圓起十界之用，五證成果地妙用之事。

Ru-Yi Translation

It is precisely because the unchanging essence of the true suchness of the ten realms can follow conditions that, when it follows conditions and creates the ten realms, its essence does not change. Now, in discussing the fruit of buddhahood: it follows pure conditions and creates the buddha dharma-realm, while the ten kinds of dharma-realm included in the nature of true suchness, together with the three truths, all remain unchanged. Only, the buddha-realm manifestly arises and the nine realms lie hidden, and this is called buddha. In reality the nine realms that are included and lie hidden are not altered in the least. Now, to cite sūtra passages broadly and prove clearly that this is not empty [talk], there are five points in all: first, proving that the fruition ground perfectly realizes the nature of the ten realms; second, proving that the fruition ground perfectly realizes the essence of the three truths; third, proving that the fruition ground perfectly gives rise to the function of the one and the many; fourth, proving that the fruition ground perfectly gives rise to the function of the ten realms; fifth, proving the matter of the wondrous function of the fruition ground.

一、證成果地圓證十界之性者

Ru-Yi Translation

First, Proving That the Fruition Ground Perfectly Realizes the Nature of the Ten Realms

Chinese Source

若廣引證諸餘大乘經典，說圓證十界，其文至多，今且近引大眾目前易見之經。如大佛頂經如來彰自證中云：我以不滅不生合如來藏，而如來藏元明心妙，即心即空，即地水火風，即眼耳鼻舌身意，即色聲香味觸法，即眼識界，如是乃至即意識界，此則如來至於果位，本具六凡法界，不失自性也。又云：即明無明，明無明盡，如是乃至即老即死，即老死盡，即苦即集，即滅即道，即智即得，此則如來至於極果，本具二乘法界，不失自性也。又云：即檀那，即尸羅，即毗黎耶，即羼提，即禪那，即般剌若，即波羅蜜多，此則如來至於果位，本具菩薩法界，不失自性也。如是乃至即怛闥阿竭，即阿羅訶三耶三菩，即大涅槃，即常即樂，即我即淨，此則如來至於果位，本具佛界，方得究竟也。以此而觀，豈非果地圓證十界之性乎？

Ru-Yi Translation

If I were to cite as proof all the other Mahāyāna sūtras that speak of perfectly realizing the ten realms, the passages would be very many; for now I will just cite, near at hand, a sūtra that the assembly can easily see before its eyes. For example, the Great Buddha's Crown Sūtra, where the Tathāgata displays his own realization, says: "I, by means of what neither perishes nor arises, unite with the tathāgatagarbha; and the tathāgatagarbha, the original bright mind, wondrous, is identical to mind, identical to space, identical to earth, water, fire, and wind, identical to the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind, identical to form, sound, smell, taste, touch, and dharmas, identical to the eye-consciousness element, and so on up to identical to the mind-consciousness element." This is the Tathāgata, having reached the stage of fruition, originally including the dharma-realms of the six ordinary [realms] without losing their own nature. It also says: "Identical to illumination and to ignorance, to the exhaustion of illumination and of ignorance, and so on up to identical to old age and death, identical to the exhaustion of old age and death; identical to suffering, identical to its origin, identical to cessation, identical to the path; identical to wisdom, identical to attainment." This is the Tathāgata, having reached the ultimate fruit, originally including the dharma-realms of the two vehicles without losing their own nature. It also says: "Identical to dāna, identical to śīla, identical to vīrya, identical to kṣānti, identical to dhyāna, identical to prajñā, identical to pāramitā." This is the Tathāgata, having reached the stage of fruition, originally including the bodhisattva dharma-realm without losing its own nature. "And so on up to identical to the tathāgata, identical to the arhat samyaksaṃbuddha, identical to great nirvāṇa, identical to permanence, identical to bliss, identical to self, identical to purity." This is the Tathāgata, having reached the stage of fruition, originally including the buddha-realm, and only then attaining the ultimate. Seen in this way, is this not the fruition ground perfectly realizing the nature of the ten realms?

二、證成果地，圓證三諦之體者

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, Proving That the Fruition Ground Perfectly Realizes the Essence of the Three Truths

Chinese Source

而如來藏妙明元心，十界俱非，正以非則遣情，纖塵而不立，即十法界皆空，般若德也。又云：而如來藏元明心妙，十界俱即，正以即則立法，萬象森然，即十法界皆有，解脫德也。又云：而如來藏妙明心元，十界俱離即離非，是即非即，正以離即俱遣，二邊不存，非即俱是，真俗宛然，法身德也。而此三諦三德，皆不離十種法界而辯，豈非因具十界三諦，修用十界三觀，果證十界三德乎？

Ru-Yi Translation

"And the tathāgatagarbha, the wondrous bright original mind," is none of the ten realms. Precisely because negation dispels [deluded] feelings, not a fine speck of dust is set up: the ten dharma-realms are all empty. This is the virtue of prajñā. It also says: "And the tathāgatagarbha, the original bright mind, wondrous," is identical to all ten realms. Precisely because identity establishes dharmas, the myriad phenomena stand in array: the ten dharma-realms all exist. This is the virtue of liberation. It also says: "And the tathāgatagarbha, the source of the wondrous bright mind," is free of both the identity and the negation of the ten realms, and is both identical and not identical. Precisely because freedom from identity dispels both, the two extremes do not remain; because negation and identity are both affirmed, the real and the conventional are distinct and clear. This is the virtue of the Dharma-body. And these three truths and three virtues are all discussed without departing from the ten kinds of dharma-realm. Is this not the cause including the three truths of the ten realms, cultivation employing the three contemplations of the ten realms, and the fruit realizing the three virtues of the ten realms?

三、證成果地圓起一多之用者

Ru-Yi Translation

Third, Proving That the Fruition Ground Perfectly Gives Rise to the Function of the One and the Many

Chinese Source

經云：我以不滅不生，合如來藏。而如來藏，惟妙覺明，圓照法界。是故於中，一為無量，無量為一。小中現大，大中現小。於一毫端，現寶王剎。坐微塵裏，轉大法輪。此正如來藏中，性具十界。依報一塵，正報一毛，一色一心，無非真如不變之體。比隨淨緣，而為依正色心之時，一毛一塵，無非法界。今稱性圓證，故稱性圓現。一即法界，乃無量之一。無量亦法界，乃一之無量。以至小亦法界，大亦法界。一毛之正報亦法界，一塵之依報亦法界。一毛一塵，法界不小。無量剎海，法界不大。不大不小，故能毛剎相容，一多自在。正類漚海器空，波濕氷水。餘之六喻，亦復如是。故發真如妙覺明性，本地風光，了非奇特也。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: "I, by means of what neither perishes nor arises, unite with the tathāgatagarbha; and the tathāgatagarbha, being solely wondrous aware brightness, perfectly illumines the dharma-realm. Therefore within it, one becomes immeasurable and the immeasurable becomes one; within the small the large appears, and within the large the small appears; on the tip of a single hair the land of a jewel king appears; sitting within a mote of dust, one turns the great Dharma wheel." This is precisely the inherent inclusion of the ten realms within the tathāgatagarbha. One mote of dust of the dependent reward, one hair of the principal reward, one form, one mind — none is not the essence of unchanging true suchness. When this follows pure conditions and becomes the form and mind of the dependent and principal [rewards], each hair and each mote of dust is none other than the dharma-realm. Now, because it is perfectly realized in accordance with the nature, it perfectly appears in accordance with the nature. The one is the dharma-realm: it is a one that is immeasurable. The immeasurable is also the dharma-realm: it is an immeasurable that is one. And so the small is also the dharma-realm, and the large is also the dharma-realm; the principal reward of one hair is also the dharma-realm, and the dependent reward of one mote of dust is also the dharma-realm. In one hair or one mote of dust, the dharma-realm is not small; in the immeasurable oceans of lands, the dharma-realm is not large. Because it is neither large nor small, hairs and lands can contain one another, and the one and the many are free. This is precisely like [the similes of] the bubble and the ocean, vessels and space, waves and wetness, ice and water; the remaining six similes are also like this. Hence the manifestation of the nature of wondrous aware brightness of true suchness, the scenery of the original ground, is not at all something extraordinary.

四、證成果地圓起十界之用者

Ru-Yi Translation

Fourth, Proving That the Fruition Ground Perfectly Gives Rise to the Function of the Ten Realms

Chinese Source

經敘觀音修耳根圓通，生滅既滅，寂滅現前，忽然超越世、出世間，十方圓明，獲二殊勝：一者、上合十方諸佛本妙覺心，與佛如來同一慈力；二者、下合十方六道眾生同一悲仰。夫上合十方諸佛，下合十方六道，豈非果位圓證十界之性歟？至於從體起用，則曰：由我供養觀音如來，蒙彼如來授我如幻，聞熏聞修金剛三昧，與佛如來同慈力故，令我身成三十二應，入諸國土；三十二應，不出十界。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra relates that Avalokiteśvara cultivated the perfect penetration of the ear faculty: "Arising and ceasing having ceased, quiescent extinction appeared before me. Suddenly I transcended the worldly and the world-transcending; the ten directions were perfectly bright, and I obtained two kinds of supremacy: first, above, I was united with the original wondrous aware mind of all the buddhas of the ten directions, sharing one and the same power of loving-kindness with the buddha-tathāgatas; second, below, I was united with all the beings of the six paths in the ten directions, sharing one and the same compassionate longing." Now, to be united above with all the buddhas of the ten directions and below with the six paths of the ten directions — is this not perfectly realizing the nature of the ten realms at the stage of fruition? As for giving rise to function from essence, it says: "Because I made offerings to the Tathāgata Avalokiteśvara, and that Tathāgata bestowed on me the illusion-like vajra samādhi of hearing-perfuming and hearing-cultivation, and because I share the same power of loving-kindness with the buddha-tathāgatas, I make my body accomplish thirty-two responses and enter all lands." The thirty-two responses do not go beyond the ten realms.

經云：若諸菩薩入三摩地，進修無漏，勝解現圓，我現佛身而為說法，令其解脫。此從所證本具佛法界而示現者也。經云：若諸有學寂靜妙明，勝解現圓，我於彼前現獨覺身而為說法，令其解脫；若諸有學斷十二緣，緣斷勝性，勝妙現圓，我於彼前現緣覺身而為說法，令其解脫。此從所證本具緣覺法界而示現者也。經云：若諸有學得四諦空，修道入滅，勝妙現圓，我於彼前現聲聞身而為說法，令其解脫。此從所證本具聲聞法界而示現者也。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: "If there are bodhisattvas who enter samādhi and advance in cultivating the outflow-free, and whose superior understanding appears perfectly, I appear in the body of a buddha and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to be liberated." This is manifesting from the buddha dharma-realm originally included in what is realized. The sūtra says: "If there are those still in training, still and quiet, wondrously bright, whose superior understanding appears perfectly, I appear before them in the body of a solitarily awakened one and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to be liberated. If there are those still in training who cut off the twelve conditions, and, the conditions being cut off, the superior nature, superior and wondrous, appears perfectly, I appear before them in the body of a pratyekabuddha and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to be liberated." This is manifesting from the pratyekabuddha dharma-realm originally included in what is realized. The sūtra says: "If there are those still in training who have attained the emptiness of the four truths, and, cultivating the path, enter cessation, and whose superior wondrousness appears perfectly, I appear before them in the body of a śrāvaka and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to be liberated." This is manifesting from the śrāvaka dharma-realm originally included in what is realized.

The sūtra says: "If there are beings whose minds of desire become clear and awakened, who do not violate the dust of desire, and whose minds of desire are pure, I appear before them in the body of a Brahmā king and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to be liberated." This is manifesting from the dharma-realm of the Brahmā heavenly king originally included in what is realized. The sūtra says: "If there are beings who wish their bodies to be free, roaming the ten directions, I appear before them in the body of the god Īśvara and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are beings who wish their bodies to be free, flying through space, I appear before them in the body of the god Maheśvara and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are beings who love to command ghosts and spirits and to save and protect the land, I appear before them in the body of a great heavenly general and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. These are all manifesting from the dharma-realm of gods originally included in what is realized.

This is manifesting from the most honored within the human dharma-realm in what is realized. The sūtra says: "If there are beings who love to be the heads of clans, deferred to by the world, I appear before them in the body of an elder and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are beings who love to discuss famous sayings and to dwell in purity, I appear before them in the body of a householder and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are beings who love to govern the land and to judge [the affairs of] states and towns, I appear before them in the body of an official and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are beings who love the arts of numbers and divination, and dwell in guarding and protecting themselves, I appear before them in the body of a brahmin and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are men who love to learn and leave home and uphold the precepts and rules, I appear before them in the body of a bhikṣu and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are women who love to learn and leave home and uphold the prohibitive precepts, I appear before them in the body of a bhikṣuṇī and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. If there are men who are happy to uphold the five precepts, I appear before them in the body of an upāsaka and teach the Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish it. Reading Notes for Part 3

Translator's notes

Gate six's name (unit 3): the gate is called 果地圓證，一無所改 here, where the list of eight gates in Part 1 has 果地融通; it is translated as written, "the fruition ground perfectly realized, with not one thing altered." 與心生對論 is read as "in relation to the mind and beings" (心、眾生 of the triad mind, buddha, beings).

Jingxi and Siming (unit 3): "When the three thousand are not yet manifest…" is found in Zhanran's Fahua xuanyi shiqian (T1717). "Further, you should know that the meaning of the six identities…" is Zhili's Miaozong chao (T1751); the English follows the round-two Miaozong chao p1, adjusted where Chuandeng abridges (一不可改 for 一一不改; the dung beetle "in name" without repeating the noun).

The Great Buddha's Crown Sūtra (units 5–11): 大佛頂經 is the Śūraṅgama Sūtra (T945). The "identical to mind, identical to space…" series is from fascicle 4; Chuandeng's three sentences in unit 7 ("is none of the ten realms… is identical to all ten… free of both") paraphrase the same passage, so only the sūtra's opening phrase is set in quotation marks. Unit 9 is also fascicle 4. Avalokiteśvara's perfect penetration and thirty-two responses (unit 11) are fascicle 6. The site has no Śūraṅgama translation; all is new. 覺 in 妙覺明, 本妙覺心 follows the Śūraṅgama rule of Part 2 (awareness).

Thirty-two responses (unit 11): the text has 勝解現圓 where T945 has 勝妙現圓 for the solitarily awakened; the English follows the text. 自在天／大自在天 are given as Īśvara and Maheśvara. 女主身，及國夫人命婦大家 is "a queen, or a lady of the realm, a titled lady, or a great matron."

Constant and variation (unit 13): 性善則常，性惡則變 is "inherent good is the constant and inherent evil is the variation"; 千常之一變 "one variation among a thousand constants."

Four sections (unit 14): the levels follow the glossary's six identities: "in the ultimate," "in a portion of the truth" (分真), "in resemblance," "in contemplative practice." The fourth section does not appear in Part 4, which passes from "resemblance" to gate seven.

Sources of the Buddha's accounts: subduing Māra — Guoqu xianzai yinguo jing (T189, fasc. 3), Za baozang jing (T203), Fo benxing jing (Fo benxing ji jing, T190, not checked line by line); the courtesan Miaoyi — Guanfo sanmei hai jing (T643, not checked line by line); 擎寶（〔香爐〕） keeps CBETA's supplied "censer" in parentheses. 億 is rendered "koṭi" throughout (unit 19 and Part 4).

Nirvāṇa Sūtra (units 24, 26, 28): the Wilderness spirit and the list of spirits (unit 24) follow the site's Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra English almost verbatim; Chuandeng numbers the list "fourth" to "ninth." The elephant (unit 26) and the opening of the Xianyu story (unit 28) are not on the site and are new. The second and third pairs of unit 28 follow the site's English, but in Chuandeng's order (the three thoughts come before the parent simile). 汝能、所問 is a corruption of 汝向所問 "what you asked just now."

Great Compassion the guide (unit 30): the text attributes this to the Nirvāṇa Sūtra, but it is not there; the story appears in Jinglü yixiang (T2121) and goes back to the Upāya-kauśalya literature (not checked). 𠒋人 is "this wicked man."

Devadatta (units 33–36): the past-life enmity (Sumati/Yajñadatta, given here in pinyin as Xumoti and Yeruoda because the Sanskrit is uncertain) and "the causes and conditions in this life" are close to Zhiyi's Textual Commentary on the "Devadatta" chapter (T1718), not checked line by line; 報思 read as 報恩; 十二韋陀 "all twelve Vedas" is literal. The hell visit is from the Dafangbian fo baoen jing (T156).

Lotus "Devadatta" chapter (units 38–45): the site's Lotus English is reused with "eon" changed to "eon" and 無生忍 changed to "acceptance of non-arising"; the verse is redistributed over Chuandeng's verse units. 推鐘 for 搥鍾.

Vimalakīrti (unit 48): the site's Vimalakīrti, chapter 4 (Jagatīṃdhara), is reused; 樂供養師 (site text 樂敬養師) is "making offerings to one's teacher." Chuandeng stops before Jagatīṃdhara's closing sentence.

Jeweled Casket Sūtra (units 50, 52): Dafangguang baoqie jing (T462), new English. Probable errors: 興大悲雲 "great clouds" (悲 not translated as "compassion"); 便法 for 便往; 次華 for 次第; 凡作己身 read as 化作; 我時不見魔王波旬，作是念言 is garbled and rendered "I did not see [this, but] the Māra king Pāpīyas thought"; 善讚法 rendered "well-praised Dharma" (probably 善說法); 有種波羅蜜 "[all] kinds of pāramitā." The list of "poisons" is very compressed and is rendered phrase by phrase.
