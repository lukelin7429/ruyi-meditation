The Seven Great Elements, the Eighth Gate, and Three Good Spiritual Friends

This part completes the seventh of Chuandeng's eight gates, "perfect cultivation following pure conditions: the whole of cultivation lies in the nature." He takes seven of the twenty-five sages of the Śūraṅgama Sūtra — Ucchuṣma (fire), Dharaṇiṃdhara (earth), the youth Moonlight (water), Lapis Lazuli Light (wind), Ākāśagarbha (space), Maitreya (consciousness), and Mahāsthāmaprāpta (the faculties) — quotes each one's account of his "perfect penetration," and comments that each began from a piece of cultivated evil (lust, rough ground, bodily fluids, restless movement) and contemplated it as identical to inherent evil. The comment on Mahāsthāmaprāpta joins the Śūraṅgama's "seeing sees seeing" to mindfulness of the Buddha and the three bodies. The eighth gate, "perfect realization: raising one, all are wholly gathered in," is treated briefly. The guest then asks why the nature must include all ten realms and why the name "inherent evil" is needed; Chuandeng gives five reasons and develops the fifth: bodhisattvas use this gate to save beings. The proof comes from the Vimalakīrti ("practicing the non-path"; "afflictions are the seed of the Tathāgata") and from three teachers of Sudhana in the Avataṃsaka: the courtesan Vasumitrā, the punishing King Insatiable, and the brahmin Jayoṣmāyatana, who leaps into fire. Fascicle Six begins inside this part.

The Seventh Gate: Fire, Earth, and Water

Chinese Source

第一、明依火大隨淨圓修全修在性者

Ru-Yi Translation

First, Making Clear Perfect Cultivation Following Pure [Conditions] Based on the Great Element of Fire, with the Whole of Cultivation Lying in the Nature

Chinese Source

經云：烏芻瑟摩於如來前，合掌頂禮佛之雙足，而白佛言：我常先憶久遠劫前，性多貪欲，有佛出世，名曰空王，說多淫人成猛火聚，教我徧觀百骸四肢諸冷煖氣，神光內凝，化多淫心成智慧火。從是諸佛皆呼召我，名為火頭。我以火光三昧力故，成阿羅漢，心發大願。諸佛成道，我為力士，親伏魔冤。佛問圓通，我以諦觀身心煖觸，無礙流道。諸漏既銷，生大寶燄，證無上覺，斯為第一。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: Ucchuṣma, before the Tathāgata, joined his palms, bowed his head at the Buddha's two feet, and said to the Buddha: "I constantly recall that, eons long ago, I was by nature much given to greed and desire. A buddha appeared in the world named King of Emptiness, who taught that people of much lust become a raging mass of fire, and taught me to contemplate everywhere the cold and warm energies throughout my hundred bones and four limbs. The spiritual light congealed within, and transformed the mind of much lust into the fire of wisdom. From then on all the buddhas called me by the name Fire-Head. By the power of the fire-light samādhi I became an arhat, and in my mind I made a great vow: when the buddhas attain the way, I will be their mighty warrior, personally subduing Māra's hostile hosts. The Buddha asks about perfect penetration: by attentively contemplating the warm contact of body and mind, flowing through without obstruction, all outflows were dissolved, a great jeweled flame arose, and I realized the unsurpassed awakening. This is foremost."

Chinese Source

釋曰：性多貪欲，九界修惡中之最下者，乃地獄之因。空王如來說多淫人成猛火聚者，示其華報有虗勞痰火尫瘵之疾，果報有地獄歷劫猛火燒然之苦也。教我遍觀百骸四肢諸冷煖氣者，正觀修惡之火為性惡之火也。神光內礙，化多淫心成智慧火者，乃觀之既久，化修惡之火為性惡之火。性惡融通，無法不趣，任運攝得佛界性善也。從是諸佛皆呼召我，名為火頭。我以火光三昧成阿羅漢，心發大願：諸佛成道，我為力士，親伏魔冤。正以從火大修惡得悟，以火光三昧而作佛事，行菩薩道，為性惡法門也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Explanation: "By nature much given to greed and desire" is the lowest within the cultivated evil of the nine realms; it is the cause of hell. "The Tathāgata King of Emptiness taught that people of much lust become a raging mass of fire": this shows that its flower retribution is the illness of consumptive exhaustion, phlegm-fire, and emaciating wasting, and its fruit retribution is the suffering of being burned by raging fire in hell through eons. "Taught me to contemplate everywhere the cold and warm energies throughout my hundred bones and four limbs": this is precisely contemplating the fire of cultivated evil as the fire of inherent evil. "The spiritual light congealed within, and transformed the mind of much lust into the fire of wisdom": having contemplated it for a long time, he transformed the fire of cultivated evil into the fire of inherent evil; inherent evil fuses and penetrates, so that there is no dharma that does not tend toward it, and it effortlessly takes in the inherent good of the buddha-realm. "From then on all the buddhas called me by the name Fire-Head. By the fire-light samādhi I became an arhat, and in my mind I made a great vow: when the buddhas attain the way, I will be their mighty warrior, personally subduing Māra's hostile hosts": precisely because he attained awakening from the cultivated evil of the great element of fire, and by the fire-light samādhi did the work of a buddha and practiced the bodhisattva path, this is the dharma-gate of inherent evil.

Chinese Source

良由無始從如來藏中，性火真空，性空真火，清淨本然，周遍法界三德，以為業、惑、苦三道。而此三道，即是三因。故今可觀修惡之火，即性惡之火。故曰：教我遍觀百骸四肢諸冷煖氣，神光內凝，化多淫心成智慧火。所謂隨緣不變，差而無差者是也。夫觀身心煖觸，無礙流通，體真而止也。遍觀百骸四肢，方便隨緣而止也。化多淫心成智慧火，恩二邊分別而止也。體真成而清淨般若之德復，方便成而本然解脫之德證，息二邊成而法身之德圓。豈非隨淨圓修，而全修在性乎？

Ru-Yi Translation

It is truly because, from beginningless time, from within the tathāgatagarbha, the three virtues — the fire of the nature is true emptiness, the emptiness of the nature is true fire, pure and originally so, pervading the dharma-realm — became the three paths of karma, delusion, and suffering. And these three paths are the three causes. Hence one can now contemplate the fire of cultivated evil as identical to the fire of inherent evil. Hence it says: "taught me to contemplate everywhere the cold and warm energies throughout my hundred bones and four limbs. The spiritual light congealed within, and transformed the mind of much lust into the fire of wisdom." This is what is called "following conditions yet unchanging: different yet without difference." Now, contemplating the warm contact of body and mind, flowing through without obstruction, is calming by embodying the real. Contemplating everywhere the hundred bones and four limbs is the calming of skillful means that follows conditions. Transforming the mind of much lust into the fire of wisdom is the calming that stills discrimination between the two extremes. When [the calming of] embodying the real is accomplished, the virtue of prajñā — "pure" — is restored; when [the calming of] skillful means is accomplished, the virtue of liberation — "originally so" — is realized; when [the calming that] stills the two extremes is accomplished, the virtue of the Dharma-body is perfected. Is this not perfect cultivation following pure [conditions], with the whole of cultivation lying in the nature?

第二、明依地大隨淨圓修全修在性者

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, Making Clear Perfect Cultivation Following Pure [Conditions] Based on the Great Element of Earth, with the Whole of Cultivation Lying in the Nature

Chinese Source

經云：持地菩薩即從座起，頂禮佛足而白佛言：我念往昔普光如來出現於世，我為比丘，常於一切要路津口、田地險隘，有不如法妨損車馬，我皆平填，或作橋梁，或負沙土，如是勤苦。經無量佛出現於世，或有眾生於闤闠處要人擎物，我先為擎，至其所詣，放物即行，不取其直。毗舍浮佛現在世時，世多饑荒，我為負人，無問遠近，惟取一錢。或有車馬被於泥溺，我有神力為其推輪，拔其苦惱。時國大王延佛設齊，我於爾時平地待佛，毗舍如來摩頂謂我：當平心地，則世界地一切皆平。我即心開，見身微塵與造世界所有微塵等無差別，微塵自性不相觸摩，乃至刀兵亦無所觸。我於法性悟無生忍，成阿羅漢，迴心今入菩薩位中，聞諸如來宣妙蓮華。佛知見地，我先證明而為上首。佛問圓通，我以諦觀身界二塵等無差別，本如來藏虗妄發塵，塵銷智圓，成無上道，斯為第一。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: The bodhisattva Dharaṇiṃdhara rose from his seat, bowed his head at the Buddha's feet, and said to the Buddha: "I recall that long ago, when the Tathāgata Universal Light appeared in the world, I was a monk. At all the main roads and ferry landings, and the narrow, dangerous places among the fields, wherever [the ground] was not as it should be and could damage carts and horses, I always leveled and filled it in; sometimes I built bridges, sometimes I carried sand and earth — laboring diligently like this. Through the appearance in the world of immeasurable buddhas, if there were beings in the marketplaces who needed someone to carry their goods, I would carry them first, all the way to where they were going, set the goods down and leave at once, without taking any payment. When the Buddha Viśvabhū was in the world, there was much famine in the world; I became a porter for people, regardless of distance, taking only one coin. If there were carts and horses stuck in the mud, I used my spiritual strength to push their wheels and pull them out of their trouble. At that time the great king of the country invited the Buddha and arranged a meal; I then leveled the ground to await the Buddha. The Tathāgata Viśvabhū stroked my head and said to me: 'You should level the ground of the mind; then all the ground of the world will be level.' My mind at once opened, and I saw that the motes of dust of my body and all the motes of dust that create the world are equal, without distinction; the self-nature of the motes of dust does not touch or rub together, and even swords and weapons have nothing to touch. In the dharma-nature I awakened to the acceptance of non-arising and became an arhat; having turned my mind, I now enter the ranks of bodhisattvas. When I heard the tathāgatas proclaim the Wondrous Lotus, the ground of the buddha's knowledge and vision, I was the first to verify it and was the leader. The Buddha asks about perfect penetration: by attentively contemplating the two kinds of dust, of the body and of the world, as equal without distinction — originally the tathāgatagarbha, falsely giving rise to dust — the dust dissolved and wisdom became perfect, and I accomplished the unsurpassed path. This is foremost."

Chinese Source

釋曰：我於爾時平地待佛者，此菩薩以平地為得悟之緣也。當平心地，則世界地一切皆平者，夫平地以身，平心以智。地為隨緣之幻色，心為性色，即真空隨緣。平地則以一念歷千差不變，平心則會千差歸一性。歷千差則萬念以紛馳，故為煩惱、為生死；歸一性則一道而坦然，故為菩提、為涅槃。是以平大地則心地反致於不平，此所謂無差而差也；平心地則世界地一切以皆平，此所謂差而無差也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Explanation: "I then leveled the ground to await the Buddha": this bodhisattva took leveling the ground as the condition for attaining awakening. "You should level the ground of the mind; then all the ground of the world will be level": now, the ground is leveled with the body, and the mind is leveled with wisdom. The ground is the illusory form that follows conditions; the mind is the form of the nature, that is, true emptiness following conditions. Leveling the ground is passing, with a single thought, through a thousand differences, unchanging; leveling the mind is bringing the thousand differences together and returning them to the one nature. Passing through a thousand differences, myriad thoughts run off in confusion, and so there are afflictions and birth-and-death; returning to the one nature, the single way is smooth and level, and so there are bodhi and nirvāṇa. Therefore leveling the great earth instead leaves the ground of the mind unlevel — this is what is called "without difference yet different"; leveling the ground of the mind makes all the ground of the world level — this is what is called "different yet without difference."

Chinese Source

故觀之未幾，我即心開，見身微塵與造世界所有微塵等無差別。此則豈惟造世界一切皆平，即依正二報之地大，亦等無差別，一切皆平也。苟非全性惡之性色不變，隨緣無差而差，以為九界依正之色，焉能身塵依色等無差別，不相觸摩，乃至刀兵亦無所觸，隨緣不變，差而無差哉？正以能達修惡之色即性惡之色，性惡融通，無法不趣，任運攝得佛界性善，故能塵銷智圓，成無上道也。以三止言之，所謂相在塵域，如器中鍠，聲出於外，禪那法身以為之主，雙遮雙照，三諦俱觀者也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Hence, before he had contemplated it long, "my mind at once opened, and I saw that the motes of dust of my body and all the motes of dust that create the world are equal, without distinction." Here not only is all [the ground] of the world that is created level; the great element of earth of the two rewards, dependent and principal, is also equal without distinction and all level. If it were not that the form of the nature, wholly of inherent evil, is unchanging, and following conditions is without difference yet different, becoming the form of the dependent and principal [rewards] of the nine realms, how could the dust of the body and the form of the dependent [reward] be equal without distinction, "not touching or rubbing together, and even swords and weapons have nothing to touch" — following conditions yet unchanging, different yet without difference? Precisely because he could penetrate that the form of cultivated evil is identical to the form of inherent evil — inherent evil fuses and penetrates, so that there is no dharma that does not tend toward it, and it effortlessly takes in the inherent good of the buddha-realm — he could "dissolve the dust, perfect wisdom, and accomplish the unsurpassed path." Speaking in terms of the three calmings, this is what is called "the marks are in the realm of dust, like the ringing within a vessel, whose sound goes forth outside": dhyāna, the Dharma-body, is the master, both negating and both illuminating, contemplating the three truths together.

第三、明依水大隨淨圓修全修在性者

Ru-Yi Translation

Third, Making Clear Perfect Cultivation Following Pure [Conditions] Based on the Great Element of Water, with the Whole of Cultivation Lying in the Nature

Chinese Source

經云：月光童子即從座起，頂禮佛足，而白佛言：我憶往昔恒河沙劫，有佛出世，名為水天。教諸菩薩修習水觀，入三摩地。觀於身中，水性無奪。初從涕唾，如是窮盡津液精血，大小便利。身中旋復，水性一同。見水身中與世界外浮幢王剎諸香水海等無差別。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: The youth Moonlight rose from his seat, bowed his head at the Buddha's feet, and said to the Buddha: "I recall that long ago, eons as many as the sands of the Ganges, a buddha appeared in the world named Water-Deva, who taught the bodhisattvas to cultivate the contemplation of water and enter samādhi. Contemplating within the body, the nature of water is without conflict. Beginning with tears and spittle, and so on to the end through saliva, essence, blood, urine and excrement, circulating and returning within the body, the nature of water is one and the same. I saw that the water within the body and the fragrant water oceans of the Floating Banner King lands beyond the world are equal without distinction. At that time I had just completed this contemplation; I saw only the water and had not yet attained being without a body.

Chinese Source

我於是時初成此觀，但見其水，未得無身。當為比丘，室中安禪。我有弟子窺窓觀室，惟見清水徧在室中，了無所見。童稚無知，取一瓦礫投於水內，激水作聲，顧盻而去。我出定後，頓覺心痛，如舍利弗遭違害鬼。我自思惟：今我已得阿羅漢道，久離病緣，云何今日忽生心痛，將無退失？爾時童子捷來我前，說如上事。我則告言：汝更見水，可即開門，入此水中，除去瓦礫。童子奉教。後入定時，還復見水，瓦礫宛然，開門除出。我後出定，身質如初，逢無量佛。如是至於山海自在通王如來，方得己身，與十方界諸香水海性合真空，無二無別。今於如來得童真名，預菩薩會。佛問圓通，我以水性一味流通，得無生忍，圓滿菩提，斯為第一。

Ru-Yi Translation

"I was then a monk, sitting in meditation in my room. I had a disciple who peeped through the window and looked into the room; he saw only clear water filling the room and nothing else at all. Being a young child without understanding, he took a piece of tile and threw it into the water; it struck the water with a sound, and he looked around and left. After I came out of samādhi, I suddenly felt pain in my heart, like Śāriputra when he was struck by the malevolent ghost. I thought to myself: 'Now I have already attained the path of an arhat and have long been free of the conditions of illness. How is it that today pain suddenly arises in my heart? Could I be regressing?' At that time the child came quickly before me and told me what had happened above. I then told him: 'When you see the water again, you may open the door at once, go into the water, and remove the tile.' The child received the instruction. Later, when I entered samādhi, he again saw the water, with the tile plainly there; he opened the door and took it out. Afterward, when I came out of samādhi, my body was as before. I met immeasurable buddhas, and so on until the Tathāgata Sovereign King Penetrating Mountains and Seas; only then did I [lose] my own body, and with the fragrant water oceans of the worlds of the ten directions my nature merged with true emptiness, without two and without distinction. Now, under the Tathāgata, I have obtained the name 'pure youth' and joined the assembly of bodhisattvas. The Buddha asks about perfect penetration: through the nature of water, of one flavor, flowing through, I attained the acceptance of non-arising and perfected bodhi. This is foremost."

Chinese Source

釋曰：觀於身中水性無奪者，悟性惡之水不二法門也。初從涕唾，如是窮盡津液精血大小便利，身中旋復水性一同，觀正報之水修惡即性惡也。見水身中與世界外浮幢王剎諸香水海等無差別者相似，證依報之水修惡即性惡也。未出分段故不得亡身，至於山海自在通王如來方得亡身，至分真變易方得與十方界諸香水海性合真空無二無別，豈非深證性水真空、性空真水清淨本然周遍法界乎？夫一自如來藏中真如不變，性水真空、性空真水清淨本然周遍法界，隨眾生心應所知量，循業發現無差而差，為九法界依正二報之水。苟非隨緣不變，焉能依正二水厥性無奪，觀之既久，能使性合真空無二無別乎？此以全性起修，用中道禪那，如器中鍠，聲出於外者為之主，故得水性一味流通，得無生忍也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Explanation: "Contemplating within the body, the nature of water is without conflict": this is awakening to the nondual dharma-gate of the water of inherent evil. "Beginning with tears and spittle, and so on to the end through saliva, essence, blood, urine and excrement, circulating and returning within the body, the nature of water is one and the same": this is contemplating that the cultivated evil of the water of the principal reward is identical to inherent evil. "I saw that the water within the body and the fragrant water oceans of the Floating Banner King lands beyond the world are equal without distinction": this is [identity in] resemblance, realizing that the cultivated evil of the water of the dependent reward is identical to inherent evil. Not yet having left segmented [birth-and-death], he could not lose his body; only at the Tathāgata Sovereign King Penetrating Mountains and Seas did he lose his body; only at [identity in] a portion of the truth, [in the birth-and-death] of change, could he, with the fragrant water oceans of the worlds of the ten directions, have his nature merge with true emptiness, without two and without distinction. Is this not deeply realizing that "the water of the nature is true emptiness, the emptiness of the nature is true water, pure and originally so, pervading the dharma-realm"? Now, from within the tathāgatagarbha, true suchness is unchanging — the water of the nature is true emptiness, the emptiness of the nature is true water, pure and originally so, pervading the dharma-realm — and "following the minds of beings, in accord with the measure of what they know, it appears according to karma," without difference yet different, becoming the water of the two rewards, dependent and principal, of the nine dharma-realms. If it were not following conditions yet unchanging, how could the two waters, dependent and principal, be without conflict in their nature, and, contemplated for a long time, be able to make the nature merge with true emptiness, without two and without distinction? This is raising cultivation from the whole of the nature, taking the dhyāna of the middle way — "like the ringing within a vessel, whose sound goes forth outside" — as the master; hence he attained "the nature of water, of one flavor, flowing through," and "attained the acceptance of non-arising."

第四、明依風大隨淨圓修全修在性者

Ru-Yi Translation

Fourth, Making Clear Perfect Cultivation Following Pure [Conditions] Based on the Great Element of Wind, with the Whole of Cultivation Lying in the Nature

Chinese Source

經云：琉璃光法王子即從座起，頂禮佛足而白佛言：我憧往昔經恒沙劫，有佛出世，名無量聲，開示菩薩本覺妙明。觀此世界及眾生身，皆是妄緣風力所轉。我於爾時觀界安立，觀世動時，觀身動止，觀心動念，諸動無二，等無差別。我時了覺此群動性，來無所從，去無所至，十方微塵顛倒眾生同一虗妄。如是乃至三千大千一世界內所有眾生，如一器中所有蚊蚋啾啾亂鳴，於分寸中鼓發狂閙。逢佛未幾，得無生忍。爾時心開，乃見東方不動佛國，為法王子，事十方佛，身心發光，洞徹無礙。佛問圓通，我以觀察風力無依，悟菩提心，入三摩地，合十方佛傳一妙心，斯為第一。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sūtra says: The Dharma-prince Lapis Lazuli Light rose from his seat, bowed his head at the Buddha's feet, and said to the Buddha: "I recall that long ago, eons as many as the Ganges' sands past, a buddha appeared in the world named Immeasurable Sound, who opened and showed the bodhisattvas the wondrous brightness of original awakening, [teaching them] to contemplate this world and the bodies of beings as all turned by the power of the wind of false conditions. At that time I contemplated how the world is set up, contemplated the times when the world moves, contemplated the body's moving and resting, and contemplated the mind's moving thoughts: all the movements are nondual, equal without distinction. I then understood that the nature of all these movements comes from nowhere and goes nowhere, and that the inverted beings of the ten directions, as many as motes of dust, are equally false. In this way, all the beings within one world of a great trichiliocosm are like mosquitoes and gnats in a single vessel, buzzing and whining in confusion, stirring up a mad racket within a space of a few inches. Not long after meeting the Buddha, I attained the acceptance of non-arising. At that time my mind opened, and I then saw the land of the Buddha Akṣobhya in the east, became a Dharma-prince, and served the buddhas of the ten directions; body and mind emitted light, penetrating clearly without obstruction. Reading Notes for Part 5

Translator's notes

Śūraṅgama passages (units 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19): the seven accounts of perfect penetration are from Shoulengyan jing fascicles 5–6 (T945); new English, using the seven-element vocabulary fixed in Part 2 (awareness for 覺, "the X of the nature is true emptiness"). The site has not translated these chapters. Copying slips are read from T945: 無礙流道 (unit 1) as 流通 "flowing through"; 神光內礙 (unit 2) as 內凝 "congealed within"; 方得己身 (unit 7) as 亡身 "lose my body," as Chuandeng himself quotes it in unit 8; 我憧往昔 (unit 10) as 我憶; 一專為憶 (unit 19) as 一人專為憶.

Three calmings (units 2, 5): Chuandeng maps the Śūraṅgama practices onto the three calmings of Tiantai (calming that embodies the real / the calming of skillful means that follows conditions / the calming that stills discrimination between the two extremes), wording as used elsewhere in this round. 恩二邊分別 is read as 息二邊分別. "The marks are in the realm of dust, like the ringing within a vessel, whose sound goes forth outside" is from the Perfect Awakening (T842, chapter of Weide Zizai) on dhyāna, not from the Śūraṅgama.

Two names for Dīpaṃkara (units 13, 16): 定光佛 (Ākāśagarbha) is "the Buddha Dīpaṃkara," 然燈佛 (Maitreya) "the Buddha Dīpaṃkara"; both are the same buddha.

Three natures (units 16–17): 圓成實／依他／遍計執 are rendered the perfectly accomplished / dependent / imagined [natures], the common usage in this round; 菴摩羅 is "amala" as in Part 2.

Unit 20 sources: the "seeing sees seeing" lines, "the wondrous road of bodhi," and "the pure true mark" are Śūraṅgama fascicle 2 (T945); "the awareness of the nature is wondrous brightness…," "falsely taking it to be bright awareness," and "because of brightness an object is set up" are fascicle 4.
