# Illuminating the Sacred Teaching — Translation Notes

These notes record recurring translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Xianyang shengjiao lun* (顯揚聖教論, Taishō T1602), composed by Asaṅga and translated into Chinese by Xuánzàng in twenty fascicles. They exist so that vocabulary stays consistent across all twenty fascicle pages, and matches the site's larger Yogācāra corpus — above all the *Treatise on the Stages of Yogic Practice* (瑜伽師地論), of which this treatise is Asaṅga's own digest, and the *Cheng Weishi Lun*, *Mahāyānasaṃgraha*, and *Abhidharmasamuccaya*.

## Title

- Chinese title: 顯揚聖教論, literally "treatise that displays and makes manifest the sacred teaching." We render it **Illuminating the Sacred Teaching**, using the Chinese alongside on first appearance per page.
- The Sanskrit is a modern reconstruction, not a surviving text: *Prakaraṇāryavācā-śāstra* (Nanjio), sometimes given as *Ārya-śāsana-prakaraṇa-śāstra*. Because no Sanskrit original survives and scholarly usage is unsettled, we do **not** use a Sanskrit title as the page title; it is noted once on the collection index page and otherwise left aside.
- Directory slug: `xianyang-shengjiao-lun`, following the site's existing precedent for texts whose Sanskrit is only a reconstruction (`cheng-weishi-lun`, `mohe-zhiguan`, `nanshan-lu`).
- One legacy rendering exists elsewhere on the site — `sutras/stages-of-yogic-practice/fascicle-088.html` calls it the "*Explanation of Illumination Treatise*" inside a reading note. That predates this project and should eventually be normalized to the title above.

## Source, and the relation between verse and prose

- Root text: Taishō T1602, 顯揚聖教論, Asaṅga, translated by Xuánzàng, 20 fascicles, 11 chapters.
- Verse companion: Taishō T1603, 顯揚聖教論頌, 1 fascicle. This is **the same author's same verses**, extracted from T1602 and circulated on their own. Collating the two confirms exactly which lines are Asaṅga's root verse and which are his own prose exposition — T1603 reproduces the verses verbatim, in order, with nothing added.
- **This treatise has no independent commentary by a second master.** Unlike the *Madhyāntavibhāga* (Maitreya's verses, Vasubandhu's prose) or the *Abhidharmasamuccaya* (with Sthiramati's T1606), here the verses and the prose are both Asaṅga's own. Reading notes on these pages therefore never claim a commentator's authority: they are the translator's notes, and where they draw on another text they say which one — most often the 瑜伽師地論, since Asaṅga states in his own opening verses that he is condensing it.
- This page translates directly from Xuánzàng's Chinese. Existing English scholarship may be consulted privately to check difficult passages, but the English wording, sentence structure, and section framing on this site must be Ru-Yi's own.

## Structure

Eleven chapters across twenty fascicles. The CBETA subdivisions (品之一, 之二, …) align exactly with fascicle boundaries, so pages are split one per fascicle, matching the site's *Stages of Yogic Practice* pages.

| Fascicles | Chapter |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 攝事品第一 Gathering the Topics |
| 5–13 | 攝淨義品第二 Gathering the Pure Meaning |
| 14 | 成善巧品第三 Establishing Skill; 成無常品第四 Establishing Impermanence |
| 15–16 | 成苦品第五 Suffering; 成空品第六 Emptiness |
| 16 | 成無性品第七 Naturelessness |
| 16–17 | 成現觀品第八 Direct Realization; 成瑜伽品第九 Yoga; 成不思議品第十 The Inconceivable |
| 17–20 | 攝勝決擇品第十一 Gathering the Superior Determinations |

Chapter one is organized by its own root verse into **nine topics** (九事): everything, the realms, defilement, the truths, the basis, the factors of enlightenment, persons, the fruits, the qualities. These topic boundaries do *not* line up with fascicle boundaries; each page states where it picks up.

## Core terminology

Renderings are matched to whatever is already dominant across the site's Yogācāra pages, even where an isolated page differs.

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 顯揚聖教論 | *Illuminating the Sacred Teaching* | "the treatise" thereafter within a page. |
| 攝事品 | Gathering the Topics | 攝 here = gathering/subsuming, as in 攝大乘論. |
| 九事 | the nine topics | 事 in this chapter means a topic of exposition, not "thing"; rendered "topic" throughout. |
| 界 (as topic two) | **the realms** | Careful: in this treatise's topic two, 界 = 三界 plus 三千世界 — realms and world-systems, *not* the eighteen dhātus. Where the same character means dhātu elsewhere, render "element(s)". |
