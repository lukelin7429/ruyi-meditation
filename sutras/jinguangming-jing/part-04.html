Fascicle Four: The Fish, the Tigress, and the Praise of the Buddha

This part gives the fourth and last fascicle of the Golden Light Sūtra (T0663, tr. Dharmakṣema), Chapters 16 to 19, followed by a Tang-period miracle tale printed with the sūtra. Chapter 16 finishes the answer to the bodhi-tree spirit's question of why ten thousand sons of the gods received predictions: the physician's son Jalavāhana, finding ten thousand fish dying in a dried-up pond, brings water on twenty borrowed elephants, feeds them, recites the name of the buddha Jewel Victory, and teaches them the twelve links of dependent origination; the fish are reborn in heaven and are now those ten thousand gods. Chapter 17 is the story of Prince Mahāsattva, who gives his body to a starving tigress about to eat her cubs; its relics are in a stūpa that rises from the earth, and the chapter retells the grief of the brothers, the queen, and the king at length in verse. Chapter 18 contains three hymns of praise, the last by the bodhi-tree spirit, who speaks of all dharmas as empty and longs to see the Buddha. Chapter 19 entrusts the sūtra to the assembly. The appended tale tells how Zhang Judao, summoned to the underworld by the animals he had slaughtered, was released after vowing to copy this sūtra.

Chapter 16: Jalavāhana, the Elder's Son

Chinese Source

金光明經流水長者子品第十六

Ru-Yi Translation

The Golden Light Sūtra, Chapter 16: Jalavāhana, the Elder's Son

Chinese Source

佛告樹神：「爾時流水長者子，於天自在光王國內，治一切眾生無量苦患已，令其身體平復如本，受諸快樂；以病除故多設福業，修行布施，尊重恭敬是長者子，作如是言：『善哉長者！能大增長福德之事，能益眾生無量壽命，汝今真是大醫之王，善治眾生無量重病，必是菩薩善解方藥。』

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha told the tree spirit: "At that time, when Jalavāhana, the elder's son, had cured the immeasurable sufferings and ailments of all beings in the country of King Heavenly Sovereign Light, and had made their bodies recover as before so that they received every happiness, [the people,] because their illnesses were removed, performed many works of merit, practiced giving, and honored and revered this elder's son, speaking thus: 'Excellent, elder! You are able greatly to increase works of blessing and virtue; you are able to extend the lives of beings without measure. You are now truly a great king of physicians, well able to cure the immeasurable grave illnesses of beings. You must surely be a bodhisattva who understands prescriptions and medicines well.'

Chinese Source

「善女天！時長者子，有妻名曰水空龍藏，而生二子：一名水空，二名水藏。時長者子將是二子，次第遊行城邑聚落，最後到一大空澤中，見諸虎狼狐犬鳥獸多食肉血，悉皆一向馳奔而去。時長者子作是念言：『是諸禽獸何因緣故一向馳走？我當隨後逐而觀之。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good goddess! At that time the elder's son had a wife named Water-Space Dragon-Treasury, who bore two sons: the first named Jalāmbara, the second named Jalagarbha. At that time the elder's son took these two sons and traveled in turn through the cities, towns, and settlements, and at last came to a great empty marsh. He saw tigers, wolves, foxes, dogs, birds, and beasts, many of them flesh- and blood-eaters, all running off in one direction. At that time the elder's son thought: 'For what cause and condition are these birds and beasts all running in one direction? I should follow after them and look.'

Chinese Source

「時長者子遂便隨逐，見有一池其水枯涸，於其池中多有諸魚，時長者子見是魚已生大悲心。時有樹神示現半身，作如是言：『善哉，善哉！大善男子。此魚可愍，汝可與水，是故號汝名為流水。復有二緣名為流水：一能流水，二能與水。汝今應當隨名定實。』時長者子問樹神言：『此魚頭數為有幾所？』樹神答言：『其數具足足滿十千。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the elder's son followed them and saw a pond whose water had dried up, and in that pond were many fish. When the elder's son saw these fish, he gave rise to a mind of great compassion. At that time there was a tree spirit who showed half her body and spoke thus: 'Excellent, excellent, great good man! These fish are pitiable; you can give them water. For this reason you are named Jalavāhana ("Flowing Water"). There are two further reasons you are named Flowing Water: first, you can make water flow; second, you can give water. You should now make your name come true.' At that time the elder's son asked the tree spirit: 'How many fish are there?' The tree spirit answered: 'Their number is fully ten thousand.'

Chinese Source

「善女天！爾時流水聞是數已，倍復增益生大悲心。善女天！時此空池為日所曝唯少水在，是十千魚將入死門，四向宛轉，見是長者心生恃賴，隨是長者所至方面，隨逐瞻視目未曾捨。是時長者馳趣四方，推求索水了不能得，便四顧望，見有大樹尋取枝葉，還到池上與作陰涼。作陰涼已，復更推求是池中水本從何來？即出四向周遍求覓莫知水處，復更疾走遠至餘處，見一大河名曰水生。爾時復有諸餘惡人，為捕此魚故，於上流懸險之處，決棄其水不令下過；然其決處懸險難補，計當修治經九十日，百千人功猶不能成，況我一身？

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good goddess! At that time Jalavāhana, hearing this number, doubly increased his mind of great compassion. Good goddess! At this time this empty pond was baked by the sun and only a little water remained; these ten thousand fish were about to enter the gate of death, writhing in all directions. Seeing this elder, their minds came to rely on him; in whatever direction this elder went, they followed him with their gaze and never took their eyes off him. At this time the elder ran in the four directions searching for water, but could not find any at all. Then he looked all around and saw a great tree; he took branches and leaves from it, went back to the pond, and made shade over it. Having made shade, he searched further: from where did the water of this pond originally come? He went out in the four directions, searching everywhere, but did not know where the water was. Further he ran quickly far off to other places and saw a great river named Water-Born. At that time there were also other wicked people who, in order to catch these fish, at a steep and dangerous place upstream had diverted the water so that it did not flow down; and the place they had breached was steep, dangerous, and hard to repair. He reckoned that repairing it would take ninety days, and the labor of a hundred thousand people still could not complete it — how much less could I alone?

Chinese Source

「時長者子，速疾還反至大王所，頭面禮拜却住一面，合掌向王說其因緣，作如是言：『我為大王國土人民治種種病，漸漸遊行至彼空澤，見有一池其水枯涸，有十千魚為日所曝，今日困厄將死不久。惟願大王，借二十大象令得負水濟彼魚命，如我與諸病人壽命。』爾時大王即勅大臣，速疾供給。爾時大臣奉王告勅，語是長者：『善哉大士！汝今自可至象厩中隨意選取，利益眾生令得快樂。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the elder's son quickly went back to the great king, bowed his head in homage, withdrew to stand to one side, joined his palms toward the king, explained the causes and conditions, and spoke thus: 'For the people of the great king's land I have been treating all kinds of illnesses, and traveling gradually I reached an empty marsh. I saw a pond whose water had dried up, where there are ten thousand fish baked by the sun; today they are in distress and will soon die. I only pray that the great king lend me twenty great elephants, so that they can carry water and save those fishes' lives, as I have given life to the sick.' At that time the great king at once commanded a great minister to supply them quickly. At that time the great minister, receiving the king's command, said to this elder: 'Excellent, great being! You yourself may now go to the elephant stables and choose as you wish, to benefit beings and give them happiness.'

Chinese Source

「是時流水及其二子，將二十大象，從治城人借索皮囊，疾至彼河上流決處，盛水象負，馳疾奔還至空澤池，從象背上下其囊水寫置池中，水遂彌滿還復如本。時長者子，於池四邊彷徉而行，是魚爾時亦復隨逐循岸而行。時長者子，復作是念：『是魚何緣隨我而行？是魚必為飢火所惱，復欲從我求索飲食，我今當與。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"At this time Jalavāhana and his two sons took twenty great elephants, borrowed leather bags from the city's workers, went quickly to the breached place upstream on that river, filled [the bags] with water for the elephants to carry, and hurried back to the pond in the empty marsh; they lowered the bags of water from the elephants' backs and poured the water into the pond, and the water then filled it as it had been before. At that time the elder's son walked back and forth around the four sides of the pond, and the fish at that time also followed him, moving along the bank. At that time the elder's son further thought: 'For what reason do these fish follow me? These fish must be tormented by the fire of hunger and want further to seek food and drink from me. I should now give it to them.'

Chinese Source

「善女天！爾時流水長者子，告其子言：『汝取一象最大力者，速至家中啟父長者：「家中所有可食之物，乃至父母飲噉之分，及以妻子奴婢之分，一切聚集悉載象上急速來還。」』爾時二子如父教勅，乘最大象往至家中，白其祖父說如上事。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good goddess! At that time Jalavāhana, the elder's son, told his sons: 'Take the strongest elephant, go quickly home, and say to my father, the elder: "Whatever food there is in the house — even the portions for [my] father and mother to eat and drink, and the portions for wife, children, and servants — gather it all, load it on the elephant, and come back with all speed."' At that time the two sons, as their father instructed, rode the largest elephant home and told their grandfather the matter as above.

Chinese Source

「爾時二子，收取家中可食之物，載象背上疾還父所至空澤池。時長者子見其子還心生歡喜踊躍無量，從子邊取飲食之物散著池中，與魚食已即自思惟：『我今已能與此魚食令其飽滿，未來之世當施法食。』復更思惟：『曾聞過去空閑之處有一比丘，讀誦大乘方等經典，其經中說，若有眾生臨命終時，得聞寶勝如來名號即生天上，我今當為是十千魚解說甚深十二因緣，亦當稱說寶勝佛名。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the two sons gathered the food in the house, loaded it on the elephant's back, and quickly returned to their father at the pond in the empty marsh. When the elder's son saw his sons return, he rejoiced, leaping with boundless joy; he took the food from his sons and scattered it in the pond. Having fed the fish, he thought: 'I have now been able to give these fish food and fill them; in future ages I shall give the food of the Dharma.' He further thought: 'I once heard that in the past, in a quiet place, there was a bhikṣu who read and recited a Great Vehicle Vaipulya scripture, and in that sūtra it said that if any being on the verge of death hears the name of Jewel Victory Tathāgata, he will at once be born in the heavens. I should now explain the very deep twelve links of dependent origination for these ten thousand fish, and also call out the name of Jewel Victory Buddha.'

Chinese Source

「時閻浮提中有二種人：一者深信大乘方等，二者毀呰不生信樂。時長者子作是思惟：『我今當入池水之中，為是諸魚說深妙法。』思惟是已，即便入水作如是言：『南無過去寶勝如來、應供、正遍知、明行足、善逝、世間解、無上士、調御丈夫、天人師、佛、世尊。寶勝如來本往昔時，行菩薩道作是誓願：「若有眾生，於十方界臨命終時聞我名者，當令是輩即命終已，尋得上生三十三天。」』爾時流水復為是魚，解說如是甚深妙法——所謂無明緣行，行緣識，識緣名色，名色緣六入，六入緣觸，觸緣受，受緣愛，愛緣取，取緣有，有緣生，生緣老死憂悲苦惱。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time in Jambudvīpa there were two kinds of people: first, those who deeply believed in the Great Vehicle Vaipulya; second, those who slandered it and did not give rise to faith and delight. At that time the elder's son thought: 'I should now enter the water of the pond and teach the deep and wondrous Dharma for these fish.' Having thought this, he at once entered the water and spoke thus: 'Homage to Jewel Victory Tathāgata of the past, Worthy of Offerings, Perfectly and Fully Awakened, Complete in Understanding and Conduct, Well-Gone, Knower of the World, Unsurpassed Being, Tamer of Those to be Tamed, Teacher of Gods and Humans, Buddha, World-Honored One. Jewel Victory Tathāgata, long ago in the past, when practicing the bodhisattva path, made this vow: "If any beings in the worlds of the ten directions hear my name on the verge of death, I will make these beings, as soon as their lives end, at once be reborn above in the Thirty-three Heavens."' At that time Jalavāhana further explained for these fish such a very deep and wondrous Dharma — namely: ignorance conditions volitional formations, volitional formations condition consciousness, consciousness conditions name-and-form, name-and-form conditions the six entrances, the six entrances condition contact, contact conditions feeling, feeling conditions craving, craving conditions grasping, grasping conditions becoming, becoming conditions birth, birth conditions old age, death, sorrow, grief, suffering, and affliction.

The Ten Thousand Fish Are Reborn in Heaven

Chinese Source

「善女天！爾時流水長者子及其二子，說是法已即共還家。是長者子復於後時，賓客聚會醉酒而臥。爾時其地卒大震動，時十千魚同日命終，既命終已生忉利天。既生天已作是思惟：『我等以何善業因緣，得生於此忉利天中？』復相謂言：『我等先於閻浮提內，墮畜生中受於魚身，流水長者子，與我等水及以飲食，復為我等解說甚深十二因緣，并稱寶勝如來名號，以是因緣令我等輩得生此天。是故我等今當往至長者子所報恩供養。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good goddess! At that time Jalavāhana, the elder's son, and his two sons, having taught this Dharma, returned home together. Later this elder's son, at a gathering of guests, became drunk and lay down. At that time the ground suddenly shook greatly, and the ten thousand fish died on the same day; having died, they were born in the Trāyastriṃśa Heaven. Having been born in heaven, they thought: 'By the causes and conditions of what wholesome karma have we been born here in the Trāyastriṃśa Heaven?' They further said to one another: 'Earlier, within Jambudvīpa, we fell among the animals and received the bodies of fish; Jalavāhana, the elder's son, gave us water and food, and further explained for us the very deep twelve links of dependent origination, and also called out the name of Jewel Victory Tathāgata. By these causes and conditions we have been born in this heaven. Therefore we should now go to the elder's son to repay his kindness and make offerings.'

Chinese Source

「爾時十千天子，從忉利天下閻浮提，至流水長者子大醫王家。時長者子在樓屋上露臥眠睡。是十千天子，以十千真珠天妙瓔珞置其頭邊，復以十千置其足邊，復以十千置右脇邊，復以十千置左脇邊；雨曼陀羅華、摩訶曼陀羅華，積至于膝；作種種天樂出妙音聲。閻浮提中，有睡眠者皆悉覺寤，流水長者子亦從睡寤。是十千天子，於上空中飛騰遊行，於天自在光王國內，處處皆雨天妙蓮華，是諸天子復至本處空澤池所復雨天華，便從此沒還忉利宮，隨意自在受天五欲。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time the ten thousand sons of the gods came down from the Trāyastriṃśa Heaven to Jambudvīpa, to the house of the great king of physicians, Jalavāhana, the elder's son. At that time the elder's son was asleep, lying in the open on the upper story of his house. These ten thousand sons of the gods placed ten thousand heavenly wondrous necklaces of true pearls by his head, a further ten thousand by his feet, a further ten thousand by his right side, and a further ten thousand by his left side; they rained māndārava flowers and great māndārava flowers, heaped up to his knees; and they made all kinds of heavenly music, sending forth wondrous sounds. Within Jambudvīpa all who were asleep awoke, and Jalavāhana, the elder's son, also awoke from sleep. These ten thousand sons of the gods flew and roamed in the sky above, and everywhere within the country of King Heavenly Sovereign Light they rained heavenly wondrous lotus flowers. These sons of the gods further went to their original place, the pond in the empty marsh, and again rained heavenly flowers; then they vanished from there and returned to the Trāyastriṃśa palace, enjoying the five heavenly desires freely as they wished.

Chinese Source

「時閻浮提過是夜已，天自在光王，問諸大臣：『昨夜何緣，示現如是淨妙瑞相有大光明？』大臣答言：『大王當知，忉利諸天於流水長者子家，雨四十千真珠瓔珞及不可計曼陀羅華。』王即告臣：『卿可往至彼長者家，善言誘喻喚令使來。』大臣受勅即至其家，宣王教令喚是長者。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time, when the night had passed in Jambudvīpa, King Heavenly Sovereign Light asked his great ministers: 'Last night for what reason were such pure and wondrous auspicious signs shown, with a great light?' The great ministers answered: 'Great king, you should know that the gods of the Trāyastriṃśa rained forty thousand necklaces of true pearls and incalculable māndārava flowers on the house of Jalavāhana, the elder's son.' The king at once told a minister: 'Go to that elder's house, persuade him with kind words, and summon him to come.' The great minister received the command, went to his house at once, proclaimed the king's order, and summoned this elder.

Chinese Source

「是時長者尋至王所。王問長者：『何緣示現如是瑞相？』長者子言：『我必定知是十千魚其命已終。』時大王言：『今可遣人審實是事。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"At this time the elder went at once to the king. The king asked the elder: 'For what reason were such auspicious signs shown?' The elder's son said: 'I know for certain that these ten thousand fish have died.' At that time the great king said: 'Now send someone to verify this matter.'

Chinese Source

「爾時流水，尋遣其子至彼池所，看是諸魚死活定實。爾時其子聞是語已，向於彼池既至池已，見其池中多有摩訶曼陀羅華，積聚成𧂐，其中諸魚悉皆命終。見已即還白其父言：『彼諸魚等悉已命終。』爾時流水知是事已，復至王所作如是言：『是十千魚悉皆命終。』王聞是已心生歡喜。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time Jalavāhana at once sent his son to that pond to see for certain whether the fish were dead or alive. At that time his son, having heard these words, went toward the pond; having reached the pond, he saw that in the pond there were many great māndārava flowers, heaped into a mound, and that the fish within had all died. Having seen it, he went back and told his father: 'Those fish have all died.' At that time Jalavāhana, knowing this, went again to the king and spoke thus: 'These ten thousand fish have all died.' Hearing this, the king rejoiced."

Chinese Source

爾時世尊，告道場菩提樹神：「善女天！欲知爾時流水長者子，今我身是；長子水空，今羅睺羅是；次子水藏，今阿難是；時十千魚者，今十千天子是。是故我今為其授阿耨多羅三藐三菩提記。爾時樹神現半身者，今汝身是。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the World-Honored One told the spirit of the bodhi tree at the site of awakening: "Good goddess! If you wish to know: Jalavāhana, the elder's son, at that time is now my own self; the elder son, Jalāmbara, is now Rāhula; the younger son, Jalagarbha, is now Ānanda; the ten thousand fish at that time are now the ten thousand sons of the gods. Therefore I now give them the prediction of unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening. The tree spirit who at that time showed half her body is now your own self."

Chapter 17: Giving Up the Body

Chinese Source

金光明經捨身品第十七

Ru-Yi Translation

The Golden Light Sūtra, Chapter 17: Giving Up the Body

Chinese Source

爾時道場菩提樹神復白佛言：「世尊！我聞世尊過去修行菩薩道時，具受無量百千苦行，捐捨身命肉血骨髓，惟願世尊，少說往昔苦行因緣，為利眾生受諸快樂。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the spirit of the bodhi tree at the site of awakening again said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I have heard that the World-Honored One, when practicing the bodhisattva path in the past, underwent immeasurable hundreds of thousands of ascetic practices in full, giving up his life, flesh, blood, bones, and marrow. I only pray that the World-Honored One tell briefly the causes and conditions of his ascetic practices of the past, to benefit beings and let them receive every happiness."

Chinese Source

爾時世尊即現神足，神足力故令此大地六種震動，於大講堂眾會之中，有七寶塔從地涌出，眾寶羅網彌覆其上。爾時大眾見是事已生希有心。爾時世尊，即從座起禮拜是塔，恭敬圍繞還就本座。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the World-Honored One at once displayed his spiritual powers. By the power of his spiritual powers he made this great earth shake in six ways, and in the midst of the assembly in the great lecture hall a stūpa of the seven jewels welled up out of the earth, covered over with nets of many jewels. At that time the great assembly, seeing this, gave rise to a sense of wonder. At that time the World-Honored One at once rose from his seat, bowed to this stūpa, reverently circled it, and returned to his own seat.

Chinese Source

爾時道場菩提樹神白佛言：「世尊！如來世雄出現於世，常為一切之所恭敬，於諸眾生最勝最尊，何因緣故禮拜是塔？」佛言：「善女天！我本修行菩薩道時，我身舍利安止是塔，因由是身令我早成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the spirit of the bodhi tree at the site of awakening said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! The Tathāgata, hero of the world, appearing in the world, is always revered by all and is the most excellent and most honored among all beings. For what cause and condition does he bow to this stūpa?" The Buddha said: "Good goddess! At this time, throughout the whole prefecture of Wenzhou, those who raised chickens, pigs, geese, and ducks for their meat all set them free; household after household gave up meat, person after person kept good thoughts, and no butcher's trade was set up. Even the neighboring prefectures and counties, hearing of this, all took up pure conduct — not just one household. If at present there are killings that show no proof [of retribution], it is because those beings' karma was full and they were due to die; therefore there is no retribution. They simply end their human life and again become animals, slaughtered by others. But if beings whose allotted days are not yet full are killed by people, they at once file suit, and people of the world die suddenly, or are sick and wasted for years and months, crying out in pain and talking wildly in their sleep: all these are the beings holding them to account, and only when the documents of the case are settled does their life end. All the many offenses are extinguished by repentance; only the killing of living things is not removed by repentance, because there are enemies who single-mindedly bring suit against one. Unless one at once makes sūtras and images for them — or if one was sent by others, or by circumstances could not stop the killing — one should feel shame and remorse and lament for them. Those who killed with the knife, cutting up flesh as if it were dried meat, or peddled it to others and took the price to enrich themselves, must all make one copy for each one, and clearly repent and chant, so that this merit may help their enemies to be born soon on the human path. Then the lawsuits will cease of themselves and no retribution will pursue them. Good men and women, take clear warning from this.

Reading Notes for Fascicle 4

Translator's notes

Jalavāhana's family (Chapter 16). The sons 水空 and 水藏 are given as Jalāmbara and Jalagarbha, the Sanskrit names whose meanings match the Chinese; Yijing's version (T0665) has 水滿 and 水藏 and identifies them differently (with Silver Banner and Silver Light). The wife 水空龍藏 is translated by meaning, "Water-Space Dragon-Treasury"; the river 水生 "Water-Born." In unit 5 the tree spirit explains the name 流水 ("Flowing Water"), so the meaning is added in parentheses once.

Twelve links (unit 11). The formula follows the site's Lotus Sūtra ("ignorance conditions volitional formations …"), except 六入, kept as "the six entrances" as fixed for this sūtra (the site's Lotus has "the six sense-bases").

Identifications (unit 17). Jalāmbara is now Rāhula and Jalagarbha Ānanda. The tree spirit is told she was the spirit who showed half her body in unit 5.

Names in Chapter 17. King 摩訶羅陀 Mahāratha; princes 摩訶波那羅 Mahāpraṇāda, 摩訶提婆 Mahādeva, 摩訶薩埵 Mahāsattva. In the verse (unit 42) the brothers appear as 大波那羅 and 大天, half-translated forms of the same names; given as Mahāpraṇāda and Mahādeva. Yijing's version calls the king 大車 and the first prince 摩訶波羅.

Rāhu (unit 29). 羅睺羅阿修羅王 is the asura who swallows the sun and moon (Rāhu); the Chinese uses the same spelling as the name of the Buddha's son Rāhula in unit 17, but the meaning differs.

Who they were (units 74–75). 輸頭檀 Śuddhodana; 摩耶 Māyā; 彌勒 Maitreya; 調達 Devadatta; 瞿夷 Gopī (one of the Buddha's wives in some accounts); 五比丘 the five bhikṣus; Śāriputra and Maudgalyāyana.

Unit 60–61. The CBETA line break falls inside the sentence 爾時亦有／無量諸人 ("At that time there were also / immeasurable people"); the English follows the split.

The queen's dream (unit 36 and verses). Breasts cut, teeth falling, three dove chicks of which a hawk takes one: the queen herself interprets it in unit 58.

Praises (Chapter 18). Several images match the praise of the Buddha in Chapter 6 (Part 2, units 33–35): teeth "like conch shell and snow" here (珂雪), the Buddha-sun, the moon in water. 迦陵頻伽 kalaviṅka and 優曇華 udumbara follow the site's Lotus. 長跪 "kneel upright" per BRIEF. 兩足世尊 "Two-Footed Honored One."

Tree spirit's verses (units 101–105). The spirit says the bodies of śrāvakas and the sphere of practice of the Buddha are "empty and quiescent" (空寂), and asks to see the Buddha; the Buddha's reply calls this "the Dharma-gate of non-arising" (無生法門).

Chapter 19 (units 109–112). The Taishō text has a nineteenth chapter, 囑累品 "Entrustment," whose heading CBETA marks as prose (unit 109); it is translated as a chapter heading. The other summaries of this sūtra speak of eighteen chapters; the Tiantai commentaries (Zhiyi's Profound Meaning and Textual Commentary) should be checked for whether they treat it.

Appendix (units 114–124). 金光明經懺悔滅罪傳, the tale of Zhang Judao 張居道, is printed after the sūtra in the Taishō edition; it is not part of the sūtra and is not by Dharmakṣema. Place names in pinyin: Wenzhou, Cangzhou, Jingcheng county, Weizhou, Chanji Monastery, Anguo county. 治中 "administrative aide"; 縣丞 "vice-magistrate"; 司命 "the Controller of Destinies"; 五道大神 "the great spirit of the five paths"; 司善 "the Office of Good." 懺唱 "repent and chant." Several phrases in units 116 and 123 are colloquial and obscure (e.g. 從來應其到時遂乃至不見, rendered "From the time [the copying] answered their arrival, they no longer appeared"); alternatively, "Whenever they came at their time, at last they no longer appeared."
