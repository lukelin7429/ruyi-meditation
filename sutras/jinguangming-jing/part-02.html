Fascicle Two: The Four Kings and the Three Goddesses

This part gives the second fascicle of the Golden Light Sūtra (T0663, tr. Dharmakṣema), four chapters in which the guardian deities of the world pledge to protect the sūtra, those who teach it, and the rulers who honor it. In Chapter 6 the Four Heavenly Kings promise that wherever the sūtra is taught they will guard the land from invasion, famine, and epidemic. The Buddha then describes how a king should receive the sūtra: humbly, on a low seat, going out on foot to meet the Dharma teacher and regarding him as the Buddha. The incense the king offers becomes a golden canopy over every heaven and every buddha-world. If a king neglects the sūtra, the gods leave his country and disaster follows. This chapter is the source of the sūtra's reputation in East Asia as a scripture for the protection of the state. Chapters 7 to 9 are short pledges by three goddesses: Sarasvatī gives eloquence and memory to the one teaching the Dharma; Śrī gives material provision, with a ritual of invoking her name and a dhāraṇī for increasing wealth; and Dṛḍhā the earth goddess hides beneath the Dharma seat and makes the earth fertile wherever the sūtra is heard. Zhili's Golden Light Repentance Rite draws its invocation of Śrī, its offering of food, and its list of buddhas and bodhisattvas from Chapter 8.

Chapter 6: The Four Heavenly Kings

Chinese Source

金光明經四天王品第六

Ru-Yi Translation

The Golden Light Sūtra, Chapter 6: The Four Heavenly Kings

Chinese Source

爾時毘沙門天王、提頭賴吒天王、毘留勒叉天王、毘留博叉天王，俱從座起偏袒右肩，右膝著地胡跪合掌，白佛言：「世尊！是金光明微妙經典眾經之王，諸佛世尊之所護念，莊嚴菩薩深妙功德，常為諸天之所恭敬，能令天王心生歡喜，亦為護世之所讚歎。此經能照諸天宮殿，是經能與眾生快樂，是經能令地獄、餓鬼、畜生諸河焦乾枯竭，是經能除一切怖畏，是經能却他方怨賊，是經能除穀貴饑饉，是經能愈一切疫病，是經能滅惡星變異，是經能除一切憂惱。舉要言之，是經能滅一切眾生無量無邊百千苦惱。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the heavenly king Vaiśravaṇa, the heavenly king Dhṛtarāṣṭra, the heavenly king Virūḍhaka, and the heavenly king Virūpākṣa together rose from their seats, bared their right shoulders, placed their right knees on the ground, knelt with one knee raised, joined their palms, and said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! This subtle and wondrous scripture, the Golden Light, king of sūtras, is held in mind and protected by the buddhas, World-Honored Ones; it adorns the deep and wondrous merit of the bodhisattvas; it is always revered by the gods; it can make the heavenly kings rejoice in their minds; and it is also praised by the world-protectors. This sūtra can illuminate the palaces of the gods; this sūtra can give happiness to beings; this sūtra can make the rivers of the hells, the hungry ghosts, and the animals scorch, dry up, and run out; this sūtra can remove all fears; this sūtra can drive back hostile invaders from other regions; this sūtra can remove the dearness of grain and famine; this sūtra can heal all epidemics; this sūtra can extinguish evil stars and strange portents; this sūtra can remove all worry and distress. To speak of the essentials, this sūtra can extinguish the immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of sufferings and distresses of all beings.

Chinese Source

「世尊！是金光明微妙經典，若在大眾廣宣說時，我等四王及餘眷屬，聞此甘露無上法味，增益身力心進勇銳具諸威德。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! When this subtle and wondrous scripture, the Golden Light, is widely proclaimed in a great assembly, we four kings and our retinues, hearing this unsurpassed Dharma-taste of sweet dew, increase our bodily strength; our minds advance, courageous and keen, and we become complete in all majestic virtue.

Chinese Source

「世尊！我等四王，能說正法修行正法，為世法王以法治世。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! We four kings are able to teach the true Dharma and cultivate the true Dharma; as Dharma-kings for the world, we govern the world by the Dharma.

Chinese Source

「世尊！我等四王及天、龍、鬼神、乾闥婆、阿脩羅、迦樓羅、緊那羅、摩睺羅伽，以法治世，遮諸惡鬼噉精氣者。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! We four kings, with the gods, dragons, ghosts and spirits, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, and mahoragas, govern the world by the Dharma and hold back the evil ghosts who devour vital essence.

Chinese Source

「世尊！我等四王二十八部諸鬼神等，及無量百千鬼神，以淨天眼過於人眼，常觀擁護此閻浮提。世尊！是故我等名護世王。若此國土有諸衰耗、怨賊侵境、饑饉疾疫種種艱難，若有比丘受持是經，我等四王當共勸請，令是比丘以我力故，疾往彼所國邑郡縣，廣宣流布是金光明微妙經典，令如是等種種百千衰耗之事悉皆滅盡。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! We four kings and the ghosts and spirits of the twenty-eight divisions, together with immeasurable hundreds of thousands of ghosts and spirits, with pure heavenly eyes surpassing human eyes, always watch over and protect this Jambudvīpa. World-Honored One! Therefore we are called world-protecting kings. If in this land there are declines and losses, hostile invaders crossing the borders, famine and epidemics, and all kinds of hardship, and if there is a bhikṣu who receives and upholds this sūtra, we four kings will together urge and request this bhikṣu, by our power, to go swiftly to those states, towns, commanderies, and counties, and widely proclaim and spread this subtle and wondrous scripture, the Golden Light, so that all such hundreds of thousands of declines and losses are entirely extinguished.

Chinese Source

「世尊！如諸國王所有土境，是持經者若至其國，是王應當往是人所，聽受如是微妙經典，聞已歡喜，復當護念恭敬是人。世尊！我等四王，復當勤心擁護是王及國人民，為除衰患令得安隱。

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! As for the territories ruled by the kings of the nations, if one who upholds this sūtra comes to their country, the king should go to where this person is and listen to and receive such a subtle and wondrous scripture; having heard it, he rejoices, and further he should hold this person in mind, protect, and revere him. World-Honored One! We four kings will further diligently guard this king and the people of his country, remove their declines and troubles, and let them be at peace and secure.

Chinese Source

「世尊！若有比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷受持是經，若諸人王有能供給施其所安，我等四王，亦當令是王及國人民一切安隱具足無患。世尊！若有四眾受持讀誦是妙經典，若諸人王有能供養恭敬尊重讚歎；我等四王，亦復當令如是人王，於諸王中常得第一供養恭敬尊重讚歎，亦令餘王欽尚羨慕稱讚其善。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"World-Honored One! If there are bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās who receive and uphold this sūtra, and if kings of humans can provide for them and give them what keeps them at ease, we four kings will also make this king and the people of his country all at peace and secure, complete and free of trouble. World-Honored One! If the fourfold assembly receives, upholds, reads, and recites this wondrous scripture, and if kings of humans can make offerings to them, revere, honor, and praise them, we four kings will also make such kings of humans always foremost among all kings in receiving offerings, reverence, honor, and praise, and will also make other kings admire and envy them and extol their goodness."

The Buddha Approves the Kings' Pledge

Chinese Source

爾時世尊讚歎護世四天王等：「善哉，善哉！汝等四王，過去已曾供養恭敬尊重讚歎無量百千萬億諸佛，於諸佛所種諸善根，說於正法修行正法，以法治世為人天王。汝等今日長夜利益於諸眾生，行大悲心，施與眾生一切樂具，能遮諸惡勤與諸善。以是義故，若有人王能供養恭敬此金光明微妙經典，汝等正應如是護念，滅其苦惱與其安樂。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the World-Honored One praised the four world-protecting heavenly kings: "Excellent, excellent! You four kings in the past have already made offerings to, revered, honored, and praised immeasurable hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of buddhas, planted wholesome roots in the presence of the buddhas, taught the true Dharma, cultivated the true Dharma, and governed the world by the Dharma as kings of humans and gods. You today, through the long night, benefit beings, practice the mind of great compassion, give beings every means of happiness, hold back evils, and diligently bestow goods. For this reason, if there is a king of humans who can make offerings to and revere this subtle and wondrous scripture, the Golden Light, you should rightly hold him in mind and protect him in this way, extinguish his sufferings and distress, and give him peace and happiness.

Chinese Source

「汝等四王及諸眷屬、無量無邊百千鬼神，若能護念如是經者，即是護持去來現在諸佛正法。汝等四王及餘天眾百千鬼神，與阿脩羅共戰鬪時，汝等諸天常得勝利。汝等若能護念此經，悉能消伏一切諸苦，所謂怨賊饑饉疾疫。若四部眾有能受持讀誦此經，汝等亦應勤心守護，為除衰惱施與安樂。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"You four kings and your retinues, immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of ghosts and spirits — if you can hold in mind and protect such a sūtra, this is protecting and upholding the true Dharma of the buddhas of the past, future, and present. When you four kings and the other hosts of gods, with hundreds of thousands of ghosts and spirits, fight in battle with the asuras, you gods will always be victorious. If you can hold in mind and protect this sūtra, you can wholly dispel all sufferings — namely hostile invaders, famine, and epidemics. If any of the four groups of the assembly can receive, uphold, read, and recite this sūtra, you should also diligently guard them, remove their declines and distress, and give them peace and happiness."

Chinese Source

爾時四王復白佛言：「世尊！是金光明微妙經典，於未來世在所流布，若國土城邑郡縣村落隨所至處，若諸國王以天律治世，復能恭敬至心聽受是妙經典，并復尊重供養供給持是經典四部之眾，以是因緣，我等時時得聞如是微妙經典，聞已即得增益身力，心進勇銳具諸威德。是故我等及無量鬼神，常當隱形隨其妙典所流布處，而作擁護令無留難；亦當護念聽是經典諸國王等及其人民，除其患難悉令安隱，他方怨賊亦使退散。若有人王聽是經時，隣國怨敵興如是念：『當具四兵壞彼國土。』世尊！以是經典威神力故，爾時隣敵更有異怨為作留難，於其境界起諸衰惱災異疫病。爾時怨敵起如是等諸惡事已，備具四兵，發向是國規往討罰。我等爾時，當與眷屬無量無邊百千鬼神，隱蔽其形為作護助，令彼怨敵自然退散，起諸怖懅種種留難。彼國兵眾尚不能到，況復當能有所破壞？」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the four kings again said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! In future ages, wherever this subtle and wondrous scripture, the Golden Light, spreads — in lands, cities, towns, commanderies, counties, villages, and hamlets, wherever it reaches — if the kings govern the world by heavenly law and can also reverently listen to and receive this wondrous scripture with utmost mind, and further honor, make offerings to, and provide for the four groups of the assembly who uphold this scripture, by these causes and conditions we will from time to time get to hear such a subtle and wondrous scripture; having heard it, we at once increase our bodily strength, our minds advance, courageous and keen, and we become complete in all majestic virtue. Therefore we and immeasurable ghosts and spirits will always, with our forms hidden, follow wherever the wondrous scripture spreads and guard it so that there are no hindrances; we will also hold in mind and protect the kings who listen to this scripture and their people, removing their troubles and difficulties and making them all at peace and secure, and hostile invaders from other regions we will also make retreat and scatter. If, when a king of humans is listening to this sūtra, a neighboring hostile country should think thus: 'We will equip the four kinds of troops and destroy that country,' World-Honored One, by the majestic spiritual power of this scripture, that neighboring enemy will at that time meet with other enemies who make hindrances for it, and within its own borders declines, distress, calamities, portents, and epidemics will arise. Then, when the hostile enemy, having met such evils, prepares the four kinds of troops and sets out toward this country planning to attack it, we at that time, with our retinue of immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of ghosts and spirits, will hide our forms and give protection and help, so that the hostile enemy naturally retreats and scatters, beset by fears and all kinds of hindrances. The troops of that country will not even be able to arrive — how much less could they destroy anything?"

Chinese Source

爾時佛讚四天王等：「善哉，善哉！汝等四王，乃能擁護我百千億那由他劫所可修習阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，及諸人王受持是經恭敬供養者，為消衰患令其安樂；復能擁護宮殿舍宅城邑村落國土邊疆，乃至怨賊悉令退散，滅其衰惱令得安隱，亦令一切閻浮提內所有諸王無諸凶衰鬪訟之事。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the Buddha praised the Four Heavenly Kings: "Excellent, excellent! You four kings are able to guard the unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening that I cultivated over hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of eons, and to guard the kings of humans who receive, uphold, revere, and make offerings to this sūtra, removing their declines and troubles and making them happy; further you are able to guard palaces, dwellings, cities, towns, villages, lands, and frontiers, and even make hostile invaders all retreat and scatter, extinguishing declines and distress and making them secure; and you also make all the kings within Jambudvīpa free of misfortune, decline, and strife.

Chinese Source

「四王當知，此閻浮提八萬四千城邑聚落、八萬四千諸人王等，各於其國娛樂快樂，各各於國而得自在；於自所有錢財珍寶，各各自足不相侵奪；如其宿世所修集業隨業受報，不生惡心貪求他國；各各自生利益之心，生於慈心、安樂之心、不諍訟心、不破壞心、無繫縛心、無楚撻心，各於其土自生愛樂，上下和睦猶如水乳，心相愛念增諸善根。以是因緣故，此閻浮提安隱豐樂，人民熾盛大地沃壤，陰陽調和時不越序，日月星宿不失常度，風雨隨時無諸災橫，人民豐實自足於財，心無貪吝亦無嫉妬，等行十善，其人壽終多生天上，天宮充滿增益天眾。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Four kings, you should know: in this Jambudvīpa are eighty-four thousand cities, towns, and settlements and eighty-four thousand kings of humans, each enjoying pleasure and happiness in his own country, each sovereign in his country; with the wealth and treasure each possesses, each is content and does not encroach on or seize from the others; according to the karma they cultivated and accumulated in past lives they receive retribution according to karma, and they do not give rise to evil minds coveting other countries; each gives rise of his own accord to a mind of benefiting — giving rise to a kind mind, a mind of peace and happiness, a mind free of strife, a mind free of destruction, a mind free of bondage, a mind free of beating; each in his own land delights in it, those above and below in harmony like water and milk, their minds cherishing one another and increasing their wholesome roots. For these causes and conditions this Jambudvīpa is secure, prosperous, and happy; its people flourish, the great earth is fertile, yin and yang are in harmony, the seasons do not overstep their order, sun, moon, stars, and constellations do not lose their regular courses, wind and rain come in their season without calamities or disasters, the people are prosperous and content with their wealth, their minds without greed or stinginess and also without jealousy; equally they practice the ten wholesome [deeds], and when these people's lives end most are born in the heavens, the heavenly palaces are filled, and the hosts of gods increase.

Chinese Source

「若未來世有諸人王聽是經典，及供養恭敬受持是經四部之眾，是王則為安樂利益汝等四王及餘眷屬無量百千諸鬼神等。何以故？汝等四王，若得時時聞是經典，則為已得正法之水，服甘露味增益身力，心進勇銳具諸威德。是諸人王，若能至心聽受是經，則為已能供養於我。若供養我，則是供養過去未來現在諸佛；若能供養過去未來現在諸佛，則得無量不可思議功德之聚。以是因緣，是諸人王應得擁護，及后妃婇女中宮眷屬諸王子等亦應得護，衰惱消滅快樂熾盛，宮殿堂宇安隱清淨無諸災變，護宅之神增長威德，亦受無量歡悅快樂。是諸國土所有人民，悉受種種五欲之樂，一切惡事悉皆消滅。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"If in future ages there are kings of humans who listen to this scripture and make offerings to and revere the four groups of the assembly who receive and uphold this sūtra, these kings thereby give peace, happiness, and benefit to you four kings and your retinues of immeasurable hundreds of thousands of ghosts and spirits. Why? If you four kings get to hear this scripture from time to time, you have thereby obtained the water of the true Dharma; you take the taste of sweet dew, increase your bodily strength, your minds advance, courageous and keen, and you become complete in all majestic virtue. If these kings of humans can listen to and receive this sūtra with utmost mind, they have thereby already made offerings to me. If they make offerings to me, this is making offerings to the buddhas of the past, future, and present; if they can make offerings to the buddhas of the past, future, and present, they obtain an immeasurable, inconceivable heap of merit. For these causes and conditions these kings of humans should receive protection, and their queens, consorts, palace women, the household of the inner palace, and the princes should also receive protection; their declines and distress are extinguished, their happiness flourishes, their palaces and halls are secure and pure, without calamities or uncanny changes; the spirits that protect their dwellings increase in majestic virtue and also receive immeasurable joy and happiness. All the people of these countries receive all kinds of pleasures of the five desires, and all evil events are wholly extinguished."

How a King Should Receive the Sūtra

Chinese Source

爾時四天王白佛言：「世尊！未來之世若有人王，欲得護身及后妃婇女諸王子等宮殿屋宅，得第一護身所王領最為殊勝，具不可思議王者功德；欲得攝取無量福聚，國土無有他方怨賊，無諸憂惱及諸苦事。世尊！如是人王，不應放逸散亂其心，應生恭敬謙下之心，應當莊嚴第一微妙最勝宮宅，種種香汁持用灑地，散種種華，敷大法座師子之座，兼以無量珍琦異物而為校飾，張施種種無數微妙幢幡寶蓋；當淨洗浴以香塗身，著好淨衣纓絡自嚴，坐卑小座不自高大，除去自在離諸放逸，謙下自卑除去驕慢，正念聽受如是妙典，於說法者生世尊想；復於宮內后妃王子婇女眷屬，生慈哀心和顏與語，勸以種種供養之具供養法師。是王爾時既勸化已，即生無量歡喜快樂，心懷悅豫倍復自勵，不生疲倦多作利益，於說法者倍生恭敬。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time the Four Heavenly Kings said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! In future ages, if there is a king of humans who wishes to protect himself and his queens, consorts, palace women, princes, palaces, and dwellings; who wishes to obtain the foremost protection of his person, the domain he rules being most outstanding, complete in the inconceivable merits of a king; who wishes to gather an immeasurable heap of blessings, and to have in his land no hostile invaders from other regions, no worries, distress, or painful affairs — World-Honored One, such a king of humans should not be heedless or let his mind be scattered; he should give rise to a reverent and humble mind; he should adorn a foremost, subtle, wondrous, and most excellent palace, sprinkle the ground with all kinds of fragrant liquids, scatter all kinds of flowers, spread out a great Dharma seat, a lion throne, ornamented with immeasurable precious and rare things, and hang all kinds of countless subtle and wondrous banners, streamers, and jeweled canopies; he should bathe himself clean, anoint his body with fragrance, put on fine clean clothes, adorn himself with necklaces, sit on a low small seat without exalting himself, set aside his sovereignty, depart from all heedlessness, be humble and lower himself, remove arrogance, and with right mindfulness listen to and receive such a wondrous scripture, regarding the one teaching the Dharma as the World-Honored One; further, toward the queens, consorts, princes, palace women, and household within the palace, he should give rise to a kind and compassionate mind, speak to them with a gentle face, and urge them to make offerings to the Dharma master with all kinds of offerings.
