# The Golden Light Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming jing* (金光明經, Taishō T16, no. 663 (tr. Dharmakṣema)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T0663, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). The translation dates from the early fifth century; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 懺悔 | repentance | The verses of the golden drum in chapter 3. |
| 四天王 | the Four Heavenly Kings | Protectors of the land where the sūtra is honored. |
| 功德天 | the goddess Śrī | Who promises abundance to those who uphold the sūtra. |
| 捨身 | giving up the body | The prince and the hungry tigress, chapter 17. |
