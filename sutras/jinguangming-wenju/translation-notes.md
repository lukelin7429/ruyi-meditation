# The Textual Commentary on the Golden Light Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming jing wenju* (金光明經文句, Taishō T39, no. 1785 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1785, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 序／正／流通 | introduction / main teaching / propagation | The threefold division of the sūtra. |
| 觀心 | contemplation of mind | Zhiyi's fourth way of reading a passage. |
| 三障 | the three obstructions | Of retribution, of afflictions, and of karma. |
| 懺悔 | repentance | Explained at length in fascicle 3. |
