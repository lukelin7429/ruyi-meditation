# Notes Expounding the Dharma Gate of Mahāyāna Calming-and-Contemplation — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Dasheng zhiguan shuji* (大乘止觀述記, CBETA G2285 (Dixian)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** CBETA G2285, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Dixian died in 1932; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 自性清淨心 | the mind pure in its own nature | The essence, in Dixian's five profound meanings. |
| 止觀 | calming-and-contemplation | The cardinal doctrine. |
| 三性 | the three natures | The spheres of practice in the root text. |
