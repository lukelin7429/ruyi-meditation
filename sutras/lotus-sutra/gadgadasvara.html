Gadgadasvara, the Bodhisattva of Wonderful Voice

妙音菩薩品第二十四

Light from Śākyamuni's own body reaches a distant, unblemished world, where a bodhisattva named Gadgadasvara — Wonderful Voice — resolves to travel here and hear this sūtra for himself. His own buddha warns him first not to look down on what he finds: a rough, uneven land with a buddha and bodhisattvas of ordinary stature, nothing like the splendor he is used to. The Buddha then reveals that this same bodhisattva takes on thirty-four different forms wherever beings need him — a king, a monk, a laywoman, even a woman in a king's own household — never losing anything of his own realization in any of them.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Source: Taishō T262 · Kumārajīva's translation, early 5th century CE Commentary guide: Zhiyi's 妙法蓮華經文句 (6th c.)
This part covers
Light from Śākyamuni's own body reaching the distant land of Vairocanaraśmipratimaṇḍitā, where the bodhisattva Gadgadasvara, master of sixteen great samādhis, resolves to travel to the sahā world; his own buddha's warning not to look down on this rough and uneven land or its comparatively small buddha and bodhisattvas; his arrival amid earthquakes and falling lotus flowers, and his offering to Śākyamuni and request to see Prabhūtaratna; and the Buddha's account of Gadgadasvara's own past offering to an earlier buddha, and the thirty-four different forms — from Brahmā and a wheel-turning king down to a common woman in a king's own household — he takes on wherever beings anywhere need him to appear, all without any loss to his own realization.
Why it matters
This chapter and the one following it form a matched pair on how one advanced bodhisattva's compassion actually reaches beings — not by broadcasting a single teaching, but by becoming, again and again, whatever shape each particular listener is able to receive. Zhiyi reads the warning against looking down on the sahā world as this sūtra's own version of the room, robe, and seat named back in Part 11, now applied to how a visiting teacher should regard the very audience they have come to help.

1. A Bodhisattva Resolves to Cross Over

Chinese Source

爾時釋迦牟尼佛放大人相肉髻光明，及放眉間白毫相光，遍照東方百八萬億那由他恒河沙等諸佛世界。過是數已，有世界名淨光莊嚴，其國有佛，號淨華宿王智如來、應供、正遍知、明行足、善逝、世間解、無上士、調御丈夫、天人師、佛、世尊，為無量無邊菩薩大眾恭敬圍繞而為說法，釋迦牟尼佛白毫光明遍照其國。爾時一切淨光莊嚴國中，有一菩薩名曰妙音，久已殖眾德本，供養親近無量百千萬億諸佛，而悉成就甚深智慧，得妙幢相三昧、法華三昧、淨德三昧、宿王戲三昧、無緣三昧、智印三昧、解一切眾生語言三昧、集一切功德三昧、清淨三昧、神通遊戲三昧、慧炬三昧、莊嚴王三昧、淨光明三昧、淨藏三昧、不共三昧、日旋三昧，得如是等百千萬億恒河沙等諸大三昧。釋迦牟尼佛光照其身，即白淨華宿王智佛言：「世尊！我當往詣娑婆世界，禮拜、親近、供養釋迦牟尼佛，及見文殊師利法王子菩薩、藥王菩薩、勇施菩薩、宿王華菩薩、上行意菩薩、莊嚴王菩薩、藥上菩薩。」 爾時淨華宿王智佛告妙音菩薩：「汝莫輕彼國，生下劣想。善男子！彼娑婆世界，高下不平，土石諸山，穢惡充滿，佛身卑小，諸菩薩眾其形亦小。而汝身四萬二千由旬，我身六百八十萬由旬，汝身第一端正，百千萬福，光明殊妙，是故汝往，莫輕彼國——若佛、菩薩及國土，生下劣想。」 妙音菩薩白其佛言：「世尊！我今詣娑婆世界，皆是如來之力，如來神通遊戲，如來功德智慧莊嚴。」於是妙音菩薩不起于座，身不動搖，而入三昧，以三昧力，於耆闍崛山，去法座不遠，化作八萬四千眾寶蓮華，閻浮檀金為莖，白銀為葉，金剛為鬚，甄叔迦寶以為其臺。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Śākyamuni Buddha sent forth light from the mark of the fleshly crown atop his head, that of a great being, and light from the white curl of hair between his brows, illuminating entirely a hundred and eight myriad koṭis of nayutas of Ganges-sands of buddha-worlds to the east. Beyond this number, there was a world named Vairocanaraśmipratimaṇḍitā, and in this land was a buddha named Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña Tathāgata, Worthy of Offerings, Perfectly and Fully Awakened, Complete in Understanding and Conduct, Well-Gone, Knower of the World, Unsurpassed Being, Tamer of Those to be Tamed, Teacher of Gods and Humans, Buddha, World-Honored One, teaching the Dharma, reverently surrounded by an immeasurable, boundless assembly of great bodhisattvas. Śākyamuni Buddha's white curl of light illuminated this entire land. At that time, within this whole land of Vairocanaraśmipratimaṇḍitā, there was a bodhisattva named Gadgadasvara, who had long ago planted every root of merit, made offerings to and drawn near immeasurable hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of buddhas, and had thereby accomplished a profound wisdom, attaining the samādhi of the wondrous banner-mark, the Lotus samādhi, the samādhi of pure virtue, the samādhi of the constellation-king's play, the samādhi free of conditions, the samādhi of the wisdom seal, the samādhi of understanding the language of every being, the samādhi that gathers every merit, the samādhi of purity, the samādhi of playing amid spiritual powers, the samādhi of wisdom's torch, the samādhi of the adorning king, the samādhi of pure light, the samādhi of the pure treasury, the samādhi shared with none other, and the samādhi of the sun's revolution — attaining, in this way, hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of Ganges-sands of such great samādhis as these. When Śākyamuni Buddha's light illuminated his own body, he at once said to Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña: "World-Honored One! I should now go to the sahā world, to bow before, draw near to, and make offerings to Śākyamuni Buddha, and to see Mañjuśrī, Dharma Prince Bodhisattva, Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva, Pradānaśūra Bodhisattva, Bhadravyūhamati Bodhisattva, Viśiṣṭacintā Bodhisattva, Vyūharāja Bodhisattva, and Bhaiṣajyasamudgata Bodhisattva." At that time Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña said to Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva: "Do not look down upon that land, giving rise to any thought of its inferiority. Good son! That sahā world is uneven, high in some places and low in others, filled with earth, stone, and mountains, with every kind of impurity and foulness; its buddha's own body is of modest stature, and the bodies of its bodhisattvas, too, are modest. Your own body is forty-two thousand yojanas tall, and my own body is six million eight hundred thousand yojanas tall; your body is foremost in its uprightness, adorned with hundreds of thousands of merits, its light exceptionally fine and wondrous — for this reason, when you go there, do not look down upon that land, whether toward its buddha, its bodhisattvas, or the land itself, with any thought of its inferiority." Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva said to his own buddha: "World-Honored One! That I now go to the sahā world is entirely due to the Tathāgata's own power, the Tathāgata's own play of spiritual penetration, the Tathāgata's own adornment of merit and wisdom." At this, Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva, without rising from his seat, his body entirely unmoving, entered into samādhi, and, by the power of this samādhi, on Vulture Peak, not far from the Dharma seat, conjured eighty-four thousand jeweled lotus flowers, their stems of Jambūnada gold, their leaves of white silver, their stamens of diamond, their calyxes made of kiṃśuka jewels.

Reading note — after Zhiyi

Zhiyi reads this buddha's warning through the same three-part framework this site has traced since Part 11's room, robe, and seat: regarding the sahā buddha's own modest stature without contempt, despite the visible difference in scale, is a matter of the seat, since a buddha's true dignity is not measured by physical size; recognizing that Śākyamuni's own teaching of provisional means conceals a wisdom no less complete than his own is a matter of the robe; and understanding that a land's outward roughness reflects the beings who inhabit it, not any deficiency in the buddha teaching there, is a matter of the room. The instruction is addressed to Gadgadasvara specifically as a visitor about to cross into an unfamiliar and, by his own standards, visibly lesser-seeming world — and Zhiyi treats it as a caution any traveler carrying real attainment into someone else's difficult circumstances would do well to hear.

2. Arrival at Vulture Peak

Chinese Source

爾時，文殊師利法王子見是蓮華，而白佛言：「世尊！是何因緣，先現此瑞？有若干千萬蓮華，閻浮檀金為莖，白銀為葉，金剛為鬚，甄叔迦寶以為其臺。」 爾時釋迦牟尼佛告文殊師利：「是妙音菩薩摩訶薩，欲從淨華宿王智佛國，與八萬四千菩薩，圍繞而來至此娑婆世界，供養、親近、禮拜於我，亦欲供養、聽法華經。」 文殊師利白佛言：「世尊！是菩薩種何善本？修何功德？而能有是大神通力？行何三昧？願為我等說是三昧名字，我等亦欲勤修行之，行此三昧，乃能見是菩薩色相大小，威儀進止。唯願世尊以神通力，彼菩薩來，令我得見。」 爾時釋迦牟尼佛告文殊師利：「此久滅度多寶如來，當為汝等而現其相。」 時多寶佛告彼菩薩：「善男子！來，文殊師利法王子欲見汝身。」 于時妙音菩薩於彼國沒，與八萬四千菩薩俱共發來。所經諸國，六種震動，皆悉雨於七寶蓮華；百千天樂，不鼓自鳴。是菩薩目如廣大青蓮華葉，正使和合百千萬月，其面貌端正復過於此，身真金色，無量百千功德莊嚴，威德熾盛，光明照曜，諸相具足，如那羅延堅固之身。入七寶臺，上昇虛空，去地七多羅樹，諸菩薩眾恭敬圍繞，而來詣此娑婆世界耆闍崛山。到已，下七寶臺，以價直百千瓔珞，持至釋迦牟尼佛所，頭面禮足，奉上瓔珞，而白佛言：「世尊！淨華宿王智佛問訊世尊，少病、少惱，起居輕利，安樂行不？四大調和不？世事可忍不？眾生易度不？無多貪欲、瞋恚、愚癡、嫉妬、慳慢不？無不孝父母、不敬沙門、邪見、不善心、不攝五情不？世尊！眾生能降伏諸魔怨不？久滅度多寶如來在七寶塔中，來聽法不？又問訊多寶如來，安隱、少惱，堪忍久住不？世尊！我今欲見多寶佛身，唯願世尊，示我令見。」 爾時釋迦牟尼佛語多寶佛：「是妙音菩薩欲得相見。」 時多寶佛告妙音言：「善哉，善哉！汝能為供養釋迦牟尼佛及聽法華經，并見文殊師利等，故來至此。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Mañjuśrī, Dharma Prince Bodhisattva, seeing these lotus flowers, said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! By what cause and condition does this auspicious sign appear first — these several thousand myriad lotus flowers, their stems of Jambūnada gold, their leaves of white silver, their stamens of diamond, their calyxes made of kiṃśuka jewels?" At that time Śākyamuni Buddha said to Mañjuśrī: "This is Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, wishing to come here from the land of Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña, together with eighty-four thousand bodhisattvas surrounding him, to this sahā world, to make offerings to me, to draw near to me, and to bow before me, and wishing, too, to make offerings to and hear the Lotus Sūtra." Mañjuśrī said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! What good root has this bodhisattva planted, what merit has he cultivated, that he should possess such great spiritual power as this? What samādhi does he practice? I beg you to tell us the names of these samādhis, for we too wish to diligently practice and cultivate them — practicing these samādhis, we might then be able to see this bodhisattva's own form, whether great or small, and his majestic bearing, his advancing and his stillness. I beg only that the World-Honored One, by the power of spiritual penetration, cause this bodhisattva to come, so that we may see him." At that time Śākyamuni Buddha said to Mañjuśrī: "Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata, extinguished so long ago, shall now, for your sake, cause his form to appear." At that time Prabhūtaratna Buddha said to that bodhisattva: "Good son, come — Mañjuśrī, Dharma Prince Bodhisattva, wishes to see your form." At this, Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva vanished from that land, and, together with eighty-four thousand bodhisattvas, set out together. Every land he passed through shook in six ways, and everywhere rained down lotus flowers of the seven jewels; hundreds of thousands of heavenly instruments sounded of themselves, without being played. This bodhisattva's eyes were like the broad petals of the blue lotus; even were a hundred thousand myriad moons combined together, his countenance's own uprightness would still surpass them; his body was the color of true gold, adorned with immeasurable hundreds of thousands of merits, his majestic virtue blazing forth, his light shining brilliantly, every mark complete upon him, his body as firm and unbreakable as Nārāyaṇa's own. Entering a platform of the seven jewels, he rose into the sky, seven tāla trees above the earth, the assembly of bodhisattvas reverently surrounding him, and came in this way to Vulture Peak in this sahā world. Having arrived, he descended from the platform of the seven jewels, and, taking up a necklace worth hundreds of thousands in value, carried it to where Śākyamuni Buddha was, bowed his head to the Buddha's feet, offered up the necklace, and said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña greets the World-Honored One, and asks: are you free of illness, free of affliction, are your movements and rest at ease, do you dwell in peace? Are the four elements in harmony within you? Are worldly affairs bearable? Are beings easily brought across? Are they free of excessive greed, anger, folly, envy, and stinginess and pride? Are they free of disrespect toward their parents, disrespect toward renunciants, wrong views, unwholesome hearts, and a failure to govern the five senses? World-Honored One, are beings able to subdue every demon and enemy? Does Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata, extinguished so long ago, come to hear the Dharma from within his stūpa of the seven jewels? And he asks, too, after Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata: is he at peace, free of affliction, able to endure and remain long in the world? World-Honored One! I now wish to see Prabhūtaratna Buddha's own form — I beg only that the World-Honored One show him to me, and let me see him." At that time Śākyamuni Buddha said to Prabhūtaratna Buddha: "This Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva wishes to see you face to face." At that time Prabhūtaratna Buddha said to Gadgadasvara: "Excellent, excellent! That you should come here for the sake of making offerings to Śākyamuni Buddha, and hearing the Lotus Sūtra, and seeing Mañjuśrī and the others."

Reading note

The string of questions Gadgadasvara relays from his own buddha — after physical health, then whether beings are easily taught, then whether they are free of anger, envy, and disrespect toward their own parents — moves in the same order this sūtra has followed since its opening chapters: from the merely physical, to the pedagogical, to what actually matters most, the state of beings' own hearts. That an inconceivably advanced bodhisattva, capable of crossing a hundred million buddha-worlds in an instant, still opens with so ordinary a greeting is itself part of what this chapter is showing: real attainment here takes the form of courtesy, not display.

3. Thirty-Four Forms, One Bodhisattva

Chinese Source

爾時華德菩薩白佛言：「世尊！是妙音菩薩，種何善根，修何功德，有是神力？」 佛告華德菩薩：「過去有佛，名雲雷音王多陀阿伽度、阿羅訶、三藐三佛陀，國名現一切世間，劫名憙見，妙音菩薩於萬二千歲，以十萬種伎樂供養雲雷音王佛，并奉上八萬四千七寶鉢。以是因緣果報，今生淨華宿王智佛國，有是神力。華德！於汝意云何？爾時雲雷音王佛所，妙音菩薩——伎樂供養、奉上寶器者，豈異人乎？今此妙音菩薩摩訶薩是。華德！是妙音菩薩，已曾供養親近無量諸佛，久殖德本，又值恒河沙等百千萬億那由他佛。 「華德！汝但見妙音菩薩其身在此，而是菩薩，現種種身，處處為諸眾生說是經典——或現梵王身，或現帝釋身，或現自在天身，或現大自在天身，或現天大將軍身，或現毘沙門天王身，或現轉輪聖王身，或現諸小王身，或現長者身，或現居士身，或現宰官身，或現婆羅門身，或現比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷身，或現長者居士婦女身，或現宰官婦女身，或現婆羅門婦女身，或現童男、童女身，或現天、龍、夜叉、乾闥婆、阿修羅、迦樓羅、緊那羅、摩睺羅伽、人非人等身，而說是經。諸有地獄、餓鬼、畜生，及眾難處，皆能救濟，乃至於王後宮，變為女身，而說是經。 「華德！是妙音菩薩，能救護娑婆世界諸眾生者，是妙音菩薩如是種種變化現身，在此娑婆國土，為諸眾生說是經典，於神通、變化、智慧無所損減。是菩薩，以若干智慧明照娑婆世界，令一切眾生各得所知；於十方恒河沙世界中，亦復如是。若應以聲聞形得度者，現聲聞形而為說法；應以辟支佛形得度者，現辟支佛形而為說法；應以菩薩形得度者，現菩薩形而為說法；應以佛形得度者，即現佛形而為說法。如是種種，隨所應度而為現形，乃至應以滅度而得度者，示現滅度。 「華德！妙音菩薩摩訶薩，成就大神通智慧之力，其事如是。」 爾時華德菩薩白佛言：「世尊！是妙音菩薩，深種善根。世尊！是菩薩住何三昧，而能如是在所變現，度脫眾生？」 佛告華德菩薩：「善男子！其三昧名現一切色身，妙音菩薩住是三昧中，能如是饒益無量眾生。」 說是妙音菩薩品時，與妙音菩薩俱來者八萬四千人，皆得現一切色身三昧；此娑婆世界無量菩薩，亦得是三昧及陀羅尼。 爾時妙音菩薩摩訶薩供養釋迦牟尼佛及多寶佛塔已，還歸本土，所經諸國，六種震動，雨寶蓮華，作百千萬億種種伎樂。既到本國，與八萬四千菩薩、圍繞至淨華宿王智佛所，白佛言：「世尊！我到娑婆世界饒益眾生，見釋迦牟尼佛，及見多寶佛塔，禮拜、供養；又見文殊師利法王子菩薩，及見藥王菩薩、得勤精進力菩薩、勇施菩薩等，亦令是八萬四千菩薩得現一切色身三昧。」說是妙音菩薩來往品時，四萬二千天子得無生法忍，華德菩薩得法華三昧。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time Padmaśrī Bodhisattva said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! What good root has this Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva planted, what merit has he cultivated, that he should possess such spiritual power as this?" The Buddha said to Padmaśrī Bodhisattva: "In the past there was a buddha named Meghadundubhisvararāja Tathāgata, Worthy of Offerings, Perfectly and Fully Awakened, whose land was named Manifesting Every World, and whose kalpa was named Delight in Seeing. For twelve thousand years, Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva made offerings to Buddha Meghadundubhisvararāja with a hundred thousand kinds of music, and offered up, besides, eighty-four thousand bowls of the seven jewels. By this very cause and condition and its result, he is now born in the land of Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña, possessed of this spiritual power. Padmaśrī, what do you think? Was that Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva, who made offerings of music and bowls of treasure at that time, in the presence of Buddha Meghadundubhisvararāja, some other person? He is none other than this Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva of today. Padmaśrī, this Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva has already made offerings to and drawn near immeasurable buddhas, long ago planting every root of merit, and has, further, encountered Ganges-sands of hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of nayutas of buddhas. "Padmaśrī! You see only Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva's own body, present here — yet this bodhisattva manifests every kind of body, teaching this sūtra to beings in every place: sometimes manifesting the body of Brahmā, sometimes the body of Śakra, sometimes the body of Īśvara, sometimes the body of Maheśvara, sometimes the body of a great heavenly general, sometimes the body of Vaiśravaṇa, the heavenly king, sometimes the body of a wheel-turning sage-king, sometimes the body of one of the lesser kings, sometimes the body of an elder, sometimes the body of a householder, sometimes the body of a minister, sometimes the body of a brahmin, sometimes the body of a monk, a nun, a layman, or a laywoman, sometimes the body of the wife of an elder or a householder, sometimes the body of the wife of a minister, sometimes the body of the wife of a brahmin, sometimes the body of a boy or a girl, sometimes the body of a god, a nāga, a yakṣa, a gandharva, an asura, a garuḍa, a kiṃnara, a mahoraga, a human or a nonhuman being — and teaches this sūtra in every one of these forms. Every being in the hells, among the hungry ghosts, among the animals, and in every other difficult circumstance, he is able to save and rescue; even within a king's own harem, he transforms into a woman's body, and teaches this sūtra there. "Padmaśrī! This is how Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva is able to save and protect the beings of the sahā world: manifesting himself, transformed into every kind of body such as this, dwelling in this sahā land, teaching this sūtra for the sake of beings, without any diminishment at all to his own spiritual power, his transformations, or his wisdom. This bodhisattva, with manifold wisdom, illuminates the sahā world, causing every being to attain what they need to know; and throughout the Ganges-sands of worlds of the ten directions, it is exactly the same. Should a being be one who is brought across by the form of a śrāvaka, he manifests the form of a śrāvaka and teaches the Dharma; should a being be one who is brought across by the form of a pratyekabuddha, he manifests the form of a pratyekabuddha and teaches the Dharma; should a being be one who is brought across by the form of a bodhisattva, he manifests the form of a bodhisattva and teaches the Dharma; should a being be one who is brought across by the form of a buddha, he manifests the very form of a buddha and teaches the Dharma. In every kind of way such as this, he manifests whatever form is needed to bring each being across, even manifesting the appearance of parinirvāṇa itself, for those who are brought across by that very means. "Padmaśrī! Such is the matter of how Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva has accomplished this power of great spiritual penetration and wisdom." At that time Padmaśrī Bodhisattva said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! This Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva has planted good roots deeply indeed. World-Honored One, in what samādhi does this bodhisattva dwell, that he is able, in this way, to manifest himself in whatever place is needed, and bring beings across to liberation?" The Buddha said to Padmaśrī Bodhisattva: "Good son! This samādhi is named the manifesting of every form; Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva, dwelling within this samādhi, is able, in this way, to greatly benefit immeasurable beings." When this chapter on Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva was taught, the eighty-four thousand people who had come together with Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva all attained the samādhi of manifesting every form; and immeasurable bodhisattvas of this sahā world attained this same samādhi, and its dhāraṇī as well. At that time Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, having made his offerings to Śākyamuni Buddha and to Prabhūtaratna Buddha's stūpa, returned to his own land. Every land he passed through shook in six ways, and rained down jeweled lotus flowers, and made hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of every kind of music. Having arrived in his own land, together with the eighty-four thousand bodhisattvas surrounding him, he went to where Buddha Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña was, and said to the buddha: "World-Honored One! I went to the sahā world to benefit its beings, and saw Śākyamuni Buddha, and saw Prabhūtaratna Buddha's own stūpa, and bowed before them and made offerings; and saw, too, Mañjuśrī, Dharma Prince Bodhisattva, and saw Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva, Pradānaśūravīryabala Bodhisattva, Pradānaśūra Bodhisattva, and others, and caused these eighty-four thousand bodhisattvas, too, to attain the samādhi of manifesting every form." When this chapter on Gadgadasvara Bodhisattva's coming and going was taught, forty-two thousand sons of the gods attained the acceptance that things are unborn, and Padmaśrī Bodhisattva attained the Lotus samādhi."

Reading note — after Zhiyi

Zhiyi counts this passage's list of forms as thirty-four in total — divided between four sagely forms (śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha, bodhisattva, buddha) and thirty ordinary ones, spanning gods, human rulers and commoners of both sexes, and the eight classes of nonhuman beings, then folds this list, together with the appearance of entering parinirvāṇa itself, into this sūtra's recurring frame of the ten Dharma-realms across the six paths of ordinary rebirth and the four stages of liberation. He treats this catalog as the direct completion of a promise this same chapter makes explicitly: that assuming every one of these forms costs Gadgadasvara nothing of his "spiritual power, transformations, or wisdom" — the shape-shifting is not a dilution of his realization spread thin across many disguises, but its full expression, undiminished, in as many forms as beings actually need to encounter it in.
