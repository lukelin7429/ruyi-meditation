Two Hundred Questions on Contemplating the Mind

This short work belongs to the Song-dynasty dispute between Siming Zhili and the "off-mountain" (shanwai) teachers over the expanded version of Zhiyi's Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra, and in particular over whether its section on contemplating the mind is authentic. Wu'en (Bringing Out the Meaning) and his followers held that the ten sets of three dharmas already reveal the nature of mind directly, so the added contemplation is unnecessary and must be a later interpolation. Zhili defended the section in his Record Resolving Difficulties and Supporting the School. Several letters followed, and in 1007 (Jingde 4) he answered Qingzhao's (the "Venerable Zhao") reply to his Letter of Ten Meanings with this list of objections, compiled later by his disciple Jizhong. After a short preface come 199 questions (the numbers in square brackets are added in this translation) and a closing challenge; no answers are recorded. The questions return again and again to a few points: what counts as the contemplation of principle; whether contemplations "attached to dharmas" must sift the object of the aggregates; whether the object contemplated is the deluded mind or the true mind; whether form as well as mind contains the three thousand; how the inner and outer contemplations of the Ten Gates of Nonduality should be read; and whether ordinary practitioners can actually cultivate these contemplations without first studying the whole of Calming and Contemplation.

Zhili's Preface to the Questions

Chinese Source

景德四年六月十五日，四明沙門比丘知禮，謹用為法之心，問義于浙陽講主昭上人（左右）。五月二十六日，本州國寧寺傳到上人，答十義書一軸云云。答釋未善讀文，縱事改張終當乖理，始末全書於妄語，披尋備見於諂心，毀人且容，壞法寧忍！欲敷後難，恐混前文，故且於十科立二百問，蓋恐上人仍前隱覆，不陳已墮之愆，更肆奸諛重改難酬之問，故先標問目，後布難詞，必冀上人依數標章覽文為答，母使一條漏失，欲令正理分明，希不延時，庶塞顒望。

Ru-Yi Translation

On the fifteenth day of the sixth month of the fourth year of Jingde, Zhili, a śramaṇa and bhikṣu of Siming, respectfully, with a mind devoted to the Dharma, puts questions on the meaning to the lecture master of Zheyang, the Venerable Zhao (to his attendants). On the twenty-sixth day of the fifth month, one scroll from the Venerable, the Answer to the Letter of Ten Meanings, was delivered to me at Guoning Monastery in this prefecture (and so on). Its answers and explanations do not read the texts well; though it changes its position to suit the matter, in the end it runs contrary to principle. From beginning to end the whole is written in false speech; leafing through it, one sees a flattering mind everywhere. Disparaging persons may still be tolerated, but how could destroying the Dharma be endured! I wish to set out further objections, but fear they would be mixed up with the earlier text; therefore for now, under ten headings, I set up two hundred questions. For I fear that the Venerable will, as before, conceal and cover over, not stating the faults he has already fallen into, and will further give free rein to deceit and flattery and again alter [his position] on questions that are hard to answer. Therefore I first mark the items of the questions and afterward lay out the words of objection. I earnestly hope that the Venerable will, following the numbers, mark the sections and, reading the text, answer, not letting a single item be missed, so that the correct principle becomes clear. I hope there will be no delay, so as to satisfy my earnest expectation.

Is This the Contemplation of Principle in Terms of Practice?

Chinese Source

問：《辨訛》云「觀有二種：一曰理觀、二曰事觀。今文不須附事而觀，蓋十法純談理觀，故且二種觀法各能觀境顯理。」既不附事相而觀，乃是直於陰入觀理，此則正是約行理觀，今那云是事法理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [1]: Discerning Errors says: "Contemplation is of two kinds: the first is called the contemplation of principle, the second the contemplation of phenomena. The present text need not contemplate by attaching to phenomena, for its ten dharmas purely discuss the contemplation of principle; so for now each of the two kinds of contemplative method can contemplate the object and reveal principle." Since it contemplates without attaching to the marks of phenomena, it directly contemplates principle in the aggregates and entrances; this is exactly the contemplation of principle in terms of practice. How can you now say that it is the contemplation of principle through phenomena and dharmas?

Chinese Source

問：夫名事法為理觀者，須託事附法入陰心，用觀顯理方名理觀。今文既不附事託陰而觀，於理何顯而名理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [2]: Now, what is called the contemplation of principle through phenomena and dharmas must rely on phenomena or attach to dharmas, enter the mind of the aggregates, and use contemplation to reveal principle; only then is it called the contemplation of principle. Since the present text contemplates without attaching to phenomena or relying on the aggregates, what principle does it reveal, that it should be called the contemplation of principle?

Chinese Source

問：附事顯理乃是一種觀法，何得標列云觀有二種：一曰理觀、二曰事觀？豈以所附事自為一事，所顯理更立為一理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [3]: Attaching to phenomena to reveal principle is one single kind of contemplative method. How can you list it under the heading "contemplation is of two kinds: the first is called the contemplation of principle, the second the contemplation of phenomena"? Do you take the phenomena attached to as one [contemplation of] phenomena by itself, and set up the principle revealed as a further contemplation of principle?

Chinese Source

問：約教明三法對觀心，三法但名為事。今文既非約觀三法，那名為所顯三諦耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [4]: When the three dharmas are clarified in terms of the teachings, as against contemplating the mind, the three dharmas are called only phenomena. Since the present text does not [treat] the three dharmas in terms of contemplation, how can they be called the three truths that are revealed?

Chinese Source

問：《辨訛》云「今文理觀事事全成於法界，心心成顯於金光」。既不附事相法相，則是直體陰入事事成不思議境，則十乘心心顯於金光。既爾，得不是約行理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [5]: Discerning Errors says: "In the contemplation of principle of the present text, phenomenon after phenomenon wholly becomes the dharma-realm, and mind after mind is accomplished and revealed in the Golden Light." Since it does not attach to the marks of phenomena or the marks of dharmas, it directly takes the aggregates and entrances as its essence, so that phenomenon after phenomenon becomes the inconceivable object, and then, with the ten modes, mind after mind is revealed in the Golden Light. That being so, how can it not be the contemplation of principle in terms of practice?

Chinese Source

問：十法若非約行理觀，那得便是普賢端坐念實相耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [6]: If the ten dharmas are not the contemplation of principle in terms of practice, how can they be Samantabhadra's "sit upright and be mindful of the true mark"?

Chinese Source

問：《答疑書》既云「普賢觀法證前圓談理觀，示可修義。」何故《釋難書》轉云「念念相續及念實相」，令依止觀修證耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [7]: The Letter Answering Doubts says: "The method of contemplation of the Samantabhadra proves that the preceding perfect discussion is the contemplation of principle, and shows the meaning that it can be cultivated." Why then does the Letter Explaining Difficulties turn around and say "thought after thought in continuity, and mindful of the true mark," having one cultivate and realize by relying on calming-and-contemplation?

Chinese Source

問：若非約行理觀，焉得念念相續？焉得入理證果耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [8]: If it is not the contemplation of principle in terms of practice, how can there be "thought after thought in continuity"? How can one enter principle and realize the fruit?

Chinese Source

問：《答疑書》云「此玄直顯心性，義同理觀，若少帶事法，且非直顯心性，唯約行理觀，直觀陰心顯性。」此玄既直觀心顯性，那非行約理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [9]: The Letter Answering Doubts says: "This Profound [Meaning] directly reveals the nature of mind; its meaning is the same as the contemplation of principle. If it carried even a little of phenomena and dharmas, it would not be directly revealing the nature of mind. Only the contemplation of principle in terms of practice directly contemplates the mind of the aggregates and reveals the nature." Since this Profound [Meaning] directly contemplates the mind and reveals the nature, how can it not be the contemplation of principle in terms of practice?

Chinese Source

問：今文既是約行理觀，那無揀陰及十乘耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [10]: Since the present text is the contemplation of principle in terms of practice, how can it lack the sifting of the aggregates and the ten modes?

Chinese Source

問：本立十法是約行理觀，故廢後附法觀心。約行觀既不成，後文觀心如何廢那？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [11]: [You] originally established the ten dharmas as the contemplation of principle in terms of practice, and therefore abolished the later contemplation of the mind attached to dharmas. Since [the claim of] contemplation in terms of practice does not hold, how can the later text on contemplating the mind be abolished?

Does This Profound Meaning Directly Reveal the Nature of Mind?

Chinese Source

問：此玄十種三法乃是正談果法，何得是直顯心性耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [12]: The ten kinds of three dharmas in this Profound [Meaning] are a proper discussion of the dharmas of the fruit; how can they be a direct revelation of the nature of mind?

Chinese Source

問：所引五章但稱「涅槃只是佛性，乃是正談果法。」該於因人佛性，豈是直顯眾生佛性耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [13]: The five sections [you] cite only state: "Nirvāṇa is simply buddha-nature; this is a proper discussion of the dharmas of the fruit." This extends to the buddha-nature of persons at the causal stage; but how is it a direct revelation of the buddha-nature of beings?

Chinese Source

問：如云「游心法界如虛空，則知諸佛之境界，乃是直顯心性。」該得佛法，豈名直顯佛法耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [14]: [You] say, for example: "'Let the mind roam through the dharma-realm as through empty space, and one thereby knows the state of all buddhas' — this is a direct revelation of the nature of mind." This extends to the Buddha-dharma; how can it be called a direct revelation of the Buddha-dharma?

Chinese Source

問：《誥難書》特問此玄正談佛法，那名直顯心性？因何不答？何得二三處改云「予不許直顯法性」耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [15]: The Letter Pressing Difficulties specifically asked: "This Profound [Meaning] properly discusses the Buddha-dharma; how can it be called a direct revelation of the nature of mind?" Why did you not answer? How can you, in two or three places, alter it to say, "I did not allow that it directly reveals the dharma-nature"?

Chinese Source

問：今既牽率而答，何得言心性處，不言直顯？言直顯處不言心性，豈非四字全書恐義乖返耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [16]: Now, since you answer by dragging [things] along, how is it that where you speak of the nature of mind you do not say "directly revealing," and where you say "directly revealing" you do not speak of the nature of mind? Is it not that you fear that if the four characters were written out in full, the meaning would turn back against you?

Chinese Source

問：《答疑書》云「此玄文直顯心性」，今何改云「學者備覽《妙玄》，已知心性遍生遍佛，故觀此果法知是心性。」此豈非《妙玄》自顯心性？此玄不顯心性，何得云此直顯心性耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [17]: The Letter Answering Doubts says: "This text of the Profound [Meaning] directly reveals the nature of mind." Why do you now alter it to say: "Students have fully studied the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] and already know that the nature of mind pervades beings and pervades buddhas; therefore, contemplating these dharmas of the fruit, they know them to be the nature of mind"? Is this not the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] itself revealing the nature of mind? This Profound [Meaning] does not reveal the nature of mind; how can you say that it directly reveals the nature of mind?

Chinese Source

問：予云「良師取意講授，義合諸文」，仁尚不伏。仁立「學者先解《妙玄》，方尋此部」，出何文耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [18]: I said, "A good teacher takes the intent and lectures and transmits it, its meaning matching the various texts," and you still do not accept it. You set up [the claim that] "students first understand the Wondrous Profound [Meaning], and only then study this work" — from what text does that come?

Chinese Source

問：既此玄直顯心性，故十法皆以理融；《妙玄》不直顯心性，故十法不以理融。學者解彼心性尚能融於他部，何不自融當部而更觀心融之耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [19]: Since this Profound [Meaning] directly reveals the nature of mind, its ten dharmas are all fused by principle; since the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] does not directly reveal the nature of mind, its ten dharmas are not fused by principle. If students who understand the nature of mind from that [work] can still fuse other works, why does it not fuse its own work by itself, instead of further contemplating the mind to fuse it?

Chinese Source

問：此玄十法，文顯標云「為未有智眼約信解分別」，那云純被《妙玄》深達心性人耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [20]: The text of this Profound [Meaning] on the ten dharmas plainly states that it is "for those who do not yet have the knowledge-eye, distinguishing in terms of faith and understanding." How can you say it is addressed purely to people who have deeply penetrated the nature of mind through the Wondrous Profound [Meaning]?

Chinese Source

問：秖為此玄附於如來所游十法，廣示心觀，故至經文不論觀解。何得據被廢此觀心耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [21]: It is just because this Profound [Meaning], attaching to the ten dharmas that the Tathāgata roams in, shows the contemplation of the mind at length, that when it comes to the text of the sūtra it does not discuss contemplative explanation. How can you, on the basis of whom it is addressed to, abolish this contemplation of the mind?

Chinese Source

問：此玄大師被在日當機，故須即示修法，《涅槃玄》是滅後私制，既非當眾策觀，故且缺如。以託講者倣諸部授人，那云學者自知耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [22]: This Profound [Meaning] the Great Master addressed to the capacities present in his lifetime, so he needed to show the method of cultivation immediately. The Profound [Meaning] of the Nirvāṇa was privately composed after his passing; since it was not urging contemplation before an assembly, it was left lacking for the time being, relying on lecturers to model themselves on the other works in teaching people. How can you say that students know it of themselves?

For Whom the Contemplation of the Mind Was Taught

Chinese Source

問：《妙經》文疏雖敘偏小，本被習圓之人，故附文作觀多分在圓，令一一文不違所習。據何文證知是久習止觀之人？豈大師講《妙經》時預為玉泉寺修止觀人示觀心耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [23]: Although the text and commentary of the Wondrous Sūtra narrate the partial and the small, they were originally addressed to people practicing the perfect; so the contemplation attached to the text is for the most part in the perfect, so that each passage does not go against what they practice. By what textual evidence do you know they were people who had long practiced calming-and-contemplation? When the Great Master lectured on the Wondrous Sūtra, did he show the contemplation of the mind in advance for people who would later cultivate calming-and-contemplation at Yuquan Monastery?

Chinese Source

問：所據觀心銷開等，欲成觀心，銷文是要，且開等具於四釋，最後旁用觀心銷之。觀銷若要，何不居初？又何文云觀心銷是要耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [24]: As to what you rely on — "the contemplation of the mind interprets, opens up, and so on" — [you say that] to accomplish the contemplation of the mind, interpreting the text is essential. Yet opening up and so on are present in all four explanations, and at the very end the contemplation of the mind is used, secondarily, to interpret [the text]. If contemplative interpretation is essential, why is it not placed first? And what text says that interpreting by the contemplation of the mind is essential?

Chinese Source

問：大師說玄疏時，尚未說圓頓止觀，何得純為久習圓頓止觀人示事法觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [25]: When the Great Master expounded the Profound [Meaning] and the Commentary, he had not yet expounded the perfect and sudden calming-and-contemplation; how can [they] have shown the contemplations of phenomena and dharmas purely for people who had long practiced the perfect and sudden calming-and-contemplation?

Chinese Source

問：《妙玄》觀心，令即聞即修，不待觀境。那云「指示行人須依止觀中修」耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [26]: The contemplation of the mind in the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] has one cultivate as soon as one hears, without waiting for the object of contemplation. How can you say, "It instructs practitioners that they must cultivate within calming-and-contemplation"?

Chinese Source

問：若廢此文觀心，何以稱久修者本習耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [27]: If the contemplation of the mind in this text is abolished, how can it match what those of long cultivation originally practiced?

Chinese Source

問：本習既是揀境修觀，今文亦揀，恰稱本習，豈以太稱而以為非耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [28]: Since what they originally practiced is sifting the object and cultivating contemplation, and the present text also sifts, it exactly matches what they originally practiced. Do you consider it wrong because it matches too well?

Chinese Source

問：若廢此文，觀心將何以指示，令於止觀中修耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [29]: If this text is abolished, by what will the contemplation of the mind give instruction, so that one cultivates within calming-and-contemplation?

Chinese Source

問：止觀既揀境修觀，今文預揀示之，有何乖違耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [30]: Since calming-and-contemplation sifts the object and cultivates contemplation, and the present text shows the sifting in advance, what contradiction is there?

The Ten Dharmas and the Need to Contemplate the Mind

Chinese Source

問：《發揮》本據十法有六即義，故不觀心，《妙玄》十法一一細示六即，何故却云「彼文須有觀心觀於十法」耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [31]: Bringing Out [the Meaning] originally relied on [the claim that] the ten dharmas have the meaning of the six identities, and therefore do not contemplate the mind. Yet the ten dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] show the six identities in detail, one by one; why then do you say, "That text must have a contemplation of the mind to contemplate the ten dharmas"?

Chinese Source

問：此玄十法以一法性貫之，故不須觀心。《妙玄》十法豈不以一理貫之？何故須有觀心耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [32]: [You say that] the ten dharmas of this Profound [Meaning] are threaded through by the one dharma-nature, and therefore do not need the contemplation of the mind. Are the ten dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] not threaded through by one principle? Why do they need the contemplation of the mind?

Chinese Source

問：仁以此三法欲類《淨名疏》法無眾生，具觀心義。彼約研心修觀辨三法，此談果證三法，那具觀心義耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [33]: You wish to class these three dharmas with "the Dharma has no living beings" in the Commentary on the Vimalakīrti, as having the meaning of contemplating the mind. That [text] distinguishes the three dharmas in terms of examining the mind and cultivating contemplation; this [text] discusses the three dharmas of the realized fruit. How does it have the meaning of contemplating the mind?

Chinese Source

問：仁立十法，只是三諦異名，故具觀心義。既類法無眾生，彼約所觀、所顯，能觀、能顯，能破、能破，助道、正道，自行、利物論三法，此既一向是所顯諦理，安類彼文具觀心義耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [34]: You hold that the ten dharmas are only different names for the three truths, and therefore have the meaning of contemplating the mind. Since you class them with "the Dharma has no living beings": that [text] discusses the three dharmas in terms of what is contemplated and what is revealed, what contemplates and what reveals, what destroys and what destroys, the aid to the path and the proper path, self-practice and benefiting beings. Since this [text] is wholly the principle of the truths that is revealed, how can it be classed with that text as having the meaning of contemplating the mind?

Chinese Source

問：又云「此三法具修性義，故具觀心義。」釋毘耶離城，具論修性三德，何故更示觀心耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [35]: You further say: "These three dharmas contain the meaning of cultivation and nature, and therefore have the meaning of contemplating the mind." In explaining the city of Vaiśālī, the three virtues of cultivation and nature are fully discussed; why does it further show the contemplation of the mind?

Chinese Source

問：此十法從三德至三道而辨《妙玄》十法，從眾生心性三道辨至極果，一一皆具六即，何故却須用觀心觀之？此文何故不用觀之耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [36]: These ten dharmas are distinguished from the three virtues down to the three paths, while the ten dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] are distinguished from the three paths of the nature of beings' minds up to the ultimate fruit, each fully containing the six identities. Why then must [the latter] use the contemplation of the mind to contemplate them? And why does this text not use it to contemplate them?

Chinese Source

問：攝事入陰用觀顯理方名攝事成理，故《妙玄》五義正觀心文俱明觀陰。仁何但云攝事歸理，不云入陰觀理耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [37]: Only gathering phenomena into the aggregates and using contemplation to reveal principle is called gathering phenomena to accomplish principle; therefore, in the five meanings of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning], the passages that properly contemplate the mind all clarify contemplating the aggregates. Why do you speak only of gathering phenomena and returning them to principle, and not of entering the aggregates to contemplate principle?

Chinese Source

問：今十法文既不攝入陰心，又無觀法顯理，那名事法理觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [38]: Since the present text on the ten dharmas neither gathers them into the mind of the aggregates nor has a method of contemplation that reveals principle, how can it be called the contemplation of principle through phenomena and dharmas?

Chinese Source

問：《辨》顯立十法純是理觀，修證之法同《普賢觀》，何故改云只有理觀義耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [39]: Discerning [Errors] plainly established that the ten dharmas are purely the contemplation of principle, and that the method of cultivation and realization is the same as the Samantabhadra Contemplation. Why do you change it to say that it only has the meaning of the contemplation of principle?

The Four Samādhis and the Two Contemplations

Chinese Source

問：常坐雖觀三道事境，既非起心末事，又非借事立觀，乃是直顯心性，那名事觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [40]: Although constant sitting contemplates the phenomenal objects of the three paths, it neither arouses the mind on trivial phenomena nor borrows phenomena to set up contemplation; it directly reveals the nature of mind. How can it be called the contemplation of phenomena?

Chinese Source

問：觀於一念及三道，皆是直附事境觀，只是一種理觀，那名事、理二觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [41]: Contemplating a single thought and the three paths is in every case contemplation directly attached to a phenomenal object; it is just one kind, the contemplation of principle. How can it be called two contemplations, of phenomena and of principle?

Chinese Source

問：常行觀相好是立事境，三觀依之顯理方成一種觀法，那名事、理二觀耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [42]: Constant walking, contemplating the marks and excellent features, sets up a phenomenal object; the three contemplations rely on it to reveal principle, and only so make one kind of contemplative method. How can it be called two contemplations, of phenomena and of principle?

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [183]: Since you grant that the Gates of Nonduality gathers up the ten wondrous aspects as the overall framework of Calming and Contemplation, how can you not allow teachers who have grasped the intent to bring the wondrous intent of the ten gates into the phenomena and dharmas as the essence of contemplation?

Question [185]: When beginners practice the contemplations of phenomena and dharmas in the Profound [Meaning] and the Commentary, a teacher takes the general explanation of Calming and Contemplation. Just as [that work] cites many sūtras to explain cultivating and entering calming-and-contemplation, would that be called cultivating and entering through the many sūtras?

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [186]: If one first practiced calming-and-contemplation without awakening, and now, passing through the contemplative gates of phenomena and dharmas, attains awakening — as when one carries the method of contemplating non-arising over into the gate of arising — would it be called awakening at the gate of non-arising?

Applying the analogy to gathering phenomena to accomplish principle: although it does not say "entering the aggregates," since the later contemplative explanation sets up the object of the aggregates, we verify that only gathering phenomena into the aggregates to contemplate principle is called gathering phenomena to accomplish principle. How can you wrongly say that I mean the dharma-nature has nothing to do with the three thousand?

Question [188]: In your Letter Answering Doubts you cited this passage as the perfect discussion of the dharma-nature, its meaning the same as the contemplation of principle. Since [you took] the contemplation of principle as not penetrating the aggregates, this verifies that at that time you did not know that [contemplation] in terms of practice must contemplate the mind of the aggregates. If not, why did you cite this to prove a contemplation of principle without the aggregates?

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [189]: The Wondrous Profound [Meaning] threads the ten dharmas through with the three tracks; this Profound [Meaning] threads the ten dharmas through with the immeasurable, very deep dharma-nature. Why can only this one be said to fuse dharmas by the dharma-nature?

問：若《妙玄》尚不以法性融圓果十法，何能融餘因果？又何能開二乘耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [190]: If the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] does not even fuse the ten dharmas of the perfect fruit by the dharma-nature, how could it fuse the other causes and fruits? And how could it open up the two vehicles?

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [191]: The Letter Answering Doubts plainly said that because that Profound [Meaning] does not fuse by the dharma-nature, it uses the contemplation of the mind to contemplate the preceding ten dharmas. How do you now turn to say that it contemplates the three tracks of the three teachings and the marks of the cited texts?

Chinese Source

問：《妙玄》三教三軌，既云從一開三，又一一皆以如來藏攝，何得却用觀心融之？此玄三教十法，未云從實開出，未成藏理融攝，何以却不用觀心融耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [192]: The three tracks of the three teachings in the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] are said to open three out of one, and further each is gathered in by the tathāgata-garbha; why should it on the contrary use the contemplation of the mind to fuse them? The ten dharmas of the three teachings in this Profound [Meaning] are not yet said to be opened out from the real, and are not yet gathered in and fused by the principle of the garbha; why then does it not use the contemplation of the mind to fuse them?

Final Questions

Chinese Source

問：《義例》自云「託事導情」，何得自撰附法觀情耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [193]: Guiding Principles of Calming and Contemplation itself says "relying on phenomena to guide feeling"; how can you make up on your own [the claim that] the contemplation attached to dharmas [concerns] feeling?

Chinese Source

問：若託事一向導情，全無修相，何故耆山觀中令於此明方便正修耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [194]: If relying on phenomena were wholly guiding feeling, with no marks of cultivation at all, why in the contemplation of Vulture Peak does it have one clarify the expedients and the proper cultivation right here?

Chinese Source

問：縱云附汝遣執教忘行之情者，《妙玄》十法皆以一念用六即辨至極果，何故更用觀心示行？此玄但約信解說四教法相，何能自遣忘行之情，那無觀心示行耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [195]: Even if you say [it is] attached [to dharmas to dispel] your feeling of clinging to the teaching and forgetting practice — the ten dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] all use the six identities on a single thought to distinguish up to the ultimate fruit; why does it further use the contemplation of the mind to show practice? This Profound [Meaning] only explains the dharma-marks of the four teachings in terms of faith and understanding; how can it by itself dispel the feeling of forgetting practice, and how can it lack the contemplation of the mind that shows practice?

Chinese Source

問：《妙玄》十法附妙法題，那非理融？此玄十法附世金喻，却得是理融耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [196]: The ten dharmas of the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] are attached to the title "Wondrous Dharma"; how are they not fused by principle? The ten dharmas of this Profound [Meaning] are attached to the worldly simile of gold; how is it that they are fused by principle?

Chinese Source

問：《止觀》結題說為結文相，事法觀心，處處言為行立，令即聞即修，安輒類之耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [197]: In Calming and Contemplation, the concluding of the titles is said to conclude the marks of the text; the contemplation of the mind on phenomena and dharmas is everywhere said to be set up for practice, having one cultivate as soon as one hears. How can you rashly class them together?

Chinese Source

問：發心中觀心約自己心數眾生，明乎與拔之觀，那自撰為結其文相及示行耶？

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [198]: The contemplation of the mind in [the section on] arousing the mind clarifies the contemplation of giving [happiness] and removing [suffering] in terms of the beings [among] the mental factors of one's own mind. How can you make it up on your own as concluding the marks of the text and showing practice?

Chinese Source

問：仁元執云「此玄所談十法純是理觀，直顯心性超過《妙玄》，故廢附法觀心」，今何全同《法界次第》列諸法數，却是學三觀者自用觀法融之，則成此玄全不談理觀、全不顯心性，乃自《發揮》至今義狀，所說一時傾敗，此文觀心自成真說。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question [199]: You originally held: "The ten dharmas discussed in this Profound [Meaning] are purely the contemplation of principle, directly revealing the nature of mind and surpassing the Wondrous Profound [Meaning]; therefore [we] abolish the contemplation of the mind attached to dharmas." Why now are you entirely the same as the Sequential Gates of the Dharma-Realm, listing the numbered categories of dharmas, so that those who study the three contemplations fuse them with the method of contemplation themselves? Then this Profound [Meaning] does not discuss the contemplation of principle at all and does not reveal the nature of mind at all; so all the statements of meaning from Bringing Out [the Meaning] until now collapse at once, and the contemplation of the mind in this text becomes of itself the true teaching.

These items of meaning all concern the intent behind [your] abolishing and setting up of the contemplation of the mind until now. If you cannot answer these questions, or answer without exhausting the principle, it will show that you falsely refuted the correct text on contemplating the mind, and further that the Venerable's mind and conduct are full of deceit, his words without standard, concealing and not speaking of the meanings he has already fallen into. I ask the Venerable only to write out the listed words of the questions truthfully in the first item, and to write the words of answer he presents in the following text. Do not put your own meanings in an earlier letter, of the kind like "not interpreting the text well"; do not say that a later section also answers, of the sort like "directly revealing the nature of mind." So that false and correct may be discerned and victory and defeat made clear, one must reach the ultimate principle, and will surely see where it returns.

Ru-Yi Translation

Two Hundred Questions on Contemplating the Mind, from the Bequeathed Writings of Fazhi

Reading Notes for Part 1

Translator's notes

Questions without answers: The text consists of Zhili's questions only, each introduced by 問 with no number; no answers by Qingzhao are recorded. The numbers "Question [1]" to "Question [199]" are added in brackets for reference. The preface promises "two hundred questions under ten headings," but the ten headings are not marked in the text; the subheadings in this translation are the translator's.

The parties and their writings: "The Venerable Zhao" (昭上人) is Qingzhao of Fantian Monastery, Qiantang; "your late teacher" (先師) is Yuanqing, author of Pointing to the Pearl (示珠指, a commentary on the Ten Gates of Nonduality). Bringing Out [the Meaning] (發揮) is Wu'en's Fahui ji on the abbreviated Golden Light Profound Meaning; Discerning Errors (辨訛) is the off-mountain reply to Zhili's Record Resolving Difficulties and Supporting the School (here Supporting the School, 扶宗). The letters Questioning Doubts (問疑書), Pressing Difficulties (詰難書), and Ten Meanings (十義書) are Zhili's; Answering Doubts (答疑書) and Explaining Difficulties (釋難書) are Qingzhao's. Zhili's Letter of Ten Meanings survives as T1936 and repeats many of the same arguments and quotations.

"This Profound [Meaning]" (此玄) is the Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra; the Wondrous Profound [Meaning] (妙玄) is the Profound Meaning of the Lotus; "the Gist" (大意) is Zhanran's Gist of Calming and Contemplation, except in Question [46], where "the Great Purport" is the first chapter of Calming and Contemplation. Miaole (妙樂) is Zhanran's Notes on the Textual Commentary on the Lotus (T1719); "this shows that the contemplative understanding differs from other sūtras" (Q 48) is found there. Its sentence "the objects of all contemplations do not go beyond the five aggregates" (Q 54–55) follows the English of the round-two Notes on the Golden Light Textual Commentary.

Quotations reused from the site: "Let the mind roam through the dharma-realm as through empty space, and one thereby knows the state of all buddhas" (Q 14) follows the site's Profound Meaning of the Lotus, which quotes it from the Avataṃsaka; "mind is like an illusory flame" (Q 68) and "beings and buddhas are lofty and vast" (Q 169, Zhili's paraphrase of "the dharma of sentient beings is exceedingly vast, and the dharma of the Buddha exceedingly lofty") are from the same work. "The Dharma has no living beings" (Q 33, 34, 147, 172) follows the site's Vimalakīrti Sūtra.

Quotations reused from round-two drafts: "Sit upright and be mindful of the true mark" (Q 6) follows the draft Samantabhadra Contemplation Sūtra; "without entering samādhi, simply through reciting and upholding" (Q 139) follows the draft Lotus Samādhi Repentance Rite (T1941). Nanyue's "reciting the Lotus with a scattered mind" is also quoted in the Fuxing (T1912). "First understand that outer form and mind are a single thought that is no thought," "stages are established to sift out false claims," and "the muddy, as it forms, is originally present" (Q 83, 106, 161) follow the vault draft of the Ten Gates of Nonduality. The Annotations' "without waiting for the object of contemplation" (T1717) follows the round-two Gleanings.

Sources partly or not located: The Vajra Scalpel sentence in Q 138, "relying on phenomena to guide feeling" (Q 193), and the Annotations' "beings and buddhas are in the mind, yet it still definitely belongs to the cause" (Q 84) were not found word for word in the Taishō files; Zhili may be paraphrasing. The Pointing to the Pearl sentences (Q 79) come from a work not in the Taishō (it is in the Supplement, X).

Contemplations of the mountain and the city (山城觀): These are the contemplative explanations in the Textual Commentary on the Lotus of "dwelling in the city of Rājagṛha, on Vulture Peak" and of the assembly of twelve thousand. "The lodging" (房宿, Q 53, 62) is a literal rendering; the exact passage was not identified. "Poduo" (婆多, Q 61) is probably Bhadrapāla, whose name in the Lotus assembly leads Zhanran to cite the Pratyutpanna Samādhi Sūtra.

The three kinds of contemplation: As in the Gleanings, 約行／附法／託事 are "in terms of practice / attached to dharmas / relying on phenomena"; 事法觀 is "the contemplations of phenomena and dharmas"; 約行理觀 "the contemplation of principle in terms of practice"; 事法理觀 "the contemplation of principle through phenomena and dharmas"; 揀境／揀陰 "sifting the object / sifting the aggregates"; 陰境 "the object of the aggregates."

Apparent copying errors: Q 15 (unit 17) 誥難書 for 詰難書 (both names are rendered Letter Pressing Difficulties); Q 34 能破、能破, probably for 能破、所破 ("what destroys and what is destroyed"), translated as written; Q 9 (unit 11) 行約理觀 for 約行理觀; Q 93 不情 apparently for 有情 ("the sentient"); Q 114 示殊 for 示珠; Q 153 誙 read as 誣 ("falsely charge"); preface 母使 read as 毋使. The final note 或作徵索 repeats the same two characters, so the variant has been lost.

Q 156, 本定經: CBETA punctuates 《本定經》 as a sūtra title, but no such sūtra is known, and the words quoted ("the three are without distinction") are from the Avataṃsaka. The translation reads 本定 as "originally to settle [it]" and 經中 as "in the sūtra." Alternatively, it could be a corrupt title.

Q 137, punctuation: The list punctuates 豈是驢車餘文？或有此斥; the translation follows the reading 豈是驢車？餘文或有此斥 ("how is that a donkey cart? Where other passages may have this rebuke…"). The words are the same; only the pause moves.

Q 89: 對陰為能對九為所 is taken as "active in relation to the aggregates, passive in relation to the nine [modes]," with the nine being the second to tenth of the ten modes. The opening 初棄於陰 is unclear; it is rendered "discarding [the others] for the aggregates."

Q 145, 頑境踏心: The meaning is uncertain; rendered literally as "dull objects and trampling the mind."
