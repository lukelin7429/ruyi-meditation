Two Hundred Questions on Contemplating the Mind.

In the Song-dynasty dispute over whether the section on contemplating the mind in Zhiyi's Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra is authentic, Siming Zhili (四明知禮, 960–1028) sent his opponent Qingzhao this list of objections in 1007. After a short preface come 199 bare questions, each pressing a point in the off-mountain position: whether the ten sets of three dharmas already reveal the mind, how the object of contemplation is sifted, and how teaching and contemplation differ. No answers are recorded; the questions themselves are the argument.

Two Hundred Questions on Contemplating the Mind
This short work belongs to the Song-dynasty dispute between Siming Zhili and the "off-mountain" (shanwai) teachers over the expanded version of Zhiyi's Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra, and in particular over whether its section on contemplating the mind is authentic.
