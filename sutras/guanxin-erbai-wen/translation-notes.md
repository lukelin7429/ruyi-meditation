# Two Hundred Questions on Contemplating the Mind — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guanxin erbai wen* (觀心二百問, Taishō T46, no. 1935 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1935, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 觀心 | contemplating the mind | The practice whose place in the Golden Light commentary is in dispute. |
| 陰境 | the object of the aggregates | The ordinary deluded mind taken as the object of contemplation. |
| 十種三法 | the ten sets of three dharmas | The series of triads in the Golden Light Profound Meaning. |
