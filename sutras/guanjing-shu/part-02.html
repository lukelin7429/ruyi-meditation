The Sixteen Contemplations, the Nine Grades, and the Transmission

Part 2 comments on the main body of the Contemplation Sūtra and on its closing section. It begins with the light from the Buddha's brow and his smile, which the commentary reads as two answers to Vaidehī's two requests: the three kinds of pure karma (the Three Blessings) answer her request for "reflection," and the sixteen contemplations answer her request for "right absorption." It then lists the sixteen contemplations and groups them: six on the dependent fruit (the land), seven on the principal reward (the Buddha and bodhisattvas), and three on the three classes and nine grades of rebirth. For each contemplation the commentary mostly gives a short outline of the sūtra passage and glosses a few terms. Longer discussions take up "this mind makes the buddha; this mind is the buddha," the three kinds of loving-kindness, a calculation showing that the stated height of Amitāyus's body must contain a translator's error, and several apparent conflicts with the Immeasurable Life Sūtra and Vasubandhu's Treatise on Rebirth — whether followers of the two vehicles, women, and those who commit the five heinous transgressions can be reborn. The commentary ends with the benefits to Vaidehī and her attendants and the twofold transmission, at the palace and on Vulture Peak.

Pure Karma: The Light and the Smile

Chinese Source

◎「爾時世尊放眉間光」下，第二、正說。文為三：初明淨業、次辯妙觀、三利益。如來眉間有白毫相，猶如珂雪，長一丈五尺。毫有八楞，周圍五寸。其毫中空，右旋宛轉，如瑠璃筒。從此發光照無量國，還住佛頂變為金臺。廣現諸國，令韋提希樂生安養。初、放光酬前請於生處。次、「世尊微笑」下，酬前淨業近答。思惟正受三種淨業，散心思量名曰思惟，十六正觀說名正受。就初有二：第一、答其生處。「惟願」下，明見淨土，更請淨國之因。初、放光普示諸土。次、「或有」下，示土差別。「韋提」下，示生處。思惟是願，願思是業。正問其因。「正受」者，非邪曰正，領納名受，即第二問觀行。

Ru-Yi Translation

From "Then the World-Honored One released light from between his eyebrows" on is the second [division], the main teaching. The text is threefold: first, making clear pure karma; next, discussing the wondrous contemplations; third, the benefits. Between the Tathāgata's brows is the mark of the white tuft, like white jade or snow, fifteen feet long. The tuft has eight edges and is five inches around. The tuft is hollow, coiling to the right, like a tube of lapis lazuli. From it he sent forth light, illuminating immeasurable lands; [the light] returned and settled on the Buddha's crown, transforming into a golden platform. He showed the various lands widely, so that Vaidehī would delight in being born in [the land of] Peaceful Nourishment. First, emitting light replies to the earlier request concerning a place of birth. Next, from "the World-Honored One smiled" on, it replies to the earlier [request about] pure karma, the near answer. Reflection and right absorption: the three kinds of pure karma, reflecting and considering with a scattered mind, are called "reflection" (思惟); the sixteen right contemplations are called "right absorption" (正受). Within the first there are two [parts]: first, answering about the place of birth; from "I ask only that" on, making clear that, having seen the pure lands, she further asks for the cause of the pure land. First, emitting light, he shows all the lands universally. Next, from "Some lands" on, he shows the differences among the lands. From "Then Vaidehī" on, he shows the place of birth. "Reflection" is the vow; vowing and reflecting is the karma; this directly asks about its cause. "Right absorption" (正受): not deviant is called "right" (正); taking in and receiving is called "absorption" (受); this is the second question, about contemplative practice.

Chinese Source

微笑中有二：初明三種淨業答思惟。「汝是凡夫」下，次明十六妙觀答正受。初業共凡夫、次共二乘、後是大乘不共之法。初淨業中有三：第一、明三種淨業。「告阿難」下，第二、歎其所問妙契佛心。從「阿難汝當受持」下，第三、略付阿難，令持獲利也。就初復三：初、明光照頻婆獲道。次、「世尊告韋提汝今知不」下，舉果勸修因。三、「欲生彼國者」下，明往生之因也。何以不直答其土因，而復放光微笑耶？解有二：初為次增道、次欲使王與夫人因光相見。王既覩光增道，知國非實，視死如眠。夫人見王無憂，觀法成果也。微笑如釋種被誅，如來光色益顯，正以如來善達因緣，業報無差對至叵避。王雖應死而獲道跡，夫人幽縶即是現淨土之緣，有此多緣，所以致笑也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Within the smile there are two [parts]: first, making clear the three kinds of pure karma, answering "reflection"; from "You are an ordinary being" on, next making clear the sixteen wondrous contemplations, answering "right absorption." The first [kind of] karma is shared with ordinary beings, the next is shared with the two vehicles, and the last is the unshared dharma of the Mahāyāna. Within the first, pure karma, there are three: first, making clear the three kinds of pure karma; from "said to Ānanda" on, second, praising her question as wondrously matching the Buddha's mind; from "Ānanda, you must uphold this teaching" on, third, briefly entrusting it to Ānanda so that he will uphold it and gain benefit. Within the first again there are three: first, making clear that the light illuminates Bimbisāra and he attains the path. Next, from "the World-Honored One said to Vaidehī: 'Do you know this now?'" on, raising the fruit to encourage cultivating the cause. Third, from "Anyone who wishes to be born in that land" on, making clear the cause of rebirth. Why did he not answer directly about the cause of that land, but instead emit light and smile? There are two explanations: first, so that [the king] would next increase in the path; second, so that the king and the queen would see each other by means of the light. The king, having seen the light and increased in the path, knew that the realm was not real and regarded death as sleep. The queen, seeing that the king was without sorrow, [would bring] the contemplative dharma to fruition. The smile is like [the time when] the Śākya clan was massacred and the Tathāgata's light and color became all the more evident: precisely because the Tathāgata thoroughly understands causes and conditions — karmic reward does not err, and when its counterpart arrives it cannot be avoided. Though the king was due to die, he gained a trace of the path; the queen's confinement was itself the condition for showing the pure land. Because there were these many conditions, he smiled.

The Three Blessings

Chinese Source

「阿那含」者，第三不還果也。「去此不遠」者，安樂國土去此十萬億佛剎，一一剎恒沙世界，何言不遠？解云：以佛力故欲見即見。又光中現土顯於佛頂，一念能緣，言不遠也。第一「孝養父母奉事師長」，敬上接下，慈心行也。「修十善業」，是其止行，身除三邪、口離四過、意斷三惡也。第二「三歸」者，佛法僧也。在家戒亦即是十戒。「具足諸戒」者，道俗備受，微細不犯。「威儀」者，三千悉皆不缺也。第三「發菩提心」是願，起意趣向，名為發心。菩提是道，佛果圓通，說為菩提。「讀誦大乘」，明修解也。行能運通，說之為乘。餘二不及，是言大也。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Anāgāmin" is the third fruit, the non-returner. "Not far from here": the land of Peace and Bliss is a hundred thousand million buddha-fields from here, each field being Ganges-sands of worlds; why say "not far"? Explanation: through the Buddha's power, whoever wishes to see it sees it. Also, the lands appearing in the light are shown on the Buddha's crown, and a single thought can take them as its object; hence it says "not far." First, "to care for and nourish one's father and mother, to serve one's teachers and elders": respecting those above and receiving those below, the practice of a compassionate mind. "To cultivate the ten wholesome actions" is the practice of restraint: the body removes the three wrongs, the mouth abandons the four faults, and the mind cuts off the three evils. Second, "the Three Refuges" are the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Saṅgha. The precepts of householders, too, are the ten precepts. "To keep every precept in full": monastics and laity receive them completely and do not violate even the subtle ones. "Proper conduct" (威儀): the three thousand [deportments] are all without lack. Third, "to give rise to the mind of awakening" is the vow: giving rise to the intention and heading toward [it] is called arousing the mind; bodhi (菩提, awakening) is the path, and the fruit of buddhahood, perfect and penetrating, is called bodhi. "To read and recite the Mahāyāna scriptures" makes clear the cultivation of understanding. Practice that can carry and penetrate is called a vehicle; the other two do not reach it, hence it is called "great."

Vaidehī's Request for Future Beings

Chinese Source

「佛告韋提：此三種業三世諸佛淨業正因」，是歎辭也。「諦聽諦聽善思念之」，諦聽令生聞慧，善思思慧念之修慧。「煩惱賊」者，此能損慧命、傷法身，故名為賊也。「即得無生忍」，是初住初地。《仁王經》說五忍：一伏、二信、三順、四無生、五寂滅。初明韋提見土之由。次一問答，明為未來眾生請見土之方法。「汝是凡夫」，彰其分齊，不能遠觀。韋提實大菩薩，此會即得無生忍，示同在凡夫。心想羸劣未得天眼，不能遠照見彼國土，有異方便令汝得見。

Ru-Yi Translation

"The Buddha said to Vaidehī: '. These three kinds of karmic action are the true and proper cause of the pure karma of every buddha of the past, the future, and the present'" is a phrase of praise. "Listen closely, listen closely, and reflect well on this": "listen closely" makes one produce the wisdom from hearing; "reflect well" [produces] the wisdom from reflection; "on this" (念之, keeping it in mind) [produces] the wisdom from cultivation. "The thief of affliction": it can damage the life of wisdom and injure the Dharma-body; hence it is called a thief. "At once attain the acceptance of the non-arising of all dharmas": this is the first abiding or the first ground. The Humane Kings Sūtra speaks of five acceptances: first, subduing; second, faith; third, compliance; fourth, non-arising; fifth, tranquil extinction. First it makes clear the reason Vaidehī sees the land. Next, one question and answer makes clear her asking, for the sake of future beings, for a method of seeing the land. "You are an ordinary being" shows her limits: she cannot see far. Vaidehī is in fact a great bodhisattva, who in this assembly at once attained the acceptance of non-arising; she shows herself as the same as ordinary beings. "Whose power of mind is weak; you have not yet attained the heavenly eye": she cannot illuminate and see that land far away. "[There is] a special skillful means that will enable you to see it."

Chinese Source

「異方便」者，即十六觀。非直觀，名方便。「以佛力故見彼國」者，亦是方便也。「韋提白佛如我今者」下，為佛滅後眾生請也。濁者五濁，不善者十不善。「五苦」者，五道非樂，故云五苦。或是五惡五痛五燒。五惡，殺、盜、邪婬、妄語、飲酒，如《大經》現遭厄難，王法刑罰。是五痛也。五燒，即當來墮三途苦毒，名五燒。「云何當見阿彌陀極樂國土」，正為啟請。答中有十六觀：一日觀、二水觀、三地觀、四樹觀、五池觀、六總觀觀一切樓地池等、七華座觀、八佛菩薩像觀、九佛身觀、十觀音觀、十一勢至觀、十二普往生觀、十三雜明佛菩薩觀、十四上品生觀、十五中品生觀、十六下品生觀。就十六觀，分文為三，初六觀觀其依果，次七觀觀其正報，後三明三輩九品往生也。

Ru-Yi Translation

"A special skillful means" is the sixteen contemplations. Because it is not direct contemplation, it is called an expedient (方便). "Through the Buddha's . power I am able to see that land": this too is an expedient. From "Then Vaidehī said to the Buddha: '. as for myself .'" on, she asks for the sake of beings after the Buddha's extinction. "Defiled" (濁) is the five turbidities; "unwholesome" (不善) is the ten unwholesome [deeds]. "The five sufferings": the five paths are not happy, hence it says "five sufferings." Or [they are] the five evils, the five pains, and the five burnings. The five evils are killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, false speech, and drinking alcohol. As in the Great Sūtra, meeting with calamity in the present and the punishments of royal law are the five pains. The five burnings are falling in the future into the poisonous sufferings of the three paths; these are called the five burnings. "How will . ever manage to see Amitābha's land of Sukhāvatī?" is the actual request. In the answer there are sixteen contemplations: first, the contemplation of the sun; second, the contemplation of water; third, the contemplation of the ground; fourth, the contemplation of the trees; fifth, the contemplation of the ponds; sixth, the general contemplation, contemplating all the towers, ground, ponds, and so on; seventh, the contemplation of the lotus throne; eighth, the contemplation of the images of the Buddha and bodhisattvas; ninth, the contemplation of the Buddha's body; tenth, the contemplation of Avalokiteśvara; eleventh, the contemplation of Mahāsthāmaprāpta; twelfth, the universal contemplation of going to be born; thirteenth, the miscellaneous contemplation making clear the Buddha and bodhisattvas; fourteenth, the contemplation of upper-grade birth; fifteenth, the contemplation of middle-grade birth; sixteenth, the contemplation of lower-grade birth. Within the sixteen contemplations the text is divided into three: the first six contemplations contemplate the dependent fruit; the next seven contemplations contemplate the principal reward; the last three make clear rebirth in the three classes and nine grades.

The Six Contemplations of the Dependent Fruit

Chinese Source

第一日觀。示令繫心。「佛告」下，略明繫念，總勸修觀。「云何」下，正明作日觀。「一切有目皆見日沒」下，舉所觀境。「當起想」下，正教觀察。「是為」下，結也。教令正觀，為除疑心。大本所明，以疑惑心修諸功德生彼國者，落在邊地，復受胎生。故作此觀，令除疑惑也。障者，大本言「唯除五逆誹謗正法」，故須作觀。五逆重罪，除六十劫生死罪等，下輩自論。

Ru-Yi Translation

First, the contemplation of the sun. It shows [beings] how to fix the mind. From "The Buddha said" on, it briefly makes clear fixing thought and encourages the cultivation of contemplation in general. From "How is this contemplation made?" on, it directly makes clear the making of the sun contemplation. From "every being . possessed of sight . has seen the sun set" on, it raises the object contemplated. From "Give rise to this thought" on, it directly teaches contemplation and examination. From "This is" on, it concludes. Teaching right contemplation is to remove the doubting mind. As the larger version makes clear, those who cultivate merits with a doubting mind and are born in that land fall into the borderland and further undergo womb-birth. Therefore one makes this contemplation, to remove doubt. As for hindrances: the larger version says, "excepting only those who commit the five heinous transgressions or slander the true Dharma"; therefore one must make the contemplation. The heavy offenses of the five heinous transgressions, the removal of the offenses of sixty eons of birth and death, and so on, the lower class discusses itself.

Chinese Source

第二水觀，初、作水想者舉所觀境界。從「見水澄清」下，正起觀行。「是為水想」下，結觀也。一作水想；二變水成氷；三變氷為瑠璃；四觀瑠璃以成大地內外映徹，地下寶柱承擎，地上諸相莊嚴，以眾寶間錯其地，一一寶出雜色光明。光明成諸樓觀，樓觀兩邊有華幢，幢上多有樂器宣說妙音也。「八種清風」者，彼處實無時節，若寄此八，謂除上下，餘四方四維，故云八。亦可用對八卦也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Second, the contemplation of water. First, "make the contemplation of water" raises the object contemplated. From "see the water clear and still" on, contemplative practice is directly aroused. From "This is the water contemplation" on, the contemplation is concluded. First, making the visualization of water; second, transforming the water into ice; third, transforming the ice into lapis lazuli; fourth, contemplating the lapis lazuli becoming the great ground, transparent within and without, with jeweled pillars beneath the ground upholding it, all kinds of adornment above the ground, the ground interspersed with many jewels, and each jewel emitting light of many colors. The light forms towers and pavilions; on both sides of the towers and pavilions there are flower banners, and on the banners there are many musical instruments proclaiming wondrous sounds. "Eight kinds of pure wind": that place in fact has no seasons; if one relies on these eight [of our world], excluding above and below, the remaining four directions and four intermediate directions make eight. They may also be matched with the eight trigrams.

Chinese Source

第三地觀。文有四：一漸想觀。從「若得三昧」下，第二實觀。「佛告」下，明利益。「作是觀」下，顯觀邪正。前水是想，不能滅罪。地觀是實，故能除斷也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Third, the contemplation of the ground. The text has four [parts]: first, the gradual visualization-contemplation; from "If one attains samādhi" on, second, the real contemplation; from "The Buddha said" on, making clear the benefits; from "To contemplate in this way" on, showing contemplation as right or wrong. The earlier water is visualization and cannot extinguish offenses. The ground contemplation is real, hence it can remove and cut them off.

Chinese Source

第四樹觀。文三：初、明結前生後；次、「觀寶樹」下，正明觀行；「是為」下，結。正觀中有五：一、明樹體；二、明莊嚴相；三、明生法；四、「有大光明」下，現佛國土；五「見樹莖葉」下，結觀也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Fourth, the contemplation of the trees. The text has three [parts]: first, making clear the conclusion of the preceding and the introduction of the following; next, from "contemplate the jeweled trees" on, directly making clear contemplative practice; from "This is" on, the conclusion. Within the direct contemplation there are five: first, making clear the substance of the trees; second, making clear the marks of adornment; third, making clear the dharma of their growth; fourth, from "radiating great light" on, the buddha-lands appear; fifth, from "seeing clearly the trunk, the branches, the leaves" on, concluding the contemplation.

Chinese Source

第五池觀。中有五：一明池體、二明池相、三明隨心適意、四明利益、第五結觀。「摩尼」者，如意珠也。「八功德」者，輕、清、冷、軟、美、不臭、飲時調適、飲已無患。清是色入，不臭香入，輕、冷、軟是觸入，美是味入，調適、無患是法入。

Ru-Yi Translation

Fifth, the contemplation of the ponds. Within it there are five: first, making clear the substance of the ponds; second, making clear the marks of the ponds; third, making clear that they follow the mind and suit one's wishes; fourth, making clear the benefits; fifth, concluding the contemplation. "Maṇi" is the wish-fulfilling jewel. "Eight merits": light, clear, cool, soft, sweet, not foul-smelling, agreeable when drunk, and harmless after drinking. "Clear" is the entrance of form; "not foul-smelling" the entrance of smell; "light," "cool," and "soft" the entrance of touch; "sweet" the entrance of taste; "agreeable" and "harmless" the entrance of dharmas.

Chinese Source

第六總觀。中有四：「眾寶國土」下，明總觀，初寶樓、二樹、三地、四池。觀樓中，初正觀樓，次觀上及虛空中諸音樂聲，結成觀想名為粗見。從「是為」下，二、結。從「若見」下，第三、利益。「作是觀」下，第四、顯觀邪正。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sixth, the general contemplation. Within it there are four: from "Throughout this land of every treasure" on, making clear the general contemplation: first, the jeweled towers; second, the trees; third, the ground; fourth, the ponds. Within contemplating the towers, first one directly contemplates the towers, next one contemplates the sounds of music above and in the sky; the visualization-contemplation, concluded and completed, is called "a rough sighting." From "This is" on, second, the conclusion. From "Whoever sees this" on, third, the benefits. From "To contemplate in this way" on, fourth, showing contemplation as right or wrong.

The Lotus Throne and the Image

Chinese Source

第七明佛身。中有四：第一、「佛告」下，勅聽許說。第二、從「說是語時」下，明佛現身相。第三、從「時韋提」下，為未來請。第四、「佛告」下，酬請廣明佛身。五種觀門：第一觀華座、第二觀像、第三觀佛身、第四觀觀音、第五觀勢至。初華座中有五：一、明成座法用并辯其相。二、「一一金色」下，明能隨機利物。三、「是為華想」下，結觀。四、「阿難如此華」下，明由願力所成。五、「若欲念彼佛」下，明觀未來有利益。

Ru-Yi Translation

Seventh, making clear the Buddha's body. Within it there are four: first, from "The Buddha said" on, commanding them to listen and consenting to speak; second, from "As he spoke these words" on, making clear the Buddha's appearing in bodily form; third, from "When Vaidehī" on, the request for the future; fourth, from "The Buddha said" on, answering the request and making clear the Buddha's body at length. There are five gates of contemplation: first, contemplating the lotus throne; second, contemplating the image; third, contemplating the Buddha's body; fourth, contemplating Avalokiteśvara; fifth, contemplating Mahāsthāmaprāpta. Within the first, the lotus throne, there are five: first, making clear the method and function of forming the throne, together with its marks; second, from "each shade of gold" on, making clear that it can benefit beings according to their capacities; third, from "This is the contemplation of the lotus throne" on, concluding the contemplation; fourth, from "Ānanda: . This wondrous flower" on, making clear that it was formed by the power of vows; fifth, from "If you wish to hold that Buddha in mindfulness" on, making clear that the contemplation brings benefit in the future.

Chinese Source

第八明像想。中有三：初、汎明諸佛法身自在從心想生。二、「是故應當」下，徧觀彼彌陀并示觀行。三、「作是觀者」下，明修觀獲利也。「法界身」者，報佛法性身也。眾生心淨，法身自在，故言入眾生心想中。如似白日昇天影現百川，即是三十二相八十種好，明佛身自在，能隨物現前，明佛菩薩此顯能隨也。又法界身是佛身，無所不遍，法界為體。「入一切眾生心想中」者，得此觀佛三昧解入相應，故言入心想中也。

Ru-Yi Translation

Eighth, making clear the visualization of the image. Within it there are three: first, generally making clear that the Dharma-body of all buddhas, sovereign, is born from the mind's visualization; second, from "therefore you should" on, contemplating Amitābha throughout and showing contemplative practice; third, from "Whoever makes this contemplation" on, making clear that cultivating the contemplation gains benefit. "A body of the dharma-realm" is the dharma-nature body of the reward-buddha. Reading Notes for Part 2

Translator's notes

Sūtra lemmas. All quoted phrases use the site's Contemplation Sūtra wording (pages "Vaidehī's Imprisonment and the Three Blessings," "The First Six Contemplations," "The Lotus Throne and the True Body," "Avalokiteśvara and Mahāsthāmaprāpta," the three "Grades of Rebirth" pages, and "The Final Entrustment"). Ellipses mark where the commentary abbreviates.

Reflection and right absorption (思惟／正受). The site renders Vaidehī's request as "teach me how to hold that land in contemplation, teach me how to enter right absorption upon it." The commentary takes 思惟 as reflection with a scattered mind, answered by the three kinds of pure karma, and 正受 as the sixteen contemplations. In the commentary's own words 思惟 is rendered "reflection." The sentence 酬前淨業近答思惟正受三種淨業 is hard to punctuate; alternatively: "it replies to the earlier [request], the near answer to 'reflection' and 'right absorption' being the three kinds of pure karma."

Visualization (想). In the commentary's own prose 想 is rendered "visualization" (水想, 像想, 漸想觀), to keep it apart from 觀 "contemplation"; the site's sūtra translation renders both as "contemplation," and its wording is kept in quotations. The ground contemplation is "real" (實) because it follows samādhi, while the water stage is still visualization.

Concentrated and dispersed (定／散). The site's Shandao commentary renders 定善／散善 as "contemplative goodness / dispersed goodness." Here 散 is "dispersed" as there; 定 is "concentrated," since "contemplative" would collide with 觀. The commentary's use of the pair (to explain why the larger sūtra excludes the five heinous transgressions) is its own and differs from Shandao's.

Without condition (無緣慈). The site renders the sūtra's 以無緣慈攝諸眾生 as "embracing all beings with a compassion that seeks no cause outside itself." The commentary explains 無緣 through the three kinds of 慈 (conditioned by beings, by dharmas, and without condition), so in its own prose 慈 is "loving-kindness" and 無緣 "without condition." The Nirvāṇa example uses the site's Nirvāṇa Sūtra wording ("I did not, in reality, go … the power of loving-kindness's own wholesome root").

Sources. The fish-roe simile is in the Great Treatise (T1509). The Pratyutpanna quotation corresponds in substance to T0417 (欲來生者當念我名…), not word for word. The Treatise on Rebirth is Vasubandhu's Upadeśa (T1524: 女人及根缺，二乘種不生). The five evils, pains, and burnings are in the Immeasurable Life Sūtra (T0360), though the commentary says 《大經》, rendered Great Sūtra per the settled list; here it probably means the larger Sukhāvatī sūtra, not the Nirvāṇa. "Excepting only those who commit the five heinous transgressions or slander the true Dharma" is the site's Immeasurable Life Sūtra wording. "Entering the deep and broad mind" is in the Daśabhūmika as cited in T1522. The "hundred gateways of Dharma-illumination" passage attributed to the Treatise on the Ten Grounds was not located in T1522; a related passage is in T1485. The Ratnagotra tuft dimensions and the Zhidu lun line on the Prajñāpāramitā: source not located.

The Buddha's height. The commentary argues from the eye-to-body ratio that 恒河沙 in the sūtra's figure is a translator's error. The site's sūtra keeps "sixty koṭi nayutas of Ganges-sands of yojanas"; the commentary's argument is translated as it stands.

Hand or head (但觀手相). The received sūtra reads 但觀首相 ("the mark upon their heads"); the commentary's text read 手相 and records two explanations, head and hand. The site's wording is kept in the lemma.

Firm practice (堅固行). This lemma does not appear in the site's Chinese for the thirteenth contemplation; it may reflect a variant text.

Seeds (習種／性種／道種). Rendered the seed of habituation / the seed of nature / the seed of the path (the bodhisattva "lineages" of the Jeweled Necklace Sūtra). The commentary first says the upper grade runs "from the seed of habituation," then assigns it to the seed of the path; the inconsistency is in the Chinese.

"Why does it not reach the nine grades?" (何以不及九品). The phrase is odd; possibly an error for 上品 ("the upper grade"). Translated as written.

Sūtra titles. The commentary's version of the second title reads 淨業生諸佛前; the lemma follows the site's sūtra ("The Purification and Removal of Karmic Hindrance …"). Its summary "also called 'Extinguishing and Removing Karmic Hindrances'" (滅除業障) is the commentary's own wording.

Emanation and transformation bodies (化身). The site's sūtra renders 化身 as "emanation-bodies" in the twelfth contemplation; the commentary's 化身 is "transformation-bodies," as in Part 1.
