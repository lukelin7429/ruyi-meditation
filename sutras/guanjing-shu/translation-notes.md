# Commentary on the Contemplation Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guan wuliangshou fo jing shu* (觀無量壽佛經疏, Taishō T37, no. 1750 (attributed to Zhiyi)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1750, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). The work dates from the Sui or early Tang; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 心觀為宗、實相為體 | the contemplation of mind as cardinal doctrine, the true mark as essence | The commentary's reading of the sūtra. |
| 想／觀 | visualization / contemplation | Kept apart in English because the commentary distinguishes them. |
| 四土 | the four lands | Where sages and ordinary beings dwell together; of expedients; of true reward; of eternal tranquil light. |
| 三身 | the three bodies | The Dharma-body, the reward-body, the response-body. |
