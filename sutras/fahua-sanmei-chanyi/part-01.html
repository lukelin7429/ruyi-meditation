Zunshi's Preface; Preparation, the Ten Methods, Offering, Invitation, and Praise

The Lotus Samādhi Repentance Rite (T1941) is Zhiyi's manual for the twenty-one-day Lotus samādhi, the practice that Calming and Contemplation classes as "half walking and half sitting" and refers to a separately circulating text. This part opens with a preface by a much later author, Ciyun Zunshi (964–1032) of the Song, who explains that he collated the old copies and printed what he took to be the original text, removing a fifth phrase that some copies had added to the fourfold investigation of mind in the seated contemplation. Zhiyi's rite then follows. Section One says who should undertake the practice and why: the Lotus is the secret treasury of the tathāgatas, and three sets of seven days of single-minded diligence can purify the six faculties and bring the sight of Samantabhadra. Section Two prescribes a week of preliminary practice; Section Three distinguishes the single mind in phenomena from the single mind in principle. Section Four lists the ten methods of the rite and begins to set them out: adorning the place of practice, purifying the body, offering with the three kinds of action, inviting the Three Jewels by name, and praising them. Throughout, spoken formulas are paired with instructions on posture and on what to visualize.

Zunshi's Preface on Establishing the Original Text

Chinese Source

法華三昧懺儀勘定元本序

Ru-Yi Translation

Preface on Establishing the Original Text of the Lotus Samādhi Repentance Rite

Chinese Source

宋天竺寺傳天台教觀沙門遵式述

Ru-Yi Translation

Composed by Zunshi, a śramaṇa of Tianzhu Monastery in the Song, transmitter of the Tiantai teaching and contemplation

Chinese Source

聖教浸遠，文句舛錯，由傳者浮昧，若不校其同異、明示得失，日增月甚，遐喪真味。《法華三昧儀》者，天台大師瓦官親筆，蓋止觀第三三昧所指別行，即其文也。若夫啟迪後學，為時所宗，破障壞魔、入佛境界，與夫文殊、普賢並驅寶輅，遊方至極者，實惟斯漸矣。

Ru-Yi Translation

The sacred teachings grow ever more distant, and their texts and phrases are confused and in error, because those who transmit them are shallow and benighted. If one does not collate where [the copies] agree and differ and clearly show what is right and wrong in them, [the errors] will increase by the day and grow by the month, and the true flavor will be lost far away. The Lotus Samādhi Rite was written by the Great Master of Tiantai in his own hand at Waguan [Monastery]; what the third samādhi in Calming and Contemplation points to as circulating separately is this very text. As for opening the way for later students and being taken as a model by its time, breaking obstructions and destroying Māra, entering the realm of the buddhas, and driving side by side with Mañjuśrī and Samantabhadra in the jeweled carriage, traveling to the very ultimate — truly it is only by this gradual [way].

Chinese Source

患其稍易舊章或亡精要，且十科行軌理觀為主，儻一以誤，九法徒施，有於坐禪觀法加成五句者，今列示之。文云「為因心故心，為不因心故心，為亦因心亦不因心故心，為非因心非不因心故心（元文），為非非因心非非不因心故心（近加）。」

Ru-Yi Translation

I am troubled that [copies] have slightly altered the old text, or lost its essentials. Moreover, among the ten sections of the rite of practice, the contemplation of principle is the chief; if that one is once in error, the other nine methods are carried out in vain. There are [copies] that, in the method of contemplation in seated meditation, have added [a phrase] to make five phrases; I now set them out to show this. The text says: "Is it mind because of a mind? Is it mind not because of a mind? Is it mind both because of a mind and not because of a mind? Is it mind neither because of a mind nor not because of a mind? (the original text) Is it mind neither not-because-of-a-mind nor not-not-because-of-a-mind? (a recent addition)"

Chinese Source

且山家凡約句法用觀，秖但至四，未知五句出自何文？又當推檢之際，第五句下準何為境？如何用觀？《輔行》自云「彼別行文但推四句，故今文中廣修象觀以廣於彼（《輔行》正文）。」況彼象觀猶是歷事，而正觀一門全今四句，豈應疑誤彼之大體哉？而復輒引經文繫乎卷末，濫回麁注錯其篇內，細碎之失莫得而舉也。故今直勘元本刻板印行，庶存先制，而今而後，求三昧者欲傳斯文，請固存此序用以區別。

Ru-Yi Translation

Now when the Mountain House applies contemplation by way of the forms of phrases, it goes only as far as four; I do not know from what text the five phrases come. Further, at the time of investigating, under the fifth phrase, what is to be taken as the object? How is contemplation to be applied? The Fuxing itself says: "That separately circulating text investigates only the four phrases; therefore in the present text the contemplation of the elephant is cultivated broadly, to expand on that one." (main text of the Fuxing) Moreover, that contemplation of the elephant is still a passing through phenomena, while the one gate of correct contemplation is entirely the four phrases of the present [text]; how could one cast doubt on it and distort its overall form? And further, [copyists] have rashly quoted passages of the sūtras and attached them at the end of the fascicle, and wrongly brought in crude notes and mixed them into its chapters; such petty faults cannot all be listed. Therefore I have now directly collated the original text and had it carved on blocks and printed, hoping to preserve the earlier composition. From now on, those who seek the samādhi and wish to transmit this text, please be sure to keep this preface with it, so as to distinguish [this edition].

Chinese Source

明三七日行法華懺法勸修第一

Ru-Yi Translation

Section One: Clarifying the Practice of the Lotus Repentance Method for Three Sets of Seven Days, and Encouraging Its Cultivation

Chinese Source

如來滅後，後五百歲濁惡世中，比丘、比丘尼、優婆塞、優婆夷誦大乘經者，欲修大乘行者、發大乘意者，欲見普賢菩薩色身者、欲見釋迦牟尼佛多寶佛塔分身諸佛及十方佛者，欲得六根清淨入佛境界通達無閡者，欲得聞十方諸佛所說一念之中悉能受持通達不忘解釋演說無障閡者，欲得與文殊師利普賢等諸大菩薩共為等侶者，欲得普現色身一念之中不起滅定遍至十方一切佛土供養一切諸佛者，欲得一念之中遍到十方一切佛剎現種種色身作種種神變放大光明說法度脫一切眾生入不思議一乘者，欲得破四魔淨一切煩惱滅一切障道罪現身入菩薩正位具一切諸佛自在功德者，先當於空閑處，三七日一心精進入法華三昧，若有現身犯五逆四重失比丘法，欲得清淨還具沙門律儀，得如上所說種種勝妙功德者，亦當於三七日中，一心精進修法華三昧。

Ru-Yi Translation

After the Tathāgata's extinction, in the last five hundred years, in the turbid and evil age: monks, nuns, laymen, and laywomen who recite the Great Vehicle sūtras; those who wish to cultivate the Great Vehicle practice; those who have aroused the Great Vehicle intention; those who wish to see the form-body of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva; those who wish to see Śākyamuni Buddha, the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna Buddha, the emanation buddhas, and the buddhas of the ten directions; those who wish to attain the purity of the six faculties and enter the realm of the buddhas, penetrating without obstruction; those who wish to be able to hear what the buddhas of the ten directions teach and, within a single thought, receive and uphold all of it, penetrate it without forgetting, and explain and expound it without obstruction; those who wish to be companions together with Mañjuśrī, Samantabhadra, and the other great bodhisattvas; those who wish to manifest form-bodies everywhere and, within a single thought, without rising from the samādhi of cessation, reach everywhere all the buddha-lands of the ten directions and make offerings to all the buddhas; those who wish, within a single thought, to reach everywhere all the buddha-fields of the ten directions, manifesting various form-bodies, performing various spiritual transformations, emitting great light, and teaching the Dharma to deliver all sentient beings so that they enter the inconceivable one vehicle; those who wish to break the four māras, purify all afflictions, extinguish all offenses that obstruct the path, enter the correct stage of the bodhisattva in this present body, and possess all the sovereign merits of all the buddhas — [all these] should first, in a secluded place, for three sets of seven days, with single-minded diligence, enter the Lotus samādhi. If there are those who in this present body have committed the five heinous [offenses] or the four grave [offenses] and lost the Dharma of a monk, and wish to become pure and again possess the discipline and deportment of a śramaṇa, and to attain the various supreme and wondrous merits described above, they too should, within three sets of seven days, cultivate the Lotus samādhi with single-minded diligence.

Chinese Source

所以者何？此《法華經》是諸如來祕密之藏，於諸經中最在其上，行大直道無留難故，如轉輪王髻中明珠不妄與人，若有得者隨意所須種種珍寶悉皆具足，法華三昧亦復如是，能與一切眾生佛法珍寶，是故菩薩行者應當不計身命，盡未來際修行此經，況三七日耶？問曰：佛道長遠，三七日修行當有何益？答：有三種益，在下當說。

Ru-Yi Translation

Why is this? This Lotus Sūtra is the secret treasury of the tathāgatas, foremost among all sūtras, because it travels the great straight path without hindrance or difficulty. It is like the bright jewel in the topknot of a wheel-turning king, which he does not give to anyone carelessly; if someone obtains it, every kind of treasure he needs is fully supplied at will. The Lotus samādhi is also like this: it can give all sentient beings the treasures of the Buddha-dharma. Therefore the bodhisattva practitioner should not reckon body and life, and should cultivate this sūtra to the end of the future — how much more for three sets of seven days? Question: The buddha-path is long and far; what benefit can there be in cultivating for three sets of seven days? Answer: There are three kinds of benefit, which will be explained below.

Sections Two and Three: Preliminaries and the Single Mind

Chinese Source

明三七日行法前方便第二

Ru-Yi Translation

Section Two: Clarifying the Preliminary Expedients for the Practice of Three Sets of Seven Days

Chinese Source

（修行有二種：一者初行，二者久行。教初行者當用此法，教久修者依〈安樂行品〉。）

Ru-Yi Translation

(There are two kinds of practice: first, beginning practice; second, long practice. Those beginning practice are taught to use this method; those who have cultivated long are taught to rely on the "Peaceful Practices" chapter.)

Chinese Source

夫一切懺悔行法，悉須作前方便。所以者何？若不先嚴淨身心卒入道場，則道心不發、行不如法，無所感降。是故當於正懺之前，一七日中，先自調伏其心，息諸緣事，供養三寶，嚴飾道場，淨諸衣服，一心繫念，自憶此身已來，及過去世所有惡業，生重慚愧，禮佛懺悔，行道誦經，坐禪觀行，發願專精，為令正行三昧身心清淨無障閡故，心所願求悉克果故，（亦須誦下諸懺悔文，悉令通利。）

Ru-Yi Translation

All methods of repentance practice must have preliminary expedients. Why? If one does not first adorn and purify body and mind but enters the place of practice abruptly, the mind of the path does not arise, the practice is not in accord with the Dharma, and nothing descends in response. Therefore, before the main repentance, for one period of seven days, one should first tame one's own mind, put to rest all conditioned affairs, make offerings to the Three Jewels, adorn the place of practice, purify one's clothing, fix one's thought with a single mind, and recall all the evil actions [committed] since [the beginning of] this body and in past lives, giving rise to heavy shame; bow to the buddhas and repent, circumambulate and recite the sūtra, sit in meditation and practice contemplation, make vows, and be concentrated and earnest — so that, in properly practicing the samādhi, body and mind may be pure and without obstruction, and so that what the mind wishes and seeks may all be brought to fruition. (One must also recite the repentance texts below until all are fluent.)

Chinese Source

明正入道場三七日修行一心精進方法第三

Ru-Yi Translation

Section Three: Clarifying the Method of Practice with Single-Minded Diligence for Three Sets of Seven Days on Properly Entering the Place of Practice

Chinese Source

（正入道場用六齋日，此日太子、四天王使者等諸天善神下來人間，檢校善惡，見修善者，即注善簿，安慰守護，為現瑞相，令行者心生歡喜，增益善根故。）

Ru-Yi Translation

(For properly entering the place of practice, use [one of] the six fast days. On these days the princes, the envoys of the Four Heavenly Kings, and the other gods and good spirits come down among humans to inspect good and evil; seeing those who cultivate good, they at once enter it in the register of good, comfort and protect them, and show auspicious signs for them, so that the practitioner's mind rejoices and his wholesome roots increase.)

Chinese Source

行者初欲入道場之時，應自安心，我於今時乃至滿三七日，於其中間，當如佛教一心精進。所以者何？若心異念，即雜諸煩惱，名不清淨。心不淨故，豈得與三昧正道相應？是故自要其心，不惜身命，一心精進，滿三七日。

Ru-Yi Translation

When the practitioner is first about to enter the place of practice, he should settle his own mind: "From this moment until the full three sets of seven days, in the time between, I shall be diligent with a single mind as the Buddha taught." Why? If the mind has other thoughts, it is mixed with afflictions, and this is called impure. Since the mind is impure, how could it accord with the correct path of samādhi? Therefore one binds one's own mind by a pledge, not sparing body or life, diligent with a single mind for the full three sets of seven days.

Chinese Source

問曰：眾生心相，隨事異緣，云何能得一心精進？答曰：有二種修一心：一者事中修一心，二者理中修一心。事中修一心者，如行者初入道場時即作是念：「我於三七日中，若禮佛時當一心禮佛，心不異緣，乃至懺悔行道，誦經坐禪，悉皆一心，在行法中無分散意。」如是經三七日，是名事中修一心精進。二者理中修一心精進，行者，初入道場時應作是念：「我從今時乃至三七日滿，於其中間諸有所作，常自照了所作之心心性不二。所以者何？如禮佛時心性不生不滅，當知一切所作種種之事，心性悉不生不滅，如是觀時見一切心悉是一心，以心性從本已來常一相故。」行者能如是反觀心源心心相續，滿三七日不得心相，是名理中修一心精進法。

Ru-Yi Translation

Question: The marks of sentient beings' minds follow affairs and take different objects; how can one attain single-minded diligence? Answer: There are two kinds of cultivating the single mind: first, cultivating the single mind in phenomena; second, cultivating the single mind in principle. Cultivating the single mind in phenomena: when the practitioner first enters the place of practice, he thinks: "During the three sets of seven days, when I bow to the buddhas I shall bow to the buddhas with a single mind, the mind not taking other objects; and so on through repentance, circumambulation, reciting the sūtra, and seated meditation — all with a single mind, with no scattered intention within the method of practice." Passing three sets of seven days in this way is called cultivating single-minded diligence in phenomena. Second, cultivating single-minded diligence in principle: when the practitioner first enters the place of practice, he should think: "From now until the three sets of seven days are complete, in whatever I do in the time between, I shall always illuminate clearly for myself that the mind that does it and the mind-nature are not two. Why? Just as, when I bow to the buddhas, the mind-nature is neither born nor extinguished, know that in all the various things one does, the mind-nature is wholly neither born nor extinguished. When contemplating in this way, one sees that all minds are a single mind, because the mind-nature has from the beginning always been of a single mark." If the practitioner can thus turn back and contemplate the source of mind, mind after mind continuing, for the full three sets of seven days, without obtaining any mark of mind, this is called the method of cultivating single-minded diligence in principle.

Section Four: The Ten Methods; Methods One and Two

Chinese Source

明初入道場正修行方法第四

Ru-Yi Translation

Section Four: Clarifying the Method of Main Practice on First Entering the Place of Practice

Chinese Source

行者初入道場，當具足十法：一者嚴淨道場，二者淨身，三者三業供養，四者奉請三寶，五者讚歎三寶，六者禮佛，七者懺悔，八者行道旋遶，九者誦《法華經》，十者思惟一實境界。行者於三七日中日夜六時，初入道場一時之中當具足修此十法，於後六時一一時中，當略去請佛一法，餘九法悉行無異。（明此十法之中，有但說施為方法，有教運心作念，有教誦文章句口自宣說，行者當好善取意而用，未必併須誦此文也。）

Ru-Yi Translation

When the practitioner first enters the place of practice, he should fully carry out ten methods: first, adorning and purifying the place of practice; second, purifying the body; third, offering with the three kinds of action; fourth, respectfully inviting the Three Jewels; fifth, praising the Three Jewels; sixth, bowing to the buddhas; seventh, repentance; eighth, circumambulation and circling; ninth, reciting the Lotus Sūtra; tenth, reflecting on the realm of the one reality. During the three sets of seven days, in the six periods of day and night, in the one period of first entering the place of practice he should fully cultivate these ten methods; in each of the six periods thereafter, he should simply omit the one method of inviting the buddhas, and carry out all the remaining nine methods without difference. (Of these ten methods, some only describe the procedure of action, some teach how to direct the mind and form thoughts, and some teach texts and phrases to be recited and proclaimed aloud oneself; the practitioner should grasp their intent well and use them; it is not necessarily required to recite all these texts.)

Chinese Source

第一明行者嚴淨道場法

Ru-Yi Translation

The First Method: Clarifying the Practitioner's Method of Adorning and Purifying the Place of Practice

Chinese Source

當於閑靜之處，嚴治一室以為道場，別安自坐之處，令與道場有隔。於道場中敷好高座，安置《法華經》一部，亦未必須安形像舍利并餘經典，唯置《法華經》，安施旛蓋種種供養具。於入道場日清旦之時，當淨掃地，香湯灌灑香泥塗地，然種種諸香油燈，散種種華及諸末香，燒眾名香，供養三寶，備於己力所辦，傾心盡意極令嚴淨。所以者何？行者內心敬重三寶超過三界，今欲奉請供養，豈可輕心？若不能拔己資財供養大乘，則終不能招賢感聖、重罪不滅，三昧何由可發。

Ru-Yi Translation

In a secluded, quiet place, one should prepare a room as the place of practice, and set up a separate place for one's own sitting, so that it is partitioned off from the place of practice. In the place of practice spread out a fine high seat and place on it one copy of the Lotus Sūtra; it is not necessary to set out images, relics, or other scriptures — place only the Lotus Sūtra — and set up banners, canopies, and various offering articles. On the day of entering the place of practice, in the early morning, one should sweep the ground clean, sprinkle it with fragrant water, and coat the ground with fragrant mud; light various lamps of fragrant oil, scatter various flowers and powdered incense, burn many famous incenses, and make offering to the Three Jewels, preparing whatever one's own strength can provide, devoting one's heart and exhausting one's intent to make it utterly adorned and pure. Why? The practitioner inwardly reveres the Three Jewels as surpassing the three realms; now that he wishes to invite them and make offerings, how could he do it with a light mind? If he cannot draw out his own resources and wealth to make offerings to the Great Vehicle, he will in the end be unable to call forth the worthies and move the sages, his grave offenses will not be extinguished, and from what could the samādhi arise?

Chinese Source

第二明行者淨身方法

Ru-Yi Translation

The Second Method: Clarifying the Practitioner's Method of Purifying the Body

Chinese Source

初入道場，當以香湯沐浴、著淨潔衣，若大衣及諸新染衣；若無當取己衣勝者，以為入道場衣。於後若出道場，至不淨處，當脫去淨衣著故衣；所為事竟，當更洗浴著本淨衣，入道場行事也。

Ru-Yi Translation

On first entering the place of practice, one should bathe in fragrant water and put on clean clothing — the great robe or newly dyed robes; if one has none, take the best of one's own clothes as the clothing for entering the place of practice. Afterward, if one leaves the place of practice and goes to an unclean place, one should take off the clean clothing and put on old clothing; when the business is finished, one should bathe again, put on the original clean clothing, and enter the place of practice to carry out the practice.

The Third Method: Offering with the Three Kinds of Action

Chinese Source

第三明行者修三業供養法（初入道場，至法座前先敷尼師壇，正身倚立，應先慈念一切眾生欲興救度，次當起殷重心慚愧懇惻存想如來，三寶畟塞十方虛空影現道場，是時手執香爐燒眾名香，散種種華供養三寶，即尋五體投地，口自唱言：）

Ru-Yi Translation

The Third Method: Clarifying the Practitioner's Method of Cultivating Offering with the Three Kinds of Action (On first entering the place of practice and coming before the Dharma seat, first spread out the niṣīdana and stand upright with the body straight. First think with loving-kindness of all sentient beings, wishing to rescue and deliver them; next give rise to an earnest mind, ashamed, sincere, and grieved, and visualize the Tathāgata: the Three Jewels crowd and fill the empty space of the ten directions, their images appearing in the place of practice. At this time, holding the censer in the hand, burn many famous incenses and scatter various flowers as an offering to the Three Jewels; then at once prostrate, casting the five limbs to the ground, and proclaim aloud oneself:)

Chinese Source

「一切恭敬！一心敬禮十方常住佛。」（心隨身口一心頂禮，無分散意，了知此身如影不實，於能禮所禮心無所得，一切眾生亦同入此禮佛法界海中，總禮十方佛。一拜已次，當正身威儀，口自唱言：）

Ru-Yi Translation

Reading Notes for Part 1

Translator's notes

Zunshi's preface — The preface (units 0–2) is by Ciyun Zunshi 慈雲遵式 (964–1032), a Song-dynasty Tiantai master, written some four centuries after Zhiyi; it is not part of Zhiyi's rite. "The Mountain House" (山家) is the self-designation of the Tiantai lineage of Zunshi and Zhili.

"The third samādhi in Calming and Contemplation" — The half-walking, half-sitting samādhi of Mohe zhiguan, fascicle 2 (T1911), which lists ten items of the Lotus practice and adds: "There is a separate fascicle called Lotus Samādhi, written by the Master of Tiantai, which circulates in the world." Its list of ten (inviting the Buddha, repentance of the six faculties … realization) differs from the ten methods of this rite.

The Fuxing quotation — Verified in Zhanran's Zhiguan fuxing zhuanhong jue (T1912): "That separately circulating text investigates only the four phrases; therefore in the present text the contemplation of the elephant is cultivated broadly, to expand on that one." The "contemplation of the elephant" is the visualization of Samantabhadra's six-tusked elephant in the Sūtra on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra.

The four (or five) phrases — The fourfold investigation ("Is it mind because of a mind? …") appears in the Tenth Method (Part 2). The fifth phrase that Zunshi rejects is translated as literally as possible; its Chinese doubles the negation of the fourth phrase.

"Truly it is only by this gradual [way]" (實惟斯漸矣) — Alternatively: "truly this alone is the first step [toward it]."

Sources of Section One — The list of those who should practice follows the Sūtra on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra ("those who recite the Great Vehicle sūtras, those who cultivate the Great Vehicle…"; see the round-two draft of that sūtra), and "the last five hundred years, in the turbid and evil age … three sets of seven days … single-minded diligence" follows the Lotus, "Samantabhadra's Encouragement" (site wording). "The secret treasury of the tathāgatas, foremost among all sūtras" and the topknot jewel of the wheel-turning king are from "The Four Peaceful Practices" on the site.

"Three kinds of benefit" — Explained in Section Five (Part 2) as the realization of the lower, middle, and upper faculties.

The six fast days — The Great Treatise (T1509) cites the Sūtra of the Four Heavenly Kings: on the six fast days of the month "the envoys, the princes, and the Four Heavenly Kings" descend to observe beings. Alternatively the rite's 太子、四天王使者 can be read as "the princes, the Four Heavenly Kings, the envoys."

The ten methods — The list matches the one Zhili quotes in Essential Purport of Repentance Practice (round-two draft), and the renderings follow that draft; there Zhili's quotation reads 清淨身器 ("purifying the body-vessel"), here simply 淨身.

"With a single mind" (一心) — The site's Great Compassion Repentance renders 一心頂禮 "With sincere mind." Here the rite itself defines the single mind in phenomena and in principle (unit 12), and Zhanran (Part 3) ties the word 一心 in the formula to that teaching, so "with a single mind" is used throughout.

"The buddhas and the Dharma of the sūtras" (佛經法) — Alternatively: "the buddhas, the sūtras, and the Dharma." The verse is close to the one in the site's Great Compassion Repentance, whose second half differs.

Names in the invitation — Candrasūryapradīpa (Lotus, ch. 1), Kamaladalavimalanakṣatrarājasaṃkusumitābhijña ("Gadgadasvara"), Flower Light (Śāriputra's prediction, "The Parable of the Burning House"), and the bodhisattvas Akṣayamati, Gadgadasvara, Padmaśrī, Mahāpratibhāna, Prajñākūṭa, Pradānaśūra, Dharaṇiṃdhara, and Viśiṣṭacāritra follow the site's Lotus. 宿王華 is rendered "Constellation King Flower" (see decisions).
