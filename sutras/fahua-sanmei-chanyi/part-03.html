Zhanran's Supplementary Visualizations for Performing the Lotus Samādhi

This short work (T1942) is not part of Zhiyi's rite but a separate supplement by Zhanran (711–782) of the Tang, printed after it as an appendix. Zhanran observes that practitioners either recite the repentance text without understanding or push through the rite while neglecting the contemplation of principle, and that the rite's instructions on visualization, though complete, are hard to carry out with a scattered mind. He therefore walks through Zhiyi's ten methods in order and supplies, for each, a short verse or thought to hold while chanting: the incense-and-flower visualization, the image of the place of practice as Indra's jewel net in which one's body appears before every buddha, and a fuller form of the "ten contrary and compliant minds" to be visualized before repentance. He then shows how the six-faculty repentance texts already contain those ten minds, which faculties may be abbreviated, and how bows, flowers, and incense are to be directed toward three places: the Three Jewels of the ten directions, the Three Jewels of the Lotus, and Samantabhadra. A brief combined repentance text closes the work, for sessions at dusk and before dawn when time is short. Much of this wording reappears in later rites, including the site's Great Compassion Repentance.

Zhanran's Purpose; the First Five Methods

Chinese Source

法華三昧行事運想補助儀

Ru-Yi Translation

Supplementary Visualizations for Performing the Lotus Samādhi

Chinese Source

唐國清沙門湛然撰

Ru-Yi Translation

Composed by Zhanran, a śramaṇa of Guoqing [Monastery] in the Tang

Chinese Source

夫禮懺法，世雖同效，事儀運想多不周旋。或粗讀懺文半不通利，或推力前拒理觀，一無效精進之風、闕入門之緒，故言勤修苦行非涅槃因。但禮念軌儀文非不委，以散情昏重想運難成。予因天台再有詳補，撮覽樞總使隨言作念、隨念成想，一一瞻視如對目前，庶時刻不虛事法成辦。依本文總歷十法，結要事理。

Ru-Yi Translation

As for the method of worship and repentance, though people of the world all imitate it alike, in the ritual of the affairs and the directing of thought they are mostly not thorough. Some read the repentance text roughly, half of it not fluent; some push ahead by force and resist the contemplation of principle; none follow the manner of diligence, and they lack the thread for entering the gate. Hence it is said, "Diligently cultivating ascetic practices is not a cause of nirvāṇa." Yet as for the procedures of worship and recollection, the text is not lacking in detail; but because scattered feelings are dim and heavy, the visualizations are hard to accomplish. Therefore, following Tiantai, I have further added detailed supplements, gathering the pivotal and the whole, so that following the words one forms thoughts, and following the thoughts one accomplishes visualizations, gazing at each as if before one's eyes, in the hope that no moment is in vain and the phenomenal method is accomplished. Following the original text, I go through the ten methods in all, binding together the essentials of phenomena and principle.

Chinese Source

初嚴淨道場（如無別屋，亦可同間。絕無己物方可外求。）二淨身（新衣舊衣新染並得，若無此二澣舊勝者。）三三業供養（若總禮三寶，未須一一隨方想念，但想己身及十方諸佛，實相理體本無能所，故無能禮所禮想，此無能所名法界海。願諸眾生同見此理，名為同入。）次運香（唱至受用作佛事竟，次生想念：不起此心能運到十方。故云一念。想曰：願此香華遍十方，以為微妙光明臺，諸天音樂天寶香，諸天餚膳天寶衣。不可思議妙法塵，一一塵出一切塵，一一塵出一切法，旋轉無閡互莊嚴。遍至一切三寶前，十方法界三寶前，皆有我身修供養，一一皆悉遍法界。彼彼無雜無障閡，盡未來際作佛事，普熏法界諸眾生，蒙熏皆發菩提心，同入無生證佛智。）

Ru-Yi Translation

First, adorning and purifying the place of practice. (If there is no separate room, the same room may also be used. Only if one has absolutely no things of one's own may one seek them from outside.) Second, purifying the body. (New clothing, old clothing, or newly dyed [robes] are all acceptable; if one has neither, wash the best of one's old clothes.) Third, offering with the three kinds of action. (When bowing to the Three Jewels all together, one need not visualize each one according to its direction; simply visualize one's own body and the buddhas of the ten directions: the essence of the principle of the true mark is originally without subject or object, so there is no visualization of the one who bows and the one bowed to. This absence of subject and object is called the ocean of the dharma-realm. Wishing that all sentient beings see this principle together is called "entering together.") Next, directing the incense. (When the chant has reached "may they receive and use it to do the work of the Buddha," next form the visualizing thought: without stirring this mind, one can direct [the offering] to the ten directions; hence it says "in a single thought." The visualization says: "May this incense and these flowers fill the ten directions, becoming a subtle and wondrous tower of light, the music of the heavens, the incense of heavenly treasures, the delicacies of the heavens, the garments of heavenly treasures, an inconceivable, wondrous mote of Dharma — each mote giving rise to every mote, each mote giving rise to every dharma, turning without hindrance and adorning one another, reaching everywhere before all the Three Jewels, before the Three Jewels of the dharma-realm of the ten directions, my own body present everywhere making this offering, each and every one of them filling the whole dharma-realm; each and every one unmixed and unobstructed, doing the work of the Buddha to the very limit of the future, universally perfuming every sentient being of the dharma-realm, so that, touched by this perfume, they all arouse the bodhi-mind, together entering the unborn and realizing the Buddha's wisdom.")

Chinese Source

請佛（我三業性如虛空，釋迦牟尼亦如是，不起真際為眾生，與眾俱來受供養。）次請佛菩薩（但改牟尼及形相來處。）五歎佛（偈前一一別想己身在三寶前立。次口宣偈，隨聲運想。我之身心本無生滅，故雖稱歎如影如響。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Inviting the buddhas. ("The nature of my three kinds of action is like empty space; Śākyamuni is also like this. Without rising from the limit of the true, for the sake of sentient beings, he comes together with the assembly to accept the offering.") Next, inviting the buddhas and bodhisattvas. (Only change "Śākyamuni" and the form and the place from which they come.) Fifth, praising the buddhas. (Before the verse, visualize distinctly one's own body standing before each of the Three Jewels. Next proclaim the verse aloud, directing the visualization along with the voice: "My body and mind are originally without birth or extinction; hence, though I praise, it is like a shadow and an echo.")

Visualizations for Bowing and Repentance

Chinese Source

六禮佛（當唱一心時，先專至敬禮，為事一心。次想能所，為理一心。應想曰：能禮所禮性空寂，感應道交難思議，我此道場如帝珠，釋迦牟尼影現中，我身影現如來前，頭面接足歸命禮。）多寶佛（多寶如來影現中，我身影現多寶前，餘同上想。）分身（分身諸佛亦同上想。）東方諸佛（東方諸佛影現中，我皆影現諸佛前，乃至十方一一皆爾，）三世諸佛（乃至過現未來，並同上想。）舍利形像等（諸佛法身如虛空，化事住世難思議，我皆影現化事前，一一皆悉歸命禮。）妙法（真空法界如虛空，常住法寶難思議，我皆影現法寶前，莫不皆悉歸命禮。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Sixth, bowing to the buddhas. (When chanting "with a single mind," first bow with concentrated, utmost reverence: this is the single mind in phenomena. Next visualize subject and object: this is the single mind in principle. One should visualize, saying: "The nature of the one who bows and the one bowed to is empty and still, yet the mutual resonance between response and appeal is inconceivable; this place of practice of mine is like Indra's jewel, Śākyamuni reflected within it, my own body reflected before the Tathāgata, and, head and face touching his feet, I take refuge and bow in homage.") Prabhūtaratna Buddha. ("The Tathāgata Prabhūtaratna reflected within it, my own body reflected before Prabhūtaratna"; the rest the same as the visualization above.) The emanation [buddhas]. (The emanation buddhas likewise with the visualization above.) The buddhas of the east. ("The buddhas of the east reflected within it, my own body reflected before all those buddhas," and so on for each of the ten directions.) The buddhas of the three times. (And so on through past, present, and future, all with the visualization above.) The relics, images, and so on. ("The Dharma-body of the buddhas is like empty space; their deeds of transformation, dwelling in the world, are inconceivable; my body is reflected before all their deeds of transformation, and to each I take refuge and bow in homage.") The Wondrous Dharma. ("The dharma-realm of true emptiness is like empty space; the eternally abiding Dharma-jewel is inconceivable; my body is reflected before the Dharma-jewel, and to all without exception I take refuge and bow in homage.")

Chinese Source

諸菩薩（彼一一菩薩影現中，乃至下方亦如是。）舍利弗等（先想聲聞皆是本來諸佛，今從初說故云聲聞。）普賢菩薩（為滅障故接足禮，餘同上）。普為（我及眾生，無始常為三業六根重罪所障，不見諸佛、不知出要，但順生死、不知妙理。我今雖知，猶與一切眾生同為一切重罪所障。今對普賢十方諸佛前，普為眾生歸命懺悔，惟願加護令障消滅。）

Ru-Yi Translation

The bodhisattvas. ("Each of those bodhisattvas reflected within it," and so on through those from below.) Śāriputra and the rest. (First visualize that the śrāvakas are all originally buddhas; now, because they are spoken of from the beginning [of their path], they are called śrāvakas.) Samantabhadra Bodhisattva. ("To dissolve the obstructions, I touch his feet in homage"; the rest as above.) "Universally on behalf of…" ("I and sentient beings, from beginningless time, have constantly been obstructed by the grave offenses of the three kinds of action and the six faculties, not seeing the buddhas, not knowing the essential way out, merely following birth-and-death, not knowing the wondrous principle. Though I now know it, I am still, together with all sentient beings, obstructed by all grave offenses. Now, before Samantabhadra and the buddhas of the ten directions, I take refuge and repent universally on behalf of sentient beings; I pray only that they add their protection, so that the obstructions may be dissolved.")

Chinese Source

七懺悔應運逆順十心云（我與眾生無始來，今由愛見故內計我人、外加惡友，不隨喜他一毫之善，唯遍三業廣作眾罪。事雖不廣，惡心遍布，晝夜相續無有間斷，覆諱過失不欲人知，不畏惡道無慚無愧，撥無因果。故於今日對十方佛、普賢大師，深信因果，生重慚愧，生大怖畏，發露懺悔斷相續心，發菩提心斷惡修善，勤策三業翻昔重過。隨喜凡聖一毫之善，念十方佛有大福慧，能救拔我及諸眾生，從二死海置三德岸。從無始來不知諸法本性空寂，廣造眾惡。今知空寂為求菩提，為眾生故，廣修諸善遍斷眾惡，惟願普賢慈悲攝受。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Seventh, repentance: one should direct the ten contrary and compliant minds, saying: ("I and sentient beings, from beginningless time until now, because of craving and views, have inwardly reckoned self and others and outwardly added evil friends, not rejoicing in even a hair's breadth of another's good, but throughout the three kinds of action broadly committing many offenses. Though the deeds were not broad, the evil mind spread everywhere, continuing day and night without interruption. Concealing and hiding my faults, unwilling to let others know, unafraid of the evil paths, without shame and without conscience, I denied cause and effect. Therefore today, before the buddhas of the ten directions and the Great Master Samantabhadra, I deeply believe in cause and effect, give rise to heavy shame, give rise to great dread, reveal and repent, cut off the mind of continuation, arouse the bodhi-mind, cut off evil and cultivate good, diligently spur on the three kinds of action to overturn past grave faults, rejoice in even a hair's breadth of the good of ordinary beings and sages, and keep in mind that the buddhas of the ten directions have great merit and wisdom and can rescue me and all sentient beings, [carrying us] from the ocean of the two kinds of death to the shore of the three virtues. From beginningless time, not knowing that the original nature of all dharmas is empty and still, I broadly created many evils. Now, knowing emptiness and stillness, to seek bodhi, for the sake of sentient beings, I broadly cultivate all goods and cut off all evils everywhere. I pray only that Samantabhadra receive me with compassion.")

Chinese Source

次懺六根（至文並須略知，文相含於逆順十種心相，縱有闕略，在四悔中。於菩提中見不清淨等，是橫計我人。毘盧遮那是觀罪性空，一切世尊是念十方佛。後四悔中有發隨喜心，以是因緣令畢竟清淨。是斷相續心歸向普賢及一切世尊，是為眾生發菩提心。一一悔文皆具三業，是修功補過。等說過等，是發露過失。當墮惡道生大怖畏等，是怖畏惡道及生慚愧、信因果等。凡宣悔詞，須加事想破於愛心。至觀罪性空，總想事理悔於見心。若欲更略眼耳鼻三根，亦可或時用。若舌身意三根，是業故不得全略，前三根縱略，並使不失逆順十心大意即得。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Next, repenting the six faculties. (One must know the text in outline: the features of the text contain the marks of the ten contrary and compliant minds; even if [some] are missing or abbreviated, they are in the four [further] repentances. "Seeing impurity within bodhi" and so on is wrongly reckoning self and others. "Vairocana" is contemplating the nature of offense as empty. "All the World-Honored Ones" is keeping in mind the buddhas of the ten directions. In the four repentances that follow there is the arousing of the mind of rejoicing. "By these causes and conditions, may they be ultimately purified": this is cutting off the mind of continuation. "Turning to Samantabhadra and all the World-Honored Ones" is arousing the bodhi-mind for the sake of sentient beings. Each repentance text possesses all three kinds of action: this is cultivating merit to make up for faults. "Telling the faults" and so on is revealing faults. "Shall fall into the evil paths," "giving rise to great dread," and so on are dreading the evil paths, as well as giving rise to shame, believing in cause and effect, and so on. Whenever proclaiming the words of repentance, one must add a phenomenal visualization to break the mind of craving. On reaching "contemplating the nature of offense as empty," visualize phenomena and principle together, to repent the mind of views. If one wishes to abbreviate further, [abbreviating] the three faculties of eye, ear, and nose may also be used at times. The three faculties of tongue, body, and mind are actions, so they may not be entirely abbreviated; even if the first three faculties are abbreviated, it is enough as long as the main intent of the ten contrary and compliant minds is not lost.)

The Further Repentances, Circumambulation, and the Refuges

Chinese Source

勸請（十方諸佛以道眼力故，知我見我勸請諸佛，唯願久住轉正法輪，所在生處常能勸請。）隨喜（十方凡聖一毫之善，我皆隨喜。福德能令見者生喜，是故我喜。）迴向（無始來時乃至今日、盡未來際一毫之善，及以懺悔勸請隨喜，皆同法界，與諸眾生共成佛道。）發願（大體須存滅罪除障，開顯法門，滿四弘誓，餘者皆須順於菩提。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Requesting. ("The buddhas of the ten directions, by the power of their path-eye, know me and see me requesting the buddhas: I pray only that they dwell long and turn the wheel of the true Dharma; wherever I am born, may I always be able to request them.") Rejoicing. ("In even a hair's breadth of the good of ordinary beings and sages of the ten directions, I rejoice. Merit can make those who see it rejoice; therefore I rejoice.") Dedication. ("Even a hair's breadth of good from beginningless time until today and to the end of the future, together with repentance, requesting, and rejoicing, all [I make] one with the dharma-realm, to accomplish the buddha-path together with all sentient beings.") Making vows. (In general one must keep [the aims of] extinguishing offenses and removing obstructions, opening and revealing the Dharma-gates, and fulfilling the four universal vows; all the rest must accord with bodhi.)

Chinese Source

行道（欲行時更須正立，想此道場猶如法界，十方三寶畟塞虛空。以次回身旋遶法座，想十方三寶心性寂滅，影現十方經聲如響，心想如夢梵聲亦然。）

Ru-Yi Translation

Circumambulation. (When about to walk, one must again stand upright and visualize this place of practice as like the dharma-realm, and the Three Jewels of the ten directions crowding and filling empty space. Then turn the body in order and circle the Dharma seat, visualizing that the mind-nature of the Three Jewels of the ten directions is still and extinguished, reflected in the ten directions; the sound of the sūtra is like an echo; the mind's visualization is like a dream, and the chanting voice is likewise.)

Chinese Source

三歸（一一各想三處三寶，與頂禮俱運。初文雖即直云十方，意亦兼於三處。若從懺悔已下凡云禮三寶者，皆須一一別起三想，頭三叩地。一十方三寶，二法華經中一切三寶，三普賢大師，對此三處求懺悔等。又凡散華，皆於言前散華之時，想三寶及普賢上。想已次宣懺悔等詞。至後文中云散華等者，心指前所散之華。若焚香者，送法座爐，亦應想身各送爐，遍至十方一一法華經前，乃至普熏一切三寶。若焚手爐但先添香，次執爐已隨事起想，然後口宣詞句。）

Ru-Yi Translation

The three refuges. (For each, visualize the Three Jewels of the three places, directing [the visualization] together with the prostration. Although the earlier text simply says "the ten directions," its intent also includes the three places. From the repentance onward, wherever it says "bow to the Three Jewels," one must raise the three visualizations separately and knock the head on the ground three times: first, the Three Jewels of the ten directions; second, all the Three Jewels in the Lotus Sūtra; third, the Great Master Samantabhadra. Before these three places one seeks repentance and so on. Further, whenever one scatters flowers, scatter them before the words; while scattering the flowers, visualize [them falling] upon the Three Jewels and Samantabhadra; having visualized, next proclaim the words of repentance and so on. Where the later text says "scattering flowers" and so on, the mind points to the flowers scattered before. If one burns incense and places it in the censer of the Dharma seat, one should also visualize one's body placing it in each censer, reaching everywhere before each Lotus Sūtra in the ten directions, and even perfuming universally all the Three Jewels. If one burns a hand censer, first just add incense; next, having taken up the censer, raise the visualization according to the matter, and then proclaim the words aloud.)

A Brief Repentance Text for Short Periods

Chinese Source

坐禪文略，應如《止觀》十法成乘等。行法華懺，黃昏五更時促，則此略文。

Ru-Yi Translation

The text on seated meditation is brief; one should [practice] as in the ten modes of contemplation that make up the vehicle in Calming and Contemplation and so on. When performing the Lotus repentance, if time is short at dusk and in the fifth watch, then [use] this brief text.

Chinese Source

我（某甲）及法界眾生，從無始世來，無明所覆顛倒迷惑，而由六根三業不善，廣造十惡四重五逆，乃至十重四十八輕，無量無邊說不可盡。十方諸佛常在世間法音不絕，妙香充塞法味盈空，放淨光明照觸一切，常住妙理遍滿虛空。我無始來六根內盲、三業昏暗，不見不聞不覺不知，以是因緣長流生死，經歷惡道百千萬劫，永無出期。經云：釋迦如來名毘盧遮那遍一切處。是故當知，一切諸法無非佛法。而我不了，隨無明流，是則於菩提中見不清淨，於解脫中而起纏縛。今始覺悟，今始改悔，生大慚愧、生大怖畏，誦持大乘，三業供養，歸向普賢菩薩及一切世尊，燒香散華發露懺悔，六根三業所作永斷相續，不復更造。以是因緣，今我與法界眾生，三業六根無始所作，現作當作、自作教他、見聞隨喜，若憶不憶、若識不識、若疑不疑、若覆若露，畢竟清淨。（懺悔已，歸命禮三寶。）

Ru-Yi Translation

"I (so-and-so) and the sentient beings of the dharma-realm, from beginningless ages, covered by ignorance, inverted and deluded, through the not-good of the six faculties and the three kinds of action, have broadly committed the ten evils, the four grave [offenses], the five heinous [offenses], and even [violations of] the ten grave and forty-eight minor [precepts], immeasurable and boundless, impossible to tell fully. The buddhas of the ten directions constantly abide in the world and their Dharma-sound does not cease; wondrous fragrance fills [all places] and the Dharma-flavor fills the sky; they emit pure light that shines upon and touches all, and the eternally abiding wondrous principle pervades all of empty space. From beginningless time my six faculties have been inwardly blind and my three kinds of action dark: I have not seen, not heard, not been aware, not known. By these causes and conditions I have long flowed in birth-and-death, passing through the evil paths for hundreds of thousands of myriads of kalpas, forever without a time of escape. The sūtra says: 'The Tathāgata Śākyamuni is called Vairocana, Pervading All Places.' Therefore know that all dharmas are none other than the Buddha-dharma. Yet I did not understand, and followed the stream of ignorance; this is to see impurity within bodhi and to give rise to bondage within liberation. Now for the first time I awaken, now for the first time I correct and repent; I give rise to great shame and give rise to great dread, recite and uphold the Great Vehicle, make offering with the three kinds of action, turn to Samantabhadra Bodhisattva and all the World-Honored Ones, burn incense and scatter flowers, reveal and repent, and forever cut off the continuation of what the six faculties and the three kinds of action have done, never to do it again. By these causes and conditions, now may all that I and the sentient beings of the dharma-realm have done with the three kinds of action and the six faculties from beginningless time — what is being done now and what will be done, done oneself or taught to others, seen, heard, and rejoiced in, whether remembered or not remembered, whether recognized or not recognized, whether doubted or not doubted, whether concealed or revealed — be ultimately purified." (Having repented, take refuge and bow to the Three Jewels.)

Reading Notes for Part 3

Translator's notes

Author and status — Zhanran 湛然 (711–782), the sixth Tiantai patriarch, is a later author than Zhiyi; this is his own supplement, which CBETA prints as T1942 after the rite (T1941). "Following Tiantai" means following Zhiyi's text.

"Diligently cultivating ascetic practices is not a cause of nirvāṇa" — Given as a saying ("hence it is said"); source not located. The same phrase is cited in Zunshi's rite for invoking Avalokiteśvara (T1949).

The ten methods — Zhanran numbers some items (first, second, third, fifth, sixth, seventh) and leaves others (inviting, circumambulation, the refuges) unnumbered; the translation keeps this unevenness.

Shared wording with the Great Compassion Repentance — The incense-and-flower verse, the bowing verses ("the one who bows and the one bowed to … Indra's jewel"), the Dharma verse, the "Universally on behalf of …" reflection, and the ten contrary and compliant minds appear, with small differences, in the site's Great Compassion Repentance (a later form of the Great Compassion rite compiled by Zhili). The site's English is reused with these changes: "dharma-realm," "bodhi-mind," "the unborn," and "repent" to match Parts 1–2 and the BRIEF; 感應道交 as "the mutual resonance between response and appeal" (glossary) instead of the site's "the meeting of response and resonance"; and 妙理 as "the wondrous principle."

The ten contrary and compliant minds (逆順十心) — The first ten (craving and views, evil friends, not rejoicing, offenses throughout the three kinds of action, the spreading evil mind, continuation day and night, concealment, not fearing the evil paths, shamelessness, denying cause and effect) go along with birth-and-death; the second ten reverse them one by one. Zhili's Essential Purport of Repentance Practice (round-two draft) explains them at length.

"Wrongly reckoning self and others" (橫計我人) — 橫 is read as "wrongly, unreasonably." Alternatively: "reckoning self and others everywhere."

Punctuation in the six-faculty note — CBETA ends a sentence after 以是因緣令畢竟清淨 and begins the next with 是; the translation takes the quoted phrase as the subject of "this is cutting off the mind of continuation," which keeps the pattern of the note.

"Because they are spoken of from the beginning" (今從初說故云聲聞) — Alternatively: "following the first [teaching], they are called śrāvakas."

The three places (三處三寶) — The Three Jewels of the ten directions, those of the Lotus Sūtra, and Samantabhadra; each "bow to the Three Jewels" in the rite thus becomes three bows.

"The ten modes of contemplation that make up the vehicle" (十法成乘) — The ten modes of contemplation of Mohe zhiguan, set out from fascicle 5 on (glossary term), here pointed to as the full form of the Tenth Method.

"The ten grave and forty-eight minor" — The bodhisattva precepts of the Brahmā's Net Sūtra (T1484).

Quotation in the brief repentance text — "The Tathāgata Śākyamuni is called Vairocana, Pervading All Places" is from the Sūtra on Contemplating the Practice of Samantabhadra (round-two draft wording); the following line on bodhi and liberation repeats the rite's mind-faculty repentance (Part 2).
