# The Lotus Samādhi Repentance Rite — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Fahua sanmei chanyi* (法華三昧懺儀, Taishō T46, nos. 1941 (Zhiyi) and 1942 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1941 and T1942, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597, Zhanran in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 三七日 | three sets of seven days | The length of the practice. |
| 六根懺悔 | repentance of the six faculties | The heart of the rite. |
| 一心 | a single mind | In phenomena and in principle; the rite defines both. |
| 勸請／隨喜／迴向／發願 | requesting / rejoicing / dedication / making vows | The four practices after repentance. |
