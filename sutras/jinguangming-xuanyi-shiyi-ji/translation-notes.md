# Gleanings on the Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming jing xuanyi shiyi ji* (金光明經玄義拾遺記, Taishō T39, no. 1784 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1784, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 觀心釋 | explanation by contemplating the mind | The disputed section. |
| 山家／山外 | home-mountain / off-mountain | The two sides of the Song dispute. |
| 十種三法 | the ten sets of three dharmas | As in the Profound Meaning. |
