# The Ten Gates of Nonduality — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Shi buer men* (十不二門, Taishō T46, no. 1927 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1927, via the CBETA digital edition. Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 不二而二，二而不二 | not two, yet two; two, yet not two | The governing formula: setting up the provisional for the real, and opening the provisional to reveal the real. |
| 一念三千 | the three thousand in a single thought | The ground of every gate. |
| 性德 / 修德 | inherent virtue / cultivated virtue | The pair behind the third gate, cultivation and nature. |
| 六即 | the six identities | Each gate is to be examined by them. |
| 依正 | the dependent and the principal | The environment and the being; the sixth gate. |
| 受潤 | receiving and moistening | From the Lotus Sūtra's parable of the rain; the tenth gate. |
