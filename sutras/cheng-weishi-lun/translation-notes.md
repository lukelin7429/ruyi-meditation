# Cheng Weishi Lun Translation Notes

These notes record recurring translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Cheng Weishi Lun* (成唯識論, Taishō T31, no. 1585). They exist so that terminology stays consistent across all ten fascicles, since this treatise builds a technical vocabulary in fascicle 1 that it relies on for the rest of the work.

## Source and independence from prior translations

- Base text: Taishō T1585, via the CBETA digital edition (public domain). We translate from this Chinese text directly.
- Two prior English translations exist — Wei Tat (1973) and Francis H. Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only* (BDK English Tripiṭaka, 1999) — both still under copyright and not licensed for reproduction on this site. We may consult them privately to check difficult passages or confirm we have not misread the Chinese, but the English wording, sentence structure, and section framing on this site must be our own. Do not paraphrase either published translation sentence-by-sentence.

## Core terminology

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 唯識 | Consciousness-Only | The core doctrine (vijñaptimātratā). Capitalize as a proper term. |
| 唯識性 | the nature of Consciousness-Only | vijñaptimātratā-dharmatā / -svabhāva |
| 我 | self | ātman; the mistaken belief in a fixed, independent self |
| 法 | dharmas | Keep as "dharmas" (lowercase) when referring to phenomena/things generally; do not translate as "laws" or "teachings" in this context |
| 二執 | the two attachments | attachment to self (我執) and attachment to dharmas (法執) |
| 二空 | the two emptinesses | the emptiness of self and the emptiness of dharmas |
| 二障 | the two obstructions | afflictive obstructions (煩惱障, kleśāvaraṇa) and cognitive obstructions (所知障, jñeyāvaraṇa) |
| 二勝果 | the two supreme fruits | liberation (解脫) and great bodhi/awakening (大菩提) |
| 阿賴耶識 | ālayavijñāna (the storehouse consciousness) | Give the Sanskrit on first use per fascicle, then may use "storehouse consciousness" |
| 末那識 | manas (the seventh consciousness) | |
| 種子 | seeds | bīja |
| 現行 | manifest activity / current activity | as opposed to seeds lying dormant |
| 護法 | Dharmapāla | the master whose interpretation Xuanzang's compilation follows as its main line |
| 世親 | Vasubandhu | author of the *Triṃśikā* (the thirty verses this treatise comments on) |

Keep this table growing as new recurring terms appear in later fascicles — add a row rather than improvising a fresh translation of a term that already has an entry.

## Traditional outline (科判)

CBETA preserves Kuiji's traditional section outline (科判) as inline headers within the Taishō text. Where practical, structure each fascicle's page around these divisions rather than inventing new ones — they represent how this text has been read and taught for over a thousand years, and they give readers a map through an otherwise very dense argument.

## Register

- Plain English, close to the Chinese, not a loose paraphrase — matching the method already used for the *Mahāprajñāpāramitā Śāstra* and the *Yogācārabhūmi Śāstra* on this site.
- Where the Chinese compresses a technical argument into very few characters, it is fine for the English to expand slightly for clarity, but do not add explanation that belongs in a reading note instead of the translation itself.
- Mark every fascicle's status honestly (e.g. "translation in progress") rather than implying more is finished than actually is.
