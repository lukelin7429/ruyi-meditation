# Explanatory Notes on the Profound Meaning of the Lotus Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Fahua xuanyi shiqian* (法華玄義釋籤, Taishō T33, no. 1717 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1717, via the CBETA digital edition. Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain. The colophon and publisher's notice at the end of the CBETA file are not part of the text.
- **Quoted base text:** lemmas from the *Fahua Xuanyi* use this site's English of that text verbatim; where Zhanran's reading differs, the explanation follows him and the reading notes say so.
- **Paragraph numbers:** where the reading notes cite a paragraph number, the paragraphs of each fascicle in the CBETA text are counted from 0, the fascicle title.
- **Fascicle numbers:** Zhanran's fascicle numbers for the *Nirvāṇa Sūtra* and other works are kept as written; the reading notes give corrections where they were found.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 迹 / 本 | the trace / the origin | The two gates of the Lotus Sūtra. |
| 十妙 | the ten wondrous aspects | Object, wisdom, practice, stage, the three tracks, sympathetic response, spiritual powers, teaching the Dharma, the retinue, and merit and benefit. |
| 三軌 | the three tracks | Real nature, contemplative illumination, and resource-and-accomplishment. |
| 實相 | the true mark | Used throughout the Tiantai texts on this site, including the Fahua Xuanyi. |
| 麁 / 妙 | coarse / wondrous | With 相待 / 絕待, the relative and the absolute wondrous. |
| 五味 | milk, cream, fresh curdled butter, ripened butter, ghee | In quotations, the site's Fahua Xuanyi wording is kept. |
| 正因 / 緣因 / 了因 | the correct cause / the conditioning cause / the revealing cause | The three causes of buddha-nature, matched to this site's Fahua Xuanyi and Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra. |
| 《止觀》第N記 | my Record on the Nth fascicle of Calming and Contemplation | Zhanran's own commentary on the Mohe Zhiguan. |
