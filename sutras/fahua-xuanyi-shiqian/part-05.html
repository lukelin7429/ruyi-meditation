The Specific Explanation Begins: The Ten Wondrous Aspects and the Wondrous of the Object

The fifth fascicle of the Explanatory Notes begins the specific explanation of "wondrous" in the Profound Meaning. It covers most of the second half of the site's fascicle-002: the outline of the ten wondrous aspects of the trace gate (marking, proof-texts, arising in sequence), and then the wondrous of the object through the four readings of dependent origination, the four kinds of Four Noble Truths, and the opening of the two truths, stopping before the two truths that accord with wisdom alone. Zhanran first shows how the five rounds of exposition arouse the five minds. He then fills in the Abhidharma background of the twelve links from the Vibhāṣā, the Kośa, and the Yingluo. He defends Zhiyi's charge that the Śrīmālā master's "uncreated" truth is only the distinct teaching. He explains why a judgment of coarse and wondrous must be made by both teaching and flavor, and why judging alone is not enough unless the coarse is opened. He also states the school's method: in explaining the Lotus, every point is first sorted, then opened, then brought into the contemplation of mind. The fascicle ends with the debates over the two truths, the Satyasiddhi masters' jar example, and Zhiyi's three senses: according with feeling, with feeling and wisdom together, and with wisdom alone.

The Nirvāṇa Sūtra. Zhanran's fascicle numbers follow the southern edition (T375). Other sources checked. The Viśeṣacinti passages ("the world, the arising of the world …"; "knowing suffering as non-arising …") are in T586. Repunctuation in paragraph 41. CBETA has 「何但道諦前苦？「滅諦」下釋滅是常」. Since the Profound Meaning text reads 「前苦滅諦，非壞法滅」, the lemma is read as 「前苦滅諦」, and the translation has "why only the truth of the path? From 'the earlier truth of suffering's own cessation' on …".

Obscure lines translated literally. Paragraph 4: 「釋者事，須略述」 is read as 「釋者，事須略述」. Paragraph 11: the name 若育多提婆 is unidentified and is given as "Yuduotipo," taking 若 as "while." Quotation marks in paragraph 9. CBETA includes 「準例，老亦不是一切常有，故不立支」 and 「以終顯始者，始謂老死，終謂憂悲……」 inside the Vibhāṣā quotation. They read like Zhanran's own comments, but the quotation is kept as CBETA marks it.

Lemmas that differ from the received Profound Meaning. Zhanran's 「能轉示他」 (T1716: 能以此法顯示於他), 「如眾流入海」 (歸海), 「若二十三家」 (二十三說), 「五「聖諦」者」 (聖者), and 「境是所師」 (a paraphrase of 是諸佛所師). In each case the quotation uses the site's English.

Terms. For the four kinds of Four Noble Truths this part follows the site's Profound Meaning: arising-and-ceasing / non-arising / immeasurable / uncreated. The glossary has the Mohe zhiguan terms (generation-and-extinction … the unconditioned) under 四種四諦, and the brief gives "without fabrication" for 無作. "Without fabrication" is kept only for 無造作 in paragraph 37. For the Śrīmālā master's 有作／無作 the site's "constructed / unconstructed" is used. 緣起／緣生 in the twelve-links section follow the site's "conditions that arise / conditions that generate." 感／應 follow the glossary's 感應道交: appeal / response. 當分／跨節 follow Part 3: "within a single stage / spanning across stages."

Cross-references. "As in the sixth [fascicle] of these notes below" (paragraph 21, the Vyākaraṇa treatise) refers to Zhanran's own later fascicle. The "first fascicle" and the five minds (paragraph 2). 「第一中云廣解五章，一一廣起五心」 is taken to refer to fascicle 1 of the Profound Meaning, where the sevenfold common exposition is said to arouse the five minds (faith, vigor, mindfulness, concentration, wisdom).
