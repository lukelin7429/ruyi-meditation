# Explaining the Outline of the Teachings and Their Contemplations — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jiaoguan gangzong shiyi* (教觀綱宗釋義, Xuzangjing X57, no. 974 (Ouyi Zhixu)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Xuzangjing X0974, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Ouyi Zhixu died in 1655; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 教觀 | teaching and contemplation | The two wings of Tiantai. |
| 二諦／三諦 | two truths / three truths | Set out in several forms for each teaching. |
| 十乘觀法 | the ten modes of contemplation | Explained mode by mode in Part 2. |
| 正因 / 緣因 / 了因 | the correct cause / the conditioning cause / the revealing cause | The three causes of buddha-nature, matched to this site's Fahua Xuanyi and Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra. |
