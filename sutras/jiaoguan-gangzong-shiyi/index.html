Explaining the Outline of the Teachings and Their Contemplations.

After writing the Jiaoguan Gangzong, Ouyi Zhixu (蕅益智旭, 1599–1655) went back through it phrase by phrase and explained the points a reader would stumble on: the half-word and full-word teachings, the four kinds of four noble truths, the many kinds of two truths and three truths, the stages of each teaching, and the ten modes of contemplation. Each heading quotes his own outline, so the two texts are meant to be read together.

How to read this collection

Each heading is a phrase from the Jiaoguan Gangzong, given in this site's English; read the matching passage there first.

Part 1 runs through the Tripiṭaka teaching and the start of the shared teaching; Part 2 covers the distinct and perfect teachings, the ten modes of contemplation, and an appendix on the three kinds of loving-kindness.
