# The Record of the Teaching and Practice of the Venerable of Siming — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Siming zunzhe jiaoxing lu* (四明尊者教行錄, Taishō T46, no. 1937 (compiled by Zongxiao)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1937, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). the compiler Zongxiao lived in the Southern Song; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 教行 | teaching and practice | Practice relies on the teaching; the teaching is revealed through practice. |
| 性具 | inherent inclusion | Zhili's central doctrine. |
| 隨緣 | following conditions | The distinct teaching's principle that follows conditions, debated in Part 4. |
| 無作四諦 | the four truths without fabrication | The perfect teaching's four truths. |
