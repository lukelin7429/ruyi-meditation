The Record of the Teaching and Practice of the Venerable of Siming.

In 1202 Zongxiao (宗曉) gathered more than a hundred of Siming Zhili's (四明知禮, 960–1028) shorter writings, together with records about him, into seven fascicles. They include a year-by-year chronology, the bodhisattva precept rite, essays and questions on doctrine, his answers to the Japanese monk Genshin, letters to officials, Chan masters, and disciples, his vows, the documents of Yanqing Monastery, stele and stūpa inscriptions, and eulogies. Together they show the teacher behind the great commentaries, in practice as well as teaching.

How to read this collection

The pieces are short and can be read in any order; the chronology in Part 1 gives the frame.

Zhili's Essential Purport of Repentance Practice appears in Part 2 with the same English as its own page.
