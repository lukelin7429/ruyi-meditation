# The Four Teachings of Tiantai — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Tiantai sijiaoyi* (天台四教儀, Taishō T46, no. 1931 (Chegwan)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1931, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Chegwan died in 971; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** an English translation by David Chappell was published in 1983; we have not used it, and this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 五時八教 | the Five Periods and Eight Teachings | Tiantai's classification of the Buddha's teaching. |
| 化儀四教／化法四教 | the Four Teachings of Method / of Content | How the Buddha taught, and what he taught. |
| 藏通別圓 | the Tripiṭaka / shared / distinct / perfect teaching | The four teachings of content. |
| 六即 | the six identities | The stages of the perfect teaching. |
