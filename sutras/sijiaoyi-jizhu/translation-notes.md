# Collected Notes on the Four Teachings of Tiantai — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Tiantai sijiaoyi jizhu* (天台四教儀集註, CBETA P1629 (Mengrun), selected edition), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** CBETA P1629 (selected edition), via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Mengrun died in 1342; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 五時八教 | the Five Periods and Eight Teachings | Zhiyi's classification of the Buddha's teaching. |
| 藏通別圓 | the Tripiṭaka, shared, distinct, and perfect teachings | The four teachings of doctrine. |
| 六即 | the six identities | The stages of the perfect teaching. |
