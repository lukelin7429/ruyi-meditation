# Inscription on Silent Illumination — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Mozhao ming* (默照銘, Taishō T48, no. 2001, fascicle 8 (Hongzhi Zhengjue)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T2001, fascicle 8, via the CBETA digital edition. Hongzhi died in 1157; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** several published translations exist; we have not used them, and this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 默照 | silent illumination | Silence and illumination held together. |
| 正偏 | the upright and the inclined | The Caodong pair of the absolute and the relative. |
| 回互 | interweaving | The upright and the inclined turning into each other. |
| 家事 | the affair of our house | The practice as the house's own inheritance. |
