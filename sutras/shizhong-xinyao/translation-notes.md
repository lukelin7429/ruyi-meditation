# The Essentials of Beginning and End — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Shizhong xinyao* (始終心要, CBETA G1977 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** CBETA G1977 (科始終心要), via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 三諦 | the three truths | Empty, provisional, and the middle. |
| 三惑 | the three delusions | Views and habituated thought, dust-and-sand, and ignorance. |
| 三觀 | the three contemplations | Of emptiness, of the provisional, and of the middle. |
| 三智 | the three wisdoms | The wisdom that knows all things, the wisdom of the paths, and the wisdom knowing all modes. |
| 三德 | the three virtues | The Dharma-body, prajñā, and liberation. |
