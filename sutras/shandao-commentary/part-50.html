The Lotus Flower's Own Adornment

Having settled the mind on a single square inch of ground, the practice now advances by exact, deliberate steps: petal, then vein, then light, then canopy, then platform, then banner, then curtain, then pearl — each stage built only once the one before it has become clear.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Source: Taishō T1753, 觀無量壽佛經疏, by Shandao (善導, 613–681) Fascicle 3 of 4: 定善義 (Meaning of the Contemplative Good)

A Sequence, Not a Single Vision: Petal to Pearl

Chinese Source

九、從令其蓮華下，至八萬四千光已來，正明寶華有種種莊嚴。即有其三。一明一一華葉備眾寶色。二明一一葉有眾多寶脈。三明一一脈有眾多光色。此令行者住心一一想之，悉令心眼得見。既見華葉已，次想葉間眾寶，次想寶出多光，光成寶蓋。次想華臺、臺上眾寶及珠網等，次想臺上四柱寶幢，次想幢上寶幔，次想幔上寶珠光明雜色遍滿虛空，各現異相。如是次第一一住心不捨，不久之間即得定心。既得定心，彼諸莊嚴一切顯現。應知。

十、從了了下，辯觀成相。

Ru-Yi Translation

Ninth, from "causing this lotus flower" down through "eighty-four thousand rays of light," this makes plain that this jeweled flower possesses manifold adornment. There are three points within it. First, this makes plain that each and every petal of this flower is possessed of an assortment of jeweled colors. Second, this makes plain that each and every petal has manifold jeweled veins. Third, this makes plain that each and every vein has manifold colors of light. This causes the practitioner to settle the mind and visualize each of these in turn, so that the mind's own eye comes to behold them all in full. Having beheld the petals, one next visualizes the manifold jewels among the petals; next visualizes these jewels giving forth manifold light, this light forming a jeweled canopy; next visualizes the flower's own platform, and the manifold jewels and pearl nets upon that platform; next visualizes the four jeweled banners standing upon this platform; next visualizes the jeweled curtains hanging upon these banners; next visualizes the jeweled pearls upon these curtains, their manifold colors of light filling the whole of space, each manifesting its own distinct mark. Proceeding thus, in sequence, settling the mind upon each in turn without relinquishing any, before long one shall attain settled concentration — and, once settled concentration is attained, every one of these adornments shall appear in full. This should be understood.

Tenth, from "clearly and distinctly" onward, this distinguishes the mark of this contemplation's own accomplishment.

A note on this sequence

This precise, cumulative ordering — never attempting the platform before the petal, never the banner before the platform — carries forward the same disciplined method already established for the sun and the jeweled trees, and answers, in practice, the very doubt raised in the previous part: a being who cannot yet see a whole distant realm can still, plausibly, attend to a single petal's own vein. Each completed stage becomes the stable ground for the next, so that what would be impossible to grasp all at once becomes, through this staged approach, merely a long sequence of individually manageable steps.

Six Marks of Each and Every Petal

Chinese Source

十一、從華葉小者下，至遍覆地上已來，正明葉葉有種種莊嚴。即有其六。一明華葉大小。二明華葉多少。三明葉間珠映多少。四明珠有千光。五明一一珠光變成寶蓋。六明寶蓋上照虛空下覆寶地。

Ru-Yi Translation

Eleventh, from "the smallest of these flower petals" down through "covering the ground entirely," this makes plain that each and every petal possesses manifold adornment. There are six points within it. First, this makes plain the size of these petals, both large and small. Second, this makes plain the number of these petals. Third, this makes plain the number of jewels reflected among these petals. Fourth, this makes plain that these jewels possess a thousand rays of light. Fifth, this makes plain that each and every one of these jeweled lights transforms into a jeweled canopy. Sixth, this makes plain that these jeweled canopies illuminate the sky above and cover the jeweled ground below.

Part 50 Complete

This closes Part 50. Part 51 continues upward from the petals to the platform itself and its four banners, followed by the inconceivable power of the jeweled light — reaching every direction, transforming into whatever form each realm's own beings most need to see.
