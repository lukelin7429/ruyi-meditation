# The Wondrous Purport — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Miaozong chao* (觀無量壽佛經疏妙宗鈔, Taishō T37, no. 1751 (Zhili)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1751, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhili died in 1028; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 約心觀佛 | contemplating the Buddha in terms of the mind | Zhili's central principle. |
| 是心作佛，是心是佛 | this mind makes buddha; this mind is buddha | From the sūtra's eighth contemplation. |
| 性具 | inherent inclusion | The mind inherently contains all dharmas. |
| 六即佛 | the buddha of the six identities | From the buddha in principle to the buddha in the ultimate. |
