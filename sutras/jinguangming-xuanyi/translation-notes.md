# The Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Jinguangming jing xuanyi* (金光明經玄義, Taishō T39, no. 1783 (Zhiyi, recorded by Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1783, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhiyi died in 597; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 十種三法 | the ten sets of three dharmas | Matched to gold, light, and brightness. |
| 三識 | amala / ālaya / ādāna consciousness | One of the ten sets. |
| 三佛性 | the three buddha-natures | The correct cause, the revealing cause, and the conditioning cause. |
| 觀心釋 | explanation by contemplating the mind | The section later disputed by Song Tiantai. |
