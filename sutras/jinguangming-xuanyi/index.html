The Profound Meaning of the Golden Light Sūtra.

As in the Fahua Xuanyi, Zhiyi (智顗, 538–597) explains the title of the Golden Light Sūtra under five headings: name, essence, cardinal doctrine, function, and the teaching. Under the name he matches the three characters gold, light, and brightness to ten sets of three — the three virtues, three jewels, three nirvāṇas, three bodies, three vehicles, three bodhis, three prajñās, three buddha-natures, three consciousnesses, and three paths. A short section on contemplating the mind became, four centuries later, the center of the dispute between Zhili and the off-mountain masters over whether it was authentic.
