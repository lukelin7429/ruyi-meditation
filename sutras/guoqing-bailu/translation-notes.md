# The Hundred Records of Guoqing — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Guoqing bailu* (國清百錄, Taishō T46, no. 1934 (Guanding)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1934, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Guanding died in 632; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 立制法 | the rules for the community | Item 1: Zhiyi's ten rules. |
| 懺法 | repentance rite | Items 4–6 outline three rites. |
| 遺書 | last testament | Zhiyi's letter to the Prince of Jin before his death. |
