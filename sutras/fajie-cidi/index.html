The Gateway to the Sequence of the Dharma Realm.

Zhiyi (智顗, 538–597) wrote this handbook for readers who stumble over the names and numbers in the sūtras and treatises. It takes sixty categories — from name and form, the aggregates, and the elements, through the afflictions, precepts, and meditative concentrations, to the thirty-seven factors, the six pāramitās, the powers and fearlessnesses, and the marks of a buddha — and for each gives the list, why it comes at this point in the sequence, and a definition of every item. It is the vocabulary of the whole Tiantai tradition in one place.
