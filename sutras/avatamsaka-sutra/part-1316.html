In Every Jewel of the Four Walls

Every jewel in the four walls of every tower shows Maitreya practicing the bodhisattva path in kalpas gone by: giving away his head and eyes, hands and feet, flesh and marrow, and even his nails and hair; giving away wives and children, cities and villages, kingdoms and thrones; freeing prisoners, loosing the bound, healing the sick, and setting the lost on the right path; serving as ship's captain, horse-king, great sage, wheel-turning king, physician-king, and dharma-teacher; and, seated on the lion-throne, guiding beings to the precepts and the Three Jewels. Then Good-Wealth sees the good spiritual friends Maitreya once served, and they turn to him and bid him welcome.

In Every Jewel of the Four Walls

Maitreya's past lives of giving, rescue, and service, seen in the walls of the tower, and the good spiritual friends who trained him.

Chinese Source

又見諸樓閣周迴四壁，一一步內，一切眾寶以為莊嚴。一一寶中，皆現彌勒曩劫修行菩薩道時，或施頭目，或施手足、脣舌、牙齒、耳鼻、血肉、皮膚、骨髓乃至爪髮，如是一切，悉皆能捨；妻妾、男女、城邑、聚落、國土、王位，隨其所須，盡皆施與。處牢獄者，令得出離；被繫縛者，使其解脫；有疾病者，為其救療；入邪徑者，示其正道。或為船師，令度大海；或為馬王，救護惡難；或為大仙，善說諸論；或為輪王，勸修十善；或為醫王，善療眾病；或孝順父母，或親近善友，或作聲聞，或作緣覺，或作菩薩，或作如來，教化調伏一切眾生；或為法師，奉行佛教，受持讀誦，如理思惟，立佛支提，作佛形像，若自供養，若勸於他，塗香散華，恭敬禮拜。如是等事，相續不絕。或見坐於師子之座，廣演說法，勸諸眾生安住十善，一心歸向佛、法、僧寶，受持五戒及八齋戒，出家聽法，受持讀誦，如理修行。乃至見於彌勒菩薩，百千億那由他阿僧祇劫，修行諸度一切色像；又見彌勒曾所承事諸善知識，悉以一切功德莊嚴；亦見彌勒在彼一一善知識所，親近供養，受行其教，乃至住於灌頂之地。時，諸知識告善財言：「善來童子！汝觀此菩薩不思議事，莫生疲厭。」

Ru-Yi Translation

He saw, moreover, the four walls surrounding every tower, adorned within the space of each and every step with every jewel. And in each and every jewel there appeared Maitreya at the time when, in kalpas gone by, he cultivated the bodhisattva path: giving away his head and eyes, or giving away his hands and feet, his lips and tongue, his teeth, his ears and nose, his blood and flesh, his skin, his marrow, and even his nails and hair, able to give up every one of these; and giving away entirely, according to whatever was needed, his wives and consorts, his sons and daughters, his cities and villages, his kingdoms and his royal throne. Those in prison he caused to be freed; those in bonds he set at liberty; those who were sick he rescued and healed; and those who had entered a wrong path he showed the right way. Or as a ship's captain he carried others across the great ocean; or as a horse-king he rescued them from peril; or as a great sage he skillfully expounded the treatises; or as a wheel-turning king he urged them to cultivate the ten wholesome deeds; or as a physician-king he skillfully healed every illness; or he was filial to his father and mother; or he drew near to good friends; or he was a śrāvaka, or a pratyekabuddha, or a bodhisattva, or a tathāgata, teaching, transforming, and taming every sentient being; or as a dharma-teacher he carried out the Buddha's teaching, receiving, upholding, reading, and reciting it, reflecting on it according to principle, raising shrines to the buddhas and making images of the buddhas, making offerings himself and urging others to do so, anointing them with fragrant unguents, scattering flowers, and paying reverent homage. Such deeds as these continued without interruption. Or he saw him seated upon the lion-throne, expounding the dharma at length, urging sentient beings to abide in the ten wholesome deeds, to turn single-mindedly toward the jewels of the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Saṃgha, to receive and uphold the five precepts and the eight precepts of the fast, to leave the household and hear the dharma, and to receive, uphold, read, recite, and practice it according to principle. And so on until he saw Maitreya Bodhisattva, through hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of asaṃkhyeya kalpas, cultivating the pāramitās, in every one of his forms; and he saw the good spiritual friends whom Maitreya had once served, all of them adorned with every merit; and he saw Maitreya in the presence of each and every one of those good spiritual friends, drawing near to them and making offerings, receiving their teaching and putting it into practice, and so on until he abided in the station of consecration. Then those good spiritual friends addressed Good-Wealth, saying, "Welcome, youth! Look upon the inconceivable deeds of this bodhisattva, and do not grow weary."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

或施頭目 — the gifts of the body are listed in nine groups, from the head and eyes down to the nails and hair, and then the gifts of what a king possesses in six more, wives and consorts, sons and daughters, cities and villages, kingdoms and throne; the two lists are kept in the source's order and neither is abridged. What follows them is a list of roles, ten in all counting each 或為 and 或作, from ship's captain to tathāgata and dharma-teacher, and the translation renders each with the phrase this site has used for it before, so that the horse-king and the physician-king of earlier fascicles are recognizable here.

善來童子 — the good spiritual friends whom Maitreya himself once served are the ones who speak, and they speak to Good-Wealth rather than to Maitreya, greeting him with the same 善來 that his own spiritual friends have used along the way. Their instruction, not to grow weary while looking, closes the long inventory of the tower's walls and prepares the shift that follows, in which the sūtra pauses to explain by what powers Good-Wealth is able to see all this at once.
