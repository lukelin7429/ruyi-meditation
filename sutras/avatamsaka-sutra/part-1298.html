Reflected Across Forty Thousand Yojanas

The paired similes continue through a pearl named Sovereign King that reflects the sun and moon's own palaces from forty thousand yojanas away, an ocean-treasury jewel, Jambu-river gold surpassed only by a single mind-jewel, and a warrior's own unbreakable armor.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1298 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Reflected Across Forty Thousand Yojanas

Six more paired similes continue the pattern — a pearl reflecting the sun and moon from vast distance, an ocean's own treasury, Jambu-river gold, mastery over every nāga, and a warrior's own armor no enemy can overcome.

Chinese Source

「善男子！如有寶珠，名：自在王，處閻浮洲，去日月輪四萬由旬，日月宮中所有莊嚴，其珠影現悉皆具足。菩薩摩訶薩發菩提心淨功德寶亦復如是，住生死中，照法界空，佛智日月一切功德悉於中現。「善男子！如有寶珠，名：自在王，日月光明所照之處，一切財寶、衣服等物，所有價直悉不能及。菩薩摩訶薩發菩提心自在王寶亦復如是，一切智光所照之處，三世所有天人、二乘漏無漏善一切功德皆不能及。「善男子！海中有寶，名曰：海藏，普現海中諸莊嚴事。菩薩摩訶薩菩提心寶亦復如是，普能顯現一切智海諸莊嚴事。「善男子！譬如天上閻浮檀金，唯除心王大摩尼寶，餘無及者。菩薩摩訶薩發菩提心閻浮檀金亦復如是，除一切智心王大寶，餘無及者。「善男子！譬如有人，善調龍法，於諸龍中而得自在。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心善調龍法，於諸一切煩惱龍中而得自在。「善男子！譬如勇士，被執鎧仗，一切怨敵無能降伏。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，被執菩提大心鎧仗，一切業惑諸惡怨敵無能屈伏。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as there is a jeweled pearl named Sovereign King, and though it sits upon Jambudvīpa a full forty thousand yojanas from the discs of the sun and moon, every adornment within the palaces of the sun and moon appears reflected within it entirely, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own jewel of pure merit that arouses the mind of bodhi is likewise: abiding within birth-and-death, illuminating the emptiness of the dharma-realm, every merit of the sun and moon of a buddha's own wisdom appears reflected within it entirely. Good son! Just as there is a jeweled pearl named Sovereign King, and wherever the light of the sun and moon shines, no worth among every treasure, garment, or other possession there can equal it, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own Sovereign King jewel that arouses the mind of bodhi is likewise: wherever the light of all-knowing wisdom shines, no merit among every deva or human of the three times, nor any wholesome dharma of the two vehicles with or without outflows, is able to equal it. Good son! Within the ocean there is a jewel named Ocean-Treasury, and it universally manifests every adornment within the ocean, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own jewel that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: it is universally able to manifest every adornment within the ocean of all-knowing wisdom. Good son! Just as, among the Jambu-river gold found in the heavens, only the great maṇi-jewel that is the mind-king can equal it and nothing else can, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own Jambu-river gold that arouses the mind of bodhi is likewise: only the great jewel that is the mind-king of all-knowing wisdom can equal it, and nothing else can. Good son! Just as, when a person is skilled in the method of taming nāgas, they attain mastery among every nāga, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the method of skillfully taming nāgas that is the mind of bodhi, they attain mastery among every nāga of affliction. Good son! Just as a courageous warrior, having donned armor and taken up weapons, cannot be overcome by any enemy whatsoever, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having donned the armor and taken up the weapons that are the great mind of bodhi, cannot be subdued by any evil enemy of karma or delusion whatsoever.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

去日月輪四萬由旬，日月宮中所有莊嚴，其珠影現悉皆具足 — the same jewel-name 自在王 (Sovereign King) is used for two separate similes in immediate succession, each drawing out a different property of the same named jewel: the first for its power to reflect, across vast distance, what it does not itself touch; the second for a worth that exceeds every possession within reach of its light. Both confirmed as distinct source passages rather than a repeated line.
