Ten Gifts from the Buddhas of Every Direction

The eleven-part "in this way" that closed Part 538 answers itself here: the instant the bodhisattva enters this samādhi, buddhas beyond counting appear before him in every direction, each bestowing one gift — and the bodhisattva who receives all ten becomes, for the world, a sun, a moon, a mountain, an ocean, a lamp.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 539 — ten gifts, five images Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Gifts from the Buddhas of Every Direction

Each gift arrives already paired with what it is for — not simply given, but given so that something specific becomes possible.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩入普賢菩薩所住如是大智慧三昧時，十方各有不可說不可說國土，一一國土各有不可說不可說佛剎微塵數如來名號，一一名號各有不可說不可說佛剎微塵數諸佛而現其前，與如來念力，令不忘失如來境界；與一切法究竟慧，令入一切智；與知一切法種種義決定慧，令受持一切佛法趣入無礙；與無上佛菩提，令入一切智開悟法界；與菩薩究竟慧，令得一切法光明，無諸黑闇；

Ru-Yi Translation

Sons of the Buddha! When the bodhisattva-mahāsattva enters this great wisdom samādhi in which Samantabhadra dwells, in each of the ten directions there are ineffably ineffable lands; in each and every land there are tathāgata-names as numerous as the dust-motes of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; and for each and every name there appear before him buddhas as numerous as the dust-motes of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands — bestowing the tathāgatas' own power of mindfulness, causing him never to forget the tathāgatas' realm; bestowing ultimate wisdom regarding all dharmas, causing him to enter all-knowing wisdom; bestowing the decisive wisdom that knows the manifold meanings of all dharmas, causing him to uphold every buddha-dharma and enter it without obstruction; bestowing unsurpassed buddha-bodhi, causing him to enter all-knowing wisdom and awaken to the dharma-realm; bestowing the bodhisattva's ultimate wisdom, causing him to attain the light of every dharma, free of all darkness;

Chinese Source

與菩薩不退智，令知時、非時，善巧方便調伏眾生；與無障礙菩薩辯才，令悟解無邊法演說無盡；與神通變化力，令現不可說不可說差別身無邊色相種種不同開悟眾生；與圓滿言音，令現不可說不可說差別音聲種種言辭開悟眾生；與不唐捐力，令一切眾生若得見形、若得聞法皆悉成就，無空過者。

Ru-Yi Translation

bestowing the bodhisattva's non-regressing wisdom, causing him to know the right time and the wrong time, taming beings with skillful means; bestowing the bodhisattva's unobstructed eloquence, causing him to awaken to boundless dharmas and expound them inexhaustibly; bestowing the power of spiritual transformation, causing him to manifest ineffably ineffable distinct bodies of boundless and varied form to awaken beings; bestowing a perfect voice, causing him to manifest ineffably ineffable distinct sounds and manifold words to awaken beings; and bestowing the power by which nothing is wasted, causing every being who sees his form or hears his teaching to attain accomplishment, with not one passing by in vain.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

與不唐捐力 — bestowing the power by which nothing is wasted. The tenth and final gift, and the one that guarantees the other nine were not given in vain either: every being who so much as sees this bodhisattva's form or hears him teach reaches accomplishment. Part 536 compared a bodhisattva's giving to a wish-fulfilling jewel; this gift is what keeps that jewel's giving from ever landing on empty ground.

Sun, Moon, Mountain, Ocean, Lamp

Having received the ten gifts, the bodhisattva is described through what he becomes for others — five images in a row, four of them about light and depth, one about simply not moving.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩如是滿足普賢行故，得如來力，淨出離道，滿一切智，以無礙辯才神通變化，究竟調伏一切眾生；具佛威德，淨普賢行，住普賢道，盡未來際，為欲調伏一切眾生，轉一切佛微妙法輪。何以故？佛子！此菩薩摩訶薩成就如是殊勝大願諸菩薩行，則為一切世間法師，則為一切世間法日，則為一切世間智月；則為一切世間須彌山王，嶷然高出，堅固不動；則為一切世間無涯智海；則為一切世間正法明燈，普照無邊，相續不斷；為一切眾生開示無邊清淨功德，皆令安住功德善根；順一切智，大願平等，修習普賢廣大之行，常能勸發無量眾生，住不可說不可說廣大行三昧，現大自在。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sons of the Buddha! Because the bodhisattva-mahāsattva has thus fulfilled the practice of Samantabhadra, he attains the power of the tathāgatas, purifies the path of release, and fulfills all-knowing wisdom; with unobstructed eloquence and spiritual transformation he ultimately tames all beings. Endowed with the buddhas' own awesome virtue, purifying the practice of Samantabhadra and abiding in the path of Samantabhadra, to the very end of the future, wishing to tame all beings, he turns every buddha's sublime and wondrous Dharma-wheel. Why is this so? Sons of the Buddha! This bodhisattva-mahāsattva, having accomplished such supreme great vows and bodhisattva practices, becomes a dharma-teacher for the whole world, becomes a sun of dharma for the whole world, becomes a moon of wisdom for the whole world; becomes the king of Mount Sumeru for the whole world, towering aloft, firm and unmoving; becomes a shoreless ocean of wisdom for the whole world; becomes a bright lamp of the true dharma for the whole world, universally illuminating without limit, continuing without break; reveals boundless and pure merit for all beings, causing them all to abide securely in wholesome roots of merit; according with all-knowing wisdom and with great vows equal to every buddha's, he cultivates the vast practice of Samantabhadra, ever able to encourage and inspire measureless beings, abiding in ineffably ineffable vast samādhis of practice, and manifesting great sovereign freedom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

則為一切世間須彌山王，嶷然高出，堅固不動 — becomes the king of Mount Sumeru for the whole world, towering aloft, firm and unmoving. Set among four images about radiating or holding light — sun, moon, ocean, lamp — the mountain does something none of the others do: it simply does not move. A world lit from every side still needs one thing in it that stays put.
