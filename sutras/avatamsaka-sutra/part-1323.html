Fascicle Seventy-Nine Complete

Maitreya's last words send Good-Wealth back to the teacher with whom his journey began. He is to go to Mañjuśrī and ask seven questions, how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva practices and enters the gate of Samantabhadra's practice, how they are accomplished, made vast, made compliant, purified, and brought to fullness. Mañjuśrī, Maitreya says, is the mother of measureless buddhas and the teacher of measureless bodhisattvas, and every good spiritual friend Good-Wealth has met, every practice heard, every gate of liberation entered, has been Mañjuśrī's own majestic power at work. Good-Wealth bows his head at Maitreya's feet, circles him countless times, and departs, and the fascicle closes.

Fascicle Seventy-Nine Complete

Maitreya sends Good-Wealth to Mañjuśrī, praises him, and reveals him behind the whole journey; Good-Wealth takes his leave.

Chinese Source

「善男子！汝當往詣文殊師利善知識所而問之言：『菩薩云何學菩薩行？云何而入普賢行門？云何成就？云何廣大？云何隨順？云何清淨？云何圓滿？』善男子！彼當為汝分別演說。何以故？文殊師利所有大願，非餘無量百千億那由他菩薩之所能有。「善男子！文殊師利童子，其行廣大，其願無邊，出生一切菩薩功德無有休息。善男子！文殊師利常為無量百千億那由他諸佛母，常為無量百千億那由他菩薩師，教化成熟一切眾生，名稱普聞十方世界；常於一切諸佛眾中為說法師，一切如來之所讚歎；住甚深智，能如實見一切諸法，通達一切解脫境界，究竟普賢所行諸行。「善男子！文殊師利童子是汝善知識，令汝得生如來家，長養一切諸善根，發起一切助道法，值遇真實善知識；令汝修一切功德，入一切願網，住一切大願；為汝說一切菩薩祕密法，現一切菩薩難思行；與汝往昔同生同行。「是故，善男子！汝應往詣文殊之所莫生疲厭，文殊師利當為汝說一切功德。何以故？汝先所見諸善知識聞菩薩行、入解脫門、滿足大願，皆是文殊威神之力，文殊師利於一切處咸得究竟。」時，善財童子頂禮其足，遶無量匝，慇懃瞻仰，辭退而去。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! You should go to the good spiritual friend Mañjuśrī and ask him, 'How does a bodhisattva learn the bodhisattva practices? How does he enter the gate of Samantabhadra's practice? How is it accomplished? How is it made vast? How is it made compliant? How is it purified? How is it brought to fullness?' Good son! He will expound it for you in full. Why is that? The great vows that Mañjuśrī possesses are such as no other bodhisattva among measureless hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas is able to possess. Good son! Mañjuśrī, the youth, is vast in his practice and boundless in his vows, and he brings forth the merits of every bodhisattva without ever resting. Good son! Mañjuśrī is ever the mother of measureless hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of buddhas, and ever the teacher of measureless hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of bodhisattvas, teaching and ripening every sentient being, and his renown is universally heard throughout the worlds of the ten directions; he is ever the dharma-teacher in the assemblies of all the buddhas, praised by every tathāgata; he abides in the most profound wisdom, is able to see every dharma as it truly is, penetrates every domain of liberation, and has brought to completion every practice that Samantabhadra practices. Good son! Mañjuśrī, the youth, is your good spiritual friend, who has caused you to be born into the family of the tathāgatas, to nurture every wholesome root, to give rise to every dharma that aids the path, and to meet true good spiritual friends; who has caused you to cultivate every merit, to enter every net of vows, and to abide in every great vow; who has taught you every secret dharma of the bodhisattvas and shown you every inconceivable practice of the bodhisattvas; and who in ages past was born together with you and practiced together with you. Therefore, good son, you should go to where Mañjuśrī is and not grow weary, and Mañjuśrī will teach you every merit. Why is that? Every good spiritual friend you have seen before now, every bodhisattva practice you have heard, every gate of liberation you have entered, and every great vow you have fulfilled, all of these have been the majestic spiritual power of Mañjuśrī, for Mañjuśrī has attained the ultimate in every place." At that time, Good-Wealth the youth bowed his head at his feet, circled him countless times, gazed up at him with earnest devotion, took his leave, and departed.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

云何 — the questions Good-Wealth is to carry to Mañjuśrī are seven in number, and the first of them, 菩薩云何學菩薩行, is the question he has put to every good spiritual friend on his journey and put to Maitreya himself at the opening of fascicle seventy-eight. The remaining six ask, in effect, what becomes of that practice once entered: how it is accomplished, made vast, made compliant, purified, and brought to fullness. The translation keeps them as seven separate questions inside the inner quotation, as the source marks them with 『 』.

皆是文殊威神之力 — Maitreya's closing revelation is that the whole pilgrimage has been Mañjuśrī's doing: every good spiritual friend Good-Wealth has met, every practice, every gate of liberation, every fulfilled vow. The measure 無量百千億那由他, measureless hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas, occurs three times in the praise of Mañjuśrī and is rendered in full each time. The four-part formula with which Good-Wealth leaves, bowing at the feet, circling countless times, gazing up with earnest devotion, and taking his leave, is the one this chapter has used at the close of his visits to earlier friends, and it closes the fascicle. The colophon line that follows, naming the fascicle, is not part of the translated text.
