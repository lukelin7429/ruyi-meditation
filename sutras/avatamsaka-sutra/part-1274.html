Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1274 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

All Such Merit

Two closing lines of the litany open this short Part; a single summary sentence closes the entire quotation begun back in Part 1272, and Good-Wealth's own turn toward verse brings it to an end.

Chinese Source

是雖行於慈而於諸眾生無所愛戀，雖行於悲而於諸眾生無所取著，雖行於喜而觀苦眾生心常哀愍，雖行於捨而不廢捨利益他事者之所住處；是雖行九次第定而不厭離欲界受生，雖知一切法無生無滅而不於實際作證，雖入三解脫門而不取聲聞解脫，雖觀四聖諦而不住小乘聖果，雖觀甚深緣起而不住究竟寂滅，雖修八聖道而不求永出世間，雖超凡夫地而不墮聲聞、辟支佛地，雖觀五取蘊而不永滅諸蘊，雖超出四魔而不分別諸魔，雖不著六處而不永滅六處，雖安住真如而不墮實際，雖說一切乘而不捨大乘。此大樓閣，是住如是等一切諸功德者之所住處。」爾時，善財童子而說頌言：「

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who, though practicing loving-kindness, holds no attachment or affection toward any sentient being, though practicing compassion, grasps at nothing among sentient beings, though practicing sympathetic joy, contemplates suffering sentient beings with a mind of constant sorrow and compassion, and though practicing equanimity, never abandons the work of benefiting others; the dwelling-place of one who, though practicing the nine successive absorptions, is not wearied of taking birth within the desire realm, though knowing that every dharma is without birth or extinction, does not realize this within the true limit, though entering the three gates of liberation, does not take up a śrāvaka's own liberation, though contemplating the four noble truths, does not abide in the fruit of a lesser vehicle, though contemplating profound dependent origination, does not abide in ultimate quiescence, though cultivating the eightfold noble path, does not seek to leave the world forever, though transcending the stage of an ordinary being, does not fall into the stage of a śrāvaka or a pratyekabuddha, though contemplating the five aggregates of clinging, does not extinguish the aggregates forever, though transcending the four māras, makes no discrimination among the māras, though unattached to the six sense-bases, does not extinguish the six sense-bases forever, though abiding in suchness, does not fall into the true limit, and though expounding every vehicle, does not abandon the great vehicle. This great tower is the dwelling-place of one who abides in every such merit." At that time, Good-Wealth then spoke a verse, saying:

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

此大樓閣，是住如是等一切諸功德者之所住處。」 — the closing line of the entire litany, and the closing mark of the quotation Good-Wealth began forming back in Part 1272. Counted end to end across Parts 1272 through 1274, the full litany runs to fifty-four parallel lines before this final summary sentence, each built on the same 是⋯者之所住處 frame — verified directly against the source text by splitting on every 「；」 across all four Parts.

The eleven-fold line closing this Part sweeps through the nine successive absorptions, the unborn nature of dharmas, the three gates of liberation, the four noble truths, dependent origination, the eightfold noble path, the ground of an ordinary being, the five aggregates of clinging, the four māras, the six sense-bases, and suchness itself — a deliberate final gathering-up of the most classic categories in the Buddhist path, each held and not clung to in the same breath.
