The Named Samādhis Continue

This second group of twenty-five samādhi names ranges from purifying every present buddha-land through decisive understanding to causing every world to become a single buddha-land, and includes a samādhi for knowing that every tathāgata, however many there are, shares a single body.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 873 — the named samādhis continue Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Every Tathāgata, One Single Body

Among this group, one samādhi makes a claim as bold as any elsewhere in this sūtra: knowing that every tathāgata, across every world and every age, shares a single body — not a resemblance between separate buddhas, but one body underlying all of them.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知一切如來同一身三昧 — the Samādhi of Knowing That Every Tathāgata Shares a Single Body. Placed between a samādhi of vajra-wisdom and one about the dharma-realm abiding within a single thought, this name makes one of the sūtra's boldest claims almost in passing: that the countless buddhas named and described throughout this entire fascicle are not many bodies resembling one another, but a single body known in full.
