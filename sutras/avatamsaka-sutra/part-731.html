Unobstructed Function Toward Vows and Domains

The unobstructed function toward vows moves from taking every bodhisattva's own vow as one's own to a final entry built from weather itself — clouds of dharma, lightning of liberation, thunder, and a rain of ambrosia; the unobstructed function toward domains that follows names ten pairs, each one abiding fully in one domain while never once abandoning its apparent opposite.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 731 — unobstructed function toward vows and domains Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Abiding in One Domain, Never Leaving Its Opposite

Every one of the ten unobstructed functions toward domains follows the same shape: abiding fully in one thing — a buddha's domain, nirvāṇa, stillness, empty space itself — while never once abandoning what would ordinarily seem its opposite: māra's domain, birth and death, distraction, the idle discourse of every sentient being. No domain here excludes its own contrary.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種願無礙用。何等為十？所謂：以一切菩薩願作自願無礙用；以一切佛成菩提願力示現自成正覺無礙用；隨所化眾生自成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提無礙用；於一切無邊際劫大願不斷無礙用；遠離識身，不著智身，以自在願現一切身無礙用；捨棄自身成滿他願無礙用；普教化一切眾生而不捨大願無礙用；於一切劫行菩薩行而大願不斷無礙用；於一毛孔現成正覺，以願力故，充遍一切諸佛國土，於不可說不可說世界，為一一眾生如是示現無礙用；說一句法遍一切法界，興大正法雲，耀解脫電光，震實法雷音，雨甘露味雨，以大願力充洽一切諸眾生界無礙用。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of unobstructed function toward vows. What are these ten? Namely: the unobstructed function of taking every bodhisattva's vow as one's own vow; the unobstructed function of manifesting one's own attainment of right awakening through the power of every buddha's vow to attain awakening; the unobstructed function of attaining unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening oneself in accordance with whichever sentient being is being transformed; the unobstructed function of never once interrupting a great vow throughout every boundless kalpa; the unobstructed function of staying far from a body of consciousness, not attached to a body of wisdom, and manifesting every kind of body through a vow of sovereign freedom; the unobstructed function of giving up one's own body to bring another's vow to fulfillment; the unobstructed function of universally teaching and transforming every sentient being without ever abandoning the great vow; the unobstructed function of carrying out bodhisattva practice throughout every kalpa without ever interrupting the great vow; the unobstructed function of manifesting the attainment of right awakening within a single pore, and, through the power of that vow, filling every buddha-land, manifesting this in just the same way for each and every sentient being throughout unspeakably, unspeakably many worlds; and the unobstructed function of speaking a single phrase of dharma that pervades the whole dharma-realm, raising great clouds of true dharma, flashing lightning of liberation, sounding the thunder of real dharma, and raining down rain with the taste of ambrosia, saturating the whole realm of sentient beings through the power of the great vow. These are the ten.

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種境界無礙用。何等為十？所謂：在法界境界而不捨眾生境界無礙用；在佛境界而不捨魔境界無礙用；在涅槃境界而不捨生死境界無礙用；入一切智境界而不斷菩薩種性境界無礙用；住寂靜境界而不捨散亂境界無礙用；住無去、無來、無戲論、無相狀、無體性、無言說、如虛空境界而不捨一切眾生戲論境界無礙用；住諸力解脫境界而不捨一切諸方所境界無礙用；入無眾生際境界而不捨教化一切眾生無礙用；住禪定解脫、神通明智、寂靜境界而於一切世界示現受生無礙用；住如來一切行莊嚴成正覺境界而現一切聲聞、辟支佛寂靜威儀無礙用。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of unobstructed function toward domains. What are these ten? Namely: the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of the dharma-realm without ever abandoning the domain of sentient beings; the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of a buddha without ever abandoning the domain of māra; the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of nirvāṇa without ever abandoning the domain of birth and death; the unobstructed function of entering the domain of all-knowing wisdom without ever cutting off the domain of the bodhisattva lineage; the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of stillness without ever abandoning the domain of distraction; the unobstructed function of abiding in a domain with no going and no coming, no idle discourse, no form, no essential nature, and no speech, exactly like empty space, without ever abandoning the domain of every sentient being's idle discourse; the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of every power and liberation without ever abandoning the domain of every place in every direction; the unobstructed function of entering the domain where no sentient being's limit can be found without ever abandoning the teaching and transformation of every sentient being; the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of meditative concentration, liberation, spiritual penetration, clear wisdom, and stillness, while still manifesting birth in every world; and the unobstructed function of abiding in the domain of every adorned tathāgata practice that attains right awakening, while still manifesting the tranquil bearing of every śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

在涅槃境界而不捨生死境界 — abiding in the domain of nirvāṇa without ever abandoning the domain of birth and death. Placed third among ten pairs of domains, this entry states most directly what the whole list is doing: the two domains are not reconciled or blended into something in between, but held together exactly as they are, each fully present without displacing the other.
