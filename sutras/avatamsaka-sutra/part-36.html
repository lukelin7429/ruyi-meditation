The Four Noble Truths Begin

Mañjuśrī now turns from the Buddha's own names to the very first teaching he ever gave — suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path — and applies to it the identical logic just established: within this Sahā world alone, each of the Four Noble Truths already carries ten different names, and the same fourfold naming repeats, differently, in every world beyond it.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 36 — continues fascicle 12 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Four Noble Truths in This Sahā World

Chinese Source

爾時，文殊師利菩薩摩訶薩告諸菩薩言：「諸佛子！苦聖諦，此娑婆世界中，或名：罪，或名：逼迫，或名：變異，或名：攀緣，或名：聚，或名：刺，或名：依根，或名：虛誑，或名：癰瘡處，或名：愚夫行。諸佛子！苦集聖諦，此娑婆世界中，或名：繫縛，或名：滅壞，或名：愛著義，或名：妄覺念，或名：趣入，或名：決定，或名：網，或名：戲論，或名：隨行，或名：顛倒根。諸佛子！苦滅聖諦，此娑婆世界中，或名：無諍，或名：離塵，或名：寂靜，或名：無相，或名：無沒，或名：無自性，或名：無障礙，或名：滅，或名：體真實，或名：住自性。諸佛子！苦滅道聖諦，此娑婆世界中，或名：一乘，或名：趣寂，或名：導引，或名：究竟無分別，或名：平等，或名：捨擔，或名：無所趣，或名：隨聖意，或名：仙人行，或名：十藏。諸佛子！此娑婆世界說四聖諦，有如是等四百億十千名；隨眾生心，悉令調伏。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva addressed the bodhisattvas, saying: "Buddha's children! The Noble Truth of Suffering, within this Sahā world, is variously named: Transgression, or named Oppression, or named Transformation and Change, or named Grasping and Conditioning, or named Accumulation, or named the Thorn, or named That Which Rests Upon the Faculties, or named Falsehood and Deception, or named the Place of the Boil and Wound, or named the Conduct of the Foolish. Buddha's children! The Noble Truth of Suffering's Origin, within this Sahā world, is variously named: Bondage, or named Destruction and Ruin, or named the Meaning of Love and Attachment, or named False, Deluded Thought, or named Entering In, or named Resolution, or named the Net, or named Idle Discourse, or named Following Conduct, or named the Root of Inversion. Buddha's children! The Noble Truth of Suffering's Cessation, within this Sahā world, is variously named: Free of Contention, or named Free of Defilement, or named Tranquility, or named Marklessness, or named Without Submergence, or named Without Any Fixed Nature, or named Without Any Obstruction, or named Cessation, or named the True Body, or named Abiding in One's Own Nature. Buddha's children! The Noble Truth of the Path to Suffering's Cessation, within this Sahā world, is variously named: the Single Vehicle, or named Hastening Toward Tranquility, or named Guidance, or named the Ultimate Without Discrimination, or named Equality, or named Laying Down the Burden, or named That Which Has No Destination, or named Following the Sage's Own Intent, or named the Sage's Own Conduct, or named the Ten Treasuries. Buddha's children! This Sahā world's teaching of the Four Noble Truths has such names as these, four hundred million and ten thousand of them; according to the mind of every living being, it causes them all to be tamed."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Even within a single world, the same truth accumulates a striking range of names — suffering as "the Thorn" beside suffering as "the Place of the Boil and Wound," suffering's cessation as "Free of Contention" beside cessation as simply "the True Body." Chengguan's commentary reads this multiplicity as already doing real doctrinal work: the Four Noble Truths are not one fixed formula memorized once, but a structure flexible enough to be recognized under an unbounded range of descriptions, each name catching some facet the others do not.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Worth noting: this chapter's list of neighboring worlds is not identical to the ten named back in Part 35 for "The Names of the Tathāgata," even though several names recur (Secret Instruction, Free of Defilement, Gathering In, Abundant Benefit, Scant and Few, Joy, the Gate-Bolt, Resounding Voice all appear in both). Here a world named Supreme takes a position that chapter used for a world named Abundant Overflow, and Abundant Overflow itself reappears later in this list instead. This translation renders each chapter exactly as its own text gives it, rather than forcing the two lists into an artificial match the source itself does not make.

Part 36 Complete

This closes Part 36, continuing fascicle twelve and the sūtra's eighth chapter, "The Four Noble Truths" (四聖諦品第八): the ten names of each truth within this Sahā world, and within three worlds beyond it — Secret Instruction, Supreme, and Free of Defilement. Part 37 continues with three more worlds.
