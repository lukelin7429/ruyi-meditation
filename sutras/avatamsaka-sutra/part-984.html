Loving-Conduct's Confession, and an Eleventh Teacher

The dhāraṇī-gate list closes on bodhicitta itself — arising, aided, purified — before Loving-Conduct turns to the same confession every teacher before her has made, and sends Good-Wealth south once more, this time to a monk whose very name, Well-Seen, promises to complete something her own gates of knowledge left open.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 984 — Loving-Conduct's confession, and an eleventh teacher Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The List Closes on Bodhicitta Itself

After more than a hundred named gates, the list ends where the entire pilgrimage began — the mind of bodhi itself, its own arising, its own aid, and finally the plain purity of one's own mind, as though every gate before it had simply been circling back toward this single starting point.

Chinese Source

入甚深法陀羅尼門、入微妙法陀羅尼門、菩提心陀羅尼門、起菩提心陀羅尼門、助菩提心陀羅尼門、諸願陀羅尼門、諸行陀羅尼門、神通陀羅尼門、出離陀羅尼門、總持清淨陀羅尼門、智輪清淨陀羅尼門、智慧清淨陀羅尼門、菩提無量陀羅尼門、自心清淨陀羅尼門。

Ru-Yi Translation

The dhāraṇī-gate of entering the profound dharma, the dhāraṇī-gate of entering the subtle and wondrous dharma, the dhāraṇī-gate of the mind of bodhi, the dhāraṇī-gate of giving rise to the mind of bodhi, the dhāraṇī-gate of aiding the mind of bodhi, the dhāraṇī-gate of every vow, the dhāraṇī-gate of every practice, the dhāraṇī-gate of spiritual penetration, the dhāraṇī-gate of renunciation, the dhāraṇī-gate of the purity of total retention, the dhāraṇī-gate of the purity of the wheel of wisdom, the dhāraṇī-gate of the purity of wisdom, the dhāraṇī-gate of the immeasurability of bodhi, and the dhāraṇī-gate of the purity of one's own mind.

South Once More, to Well-Seen

Having just shown Good-Wealth over a hundred million dhāraṇī-gates, Loving-Conduct still calls her own knowledge a single gate among countless others, and sends him onward to a monk in the land of Three Eyes — a name that seems to promise more sight than her own hall of mirrors already offered.

Chinese Source

「善男子！我唯知此般若波羅蜜普莊嚴門。如諸菩薩摩訶薩，其心廣大，等虛空界，入於法界，福德成滿，住出世法，遠世間行，智眼無瞖，普觀法界，慧心廣大猶如虛空，一切境界悉皆明見，獲無礙地大光明藏，善能分別一切法義，行於世行不染世法，能益於世非世所壞，普作一切世間依止，普知一切眾生心行，隨其所應而為說法，於一切時恒得自在；而我云何能知能說彼功德行？「善男子！於此南方，有一國土，名為：三眼；彼有比丘，名曰：善見。汝詣彼問：菩薩云何學菩薩行、修菩薩道？」時，善財童子頂禮其足，遶無數匝，戀慕瞻仰，辭退而行。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, I know only this Universal Adornment Gate of Prajñāpāramitā. As for the great bodhisattvas, their minds vast and expansive, equal to the realm of empty space, entering the dharma-realm, their merit fulfilled and complete, abiding in the dharma that transcends the world, far removed from worldly conduct, their eye of wisdom without any cataract, universally observing the dharma-realm, their mind of wisdom vast and expansive just like empty space, clearly seeing every domain entirely, attaining the great treasury of light of the unobstructed ground, skillfully able to discern the meaning of every dharma, practicing worldly conduct without being stained by worldly dharmas, able to benefit the world without being destroyed by the world, universally becoming a support for every world, universally knowing every being's own mind and conduct, teaching the dharma in accordance with what is fitting for them, and forever attaining sovereignty at every moment — how could I possibly know or describe their own meritorious practice? Good son, to the south of here, there is a land named Three Eyes; there is a monk there named Well-Seen. Go to him and ask: 'How does a bodhisattva learn the bodhisattva practices, and cultivate the bodhisattva path?'" At that time, the boy Good-Wealth bowed his head at her feet, circumambulated her countless times, gazed up at her with longing affection, took his leave, and departed.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

戀慕瞻仰 — gazing up at her with longing affection. This particular phrase for Good-Wealth's parting gaze has not appeared with any teacher before Loving-Conduct — neither the tears of Part 954 nor the joy of Part 963, but something closer to reluctance, as if this parting were harder to make than most.
