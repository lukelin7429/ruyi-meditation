Even to Hear His Name

Samantabhadra tells Good-Wealth to look upon this form-body of his, built by measureless oceans of kalpas and rarely seen or heard even across countless myriads of kalpas. A being who has planted no wholesome root, or only a little, even a śrāvaka or a bodhisattva, still cannot so much as hear his name, let alone see his body; but whoever hears his name never turns back from anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, and seeing him, touching him, welcoming him, escorting him, following him for a moment, or even seeing and hearing him in a dream all have the same effect. Beings ripen at recollecting him for a single day and night, or for seven days, a half-month, a month, half a year, a year, a hundred years, a thousand years, a kalpa, a hundred kalpas, or ineffably many kalpas; or across one life, a hundred lives, or ineffably many lives; or simply by seeing his great light, seeing him shake buddha-lands, or by fear or by joy. Then he sees Samantabhadra, in each ocean of worlds, sending forth clouds of transformation-bodies to teach every being toward bodhi.

Even to Hear His Name

Samantabhadra's rarely-seen body, the beings who cannot hear his name, and the many spans of time across which recollecting him brings beings to maturity.

Chinese Source

善男子！汝且觀我如是色身；我此色身，無邊劫海之所成就，無量千億那由他劫難見難聞。「善男子！若有眾生未種善根，及種少善根聲聞、菩薩，猶尚不得聞我名字，況見我身！善男子！若有眾生得聞我名，於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提不復退轉；若見若觸，若迎若送，若暫隨逐，乃至夢中見聞我者，皆亦如是。或有眾生，一日一夜憶念於我即得成熟；或七日七夜、半月一月、半年一年、百年千年、一劫百劫，乃至不可說不可說佛剎微塵數劫，憶念於我而成熟者；或一生、或百生，乃至不可說不可說佛剎微塵數生，憶念於我而成熟者；或見我放大光明，或見我震動佛剎，或生怖畏，或生歡喜，皆得成熟。善男子！我以如是等佛剎微塵數方便門，令諸眾生於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提得不退轉。「善男子！若有眾生見聞於我清淨剎者，必得生此清淨剎中；若有眾生見聞於我清淨身者，必得生我清淨身中。善男子！汝應觀我此清淨身。」爾時，善財童子觀普賢菩薩身，相好肢節，一一毛孔中，皆有不可說不可說佛剎海；一一剎海，皆有諸佛出興于世，大菩薩眾所共圍遶。又復見彼一切剎海，種種建立、種種形狀、種種莊嚴、種種大山周匝圍遶，種種色雲彌覆虛空，種種佛興演種種法；如是等事，各各不同。又見普賢於一一世界海中，出一切佛剎微塵數佛化身雲，周遍十方一切世界，教化眾生，令向阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! Look now upon this form-body of mine; this form-body of mine has been accomplished by measureless oceans of kalpas, and is rarely seen and rarely heard of even across measureless thousands of koṭis of nayutas of kalpas. Good son! If there are sentient beings who have planted no wholesome root, or who have planted but a little wholesome root, śrāvakas and bodhisattvas among them, they still cannot manage to hear my name, let alone see my body! Good son! If there are sentient beings who hear my name, they never turn back from anuttara-samyak-sambodhi; whether they see me, touch me, welcome me, escort me, follow me for even a moment, or even see and hear me in a dream, it is likewise so for all of them. Some sentient beings, recollecting me for a single day and night, at once attain maturity; some, recollecting me for seven days and seven nights, a half-month, a month, half a year, a year, a hundred years, a thousand years, a kalpa, a hundred kalpas, and even kalpas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, attain maturity through recollecting me; some, across one life, or a hundred lives, and even lives as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, attain maturity through recollecting me; some, by seeing me emit great light, or seeing me shake a buddha-land, or by giving rise to fear, or by giving rise to joy, all attain maturity. Good son! Through gates of skillful means such as these, as numerous as the fine motes of dust of a buddha-land, I cause sentient beings to attain non-retrogression from anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Good son! If there are sentient beings who see or hear of my pure land, they will certainly come to be born within this pure land; if there are sentient beings who see or hear of my pure body, they will certainly come to be born within my pure body. Good son! You should now look upon this pure body of mine." At that time, Good-Wealth the youth looked upon Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's body, its excellent marks and every limb and joint, and within each and every pore there were ineffably ineffable oceans of buddha-lands; and within each and every ocean of lands there were buddhas appearing in the world, surrounded together by the great host of bodhisattvas. He saw, moreover, that all those oceans of lands were established in every kind of way, shaped in every kind of form, adorned in every kind of manner, encircled all about by every kind of great mountain, their space covered over by clouds of every color, and buddhas of every kind appearing to expound every kind of dharma; and such things as these were each and every one distinct. He saw, moreover, that Samantabhadra, within each and every ocean of worlds, sent forth clouds of transformation-bodies of a buddha as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading every world of the ten directions, teaching and transforming sentient beings and turning them toward anuttara-samyak-sambodhi.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一日一夜……乃至不可說不可說佛剎微塵數劫 — the spans across which recollection ripens a being are given as a single ascending list, a day and night, seven days, a half-month, a month, half a year, a year, a hundred years, a thousand years, a kalpa, a hundred kalpas, and on to kalpas beyond counting, and then, in a second list, lifetimes rather than kalpas, one life up to lives beyond counting. The translation keeps the full ladder in the source's order rather than summarizing it, since the point of the passage is precisely that maturity comes at every one of these different speeds.

若見若觸，若迎若送，若暫隨逐 — the five modes of contact with Samantabhadra, seeing, touching, welcoming, escorting, and briefly following, are followed by a sixth that the others build toward, 乃至夢中見聞, even seeing and hearing him in a dream, which the source marks as 皆亦如是, likewise so for all of them: the least of these six, a dream, carries the same weight as the others. The part ends as Good-Wealth begins to look at what he has just been told to look at, finding an ocean of buddha-lands in every pore of Samantabhadra's body.
