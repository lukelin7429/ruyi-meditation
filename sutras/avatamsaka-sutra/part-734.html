Ten Kinds of Playful Ease

Nine short pairs of transformations — sentient being and land, buddha and śrāvaka, nirvāṇa and birth-and-death — each swap their forms without damaging either side; the tenth and longest entry then multiplies a single unmoving body into unspeakably many samādhi-bodies, more than every kalpa could ever exhaust.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 734 — ten kinds of playful ease Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Swapping Forms Without Loss

Each of the first nine kinds of playful ease follows an identical pattern: one thing takes on the appearance of its counterpart — a sentient being becomes a land, a śrāvaka becomes a buddha, birth-and-death becomes nirvāṇa — while the original is explicitly said to suffer no damage, diminishment, or increase. The tenth abandons pairing altogether for something closer to infinite regress: one unmoving body giving rise to bodies that give rise to further bodies, outpacing every kalpa that could ever try to count them.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種遊戲，何等為十？所謂：以眾生身作剎身，而亦不壞眾生身，是菩薩遊戲；以剎身作眾生身，而亦不壞於剎身，是菩薩遊戲；於佛身示現聲聞、獨覺身，而不損減如來身，是菩薩遊戲；於聲聞、獨覺身示現如來身，而不增長聲聞、獨覺身，是菩薩遊戲；於菩薩行身示現成正覺身，而亦不斷菩薩行身，是菩薩遊戲；於成正覺身示現修菩薩行身，而亦不減成菩提身，是菩薩遊戲；於涅槃界示現生死身，而不著生死，是菩薩遊戲；於生死界示現涅槃，亦不究竟入於涅槃，是菩薩遊戲；入於三昧而示現行、住、坐、臥一切業，亦不捨三昧正受，是菩薩遊戲；

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of playful ease. What are these ten? Namely: turning a sentient being's body into a land's body, without ever destroying the sentient being's own body — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; turning a land's body into a sentient being's body, without ever destroying the land's own body — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting the body of a śrāvaka or a solitary awakened one within a buddha's body, without ever diminishing the tathāgata's own body — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting a tathāgata's body within the body of a śrāvaka or a solitary awakened one, without ever enlarging the body of that śrāvaka or solitary awakened one — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting the body that attains right awakening within the body of bodhisattva practice, without ever interrupting the body of bodhisattva practice — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting the body that cultivates bodhisattva practice within the body that has attained right awakening, without ever diminishing the body that has attained awakening — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting a body of birth and death within the realm of nirvāṇa, without ever being attached to birth and death — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; manifesting nirvāṇa within the realm of birth and death, without ever ultimately entering nirvāṇa — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease; entering samādhi and yet manifesting every activity of walking, standing, sitting, and lying down, without ever abandoning the proper absorption of samādhi — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease;

Chinese Source

在一佛所聞法受持，其身不動，而以三昧力，於不可說諸佛會中各各現身，亦不分身，亦不起定，而聞法受持相續不斷，如是念念於一一三昧身各出生不可說不可說三昧身，如是次第一切諸劫猶可窮盡，而菩薩三昧身不可窮盡，是菩薩遊戲。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上大智遊戲。

Ru-Yi Translation

and hearing and upholding the dharma at the place of a single buddha, one's own body unmoving, and yet, through the power of samādhi, manifesting a separate body within each of unspeakably many buddhas' assemblies — without ever dividing that body, and without ever rising from that absorption — hearing and upholding the dharma there without interruption; and in this way, within thought after thought, each and every samādhi-body gives rise to unspeakably, unspeakably many further samādhi-bodies, so that, proceeding in this order, every kalpa could still be exhausted before a bodhisattva's samādhi-bodies ever could be — this is a bodhisattva's playful ease. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great wisdom of playful ease.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

如是次第一切諸劫猶可窮盡，而菩薩三昧身不可窮盡 — proceeding in this order, every kalpa could still be exhausted before a bodhisattva's samādhi-bodies ever could be. Where the first nine kinds of playful ease each protect one specific thing from loss, the tenth measures a bodhisattva's samādhi-bodies against time itself and declares them the larger quantity — kalpas run out; the bodies do not.
