Born into the Family of the Tathāgatas

Since Maitreya has spoken of his birthplace, Good-Wealth asks what a bodhisattva's birthplace is, and Maitreya names ten: the mind of bodhi, the profound mind, the grounds, great vows, great compassion, observation according to principle, the Great Vehicle, the teaching of beings, wisdom and skillful means, and the practice of every dharma, each a birth into a particular family. Then the family itself is drawn: the pāramitā of wisdom as mother, skillful means as father, giving as wet-nurse, moral discipline as foster mother, the good spiritual friend as teacher, every bodhisattva as brother, the mind of bodhi as home, and the bodhisattva of a single remaining birth, consecrated with the water of the dharma, as crown prince. So born, the bodhisattva passes beyond the ground of the ordinary person and is honored by devas, humans, māras, Brahmās, śramaṇas, and brāhmaṇas alike.

Born into the Family of the Tathāgatas

Ten birthplaces, one family, and the standing that birth into it confers.

Chinese Source

善財童子言：「聖者！何者是菩薩生處？」答言：「善男子！菩薩有十種生處。何者為十？善男子！菩提心是菩薩生處，生菩薩家故；深心是菩薩生處，生善知識家故；諸地是菩薩生處，生波羅蜜家故；大願是菩薩生處，生妙行家故；大悲是菩薩生處，生四攝家故；如理觀察是菩薩生處，生般若波羅蜜家故；大乘是菩薩生處，生方便善巧家故；教化眾生是菩薩生處，生佛家故；智慧方便是菩薩生處，生無生法忍家故；修行一切法是菩薩生處，生過、現、未來一切如來家故。「善男子！菩薩摩訶薩，以般若波羅蜜為母，方便善巧為父，檀波羅蜜為乳母，尸波羅蜜為養母，忍波羅蜜為莊嚴具，勤波羅蜜為養育者，禪波羅蜜為澣濯人，善知識為教授師，一切菩提分為伴侶，一切善法為眷屬，一切菩薩為兄弟，菩提心為家，如理修行為家法，諸地為家處，諸忍為家族，大願為家教，滿足諸行為順家法，勸發大乘為紹家業，法水灌頂一生所繫菩薩為王太子，成就菩提為能淨家族。「善男子！菩薩如是超凡夫地，入菩薩位，生如來家，住佛種性，能修諸行，不斷三寶，善能守護菩薩種族，淨菩薩種，生處尊勝，無諸過惡，一切世間天、人、魔、梵、沙門、婆羅門恭敬讚歎。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good-Wealth the youth said, "Noble one, what is a bodhisattva's birthplace?" He answered, "Good son! A bodhisattva has ten kinds of birthplace. What are the ten? Good son! The mind of bodhi is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of the bodhisattvas; the profound mind is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of the good spiritual friends; the grounds are a bodhisattva's birthplace, because they are birth into the family of the pāramitās; great vows are a bodhisattva's birthplace, because they are birth into the family of wondrous practices; great compassion is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of the four means of gathering; observation according to principle is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of the pāramitā of wisdom; the Great Vehicle is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of skillful means; the teaching and transforming of sentient beings is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of the buddhas; wisdom and skillful means are a bodhisattva's birthplace, because they are birth into the family of the acceptance of the non-arising of dharmas; and the practice of every dharma is a bodhisattva's birthplace, because it is birth into the family of every tathāgata of the past, present, and future. Good son! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has the pāramitā of wisdom as mother, skillful means as father, the pāramitā of giving as wet-nurse, the pāramitā of moral discipline as foster mother, the pāramitā of patience as ornament, the pāramitā of diligence as the one who rears him, the pāramitā of meditation as the one who washes him, the good spiritual friends as teachers, every factor of awakening as companions, every wholesome dharma as retinue, every bodhisattva as brothers, the mind of bodhi as home, practice according to principle as the rule of the house, the grounds as the family dwelling, the acceptances as the family clan, great vows as the family teaching, the fulfillment of every practice as compliance with the rule of the house, the urging of others toward the Great Vehicle as carrying on the family enterprise, the bodhisattva of a single remaining birth consecrated with the water of the dharma as crown prince, and the accomplishment of bodhi as the purifier of the family clan. Good son! In this way a bodhisattva passes beyond the ground of the ordinary person, enters the bodhisattva station, is born into the family of the tathāgatas, abides in the buddha-lineage, is able to cultivate every practice, does not let the Three Jewels be cut off, skillfully guards the clan of the bodhisattvas, purifies the bodhisattva seed, and is supreme in his place of birth, free of every fault; and every deva, human, māra, Brahmā, śramaṇa, and brāhmaṇa in the world reveres and praises him.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

生處 — the same word that Maitreya used a moment ago for the country he was born in, 生處摩羅提國, is now made a term of the path: a bodhisattva has ten birthplaces, and each is defined by the 家, the family, into which it gives birth. The ten pairs are kept as pairs, birthplace and family, and the translation repeats the whole formula each time rather than tabulating it, because the repetition of 家 is what leads into the next paragraph.

以般若波羅蜜為母 — the family is then drawn member by member, twenty items in all, from mother and father through nurse, foster mother, teacher, companions, and brothers to the household itself, its rule, its dwelling, its clan, its teaching, its enterprise, and its heir. The five pāramitās after wisdom fill the first five places after the parents, in their standard order, giving, moral discipline, patience, diligence, and meditation, and the heir is 法水灌頂一生所繫菩薩, the bodhisattva of a single remaining birth consecrated with the water of the dharma, the rank that has already appeared four times in this fascicle, as 一生所繫 or 一生菩薩, and is here made the crown prince of the house.
