Eleven Questions

Nine reasons in all explain why Maitreya dwells in this tower, and then Good-Wealth's referral ends the only way this journey ever sends him onward — with a question. This time, eleven of them, one after another.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1258 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Eleven Questions

The reasons for Maitreya's own presence keep multiplying until they become reasons for teaching Good-Wealth directly, and the referral closes not with a single question but with eleven, spoken in a single breath.

Chinese Source

又欲令彼同受生、同修行眾生，於大乘中得堅固故；又欲令彼一切眾生，隨住地、隨善根皆成就故；又欲為汝顯示菩薩解脫門故，顯示菩薩遍一切處受生自在故，顯示菩薩以種種身普現一切眾生之前常教化故，顯示菩薩以大悲力普攝一切世間資財而不厭故，顯示菩薩具修諸行知一切行離諸相故，顯示菩薩處處受生了一切生皆無相故。汝詣彼問：菩薩云何行菩薩行？云何修菩薩道？云何學菩薩戒？云何淨菩薩心？云何發菩薩願？云何集菩薩助道具？云何入菩薩所住地？云何滿菩薩波羅蜜？云何獲菩薩無生忍？云何具菩薩功德法？云何事菩薩善知識？

Ru-Yi Translation

And wishing, moreover, that those beings born and cultivating alongside him become firm within the Great Vehicle; and wishing, moreover, that every one of those beings, according to their own ground and their own wholesome roots, all reach accomplishment; and wishing, moreover, to reveal to you a bodhisattva's own liberation-gate, to reveal a bodhisattva's own sovereign mastery of taking birth in every place, to reveal a bodhisattva manifesting every kind of body before every being, forever teaching them, to reveal a bodhisattva who, through the power of great compassion, universally gathers in every kind of worldly wealth without ever growing weary, to reveal a bodhisattva who, fully cultivating every practice, knows that every practice is free of every mark, and to reveal a bodhisattva who, taking birth in every place, understands that every birth is itself without any mark. Go to him, and ask: how does a bodhisattva practice the bodhisattva's own practice? How does one cultivate the bodhisattva's own path? How does one study the bodhisattva's own precepts? How does one purify the bodhisattva's own mind? How does one give rise to the bodhisattva's own vows? How does one gather the very equipment that assists the bodhisattva's own path? How does one enter the bodhisattva's own abiding grounds? How does one fulfill the bodhisattva's own pāramitās? How does one attain the bodhisattva's own forbearance of non-arising? How does one become endowed with the bodhisattva's own qualities of merit? And how does one serve the bodhisattva's own good spiritual friends?

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

菩薩云何行菩薩行？云何修菩薩道？⋯云何事菩薩善知識？ — how does a bodhisattva practice the bodhisattva's own practice? How does one cultivate the bodhisattva's own path?⋯And how does one serve the bodhisattva's own good spiritual friends? Eleven questions in all, counted directly from the source text, moving from practice and path in general down to the precise, practical question of how to serve a teacher at all.
