Up to Rucika

Ninety-one more buddha names close out the list Māyā began in the last Part, before the sūtra folds every unnamed buddha of the Fortunate Eon into a single phrase — up to the tathāgata Rucika — and she claims motherhood of every one of them.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1244 — fascicle 76 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Up to Rucika

The list picks up exactly where it left off, and closes not on a name but on a phrase that swallows every name still left to give.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

如是乃至樓至如來，在賢劫中⋯悉為其母 — and so on, all the way up to the tathāgata Rucika⋯of every one of them, I am the mother. Between this Part and the last, Māyā names one hundred and ninety-three buddhas outright — five of those names recurring, so that a hundred and eighty-eight distinct titles cover the full count — before the text folds every remaining buddha of the thousand this Fortunate Eon will hold into four words: up to Rucika, its last.
