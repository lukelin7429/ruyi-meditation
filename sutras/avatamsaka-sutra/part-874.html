The Named Samādhis Continue Further

This third group of twenty-five samādhi names moves from the unobstructed limit of the three times through the inexhaustible treasury of merit, and includes one naming a single, secret treasury that every buddha shares in common, echoing this list's earlier claim that every tathāgata shares one body.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 874 — the named samādhis continue further Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Single Secret Shared by Every Buddha

Two parts ago, this list named a samādhi for knowing that every tathāgata shares a single body; here it names a companion claim — a single, secret treasury that belongs to every buddha in common, deepening the same underlying unity from body to something even more hidden.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一切佛一密藏三昧 — the Samādhi of the Single, Secret Treasury Shared by Every Buddha. Two parts ago this same list named a samādhi for knowing that every tathāgata shares one body; here it names the companion claim, that behind every buddha's own outward teaching there lies one shared, hidden treasury — unity carried from the body inward to whatever that body conceals.
