A Single Drop of Lion's Milk

The paired similes turn to the lion and to creatures of power: the lion king's roar that strengthens cubs and scatters beasts, a musical string of lion's sinew that snaps every other string, a single drop of lion's milk that spoils an ocean of milk, the kalaviṅka still inside its shell, the garuḍa king's newborn son, a strong man's spear through close-fitted armor, and a mahānāga warrior whom no one in Jambudvīpa can subdue.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1301 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Single Drop of Lion's Milk

Seven more paired similes continue the pattern — three drawn from the lion, two from birds of extraordinary power, and two from warriors whom nothing can withstand.

Chinese Source

「善男子！如師子王哮吼之時，師子兒聞皆增勇健，餘獸聞之即皆竄伏。佛師子王菩提心吼應知亦爾，諸菩薩聞增長功德，有所得者聞皆退散。「善男子！譬如有人，以師子筋而為樂絃；其音既奏，餘絃悉絕。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，以如來師子波羅蜜身菩提心筋為法樂絃；其音既奏，一切五欲及以二乘諸功德絃悉皆斷滅。「善男子！譬如有人，以牛羊等種種諸乳，假使積集盈於大海，以師子乳一滴投中，悉令變壞，直過無礙。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，以如來師子菩提心乳，著無量劫業煩惱乳大海之中，悉令壞滅，直過無礙，終不住於二乘解脫。「善男子！譬如迦陵頻伽鳥，在卵㲉中有大勢力，一切諸鳥所不能及。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，於生死㲉發菩提心，所有大悲功德勢力，聲聞、緣覺無能及者。「善男子！如金翅鳥王子，初始生時，目則明利，飛則勁捷，一切諸鳥雖久成長無能及者。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，發菩提心，為佛王子，智慧清淨，大悲勇猛，一切二乘雖百千劫久修道行所不能及。「善男子！如有壯夫，手執利矛，刺堅密甲，直過無礙。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，執菩提心銛利快矛，刺諸邪見隨眠密甲，悉能穿徹無有障礙。「善男子！譬如摩訶那伽大力勇士，若奮威怒，於其額上必生瘡疱；瘡若未合，閻浮提中一切人民無能制伏。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，若起大悲，必定發於菩提之心；心未捨來，一切世間魔及魔民不能為害。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as, when the lion king roars, the lion's cubs who hear it all grow in courage and strength, while the other beasts who hear it at once flee and hide, know that the roar of the mind of bodhi of the Buddha, the lion king, is the same: the bodhisattvas who hear it grow in merit, while those who hold to attainment, hearing it, all fall back and scatter. Good son! Just as, when a person makes a musical string from a lion's sinew, once its note is played every other string snaps, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: making the sinew that is the mind of bodhi of the pāramitā-body of the Tathāgata, the lion, into a string for the music of dharma, once its note is played, every string of the five desires and of the merits of the two vehicles is entirely severed and destroyed. Good son! Just as, when a person gathers every kind of milk from cows, goats, and the like, and even were it heaped up until it filled the great ocean, a single drop of lion's milk cast into it causes all of it to spoil, passing straight through without hindrance, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: placing the lion's milk that is the mind of bodhi of the Tathāgata, the lion, into the great ocean of the milk of karma and afflictions of measureless kalpas, they cause all of it to be destroyed, passing straight through without hindrance, and never come to rest in the liberation of the two vehicles. Good son! Just as the kalaviṅka bird, while still within its eggshell, has a great power that no other bird can equal, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: arousing the mind of bodhi within the eggshell of birth-and-death, the power of merit of their great compassion is such that no śrāvaka or pratyekabuddha is able to equal it. Good son! Just as the son of the garuḍa king, at the very moment of his birth, has eyes already bright and keen and a flight already strong and swift, which no other bird, however long it has been grown, is able to equal, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: arousing the mind of bodhi, they become a prince of the Buddha, pure in wisdom and courageous in great compassion, whom no follower of the two vehicles, even after long cultivating the practices of the path for a hundred thousand kalpas, is able to equal. Good son! Just as a strong man, holding a sharp spear in his hand, pierces hard and close-fitted armor, passing straight through without hindrance, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: holding the keen and swift spear that is the mind of bodhi, they pierce the close-fitted armor of every wrong view and latent tendency, able to penetrate all of it without any obstruction. Good son! Just as, when a mahānāga warrior of great strength rouses his fury, a sore is sure to rise upon his forehead, and so long as that sore has not closed, no one among all the people of Jambudvīpa is able to subdue him, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: once great compassion has arisen in them, they are certain to give rise to the mind of bodhi, and so long as that mind has not been abandoned, Māra and Māra's own subjects in any world are unable to harm them.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

師子 — the lion supplies three successive similes here, each turning on a single property: the roar that strengthens its own kind and scatters everything else, the sinew whose note silences every other string, and the milk whose single drop spoils an ocean. In the second and third of these the passage names the source of the sinew and the milk as 如來師子, the Tathāgata as lion, so that the bodhisattva's mind of bodhi is drawn from the Buddha's own nature rather than from the bodhisattva's.

卵㲉 — the rare character 㲉, an eggshell, appears twice in this passage, once for the shell the kalaviṅka has not yet left and once for 生死㲉, the shell of birth-and-death within which the bodhisattva first arouses the mind of bodhi. 摩訶那伽 is a straightforward transliteration of mahānāga, 摩訶 mahā joined to 那伽 nāga, a great nāga, used here as a title for a warrior of surpassing strength. The name is restored rather than left in pinyin because both halves are unambiguous: 摩訶 mahā is one of the most frequent transliterated elements in the whole sūtra, as in 菩薩摩訶薩, while 那伽 is rare in this transliterated form only because the sūtra ordinarily writes 龍 for nāga.
