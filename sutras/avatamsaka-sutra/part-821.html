A Heart Set Like a Great Mountain

Freedom from attachment, mastery of every liberation, wandering as freely as through a garden grove, and a palace built from pure dharma all lead toward the verse's own closing image of stability: a resolve set as firmly as a great mountain, and virtue planted as deep as the ocean.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 821 — a heart set like a great mountain Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Bonds Left Behind, a Garden Entered Freely

Liberation here is not described as an escape from the world but as free movement within it: every bond and entanglement forever left behind, and what remains is wandering at will as though through a garden grove — the same world that once confined now entered and moved through freely, at leisure.

Chinese Source

遠離於諸著，演說心平等，
出生於智慧，變化得菩提。

住持一切劫，智者大欣慰，
深入及依止，無畏無疑惑。

了達不思議，巧密善分別，
善入諸三昧，普見智境界。

究竟諸解脫，遊戲諸通明，
纏縛悉永離，園林恣遊處。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Forever free of every attachment, expounding the equality of mind; bringing forth wisdom, and through transformation attaining awakening.

Sustaining and upholding every kalpa, the wise one is greatly comforted and gladdened; penetrating deeply into it and relying upon it, with no fear and no doubt at all.

Fully understanding the inconceivable, skillfully and subtly discerning it well; skillfully entering every samādhi, universally seeing the domain of wisdom.

Ultimately mastering every liberation, playing freely within every superknowledge and clarity; forever free of every bond and entanglement, wandering at will as though through a garden grove.

Chinese Source

白法為宮殿，諸行可欣樂，
現無量莊嚴，於世心無動。

深心善觀察，妙辯能開演，
清淨菩提印，智光照一切。

所住無等比，其心不下劣，
立志如大山，種德若深海。

Ru-Yi Translation

Pure, wholesome dharma is his own palace, every practice something to delight in; manifesting measureless adornment, his mind unmoved within the world.

With a deep mind he observes well, and his wondrous eloquence is able to open up and expound; the pure seal of awakening, the light of wisdom illuminating everything.

Where he dwells has nothing equal to compare it to, his mind never inferior or base; his resolve is set as firmly as a great mountain, and his planted virtue is as deep as the ocean.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

纏縛悉永離，園林恣遊處 — forever free of every bond and entanglement, wandering at will as though through a garden grove. Liberation is imagined here not as leaving the world behind but as a change in how the same world is inhabited: what confined as a bond is entered, once freed, the way one wanders a garden — at leisure, and by choice.
