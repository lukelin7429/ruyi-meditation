Every Buddha-Land Entirely Pure

Fascicle thirty-three opens with the tenth dedication's newest vow: that every buddha-land, vast as the dharma-realm itself, becomes entirely pure, each one able to manifest every other buddha-land within it. Fourteen kinds of jeweled adornment follow — thrones, canopies, bells that forever sound every buddha's own voice, trees bearing immeasurable wondrous jewels as their flowers and fruit — all of it arising from none other than the Tathāgata's own wholesome roots.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 385 — every buddha-land, adorned with countless jeweled treasuries Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Fourteen Jeweled Adornments

The vow opens with every buddha-land made vast as the dharma-realm itself, each land able to manifest every other land within it — the same interpenetration this sūtra returns to throughout — before descending into fourteen concrete kinds of adornment: thrones spread with jeweled garments, bells that forever sound every buddha's own voice throughout the dharma-realm, and trees bearing immeasurable wondrous jewels in place of flowers and fruit.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩復以法施所修善根如是迴向：『願一切佛剎皆悉清淨，以不可說不可說莊嚴具而莊嚴之。一一佛剎，其量廣大，同於法界，純善無礙，清淨光明，諸佛於中現成正覺。一佛剎中清淨境界，悉能顯現一切佛剎；如一佛剎，一切佛剎亦復如是。其一一剎，悉以等法界無量無邊清淨妙寶莊嚴之具而為嚴飾。所謂：阿僧祇清淨寶座，敷眾寶衣；阿僧祇寶帳，寶網垂布；阿僧祇寶蓋，一切妙寶互相映徹；阿僧祇寶雲，普雨眾寶；阿僧祇寶華，周遍清淨；阿僧祇眾寶所成欄、楯、軒、檻，清淨莊嚴；阿僧祇寶鈴，常演諸佛微妙音聲，周流法界；阿僧祇寶蓮華，種種寶色開敷榮耀；阿僧祇寶樹，周匝行列，無量妙寶以為華果；阿僧祇寶宮殿，無量菩薩止住其中，阿僧祇寶樓閣，廣博崇麗，延袤遠近；阿僧祇寶却敵，大寶所成，莊嚴妙好；阿僧祇寶門闥，妙寶瓔珞周匝垂布；阿僧祇寶窓牖，不思議寶清淨莊嚴；阿僧祇寶多羅，形如半月，眾寶集成。如是一切，悉以眾寶而為嚴飾，離垢清淨，不可思議，無非如來善根所起，具足無數寶藏莊嚴。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! This bodhisattva mahāsattva further dedicates the wholesome roots cultivated through the giving of Dharma in this way: 'May every buddha-land become entirely pure, adorned with inexpressible, inexpressible adornments. Each and every buddha-land, its measure vast, the same as the dharma-realm, wholly wholesome and unobstructed, pure and radiant with light, where every buddha manifests the accomplishment of true awakening. The pure domain within a single buddha-land is entirely able to manifest every buddha-land; and just as within that single buddha-land, so too is it within every buddha-land. Each and every land is adorned entirely with pure, wondrous jeweled adornments, immeasurable and boundless, equal to the dharma-realm. Namely: asaṃkhyeya pure, jeweled thrones, spread with every kind of jeweled garment; asaṃkhyeya jeweled canopies, jeweled nets hanging down; asaṃkhyeya jeweled parasols, every wondrous jewel reflecting one another; asaṃkhyeya jeweled clouds, universally raining down every jewel; asaṃkhyeya jeweled flowers, pure throughout; asaṃkhyeya railings, balustrades, eaves, and lattices formed entirely of every jewel, purely adorned; asaṃkhyeya jeweled bells, forever sounding every buddha's own subtle and wondrous voice, flowing throughout the dharma-realm; asaṃkhyeya jeweled lotus flowers, blossoming in every kind of jeweled color, radiant and flourishing; asaṃkhyeya jeweled trees, arranged in rows all around, with immeasurable wondrous jewels as their flowers and fruit; asaṃkhyeya jeweled palaces, with immeasurable bodhisattvas dwelling within them; asaṃkhyeya jeweled towers, vast and lofty, stretching far and near; asaṃkhyeya jeweled parapets, formed of great jewels, wondrously adorned; asaṃkhyeya jeweled gateways, wondrous jeweled necklaces hanging down all around; asaṃkhyeya jeweled windows, adorned purely with inconceivable jewels; and asaṃkhyeya jeweled crescent ornaments, shaped like a half-moon, formed from a gathering of every jewel. All of this is entirely adorned with every jewel, free of defilement and pure, inconceivable, arising from none other than the Tathāgata's own wholesome roots, fully endowed with the adornment of countless jeweled treasuries.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

"Bells that forever sound every buddha's own subtle and wondrous voice, flowing throughout the dharma-realm" turns architectural adornment into something closer to a teaching instrument — the jewels are not decorative in the way a palace's decorations might be, but continuously active, still speaking the Dharma long after any buddha has finished a sermon.
