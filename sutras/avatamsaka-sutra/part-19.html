The Fragrant Water Oceans, Their Rivers, and the Land Between

Samantabhadra continues down into the ground of the Flower-Treasury World-System just described: ineffable numbers of fragrant water oceans fill it, each ringed by rivers that circle it to the right, and each river bordered by jeweled land where trees, flowers, and maṇi jewels manifest every buddha's own conduct and every living being's karmic history.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 19 — continues fascicle 8 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Fragrant Water Oceans

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩復告大眾言：「諸佛子！此世界海大地中，有不可說佛剎微塵數香水海，一切妙寶莊嚴其底，妙香摩尼莊嚴其岸，毘盧遮那摩尼寶王以為其網；香水映徹，具眾寶色，充滿其中；種種寶華，旋布其上；栴檀細末，澄垽其下；演佛言音，放寶光明；無邊菩薩，持種種蓋，現神通力。一切世界所有莊嚴，悉於中現。十寶階陛，行列分布；十寶欄楯，周匝圍遶；四天下微塵數一切寶莊嚴芬陀利華，敷榮水中；不可說百千億那由他數十寶尸羅幢，恒河沙數一切寶衣鈴網幢，恒河沙數無邊色相寶華樓閣，百千億那由他數十寶蓮華城，四天下微塵數眾寶樹林——寶焰摩尼以為其網，恒河沙數栴檀香、諸佛言音光焰摩尼、不可說百千億那由他數眾寶垣牆，悉共圍遶，周遍嚴飾。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva further addressed the great assembly, saying: "Buddha's children! Within this world-ocean's great ground, there are fragrant water oceans as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ineffable buddha-lands. Every wondrous jewel adorns their floor; wondrous fragrant maṇi adorns their shores; the sovereign maṇi jewel of Vairocana forms their net. The fragrant water is radiant and clear, endowed with the color of every jewel, filling them entirely; every kind of jeweled flower whirls and spreads upon its surface; fine sandalwood powder settles and clears at its bottom; it proclaims the Buddha's own speech and voice, and emits jeweled light; boundless bodhisattvas hold every kind of canopy, manifesting the power of spiritual transmutation. The adornment possessed by every world is entirely manifest within it. Ten jeweled stairways are arrayed in ranks; ten jeweled balustrades encircle it all around; puṇḍarīka flowers adorned with every jewel, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four continents, bloom and flourish within the water; ineffable hundreds of thousands of millions of nayutas of ten-jeweled precept-banners; banners of every jeweled garment, bell, and net, as numerous as the sands of the Ganges; pavilions of jeweled flowers of boundless form and color, as numerous as the sands of the Ganges; hundreds of thousands of millions of nayutas of ten-jeweled lotus-flower cities; forests of every jewel's trees, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four continents — jeweled flame maṇi forming their net; sandalwood fragrances as numerous as the sands of the Ganges, and maṇi of light and flame proclaiming the speech of every buddha; ineffable hundreds of thousands of millions of nayutas of walls of every jewel, all together encircling it, adorning it completely all around."

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩欲重宣其義，承佛神力，觀察十方而說頌言：

「此世界中大地上，有香水海摩尼嚴，
清淨妙寶布其底，安住金剛不可壞。
香藏摩尼積成岸，日焰珠輪布若雲，
蓮華妙寶為瓔珞，處處莊嚴淨無垢。

香水澄渟具眾色，寶華旋布放光明，
普震音聲聞遠近，以佛威神演妙法。
階陛莊嚴具眾寶，復以摩尼為間飾，
周迴欄楯悉寶成，蓮華珠網如雲布。

摩尼寶樹列成行，華蘂敷榮光赫奕，
種種樂音恒競奏，佛神通力令如是。
種種妙寶芬陀利，敷布莊嚴香水海，
香焰光明無暫停，廣大圓滿皆充遍。

明珠寶幢恒熾盛，妙衣垂布為嚴飾，
摩尼鈴網演法音，令其聞者趣佛智。
妙寶蓮華作城廓，眾彩摩尼所嚴瑩，
真珠雲影布四隅，如是莊嚴香水海。

垣牆繚繞皆周匝，樓閣相望布其上，
無量光明恒熾然，種種莊嚴清淨海。
毘盧遮那於往昔，種種剎海皆嚴淨，
如是廣大無有邊，悉是如來自在力。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, wishing to proclaim this meaning once more, sustained by the Buddha's spiritual power, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Within this world, upon the great ground, there are fragrant water oceans adorned with maṇi,
pure and wondrous jewels spread across their floor, abiding securely upon vajra, unable to be destroyed.
Fragrant treasury maṇi piled up forms their shores, the sun's own flaming pearl-wheel spread like clouds,
lotus flowers and wondrous jewels forming their necklaces, adorned in every place, pure and free of defilement.

Fragrant water settles clear, endowed with every color, jeweled flowers whirling and spreading, emitting light,
universally shaking forth a voice heard far and near, proclaiming the wondrous Dharma through the Buddha's own majestic spirit.
Their stairways, adorned and complete with every jewel, further ornamented and interspersed with maṇi,
their encircling balustrades entirely formed of jewels, lotus flowers and pearl nets spread like clouds.

Maṇi jeweled trees arrayed in rows, their flowers and pistils blooming in splendor, their light brilliant,
every kind of music forever competing to play, the Buddha's power of spiritual transmutation causing it to be thus.
Every kind of wondrous jewel's puṇḍarīka flower, spread and adorning the fragrant water ocean,
their fragrant flame and light never ceasing for a moment, vast and full, filling everything completely.

Bright pearls and jeweled banners forever blazing, wondrous garments hanging draped as their ornament,
maṇi bells and nets proclaiming the voice of the Dharma, causing whoever hears it to hasten toward the Buddha's wisdom.
Wondrous jewels and lotus flowers form their city walls, adorned and polished bright with every colored maṇi,
true pearls' cloud-shadows spread across their four corners — thus is the fragrant water ocean adorned.

Walls encircling all around them completely, pavilions facing one another, arrayed above them,
immeasurable light forever blazing, every kind of adornment upon a pure ocean.
Vairocana, in ages past, adorned and purified every kind of land-ocean,
and such vastness, without any bound, is entirely the Tathāgata's own sovereign power."

The Rivers That Circle Them

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩復告大眾言：「諸佛子！一一香水海，各有四天下微塵數香水河，右旋圍遶，一切皆以金剛為岸，淨光摩尼以為嚴飾，常現諸佛寶色光雲，及諸眾生所有言音；其河所有漩澓之處，一切諸佛所修因行種種形相皆從中出；摩尼為網，眾寶鈴鐸，諸世界海所有莊嚴悉於中現；摩尼寶雲以覆其上，其雲普現華藏世界毘盧遮那十方化佛，及一切佛神通之事；復出妙音，稱揚三世佛菩薩名；其香水中，常出一切寶焰光雲，相續不絕。若廣說者，一一河各有世界海微塵數莊嚴。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva further addressed the great assembly, saying: "Buddha's children! Each and every fragrant water ocean has fragrant water rivers, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four continents, circling it to the right. All of them have vajra for their shores, adorned with pure light maṇi, constantly manifesting clouds of light of every buddha's jeweled color, and every voice possessed by every living being. Wherever these rivers eddy and whirl, every kind of form that every buddha's cultivated causal conduct has taken emerges entirely from within them. Maṇi forms their net, and every jeweled bell and chime; the adornment possessed by every world-ocean is entirely manifest within it. A cloud of maṇi jewels covers over them, and that cloud universally manifests the transformation-buddhas of Vairocana of the Flower-Treasury World throughout the ten directions, and the deeds of every buddha's spiritual transmutation; it further sends forth a wondrous voice, extolling the name of every buddha and bodhisattva of the three periods of time. Within that fragrant water, clouds of light of every jeweled flame constantly emerge, continuing without interruption. Were it to be stated fully, each and every river would have adornments as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a world-ocean."

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩欲重宣其義，承佛神力，觀察十方而說頌言：

「清淨香流滿大河，金剛妙寶為其岸，
寶末為輪布其地，種種嚴飾皆珍好。
寶階行列妙莊嚴，欄楯周迴悉殊麗，
真珠為藏眾華飾，種種纓鬘共垂下。

香水寶光清淨色，恒吐摩尼競疾流，
眾華隨浪皆搖動，悉奏樂音宣妙法。
細末栴檀作泥垽，一切妙寶同洄澓，
香藏氛氳布在中，發焰流芬普周遍。

河中出生諸妙寶，悉放光明色熾然，
其光布影成臺座，華蓋珠瓔皆具足。
摩尼王中現佛身，光明普照十方剎，
以此為輪嚴飾地，香水映徹常盈滿。

摩尼為網金為鐸，遍覆香河演佛音，
克宣一切菩提道，及以普賢之妙行。
寶岸摩尼極清淨，恒出如來本願音，
一切諸佛曩所行，其音普演皆令見。

其河所有漩流處，菩薩如雲常踊出，
悉往廣大剎土中，乃至法界咸充滿。
清淨珠王布若雲，一切香河悉彌覆，
其珠等佛眉間相，炳然顯現諸佛影。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, wishing to proclaim this meaning once more, sustained by the Buddha's spiritual power, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Pure fragrance flows and fills the great rivers, vajra and wondrous jewels forming their shores,
jeweled dust forming a wheel spread across their bed, every kind of ornament excellent and fine.
Jeweled stairways arrayed in rows, wondrously adorned, their balustrades circling all around, entirely beautiful,
true pearls forming their treasury, every kind of flower ornamenting them, every kind of tasseled garland hanging draped down.

Fragrant water, jeweled light, pure in color, forever sending forth maṇi, competing to flow swiftly,
every flower moving and swaying along with the waves, all of them playing music and proclaiming the wondrous Dharma.
Fine sandalwood powder settling as sediment, every wondrous jewel likewise eddying and whirling together,
the fragrant treasury's mist spread throughout, sending forth flame and streaming fragrance, pervading everywhere.

Within the rivers, every kind of wondrous jewel is born, all emitting light, their color blazing,
their light spreading shadows forming daises and thrones, flower-canopies and pearl necklaces fully complete.
Within the sovereign maṇi, the Buddha's body is manifest, its light universally illumining every land of the ten directions,
forming, through this, a wheel adorning the ground, the fragrant water radiant and clear, forever full and complete.

Maṇi forms their net, gold forms their chimes, covering the fragrant rivers everywhere, proclaiming the Buddha's voice,
fully expounding every path to bodhi, together with Samantabhadra's own wondrous conduct.
The jeweled shores' maṇi, utterly pure, forever sending forth the voice of the Tathāgata's own original vow,
the conduct of every buddha in ages past — that voice universally proclaims it, causing all to see it.

Wherever these rivers eddy and flow, bodhisattvas, like clouds, forever leap forth,
going entirely to vast lands, reaching even the whole Dharma-realm, filling it completely.
Pure sovereign pearls spread like clouds, covering every fragrant river entirely,
those pearls equal to the mark between the Buddha's own brows, brilliantly revealing the reflected image of every buddha."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Notice what the rivers are said to be made of, functionally rather than materially: wherever they eddy, "every kind of form that every buddha's cultivated causal conduct has taken emerges entirely from within them." The geography is doing the work usually done by narrative — a river's whirlpool is not simply water in motion but the visible trace of accumulated practice across countless buddhas, exactly as a real river's course is the visible trace of accumulated erosion. Nothing in this cosmology is inert scenery; every physical feature encodes a causal history.

The Land Between the Rivers

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩復告大眾言：「諸佛子！此諸香水河兩間之地，悉以妙寶種種莊嚴，一一各有四天下微塵數；眾寶莊嚴芬陀利華周匝遍滿，各有四天下微塵數；眾寶樹林次第行列，一一樹中恒出一切諸莊嚴雲，摩尼寶王照耀其間，種種華香處處盈滿；其樹復出微妙音聲，說諸如來一切劫中所修大願；復散種種摩尼寶王，充遍其地，所謂：蓮華輪摩尼寶王、香焰光雲摩尼寶王、種種嚴飾摩尼寶王、現不可思議莊嚴色摩尼寶王、日光明衣藏摩尼寶王、周遍十方普垂布光網雲摩尼寶王、現一切諸佛神變摩尼寶王、現一切眾生業報海摩尼寶王……如是等，有世界海微塵數。其香水河，兩間之地，一一悉具如是莊嚴。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva further addressed the great assembly, saying: "Buddha's children! The land lying between these fragrant water rivers is entirely adorned with every kind of wondrous jewel, each and every kind as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four continents; puṇḍarīka flowers adorned with every jewel fill it completely all around, each as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four continents; forests of every jewel's trees are arrayed in ranks one after another, and within each and every tree a cloud of every adornment constantly emerges, sovereign maṇi jewels shining brightly among them, every kind of flower and fragrance filling every place; those trees further send forth a subtle, wondrous voice, telling of every great vow that every Tathāgata has cultivated across every kalpa; further scattering every kind of sovereign maṇi jewel, filling that land completely — that is to say: the sovereign maṇi jewel of the lotus-flower wheel, the sovereign maṇi jewel of the fragrant flame's cloud of light, the sovereign maṇi jewel of every kind of ornament, the sovereign maṇi jewel manifesting inconceivable colors of adornment, the sovereign maṇi jewel of the sun's bright light's treasury of garments, the sovereign maṇi jewel pervading the ten directions and universally spreading a cloud-net of light, the sovereign maṇi jewel manifesting the spiritual transformation of every buddha, the sovereign maṇi jewel manifesting the ocean of karmic recompense of every living being... Such as these, there are as many as the fine motes of dust in a world-ocean. The land lying between these fragrant water rivers is each and every one entirely endowed with adornment such as this."

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩欲重宣其義，承佛神力，觀察十方而說頌言：

「其地平坦極清淨，真金摩尼共嚴飾，
諸樹行列蔭其中，聳幹垂條華若雲。
枝條妙寶所莊嚴，華焰成輪光四照，
摩尼為果如雲布，普使十方常現覩。

摩尼布地皆充滿，眾華寶末共莊嚴，
復以摩尼作宮殿，悉現眾生諸影像。
諸佛影像摩尼王，普散其地靡不周，
如是赫奕遍十方，一一塵中咸見佛。

妙寶莊嚴善分布，真珠燈網相間錯，
處處悉有摩尼輪，一一皆現佛神通。
眾寶莊嚴放大光，光中普現諸化佛，
一一周行靡不遍，悉以十力廣開演。

摩尼妙寶芬陀利，一切水中咸遍滿，
其華種種各不同，悉現光明無盡歇。
三世所有諸莊嚴，摩尼果中皆顯現，
體性無生不可取，此是如來自在力。

此地一切莊嚴中，悉現如來廣大身，
彼亦不來亦不去，佛昔願力皆令見。
此地一一微塵中，一切佛子修行道，
各見所記當來剎，隨其意樂悉清淨。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, wishing to proclaim this meaning once more, sustained by the Buddha's spiritual power, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Its ground is level and utterly pure, true gold and maṇi together ornamenting it,
every tree arrayed in rows, shading it, their trunks rising and branches drooping, their flowers like clouds.
Their branches adorned with wondrous jewels, flower and flame forming a wheel, their light shining in every direction,
maṇi forming their fruit, spread like clouds, universally causing it to be constantly beheld throughout the ten directions.

Maṇi spread across the ground, filling it entirely, every flower and jeweled dust ornamenting it together,
and further, maṇi forms palaces, entirely manifesting the reflected images of every living being.
The sovereign maṇi of every buddha's reflected image, universally scattered across the ground without exception,
shining brilliantly thus throughout the ten directions, the buddha beheld within each and every mote of dust.

Wondrous jewels, well adorned and distributed, true pearls and lamp-nets interspersed with one another,
maṇi wheels present in every single place, each and every one manifesting the Buddha's own spiritual transmutation.
Every jewel's adornment emitting great light, and within that light every transformation-buddha universally manifest,
each and every one journeying everywhere without exception, universally expounding through the ten powers.

Maṇi's wondrous puṇḍarīka, filling every body of water entirely,
its flowers each distinct and different, all manifesting light, never exhausted or ceasing.
Every adornment that exists across the three periods of time is manifest entirely within the maṇi's own fruit,
its very nature unborn and ungraspable — such is the Tathāgata's own sovereign power.

Within every adornment of this ground, the Tathāgata's own vast body is manifest entirely,
and yet it neither comes nor goes — through the Buddha's power of vow in ages past, all is made to be seen.
Within each and every mote of dust of this ground, every buddha's child cultivates the path,
each beholding the land of the future they have been foretold, entirely pure according to their own delight."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The closing stanza turns from cosmology to prophecy in a single line: within each mote of dust of this jeweled land, "every buddha's child cultivates the path, each beholding the land of the future they have been foretold." What began as a description of geography — oceans, rivers, the land between them — ends by locating the reader's own future awakening within that same geography. Chengguan's commentary frequently makes this move explicit: the Flower-Treasury World-System is not merely Vairocana's world to be admired from outside, but the very ground practitioners are already standing on, prophecy and place folded into one.

Part 19 Complete

This closes Part 19, continuing fascicle eight and the sūtra's fifth chapter, "The Flower-Treasury World-System" (華藏世界品第五): the ineffable fragrant water oceans that fill the ground already described, the rivers that circle each of them, and the jeweled land lying between those rivers, each with Samantabhadra's teaching in prose followed by a verse. Part 20 turns to a summary statement on this whole world-ocean's inconceivable adornment, and begins the sūtra's account of world-seeds (世界種) — the still-larger structures each fragrant water ocean's central lotus supports.
