Ascending to the Palace of the Yāma Heaven

The sūtra rises to a new heaven, exactly as it rose to the Trāyastriṃśa heaven many fascicles ago — the Buddha, without ever leaving the bodhi tree or the summit of Mount Sumeru, ascends to a throne within the Yāma heaven, adorned a million times over, welcomed by its king with an invitation echoing across all ten directions at once.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 117 — chapter 19 begins Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Present Everywhere Without Leaving Anywhere

Chinese Source

爾時，如來威神力故，十方一切世界，一一四天下南閻浮提及須彌頂上，皆見如來處於眾會。彼諸菩薩悉以佛神力故而演說法，莫不自謂恒對於佛。爾時，世尊不離一切菩提樹下及須彌山頂，而向於彼夜摩天宮寶莊嚴殿。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, through the Tathāgata's own awesome spiritual power, throughout every world of the ten directions, in each and every four continents' own southern continent of Jambudvīpa and atop the very summit of Mount Sumeru, everyone saw the Tathāgata seated within the great assembly. Every one of those bodhisattvas, through the Buddha's own spiritual power, expounded the Dharma, and not one of them failed to consider themselves forever in the Buddha's own presence. At that time, the World-Honored One, without ever leaving the foot of any bodhi tree or the summit of Mount Sumeru, proceeded toward the jewel-adorned palace of the Yāma heaven.

A Throne Adorned a Million Times Over

Chinese Source

時，夜摩天王遙見佛來，即以神力，於其殿內化作寶蓮華藏師子之座，百萬層級以為莊嚴，百萬金網以為交絡，百萬華帳、百萬鬘帳、百萬香帳、百萬寶帳彌覆其上，華蓋、鬘蓋、香蓋、寶蓋各亦百萬周迴布列，百萬光明而為照耀。百萬夜摩天王恭敬頂禮；百萬梵王踊躍歡喜；百萬菩薩稱揚讚歎；百萬天樂各奏百萬種法音，相續不斷；百萬種華雲，百萬種鬘雲，百萬種莊嚴具雲，百萬種衣雲，周匝彌覆；百萬種摩尼雲，光明照耀。從百萬種善根所生，百萬諸佛之所護持，百萬種福德之所增長，百萬種深心、百萬種誓願之所嚴淨，百萬種行之所生起，百萬種法之所建立，百萬種神通之所變現，恒出百萬種言音顯示諸法。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the King of the Yāma Heaven, seeing the Buddha approach from afar, at once, through his own spiritual power, conjured within his palace a lion-throne of the treasury of jeweled lotus flowers, adorned with a million tiers, interlaced with a million golden nets; a million flower-canopies, a million garland-canopies, a million fragrance-canopies, and a million jewel-canopies entirely covered it above; flower-parasols, garland-parasols, fragrance-parasols, and jewel-parasols, likewise a million each, were arrayed all around it, and a million lights shone forth to illumine it. A million kings of the Yāma heaven bowed their heads in reverence; a million Brahmā-kings leapt for joy; a million bodhisattvas extolled and praised it; a million kinds of heavenly music each played a million kinds of Dharma-sounds, continuing without interruption; a million kinds of flower-clouds, a million kinds of garland-clouds, a million kinds of clouds of adorning implements, and a million kinds of clouds of garments entirely covered it all around; a million kinds of maṇi-jewel clouds shone with radiant light. It arose from a million kinds of wholesome roots; it was guarded and sustained by a million buddhas; it was increased by a million kinds of merit; it was adorned and purified by a million kinds of profound mind and a million kinds of vow; it was brought forth by a million kinds of practice; it was established by a million kinds of dharma; it was manifested through transformation by a million kinds of spiritual power; and it forever emitted a million kinds of speech and sound, revealing every dharma.

Welcome, World-Honored One

Chinese Source

時，彼天王敷置座已，向佛世尊曲躬合掌，恭敬尊重而白佛言：「善來世尊！善來善逝！善來如來、應、正等覺！唯願哀愍，處此宮殿！」時，佛受請，即昇寶殿；一切十方，悉亦如是。爾時，天王即自憶念過去佛所所種善根，承佛神力而說頌言：

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, once that heavenly king had arranged and set out the throne, he bowed toward the Buddha, the World-Honored One, folded his palms together, and, with reverence and respect, spoke to the Buddha, saying: "Welcome, World-Honored One! Welcome, Well-Gone One! Welcome, Tathāgata, Worthy One, Fully and Perfectly Awakened One! I beg only that you, out of compassion, dwell within this palace!" At that time, the Buddha accepted the invitation and at once ascended the jeweled palace; and throughout all the ten directions, it was likewise exactly so. At that time, the heavenly king himself called to mind the wholesome roots he had planted at the place of buddhas in ages past, and, relying upon the Buddha's own spiritual power, spoke a verse, saying:

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This chapter opens by repeating, almost word for word, the structure that opened the sūtra's ascent to the Trāyastriṃśa heaven many fascicles ago: the Buddha never leaves the bodhi tree, yet is simultaneously present at the summit of every heaven throughout the ten directions. Each new heaven the sūtra visits — Trāyastriṃśa, and now Yāma — receives its own version of the same welcome, the same hyperbolic adornment counted in the millions, and the same insistence that none of it displaces the Buddha from where he already sits.
