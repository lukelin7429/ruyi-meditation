Three Assemblies

Gopā's knowledge widens from the buddhas themselves to the three kinds of assembly gathered at their side — śrāvakas, pratyekabuddhas, and bodhisattvas — each described in its own terms, the last in a single dense catalogue running the length of the whole path.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1205 — fascicle 75 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Three Assemblies

Every buddha's assembly turns out to contain three distinct kinds of practitioner, and Gopā's knowledge covers each one differently — the śrāvakas by their wisdom, the pratyekabuddhas by their quiescence, and the bodhisattvas by an entire vocabulary of their own.

Chinese Source

亦知彼佛眾會差別，其眾會中有諸眾生依聲聞乘而得出離，其聲聞眾過去修習一切善根，及其所得種種智慧，我悉了知。有諸眾生依獨覺乘而得出離，其諸獨覺所有善根、所得菩提、寂滅解脫、神通變化、成熟眾生、入於涅槃，我悉了知。亦知彼佛諸菩薩眾，其諸菩薩從初發心，修習善根，出生無量諸大願行，成就滿足諸波羅蜜種種莊嚴菩薩之道，以自在力，入菩薩地，住菩薩地，觀菩薩地，淨菩薩地，菩薩地相、菩薩地智、菩薩攝智、菩薩教化眾生智、菩薩建立智、菩薩廣大行境界、菩薩神通行、菩薩三昧海、菩薩方便，菩薩於念念中所入三昧海、所得一切智光明、所獲一切智電光雲、所得實相忍、所通達一切智、所住剎海、所入法海、所知眾生海、所住方便、所發誓願、所現神通，我悉了知。

Ru-Yi Translation

I likewise know the different assemblies of those buddhas: within those assemblies there were beings who attained release by means of the śrāvaka vehicle, and I entirely and fully know the wholesome roots that assembly of śrāvakas had cultivated in ages past, together with every kind of wisdom they had attained. There were beings who attained release by means of the pratyekabuddha vehicle, and I entirely and fully know the wholesome roots of every one of those pratyekabuddhas, the bodhi they attained, their liberation in quiescence, their spiritual powers and transformations, their ripening of beings, and their entry into nirvāṇa. I likewise know the bodhisattva assemblies of those buddhas: how those bodhisattvas, from their very first giving rise to the resolve, cultivated wholesome roots, brought forth measureless great vows and practices, accomplished and fully completed every pāramitā and every kind of adornment of the bodhisattva path; and, through their own sovereign power, entered the bodhisattva grounds, abided in the bodhisattva grounds, contemplated the bodhisattva grounds, and purified the bodhisattva grounds; the marks of the bodhisattva grounds, the wisdom of the bodhisattva grounds, the bodhisattva's own gathering wisdom, the bodhisattva's wisdom in teaching and transforming beings, the bodhisattva's wisdom of establishment, the domain of the bodhisattva's vast and expansive practice, the bodhisattva's own practice of spiritual powers, the bodhisattva's own ocean of samādhi, and the bodhisattva's own skilful means; and, in thought after thought, the ocean of samādhi a bodhisattva enters, the light of all-knowing wisdom he attains, the cloud of all-knowing wisdom's lightning he obtains, the forbearance of the real nature of things he attains, the all-knowing wisdom he penetrates, the ocean of lands he abides in, the ocean of dharma he enters, the ocean of beings he knows, the skilful means he abides in, the vows he brings forth, and the spiritual powers he manifests — all of this, I entirely and fully know.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

依聲聞乘⋯⋯依獨覺乘⋯⋯亦知彼佛諸菩薩眾 — by means of the śrāvaka vehicle⋯⋯by means of the pratyekabuddha vehicle⋯⋯I likewise know the bodhisattva assemblies. Three vehicles, three assemblies, and three very different lengths of description: a single sentence for the śrāvakas, a single sentence for the pratyekabuddhas, and then a catalogue for the bodhisattvas that outruns both combined many times over — grounds entered, abided in, contemplated, and purified, followed by an unbroken run of eleven further things known "in thought after thought" alone.
