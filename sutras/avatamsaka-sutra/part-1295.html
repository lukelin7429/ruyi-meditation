Gathering Every Treasure

The paired similes continue — a tree that grows every tree in Jambudvīpa, a memory medicine, a lotus granting a kalpa's own lifespan, an invisibility charm — closing on an ocean pearl that no fire at the end of an eon could ever exhaust.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1295 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Gathering Every Treasure

Eight more paired similes continue the pattern — trees, medicines, and jewels, each one first named and described, then matched directly to what the mind of bodhi accomplishes.

Chinese Source

「善男子！如有藥樹，名：無生根，以其力故，增長一切閻浮提樹。菩薩摩訶薩菩提心樹亦復如是，以其力故，增長一切學與無學及諸菩薩所有善法「善男子！譬如有藥，名：阿藍婆，若用塗身，身之與心咸有堪能。菩薩摩訶薩得菩提心阿藍婆藥亦復如是，令其身心增長善法。「善男子！譬如有人，得念力藥，凡所聞事憶持不忘。菩薩摩訶薩得菩提心念力妙藥，悉能聞持一切佛法皆無忘失。「善男子！譬如有藥，名：大蓮華，其有服者住壽一劫。菩薩摩訶薩服菩提心大蓮華藥亦復如是，於無數劫，壽命自在。「善男子！譬如有人，執翳形藥，人與非人悉不能見。菩薩摩訶薩執菩提心翳形妙藥，一切諸魔不能得見。「善男子！如海有珠，名：普集眾寶，此珠若在，假使劫火焚燒世間，能令此海減於一滴，無有是處。菩薩摩訶薩菩提心珠亦復如是，住於菩薩大願海中，若常憶持不令退失，能壞菩薩一善根者，終無是處；若退其心，一切善法即皆散滅。「善男子！如有摩尼，名：大光明，有以此珠瓔珞身者，映蔽一切寶莊嚴具，所有光明悉皆不現。菩薩摩訶薩菩提心寶亦復如是，瓔珞其身，映蔽一切二乘心寶，諸莊嚴具悉無光彩。「善男子！如水清珠，能清濁水。菩薩摩訶薩菩提心珠亦復如是，能清一切煩惱垢濁。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as there is a medicinal tree named Unborn-Root, and through its power every tree of Jambudvīpa is caused to grow, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own tree that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: through its power, it causes every wholesome dharma belonging to those still learning, those beyond learning, and every bodhisattva to grow. Good son! Just as there is a medicine named Ālánpó, and when applied to the body, both body and mind together become capable, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva having obtained the Ālánpó medicine that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: it causes their body and mind to grow in wholesome dharmas. Good son! Just as, when a person obtains the medicine of the power of memory, whatever they hear they retain and never forget, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva having obtained the wondrous medicine of the power of memory that is the mind of bodhi is entirely able to hear and retain every buddha-dharma without ever forgetting it. Good son! Just as there is a medicine named Great Lotus, and whoever takes it lives for a full kalpa, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva who partakes of the Great Lotus medicine that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: throughout countless kalpas, their lifespan is entirely their own to command. Good son! Just as, when a person holds the invisibility medicine, neither humans nor non-humans are able to see them, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva holding the wondrous invisibility medicine that is the mind of bodhi cannot be seen by any māra at all. Good son! Just as there is a pearl in the ocean named Gathering-Every-Treasure, and so long as this pearl remains, even should the fire at the end of an eon burn the entire world, there is no possibility that the ocean could be reduced by so much as a single drop, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own pearl that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: abiding within the ocean of a bodhisattva's own great vows, so long as it is constantly held in mind without ever being allowed to regress, there is no possibility that anyone could destroy even a single one of the bodhisattva's own wholesome roots; yet should this mind regress, every wholesome dharma would at once scatter and vanish. Good son! Just as there is a maṇi-jewel named Great Radiance, and one who wears this pearl as a necklace outshines every jeweled ornament, so that their every light no longer appears, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own jewel that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: worn as a necklace upon the body, it outshines the mind-jewel of every follower of the two vehicles, so that every one of their ornaments loses all its brilliance. Good son! Just as a water-clarifying pearl is able to clarify muddy water, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own pearl that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: able to clarify every turbidity of affliction.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

譬如有藥，名：阿藍婆 — 阿藍婆 reads as a phonetic transliteration whose Sanskrit or Pali source cannot be identified with confidence from this passage alone, and is kept here as pinyin (Ālánpó) rather than assigned a guessed reconstruction, following the same conservative treatment already given to 毘笈摩 and 珊陀那 in earlier Parts.

能壞菩薩一善根者，終無是處；若退其心，一切善法即皆散滅 — this pearl-simile carries its own warning within it: the mind of bodhi is unbreakable from without, yet can still be lost from within, if the bodhisattva's own resolve is allowed to regress.
