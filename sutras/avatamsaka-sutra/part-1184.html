Light and Shadow, Echo and Dream

Four more birth-treasuries follow — equal and universal light, birth into the tathāgata's own family, a buddha's own power reflected through six similes for an illusory world, and the paradox of entering measureless realms from within the smallest one.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1184 — fascicle 74 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Light and Shadow, Echo and Dream

The elaboration continues through birth-treasuries five to eight, moving from a bodhisattva's outward conduct to a set of similes describing how he sees the whole of appearance.

Chinese Source

「云何名：平等光明受生藏？善男子！此菩薩具足眾行，普化眾生；一切所有，悉皆能捨；住佛究竟淨戒境界；具足忍法，成就諸佛法忍光明；以大精進，趣一切智，到於彼岸；修習諸禪，得普門定；淨智圓滿，以智慧日，明照諸法；得無礙眼，見諸佛海，悟入一切真實法性；一切世間，見者歡喜，善能修習如實法門。是為菩薩第五受生藏。「云何名：生如來家受生藏？善男子！此菩薩生如來家，隨諸佛住，成就一切甚深法門，具三世佛清淨大願，得一切佛同一善根，與諸如來共一體性，具出世行白淨善法，安住廣大功德法門；入諸三昧，見佛神力；隨所應化，淨諸眾生；如問而對，辯才無盡。是為菩薩第六受生藏。「云何名：佛力光明受生藏？善男子！此菩薩深入佛力，遊諸佛剎心無退轉，供養承事菩薩眾會無有疲厭，了一切法皆如幻起，知諸世間如夢所見，一切色相猶如光影，神通所作皆如變化，一切受生悉皆如影，諸佛說法皆如谷響，開示法界咸令究竟。是為菩薩第七受生藏。「云何名：觀普智門受生藏？善男子！此菩薩住童真位，觀一切智一一智門，盡無量劫開演一切菩薩所行，於諸菩薩甚深三昧心得自在，念念生於十方世界諸如來所，於有差別境入無差別定，於無差別法現有差別智，於無量境知無境界，於少境界入無量境，通達法性廣大無際，知諸世間悉假施設，一切皆是識心所起。是為菩薩第八受生藏。

Ru-Yi Translation

"What is meant by the birth-treasury of equal and universal light? Good son, this bodhisattva is fully endowed with every practice, and universally transforms beings; whatever he possesses, he is able to give up entirely; he abides in a buddha's own ultimate realm of pure precepts; he is fully endowed with the dharma of forbearance, and accomplishes the light of every buddha's dharma-forbearance; through great vigour he advances toward all-knowing wisdom, and reaches its very far shore; he cultivates every dhyāna, and attains the concentration of the universal gate; his pure wisdom is perfect and full, and with the sun of wisdom he brightly illuminates every dharma; he attains the eye free of obstruction, beholds the ocean of every buddha, and awakens into the real nature of every dharma; and throughout every world, all who see him rejoice, for he is skilled in cultivating the dharma-gate that accords with reality. This is the bodhisattva's fifth birth-treasury. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of being born into the tathāgata's own family? Good son, this bodhisattva, born into the tathāgata's own family, abides wherever every buddha abides, and accomplishes every profound dharma-gate; he is endowed with the pure great vows of the buddhas of the three times, attains the very same wholesome root shared by every buddha, and shares one single nature together with every tathāgata; he is endowed with the pure and wholesome dharmas of practice beyond the world, and abides firmly in the vast and expansive dharma-gate of merit; he enters every samādhi, and beholds a buddha's own spiritual power; according to whoever he should transform, he purifies that being; and, answering exactly as he is asked, his eloquence is inexhaustible. This is the bodhisattva's sixth birth-treasury. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of a buddha's own power and light? Good son, this bodhisattva enters deeply into a buddha's own power; he travels through every buddha-land with a mind that never retreats; he makes offerings to and serves the bodhisattva assembly without ever tiring of it; he fully understands that every dharma arises just like an illusion; he knows that every world is seen just as in a dream; every form and mark is just like light and shadow; whatever his spiritual powers produce is just like a magical transformation; every taking of birth is entirely just like a reflection; and every buddha's expounding of the dharma is just like an echo in a valley — and, opening and revealing the dharma-realm, he brings every one of these to their very completion. This is the bodhisattva's seventh birth-treasury. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of contemplating the gate of universal wisdom? Good son, this bodhisattva abides in the ground of genuine youth, contemplates all-knowing wisdom through each and every gate of wisdom, and, throughout measureless kalpas, opens up and expounds every practice of the bodhisattvas; he attains sovereign mastery of mind within the bodhisattvas' own profound samādhis, and, in thought after thought, is born at the place of every tathāgata throughout the worlds of the ten directions; within a realm of differentiation he enters concentration free of all differentiation, and within a dharma free of differentiation he manifests wisdom that differentiates; within measureless realms he knows there is no realm at all, and within the smallest realm he enters measureless realms; he penetrates the nature of dharmas, vast and without boundary, and knows that every world is entirely a provisional construction, all of it arising only from the discriminating mind. This is the bodhisattva's eighth birth-treasury.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

了一切法皆如幻起⋯⋯諸佛說法皆如谷響 — he fully understands that every dharma arises just like an illusion⋯⋯every buddha's expounding of the dharma is just like an echo in a valley. Counted directly, the seventh birth-treasury names exactly six similes in a row — illusion, dream, light and shadow, magical transformation, reflection, and echo — each attached to a different object: dharmas, worlds, forms, spiritual powers, birth itself, and finally the dharma being spoken right now. The list ends by folding the very teaching in progress into the same illusoriness it has just finished describing.
