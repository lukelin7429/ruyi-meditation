Welcome, Stanzas 59–78

Ocean after ocean of merit, wisdom, and vow, a cloud of buddhas, Indra's Net itself — Maitreya's prophecy builds toward a single eight-line stanza marveling that a buddha-child such as Good-Wealth is hard to meet even once in a hundred million kalpas.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1282 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 59–78

A run of "ocean" and "gate" stanzas continues the 當 ("you shall") prophecy; Indra's Net appears by name; and a single extended stanza marvels at how rarely a buddha-child such as Good-Wealth is ever encountered.

Chinese Source

當開天趣門，
當開佛道門，
當示解脫門，
普使眾生入。

當示於正道，
當絕於邪道；
如是勤修行，
成就菩提道。

當修功德海，
當度三有海；
普使群生海，
出於眾苦海。

當於眾生海，
消竭煩惱海，
令修諸行海，
疾入大智海。

汝當增智海，
汝當修行海；
諸佛大願海，
汝當咸滿足。

汝當入剎海，
汝當觀眾海；
汝當以智力，
普飲諸法海。

當覲諸佛雲，
當起供養雲，
當聽妙法雲，
當興此願雲。

普遊三有室，
普壞眾惑室，
普入如來室，
當行如是道。

普入三昧門，
普遊解脫門，
普住神通門，
周行於法界。

普現眾生前，
普對諸佛前，
譬如日月光，
當成如是力。

所行無動亂，
所行無染著，
如鳥行虛空，
當成此妙用。

譬如因陀網，
剎網如是住；
汝當悉往詣，
如風無所礙。

汝當入法界，
遍往諸世界，
普見三世佛，
心生大歡喜。

汝於諸法門，
已得及當得，
應生大喜躍，
無貪亦無厭。

汝是功德器，
能隨諸佛教，
能修菩薩行，
得見此奇特。

如是諸佛子，
億劫難可遇；
況見其功德，
所修諸妙道！
汝生於人中，
大獲諸善利，
得見文殊等，
無量諸功德。

已離諸惡道，
已出諸難處，
已超眾苦患，
善哉勿懈怠。

已離凡夫地，
已住菩薩地，
當滿智慧地，
速入如來地。

菩薩行如海，
佛智同虛空，
汝願亦復然，
應生大欣慶。

諸根不懈倦，
志願恒決定，
親近善知識，
不久悉成滿。

Ru-Yi Translation

You shall open the gate to the deva destiny,
you shall open the gate to the buddha path,
you shall show the gate of liberation,
causing every sentient being to enter universally.

You shall show the right path,
you shall sever the deviant path;
diligently cultivating practice in this way,
you shall accomplish the path of bodhi.

You shall cultivate the ocean of merit,
you shall cross the ocean of the three states of existence;
causing the ocean of every kind of being
to emerge from the ocean of every suffering.

Within the ocean of every sentient being,
you shall dry up the ocean of affliction,
causing the ocean of every practice to be cultivated,
and swiftly entering the great ocean of wisdom.

You shall increase the ocean of wisdom,
you shall cultivate the ocean of practice;
the ocean of every buddha's own great vow —
you shall fulfill every bit of it.

You shall enter the ocean of every land,
you shall contemplate the ocean of every assembly;
you shall, with the power of wisdom,
universally drink from the ocean of every dharma.

You shall behold the cloud of every buddha,
you shall raise the cloud of offering,
you shall listen to the cloud of wondrous dharma,
and raise this very cloud of vows.

Universally traveling the chamber of the three states of existence,
universally destroying the chamber of every delusion,
universally entering the chamber of the tathāgata —
you shall walk a path such as this.

Universally entering the gate of samādhi,
universally traveling the gate of liberation,
universally abiding in the gate of spiritual penetration,
traveling throughout the entire dharma-realm.

Universally manifesting before every sentient being,
universally facing before every buddha,
just like the light of the sun and moon,
you shall accomplish a power such as this.

Whatever he practices holds no agitation,
whatever he practices holds no defilement or attachment;
like a bird traveling through empty space,
you shall accomplish this wondrous function.

Just as with Indra's Net,
so does the net of every land abide;
you shall travel to every one of them,
like the wind, without any obstruction.

You shall enter the dharma-realm,
traveling universally through every world,
universally beholding every buddha of the three times,
your mind giving rise to great joy.

Toward every dharma-gate,
both what you have already attained and what you shall attain,
you should give rise to great joyful leaping,
without craving and without weariness.

You are a vessel of merit,
able to follow every buddha's own teaching,
able to cultivate the bodhisattva practices,
and so are able to see this rare and wondrous thing.

Buddha-children such as this
are hard to encounter even in a hundred million kalpas;
how much more so to see their own merit,
the wondrous path they cultivate!
You were born among humankind,
and have greatly gained every wholesome benefit,
coming to see Mañjuśrī and the rest,
and their measureless merit.

You have already left every evil destiny,
already emerged from every difficult place,
already transcended every affliction and suffering —
excellent indeed; never grow weary.

You have already left the ground of an ordinary being,
already abide upon the ground of a bodhisattva;
you shall fulfill the ground of wisdom,
and swiftly enter the ground of a tathāgata.

A bodhisattva's own practice is like an ocean,
a buddha's own wisdom is the same as empty space;
your own vow is also just the same —
you should give rise to great rejoicing.

Every faculty never growing weary,
your resolve and aspiration ever firm,
drawing near to a good spiritual friend —
before long, everything shall be brought to fulfillment.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

譬如因陀網，剎網如是住 — Indra's Net appears here by name, comparing the net of every land to the deva Indra's own jeweled net, a simile already established elsewhere on this site. The sixteenth stanza in this Part runs to eight lines rather than four — one line longer than usual because its third couplet ends in an exclamation mark (！) rather than a comma, confirmed directly against the source text and matched with seven 、
、 breaks on both sides of the table rather than the usual three.
