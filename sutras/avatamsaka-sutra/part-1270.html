Toward Sea-Coast

Good-Wealth takes his leave in great joy, then recollects nine faults from his own past lives — each one answered at once by a matching resolve — before a closing vision of bodhisattva practice pervading the ten directions, all of it born from believing and receiving a good spiritual friend's teaching.

Toward Sea-Coast

Good-Wealth's own farewell to Virtue-Born and Possessing Virtue opens this Part; his private recollection of nine past faults, each met at once by a resolve, and a closing chain of contemplative attainments bring it to an end.

Chinese Source

時，善財童子聞善知識如是功德，能開示無量菩薩妙行，能成就無量廣大佛法，踊躍歡喜，頂禮德生及有德足，遶無量匝，慇懃瞻仰，辭退而去。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth, having heard of such merit belonging to a good spiritual friend — able to reveal measureless bodhisattva wondrous practices, able to accomplish measureless vast buddha-dharma — leapt with joy; he bowed his head to the feet of Virtue-Born and Possessing Virtue, circled them a measureless number of times, gazed upon them with deep reverence, and took his leave.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子聞善知識教，潤澤其心，正念思惟諸菩薩行，向海岸國。自憶往世不修禮敬，即時發意勤力而行；復憶往世身心不淨，即時發意專自治潔；復憶往世作諸惡業，即時發意專自防斷；復憶往世起諸妄想，即時發意恒正思惟；復憶往世所修諸行但為自身，即時發意令心廣大普及含識；復憶往世追求欲境常自損耗無有滋味，即時發意修行佛法長養諸根以自安隱；復憶往世起邪思念顛倒相應，即時發意生正見心起菩薩願；復憶往世日夜劬勞作諸惡事，即時發意起大精進成就佛法；復憶往世受五趣生於自他身皆無利益，即時發意願以其身饒益眾生成就佛法承事一切諸善知識。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth, having heard a good spiritual friend's own teaching, felt his mind moistened and enriched by it; with right mindfulness he contemplated the bodhisattva practices, and set out toward the country of Sea-Coast. He recollected that in past lives he had failed to cultivate reverence, and at once resolved to practice with diligent effort; he recollected that in past lives body and mind had been impure, and at once resolved to purify himself with single-minded devotion; he recollected that in past lives he had created every kind of evil karma, and at once resolved to guard against and cut it off with single-minded devotion; he recollected that in past lives he had given rise to every kind of deluded thought, and at once resolved to contemplate always with rightness; he recollected that in past lives whatever practices he cultivated had served himself alone, and at once resolved to make his mind vast and extend it universally to every sentient being; he recollected that in past lives pursuing objects of desire had only worn him down without ever yielding true savor, and at once resolved to practice the buddha-dharma, nourishing every faculty to bring himself peace; he recollected that in past lives he had given rise to deviant thoughts bound up with inverted views, and at once resolved to give rise to a mind of right view and raise the bodhisattva vow; he recollected that in past lives day and night he had toiled at every kind of evil deed, and at once resolved to arouse great diligence and accomplish the buddha-dharma; he recollected that in past lives receiving birth within the five destinies had brought no benefit to himself or to others, and at once resolved to vow that with this very body he would benefit sentient beings, accomplish the buddha-dharma, and serve every good spiritual friend.

Chinese Source

如是思惟，生大歡喜。復觀此身是生、老、病、死眾苦之宅，願盡未來劫，修菩薩道教化眾生，見諸如來成就佛法，遊行一切佛剎，承事一切法師，住持一切佛教，尋求一切法侶，見一切善知識，集一切諸佛法，與一切菩薩願智身而作因緣。

Ru-Yi Translation

Reflecting in this way, he gave rise to great joy. He further contemplated that this body is a dwelling of the many sufferings of birth, old age, sickness, and death, and vowed that throughout every kalpa yet to come he would cultivate the bodhisattva path and teach and transform sentient beings; behold every tathāgata and accomplish the buddha-dharma; travel to every buddha-land; serve every dharma-master; uphold and maintain every buddhist teaching; seek out every dharma-companion; behold every good spiritual friend; gather every buddha-dharma; and form a causal bond with every bodhisattva's own body of vow and wisdom.

Chinese Source

作是念時，長不思議無量善根，即於一切菩薩深信尊重，生希有想，生大師想；諸根清淨，善法增益，起一切菩薩恭敬供養，作一切菩薩曲躬合掌，生一切菩薩普見世間眼，起一切菩薩普念眾生想；現一切菩薩無量願化身，出一切菩薩清淨讚說音想；見過、現一切諸佛及諸菩薩，於一切處示現成道神通變化，乃至無有一毛端處而不周遍；又得清淨智光明眼，見一切菩薩所行境界；其心普入十方剎網，其願普遍虛空法界，三世平等，無有休息。如是一切，皆以信受善知識教之所致耳。

Ru-Yi Translation

As he formed this thought, inconceivable and measureless wholesome roots grew within him; at once, toward every bodhisattva he gave rise to deep faith and reverence, gave rise to a sense of rarity, and gave rise to a sense of a great teacher. His every faculty became pure, and wholesome dharmas increased; he raised, toward every bodhisattva, reverent offering; he formed, toward every bodhisattva, a bow of the body with palms joined; he gave rise to an eye, belonging to every bodhisattva, that universally beholds the world; he raised a thought, belonging to every bodhisattva, that universally minds every sentient being. He manifested measureless vow-born transformation-bodies belonging to every bodhisattva; he brought forth a sound of pure praise and speech belonging to every bodhisattva. He beheld every buddha and every bodhisattva of the past and the present, manifesting the accomplishment of the way and spiritual transformations in every place, down to there being no place the size of a single hair-tip left unpervaded. He further attained a pure eye of wisdom's light, and beheld the very realm in which every bodhisattva practices; his mind universally entered the net of lands in the ten directions, and his vow universally pervaded empty space and the dharma-realm, equal throughout the three times, never once resting. All of this came about entirely through believing and receiving a good spiritual friend's own teaching.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

自憶往世不修禮敬，即時發意勤力而行 — nine times in this Part, Good-Wealth recollects a fault from his own past lives and, in the very same breath, resolves its opposite: counted directly from the source text against nine occurrences of 即時發意 ("at once resolved"). The nine faults run from failing to show reverence through the futility of pursuing desire to the fruitlessness of the five destinies themselves; each is answered immediately, never left to stand alone.

修菩薩道教化眾生，見諸如來成就佛法⋯與一切菩薩願智身而作因緣 — a second list follows just after, nine aspirations this time rather than nine corrections, each opening onto "every" (一切) tathāgata, buddha-land, dharma-master, dharma-companion, and good spiritual friend Good-Wealth might yet encounter. Item count verified the same way, directly against the source.
