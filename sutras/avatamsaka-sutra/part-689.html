Knowing One's Own Prediction, Entering Bodhisattvas, Tathāgatas, and Worlds

Ten dharmas let a bodhisattva know their own prediction of future buddhahood without ever being told, and four further ten-fold lists describe what it means to "enter" — into every bodhisattva's own vow and practice, every tathāgata's boundless awakening, every sentient being's conduct across the three times, and every kind of world from the defiled to the pure.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 689 — five more ten-fold answers Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Prediction Known Without Being Told

The first list answers a striking question — how does a bodhisattva know their own prediction of awakening without anyone telling them? — with ten qualities of conduct rather than any sign or vision; from there the questions pivot to a repeated verb, "entering," applied in turn to bodhisattvas, tathāgatas, sentient beings' own conduct, and the full range of worlds, from the defiled to the pure, from the vast to a single dust-mote.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種受記法，菩薩以此自知受記。何等為十？所謂：以殊勝意發菩提心，自知受記；永不厭捨諸菩薩行，自知受記；住一切劫行菩薩行，自知受記；修一切佛法，自知受記；於一切佛教一向深信，自知受記；修一切善根皆令成就，自知受記；置一切眾生於佛菩提，自知受記；於一切善知識和合無二，自知受記；於一切善知識起如來想，自知受記；恒勤守護菩提本願，自知受記。是為十。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種入，入諸菩薩。何等為十？所謂：入本願；入行；入聚；入諸波羅蜜；入成就；入差別願；入種種解；入莊嚴佛土；入神力自在；入示現受生。是為十。菩薩以此普入三世一切菩薩。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten dharmas of receiving a prediction, through which a bodhisattva knows their own prediction without being told. What are these ten? Namely: giving rise to the mind of awakening with a supreme intention — they know their own prediction; forever never growing weary of or abandoning bodhisattva practice — they know their own prediction; abiding through every kalpa while carrying out bodhisattva practice — they know their own prediction; cultivating every dharma of the Buddha — they know their own prediction; having single-minded, profound faith in every teaching of the buddhas — they know their own prediction; cultivating every root of goodness and bringing every one of them to fulfillment — they know their own prediction; placing every sentient being upon the Buddha's own awakening — they know their own prediction; being harmonized with every good spiritual friend as without duality — they know their own prediction; giving rise to the thought of the tathāgata toward every good spiritual friend — they know their own prediction; and forever diligently guarding their own original vow for awakening — they know their own prediction. These are the ten. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of entry, entering into every bodhisattva. What are these ten? Namely: entering their original vow; entering their practice; entering their assembly; entering the pāramitās; entering their accomplishment; entering their distinct vows; entering their various understandings; entering their adornment of buddha-lands; entering their sovereign freedom of spiritual power; and entering their manifested taking of birth. These are the ten. Through these, a bodhisattva universally enters every bodhisattva of the three times.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種入，入諸如來。何等為十？所謂：入無邊成正覺；入無邊轉法輪；入無邊方便法；入無邊差別音聲；入無邊調伏眾生；入無邊神力自在；入無邊種種差別身；入無邊三昧；入無邊力、無所畏；入無邊示現涅槃。是為十。菩薩以此普入三世一切如來。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種入眾生行。何等為十？所謂：入一切眾生過去行；入一切眾生未來行；入一切眾生現在行；入一切眾生善行；入一切眾生不善行；入一切眾生心行；入一切眾生根行；入一切眾生解行；入一切眾生煩惱習氣行；入一切眾生教化調伏時、非時行。是為十。菩薩以此普入一切諸眾生行。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種入世界。何等為十？所謂：入染世界；入淨世界；入小世界；入大世界；入微塵中世界；入微細世界；入覆世界；入仰世界；入有佛世界；入無佛世界。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of entry, entering into every tathāgata. What are these ten? Namely: entering their boundless attainment of perfect awakening; entering their boundless turning of the dharma-wheel; entering their boundless dharmas of skillful means; entering their boundless, distinct spoken sounds; entering their boundless taming of sentient beings; entering their boundless sovereign freedom of spiritual power; entering their boundless variety of distinct bodies; entering their boundless samādhis; entering their boundless powers and fearlessnesses; and entering their boundless manifestation of nirvāṇa. These are the ten. Through these, a bodhisattva universally enters every tathāgata of the three times. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of entry into the conduct of sentient beings. What are these ten? Namely: entering every sentient being's past conduct; entering every sentient being's future conduct; entering every sentient being's present conduct; entering every sentient being's wholesome conduct; entering every sentient being's unwholesome conduct; entering every sentient being's mental conduct; entering every sentient being's conduct of faculties; entering every sentient being's conduct of understanding; entering every sentient being's conduct shaped by afflictive habitual tendency; and entering the timeliness or untimeliness of teaching and taming every sentient being's conduct. These are the ten. Through these, a bodhisattva universally enters the conduct of every sentient being. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of entry into the world. What are these ten? Namely: entering defiled worlds; entering pure worlds; entering small worlds; entering great worlds; entering worlds within a single dust-mote; entering minute worlds; entering worlds turned face-down; entering worlds turned face-up; entering worlds with a buddha; and entering worlds without a buddha. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

以殊勝意發菩提心，自知受記 — giving rise to the mind of awakening with a supreme intention — they know their own prediction. None of the ten signs of a self-known prediction is a vision, a voice, or an external confirmation of any kind; every single one is simply a description of sustained conduct — never wearying, never doubting, never abandoning the original vow. The prediction, on this account, is not something received from outside so much as something a bodhisattva can read directly off the shape of their own practice.
