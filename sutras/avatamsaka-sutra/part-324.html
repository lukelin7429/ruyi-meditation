Every Article of Sustenance Given, No Reward Sought

After the great formal assemblies of giving, the bodhisattva turns to the plainer work of giving away every article people actually need to live — food, clothing, and every implement of daily life — tailored to each sentient being's own distinct wants, and adorned to be wondrous and fine regardless of the recipient. The ten vows that follow move from that practical generosity outward into the widest possible aspiration: wisdom pervading the whole Dharma-realm, and the bodhisattva practice carried out across every world without exception.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 324 — giving away articles for sustaining life, opening and ten dedication-vows Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ordinary Necessities, Given Without Hope of Reward

This passage returns to the register of the very first gift-dedications in this dedication — ordinary articles, tailored to what each sentient being actually uses and asks for — but its vows range far beyond the ordinary, reaching toward wisdom that fills the whole of empty space and a bodhisattva practice that leaves no world, however coarse or fine, however small or vast, untouched.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩布施一切資生之物，心無貪惜，不求果報；於世富樂無所希望，離妄想心，善思惟法；為欲利益一切眾生，審觀一切諸法實性；隨諸眾生種種不同，所用所求各各差別，成辦無量資生之具，所有嚴飾悉皆妙好；行無邊施，行一切施，盡內外施；行此施時，增志樂力，獲大功德，成就心寶；常能守護一切眾生，皆令發生殊勝志願，初未曾有求反報心；所有善根等三世佛，悉以圓滿一切種智。佛子！菩薩摩訶薩以此布施所有善根迴向眾生：『願一切眾生清淨調伏；願一切眾生滅除煩惱，嚴淨一切諸佛剎土；願一切眾生以清淨心，於一念中周遍法界；願一切眾生智慧充滿虛空法界；願一切眾生得一切智，普入三世調伏眾生，於一切時常轉清淨不退法輪；願一切眾生具一切智，善能示現神通方便，饒益眾生；願一切眾生悉能悟入諸佛菩提，盡未來劫，於十方界，常說正法，曾無休息，令諸眾生普得聞知；願一切眾生於無量劫修菩薩行，悉得圓滿；願一切眾生於一切世界若染、若淨、若小、若大、若麁、若細、若覆、若仰，或一莊嚴，或種種莊嚴所可演說，在世界數諸世界中，修菩薩行靡不周遍；願一切眾生於念念中常作三世一切佛事，教化眾生向一切智。』

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! The bodhisattva mahāsattva gives away every article for sustaining life, his mind free of covetousness, seeking no reward in return; he holds no hope at all for worldly wealth and pleasure, leaving behind a mind of deluded thought, skillfully reflecting upon the Dharma; wishing to benefit all sentient beings, he carefully contemplates the true nature of every dharma; according to the various differences among sentient beings, each with their own distinct uses and needs, he brings to completion immeasurable articles for sustaining life, every one of their adornments entirely wondrous and fine; he practices boundless giving, practices every kind of giving, giving entirely both what is internal and what is external; as he practices this giving, his strength of aspiration and joy increases, he obtains great merit, and accomplishes the jewel of the mind; he is forever able to protect all sentient beings, causing them all to give rise to a supreme aspiration, never once, from the very start, having any mind that seeks a reward in return; every wholesome root he holds equals that of the buddhas of the three times, all of it perfecting the wisdom of all modes. Child of the Buddha! The bodhisattva mahāsattva, with every wholesome root from this giving, dedicates it to sentient beings: 'May all sentient beings be purely tamed; may all sentient beings extinguish every affliction, adorning and purifying every buddha-land; may all sentient beings, with a pure mind, pervade the Dharma-realm within a single thought; may all sentient beings' wisdom fill the Dharma-realm of empty space; may all sentient beings attain all-wisdom, universally entering the three times to tame sentient beings, forever turning the pure, non-regressing wheel of the Dharma at all times; may all sentient beings possess all-wisdom, skillfully able to manifest spiritual powers and skillful means, benefiting sentient beings; may all sentient beings be entirely able to awaken to and enter the enlightenment of every buddha, and throughout every future eon, within realms in the ten directions, forever expound the true Dharma without any rest, causing sentient beings everywhere to hear and know it; may all sentient beings, throughout immeasurable eons, cultivate the practice of a bodhisattva, and bring it entirely to fulfillment; may all sentient beings, within every world — whether defiled or pure, whether small or great, whether coarse or fine, whether inverted or upright, whether adorned with a single adornment or with every manner of adornment that can be described — throughout worlds as numerous as the very number of worlds, cultivate the practice of a bodhisattva pervading them without exception; may all sentient beings, thought after thought, forever perform the buddha-work of the three times, teaching and transforming sentient beings toward all-wisdom.'"

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This dedication skips its usual closing formula — the "this is the bodhisattva's dedication of wholesome roots... so that" line that has ended nearly every gift-item passage so far — and lets the tenth vow itself stand as the close: sentient beings, thought after thought, performing the buddha-work of every age. The vow becomes its own summary.
