Verse, Stanzas 21–34

Fourteen more seven-character stanzas close out the meter that opened this verse — oceans of practice, a single hair-tip holding measureless lands, and a mind that has never once attained anything at all.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1276 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Verse, Stanzas 21–34

Fourteen stanzas continue the same seven-character meter and the same closing refrain, completing this portion of Good-Wealth's verse before it shifts to a shorter line in the next Part.

Chinese Source

遍遊一切世界海，
普入一切道場海，
供養一切如來海，
此修行者之住處。

修行一切妙行海，
發起無邊大願海，
如是經於眾劫海，
此功德者之住處。

一毛端處無量剎，
佛眾生劫不可說，
如是明見靡不周，
此無礙眼之住處。

一念普攝無邊劫，
國土諸佛及眾生，
智慧無礙悉正知，
此具德人之住處。

十方國土碎為塵，
一切大海以毛滴，
菩薩發願數如是，
此無礙者之住處。

成就總持三昧門，
大願諸禪及解脫，
一一皆住無邊劫，
此真佛子之住處。

無量無邊諸佛子，
種種說法度眾生，
亦說世間眾技術，
此修行者之住處。

成就神通方便智，
修行如幻妙法門，
十方五趣悉現生，
此無礙者之住處。

菩薩始從初發心，
具足修行一切行，
化身無量遍法界，
此神力者之住處。

一念成就菩提道，
普作無邊智慧業，
世情思慮悉發狂，
此難量者之住處。

成就神通無障礙，
遊行法界靡不周，
其心未甞有所得，
此淨慧者之住處。

菩薩修行無礙慧，
入諸國土無所著，
以無二智普照明，
此無我者之住處。

了知諸法無依止，
本性寂滅同虛空，
常行如是境界中，
此離垢人之住處。

普見群生受諸苦，
發大仁慈智慧心，
願常利益諸世間，
此悲愍者之住處。

Ru-Yi Translation

Traveling universally through the ocean of every world,
entering universally into the ocean of every place of practice,
making offerings to the ocean of every tathāgata —
this is the dwelling-place of one who cultivates practice.

Cultivating the ocean of every wondrous practice,
arousing the ocean of every boundless great vow,
passing in this way through the ocean of every kalpa —
this is the dwelling-place of one of true merit.

Within the space of a single hair-tip, measureless lands,
buddhas, sentient beings, and kalpas beyond all telling —
seeing all of this clearly, with nothing left unseen —
this is the dwelling-place of the unobstructed eye.

Within a single thought, universally gathering every boundless kalpa,
every land, every buddha, and every sentient being,
his wisdom unobstructed, knowing all of it truly —
this is the dwelling-place of one endowed with virtue.

Every land of the ten directions ground into dust,
every great ocean measured drop by drop upon a hair —
a bodhisattva's own vows number just as many —
this is the dwelling-place of one without obstruction.

Accomplishing every gate of dhāraṇī and samādhi,
every great vow, every dhyāna, and every liberation,
abiding within each and every one for boundless kalpas —
this is the dwelling-place of a true buddha-child.

Measureless and boundless buddha-children,
expounding every kind of dharma to save sentient beings,
also teaching every worldly skill and craft —
this is the dwelling-place of one who cultivates practice.

Accomplishing spiritual penetration and the wisdom of skillful means,
cultivating the wondrous dharma-gate that is like an illusion,
manifesting birth within the five destinies throughout the ten directions —
this is the dwelling-place of one without obstruction.

A bodhisattva, from the very first arousing of the mind,
fully cultivating every practice without exception,
his transformation-bodies measureless, pervading the dharma-realm —
this is the dwelling-place of one of spiritual power.

Within a single thought, accomplishing the path of bodhi,
universally performing the boundless deeds of wisdom —
every worldly feeling and thought is driven to madness before it —
this is the dwelling-place of one beyond measure.

Accomplishing spiritual penetration without any hindrance,
traveling through the dharma-realm with nothing left untouched,
his mind never once has attained anything at all —
this is the dwelling-place of one of pure wisdom.

A bodhisattva cultivating unobstructed wisdom,
entering every land without any attachment,
illuminating everything universally with non-dual wisdom —
this is the dwelling-place of one without a self.

Understanding that every dharma has nothing to rely upon,
its fundamental nature quiescent, the same as empty space,
always moving within a realm exactly such as this —
this is the dwelling-place of one free of every stain.

Universally seeing every being receiving every suffering,
he raises a mind of great kindness and wisdom,
vowing always to benefit every world —
this is the dwelling-place of one who feels their sorrow.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

遍遊一切世界海，普入一切道場海 — fourteen stanzas, each still built on four seven-character lines and the same closing refrain, counted directly against the source text. This is the last stretch of the seven-character meter: the next Part shifts to five-character lines and a new opening refrain, 佛子住於此 ("a buddha-child, abiding here").
