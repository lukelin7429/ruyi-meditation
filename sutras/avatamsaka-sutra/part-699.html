Ten Things a Bodhisattva Relies Upon, Ten Fearless Minds

Ten kinds of reliance — offerings to buddhas, roots of goodness, the pāramitās, the measureless mind of awakening itself — ground bodhisattva practice, and ten further conditions give rise to a fearless mind, from protecting the true dharma after the Buddha's nirvāṇa to taming every class of deva, nāga, and spirit being by name.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 699 — ten reliances and ten fearless minds Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

What Practice Stands On, What Fear Cannot Touch

The list of ten reliances reads almost as a foundation poured beneath everything the chapter has already described — offerings, taming beings, drawing near good friends, the pāramitās — before the questions turn to fearlessness itself, naming conditions that range from destroying false doctrines to simply never sparing one's own body or life.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種依止，菩薩依此行菩薩行。何等為十？所謂：依止供養一切諸佛，行菩薩行；依止調伏一切眾生，行菩薩行；依止親近一切善友，行菩薩行；依止積集一切善根，行菩薩行；依止嚴淨一切佛土，行菩薩行；依止不捨一切眾生，行菩薩行；依止深入一切波羅蜜，行菩薩行；依止滿足一切菩薩願，行菩薩行；依止無量菩提心，行菩薩行；依止一切佛菩提，行菩薩行。是為十。菩薩依此行菩薩行。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of reliance, and relies upon them to carry out bodhisattva practice. What are these ten? Namely: relying on making offerings to every buddha, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on taming every sentient being, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on drawing near to every good friend, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on accumulating every root of goodness, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on adorning and purifying every buddha-land, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on never abandoning any sentient being, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on entering deeply into every pāramitā, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on fulfilling every bodhisattva vow, they carry out bodhisattva practice; relying on the measureless mind of awakening, they carry out bodhisattva practice; and relying on the awakening of every buddha, they carry out bodhisattva practice. These are the ten. A bodhisattva relies upon these to carry out bodhisattva practice.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種發無畏心。何等為十？所謂：滅一切障礙業，發無畏心；於佛滅後護持正法，發無畏心；降伏一切魔，發無畏心；不惜身命，發無畏心；摧破一切外道邪論，發無畏心；令一切眾生歡喜，發無畏心；令一切眾會皆悉歡喜，發無畏心；調伏一切天、龍、夜叉、乾闥婆、阿脩羅、迦樓羅、緊那羅、摩睺羅伽，發無畏心；離二乘地，入甚深法，發無畏心；於不可說不可說劫行菩薩行，心無疲厭，發無畏心。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上大智無所畏心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten ways of giving rise to a fearless mind. What are these ten? Namely: extinguishing every obstructing karma, they give rise to a fearless mind; upholding and protecting the true dharma after the Buddha's nirvāṇa, they give rise to a fearless mind; subduing every māra, they give rise to a fearless mind; never sparing their own body or life, they give rise to a fearless mind; destroying every heretical, false doctrine, they give rise to a fearless mind; bringing every sentient being to joy, they give rise to a fearless mind; bringing every assembly entirely to joy, they give rise to a fearless mind; taming every deva, nāga, yakṣa, gandharva, asura, garuḍa, kiṃnara, and mahoraga, they give rise to a fearless mind; leaving behind the grounds of the two vehicles and entering a profound dharma, they give rise to a fearless mind; and carrying out bodhisattva practice across unspeakably, unspeakably many kalpas with a mind never weary, they give rise to a fearless mind. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great, fearless mind of wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

於佛滅後護持正法，發無畏心 — upholding and protecting the true dharma after the Buddha's nirvāṇa, they give rise to a fearless mind. Placed second in the list, right after extinguishing obstructing karma, this entry locates fearlessness not in facing an external threat but in a specific historical responsibility: continuing to protect the teaching once its teacher is no longer present to defend it himself.
