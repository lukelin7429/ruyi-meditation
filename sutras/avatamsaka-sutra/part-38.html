The Four Noble Truths Complete, Fascicle Twelve Complete

Mañjuśrī names the Four Noble Truths in four more worlds — Scant and Few, Joy, the Gate-Bolt, and Resounding Voice — before closing the chapter with the same gesture that closed "The Names of the Tathāgata": this entire structure, repeated across ten worlds here, is declared to hold without exception throughout every world of the ten directions, without number and without end.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 38 — completes fascicle 12 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Without End, Throughout the Ten Directions

Chinese Source

「諸佛子！如此娑婆世界中，說四聖諦，有四百億十千名。如是，東方百千億無數無量、無邊無等、不可數、不可稱、不可思、不可量、不可說，盡法界、虛空界、所有世界，彼一一世界中，說四聖諦，亦各有四百億十千名；隨眾生心，悉令調伏。如東方，南、西、北方，四維、上、下，亦復如是。諸佛子！如娑婆世界，有如上所說；十方世界，彼一切世界亦各有如是。十方世界，一一世界中，說苦聖諦有百億萬種名，說集聖諦、滅聖諦、道聖諦亦各有百億萬種名；皆隨眾生心之所樂，令其調伏。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Buddha's children! Just as, within this Sahā world, the teaching of the Four Noble Truths has four hundred million and ten thousand names — so too, to the east, throughout hundreds of thousands of millions, countless, immeasurable, boundless, unequaled, uncountable, inexpressible, inconceivable, immeasurable, ineffable worlds that exist throughout the whole extent of the Dharma-realm and the realm of empty space, within each and every one of those worlds, the teaching of the Four Noble Truths likewise each has four hundred million and ten thousand names; according to the mind of every living being, it causes them all to be tamed. And it is likewise so to the south, west, and north, in the four intermediate directions, and above and below, just as to the east. Buddha's children! Just as this Sahā world has what has been stated above, so too every world of the ten directions likewise each has just the same. Within every world of the ten directions, each and every world, the teaching of the Noble Truth of Suffering has a hundred thousand million kinds of names, and the teaching of the Noble Truth of Suffering's Origin, the Noble Truth of Suffering's Cessation, and the Noble Truth of the Path likewise each has a hundred thousand million kinds of names; all according to what is in the mind and delight of every living being, causing them to be tamed."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Chapters seven and eight now stand as a matched pair: both open with the same assembly, both proceed through the same sequence of worlds, and both close on the identical rhetorical move — take everything just enumerated, however vast, and multiply it by every world in the Dharma-realm without limit. Fazang's commentary reads the pairing as deliberate: a name for the teacher (chapter seven) and a name for what the teacher taught (chapter eight) are treated as structurally the same kind of thing — both infinitely various in expression, both, the text implies, pointing at something that does not itself vary. Fascicle twelve closes here, with two full chapters spent establishing that even the most basic vocabulary of the Buddhist path — who the Buddha is, what suffering is — was never meant to be fixed in any single language to begin with.

Part 38 Complete — Fascicle Twelve Complete

This closes Part 38 and fascicle twelve: the sūtra's seventh chapter, "The Names of the Tathāgata" (如來名號品第七), and eighth chapter, "The Four Noble Truths" (四聖諦品第八), both complete — the second assembly's opening at the Hall of Universal Light, the arrival of Mañjuśrī and nine companion bodhisattvas, and two full teachings on how thoroughly a single truth can be renamed without ceasing to be itself, repeated across worlds within worlds until the pattern is declared to hold throughout the whole Dharma-realm. Fascicle thirteen continues this same assembly with the sūtra's ninth chapter.
