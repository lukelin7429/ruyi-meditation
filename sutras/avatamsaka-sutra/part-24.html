The Fifth and Sixth Surrounding World-Seeds

Samantabhadra continues clockwise around the central world-seed, describing the fifth and sixth of the ten that surround it — each again with its own fragrant water ocean, its own lotus, its own essence, and its own twenty tiers of worlds and buddhas.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 24 — continues fascicle 9 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Fifth World-Seed

Chinese Source

「諸佛子！此帝青寶莊嚴香水海右旋，次有香水海，名：金剛輪莊嚴底；世界種，名：妙寶間錯因陀羅網，普賢智所生音聲為體。此中最下方，有世界，名：蓮華網；其狀猶如須彌山形，依眾妙華山幢海住，佛境界摩尼王帝網雲而覆其上，佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：法身普覺慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無盡日光明；佛號：最勝大覺慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：普放妙光明；佛號：大福雲無盡力。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：樹華幢；佛號：無邊智法界音。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：真珠蓋；佛號：波羅蜜師子頻申。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無邊音；佛號：一切智妙覺慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：普見樹峯；佛號：普現眾生前。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：師子帝網光；佛號：無垢日金色光焰雲。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：眾寶間錯；佛號：帝幢最勝慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無垢光明地；佛號：一切力清淨月。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：恒出歎佛功德音；佛號：如虛空普覺慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：高焰藏；佛號：化現十方大雲幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：光嚴道場；佛號：無等智遍照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：出生一切寶莊嚴；佛號：廣度眾生神通王。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：光嚴妙宮殿；佛號：一切義成廣大慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：離塵寂靜；佛號：不唐現。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：摩尼華幢；佛號：悅意吉祥音。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：普雲藏；其狀猶如樓閣之形，依種種宮殿香水海住，一切寶燈雲彌覆其上，二十佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：最勝覺神通王。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Buddha's children! Continuing clockwise from this Adornment of Indranīla Jewel fragrant water ocean, there is next a fragrant water ocean named the Base of Vajra Wheel's Adornment; its world-seed is named Indra's Net Interspersed with Wondrous Jewels, taking the voice born from Samantabhadra's own wisdom as its essence. At its very lowest is a world named the Lotus-Flower Net; its shape is just like the form of Sumeru, abiding resting upon an ocean of a mountain-banner of every wondrous flower, and a cloud, the sovereign maṇi's own net of Indra manifesting the Buddha's domain, covers over it. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land encircle it all around, purely one and entirely pure. Its buddha is named the Dharma-Body's Universal Wisdom of Awakening. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Inexhaustible Light of the Sun. Its buddha is named the Supreme, Great Wisdom of Awakening. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Universally Emitting Wondrous Light. Its buddha is named the Cloud of Great Blessing's Inexhaustible Power. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Banner of a Tree's Flower. Its buddha is named the Boundless Wisdom of the Dharma-Realm's Voice. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Canopy of True Pearls. Its buddha is named the Wondrous Wisdom of Awakening of Omniscience. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Universally Beholding the Tree's Peak. Its buddha is named Universally Manifesting Before Every Living Being. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Lion's Light of Indra's Net. Its buddha is named the Cloud of Golden Light and Flame of the Sun Free of Defilement. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Every Jewel Interspersed. Its buddha is named the Supreme Wisdom of Indra's Banner. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Wondrous Palace Adorned with Light. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Banner of a Maṇi Flower. Its buddha is named the Delightful, Auspicious Voice. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in twenty buddha-lands encircle it all around, purely one and entirely pure. Its buddha is named the King of Spiritual Transmutation of Supreme Awakening."

The Sixth World-Seed

Chinese Source

「諸佛子！此金剛輪莊嚴底香水海右旋，次有香水海，名：蓮華因陀羅網；世界種，名：普現十方影，依一切香摩尼莊嚴蓮華住，一切佛智光音聲為體。此中最下方，有世界，名：眾生海寶光明；其狀猶如真珠之藏，依一切摩尼瓔珞海漩住，水光明摩尼雲而覆其上，佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：不思議功德遍照月。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙香輪；佛號：無量力幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙光輪；佛號：法界光音覺悟慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：吼聲摩尼幢；佛號：蓮華光恒垂妙臂。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：極堅固輪；佛號：不退轉功德海光明。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：眾行光莊嚴；佛號：一切智普勝尊。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：師子座遍照；佛號：師子光無量力覺慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寶焰莊嚴；佛號：一切法清淨智。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無量燈；佛號：無憂相。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：常聞佛音；佛號：自然勝威光。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：清淨變化；佛號：金蓮華光明。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：普入十方；佛號：觀法界頻申慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：熾然焰；佛號：光焰樹緊那羅王。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：香光遍照；佛號：香燈善化王。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無量華聚輪；佛號：普現佛功德。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：眾妙普清淨；佛號：一切法平等神通王。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：金光海；佛號：十方自在大變化。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：真珠華藏；佛號：法界寶光明不可思議慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：帝釋須彌師子座；佛號：勝力光。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無邊寶普照；其形四方，依華林海住，普雨無邊色摩尼王帝網彌覆其上，二十佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：遍照世間最勝音。」

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The sixth world-seed's buddha-names widen the register further than any so far: alongside the expected wisdoms and lights stands "the King of Kinnaras of the Tree of Light and Flame" — a title borrowed directly from the retinue of non-human beings (kinnaras, gandharvas, garuḍas, and the like) already catalogued at length back in fascicle one's assembly of world-lords. The world-seeds are not populated only by buddhas whose names sound like abstract virtues; the sūtra's cosmology keeps reaching back into its own earlier cast of characters, suggesting these tiers are continuous with, not separate from, everything already described.
