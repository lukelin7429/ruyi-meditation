Great Merits, Ultimate Purity, and Constant Buddha-Work

Three more lists of ten: great merits each closed with the same refrain of being free of fault and purified; a shorter list of what "ultimate purity" reaches into, from vows to retinue to the body itself; and ten buddha-works numbered plainly, one through ten, carried out in every world at every time without exception.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 584 — merit, purity, and work without end Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Great Merits, Free of Every Fault

This list names ten great merits of a buddha, and closes every single item with the same four-character refrain — free of fault, purified — turning the whole list into something closer to a chant than an inventory.

Chinese Source

「佛子！諸佛世尊有十種大功德，離過清淨。何等為十？所謂：一切諸佛具大威德，離過清淨；一切諸佛悉於三世如來家生，種族調善，離過清淨；一切諸佛盡未來際心無所住，離過清淨；一切諸佛於三世法皆無所著，離過清淨；一切諸佛知種種性皆是一性，無所從來，離過清淨；一切諸佛前際、後際福德無盡，等於法界，離過清淨；一切諸佛無邊身相遍十方剎，隨時調伏一切眾生，離過清淨；一切諸佛獲四無畏，離諸恐怖，於眾會中大師子吼，明了分別一切諸法，離過清淨；一切諸佛於不可說不可說劫入般涅槃，眾生聞名獲無量福，如佛現在功德無異，離過清淨；一切諸佛遠在不可說不可說世界中，若有眾生一心正念則皆得見，離過清淨。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Sons of the Buddha! The buddhas, the World-Honored Ones, have ten kinds of great merit, free of fault and purified. What are these ten? They are: every buddha is endowed with great majestic virtue, free of fault and purified; every buddha is born into the tathāgata's family across the three times, of a well-tamed lineage, free of fault and purified; every buddha, to the very end of future time, has a mind that abides nowhere, free of fault and purified; every buddha is unattached to any dharma of the three times, free of fault and purified; every buddha knows that every manner of nature is a single nature, coming from nowhere at all, free of fault and purified; every buddha's merit, in every time before and after, is inexhaustible, equal to the dharma-realm, free of fault and purified; every buddha's boundless bodily marks pervade the lands of the ten directions, taming every sentient being at the right time, free of fault and purified; every buddha, having attained the four fearlessnesses, free of every terror, roars the great lion's roar within the assembly, clearly distinguishing every dharma, free of fault and purified; every buddha, entering parinirvāṇa after unspeakable, unspeakable kalpas, causes sentient beings who merely hear the name to obtain measureless merit, no different from the buddha's merit while present in the world, free of fault and purified; and every buddha, however far off in unspeakable, unspeakable worlds, is seen entirely by any sentient being who holds them in mind with single-pointed, right mindfulness, free of fault and purified. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

眾生聞名獲無量福，如佛現在功德無異 — sentient beings who merely hear the name obtain measureless merit, no different from the buddha's merit while present in the world. This single clause is the doctrinal seed of an entire later tradition of name-recitation practice: a buddha who has already entered parinirvāṇa is said to still be reachable through nothing more than hearing and holding the name, with no lesser effect than encountering that buddha in person.

Ten Kinds of Ultimate Purity

A shorter, plainer list follows: not qualities described at length, but ten domains — vows, conduct, land, retinue, lineage, body, dharma-body, wisdom, liberation — each simply named as ultimately pure.

Chinese Source

「佛子！諸佛世尊有十種究竟清淨。何等為十？所謂：一切諸佛往昔大願究竟清淨；一切諸佛所持梵行究竟清淨；一切諸佛離世眾惑究竟清淨；一切諸佛莊嚴國土究竟清淨；一切諸佛所有眷屬究竟清淨；一切諸佛所有種族究竟清淨；一切諸佛色身相好究竟清淨；一切諸佛法身無染究竟清淨；一切諸佛一切智智無有障礙究竟清淨；一切諸佛解脫自在，所作已辦，到於彼岸，究竟清淨。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Sons of the Buddha! The buddhas, the World-Honored Ones, have ten kinds of ultimate purity. What are these ten? They are: every buddha's great vows of long ago are ultimately pure; every buddha's upheld pure conduct is ultimately pure; every buddha's departure from every worldly delusion is ultimately pure; every buddha's adorned land is ultimately pure; every buddha's every retinue is ultimately pure; every buddha's every lineage is ultimately pure; every buddha's physical body and its excellent marks are ultimately pure; every buddha's dharma-body, undefiled, is ultimately pure; every buddha's all-knowing wisdom, unobstructed, is ultimately pure; and every buddha's liberation and mastery, their work already done, having reached the far shore, is ultimately pure. These are the ten.

Ten Buddha-Works, in Every World, at Every Time

For once the list is numbered plainly, one through ten, rather than repeated with "every buddha" at the head of each clause — ten things a buddha does in every world, at every time, without exception.

Chinese Source

「佛子！諸佛世尊於一切世界、一切時，有十種佛事。何等為十？一者，若有眾生專心憶念，則現其前；二者，若有眾生心不調順，則為說法；三者，若有眾生能生淨信，必令獲得無量善根；四者，若有眾生能入法位，悉皆現證，無不了知；五者，教化眾生無有疲厭；六者，遊諸佛剎，往來無礙；七者，大悲不捨一切眾生；八者，現變化身，恒不斷絕；九者，神通自在，未嘗休息；十者，安住法界，能遍觀察。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Sons of the Buddha! The buddhas, the World-Honored Ones, in every world and at every time, carry out ten kinds of buddha-work. What are these ten? First, if there is a sentient being who single-mindedly holds them in mind, the buddha appears before them; second, if there is a sentient being whose mind is not yet tamed and compliant, the buddha teaches them the dharma; third, if there is a sentient being able to give rise to pure faith, the buddha causes them without fail to obtain measureless wholesome roots; fourth, if there is a sentient being able to enter the station of dharma, the buddha causes it all to be directly realized, with nothing left uncomprehended; fifth, the buddha teaches and transforms sentient beings without ever growing weary; sixth, the buddha travels among every buddha-land, going and coming without obstruction; seventh, the buddha's great compassion never abandons any sentient being; eighth, the buddha manifests transformation-bodies that are never interrupted; ninth, the buddha's spiritual mastery is never once at rest; and tenth, the buddha abides in the dharma-realm, able to observe it universally. These are the ten.
