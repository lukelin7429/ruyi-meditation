Peace of Mind, Ripening Beings, and Ten Precepts

Every one of the ten ways a bodhisattva's mind attains peace is doubled — what they secure for themselves, they secure for another in the same breath — before the answers turn to ten ways of bringing sentient beings to accomplishment, from ordinary generosity to blazing manifestation across every world, and finally ten precepts anchored first and last in never abandoning the mind of awakening.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 688 — peace of mind, ripening beings, and ten precepts Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Peace That Comes in Pairs

The tenfold list of peace of mind repeats a single structure ten times over — securing something for oneself only ever alongside securing it for another — before the questions turn outward to method, listing ten distinct ways of bringing beings to accomplishment, and then inward again to ten precepts that begin and end with the mind of awakening itself.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種心得安隱。何等為十？所謂：自住菩提心，亦當令他住菩提心，心得安隱；自究竟離忿諍，亦當令他離忿諍，心得安隱；自離凡愚法，亦令他離凡愚法，心得安隱；自勤修善根，亦令他勤修善根，心得安隱；自住波羅蜜道，亦令他住波羅蜜道，心得安隱；自生在佛家，亦當令他生於佛家，心得安隱；自深入無自性真實法，亦令他入無自性真實法，心得安隱；自不誹謗一切佛法，亦令他不誹謗一切佛法，心得安隱；自滿一切智菩提願，亦令他滿一切智菩提願，心得安隱；自深入一切如來無盡智藏，亦令他入一切如來無盡智藏，心得安隱。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上大智安隱。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten ways the mind attains peace and stability. What are these ten? Namely: abiding themselves in the mind of awakening, and also causing others to abide in the mind of awakening — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves ultimately leaving behind anger and contention, and also causing others to leave behind anger and contention — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves leaving behind the ways of the ordinary and foolish, and causing others to leave behind the ways of the ordinary and foolish — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves diligently cultivating roots of goodness, and causing others to diligently cultivate roots of goodness — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves abiding on the path of the pāramitās, and causing others to abide on the path of the pāramitās — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves being born into the Buddha's own family, and also causing others to be born into the Buddha's own family — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves entering deeply into the true dharma of no fixed nature, and causing others to enter the true dharma of no fixed nature — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves never slandering any dharma of the Buddha, and causing others never to slander any dharma of the Buddha — the mind attains peace and stability; themselves fulfilling their vow for all-knowing wisdom and awakening, and causing others to fulfill their own vow for all-knowing wisdom and awakening — the mind attains peace and stability; and themselves entering deeply into the inexhaustible treasury of wisdom of every tathāgata, and causing others to enter the inexhaustible treasury of wisdom of every tathāgata — the mind attains peace and stability. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great peace of wisdom.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種成就眾生。何等為十？所謂：以布施成就眾生；以色身成就眾生；以說法成就眾生；以同行成就眾生；以無染著成就眾生；以開示菩薩行成就眾生；以熾然示現一切世界成就眾生；以示現佛法大威德成就眾生；以種種神通變現成就眾生；以種種微密善巧方便成就眾生。是為十。菩薩以此成就眾生界。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種戒。何等為十？所謂：不捨菩提心戒；遠離二乘地戒；觀察利益一切眾生戒；令一切眾生住佛法戒；修一切菩薩所學戒；於一切法無所得戒；以一切善根迴向菩提戒；不著一切如來身戒；思惟一切法離取著戒；諸根律儀戒。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上廣大戒波羅蜜。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten ways of bringing sentient beings to accomplishment. What are these ten? Namely: bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through generosity; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through their own bodily form; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through expounding the dharma; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through practicing alongside them; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through being free of any defiled attachment; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through disclosing bodhisattva practice; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through blazingly manifesting throughout every world; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through manifesting the great majestic virtue of the Buddha's dharma; bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through every kind of spiritual transformation and manifestation; and bringing sentient beings to accomplishment through every kind of subtle, secret, skillful means. These are the ten. Through these, a bodhisattva brings the whole realm of sentient beings to accomplishment. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of moral discipline. What are these ten? Namely: the precept of never abandoning the mind of awakening; the precept of staying far from the grounds of the two vehicles; the precept of observing what benefits every sentient being; the precept of causing every sentient being to abide in the Buddha's dharma; the precept of practicing everything a bodhisattva must learn; the precept of attaining nothing at all with regard to every dharma; the precept of dedicating every root of goodness toward awakening; the precept of not clinging to the body of any tathāgata; the precept of reflecting on every dharma while free of grasping and attachment; and the precept of discipline over every sense faculty. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, vast pāramitā of moral discipline.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

自住菩提心，亦當令他住菩提心，心得安隱 — abiding themselves in the mind of awakening, and also causing others to abide in the mind of awakening — the mind attains peace and stability. Every one of the ten entries in this list follows the same shape: self, then other, then peace — a structure that quietly denies the possibility of a private peace of mind in this teaching. None of the ten states listed ever count as achieved on the strength of the bodhisattva's own attainment alone.
