Ten Similes

After lists of measureless practices and named abilities, the teaching turns to image instead of enumeration — ten similes, each pairing a single relationship with a single reason, closing this stretch of instruction on concrete pictures rather than abstractions.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1265 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Similes

Where the last several Parts counted abstractions by the dozen, this one offers ten concrete pictures instead — a mother, a father, a nurse, a teacher, a guide, a physician, a mountain, a general, a ferryman, and a boatman.

Chinese Source

「善男子！善知識者，如慈母，出生佛種故；如慈父，廣大利益故；如乳母，守護不令作惡故；如教師，示其菩薩所學故；如善導，能示波羅蜜道故；如良醫，能治煩惱諸病故；如雪山，增長一切智藥故；如勇將，殄除一切怖畏故；如濟客，令出生死暴流故；如船師，令到智慧寶洲故。善男子！常當如是正念思惟諸善知識。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! A good spiritual friend is like a compassionate mother, because they give birth to the very seed of buddhahood; like a compassionate father, because of their vast benefit; like a wet-nurse, because they guard one from ever doing evil; like a teacher, because they show what a bodhisattva ought to study; like a good guide, because they are able to show the road of the pāramitās; like a good physician, because they are able to heal every sickness of affliction; like a snow mountain, because they increase every medicine of wisdom; like a valiant general, because they eliminate every fear; like a ferryman for travelers, because they cause one to escape the violent torrent of birth-and-death; and like a boatman, because they cause one to reach the jeweled island of wisdom. Good son! You should forever, with right mindfulness, contemplate every good spiritual friend in this way."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

如慈母，出生佛種故⋯如船師，令到智慧寶洲故 — like a compassionate mother, because they give birth to the very seed of buddhahood⋯like a boatman, because they cause one to reach the jeweled island of wisdom. Ten similes in all, counted directly from the source text, moving from images of birth and care (mother, father, nurse) through images of instruction (teacher, guide, physician) to images of a completed journey (general, ferryman, boatman) — the whole relationship compressed into ten single-clause pictures.
