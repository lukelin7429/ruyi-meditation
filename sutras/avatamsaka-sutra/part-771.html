Ten Kinds of Pure Patience, Ten Kinds of Pure Diligence

Ten kinds of pure patience move from enduring insult and physical harm to a final entry that departs from endurance altogether: an acceptance of every dharma's unborn nature, arrived at without being taught by another. Ten kinds of pure diligence follow, closing on a diligence so complete it manifests every deed of Samantabhadra — birth, going forth, awakening, teaching, and cessation — through a single outflow-free body.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 771 — pure patience, pure diligence Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

From Endurance to Understanding

The first nine kinds of pure patience all describe enduring something — insult, blade and staff, mutilation, wrong done without retaliation. The tenth breaks this pattern entirely, moving from endurance to direct understanding: an acceptance of the unborn nature of every dharma, reached without any teacher, closing the list on insight rather than forbearance.

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種清淨忍。何等為十？所謂：安受呰辱清淨忍，護諸眾生故；安受刀杖清淨忍，善護自他故；不生恚害清淨忍，其心不動故；不責卑賤清淨忍，為上能寬故；有歸咸救清淨忍，捨自身命故；遠離我慢清淨忍，不輕未學故；殘毀不瞋清淨忍，觀察如幻故；有犯無報清淨忍，不見自他故；不隨煩惱清淨忍，離諸境界故；隨順菩薩真實智知一切法無生清淨忍，不由他教，入一切智境界故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住其中，則得一切諸佛不由他悟無上法忍。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of pure patience. What are these ten? Namely: the pure patience of calmly enduring insult and disgrace, because it protects every sentient being; the pure patience of calmly enduring blade and staff, because it skillfully protects both self and other; the pure patience of never giving rise to hatred and harm, because the mind is unmoving; the pure patience of never reproaching the lowly, because being superior, it is able to be magnanimous; the pure patience of rescuing every one who takes refuge, because it gives up its own body and life; the pure patience of staying far from pride, because it never looks down on those who have not yet learned; the pure patience of never being angered even by mutilation, because it observes it as exactly like an illusion; the pure patience of never retaliating even when wronged, because it perceives neither self nor other; the pure patience of never following affliction, because it stays free of every domain; and the pure patience according with a bodhisattva's own true wisdom that knows every dharma as unborn, because, without being taught by another, it enters the domain of all-knowing wisdom. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within these, they attain every buddha's own unsurpassed acceptance of the dharma, awakened without being taught by another.

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種清淨精進。何等為十？所謂：身清淨精進，承事供養諸佛菩薩及諸師長，尊重福田不退轉故；語清淨精進，隨所聞法廣為他說，讚佛功德無疲倦故；意清淨精進，善能入出慈、悲、喜、捨、禪定、解脫及諸三昧無休息故；正直心清淨精進，無誑無諂，無曲無偽，一切勤修無退轉故；增勝心清淨精進，志常趣求上上智慧，願具一切白淨法故；不唐捐清淨精進，攝取布施、戒、忍、多聞及不放逸乃至菩提無中息故；摧伏一切魔清淨精進，悉能除滅貪欲、瞋恚、愚癡、邪見、一切煩惱、諸纏蓋故；成滿智慧光清淨精進，有所施為悉善觀察，咸使究竟，不令後悔，得一切佛不共法故；無來無去清淨精進，得如實智，入法界門，身、語及心皆悉平等，了相非相，無所著故；成就法光清淨精進，超過諸地，得佛灌頂，以無漏身而示歿生、出家、成道、說法、滅度，具足如是普賢事故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上大清淨精進。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of pure diligence. What are these ten? Namely: the pure diligence of the body, because it attends upon and makes offerings to every buddha, bodhisattva, and teacher, revering this field of merit without ever regressing; the pure diligence of speech, because it widely teaches others whatever dharma it has heard, praising the buddha's own merit without ever growing weary; the pure diligence of the mind, because it is able to enter and emerge from loving-kindness, compassion, joy, equanimity, meditative absorption, liberation, and every samādhi without ever resting; the pure diligence of an upright mind, because, free of deceit and flattery, free of crookedness and falseness, it diligently cultivates everything without ever regressing; the pure diligence of an ever-heightening mind, because its resolve constantly seeks higher and higher wisdom, vowing to be complete in every pure, wholesome dharma; the pure diligence that is never wasted, because it gathers in giving, precepts, patience, extensive learning, and heedfulness, never once pausing all the way to awakening; the pure diligence that subdues every māra, because it is able to eliminate greed, hatred, delusion, wrong view, and every affliction, entanglement, and hindrance; the pure diligence that fulfills the light of wisdom, because whatever it undertakes it skillfully observes, bringing everything to its ultimate completion without ever regretting it, attaining every buddha's own unshared dharma; the pure diligence of neither coming nor going, because it attains wisdom exactly as things truly are, enters the gate of the dharma-realm, and its body, speech, and mind are all entirely equal, understanding mark as no mark, attached to nothing; and the pure diligence that accomplishes the light of dharma, because, transcending every ground, receiving the buddha's own consecration, it manifests, through an outflow-free body, passing away and being born, going forth from home, attaining the path, teaching the dharma, and entering cessation, being complete in every one of these deeds of Samantabhadra. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great, pure diligence.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

以無漏身而示歿生、出家、成道、說法、滅度，具足如是普賢事故 — through an outflow-free body, passing away and being born, going forth from home, attaining the path, teaching the dharma, and entering cessation, being complete in every one of these deeds of Samantabhadra. The tenth diligence names, as its own accomplishment, the full traditional life-cycle of a buddha's appearance in the world — every stage from birth to final cessation folded into a single body already free of every outflow.
