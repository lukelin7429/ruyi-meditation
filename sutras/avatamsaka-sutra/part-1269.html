Its Very Root

Ten measureless attainments, a nine-part summary of everything a bodhisattva practices, and a single closing claim bring this entire teaching to its end — that a good spiritual friend is the very root of every bodhisattva's own accomplishment.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1269 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Its Very Root

Ten attainments measured in the largest numbers this teaching has used, a summary of nine bodhisattva categories, and a final declaration close the entire discourse Virtue-Born and Possessing Virtue began several Parts ago.

Chinese Source

「善男子！以要言之，菩薩摩訶薩若能隨順善知識教，得十不可說百千億那由他功德，淨十不可說百千億那由他深心，長十不可說百千億那由他菩薩根，淨十不可說百千億那由他菩薩力，斷十不可說百千億阿僧祇障，超十不可說百千億阿僧祇魔境，入十不可說百千億阿僧祇法門，滿十不可說百千億阿僧祇助道，修十不可說百千億阿僧祇妙行，發十不可說百千億阿僧祇大願。「善男子！我復略說一切菩薩行、一切菩薩波羅蜜、一切菩薩地、一切菩薩忍、一切菩薩總持門、一切菩薩三昧門、一切菩薩神通智、一切菩薩迴向、一切菩薩願。一切菩薩成就佛法，皆由善知識力，以善知識而為根本，依善知識生，依善知識出，依善知識長，依善知識住，善知識為因緣，善知識能發起。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! To put it briefly, if a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is able to accord with a good spiritual friend's own teaching, that bodhisattva attains ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of nayutas of merit; purifies ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of nayutas of profound resolve; lengthens ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of nayutas of bodhisattva roots; purifies ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of nayutas of bodhisattva powers; severs ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of hindrances; transcends ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of māra-domains; enters ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of dharma-gates; fulfills ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of aids to the path; cultivates ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of wondrous practices; gives rise to ten ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of asaṃkhyeyas of great vows. Good son! I will, once more, briefly speak of every bodhisattva's own practice, every bodhisattva's own pāramitā, every bodhisattva's own ground, every bodhisattva's own forbearance, every bodhisattva's own dhāraṇī-gate, every bodhisattva's own samādhi-gate, every bodhisattva's own wisdom of spiritual penetration, every bodhisattva's own dedication of merit, and every bodhisattva's own vow. Every bodhisattva's own accomplishment of the buddha-dharma comes entirely through the power of a good spiritual friend, taking a good spiritual friend as its very root — born through a good spiritual friend, emerging through a good spiritual friend, growing through a good spiritual friend, and abiding through a good spiritual friend; a good spiritual friend is its very cause and condition, and a good spiritual friend is what is able to give rise to it."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

依善知識生，依善知識出，依善知識長，依善知識住 — born through a good spiritual friend, emerging through a good spiritual friend, growing through a good spiritual friend, and abiding through a good spiritual friend. Ten measureless attainments open this Part, a nine-part summary of every bodhisattva category follows, and these four parallel clauses close it — verified directly from the source text, and marking the closing quotation mark of the entire teaching this journey has followed since Virtue-Born and Possessing Virtue first spoke.
