Clouds from Every Pore

The sentence that began in the previous part goes on. From each and every pore of Samantabhadra's body come clouds of wondrous garments and clouds of jeweled trees raining maṇi; clouds of the bodies of the devas of the form realm praising the mind of bodhi, of Brahmā devas urging the tathāgatas to turn the wheel of dharma, and of the lords of the desire realm guarding it; and then, in thought after thought, clouds of buddha-lands of the three times that become refuge, shelter, and support for beings who have none, clouds of pure lands in which buddhas appear, clouds of lands mixed and impure that purify every defiled being, and clouds of the bodies of beings and of bodhisattvas that turn every being toward bodhi and extol the names of the buddhas.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1328 — fascicle 80 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Clouds from Every Pore

The pore-cloud sentence resumed and completed — garments, trees, the bodies of devas, buddha-lands of every kind, and the bodies of beings and bodhisattvas.

Chinese Source

見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數妙衣雲，遍法界、虛空界一切諸佛眾會道場，雨眾妙衣；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數寶樹雲，遍法界、虛空界一切諸佛眾會道場，雨摩尼寶；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數色界天身雲，充滿法界，歎菩提心；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數梵天身雲，勸諸如來轉妙法輪；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數欲界天主身雲，護持一切如來法輪；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數三世佛剎雲，遍法界、虛空界，為諸眾生，無歸趣者為作歸趣，無覆護者為作覆護，無依止者為作依止；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數清淨佛剎雲，遍法界、虛空界，一切諸佛於中出世，菩薩眾會悉皆充滿；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數淨不淨佛剎雲，遍法界、虛空界，令雜染眾生皆得清淨；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數不淨淨佛剎雲，遍法界、虛空界，令雜染眾生皆得清淨；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數不淨佛剎雲，遍法界、虛空界，令純染眾生皆得清淨；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數眾生身雲，遍法界、虛空界，隨其所應，教化眾生，皆令發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數菩薩身雲，遍法界、虛空界，稱揚種種諸佛名號，令諸眾生增長善根。

Ru-Yi Translation

he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of wondrous garments as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the sites of awakening of the assemblies of all the buddhas throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space and raining down every wondrous garment; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of jeweled trees as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the sites of awakening of the assemblies of all the buddhas throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space and raining down maṇi jewels; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of the bodies of the devas of the form realm as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, filling the dharma-realm and praising the mind of bodhi; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of the bodies of Brahmā devas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, urging the tathāgatas to turn the wondrous wheel of dharma; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of the bodies of the lords of the devas of the desire realm as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, guarding and upholding the wheel of dharma of every tathāgata; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of buddha-lands of the three times as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, becoming for sentient beings a refuge for those without refuge, a shelter for those without shelter, and a support for those without support; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of pure buddha-lands as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, in which all the buddhas appeared in the world and the assemblies of bodhisattvas were everywhere full; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of buddha-lands pure and impure as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, causing beings of mixed defilement all to attain purity; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of buddha-lands impure and pure as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, causing beings of mixed defilement all to attain purity; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of impure buddha-lands as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, causing purely defiled beings all to attain purity; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of the bodies of sentient beings as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, teaching and transforming sentient beings according to what suited them and causing them all to arouse the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi; and he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of the bodies of bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, extolling the manifold names of the buddhas and causing sentient beings to increase their wholesome roots.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

見一一毛孔 — this part resumes the sentence broken at a semicolon in Part 1327 and carries it to its full stop: twelve further clouds, making sixteen in the whole sentence, and the translation accordingly opens in the middle of a sentence, without a capital, and closes with the first full stop the source allows. From the sixth cloud of this part onward the source adds 念念中, in thought after thought, to each item, so that the clouds of the later kind are not merely present but continuously produced, and the translation keeps the phrase at each of its seven occurrences.

淨、淨不淨、不淨淨、不淨 — the four kinds of buddha-land cloud reproduce the fourfold set of worlds that Part 1314 met in the jeweled mirrors, pure, pure and impure, impure and pure, and impure, and the order of the characters is again kept. Their effects are graded to match: the mixed lands, in both orders, purify 雜染眾生, beings of mixed defilement, and the impure lands purify 純染眾生, purely defiled beings, a contrast the site first drew, with the same words for 純 and 雜, in Part 17, in fascicle 7. The triad 歸趣、覆護、依止, refuge, shelter, and support, each given to those who lack it, is rendered with the site's usual terms.
