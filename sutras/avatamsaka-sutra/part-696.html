Wisdom Giving Rise to Wisdom, Transformation, and Sustaining Power

Fascicle fifty-three closes with three more ten-fold answers: wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom by knowing everything from a sentient being's understanding to an unbroken buddha-seed, ten kinds of transformation reaching from a bodhisattva's own body to the act of teaching itself, and ten kinds of sustaining power spanning the Buddha, karma, time, and wisdom.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 696 — fascicle 53 complete Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Wisdom That Multiplies Itself

The fascicle's final list names a wisdom that produces more wisdom simply by knowing things — a sentient being's understanding, a world turned face-down or face-up, an unbroken buddha-seed — before the questions turn to transformation and to sustaining power, ending fascicle fifty-three on ten kinds of support ranging from the Buddha's own power down to time itself.

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種出生智慧。何等為十？所謂：知一切眾生解出生智慧；知一切佛剎種種差別出生智慧；知十方網分齊出生智慧；知覆仰等一切世界出生智慧；知一切法一性、種種性廣大住出生智慧；知一切種種身出生智慧；知一切世間顛倒妄想悉無所著出生智慧；知一切法究竟皆以一道出離出生智慧；知如來神力能入一切法界出生智慧；知三世一切眾生佛種不斷出生智慧。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則於諸法無不了達。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種變化。何等為十？所謂：一切眾生變化；一切身變化；一切剎變化；一切供養變化；一切音聲變化；一切行願變化；一切教化調伏眾生變化；一切成正覺變化；一切說法變化；一切加持變化。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得具足一切無上變化法。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of wisdom that give rise to further wisdom. What are these ten? Namely: the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing the understanding of every sentient being; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing the many distinct differences among every buddha-land; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing the boundaries of the net of the ten directions; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing every world turned face-down, face-up, and every other way; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing the single nature and the vast abiding of every distinct nature within every dharma; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing every kind of distinct body; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing that every worldly inverted, deluded thought has nothing at all to attach to; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing that every dharma, in the end, transcends through one and the same path; the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing that the tathāgata's own spiritual power can enter the whole dharma-realm; and the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing that the buddha-seed within every sentient being of the three times is never cut off. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, there is no dharma they do not fully penetrate. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of transformation. What are these ten? Namely: transformation regarding every sentient being; transformation regarding every body; transformation regarding every land; transformation regarding every offering; transformation regarding every sound; transformation regarding every practice and vow; transformation regarding teaching and taming every sentient being; transformation regarding every attainment of perfect awakening; transformation regarding every expounding of the dharma; and transformation regarding every empowerment. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they become entirely complete in every unsurpassed dharma of transformation.

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種力持。何等為十？所謂：佛力持；法力持；眾生力持；業力持；行力持；願力持；境界力持；時力持；善力持；智力持。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則於一切法得無上自在力持。大方廣佛華嚴經卷第五十三

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of sustaining power. What are these ten? Namely: the sustaining power of the Buddha; the sustaining power of the dharma; the sustaining power of sentient beings; the sustaining power of karma; the sustaining power of practice; the sustaining power of vow; the sustaining power of domain; the sustaining power of time; the sustaining power of goodness; and the sustaining power of wisdom. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they attain unsurpassed, sovereign sustaining power over every dharma. Fascicle Fifty-Three of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知三世一切眾生佛種不斷出生智慧 — the wisdom that gives rise to further wisdom through knowing that the buddha-seed within every sentient being of the three times is never cut off. Placed as the final entry in the list, this knowledge closes the set on a claim about continuity rather than about any particular skill or domain — fitting, since the list itself is named for wisdom that keeps producing more of itself, mirroring at the level of practice the very unbrokenness it describes in the buddha-seed.
