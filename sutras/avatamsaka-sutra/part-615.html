The Left Side of the Chest Mark, and the Right Hand Begins

Five marks to the left of the chest's svastika complete that cluster — mirroring the four already given to its right — before the catalogue moves outward to the tathāgata's right hand, opening with four marks of its own.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 615 — marks fifty-nine through sixty-seven Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Left of the Auspicious Mark, Then the Right Hand

Where the right side of the chest svastika received three named marks, the left side receives five, closing out the chest cluster before the tathāgata's right hand opens a new region of the body.

Chinese Source

吉祥相左邊有大人相，名：示現光明雲，無數菩薩坐寶蓮華以為莊嚴，放摩尼王種種間錯寶焰光明，普淨一切諸法界海，於中示現無量諸佛，及佛妙音演說諸法，是為五十九。吉祥相左邊復有大人相，名：示現遍法界光明雲，摩尼寶海以為莊嚴，放大光明遍一切剎，於中普現諸菩薩眾，是為六十。吉祥相左邊復有大人相，名：普勝雲，日光明摩尼王寶輪鬘而為莊嚴，放大光焰充滿法界諸世界海，於中示現一切世界、一切如來、一切眾生，是為六十一。吉祥相左邊復有大人相，名：轉法輪妙音雲，一切法燈清淨香蘂以為莊嚴，放大光明充滿法界，於中普現一切諸佛所有相海及以心海，是為六十二。吉祥相左邊復有大人相，名：莊嚴雲，以去、來、今一切佛海而為莊嚴，放淨光明嚴淨一切諸佛國土，於中普現十方一切諸佛菩薩及佛菩薩所行之行，是為六十三。

Ru-Yi Translation

"To the left of the auspicious mark there is a mark of a great being named Cloud of Manifesting Light, adorned with countless bodhisattvas seated upon jeweled lotus flowers, emitting the interlaced jeweled-flame light of every maṇi king, universally purifying every ocean of the dharma-realm, displaying within it measureless buddhas, and the wondrous sound of the buddhas expounding every dharma. This is the fifty-ninth. To the left of the auspicious mark there is further a mark of a great being named Cloud of Light Manifesting Throughout the Dharma-Realm, adorned with an ocean of maṇi jewels, emitting great light that pervades every land, universally manifesting within it every assembly of bodhisattvas. This is the sixtieth. To the left of the auspicious mark there is further a mark of a great being named Cloud of Universal Victory, adorned with a garland of jeweled wheels of sunlit maṇi kings, emitting a great flame of light that fills every ocean of worlds in the dharma-realm, displaying within it every world, every tathāgata, and every sentient being. This is the sixty-first. To the left of the auspicious mark there is further a mark of a great being named Cloud of the Wondrous Sound Turning the Dharma Wheel, adorned with the pure fragrant calyx of every dharma-lamp, emitting great light that fills the dharma-realm, universally manifesting within it the ocean of marks and the ocean of minds possessed by every buddha. This is the sixty-second. To the left of the auspicious mark there is further a mark of a great being named Cloud of Adornment, adorned with the ocean of every buddha of the past, future, and present, emitting pure light that purifies and adorns every buddha-land, universally manifesting within it every buddha and bodhisattva of the ten directions, and the practices carried out by those buddhas and bodhisattvas. This is the sixty-third.

Chinese Source

「如來右手有大人相，名：海照雲，眾寶莊嚴，恒放月焰清淨光明，充滿虛空一切世界，發大音聲歎美一切諸菩薩行，是為六十四。如來右手復有大人相，名：影現照耀雲，以毘瑠璃、帝青、摩尼寶華而為莊嚴，放大光明普照十方菩薩所住蓮華藏、摩尼藏等一切世界，於中悉現無量諸佛，以淨法身坐菩提樹，震動一切十方國土，是為六十五。如來右手復有大人相，名：燈焰鬘普嚴淨雲，毘盧遮那寶以為莊嚴，放大光明成變化網，於中普現諸菩薩眾，咸戴寶冠演諸行海，是為六十六。如來右手復有大人相，名：普現一切摩尼雲，蓮華焰燈而為莊嚴，放海藏光充遍法界，於中普現無量諸佛坐蓮華座，是為六十七。

Ru-Yi Translation

"The tathāgata's right hand has a mark of a great being named Cloud of the Ocean's Illumination, adorned with every jewel, constantly emitting the pure light of a moon-flame, filling every world of empty space, giving voice to a great sound praising every practice of the bodhisattvas. This is the sixty-fourth. The tathāgata's right hand further has a mark of a great being named Cloud Reflecting and Shining Brightly, adorned with vaidūrya, sapphire, and maṇi jeweled flowers, emitting great light that universally illuminates every world where bodhisattvas dwell in the ten directions, including lotus treasuries and maṇi treasuries, displaying within it measureless buddhas, seated beneath the bodhi tree in a pure dharma-body, shaking every land of the ten directions. This is the sixty-fifth. The tathāgata's right hand further has a mark of a great being named Cloud of the Garland of Lamp-Flames Universally Adorning and Purifying, adorned with Vairocana jewels, emitting great light that forms a net of transformations, universally manifesting within it every assembly of bodhisattvas, all wearing jeweled crowns and proclaiming the ocean of practices. This is the sixty-sixth. The tathāgata's right hand further has a mark of a great being named Cloud of Universally Manifesting Every Maṇi, adorned with lotus-flame lamps, emitting the light of an ocean treasury that pervades and fills the dharma-realm, universally manifesting within it measureless buddhas seated upon lotus-flower seats. This is the sixty-seventh.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The left side of the chest's auspicious mark receives five named marks (fifty-nine through sixty-three) against only three on the right side (fifty-four through fifty-six) — an asymmetry in count, though every individual mark still follows the identical formula of naming, adornment, radiance, and vision. The catalogue then leaves the torso behind entirely and moves to the right hand, the first limb named in the sequence since the shoulders.
