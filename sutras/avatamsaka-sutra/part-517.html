Ten Titles, Ten Lights, Ten Things Not Done

Three lists in a row, and the third undoes the first two. Having been called a tathāgata, a buddha, a guide and a lamp, and having obtained ten kinds of radiance, this bodhisattva is then described in terms of what is not done — in body, in speech, in mind, and in the spiritual powers.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 517 — the names, the radiances, the non-doings Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

What Such a One Is Called

Each title is given with the reason it is deserved, and the reasons are all about capacity or service — knowing, being relied on, leading, lighting.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住此三昧，得十種稱讚法之所稱讚。何者為十？所謂：入真如故，名為如來；覺一切法故，名之為佛；為一切世間所稱讚故，名為法師；知一切法故，名一切智；為一切世間所歸依故，名所依處；了達一切法方便故，名為導師；引一切眾生入薩婆若道故，名大導師；為一切世間燈故，名為光明；心志圓滿，義利成就，所作皆辦，住無礙智，分別了知一切諸法故，名為十力自在；通達一切法輪故，名一切見者。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! A bodhisattva mahāsattva abiding in this samādhi is praised with ten praiseworthy dharmas. What are the ten? Namely: because they have entered suchness, they are named a Tathāgata; because they are awake to all dharmas, they are named a buddha; because they are praised by the whole world, they are named a Dharma-master; because they know all dharmas, they are named all-knowing; because the whole world takes refuge in them, they are named a place of reliance; because they penetrate the skillful means of all dharmas, they are named a guide; because they lead all beings into the path of all-knowing wisdom, they are named a great guide; because they are a lamp for the whole world, they are named radiance; because their heart's purpose is fulfilled, their benefit accomplished, all that is to be done done, and because abiding in unobstructed wisdom they discriminate and comprehend all dharmas, they are named sovereign in the ten powers; and because they penetrate every wheel of Dharma, they are named one who sees all. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

入真如故，名為如來 — because they have entered suchness, they are named a Tathāgata. The etymology is being used: 如來, Tathāgata, is "thus-come", and 真如 is suchness. The title is granted on a grammatical rather than a metaphysical ground, which is the chapter's habitual carefulness about naming.

Radiance, and Then Nothing Done

Ten lights, each with what it is for; and then ten sentences in which the same subject is said to do nothing at all.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住此三昧，復得十種光明照耀。何者為十？所謂：得一切諸佛光明，與彼平等故；得一切世界光明，普能嚴淨故；得一切眾生光明，悉往調伏故；得無量無畏光明，法界為場演說故；得無差別光明，知一切法無種種性故；得方便光明，於一切法離欲際而證入故；得真實光明，於一切法離欲際心平等故；得遍一切世間神變光明，蒙佛所加恒不息故；得善思惟光明，到一切佛自在岸故；得一切法真如光明，於一毛孔中善說一切故。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

Child of the Buddha! A bodhisattva mahāsattva abiding in this samādhi further obtains ten illuminating radiances. What are the ten? Namely: they obtain the radiance of all the buddhas, because they are equal with them; the radiance of all worlds, because they can universally adorn and purify them; the radiance of all beings, because they go to all of them and tame them; the radiance of measureless fearlessness, because they take the dharma-realm as their place of expounding; the radiance of non-differentiation, because they know that dharmas have no various natures; the radiance of skillful means, because they realize and enter the limit free of desire in all dharmas; the radiance of what is real, because at the limit free of desire in all dharmas their mind is even; the radiance of transformations pervading every world, because they are supported by the Buddha and never rest; the radiance of good deliberation, because they reach the shore of all the buddhas' sovereign freedom; and the radiance of the suchness of all dharmas, because within a single pore they expound everything well. These are the ten.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住此三昧，復得十種無所作。何者為十？所謂：身業無所作，語業無所作，意業無所作，神通無所作，了法無性無所作，知業不壞無所作，無差別智無所作，無生起智無所作，知法無滅無所作，隨順於文不壞於義無所作。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

Child of the Buddha! A bodhisattva mahāsattva abiding in this samādhi further obtains ten kinds of nothing-done. What are the ten? Namely: nothing done in the works of body; nothing done in the works of speech; nothing done in the works of mind; nothing done in spiritual penetration; nothing done in comprehending that dharmas have no nature; nothing done in knowing that karma is not destroyed; nothing done in undifferentiating wisdom; nothing done in the wisdom of non-origination; nothing done in knowing that dharmas do not cease; and nothing done in according with the letter while not destroying the meaning. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知業不壞無所作 — nothing done in knowing that karma is not destroyed. Among nine items about emptiness and non-origination, this one insists that karma holds. The pairing is deliberate: the eighth ground's 無功用, effortlessness, is being restated here as 無所作, and neither of them means that what people do stops mattering.

隨順於文不壞於義 — according with the letter while not destroying the meaning. The last of the ten, and it describes exactly what a translator does. It is placed among things in which nothing is done, which is to say that following the words faithfully is not an operation performed on the meaning.
