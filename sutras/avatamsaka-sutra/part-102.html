The Ten Dharmas of Non-Negligence

Dharma Wisdom Bodhisattva praises the question and begins his answer with the chapter's first of many lists of ten: the ten dharmas that make up non-negligence, and the ten kinds of purity a bodhisattva who abides in them attains.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 102 — the ten dharmas of non-negligence Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Dharma Wisdom Begins His Answer

Chinese Source

爾時，法慧菩薩告精進慧菩薩言：

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Dharma Wisdom Bodhisattva said to Diligent Wisdom Bodhisattva:

Chinese Source

「善哉！佛子！汝今為欲多所饒益、多所安樂、多所惠利，哀愍世間諸天及人，問於如是菩薩所修清淨之行。佛子！汝住實法，發大精進，增長不退，已得解脫；能作是問，同於如來。諦聽！諦聽！善思念之！我今承佛威神之力，為汝於中說其少分。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Excellent! Excellent, son of the Buddha! You now, wishing to bring much benefit, much peace and happiness, much favor and profit, and out of compassion for the devas and humans of the world, have asked about just such a pure practice as bodhisattvas cultivate. Son of the Buddha, you abide in the true Dharma, having given rise to great diligence, ever increasing and never turning back, having already attained liberation — that you are able to ask such a question is the very same as a Tathāgata himself. Listen closely! Listen closely! Reflect well upon it! I shall now, relying upon the Buddha's own awesome spiritual power, expound for you a small portion of it."

The Ten Dharmas of Non-Negligence

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩已發一切智心，應離癡暗，精勤守護，無令放逸。佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住十種法，名：不放逸。何者為十？一者，護持眾戒；二者，遠離愚癡，淨菩提心；三者，心樂質直，離諸諂誑；四者，勤修善根，無有退轉；五者，恒善思惟，自所發心；六者，不樂親近在家、出家一切凡夫；七者，修諸善業而不願求世間果報；八者，永離二乘，行菩薩道；九者，樂修眾善，令不斷絕；十者，恒善觀察自相續力。佛子！若諸菩薩行此十法，是則名為住不放逸。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva who has already given rise to the mind of all-knowing wisdom should leave behind the darkness of delusion, diligently and vigilantly guarding it, never allowing negligence to arise. Son of the Buddha, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva who abides in ten kinds of dharma is called one who abides in non-negligence. What are the ten? First, upholding and guarding every precept. Second, staying far from foolishness and delusion, purifying the mind of bodhi. Third, delighting in a mind that is honest and upright, leaving behind every flattery and deception. Fourth, diligently cultivating wholesome roots, never turning back. Fifth, forever skillfully reflecting on the very mind they themselves gave rise to. Sixth, taking no delight in growing close to any ordinary person at all, whether householder or renunciant. Seventh, cultivating every wholesome deed while wishing for no worldly recompense in return. Eighth, forever leaving behind the two vehicles, practicing the bodhisattva path. Ninth, delighting in cultivating every wholesome deed, never letting it be interrupted. Tenth, forever skillfully contemplating their own continuous power. Son of the Buddha, if bodhisattvas practice these ten dharmas, this is then called abiding in non-negligence."

The Ten Kinds of Purity That Follow

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住不放逸，得十種清淨。何者為十？一者，如說而行；二者，念智成就；三者，住於深定，不沈不舉；四者，樂求佛法，無有懈息；五者，隨所聞法，如理觀察，具足出生巧妙智慧；六者，入深禪定，得佛神通；七者，其心平等，無有高下；八者，於諸眾生上、中、下類，心無障礙，猶如大地等作利益；九者，若見眾生乃至一發菩提之心，尊重承事猶如和尚；十者，於授戒和尚及阿闍梨、一切菩薩、諸善知識、法師之所，常生尊重，承事供養。佛子！是名菩薩住不放逸十種清淨。佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住不放逸，發大精進；起於正念，生勝欲樂，所行不息；於一切法，心無依處；於甚深法，能勤修習；入無諍門，增廣大心；佛法無邊，能順了知，令諸如來皆悉歡喜。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva who abides in non-negligence attains ten kinds of purity. What are the ten? First, practicing exactly as it has been taught. Second, mindfulness and wisdom brought to accomplishment. Third, abiding in profound concentration, neither sinking nor becoming agitated. Fourth, delighting in seeking the buddha-dharma, without any slackening or rest. Fifth, contemplating in accordance with reason whatever Dharma they have heard, fully bringing forth a skillful and wondrous wisdom. Sixth, entering profound meditative absorption and attaining the Buddha's own spiritual powers. Seventh, a mind that is equal, with nothing high and nothing low. Eighth, toward every living being of the higher, middling, and lower kinds, a mind without any obstruction at all, benefiting them equally, just as the great earth does. Ninth, upon seeing any living being, even one who has given rise to the mind of bodhi only once, revering and attending them just as one would a preceptor. Tenth, toward the preceptor who confers the precepts, and toward every teacher, every bodhisattva, every good spiritual friend, and every Dharma-master, forever giving rise to reverence, attending upon and making offerings to them. Son of the Buddha, this is called the bodhisattva's ten kinds of purity in abiding in non-negligence. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva who abides in non-negligence gives rise to great diligence; arousing right mindfulness, they give rise to a supreme delight, their practice never resting; toward every dharma their mind has no place of reliance at all; toward the most profound dharma they are able to diligently cultivate and practice; entering the gate of contentionlessness, they increase their vast and expansive mind; the buddha-dharma is boundless, and they are able to understand it in accordance, causing every Tathāgata to entirely rejoice."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This chapter's title, "Bright Dharma" (明法), is borne out in its method here: rather than a single sweeping teaching, Dharma Wisdom answers with lists — ten dharmas, then ten more, each building on the last. Non-negligence (不放逸) comes first because it is foundational: everything that follows in the chapter depends on a bodhisattva actually sustaining the vigilance described in this opening list, not merely holding the resolve as an idea.
