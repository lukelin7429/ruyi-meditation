Ten Kinds of Hidden Treasury, Ten Kinds of Discipline

Ten kinds of hidden treasury locate the treasury not in any place but in every dharma itself — a treasury of right reflection, of eloquence, of skillfully understanding that even a kalpa abides like an illusion; ten kinds of discipline then follow, moving from an unbreakable faith toward every buddha to the plain, active work of subduing every māra.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 727 — ten kinds of hidden treasury, ten kinds of discipline Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Treasury Is the Dharma Itself

Every one of the ten hidden treasuries names something already discussed at length elsewhere in this chapter — right reflection, dhāraṇī, eloquence, seeing buddhas, joy and faith — and simply calls it a treasury, hidden in plain sight within the dharma rather than buried anywhere else; the ten kinds of discipline that follow shift from this inward description to outward conduct, closing on the active work of subduing every māra.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種伏藏。何等為十？所謂：知一切法是起功德行藏；知一切法是正思惟藏；知一切法是陀羅尼照明藏；知一切法是辯才開演藏；知一切法是不可說善覺真實藏；知一切佛自在神通是觀察示現藏；知一切法是善巧出生平等藏；知一切法是常見一切諸佛藏；知一切不思議劫是善了皆如幻住藏；知一切諸佛菩薩是發生歡喜淨信藏。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得一切諸佛無上智慧法藏，悉能調伏一切眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of hidden treasury. What are these ten? Namely: knowing that every dharma is a treasury for arousing meritorious practice; knowing that every dharma is a treasury of right reflection; knowing that every dharma is a treasury of dhāraṇī's illumination; knowing that every dharma is a treasury for eloquent exposition; knowing that every dharma is an unspeakable treasury of true, wholesome awakening; knowing that every buddha's own sovereign spiritual power is a treasury of observation and manifestation; knowing that every dharma is a treasury that skillfully brings forth equality; knowing that every dharma is a treasury for constantly seeing every buddha; knowing that every inconceivable kalpa is a treasury of skillfully understanding that all abides exactly like an illusion; and knowing that every buddha and bodhisattva is a treasury for giving rise to joy and pure faith. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the unsurpassed, great treasury of wisdom-dharma of every buddha, and are able to tame every sentient being.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種律儀。何等為十？所謂：於一切佛法不生誹謗律儀；於一切佛所信樂心不可壞律儀；於一切菩薩所起尊重恭敬律儀；於一切善知識所終不捨愛樂心律儀；於一切聲聞、獨覺不生憶念心律儀；遠離一切退菩薩道律儀；不起一切損害眾生心律儀；修一切善根皆令究竟律儀；於一切魔悉能降伏律儀；於一切波羅蜜皆令滿足律儀。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得無上大智律儀。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of discipline. What are these ten? Namely: the discipline of never slandering any buddha-dharma; the discipline of an unbreakable mind of joyful faith toward every buddha; the discipline of giving rise to reverence and respect toward every bodhisattva; the discipline of never abandoning a mind of loving delight toward every good spiritual friend; the discipline of never giving rise to a mind that dwells on becoming a śrāvaka or a solitary awakened one; the discipline of staying far from anything that would cause regression from the bodhisattva path; the discipline of never giving rise to a mind that would harm any sentient being; the discipline of cultivating every root of goodness to its ultimate completion; the discipline of being able to subdue every māra; and the discipline of bringing every pāramitā to complete fulfillment. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the unsurpassed, great discipline of wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知一切不思議劫是善了皆如幻住藏 — knowing that every inconceivable kalpa is a treasury of skillfully understanding that all abides exactly like an illusion. Time itself becomes a treasury here, not because kalpas contain anything hidden, but because grasping their illusion-like nature is itself the wealth a bodhisattva draws from contemplating them.
