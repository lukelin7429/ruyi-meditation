The Sage-Assembly Scatters Flowers on Good-Wealth

Hearing their teacher's words, the whole assembly of forest sages showers Good-Wealth with wondrous flowers, throws themselves down before him, and circles him with reverence, declaring fourteen things this boy will certainly do — from rescuing every being to making the world discern what is perilous and what is easy.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 958 — the sage-assembly scatters flowers on Good-Wealth Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Fourteen Things He Will Certainly Do

Every clause in this prophecy begins the same way — "will certainly" — moving from rescuing beings and closing hell's own gates, through drying up the ocean of craving, to surrounding the world itself with a mountain of merit and revealing it through a Sumeru of wisdom.

Chinese Source

時，諸仙眾聞是語已，各以種種上妙香華散善財上，投身作禮，圍遶恭敬，作如是言：「今此童子，必當救護一切眾生，必當除滅諸地獄苦，必當永斷諸畜生道，必當轉去閻羅王界，必當關閉諸難處門，必當乾竭諸愛欲海，必令眾生永滅苦蘊，必當永破無明黑闇，必當永斷貪愛繫縛，必以福德大輪圍山圍遶世間，必以智慧大寶須彌顯示世間，必當出現清淨智日，必當開示善根法藏，必使世間明識險易。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, having heard these words, the assembly of sages each scattered every kind of supreme, wondrous, fragrant flower over Good-Wealth, threw themselves down in prostration, circled around him with reverence, and spoke these words: "This boy here will certainly rescue and protect every being; will certainly remove and extinguish the suffering of every hell; will certainly forever cut off the path of every animal realm; will certainly turn beings away from the realm of King Yama; will certainly close the gates of every place of difficulty; will certainly dry up the ocean of every craving and desire; will certainly cause every being's own aggregates of suffering to be forever extinguished; will certainly forever break through the black darkness of ignorance; will certainly forever cut the bonds of greed and craving; will certainly surround the world with the great encircling mountain of merit; will certainly reveal the world through the great jeweled Sumeru of wisdom; will certainly cause the pure sun of wisdom to appear; will certainly open up and reveal the dharma-treasury of every root of goodness; and will certainly cause the world to clearly discern what is perilous and what is easy."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

必以福德大輪圍山圍遶世間 — will certainly surround the world with the great encircling mountain of merit. This same great encircling mountain first appeared far earlier in this translation, ringing the Flower-Treasury World-Ocean itself in fascicle eight — here it reappears not as cosmology, but as a metaphor for what one boy's own merit will one day do.
