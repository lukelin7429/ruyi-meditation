Chapter 38, Detachment from the World, Begins

A new chapter opens in the same hall as before — the Hall of Universal Light, at the site of awakening in Magadha — with the Buddha described through a cascade of completed attainments, and an assembly of bodhisattvas gathered from every direction, every one of them already assured of unsurpassed awakening within a single lifetime.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 683 — chapter 38 opens, fascicle 53 begins Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

An Assembly Already Assured of Awakening

Chapter thirty-eight opens not with a question but with description: first the Buddha himself, catalogued through attainment after attainment, and then the assembly around him — bodhisattvas gathered from every land, each one already guaranteed unsurpassed awakening within a single lifetime, and each fully endowed with a long list of skillful abilities that reads almost as a preview of the entire chapter to come.

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊在摩竭提國阿蘭若法菩提場中普光明殿，坐蓮華藏師子之座，妙悟皆滿，二行永絕，達無相法；住於佛住，得佛平等，到無障處不可轉法；所行無礙，立不思議，普見三世；身恒充遍一切國土，智恒明達一切諸法；了一切行，盡一切疑，無能測身；一切菩薩等所求智，到佛無二究竟彼岸，具足如來平等解脫，證無中邊佛平等地，盡於法界等虛空界。與不可說百千億那由他佛剎微塵數菩薩摩訶薩俱，皆一生當得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，各從他方種種國土而共來集，悉具菩薩方便智慧。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the World-Honored One was in the country of Magadha, in the forest hermitage called Dharma, at the site of awakening, within the Hall of Universal Light, seated upon the lion-throne of the lotus treasury. His wondrous awakening was entirely complete; the two paths were forever cut off; he had penetrated the dharma of no mark; he abode in the abode of a buddha, attained the equality of a buddha, and had reached the unobstructed place, the dharma that cannot be turned back. His every action was unobstructed; he stood in the inconceivable; he universally beheld the three times; his body forever filled and pervaded every land; his wisdom forever illuminated and comprehended every dharma. He fully understood every practice, exhausted every doubt, and had a body no one could fathom; the very wisdom that every bodhisattva seeks, he had reached the ultimate, undivided far shore of the Buddha; he was fully endowed with the tathāgata's own equal liberation; he had realized the buddha-ground of equality without any middle or edge, reaching to the very limit of the dharma-realm, equal to the realm of empty space itself. He was accompanied by bodhisattva-mahāsattvas as numerous as the dust-motes of unspeakably many hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of buddha-lands, every one of whom would attain unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening within a single lifetime; they had each come together from every kind of land in every other direction, and every one of them was fully endowed with a bodhisattva's skillful means and wisdom.

Chinese Source

所謂：善能觀察一切眾生，以方便力，令其調伏，住菩薩法；善能觀察一切世界，以方便力，普皆往詣；善能觀察涅槃境界，思惟籌量永離一切戲論分別，而修妙行無有間斷；善能攝受一切眾生，善入無量諸方便法，知諸眾生空無所有而不壞業果；善知眾生心使、諸根境界方便種種差別；悉能受持三世佛法，自得解了，復為他說；於世、出世無量諸法，皆善安住，知其真實；於有為、無為一切諸法，悉善觀察，知無有二；於一念中，悉能獲得三世諸佛所有智慧；於念念中，悉能示現成等正覺，令一切眾生發心成道；於一眾生心之所緣，悉知一切眾生境界；雖入如來一切智地，而不捨菩薩行諸所作業，智慧方便而無所作；為一一眾生住無量劫，而於阿僧祇劫難可值遇，轉正法輪調伏眾生皆不唐捐，三世諸佛清淨行願悉已具足；成就如是無量功德，一切如來於無邊劫說不可盡。

Ru-Yi Translation

Namely: they were skillfully able to observe every sentient being, and through the power of skillful means, brought them to be tamed and made them abide in the bodhisattva dharma; they were skillfully able to observe every world, and through the power of skillful means, universally journeyed to each and every one; they were skillfully able to observe the domain of nirvāṇa, reflecting and considering it while forever leaving behind every idle discourse and discrimination, and cultivated wondrous practice without any interruption at all; they were skillfully able to gather in every sentient being, skillfully entering measureless gateways of skillful means, and knew that every sentient being is empty and has nothing at all, and yet never destroyed the fruits of karma; they skillfully knew every sentient being's mental afflictions, every faculty and domain, and every kind of distinct skillful means; they were entirely able to uphold the dharma of every buddha of the three times, understanding it themselves and expounding it again for others; in every measureless worldly and transcendent dharma, they were all well established, knowing its true reality; in every conditioned and unconditioned dharma, they observed it all skillfully, knowing it to be without duality; within a single thought, they were entirely able to obtain the wisdom possessed by every buddha of the three times; in thought after thought, they were entirely able to manifest the attainment of perfect awakening, causing every sentient being to give rise to the resolve and accomplish the path; within the object of a single sentient being's mind, they entirely knew the domain of every sentient being; though they had entered the tathāgata's own ground of all-knowing wisdom, they never abandoned every activity of bodhisattva practice, acting through wisdom and skillful means without any contrived action at all; for the sake of each and every sentient being they abided for measureless kalpas, and though difficult to encounter even across asaṃkhyeya kalpas, they turned the wheel of the true dharma and tamed sentient beings, never wasting the effort; they were entirely complete with the pure practices and vows of every buddha of the three times; having accomplished measureless merit such as this, every tathāgata, even across boundless kalpas, could never finish describing it in full.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

雖入如來一切智地，而不捨菩薩行諸所作業，智慧方便而無所作 — though they had entered the tathāgata's own ground of all-knowing wisdom, they never abandoned every activity of bodhisattva practice, acting through wisdom and skillful means without any contrived action at all. The list quietly refuses to let its bodhisattvas graduate out of ordinary work: even having reached a buddha's own wisdom, they keep doing bodhisattva activity, and the text's real point is that this is not a contradiction — acting "without any contrived action" describes how the doing itself has changed, not that the doing has stopped.
