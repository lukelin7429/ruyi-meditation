Enlightened Wisdom's Own Praise

Before answering Good-Wealth's own question, Enlightened Wisdom praises the mind of bodhi itself as something rare beyond measure, names ten things that follow for anyone who truly seeks a good spiritual friend, and then reveals that his own vast assembly of attendants all reached that same mind through him.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1005 — Enlightened Wisdom's own praise Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Things That Follow From Seeking a Good Spiritual Friend

Enlightened Wisdom's own praise moves quickly from bodhicitta itself to what it makes possible — ten distinct qualities, each a different way of never wearying, never abandoning, and never growing lax in the pursuit of a good spiritual friend.

Chinese Source

居士告言：「善哉善哉！善男子！汝乃能發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心。「善男子！發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，是人難得。若能發心，是人則能求菩薩行，值遇善知識恒無厭足，親近善知識恒無勞倦，供養善知識恒不疲懈，給侍善知識不生憂慼，求覓善知識終不退轉，愛念善知識終不放捨，承事善知識無暫休息，瞻仰善知識無時憩止，行善知識教未曾怠惰，稟善知識心無有誤失。

Ru-Yi Translation

The householder said: "Excellent, excellent! Good son, you have indeed been able to give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Good son, giving rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi — such a person is difficult to find. If one is able to give rise to this mind, such a person is then able to seek the bodhisattva practices, encountering good spiritual friends forever without weariness or satiation, drawing near to good spiritual friends forever without fatigue or weariness, making offerings to good spiritual friends forever without tiring or growing lax, attending and serving good spiritual friends without ever giving rise to sorrow, seeking out good spiritual friends never regressing, cherishing good spiritual friends never abandoning them, attending and serving good spiritual friends without a moment's rest, gazing up at good spiritual friends without ever pausing, practicing good spiritual friends' own teaching, never once growing lazy, and receiving good spiritual friends' own mind without any error or fault.

An Entire Assembly, All Reached Through Him

Enlightened Wisdom now points to his own gathered attendants — the same ten thousand Good-Wealth already saw surrounding his throne — and reveals that every one of them owes their own mind of bodhi, their own rebirth into the tathāgata's family, to him alone.

Chinese Source

「善男子！汝見我此眾會人不？」善財答言：「唯然！已見。」居士言：「善男子！我已令其發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，生如來家，增長白法，安住無量諸波羅蜜，學佛十力，離世間種，住如來種，棄生死輪，轉正法輪，滅三惡趣，住正法趣，如諸菩薩悉能救護一切眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, have you seen this assembly of people around me?" Good-Wealth replied: "Yes indeed! I have seen them." The householder said: "Good son, I have already caused them to give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, to be born into the tathāgata's own family, to grow in wholesome dharmas, to abide securely in every measureless pāramitā, to study the buddha's own ten powers, to leave behind the seed of the world, to abide in the seed of the tathāgata, to abandon the wheel of birth and death, to turn the wheel of the true dharma, to extinguish the three evil destinies, and to abide in the destiny of the true dharma, so that, like every bodhisattva, they are entirely able to rescue and protect every being."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

我已令其發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心 — I have already caused them to give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. The ten thousand attendants Good-Wealth admired for their own splendor back in Part 1003 turn out to be Enlightened Wisdom's own students — every ornament and every note of music surrounding his throne was, in effect, already evidence of a teaching already given.
