She Is None Other Than Myself

The forest-spirit's answer reaches back to an ancient world's very first buddha, a queen named Joyful Light who gave birth beneath a tree exactly as Māyā just did, and a wet-nurse who received the newborn bodhisattva and attained a liberation on the spot — revealed, at the story's end, to be the forest-spirit herself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1192 — fascicle 74 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

She Is None Other Than Myself

Asked how long she has held this liberation, the forest-spirit answers with a story of her own — an ancient world, a queen who gave birth just as Māyā did, and a wet-nurse whose identity closes the account.

Chinese Source

乃往古世，過億佛剎微塵數劫，復過是數。時，有世界名為：普寶，劫名：悅樂，八十那由他佛於中出現；其第一佛，名：自在功德幢，十號具足。彼世界中，有四天下，名：妙光莊嚴；其四天下閻浮提中，有一王都，名：須彌莊嚴幢；其中有王，名：寶焰眼；其王夫人，名曰：喜光。善男子！如此世界摩耶夫人，為毘盧遮那如來之母；彼世界中喜光夫人，為初佛母，亦復如是。「善男子！其喜光夫人將欲誕生菩薩之時，與二十億那由他采女詣金華園；園中有樓，名：妙寶峯；其邊有樹，名：一切施。喜光夫人攀彼樹枝而生菩薩，諸天王眾各持香水共以洗沐。時，有乳母名為：淨光，侍立其側。既洗沐已，諸天王眾授與乳母。乳母敬受，生大歡喜，即得菩薩普眼三昧；得此三昧已，普見十方無量諸佛，復得菩薩於一切處示現受生自在解脫。如初受胎識，速疾無礙；得此解脫故，見一切佛乘本願力受生自在，亦復如是。善男子！於汝意云何？彼乳母者，豈異人乎？我身是也。我從是來，念念常見毘盧遮那佛示現菩薩受生海調伏眾生自在神力。如見毘盧遮那佛乘本願力，念念於此三千大千，乃至十方一切世界微塵之內，皆現菩薩受生神變；見一切佛悉亦如是，我皆恭敬承事供養，聽所說法，如說修行。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"In an age long past, beyond kalpas as many as the fine motes of dust in a koṭi of buddha-lands, and beyond that number still, there was then a world named Universal Jewel, and a kalpa named Joyful Delight; eighty nayutas of buddhas arose within it, and the very first of them was named Sovereign Power's Banner of Merit, complete in all ten epithets. Within that world there was a fourfold continent named Wondrous Light Adornment; and within the Jambudvīpa of that fourfold continent, there was a royal capital named Sumeru's Adorning Banner; within it there was a king named Jewel-Flame Eye, and that king's consort was named Joyful Light. Good son, just as, in this world, Māyā is the mother of the tathāgata Vairocana, so too, in that world, Queen Joyful Light was likewise the mother of that first buddha. "Good son, when Queen Joyful Light was about to give birth to that bodhisattva, she went, together with twenty koṭis of nayutas of court ladies, to the Golden Flower Garden; within that garden there was a tower named Wondrous Jewel Peak, and beside it there stood a tree named All-Giving. Queen Joyful Light took hold of a branch of that tree and gave birth to the bodhisattva, and the kings of the gods each took fragrant water and together bathed him. At that time, there was a wet-nurse named Pure Light, standing attendant at her side. Once the bathing was complete, the kings of the gods handed the child to that wet-nurse. The wet-nurse reverently received him, and, giving rise to great joy, at once attained the bodhisattva's own samādhi of the universal eye; having attained this samādhi, she universally beheld measureless buddhas throughout the ten directions, and further attained the bodhisattva's own liberation of manifesting sovereign birth in every place. Just as the first consciousness entering the womb is swift and unobstructed, so too, because she had attained this liberation, she saw every buddha, sustained by the power of his own original vow, take sovereign birth in just the same way. "Good son, what do you think? Could that wet-nurse be anyone else? She is none other than myself. From that time on, in thought after thought, I have forever beheld the tathāgata Vairocana manifesting the ocean of a bodhisattva's own birth and the sovereign spiritual power that tames beings. Just as I see the tathāgata Vairocana, sustained by the power of his own original vow, in thought after thought manifesting a bodhisattva's own spiritual marvels of birth throughout this great trichiliocosm and even within every mote of dust of every world in the ten directions, so too do I see every buddha, in just the same way; and I reverently serve and make offerings to every one of them, hearing the dharma they expound, and cultivating in accordance with what they teach."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

喜光夫人攀彼樹枝而生菩薩 — Queen Joyful Light took hold of a branch of that tree and gave birth to the bodhisattva. The ancient world's story repeats the present one almost exactly — a queen giving birth beneath a tree while grasping its branch, gods bathing the newborn, a wet-nurse standing ready — down to the detail, stated outright two sentences earlier, that this queen played the identical role Māyā herself now plays. The forest-spirit answers "how long" not with a number of kalpas but with a second birth-story built as a mirror of the first.
