Ten Kinds of Buddha-Deeds

Nine brief entries move from guiding beings according to the right moment to protecting a sentient being of immature faculties from a teaching not yet fit for them. The tenth entry expands into an eleven-fold chain of paradoxes — knowing yet still doing, understanding yet still acting — that together define what it means to carry expedient means and true realization forward side by side.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 784 — ten kinds of buddha-deeds Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Expedient and True, Practiced Together

The tenth buddha-deed unfolds as eleven successive "though...still" clauses: though knowing every dharma is unfashioned, still not abandoning any dharma's own mark; though knowing the dharma-realm is unmoving, still manifesting every transformation; though knowing no dharma can be spoken, still turning the wheel of dharma. Each clause holds insight and action together without letting either cancel the other.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種佛業。何等為十？所謂：隨時開導，是佛業；令正修行故。夢中令見，是佛業；覺昔善根故。為他演說所未聞經，是佛業；令生智斷疑故。為悔纏所纏者說出離法，是佛業；令離疑心故。若有眾生起慳吝心，乃至惡慧心、二乘心、損害心、疑惑心、散動心、憍慢心，為現如來眾相莊嚴身，是佛業；生長過去善根故。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of buddha-deeds. What are these ten? Namely: guiding beings according to the right moment — this is a buddha-deed, because it causes right practice. Causing them to see in a dream — this is a buddha-deed, because it awakens roots of goodness planted in the past. Expounding for another a scripture they have never before heard — this is a buddha-deed, because it gives rise to wisdom and cuts off doubt. Teaching the dharma of renunciation to one bound by the fetter of regret — this is a buddha-deed, because it causes them to leave behind a doubting mind. If a sentient being gives rise to a mind of stinginess, or even a mind of corrupt wisdom, a mind fixed on the two lesser vehicles, a mind of harm, a mind of doubt, a scattered mind, or a mind of arrogance, manifesting for them the tathāgata's own body adorned with every excellent mark — this is a buddha-deed, because it causes roots of goodness planted in the past to grow.

Chinese Source

於正法難遇時，廣為說法，令其聞已，得陀羅尼智、神通智，普能利益無量眾生，是佛業；勝解清淨故。若有魔事起，能以方便現虛空界等聲，說不損惱他法以為對治，令其開悟，眾魔聞已威光歇滅，是佛業；志樂殊勝，威德大故。其心無間，常自守護，不令證入二乘正位，若有眾生根性未熟，終不為說解脫境界，是佛業；本願所作故。生死結漏一切皆離，修菩薩行相續不斷，以大悲心攝取眾生，令其起行究竟解脫，是佛業；不斷修行菩薩行故。菩薩摩訶薩了達自身及以眾生本來寂滅不驚不怖而勤修福智無有厭足，雖知一切法無有造作而亦不捨諸法自相，雖於諸境界永離貪欲而常樂瞻奉諸佛色身，雖知不由他悟入於法而種種方便求一切智，雖知諸國土皆如虛空而常樂莊嚴一切佛剎，雖恒觀察無人無我而教化眾生無有疲厭，雖於法界本來不動而以神通智力現眾變化，雖已成就一切智智而修菩薩行無有休息，雖知諸法不可言說而轉淨法輪令眾心喜，雖能示現諸佛神力而不厭捨菩薩之身，雖現入於大般涅槃而一切處示現受生，能作如是權實雙行法，是佛業。是為十。若諸菩薩安住其中，則得不由他教無上無師廣大業。

Ru-Yi Translation

At a time when the true dharma is difficult to encounter, widely expounding the dharma so that, having heard it, they attain the wisdom of dhāraṇī and the wisdom of spiritual superknowledge, universally able to benefit measureless sentient beings — this is a buddha-deed, because its resolute understanding is pure. If any affair of māra arises, being able through skillful means to manifest a sound equal to the realm of empty space, expounding a dharma that harms no one as its antidote, causing them to awaken, so that every māra, upon hearing it, has its awesome light extinguished — this is a buddha-deed, because its aspiration is supremely excellent and its awesome virtue is great. The mind never interrupted, constantly guarding itself, never allowing entry into the fixed stage of the two lesser vehicles; if a sentient being's own faculties are not yet mature, never in the end teaching them the domain of liberation — this is a buddha-deed, because it is what the original vow has brought about. Being entirely free of every fetter and outflow of birth-and-death, cultivating bodhisattva practice in unbroken continuity, gathering in sentient beings with a mind of great compassion, causing them to give rise to practice and reach ultimate liberation — this is a buddha-deed, because it never interrupts the cultivation of bodhisattva practice. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva fully understands that both their own self and every sentient being are, from the very root, still and extinguished, without alarm and without fear, and yet diligently cultivates merit and wisdom without ever growing weary; though knowing that no dharma is fashioned by any maker, still does not abandon the own-mark of any dharma; though forever free of craving toward every object, still constantly delights in beholding and honoring every buddha's own form-body; though knowing that awakening into the dharma comes not from another's teaching, still seeks all-knowing wisdom through every kind of skillful means; though knowing every land is exactly like empty space, still constantly delights in adorning every buddha-land; though constantly observing that there is no person and no self, still teaches and transforms sentient beings without ever growing weary; though the dharma-realm is, from the very root, unmoving, still manifests every kind of transformation through the power of spiritual superknowledge and wisdom; though already accomplished in the wisdom of all-knowing wisdom, still cultivates bodhisattva practice without ever resting; though knowing that no dharma can be spoken in words, still turns the pure wheel of dharma, causing the minds of the multitude to rejoice; though able to manifest the spiritual power of every buddha, still does not weary of or abandon the bodhisattva's own body; and though manifesting entry into great parinirvāṇa, still manifests taking rebirth in every place — being able to carry out this dharma of expedient means and true realization practiced together in this way, this too is a buddha-deed. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within these, they attain the unsurpassed, teacherless, vast deed, without needing to be taught by another.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

能作如是權實雙行法，是佛業 — being able to carry out this dharma of expedient means and true realization practiced together in this way, this too is a buddha-deed. The eleven paradoxes leading to this line never resolve their own tension: each "though...still" clause keeps both halves standing, insight into emptiness and continued action in the world, refusing to let the deeper truth cancel the practical deed or the deed obscure the truth.
