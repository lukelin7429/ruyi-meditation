Rarer Still

Maitreya names how rare it is merely to arouse the mind of bodhi — and rarer still to sustain it — before turning to Good-Wealth directly: what takes every other bodhisattva countless kalpas, he has already accomplished within a single lifetime.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1289 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Rarer Still

A chain of "rarer still" qualifications opens this Part; a direct comparison to every other bodhisattva's own countless kalpas follows; and Maitreya turns at last to address Good-Wealth by name, setting up the question he is about to answer.

Chinese Source

「諸仁者！若有眾生能發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，是為希有；若發心已，又能如是精進方便集諸佛法，倍為希有；又能如是求菩薩道，又能如是淨菩薩行，又能如是事善知識，又能如是如救頭然，又能如是順知識教，又能如是堅固修行，又能如是集菩提分，又能如是不求一切名聞利養，又能如是不捨菩薩純一之心，又能如是不樂家宅、不著欲樂、不戀父母親戚知識，但樂追求菩薩伴侶，又能如是不顧身命，唯願勤修一切智道，應知展轉倍更難得。

Ru-Yi Translation

Noble ones! If there is a sentient being able to arouse the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, this is already rare indeed; and if, having aroused this mind, one is also able, with diligence and skillful means, to gather every buddha-dharma in just this way, this is twice as rare; and if one is also able in just this way to seek the bodhisattva path, to purify the bodhisattva practices, to serve a good spiritual friend, to act as though putting out a fire upon one's own head, to accord with a spiritual friend's own teaching, to cultivate practice with firm resolve, to gather the factors of bodhi, to seek no fame or worldly gain whatsoever, to never abandon the bodhisattva's own single-minded resolve, to take no delight in house or home, to hold no attachment to the pleasures of desire, to feel no longing for parents, relatives, or acquaintances, but only to delight in seeking out bodhisattva companions, and to have no regard for one's own body or life, wishing only to diligently cultivate the path of all-knowing wisdom — know that, turn after turn, this becomes rarer and rarer still.

Chinese Source

「諸仁者！餘諸菩薩經於無量百千萬億那由他劫，乃能滿足菩薩願行，乃能親近諸佛菩提；此長者子，於一生內，則能淨佛剎，則能化眾生，則能以智慧深入法界，則能成就諸波羅蜜，則能增廣一切諸行，則能圓滿一切大願，則能超出一切魔業，則能承事一切善友，則能清淨諸菩薩道，則能具足普賢諸行。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Noble ones! Every other bodhisattva must pass through measureless hundreds of thousands of millions of koṭis of nayutas of kalpas before being able to fulfill the bodhisattva's own vows and practices, before being able to draw near to the bodhi of every buddha; yet this elder's son, within a single lifetime, is already able to purify buddha-lands, is already able to transform sentient beings, is already able through wisdom to enter deeply into the dharma-realm, is already able to accomplish every pāramitā, is already able to broaden and increase every practice, is already able to bring every great vow to fulfillment, is already able to transcend every deed of māra, is already able to serve every good friend, is already able to purify every bodhisattva path, and is already able to become endowed with every practice of Samantabhadra."

Chinese Source

爾時，彌勒菩薩摩訶薩如是稱歎善財童子種種功德，令無量百千眾生發菩提心已，告善財言：「善哉善哉！善男子！汝為饒益一切世間，汝為救護一切眾生，汝為勤求一切佛法故，發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心。「善男子！汝獲善利，汝善得人身，汝善住壽命，汝善值如來出現，汝善見文殊師利大善知識。汝身是善器，為諸善根之所潤澤。汝為白法之所資持，所有解欲悉已清淨，已為諸佛共所護念，已為善友共所攝受。何以故？

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, having praised Good-Wealth's various merits in this way and caused measureless hundreds of thousands of sentient beings to arouse the mind of bodhi, spoke to Good-Wealth, saying: "Excellent indeed, excellent indeed! Good son! For the sake of benefiting the entire world, for the sake of rescuing and protecting every sentient being, and for the sake of diligently seeking every buddha-dharma, you have aroused the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. Good son! You have gained excellent benefit; you have excellently attained a human body; you excellently abide in your own lifespan; you have excellently encountered a tathāgata's own appearance in the world; and you have excellently beheld Mañjuśrī, the great good spiritual friend. Your own body is a wholesome vessel, moistened and enriched by every wholesome root. You are sustained by every white and pure dharma; every desire for understanding you hold has already been purified; you have already been guarded and minded by every buddha together; and you have already been gathered in by every good friend together. Why is this?

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

是為希有⋯倍為希有⋯應知展轉倍更難得 — the passage builds rarity upon rarity: merely arousing bodhicitta is already rare, sustaining it with the listed qualities is "twice as rare," and the whole chain closes on 展轉倍更難得 ("turn after turn, rarer and rarer still") — the source's own words, not an added rhetorical flourish.

Maitreya's closing question 何以故？("Why is this?") sets up what comes next: the famous extended simile for the mind of bodhi itself, comparing it in turn to a seed, a field, the great earth, water, wind, fire, the sun, and dozens more — beginning in Part 1290.
