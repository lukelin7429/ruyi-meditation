Like a Great River

The chain of similes for the mind of bodhi continues through a river, a nāga king, nectar, a net, a lasso, and a run of medicines and jewels — lapis lazuli, an indranīla gem, Jambu-river gold — thirty-seven more comparisons in all.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1292 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Like a Great River

Thirty-seven more similes continue the unbroken chain — a bed, a hidden treasury, a gushing spring, a bright mirror, a great river, a nāga king's own rain, nectar, a net, a lasso, medicines, and finally a run of jewels ending on the king of mountains.

Chinese Source

菩提心者，猶如臥具，息除生死諸勞苦故；菩提心者，如善知識，能解一切生死縛故；菩提心者，如好珍財，能除一切貧窮事故；菩提心者，如大導師，善知菩薩出要道故；菩提心者，猶如伏藏，出功德財無匱乏故；菩提心者，猶如涌泉，生智慧水無窮盡故；菩提心者，猶如明鏡，普現一切法門像故；菩提心者，猶如蓮華，不染一切諸罪垢故；菩提心者，猶如大河，流引一切度攝法故；菩提心者，如大龍王，能雨一切妙法雨故；菩提心者，猶如命根，任持菩薩大悲身故；菩提心者，猶如甘露，能令安住不死界故；菩提心者，猶如大網，普攝一切諸眾生故；菩提心者，猶如羂索，攝取一切所應化故；菩提心者，猶如鉤餌，出有淵中所居者故；菩提心者，如阿伽陀藥，能令無病永安隱故；菩提心者，如除毒藥，悉能消歇貪愛毒故；菩提心者，如善持呪，能除一切顛倒毒故；菩提心者，猶如疾風，能卷一切諸障霧故；菩提心者，如大寶洲，出生一切覺分寶故；菩提心者，如好種性，出生一切白淨法故；菩提心者，猶如住宅，諸功德法所依處故；菩提心者，猶如市肆，菩薩商人貿易處故；菩提心者，如鍊金藥，能治一切煩惱垢故；菩提心者，猶如好蜜，圓滿一切功德味故；菩提心者，猶如正道，令諸菩薩入智城故；菩提心者，猶如好器，能持一切白淨法故；菩提心者，猶如時雨，能滅一切煩惱塵故；菩提心者，則為住處，一切菩薩所住處故；菩提心者，則為壽行，不取聲聞解脫果故；菩提心者，如淨瑠璃，自性明潔無諸垢故；菩提心者，如帝青寶，出過世間二乘智故；菩提心者，如更漏鼓，覺諸眾生煩惱睡故；菩提心者，如清淨水，性本澄潔無垢濁故；菩提心者，如閻浮金，映奪一切有為善故；菩提心者，如大山王，超出一切諸世間故；菩提心者，則為所歸，不拒一切諸來者故；

Ru-Yi Translation

The mind of bodhi is like a bed, since it brings rest from the toil and hardship of birth-and-death; the mind of bodhi is like a good spiritual friend, since it is able to loosen every bond of birth-and-death; the mind of bodhi is like fine treasure, since it removes every matter of poverty; the mind of bodhi is like a great guide, since it well knows the essential path by which a bodhisattva departs; the mind of bodhi is like a hidden treasury, since it brings forth the wealth of merit without ever running short; the mind of bodhi is like a gushing spring, since it produces the water of wisdom without end; the mind of bodhi is like a bright mirror, since it universally manifests the image of every dharma-gate; the mind of bodhi is like a lotus flower, since it is unstained by every fault and defilement; the mind of bodhi is like a great river, since it flows and draws along every dharma of crossing and gathering; the mind of bodhi is like a great nāga king, since it rains down the rain of every wondrous dharma; the mind of bodhi is like the life-faculty itself, since it sustains and upholds a bodhisattva's own body of great compassion; the mind of bodhi is like sweet nectar, since it allows one to abide securely in the realm free of death; the mind of bodhi is like a great net, since it universally gathers in every sentient being; the mind of bodhi is like a lasso, since it draws in every being fit to be transformed; the mind of bodhi is like a hook and bait, since it draws out those dwelling within the depths of existence; the mind of bodhi is like the agada medicine, since it grants freedom from every sickness and lasting peace and security; the mind of bodhi is like a poison-removing medicine, since it is entirely able to dissolve away the poison of craving and desire; the mind of bodhi is like a well-held mantra, since it removes every poison of inverted views; the mind of bodhi is like a swift wind, since it sweeps away every fog of obstruction; the mind of bodhi is like a great jeweled isle, since it gives rise to every jewel of the factors of awakening; the mind of bodhi is like a fine lineage, since it gives rise to every white and pure dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a dwelling, since it is the very place every meritorious dharma relies upon; the mind of bodhi is like a marketplace, since it is the very place where bodhisattva merchants trade; the mind of bodhi is like a gold-refining elixir, since it treats every stain of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like fine honey, since it perfects the flavor of every merit; the mind of bodhi is like the right path, since it causes every bodhisattva to enter the city of wisdom; the mind of bodhi is like a fine vessel, since it holds every white and pure dharma; the mind of bodhi is like timely rain, since it settles every dust of affliction; the mind of bodhi becomes a dwelling-place, since it is the very dwelling-place of every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi becomes a life-sustaining practice, since it does not take up a śrāvaka's own fruit of liberation; the mind of bodhi is like pure lapis lazuli, since its own nature is bright and clean, without any stain; the mind of bodhi is like an indranīla jewel, since it surpasses the wisdom of the world's two vehicles; the mind of bodhi is like a watch-drum marking the hours, since it awakens every sentient being from the sleep of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like pure water, since its nature is originally clear and clean, without any turbidity; the mind of bodhi is like Jambu-river gold, since its brilliance outshines every conditioned goodness; the mind of bodhi is like the king of great mountains, since it rises above every world; the mind of bodhi becomes a refuge, since it turns away none who come to it;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Thirty-seven similes in this Part, counted directly against the source text by splitting on every 「；」, continuing the same unbroken quotation without a break. Jambu-river gold (閻浮金) and the indranīla jewel (帝青寶) both follow this site's own established renderings, confirmed directly against their prior use elsewhere in this translation.
