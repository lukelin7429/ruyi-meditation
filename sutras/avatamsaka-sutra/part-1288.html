A Great Dharma-Ship

Maitreya names, in one unbroken run of twenty-one similes, everything Good-Wealth has already become for suffering beings — a ship for those adrift, a bridge for those drowning, a lamp for those benighted in darkness — before turning to his own unbroken diligence.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1288 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Great Dharma-Ship

Twenty-one similes, each its own complete image of rescue, open this Part; a shorter list of ten unbroken practices — never resting, never weary, never satisfied — brings it to a close.

Chinese Source

「諸仁者！此長者子，為被四流漂泊者，造大法船；為被見泥沒溺者，立大法橋；為被癡暗昏迷者，然大智燈；為行生死曠野者，開示聖道；為嬰煩惱重病者，調和法藥；為遭生、老、死苦者，飲以甘露，令其安隱；為入貪、恚、癡火者，沃以定水，使得清涼；多憂惱者，慰諭使安；繫有獄者，曉誨令出；入見網者，開以智劍；住界城者，示諸脫門；在險難者，導安隱處；懼結賊者，與無畏法；墮惡趣者，授慈悲手；拘害蘊者，示涅槃城；界蛇所纏，解以聖道；著於六處空聚落者，以智慧光引之令出；住邪濟者，令入正濟；近惡友者，示其善友；樂凡法者，誨以聖法；著生死者，令其趣入一切智城。

Ru-Yi Translation

Noble ones! This elder's son, for those adrift in the four floods, builds a great dharma-ship; for those sunk and drowning in the mire of wrong view, raises a great dharma-bridge; for those benighted and confused in the darkness of delusion, kindles a great lamp of wisdom; for those traveling the wilderness of birth-and-death, reveals the noble path; for those afflicted with the grave sickness of affliction, blends the medicine of the dharma; for those meeting the suffering of birth, old age, and death, gives them sweet nectar to drink, bringing them peace and security; for those who have entered the fire of craving, anger, and delusion, pours upon them the water of samādhi, bringing them coolness; for those with much sorrow and vexation, consoles and comforts them into peace; for those bound within the prison of existence, instructs and guides them out; for those caught in the net of wrong view, opens a way with the sword of wisdom; for those dwelling in the city of the elements, shows every gate of liberation; for those in peril, guides them to a place of peace and security; for those who fear the bandit of the fetters, gives them the dharma of fearlessness; for those fallen into an evil destiny, extends a hand of loving-kindness and compassion; for those confined and harmed by the aggregates, shows the city of nirvāṇa; for those entwined by the serpent of the elements, frees them with the noble path; for those attached to the empty village of the six sense-bases, leads them out with the light of wisdom; for those dwelling upon a deviant crossing, causes them to enter the right crossing; for those who draw near to bad friends, shows them a good friend; for those who delight in ordinary dharmas, instructs them with the noble dharma; and for those attached to birth-and-death, causes them to enter the city of all-knowing wisdom.

Chinese Source

「諸仁者！此長者子，恒以此行救護眾生，發菩提心未甞休息，求大乘道曾無懈倦，飲諸法水不生厭足，恒勤積集助道之行，常樂清淨一切法門，修菩薩行不捨精進，成滿諸願善行方便，見善知識情無厭足，事善知識身不疲懈，聞善知識所有教誨常樂順行未曾違逆。

Ru-Yi Translation

Noble ones! This elder's son, constantly rescuing and protecting sentient beings through practices such as these, has never once rested from arousing the mind of bodhi; has never once grown weary in seeking the path of the great vehicle; is never satisfied drinking from the waters of the dharma; is forever diligent in gathering practices that aid the path; constantly delights in purifying every dharma-gate; cultivates the bodhisattva practices without abandoning diligence; brings every vow to fulfillment through skillful, wholesome practice; is never satisfied in feeling toward a good spiritual friend; never grows weary in body serving a good spiritual friend; and, hearing whatever teaching a good spiritual friend gives, constantly delights in following it and has never once gone against it.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

為被四流漂泊者，造大法船 — twenty-one similes in all, counted directly against the source text by splitting on every clause boundary, each pairing one kind of suffering with one image of rescue: a ship, a bridge, a lamp, medicine, nectar, cool water, a sword, a hand, a city. The list closes on the same 城 ("city") image that opened Good-Wealth's own pilgrimage — 福城, the City of Blessings — now transformed into 一切智城, the city of all-knowing wisdom.
