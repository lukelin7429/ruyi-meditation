The Mirror That Reflects Every World

The drum reaches for its clearest image yet — a mirror vast enough to hold ten world-systems, in which every reflection appears without ever entering or leaving — to teach that karmic results arise and are seen without any true coming or going; hearing this, sons of heaven beyond counting attain forbearance, and heavenly women renounce the very notion of female form.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 623 — the mirror simile and its fruits Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Neither Coming Into the Mirror Nor Leaving It

The drum multiplies its similes once more — sound that neither arises nor ceases, a mirror large enough to hold ten worlds, a magician's illusion — before naming what recognizing all of this truly is: repentance itself.

Chinese Source

諸天子！如我天鼓，說業、說報、說行、說戒、說喜、說安、說諸三昧；諸佛菩薩亦復如是，說我、說我所、說眾生、說貪恚癡種種諸業，而實無我、無有我所。諸所作業、六趣果報，十方推求悉不可得。諸天子！譬如我聲，不生不滅，造惡諸天不聞餘聲，唯聞以地獄覺悟之聲；一切諸業亦復如是，非生非滅，隨有修集則受其報。諸天子！如我天鼓所出音聲，於無量劫不可窮盡、無有間斷，若來若去皆不可得。諸天子！若有去來則有斷常，一切諸佛終不演說有斷常法，除為方便成熟眾生。諸天子！譬如我聲，於無量世界，隨眾生心皆使得聞；一切諸佛亦復如是，隨眾生心悉令得見。

Ru-Yi Translation

'Sons of heaven! Just as I, the heavenly drum, speak of karma, speak of retribution, speak of conduct, speak of precepts, speak of joy, speak of ease, and speak of every samādhi, every buddha and bodhisattva is likewise just the same: speaking of a self, speaking of what belongs to a self, speaking of sentient beings, speaking of every manner of karma born of greed, hatred, and delusion, and yet there is truly no self, and nothing at all that belongs to a self. Every action performed, and the resulting fruits of the six destinies, when sought throughout the ten directions, can nowhere at all be found. Sons of heaven! Just as my voice neither arises nor ceases, and the heavenly beings who commit evil do not hear any other sound but only the sound that awakens them to the hells, every karmic action is likewise just the same: neither arising nor ceasing, and yet its fruit is received wherever it has been cultivated and accumulated. Sons of heaven! Just as the sound that I, the heavenly drum, produce is inexhaustible and unbroken through measureless kalpas, and can nowhere be found whether coming or going, sons of heaven, if there were a coming and a going, then there would be annihilation and permanence, and no buddha ever teaches a dharma of annihilation or permanence, except as a skillful means for bringing sentient beings to maturity. Sons of heaven! Just as my voice, throughout measureless worlds, is heard by every sentient being according to their own mind, every buddha is likewise just the same: seen by every sentient being according to their own mind.

Ru-Yi Translation

Sons of heaven! Suppose there were a crystal mirror named Able to Reflect, pure and utterly translucent, exactly equal in measure to ten world-systems; within measureless, boundless lands, every mountain and river, every sentient being, down to the hells, the animal realm, and the hungry ghosts, all of their reflected images appear within it. Sons of heaven! What do you think? Could it be said that those reflected images come into the mirror, or depart from the mirror?' They answered: 'No, it could not.' 'Sons of heaven! Every karmic action is likewise just the same: though able to give rise to every fruit of karmic retribution, it has no place from which it comes or to which it goes. Sons of heaven! Just as a magician bewilders people's eyes, you should know that every karmic action is likewise just the same. If you can know it thus, this is true and genuine repentance, and every sin and evil is thereby entirely purified.' "When this dharma was spoken, sons of heaven throughout worlds as numerous as motes of dust in a hundred thousand koṭis of nayutas of buddha-lands in the Tuṣita heaven attained the forbearance of the non-arising of dharmas; measureless, inconceivable asaṃkhyeyas of sons of heaven throughout the six heavens of desire gave rise to the mind of unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening; and every heavenly woman in the six heavens of desire renounced her female body and gave rise to the intention of unsurpassed awakening.

This is the only moment in the drum's entire teaching where the sons of heaven answer a direct question rather than simply listening; the drum has built its case through five successive similes — voice, hell-body, light, heavenly ornaments, and now the mirror — but only the mirror is put to them as a question they must themselves resolve, making their own answer the hinge on which the teaching of true repentance turns.
