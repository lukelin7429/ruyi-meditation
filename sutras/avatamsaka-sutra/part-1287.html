Hard to Hear, Hard to See

Good-Wealth completes his question, and Maitreya turns to the entire assembly — naming his one hundred and ten teachers, his unbroken diligence, and a long unbroken list of exactly what he has set out to rescue every sentient being from.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1287 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Hard to Hear, Hard to See

Good-Wealth's plea to be taught closes this Part's opening; Maitreya then turns from Good-Wealth to the whole assembly, naming his own journey through one hundred and ten good spiritual friends before a single unbroken list of everything his vow has set out to undo.

Chinese Source

「大聖！菩薩云何學菩薩行？云何修菩薩道？隨所修學，疾得具足一切佛法，悉能度脫所念眾生，普能成滿所發大願，普能究竟所起諸行，普能安慰一切天人，不負自身，不斷三寶，不虛一切佛菩薩種，能持一切諸佛法眼。如是等事，願皆為說！」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Great sage! How does a bodhisattva learn the bodhisattva practices? How does a bodhisattva cultivate the bodhisattva path? Following whatever is learned and cultivated, may I swiftly become endowed with every buddha-dharma, be able to liberate every sentient being held in mind, universally bring every vow I have raised to fulfillment, universally bring every practice I have begun to its ultimate end, universally comfort every deva and human, never fail myself, never sever the Three Jewels, never render empty the seed of any buddha or bodhisattva, and be able to uphold the dharma-eye of every buddha. May you explain every such matter to me!"

Chinese Source

爾時，彌勒菩薩摩訶薩觀察一切道場眾會，指示善財而作是言：「諸仁者！汝等見此長者子，今於我所問菩薩行諸功德不？諸仁者！此長者子，勇猛精進，志願無雜，深心堅固，恒不退轉；具勝希望，如救頭然，無有厭足；樂善知識，親近供養，處處尋求，承事請法。諸仁者！此長者子，曩於福城受文殊教，展轉南行求善知識，經由一百一十善知識已，然後而來至於我所，未曾暫起一念疲懈。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva surveyed the entire assembly of the place of practice, pointed toward Good-Wealth, and spoke these words: "Noble ones! Do you all see this elder's son, now asking me about the meritorious qualities of the bodhisattva practices? Noble ones! This elder's son is courageous and diligent, his resolve and aspiration unmixed, his deep mind firm, forever without regression; he holds a superior aspiration, as though putting out a fire upon his own head, never satisfied; he delights in good spiritual friends, drawing near to them and making offerings, seeking them out everywhere, serving them and requesting the dharma. Noble ones! This elder's son, having long ago in the City of Blessings received Mañjuśrī's own teaching, traveled onward toward the south seeking good spiritual friends, and having passed through one hundred and ten good spiritual friends, has now come at last to where I am, never once, even for a moment, having given rise to a thought of weariness.

Chinese Source

「諸仁者！此長者子甚為難有，趣向大乘，乘於大慧，發大勇猛，擐大悲甲，以大慈心救護眾生，起大精進波羅蜜行，作大商主護諸眾生，為大法船度諸有海，住於大道，集大法寶，修諸廣大助道之法；如是之人，難可得聞，難可得見，難得親近、同居、共行。何以故？此長者子發心救護一切眾生，令一切眾生，解脫諸苦，超諸惡趣，離諸險難，破無明闇，出生死野，息諸趣輪，度魔境界，不著世法，出欲淤泥，斷貪鞅，解見縛，壞想宅，絕迷道，摧慢幢，拔惑箭，撤睡蓋，裂愛網，滅無明，度有流，離諂幻，淨心垢，斷癡惑，出生死。

Ru-Yi Translation

Noble ones! This elder's son is exceedingly rare — turning toward the great vehicle, riding upon great wisdom, arousing great courage, donning the armor of great compassion, rescuing and protecting sentient beings with a mind of great loving-kindness, arousing the practice of the pāramitā of great diligence, becoming a great caravan leader who protects every sentient being, becoming a great dharma-ship that ferries across the ocean of every state of existence, abiding upon the great path, gathering the great treasure of the dharma, and cultivating every vast and great dharma that aids the path; a person such as this is hard to hear of, hard to see, and hard to draw near to, dwell together with, or accompany. Why is this? Because this elder's son has aroused the mind to rescue and protect every sentient being, causing every sentient being to be liberated from every suffering, to transcend every evil destiny, to leave behind every peril, to shatter the darkness of ignorance, to emerge from the wilderness of birth-and-death, to bring to rest the wheel of every destiny, to cross beyond the domain of māra, to remain unattached to any worldly dharma, to emerge from the mire of desire, to sever the yoke of craving, to loosen the bonds of wrong view, to destroy the dwelling of every notion, to cut off the deviant path, to topple the banner of conceit, to pull out the arrow of delusion, to remove the covering of drowsiness, to tear apart the net of craving, to extinguish ignorance, to cross beyond the stream of existence, to leave behind flattery and deceit, to purify the stains of the mind, to sever the confusion of delusion, and to emerge from birth-and-death.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

經由一百一十善知識已 — the count of one hundred and ten good spiritual friends is repeated here from earlier in the pilgrimage, confirmed directly against the source text and consistent with its prior use elsewhere in this translation.

令一切眾生，解脫諸苦，超諸惡趣⋯斷癡惑，出生死 — a run of twenty-three short clauses, each a fresh image for the same rescue: shattering, emerging, resting, crossing, toppling, pulling out, tearing apart. Counted directly against the source text.
