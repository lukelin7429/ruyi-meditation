Through a Good Spiritual Friend

Every instruction in the last Part gets its reason here — thirty-five distinct things a bodhisattva gains through a good spiritual friend, each one a different verb applied to the same object: every bodhisattva's own path.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1263 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Through a Good Spiritual Friend

Having just demanded unwavering devotion to a good spiritual friend, the teaching now explains why — not in the abstract, but as thirty-five separate things that pass from teacher to student through that relationship alone.

Chinese Source

何以故？善男子！菩薩因善知識，聽聞一切菩薩諸行，成就一切菩薩功德，出生一切菩薩大願，引發一切菩薩善根，積集一切菩薩助道，開發一切菩薩法光明，顯示一切菩薩出離門，修學一切菩薩清淨戒，安住一切菩薩功德法，清淨一切菩薩廣大志，增長一切菩薩堅固心，具足一切菩薩陀羅尼辯才門，得一切菩薩清淨藏，生一切菩薩定光明，得一切菩薩殊勝願，與一切菩薩同一願，聞一切菩薩殊勝法，得一切菩薩祕密處，至一切菩薩法寶洲，增一切菩薩善根芽，長一切菩薩智慧身，護一切菩薩深密藏，持一切菩薩福德聚，淨一切菩薩受生道，受一切菩薩正法雲，入一切菩薩大願路，趣一切如來菩提果，攝取一切菩薩妙行，開示一切菩薩功德，往一切方聽受妙法，讚一切菩薩廣大威德，生一切菩薩大慈悲力，攝一切菩薩勝自在力，生一切菩薩菩提分，作一切菩薩利益事。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Why is this? Good son! Through a good spiritual friend, a bodhisattva hears of every bodhisattva's own practice; accomplishes every bodhisattva's own merit; gives rise to every bodhisattva's own great vows; draws forth every bodhisattva's own wholesome roots; accumulates every aid to a bodhisattva's own path; opens up every bodhisattva's own light of dharma; reveals every bodhisattva's own gate of renunciation; studies and cultivates every bodhisattva's own pure precepts; abides in every bodhisattva's own dharma of merit; purifies every bodhisattva's own vast resolve; increases every bodhisattva's own firm mind; becomes endowed with every bodhisattva's own gate of dhāraṇī and eloquence; attains every bodhisattva's own pure treasury; gives rise to every bodhisattva's own light of samādhi; attains every bodhisattva's own supreme vows; shares the very same vow as every bodhisattva; hears every bodhisattva's own supreme dharma; attains every bodhisattva's own secret place; arrives at every bodhisattva's own island of dharma-treasure; increases every bodhisattva's own sprout of wholesome roots; grows every bodhisattva's own body of wisdom; guards every bodhisattva's own profound and secret treasury; upholds every bodhisattva's own accumulation of merit; purifies every bodhisattva's own path of taking birth; receives every bodhisattva's own cloud of true dharma; enters every bodhisattva's own road of great vows; advances toward every tathāgata's own fruit of bodhi; gathers in every bodhisattva's own wondrous practice; reveals every bodhisattva's own merit; goes to every direction to hear and receive the wondrous dharma; praises every bodhisattva's own vast and majestic virtue; gives rise to every bodhisattva's own power of great loving-kindness and compassion; gathers every bodhisattva's own supreme power of sovereign mastery; gives rise to every bodhisattva's own factor of bodhi; performs every bodhisattva's own beneficial deed."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

菩薩因善知識，聽聞一切菩薩諸行⋯作一切菩薩利益事 — through a good spiritual friend, a bodhisattva hears of every bodhisattva's own practice⋯performs every bodhisattva's own beneficial deed. Thirty-five items in all, counted directly from the source text — each one a different verb (hearing, accomplishing, guarding, praising, arriving, sharing) applied again and again to the same recurring object, "every bodhisattva's own." Nothing on this list could exist for a bodhisattva who had never met one.
