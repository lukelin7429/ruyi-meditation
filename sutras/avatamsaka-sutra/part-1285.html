Fascicle Seventy-Seven Complete

Good-Wealth weeps for joy at Maitreya's praise, and flowers, necklaces, and jewels fill his hands unbidden through Mañjuśrī's own power. Maitreya touches his head and speaks a verse of blessing; Good-Wealth answers with a verse of his own, and the fascicle closes.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Fascicle 77 complete — 77 of 80 fascicles done Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Fascicle Seventy-Seven Complete

Good-Wealth's overwhelmed response and his offering of spontaneous flowers open this Part; two short verses, exchanged between Maitreya and Good-Wealth, close it — and with them, fascicle seventy-seven itself.

Chinese Source

」爾時，彌勒菩薩摩訶薩在眾會前，稱讚善財大功德藏。善財聞已，歡喜踊躍，身毛皆豎，悲泣哽噎；起立合掌，恭敬瞻仰，遶無量匝。以文殊師利心念力故，眾華、瓔珞、種種妙寶不覺忽然自盈其手；善財歡喜，即以奉散彌勒菩薩摩訶薩上。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, before the great assembly, praised Good-Wealth's own great treasury of merit. Good-Wealth, having heard this, leapt with joy, every hair on his body standing on end, weeping until his voice caught; he rose to his feet, joined his palms, gazed upon Maitreya with reverence, and circled him a measureless number of times. Through the mindful power of Mañjuśrī, every kind of flower, necklace, and wondrous jewel unknowingly and suddenly filled his very hands; Good-Wealth, filled with joy, at once offered and scattered them upon Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva.

Chinese Source

時，彌勒菩薩摩善財頂，為說頌言：「

善哉善哉真佛子！
普策諸根無懈倦，
不久當具諸功德，
猶如文殊及與我。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then Maitreya Bodhisattva placed his hand upon Good-Wealth's head, and spoke a verse for him, saying:

"Excellent indeed, excellent indeed, true buddha-child!
Universally spurring on every faculty without ever growing weary,
before long you shall be endowed with every merit,
just like Mañjuśrī and just like myself."

Chinese Source

時，善財童子以頌答曰：「

我念善知識，
億劫難值遇；
今得咸親近，
而來詣尊所。

我以文殊故，
見諸難見者；
彼大功德尊，
願速還瞻覲。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then Good-Wealth answered with a verse of his own, saying:

"I recollect a good spiritual friend,
one hard to encounter even in a hundred million kalpas;
now I have been able to draw near to you entirely,
and so I have come to this honored place.

Because of Mañjuśrī,
I have seen one so rarely seen;
may I, before that one of such great merit,
swiftly return to look upon and pay homage again."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

以文殊師利心念力故，眾華、瓔珞、種種妙寶不覺忽然自盈其手 — a small but striking detail: the flowers Good-Wealth offers to Maitreya do not come from his own effort but appear in his hands through Mañjuśrī's own mindful power, reaching across the distance between them even in Mañjuśrī's physical absence from this scene.

The colophon line 大方廣佛華嚴經卷第七十七 ("Avataṃsaka Sūtra, Fascicle Seventy-Seven") that follows in the source is a structural marker rather than narrative text, and is not quoted or translated here — this Part's own framing serves the same purpose.
