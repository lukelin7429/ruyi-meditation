Jewel-Like Abidings Conclude, Diamond-Like Vows Begin

The ninth jewel-like abiding treats a buddha's own teaching and nirvāṇa as constructions of the mind, and the tenth closes the set with a bodhisattva who hears endless predictions of awakening without ever flinching; a new list of ten diamond-like Mahāyāna vow-minds then opens with three vows scaled to the entire dharma-realm — to know every dharma, to liberate every being, and to adorn every world with the finest of every buddha-land.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 720 — jewel-like abidings conclude, diamond-like vows begin Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Vows Scaled to the Whole Dharma-Realm

The jewel-like abidings end on hearing, not startled, unspeakably many predictions of awakening from unspeakably many buddhas — and immediately, without pause, a new list begins that measures every vow against the dharma-realm itself: understanding every dharma without remainder, liberating every being even in the space of a single hair-tip, and adorning every world in the ten directions with the finest ornaments any buddha-land possesses.

Chinese Source

菩薩摩訶薩知善巧說法、示現涅槃，為度眾生所有方便，一切皆是心想建立，非是顛倒，亦非虛誑。何以故？菩薩了知一切諸法三世平等、如如不動、實際無住，不見有一眾生已受化、今受化、當受化，亦自了知無所修行，無有少法若生若滅而可得者，而依於一切法，令所願不空。是為第九如寶住。菩薩摩訶薩於不思議無量諸佛一一佛所，聞不可說不可說授記法，名號各異，劫數不同；從於一劫乃至不可說不可說劫常如是聞，聞已修行，不驚不怖，不迷不惑，知如來智不思議故，如來授記言無二故，自身行願殊勝力故，隨應受化令成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提滿等法界一切願故，是為第十如寶住。佛子！是為菩薩摩訶薩於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提十種如寶住。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得諸佛無上大智慧寶。

Ru-Yi Translation

"A bodhisattva-mahāsattva knows that skillfully teaching the dharma and manifesting nirvāṇa, as the means for delivering sentient beings, are all constructions of the mind alone, neither a distortion nor a deception. Why is this? Because a bodhisattva fully understands that every dharma is equal across the three times, thus-like and unmoving, and that ultimate reality abides nowhere; they see no single sentient being who has already been converted, is now being converted, or will be converted, and also understand for themselves that there is nothing at all to cultivate, not the slightest dharma that can ever be found arising or ceasing — and yet, relying on every dharma, they still cause their vow to remain not empty. This is the ninth jewel-like abiding. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva, at the place of each and every one of the inconceivable, measureless buddhas, hears unspeakably, unspeakably many predictions of awakening, each with its own different name and different number of kalpas; from a single kalpa all the way to unspeakably, unspeakably many kalpas, always hearing in this way, and having heard, carrying out practice, is neither startled nor afraid, neither confused nor deluded, because they know the tathāgata's wisdom is inconceivable, because a tathāgata's words of prediction are never twofold, because their own practice and vow carry an excellent power, and because, being converted according to what is fitting, they cause every vow throughout the whole dharma-realm to be fulfilled in attaining unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening — this is the tenth jewel-like abiding. Son of the Buddha! These are the bodhisattva-mahāsattva's ten kinds of jewel-like abiding regarding unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the unsurpassed, great jewel of wisdom of every buddha.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩發十種如金剛大乘誓願心。何等為十？佛子！菩薩摩訶薩作如是念：『一切諸法，無有邊際，不可窮盡。我當以盡三世智，普皆覺了，無有遺餘。』是為第一如金剛大乘誓願心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『於一毛端處有無量無邊眾生，何況一切法界！我當皆以無上涅槃而滅度之。』是為第二如金剛大乘誓願心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『十方世界，無量無邊，無有齊限，不可窮盡。我當以諸佛國土最上莊嚴，莊嚴如是一切世界，所有莊嚴皆悉真實。』是為第三如金剛大乘誓願心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva gives rise to ten kinds of diamond-like Mahāyāna vow-mind. What are these ten? Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva thinks in this way: 'Every dharma is without boundary or limit, and can never be exhausted. I shall, with wisdom that spans the three times, awaken to and fully understand every one of them, leaving nothing whatsoever left out.' This is the first diamond-like Mahāyāna vow-mind. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'Within the space of a single hair-tip there are measureless, boundless sentient beings — how much more so throughout the whole dharma-realm! I shall bring every one of them to cessation through unsurpassed nirvāṇa.' This is the second diamond-like Mahāyāna vow-mind. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'The worlds of the ten directions are measureless and boundless, without limit or bound, and can never be exhausted. I shall adorn every one of these worlds with the supreme adornments of every buddha's own land, so that every adornment there is entirely real.' This is the third diamond-like Mahāyāna vow-mind.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

於一毛端處有無量無邊眾生，何況一切法界 — within the space of a single hair-tip there are measureless, boundless sentient beings — how much more so throughout the whole dharma-realm! The second diamond-like vow-mind builds its scale in a single stroke: it names the smallest imaginable space, fills it with countless beings, and then simply gestures at the entire dharma-realm as obviously vaster still, letting the comparison do the work of describing a scope no number could state directly.
