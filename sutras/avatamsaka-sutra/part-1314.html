In Every Jeweled Mirror

The sounds of the dharma leave Good-Wealth joyful, soft, and radiant, and he gains measureless gates of dhāraṇī, eloquence, and samādhi. Then the tower's surfaces begin to show him things: in every jeweled mirror, the assemblies of buddhas, bodhisattvas, śrāvakas, and pratyekabuddhas, and worlds of every kind, pure and impure, with a buddha and without, small and great, inverted and upward-facing; in every jeweled pillar, a net of maṇi light in every color; and the golden maidens and jeweled images, holding clouds of flowers and garments, incense and nets, chains and necklaces, bending low and gazing up without once looking away.

In Every Jeweled Mirror

What Good-Wealth gains from the sounds, and what he then sees in the mirrors, the pillars, and the golden figures of the tower.

Chinese Source

善財童子聞如是等不可思議微妙法音，身心歡喜，柔軟悅澤，即得無量諸總持門、諸辯才門、諸禪、諸忍、諸願、諸度、諸通、諸明，及諸解脫、諸三昧門。又見一切諸寶鏡中種種形像。所謂：或見諸佛眾會道場，或見菩薩眾會道場，或見聲聞眾會道場，或見緣覺眾會道場，或見淨世界，或見不淨世界，或見淨不淨世界，或見不淨淨世界，或見有佛世界，或見無佛世界，或見小世界，或見中世界，或見大世界，或見因陀羅網世界，或見覆世界，或見仰世界，或見平坦世界，或見地獄、畜生、餓鬼所住世界，或見天人充滿世界。於如是等諸世界中，見有無數大菩薩眾，或行或坐作諸事業，或起大悲憐愍眾生，或造諸論利益世間，或受或持，或書或誦，或問或答，三時懺悔，迴向發願。又見一切諸寶柱中，放摩尼王大光明網，或青、或黃、或赤、或白、或玻瓈色、或水精色、或帝青色、或虹霓色、或閻浮檀金色，或作一切諸光明色。又見彼閻浮檀金童女及眾寶像，或以其手而執華雲，或執衣雲，或執幢幡，或執鬘蓋，或持種種塗香、末香，或持上妙摩尼寶網，或垂金鎖，或挂瓔珞，或舉其臂捧莊嚴具，或低其首垂摩尼冠，曲躬瞻仰，目不暫捨。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good-Wealth the youth, hearing such inconceivable, subtle, and wondrous sounds of the dharma as these, was joyful in body and mind, soft, pliant, and radiant, and at once attained measureless gates of dhāraṇī, gates of eloquence, dhyānas, acceptances, vows, pāramitās, spiritual powers, and clear knowledges, together with liberations and gates of samādhi. He saw, moreover, every kind of image within all the jeweled mirrors. That is to say, he saw the assemblies of buddhas at their sites of awakening, or the assemblies of bodhisattvas at their sites of awakening, or the assemblies of śrāvakas at their sites of awakening, or the assemblies of pratyekabuddhas at their sites of awakening; or he saw pure worlds, or impure worlds, or worlds pure and impure, or worlds impure and pure; or worlds with a buddha, or worlds without a buddha; or small worlds, or middling worlds, or great worlds; or worlds of Indra's Net; or inverted worlds, or upward-facing worlds, or level worlds; or worlds inhabited by the hells, the animals, and the hungry ghosts; or worlds filled with devas and humans. Within all such worlds as these he saw countless assemblies of great bodhisattvas, walking or sitting as they carried out their various works, giving rise to great compassion and pity for sentient beings, composing treatises for the benefit of the world, receiving or upholding, copying or reciting, asking or answering, repenting during the three periods of the day, dedicating merit and making vows. He saw, moreover, within all the jeweled pillars, great nets of light sent forth by maṇi-kings, now blue, now yellow, now red, now white, now the color of crystal, now the color of quartz, now the color of indranīla, now the color of the rainbow, now the color of Jambu-river gold, or taking on every color of light there is. He saw, moreover, the maidens of Jambu-river gold and the images of every jewel, some holding clouds of flowers in their hands, some holding clouds of garments, some holding banners and pennants, some holding garlands and canopies, some holding every kind of fragrant unguent and powdered incense, some holding nets of the finest and most wondrous maṇi, some letting golden chains hang down, some hanging necklaces, some raising their arms to hold up articles of adornment, some lowering their heads so that their maṇi crowns hung forward, bending low and gazing up, their eyes never leaving him for a single moment.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

或見……世界 — the mirrors show fifteen kinds of world in a single list, and four of them come as a matched set: 淨世界, 不淨世界, 淨不淨世界, and 不淨淨世界, pure, impure, pure and impure, and impure and pure. The last two are not the same world described twice; the order of the characters is the point, a world that is pure with impurity in it against a world that is impure with purity in it, and the translation keeps the order of the two words in each case rather than merging them into a single mixed world.

玻瓈色、水精色 — two of the colors of the maṇi light are named after two different clear stones, 玻瓈 and 水精, set side by side in this list; they are rendered crystal and quartz here so that the pair stays distinct in English rather than collapsing into a single word. The golden maidens who close the part are the same 閻浮檀金童女像 that Part 1309 counted among the tower's asaṃkhyeya adornments, now seen in motion, each holding something and all of them bent low with their eyes fixed on Good-Wealth.
