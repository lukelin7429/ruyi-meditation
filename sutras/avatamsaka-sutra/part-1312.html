A Tower Above All the Others

The visions widen: bodhisattvas in the samādhi of transformation send clouds of every kind of body from every pore, from devas and nāgas down to elders and householders; wondrous sounds praise every gate of the dharma, from the mind of bodhi through the pāramitās to the eightfold noble path; tathāgatas appear with their assemblies, each with their own birthplace, lineage, lifespan, and name; and in one tower higher and more splendid than all the rest, Good-Wealth sees the whole of Maitreya's life, from descent and birth to the distribution of his relics, unfolding differently in each of a hundred koṭis of worlds.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1312 — fascicle 79 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Tower Above All the Others

Clouds of bodies, a catalogue of dharma-gates, the tathāgatas in their assemblies, and the one tower in which Maitreya's life is seen from beginning to end.

Chinese Source

或見諸菩薩入變化三昧，各於其身一一毛孔，出於一切變化身雲；或見出天眾身雲，或見出龍眾身雲，或見出夜叉、乾闥婆、緊那羅、阿脩羅、迦樓羅、摩睺羅伽、釋、梵、護世、轉輪聖王、小王、王子、大臣、官屬、長者、居士身雲，或見出聲聞、緣覺及諸菩薩、如來身雲，或見出一切眾生身雲。或見出妙音，讚諸菩薩種種法門。所謂：讚說菩提心功德門；讚說檀波羅蜜乃至智波羅蜜功德門；讚說諸攝、諸禪、諸無量心，及諸三昧、三摩鉢底、諸通、諸明、總持、辯才、諸諦、諸智、止觀、解脫、諸緣、諸依、諸說法門；讚說念處、正勤、神足、根、力、七菩提分、八聖道分、諸聲聞乘、諸獨覺乘、諸菩薩乘、諸地、諸忍、諸行、諸願，如是等一切諸功德門。或復於中，見諸如來，大眾圍遶；亦見其佛生處、種姓、身形、壽命、剎劫、名號、說法利益、教住久近，乃至所有道場眾會種種不同，悉皆明見。又復於彼莊嚴藏內諸樓閣中，見一樓閣，高廣嚴飾，最上無比；於中悉見三千世界百億四天下、百億兜率陀天，一一皆有彌勒菩薩降神誕生、釋梵天王捧持頂戴、遊行七步、觀察十方、大師子吼、現為童子、居處宮殿、遊戲園苑、為一切智出家苦行、示受乳糜、往詣道場、降伏諸魔、成等正覺、觀菩提樹、梵王勸請轉正法輪、昇天宮殿而演說法、劫數壽量、眾會莊嚴、所淨國土、所修行願、教化成熟眾生方便、分布舍利、住持教法，皆悉不同。

Ru-Yi Translation

Or he saw bodhisattvas entering the samādhi of transformation and sending forth, from each and every pore of their bodies, clouds of every kind of transformation-body; or he saw them send forth clouds of deva-bodies, or clouds of nāga-bodies, or clouds of the bodies of yakṣas, gandharvas, kiṃnaras, asuras, garuḍas, mahoragas, Śakras, Brahmās, world-protectors, wheel-turning sage kings, lesser kings, princes, great ministers, officials, elders, and householders, or clouds of the bodies of śrāvakas, pratyekabuddhas, bodhisattvas, and tathāgatas, or clouds of the bodies of every sentient being. Or he saw them send forth wondrous sounds praising the manifold gates of the dharma of the bodhisattvas. That is to say: praising the gate of the merits of the mind of bodhi; praising the gates of the merits from the pāramitā of giving up to the pāramitā of knowledge; praising the means of gathering, the dhyānas, and the immeasurable minds, together with the samādhis, the samāpattis, the spiritual powers, the clear knowledges, the dhāraṇīs, eloquence, the truths, the wisdoms, calming and insight, liberation, the conditions, the supports, and the gates of teaching the dharma; praising the foundations of mindfulness, the right efforts, the bases of spiritual power, the faculties, the powers, the seven factors of awakening, the eightfold noble path, the śrāvaka vehicles, the pratyekabuddha vehicles, the bodhisattva vehicles, the grounds, the acceptances, the practices, and the vows, and every gate of merit such as these. Or again, within it, he saw tathāgatas surrounded by great assemblies, and saw also each buddha's birthplace, lineage, bodily form, lifespan, land and kalpa, name, the benefit of his teaching, and how long or how briefly his teaching abides, and even the manifold differences among the assemblies at their sites of awakening. All of this he saw with clarity. And again, among the towers within that Adorned Treasury, he saw one tower, lofty, vast, and finely adorned, supreme and beyond compare; within it he saw in full the hundred koṭis of sets of four continents and the hundred koṭis of Tuṣita heavens of a trichiliocosm, and in each and every one of them Maitreya Bodhisattva descending and being born, Śakra and the Brahmā kings holding him up and bearing him upon their heads, his walking seven steps, his gazing into the ten directions, his great lion's roar, his appearing as a youth, his dwelling in the palace, his playing in the gardens, his leaving home and undertaking ascetic practice for the sake of all-knowing wisdom, his showing the acceptance of milk-gruel, his going to the site of awakening, his subduing every māra, his attaining perfect enlightenment, his gazing upon the bodhi tree, the Brahmā king's entreating him to turn the wheel of the true dharma, his ascending to the palaces of the heavens and expounding the dharma, the number of his kalpas and the length of his life, the adornment of his assemblies, the lands he purified, the practices and vows he cultivated, his skillful means for teaching and ripening sentient beings, the distribution of his relics, and the upholding of his teaching, all of them different.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

見一樓閣，高廣嚴飾，最上無比 — among the towers within the tower, one stands above all the others, and in it Good-Wealth sees the whole career of Maitreya, not once but in a hundred koṭis of sets of four continents and a hundred koṭis of Tuṣita heavens at the same time, 皆悉不同, each different from the rest. The passage lists twenty-three stages of that career in a single run, from 降神誕生, descending and being born, to 住持教法, the upholding of his teaching, and the translation keeps them in one sentence as the source does.

獨覺 — this part names the second vehicle as 諸獨覺乘, where the previous part wrote 緣覺; both terms are rendered pratyekabuddha, as elsewhere on this site, since the sūtra uses the two interchangeably. The list of beings whose bodies pour from the bodhisattvas' pores is likewise kept whole: between the yakṣas and the householders it runs to sixteen kinds in a single clause, reaching from the eight classes down through Śakra, Brahmā, and the world-protectors to the ranks of an ordinary human court.
