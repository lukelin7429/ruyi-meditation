Teaching Every Assembly

The visions inside the tower continue: Maitreya in the realm of King Yama sending light into the hells, feeding the hungry ghosts, and taming the animals; Maitreya teaching the dharma to the assemblies of the world-protectors, the kings of every heaven, the nāgas, yakṣas, rākṣasas, gandharvas, kiṃnaras, asuras, dānavas, garuḍas, and mahoragas, the śrāvakas, the pratyekabuddhas, and the bodhisattvas; Maitreya praising the merits of every ground and every gate; and Maitreya passing hundreds of thousands of years in walking meditation, recitation, copying, and samādhi.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1311 — fascicle 79 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Teaching Every Assembly

Three lists follow one another — the three evil destinies where Maitreya brings relief, the assemblies to which he teaches, and the merits he praises — before a closing glimpse of his own long practice.

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

Or again he saw him dwelling in the realm of King Yama, sending forth great light and relieving the sufferings of the hells; or he saw him in the abode of the hungry ghosts, giving food and drink and rescuing them from hunger and thirst; or he saw him in the destiny of the animals, taming sentient beings by every kind of skillful means. Or again he saw him teaching the dharma to the assembly of the world-protector deva kings, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the king of the Trāyastriṃśa Heaven, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the king of the Yāma Heaven, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the king of the Tuṣita Heaven, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the king of the Nirmāṇarati Heaven, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the king of the Paranirmita-vaśavartin Heaven, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the great Brahmā king, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the nāga kings, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the yakṣa and rākṣasa kings, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the gandharva and kiṃnara kings, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the asura and dānava kings, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the garuḍa and mahoraga kings, or teaching the dharma to the assemblies of all other humans and non-humans, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the śrāvakas, or teaching the dharma to the assembly of the pratyekabuddhas, or expounding the dharma to the assembly of the bodhisattvas, from those who have first aroused the mind up to those bound to a single remaining birth who have already received consecration. Or he saw him praising all the merits of the grounds from the first up to the tenth, or praising the fulfillment of every pāramitā, or praising the entry into the gates of the acceptances, or praising the gates of the great samādhis, or praising the gates of profound liberation, or praising the domains of the dhyānas, the samādhis, and the spiritual powers, or praising the practices of the bodhisattvas, or praising the great vows, or, together with his fellow bodhisattvas, praising the crafts and necessities of life of the world and every kind of skillful means that benefits sentient beings, or, together with the bodhisattvas of a single remaining birth, praising the gates of consecration of all the buddhas. Or he saw Maitreya, over hundreds of thousands of years, in walking meditation, reciting, and copying the scriptures, diligently seeking and contemplating, and teaching the dharma to the assembly; or entering the dhyānas and the four immeasurables; or entering the totalities and the liberations; or entering samādhi and, through the power of skillful means, displaying every spiritual transformation.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

或復見為……眾會說法 — the formula is repeated sixteen times in the second list, once for each assembly Maitreya teaches, and the translation repeats the whole clause each time rather than collapsing the list, so that the descent from the world-protectors through the kings of the heavens, the eight classes of beings, humans and non-humans, and the two vehicles down to the bodhisattvas remains a procession. The third list, 或見讚說, runs to ten items. 陀那婆, paired here with the asuras, is a straightforward transliteration of dānava, a class of beings usually named alongside them, and is restored as dānava rather than left in pinyin.

初發心乃至一生所繫已灌頂者 — the bodhisattva assembly is described by its whole range, from those who have only just aroused the mind to those 一生所繫, bound to a single remaining birth, who have already received consecration. The same rank appears again a few lines later as 一生菩薩, the bodhisattvas of a single remaining birth, with whom Maitreya praises the gates of consecration; both are rendered with the same phrase so that the connection between the two mentions is kept.
