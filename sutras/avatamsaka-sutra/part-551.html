Bodies Without Number, Colors Without Name

The eighth penetration opens by declaring that form has no true mark of color at all — not blue, not yellow, not red, not white — and then, in the same breath, begins a list of named colors that runs longer than any catalogue this chapter has offered yet.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 551 — the eighth penetration begins Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Nearly Eighty Named Colors

What follows the denial of color is not silence but excess — as if the only way to make good on "no fixed color" is to name so many colors that none of them can be mistaken for the final word.

Ru-Yi Translation

Sons of the Buddha! Through the penetration of the wisdom that produces measureless asaṃkhyeyas of adorned bodies of form, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva knows that all dharmas are far removed from any mark of form — without distinguishing marks, without manifold marks, without measureless marks, without discriminated marks, without marks of blue, yellow, red, or white. Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知一切法遠離色相…無青、黃、赤、白相。菩薩如是入於法界，能現其身，作種種色 — knows that all dharmas are far removed from any mark of color… without blue, yellow, red, or white. The bodhisattva, entering the dharma-realm in this way, is able to manifest his body, taking on manifold colors. The denial and the list are not in tension — the same move Part 538 made with mind and illusion: knowing form has no fixed mark is exactly what frees the bodhisattva to manifest it in every mark there is.

Reading through the list itself, almost none of the nearly eighty terms so far name an actual hue. 色 here names a manifested quality — compassion, fearlessness, purity, nobility — each one called a "color" the bodhisattva's body can take on. Blue, yellow, red, and white, denied in the opening line, never reappear.

Part 551 Complete

This continues chapter twenty-eight, 十通品, The Ten Kinds of Spiritual Penetration, in fascicle forty-four. Part 552 completes the list of colors — turning, in its last stretch, to actual images of light and fragrance: a net of countless lights, a sun's wheel, an unequalled moon-body, clouds of flowers — before explaining what all of it is for.
