Like a Seed

Maitreya opens one of this sūtra's most celebrated passages — an unbroken chain of similes for the mind of bodhi itself, thirty-five of them in this Part alone, running from a seed and a field through mountains, an elephant, and a horse.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1290 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Like a Seed

Thirty-five similes open the famous "mind of bodhi" passage, each built on the same frame — 菩提心者 ("the mind of bodhi") followed by an image and the reason it fits — running from a seed and the great earth through Sumeru, the Snowy Mountains, and a well-tamed elephant.

Chinese Source

「善男子！菩提心者，猶如種子，能生一切諸佛法故；菩提心者，猶如良田，能長眾生白淨法故；菩提心者，猶如大地，能持一切諸世間故；菩提心者，猶如淨水，能洗一切煩惱垢故；菩提心者，猶如大風，普於世間無所礙故；菩提心者，猶如盛火，能燒一切諸見薪故；菩提心者，猶如淨日，普照一切諸世間故；菩提心者，猶如盛月，諸白淨法悉圓滿故；菩提心者，猶如明燈，能放種種法光明故；菩提心者，猶如淨目，普見一切安危處故；菩提心者，猶如大道，普令得入大智城故；菩提心者，猶如正濟，令其得離諸邪法故；菩提心者，猶如大車，普能運載諸菩薩故；菩提心者，猶如門戶，開示一切菩薩行故；菩提心者，猶如宮殿，安住修習三昧法故；菩提心者，猶如園苑，於中遊戲受法樂故；菩提心者，猶如舍宅，安隱一切諸眾生故；菩提心者，則為所歸，利益一切諸世間故；菩提心者，則為所依，諸菩薩行所依處故；菩提心者，猶如慈父，訓導一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，猶如慈母，生長一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，猶如乳母，養育一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，猶如善友，成益一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，猶如君主，勝出一切二乘人故；菩提心者，猶如帝王，一切願中得自在故；菩提心者，猶如大海，一切功德悉入中故；菩提心者，如須彌山，於諸眾生心平等故；菩提心者，如鐵圍山，攝持一切諸世間故；菩提心者，猶如雪山，長養一切智慧藥故；菩提心者，猶如香山，出生一切功德香故；菩提心者，猶如虛空，諸妙功德廣無邊故；菩提心者，猶如蓮華，不染一切世間法故；菩提心者，如調慧象，其心善順不獷戾故；菩提心者，如良善馬，遠離一切諸惡性故；菩提心者，如調御師，守護大乘一切法故；

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! The mind of bodhi is like a seed, since it gives rise to every buddha-dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a good field, since it grows every sentient being's own white and pure dharma; the mind of bodhi is like the great earth, since it upholds every world; the mind of bodhi is like pure water, since it washes away every stain of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like a great wind, since it moves universally through the world without any obstruction; the mind of bodhi is like a blazing fire, since it burns away the very firewood of every wrong view; the mind of bodhi is like the pure sun, since it universally illuminates every world; the mind of bodhi is like the full moon, since every white and pure dharma is fully complete within it; the mind of bodhi is like a bright lamp, since it emits every kind of dharma's own light; the mind of bodhi is like a pure eye, since it universally sees every place of safety and danger; the mind of bodhi is like a great road, since it universally allows one to enter the city of great wisdom; the mind of bodhi is like the right crossing, since it allows one to leave behind every deviant dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a great cart, since it universally carries every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like a doorway, since it opens and reveals every bodhisattva practice; the mind of bodhi is like a palace, since one abides securely within it cultivating the dharma of samādhi; the mind of bodhi is like a garden, since one plays freely within it receiving the joy of the dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a house, since it brings peace and security to every sentient being; the mind of bodhi becomes a refuge, since it benefits every world; the mind of bodhi becomes a reliance, since it is the very place every bodhisattva practice relies upon; the mind of bodhi is like a loving father, since it instructs and guides every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like a loving mother, since it gives birth to and raises every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like a wet-nurse, since it nurtures and raises every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like a good friend, since it accomplishes benefit for every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like a sovereign lord, since it surpasses every person of the two vehicles; the mind of bodhi is like a great king, since it attains mastery within every vow; the mind of bodhi is like the great ocean, since every merit enters entirely within it; the mind of bodhi is like Sumeru, since it is equal-minded toward every sentient being; the mind of bodhi is like Cakravāḍa, since it gathers in and upholds every world; the mind of bodhi is like the Snowy Mountains, since it grows and nurtures the medicine of every wisdom; the mind of bodhi is like the Fragrant Mountain, since it gives rise to the fragrance of every merit; the mind of bodhi is like empty space, since its every wondrous merit is vast and boundless; the mind of bodhi is like a lotus flower, since it is unstained by any worldly dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a well-tamed, wise elephant, since its mind is gentle and compliant, never fierce or unruly; the mind of bodhi is like a fine, good horse, since it is far removed from every evil nature; the mind of bodhi is like a skilled tamer, since it guards and protects every dharma of the great vehicle;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

菩提心者，猶如種子，能生一切諸佛法故 — this opens one of the most celebrated passages in the entire Avataṃsaka, an unbroken chain of similes for the mind of bodhi that runs far beyond this single Part. Thirty-five similes here, counted directly against the source text by splitting on every 「；」, each following the same frame: 菩提心者 ("the mind of bodhi"), an image, and 故 ("since") giving the reason. The quotation itself stays open at the end of this Part and continues without a break into Part 1291.
