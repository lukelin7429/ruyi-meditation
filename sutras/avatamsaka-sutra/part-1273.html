Dwelling-Place, Part Two

The litany continues without a break — twenty-one more lines on the tower's dwelling-place, moving from a bodhisattva who adorns every buddha-land without leaving his own place to one who, though free of birth-and-death itself, still shows himself receiving it for every sentient being's sake.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1273 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Dwelling-Place, Part Two

Eleven lines of universal reach open this Part; four lines on rare dharmas and a bodhisattva's own attainments follow; six lines of paradox — free of the world, yet acting within it for every being's sake — bring it to its end, the quotation still unclosed.

Chinese Source

是雖已出一切世間，為化眾生故而恒於中現身者之所住處；是不著一切剎，為供養諸佛故而遊一切剎者之所住處；是不動本處，能普詣一切佛剎而莊嚴者之所住處；是親近一切佛而不起佛想者之所住處；是依止一切善知識而不起善知識想者之所住處；是住一切魔宮而不耽著欲境界者之所住處；是永離一切心想者之所住處；是雖於一切眾生中而現其身，然於自他不生二想者之所住處；是能普入一切世界而於法界無差別想者之所住處；是願住未來一切劫而於諸劫無長短想者之所住處；是不離一毛端處而普現身一切世界者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who, though he has gone beyond every world, for the sake of transforming sentient beings constantly manifests his body within it; the dwelling-place of one who is unattached to every land, yet travels through every land for the sake of making offerings to every buddha; the dwelling-place of one who, without moving from his original place, is able to travel universally to every buddha-land and adorn it; the dwelling-place of one who draws near to every buddha without giving rise to any notion of a buddha; the dwelling-place of one who relies on every good spiritual friend without giving rise to any notion of a good spiritual friend; the dwelling-place of one who dwells within every māra's palace without indulging in any object of desire; the dwelling-place of one who has forever left behind every notion of mind; the dwelling-place of one who, though he manifests his body among every sentient being, gives rise to no notion of two between self and other; the dwelling-place of one who is able to universally enter every world while holding no notion of any difference within the dharma-realm; the dwelling-place of one who vows to abide throughout every kalpa yet to come while holding no notion of long or short among the kalpas; the dwelling-place of one who, without leaving the space of a single hair-tip, universally manifests his body throughout every world;

Chinese Source

是能演說難遭遇法者之所住處；是能住難知法、甚深法、無二法、無相法、無對治法、無所得法、無戲論法者之所住處；是住大慈大悲者之所住處；是已度一切二乘智、已超一切魔境界、已於世法無所染、已到菩薩所到岸、已住如來所住處者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who is able to expound a dharma rarely encountered; the dwelling-place of one who is able to abide in the dharma hard to know, the profound dharma, the dharma of non-duality, the dharma without characteristics, the dharma without any antidote, the dharma without anything attained, and the dharma without idle discourse; the dwelling-place of one who abides in great loving-kindness and great compassion; the dwelling-place of one who has already crossed beyond the wisdom of the two vehicles, already transcended every domain of māra, is no longer stained by any worldly dharma, has already reached the shore a bodhisattva reaches, and already abides where every tathāgata abides;

Chinese Source

是雖離一切諸相而亦不入聲聞正位，雖了一切法無生而亦不住無生法性者之所住處；是雖觀不淨而不證離貪法亦不與貪欲俱，雖修於慈而不證離瞋法亦不與瞋垢俱，雖觀緣起而不證離癡法亦不與癡惑俱者之所住處；是雖住四禪而不隨禪生，雖行四無量為化眾生故而不生色界，雖修四無色定以大悲故而不住無色界者之所住處；是雖勤修止觀為化眾生故而不證明脫，雖行於捨而不捨化眾生事者之所住處；是雖觀於空而不起空見，雖行無相而常化著相眾生，雖行無願而不捨菩提行願者之所住處；是雖於一切業煩惱中而得自在為化眾生故而現隨順諸業煩惱，雖無生死為化眾生故示受生死，雖已離一切趣為化眾生故示入諸趣者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who, though free of every characteristic, does not enter the fixed station of a śrāvaka, and though he understands that every dharma is unborn, does not abide in the unborn nature of dharmas; the dwelling-place of one who, though contemplating impurity, does not realize the dharma free of craving, yet is not bound up with craving and desire either, and though cultivating loving-kindness, does not realize the dharma free of anger, yet is not bound up with the defilement of anger either, and though contemplating dependent origination, does not realize the dharma free of delusion, yet is not bound up with the confusion of delusion either; the dwelling-place of one who, though abiding in the four dhyānas, is not born through the power of dhyāna, and though practicing the four immeasurables, for the sake of transforming sentient beings is not born into the form realm, and though cultivating the four formless absorptions, through great compassion does not abide in the formless realm; the dwelling-place of one who, though diligently cultivating calming and insight, for the sake of transforming sentient beings does not realize liberation through clear knowledge, and though practicing equanimity, does not abandon the work of transforming sentient beings; the dwelling-place of one who, though contemplating emptiness, does not give rise to any view of emptiness, though practicing signlessness, constantly transforms sentient beings attached to characteristics, and though practicing wishlessness, does not abandon the practice and vow of bodhi; the dwelling-place of one who, though he has attained mastery within every karma and affliction, for the sake of transforming sentient beings manifests conformity with every karma and affliction, though free of birth-and-death, for the sake of transforming sentient beings shows himself receiving birth-and-death, and though he has already left behind every destiny, for the sake of transforming sentient beings shows himself entering every destiny;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

是雖已出一切世間，為化眾生故而恒於中現身 — twenty-one more lines of the litany, counted directly against the source text by splitting on every 「；」, continuing the same unbroken quotation opened in Part 1272 without a single closing mark. The pattern shifts partway through this Part, from the earlier "the dwelling-place of one who is/does X" toward a denser paradox: "though X, [he does] not Y" — a bodhisattva who has gone beyond the world yet never leaves it, who touches every affliction yet is never caught by one.
