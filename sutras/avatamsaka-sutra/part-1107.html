Nine Lights, and Seven Days

The countdown runs out. Each thousand years brings one more light and one more capacity, the gifts climbing from knowing one's own deeds to a samādhi of seeing buddhas — and then the last seven days pass and the ground moves.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1107 — the countdown completes Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Eight, Seven, Six

The verb changes from pure light to great light, and the first three of the great ones work on what a being is rather than what a being sees.

Chinese Source

八千年前，放大光明，名：一切眾生業果音；若有眾生遇斯光者，悉得自知諸業果報，知八千年後佛當出現。七千年前，放大光明，名：生一切善根音；若有眾生遇斯光者，一切諸根悉得圓滿，知七千年後佛當出現。六千年前，放大光明，名：佛不思議境界音；若有眾生遇斯光者，其心廣大，普得自在，知六千年後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Eight thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Sound of the Fruits of All Beings' Deeds. If any being encountered that light, they came to know for themselves the recompense of all their own deeds, and they knew that eight thousand years later a buddha would appear. Seven thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Sound That Gives Birth to Every Root of Goodness. If any being encountered that light, every one of their faculties became perfect and complete, and they knew that seven thousand years later a buddha would appear. Six thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Sound of the Buddha's Inconceivable Domain. If any being encountered that light, their mind became vast and they attained sovereign freedom everywhere, and they knew that six thousand years later a buddha would appear.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Every one of these three lights is named 音, a sound, and so is the one that opens the next section. Four consecutive lights of the eleven carry that ending — 一切眾生業果音, 生一切善根音, 佛不思議境界音, 嚴淨一切佛剎音 — yet the first thing each of them does is not to be heard but to be met: 若有眾生遇斯光者, if any being encountered that light. The sūtra is not being careless. A buddha's approach is announced in a medium that is simultaneously seen and heard, because there is no sense in which what is arriving belongs to one channel rather than another.

悉得自知諸業果報 — they came to know for themselves the recompense of all their own deeds. This is the third rung of the ladder and the first one that is unpleasant. Part 1105 ended with a whole population shortening its own life by contempt, and the first substantive gift the countdown gives that population is the ability to see exactly what their deeds have bought them. The light does not console. It shows the bill, and only then does the buddha come.

Five, Four, Three

Halfway down, the gifts turn outward. Having been given a mind, the beings are now given somewhere to look, someone to visit, and a past to see.

Chinese Source

五千年前，放大光明，名：嚴淨一切佛剎音；若有眾生遇斯光者，悉見一切清淨佛土，知五千年後佛當出現。四千年前，放大光明，名：一切如來境界無差別燈；若有眾生遇斯光者，悉能往覲一切諸佛，知四千年後佛當出現。三千年前，放大光明，名：三世明燈；若有眾生遇斯光者，悉能現見一切如來諸本事海，知三千年後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Five thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Sound of Adorning and Purifying Every Buddha-Land. If any being encountered that light, they saw every pure buddha-land, and they knew that five thousand years later a buddha would appear. Four thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Lamp of the Undifferentiated Domain of Every Tathāgata. If any being encountered that light, they were able to go and pay court to every buddha, and they knew that four thousand years later a buddha would appear. Three thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Bright Lamp of the Three Times. If any being encountered that light, they were able to see before them the ocean of the past deeds of every tathāgata, and they knew that three thousand years later a buddha would appear.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The names change here. After four lights ending 音, sound, come three ending 燈, lamp — 無差別燈, 三世明燈, 離翳智慧燈. The turn is exactly where the gifts stop altering the recipient and start showing them things: pure lands, buddhas to visit, an ocean of past deeds. Sound does the work on a person; lamps do the work on a view. The sūtra shifts its own metaphor at the hinge of its own list.

悉能現見一切如來諸本事海 — they were able to see before them the ocean of the past deeds of every tathāgata. It is worth noticing what this gift is, at the three-thousand-year mark, inside a story that is itself a night-spirit's account of her own distant past. The light that arrives three thousand years before a buddha gives beings the power to do the exact thing Good-Wealth has been doing for forty parts of this pilgrimage — look at what buddhas did long ago. The capacity being handed out is the capacity to hear this kind of story.

Two, One

Near the end the gifts become both wider and more specific — a universal eye, and then a samādhi with a single object.

Chinese Source

二千年前，放大光明，名：如來離翳智慧燈；若有眾生遇斯光者，則得普眼見一切如來神變、一切諸佛國土、一切世界眾生，知二千年後佛當出現。一千年前，放大光明，名：令一切眾生見佛集諸善根；若有眾生遇斯光者，則得成就見佛三昧，知一千年後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Two thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Lamp of the Tathāgata's Wisdom Free of Cataract. If any being encountered that light, they obtained the universal eye and saw the spiritual transformations of every tathāgata, every buddha-land, and the beings of every world, and they knew that two thousand years later a buddha would appear. One thousand years beforehand, it sent out a great light named Causing All Beings to See the Buddha and Gather Roots of Goodness. If any being encountered that light, they accomplished the samādhi of seeing the buddhas, and they knew that one thousand years later a buddha would appear.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

如來離翳智慧燈 — Lamp of the Tathāgata's Wisdom Free of Cataract. The image has been standing in this fascicle since Part 1103, where Good-Wealth praised this same night-spirit for a light from her eyes that 悉滅世癡翳, dissolved the world's cataract of delusion. There the cataract was removed by a spirit in the present; here it is removed by a flower two thousand years before a buddha. The same operation is being performed at two scales of the same story, and the reader is expected to notice that the night-spirit's power is not hers alone but a thing this world has always done.

則得成就見佛三昧 — they accomplished the samādhi of seeing the buddhas. The last of the ten millennial lights gives a capacity whose entire content is the thing that is about to happen anyway. A thousand years before a buddha arrives, beings are given the ability to see buddhas. It is the sūtra's characteristic way of describing preparation: one is not made ready for an event so much as given, in advance, the faculty the event will consist of.

Seven Days

The unit changes from a thousand years to a week, and the eleventh light gives no faculty at all — only joy. Then the week runs out.

Chinese Source

次七日前，放大光明，名：一切眾生歡喜音；若有眾生遇斯光者，得普見諸佛生大歡喜，知七日後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Next, seven days beforehand, it sent out a great light named Sound of the Joy of All Beings. If any being encountered that light, they came to see all the buddhas everywhere and gave rise to great joy, and they knew that seven days later a buddha would appear.

Chinese Source

滿七日已，一切世界悉皆震動，純淨無染，念念普現十方一切清淨佛剎，亦現彼剎種種莊嚴；若有眾生根性淳熟，應見佛者，咸詣道場。

Ru-Yi Translation

When the seven days were complete, every world shook throughout, becoming wholly pure and without stain, and in thought after thought manifesting universally every pure buddha-land of the ten directions and the manifold adornments of those lands as well. And any being whose faculties and nature were fully ripened, who was fit to see the Buddha, came to the awakening-site.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The eleventh light breaks the pattern the other ten kept. Each of those gave a capacity — an opened mind, a clean eye, knowledge of one's deeds, complete faculties, a vast mind, sight of pure lands, access to buddhas, sight of their past, a universal eye, a samādhi. The last one gives 大歡喜, great joy, which is not a capacity for anything. Seven days out, there is nothing left to become able to do.

一切世界悉皆震動，純淨無染 — every world shook throughout, becoming wholly pure and without stain. The world this happens to was introduced in Part 1104 as 淨穢合成, formed of the pure and the foul combined, and the city at its heart was, in Part 1105, busy shortening its own lifespan by contempt. 純淨無染 is the exact reversal of both. For the duration of a buddha's appearance, a mixed world is unmixed.

若有眾生根性淳熟，應見佛者，咸詣道場 — any being whose faculties and nature were fully ripened, who was fit to see the Buddha, came to the awakening-site. After ten thousand years of notice, the attendance is still conditional. The sentence does not say everyone came; it says the ripe came. That is not a restriction the sūtra apologises for — it has just spent eleven lights describing precisely how a being gets ripe, and the whole countdown exists so that the condition can be met.

Part 1107 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy. Part 1108 gives what the world does when the buddha appears — every encircling mountain, every sea, every wall and palace and language giving out sound in praise, and clouds of incense, jewels, garlands and light pouring out to show that a tathāgata has come into the world.
