Welcome, Stanzas 79–97

Maitreya tells Good-Wealth directly that measureless bodhisattvas have practiced the path for measureless kalpas without accomplishing what he has now gained in a single lifetime — and names what it truly takes to hear a teaching such as this at all.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1283 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 79–97

Maitreya names the rarity of the buddha-children Good-Wealth has met, then turns to Good-Wealth's own accomplishment directly — comparing it to measureless bodhisattvas across measureless kalpas — before closing on what it takes to hear such a teaching at all.

Chinese Source

菩薩種種行，
皆為調眾生，
普行諸法門，
慎勿生疑惑。

汝具難思福，
及以真實信；
是故於今日，
得見諸佛子。

汝見諸佛子，
悉獲廣大利，
一一諸大願，
一切咸信受。

汝於三有中，
能修菩薩行；
是故諸佛子，
示汝解脫門。

非是法器人，
與佛子同住，
設經無量劫，
莫知其境界。

汝見諸菩薩，
得聞如是法，
世間甚難有，
應生大喜慶。

諸佛護念汝，
菩薩攝受汝，
能順其教行，
善哉住壽命。

已生菩薩家，
已具菩薩德，
已長如來種，
當昇灌頂位。

不久汝當得，
與諸佛子等，
見苦惱眾生，
悉置安隱處。

如下如是種，
必獲如是果，
我今慶慰汝，
汝應大欣悅。

無量諸菩薩，
無量劫行道，
未能成此行，
今汝皆獲得。

信樂堅進力，
善財成此行；
若有敬慕心，
亦當如是學。

一切功德行，
皆從願欲生；
善財已了知，
常樂勤修習。

如龍布密雲，
必當霔大雨；
菩薩起願智，
決定修諸行。

若有善知識，
示汝普賢行；
汝當好承事，
慎勿生疑惑。

汝於無量劫，
為欲妄捨身；
今為求菩提，
此捨方為善。

汝於無量劫，
具受生死苦，
不曾事諸佛，
未聞如是行。

汝今得人身，
值佛善知識，
聽受菩提行，
云何不歡喜！
雖遇佛興世，
亦值善知識；
其心不清淨，
不聞如是法。

若於善知識，
信樂心尊重，
離疑不疲厭，
乃聞如是法。

Ru-Yi Translation

Every kind of bodhisattva practice
is entirely for the sake of taming sentient beings;
universally practicing every dharma-gate —
be careful never to give rise to doubt.

You are endowed with merit beyond conception,
together with true and genuine faith;
and so it is that on this very day,
you are able to see every buddha-child.

You have seen every buddha-child,
and have entirely gained vast benefit;
every single one of their great vows,
you have accepted and believed in fully.

Within the three states of existence,
you are able to cultivate the bodhisattva practices;
and so it is that every buddha-child
shows you the gate of liberation.

One who is not a vessel for the dharma,
even dwelling together with a buddha-child,
though it be for measureless kalpas,
could never know their realm.

You have seen every bodhisattva,
and have been able to hear a dharma such as this,
something exceedingly rare in the world —
you should give rise to great rejoicing.

Every buddha guards and minds you,
every bodhisattva gathers you in;
able to accord with their teaching and practice —
excellent indeed; may your life abide long.

You have already been born into a bodhisattva's own family,
already endowed with a bodhisattva's own virtue,
already grown the tathāgata-seed —
you shall ascend to the station of consecration.

Before long you shall attain
equality with every buddha-child;
seeing every suffering, troubled being,
you shall place them all in a place of peace and security.

Just as such a seed is sown,
so must such a fruit be gained;
I now rejoice with you and console you —
you should feel great joy.

Measureless bodhisattvas,
practicing the path for measureless kalpas,
have not yet been able to accomplish this practice —
yet now you have gained it entirely.

With the power of faith, joy, firmness, and diligence,
Good-Wealth has accomplished this practice;
if anyone holds a heart of reverence and admiration,
they too should learn in just this way.

Every meritorious practice
arises entirely from vow and aspiration;
Good-Wealth has already understood this fully,
and constantly delights in diligent cultivation.

Just as a nāga spreads dense clouds
and must then pour down great rain,
so a bodhisattva raises the wisdom of vow,
and resolutely cultivates every practice.

If there is a good spiritual friend
who shows you the practices of Samantabhadra,
you should serve them well —
be careful never to give rise to doubt.

Throughout measureless kalpas,
you have vainly given up your body for the sake of desire;
now, seeking bodhi instead,
this relinquishing at last becomes wholesome.

Throughout measureless kalpas,
you have fully received the suffering of birth-and-death,
never once having served any buddha,
never having heard a practice such as this.

Now you have attained a human body,
encountered a buddha and a good spiritual friend,
and heard and received the practice of bodhi —
how could you not rejoice!
Though one may encounter a buddha's own arising in the world,
and also encounter a good spiritual friend,
if the mind is not pure,
such a one will not hear a dharma such as this.

Only if, toward a good spiritual friend,
one holds a mind of faith, joy, and reverence,
free of doubt and never growing weary,
will one come to hear a dharma such as this.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

無量諸菩薩，無量劫行道，未能成此行，今汝皆獲得 — the turning point of this Part: measureless bodhisattvas across measureless kalpas have not accomplished what Good-Wealth has now gained. The eighteenth stanza runs to eight lines rather than four, ending its first half on an exclamation mark (！) rather than a comma — confirmed directly against the source and matched with seven 、
、 breaks on both sides, the same pattern already seen in Part 1282's sixteenth stanza.
