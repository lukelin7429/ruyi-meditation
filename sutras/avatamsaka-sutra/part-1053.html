Every Realm, Its Own Dharma

Unsurpassed Victory's own teaching climbs from the desire realm into the Brahmā heavens and then scales outward to worlds beyond counting, giving each realm of existence — hell, the animal realm, King Yama's world, heaven, and the human world — its own matched dharma of condition, feeling, and path.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1053 — every realm, its own dharma Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Five Realms, Each in Three Parts

Every realm of existence Unsurpassed Victory names is given exactly three things — its own nature, its own suffering or pleasure, and its own path leading there — a structure repeated identically from the depths of hell up through the human world.

Chinese Source

乃至色界一切梵天，我亦為其說超勝法。如於此三千大千世界，乃至十方十不可說百千億那由他佛剎微塵數世界中，我皆為說佛法、菩薩法、聲聞法、獨覺法；說地獄，說地獄眾生，說向地獄道；說畜生，說畜生差別，說畜生受苦，說向畜生道；說閻羅王世間，說閻羅王世間苦，說向閻羅王世間道；說天世間，說天世間樂，說向天世間道；說人世間，說人世間苦樂，說向人世間道。

Ru-Yi Translation

"And on up through every Brahmā heaven of the form realm, I likewise teach them the supreme dharma. Just as in this great trichiliocosm, so too throughout worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten unspeakable hundreds of thousands of millions of nayutas of buddha-lands in the ten directions, I teach in every one of them the buddha-dharma, the bodhisattva-dharma, the śrāvaka-dharma, and the pratyekabuddha-dharma; I speak of hell, I speak of the beings in hell, and I speak of the path leading to hell; I speak of the animal realm, I speak of the differences among animals, I speak of the suffering animals undergo, and I speak of the path leading to the animal realm; I speak of King Yama's world, I speak of the suffering in King Yama's world, and I speak of the path leading to King Yama's world; I speak of the heavenly world, I speak of the pleasure in the heavenly world, and I speak of the path leading to the heavenly world; I speak of the human world, I speak of the suffering and pleasure in the human world, and I speak of the path leading to the human world.

Eleven Reasons for Teaching It All

Unsurpassed Victory closes with eleven distinct reasons behind this entire vast undertaking — reasons that move from revealing the bodhisattva's own merit all the way to turning the tathāgata's own wheel of dharma.

Chinese Source

為欲開顯菩薩功德，為令捨離生死過患，為令知見一切智人諸妙功德，為欲令知諸有趣中迷惑受苦，為令知見無障礙法，為欲顯示一切世間生起所因，為欲顯示一切世間寂滅為樂，為令眾生捨諸想著，為令證得佛無依法，為令永滅諸煩惱輪，為令能轉如來法輪，我為眾生說如是法。

Ru-Yi Translation

Wishing to reveal the bodhisattva's own merit, wishing to cause them to abandon the faults and troubles of birth and death, wishing to cause them to know and see the wondrous merit of every all-knowing person, wishing to cause them to know the confusion and suffering within every destiny of existence, wishing to cause them to know and see the unobstructed dharma, wishing to reveal the causes from which every world arises, wishing to reveal that the quiescence of every world is bliss, wishing to cause beings to abandon every notion and attachment, wishing to cause them to realize the buddha's own dharma of non-reliance, wishing to cause them to forever extinguish the wheel of every affliction, and wishing to cause them to be able to turn the tathāgata's own wheel of dharma — for the sake of beings, I teach the dharma in this way.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

說天世間，說天世間樂 — I speak of the heavenly world, I speak of the pleasure in the heavenly world. Even heaven's own pleasure is named as something to be taught about rather than simply enjoyed — Unsurpassed Victory's own dharma treats every realm, pleasant or painful, as equally a subject requiring the same threefold analysis of nature, feeling, and path.
