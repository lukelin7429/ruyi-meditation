Ten Similes, Once More

A single sentence closes the whole sequence of minds and contemplations — such devotion purifies one's own resolve forever — before a second set of ten similes returns to natural images: mountains, oceans, fire, moon, and sun.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1268 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Similes, Once More

After twenty-one minds and ten paired contemplations, a single closing sentence names what all of it was for, before the teaching turns one more time to image — a second set of ten similes for the good spiritual friend themself.

Chinese Source

「善男子！汝應發如是心，作如是意近善知識。何以故？以如是心近善知識，令其志願永得清淨。「復次，善男子！善知識者長諸善根，譬如雪山長諸藥草；善知識者是佛法器，譬如大海吞納眾流；善知識者是功德處，譬如大海出生眾寶；善知識者淨菩提心，譬如猛火能鍊真金；善知識者出過世法，如須彌山出於大海；善知識者不染世法，譬如蓮華不著於水；善知識者不受諸惡，譬如大海不宿死屍；善知識者增長白法，譬如白月光色圓滿；善知識者照明法界，譬如盛日照四天下；善知識者長菩薩身，譬如父母養育兒子。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! You should give rise to a mind such as this, and hold such an intention, in drawing near to a good spiritual friend. Why is this? Because drawing near to a good spiritual friend with a mind such as this causes one's own resolve to attain purity forever. Moreover, good son! A good spiritual friend increases every wholesome root, just as a snow mountain increases every medicinal herb; a good spiritual friend is a vessel for the buddha-dharma, just as the great ocean swallows in every stream; a good spiritual friend is the very place of merit, just as the great ocean gives birth to every treasure; a good spiritual friend purifies the mind of bodhi, just as a fierce fire is able to refine true gold; a good spiritual friend transcends every worldly dharma, just as Sumeru rises above the great ocean; a good spiritual friend is unstained by worldly dharma, just as a lotus flower is never attached to water; a good spiritual friend accepts no evil at all, just as the great ocean harbors no corpse; a good spiritual friend increases every wholesome dharma, just as the white moon's own light and form become perfectly full; a good spiritual friend illumines the dharma-realm, just as the blazing sun illumines the four continents; and a good spiritual friend grows a bodhisattva's own body, just as parents raise and nurture their own child."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

善知識者長諸善根，譬如雪山長諸藥草⋯善知識者長菩薩身，譬如父母養育兒子 — a good spiritual friend increases every wholesome root, just as a snow mountain increases every medicinal herb⋯a good spiritual friend grows a bodhisattva's own body, just as parents raise and nurture their own child. Ten similes in all, counted directly from the source text — a second such set in this same teaching, distinct from the ten in an earlier Part, built now entirely from images of nature: a mountain, an ocean named four separate times, fire, a lotus, Sumeru, the moon, and the sun.

Part 1268 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy-seven. Part 1269 turns to ten things a bodhisattva attains by following a good spiritual friend's teaching, each measured in ineffable hundred-thousand-koṭis of nayutas or asaṃkhyeyas — merit, roots, powers, dharma-gates, and vows among them.
