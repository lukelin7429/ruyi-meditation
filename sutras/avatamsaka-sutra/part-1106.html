Ten Thousand Years of Notice

North of the city stands a tree whose root is not a substance but an event. In the pool before it a lotus opens, and out of the lotus comes a buddha — but first, for ten thousand years, the flower sends out lights to say that he is coming.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1106 — the tree and the first light Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Tree with a Maṇi King for a Root

The story leaves the declining city and goes north of it, where nothing is declining at all. The tree is described part by part, and the first part is the strangest.

Chinese Source

「時，彼城北有菩提樹，名：普光法雲音幢，以念念出現一切如來道場莊嚴堅固摩尼王而為其根，一切摩尼以為其幹，眾雜妙寶以為其葉，次第分布，並相稱可，四方上下，圓滿莊嚴；放寶光明，出妙音聲，說一切如來甚深境界。

Ru-Yi Translation

"At that time, north of that city there was a bodhi tree named Banner of the Sound of the Dharma-Cloud of Universal Light. For its root it had the firm maṇi king that in thought after thought brings forth the adornments of every tathāgata's awakening-site; for its trunk, every kind of maṇi; and for its leaves, all manner of wondrous jewels, distributed in due order and well matched to one another, so that in the four directions and above and below its adornment was perfect and complete. It sent out a jewelled light and gave forth a wondrous sound, expounding the profound domain of every tathāgata.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The tree is given in three parts, each on the same frame — 而為其根, 以為其幹, 以為其葉, for its root, for its trunk, for its leaves. The last two are materials, maṇi and mixed jewels. The first is not. What the root is made of is 念念出現一切如來道場莊嚴堅固摩尼王 — the firm maṇi king that in thought after thought brings forth the adornments of every tathāgata's awakening-site. That is not a substance; it is a continuous activity, described in the present, with 念念 in it. The tree is rooted in something that is happening.

時，彼城北有菩提樹 — at that time, north of that city there was a bodhi tree. The city in question is the one Part 1105 left mid-decline, where a boast about a well-formed body had just started shortening everybody's life. The story turns from that to this without a transition. Both things are true of the same moment: contempt is spreading through the streets, and north of the walls a tree is expounding the domain of every tathāgata.

The Pool, and the Lotus in It

Before the tree is water, and the water has a voice. Then the lotus rises, and it contains more buddhas than there are motes of dust in Sumeru.

Chinese Source

於彼樹前，有一香池，名：寶華光明，演法雷音；妙寶為岸，百萬億那由他寶樹圍遶，一一樹形如菩提樹，眾寶瓔珞周匝垂下，無量樓閣皆寶所成，周遍道場以為嚴飾。

Ru-Yi Translation

Before that tree there was a fragrance-pool named Light of Jewelled Flowers, which uttered the thunder-voice of the dharma. Wondrous jewels formed its banks; a million koṭis of nayutas of jewel-trees encircled it, each single tree shaped like the bodhi tree; necklaces of every jewel hung down all around; and measureless towered pavilions, all of them made of jewels, ornamented the whole awakening-site.

Chinese Source

彼香池內出大蓮華，名：普現三世一切如來莊嚴境界雲，須彌山微塵數佛於中出現。其第一佛，名：普智寶焰妙德幢，於此華上，最初得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，無量千歲演說正法成熟眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

From within that fragrance-pool there came forth a great lotus flower named Cloud of the Adorned Domain of Every Tathāgata of the Three Times Universally Manifest, in which buddhas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of Mount Sumeru appeared. The first of those buddhas was named Banner of Wondrous Virtue and the Jewel-Flame of Universal Wisdom. Upon this flower he first attained anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, and for measureless thousands of years he expounded the true dharma and brought beings to ripeness.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一一樹形如菩提樹 — each single tree shaped like the bodhi tree. A million koṭis of nayutas of jewel-trees stand around the pool, and every one of them is a copy of the tree they surround. The arrangement is the Huayan claim in landscape form. The centre is not distinguished from the circumference by being different; it is distinguished only by being the centre, and everything around it already has the same shape.

其第一佛，名：普智寶焰妙德幢 — the first of those buddhas was named Banner of Wondrous Virtue and the Jewel-Flame of Universal Wisdom. Part 1105 introduced the wheel-turning king's daughter under the name 普智焰妙德眼, Eye of Wondrous Virtue and the Flame of Universal Wisdom. The two names differ by one character inserted in the middle and one at the end — 寶 and 幢 against 眼. The sūtra has said nothing about any connection and there is no need to assume one yet. But a storyteller who has already put 妙德 from her own name into the princess has now given the buddha of that age a name that differs from the princess's by almost nothing.

Ten Thousand Years of Notice

Before the buddha there are lights, and the lights are a countdown. The first two are pure light; the nine that follow are great light.

Chinese Source

「其彼如來未成佛時，十千年前，此大蓮華放淨光明，名：現諸神通成熟眾生；若有眾生遇斯光者，心自開悟，無所不了，知十千年後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Before that tathāgata had become a buddha, ten thousand years beforehand, this great lotus flower sent out a pure light named Manifesting Spiritual Powers and Ripening Beings. If any being encountered that light, their mind opened and awoke of itself and there was nothing they did not understand, and they knew that ten thousand years later a buddha would appear.

Chinese Source

九千年前，放淨光明，名：一切眾生離垢燈；若有眾生遇斯光者，得清淨眼，見一切色，知九千年後佛當出現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Nine thousand years beforehand, it sent out a pure light named Lamp That Frees All Beings From Defilement. If any being encountered that light, they obtained a pure eye and saw every form, and they knew that nine thousand years later a buddha would appear.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The countdown that opens here runs to eleven lights in all — ten thousand years, then nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two and one thousand, and then a light seven days before. The verb phrase changes exactly once, and it changes here. These first two are 放淨光明, sent out a pure light; the nine that follow are all 放大光明, sent out a great light. Two lights to make the eye clean, and then nine to fill it. Part 1107 gives the rest.

知十千年後佛當出現 — and they knew that ten thousand years later a buddha would appear. Every one of the eleven lights ends in this same clause with a different number in it, so what the lights actually deliver is not illumination but a date. The gift each light carries — an opened mind, a clean eye, knowledge of one's own deeds — is stated first and the announcement second, as though the capacity were only the means of receiving the news.

十千年 — ten thousands of years, rather than the ordinary Chinese 一萬. The form is a calque on the Indic way of counting, and Śikṣānanda keeps it: across the whole of T279 he writes 十千 one hundred and seven times against 一萬 twenty-five. Reading it aloud, one hears a translator's hand rather than a Chinese author's.

Part 1106 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy. Part 1107 runs out the remaining nine lights of the countdown, one per thousand years and then one seven days before, and reaches the moment the seven days are up and every world shakes.
