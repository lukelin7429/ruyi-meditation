Equal to Empty Space

Good-Wealth arrives at Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury tower, throws his five limbs to the ground, and enters a gate of equality that carries his own manifested body before every buddha, every bodhisattva, and every being of the ten directions — followed by a chain of similes and certainties on how every single dharma comes to arise.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1271 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Equal to Empty Space

Good-Wealth's own prostration before the tower opens this Part; four chains of parallel clauses — equalities, similes, arisings, and freedoms from view — bring it to a close just before his understanding is named complete.

Chinese Source

善財童子以如是尊重、如是供養、如是稱讚、如是觀察、如是願力、如是想念、如是無量智慧境界，於毘盧遮那莊嚴藏大樓閣前，五體投地，暫時斂念，思惟觀察。以深信解、大願力故，入遍一切處智慧身平等門，普現其身在於一切如來前、一切菩薩前、一切善知識前、一切如來塔廟前、一切如來形像前、一切諸佛諸菩薩住處前、一切法寶前、一切聲聞辟支佛及其塔廟前、一切聖眾福田前、一切父母尊者前、一切十方眾生前，皆如上說，尊重禮讚，盡未來際無有休息。

Ru-Yi Translation

With such reverence, such offering, such praise, such contemplation, such power of vow, such mindfulness, and such a measureless realm of wisdom, Good-Wealth, before Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury tower, threw his five limbs to the ground, gathered his thoughts for a moment, and contemplated and observed. Through the power of his deep faith and understanding and his great vow, he entered the gate of the equality of the wisdom-body that pervades every place, and universally manifested his own body before every tathāgata, before every bodhisattva, before every good spiritual friend, before every stūpa and shrine of every tathāgata, before every image of every tathāgata, before every dwelling-place of every buddha and every bodhisattva, before every dharma-jewel, before every śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha and their stūpas, before every field of merit belonging to the noble assembly, before every father, mother, and elder, and before every sentient being of the ten directions — everywhere, exactly as described above, revering and praising them without rest until the very end of future time.

Chinese Source

等虛空，無邊量故；等法界，無障礙故；等實際，遍一切故；等如來，無分別故。猶如影，隨智現故；猶如夢，從思起故；猶如像，示一切故；猶如響，緣所發故；無有生，遞興謝故；無有性，隨緣轉故。

Ru-Yi Translation

Equal to empty space, since it is without bounds or measure; equal to the dharma-realm, since it is without obstruction; equal to the true limit, since it pervades everything; equal to every tathāgata, since it is without discrimination. Like a shadow, since it appears in accordance with wisdom; like a dream, since it arises from thought; like a reflected image, since it displays everything; like an echo, since it is brought forth by conditions; without birth, since it alternately arises and passes away; without a fixed nature, since it turns in accordance with conditions.

Chinese Source

又決定知一切諸報皆從業起，一切諸果皆從因起，一切諸業皆從習起，一切佛興皆從信起，一切化現諸供養事皆悉從於決定解起，一切化佛從敬心起，一切佛法從善根起，一切化身從方便起，一切佛事從大願起，一切菩薩所修諸行從迴向起，一切法界廣大莊嚴從一切智境界而起。

Ru-Yi Translation

He further knew with certainty that every karmic retribution arises entirely from karma; every result arises entirely from its cause; every karmic act arises entirely from habituated tendency; every buddha's arising arises entirely from faith; every manifested act of offering arises entirely from decisive understanding; every transformation-buddha arises from a reverent mind; every buddha-dharma arises from wholesome roots; every transformation-body arises from skillful means; every buddha-deed arises from a great vow; every practice a bodhisattva cultivates arises from the dedication of merit; and the vast adornment of the entire dharma-realm arises from the very realm of all-knowing wisdom.

Chinese Source

離於斷見，知迴向故；離於常見，知無生故；離無因見，知正因故；離顛倒見，知如實理故；離自在見，知不由他故；離自他見，知從緣起故；離邊執見，知法界無邊故；離往來見，知如影像故；離有無見，知不生滅故；離一切法見，知空無生故，知不自在故，知願力出生故；離一切相，見入無相際故。知一切法如種生芽故，如印生文故。知質如像故，知聲如響故，知境如夢故，知業如幻故。了世心現故，了果因起故，了報業集故，了知一切諸功德法皆從菩薩善巧方便所流出故。

Ru-Yi Translation

He was freed from the view of annihilation, since he knew the meaning of dedication of merit; freed from the view of permanence, since he knew non-arising; freed from the view of no cause, since he knew the true cause; freed from inverted views, since he knew the principle as it truly is; freed from the view of a sovereign self, since he knew that nothing comes from another; freed from the view of self and other, since he knew that everything arises through conditions; freed from views of extremes, since he knew the dharma-realm to be without bounds; freed from views of coming and going, since he knew all things to be like a reflected image; freed from views of existence and nonexistence, since he knew that nothing arises or perishes; freed from every view of dharmas, since he knew emptiness to be without arising, knew there is no sovereign self, and knew that everything is born from the power of vow; and freed from every characteristic, since his insight had entered the very limit that is without any mark. He knew that every dharma is like a seed giving rise to a sprout, like a seal giving rise to an imprint. He knew that substance is like a reflected image, that sound is like an echo, that every object is like a dream, and that every karmic act is like an illusion. He understood that the world manifests from mind, that every result arises from its cause, and that every retribution gathers from karma, and he fully understood that every meritorious dharma flows forth entirely from a bodhisattva's own skillful means.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

等虛空，無邊量故 — a four-part chain of equalities (empty space, the dharma-realm, the true limit, every tathāgata) opens the second passage above, followed by a six-part chain of similes (shadow, dream, reflected image, echo, no birth, no fixed nature) — ten parallel clauses in all, counted directly from the source text.

又決定知一切諸報皆從業起⋯一切智境界而起 — eleven things Good-Wealth knows with certainty to "arise from" something else, running from karmic retribution arising from karma to the whole dharma-realm's adornment arising from all-knowing wisdom itself. The following passage answers it with eleven views he is freed from — the tenth of the eleven carries three sub-clauses of its own (emptiness without arising, no sovereign self, birth from the power of vow), all counted directly against the source text.

Part 1271 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy-seven. Part 1272 turns to Good-Wealth entering this understanding completely, bowing his entire body to the ground before the tower a second time, then rising to circle it in reverence before a single further prostration.
