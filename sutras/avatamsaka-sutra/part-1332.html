Not for a Single Thought-Moment

Samantabhadra answers Good-Wealth's wonder with his own history. Through ineffably ineffable kalpas he practiced the bodhisattva path in search of all-knowing wisdom, and in each and every kalpa he served as many buddhas, held as many vast assemblies of giving in which whatever anyone asked for was granted, gave away as much wealth, and gave away cities, villages, kingdoms, thrones, wife and children, eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, flesh, hands, feet, and life itself, and as many heads, and served the tathāgatas with robes, bedding, food and drink, and medicines, going forth from home under their dharma. And in all those oceans of kalpas, he says, he cannot recall a single thought-moment in which he disobeyed the Buddha's teaching, or gave rise to a mind of anger and harm, of I and mine, of self against other, of distance from bodhi, of weariness with birth-and-death, of indolence, of obstruction, or of delusion; he abided only in the great mind of bodhi that cannot be broken.

Not for a Single Thought-Moment

Samantabhadra's account of his kalpas of practice, and the eight minds he never once gave rise to.

Chinese Source

普賢告言：「善男子！我於過去不可說不可說佛剎微塵數劫，行菩薩行，求一切智；一一劫中，為欲清淨菩提心故，承事不可說不可說佛剎微塵數佛；一一劫中，為集一切智福德具故，設不可說不可說佛剎微塵數廣大施會，一切世間咸使聞知，凡有所求悉令滿足；一一劫中，為求一切智法故，以不可說不可說佛剎微塵數財物布施；一一劫中，為求佛智故，以不可說不可說佛剎微塵數城邑、聚落、國土、王位、妻子、眷屬、眼、耳、鼻、舌、身、肉、手、足乃至身命而為布施；一一劫中，為求一切智首故，以不可說不可說佛剎微塵數頭而為布施；一一劫中，為求一切智故，於不可說不可說佛剎微塵數諸如來所，恭敬尊重，承事供養，衣服、臥具、飲食、湯藥，一切所須悉皆奉施，於其法中出家學道，修行佛法，護持正教。「善男子！我於爾所劫海中，自憶未曾於一念間不順佛教，於一念間生瞋害心、我我所心、自他差別心、遠離菩提心、於生死中起疲厭心、懶惰心、障礙心、迷惑心，唯住無上不可沮壞集一切智助道之法大菩提心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Samantabhadra said, "Good son! In the past, through kalpas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, I practiced the bodhisattva practices and sought all-knowing wisdom. In each and every kalpa, in order to purify the mind of bodhi, I served buddhas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; in each and every kalpa, in order to gather the provisions of merit for all-knowing wisdom, I held vast and great assemblies of giving as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, causing the whole world to hear of them and to know, and whatever anyone sought I caused them to have in full; in each and every kalpa, in order to seek the dharma of all-knowing wisdom, I gave in charity wealth and goods as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; in each and every kalpa, in order to seek the wisdom of a buddha, I gave in charity cities, villages, kingdoms, royal thrones, wives and children, retinues, eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, flesh, hands, and feet, and even my body and life, as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; in each and every kalpa, in order to seek the head of all-knowing wisdom, I gave in charity heads as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; and in each and every kalpa, in order to seek all-knowing wisdom, in the presence of tathāgatas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, I revered and honored them, served them and made offerings to them, offering up robes, bedding, food and drink, and medicines and everything they required, and under their dharma I went forth from home and studied the path, cultivated the buddha-dharma, and guarded and upheld the true teaching. Good son! Through all those oceans of kalpas I recall that I never, for a single thought-moment, failed to comply with the Buddha's teaching, and never, for a single thought-moment, gave rise to a mind of anger and harm, a mind of I and mine, a mind that distinguishes self from other, a mind that strays far from bodhi, a mind weary of birth-and-death, an indolent mind, an obstructed mind, or a deluded mind; I abided only in the unsurpassed great mind of bodhi that cannot be broken or destroyed and that gathers the dharmas that aid the path to all-knowing wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一一劫中 — Samantabhadra's account is built on the phrase in each and every kalpa, repeated six times, and on the measure 不可說不可說佛剎微塵數, as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, which occurs seven times in the passage, once for the kalpas themselves and once for each thing counted within them, buddhas, assemblies, wealth, possessions, heads, and tathāgatas. Both are kept in full at every occurrence. The list of what was given away runs from cities to life itself, and the gift of heads is set apart in its own clause, tied to the seeking of 一切智首, the head of all-knowing wisdom.

未曾於一念間 — the second paragraph turns from what he did to what he never once did, in a single sentence that names eight minds, from a mind of anger and harm to a deluded mind, none of which arose in him for so much as a thought-moment across all those kalpas; and it closes on the one mind in which he did abide, 不可沮壞, the mind of bodhi that cannot be broken or destroyed, a phrase the site has rendered in the same words before for a body that could not be broken. 出家學道 is given as went forth from home and studied the path, following the site's usual went forth.
