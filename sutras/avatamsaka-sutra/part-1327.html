At Last He Sees Samantabhadra

Having seen the ten signs of light, Good-Wealth is certain: he will now see Samantabhadra. He gathers all his faculties, gives rise to great diligence, and with the universal eye regards everything seen by every buddha and bodhisattva in the ten directions as a way of seeing Samantabhadra; with the eye of wisdom he looks upon Samantabhadra's path, his mind vast as space and his compassion firm as vajra, vowing to follow Samantabhadra to the end of the future. And then he sees him, seated among the assembly before the Tathāgata on a lion-throne of jeweled lotus, surrounded by bodhisattvas, extraordinary and without equal in the world, his domain of wisdom equal to the buddhas of the three times. From each and every pore of Samantabhadra's body come clouds of light that end the suffering of every being, clouds of flames of every color and fragrance, clouds of flowers, and clouds of fragrant trees, filling the dharma-realm and the realm of space.

At Last He Sees Samantabhadra

The resolve, the two eyes, the sight of Samantabhadra on his throne, and the first four of the clouds that pour from his pores.

Chinese Source

時，善財童子見此十種光明相已，即作是念：「我今必見普賢菩薩，增益善根，見一切佛；於諸菩薩廣大境界，生決定解，得一切智。」於時，善財普攝諸根，一心求見普賢菩薩，起大精進，心無退轉。即以普眼觀察十方一切諸佛、諸菩薩眾所見境界，皆作得見普賢之想；以智慧眼觀普賢道，其心廣大猶如虛空，大悲堅固猶如金剛，願盡未來常得隨逐普賢菩薩，念念隨順，修普賢行，成就智慧，入如來境，住普賢地。時，善財童子即見普賢菩薩，在如來前眾會之中，坐寶蓮華師子之座，諸菩薩眾所共圍遶，最為殊特，世無與等；智慧境界無量無邊，難測難思，等三世佛，一切菩薩無能觀察。見普賢身一一毛孔，出一切世界微塵數光明雲，遍法界、虛空界、一切世界，除滅一切眾生苦患，令諸菩薩生大歡喜；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數種種色香焰雲，遍法界、虛空界一切諸佛眾會道場，而以普熏；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數雜華雲，遍法界、虛空界一切諸佛眾會道場，雨眾妙華；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數香樹雲，遍法界、虛空界一切諸佛眾會道場，雨眾妙香；

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth the youth, having seen these ten kinds of sign of light, at once thought, "Now I shall surely see Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, increase my wholesome roots, and see every buddha; toward the vast domain of the bodhisattvas I shall give rise to definitive understanding and attain all-knowing wisdom." Thereupon Good-Wealth gathered in all his faculties and sought single-mindedly to see Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, giving rise to great diligence, his mind never turning back. With the universal eye he at once observed the domains seen by all the buddhas and all the assemblies of bodhisattvas in the ten directions, and regarded them all as the seeing of Samantabhadra; with the eye of wisdom he looked upon the path of Samantabhadra, his mind vast and great like space, his great compassion firm like vajra, vowing that to the end of the future he would ever follow after Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, complying with him thought after thought, cultivating Samantabhadra's practices, accomplishing wisdom, entering the domain of the tathāgatas, and abiding on the ground of Samantabhadra. At that time, Good-Wealth the youth at once saw Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, within the assembly before the Tathāgata, seated upon a lion-throne of jeweled lotus, surrounded by the hosts of bodhisattvas, most extraordinary and without equal in the world; the domain of his wisdom was measureless and boundless, hard to fathom and hard to conceive, equal to the buddhas of the three times, such that no bodhisattva was able to observe it. He saw that from each and every pore of Samantabhadra's body there came forth clouds of light as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, pervading the dharma-realm, the realm of space, and every world, extinguishing the suffering and affliction of every sentient being and causing every bodhisattva to give rise to great joy; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of flames of every color and fragrance as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the sites of awakening of the assemblies of all the buddhas throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space and perfuming them universally; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of mixed flowers as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the sites of awakening of the assemblies of all the buddhas throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space and raining down every wondrous flower; he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of fragrant trees as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the sites of awakening of the assemblies of all the buddhas throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space and raining down every wondrous fragrance;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

即見普賢菩薩 — the meeting toward which the whole chapter has moved is given in six characters, he at once saw Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, and it comes only after Good-Wealth has already been told to regard everything seen by every buddha as the seeing of Samantabhadra. The two eyes named in that preparation are distinct: 普眼, the universal eye, takes in the domains of all the buddhas and bodhisattvas; 智慧眼, the eye of wisdom, looks upon Samantabhadra's path. Both are rendered with the terms the site has used for them before.

見一一毛孔 — the clouds from Samantabhadra's pores begin here and run on as a single sentence far beyond this part, its items separated by semicolons. This part carries the first four of them, light, fragrant flames, flowers, and fragrant trees, and ends on the semicolon after the fourth so that the sentence is broken only at one of its own joints; the translation therefore ends without a full stop, and Part 1328 resumes the same sentence with the next item. Each item carries the same measure, 微塵數, as numerous as the fine motes of dust, and the same reach, 遍法界、虛空界, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, and both are kept at every occurrence.
