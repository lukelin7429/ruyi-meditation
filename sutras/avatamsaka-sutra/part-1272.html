Dwelling-Place, Part One

A second prostration, a rising, and a reverent circling bring Good-Wealth to a single thought — the opening thirty-one lines of a litany naming everything this great tower is the dwelling-place of, a list long enough to carry on well past this Part.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1272 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Dwelling-Place, Part One

Good-Wealth's second prostration and his rise into reverent circling open this Part; thirty-one lines of an unbroken litany — the first portion of a much longer list — carry it to its end, still open, still unfinished.

Chinese Source

善財童子入如是智，端心潔念；於樓觀前，舉體投地，慇懃頂禮；不思議善根流注身心，清涼悅懌。從地而起，一心瞻仰，目不暫捨，合掌圍遶，經無量匝，作是念言：「此大樓閣，是解空、無相、無願者之所住處；是於一切法無分別者之所住處；是了法界無差別者之所住處；是知一切眾生不可得者之所住處；是知一切法無生者之所住處；是不著一切世間者之所住處；是不著一切窟宅者之所住處；是不樂一切聚落者之所住處；是不依一切境界者之所住處；是離一切想者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

Good-Wealth, having entered this wisdom, settled his mind and purified his thought; before the tower he threw his entire body to the ground, bowing with deep devotion. Inconceivable wholesome roots flowed through his body and mind, cool and joyful. Rising from the ground, he gazed single-mindedly, never once looking away, circled the tower with palms joined a measureless number of times, and formed this thought: "This great tower is the dwelling-place of one who has understood emptiness, signlessness, and wishlessness; the dwelling-place of one who is without discrimination toward every dharma; the dwelling-place of one who has understood the dharma-realm to be without distinction; the dwelling-place of one who knows that every sentient being cannot be apprehended; the dwelling-place of one who knows that every dharma is unborn; the dwelling-place of one who is unattached to every world; the dwelling-place of one who is unattached to every dwelling and lair; the dwelling-place of one who takes no delight in any village or town; the dwelling-place of one who relies on no objects whatsoever; the dwelling-place of one who is free of every notion;

Chinese Source

是知一切法無自性者之所住處；是斷一切分別業者之所住處；是離一切想心、意、識者之所住處；是不入不出一切道者之所住處；是入一切甚深般若波羅蜜者之所住處；是能以方便住普門法界者之所住處；是息滅一切煩惱火者之所住處；是以增上慧除斷一切見、愛、慢者之所住處；是出生一切諸禪解脫三昧通明而遊戲者之所住處；是觀察一切菩薩三昧境界者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who knows every dharma to be without any nature of its own; the dwelling-place of one who has severed every discriminating karma; the dwelling-place of one who is free of every notion of mind, intellect, and consciousness; the dwelling-place of one who neither enters nor leaves any path; the dwelling-place of one who has entered the profound prajñā-pāramitā entirely; the dwelling-place of one who is able, through skillful means, to abide within the universal gate that is the dharma-realm; the dwelling-place of one who has extinguished the fire of every affliction; the dwelling-place of one who, through superior wisdom, has cut off every view, craving, and conceit; the dwelling-place of one who brings forth every dhyāna, liberation, samādhi, superknowledge, and clarity, and plays freely within them; the dwelling-place of one who contemplates the very realm of every bodhisattva's own samādhi;

Chinese Source

是安住一切如來所者之所住處；是以一劫入一切劫，以一切劫入一劫，而不壞其相者之所住處；是以一剎入一切剎，以一切剎入一剎，而不壞其相者之所住處；是以一法入一切法，以一切法入一法，而不壞其相者之所住處；是以一眾生入一切眾生，以一切眾生入一眾生，而不壞其相者之所住處；是以一佛入一切佛，以一切佛入一佛，而不壞其相者之所住處；是於一念中而知一切三世者之所住處；是於一念中往詣一切國土者之所住處；是於一切眾生前悉現其身者之所住處；是心常利益一切世間者之所住處；是能遍至一切處者之所住處；

Ru-Yi Translation

the dwelling-place of one who abides wherever every tathāgata abides; the dwelling-place of one who enters a single kalpa into every kalpa and every kalpa into a single kalpa without destroying their character; the dwelling-place of one who enters a single land into every land and every land into a single land without destroying their character; the dwelling-place of one who enters a single dharma into every dharma and every dharma into a single dharma without destroying their character; the dwelling-place of one who enters a single sentient being into every sentient being and every sentient being into a single sentient being without destroying their character; the dwelling-place of one who enters a single buddha into every buddha and every buddha into a single buddha without destroying their character; the dwelling-place of one who, within a single thought, knows all three times; the dwelling-place of one who, within a single thought, travels to every land; the dwelling-place of one who manifests his own body before every sentient being; the dwelling-place of one whose mind constantly benefits every world; the dwelling-place of one who is able to reach every place universally;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

此大樓閣，是解空、無相、無願者之所住處 — the famous litany opens here, each line built on the same frame, 是⋯者之所住處 ("the dwelling-place of one who⋯"). This Part carries the first thirty-one lines, counted directly against the source text by splitting on every 「；」; the quotation itself stays open at the end of this Part, exactly as it does in the source, and continues without a break into Part 1273.

通明 in line nineteen is rendered conservatively as "superknowledge, and clarity" rather than mapped onto any specific numbered doctrinal set (such as the three insights or six penetrations), since the compound's precise scope here cannot be verified with confidence from this passage alone.
