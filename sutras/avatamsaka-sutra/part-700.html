Ten Kinds of Mind Free of Doubt Begin

Each of the ten minds free of doubt takes the same shape: a bodhisattva resolves on something vast — drawing in every sentient being through the pāramitās, adorning every world with light, cultivating practice for a number of beings beyond every possible reckoning — and the text declares, after each one, that doubt arising in response to it is simply not possible.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 700 — the first seven minds free of doubt Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Doubt Declared Impossible, Ten Times Over

Each entry follows an identical pattern: a bodhisattva's own resolve, stated in full, followed by the same closing formula — that doubt at this exact moment is decisively impossible. The resolves themselves range from ordinary generosity to a vow to teach a number of beings that exceeds every unit of counting this sūtra has ever used, including "unspeakably unspeakable" itself.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩發十種無疑心，於一切佛法心無疑惑。何等為十？所謂：菩薩摩訶薩發如是心：『我當以布施，攝一切眾生；以戒、忍、精進、禪定、智慧、慈、悲、喜、捨，攝一切眾生。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第一發無疑心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『未來諸佛出興于世，我當一切承事供養。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第二發無疑心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『我當以種種奇妙光明網，周遍莊嚴一切世界。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第三發無疑心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『我當盡未來劫修菩薩行。無數、無量、無邊、無等、不可數、不可稱、不可思、不可量、不可說、不可說不可說，過諸算數，究竟法界、虛空界一切眾生，我當悉以無上教化調伏法而成熟之。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第四發無疑心。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva gives rise to ten kinds of mind free of doubt, so that their mind harbors no doubt or confusion regarding any dharma of the Buddha. What are these ten? Namely: a bodhisattva-mahāsattva gives rise to a thought such as this: 'I shall draw in every sentient being through generosity; I shall draw in every sentient being through moral discipline, patience, diligence, meditative concentration, wisdom, loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the first mind free of doubt. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'Whatever buddhas appear in the world in the future, I shall attend upon and make offerings to every one of them.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the second mind free of doubt. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'I shall adorn every world entirely with every kind of wondrous net of light.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the third mind free of doubt. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'I shall cultivate bodhisattva practice throughout every future kalpa. Sentient beings without number, without measure, without limit, without equal, uncountable, inexpressible, inconceivable, immeasurable, unspeakable, and unspeakably unspeakable, beyond every reckoning, throughout the entire dharma-realm and realm of empty space — I shall bring every one of them to ripeness entirely through unsurpassed teaching and taming.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the fourth mind free of doubt.

Chinese Source

菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『我當修菩薩行，滿大誓願，具一切智，安住其中。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第五發無疑心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『我當普為一切世間行菩薩行，為一切法清淨光明，照明一切所有佛法。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第六發無疑心。菩薩摩訶薩又作是念：『我當知一切法皆是佛法，隨眾生心，為其演說，悉令開悟。』發此心時，決定無疑；若生疑心，無有是處。是為第七發無疑心。

Ru-Yi Translation

A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'I shall cultivate bodhisattva practice, fulfill my great vow, become complete in all-knowing wisdom, and abide within it.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the fifth mind free of doubt. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'I shall universally carry out bodhisattva practice on behalf of every world, becoming a pure light for every dharma, illuminating every dharma of the Buddha there is.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the sixth mind free of doubt. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva further thinks: 'I shall know that every dharma is entirely the Buddha's own dharma, and, according to the minds of sentient beings, expound it for them, entirely bringing them to awakened understanding.' At the moment this thought arises, it is decisively free of doubt; that any doubt should arise is simply not possible. This is the seventh mind free of doubt.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

無數、無量、無邊、無等、不可數、不可稱、不可思、不可量、不可說、不可說不可說 — without number, without measure, without limit, without equal, uncountable, inexpressible, inconceivable, immeasurable, unspeakable, and unspeakably unspeakable. The fourth vow pauses to stack ten separate terms for "beyond counting" before naming its actual object — the totality of sentient beings — as if ordinary language needed this much preparation before it could even gesture at the scale involved, let alone the vow to teach every one of them.

Part 700 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-eight, 離世間品, in fascicle fifty-four. Part 701 completes the ten kinds of mind free of doubt with vows to enter every dharma without obstruction and to attain perfect awakening beyond every mark, then opens ten kinds of inconceivable dharma.
