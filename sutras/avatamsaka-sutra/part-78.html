The Ten Abodes Complete, Prince and Consecration

The final two abodes complete the image of royal succession first named back at the fourth abode, Noble Birth. The Abode of the Dharma-Prince masters ten kinds of skillful knowledge and enters directly into the Dharma-king's own domain — its palace, its rules, its fearlessness. The Abode of Consecration then reaches a wisdom so vast that not even a Dharma-prince bodhisattva can fully fathom it, completing the full sequence of ten abodes.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 78 — chapter 15 continues, abodes 9–10 of 10 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Ninth Abode: The Dharma-Prince

This bodhisattva skillfully knows the arising of birth, affliction, and habitual tendency, and how to proclaim both worldly and ultimate truth — deepened further by entering ten aspects of the Dharma-king's own domain: its palace, its rules, its consecration, its fearlessness, even its repose.

Chinese Source

「佛子！云何為菩薩王子住？此菩薩善知十種法。何者為十？所謂：善知諸眾生受生、善知諸煩惱現起、善知習氣相續、善知所行方便、善知無量法、善解諸威儀、善知世界差別、善知前際後際事、善知演說世諦、善知演說第一義諦。是為十。佛子！此菩薩應勸學十種法。何者為十？所謂：法王處善巧、法王處軌度、法王處宮殿、法王處趣入、法王處觀察、法王灌頂、法王力持、法王無畏、法王宴寢、法王讚歎。何以故？欲令增進，心無障礙；有所聞法，即自開解，不由他教故。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! What is meant by the bodhisattva's Abode of the Dharma-Prince? This bodhisattva skillfully knows ten kinds of dharmas. What are these ten? Namely: skillfully knowing how every living being receives birth; skillfully knowing the arising of every affliction; skillfully knowing the continuity of habitual tendencies; skillfully knowing the skillful means one practices; skillfully knowing immeasurable dharmas; skillfully understanding every deportment; skillfully knowing the distinctions among worlds; skillfully knowing matters of the past and the future; skillfully knowing how to proclaim the worldly truth; and skillfully knowing how to proclaim the ultimate truth. These are the ten. Son of the Buddha! This bodhisattva should be urged to study ten further dharmas. What are these ten? Namely: skillful mastery of the Dharma-king's own domain; the Dharma-king's own rules and measures; the Dharma-king's own palace; entering into the Dharma-king's own domain; contemplating the Dharma-king's own domain; the Dharma-king's own consecration; the Dharma-king's own sustaining power; the Dharma-king's own fearlessness; the Dharma-king's own repose; and the Dharma-king's own praise. Why is this? It is in order to cause one to advance further, the mind free of any obstruction at all; so that, whatever Dharma he hears, he opens and understands it entirely of himself, and not by means of another's teaching."

The Tenth Abode: Consecration

The final abode reaches a wisdom of astonishing scale — shaking, illumining, and sustaining countless worlds, opening the Dharma to countless living beings — a domain of body, wisdom, and spiritual power so vast that the sūtra declares it unknowable even to a Dharma-prince bodhisattva, one stage below. This completes the full sequence of ten abodes.

Chinese Source

「佛子！云何為菩薩灌頂住？此菩薩得成就十種智。何者為十？所謂：震動無數世界、照耀無數世界、住持無數世界、往詣無數世界、嚴淨無數世界、開示無數眾生、觀察無數眾生、知無數眾生根、令無數眾生趣入、令無數眾生調伏。是為十。佛子！此菩薩身及身業，神通變現，過去智、未來智、現在智成就佛土，心境界、智境界皆不可知，乃至法王子菩薩亦不能知。佛子！此菩薩應勸學諸佛十種智。何者為十？所謂：三世智、佛法智、法界無礙智、法界無邊智、充滿一切世界智、普照一切世界智、住持一切世界智、知一切眾生智、知一切法智、知無邊諸佛智。何以故？欲令增長一切種智；有所聞法，即自開解，不由他教故。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! What is meant by the bodhisattva's Abode of Consecration? This bodhisattva attains and accomplishes ten kinds of wisdom. What are these ten? Namely: shaking countless worlds; illumining countless worlds; sustaining countless worlds; going to countless worlds; adorning and purifying countless worlds; opening up and revealing the Dharma to countless living beings; observing and contemplating countless living beings; knowing the faculties of countless living beings; causing countless living beings to enter the path; and causing countless living beings to be tamed and subdued. These are the ten. Son of the Buddha! This bodhisattva's own body and bodily deeds, his spiritual powers and transformative manifestations, his wisdom of the past, his wisdom of the future, and his wisdom of the present that accomplishes buddha-lands, the domain of his own mind and the domain of his own wisdom — all of these cannot be known, not even by a Dharma-prince bodhisattva. Son of the Buddha! This bodhisattva should be urged to study every buddha's ten kinds of wisdom. What are these ten? Namely: the wisdom of the three periods of time; the wisdom of the Buddha's own Dharma; the unobstructed wisdom of the Dharma-realm; the boundless wisdom of the Dharma-realm; the wisdom that fills every world entirely; the wisdom that universally illumines every world; the wisdom that sustains every world; the wisdom that knows every living being; the wisdom that knows every dharma; and the wisdom that knows every buddha throughout the boundless. Why is this? It is in order to cause the growth of omniscience itself; so that, whatever Dharma he hears, he opens and understands it entirely of himself, and not by means of another's teaching."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This closes the prose teaching of the full Ten Abodes, from the first stirring of the awakening-mind through to a wisdom the text itself declares unknowable even one stage below. The image of royal succession, first opened at the fourth abode's Noble Birth, reaches its natural conclusion here: consecration (灌頂) is the same rite Part 50 described the tenth-ground bodhisattva receiving from every buddha's own hand, poured now upon a bodhisattva only at the very first of the sūtra's four great stages of the path — Abodes, Practices, Dedications, and Grounds. Read against that later scene, the Ten Abodes' own consecration reads less as an ending than as the first of several such moments the path will offer, each one a genuine culmination in its own right and, at the same time, only a beginning relative to what still lies ahead.
