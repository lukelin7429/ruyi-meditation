Pure Meditative Absorption, Pure Wisdom, Six Pāramitās Complete

Ten kinds of pure meditative absorption move from going forth from home to a final entry of sovereign, playful ease entered through knowing there is no self. Ten kinds of pure wisdom then close this six-part sequence, which purified giving, precepts, patience, diligence, meditative absorption, and now wisdom in turn — each pāramitā given its own set of ten refinements.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 772 — pure meditative absorption, pure wisdom, six pāramitās complete Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Wisdom Beyond Every Adversary

The seventh kind of pure wisdom stands apart from the others by naming exactly who cannot reach it — no māra, no follower of another path, no śrāvaka, no solitary awakened one — defining this wisdom's purity not by what it contains but by whose comprehension it categorically exceeds, because it penetrates deeply into every tathāgata's own wisdom alone.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種清淨禪。何等為十？所謂：常樂出家清淨禪，捨一切所有故；得真善友清淨禪，示教正道故；住阿蘭若忍風雨等清淨禪，離我、我所故；離憒閙眾生清淨禪，常樂寂靜故；心業調柔清淨禪，守護諸根故；心智寂滅清淨禪，一切音聲、諸禪定刺不能亂故；覺道方便清淨禪，觀察一切皆現證故；離於味著清淨禪，不捨欲界故；發起通明清淨禪，知一切眾生根性故；自在遊戲清淨禪，入佛三昧，知無我故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住其中，則得如來無上大清淨禪。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of pure meditative absorption. What are these ten? Namely: the pure absorption of constantly delighting in going forth from home, because it gives up everything it possesses; the pure absorption of attaining a true, good friend, because it reveals and teaches the right path; the pure absorption of dwelling in the forest hermitage, enduring wind and rain and the like, because it is free of self and what belongs to self; the pure absorption of staying far from noisy, crowded sentient beings, because it forever delights in stillness; the pure absorption of a mind and its deeds gentle and pliant, because it guards every faculty; the pure absorption of a mind and wisdom at cessation, because no sound and no thorn of any meditative absorption is able to disturb it; the pure absorption of the skillful means of the path of awakening, because it observes everything as directly realized; the pure absorption free of savoring attachment, because it never abandons the realm of desire; the pure absorption that gives rise to spiritual penetration and clarity, because it knows every sentient being's own faculty and nature; and the pure absorption of sovereign, playful ease, because it enters a buddha's own samādhi, knowing there is no self. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within these, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great, pure meditative absorption.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種清淨慧。何等為十？所謂：知一切因清淨慧，不壞果報故；知一切緣清淨慧，不違和合故；知不斷不常清淨慧，了達緣起皆如實故；拔一切見清淨慧，於眾生相無取捨故；觀一切眾生心行清淨慧，了知如幻故；廣大辯才清淨慧，分別諸法、問答無礙故；一切諸魔、外道、聲聞、獨覺所不能知清淨慧，深入一切如來智故；見一切佛微妙法身、見一切眾生本性清淨、見一切法皆悉寂滅、見一切剎同於虛空清淨慧，知一切相皆無礙故；一切總持、辯才、方便波羅蜜清淨慧，令得一切最勝智故；一念相應金剛智了一切法平等清淨慧，得一切法最尊智故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住其中，則得如來無障礙大智慧。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of pure wisdom. What are these ten? Namely: the pure wisdom of knowing every cause, because it never damages the resulting fruit; the pure wisdom of knowing every condition, because it never contradicts their coming together; the pure wisdom of knowing neither annihilation nor permanence, because it fully understands dependent origination exactly as it truly is; the pure wisdom that uproots every view, because it neither grasps nor rejects the mark of any sentient being; the pure wisdom of observing every sentient being's own mental conduct, because it fully understands it as exactly like an illusion; the pure wisdom of vast eloquence, because it discerns every dharma and answers every question without any obstruction; the pure wisdom that no māra, follower of another path, śrāvaka, or solitary awakened one is able to know, because it penetrates deeply into every tathāgata's own wisdom; the pure wisdom of seeing every buddha's own subtle and wondrous dharma-body, seeing that every sentient being's own root nature is pure, seeing that every dharma is entirely still and extinguished, and seeing that every land is exactly the same as empty space, because it knows that every mark is entirely unobstructed; the pure wisdom of every dhāraṇī, eloquence, and skillful-means pāramitā, because it causes the attainment of every most excellent wisdom; and the pure wisdom of diamond wisdom in accord with a single thought, fully understanding the equality of every dharma, because it attains the most revered wisdom of every dharma. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within these, they attain the tathāgata's own unobstructed, great wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一切諸魔、外道、聲聞、獨覺所不能知清淨慧，深入一切如來智故 — the pure wisdom that no māra, follower of another path, śrāvaka, or solitary awakened one is able to know, because it penetrates deeply into every tathāgata's own wisdom. This entry closes the entire six-part purification sequence — giving, precepts, patience, diligence, meditative absorption, and now wisdom — on a wisdom defined by its exclusivity: reserved for the tathāgata's own domain alone, beyond even the reach of solitary awakened ones.
