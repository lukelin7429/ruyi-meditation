Have You Seen This Power of Mine

The hand on his head confers gates of samādhi as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, each attended by as many samādhis again, and in each of them Good-Wealth sees oceans of buddhas never seen before, gathers the aids to the path, gives rise to the finest dharmas and the great vows, enters oceans of vows, abides on the essential path of departure, cultivates the practices, rouses great diligence, and gains the pure light of all-knowing wisdom. And what Samantabhadra does here before Vairocana in the Sahā world he does in every world and in every mote of dust of every world, before every buddha, laying his hand on Good-Wealth's head, and what is gained is everywhere the same. Then the first words between them: Have you seen this power of mine? So it is, I have seen it, Great Sage, and only a tathāgata could know it.

Have You Seen This Power of Mine

What the touch confers, the touch repeated in every world, and the first exchange of words between Samantabhadra and Good-Wealth.

Chinese Source

既摩頂已，善財即得一切佛剎微塵數三昧門，各以一切佛剎微塵數三昧而為眷屬；一一三昧，悉見昔所未見一切佛剎微塵數佛大海，集一切佛剎微塵數一切智助道具，生一切佛剎微塵數一切智上妙法，發一切佛剎微塵數一切智大誓願，入一切佛剎微塵數大願海，住一切佛剎微塵數一切智出要道，修一切佛剎微塵數諸菩薩所修行，起一切佛剎微塵數一切智大精進，得一切佛剎微塵數一切智淨光明。如此娑婆世界毘盧遮那佛所，普賢菩薩摩善財頂；如是十方所有世界，及彼世界一一塵中一切世界一切佛所，普賢菩薩悉亦如是摩善財頂，所得法門亦皆同等。爾時，普賢菩薩摩訶薩告善財言：「善男子！汝見我此神通力不？」「唯然！已見。大聖！此不思議大神通事，唯是如來之所能知。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Once the crown of his head had been touched, Good-Wealth at once attained gates of samādhi as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, each of them having samādhis as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land as its retinue; and in each and every samādhi he saw, as never before, great oceans of buddhas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, gathered aids to the path of all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, gave rise to supreme and wondrous dharmas of all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, made great vows of all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, entered oceans of great vows as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, abided on essential paths of departure to all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, cultivated practices cultivated by the bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, roused great diligence toward all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, and attained pure lights of all-knowing wisdom as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land. As here in the Sahā world, in the presence of the Buddha Vairocana, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva touched Good-Wealth's head, so in every world of the ten directions, and in every world within each and every mote of dust of those worlds, in the presence of every buddha, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva likewise touched Good-Wealth's head in the same way, and the gates of the dharma he attained were everywhere the same. At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva said to Good-Wealth, "Good son! Have you seen this power of spiritual penetration of mine?" "So it is! I have seen it. Great Sage, this inconceivable deed of great spiritual penetration is such as only a tathāgata is able to know."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一切佛剎微塵數 — the measure occurs eleven times in the sentence that describes what the touch confers, once for the gates of samādhi, once for their retinue, and once for each of the nine things seen, gathered, produced, or attained within each samādhi. The translation repeats it in full at every occurrence, as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, because the sentence is built on the repetition; to thin it would be to translate a different sentence.

摩善財頂 — the touch is then multiplied like everything else in this fascicle: what happens before Vairocana in the Sahā world happens before every buddha in every world and in every mote of dust of every world, and 所得法門亦皆同等, what is attained is everywhere the same. The exchange that follows is the first speech between the two, and it is short: Samantabhadra's question of eight characters, 汝見我此神通力不, and Good-Wealth's answer, which begins with 唯然, so it is, and ends by placing the knowledge of what he has seen beyond himself, 唯是如來之所能知. Both are kept as short in English as the source allows.
