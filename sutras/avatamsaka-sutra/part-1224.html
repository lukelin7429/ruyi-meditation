Buddhas Such as These

The maiden reveals herself at last — Gopā herself, all along — and then names forty-eight buddhas she served after Victorious Sun Body's own passing, a list standing in for a total the text itself places beyond counting.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1224 — fascicle 75 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Buddhas Such as These

The last identity in the story is Gopā's own, and it arrives almost in passing, folded into the opening of a list that will run to forty-eight names before it even claims to be complete.

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! The maiden Wondrous Virtue, who together with the wheel-turning king Awesome Virtue Lord made the four kinds of offering to the tathāgata Victorious Sun Body — that was myself. After that buddha's own parinirvāṇa, within that world, six billion hundred-thousand nayutas of buddhas appeared in the world, and I, together with the king, served and made offerings to every one of them. The first of these buddhas was named Pure Body; the next, Moon of All-Knowing Wisdom, Body of Light; the next, King of the Light of Jambūnada Gold; the next, Body Adorned with Every Mark; the next, Wondrous Moonlight; the next, Banner of Wisdom's Contemplation; the next, Great Light of Wisdom; the next, Vajra Nārāyaṇa Diligence; the next, Unsurpassed Power of Wisdom; the next, Universally Serene Wisdom; the next, Cloud of Stainless, Supreme Wisdom; the next, Lion of Wisdom's Light; the next, Topknot of Light; the next, Banner of Merit's Light; the next, Banner of the Sun of Wisdom; the next, Body of the Blossoming Jeweled Lotus; the next, Light Adorned and Purified by Merit; the next, Cloud of Wisdom's Flame; the next, Moon of Universal Illumination; the next, Wondrous Voice of the Adorned Canopy; the next, Lion's Courageous Light of Wisdom; the next, Moon of the Dharma-Realm; the next, Manifesting Reflections in Empty Space to Awaken the Minds of Every Being; the next, Forever Scenting the Fragrance of Quiescence; the next, Universally Resounding the Voice of Tranquility; the next, Mountain of Ambrosia; the next, Voice of the Ocean of Dharma; the next, Firm and Steadfast Net; the next, Topknot of the Buddha's Reflection; the next, Moonlight Curl; the next, Mouth of Eloquence; the next, Wisdom of the Awakening Flower; the next, Mountain of Jeweled Flame; the next, Star of Merit; the next, Banner of the Jeweled Moon; the next, Body of Samādhi; the next, King of Jeweled Light; the next, Universal Practice of Wisdom; the next, Lamp of the Ocean of Flame; the next, King of the Stainless Dharma-Voice; the next, Banner of Renown, Merit Beyond Compare; the next, Long Arm; the next, Moon Pure with the Original Vow; the next, Lamp Illumining the Meaning; the next, Profound and Far-Reaching Voice; the next, King of Vairocana's Supreme Treasury; the next, Banner of Every Vehicle; and the next, Wondrous Lotus of the Dharma-Ocean. Son of the Buddha! Within that kalpa, buddhas such as these — six billion hundred-thousand nayutas of them — appeared in the world, and I drew near to every one of them, served them, and made offerings to them.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

佛子！彼劫中，有如是等六十億百千那由他佛出興于世 — Son of the Buddha! Within that kalpa, buddhas such as these — six billion hundred-thousand nayutas of them — appeared in the world. Only forty-eight of that number are ever named. Every other one, the text folds into a single word, 等, "such as these" — forty-eight standing in for a total no list could ever actually hold.
