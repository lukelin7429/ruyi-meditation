Not Long From Now

Good-Wealth heads toward Kapilavastu holding fast to the liberation he has just been given, and arrives at a hall where a spirit named Sorrowless Virtue, with ten thousand palace-guardian spirits at her side, greets him with a cascade of predictions, each one promising an attainment not long from now.

Not Long From Now

Before Good-Wealth ever reaches the teacher he was sent to find, a spirit at the threshold of Kapilavastu greets him with a run of predictions about what lies just ahead of him.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子向迦毘羅城，思惟修習受生解脫，增長廣大，憶念不捨。漸次遊行，至菩薩集會普現法界光明講堂，其中有神，號：無憂德，與一萬主宮殿神俱，來迎善財，作如是言：「善來丈夫！有大智慧，有大勇猛，能修菩薩不可思議自在解脫，心恒不捨廣大誓願，善能觀察諸法境界；安住法城，入於無量諸方便門，成就如來功德大海；得妙辯才，善調眾生，獲聖智身，恒順修行，知諸眾生心行差別，令其歡喜趣向佛道。「我觀仁者修諸妙行心無暫懈，威儀所行悉皆清淨，汝當不久得諸如來清淨莊嚴無上三業，以諸相好莊嚴其身，以十力智瑩飾其心，遊諸世間。我觀仁者勇猛精進而無有比，不久當得普見三世一切諸佛聽受其法，不久當得一切菩薩禪定解脫諸三昧樂，不久當入諸佛如來甚深解脫。何以故？見善知識親近供養，聽受其教，憶念修行，不懈不退，無憂無悔，無有障礙，魔及魔民不能為難，不久當成無上果故。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth, heading toward the city of Kapilavastu, contemplated and cultivated the liberation of taking birth, causing it to increase and grow vast, calling it to mind without ever letting it go. Travelling onward by stages, he arrived at the Lecture Hall of the Bodhisattva Assembly Universally Manifesting the Dharma-Realm's Light. Within it there was a spirit, named Sorrowless Virtue, who, together with ten thousand palace-guardian spirits, came out to welcome Good-Wealth, and spoke to him thus: "Welcome, great hero! You possess great wisdom, and possess great courage; you are able to cultivate the bodhisattva's inconceivable and sovereign liberation, your mind forever holding fast to vast and expansive vows, skilfully able to contemplate the domain of every dharma; abiding firmly in the city of dharma, you have entered measureless gates of skilful means, and accomplished the great ocean of the tathāgata's own merit; you have attained wondrous eloquence, are skilled at taming beings, have obtained the noble body of wisdom, and forever act in accordance with your cultivation, knowing the differing mental conduct of every being, and causing them to rejoice and advance toward the path of buddhahood. "I observe that you, sir, cultivate every wondrous practice with a mind that never slackens even for an instant, and that your every dignified bearing and conduct is entirely pure; before long, you shall attain the pure and adorned, unsurpassed three actions of every tathāgata, adorning your body with every excellent mark, and burnishing your mind with the wisdom of the ten powers, as you journey through every world. "I observe that you, sir, are courageous and vigorous beyond all compare; before long, you shall universally behold every buddha of the three times and hear and receive their dharma; before long, you shall attain the joy of every bodhisattva's dhyāna, liberation, and every samādhi; before long, you shall enter the profound liberation of every buddha and tathāgata. "Why is this? Because, having seen a good spiritual friend, drawn near to her and made offerings, heard and received her teaching, called it to mind and cultivated it, never slackening, never retreating, without sorrow, without regret, and without any obstruction — Māra and Māra's own subjects will be unable to trouble you — before long, you shall accomplish the unsurpassed fruit."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Counted directly, the word 不久, before long, opens five separate predictions in a row — the three tathāgata-actions, seeing every buddha, the joy of every samādhi, entering every buddha's profound liberation, and the unsurpassed fruit itself. This spirit is not yet the teacher Good-Wealth was sent to find; she meets him at the threshold and tells him, in effect, how close he already is.
