Ten Signs, Ten Lights

As Good-Wealth arouses the eleven minds, three powers meet, his own wholesome roots, the blessing of all the tathāgatas, and the wholesome roots he shares with Samantabhadra, and he sees ten auspicious signs: every buddha-land pure, and in it every tathāgata attaining perfect enlightenment, no evil destinies, lotuses everywhere, every being pure in body and mind, every being kind to every other, every mind constantly mindful of the Buddha. Then ten signs of light: from every mote of dust in every world come clouds of nets and wheels of buddha-light, clouds of fragrance praising Samantabhadra's vows, clouds of suns, moons, and stars shining with his light, and clouds of the bodies of beings, bodhisattvas, and tathāgatas that fill the dharma-realm.

Ten Signs, Ten Lights

Two lists of ten — the signs that appear as Good-Wealth arouses his mind, and the lights that pour from every mote of dust.

Chinese Source

善財童子起如是心時，由自善根力、一切如來所加被力、普賢菩薩同善根力故，見十種瑞相。何等為十？所謂：見一切佛剎清淨，一切如來成正等覺；見一切佛剎清淨，無諸惡道；見一切佛剎清淨，眾妙蓮華以為嚴飾；見一切佛剎清淨，一切眾生身心清淨；見一切佛剎清淨，種種眾寶之所莊嚴；見一切佛剎清淨，一切眾生諸相嚴身；見一切佛剎清淨，諸莊嚴雲以覆其上；見一切佛剎清淨，一切眾生互起慈心，遞相利益，不為惱害；見一切佛剎清淨，道場莊嚴；見一切佛剎清淨，一切眾生心常念佛。是為十。又見十種光明相。何等為十？所謂：見一切世界所有微塵，一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數佛光明網雲，周遍照耀；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數佛光明輪雲，種種色相周遍法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數佛色像寶雲，周遍法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數佛光焰輪雲，周遍法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數眾妙香雲，周遍十方，稱讚普賢一切行願大功德海；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數日月星宿雲，皆放普賢菩薩光明，遍照法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數一切眾生身色像雲，放佛光明，遍照法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數一切佛色像摩尼雲，周遍法界；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數菩薩身色像雲，充滿法界，令一切眾生皆得出離、所願滿足；一一塵中，出一切世界微塵數如來身色像雲，說一切佛廣大誓願，周遍法界。是為十。

Ru-Yi Translation

When Good-Wealth the youth aroused such minds as these, through the power of his own wholesome roots, the power of the blessing of all the tathāgatas, and the power of the wholesome roots he shares with Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, he saw ten kinds of auspicious sign. What are the ten? That is to say: he saw every buddha-land pure, with every tathāgata attaining perfect enlightenment; he saw every buddha-land pure, without any evil destinies; he saw every buddha-land pure, adorned with wondrous lotus flowers; he saw every buddha-land pure, with every sentient being pure in body and mind; he saw every buddha-land pure, adorned with every kind of jewel; he saw every buddha-land pure, with every sentient being adorned in body with the marks; he saw every buddha-land pure, with clouds of adornment covering it above; he saw every buddha-land pure, with every sentient being giving rise to loving-kindness toward every other, benefiting one another, and doing one another no harm; he saw every buddha-land pure, its site of awakening adorned; and he saw every buddha-land pure, with the mind of every sentient being constantly mindful of the Buddha. These are the ten. He saw, moreover, ten kinds of sign of light. What are the ten? That is to say: he saw that from each and every mote of dust of all the worlds there came forth clouds of nets of buddha-light as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, shining everywhere; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of wheels of buddha-light as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, their many colors pervading the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth jeweled clouds of buddha-images as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, pervading the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of wheels of flaming buddha-light as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, pervading the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of every wondrous fragrance as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, pervading the ten directions and praising the great ocean of merit of all Samantabhadra's practices and vows; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of suns, moons, and stars and constellations as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, all of them sending out the light of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva and shining throughout the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of images of the bodies of every sentient being as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, sending out buddha-light and shining throughout the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth maṇi clouds of the images of every buddha as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, pervading the dharma-realm; from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of images of the bodies of bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, filling the dharma-realm and causing every sentient being to attain escape and to have their wishes fulfilled; and from each and every mote of dust there came forth clouds of images of the bodies of tathāgatas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, proclaiming the vast and great vows of all the buddhas and pervading the dharma-realm. These are the ten.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

見一切佛剎清淨 — every one of the ten auspicious signs opens with the same four characters after 見, he saw every buddha-land pure, and adds one particular of that purity: enlightenment, no evil destinies, lotuses, pure bodies and minds, jewels, the marks, clouds, mutual kindness, the adorned site of awakening, mindfulness of the Buddha. The refrain is repeated in full ten times in the translation, since the sign in each case is not the particular alone but the pure land in which it is seen.

一一塵中 — the ten signs of light are built on a second refrain, from each and every mote of dust, and on a measure, 一切世界微塵數, as numerous as the fine motes of dust of all the worlds, which recurs in every item; the clouds counted by that measure come out of the very motes it counts. The translation keeps both the refrain and the measure at every occurrence. The powers named at the outset are three: Good-Wealth's own wholesome roots, the blessing of the tathāgatas, and 普賢菩薩同善根力, the power of the wholesome roots he shares with Samantabhadra, the last of which explains why two of the clouds in the second list, the clouds of fragrance and the clouds of suns, moons, and stars, praise or shine with Samantabhadra's own vows and light.
