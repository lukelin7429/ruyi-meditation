The Liberation Named Unsurpassed Banner

Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa turns from praising Good-Wealth to declaring his own attainment, and when Good-Wealth asks what this liberation actually is, the sage answers not with an explanation but with a touch — extending his right hand to stroke the boy's head and take hold of his hand.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 959 — the liberation named Unsurpassed Banner Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

An Answer Given by Touch, Not by Word

Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa names his liberation Unsurpassed Banner, and when Good-Wealth asks what its state actually is, the sage does not describe it at all — he simply reaches out and touches him, letting whatever happens next stand as the only answer needed.

Chinese Source

時，毘目瞿沙告群仙言：「善男子！若有能發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，必當成就一切智道。此善男子已發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，當淨一切佛功德地。」時，毘目瞿沙告善財童子言：「善男子！我得菩薩無勝幢解脫。」善財白言：「聖者！無勝幢解脫境界云何？」時，毘目仙人即申右手，摩善財頂，執善財手。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa said to his group of sages: "Good sons, if there is anyone able to give rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, they will certainly accomplish the path of omniscience. This good son has already given rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, and will purify the ground of every buddha's own merit." At that time, Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa said to the boy Good-Wealth: "Good son, I have attained the bodhisattva liberation Unsurpassed Banner." Good-Wealth said: "Noble one, what is the state of the liberation Unsurpassed Banner?" At that time, the sage Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa at once extended his right hand, stroked the crown of Good-Wealth's head, and grasped Good-Wealth's hand.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

摩善財頂，執善財手 — stroked the crown of Good-Wealth's head, and grasped Good-Wealth's hand. Every teacher so far has answered Good-Wealth's question about their own liberation in speech, however brief. Bhīṣmottaranirghoṣa is the first to answer with contact alone — the demonstration itself becomes the explanation, as the next Part will show.

Part 959 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle sixty-four. Part 960 turns to what Good-Wealth sees the instant he is touched — his own body traveling to ten buddha-lands' worth of dust-mote-number worlds, arriving before that same number of buddhas.
