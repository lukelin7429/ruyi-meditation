Equal to Every Bodhisattva

Forty-seven more measureless things close out the list this Part inherits mid-sentence, ending not on another single item but on a call to be equal to every bodhisattva there is, across boundless kalpas.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1261 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Equal to Every Bodhisattva

The list moves from dharma-gates and wisdom-lights through a bodhisattva's own vast domain and difficult stations, before its final item breaks the pattern — not one more measureless thing, but equality itself.

Chinese Source

Ru-Yi Translation

A bodhisattva-mahāsattva should distinguish measureless dharma-gates; should thoroughly understand measureless dharma-gates; should purify measureless dharma-gates; should give rise to the measureless light of dharma; should make measureless dharma shine forth; should illumine the measureless kinds of faculties; should know the measureless sicknesses of affliction; should gather the measureless medicine of wondrous dharma; should heal the measureless illnesses of beings; should adorn and prepare measureless offerings of ambrosia; should journey to measureless buddha-lands; should make offerings to measureless tathāgatas; should enter measureless bodhisattva assemblies; should receive the measureless teachings of every buddha; should bear with the measureless faults of beings; should extinguish the measureless hardships of the evil destinies; should cause measureless beings to be born in a good destiny; should gather in measureless beings through the four means of attraction; should cultivate measureless dhāraṇī-gates; should give rise to measureless gates of great vows; should cultivate the measureless power of great loving-kindness and great vows; should diligently seek the measureless dharma, forever without rest; should arouse the measureless power of contemplation; should arouse measureless deeds of spiritual power; should purify the measureless light of wisdom; should go to the measureless destinies of beings; should receive measureless births in every state of existence; should manifest measureless distinct bodies; should know the measureless dharma of speech and expression; should enter the measureless distinct minds of beings; should know a bodhisattva's own vast domain; should abide in a bodhisattva's own vast palace; should contemplate a bodhisattva's own profound and wondrous dharma; should know a bodhisattva's own domain that is hard to know; should perform a bodhisattva's own every practice that is hard to perform; should be endowed with a bodhisattva's own revered and majestic virtue; should tread a bodhisattva's own true station, hard to enter; should know a bodhisattva's own every kind of practice; should manifest a bodhisattva's own universal spiritual power; should receive a bodhisattva's own cloud of equal dharma; should widen a bodhisattva's own boundless net of practice; should fulfill a bodhisattva's own every boundless pāramitā; should receive a bodhisattva's own measureless predictions; should enter a bodhisattva's own measureless gates of forbearance; should master a bodhisattva's own measureless grounds; should purify a bodhisattva's own measureless dharma-gates; and should be equal to every bodhisattva: abiding in boundless kalpas, making offerings to measureless buddhas, purifying ineffable buddha-lands, and giving rise to ineffable bodhisattva vows.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

應同諸菩薩，安住無邊劫，供養無量佛，嚴淨不可說佛國土，出生不可說菩薩願 — should be equal to every bodhisattva: abiding in boundless kalpas, making offerings to measureless buddhas, purifying ineffable buddha-lands, and giving rise to ineffable bodhisattva vows. Ninety-five items in all, counted directly across this Part and the last — forty-eight there, forty-seven here — and the very last one is the only item in the entire list that names no fixed practice at all, only parity with every bodhisattva who has ever kept it.
