His Own Body in Every Tower

Seeing the tower's inconceivable domain, Good-Wealth is filled with joy, his body and mind grow soft and pliant, and he enters the door of unobstructed liberation. The moment he bows his head, Maitreya's majestic spiritual power lets him see his own body present in every tower at once, and in them he sees Maitreya's first arousing of the mind of bodhi, the whole course of his practice up to the prediction of unsurpassed bodhi, and his appearances as a wheel-turning king, as the kings of one heaven after another, as a king of māras, as a Brahmā king, and as an asura king.

His Own Body in Every Tower

Good-Wealth's joy, his bow, and the first of the visions inside the tower — Maitreya's own story, from the arousing of the mind to the many forms in which he teaches.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子見毘盧遮那莊嚴藏樓閣如是種種不可思議自在境界，生大歡喜，踊躍無量，身心柔軟，離一切想，除一切障，滅一切惑，所見不忘，所聞能憶，所思不亂，入於無礙解脫之門。普運其心，普見一切，普申敬禮，纔始稽首，以彌勒菩薩威神之力，自見其身遍在一切諸樓閣中，具見種種不可思議自在境界。所謂：或見彌勒菩薩初發無上菩提心時如是名字、如是種族，如是善友之所開悟，令其種植如是善根、住如是壽、在如是劫、值如是佛、處於如是莊嚴剎土、修如是行、發如是願；彼諸如來如是眾會、如是壽命，經爾許時親近供養。——悉皆明見。或見彌勒最初證得慈心三昧，從是已來，號為慈氏；或見彌勒修諸妙行，成滿一切諸波羅蜜；或見得忍，或見住地，或見成就清淨國土，或見護持如來正教，為大法師，得無生忍，某時、某處、某如來所受於無上菩提之記。或見彌勒為轉輪王，勸諸眾生住十善道；或為護世，饒益眾生；或為釋天，訶責五欲；或為焰摩天王，讚不放逸；或為兜率天王，稱歎一生菩薩功德；或為化樂天王，為諸天眾現諸菩薩變化莊嚴；或為他化自在天王，為諸天眾演說一切諸佛之法；或作魔王，說一切法皆悉無常；或為梵王，說諸禪定無量喜樂；或為阿脩羅王，入大智海，了法如幻，為其眾會常演說法，斷除一切憍慢醉傲。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth the youth, seeing such manifold and inconceivable domains of sovereign freedom in the tower of Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury, was filled with great joy and exulted beyond measure; his body and mind grew soft and pliant; he left behind every notion, removed every obstruction, and extinguished every delusion; he forgot nothing that he saw, could recall all that he heard, and was unconfused in all that he thought; and he entered the door of unobstructed liberation. He set his mind everywhere, saw everything everywhere, and everywhere offered his reverence, and the moment he first bowed his head, through the majestic spiritual power of Maitreya Bodhisattva he saw his own body present in every one of the towers, and saw in full their manifold and inconceivable domains of sovereign freedom. That is to say, he saw Maitreya Bodhisattva at the time when he first aroused the mind of unsurpassed bodhi, with such a name and such a clan, awakened by such a good friend, who caused him to plant such wholesome roots, to live such a lifespan, to be in such a kalpa, to meet such a buddha, to dwell in such an adorned land, to cultivate such practices, and to make such vows; and such assemblies of those tathāgatas, and such lifespans, and for such a length of time he drew near to them and made offerings. All of this he saw with clarity. Or he saw Maitreya first attaining the samādhi of loving-kindness, from which time onward he has been called the Loving One; or he saw Maitreya cultivating every wondrous practice and bringing every pāramitā to fullness; or he saw him attaining the acceptances, or abiding in the grounds, or accomplishing pure lands, or protecting and upholding the true teaching of the tathāgatas as a great dharma-teacher, attaining the acceptance of the non-arising of dharmas, and at such a time, in such a place, before such a tathāgata, receiving the prediction of unsurpassed bodhi. Or he saw Maitreya as a wheel-turning king, urging sentient beings to abide in the ten wholesome paths; or as a world-protector, benefiting sentient beings; or as Śakra, rebuking the five desires; or as the king of the Yāma Heaven, praising non-negligence; or as the king of the Tuṣita Heaven, extolling the merits of the bodhisattvas of a single remaining birth; or as the king of the Nirmāṇarati Heaven, displaying to the assemblies of devas the transformations and adornments of the bodhisattvas; or as the king of the Paranirmita-vaśavartin Heaven, expounding to the assemblies of devas the dharma of all the buddhas; or as a king of māras, teaching that all dharmas are without exception impermanent; or as a Brahmā king, teaching the measureless joy and bliss of the dhyānas; or as an asura king, entering the great ocean of wisdom, understanding that dharmas are like an illusion, and constantly expounding the dharma to his assembly, cutting off all conceit, arrogance, and drunken haughtiness.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

慈氏 — the passage itself explains the name: from the time Maitreya first attained 慈心三昧, the samādhi of loving-kindness, he has been 號為慈氏, called by a name built on 慈, loving-kindness. That is the translated form of the name whose transliteration is 彌勒, and the two forms sit side by side in this very sentence, 或見彌勒最初證得慈心三昧，從是已來，號為慈氏. The translation keeps Maitreya for 彌勒 throughout and renders the explanatory 慈氏 as the Loving One, so that the derivation the sentence is making stays audible in English.

如是 — in the account of Maitreya's first arousing of the mind, the word 如是, such, is repeated as a placeholder before every particular: such a name, such a clan, such a good friend, such roots, such a lifespan, such a kalpa, such a buddha, such a land, such practices, such vows, such assemblies, such lifespans. The passage is not withholding the details so much as indicating that Good-Wealth saw each of them exactly; the translation keeps every such rather than summarising the list.
