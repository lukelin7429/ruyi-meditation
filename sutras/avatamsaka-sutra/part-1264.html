Fears No Māra

Fourteen falls a good spiritual friend prevents give way to forty-one things that same friend is actively able to do — a shift from protection to power, verified item by item straight from the source text.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1264 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Fears No Māra

Fourteen dangers named one after another, each one prevented by a different aspect of the same relationship — sustaining, embrace, mindful protection, guarding, nurturing, and more, each with its own specific danger averted.

Chinese Source

「善男子！菩薩由善知識任持，不墮惡趣；由善知識攝受，不退大乘；由善知識護念，不毀犯菩薩戒；由善知識守護，不隨逐惡知識；由善知識養育，不缺減菩薩法；由善知識攝取，超越凡夫地；由善知識教誨，超越二乘地；由善知識示導，得出離世間；由善知識長養，能不染世法；由承事善知識，修一切菩薩行；由供養善知識，具一切助道法；由親近善知識，不為業惑之所摧伏；由恃怙善知識，勢力堅固，不怖諸魔；由依止善知識，增長一切菩提分法。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! A bodhisattva, through a good spiritual friend's own sustaining, does not fall into an evil destiny; through a good spiritual friend's own embrace, does not retrogress from the Great Vehicle; through a good spiritual friend's own mindful protection, does not violate the bodhisattva precepts; through a good spiritual friend's own guarding, does not follow after evil companions; through a good spiritual friend's own nurturing, never has its bodhisattva-dharma diminished; through a good spiritual friend's own gathering-in, transcends the ground of the ordinary person; through a good spiritual friend's own instruction, transcends the ground of the two vehicles; through a good spiritual friend's own guidance, attains renunciation of the world; through a good spiritual friend's own fostering, is able to remain unstained by worldly dharmas; through serving a good spiritual friend, cultivates every bodhisattva practice; through making offerings to a good spiritual friend, is endowed with every dharma that assists the path; through drawing near to a good spiritual friend, is never overwhelmed by karma and affliction; through relying upon a good spiritual friend, has its own strength made firm, and fears no māra; through relying upon a good spiritual friend, increases every factor of bodhi.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

由恃怙善知識，勢力堅固，不怖諸魔 — through relying upon a good spiritual friend, one's own strength becomes firm, and one fears no māra. Fourteen distinct protections in all, counted directly from the source text, each naming both the specific aspect of the relationship (sustaining, embrace, guarding, nurturing) and the specific danger it prevents.

Forty-One Abilities

The register shifts from protection to power: forty-one things a good spiritual friend is simply able to do, moving from purifying hindrances and shattering ignorance's darkness all the way to opening every gate of buddha-dharma.

Chinese Source

何以故？善男子！善知識者，能淨諸障，能滅諸罪，能除諸難，能止諸惡，能破無明長夜黑暗，能壞諸見堅固牢獄，能出生死城，能捨世俗家，能截諸魔網，能拔眾苦箭，能離無智險難處，能出邪見大曠野，能度諸有流，能離諸邪道，能示菩提路，能教菩薩法，能令安住菩薩行，能令趣向一切智，能淨智慧眼，能長菩提心，能生大悲，能演妙行，能說波羅蜜，能擯惡知識，能令住諸地，能令獲諸忍，能令修習一切善根，能令成辦一切道具，能施與一切大功德，能令到一切種智位，能令歡喜集功德，能令踊躍修諸行，能令趣入甚深義，能令開示出離門，能令杜絕諸惡道，能令以法光照耀，能令以法雨潤澤，能令消滅一切惑，能令捨離一切見，能令增長一切佛智慧，能令安住一切佛法門。

Ru-Yi Translation

Why is this? Good son! A good spiritual friend is able to purify every hindrance; is able to extinguish every transgression; is able to remove every difficulty; is able to halt every evil; is able to shatter the long night's darkness of ignorance; is able to destroy the firm prison of every view; is able to escape the city of birth-and-death; is able to leave behind the household of worldly convention; is able to cut through every net of Māra; is able to pull out every arrow of suffering; is able to leave the perilous place of ignorance; is able to emerge from the vast wilderness of wrong views; is able to cross every stream of existence; is able to leave every wrong path; is able to show the road of bodhi; is able to teach the bodhisattva's own dharma; is able to cause one to abide in the bodhisattva's own practice; is able to cause one to advance toward all-knowing wisdom; is able to purify the eye of wisdom; is able to lengthen the mind of bodhi; is able to give rise to great compassion; is able to expound wondrous practice; is able to speak of the pāramitās; is able to expel evil companions; is able to cause one to abide in every ground; is able to cause one to attain every forbearance; is able to cause one to cultivate every wholesome root; is able to cause one to accomplish every requisite of the path; is able to bestow every great merit; is able to cause one to reach the station of omniscience; is able to cause one to joyfully gather merit; is able to cause one to leap for joy in cultivating every practice; is able to cause one to enter into the profound meaning; is able to cause the gate of renunciation to be revealed; is able to cause every evil destiny to be sealed off; is able to cause one to shine with the light of dharma; is able to cause one to be enriched by the rain of dharma; is able to cause every delusion to be dissolved; is able to cause every view to be relinquished; is able to cause every buddha-wisdom to increase; is able to cause one to abide in every buddha-dharma-gate."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

能破無明長夜黑暗⋯能令安住一切佛法門 — is able to shatter the long night's darkness of ignorance⋯is able to cause one to abide in every buddha-dharma-gate. Forty-one abilities in all, counted directly from the source text — the longest single list of what a good spiritual friend actually does that this journey has yet offered, moving steadily from rescue (escaping cities, nets, arrows, wildernesses) to attainment (grounds, forbearances, wisdom, buddha-dharma itself).
