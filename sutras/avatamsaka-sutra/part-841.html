A Bodhisattva Arrives from the North

The fourth arrival brings a fourth kind of offering: ten clouds, every one of them made of jeweled garments, ranging from garments of yellow light and starlike maṇi to garments woven from awesome, blazing power itself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 841 — a bodhisattva arrives from the north Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Garments Made of Light

Where the west brought clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, the north brings clouds shaped like garments — every one of them, in the end, actually made of light and maṇi jewels rather than any woven thread, a wardrobe built entirely out of radiance.

Chinese Source

北方過不可說佛剎微塵數世界海外有世界，名：寶衣光明幢，佛號：照虛空法界大光明。彼佛眾中有菩薩，名：無礙勝藏王，與世界海微塵數菩薩俱，來向佛所，悉以神力興一切寶衣雲，所謂：黃色寶光明衣雲、種種香所熏衣雲、日幢摩尼王衣雲、金色熾然摩尼衣雲、一切寶光焰衣雲、一切星辰像上妙摩尼衣雲、白玉光摩尼衣雲、光明遍照殊勝赫奕摩尼衣雲、光明遍照威勢熾盛摩尼衣雲、莊嚴海摩尼衣雲，充遍虛空。至佛所已，頂禮佛足，即於北方化作摩尼寶海莊嚴樓閣及毘瑠璃寶蓮華藏師子之座，以師子威德摩尼王網羅覆其身，清淨寶王為髻明珠，與其眷屬結跏趺坐。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, in the north, beyond an ocean of worlds as numerous as unspeakably many fine motes of dust in every buddha-land, there was a world named Jeweled-Garment Light Banner, whose buddha was named Great Light Illuminating Empty Space and the Dharma-Realm. In that buddha's own assembly there was a bodhisattva named Unobstructed Victorious Treasury King, who, together with bodhisattvas as numerous as fine motes of dust in an ocean of worlds, came toward the place where the Buddha was, raising up through spiritual power alone every kind of cloud of jeweled garments, namely: clouds of garments of yellow jeweled light, clouds of garments perfumed with every kind of incense, clouds of garments of sun-banner maṇi king, clouds of garments of blazing golden maṇi, clouds of garments of every kind of jeweled blazing light, clouds of garments of supremely wondrous maṇi shaped like every star and constellation, clouds of garments of white-jade-light maṇi, clouds of garments of maṇi whose light universally illuminates with the most excellent, blazing brilliance, clouds of garments of maṇi whose light universally illuminates with awesome, blazing power, and clouds of garments of ocean-adorning maṇi — filling the whole of empty space. Having arrived where the Buddha was, they bowed in reverence at his feet, and then, in the north, conjured a pavilion adorned with an ocean of maṇi jewels, together with a lion-seat of lapis-lazuli jeweled lotus-treasury; covered their own bodies with nets of lion awesome-virtue maṇi kings; wore a pure jewel-king as the bright pearl in their own topknot; and sat down cross-legged there together with their own retinues.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

黃色寶光明衣雲 — clouds of garments of yellow jeweled light. This first of the ten garment-clouds sets the pattern for all that follow: what is called a garment here is never cloth at all, but a specific configuration of light and jewel, so that to wear one of these garments is really to be clothed in a particular quality of radiance.
