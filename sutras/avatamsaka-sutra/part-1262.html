Never Give Rise to Suspicion

The ninety-five-item list compresses to eleven universal practices, and then the teaching turns practical — ten exact instructions for how to seek, see, question, and never doubt a good spiritual friend, however that friend may act.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1262 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Eleven, Summarized

Ninety-five measureless practices across the last two Parts compress here into eleven universal ones — the same scope, stated as briefly as this teaching ever states anything.

Chinese Source

善男子！舉要言之，應普修一切菩薩行，應普化一切眾生界，應普入一切劫，應普生一切處，應普知一切世，應普行一切法，應普淨一切剎，應普滿一切願，應普供一切佛，應普同一切菩薩願，應普事一切善知識。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! To sum it up briefly: a bodhisattva-mahāsattva should universally cultivate every bodhisattva practice; should universally transform every realm of beings; should universally enter every kalpa; should universally take birth in every place; should universally know every world; should universally practice every dharma; should universally purify every land; should universally fulfill every vow; should universally make offerings to every buddha; should universally be one with every bodhisattva's own vows; and should universally serve every good spiritual friend."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

舉要言之 — to sum it up briefly. Eleven items replace the ninety-five just given, each one now carrying 普 ("universally") instead of 無量 ("measureless") — the same total scope, restated in the shortest form this teaching offers.

Never Give Rise to Suspicion

The teaching turns from what a bodhisattva cultivates to something far more specific — exactly how Good-Wealth himself should behave toward any good spiritual friend he meets, this one included.

Chinese Source

「善男子！汝求善知識，不應疲倦；見善知識，勿生厭足；請問善知識，勿憚勞苦；親近善知識，勿懷退轉；供養善知識，不應休息；受善知識教，不應倒錯；學善知識行，不應疑惑；聞善知識演說出離門，不應猶豫；見善知識隨順煩惱行，勿生嫌怪；於善知識所生深信尊敬心，不應變改。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! In seeking a good spiritual friend, you should never grow weary; in seeing a good spiritual friend, never give rise to a feeling of having had enough; in questioning a good spiritual friend, never shrink from toil and hardship; in drawing near to a good spiritual friend, never harbor retrogression; in making offerings to a good spiritual friend, you should never rest; in receiving a good spiritual friend's own teaching, you should never distort it; in studying a good spiritual friend's own practice, you should never doubt it; in hearing a good spiritual friend proclaim the gate of renunciation, you should never hesitate; in seeing a good spiritual friend act in accordance with affliction, never give rise to suspicion or reproach; and the profound faith and reverent mind you give rise to toward a good spiritual friend should never once change."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

見善知識隨順煩惱行，勿生嫌怪 — in seeing a good spiritual friend act in accordance with affliction, never give rise to suspicion or reproach. Ten instructions in all, verified directly from the source text, and this one stands apart from the rest — it is the only instruction that anticipates a teacher who might look, on the surface, exactly like someone still caught in the very afflictions the path is meant to end.
