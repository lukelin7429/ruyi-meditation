Like Vajra

The chain of similes for the mind of bodhi continues through medicines and fragrances, deities and jewels, then a run of weapons — spear, sword, saw, axe — closing on the smallest of instruments, a pair of tweezers.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1291 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Like Vajra

Thirty-seven more similes continue the unbroken chain — medicines, incense, jewels, the deities Śakra, Vaiśravaṇa, and Nārāyaṇa, and finally a run of weapons, each one still answering the same question: what is the mind of bodhi like?

Chinese Source

菩提心者，猶如良藥，能治一切煩惱病故；菩提心者，猶如坑穽，陷沒一切諸惡法故；菩提心者，猶如金剛，悉能穿徹一切法故；菩提心者，猶如香篋，能貯一切功德香故；菩提心者，猶如妙華，一切世間所樂見故；菩提心者，如白栴檀，除眾欲熱使清涼故；菩提心者，如黑沈香，能熏法界悉周遍故；菩提心者，如善見藥王，能破一切煩惱病故；菩提心者，如毘笈摩藥，能拔一切諸惑箭故；菩提心者，猶如帝釋，一切主中最為尊故；菩提心者，如毘沙門，能斷一切貧窮苦故；菩提心者，如功德天，一切功德所莊嚴故；菩提心者，如莊嚴具，莊嚴一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，如劫燒火，能燒一切諸有為故；菩提心者，如無生根藥，長養一切諸佛法故；菩提心者，猶如龍珠，能消一切煩惱毒故；菩提心者，如水清珠，能清一切煩惱濁故；菩提心者，如如意珠，周給一切諸貧乏故；菩提心者，如功德瓶，滿足一切眾生心故；菩提心者，如如意樹，能雨一切莊嚴具故；菩提心者，如鵝羽衣，不受一切生死垢故；菩提心者，如白㲲線，從本已來性清淨故；菩提心者，如快利犁，能治一切眾生田故；菩提心者，如那羅延，能摧一切我見敵故；菩提心者，猶如快箭，能破一切諸苦的故；菩提心者，猶如利矛，能穿一切煩惱甲故；菩提心者，猶如堅甲，能護一切如理心故；菩提心者，猶如利刀，能斬一切煩惱首故；菩提心者，猶如利劍，能斷一切憍慢鎧故；菩提心者，如勇將幢，能伏一切諸魔軍故；菩提心者，猶如利鋸，能截一切無明樹故；菩提心者，猶如利斧，能伐一切諸苦樹故；菩提心者，猶如兵仗，能防一切諸苦難故；菩提心者，猶如善手，防護一切諸度身故；菩提心者，猶如好足，安立一切諸功德故；菩提心者，猶如眼藥，滅除一切無明翳故；菩提心者，猶如鉗鑷，能拔一切身見刺故；

Ru-Yi Translation

The mind of bodhi is like a fine medicine, since it cures every sickness of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like a pit-trap, since it swallows up every evil dharma; the mind of bodhi is like vajra, since it is able to pierce through every dharma entirely; the mind of bodhi is like a fragrant casket, since it stores the fragrance of every merit; the mind of bodhi is like a wondrous flower, since the entire world delights to see it; the mind of bodhi is like white sandalwood, since it removes the heat of every desire and brings coolness; the mind of bodhi is like black agarwood, since its fragrance pervades the entire dharma-realm; the mind of bodhi is like the well-seen king of medicine, since it defeats every sickness of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like the pí-jí-mó medicine, since it pulls out every arrow of delusion; the mind of bodhi is like Śakra, since it is the most honored among every lord; the mind of bodhi is like Vaiśravaṇa, since it severs every suffering of poverty; the mind of bodhi is like the goddess of merit, since it is adorned by every merit; the mind of bodhi is like an ornament, since it adorns every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is like the fire at the end of an eon, since it burns away everything conditioned; the mind of bodhi is like the unborn-root medicine, since it grows and nurtures every buddha-dharma; the mind of bodhi is like a nāga's own pearl, since it dissolves every poison of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like a water-clarifying pearl, since it clears every turbidity of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like a wish-fulfilling pearl, since it universally provides for every want; the mind of bodhi is like a vessel of merit, since it satisfies the mind of every sentient being; the mind of bodhi is like a wish-fulfilling tree, since it rains down every ornament; the mind of bodhi is like a robe of goose-down, since it takes on none of the stains of birth-and-death; the mind of bodhi is like a thread of white cotton, since its nature has been pure from the very origin; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp plow, since it tills the field of every sentient being; the mind of bodhi is like Nārāyaṇa, since it crushes every enemy of the view of self; the mind of bodhi is like a swift arrow, since it strikes through every target of suffering; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp spear, since it pierces through every armor of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like sturdy armor, since it protects every mind in accord with the principle; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp blade, since it severs the very head of every affliction; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp sword, since it cuts through every armor of pride and conceit; the mind of bodhi is like a courageous general's own banner, since it subdues every army of māra; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp saw, since it cuts down the very tree of ignorance; the mind of bodhi is like a sharp axe, since it fells the very tree of every suffering; the mind of bodhi is like a weapon of war, since it guards against every hardship and peril; the mind of bodhi is like a skillful hand, since it guards and protects the very body of every pāramitā; the mind of bodhi is like a sturdy foot, since it establishes every merit securely; the mind of bodhi is like an eye-medicine, since it dissolves away every cataract of ignorance; the mind of bodhi is like tweezers, since they pull out every thorn of the view of a self in the body;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

如毘笈摩藥 — 毘笈摩 reads as a phonetic transliteration rather than a translated meaning, but no Sanskrit or Pali source term can be identified with confidence from this passage alone; per this project's own standing rule against inventing unverified reconstructions, it is kept here as the pinyin "pí-jí-mó" rather than assigned a guessed Sanskrit name.
