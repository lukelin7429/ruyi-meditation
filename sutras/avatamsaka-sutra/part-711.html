Seals Conclude, Wisdom-Illumination, Unequaled Abidings Begin

The final seals close on shared lineage with every buddha of the three times, before ten kinds of wisdom-illumination range from beholding every buddha to seeing every being die in one place and take birth in another, and a new question opens ten abidings that no sentient being, śrāvaka, or solitary awakened one can ever equal — beginning with observing ultimate reality without ever stopping to claim its realization.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 711 — seals conclude, wisdom-illumination, unequaled abidings begin Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Realizing Without Claiming Realization

The final seals move outward toward helping other beings never regress, before wisdom-illumination lists ten distinct kinds of seeing — a buddha, a being's own death and rebirth, a whole dharma-gateway — and a new list of unequaled abidings opens with a paradox this chapter returns to often: observing the truth directly while deliberately withholding the claim to have realized it, precisely because the work is not yet finished.

Chinese Source

菩薩摩訶薩若見善男子、善女人趣大乘者，令其增長求佛法心，令其安住一切善根，令其攝取一切智心，令其不退無上菩提，是為第八印。菩薩摩訶薩令一切眾生得平等心，勸令勤修一切智道，以大悲心而為說法，令於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提永不退轉，是為第九印。菩薩摩訶薩與三世諸佛同一善根，不斷一切諸佛種性，究竟得至一切智智，是為第十印。佛子！是為菩薩摩訶薩十種印。菩薩以此速成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，具足如來一切法無上智印。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種智光照。何等為十？所謂：知定當成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提智光照；見一切佛智光照；見一切眾生死此生彼智光照；解一切修多羅法門智光照；依善知識發菩提心集諸善根智光照；示現一切諸佛智光照；教化一切眾生悉令安住如來地智光照；演說不可思議廣大法門智光照；善巧了知一切諸佛神通威力智光照；滿足一切諸波羅蜜智光照。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得一切諸佛無上智光照。

Ru-Yi Translation

"A bodhisattva-mahāsattva, upon seeing a good son or good daughter heading toward the Great Vehicle, causes their mind that seeks the Buddha's dharma to increase, causes them to abide in every root of goodness, causes them to embrace the mind of all-knowing wisdom, and causes them never to regress from unsurpassed awakening — this is the eighth seal. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva causes every sentient being to attain a mind of equality, urges them to diligently cultivate the path of all-knowing wisdom, and, with a mind of great compassion, expounds the dharma for them, causing them never to regress from unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening forever — this is the ninth seal. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva shares one and the same root of goodness with every buddha of the three times, never cutting off the lineage of any buddha, and ultimately arrives at the wisdom of all-knowing wisdom itself — this is the tenth seal. Son of the Buddha! These are the bodhisattva-mahāsattva's ten kinds of seal. Through these, a bodhisattva swiftly attains unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening, becoming entirely complete in the tathāgata's own unsurpassed seal of wisdom over every dharma. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of wisdom-illumination. What are these ten? Namely: the wisdom-illumination of knowing they will certainly attain unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening; the wisdom-illumination of beholding every buddha; the wisdom-illumination of seeing every sentient being die here and be born there; the wisdom-illumination of understanding every dharma-gateway of the sūtras; the wisdom-illumination of, relying on a good spiritual friend, giving rise to the mind of awakening and accumulating every root of goodness; the wisdom-illumination of manifesting every buddha; the wisdom-illumination of teaching and transforming every sentient being, causing them all to abide in the tathāgata's own ground; the wisdom-illumination of expounding the inconceivable, vast dharma-gateway; the wisdom-illumination of skillfully and fully understanding the spiritual power and majestic might of every buddha; and the wisdom-illumination of fulfilling every pāramitā completely. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide in this dharma, they attain the unsurpassed wisdom-illumination shared by every buddha.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種無等住，一切眾生、聲聞、獨覺悉無與等。何等為十？所謂：菩薩摩訶薩雖觀實際而不取證，以一切願未成滿故，是為第一無等住。菩薩摩訶薩種等法界一切善根，而不於中有少執著，是為第二無等住。菩薩摩訶薩修菩薩行，知其如化，以一切法悉寂滅故，而於佛法不生疑惑，是為第三無等住。菩薩摩訶薩雖離世間所有妄想，然能作意，於不可說劫行菩薩行，滿足大願，終不中起疲厭之心，是為第四無等住。菩薩摩訶薩於一切法無所取著，以一切法性寂滅故，而不證涅槃。何以故？一切智道未成滿故，是為第五無等住。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten unequaled abidings, which no sentient being, śrāvaka, or solitary awakened one can ever equal. What are these ten? Namely: a bodhisattva-mahāsattva, though observing ultimate reality, does not take hold of its realization, because every one of their vows has not yet been fulfilled — this is the first unequaled abiding. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva plants roots of goodness equal in extent to the whole dharma-realm, and yet has not the slightest attachment within them — this is the second unequaled abiding. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva cultivates bodhisattva practice, knowing it to be like a transformation, because every dharma is entirely still and extinguished, and yet gives rise to no doubt at all regarding the Buddha's dharma — this is the third unequaled abiding. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva, though having left behind every worldly delusion, is still able to set their mind on carrying out bodhisattva practice across unspeakably many kalpas, fulfilling their great vow, and never once giving rise to a weary or wearied mind partway through — this is the fourth unequaled abiding. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has no grasping or attachment toward any dharma at all, because the nature of every dharma is still and extinguished, and yet does not realize nirvāṇa. Why is this? Because the path of all-knowing wisdom has not yet been fulfilled — this is the fifth unequaled abiding.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

菩薩摩訶薩雖觀實際而不取證，以一切願未成滿故 — a bodhisattva-mahāsattva, though observing ultimate reality, does not take hold of its realization, because every one of their vows has not yet been fulfilled. What makes this abiding unequaled by even a śrāvaka or a solitary awakened one is not the vision itself, which the text treats as directly available, but the deliberate withholding of a claim that vision would otherwise entitle a person to make — a discipline of postponement, not a limitation of insight.
