The Bodhisattva Roll Call Continues

The roll call moves on from eye to crown, from crown to a series of elaborate topknots — several of them among the longest single names in the entire sūtra — and then into a run of names built on light, before beginning a new run built on banner. Each name remains a distinct epithet rather than a repeated formula.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 829 — the bodhisattva roll call continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Topknots Long Enough to Be Sentences

Most names in this list are two or three characters compressed into a single epithet, but the topknot series breaks that pattern: several names stretch into full clauses, such as the topknot adorned by the maṇi-king manifesting the equal mark of all empty space — a name that reads less like a label and more like a description in miniature.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

示現一切虛空平等相摩尼王莊嚴髻 — the topknot adorned by the maṇi-king manifesting the equal mark of all empty space. Names of this length are unusual even within this list; most epithets pack a single image into two or three characters, while this one and its neighbor a few names later each carry a complete teaching about emptiness or spiritual transformation folded into a single bodhisattva's title.
