Countless Bodhisattvas Named Samantabhadra

The cascade of ten dharmas reaches its final tier — ten kinds of skillful wisdom — and Samantabhadra urges the assembly to receive this teaching with reverence; the ground quakes across worlds beyond counting, and bodhisattvas as numerous as motes of dust arrive from every direction, every one of them sharing the name Samantabhadra, to testify that his teaching holds equally everywhere.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 629 — skillful wisdom, the cosmic response, and the witnesses Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Kinds of Skillful Wisdom, Then the World Itself Answers

The teaching's final tier of ten dharmas closes with an exhortation to uphold it; the world responds with quaking and rains of every kind of offering, and bodhisattvas identical in name to Samantabhadra himself arrive from beyond counting to bear witness.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩住此十種勝妙心已，則得十種佛法善巧智。何等為十？所謂：了達甚深佛法善巧智，出生廣大佛法善巧智，宣說種種佛法善巧智，證入平等佛法善巧智，明了差別佛法善巧智，悟解無差別佛法善巧智，深入莊嚴佛法善巧智，一方便入佛法善巧智，無量方便入佛法善巧智，知無邊佛法無差別善巧智，以自心自力於一切佛法不退轉善巧智。是為十。「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩聞此法已，咸應發心，恭敬受持。何以故？菩薩摩訶薩持此法者，少作功力，疾得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提，皆得具足一切佛法，悉與三世諸佛法等。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Sons of the Buddha! Once a bodhisattva-mahāsattva dwells in these ten kinds of sublime mind, they thereby attain ten kinds of skillful wisdom in the buddha-dharma. What are these ten? They are: the skillful wisdom of penetrating the profound buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of giving rise to the vast and great buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of expounding every manner of buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of realizing and entering the equal buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of clearly discerning the differentiated buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of understanding the undifferentiated buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of entering deeply into the adorned buddha-dharma; the skillful wisdom of entering the buddha-dharma through a single skillful means; the skillful wisdom of entering the buddha-dharma through measureless skillful means; the skillful wisdom of knowing the non-differentiation of the boundless buddha-dharma; and the skillful wisdom of never retreating from any buddha-dharma at all, through the power of one's own mind and one's own strength. This is the ten. "Sons of the Buddha! Having heard this dharma, all should give rise to the resolve to receive and uphold it with reverence. Why is this? Whatever bodhisattva-mahāsattva upholds this dharma, exerting but little effort, swiftly attains unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening, becomes entirely endowed with every buddha-dharma, and becomes wholly equal to the dharma of every buddha of the three times."

Chinese Source

爾時，佛神力故，法如是故，十方各有十不可說百千億那由他佛剎微塵數世界六種震動，雨出過諸天一切華雲、香雲、末香雲、衣蓋、幢幡、摩尼寶等及以一切莊嚴具雲，雨眾妓樂雲，雨諸菩薩雲，雨不可說如來色相雲，雨不可說讚歎如來善哉雲，雨如來音聲充滿一切法界雲，雨不可說莊嚴世界雲，雨不可說增長菩提雲，雨不可說光明照耀雲，雨不可說神力說法雲。如此世界四天下菩提樹下菩提場菩薩宮殿中，見於如來成等正覺演說此法，十方一切諸世界中悉亦如是。爾時，佛神力故，法如是故，十方各過十不可說佛剎微塵數世界外，有十佛剎微塵數菩薩摩訶薩來詣此土，充滿十方，作如是言：「善哉善哉！佛子！乃能說此諸佛如來最大誓願授記深法。佛子！我等一切同名普賢，各從普勝世界普幢自在如來所來詣此土，悉以佛神力故，於一切處演說此法；如此眾會，如是所說，一切平等無有增減。我等皆承佛威神力，來此道場為汝作證。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, through the Buddha's spiritual power, and because the dharma is such as this, worlds as numerous as motes of dust in ten unspeakable hundred thousand koṭis of nayutas, in each of the ten directions, quaked in six ways, and there rained down clouds of flowers, clouds of incense, clouds of powdered incense, robes, canopies, banners, and streamers, maṇi jewels, and every kind of adornment, surpassing anything found among the gods; there rained down clouds of musical instruments, clouds of bodhisattvas, unspeakable clouds of the tathāgata's form and countenance, unspeakable clouds proclaiming excellent praise for the tathāgata, clouds of the tathāgata's voice filling the entire dharma-realm, unspeakable clouds adorning the worlds, unspeakable clouds increasing awakening, unspeakable clouds of shining light, and unspeakable clouds of spiritual power expounding the dharma. Just as, within the bodhisattva's palace at the seat of awakening beneath the bodhi tree, in the four continents of this world, one beheld the tathāgata attain perfect awakening and expound this dharma, so too was it just the same throughout every world of the ten directions. At that time, through the Buddha's spiritual power, and because the dharma is such as this, beyond worlds as numerous as motes of dust in ten unspeakable buddha-lands, in each of the ten directions, there were bodhisattva-mahāsattvas as numerous as motes of dust in ten buddha-lands who arrived at this land, filling the ten directions, and spoke thus: "Excellent, excellent! Son of the Buddha! You have indeed been able to expound this profound dharma of the great vows and predictions of every buddha tathāgata. Son of the Buddha! We are all alike named Samantabhadra, and have each come to this land from the presence of the tathāgata Universal Banner of Mastery, in the world Universally Supreme; all of us, through the Buddha's spiritual power, expound this very dharma in every place; and this assembly, and what is spoken here, are in every place equal, without increase or decrease. All of us, sustained by the Buddha's majestic and spiritual power, have come to this seat of awakening to bear witness for you.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

我等一切同名普賢 — we are all alike named Samantabhadra. The bodhisattvas who arrive to bear witness do not merely resemble Samantabhadra or act in his manner; they share his exact name, coming from a different buddha under a different tathāgata in a different world, which the text offers as proof that the teaching itself holds identically everywhere — the sameness of name standing in for the sameness of the dharma being confirmed.
