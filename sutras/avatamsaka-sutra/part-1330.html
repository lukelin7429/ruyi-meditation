Ten Pāramitās of Knowledge, Eleven Listed

The vision reaches its full extent: in every mote of dust of every buddha-land in the ten directions there are assemblies of buddhas, and before each buddha Samantabhadra sits on his lion-throne of jeweled lotus displaying the same power, and within each of those bodies of Samantabhadra the whole of the three times appears, every land, every being, every buddha's appearing, every voice, every wheel of dharma, every practice. Seeing this, Good-Wealth attains the pāramitās of knowledge, which the sūtra heads as ten and then lists as eleven, each exercised in thought after thought, from pervading every buddha-land to having Samantabhadra's practice of wisdom present before him. Then Samantabhadra stretches out his right hand and touches the crown of his head.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1330 — fascicle 80 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Pāramitās of Knowledge, Eleven Listed

Samantabhadra in every mote of dust, the three times within each of his bodies, the pāramitās of knowledge Good-Wealth attains, and the hand laid on his head.

Chinese Source

如十方一切世界；如是十方一切佛剎，一一塵中皆有法界諸佛眾會，一一佛所普賢菩薩坐寶蓮華師子座上現神通力悉亦如是。彼一一普賢身中，皆現三世一切境界、一切佛剎、一切眾生、一切佛出現、一切菩薩眾，及聞一切眾生言音、一切佛言音、一切如來所轉法輪、一切菩薩所成諸行、一切如來遊戲神通。善財童子見普賢菩薩如是無量不可思議大神通力，即得十種智波羅蜜。何等為十？所謂：於念念中，悉能周遍一切佛剎智波羅蜜；於念念中，悉能往詣一切佛所智波羅蜜；於念念中，悉能供養一切如來智波羅蜜；於念念中，普於一切諸如來所聞法受持智波羅蜜；於念念中，思惟一切如來法輪智波羅蜜；於念念中，知一切佛不可思議大神通事智波羅蜜；於念念中，說一句法盡未來際辯才無盡智波羅蜜；於念念中，以深般若觀一切法智波羅蜜；於念念中，入一切法界實相海智波羅蜜；於念念中，知一切眾生心智波羅蜜；於念念中，普賢慧行皆現在前智波羅蜜。善財童子既得是已，普賢菩薩即伸右手摩觸其頂。

Ru-Yi Translation

As in every world of the ten directions, so in every buddha-land of the ten directions, in each and every mote of dust there were assemblies of the buddhas of the dharma-realm, and in the presence of each and every buddha Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, seated upon a lion-throne of jeweled lotus, displayed the power of spiritual penetration in exactly the same way. Within each and every one of those bodies of Samantabhadra there appeared every domain of the three times, every buddha-land, every sentient being, every appearing of a buddha, and every assembly of bodhisattvas, and there were heard the voices of every sentient being, the voices of every buddha, the wheels of dharma turned by every tathāgata, the practices accomplished by every bodhisattva, and the play of spiritual power of every tathāgata. Good-Wealth the youth, seeing such measureless and inconceivable power of great spiritual penetration in Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, at once attained ten kinds of pāramitā of knowledge. What are the ten? That is to say: the pāramitā of knowledge of being able, in thought after thought, to pervade every buddha-land; the pāramitā of knowledge of being able, in thought after thought, to go to the presence of every buddha; the pāramitā of knowledge of being able, in thought after thought, to make offerings to every tathāgata; the pāramitā of knowledge of hearing and upholding the dharma, in thought after thought, in the presence of all the tathāgatas everywhere; the pāramitā of knowledge of contemplating, in thought after thought, the wheel of dharma of every tathāgata; the pāramitā of knowledge of knowing, in thought after thought, the inconceivable deeds of great spiritual penetration of every buddha; the pāramitā of knowledge of teaching a single phrase of dharma, in thought after thought, with inexhaustible eloquence to the end of the future; the pāramitā of knowledge of observing all dharmas, in thought after thought, with profound prajñā; the pāramitā of knowledge of entering, in thought after thought, the ocean of the true nature of the whole dharma-realm; the pāramitā of knowledge of knowing, in thought after thought, the minds of all sentient beings; and the pāramitā of knowledge of having Samantabhadra's practice of wisdom, in thought after thought, entirely present before him. When Good-Wealth the youth had attained these, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva at once stretched out his right hand and touched the crown of his head.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

十種智波羅蜜 — the passage announces ten kinds of pāramitā of knowledge and asks 何等為十, what are the ten, and the list that follows contains eleven items, each marked by 於念念中, in thought after thought, and each ending in 智波羅蜜. The source is followed exactly: the heading says ten, the translation says ten, and the eleven items are all rendered, with the mismatch left visible rather than corrected by dropping one. Lists headed by a round number and running one over it are not unknown in this chapter, and the previous part's eleven minds are a recent example.

智波羅蜜 — the term is rendered pāramitā of knowledge, the form the site has used for it since fascicle thirty-seven, and kept apart from 般若波羅蜜, the pāramitā of wisdom, which stands elsewhere as the mother of the bodhisattva's family; within the list 深般若 is given as profound prajñā and 實相 as true nature, both in the site's established forms. The part ends on 摩觸其頂, the touch on the crown of the head with which Samantabhadra confers what the next part will unfold, a gesture the sūtra has used at earlier moments of conferral as well.
