From Back and Shoulders, Teaching and Guarding

Sea-Banner's own back gives rise to teachers who diagnose before they speak — a different remedy for each of nine distinct afflictions — while his shoulders give rise to fearsome guardians whose only task is protection: of the good, of the suffering, and of every buddha's own dwelling place.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 932 — from back and shoulders, teaching and guarding Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Nine Afflictions, Nine Remedies

The śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas emerging from Sea-Banner's own back never teach a single fixed doctrine; they diagnose first — attachment to a self, grasping at permanence, greed, hatred, delusion — and speak only the specific remedy each condition calls for.

Chinese Source

從其背上，為應以二乘而得度者，出無數百千億聲聞、獨覺；為著我者，說無有我；為執常者，說一切行皆悉無常；為貪行者，說不淨觀；為瞋行者，說慈心觀；為癡行者，說緣起觀；為等分行者，說與智慧相應境界法；為樂著境界者，說無所有法；為樂著寂靜處者，說發大誓願普饒益一切眾生法。如是所作，周遍法界。

Ru-Yi Translation

From his own back, for the sake of those who ought to be liberated through the two vehicles, there emerged countless hundreds of thousands of millions of śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas; for those attached to a self, they spoke of there being no self; for those who grasp at permanence, they spoke of how every conditioned thing is entirely impermanent; for those whose conduct is greedy, they spoke of the contemplation of impurity; for those whose conduct is hateful, they spoke of the contemplation of loving-kindness; for those whose conduct is deluded, they spoke of the contemplation of dependent origination; for those whose conduct is evenly mixed, they spoke of the dharma of the domain that accords with wisdom; for those who delight in and are attached to sense-objects, they spoke of the dharma of having nothing at all; and for those who delight in and are attached to quiescent places, they spoke of the dharma of giving rise to great vows that universally benefit every sentient being. All such deeds as these pervade the entire dharma-realm.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

為樂著寂靜處者，說發大誓願普饒益一切眾生法 — for those who delight in and are attached to quiescent places, they spoke of the dharma of giving rise to great vows that universally benefit every sentient being. The final remedy is the most pointed: those who cling to stillness itself are given, not more stillness, but a vow that pulls them back out into the work of benefiting others.

Fearsome Guardians, Gentle Purpose

The yakṣas and rākṣasas from Sea-Banner's own shoulders look exactly as their kind traditionally does — tall or short, every one of them fearsome — and yet every task named for them is protective and healing rather than threatening.

Chinese Source

從其兩肩，出無數百千億諸夜叉、羅剎王，種種形貌、種種色相，或長或短，皆可怖畏，無量眷屬而自圍遶，守護一切行善眾生，并諸賢聖、菩薩眾會，若向正住及正住者；或時現作執金剛神，守護諸佛及佛住處，或遍守護一切世間。有怖畏者，令得安隱；有疾病者，令得除差；有苦惱者，令得免離；有過惡者，令其厭悔；有災橫者，令其息滅。如是利益一切眾生，皆悉令其捨生死輪轉正法輪。

Ru-Yi Translation

From his two shoulders, there emerged countless hundreds of thousands of millions of yakṣa and rākṣasa kings, of every kind of appearance and every kind of form, some tall and some short, all of them fearsome to behold, surrounded by their own measureless retinues, guarding every sentient being who practices goodness, together with every sage and worthy and every bodhisattva assembly, whether proceeding toward right abiding or already rightly abiding; sometimes manifesting as vajra-bearing spirits, guarding every buddha and every place a buddha dwells, and sometimes guarding the entire world universally. Whoever is fearful, they cause to attain peace and security; whoever is sick, they cause to be cured; whoever suffers affliction, they cause to be freed from it; whoever has committed fault or evil, they cause to feel remorse; and whoever meets with disaster or misfortune, they cause it to be extinguished. Benefiting every sentient being in this way, they cause every one of them to abandon the wheel of birth and death and turn the wheel of the true dharma.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

皆可怖畏……有怖畏者，令得安隱 — all of them fearsome to behold … whoever is fearful, they cause to attain peace and security. The same passage names these guardians as fearsome in appearance and then immediately credits them with removing fear from others — their own terrifying form apparently no obstacle at all to the comfort they actually provide.
