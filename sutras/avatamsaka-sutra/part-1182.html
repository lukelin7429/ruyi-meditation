Ten Birth-Treasuries

Good-Wealth leaves the night-spirit's assembly behind and searches out a garden at Lumbinī, where the fortieth teacher sits teaching twenty koṭis of nayutas of gods — and answers his standing question with the names of ten birth-treasuries that bring a bodhisattva to be born into the tathāgata's own family.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1182 — fascicle 74 opens Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Searching Everywhere for the Spirit

Good-Wealth's search leads him away from the awakening-site entirely, to a named garden where a new teacher sits amid an assembly of gods.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子於大願精進力救護一切眾生夜神所，得菩薩解脫已，憶念修習，了達增長。漸次遊行，至嵐毘尼林，周遍尋覓彼妙德神，見在一切寶樹莊嚴樓閣中，坐寶蓮華師子之座，二十億那由他諸天恭敬圍遶，為說菩薩受生海經，令其皆得生如來家，增長菩薩大功德海。善財見已，頂禮其足，合掌前立，白言：「大聖！我已先發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，而未能知菩薩云何修菩薩行、生如來家、為世大明？」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth, having attained this bodhisattva liberation at the place of the night-spirit Power of Great Vows and Diligence Who Rescues and Protects Every Being, called it to mind and cultivated it, coming to understand and increase it fully. Travelling onward by stages, he reached the grove of Lumbinī, and searched everywhere for that spirit Wondrous Virtue, Perfect and Full. He saw her seated within a tower adorned with every kind of jewelled tree, upon a lion-seat of jewelled lotus-flowers, reverently surrounded by twenty koṭis of nayutas of gods, expounding for them the Sūtra of the Ocean of Bodhisattva Birth, causing every one of them to be born into the tathāgata's own family, and to increase the ocean of the bodhisattva's own great merit. Having seen her, Good-Wealth bowed at her feet, joined his palms, and stood before her, saying: "Great sage, I have already, before this, given rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, yet I am not yet able to know how a bodhisattva cultivates the bodhisattva practices, is born into the tathāgata's own family, and becomes a great light for the world."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

周遍尋覓彼妙德神 — searched everywhere for that spirit Wondrous Virtue, Perfect and Full. Every teacher so far in this chapter has been found already in place, waiting in an assembly Good-Wealth walks straight into. This is the first time the text says he had to search — a small detail matching the larger shift already visible in where she is found: not an awakening-site, but a named garden, the first time this chapter has sent him somewhere else entirely.

Ten Birth-Treasuries

The fortieth teacher answers with a name for ten distinct treasuries, each said to bring a bodhisattva to birth in the tathāgata's own family.

Chinese Source

彼神答言：「善男子！菩薩有十種受生藏，若菩薩成就此法，則生如來家，念念增長菩薩善根，不疲不懈，不厭不退，無斷無失，離諸迷惑，不生怯劣、惱悔之心，趣一切智，入法界門，發廣大心，增長諸度，成就諸佛無上菩提，捨世間趣，入如來地，獲勝神通，諸佛之法常現在前，順一切智真實義境。「何等為十？一者，願常供養一切諸佛受生藏；二者，發菩提心受生藏；三者，觀諸法門勤修行受生藏；四者，以深淨心普照三世受生藏；五者，平等光明受生藏；六者，生如來家受生藏；七者，佛力光明受生藏；八者，觀普智門受生藏；九者，普現莊嚴受生藏；十者，入如來地受生藏。

Ru-Yi Translation

That spirit answered, saying: "Good son, a bodhisattva has ten kinds of birth-treasury. If a bodhisattva accomplishes this dharma, he is thereby born into the tathāgata's own family, and, in thought after thought, increases the bodhisattva's wholesome roots — never tiring, never slackening, never wearying of it, never retreating, without interruption and without loss; he is free of every confusion, and gives rise to no mind of cowardice, weakness, distress, or regret; he advances toward all-knowing wisdom, and enters the gate of the dharma-realm; he brings forth a vast and expansive mind, and increases every pāramitā; he accomplishes every buddha's unsurpassed bodhi, abandons the destinies of the worldly realm, and enters the tathāgata's own ground; he gains supreme spiritual powers, and every buddha's dharma is forever manifest before him, in accordance with the real meaning of the domain of all-knowing wisdom. "What are these ten? First, the birth-treasury of the vow to constantly make offerings to every buddha; second, the birth-treasury of giving rise to the mind of bodhi; third, the birth-treasury of contemplating every dharma-gate and diligently cultivating it; fourth, the birth-treasury of a mind profound and pure, universally illuminating the three times; fifth, the birth-treasury of equal and universal light; sixth, the birth-treasury of being born into the tathāgata's own family; seventh, the birth-treasury of a buddha's own power and light; eighth, the birth-treasury of contemplating the gate of universal wisdom; ninth, the birth-treasury of universally manifesting adornment; and tenth, the birth-treasury of entering the tathāgata's own ground.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一者⋯⋯十者，入如來地受生藏 — first⋯⋯tenth, the birth-treasury of entering the tathāgata's own ground. Counted directly, exactly ten items are named, each one individually labelled 受生藏, birth-treasury — a term this pilgrimage has not used before. The list runs from a vow made at the very start of the path (constantly making offerings to every buddha) to the very last ground before buddhahood itself (entering the tathāgata's own ground), naming the whole span in ten titles before a single one is explained.
