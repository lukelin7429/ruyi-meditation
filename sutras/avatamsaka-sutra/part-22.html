The World-Seeds of the East and South

Fascicle nine opens the second half of "The Flower-Treasury World-System" chapter, moving outward from the central world-seed described in fascicle eight to the ten world-seeds surrounding it — one in each direction. Samantabhadra describes each with the same structure as before: a name, a lotus, an essence, and twenty stacked tiers of worlds, each with its own name and buddha. This installment covers the first two: the world-seeds to the east and south.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 22 — opens fascicle 9 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The World-Seed to the East

Chinese Source

爾時，普賢菩薩復告大眾言：「諸佛子！此無邊妙華光香水海東，次有香水海，名：離垢焰藏；出大蓮華，名：一切香摩尼王妙莊嚴；有世界種而住其上，名：遍照剎旋，以菩薩行吼音為體。此中最下方，有世界，名：宮殿莊嚴幢；其形四方，依一切寶莊嚴海住，蓮華光網雲彌覆其上，佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：眉間光遍照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：德華藏；其形周圓，依一切寶華蘂海住，真珠幢師子座雲彌覆其上，二佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：一切無邊法海慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：善變化妙香輪；形如金剛，依一切寶莊嚴鈴網海住，種種莊嚴圓光雲彌覆其上，三佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：功德相光明普照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙色光明；其狀猶如摩尼寶輪，依無邊色寶香水海住，普光明真珠樓閣雲彌覆其上，四佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：善眷屬出興遍照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：善蓋覆；狀如蓮華，依金剛香水海住，離塵光明香水雲彌覆其上，五佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：法喜無盡慧。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：尸利華光輪；其形三角，依一切堅固寶莊嚴海住，菩薩摩尼冠光明雲彌覆其上，六佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：清淨普光明雲。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寶蓮華莊嚴；形如半月，依一切蓮華莊嚴海住，一切寶華雲彌覆其上，七佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：功德華清淨眼。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：無垢焰莊嚴；其狀猶如寶燈行列，依寶焰藏海住，常雨香水種種身雲彌覆其上，八佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：慧力無能勝。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙梵音；形如卐字，依寶衣幢海住，一切華莊嚴帳雲彌覆其上，九佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：廣大目如空中淨月。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：微塵數音聲；其狀猶如因陀羅網，依一切寶水海住，一切樂音寶蓋雲彌覆其上，十佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：金色須彌燈。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寶色莊嚴；形如卐字，依帝釋形寶王海住，日光明華雲彌覆其上，十一佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：迥照法界光明智。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：金色妙光；其狀猶如廣大城廓，依一切寶莊嚴海住，道場寶華雲彌覆其上，十二佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：寶燈普照幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：遍照光明輪；狀如華旋，依寶衣旋海住，佛音聲寶王樓閣雲彌覆其上，十三佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：蓮華焰遍照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寶藏莊嚴；狀如四洲，依寶瓔珞須彌住，寶焰摩尼雲彌覆其上，十四佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：無盡福開敷華。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：如鏡像普現；其狀猶如阿脩羅身，依金剛蓮華海住，寶冠光影雲彌覆其上，十五佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：甘露音。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：栴檀月；其形八隅，依金剛栴檀寶海住，真珠華摩尼雲彌覆其上，十六佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：最勝法無等智。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：離垢光明；其狀猶如香水漩流，依無邊色寶光海住，妙香光明雲彌覆其上，十七佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：遍照虛空光明音。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙華莊嚴；其狀猶如旋遶之形，依一切華海住，一切樂音摩尼雲彌覆其上，十八佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：普現勝光明。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：勝音莊嚴；其狀猶如師子之座，依金師子座海住，眾色蓮華藏師子座雲彌覆其上，十九佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，佛號：無邊功德稱普光明。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：高勝燈；狀如佛掌，依寶衣服香幢海住，日輪普照寶王樓閣雲彌覆其上，二十佛剎微塵數世界圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：普照虛空燈。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva further addressed the great assembly, saying: "Buddha's children! To the east of this Boundless, Wondrous Flower-Light fragrant water ocean, there is next a fragrant water ocean named the Treasury of Flame Free of Defilement; from it emerges a great lotus flower, named the Wondrous Adornment of the Sovereign Maṇi of Every Fragrance; and a world-seed abides upon it, named the Whirl of Lands Universally Illumining, taking the roaring voice of bodhisattva conduct as its essence. At its very lowest is a world named the Banner of Palace Adornment; its shape is square, abiding resting upon an ocean of every jewel's adornment, and a cloud, a net of lotus-flower light, covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land encircle it all around, purely one and entirely pure. Its buddha is named the Light Between the Brows, Universally Illumining. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Treasury of Virtue's Flower; its shape is entirely round, abiding resting upon an ocean of every jewel's flower-pistil, and a cloud of lion-thrones, banners of true pearls, covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in two buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named the Wisdom of Every Boundless Ocean of the Dharma. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Wondrous Fragrance-Wheel of Excellent Transformation; its shape is like vajra, abiding resting upon an ocean of nets of bells adorned by every jewel, and a cloud of round light adorned in every kind covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in three buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named the Light of the Mark of Merit, Universally Illumining. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Wondrous Light of Form; its shape is just like a maṇi jeweled wheel, abiding resting upon a fragrant water ocean of jewels of boundless color, and a cloud of pavilions of true pearls, universal light, covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in four buddha-lands encircle it all around, purely one and entirely pure. Its buddha is named Excellent Retinue's Appearing, Universally Illumining. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Excellent Covering Canopy; its shape is like a lotus flower, abiding resting upon a vajra fragrant water ocean, and a cloud of fragrant water, light free of defilement, covers over it entirely. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Adornment of a Jeweled Lotus Flower; its shape is like a half-moon, abiding resting upon an ocean adorned by every lotus flower, and a cloud of every jeweled flower covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in seven buddha-lands encircle it all around, purely one and entirely pure. Its buddha is named the Pure Eye of Merit's Flower. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Adornment of Flame Free of Defilement; its shape is just like jeweled lamps arrayed in rows, abiding resting upon an ocean, a treasury of jeweled flame, and a cloud of every kind of body, forever raining fragrant water, covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in eight buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named the Power of Wisdom, Unable to Be Surpassed. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Wondrous Brahmā-Voice; its shape is like the svastika mark, abiding resting upon an ocean of jeweled-garment banners, and a cloud of canopies adorned by every flower covers over it entirely. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in nine buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named the Vast Eye, Like the Pure Moon Within the Sky. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Voices as Numerous as Motes of Dust; its shape is just like Indra's own net, abiding resting upon an ocean of every jeweled water, and a cloud of jeweled canopies, every kind of music, covers over it entirely. Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This world-seed to the east is given the same full treatment as the central one in Part 21: every one of its twenty tiers named individually, with its own shape, its own foundation, its own covering cloud, and its own buddha. Nothing here is compressed into "and so on," in keeping with this project's practice of never abbreviating a list the source itself states in full — even when, as here, the sheer accumulation of names is clearly part of the passage's own rhetorical effect.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Notice the shift in the source text's own density between this world-seed and the previous one: where the eastern world-seed gave a full shape, foundation, and covering cloud for every one of its twenty tiers, this southern one supplies that level of detail only for its lowest and highest tiers, giving every tier in between simply a name and a buddha. This is not an inconsistency this translation has smoothed over — it is a feature of the Chinese source itself, and it will recur through the remaining eight world-seeds still to come: some fully elaborated, most given in this terser register. Twice in this list a world is named "無量莊嚴," Immeasurable Adornment — the source repeats itself, and so does this translation, since nothing here licenses inventing a distinguishing epithet the text does not supply.
