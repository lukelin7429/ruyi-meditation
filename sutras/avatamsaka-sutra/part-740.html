Unshared Dharma Concludes, Ten Kinds of Karmic Activity

The ninth unshared dharma offers a rare simile — a jewel called "Pure Adornment" that keeps its color in mud and clarifies the very water around it — before the tenth closes on a bodhisattva who reaches ultimate freedom yet never stops staying close to a good spiritual friend. Ten kinds of karmic activity then follow, each one a specific undertaking matched to its own reason.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 740 — unshared dharma concludes, ten kinds of karmic activity Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Jewel That Clarifies the Mud Around It

The simile of the jewel "Pure Adornment" does more than protect the bodhisattva's own purity amid mud — it actively clears the turbid water it sits in, giving this ninth unshared dharma an outward effect the others in this list do not claim. Ten kinds of karmic activity then close this chapter's long run of "ten kinds" lists, each activity paired directly with the reason it counts as karmic activity at all.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩於薩婆若大誓莊嚴志樂堅固，雖處凡夫、聲聞、獨覺險難之處，終不退失一切智心明淨妙寶。佛子！如有寶珠，名：淨莊嚴，置泥澇中光色不改，能令濁水悉皆澄淨。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，雖在凡愚雜濁等處，終不失壞求一切智清淨寶心，而能令彼諸惡眾生遠離妄見、煩惱、穢濁，得求一切智清淨心寶。是為第九不由他教在眾難處不失一切智心寶不共法。佛子！菩薩摩訶薩成就自覺境界智，無師自悟，究竟自在到於彼岸，離垢法繒以冠其首，而於善友不捨親近，於諸如來常樂尊重，是為第十不由他教得最上法不離善知識、不捨尊重佛不共法。佛子！是為菩薩摩訶薩十種不共法。若諸菩薩安住其中，則得如來無上廣大不共法。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva, adorned with the great vow of all-knowing wisdom, their resolve and delight firm, even while dwelling amid the perilous places of ordinary beings, śrāvakas, or solitary awakened ones, never once loses or regresses from the bright, pure, wondrous jewel that is the mind of all-knowing wisdom. Son of the Buddha! It is like a jewel called 'Pure Adornment' — placed within mud and filth, its light and color never change, and it is able to make even turbid water entirely clear. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva is exactly like this: even while amid the mixed and turbid places of ordinary, foolish beings, they never once lose or damage the pure jewel-mind that seeks all-knowing wisdom, and are able to cause those very unwholesome sentient beings to leave far behind false views, affliction, and defilement, and attain the pure jewel-mind that seeks all-knowing wisdom. This is the ninth unshared dharma: never losing the jewel-mind of all-knowing wisdom amid every perilous place, without being taught by another. Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva accomplishes the wisdom of self-awakened domain, awakening by themselves with no teacher, reaching sovereign freedom at its ultimate far shore, crowned with the stainless silk banner of the dharma upon their head — and yet never once abandons closeness to a good spiritual friend, and forever delights in revering every tathāgata. This is the tenth unshared dharma: attaining the supreme dharma without ever departing from a good spiritual friend or ceasing to revere every buddha, without being taught by another. Son of the Buddha! These are the bodhisattva-mahāsattva's ten kinds of unshared dharma. If bodhisattvas abide within these, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, vast unshared dharma.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種業。何等為十？所謂：一切世界業，悉能嚴淨故；一切諸佛業，悉能供養故；一切菩薩業，同種善根故；一切眾生業，悉能教化故；一切未來業，盡未來際攝取故；一切神力業，不離一世界遍至一切世界故；一切光明業，放無邊色光明，一一光中有蓮華座，各有菩薩結跏趺坐而顯現故；一切三寶種不斷業，諸佛滅後，守護住持諸佛法故；一切變化業，於一切世界說法教化諸眾生故；一切加持業，於一念中隨諸眾生心之所欲皆為示現，令一切願悉成滿故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上廣大業。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of karmic activity. What are these ten? Namely: the karmic activity toward every world, because they are able to adorn and purify every one of them; the karmic activity toward every buddha, because they are able to make offerings to every one of them; the karmic activity toward every bodhisattva, because they plant the same roots of goodness together with them; the karmic activity toward every sentient being, because they are able to teach and transform every one of them; the karmic activity toward every future age, because they gather it in to the very limit of the future; the karmic activity of every spiritual power, because, without ever leaving a single world, they reach every world; the karmic activity of every radiance, because they emit boundless light of every color, and within each and every ray of light there is a lotus-seat, upon which a bodhisattva appears seated cross-legged; the karmic activity of never letting the seed of the three jewels be cut off, because, after every buddha's cessation, they guard and uphold every buddha's dharma; the karmic activity of every transformation, because they teach the dharma throughout every world, transforming every sentient being; and the karmic activity of every empowerment, because, within a single thought, they manifest in accordance with whatever every sentient being's mind desires, causing every vow to be entirely fulfilled. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, vast karmic activity.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

置泥澇中光色不改，能令濁水悉皆澄淨 — placed within mud and filth, its light and color never change, and it is able to make even turbid water entirely clear. Most similes in this chapter describe a bodhisattva's own quality surviving contact with impurity; this one goes further, having the jewel actively transform the mud around it into clarity, which the text then applies directly to a bodhisattva causing unwholesome beings to leave behind their own defilement.
