Fascicle Seventy-Eight Complete

The vajra series closes with four last images: the foundation of the vajra realm that holds up the great earth, vajra that lies long in water without rotting, vajra that no fire can heat, and the vajra seat on which alone the buddhas sit to attain enlightenment. Maitreya then gathers the whole chain of similes into a single sentence on the merit of the mind of bodhi, tells Good-Wealth that he has gained excellent benefit, and answers the question that opened the fascicle by sending him into the great tower of Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury.

Fascicle Seventy-Eight Complete

Four final vajra similes, then Maitreya's summary of everything the chain of similes has said, and his instruction that closes the fascicle.

Chinese Source

「善男子！如金剛際，能持大地，不令墜沒。菩提之心亦復如是，能持菩薩一切行願，不令墜沒入於三界。「善男子！譬如金剛，久處水中，不爛不濕。菩提之心亦復如是，於一切劫，處在生死業惑水中，無壞無變。「善男子！譬如金剛，一切諸火不能燒然、不能令熱。菩提之心亦復如是，一切生死諸煩惱火不能燒然、不能令熱。「善男子！譬如三千世界之中金剛座上，能持諸佛坐於道場、降伏諸魔、成等正覺，非是餘座之所能持。菩提心座亦復如是，能持菩薩一切願行、諸波羅蜜、諸忍、諸地、迴向、受記、修集菩提助道之法、供養諸佛、聞法受行，一切餘心所不能持。「善男子！菩提心者，成就如是無量無邊乃至不可說不可說殊勝功德。若有眾生發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，則獲如是勝功德法。是故，善男子！汝獲善利！汝發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，求菩薩行，已得如是大功德故。「善男子！如汝所問：菩薩云何學菩薩行、修菩薩道？善男子！汝可入此毘盧遮那莊嚴藏大樓閣中周遍觀察，則能了知學菩薩行，學已成就無量功德。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as the foundation of the vajra realm is able to hold up the great earth and not let it sink, the mind of bodhi is likewise: it is able to hold up every practice and vow of a bodhisattva and not let them sink into the three realms. Good son! Just as vajra, though it lies long in water, neither rots nor becomes wet, the mind of bodhi is likewise: through every kalpa it lies within the water of the karma and delusion of birth-and-death, undamaged and unchanged. Good son! Just as no fire whatsoever is able to burn vajra or to make it hot, the mind of bodhi is likewise: no fire of the afflictions of birth-and-death is able to burn it or to make it hot. Good son! Just as, within a trichiliocosm, it is the vajra seat that is able to hold the buddhas as they sit at the site of awakening, subdue every māra, and attain perfect enlightenment, and no other seat is able to hold them, the seat that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: it is able to hold every vow and practice of a bodhisattva, the pāramitās, the acceptances, the grounds, dedication, the prediction, the cultivating and gathering of the dharmas that aid the path to bodhi, the making of offerings to the buddhas, and the hearing of the dharma and receiving and practicing it, and no other mind is able to hold them. Good son! The mind of bodhi accomplishes such measureless and boundless, indeed ineffably ineffable, supreme merit. If any sentient being arouses the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, they obtain the dharma of such supreme merit. Therefore, good son, you have gained excellent benefit! For you have aroused the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi and sought the bodhisattva's practices, and have already obtained such great merit. Good son! As to what you asked, how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva practices and cultivates the bodhisattva path: good son, you may enter this great tower of Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury and look all around within it and observe, and then you will be able to understand how to learn the bodhisattva practices, and having learned them, to accomplish measureless merit.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

」 — the closing bracket at the very end of this passage is the first to appear since the chain of similes began with 菩提心者，猶如種子, the mind of bodhi is like a seed, in Part 1290. Every 「善男子！ between that point and this one opened a fresh address without closing the last, and the single 」 here ends the whole of Maitreya's speech at once. The English does not carry an opening quotation mark from so far back, so it does not add a closing one here; the reading note records the boundary instead.

如汝所問 — Maitreya's last words return to the question Good-Wealth put in the opening lines of this fascicle, 云何學菩薩行？云何修菩薩道？, and answer it not with a further teaching but with an instruction to enter the tower and look. 金剛際 in the first simile is rendered the foundation of the vajra realm, as it was in Part 1190, where a great lotus rose from it; the four vajra similes here bring the series that began in Part 1306 to a total of eighteen drawn from vajra itself, with the tree named Rootless as the single exception. Fascicle seventy-eight is now complete; the sūtra's own colophon line that follows, naming the fascicle, is not part of the translated text.
