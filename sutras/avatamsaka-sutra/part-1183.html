Ten Minds for One Vow

The first four birth-treasuries are unpacked one at a time — a single vow to revere every buddha, the mind of bodhi itself opened into ten distinct minds, diligent cultivation across a further nine, and a mind grown firm as vajra, illuminating the three times.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1183 — fascicle 74 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Minds for One Vow

The garden-spirit turns from naming all ten birth-treasuries at once to explaining them one by one, beginning with a vow made at the very moment the bodhisattva's resolve first arises.

Chinese Source

「善男子！云何名：願常供養一切佛受生藏？善男子！菩薩初發心時，作如是願：『我當尊重、恭敬、供養一切諸佛，見佛無厭，於諸佛所，常生愛樂，常起深信，修諸功德，恒無休息。』是為菩薩為一切智始集善根受生藏。「云何名：發菩提心受生藏？善男子！此菩薩發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心。所謂：起大悲心，救護一切眾生故；起供養佛心，究竟承事故；起普求正法心，一切無吝故；起廣大趣向心，求一切智故；起慈無量心，普攝眾生故；起不捨一切眾生心，被求一切智堅誓甲故；起無諂誑心，得如實智故；起如說行心，修菩薩道故；起不誑諸佛心，守護一切佛大誓願故；起一切智願心，盡未來化眾生不休息故。菩薩以如是等佛剎微塵數菩提心功德故，得生如來家。是為菩薩第二受生藏。「云何名：觀諸法門勤修行受生藏？善男子！此菩薩摩訶薩，起觀一切法門海心，起迴向一切智圓滿道心，起正念無過失業心，起一切菩薩三昧海清淨心，起修成一切菩薩功德心，起莊嚴一切菩薩道心，起求一切智大精進行、修諸功德如劫火熾然無休息心，起修普賢行教化一切眾生心，起善學一切威儀、修菩薩功德、捨離一切所有、住無所有真實心。是為菩薩第三受生藏。「云何名：以深淨心普照三世受生藏？善男子！此菩薩具清淨增上心，得如來菩提光，入菩薩方便海，其心堅固猶若金剛，背捨一切諸有趣生，成就一切佛自在力，修殊勝行，具菩薩根，其心明潔，願力不動，常為諸佛之所護念，破壞一切諸障礙山，普為眾生作所依處。是為菩薩第四受生藏。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, what is meant by the birth-treasury of the vow to constantly make offerings to every buddha? Good son, when a bodhisattva first gives rise to the resolve, he makes this vow: 'I shall revere, honour, and make offerings to every buddha; seeing a buddha, I shall never grow weary of it; toward every buddha I shall constantly give rise to love and delight, constantly give rise to deep faith, cultivate every merit, and forever be without rest.' This is the bodhisattva's birth-treasury of first gathering wholesome roots for the sake of all-knowing wisdom. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of giving rise to the mind of bodhi? Good son, this bodhisattva gives rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. That is to say: he gives rise to the mind of great compassion, because he rescues and protects every being; he gives rise to the mind of making offerings to the Buddha, because he serves him to the very end; he gives rise to the mind of universally seeking the true dharma, because he withholds nothing at all; he gives rise to the mind of vast and expansive aspiration, because he seeks all-knowing wisdom; he gives rise to the mind of measureless loving-kindness, because he universally gathers in every being; he gives rise to the mind that never abandons any being, because he dons the firm armour of the vow to seek all-knowing wisdom; he gives rise to the mind free of flattery and deceit, because he attains wisdom that accords with reality; he gives rise to the mind that acts as it speaks, because he cultivates the bodhisattva path; he gives rise to the mind that never deceives any buddha, because he guards every buddha's great vow; and he gives rise to the mind that vows all-knowing wisdom, because he transforms beings to the very end of future time, without rest. Through the merit of resolves for bodhi as many as this, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, the bodhisattva comes to be born into the tathāgata's own family. This is the bodhisattva's second birth-treasury. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of contemplating every dharma-gate and diligently cultivating it? Good son, this bodhisattva-mahāsattva gives rise to the mind that contemplates the whole ocean of every dharma-gate; gives rise to the mind that dedicates itself to the perfect and complete path of all-knowing wisdom; gives rise to the mind of right mindfulness free of any faulty action; gives rise to the mind purified through the whole ocean of every bodhisattva samādhi; gives rise to the mind that cultivates and accomplishes every bodhisattva merit; gives rise to the mind that adorns every bodhisattva path; gives rise to the mind of vigorous practice that seeks all-knowing wisdom, cultivating every merit as blazing and unresting as a kalpa-ending fire; gives rise to the mind that cultivates Samantabhadra's own practices to teach and transform every being; and gives rise to the real mind that skilfully learns every dignified bearing, cultivates the bodhisattva's merit, relinquishes every possession, and abides in having nothing at all. This is the bodhisattva's third birth-treasury. "What is meant by the birth-treasury of a mind profound and pure, universally illuminating the three times? Good son, this bodhisattva is endowed with a pure mind of ever-increasing resolve, attains the light of the tathāgata's own bodhi, and enters the ocean of the bodhisattva's skilful means; his mind is firm as vajra itself, and he turns his back on every destiny of existence; he accomplishes every buddha's own sovereign power, cultivates supreme practices, and is endowed with the bodhisattva's own faculties; his mind is bright and clear, and the power of his vow is unmoving; he is forever held in the mindful protection of every buddha, breaks apart every mountain of obstruction, and universally becomes a refuge for every being. This is the bodhisattva's fourth birth-treasury.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

起大悲心⋯⋯起一切智願心 — he gives rise to the mind of great compassion⋯⋯he gives rise to the mind that vows all-knowing wisdom. Counted directly, the second birth-treasury alone unpacks a single act — giving rise to the mind of bodhi — into exactly ten distinct minds, each paired with its own reason, joined by 故, because. The list this Part opened with named ten birth-treasuries in ten short titles; this one shows what just the second of those ten actually contains once opened up.
