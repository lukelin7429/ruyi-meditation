The Bodhisattvas' Own Extraordinary Qualities

Having entered all one hundred and one samādhis, these bodhisattvas are described in an unbroken run of qualities — traveling to every buddha-land attached to nothing, observing every dharma while abiding nowhere — closing on five likenesses applied to five different things at once.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 876 — the bodhisattvas' own extraordinary qualities Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Five Likenesses, Five Subjects

Earlier in this fascicle, six likenesses were applied evenly across six subjects — illusion, reflection, dream, mirror-image, heat-mirage, and transformation, one each for beings, buddhas, rebirth, karma, arising existence, and worlds. Here the sūtra returns to the same teaching with a related but distinct set: illusion, dream, reflection, echo, and transformation, applied respectively to dharma-gateways, sentient beings, tathāgatas, speech, and every dharma at once.

Chinese Source

其諸菩薩皆悉具足大智神通，明利自在，住於諸地，以廣大智普觀一切；從諸智慧種性而生，一切智智常現在前，得離癡翳清淨智眼，為諸眾生作調御師；住佛平等，於一切法無有分別；了達境界，知諸世間性皆寂滅無有依處；普詣一切諸佛國土而無所著，悉能觀察一切諸法而無所住，遍入一切妙法宮殿而無所來；教化調伏一切世間，普為眾生現安隱處；智慧解脫，為其所行；恒以智身住離貪際，超諸有海，示真實際；智光圓滿，普見諸法；住於三昧，堅固不動；於諸眾生恒起大悲，知諸法門悉皆如幻，一切眾生悉皆如夢，一切如來悉皆如影，一切言音悉皆如響，一切諸法悉皆如化。

Ru-Yi Translation

All these bodhisattvas are fully endowed with great wisdom and spiritual superknowledge, sharp and clear, sovereign and free, abiding upon every ground, universally observing everything through vast wisdom; born from the very lineage of wisdom itself, the wisdom of all-knowing wisdom constantly present before them, having attained the pure eye of wisdom free of the cataract of ignorance, they act as the tamer and guide for every sentient being. They abide in the buddha's own equality, making no distinction whatsoever among any dharma; having fully penetrated every domain, they know that the nature of every world is entirely quiescent, with no place to rely upon at all. They universally travel to every buddha-land while attached to nothing at all, are able to observe every dharma completely while abiding nowhere, and enter every wondrous dharma-palace everywhere while coming from nowhere. They teach and tame every world, universally manifesting a place of peace and security for every sentient being; wisdom and liberation are what they practice. They constantly abide, through their own wisdom-body, at the limit free of craving, transcending every ocean of existence, revealing the true, ultimate limit; the light of their own wisdom is fully perfected, universally seeing every dharma; they abide within samādhi, firm and unmoving. Toward every sentient being they constantly arouse great compassion, knowing that every dharma-gateway is entirely like an illusion, that every sentient being is entirely like a dream, that every tathāgata is entirely like a reflection, that every sound of speech is entirely like an echo, and that every dharma is entirely like a magical transformation.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知諸法門悉皆如幻……一切諸法悉皆如化 — knowing every dharma-gateway is entirely like an illusion, all the way to every dharma being entirely like a magical transformation. This teaching returns from earlier in this fascicle, but the pairing has shifted: dharma-gateways, beings, tathāgatas, speech, and dharma itself each now receive their own likeness, compassion arising precisely from seeing all five this way rather than despite it.
