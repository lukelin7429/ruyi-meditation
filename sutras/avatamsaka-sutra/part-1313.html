In a Certain Place, a Certain Bodhisattva

Good-Wealth sees his own body before every one of those tathāgatas and forgets nothing of what he sees. Then the jeweled nets, bells, and musical instruments of every tower begin to sound the dharma, and what they tell is a series of lives: a certain bodhisattva in a certain place, urged by a certain good spiritual friend, who arouses the mind, cultivates the pāramitās, gives away his kingdom and his own body, upholds the true dharma and builds stūpas and shrines; and a certain tathāgata who attains enlightenment in a certain kalpa and teaches measureless beings.

In a Certain Place, a Certain Bodhisattva

The sounds of the tower, and the five stories they tell — four of an unnamed bodhisattva and one of an unnamed tathāgata.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財自見其身，在彼一切諸如來所；亦見於彼一切眾會、一切佛事，憶持不忘，通達無礙。復聞一切諸樓閣內，寶網鈴鐸及諸樂器，皆悉演暢不可思議微妙法音，說種種法。所謂：或說菩薩發菩提心，或說修行波羅蜜行，或說諸願，或說諸地，或說恭敬供養如來，或說莊嚴諸佛國土，或說諸佛說法差別。如上所說一切佛法，悉聞其音，敷暢辨了。又聞某處，有某菩薩，聞某法門，某善知識之所勸導發菩提心，於某劫、某剎、某如來所、某大眾中，聞於某佛如是功德，發如是心，起如是願，種於如是廣大善根；經若干劫修菩薩行，於爾許時當成正覺，如是名號，如是壽量，如是國土，具足莊嚴，滿如是願，化如是眾，如是聲聞、菩薩眾會；般涅槃後，正法住世，經爾許劫，利益如是無量眾生。或聞某處，有某菩薩，布施、持戒、忍辱、精進、禪定、智慧，修習如是諸波羅蜜。或聞某處有某菩薩，為求法故，棄捨王位及諸珍寶、妻子、眷屬、手、足、頭、目，一切身分皆無所吝。或聞某處，有某菩薩，守護如來所說正法，為大法師，廣行法施，建法幢，吹法螺，擊法鼓，雨法雨，造佛塔廟，作佛形像，施諸眾生一切樂具。或聞某處，有某如來，於某劫中，成等正覺，如是國土，如是眾會，如是壽命，說如是法，滿如是願，教化如是無量眾生。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth saw his own body in the presence of every one of those tathāgatas, and saw also all of their assemblies and all of their buddha-work, keeping it in mind without forgetting and penetrating it without obstruction. He heard, moreover, within all the towers, the jeweled nets, the bells, and every musical instrument all proclaiming inconceivable, subtle, and wondrous sounds of the dharma, teaching every kind of dharma. That is to say, some told of the bodhisattva's arousing of the mind of bodhi, some told of the cultivation of the practices of the pāramitās, some told of the vows, some told of the grounds, some told of reverence and offering to the tathāgatas, some told of the adornment of the buddha-lands, and some told of the differences among the buddhas' ways of teaching the dharma. Every buddha-dharma spoken of above he heard in those sounds, set forth in full and clearly distinguished. He heard, too, that in a certain place there was a certain bodhisattva who heard a certain gate of the dharma, who was urged by a certain good spiritual friend to arouse the mind of bodhi, and who in a certain kalpa, in a certain land, in the presence of a certain tathāgata, within a certain great assembly, hearing of such merits of a certain buddha, aroused such a mind, gave rise to such vows, and planted such vast and great wholesome roots; that over so many kalpas he cultivated the bodhisattva's practices, and after such a length of time would attain perfect enlightenment, with such a name, such a lifespan, and such a land, fully adorned, fulfilling such vows, transforming such beings, with such an assembly of śrāvakas and bodhisattvas; and that after his parinirvāṇa the true dharma would abide in the world for such a number of kalpas, benefiting such measureless sentient beings. Or he heard that in a certain place there was a certain bodhisattva who cultivated such pāramitās as these, giving, upholding the precepts, patience, diligence, meditative concentration, and wisdom. Or he heard that in a certain place there was a certain bodhisattva who, for the sake of seeking the dharma, gave up his royal throne together with every precious treasure, his wife and children, his retinue, and his hands, feet, head, and eyes, begrudging no part of his body at all. Or he heard that in a certain place there was a certain bodhisattva who guarded the true dharma spoken by the tathāgatas, and as a great dharma-teacher widely practiced the giving of the dharma, raising the banner of the dharma, blowing the conch of the dharma, beating the drum of the dharma, raining down the rain of the dharma, building stūpas and shrines for the buddhas, making images of the buddhas, and giving sentient beings every requisite of pleasure. Or he heard that in a certain place there was a certain tathāgata who, in a certain kalpa, attained perfect enlightenment, with such a land, such an assembly, and such a lifespan, teaching such a dharma, fulfilling such vows, and teaching and transforming such measureless sentient beings.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

某 — the character occurs eighteen times in this passage, and 如是 eighteen times, and between them they carry the whole of what the sounds tell. 某, a certain, marks what is left unnamed, the place, the bodhisattva, the friend, the kalpa, the land, the tathāgata; 如是, such, marks what was heard in full but is not repeated, the name, the lifespan, the land, the vows. The translation keeps both markers at every occurrence rather than supplying invented particulars or smoothing them away, since the passage is describing the form of what Good-Wealth heard rather than reporting its contents.

或聞某處 — the accounts that follow the first are introduced by the same formula, and there are five of them in all counting the first, which opens with 又聞某處: a bodhisattva's whole career from arousing the mind to the abiding of the true dharma after parinirvāṇa; a bodhisattva cultivating the six pāramitās, named here in their standard order; a bodhisattva giving up kingdom, family, and the parts of his own body for the sake of the dharma; a bodhisattva as dharma-teacher, with the banner, conch, drum, and rain of the dharma; and a tathāgata's enlightenment, land, assembly, and lifespan.
