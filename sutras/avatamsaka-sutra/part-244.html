The One of Most Supreme Wisdom

Vajra Banner Bodhisattva's verse reaches its final stanzas, gathering every living being throughout the ten directions without exception into the bodhisattva's dedication, seeking benefit for no self at all but only that every being attain peace and happiness. The verse's closing lines name a wisdom foremost, vast, free of falsehood, free of inversion, equal, true, and pure — completing both the verse and the entire third dedication.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 244 — the verse concludes, the third dedication complete Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Gathered Without Exception

The verse's final movement widens its scope one last time — every living being throughout the ten directions, every sentient kind, every child of the Buddha — gathered in with an equal mind that leaves nothing out at all. Its closing image turns from scope to substance, naming a wisdom so thoroughly qualified — foremost, vast, unerring, equal, true, pure — that the accumulation of epithets itself performs what the verse has been describing all along: dedication offered without holding anything back.

Chinese Source

十方一切諸世界，所有眾生咸攝受，
悉以善根迴向彼，願令具足安隱樂。
不為自身求利益，欲令一切悉安樂，
未曾暫起戲論心，但觀諸法空無我。
十方無量諸最勝，所見一切真佛子，
悉以善根迴向彼，願使速成無上覺。
一切世間含識類，等心攝取無有餘，
以我所行諸善業，令彼眾生速成佛。
無量無邊諸大願，無上導師所演說，
願諸佛子皆清淨，隨其心樂悉成滿。
普觀十方諸世界，悉以功德施於彼，
願令皆具妙莊嚴，菩薩如是學迴向。

Ru-Yi Translation

Throughout every world in the ten directions, they gather in every living being there entirely; dedicating every wholesome root toward them entirely, wishing to cause them to be fully endowed with peaceful and secure bliss. Not seeking benefit for their own self, but wishing that all attain peace and happiness entirely; never once giving rise to a mind of frivolous discourse even for a moment, but only contemplating that every dharma is empty and without self. Every immeasurable most supreme one throughout the ten directions, and every true child of the Buddha they behold; they dedicate every wholesome root toward them entirely, wishing to cause them to swiftly accomplish unsurpassed awakening. Every sentient kind throughout the world, they gather in with an equal mind, leaving nothing out at all; through every wholesome karmic deed that I myself practice, may those living beings swiftly accomplish buddhahood. Immeasurable, boundless great vows, expounded by the unsurpassed Guiding Teacher himself; may every child of the Buddha become entirely pure, and according to whatever their mind delights in, may it all be entirely fulfilled. Universally beholding every world throughout the ten directions, they give every merit to them entirely; wishing to cause them all to be fully endowed with wondrous adornment — the bodhisattva studies dedication in just this way.

十方一切諸世間，悉是眾生想分別，
於想非想無所得，如是了達於諸想。
彼諸菩薩身淨已，則意清淨無瑕穢，

及以現在天人尊，悉學於其所說法。
三世一切諸如來，智慧明達心無礙，
為欲利益眾生故，迴向菩提集眾業。
彼第一慧廣大慧，不虛妄慧無倒慧，
平等實慧清淨慧，最勝慧者如是說。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Their mind does not weigh and measure any dual dharma at all, but forever penetrates and understands that dharmas are without duality; whether dharmas are dual or non-dual, within them there is, in the end, nothing at all to cling to. Every world throughout the ten directions is entirely the discriminating perception of living beings; within perception and non-perception alike, nothing at all can be obtained — one penetrates and understands every perception in just this way. Once those very bodhisattvas' own bodies are pure, then their intention is likewise pure, without any blemish or defilement; their verbal karma already pure, without any fault at all, one should know that their intention is pure, clinging to nothing at all. With a single mind and right mindfulness they recollect the buddhas of the past, and likewise remember every Guiding Teacher of the future; and also the Honored One of gods and humans in the present — they study the Dharma each of them has expounded entirely. Every Tathāgata of the three periods of time, their wisdom bright and penetrating, their mind unobstructed; wishing to benefit living beings, they dedicate toward bodhi, gathering every karmic deed. That very foremost wisdom, vast and expansive wisdom, wisdom free of falsehood, wisdom free of inversion; equal and true wisdom, pure wisdom — the one of most supreme wisdom speaks in just this way."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

"Never once giving rise to a mind of frivolous discourse even for a moment, but only contemplating that every dharma is empty and without self" names the discipline underlying everything else this verse claims — the bodhisattva's vast dedications toward every being in every world are held together not by ambition but by a mind that has stopped generating idle speculation, leaving only the direct contemplation of emptiness and non-self as its steady ground.

Third Dedication Complete — 第三等一切佛迴向

With this verse's close, the third of the ten dedications — the dedication equal to every buddha — is now complete. Its exposition opened with equanimity before every form and touch, moved through ten kinds of buddha-bliss and dedications extended in turn toward bodhisattvas and every living being down to a single grain of food offered to an animal, named nineteen honorific qualifiers for the dedication itself, and closed with this verse's own inventory of bliss offered without attachment to the offering.
