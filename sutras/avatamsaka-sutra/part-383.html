A Pure Dharma Body, Free of Every Fault

Having wished every sentient being a pure, wondrous voice in the previous Part, the dedication turns to the body — first ten qualities free of every fault, from a pure Dharma body to pure eloquence, then ten kinds of pure, wondrous body, radiant and unobstructed, each one like a bright mirror in which every form spontaneously appears, showing sentient beings the entire path to buddhahood.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 383 — a pure Dharma body, and ten kinds of wondrous body Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ten Things Free of Every Fault

Each clause repeats the same refrain — free of every fault — applied in turn to the Dharma body, to merit, to the marks of a buddha, to karmic fruit, to the mind of omniscience, and finally to right mindfulness, wisdom, and eloquence together.

Chinese Source

』「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩復以諸善根如是迴向，所謂：『願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨法身，願一切眾生得離眾過惡淨妙功德，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨妙相，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨業果，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨一切智心，願一切眾生得離眾過惡無量清淨菩提心，願一切眾生得離眾過惡了知諸根清淨方便，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨信解，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨勤修無礙行願，願一切眾生得離眾過惡清淨正念、智慧辯才。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! This bodhisattva mahāsattva further dedicates every wholesome root in this way, namely: may every sentient being attain a pure Dharma body, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain pure, wondrous merit, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain a pure, wondrous mark, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain a pure karmic fruit, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain a pure mind of omniscience, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain an immeasurable, pure mind of enlightenment, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain the pure skillful means of comprehending every faculty, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain pure faith and understanding, free of every fault; may every sentient being attain the pure, diligent cultivation of unobstructed practice and vow, free of every fault; and may every sentient being attain pure right mindfulness, wisdom, and eloquence, free of every fault.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

"Free of every fault" attaches to ten very different things — a body, merit, a mark, a karmic fruit, a mind — as though purity were not a special achievement reserved for the most refined categories but a single condition equally available to anything at all, however concrete or abstract.

Ten Kinds of Wondrous Body, Like a Bright Mirror

The passage turns from the abstract to the visible — ten kinds of pure body, radiant and unobstructed — before comparing them all to a bright mirror in which every form spontaneously appears, showing sentient beings everything from the great practice of enlightenment to every buddha's own arising in the world.

Chinese Source

』「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩復以諸善根，為一切眾生如是迴向：『願得種種清淨妙身，所謂：光明身、離濁身、無染身、清淨身、極清淨身、離塵身、極離塵身、離垢身、可愛樂身、無障礙身。於一切世界現諸業像，於一切世間現言說像，於一切宮殿現安立像。如淨明鏡，種種色像自然顯現，示諸眾生大菩提行，示諸眾生甚深妙法，示諸眾生種種功德，示諸眾生修行之道，示諸眾生成就之行，示諸眾生菩薩行願，示諸眾生於一世界、一切世界佛興於世，示諸眾生一切諸佛神通變化，示諸眾生一切菩薩不可思議解脫威力，示諸眾生成滿普賢菩薩行願一切智性。』菩薩摩訶薩以如是等微妙淨身，方便攝取一切眾生，悉令成就清淨功德一切智身。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! This bodhisattva mahāsattva further dedicates every wholesome root for the sake of every sentient being in this way: may they attain every kind of pure, wondrous body, namely: a radiant body, a body free of turbidity, a body free of defilement, a pure body, a supremely pure body, a body free of dust, a body utterly free of dust, a body free of stain, a lovable and delightful body, and an unobstructed body. Manifesting images of every kind of karma throughout every world, manifesting images of speech throughout every world, manifesting images of dwelling throughout every palace. Like a pure, bright mirror, every kind of form and image spontaneously appears, showing sentient beings the great practice of enlightenment, showing sentient beings the profound, wondrous Dharma, showing sentient beings every kind of merit, showing sentient beings the path of cultivation, showing sentient beings the practice of accomplishment, showing sentient beings a bodhisattva's own practice and vow, showing sentient beings a buddha's own arising in the world, whether within a single world or throughout every world, showing sentient beings every buddha's own spiritual transformation, showing sentient beings every bodhisattva's own inconceivable, majestic power of liberation, and showing sentient beings the nature of omniscience that fulfills and completes Samantabhadra's own bodhisattva practice and vow." With subtle, pure bodies such as these, a bodhisattva mahāsattva skillfully gathers in every sentient being, causing them all to accomplish the body of omniscience adorned with pure merit.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The mirror does not merely reflect; it teaches. Every image that spontaneously appears in it is described as "showing sentient beings" something — the path of cultivation, a buddha's own arising, the completion of Samantabhadra's own vow. A pure body, in this passage, is valuable not for its own radiance but for what beings standing before it come to see.
