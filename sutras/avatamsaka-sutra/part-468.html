A Million Asaṃkhyeyas of Dhāraṇī

The four wisdoms finish their rounds on the largest possible subject — the vehicles, the ten grounds, the buddhas' awakening in a single thought, the Tathāgata's own speech. Then the retention-gateways arrive, and a seat is set out, and a whole trichiliocosm of beings is taught at once.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 468 — the dhāraṇīs, and the Dharma-seat Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Last Rounds

One vehicle and many vehicles; the ten grounds as stages and as one road; the buddhas awakening in a single thought and in every time and place; and finally the Tathāgata's voice itself, differentiated into eighty-four thousand kinds of hearer.

Chinese Source

復次，法無礙智，知一乘平等性；義無礙智，知諸乘差別性；辭無礙智，說一切乘無差別；樂說無礙智，說一一乘無邊法。復次，法無礙智，知一切菩薩行、智行、法行智隨證；義無礙智，知十地分位義差別；辭無礙智，說地道無差別相；樂說無礙智，說一一地無邊行相。復次，法無礙智，知一切如來一念成正覺；義無礙智，知種種時、種種處等各差別；辭無礙智，說成正覺差別；樂說無礙智，於一一句法無量劫說不盡。

Ru-Yi Translation

Again, the unhindered wisdom of dharmas knows the sameness of the one vehicle; the unhindered wisdom of meaning knows the differing natures of the several vehicles; the unhindered wisdom of language expounds all vehicles as without difference; and the unhindered wisdom of eloquence expounds the boundless dharmas of each single vehicle. Again, the unhindered wisdom of dharmas knows all bodhisattva practice, the conduct of wisdom, and the conduct of dharma, its wisdom realizing them as they go; the unhindered wisdom of meaning knows the differences in meaning among the divisions and stations of the ten grounds; the unhindered wisdom of language expounds the aspect in which the path of the grounds is without difference; and the unhindered wisdom of eloquence expounds the boundless aspects of practice of each single ground. Again, the unhindered wisdom of dharmas knows that all the Tathāgatas accomplish perfect awakening in a single thought; the unhindered wisdom of meaning knows the differences among the various times and the various places and the rest; the unhindered wisdom of language expounds the differences in that accomplishing of perfect awakening; and the unhindered wisdom of eloquence, upon each single phrase of the Dharma, could speak for measureless kalpas without exhausting it.

Chinese Source

復次，法無礙智，知一切如來語、力、無所畏、不共佛法，大慈大悲，辯才方便，轉法輪，一切智智隨證；義無礙智，知如來隨八萬四千眾生心、行、根、解、差別音聲；辭無礙智，隨一切眾生行，以如來音聲差別說；樂說無礙智，隨眾生信解，以如來智清淨行圓滿說。

Ru-Yi Translation

Again, the unhindered wisdom of dharmas knows the Tathāgata's speech, his powers, his fearlessnesses and the unshared buddha-dharmas, his great loving-kindness and great compassion, his eloquence and skillful means, and his turning of the Dharma-wheel, its all-knowing wisdom realizing them as they go; the unhindered wisdom of meaning knows the Tathāgata's differentiated voice, following the minds, the conduct, the faculties and the understandings of eighty-four thousand kinds of being; the unhindered wisdom of language, following the conduct of every being, speaks with the Tathāgata's voice in its differences; and the unhindered wisdom of eloquence, following what beings have faith in and understand, speaks with the fullness of the Tathāgata's wisdom and pure conduct.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知一乘平等性…知諸乘差別性 — knows the sameness of the one vehicle, knows the differing natures of the several vehicles. The two halves are assigned to different wisdoms rather than argued between. The one-vehicle teaching and the three-vehicle teaching are not reconciled here; they are given as what two distinct faculties respectively see, and the Dharma-master needs both.

The Seat, and What Is Retained

Ten dhāraṇīs are named and then multiplied past counting; and then, on the Dharma-seat, the only beings not outshone are buddhas and bodhisattvas of the tenth ground.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩住第九地，得如是善巧無礙智，得如來妙法藏，作大法師，得義陀羅尼、法陀羅尼、智陀羅尼、光照陀羅尼、善慧陀羅尼、眾財陀羅尼、威德陀羅尼、無礙門陀羅尼、無邊際陀羅尼、種種義陀羅尼，如是等百萬阿僧祇陀羅尼門皆得圓滿，以百萬阿僧祇善巧音聲辯才門而演說法。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! A bodhisattva abiding on the ninth ground, having attained such skillful unhindered wisdom, obtains the Tathāgata's wondrous treasury of Dharma and becomes a great Dharma-master. They obtain the dhāraṇī of meaning, the dhāraṇī of dharma, the dhāraṇī of wisdom, the dhāraṇī of illuminating radiance, the dhāraṇī of excellent wisdom, the dhāraṇī of abundant wealth, the dhāraṇī of awesome virtue, the dhāraṇī of the unhindered gateway, the dhāraṇī of the boundless, and the dhāraṇī of manifold meanings; a million asaṃkhyeyas of such dhāraṇī-gateways as these are all brought to fullness in them, and with a million asaṃkhyeyas of gateways of skillful voice and eloquence they expound the Dharma.

Chinese Source

此菩薩得如是百萬阿僧祇陀羅尼門已，於無量佛所一一佛前，悉以如是百萬阿僧祇陀羅尼門聽聞正法，聞已不忘，以無量差別門為他演說。此菩薩初見於佛，頭頂禮敬，即於佛所得無量法門；此所得法門，非彼聞持諸大聲聞，於百千劫所能領受。此菩薩得如是陀羅尼、如是無礙智，坐於法座而說於法；大千世界滿中眾生，隨其心樂差別為說；唯除諸佛及受職菩薩，其餘眾會威德光明無能與比。

Ru-Yi Translation

Having obtained a million asaṃkhyeyas of dhāraṇī-gateways such as these, this bodhisattva, in the presence of measureless buddhas, before each single buddha, hears the true Dharma by means of these million asaṃkhyeyas of dhāraṇī-gateways; and having heard it, does not forget it, and expounds it for others through measureless differentiated gateways. When this bodhisattva first sees a buddha and bows their head to the ground in reverence, they at once obtain measureless dharma-gateways in that buddha's presence; and the dharma-gateways so obtained are such as those great śrāvakas famed for hearing and retaining could not take in through a hundred thousand kalpas. Having obtained such dhāraṇīs and such unhindered wisdom, this bodhisattva sits upon the Dharma-seat and expounds the Dharma; and for the beings filling a great trichiliocosm they expound it according to the differences in what each mind delights in. Excepting only the buddhas and the bodhisattvas who have received consecration, in that whole assembly there is none whose awesome virtue and radiance can be compared with theirs.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

陀羅尼 — dhāraṇī, from a root meaning to hold or retain. What is described here is not a formula recited but a capacity not to lose what has been heard, which is why the passage immediately measures it against the great śrāvakas famed for exactly that. 聞持, hearing and retaining, was Ānanda's particular excellence.

唯除諸佛及受職菩薩 — excepting only the buddhas and the bodhisattvas who have received consecration. 受職 is the tenth ground's consecration, and the exception is a signpost: the only station above this one is the one the chapter has left to describe.
