The Tathāgata's Manifestation Begins

Fascicle six opens the sūtra's second chapter, "The Tathāgata's Manifestation" (如來現相品) — the first new chapter since the extraordinary assembly-gathering that has run continuously since the sūtra's opening line. The bodhisattvas and world-lords silently wonder what the ground, domain, and power of a buddha even are; light from the Buddha's own teeth answers not in words but by revealing ten world-systems in the ten directions, each with its own buddha, land, and bodhisattva leader, who arrive bearing clouds of offering unique to their own world.

The Assembly's Unspoken Question

Chinese Source

爾時，諸菩薩及一切世間主，作是思惟：「云何是諸佛地？云何是諸佛境界？云何是諸佛加持？云何是諸佛所行？云何是諸佛力？云何是諸佛無所畏？云何是諸佛三昧？云何是諸佛神通？云何是諸佛自在？云何是諸佛無能攝取？云何是諸佛眼？云何是諸佛耳？云何是諸佛鼻？云何是諸佛舌？云何是諸佛身？云何是諸佛意？云何是諸佛身光？云何是諸佛光明？云何是諸佛聲？云何是諸佛智？唯願世尊，哀愍我等，開示演說！又，十方世界海一切諸佛，皆為諸菩薩說世界海、眾生海、法界安立海、佛海、佛波羅蜜海、佛解脫海、佛變化海、佛演說海、佛名號海、佛壽量海，及一切菩薩誓願海、一切菩薩發趣海、一切菩薩助道海、一切菩薩乘海、一切菩薩行海，一切菩薩出離海、一切菩薩神通海、一切菩薩波羅蜜海、一切菩薩地海、一切菩薩智海。願佛世尊，亦為我等，如是而說！」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the bodhisattvas and every lord of the world had this thought: "What is the ground of every buddha? What is the domain of every buddha? What is the empowerment of every buddha? What is the conduct of every buddha? What is the power of every buddha? What is the fearlessness of every buddha? What is the samādhi of every buddha? What is the spiritual transmutation of every buddha? What is the sovereignty of every buddha? What is the ungraspable, unequaled nature of every buddha? What is the eye of every buddha? What is the ear of every buddha? What is the nose of every buddha? What is the tongue of every buddha? What is the body of every buddha? What is the mind of every buddha? What is the bodily light of every buddha? What is the radiance of every buddha? What is the voice of every buddha? What is the wisdom of every buddha? We beseech the World-Honored One, in compassion for us, to reveal and expound this! Moreover, every buddha throughout the ocean of worlds of the ten directions teaches every bodhisattva the ocean of world-systems, the ocean of living beings, the ocean of the establishment of the Dharma-realm, the ocean of buddhas, the ocean of the buddhas' pāramitās, the ocean of the buddhas' liberation, the ocean of the buddhas' transformations, the ocean of the buddhas' proclamations, the ocean of the buddhas' names, and the ocean of the buddhas' lifespans, together with the ocean of every bodhisattva's vow, the ocean of every bodhisattva's setting-out, the ocean of every bodhisattva's practices that assist the path, the ocean of every bodhisattva's vehicle, the ocean of every bodhisattva's conduct, the ocean of every bodhisattva's going-forth, the ocean of every bodhisattva's spiritual transmutation, the ocean of every bodhisattva's pāramitās, the ocean of every bodhisattva's grounds, and the ocean of every bodhisattva's wisdom. May the Buddha, the World-Honored One, also teach us in just this way!"

「無量劫中修行滿，菩提樹下成正覺，
為度眾生普現身，如雲充遍盡未來。
眾生有疑皆使斷，廣大信解悉令發，
無邊際苦普使除，諸佛安樂咸令證。

菩薩無數等剎塵，俱來此會同瞻仰，
願隨其意所應受，演說妙法除疑惑！
云何了知諸佛地？云何觀察如來境？
佛所加持無有邊，願示此法令清淨！

云何是佛所行處，而以智慧能明入？
佛力清淨廣無邊，為諸菩薩應開示！
云何廣大諸三昧？云何淨治無畏法？
神通力用不可量，願隨眾生心樂說！

諸佛法王如世主，所行自在無能制，
及餘一切廣大法，為利益故當開演！
佛眼云何無有量？耳鼻舌身亦復然？
意無有量復云何？願示能知此方便！

如諸剎海眾生海，法界所有安立海，
及諸佛海亦無邊，願為佛子咸開暢！
永出思議眾度海，普入解脫方便海，
所有一切法門海，此道場中願宣說！」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, through the majestic, spiritual power of the bodhisattvas, from within every cloud of offering, a voice arose spontaneously, speaking a verse, saying:

"Across immeasurable kalpas, his practice brought to fulfillment, beneath the bodhi tree he accomplished true awakening;
to liberate living beings he manifests his body universally, filling everything like a cloud, to the very end of the future.
Every doubt in living beings he causes to be severed entirely, every vast faith and understanding he causes to arise;
every boundless suffering he causes to be removed universally, and the peace and joy of every buddha he causes all to realize.

Bodhisattvas beyond number, equal to the motes of dust in every land, have come together to this very assembly, gazing up at him together;
may he, according to what is fitting for their minds to receive, expound the wondrous Dharma and remove their doubt and confusion!
How may one understand the ground of every buddha? How may one contemplate the Tathāgata's own domain?
The empowerment of the Buddha is without any bound — may he reveal this Dharma and cause it to become pure!

What is the very place where the Buddha conducts himself, that one might, through wisdom, be able to clearly enter it?
The Buddha's power is pure, vast, and boundless — for the sake of the bodhisattvas, may he reveal it!
What are the vast samādhis of every kind? How is the Dharma of fearlessness purified and cultivated?
The function of his power of spiritual transmutation cannot be measured — may he teach it according to what living beings' minds delight in!

Every buddha, the Dharma-King, is like a sovereign of the world, his own conduct free and sovereign, controlled by nothing;
and every other vast Dharma besides — for the sake of benefiting us, may he open and expound it!
How is the Buddha's eye without any measure? And his ear, nose, tongue, and body likewise so?
And how is his mind, too, without any measure? May he reveal the skillful means by which this may be known!

Just as there is the ocean of every land and the ocean of every living being, and the ocean of the establishment of everything in the Dharma-realm,
and the ocean of every buddha likewise without bound — may he open and expound all of it fully for the sake of the Buddha's children!
Forever going beyond the ocean of every conceivable pāramitā, universally entering the ocean of the skillful means of liberation,
and the ocean of every Dharma-gateway that exists — within this very awakening-site, may he proclaim it!"

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This unspoken question, never voiced aloud by any single questioner, functions across the whole sūtra as something close to a table of contents: the ten questions about a buddha's ground, domain, empowerment, and so on, together with the twenty "oceans" of the bodhisattva path, name topics this text and its companion chapters return to at length. The chapter's own answer, notably, does not come as speech — the whole of what follows in this fascicle is the Buddha responding through light, through the arrival of bodhisattvas from countless worlds, and finally through silence, before any teaching is spoken in words at all.

Light from the Buddha's Teeth

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊知諸菩薩心之所念，即於面門眾齒之間，放佛剎微塵數光明，所謂：眾寶華遍照光明、出種種音莊嚴法界光明、垂布微妙雲光明、十方佛坐道場現神變光明、一切寶焰雲蓋光明、充滿法界無礙光明、遍莊嚴一切佛剎光明、逈建立清淨金剛寶幢光明、普莊嚴菩薩眾會道場光明、妙音稱揚一切佛名號光明……。如是等佛剎微塵數，一一復有佛剎微塵數光明以為眷屬，其光悉具眾妙寶色，普照十方各一億佛剎微塵數世界海。彼世界海諸菩薩眾，於光明中，各得見此華藏莊嚴世界海。以佛神力，其光於彼一切菩薩眾會之前而說頌言：

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the World-Honored One, knowing what was in the minds and thoughts of the bodhisattvas, then, from between the teeth within the very gate of his face, emitted light as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land. These were: the light of every jewel's flower, illumining everywhere; the light adorning the Dharma-realm, sending forth every kind of sound; the light of subtle, wondrous clouds hanging draped down; the light of every buddha of the ten directions, seated at the awakening-site, manifesting spiritual transmutation; the light of every jeweled flame's canopy of clouds; the unobstructed light filling the Dharma-realm entirely; the light universally adorning every buddha-land; the light establishing, towering high, the pure vajra jeweled banner; the light universally adorning the awakening-site of the bodhisattva assembly; and the light of a wondrous voice proclaiming the name of every buddha — lights such as these, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, and each and every one of them, moreover, had a retinue of light as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, that light entirely endowed with every wondrous jeweled color, universally illumining, throughout the ten directions, a hundred million world-oceans, each as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land. The bodhisattva assemblies of those world-oceans, within that very light, each came to behold this Flower-Treasury Adorned World-System Ocean. Through the Buddha's spiritual power, that light, before the whole assembly of every bodhisattva there, spoke a verse, saying:

Ru-Yi Translation

"Across an immeasurable ocean of kalpas, cultivating the whole ocean of practice, making offerings to the whole ocean of every buddha of the ten directions,
transforming and liberating the whole ocean of every living being, he has now accomplished wondrous awakening, the universally illumining, honored one.
From within his very pores emerge clouds of transformation, their light shining universally throughout the ten directions,
awakening entirely those fit to be transformed, causing them to hasten toward bodhi, pure and unobstructed.

The Buddha, in ages past, came and went amid every realm of rebirth, teaching and ripening every multitude of living beings,
his sovereign power of spiritual transmutation without any bound, causing all, within a single thought, to attain liberation.
Beneath the wondrous bodhi tree of maṇi jewels, adorned in every kind, all singular and excellent,
the Buddha accomplished true awakening, emitting great light, majestically radiant everywhere.

His great voice, roaring, pervades the ten directions, broadly and universally proclaiming the tranquil, extinguished Dharma,
and, according to what the minds of living beings delight in, through every kind of skillful means, he causes them to be opened and enlightened.
Every pāramitā he practiced in ages past is entirely perfected, equal in number to the fine motes of dust in a thousand lands,
and every power is already accomplished entirely — you should all go there together to gaze upon him and pay homage.

The Buddha's children of the ten directions, as numerous as the motes of dust in every land, all rejoice together and gather here,
having already rained down every cloud as their offering, now before the Buddha, gazing up at him with singular devotion.
The Tathāgata's single voice is without any measure, able to proclaim the deep, great ocean of the scriptures,
universally raining down the wondrous Dharma in response to the multitude's minds — that most honored one among the two-footed, it is fitting to go and behold him.

Every vow of every buddha of the three periods of time, he proclaims it fully beneath the bodhi tree,
and within a single instant it is all made manifest before us — you should swiftly go to the Tathāgata's very place.
Vairocana, the great ocean of wisdom, unfurls light from the very gate of his face, seen by all without exception;
now, awaiting the assembly to gather, he is about to proclaim his voice — you should go and behold it, and hear what he teaches."

Bodhisattvas Arrive from the Ten Directions

Summoned by the light, the assembly of every world-ocean throughout the ten directions came to Vairocana's own place. Ten are named as representative — one from each of the four cardinal directions, the four intermediate directions, and above and below — each with its own world-ocean, land, buddha, and bodhisattva leader, and each arriving with clouds of offering unique to that world alone.

East — Pure Light's Lotus Adornment

清淨光蓮華莊嚴 · 摩尼瓔珞金剛藏 · 法水覺虛空無邊王 · 觀察勝法蓮華幢

To the east of this Flower-Treasury Adorned World-System Ocean, there is next a world-ocean named Pure Light's Lotus Adornment. Within that world-seed there is a land named Maṇi Garland's Vajra Treasury, whose buddha is called the King of Dharma-Water's Awakening, Boundless as Space. Within the great ocean of that Tathāgata's assembly there is a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named Contemplating the Supreme Dharma's Lotus Banner, who, together with bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust in that world-ocean, came to the Buddha's own place. Each of them manifested ten kinds of clouds of bodhisattva bodily forms, filling the whole of space without ever dispersing; and further manifested clouds raining the light of every jeweled lotus, clouds of Sumeru's jeweled peaks, clouds of the sun-wheel's light, clouds of jeweled flower-garlands, clouds of every kind of music, clouds of powdered-incense trees, clouds of every color of anointing incense and burning incense, and clouds of every kind of fragrant tree — clouds of offering such as these, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a world-ocean, filling the whole of space entirely without ever dispersing. Having manifested these clouds, they bowed in homage to the Buddha, making of them their offering. Then, in the eastern direction, each conjured a lion-throne of the treasury of light of every kind of flower, and, upon that very throne, sat cross-legged.

South — Every Jewel Moon's Light-Adorned Treasury

一切寶月光明莊嚴藏 · 無邊光圓滿莊嚴 · 普智光明德須彌王 · 普照法海慧

To the south of this Flower-Treasury World-System Ocean, there is next a world-ocean named Every Jewel Moon's Light-Adorned Treasury. Within that world-seed there is a land named Boundless Light's Perfect Adornment, whose buddha is called King Sumeru of Universal Wisdom's Light and Virtue. Within the great ocean of that Tathāgata's assembly there is a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named Universally Illumining Ocean of Dharma's Wisdom, who, together with bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust in that world-ocean, came to the Buddha's own place. Each of them manifested ten kinds of sovereign maṇi clouds of the treasury of every adorning light, filling the whole of space without ever dispersing; and further manifested sovereign maṇi clouds raining every jeweled ornament, universally shining; sovereign maṇi clouds of blazing jeweled flame, proclaiming the name of every buddha; sovereign maṇi clouds expounding every buddha-dharma; sovereign maṇi clouds of every wondrous tree adorning the awakening-site; sovereign maṇi clouds of jeweled light universally shining, manifesting every conjured buddha; sovereign maṇi clouds universally manifesting the adorned image of every awakening-site; sovereign maṇi clouds of the hidden flame-lamp expounding the domain of every buddha; sovereign maṇi clouds of the image of every inconceivable buddha-land's palace; and sovereign maṇi clouds universally manifesting the image of the body of every buddha of the three periods of time — sovereign maṇi clouds such as these, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a world-ocean, filling the whole of space entirely without ever dispersing. Having manifested these clouds, they bowed in homage to the Buddha, making of them their offering. Then, in the southern direction, each conjured a lion-throne of the lotus-treasury of indranīla jewel and Jambūnada gold, and, upon that very throne, sat cross-legged.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Each of these ten arrivals is structured identically — world-ocean, land, buddha, bodhisattva, then nine or ten named clouds of offering unique to that world alone — and yet no two clouds repeat across all ten descriptions: pavilions in the west, trees in the north, lion-thrones in the northeast, canopies in the southeast, parasols in the southwest, perfect light in the northwest, plain light below, light-flame above. Traditional Huayan reading treats this insistence on structural repetition paired with total content variation as itself a teaching: the same pattern (a world, a buddha, a bodhisattva, an offering) recurs universally across the cosmos without any two instances of it ever being identical — sameness of form holding open, rather than closing down, endless particularity.

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, from within the light of these bodhisattvas, a voice arose simultaneously, speaking this verse, saying:

"From within every light a wondrous voice is sent forth, pervading every land of the ten directions universally,
proclaiming every merit of the Buddha's children, able to enter the wondrous path of bodhi.
Practicing across an ocean of kalpas without ever growing weary, causing suffering living beings to attain liberation,
their minds never inferior, never wearied or fatigued — the Buddha's children skillfully enter this very skillful means.

Exhausting every ocean of kalpas cultivating skillful means, immeasurable, boundless, without remainder,
there is no Dharma-gateway they fail to enter, and yet they forever proclaim its nature as tranquil and extinguished.
Every vow of every buddha of the three periods of time, they cultivate and fulfill it entirely,
and, through this very benefit to every living being, make it their own pure deed of practice.

Within the assembly of every buddha, they go universally throughout the ten directions without exception,
entirely through the profound ocean of wisdom, entering that very Tathāgata's own tranquil, extinguished Dharma.
Each and every light is without any bound, entering every land hard to conceive entirely,
and their pure eye of wisdom is universally able to see it — this is the very domain these bodhisattvas walk.

The bodhisattva is able to dwell at the very tip of a single hair, shaking every land of the ten directions entirely,

Every kalpa that exists across the three periods of time, they are entirely able to manifest within a single instant,

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The sūtra's habit of naming everything at the scale of "as numerous as the fine motes of dust" in some vast unit reaches an almost dizzying density in the summary paragraph above — worlds, bodhisattvas, lights, other bodhisattvas within those lights, motes of dust within the Dharma-realm, and lands within each mote, is a numerical cascade with no final term. Read as cosmology, it is meant to defeat the very attempt to keep count. Read as psychology, the ten Dharma-gateways of awakening this passage lists, from dreams to earthquakes to the simple sharing of Samantabhadra's own vow, suggest something more grounded: that awakening comes through a very wide range of doors, not one privileged method, and that the sheer scale of "as numerous as the fine motes of dust in Mount Sumeru" attached to each kind of person helped — those fallen into evil destinies, those already on the bodhisattva path, everyone in between — insists that no category of being is either too far gone or already too advanced to be included.
