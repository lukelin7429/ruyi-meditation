Nārāyaṇa-Like Valor Completes

The tenth and final Nārāyaṇa-like valor gathers wisdom, vow, and teaching into a single closing statement: a buddha's wisdom equal to every tathāgata's own, appearing in the world at exactly the right moment for beings whose number, like the dharma-realm itself, has no boundary at all.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 604 — the tenth valor, and teaching that never rests Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Never Missing the Right Time

The tenth valor closes the list not with another feat of physical scale, but with timing: a buddha's appearance in the world, exactly when a being is ready, repeated across a boundlessness of beings, worlds, and time that matches the dharma-realm's own boundlessness.

Chinese Source

「佛子！一切諸佛等悟一切諸如來法，等修一切諸菩薩行；若願若智，清淨平等，猶如大海，悉得滿足；行力尊勝，未曾退怯，住諸三昧無量境界，示一切道，勸善誡惡；智力第一演法無畏，隨有所問悉能善答，智慧說法平等清淨，身、語、意行悉皆無雜，住佛所住諸佛種性，以佛智慧而作佛事；住一切智，演無量法，無有根本，無有邊際，神通智慧不可思議，一切世間無能解了；智慧深入，見一切法微妙廣大無量無邊，三世法門咸善通達，一切世界悉能開曉；以出世智，於諸世間作不可說種種佛事，成不退智，入諸佛數；雖已證得不可言說離文字法，而能開示種種言辭；以普賢智集諸善行，成就一念相應妙慧，於一切法悉能覺了，如先所念一切眾生，皆依自乘而施其法；一切諸法、一切世界、一切眾生、一切三世，於法界內，如是境界其量無邊，以無礙智悉能知見。佛子！一切諸佛於一念頃，隨所應化出興於世，住清淨土，成等正覺，現神通力，開悟三世一切眾生心、意及識不失於時。佛子！眾生無邊，世界無邊，法界無邊，三世無邊，諸佛最勝亦無有邊，悉現於中成等正覺，以佛智慧方便開悟無有休息。佛子！一切諸佛以神通力，現最妙身，住無邊處，大悲方便，心無障礙，於一切時常為眾生演說妙法。是為諸佛第十大那羅延幢勇健法。「佛子！此一切諸佛大那羅延幢勇健法無量無邊、不可思議，去、來、現在一切眾生及以二乘不能解了，唯除如來神力所加。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Sons of the Buddha! Every buddha equally awakens to the dharma of every tathāgata, equally cultivates every bodhisattva practice; whether in vow or in wisdom, pure and equal, like the great ocean, entirely fulfilled; their power of practice is honored and supreme, never once retreating or faltering, abiding in every samādhi's measureless sphere, displaying every path, encouraging good and admonishing evil; their power of wisdom is foremost, expounding the dharma fearlessly, able to skillfully answer whatever is asked, their wisdom in teaching the dharma equal and pure, their bodily, verbal, and mental conduct entirely without any admixture, abiding where the buddhas abide, in the buddhas' own spiritual lineage, carrying out buddha-work through the buddha's own wisdom; abiding in all-knowing wisdom, expounding measureless dharmas, without any root and without any boundary, their spiritual penetration and wisdom inconceivable, which no one in the entire world is able to comprehend; their wisdom penetrating deeply, seeing that every dharma is subtle, vast, measureless, and boundless, thoroughly comprehending the dharma-gateways of the three times, able to bring every world to full understanding; through world-transcending wisdom, they carry out every manner of unspeakable buddha-work amid the world, accomplishing the wisdom of never turning back, entering into the ranks of the buddhas; though they have already realized the unspeakable dharma free of any written word, they are nevertheless able to disclose it through every manner of speech; through Samantabhadra's wisdom, they gather every wholesome practice, accomplishing the wondrous wisdom that accords with a single thought, able to awaken to every dharma entirely, and just as they have thought of every sentient being beforehand, they bestow the dharma on each according to their own vehicle; every dharma, every world, every sentient being, and every one of the three times, within the dharma-realm, this measure of sphere being boundless, they are entirely able to know and see it through unobstructed wisdom. Sons of the Buddha! Every buddha, within a single thought-moment, appears in the world according to whoever is fit to be transformed, abides in a pure land, attains perfect enlightenment, manifests spiritual power, and awakens every sentient being's mind, intention, and consciousness across the three times, never missing the right time. Sons of the Buddha! Sentient beings are boundless, worlds are boundless, the dharma-realm is boundless, the three times are boundless, and the buddhas' own supremacy is likewise without boundary, all of them appearing within it to attain perfect enlightenment, awakening beings through the buddha's own wisdom and skillful means without ever resting. Sons of the Buddha! Every buddha, through spiritual power, manifests the most wondrous body, abiding in a boundless place, with the skillful means of great compassion, their mind without any obstruction, constantly, at every time, expounding the wondrous dharma for the sake of sentient beings. This is the buddhas' tenth great Nārāyaṇa-banner valor and strength. Sons of the Buddha! These great Nārāyaṇa-banner valors and strengths of the buddhas are measureless, boundless, and inconceivable — no sentient being of the past, future, or present, nor the two vehicles, is able to comprehend them, except through what the tathāgata's spiritual power bestows.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

眾生無邊，世界無邊，法界無邊，三世無邊，諸佛最勝亦無有邊 — sentient beings are boundless, worlds are boundless, the dharma-realm is boundless, the three times are boundless, and the buddhas' own supremacy is likewise without boundary. This closes the list of ten Nārāyaṇa-like valors on a claim that matches every earlier feat of scale — mountains in a pore, unspeakable heads and tongues — to something far less visual: a boundlessness of beings met, one at a time, at exactly the moment each is ready, without the effort of it ever slackening.
