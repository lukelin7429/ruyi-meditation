Ten Bodhisattvas Named, a Samādhi Shakes Every World

Ten bodhisattvas are named, led by Samantabhadra himself, each having fulfilled Samantabhadra's own vows completely; Samantabhadra then enters a samādhi named Buddha-Flower Adornment, setting every world-system trembling, and Universal Wisdom opens the chapter's long list of questions — the first forty-nine of which begin here.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 684 — the named bodhisattvas, the samādhi, and the questions begin Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Chapter's Questions Begin

After ten named bodhisattvas and a world-shaking samādhi, Universal Wisdom asks Samantabhadra to expound a single, unbroken sequence of questions — not a handful, but the opening stretch of what becomes one of this sūtra's longest and most famous lists, each entry no more than a few words, together sketching the entire shape of bodhisattva practice before a single answer has been given.

Chinese Source

其名曰：普賢菩薩、普眼菩薩、普化菩薩、普慧菩薩、普見菩薩、普光菩薩、普觀菩薩、普照菩薩、普幢菩薩、普覺菩薩……。如是等十不可說百千億那由他佛剎微塵數，皆悉成就普賢行願，深心大願皆已圓滿；一切諸佛出興世處，悉能往詣請轉法輪；善能受持諸佛法眼，不斷一切諸佛種性；善知一切諸佛興世授記次第、名號、國土、成等正覺、轉於法輪；無佛世界現身成佛，能令一切雜染眾生皆悉清淨；能滅一切菩薩業障，入於無礙清淨法界。爾時，普賢菩薩摩訶薩入廣大三昧，名：佛華莊嚴；入此三昧時，十方所有一切世界六種、十八相動，出大音聲靡不皆聞；然後從其三昧而起。

Ru-Yi Translation

Their names were: Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, Universal Eye Bodhisattva, Universal Transformation Bodhisattva, Universal Wisdom Bodhisattva, Universal Vision Bodhisattva, Universal Light Bodhisattva, Universal Contemplation Bodhisattva, Universal Illumination Bodhisattva, Universal Banner Bodhisattva, Universal Awakening Bodhisattva, and so on. Such bodhisattvas as these, numbering ten unspeakably many hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of dust-motes of buddha-lands, had all entirely accomplished Samantabhadra's own practices and vows, and their profound aspirations and great vows were all entirely fulfilled; wherever any buddha appeared in the world, they were entirely able to go there and request the turning of the dharma-wheel; they were skillfully able to uphold the dharma-eye of every buddha, never letting the lineage of any buddha be cut off; they skillfully knew the sequence, names, lands, attainment of perfect awakening, and turning of the dharma-wheel of every buddha who appears in the world, and every prediction bestowed; in worlds without a buddha, they manifested their own bodies and attained buddhahood there, causing every defiled sentient being to become entirely pure; they were able to extinguish every karmic obstruction of every bodhisattva, entering the unobstructed, pure dharma-realm. At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva entered a vast samādhi named Buddha-Flower Adornment; at the moment he entered this samādhi, every world-system in every one of the ten directions shook in the six kinds and eighteen distinct ways of movement, and a great sound emerged that was heard by all without exception; only then did he rise from that samādhi.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

其名曰：普賢菩薩、普眼菩薩、普化菩薩、普慧菩薩……如是等十不可說百千億那由他佛剎微塵數 — their names were: Samantabhadra, Universal Eye, Universal Transformation, Universal Wisdom, and so on — such bodhisattvas as these, numbering ten unspeakably many hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of dust-motes of buddha-lands. Only ten names are actually given before the list dissolves into "and so on," yet all of them share the prefix "Universal" (普) — a naming pattern that turns the entire assembly, whatever their individual names might otherwise be, into variations on a single theme of comprehensive reach, fitting for a chapter about to catalogue bodhisattva practice down to its smallest components.
