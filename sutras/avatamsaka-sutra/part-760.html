Ten Kinds of Weapon, Ten Kinds of Head

Ten kinds of weapon range from the expected — wisdom, right livelihood — to the startling: greed, hatred, and delusion themselves named as a bodhisattva's own weapon, delivering beings through the very gate of affliction, and birth and death itself wielded as a tool for teaching. Ten kinds of head follow, opening with a crown so exalted that no one is able to see its top.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 760 — ten kinds of weapon, ten kinds of head Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Affliction Itself as a Weapon

The seventh weapon on this list does something none of the others attempt: rather than opposing affliction with a virtue, it names greed, hatred, delusion, and every affliction directly as the bodhisattva's own weapon, delivering sentient beings through that very gate rather than around it — and the eighth extends the same logic to birth and death itself.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種器仗。何等為十？所謂；布施是菩薩器仗，摧破一切慳吝故；持戒是菩薩器仗，棄捨一切毀犯故；平等是菩薩器仗，斷除一切分別故；智慧是菩薩器仗，消滅一切煩惱故；正命是菩薩器仗，遠離一切邪命故；善巧方便是菩薩器仗，於一切處示現故；略說貪、瞋、癡等一切煩惱是菩薩器仗，以煩惱門度眾生故；生死是菩薩器仗，不斷菩薩行教化眾生故；說如實法是菩薩器仗，能破一切執著故；一切智是菩薩器仗，不捨菩薩行門故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則能除滅一切眾生長夜所集煩惱結使。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of weapon. What are these ten? Namely: giving is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it destroys every stinginess; upholding precepts is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it casts aside every violation; equality is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it cuts off every discrimination; wisdom is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it extinguishes every affliction; right livelihood is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it stays far from every wrong livelihood; skillful means is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it manifests everywhere; in brief, greed, hatred, delusion, and every other affliction are a bodhisattva's weapon, because it delivers sentient beings through the very gate of affliction; birth and death are a bodhisattva's weapon, because it never interrupts bodhisattva practice in teaching and transforming sentient beings; teaching the dharma exactly as it truly is is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it is able to break every attachment; and all-knowing wisdom is a bodhisattva's weapon, because it never abandons the gate of bodhisattva practice. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they are able to eliminate every affliction and fetter that sentient beings have accumulated throughout the long night.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種首。何等為十？所謂：涅槃首，無能見頂故；尊敬首，一切人、天所敬禮故；廣大勝解首，三千界中最為勝故；第一善根首，三界眾生咸供養故；荷戴眾生首，成就頂上肉髻相故；不輕賤他首，於一切處常尊勝故；般若波羅蜜首，長養一切功德法故；方便智相應首，普現一切同類身故；教化一切眾生首，以一切眾生為弟子故；守護諸佛法眼首，能令三寶種不斷絕故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來無上大智慧首。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of head. What are these ten? Namely: the head of nirvāṇa, because no one is able to see its crown; the head of reverence, because every human and deva bows to it in respect; the head of vast, excellent understanding, because it is the most excellent throughout the great trichiliocosm; the head of foremost roots of goodness, because every sentient being throughout the three realms makes offerings to it; the head that carries sentient beings, because it accomplishes the fleshy protuberance upon the crown; the head that never disparages others, because it is forever excellent and honored everywhere; the head of the perfection of wisdom, because it nurtures every dharma of merit; the head in accord with the wisdom of skillful means, because it universally manifests bodies of every kind; the head that teaches and transforms every sentient being, because it takes every sentient being as its own disciple; and the head that guards every buddha's own dharma-eye, because it is able to keep the seed of the three jewels from ever being cut off. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed head of great wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

略說貪、瞋、癡等一切煩惱是菩薩器仗，以煩惱門度眾生故 — in brief, greed, hatred, delusion, and every other affliction are a bodhisattva's weapon, because it delivers sentient beings through the very gate of affliction. Where the surrounding entries oppose affliction with something contrary to it, this one repurposes affliction directly as the tool of delivery, treating the very gate through which beings suffer as the gate through which they are also reached.
