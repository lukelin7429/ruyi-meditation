Jeweled Rivers, Oceans, and Lotus Flowers

The catalogue of jeweled adornments continues without pause — rivers flowing with every pure dharma, oceans brimming with the water of the Dharma, Mount Sumeru itself rendered in jewels, necklaces adorning hundreds of thousands of bodhisattvas at once — before the list narrows into shorter, single-line entries: jeweled treasuries opening the true Dharma, jeweled banners bearing the Tathāgata's own mark, and jeweled gardens giving rise to a bodhisattva's own bliss of samādhi.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 386 — jeweled rivers, oceans, and every other kind of adornment Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Twenty-Two More Kinds of Jeweled Adornment

The list moves through landscape (rivers, oceans, Mount Sumeru itself), through ornament (silks, banners, streamers, sashes, necklaces), and through sensation (jeweled coverings that give rise to subtle, blissful touch), closing on light that manifests every other light — a fitting image for a catalogue that has, by now, exceeded sixty distinct items.

Chinese Source

復有阿僧祇寶河，流出一切清淨善法；阿僧祇寶海，法水盈滿；阿僧祇寶芬陀利華，常出妙法芬陀利聲；阿僧祇寶須彌山，智慧山王秀出清淨；阿僧祇八楞妙寶，寶線貫穿，嚴淨無比；阿僧祇淨光寶，常放無礙大智光明，普照法界；阿僧祇寶鈴鐸，更相扣擊，出妙音聲；阿僧祇清淨寶，諸菩薩寶具足充滿；阿僧祇寶繒綵，處處垂下，色相光潔；阿僧祇妙寶幢，以寶半月而為嚴飾；阿僧祇寶幡，悉能普雨無量寶幡；阿僧祇寶帶，垂布空中，莊嚴殊妙；阿僧祇寶敷具，能生種種微細樂觸；阿僧祇妙寶旋，示現菩薩一切智眼；阿僧祇寶瓔珞，一一瓔珞百千菩薩上妙莊嚴；阿僧祇寶宮殿，超過一切妙絕無比；阿僧祇寶莊嚴具，金剛摩尼以為嚴飾；阿僧祇種種妙寶莊嚴具，常現一切清淨妙色；阿僧祇清淨寶，殊形異彩，光鑒映徹；阿僧祇寶山，以為垣牆，周匝圍遶，清淨無礙；阿僧祇寶香，其香普熏一切世界；阿僧祇寶化事，一一化事周遍法界；阿僧祇寶光明，一一光明現一切光。

Ru-Yi Translation

There are further asaṃkhyeya jeweled rivers, flowing out with every pure, wholesome dharma; asaṃkhyeya jeweled oceans, brimming full with the water of the Dharma; asaṃkhyeya jeweled white lotus flowers, forever sounding the wondrous Dharma's own puṇḍarīka voice; asaṃkhyeya jeweled Mount Sumerus, the king of mountains of wisdom, rising up pure and resplendent; asaṃkhyeya wondrous eight-edged jewels, strung through with jeweled thread, adorned and pure beyond compare; asaṃkhyeya pure light jewels, forever emitting the unobstructed light of great wisdom, universally illuminating the dharma-realm; asaṃkhyeya jeweled bells and clappers, striking against one another, sounding a wondrous voice; asaṃkhyeya pure jewels, every bodhisattva's own jeweled adornment fully abundant; asaṃkhyeya jeweled silks and ribbons, hanging down everywhere, their color and form bright and clean; asaṃkhyeya wondrous jeweled banners, adorned with jeweled crescent moons; asaṃkhyeya jeweled streamers, entirely able to universally rain down immeasurable jeweled streamers; asaṃkhyeya jeweled sashes, hanging spread through the air, supremely and wondrously adorned; asaṃkhyeya jeweled coverings, able to give rise to every kind of subtle, blissful sensation; asaṃkhyeya wondrous jeweled spirals, manifesting a bodhisattva's own eye of omniscience; asaṃkhyeya jeweled necklaces, each and every necklace the supreme, wondrous adornment of hundreds of thousands of bodhisattvas; asaṃkhyeya jeweled palaces, surpassing everything, wondrous beyond compare; asaṃkhyeya jeweled adornments, adorned with vajra and maṇi jewels; asaṃkhyeya every kind of wondrous jeweled adornment, forever manifesting every pure, wondrous color; asaṃkhyeya pure jewels, of extraordinary form and unusual color, their light reflecting and shining through; asaṃkhyeya jeweled mountains, forming walls and ramparts, surrounding all around, pure and unobstructed; asaṃkhyeya jeweled incense, its fragrance universally perfuming every world; asaṃkhyeya jeweled transformative events, each and every transformative event pervading the whole dharma-realm; asaṃkhyeya jeweled lights, each and every light manifesting every other light.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

"Each and every light manifesting every other light" closes this catalogue on the same interpenetrating logic that has run through the whole dedication — no single jewel among these dozens is merely one thing among many; each one already contains and reflects the rest, the way this entire sūtra insists a single mote of dust can hold an entire world.

Eight Further Adornments

The catalogue narrows into single-line entries — jeweled lights that illuminate every dharma, jeweled treasuries that open the true Dharma, jeweled banners bearing the Tathāgata's own mark, jeweled gardens that give rise to a bodhisattva's own bliss of samādhi — each one compressed to its essential function.

Chinese Source

復有阿僧祇寶光明，清淨智光照了諸法。復有阿僧祇無礙寶光明，一一光明周遍法界。有阿僧祇寶處，一切諸寶皆悉具足。阿僧祇寶藏，開示一切正法藏寶。阿僧祇寶幢，如來幢相迥然高出。阿僧祇寶賢，大智賢像，具足清淨。阿僧祇寶園，生諸菩薩三昧快樂。阿僧祇寶音，如來妙音，普示世間。阿僧祇寶形，其一一形皆放無量妙法光明。

Ru-Yi Translation

There are further asaṃkhyeya jeweled lights, the light of pure wisdom illuminating and comprehending every dharma. There are further asaṃkhyeya unobstructed jeweled lights, each and every light pervading the whole dharma-realm. There are asaṃkhyeya jeweled places, every kind of jewel entirely and fully present within them. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled treasuries, opening up and revealing the jeweled treasury of the true Dharma entire. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled banners, the Tathāgata's own banner-mark rising up far and high. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled sages, images of great wise sages, fully pure. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled gardens, giving rise to every bodhisattva's own bliss of samādhi. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled sounds, the Tathāgata's own wondrous voice, universally shown throughout the world. Asaṃkhyeya jeweled forms, each and every form emitting the immeasurable, wondrous light of the Dharma.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Even a garden, in this list, is not simply beautiful — it is a place "giving rise to every bodhisattva's own bliss of samādhi," its purpose stated as plainly as a tool's. Nothing in this whole catalogue of jewels exists for its own sake; each item is defined by what it does for a being who encounters it.
