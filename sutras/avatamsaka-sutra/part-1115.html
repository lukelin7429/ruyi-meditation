Eight Buddhas, Eight Bodies

A catalogue with one entry per buddha, and each entry names what she was at the time. A wheel-turning king twice, an elder's daughter, an asura king, a nāga's daughter, a sea-spirit, a sage, an earth-spirit. Nothing about her stays the same except that she turns up.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1115 — the catalogue of forms Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Two Kings and an Elder's Daughter

The pattern is set in the first entry and repeated without variation — a buddha, a body, an offering, a sūtra, and the size of the sūtra's retinue.

Chinese Source

次有佛出，名：金剛那羅延幢；我為轉輪王，恭敬供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：一切佛出現，十佛剎微塵數修多羅以為眷屬。次有佛出，名：金剛無礙德；我於彼時為轉輪王，恭敬供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：普照一切眾生根，須彌山微塵數修多羅而為眷屬；我皆受持。次有佛出，名：火焰山妙莊嚴；我於彼時為長者女；其佛為我說修多羅，名：普照三世藏，閻浮提微塵數修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，如法受持。

Ru-Yi Translation

Next a buddha appeared, named Banner of the Vajra Nārāyaṇa; I was a wheel-turning king, and made offerings to him reverently. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named The Appearance of Every Buddha, with sūtras as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ten buddha-lands as its retinue. Next a buddha appeared, named Vajra Unobstructed Virtue; at that time I was a wheel-turning king, and made offerings to him reverently. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Universally Illuminating the Faculties of All Beings, with sūtras as numerous as the fine motes of dust of Mount Sumeru as its retinue; and I upheld them all. Next a buddha appeared, named Wondrous Adornment of the Mountain of Flames; at that time I was an elder's daughter. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named The Treasury That Universally Illuminates the Three Times, with sūtras as numerous as the fine motes of dust of Jambudvīpa as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them according to the dharma.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

我為轉輪王 — I was a wheel-turning king. The speaker is a night-spirit and she says it without any framing at all. In the story she has just finished telling, a wheel-turning king was the most powerful being in a world, ruled four continents, and turned out to be Maitreya. Here the same office is an item in a list of things she has been, mentioned twice in two sentences, and less remarkable than the sūtra she heard while holding it.

The third entry is the only one of the eight that names no offering at all. The elder's daughter does not give anything; the buddha simply speaks a sūtra for her and she upholds it. In a catalogue whose other seven entries all record what she brought, the blank is worth seeing. Being taught is not always something one pays for.

An Asura King, a Nāga's Daughter, a Sea-Spirit

The forms leave the human realm, and as they do the offerings change from things carried to things rained.

Chinese Source

次有佛出，名：一切法海高勝王；我為阿脩羅王，恭敬供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：分別一切法界，五百修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，如法受持。次有佛出，名：海嶽法光明；我為龍王女，雨如意摩尼寶雲而為供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：增長歡喜海，百萬億修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，如法受持。次有佛出，名：寶焰山燈；我為海神，雨寶蓮華雲恭敬供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：法界方便海光明，佛剎微塵數修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，如法受持。

Ru-Yi Translation

Next a buddha appeared, named High and Victorious King of the Ocean of All Dharmas; I was an asura king, and made offerings to him reverently. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Distinguishing the Whole Dharma-Realm, with five hundred sūtras as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them according to the dharma. Next a buddha appeared, named Light of the Dharma of Ocean and Peak; I was a nāga king's daughter, and rained down clouds of wish-fulfilling maṇi jewels as an offering. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Increasing the Ocean of Joy, with a million koṭis of sūtras as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them according to the dharma. Next a buddha appeared, named Lamp of the Mountain of Jewel-Flames; I was a sea-spirit, and rained down clouds of jewelled lotus flowers as a reverent offering. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Light of the Ocean of Skilful Means of the Dharma-Realm, with sūtras as numerous as the fine motes of dust of a buddha-land as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them according to the dharma.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The verb for offering changes exactly at the fifth entry. The first four bodies — two kings, a daughter, an asura king — either bring 恭敬供養, reverent offerings, or bring nothing. From the nāga king's daughter onward, every entry uses 雨, to rain: clouds of maṇi jewels, clouds of lotus flowers, clouds of fragrant flowers, clouds of jewel-trees. Four entries rain and four do not, and the split falls where the forms stop being kinds of person and start being kinds of power.

五百修多羅而為眷屬 — with five hundred sūtras as its retinue. The retinue sizes in this catalogue do not climb. They run: ten buddha-lands' dust, Sumeru's dust, Jambudvīpa's dust, five hundred, a million koṭis, a buddha-land's dust, sixty thousand, measureless. A plain five hundred sits between two figures beyond counting, and nothing in the text marks it as a demotion. The sūtra is not ranking these buddhas. It is simply saying how many there were each time.

我為阿脩羅王 — I was an asura king. The asuras are the jealous demigods, the beings whose defining fault in Buddhist cosmology is exactly the 憍慢 that ruined the city she came from. She has been one of them, and mentions it in the same tone as everything else.

A Sage and an Earth-Spirit

The last two entries are the only ones with company, and the last is the only one where she gives her own name.

Chinese Source

次有佛出，名：功德海光明輪；我於彼時為五通仙，現大神通，六萬諸仙前後圍遶，雨香華雲而為供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：無著法燈，六萬修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，如法受持。次有佛出，名：毘盧遮那功德藏；我於彼時，為主地神，名：出生平等義，與無量地神俱，雨一切寶樹、一切摩尼藏、一切寶瓔珞雲而為供養；其佛為我說修多羅，名：出生一切如來智藏，無量修多羅而為眷屬；我皆聽聞，受持不忘。

Ru-Yi Translation

Next a buddha appeared, named Wheel of Light of the Ocean of Merit; at that time I was a sage possessed of the five spiritual penetrations. I displayed great spiritual power, with sixty thousand sages surrounding me before and behind, and rained down clouds of fragrant flowers as an offering. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Lamp of the Unattached Dharma, with sixty thousand sūtras as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them according to the dharma. Next a buddha appeared, named Vairocana Treasury of Merit; at that time I was an earth-spirit, named Bringing Forth the Meaning of Equality, and together with measureless earth-spirits I rained down clouds of every jewel-tree, every maṇi treasury, and every jewelled necklace as an offering. That buddha spoke a sūtra for me, named Bringing Forth the Wisdom-Treasury of Every Tathāgata, with measureless sūtras as its retinue; and I heard them all and upheld them without forgetting.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

六萬諸仙前後圍遶 … 六萬修多羅而為眷屬 — sixty thousand sages surrounding me before and behind … sixty thousand sūtras as its retinue. The number occurs twice in the seventh entry and it is the same number both times. She arrives with sixty thousand followers and the buddha's teaching arrives with sixty thousand companions. The word 眷屬, retinue, is used for the sūtras exactly as it is used elsewhere for a king's household. A teaching in this sūtra travels the way a person of rank travels.

為主地神，名：出生平等義 — I was an earth-spirit, named Bringing Forth the Meaning of Equality. This is the only one of the eight entries that gives her name in the form she was wearing, and the name it gives is the one that fits an earth-spirit best. Ground does not choose what it holds up. A being who bears everything without preference is the natural inhabitant of a name about equality, and the buddha she met while wearing it was called Vairocana Treasury of Merit.

毘盧遮那功德藏 — Vairocana Treasury of Merit. The name Vairocana attaches to four different things in fascicle seventy, and this is the last of them: a buddha in Good-Wealth's verse at Part 1103, the world 毘盧遮那大威德 at Part 1104, the wheel-turning king at Part 1105, and now a buddha of a later world. No effort is made to distinguish them. In this sūtra Vairocana is less a person one could confuse with another than a name that keeps turning up wherever illumination is total.

Part 1115 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy. Part 1116 reaches the last buddha of that world, where she is a singing girl named Lovely Face who dances before him in the street, rises into the sky, and praises him in a thousand verses.
