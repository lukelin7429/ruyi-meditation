The Roll Call Completes, Ten Shared Qualities Begin

The name list closes with a run of sovereign kings, a further run of sounds, and a final run built on awakening — the text trailing off with an ellipsis after the hundred-and-forty-first name, then simply stating that five hundred bodhisattvas of this kind were gathered together. Ten qualities shared by every one of them follow immediately, opening with a claim as striking as anything in the roll call itself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 831 — the roll call completes, ten shared qualities begin Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

No Sentient Being Within the Realm of Sentient Beings

Among the ten qualities shared by this entire assembly, one stands out from the rest: extinguishing the film of delusion, because they understand that within the realm of sentient beings there is no sentient being to be found at all. Named alongside qualities like unobstructed reach and boundless light, this one turns directly on the sūtra's teaching of emptiness applied to beings themselves.

Ru-Yi Translation

Every one of these bodhisattvas had accomplished Samantabhadra's own practice and vow: their domain unobstructed, because they universally pervaded every buddha-land; their manifested bodies measureless, because they drew close to every tathāgata; their pure eyes unhindered, because they saw the spiritually transformative deeds of every buddha; their reach unlimited, because they constantly and universally attended wherever any tathāgata attained perfect awakening; their light boundless, because through the light of wisdom they universally illuminated the ocean of every true dharma; their teaching of the dharma inexhaustible, because their pure eloquence was without limit or end across every kalpa; equal to the realm of empty space, because the domain of their own wisdom was entirely pure; relying on nothing at all, because they manifested a form-body according to the mind of each sentient being; extinguishing the film of delusion, because they understood that within the realm of sentient beings there is no sentient being to be found; and equal to the wisdom of empty space, because through a great net of light they illuminated the dharma-realm.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

了眾生界無眾生故 — because they understood that within the realm of sentient beings there is no sentient being to be found. Set among nine other qualities describing unobstructed reach, boundless light, and inexhaustible teaching, this one alone turns the assembly's own attention back onto the very beings they teach — the delusion it extinguishes is the assumption that a sentient being, as such, could ever be found within the realm named for them.
