Maitreya Arrives

Good-Wealth's own verse closes with a direct prayer to Maitreya by name — and then, for the first time in this whole long scene, Maitreya Bodhisattva himself actually arrives, with a retinue beyond counting, and Good-Wealth throws his five limbs to the ground.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1278 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Maitreya Arrives

The final stanza of Good-Wealth's verse closes this Part's opening; the quotation that has run continuously since Part 1272 closes with it, and Maitreya's own arrival — the moment this entire tower scene has been building toward — brings the rest of the Part to its end.

Chinese Source

諸佛之長子，
聖德慈氏尊；
我今恭敬禮，
願垂顧念我！」

Ru-Yi Translation

Every buddha's own eldest son,
the one of noble virtue, honored Maitreya —
I now bow to you in reverence,
and pray that you look upon me with kindness!"

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子以如是等一切菩薩無量稱揚讚歎法，而讚毘盧遮那莊嚴藏大樓閣中諸菩薩已，曲躬合掌，恭敬頂禮，一心願見彌勒菩薩親近供養；乃見彌勒菩薩摩訶薩從別處來，無量天、龍、夜叉、乾闥婆、阿脩羅、迦樓羅、緊那羅、摩睺羅伽王，釋、梵、護世，及本生處無量眷屬、婆羅門眾，及餘無數百千眾生，前後圍遶而共來向莊嚴藏大樓觀所。善財見已，歡喜踊躍，五體投地。時，彌勒菩薩觀察善財，指示大眾，歎其功德，而說頌曰：「

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth, having with all such measureless dharmas of praise and admiration extolled every bodhisattva within Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury tower, bowed his body and joined his palms, bowing his head in reverence, wishing with his whole mind to behold Maitreya Bodhisattva and draw near to make offerings to him. And then he beheld Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva coming from another place, with measureless kings of devas, nāgas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, and mahoragas, with Śakra, Brahmā, and the guardians of the world, and with measureless retainers from his own place of birth, an assembly of brāhmins, and countless hundreds of thousands of other sentient beings besides, all surrounding him before and behind as they came together toward the place of the Great Adorned Treasury tower-pavilion. Seeing this, Good-Wealth leapt with joy and threw his five limbs to the ground. Then Maitreya Bodhisattva, observing Good-Wealth, pointed him out to the great assembly, praised his merit, and spoke a verse, saying:
