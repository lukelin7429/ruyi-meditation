A Body Abiding Throughout Every World

Fascicle thirty-two closes mid-vow, still within the tenth dedication's prose section, as the bodhisattva mahāsattva wishes his own body to abide throughout every world, cultivating a bodhisattva's own practice to the very end of future kalpas — entering every sentient being, every buddha-land, every dharma, and finally the boundless dharma-realm itself, abiding securely within it.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 384 — fascicle 32 complete, the tenth dedication continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Entering Every Sentient Being, Every Buddha-Land, the Whole Dharma-Realm

The vow moves from a body that abides throughout every world to a mind that never once loosens its mindfulness of every good spiritual friend and every buddha, before closing in a cascade of "entering" — every sentient being, every buddha-land, every dharma, every one of the three times, and at last the boundless dharma-realm itself, where the bodhisattva abides securely to continue his own practice.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩復以法施所生善根如是迴向：『願身隨住一切世界修菩薩行，眾生見者皆悉不虛，發菩提心永無退轉，順真實義不可傾動；於一切世界，盡未來劫，住菩薩道而無疲厭；大悲均普，量同法界；知眾生根，應時說法，常不休息；於善知識，心常正念，乃至不捨一剎那頃；一切諸佛常現在前，心常正念未曾暫懈，修諸善根無有虛偽；置諸眾生於一切智，令不退轉；具足一切佛法光明，持大法雲，受大法雨，修菩薩行；入一切眾生，入一切佛剎，入一切諸法，入一切三世，入一切眾生業報智，入一切菩薩善巧方便智，入一切菩薩出生智，入一切菩薩清淨境界智，入一切佛自在神通，入一切無邊法界，於此安住，修菩薩行。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! This bodhisattva mahāsattva further dedicates the wholesome roots born from the giving of Dharma in this way: 'May my body abide throughout every world, cultivating a bodhisattva's own practice; whatever sentient being sees it, none in vain; giving rise to the mind of enlightenment, forever without regression; according with true meaning, unable to be shaken; throughout every world, to the very end of future kalpas, abiding in the path of a bodhisattva without weariness; great compassion, equal and universal, its measure the same as the dharma-realm; knowing the faculties of sentient beings, expounding the Dharma at the fitting time, forever without rest; toward every good spiritual friend, the mind forever rightly mindful, never abandoning it for even the space of a single instant; every buddha forever manifest before me, my mind forever rightly mindful, never once slackening; cultivating every wholesome root, without any falseness; placing every sentient being in omniscience, causing them never to regress; fully possessing the light of every buddha's own Dharma, upholding the great cloud of the Dharma, receiving the great rain of the Dharma, cultivating a bodhisattva's own practice; entering every sentient being, entering every buddha-land, entering every dharma, entering every one of the three times, entering the wisdom of every sentient being's own karmic retribution, entering every bodhisattva's own skillful means and wisdom, entering every bodhisattva's own wisdom of giving rise, entering every bodhisattva's own wisdom of pure domain, entering every buddha's own sovereign spiritual power, entering the boundless dharma-realm, abiding securely in this, cultivating a bodhisattva's own practice.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

"Whatever sentient being sees it, none in vain" opens this vow with a striking condition — the bodhisattva's own body is wished to be efficacious simply by being seen, before anything is taught or given. Everything that follows, the mindfulness, the entering of every world and every dharma, reads as an elaboration of what a body that powerful must actually be like.
