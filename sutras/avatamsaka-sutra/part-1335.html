Equal to Samantabhadra, Equal to Every Buddha

Good-Wealth sees his own body within Samantabhadra's, teaching sentient beings throughout the ten directions; and he finds that the wholesome roots and light of wisdom he gained by drawing near to good spiritual friends as numerous as the dust of a buddha-land are not equal to one part in a hundred, a thousand, or a hundred thousand koṭis of what Samantabhadra has gained, beyond any number or comparison. What Good-Wealth entered from his first arousing of the mind until now is exceeded, in a single thought within a single pore of Samantabhadra, by ineffably ineffable multiples; and walking one step within that pore he passes ineffably ineffable worlds, yet were he to walk so for kalpas without end he could never know the full extent of even one pore's ocean of lands, oceans of buddhas, or oceans of bodhisattvas. Sinking from no land and appearing in no other, in thought after thought he pervades boundless oceans of lands, teaching beings toward bodhi, until at last he attains, in sequence, the whole ocean of Samantabhadra's practices and vows, and becomes equal to Samantabhadra, equal to every buddha, one body filling every world, equal in land, in practice, in enlightenment, in spiritual power, in the wheel of dharma, in eloquence, in speech, in voice, in the powers and fearlessnesses, in the abode of the buddhas, in great compassion, and in the inconceivable sovereign freedom of liberation.

Equal to Samantabhadra, Equal to Every Buddha

Good-Wealth's own wholesome roots measured against Samantabhadra's, a single pore too vast to be walked through, and the equality he finally attains.

Chinese Source

時，善財童子又見自身在普賢身內，十方一切諸世界中教化眾生。又，善財童子親近佛剎微塵數諸善知識所得善根、智慧光明，比見普賢菩薩所得善根，百分不及一，千分不及一，百千分不及一，百千億分乃至算數譬諭亦不能及是。善財童子從初發心，乃至得見普賢菩薩，於其中間所入一切諸佛剎海，今於普賢一毛孔中一念所入諸佛剎海，過前不可說不可說佛剎微塵數倍；如一毛孔，一切毛孔悉亦如是。善財童子於普賢菩薩毛孔剎中，行一步，過不可說不可說佛剎微塵數世界；如是而行，盡未來劫，猶不能知一毛孔中剎海次第、剎海藏、剎海差別、剎海普入、剎海成、剎海壞、剎海莊嚴所有邊際；亦不能知佛海次第、佛海藏、佛海差別、佛海普入、佛海生、佛海滅所有邊際；亦不能知菩薩眾海次第、菩薩眾海藏、菩薩眾海差別、菩薩眾海普入、菩薩眾海集、菩薩眾海散所有邊際；亦不能知入眾生界、知眾生根、教化調伏諸眾生智、菩薩所住甚深自在、菩薩所入諸地諸道，如是等海所有邊際。善財童子於普賢菩薩毛孔剎中，或於一剎經於一劫如是而行，乃至或有經不可說不可說佛剎微塵數劫如是而行，亦不於此剎沒、於彼剎現，念念周遍無邊剎海，教化眾生，令向阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。當是之時，善財童子則次第得普賢菩薩諸行願海，與普賢等，與諸佛等，一身充滿一切世界，剎等、行等、正覺等、神通等、法輪等、辯才等、言辭等、音聲等、力無畏等、佛所住等、大慈悲等、不可思議解脫自在悉皆同等。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth the youth saw, moreover, his own body within Samantabhadra's body, teaching and transforming sentient beings throughout every world of the ten directions. Good-Wealth the youth, moreover, comparing the wholesome roots and light of wisdom he had gained by drawing near to good spiritual friends as numerous as the fine motes of dust of a buddha-land against the wholesome roots that Samantabhadra Bodhisattva had gained, found them not equal to one part in a hundred, not equal to one part in a thousand, not equal to one part in a hundred thousand, and not equal to any part expressible by number or comparison even down to one part in a hundred thousand koṭis. From his first arousing of the mind up until now, when he had come to see Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, every ocean of buddha-lands that Good-Wealth the youth had entered in the time between was exceeded, by ineffably ineffable multiples as numerous as the fine motes of dust of a buddha-land, by the ocean of buddha-lands entered in a single thought within a single pore of Samantabhadra; and as with that one pore, so it was with every pore. Good-Wealth the youth, within the lands of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's pores, in walking a single step, passed through worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands; and walking on in this way to the very end of future kalpas, he still could not know the full extent of the sequence of the oceans of lands within a single pore, their treasury, their distinctions, their universal entry, their formation, their dissolution, and their adornment; nor could he know the full extent of the sequence of the oceans of buddhas, their treasury, their distinctions, their universal entry, their arising, and their passing; nor could he know the full extent of the sequence of the oceans of the bodhisattva assemblies, their treasury, their distinctions, their universal entry, their gathering, and their dispersing; nor could he know the full extent of such oceans as entering the realm of sentient beings, knowing the faculties of sentient beings, the wisdom that teaches, transforms, and tames sentient beings, the bodhisattvas' profound sovereign freedom in which they abide, and the grounds and paths that the bodhisattvas enter. Good-Wealth the youth, within the lands of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's pores, walked in this way through one kalpa within a single land, and even walked in this way through kalpas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of ineffably ineffable buddha-lands, yet never sank from this land to appear in that one, but in thought after thought pervaded boundless oceans of lands, teaching sentient beings and turning them toward anuttara-samyak-sambodhi. At that time, Good-Wealth the youth thereby attained, in sequence, the whole ocean of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's practices and vows, and became equal to Samantabhadra, equal to every buddha, one body filling every world, equal in lands, equal in practices, equal in perfect enlightenment, equal in spiritual powers, equal in the wheel of dharma, equal in eloquence, equal in speech, equal in voice, equal in the powers and fearlessnesses, equal in the abode of the buddhas, equal in great compassion, and altogether equal in inconceivable sovereign freedom of liberation.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

百分不及一，千分不及一，百千分不及一 — the comparison runs through three named fractions, a hundredth, a thousandth, and a hundred-thousandth, before the source gives up naming fractions at all and simply says 乃至算數譬諭亦不能及是, not reachable even by number or comparison; the translation keeps all three named fractions and then the same admission of failure, rather than trimming the list to one representative term.

剎海次第、剎海藏、剎海差別、剎海普入、剎海成、剎海壞、剎海莊嚴 — the single pore Good-Wealth cannot exhaust is measured by three parallel sets, applied in turn to oceans of lands, oceans of buddhas, and oceans of bodhisattva assemblies, each opening with the same four terms, sequence, treasury, distinction, and universal entry, and then closing with a pair specific to each ocean, formation and dissolution for lands, arising and passing for buddhas, gathering and dispersing for bodhisattvas; the set for lands alone adds a seventh term, adornment, at its end. The translation keeps all three sets in full rather than translating the first and referring back to it for the other two, since the source itself writes out each set separately.
