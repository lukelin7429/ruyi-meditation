Welcome, Stanzas 39–58

Maitreya's verse turns from describing Good-Wealth to foretelling him — stanza after stanza opening on 當 ("you shall"), naming every destiny, net, ocean, and wheel he is yet to master.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1281 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 39–58

Two stanzas on what Good-Wealth still seeks open this Part; eighteen more, nearly all built on the same 當 ("you shall") prophecy, follow — nets, oceans, wheels, seeds, and lights, each stanza naming one more thing still to come.

Chinese Source

欲生一切德，
欲問一切法，
欲斷一切疑，
專求善知識。

欲破諸惑魔，
欲除諸見垢，
欲解眾生縛，
專求善知識。

當滅諸惡道，
當示人天路，
令修功德行，
疾入涅槃城。

當度諸見難，
當截諸見網，
當枯愛欲水，
當示三有道。

當為世依怙，
當作世光明，
當成三界師，
示其解脫處。

亦當令世間，
普離諸想著，
普覺煩惱睡，
普出愛欲泥。

當了種種法，
當淨種種剎；
一切咸究竟，
其心大歡喜。

汝行極調柔，
汝心甚清淨，
所欲修功德，
一切當圓滿。

不久見諸佛，
了達一切法，
嚴淨眾剎海，
成就大菩提。

當滿諸行海，
當知諸法海，
當度眾生海，
如是修諸行。

當到功德岸，
當生諸善品，
當與佛子等，
如是心決定。

當斷一切惑，
當淨一切業，
當伏一切魔，
滿足如是願。

當生妙智道，
當開正法道，
不久當捨離，
惑業諸苦道。

一切眾生輪，
沈迷諸有輪；
汝當轉法輪，
令其斷苦輪。

汝當持佛種，
汝當淨法種，
汝能集僧種，
三世悉周遍。

當斷眾愛網，
當裂眾見網，
當救眾苦網，
當成此願網。

當度眾生界，
當淨國土界，
當集智慧界，
當成此心界。

當令眾生喜，
當令菩薩喜，
當令諸佛喜，
當成此歡喜。

當見一切趣，
當見一切剎，
當見一切法，
當成此佛見。

當放破暗光，
當放息熱光，
當放滅惡光，
滌除三有苦。

Ru-Yi Translation

Wishing to give rise to every virtue,
wishing to ask about every dharma,
wishing to cut off every doubt,
single-mindedly he seeks a good spiritual friend.

Wishing to defeat every māra of delusion,
wishing to remove every stain of wrong view,
wishing to loosen the bonds of sentient beings,
single-mindedly he seeks a good spiritual friend.

You shall extinguish every evil destiny,
you shall show the path of humans and devas,
causing them to cultivate meritorious practice,
and swiftly enter the city of nirvāṇa.

You shall cross beyond every difficulty of wrong view,
you shall cut through every net of wrong view,
you shall dry up the waters of craving and desire,
and show the path through the three states of existence.

You shall become a refuge for the world,
you shall become a light for the world,
you shall become a teacher of the three realms,
showing them a place of liberation.

You shall also cause the world
to universally leave behind every attached notion,
to universally awaken from the sleep of affliction,
and to universally emerge from the mire of craving and desire.

You shall understand every kind of dharma,
you shall purify every kind of land;
with everything brought to its ultimate completion,
your mind shall know great joy.

Your practice is utterly gentle,
your mind is deeply pure;
whatever merit you wish to cultivate,
every bit of it shall come to fulfillment.

Before long you shall behold every buddha,
fully understand every dharma,
adorn and purify the ocean of every land,
and accomplish great bodhi.

You shall fulfill the ocean of every practice,
you shall know the ocean of every dharma,
you shall ferry across the ocean of every sentient being —
cultivating every practice in just this way.

You shall reach the shore of merit,
you shall give rise to every wholesome quality,
you shall become equal to every buddha-child —
your mind resolved in just this way.

You shall cut off every delusion,
you shall purify every karma,
you shall subdue every māra,
fulfilling every vow such as this.

You shall give rise to the path of wondrous wisdom,
you shall open the path of the true dharma,
and before long you shall abandon
the path of delusion, karma, and every suffering.

Every sentient being turns upon a wheel,
sunk in delusion upon the wheel of every state of existence;
you shall turn the wheel of dharma,
causing them to sever the wheel of suffering.

You shall uphold the buddha-seed,
you shall purify the dharma-seed,
you are able to gather the saṃgha-seed,
pervading fully throughout the three times.

You shall cut through the net of every craving,
you shall tear apart the net of every wrong view,
you shall rescue beings from the net of every suffering,
and bring to completion this very net of vows.

You shall ferry across the realm of every sentient being,
you shall purify the realm of every land,
you shall gather the realm of wisdom,
and bring to completion this very realm of mind.

You shall bring joy to every sentient being,
you shall bring joy to every bodhisattva,
you shall bring joy to every buddha,
and bring to completion this very joy.

You shall see every destiny,
you shall see every land,
you shall see every dharma,
and bring to completion this very vision of a buddha.

You shall emit a light that breaks apart darkness,
you shall emit a light that brings rest from heat,
you shall emit a light that dissolves away evil,
cleansing away the suffering of the three states of existence.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

當滅諸惡道，當示人天路 — from the third stanza on, the verse shifts almost entirely onto 當 ("you shall"), turning from praise of what Good-Wealth already is into prophecy of what he is yet to accomplish. Eighteen of this Part's twenty stanzas carry at least one 當 clause, counted directly against the source text.
