The Twenty Tiers, and This Very Sahā World

At the very center of the Flower-Treasury World-System stands one particular world-seed, and Samantabhadra now describes it tier by tier, stacked one above the other like floors of a single tower — twenty of them in all, each with its own name, its own boundary, its own shape, its own encircling worlds, and its own buddha. The thirteenth tier is named Sahā (娑婆): this very world, whose buddha is said to be none other than Vairocana Tathāgata himself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 21 — completes fascicle 8 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Central Fragrant Water Ocean, and Its World-Seed

Chinese Source

「諸佛子！此不可說佛剎微塵數香水海，在華藏莊嚴世界海中，如天帝網分布而住。諸佛子！此最中央香水海，名：無邊妙華光，以現一切菩薩形摩尼王幢為底；出大蓮華，名：一切香摩尼王莊嚴；有世界種而住其上，名：普照十方熾然寶光明，以一切莊嚴具為體，有不可說佛剎微塵數世界於中布列。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Buddha's children! These fragrant water oceans, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ineffable buddha-lands, dwell arrayed throughout the Flower-Treasury Adorned World-Ocean just like Indra's own net. Buddha's children! This very central fragrant water ocean is named Boundless, Wondrous Flower-Light, with the sovereign maṇi banner manifesting the form of every bodhisattva as its floor. From it emerges a great lotus flower, named the Adornment of the Sovereign Maṇi of Every Fragrance; and a world-seed abides upon it, named Universally Illumining the Ten Directions with Blazing, Jeweled Light, taking every adorning implement as its essence, with worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ineffable buddha-lands arrayed within it."

Tiers One Through Five

Chinese Source

其最下方有世界，名：最勝光遍照，以一切金剛莊嚴光耀輪為際，依眾寶摩尼華而住；其狀猶如摩尼寶形，一切寶華莊嚴雲彌覆其上，佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，種種安住，種種莊嚴，佛號：淨眼離垢燈。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：種種香蓮華妙莊嚴，以一切莊嚴具為際，依寶蓮華網而住；其狀猶如師子之座，一切寶色珠帳雲彌覆其上，二佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：師子光勝照。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：一切寶莊嚴普照光，以香風輪為際，依種種寶華瓔珞住；其形八隅，妙光摩尼日輪雲而覆其上，三佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：淨光智勝幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：種種光明華莊嚴，以一切寶王為際，依眾色金剛尸羅幢海住；其狀猶如摩尼蓮華，以金剛摩尼寶光雲而覆其上，四佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：金剛光明無量精進力善出現。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：普放妙華光，以一切寶鈴莊嚴網為際，依一切樹林莊嚴寶輪網海住；其形普方而多有隅角，梵音摩尼王雲以覆其上，五佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：香光喜力海。

Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named the Adornment of Every Kind of Light and Flower, with every sovereign jewel as its boundary, abiding resting upon an ocean of vajra precept-banners of every color; its shape is just like a maṇi lotus flower, and a cloud of vajra maṇi's jeweled light covers over it. Tiers Six Through Ten

Chinese Source

Tiers Eleven Through Thirteen — This Very World

Chinese Source

此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：恒出現帝青寶光明，以極堅牢不可壞金剛莊嚴為際，依種種殊異華海住；其狀猶如半月之形，諸天寶帳雲而覆其上，十一佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：無量功德法。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：光明照耀，以普光莊嚴為際，依華旋香水海住；狀如華旋，種種衣雲而覆其上，十二佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：超釋梵。

Ru-Yi Translation

Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Forever Manifesting the Light of Indranīla Jewel, with the utterly firm, indestructible adornment of vajra as its boundary, abiding resting upon an ocean of every kind of extraordinary flower; its shape is just like a half-moon, and a cloud of canopies of every heaven's jewels covers over it. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in eleven buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named the Dharma of Immeasurable Merit. Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, there is a world named Shining, Radiant Light, with universal light's adornment as its boundary, abiding resting upon a fragrant water ocean whirling with flowers; its shape is like a whirl of flowers, and a cloud of every kind of garment covers over it. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in twelve buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its buddha is named Surpassing Śakra and Brahmā.

Chinese Source

此上過佛剎微塵數世界，至此世界，名：娑婆，以金剛莊嚴為際，依種種色風輪所持蓮華網住；狀如虛空，以普圓滿天宮殿莊嚴虛空雲而覆其上，十三佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，其佛即是毘盧遮那如來世尊。

Ru-Yi Translation

Above this, past worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, one reaches this very world, named Sahā, with vajra's adornment as its boundary, abiding resting upon a net of lotus flowers upheld by wind-wheels of every color; its shape is like empty space itself, and a cloud of universally full and complete heavenly palaces' adornment covers over it. Worlds as numerous as the fine motes of dust in thirteen buddha-lands encircle it all around. Its very buddha is none other than Vairocana Tathāgata, the World-Honored One.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

This is the sentence the whole chapter has been building toward since fascicle eight opened: the thirteenth tier of this central world-seed, reached only after passing through twelve tiers each already vaster than the last, is named Sahā — the very name used elsewhere across the Buddhist canon for the world in which Śākyamuni Buddha taught. And its buddha, the text says, "is none other than Vairocana Tathāgata, the World-Honored One." Chengguan's commentary and the broader Huayan tradition read this identification as settling, cosmologically, what the sūtra has been arguing philosophically since fascicle six: that Śākyamuni's historical teaching career and Vairocana's own body pervading the whole Dharma-realm are not two buddhas but one — the ordinary, suffering-marked name "Sahā" (from a root meaning "to endure") and the boundless Flower-Treasury World-System are the same place, seen at two different registers of description.

Tiers Fourteen Through Twenty

Chinese Source

此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寂靜離塵光，以一切寶莊嚴為際，依種種寶衣海住；其狀猶如執金剛形，無邊色金剛雲而覆其上，十四佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：遍法界勝音。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：眾妙光明燈，以一切莊嚴帳為際，依淨華網海住；其狀猶如卐字之形，摩尼樹香水海雲而覆其上，十五佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：不可摧伏力普照幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：清淨光遍照，以無盡寶雲摩尼王為際，依種種香焰蓮華海住；其狀猶如龜甲之形，圓光摩尼輪栴檀雲而覆其上，十六佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：清淨日功德眼。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：寶莊嚴藏，以一切眾生形摩尼王為際，依光明藏摩尼王海住；其形八隅，以一切輪圍山寶莊嚴華樹網彌覆其上，十七佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：無礙智光明遍照十方。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：離塵，以一切殊妙相莊嚴為際，依眾妙華師子座海住；狀如珠瓔，以一切寶香摩尼王圓光雲而覆其上，十八佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：無量方便最勝幢。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：清淨光普照，以出無盡寶雲摩尼王為際，依無量色香焰須彌山海住；其狀猶如寶華旋布，以無邊色光明摩尼王帝青雲而覆其上，十九佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，佛號：普照法界虛空光。此上過佛剎微塵數世界，有世界，名：妙寶焰，以普光明日月寶為際，依一切諸天形摩尼王海住；其狀猶如寶莊嚴具，以一切寶衣幢雲及摩尼燈藏網而覆其上，二十佛剎微塵數世界周匝圍遶，純一清淨，佛號：福德相光明。

Closing the World-Seed

Chinese Source

「諸佛子！此遍照十方熾然寶光明世界種，有如是等不可說佛剎微塵數廣大世界，各各所依住，各各形狀，各各體性，各各方面，各各趣入，各各莊嚴，各各分齊，各各行列，各各無差別，各各力加持，周匝圍遶。所謂：十佛剎微塵數迴轉形世界、十佛剎微塵數江河形世界、十佛剎微塵數漩流形世界、十佛剎微塵數輪輞形世界、十佛剎微塵數壇墠形世界、十佛剎微塵數樹林形世界、十佛剎微塵數樓觀形世界、十佛剎微塵數尸羅幢形世界、十佛剎微塵數普方形世界、十佛剎微塵數胎藏形世界、十佛剎微塵數蓮華形世界、十佛剎微塵數佉勒迦形世界、十佛剎微塵數種種眾生形世界、十佛剎微塵數佛相形世界、十佛剎微塵數圓光形世界、十佛剎微塵數雲形世界、十佛剎微塵數網形世界、十佛剎微塵數門闥形世界……。如是等，有不可說佛剎微塵數。此一一世界，各有十佛剎微塵數廣大世界周匝圍遶。此諸世界，一一復有如上所說微塵數世界而為眷屬。如是所說一切世界，皆在此無邊妙華光香水海及圍遶此海香水河中。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Buddha's children! This world-seed, Universally Illumining the Ten Directions with Blazing, Jeweled Light, has vast worlds such as these, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ineffable buddha-lands, each resting upon its own foundation, each with its own shape, each with its own essence, each with its own direction, each with its own manner of approach, each with its own adornment, each with its own boundary, each with its own arrangement, each with its own non-difference, each sustained by its own power, encircling one another all around. That is to say: worlds in a whirling form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a river, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a whirlpool form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a wheel's rim, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of an altar-terrace, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a forest of trees, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a tower or belvedere, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a precept-banner, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a universally square form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a womb-treasury form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a lotus-flower form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a khalka-vessel form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in every kind of living being's form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a buddha's marks, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a round-light form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a cloud form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in a net form, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands; worlds in the form of a gate or portal, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands... Such as these, there are as many as the fine motes of dust in ineffable buddha-lands. Each and every one of these worlds has vast worlds, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in ten buddha-lands, encircling it all around. Each and every one of these worlds, moreover, further has worlds as numerous as the motes of dust just described above, as its own retinue. Every world stated thus is entirely within this Boundless, Wondrous Flower-Light fragrant water ocean, and within the fragrant water rivers that encircle this very ocean."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Notice what the closing paragraph does to everything just narrated: after twenty tiers described in careful, individual, ascending detail — each with its own name, boundary, buddha — this entire stack turns out to be only one world-seed among ineffable numbers of others, itself resting in only one of the ineffable fragrant water oceans described in Part 20, which is itself only one part of one lotus flower within one world-ocean upheld by wind-wheels beneath the whole edifice. The text does not let the reader settle into treating this one vertical column, however dramatic its placement of Sahā near the top, as any kind of privileged center. It is simply the one Samantabhadra happened to describe, because it happens to be the one this audience is standing in.

Part 21 Complete — Fascicle Eight Complete

This closes Part 21 and fascicle eight: the sūtra's fifth chapter, "The Flower-Treasury World-System" (華藏世界品), continuing with its wind-wheels, encircling mountain, ground, fragrant water oceans, rivers, and the land between them, before arriving at its central passage — the twenty-tiered world-seed at the very heart of the whole system, whose thirteenth tier, named Sahā, is this very world, and whose buddha is said to be none other than Vairocana Tathāgata. Fascicle nine continues this same chapter with the world-seeds surrounding this central one in each of the ten directions.
