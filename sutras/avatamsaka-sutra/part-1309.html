Within One Place, Every Place

Fascicle seventy-nine opens with Good-Wealth asking Maitreya to open the door of the tower. Maitreya snaps his fingers, the door opens, and Good-Wealth steps inside to find a tower as vast as space, adorned with asaṃkhyeya palaces, gates, balustrades, necklaces, bells, lamps, mirrors, seats, flowers, and images, and within it measureless further towers, each adorned in the same way and none obstructing another. Within one place he sees every place.

Within One Place, Every Place

The door opens at a snap of the fingers, and the long description of the tower's adornments begins — a single sentence in which the word asaṃkhyeya returns before almost every item.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子恭敬右遶彌勒菩薩摩訶薩已，而白之言：「唯願大聖開樓閣門，令我得入！」時，彌勒菩薩前詣樓閣，彈指出聲，其門即開，命善財入。善財心喜，入已還閉。見其樓閣廣博無量同於虛空，阿僧祇寶以為其地；阿僧祇宮殿、阿僧祇門闥、阿僧祇窓牖、阿僧祇階陛、阿僧祇欄楯、阿僧祇道路，皆七寶成；阿僧祇幡、阿僧祇幢、阿僧祇蓋，周迴間列；阿僧祇眾寶瓔珞、阿僧祇真珠瓔珞、阿僧祇赤真珠瓔珞、阿僧祇師子珠瓔珞，處處垂下；阿僧祇半月、阿僧祇繒帶、阿僧祇寶網，以為嚴飾；阿僧祇寶鐸風動成音，散阿僧祇天諸雜華，懸阿僧祇天寶鬘帶，嚴阿僧祇眾寶香爐，雨阿僧祇細末金屑，懸阿僧祇寶鏡，然阿僧祇寶燈，布阿僧祇寶衣，列阿僧祇寶帳，設阿僧祇寶座，阿僧祇寶繒以敷座上；阿僧祇閻浮檀金童女像、阿僧祇雜寶諸形像、阿僧祇妙寶菩薩像，處處充遍；阿僧祇眾鳥出和雅音；阿僧祇寶優鉢羅華、阿僧祇寶波頭摩華、阿僧祇寶拘物頭華、阿僧祇寶芬陀利華，以為莊嚴；阿僧祇寶樹次第行列，阿僧祇摩尼寶放大光明。如是等無量阿僧祇諸莊嚴具，以為莊嚴。又見其中，有無量百千諸妙樓閣，一一嚴飾悉如上說；廣博嚴麗皆同虛空，不相障礙亦無雜亂。善財童子於一處中見一切處，一切諸處悉如是見。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth the youth, having reverently circled Maitreya Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva to the right, addressed him, saying, "I only pray that the Great Sage will open the door of the tower and let me enter!" Then Maitreya Bodhisattva went before the tower and snapped his fingers, and at that sound the door opened at once, and he bade Good-Wealth enter. Good-Wealth, glad at heart, entered, and once he was within, the door closed again. He saw that the tower was vast and measureless, the same as space; asaṃkhyeya jewels formed its ground; asaṃkhyeya palaces, asaṃkhyeya gates, asaṃkhyeya windows, asaṃkhyeya stairways, asaṃkhyeya balustrades, and asaṃkhyeya roads were all made of the seven jewels; asaṃkhyeya pennants, asaṃkhyeya banners, and asaṃkhyeya canopies were arrayed at intervals all around; asaṃkhyeya necklaces of every jewel, asaṃkhyeya necklaces of pearls, asaṃkhyeya necklaces of red pearls, and asaṃkhyeya necklaces of lion pearls hung down everywhere; asaṃkhyeya half-moons, asaṃkhyeya silk ribbons, and asaṃkhyeya jeweled nets served as its ornament; asaṃkhyeya jeweled bells, stirred by the wind, gave forth sound; asaṃkhyeya heavenly flowers of every kind were scattered; asaṃkhyeya heavenly jeweled garland-sashes were hung; asaṃkhyeya censers of every jewel were set as adornment; asaṃkhyeya fine gold dust rained down; asaṃkhyeya jeweled mirrors were hung; asaṃkhyeya jeweled lamps were lit; asaṃkhyeya jeweled garments were spread; asaṃkhyeya jeweled curtains were ranged; asaṃkhyeya jeweled seats were set out, with asaṃkhyeya jeweled silks spread upon the seats; asaṃkhyeya images of maidens in Jambu-river gold, asaṃkhyeya images of every kind in mixed jewels, and asaṃkhyeya images of bodhisattvas in wondrous jewels filled every place; asaṃkhyeya flocks of birds gave forth sweet and harmonious sounds; asaṃkhyeya jeweled utpala flowers, asaṃkhyeya jeweled padma flowers, asaṃkhyeya jeweled kumuda flowers, and asaṃkhyeya jeweled puṇḍarīka flowers served as adornment; asaṃkhyeya jeweled trees stood in rows in order; and asaṃkhyeya maṇi jewels gave off great light. Measureless asaṃkhyeya adornments such as these served as its adornment. He saw, moreover, that within it there were measureless hundreds of thousands of wondrous towers, each one adorned exactly as described above, vast and beautifully adorned, all the same as space, neither obstructing one another nor in any confusion. Good-Wealth the youth, within one place, saw every place, and in every place he saw the same.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

阿僧祇 — the word occurs thirty-nine times in this passage alone, once before nearly every item in the description of the tower, and the translation keeps it as asaṃkhyeya every time rather than thinning it to countless; the insistence of the repetition is part of what the passage is doing, since the tower that is 同於虛空, the same as space, is being measured item by item in a number that cannot be counted. The four lotus names 優鉢羅, 波頭摩, 拘物頭, and 芬陀利 are the usual transliterations utpala, padma, kumuda, and puṇḍarīka, and 師子珠, the lion pearl among the four kinds of necklace, is rendered as the site has rendered it before.

於一處中見一切處 — the passage closes on the line that governs everything that follows in this fascicle: within one place Good-Wealth sees every place, and in every place he sees the same. The measureless towers within the tower are each adorned 悉如上說, exactly as already described, and are 不相障礙亦無雜亂, neither obstructing one another nor confused, so that the whole description just given is understood to be repeated, unhindered, in every one of them.
