A Sound with Nothing to It at All

Terrifying sounds, pleasing sounds, sounds vast enough to fill asaṃkhyeya worlds and continue for immeasurable kalpas without ceasing — none of them can disturb the bodhisattva's own mind, and a direct contemplation reveals why: sound itself, examined closely, has no maker, no origin, and no fixed nature at all.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 138 — the practice free of delusion concludes Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

No Sound Can Disturb This Mind

Chinese Source

復次，此菩薩摩訶薩，種種音聲不能惑亂。所謂：高大聲、麁濁聲、極令人恐怖聲、悅意聲、不悅意聲、諠亂耳識聲、沮壞六根聲。此菩薩聞如是等無量無數好惡音聲，假使充滿阿僧祇世界，未曾一念心有散亂。所謂：正念不亂、境界不亂、三昧不亂、入甚深法不亂、行菩提行不亂、發菩提心不亂、憶念諸佛不亂、觀真實法不亂、化眾生智不亂、淨眾生智不亂、決了甚深義不亂。不作惡業故，無惡業障；不起煩惱故，無煩惱障；不輕慢法故，無有法障；不誹謗正法故，無有報障。佛子！如上所說如是等聲，一一充滿阿僧祇世界，於無量無數劫未曾斷絕，悉能壞亂眾生身心一切諸根，而不能壞此菩薩心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, this bodhisattva-mahāsattva cannot be confused or disturbed by any kind of sound at all. Namely: loud and towering sounds, coarse and turbid sounds, sounds that terrify people utterly, pleasing sounds, displeasing sounds, sounds that disturb and disorder the ear-consciousness, and sounds that undermine and destroy the six faculties. This bodhisattva, hearing immeasurable, countless good and bad sounds such as these, even were they to fill asaṃkhyeya worlds entirely, has never once, for even a single thought, had their mind become distracted. Namely: right mindfulness is not disturbed, the object of concentration is not disturbed, samādhi is not disturbed, entering the most profound Dharma is not disturbed, practicing the conduct of bodhi is not disturbed, giving rise to the mind of bodhi is not disturbed, holding every buddha in mind is not disturbed, contemplating the true Dharma is not disturbed, the wisdom that transforms living beings is not disturbed, the wisdom that purifies living beings is not disturbed, and resolutely understanding the most profound meaning is not disturbed. Because they do no evil karma, they have no obstruction of evil karma; because they give rise to no affliction, they have no obstruction of affliction; because they never treat the Dharma lightly, they have no obstruction of dharma; because they never slander the true Dharma, they have no obstruction of recompense. Son of the Buddha, sounds such as those spoken of above, each and every one filling asaṃkhyeya worlds, continuing through immeasurable, countless kalpas without ever once ceasing, are entirely able to destroy and disturb every faculty of a living being's own body and mind — and yet are unable to destroy this bodhisattva's own mind.

Chinese Source

菩薩入三昧中，住於聖法，思惟觀察一切音聲，善知音聲生、住、滅相，善知音聲生、住、滅性。如是聞已，不生於貪，不起於瞋，不失於念，善取其相而不染著；知一切聲皆無所有，實不可得，無有作者，亦無本際，與法界等，無有差別。菩薩如是成就寂靜身、語、意行，至一切智，永不退轉；善入一切諸禪定門，知諸三昧同一體性，了一切法無有邊際，得一切法真實智慧，得離音聲甚深三昧，得阿僧祇諸三昧門，增長無量廣大悲心。

Ru-Yi Translation

The bodhisattva, entering into samādhi, abiding within the noble Dharma, reflects upon and contemplates every kind of sound, skillfully knowing the marks of a sound's arising, abiding, and ceasing, and skillfully knowing the very nature of a sound's arising, abiding, and ceasing. Having heard in this way, they give rise to no greed, arouse no anger, and never lose their mindfulness; skillfully grasping its own mark, and yet never staining themselves with attachment to it; knowing that every sound has nothing to it at all, truly cannot be found, has no maker, and has no original limit either, being equal to the Dharma-realm itself, without any distinction at all. The bodhisattva, having accomplished such tranquil conduct of body, speech, and mind as this, arrives at all-knowing wisdom, forever never turning back; skillfully entering every gate of meditative absorption, knowing that every samādhi shares one and the same essential nature, understanding that every dharma has no boundary at all, attaining the true wisdom of every dharma, attaining the most profound samādhi that is free of sound, attaining asaṃkhyeya gates of samādhi, and increasing an immeasurable, vast and expansive mind of compassion.

Chinese Source

是時，菩薩於一念中，得無數百千三昧，聞如是聲，心不惑亂，令其三昧，漸更增廣。作如是念：『我當令一切眾生安住無上清淨念中，於一切智得不退轉，究竟成就無餘涅槃。』是名菩薩摩訶薩第五離癡亂行。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the bodhisattva, in a single thought, attains countless hundreds of thousands of samādhis; hearing such sounds as these, their mind is never confused or disturbed, and their samādhi, gradually, becomes even more expansive. They reflect in this way: 'I shall cause every living being to abide settled within an unsurpassed, pure mindfulness, to attain never turning back in all-knowing wisdom, and to accomplish, in the end, nirvāṇa without remainder.' This is called the bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own fifth practice, the practice free of delusion and confusion.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The eleven-fold list of "not disturbed" states names precisely what the earlier disturbance would have threatened — not simply peace of mind in general, but specific functions: mindfulness, concentration, samādhi, the mind of bodhi, memory of the buddhas. The contemplation that follows explains why none of it can actually be shaken: sound examined at its arising, abiding, and ceasing turns out to have no maker and no fixed nature, so there is, in the end, nothing solid enough there to disturb anything.
