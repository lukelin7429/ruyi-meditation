The Tower by the Sea

A blessing that leaves Good-Wealth's own body softened and lustrous opens onto a tower named for Vairocana himself, and a name Good-Wealth has heard only once before, in the previous fascicle: Maitreya.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1257 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Tower by the Sea

The youth and maiden's humility formula ends almost as soon as it begins, and their referral does something no teacher before them has done — it physically changes Good-Wealth's own body before a single word of directions is given.

Chinese Source

「善男子！我等二人但能知此幻住解脫。如諸菩薩摩訶薩善入無邊諸事幻網，彼功德行，我等云何能知能說？」時，童子、童女說自解脫已，以不思議諸善根力，令善財身柔軟光澤，而告之言：「善男子！於此南方，有國名：海岸，有園名：大莊嚴，其中有一廣大樓閣，名：毘盧遮那莊嚴藏，從菩薩善根果報生，從菩薩念力、願力、自在力、神通力生，從菩薩善巧方便生，從菩薩福德智慧生。「善男子！住不思議解脫菩薩，以大悲心，為諸眾生，現如是境界，集如是莊嚴。彌勒菩薩摩訶薩安處其中，為欲攝受本所生處父母、眷屬及諸人民，令成熟故；

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son! We two know only this liberation-gate of Dwelling in Illusion. As for the bodhisattva-mahāsattvas, who skillfully enter the boundless illusion-net of every phenomenon — how could we possibly know, or possibly speak, of their own virtuous practice?" At that time, having spoken of their own liberation-gate, the youth and maiden, through the power of their own inconceivable wholesome roots, caused Good-Wealth's body to become soft and lustrous, and said to him: "Good son! To the south of here, there is a country, named Sea-Coast; there is a grove there, named Great Adornment; and within it there is a vast and expansive tower, named Vairocana's Own Adorned Treasury, born from the fruit and reward of a bodhisattva's own wholesome roots, born from a bodhisattva's own power of mindfulness, power of vows, power of sovereign mastery, and power of spiritual penetration, born from a bodhisattva's own skillful means, and born from a bodhisattva's own merit and wisdom. Good son! A bodhisattva who abides in this inconceivable liberation, out of great compassion, manifests for the sake of every being a domain such as this, and gathers together an adornment such as this. The bodhisattva-mahāsattva Maitreya himself dwells within it, wishing to gather in and bring to maturity his own birthplace's parents, relatives, and the people there."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

彌勒菩薩摩訶薩安處其中 — the bodhisattva-mahāsattva Maitreya himself dwells within it. Maitreya was named once before in this journey, in the previous fascicle, as the fifth buddha of this Fortunate Eon, still to come. Here he appears not as a future buddha but as a bodhisattva already dwelling in a tower born from vows, spiritual power, and wisdom — the teacher this entire visit has been leading toward.
