A Trichiliocosm in Every Pore

The last clouds pour from Samantabhadra's pores, bodhisattva bodies proclaiming the wholesome roots of every buddha from their first resolve, clouds of Samantabhadra's own practice, and bodies of perfect enlightenment attaining enlightenment in every land. Then Good-Wealth, exulting, looks again, and in each part of Samantabhadra's body and each pore he sees a whole trichiliocosm: its wind-wheels, water-wheels, earth-wheels, and fire-wheels, its oceans and rivers, Sumeru and Cakravāḍa, its villages and palaces, its hells and hungry ghosts and the realm of King Yama, the eight classes of beings, the three realms, suns and stars, wind and thunder, days and nights and kalpas, buddhas appearing and assemblies gathered. He sees the same in every world of the ten directions and of the prior and later limits, and Samantabhadra displaying the same spiritual power before Bhadraśrī in the eastern world Lotus Virtue, and before every tathāgata in every direction.

A Trichiliocosm in Every Pore

The end of the pore-clouds, and the second look — whole worlds inside each pore, and the same vision extended across all space and all time.

Chinese Source

見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數菩薩身雲，遍法界、虛空界一切佛剎，宣揚一切諸佛菩薩從初發意所生善根；見一一毛孔，念念中出一切佛剎微塵數菩薩身雲，遍法界、虛空界，於一切佛剎一一剎中，宣揚一切菩薩願海及普賢菩薩清淨妙行；見一一毛孔，念念中出普賢菩薩行雲，令一切眾生心得滿足，具足修集一切智道；見一一毛孔，出一切佛剎微塵數正覺身雲，於一切佛剎，現成正覺，令諸菩薩增長大法、成一切智。爾時，善財童子見普賢菩薩如是自在神通境界，身心遍喜，踊躍無量；重觀普賢一一身分、一一毛孔，悉有三千大千世界。風輪、水輪、地輪、火輪，大海、江河及諸寶山、須彌、鐵圍，村營、城邑、宮殿、園苑，一切地獄、餓鬼、畜生、閻羅王界，天龍八部、人與非人，欲界、色界、無色界處，日月星宿、風雲雷電、晝夜月時及以年劫、諸佛出世、菩薩眾會、道場莊嚴；如是等事，悉皆明見。如見此世界，十方所有一切世界悉如是見；如見現在十方世界，前際、後際一切世界亦如是見，各各差別，不相雜亂。如於此毘盧遮那如來所，示現如是神通之力；於東方蓮華德世界賢首佛所，現神通力亦復如是。如賢首佛所；如是東方一切世界。如東方；南、西、北方，四維、上、下，一切世界諸如來所，現神通力當知悉爾。

Ru-Yi Translation

He saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of the bodies of bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading every buddha-land throughout the dharma-realm and the realm of space, proclaiming the wholesome roots that every buddha and bodhisattva has given rise to from their first resolve; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of the bodies of bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, pervading the dharma-realm and the realm of space, proclaiming in each and every land of all the buddha-lands the ocean of every bodhisattva's vows and the pure and wondrous practices of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva; he saw that from each and every pore, in thought after thought, there came forth clouds of the practices of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, causing the minds of every sentient being to attain fulfillment and to cultivate and gather in full the path of all-knowing wisdom; and he saw that from each and every pore there came forth clouds of bodies of perfect enlightenment as numerous as the fine motes of dust of every buddha-land, displaying in every buddha-land the attainment of perfect enlightenment, causing the bodhisattvas to grow in the great dharma and to accomplish all-knowing wisdom. At that time, Good-Wealth the youth, seeing such a domain of sovereign spiritual power of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, was joyful through body and mind and exulted beyond measure; and looking again at each and every part of Samantabhadra's body and each and every pore, he saw that in every one there was a great trichiliocosm: wind-wheels, water-wheels, earth-wheels, and fire-wheels; great oceans and rivers, and the jeweled mountains, Sumeru, and Cakravāḍa; villages and encampments, cities and towns, palaces and gardens; every hell, hungry ghost, and animal, and the realm of King Yama; the eight classes of devas and nāgas, humans and non-humans; the abodes of the desire realm, the form realm, and the formless realm; suns and moons and stars and constellations, wind and clouds and thunder and lightning, days and nights, months and seasons, and years and kalpas; the appearing of the buddhas in the world, the assemblies of the bodhisattvas, and the adornments of the sites of awakening. All such things as these he saw with clarity. As he saw this world, so he saw every world in the ten directions; as he saw the worlds of the ten directions in the present, so he saw every world of the prior limit and the later limit, each distinct and none confused with another. As Samantabhadra displayed such a power of spiritual penetration here in the presence of the Tathāgata Vairocana, so in the eastern world Lotus Virtue, in the presence of the buddha Bhadraśrī, he displayed the same power of spiritual penetration. As in the presence of the buddha Bhadraśrī, so in every world of the east. As in the east, so in the presence of every tathāgata in every world of the south, the west, the north, the four intermediate directions, the zenith, and the nadir, it should be known that he displayed the power of spiritual penetration in the same way.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

重觀 — Good-Wealth's second look at Samantabhadra reverses the direction of the previous parts: where the pores were sending clouds outward into the dharma-realm, they now hold whole worlds inward, 悉有三千大千世界, each part of the body and each pore containing a great trichiliocosm. The inventory of that world runs to twenty-seven items, from the four wheels that underlie a world system to the adornments of its sites of awakening, and is given in the source's own groupings; the four wheels are rendered with the site's established wind-wheel and water-wheel and the same form for earth and fire.

如見此世界 — the vision is then extended by a chain of 如, as, in five steps: as this world, so the ten directions; as the present, so the prior limit and the later limit; as Vairocana's presence, so the world Lotus Virtue and the buddha Bhadraśrī; as Bhadraśrī, so all the east; as the east, so every direction. 賢首 is restored as Bhadraśrī, the form the site has used for the name since Part 48, and 蓮華德 is rendered Lotus Virtue as before. The formula 當知悉爾, it should be known that it is so everywhere, closes the chain and hands the passage to the next part.
