Welcome, Stanzas 98–107

Maitreya's entire verse to Good-Wealth closes with its final ten stanzas, moving from what it takes to hear this teaching to a direct instruction: return to Mañjuśrī, who alone shall grant the profound practices of Samantabhadra.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1284 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 98–107

Ten stanzas close Maitreya's entire verse, ending the quotation opened back in Part 1278 — a lotus growing from water, a true vessel of the dharma, and a final instruction pointing Good-Wealth back toward Mañjuśrī.

Chinese Source

若有聞此法，
而興誓願心；
當知如是人，
已獲廣大利。

如是心清淨，
常得近諸佛，
亦近諸菩薩，
決定成菩提。

若入此法門，
則具諸功德，
永離眾惡趣，
不受一切苦。

不久捨此身，
往生佛國土，
常見十方佛，
及以諸菩薩。

往因今淨解，
及事善友力，
增長諸功德，
如水生蓮華。

樂事善知識，
勤供一切佛，
專心聽聞法，
常行勿懈倦。

汝是真法器，
當具一切法，
當修一切道，
當滿一切願。

汝以信解心，
而來禮敬我，
不久當普入，
一切諸佛會。

善哉真佛子，
恭敬一切佛，
不久具諸行，
到佛功德岸。

汝當往大智，
文殊師利所；
彼當令汝得，
普賢深妙行。

Ru-Yi Translation

If there is one who hears this dharma
and arouses a mind of vowed resolve,
know that such a person as this
has already gained vast benefit.

With a mind pure in just this way,
one constantly draws near to every buddha,
and also draws near to every bodhisattva,
certain to accomplish bodhi.

If one enters this dharma-gate,
one is then endowed with every merit,
forever free of every evil destiny,
never again receiving any suffering.

Before long, relinquishing this body,
one shall be born into a buddha's own land,
constantly beholding every buddha of the ten directions,
together with every bodhisattva.

Through past causes and present pure understanding,
and the power of serving a good friend,
every merit increases and grows,
just as a lotus grows from water.

Delighting in serving a good spiritual friend,
diligently making offerings to every buddha,
single-mindedly listening to and receiving the dharma,
always practicing, never growing weary.

You are a true vessel of the dharma,
you shall be endowed with every dharma,
you shall cultivate every path,
and you shall fulfill every vow.

With a mind of faith and understanding,
you have come to pay reverent homage to me;
before long you shall universally enter
every assembly of every buddha.

Excellent indeed, true buddha-child,
reverencing every buddha;
before long you shall be endowed with every practice,
and reach the shore of a buddha's own merit.

You shall go to the great wisdom
of Mañjuśrī's own place;
he shall cause you to attain
the profound and wondrous practices of Samantabhadra.
