The Tenth Dedication Named

Twenty-nine kinds of equality, each anchored in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable nature — of form, sensation, sentient beings, buddha-lands, and every dharma at once — culminate in a bodhisattva who abides securely in a pure body, speech, and mind equal to the dharma-realm itself. The sūtra then names what it has just finished describing: a bodhisattva mahāsattva's tenth dedication, abiding in the immeasurable dedication equal to the dharma-realm.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 391 — the tenth dedication is named Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Twenty-Nine Kinds of Equality

Nearly thirty times over, the same formula repeats — dedicating by abiding in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable equality — applied to bodily, verbal, and mental karma, to the five aggregates individually and together, to what is inward and outward, to sentient beings and buddha-lands, and finally to the dharma-realm's own non-duality with itself.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The list closes on "the dharma-realm's own immeasurable equality of the dharma-realm's own non-duality" — the dharma-realm made equal to its own non-duality with itself, a claim that folds the entire preceding list back onto the single term organizing it. Every other equality named here turns out to be one instance of this final, self-referential one.

Named: The Dedication Equal to the Dharma-Realm

Abiding securely in a pure body, speech, and mind equal to the dharma-realm, in a wisdom that broadly expounds every dharma, and in a light able to sever every sentient being's own net of doubt with a single voice — the sūtra pauses to name what it has just finished describing: the tenth and final dedication.

』「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩如是迴向時，安住法界無量平等清淨身，安住法界無量平等清淨語，安住法界無量平等清淨心，安住法界無量平等諸菩薩清淨行願，安住法界無量平等清淨眾會道場，安住法界無量平等為一切菩薩廣說諸法清淨智，安住法界無量平等能入盡法界一切世界身，安住法界無量平等一切法光明清淨無畏；能以一音盡斷一切眾生疑網，隨其根欲皆令歡喜，住於無上一切種智、力、無所畏、自在神通、廣大功德、出離法中。「佛子！是為菩薩摩訶薩第十住等法界無量迴向。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha! When this bodhisattva mahāsattva dedicates in this way, he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, and pure body; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, and pure speech; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, and pure mind; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, and pure practice and vow of every bodhisattva; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, and pure assembly at the seat of enlightenment; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal, pure wisdom that broadly expounds every dharma for every bodhisattva; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal body able to enter every world throughout the whole dharma-realm; he abides securely in the dharma-realm's own immeasurable, equal light of every dharma, pure and fearless; with a single voice he is entirely able to sever the net of doubt of every sentient being, according with their faculty and desire, causing them all to rejoice, abiding within the unsurpassed omniscience, power, fearlessness, sovereign spiritual power, vast merit, and dharma of renunciation. Child of the Buddha! This is called a bodhisattva mahāsattva's tenth dedication, abiding in the immeasurable dedication equal to the dharma-realm.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The naming lands quietly, exactly as it did for the ninth dedication — a single short sentence following an immense catalogue, rather than any special fanfare. What follows this naming, as with the ninth, is not silence but a further passage on what abiding in this dedication accomplishes, before the verse recapitulation begins.

Part 391 Complete

This names the tenth and final dedication of "The Ten Dedications" chapter, though its prose section does not yet close — a further passage on what this dedication accomplishes still remains. Chapter 25 continues in Part 392 as the bodhisattva mahāsattva who dedicates in this way fulfills Samantabhadra's own immeasurable, boundless practice and vow, purifying every buddha-land throughout the whole of empty space and the entire dharma-realm.
