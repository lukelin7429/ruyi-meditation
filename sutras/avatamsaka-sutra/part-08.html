The Lords of Fire, Water, Sea, River, Harvest, Herb, and Forest Speak

Fascicle four continues the pattern begun in fascicles two and three, now reaching the remaining classes of nature-spirit first named in fascicle one: the lords of fire, water, the sea, rivers, the harvest, medicinal herbs, and forests. Each class restates its roster with a liberation gateway unique to every lord, and one lord speaks a verse in praise of the Buddha on the whole class's behalf, each stanza naming another lord in turn.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 8 — opens fascicle 4 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Lords of Fire

Chinese Source

復次，普光焰藏主火神，得悉除一切世間闇解脫門；普集光幢主火神，得能息一切眾生諸惑漂流熱惱苦解脫門，大光遍照主火神，得無動福力大悲藏解脫門；眾妙宮殿主火神，得觀如來神通力示現無邊際解脫門；無盡光髻主火神，得光明照耀無邊虛空界解脫門；種種焰眼主火神，得種種福莊嚴寂靜光解脫門；十方宮殿如須彌山主火神，得能滅一切世間諸趣熾然苦解脫門；威光自在主火神，得自在開悟一切世間解脫門；光照十方主火神，得永破一切愚癡執著見解脫門；雷音電光主火神，得成就一切願力大震吼解脫門。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, the Fire-Lord of Universal Light's Treasury of Flame attained the liberation gateway of entirely removing the darkness of every world. The Fire-Lord Universally Gathering the Light-Banner attained the liberation gateway of being able to still the burning affliction of every living being adrift in confusion. The Fire-Lord of Great Light Universally Shining attained the liberation gateway of the treasury of great compassion, the unmoving power of blessing. The Fire-Lord of Every Wondrous Palace attained the liberation gateway of beholding the Tathāgata's power of spiritual transmutation manifesting without any bound. The Fire-Lord of the Inexhaustible Light-Topknot attained the liberation gateway of light shining forth throughout the boundless realm of space. The Fire-Lord of the Flame-Eye of Every Kind attained the liberation gateway of tranquil light adorned by every kind of blessing. The Fire-Lord Whose Palaces of the Ten Directions Are Like Mount Sumeru attained the liberation gateway of being able to extinguish the blazing suffering of every realm of rebirth in every world. The Fire-Lord of Majestic Light, Sovereign, attained the liberation gateway of sovereignly awakening every world. The Fire-Lord Illumining the Ten Directions attained the liberation gateway of forever breaking every view of ignorant attachment. And the Fire-Lord of the Thunder-Voice and the Lightning-Light attained the liberation gateway of the great, roaring thunder that accomplishes the power of every vow.

Chinese Source

爾時，普光焰藏主火神，承佛威力，遍觀一切主火神眾而說頌言：

「汝觀如來精進力，廣大億劫不思議，
為利眾生現世間，所有暗障皆令滅。
眾生愚癡起諸見，煩惱如流及火然，
導師方便悉滅除，普集光幢於此悟。

福德如空無有盡，求其邊際不可得，
此佛大悲無動力，光照悟入心生喜。
我觀如來之所行，經於劫海無邊際，
如是示現神通力，眾妙宮神所了知。

億劫修成不可思，求其邊際莫能知，
演法實相令歡喜，無盡光神所觀見。
十方所有廣大眾，一切現前瞻仰佛，
寂靜光明照世間，此妙焰神所能了。

牟尼出現諸世間，坐於一切宮殿中，
普雨無邊廣大法，此十方神之境界。
諸佛智慧最甚深，於法自在現世間，
能悉闡明真實理，威光悟此心欣慶。

諸見愚癡為暗蓋，眾生迷惑常流轉，
佛為開闡妙法門，此照方神能悟入。
願門廣大不思議，力度修治已清淨，
如昔願心皆出現，此震音神之所了。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the Fire-Lord of Universal Light's Treasury of Flame, sustained by the Buddha's majestic power, beheld the whole assembly of Fire-Lords everywhere, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Behold the Tathāgata's power of diligence, vast across a hundred million kalpas, inconceivable,
appearing in the world for the benefit of living beings, causing every dark obstruction to be extinguished entirely.
Living beings, in their ignorance, give rise to every kind of view, their afflictions flowing like a current and blazing like fire;
the Guide's skillful means extinguishes it all entirely — the Fire-Lord Universally Gathering the Light-Banner awakens to this.

Merit is like space, without end, and seeking its farther bound is impossible;
this is the Buddha's unmoving power of great compassion — the Fire-Lord of Light awakens to and enters this, his heart giving rise to joy.
I behold the Tathāgata's own conduct, passing through an ocean of kalpas without any bound;
such is the power of spiritual transmutation he manifests — the Fire-Lord of Every Wondrous Palace has fully understood this.

Accomplished across a hundred million kalpas, inconceivable, seeking its farther bound none is able to know it,
expounding the true mark of the Dharma to bring joy — the Fire-Lord of Inexhaustible Light beholds this.
Every vast assembly of the ten directions gazes up at the Buddha, present before them entirely,
his tranquil light illumining the world — this the Fire-Lord of Wondrous Flame is able to fully understand.

The Sage appears throughout every world, sitting within every palace,
raining down the boundless, vast Dharma universally — this is the domain of the Fire-Lord of the Ten Directions.
Every buddha's wisdom is most profound, sovereign with respect to the Dharma, appearing in the world,
able to reveal the true principle entirely — the Fire-Lord of Majestic Light awakens to this, his heart rejoicing.

Every view born of ignorance forms a dark covering, and living beings, confused, forever turn and flow in transmigration;
the Buddha opens wide the wondrous Dharma-gateway for their sake — this the Fire-Lord Illumining Every Direction is able to awaken to and enter.
The gateway of his vow is vast and inconceivable, the pāramitā of strength cultivated and already purified;
just as his vow-mind was in the past, so it now appears entirely — this the Fire-Lord of the Roaring Thunder-Voice has fully understood."

The Lords of Water

Chinese Source

復次，普興雲幢主水神，得平等利益一切眾生慈解脫門；海潮雲音主水神，得無邊法莊嚴解脫門；妙色輪髻主水神，得觀所應化方便普攝解脫門；善巧漩澓主水神，得普演諸佛甚深境界解脫門；離垢香積主水神，得普現清淨大光明解脫門；福橋光音主水神，得清淨法界無相無性解脫門；知足自在主水神，得無盡大悲海解脫門；淨喜善音主水神，得於菩薩眾會道場中為大歡喜藏解脫門；普現威光主水神，得以無礙廣大福德力普出現解脫門；吼聲遍海主水神，得觀察一切眾生發起如虛空調伏方便解脫門。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, the Water-Lord Universally Raising the Cloud-Banner attained the liberation gateway of the loving-kindness that benefits every living being equally. The Water-Lord of the Ocean-Tide's Cloud-Voice attained the liberation gateway of the boundless adornment of the Dharma. The Water-Lord of Wondrous Form and the Wheel-Topknot attained the liberation gateway of universally gathering in, through skillful means, those ready to be transformed. The Water-Lord of the Skillful Whirlpool attained the liberation gateway of universally proclaiming the profound domain of every buddha. The Water-Lord Free of Defilement with Accumulated Fragrance attained the liberation gateway of universally manifesting pure, great light. The Water-Lord of the Bridge of Blessing's Light-Voice attained the liberation gateway of the pure Dharma-realm, without mark and without nature. The Water-Lord Contented and Sovereign attained the liberation gateway of the inexhaustible ocean of great compassion. The Water-Lord of Pure Joy's Wholesome Voice attained the liberation gateway of becoming the great treasury of joy within the awakening-site assembly of bodhisattvas. The Water-Lord Universally Manifesting Majestic Light attained the liberation gateway of universally appearing through the unobstructed, vast power of merit. And the Water-Lord of the Roaring Voice Pervading the Sea attained the liberation gateway of contemplating every living being and rousing skillful means of taming as vast as space itself.

Chinese Source

爾時，普興雲幢主水神，承佛威力，遍觀一切主水神眾而說頌言：

「清淨慈門剎塵數，共生如來一妙相，
一一諸相莫不然，是故見者無厭足。
世尊往昔修行時，普詣一切如來所，
種種修治無懈倦，如是方便雲音入。

佛於一切十方中，寂然不動無來去，
應化眾生悉令見，此是髻輪之所知。
如來境界無邊量，一切眾生不能了，
妙音演說遍十方，此善旋神所行處。

世尊光明無有盡，充遍法界不思議，
說法教化度眾生，此淨香神所觀見。
如來清淨等虛空，無相無形遍十方，
而令眾會靡不見，此福光神善觀察。

佛昔修習大悲門，其心廣遍等眾生，
是故如雲現於世，此解脫門知足了。
十方所有諸國土，悉見如來坐於座，
朗然開悟大菩提，如是喜音之所入。

如來所行無罣礙，遍往十方一切剎，
處處示現大神通，普現威光已能悟。
修習無邊方便行，等眾生界悉充滿，
神通妙用靡暫停，吼聲遍海斯能入。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the Water-Lord Universally Raising the Cloud-Banner, sustained by the Buddha's majestic power, beheld the whole assembly of Water-Lords everywhere, and spoke a verse, saying:

"As numerous as the fine motes of dust in every land, the gateways of pure loving-kindness together give rise to a single, wondrous mark of the Tathāgata;
each and every mark is exactly so, and thus those who behold it are never satiated.
The World-Honored One, in the time of his practice in ages past, went universally to the place of every Tathāgata,
cultivating every kind of practice without ever growing weary — such skillful means the Water-Lord of the Ocean-Tide's Cloud-Voice has entered.

The Buddha, within all the ten directions, remains tranquil and unmoving, neither coming nor going,
and yet causes every being ready to be transformed to behold him — this the Water-Lord of the Wheel-Topknot knows.
The Tathāgata's domain is without any bound, and not one living being is able to fathom it,
his wondrous voice proclaiming it throughout the ten directions — this is the domain the Water-Lord of Skillful Whirling walks.

The World-Honored One's light is without end, filling the Dharma-realm inconceivably,
teaching the Dharma to transform and liberate living beings — this the Water-Lord Free of Defilement and Fragrant beholds.
The Tathāgata's purity is equal to empty space, without mark, without form, pervading the ten directions,
and yet causing every assembly to behold him without exception — this the Water-Lord of the Bridge of Blessing skillfully contemplates.

The Buddha, in ages past, cultivated the gateway of great compassion, his mind vast and pervading, equal to living beings themselves;
and so he appears in the world like a cloud — this liberation gateway the Water-Lord of Contentment has fully understood.
Every land of the ten directions beholds the Tathāgata seated upon his throne,
radiantly awakening to great bodhi — thus has the Water-Lord of the Wholesome Voice of Joy entered this.

The Tathāgata's conduct is without any obstruction, going universally to every land of the ten directions,
manifesting great spiritual transmutation in every place — the Water-Lord Universally Manifesting Majestic Light has already awakened to this.
Cultivating boundless, skillful conduct, filling entirely the realm of living beings,
his wondrous use of spiritual transmutation never pausing for an instant — the Water-Lord of the Roaring Voice Pervading the Sea is able to enter this."

The Lords of the Sea

Chinese Source

復次，出現寶光主海神，得以等心施一切眾生福德海眾寶莊嚴身解脫門；不可壞金剛幢主海神，得巧方便守護一切眾生善根解脫門；不雜塵垢主海神，得能竭一切眾生煩惱海解脫門；恒住波浪主海神，得令一切眾生離惡道解脫門；吉祥寶月主海神，得普滅大癡暗解脫門；妙華龍髻主海神，得滅一切諸趣苦與安樂解脫門；普持光味主海神，得淨治一切眾生諸見愚癡性解脫門；寶焰華光主海神，得出生一切寶種性菩提心解脫門；金剛妙髻主海神，得不動心功德海解脫門；海潮雷音主海神，得普入法界三昧門解脫門。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, the Sea-Lord Manifesting Jeweled Light attained the liberation gateway of the body adorned with every jewel, an ocean of merit given with an equal mind to every living being. The Sea-Lord of the Indestructible Vajra Banner attained the liberation gateway of skillfully guarding the wholesome roots of every living being through skillful means. The Sea-Lord Unmixed with Dust and Defilement attained the liberation gateway of being able to exhaust the ocean of every living being's affliction. The Sea-Lord Forever Abiding in the Waves attained the liberation gateway of causing every living being to depart from every evil destiny. The Sea-Lord of the Auspicious, Jeweled Moon attained the liberation gateway of universally extinguishing the great darkness of ignorance. The Sea-Lord of the Wondrous Flower's Nāga Topknot attained the liberation gateway of extinguishing the suffering of every realm of rebirth and bestowing peace and joy. The Sea-Lord Universally Upholding Light and Flavor attained the liberation gateway of purifying the nature of every view and ignorance in living beings. The Sea-Lord of the Jeweled Flame's Flower-Light attained the liberation gateway of bringing forth the bodhi-mind of every precious lineage. The Sea-Lord of the Vajra's Wondrous Topknot attained the liberation gateway of the ocean of merit of the unmoving mind. And the Sea-Lord of the Ocean-Tide's Thunder-Voice attained the liberation gateway of universally entering the samādhi-gate of the Dharma-realm.

Chinese Source

爾時，出現寶光主海神，承佛威力，普觀一切主海神眾而說頌言：

「不可思議大劫海，供養一切諸如來，
普以功德施群生，是故端嚴最無比。
一切世間皆出現，眾生根欲靡不知，
普為弘宣大法海，此是堅幢所欣悟。一切世間眾導師，法雲大雨不可測，消竭無窮諸苦海，此離垢塵入法門。

一切眾生煩惱覆，流轉諸趣受眾苦，
為其開示如來境，普水宮神入此門。
佛於難思劫海中，修行諸行無有盡，
永截眾生癡惑網，寶月於此能明入。

佛見眾生常恐怖，流轉生死大海中，
示彼如來無上道，龍髻悟解生欣悅。
諸佛境界不思議，法界虛空平等相，
能淨眾生癡惑網，如是持味能宣說。

佛眼清淨不思議，一切境界悉該覽，
普示眾生諸妙道，此是華光心所悟。
魔軍廣大無央數，一剎那中悉摧滅，
心無傾動難測量，金剛妙髻之方便。

普於十方演妙音，其音法界靡不周，
此是如來三昧境，海潮音神所行處。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the Sea-Lord Manifesting Jeweled Light, sustained by the Buddha's majestic power, universally beheld the whole assembly of Sea-Lords, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Inconceivable, a great ocean of kalpas, making offerings to every Tathāgata, universally bestowing the fruits of merit upon the multitude —
and so his adornment is the most peerless of all. He appears throughout every world, and there is no living being's capacity or desire that he fails to know,
universally proclaiming the great ocean of the Dharma — this the Sea-Lord of the Firm Banner has awakened to with delight. The guide of every world, his cloud of the Dharma raining down immeasurably,
exhausting the boundless ocean of every suffering — this is the Dharma-gateway the Sea-Lord Free of Dust and Defilement has entered.

Every living being, covered by affliction, turns and flows amid every realm of rebirth, suffering every torment;
for their sake he reveals the Tathāgata's domain — the Sea-Lord of the Universal-Water Palace enters this gateway.
The Buddha, within an ocean of inconceivable kalpas, practices every practice without end,
forever severing the net of confusion in living beings — the Sea-Lord of the Jeweled Moon is able to clearly enter this.

The Buddha beholds living beings forever fearful, turning and flowing in the great ocean of birth-and-death,
and reveals to them the Tathāgata's unsurpassed path — the Sea-Lord of the Nāga Topknot awakens to this with delight.
The domain of every buddha is inconceivable, the mark of the Dharma-realm and the realm of space equal alike,
able to purify the net of confusion in living beings — thus the Sea-Lord Universally Upholding Light and Flavor is able to proclaim this.

The Buddha's eye is pure and inconceivable, surveying every domain entirely,
universally showing living beings every wondrous path — this the Sea-Lord of the Jeweled Flame's Flower-Light has awakened to in his heart.
Māra's army, vast beyond number, is entirely crushed within a single instant,
his mind unwavering, beyond measure — this is the skillful means of the Sea-Lord of the Vajra's Wondrous Topknot.

Universally, throughout the ten directions, he proclaims a wondrous voice, and that voice pervades the Dharma-realm entirely —
this is the very samādhi-domain of the Tathāgata, the very ground the Sea-Lord of the Ocean-Tide's Voice walks."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The Chinese source's own line-grouping runs unevenly through the Sea-Lords' hymn — three couplets appear joined in what would otherwise be a single four-line stanza — most likely reflecting how the underlying manuscript tradition itself divided the verse rather than any irregularity introduced in translation. This translation follows the source's own grouping exactly rather than smoothing it into a uniform stanza pattern.

The Lords of Rivers

Chinese Source

復次，普發迅流主河神，得普雨無邊法雨解脫門；普潔泉澗主河神，得普現一切眾生前令永離煩惱解脫門；離塵淨眼主河神，得以大悲方便普滌一切眾生諸惑塵垢解脫門；十方遍吼主河神，得恒出饒益眾生音解脫門；普救護眾生主河神，得於一切含識中恒起無惱害慈解脫門；無熱淨光主河神，得普示一切清涼善根解脫門；普生歡喜主河神，得修行具足施令一切眾生永離慳著解脫門；廣德勝幢主河神，得作一切歡喜福田解脫門；光照普世主河神，得能令一切眾生雜染者清淨瞋毒者歡喜解脫門；海德光明主河神，得能令一切眾生入解脫海恒受具足樂解脫門。

Ru-Yi Translation

Furthermore, the River-Lord Universally Sending Forth a Swift Current attained the liberation gateway of universally raining the boundless rain of the Dharma. The River-Lord Universally Purifying Every Spring and Stream attained the liberation gateway of universally manifesting before every living being, causing them to forever depart from affliction. The River-Lord Free of Dust with a Pure Eye attained the liberation gateway of universally washing away every confusion and defilement in living beings through the skillful means of great compassion. The River-Lord Roaring Throughout the Ten Directions attained the liberation gateway of forever uttering a voice that enriches living beings. The River-Lord Universally Rescuing and Protecting Living Beings attained the liberation gateway of forever rousing loving-kindness free of vexation and harm toward every sentient being. The River-Lord Free of Heat with Pure Light attained the liberation gateway of universally revealing the cool, wholesome root of all. The River-Lord Universally Bringing Forth Joy attained the liberation gateway of cultivating complete generosity, causing every living being to depart forever from stinginess and attachment. The River-Lord of Vast Virtue's Supreme Banner attained the liberation gateway of becoming the field of blessing for every joy. The River-Lord Whose Light Illumines Every World attained the liberation gateway of causing those defiled among living beings to become pure, and those poisoned by anger to rejoice. And the River-Lord of the Ocean-Virtue's Light attained the liberation gateway of causing every living being to enter the ocean of liberation and forever receive complete joy.

Chinese Source

爾時，普發迅流主河神，承佛威力，遍觀一切主河神眾而說頌言：

「如來往昔為眾生，修治法海無邊行，
譬如霈澤清炎暑，普滅眾生煩惱熱。
佛昔難宣無量劫，以願光明淨世間，
諸根熟者令悟道，此普潔神心所悟。

大悲方便等眾生，悉現其前常化誘，
普使淨治煩惱垢，淨眼見此深歡悅。
佛演妙音普使聞，眾生愛樂心歡喜，
悉使滌除無量苦，此遍吼神之解脫。

佛昔修習菩提行，為利眾生無量劫，
是故光明遍世間，護神憶念生歡喜。
佛昔修行為眾生，種種方便令成熟，
普淨福海除眾苦，無熱見此心欣慶。

施門廣大無窮盡，一切眾生咸利益，
能令見者無慳著，此普喜神之所悟。
佛昔修行實方便，成就無邊功德海，
能令見者靡不欣，此勝幢神心悟悅。

眾生有垢咸淨治，一切怨害等生慈，
故得光照滿虛空，普世河神見歡喜。
佛是福田功德海，能令一切離諸惡，
乃至成就大菩提，此海光神之解脫。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the River-Lord Universally Sending Forth a Swift Current, sustained by the Buddha's majestic power, beheld the whole assembly of River-Lords everywhere, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Tathāgata, in ages past, for the sake of living beings, cultivated the boundless conduct of the ocean of the Dharma,
just as a heavy rain cools the burning heat of summer, universally extinguishing the heat of affliction in living beings.
The Buddha, in ages past, through immeasurable kalpas hard to describe, purified the world with the light of his vow,
causing those whose faculties had ripened to awaken to the path — the River-Lord of Universal Purity has awakened to this in his heart.

His great compassion's skillful means treats every living being equally, manifesting before them all, constantly guiding and transforming them,
universally purifying the defilement of affliction — the River-Lord of the Pure Eye beholds this with deep delight.
The Buddha proclaims a wondrous voice, causing all universally to hear it, and living beings, delighted, rejoice in their hearts,
causing every immeasurable suffering to be washed away — this is the liberation of the River-Lord Roaring Everywhere.

The Buddha, in ages past, cultivated the practice of bodhi, for the benefit of living beings across immeasurable kalpas,
and so his light pervades the world — the River-Lord of Protection recollects this and rejoices.
The Buddha, in ages past, practiced for the sake of living beings, ripening them through every kind of skillful means,
universally purifying the ocean of blessing and removing every suffering — the River-Lord Free of Heat beholds this, his heart delighted.

The gateway of giving is vast and inexhaustible, benefiting every living being entirely,
causing those who behold it to be free of stinginess and attachment — this the River-Lord Universally Bringing Forth Joy has awakened to.
The Buddha, in ages past, practiced true skillful means, accomplishing the boundless ocean of merit,

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Fire, water, sea, river, harvest, herb, and forest close out seven of the fifteen terrestrial classes fascicle one first named. Their liberation gateways trend consistently practical compared to the heavens above them: extinguishing sickness, exhausting affliction "like a heavy rain cools the burning heat of summer," causing seeds "sown in it never to be lost" — nature-spirits whose own domains are the world's actual means of survival are given gateways that mirror that domain's own function, medicine healing and rivers cooling exactly as they would in the world these spirits are said to govern. Part 9 completes fascicle four's roster with mountain, earth, city, the awakening-site spirits, foot-walking spirits, body-attending spirits, and the vajra-wielding spirits — the very last class remaining, and the first one fascicle one ever named.
