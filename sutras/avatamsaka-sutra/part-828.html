Entering the Dharma-Realm Begins

The sūtra's final chapter opens with its own scene: the Buddha at Jetavana, in Anāthapiṇḍada's Garden, seated within a great pavilion together with five hundred bodhisattva-mahāsattvas, Samantabhadra and Mañjuśrī foremost among them. What follows is the beginning of a name list unlike any other in the sūtra — each bodhisattva named individually, one after another, for well over a hundred entries.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 828 — entering the dharma-realm begins Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Roll Call Five Hundred Names Long

Rather than describing the assembly in general terms, the sūtra names its members one by one — Blazing-Light Banner, Sumeru Banner, Jewel Banner, and onward — a roll call that will continue across several more Parts before the text finally says "such bodhisattvas as these, five hundred in number, gathered together," acknowledging that even this long a list only begins to name them all.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Each name in this list is a meaningful epithet rather than a mere label — Sumeru Banner, Diamond-Wisdom Power, Lotus-Virtue Treasury — and this translation renders every one as English rather than transliterating them, following the same practice used throughout this project. Proper nouns and fascicle divisions have been verified directly against the CBETA source text rather than assumed.
