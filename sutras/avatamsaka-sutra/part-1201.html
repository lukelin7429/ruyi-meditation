Indra's Net

Gopā answers eleven paradoxes at once with a single named practice — Indra's Net, the Light of Universal Wisdom — and names ten dharmas that bring it to full completion, from relying on a good spiritual friend to severing every form of birth-and-death through the power of wisdom.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1201 — fascicle 75 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Indra's Net

Gopā praises the very form of Good-Wealth's question before answering it, and names the practice all eleven paradoxes turn out to belong to.

Chinese Source

時，瞿波女告善財言：「善哉善哉！善男子！汝今能問菩薩摩訶薩如是行法，修習普賢諸行願者能如是問。諦聽諦聽！善思念之！我當承佛神力，為汝宣說。「善男子！若諸菩薩成就十法，則能圓滿因陀羅網普智光明菩薩之行。何等為十？所謂：依善知識故，得廣大勝解故，得清淨欲樂故，集一切福智故，於諸佛所聽聞法故，心恒不捨三世佛故，同於一切菩薩行故，一切如來所護念故，大悲妙願皆清淨故，能以智力普斷一切諸生死故。是為十。若諸菩薩成就此法，則能圓滿因陀羅網普智光明菩薩之行。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Gopā said to Good-Wealth: "Excellent, excellent! Good son, that you are now able to ask about such a practice of the great bodhisattva-mahāsattvas is exactly how one who cultivates Samantabhadra's every practice and vow ought to ask. Listen carefully, listen carefully, and consider it well! I shall, sustained by the Buddha's own spiritual power, now expound it for you. "Good son, if a bodhisattva accomplishes ten dharmas, he is thereby able to bring to perfect fulfilment the bodhisattva practice of Indra's Net, the Light of Universal Wisdom. What are these ten? They are: because he relies upon a good spiritual friend; because he attains vast and expansive superior understanding; because he attains pure and undefiled aspiration; because he gathers every kind of merit and wisdom; because he hears the dharma at the place of every buddha; because his mind forever never abandons the buddhas of the three times; because he shares in every bodhisattva's own practice; because he is held in the mindful protection of every tathāgata; because his great compassion and wondrous vows are entirely pure; and because he is able, through the power of wisdom, to universally sever every form of birth-and-death. These are the ten. If a bodhisattva accomplishes these dharmas, he is thereby able to bring to perfect fulfilment the bodhisattva practice of Indra's Net, the Light of Universal Wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

因陀羅網普智光明菩薩之行 — the bodhisattva practice of Indra's Net, the Light of Universal Wisdom. Gopā never answers Good-Wealth's eleven paradoxes one by one; she answers them all at once, with the name of a single practice, framed by a sentence that opens and closes this passage nearly word for word — the same claim stated once as a promise and once, ten items later, as a confirmed result.
