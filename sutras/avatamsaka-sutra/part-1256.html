Dwelling in Illusion

For the first time in this journey, two teachers answer as one — a youth and a maiden sharing a single liberation-gate, and a list of ten things, from every world to every bodhisattva assembly, that all share its illusory nature.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1256 — fascicle 77 opens Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Dwelling in Illusion

Fascicle seventy-seven opens on a single joint answer — Virtue-Born and Possessing Virtue speak together, and their shared liberation-gate turns out to describe everything Good-Wealth has met on this journey so far.

Chinese Source

爾時，善財童子漸次南行，至妙意華門城，見德生童子、有德童女，頂禮其足，右遶畢已，於前合掌而作是言：「聖者！我已先發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，而未知菩薩云何學菩薩行？云何修菩薩道？唯願慈哀，為我宣說！」時，童子、童女告善財言：「善男子！我等證得菩薩解脫，名為：幻住。得此解脫故，見一切世界皆幻住，因緣所生故；一切眾生皆幻住，業煩惱所起故；一切世間皆幻住，無明、有愛等展轉緣生故；一切法皆幻住，我見等種種幻緣所生故；一切三世皆幻住，我見等顛倒智所生故；一切眾生生滅、生老病死、憂悲苦惱皆幻住，虛妄分別所生故；一切國土皆幻住，想倒、心倒、見倒無明所現故；一切聲聞、辟支佛皆幻住，智斷分別所成故；一切菩薩皆幻住，能自調伏教化眾生諸行願法之所成故；一切菩薩眾會、變化、調伏、諸所施為皆幻住，願智幻所成故。善男子！幻境自性不可思議。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Good-Wealth gradually journeyed south, and arrived at the city of Wondrous Mind's Flower Gate, where he saw the youth Virtue-Born and the maiden Possessing Virtue. He bowed at their feet, circled them to the right, and having finished, stood before them with his palms joined, and said this: "Noble ones! I have already given rise to the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, yet I do not yet know how a bodhisattva studies the bodhisattva's own practice, or how one cultivates the bodhisattva's own path. I beg you, out of compassion, to proclaim it for me!" At that time, the youth and maiden together said to Good-Wealth: "Good son! We two have realized a bodhisattva's own liberation-gate, named Dwelling in Illusion. Because we have attained this liberation-gate, we see that every world is illusion-dwelling, born from causes and conditions; every being is illusion-dwelling, arisen from karma and affliction; every realm is illusion-dwelling, born through the mutual conditioning of ignorance, craving for existence, and the like; every dharma is illusion-dwelling, born from every kind of illusory condition such as the view of self; all three periods of time are illusion-dwelling, born from the inverted wisdom of the view of self and the like; every being's own birth and death, birth, old age, sickness, and death, and every grief and sorrow and suffering is illusion-dwelling, born from false discrimination; every land is illusion-dwelling, manifested by inverted perception, inverted mind, inverted view, and ignorance; every śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha is illusion-dwelling, formed by wisdom's own discriminating and cutting-off; every bodhisattva is illusion-dwelling, formed by the very practices and vows through which they tame themselves and teach and transform beings; and every bodhisattva assembly, every transformation, every taming, and every activity performed is illusion-dwelling, formed by the illusion-like nature of vows and wisdom. Good son! The own-nature of this illusory state is inconceivable."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一切世界皆幻住⋯一切菩薩眾會、變化、調伏、諸所施為皆幻住 — every world is illusion-dwelling⋯every bodhisattva assembly, every transformation, every taming, and every activity performed is illusion-dwelling. Ten things named in all, each with its own cause: worlds, beings, realms, dharmas, the three times, suffering itself, lands, śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas, bodhisattvas, and finally bodhisattva assemblies — moving from the most ordinary categories to the very practice this journey has been documenting, and naming all of it equally illusory.
