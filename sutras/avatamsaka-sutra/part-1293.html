Equal to Every Buddha-Dharma

Nine final similes close the great chain that opened in Part 1290 — Indra's Net, Indra's own fire, a buddha's own shrine — and Maitreya sums the whole passage up in a single claim: every tathāgata of the three times arises from the mind of bodhi itself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1293 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Equal to Every Buddha-Dharma

Nine similes close the great chain begun in Part 1290; a summary statement follows, distilling everything into a single claim about where every buddha, past, present, and future, actually comes from.

Chinese Source

菩提心者，則為義利，能除一切衰惱事故；菩提心者，則為妙寶，能令一切心歡喜故；菩提心者，如大施會，充滿一切眾生心故；菩提心者，則為尊勝，諸眾生心無與等故；菩提心者，猶如伏藏，能攝一切諸佛法故；菩提心者，如因陀羅網，能伏煩惱阿脩羅故；菩提心者，如婆樓那風，能動一切所應化故；菩提心者，如因陀羅火，能燒一切諸惑習故；菩提心者，如佛支提，一切世間應供養故。「善男子！菩提心者，成就如是無量功德；舉要言之，應知悉與一切佛法諸功德等。何以故？因菩提心出生一切諸菩薩行，三世如來從菩提心而出生故。是故，善男子！若有發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心者，則已出生無量功德，普能攝取一切智道。

Ru-Yi Translation

The mind of bodhi becomes benefit itself, since it removes every matter of decline and vexation; the mind of bodhi becomes a wondrous jewel, since it brings joy to every mind; the mind of bodhi is like a great assembly of giving, since it fully satisfies the mind of every sentient being; the mind of bodhi becomes the most honored and supreme, since no sentient being's mind can equal it; the mind of bodhi is like a hidden treasury, since it gathers in every buddha-dharma; the mind of bodhi is like Indra's Net, since it subdues the asura of affliction; the mind of bodhi is like the wind of Varuṇa, since it stirs every being fit to be transformed; the mind of bodhi is like Indra's own fire, since it burns away every habitual tendency of delusion; the mind of bodhi is like a buddha's own shrine, since the entire world should make offerings to it. Good son! The mind of bodhi accomplishes measureless merit such as this; to speak of it briefly, know that it is equal to the merit of every buddha-dharma entirely. Why is this? Because every bodhisattva practice arises from the mind of bodhi, and every tathāgata of the three times arises from the mind of bodhi. Therefore, good son, if there is one who arouses the mind of anuttara-samyak-sambodhi, that one has already given rise to measureless merit, and is universally able to gather in the path of all-knowing wisdom.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

菩提心者，成就如是無量功德⋯應知悉與一切佛法諸功德等 — this closes the entire simile chain that opened in Part 1290. Counted across all four Parts (1290 through 1293), the chain runs to well over a hundred and thirty parallel similes for the mind of bodhi — verified stanza by stanza against the source text — before this single summary claim: bodhicitta is not merely like every buddha-dharma's own merit, it is equal to it, since every buddha across the three times arises from it directly.
