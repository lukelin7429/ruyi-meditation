Fascicle Seventy-Three Complete

The verse retelling ends, and the night-spirit closes her whole account with a plain admission of what lies beyond her — before naming a garden-spirit waiting at Lumbinī as the fortieth teacher, and sending Good-Wealth onward as the fascicle comes to a close.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Fascicle 73 complete — 73 of 80 fascicles done Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

I Only Know This One Gate

The verse falls silent, and the night-spirit returns to plain speech with the same confession every teacher in this pilgrimage eventually makes.

Chinese Source

「善男子！我唯知此教化眾生令生善根解脫門。如諸菩薩摩訶薩，超諸世間，現諸趣身，不住攀緣，無有障礙，了達一切諸法自性，善能觀察一切諸法，得無我智，證無我法，教化調伏一切眾生恒無休息，心常安住無二法門，普入一切諸言辭海；我今云何能知能說彼功德海、彼勇猛智、彼心行處、彼三昧境、彼解脫力？

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, I only know this liberation-gate of Teaching and Transforming Beings So They Give Rise to Wholesome Roots. As for the great bodhisattva-mahāsattvas — who transcend every worldly realm, manifest bodies in every destiny, do not abide in any clinging, and are free of every obstruction; who fully understand the intrinsic nature of every dharma, and are skilled in contemplating every dharma; who have attained the wisdom of no-self, and realised the dharma of no-self; who teach, transform, and tame every being without ever resting; whose minds forever abide in the dharma-gate of non-duality, and who universally enter the whole ocean of every mode of speech — how could I now possibly know or speak of their ocean of merit, their courageous wisdom, the ground of their mental conduct, the realm of their samādhi, or the power of their liberation?

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

我唯知此教化眾生令生善根解脫門 — I only know this liberation-gate of Teaching and Transforming Beings So They Give Rise to Wholesome Roots. This is the same name given twice already — first by the narrator in Part 1173, describing what the prince attained at the story's climax, and here, spoken by the night-spirit herself in the very words that opened her whole answer back in Part 1165. Sixteen Parts of hymn, catalogue, story, and its verse reprise all turn out to have been one gate, named identically at both ends.

Referred Onward, and the Fascicle Closes

The pilgrimage's standing gesture closes this teacher's account exactly as it has closed every other — a name for the next one, a single question, and a wordless departure.

Chinese Source

「善男子！此閻浮提，有一園林，名：嵐毘尼；彼園有神，名：妙德圓滿。汝詣彼問：菩薩云何修菩薩行、生如來家、為世光明，盡未來劫而無厭倦？」時，善財童子頂禮其足，遶無量匝，合掌瞻仰，辭退而去。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, in this Jambudvīpa there is a garden-forest named Lumbinī; within that garden there is a spirit named Wondrous Virtue, Perfect and Full. You should go to her and ask: how does a bodhisattva cultivate the bodhisattva practices, be born into the tathāgata's own family, and become a light for the world, never growing weary even to the very end of future kalpas?" At that time, Good-Wealth bowed at her feet, circled her countless times, gazed up at her with palms joined in devotion, took his leave, and departed.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

此閻浮提，有一園林，名：嵐毘尼 — in this Jambudvīpa there is a garden-forest named Lumbinī. Every teacher so far in this chapter has been a night-spirit dwelling at the bodhi-tree awakening-site itself; the fortieth teacher is the first in a long while to be found somewhere else — a named garden, rather than the assembly Good-Wealth has stood in since fascicle after fascicle began. Lumbinī needs no introduction to any reader who already knows why that name matters.
