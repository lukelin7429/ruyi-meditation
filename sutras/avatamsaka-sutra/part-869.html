The Buddha Emits Light from His Own Brows

Rather than continuing to describe the samādhi in words, the Buddha now lets the assembly see it directly: a great, named light emerges from between his own brows, and within it every land, and every possible kind of being in every possible kind of place, comes into view at once.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 869 — the Buddha emits light from his own brows Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Every Realm, Every Kind of Being

What this light reveals is not confined to buddha-lands and awakened bodhisattvas; it extends outward through the palaces of devas, nāgas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, and mahoragas, all the way down to ordinary human villages and royal cities — every possible category of being, in every possible kind of place.

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊欲令諸菩薩安住如來師子頻申廣大三昧故，從眉間白毫相放大光明，其光名：普照三世法界門，以不可說佛剎微塵數光明而為眷屬，普照十方一切世界海諸佛國土。時，逝多林菩薩大眾，悉見一切盡法界、虛空界一切佛剎一一微塵中，各有一切佛剎微塵數諸佛國土，種種名、種種色、種種清淨、種種住處、種種形相。如是一切諸國土中，皆有大菩薩坐於道場師子座上成等正覺，菩薩大眾前後圍遶，諸世間主而為供養；或見於不可說佛剎量大眾會中，出妙音聲充滿法界，轉正法輪；或見在天宮殿、龍宮殿、夜叉宮殿，乾闥婆、阿脩羅、迦樓羅、緊那羅、摩睺羅伽、人、非人等諸宮殿中，或在人間村邑聚落、王都大處，現種種姓、種種名、種種身、種種相、種種光明，住種種威儀，入種種三昧，現種種神變，或時自以種種言音，或令種種諸菩薩等在於種種大眾會中種種言辭說種種法。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the World-Honored One, wishing to cause the bodhisattvas to abide securely in the tathāgata's own vast Lion's Stretching-Forth Samādhi, emitted great light from the mark of the white curl between his own brows, this light being named: The Gate Universally Illuminating the Dharma-Realm Across the Three Times, accompanied by unspeakably many lesser lights, as numerous as motes of dust in every buddha-land, as its own retinue, universally illuminating every buddha-land within the ocean of every world in the ten directions. At that time, the bodhisattva assembly in the Jeta Grove saw completely that, within every single mote of dust in every buddha-land throughout the entire dharma-realm and realm of empty space, there were, in each one, buddha-lands as numerous as motes of dust in every buddha-land, each with its own name, its own color, its own purity, its own abode, and its own form. Within every one of these lands, there was a great bodhisattva seated upon a lion-seat at the site of awakening, attaining perfect awakening, surrounded before and behind by the bodhisattva assembly, with every ruler of the world making offerings to him; or they saw, within an assembly as vast as unspeakably many buddha-lands, a wondrous sound emerging and filling the entire dharma-realm, turning the true wheel of dharma; or they saw him within the palaces of devas, the palaces of nāgas, the palaces of yakṣas, and the palaces of gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, and non-humans, or within human villages, towns, and settlements, and great royal capitals, manifesting every kind of lineage, every kind of name, every kind of body, every kind of appearance, and every kind of light, abiding in every kind of dignified manner, entering every kind of samādhi, and manifesting every kind of spiritual transformation, at times himself speaking in every kind of language, and at times causing various bodhisattvas to speak every kind of dharma in every kind of language within every kind of assembly.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

從眉間白毫相放大光明 — emitted great light from the mark of the white curl between his own brows. This same mark, the ūrṇā, was named earlier in this fascicle's story as one of the sources of light offered by an arriving bodhisattva's own cloud; here it becomes the actual source of this vision, the sūtra quietly making good on an offering made many parts before.
