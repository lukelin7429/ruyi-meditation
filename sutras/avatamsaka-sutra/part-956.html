A Ten-Fold Praise of the Good Spiritual Friend

Still prostrate on the ground, Good-Wealth names ten things a good spiritual friend has been to him — gate, vehicle, boat, torch, road, lamp, bridge, canopy, eye, and tide — each paired with exactly what it makes possible, before rising to ask the same question he has carried to every teacher so far.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 956 — a ten-fold praise of the good spiritual friend Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Gate, Vehicle, Boat, Torch, Road

The first five images move Good-Wealth forward and upward at once — a gate he enters, a vehicle that carries him, a boat that crosses water, a torch that gives him light, and a road that leads all the way to the city of nirvāṇa itself.

Chinese Source

「我今得遇真善知識。善知識者，則是趣向一切智門，令我得入真實道故；善知識者，則是趣向一切智乘，令我得至如來地故；善知識者，則是趣向一切智船，令我得至智寶洲故；善知識者，則是趣向一切智炬，令我得生十力光故；善知識者，則是趣向一切智道，令我得入涅槃城故；

Ru-Yi Translation

"I have now encountered a true good spiritual friend. A good spiritual friend is the very gate leading toward omniscience, causing me to enter the true path; a good spiritual friend is the very vehicle leading toward omniscience, causing me to reach the tathāgata's own ground; a good spiritual friend is the very boat leading toward omniscience, causing me to reach the island of the treasury of wisdom; a good spiritual friend is the very torch leading toward omniscience, causing the light of the ten powers to arise within me; a good spiritual friend is the very road leading toward omniscience, causing me to enter the city of nirvāṇa;

Lamp, Bridge, Canopy, Eye, Tide

The second five images turn toward seeing and shelter — a lamp that reveals which roads are safe, a bridge across danger, a canopy of cool compassion, an eye onto the true nature of things, and finally a tide that fills him entirely with great compassion's own water.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

善知識者，則是趣向一切智潮 — a good spiritual friend is the very tide leading toward omniscience. Every one of these ten images names something Good-Wealth travels through or by — a gate, a road, a bridge — except this last one, where the good spiritual friend instead arrives at him, the way a tide rises to fill whatever is waiting on the shore.
