Welcome, Stanzas 1–18

Maitreya turns to the great assembly and speaks his own verse, opening with a repeating "welcome" refrain naming Good-Wealth's every quality, then closing this Part with two extended stanzas of similes for what a good spiritual friend truly is.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1279 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 1–18

Sixteen short stanzas of five-character lines build a repeating "welcome" refrain around Good-Wealth himself, before two much longer stanzas break the pattern to spell out, simile by simile, what a good spiritual friend actually is.

Chinese Source

汝等觀善財，
智慧心清淨，
為求菩提行，
而來至我所。

善來圓滿慈，
善來清淨悲，
善來寂滅眼，
修行無懈倦。

善來清淨意，
善來廣大心，
善來不退根，
修行無懈倦。

善來不動行，
常求善知識，
了達一切法，
調伏諸群生。

善來行妙道，
善來住功德，
善來趣佛果，
未曾有疲倦。

善來德為體，
善來法所滋，
善來無邊行，
世間難可見。

善來離迷惑，
世法不能染，
利衰毀譽等，
一切無分別。

善來施安樂，
調柔堪受化；
諂誑瞋慢心，
一切悉除滅。

善來真佛子，
普詣於十方，
增長諸功德，
調柔無懈倦。

善來三世智，
遍知一切法，
普生功德藏，
修行不疲厭。

文殊德雲等，
一切諸佛子，
令汝至我所，
示汝無礙處。

具修菩薩行，
普攝諸群生；
如是廣大人，
今來至我所。

為求諸如來，
清淨之境界，
問諸廣大願，
而來至我所。

去來現在佛，
所成諸行業，
汝欲皆修學，
而來至我所。

汝於善知識，
欲求微妙法，
欲受菩薩行，
而來至我所。

汝念善知識，
諸佛所稱歎，
令汝成菩提，
而來至我所。

汝念善知識，
生我如父母，
養我如乳母，
增我菩提分，
如醫療眾疾，
如天灑甘露，
如日示正道，
如月轉淨輪，
如山不動搖，
如海無增減，
如船師濟渡，
而來至我所。

汝觀善知識，
猶如大猛將，
亦如大商主，
又如大導師，
能建正法幢，
能示佛功德，
能滅諸惡道，
能開善趣門，
能顯諸佛身，
能守諸佛藏，
能持諸佛法，
是故願瞻奉。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Look upon Good-Wealth, all of you —
his wisdom and mind are pure;
seeking the practice of bodhi,
he has come to where I am.

Welcome, one of perfected loving-kindness;
welcome, one of pure compassion;
welcome, one of the eye of quiescence —
cultivating practice without ever growing weary.

Welcome, one of a pure mind;
welcome, one of a vast and great heart;
welcome, one of a faculty that never turns back —
cultivating practice without ever growing weary.

Welcome, one of unmoving practice,
who always seeks a good spiritual friend,
who has fully understood every dharma,
and tames every kind of being.

Welcome, one who walks the wondrous path;
welcome, one who abides in merit;
welcome, one who hastens toward the buddha-fruit —
never once has he grown weary.

Welcome, one whose very body is virtue;
welcome, one nourished by the dharma;
welcome, one of boundless practice —
rarely seen anywhere in the world.

Welcome, one free of delusion,
whom no worldly dharma can stain;
gain and loss, blame and praise, and all such pairs —
toward every one of them, no discrimination.

Welcome, one who gives peace and joy,
gentle and fit to receive transformation;
flattery, deceit, anger, and conceit —
every one of them entirely dissolved away.

Welcome, true buddha-child,
who travels universally throughout the ten directions,
increasing every kind of merit,
gentle and never growing weary.

Welcome, one of the wisdom of the three times,
who fully knows every dharma,
universally giving rise to the treasury of merit,
cultivating practice without ever tiring.

Mañjuśrī, Virtue-Cloud, and the rest —
every one of these buddha-children —
have caused you to come to where I am,
showing you this place without obstruction.

Fully cultivating the bodhisattva practices,
universally gathering in every kind of being —
such a vast and great person as this
has now come to where I am.

Seeking the pure realm
of every tathāgata,
asking about every vast and great vow,
he has come to where I am.

Every deed accomplished
by every buddha, past, future, and present —
wishing to cultivate and learn them all,
he has come to where I am.

From a good spiritual friend,
wishing to seek the subtle and wondrous dharma,
wishing to receive the bodhisattva practices,
he has come to where I am.

You recollect the good spiritual friend
praised by every buddha,
who causes you to accomplish bodhi —
and so you have come to where I am.

You recollect the good spiritual friend:
giving you birth as would a father or mother,
nurturing you as would a wet-nurse,
increasing your every portion of bodhi;
like a physician healing every sickness,
like the heavens sprinkling sweet nectar,
like the sun showing the right path,
like the moon turning its pure wheel,
like a mountain that never sways,
like the ocean that neither grows nor shrinks,
like a ship's captain who ferries beings across —
and so you have come to where I am.

You regard the good spiritual friend
just as you would a great and fearless general,
also as you would a great caravan leader,
and also as you would a great guide;
able to raise the banner of the true dharma,
able to reveal a buddha's own merit,
able to dissolve away every evil destiny,
able to open the gate to every wholesome destiny,
able to manifest every buddha's own body,
able to guard every buddha's own treasury,
able to uphold every buddha's own dharma —
and so you wish to look up to and honor them.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

善來圓滿慈，善來清淨悲 — Maitreya's own verse to Good-Wealth opens with sixteen short stanzas, ten of them built on the repeating 善來 ("welcome") refrain. Stanza and line counts verified directly against the source text; two stanzas in this Part use 、；、 rather than 、，、 partway through their third line, confirmed directly against the source rather than assumed.

汝念善知識，生我如父母⋯而來至我所 — the pattern breaks sharply at the seventeenth stanza: instead of four lines, eleven similes for a good spiritual friend unfold across twelve lines each in these final two stanzas — parent, wet-nurse, physician, the heavens, the sun, the moon, a mountain, the ocean, a ship's captain, a general, a caravan leader, a guide.
