Welcome, Stanzas 19–38

Maitreya turns from welcoming Good-Wealth to describing him directly — a captain who ferries beings across, the sun and moon of true awakening, a lamp lit with the oil of compassion, and an embryo of awakening growing limb by limb within the tathāgata-treasury.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1280 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Welcome, Stanzas 19–38

Five stanzas on Good-Wealth's own renunciation open this Part, followed by a long run of images naming him directly — captain, sun, moon, ocean, nāga, lamp, embryo, tree — each stanza its own complete metaphor.

Chinese Source

欲滿清淨智，
欲具端正身，
欲生尊貴家，
而來至我所。

汝等觀此人，
親近善知識，
隨其所修學，
一切應順行。

以昔福因緣，
文殊令發心，
隨順無違逆，
修行不懈倦。

父母與親屬，
宮殿及財產，
一切皆捨離，
謙下求知識。

淨治如是意，
永離世間身，
當生佛國土，
受諸勝果報。

善財見眾生，
生老病死苦，
為發大悲意，
勤修無上道。

善財見眾生，
五趣常流轉，
為求金剛智，
破彼諸苦輪。

善財見眾生，
心田甚荒穢，
為除三毒刺，
專求利智犁。

眾生處癡暗，
盲冥失正道；
善財為導師，
示其安隱處。

忍鎧解脫乘，
智慧為利劍，
能於三有內，
破諸煩惱賊。

善財法船師，
普濟諸含識，
令過爾焰海，
疾至淨寶洲。

善財正覺日，
智光大願輪，
周行法界空，
普照群迷宅。

善財正覺月，
白法悉圓滿，
慈定清涼光，
等照眾生心。

善財勝智海，
依於直心住，
菩提行漸深，
出生眾法寶。

善財大心龍，
昇於法界空，
興雲霔甘澤，
生成一切果。

善財然法燈，
信炷慈悲油，
念器功德光，
滅除三毒暗。

覺心迦羅邏，
悲胞慈為肉，
菩提分肢節，
長於如來藏。

增長福德藏，
清淨智慧藏，
開顯方便藏，
出生大願藏。

如是大莊嚴，
救護諸群生；
一切天人中，
難聞難可見。

如是智慧樹，
根深不可動，
眾行漸增長，
普蔭諸群生。

Ru-Yi Translation

Wishing to fulfill pure wisdom,
wishing to be endowed with an upright body,
wishing to be born into an honored family,
he has come to where I am.

Look upon this person, all of you —
drawing near to a good spiritual friend,
following whatever is cultivated and learned there,
everyone should accord with such practice.

Through the causes and conditions of past merit,
Mañjuśrī caused him to arouse the mind;
according with it, never once turning against it,
he cultivates practice without ever growing weary.

Father, mother, and every relative,
palace and every possession —
all of these he has entirely relinquished,
humbling himself to seek a spiritual friend.

Purifying and refining a mind such as this,
forever leaving behind a worldly body,
he shall be born into a buddha's own land,
and receive every excellent fruit of reward.

Good-Wealth, seeing sentient beings
suffer birth, old age, sickness, and death,
for their sake aroused a mind of great compassion,
and diligently cultivates the unsurpassed path.

Good-Wealth, seeing sentient beings
ever flowing and turning through the five destinies,
seeks the vajra-wisdom
to break apart the wheel of their every suffering.

Good-Wealth, seeing sentient beings
whose field of mind is thick with weeds,
to remove the thorns of the three poisons,
single-mindedly seeks the sharp plow of wisdom.

Sentient beings dwell within the darkness of delusion,
blind and lost, having strayed from the right path;
Good-Wealth becomes their guide,
showing them a place of peace and security.

With the armor of forbearance and the vehicle of liberation,
and wisdom serving as a sharp sword,
he is able, within the three states of existence,
to defeat every bandit of affliction.

Good-Wealth, a captain of the dharma-ship,
universally ferries every sentient being across,
bringing them past the ocean of that blazing heat,
swiftly to the pure jeweled isle.

Good-Wealth, the sun of true awakening,
the wheel of a great vow's wisdom-light,
travels throughout the emptiness of the dharma-realm,
universally illuminating the dwellings of every deluded being.

Good-Wealth, the moon of true awakening,
every wholesome dharma fully complete within him,
the cool, clear light of loving-kindness and samādhi
illuminates equally the minds of every sentient being.

Good-Wealth, an ocean of surpassing wisdom,
abiding upon a straightforward mind,
his bodhi-practice gradually deepening,
gives rise to every treasure of the dharma.

Good-Wealth, a nāga of great mind,
rising into the emptiness of the dharma-realm,
raises clouds and pours down sweet rain,
bringing every fruit to ripeness.

Good-Wealth kindles the lamp of dharma,
faith as its wick, loving compassion as its oil,
mindfulness as its vessel, merit as its light,
extinguishing entirely the darkness of the three poisons.

The mind of awakening is the kalala,
compassion its womb, loving-kindness its flesh,
the factors of bodhi its every limb and joint,
growing within the tathāgata-treasury.

Increasing the treasury of merit,
purifying the treasury of wisdom,
opening and revealing the treasury of skillful means,
giving rise to the treasury of great vows.

An adornment as great as this,
rescuing and protecting every kind of being —
among every deva and every human,
rarely heard of, rarely seen.

A tree of wisdom such as this,
its roots deep and unshakable,
every practice gradually increasing,
universally shading every kind of being.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

善財法船師，普濟諸含識 — from the eleventh stanza on, the refrain drops "welcome" entirely and turns to naming Good-Wealth directly by his own name, one image per stanza: ship's captain, sun, moon, ocean, nāga, lamp. Stanza count and line count verified directly against the source text.

覺心迦羅邏，悲胞慈為肉 — 迦羅邏 (kalala) is the classical Buddhist term for the very first stage of embryonic development; this stanza maps the stages of gestation directly onto the stages of a bodhisattva's awakening, growing "within the tathāgata-treasury" itself.
