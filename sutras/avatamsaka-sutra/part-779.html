Ten Kinds of Light-Sufficiency, Ten Kinds of Seeking the Dharma

Ten kinds of light-sufficiency move from skillfully discerning every dharma to the classical powers of the divine eye, recollection of past lives, and extinction of outflows. Ten kinds of seeking the dharma follow, moving from an upright mind free of flattery to a final entry seeking only to fulfill the buddha-dharma, with no delight in any other vehicle.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 779 — ten kinds of light-sufficiency, ten kinds of seeking the dharma Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Not for Fame, but to Cut Off Suffering

Both lists here define their own purpose by explicit contrast: the fourth kind of seeking the dharma is undertaken to cut off every sentient being's affliction, not for fame, profit, or reverence, and the sixth seeks wisdom itself while taking no delight in mere words — each entry naming not only what is sought but what it is emphatically not sought for.

Chinese Source

佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種明足。何等為十？所謂：善分別諸法明足；不取著諸法明足；離顛倒見明足；智慧光照諸根明足；巧發起正精進明足；能深入真諦智明足；滅煩惱業成就盡智無生智明足；天眼智普觀察明足；宿住念知前際清淨明足；漏盡神通智斷眾生諸漏明足。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得如來於一切佛法無上大光明。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of light-sufficiency. What are these ten? Namely: the light-sufficiency of skillfully discerning every dharma; the light-sufficiency of not grasping at or attaching to any dharma; the light-sufficiency of leaving behind every inverted view; the light-sufficiency of wisdom's light illuminating every faculty; the light-sufficiency of skillfully arousing right diligence; the light-sufficiency of the wisdom able to penetrate deeply into the truth; the light-sufficiency of extinguishing afflictions and karma, accomplishing the wisdom of exhaustion and the wisdom of non-arising; the light-sufficiency of the wisdom of the divine eye universally observing; the light-sufficiency of the recollection of past lives knowing the purity of the prior limit; and the light-sufficiency of the spiritual wisdom of the extinction of outflows cutting off every outflow of sentient beings. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the tathāgata's own unsurpassed, great light within every buddha-dharma.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩有十種求法。何等為十？所謂：直心求法，無有諂誑故；精進求法，遠離懈慢故；一向求法，不惜身命故；為斷一切眾生煩惱求法，不為名利恭敬故；為饒益自他一切眾生求法，不但自利故；為入智慧求法，不樂文字故；為出生死求法，不貪世樂故；為度眾生求法，發菩提心故；為斷一切眾生疑求法，令無猶豫故；為滿足佛法求法，不樂餘乘故。是為十。若諸菩薩安住此法，則得不由他教一切佛法大智慧。

Ru-Yi Translation

Son of the Buddha! A bodhisattva-mahāsattva has ten kinds of seeking the dharma. What are these ten? Namely: seeking the dharma with an upright mind, because it is free of flattery and deceit; seeking the dharma with diligence, because it stays far from laziness and conceit; seeking the dharma single-mindedly, because it does not spare body or life; seeking the dharma in order to cut off every sentient being's affliction, not for the sake of fame, profit, or reverence; seeking the dharma to benefit oneself and every other sentient being, not for one's own benefit alone; seeking the dharma in order to enter wisdom, taking no delight in mere words; seeking the dharma in order to escape birth-and-death, not craving any worldly pleasure; seeking the dharma in order to deliver sentient beings, giving rise to the mind of awakening; seeking the dharma in order to cut off every sentient being's doubt, so that none remains hesitant; and seeking the dharma in order to fulfill the buddha-dharma, taking no delight in any other vehicle. These are the ten. If bodhisattvas abide within this dharma, they attain the great wisdom of every buddha-dharma without needing to be taught by another.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

則得不由他教一切佛法大智慧 — they attain the great wisdom of every buddha-dharma without needing to be taught by another. The tenth kind of seeking the dharma, fulfilling the buddha-dharma itself rather than settling for any lesser vehicle, is what the closing line ties directly to a wisdom that no longer depends on instruction from outside.
