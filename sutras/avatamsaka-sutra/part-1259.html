Anointed with Ambrosia

Eight things Maitreya has already completed answer why he can answer Good-Wealth's eleven questions at all, and ten more name exactly what a good spiritual friend actually gives — not vague blessing, but ten distinct and namable gifts.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1259 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Anointed with Ambrosia

The eleven questions from the last Part get their answer in advance: eight completed accomplishments explain why Maitreya can answer at all, climbing from every pāramitā fulfilled to a crown already anointed by every tathāgata.

Chinese Source

「何以故？善男子！彼菩薩摩訶薩通達一切菩薩行，了知一切眾生心，常現其前教化調伏。彼菩薩已滿一切波羅蜜，已住一切菩薩地，已證一切菩薩忍，已入一切菩薩位，已蒙授與具足記，已遊一切菩薩境，已得一切佛神力，已蒙一切如來以一切智甘露法水而灌其頂。善男子！彼善知識能潤澤汝諸善根，能增長汝菩提心，能堅汝志，能益汝善，能長汝菩薩根，能示汝無礙法，能令汝入普賢地，能為汝說菩薩願，能為汝說普賢行，能為汝說一切菩薩行願所成功德。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Why is this? Good son! That bodhisattva-mahāsattva has thoroughly penetrated every bodhisattva practice, understands entirely the minds of every being, and forever appears before them to teach and tame them. That bodhisattva has already fulfilled every pāramitā, already abides in every bodhisattva ground, already realized every bodhisattva forbearance, already entered every bodhisattva station, already received a complete prediction, already traveled through every bodhisattva domain, already attained the spiritual power of every buddha, and already been anointed on the crown by every tathāgata with the ambrosia-like dharma-water of all-knowing wisdom. Good son! That good spiritual friend is able to moisten and enrich your every wholesome root, able to increase your mind of bodhi, able to make firm your resolve, able to benefit your every virtue, able to lengthen your bodhisattva roots, able to show you the unobstructed dharma, able to cause you to enter Samantabhadra's own ground, able to speak to you of the bodhisattva's own vows, able to speak to you of Samantabhadra's own practice, and able to speak to you of the merit accomplished by every bodhisattva's own practices and vows."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

已滿一切波羅蜜⋯已蒙一切如來以一切智甘露法水而灌其頂 — already fulfilled every pāramitā⋯already been anointed on the crown by every tathāgata with the ambrosia-like dharma-water of all-knowing wisdom. Eight accomplishments in all, each one a stage further along the same path Good-Wealth has just been told to ask about — pāramitās, grounds, forbearance, and finally the crown-anointing every buddha shares. And immediately after, ten separate gifts the text says a good spiritual friend actually gives, not one of them a synonym for the others: wholesome roots, the mind of bodhi, resolve, virtue, bodhisattva roots, the unobstructed dharma, Samantabhadra's own ground, vows, practice, and the merit of every practice and vow together.
