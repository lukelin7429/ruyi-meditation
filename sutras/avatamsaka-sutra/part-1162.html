Forty-Two Things Held in Common

Good-Wealth's ten minds toward the night-spirit are met with a list far longer than his own — the same mindfulness, the same wisdom, the same fearlessness, each one shared not with him but with every bodhisattva everywhere, running here to forty-two items before it is even half finished.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1162 — fascicle 73 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Fourteen Qualities of Mind

The list opens with a single sentence naming what the night-spirit now holds in common with countless bodhisattvas, and runs, without pause, through fourteen qualities of mind and realization before it even slows down.

Chinese Source

發是心已，得彼夜神與諸菩薩佛剎微塵數同行。所謂：同念，心常憶念十方三世一切佛故；同慧，分別決了一切法海差別門故；同趣，能轉一切諸佛如來妙法輪故；同覺，以等空智普入一切三世間故；同根，成就菩薩清淨光明智慧根故；同心，善能修習無礙功德，莊嚴一切菩薩道故；同境，普照諸佛所行境故；同證，得一切智照實相海淨光明故；同義，能以智慧了一切法真實性故；同勇猛，能壞一切障礙山故；同色身，隨眾生心示現身故；同力，求一切智不退轉故；同無畏，其心清淨如虛空故；同精進，於無量劫行菩薩行無懈倦故；

Ru-Yi Translation

Having given rise to these minds, Good-Wealth came to share, with that night-spirit and with bodhisattvas as many as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, practices held in common. Namely: the same mindfulness, because her mind constantly calls to mind every buddha of the ten directions and the three times; the same wisdom, because she distinguishes and resolves every gate of distinction within the ocean of dharmas; the same destination, because she is able to turn the wondrous dharma-wheel of every buddha and tathāgata; the same awakening, because with wisdom equal to space she universally enters every world of the three times; the same faculty, because she has accomplished the bodhisattva's pure and radiant faculty of wisdom; the same mind, because she is skilfully able to cultivate unobstructed merit, adorning every bodhisattva path; the same domain, because she universally illuminates the domain in which every buddha moves; the same realisation, because she has attained the pure and radiant light of all-knowing wisdom that illuminates the ocean of true reality; the same meaning, because with wisdom she understands the true nature of every dharma; the same courage, because she is able to destroy the mountain of every hindrance; the same form-body, because she manifests a body according to the minds of beings; the same power, because she seeks all-knowing wisdom without ever retreating; the same fearlessness, because her mind is pure like empty space; the same vigour, because throughout measureless kalpas she practises the bodhisattva path without ever growing weary;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

得彼夜神與諸菩薩佛剎微塵數同行 — came to share, with that night-spirit and with bodhisattvas as many as the fine motes of dust in a buddha-land, practices held in common. Good-Wealth's own ten minds, given at the close of Part 1161, are never referred back to directly — the text moves straight from what he felt to what he now shares, as though the ten minds were themselves the mechanism that produced this sharing rather than a step that needs to be credited by name. What he gains is not described as a gift from the night-spirit to him; it is described as something he now holds in common with her and with an entire order of magnitude of other beings at once.

Fourteen More, Including a Threefold Karma

The list continues through voice, virtue, and finally the three classical channels of action — body, speech, and mind — named in a row for the first time here.

Chinese Source

同辯才，得法無礙智光明故；同無等，身相清淨超世間故；同愛語，令一切眾生皆歡喜故；同妙音，普演一切法門海故；同滿音，一切眾生隨類解故；同淨德，修習如來淨功德故；同智地，一切佛所受法輪故；同梵行，安住一切佛境界故；同大慈，念念普覆一切國土眾生海故；同大悲，普雨法雨潤澤一切諸眾生故；同身業，以方便行教化一切諸眾生故；同語業，以隨類音演說一切諸法門故；同意業，普攝眾生置一切智境界中故；同莊嚴，嚴淨一切諸佛剎故；

Ru-Yi Translation

The same eloquence, because she has attained the unobstructed light of the wisdom of the dharma; the same peerlessness, because her bodily form is pure, surpassing the world; the same loving speech, because she causes every being to rejoice; the same wondrous voice, because she universally proclaims the ocean of every dharma-gate; the same complete voice, because every being, each according to its own kind, understands it; the same pure virtue, because she cultivates the tathāgata's own pure merit; the same ground of wisdom, because she has received the dharma-wheel at the place of every buddha; the same pure conduct, because she abides securely in the domain of every buddha; the same great loving-kindness, because in thought after thought she universally covers the ocean of beings in every land; the same great compassion, because she universally rains down the rain of dharma, moistening every being; the same bodily karma, because through skilful conduct she teaches and transforms every being; the same verbal karma, because with a voice suited to every kind she proclaims every dharma-gate; the same mental karma, because she universally gathers in beings and places them within the domain of all-knowing wisdom; the same adornment, because she adorns and purifies every buddha-land;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

同身業⋯⋯同語業⋯⋯同意業 — the same bodily karma⋯⋯the same verbal karma⋯⋯the same mental karma. Of the twenty-eight items named so far, these three alone form a recognisable classical set — the three karmas of body, speech, and mind — arriving together and in this exact order, with nothing else in the surrounding list grouped so tightly. Everywhere else the list moves associatively, one quality suggesting the next; here, for three items only, it borrows a fixed doctrinal triad wholesale.

Fourteen More Still — Entry, Retinue, Excellence

The list turns from qualities of virtue toward qualities of practice — drawing near, entreating, entering, until it names an excellence that admits no comparison.

Chinese Source

同親近，有佛出世皆親近故；同勸請，請一切佛轉法輪故；同供養，常樂供養一切佛故；同教化，調伏一切諸眾生故；同光明，照了一切諸法門故；同三昧，普知一切眾生心故；同充遍，以自在力充滿一切諸佛剎海修諸行故；同住處，住諸菩薩大神通故；同眷屬，一切菩薩共止住故；同入處，普入世界微細處故；同心慮，普知一切諸佛剎故；同往詣，普入一切佛剎海故；同方便，悉現一切諸佛剎故；同超勝，於諸佛剎皆無比故；

Ru-Yi Translation

The same drawing-near, because whenever a buddha appears in the world she draws near to him; the same entreaty, because she entreats every buddha to turn the dharma-wheel; the same offering, because she constantly delights in making offerings to every buddha; the same teaching, because she tames and subdues every being; the same light, because she illuminates and fully understands every dharma-gate; the same samādhi, because she universally knows the minds of every being; the same pervasion, because with sovereign power she fills the ocean of every buddha-land, cultivating every practice; the same dwelling-place, because she abides in every bodhisattva's great spiritual power; the same retinue, because she dwells together with every bodhisattva; the same place of entry, because she universally enters the most subtle places of every world; the same consideration of mind, because she universally knows every buddha-land; the same going-forth, because she universally enters the ocean of every buddha-land; the same skilful means, because she manifests every buddha-land entirely; the same surpassing excellence, because in every buddha-land she is without equal;

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

同入處⋯⋯同往詣 — the same place of entry⋯⋯the same going-forth. Both items name entering every buddha-land, phrased two different ways nine items apart in the same list — 普入世界微細處, universally entering the most subtle places of every world, and 普入一切佛剎海, universally entering the ocean of every buddha-land. This list does not appear to be organised to avoid repetition of theme; the same claim about universal entry surfaces more than once, in different words, as the enumeration continues to grow.

Part 1162 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle seventy-three. Part 1163 carries the same list through its remaining forty-two items, closing on a great trembling of every land in the ten directions, before Good-Wealth's own answering verse.
