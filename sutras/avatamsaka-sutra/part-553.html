The Wisdom of All Dharmas

After eight penetrations reaching outward — into minds, into the past and future, into every sound and every color — the ninth turns inward on language itself, naming nearly thirty pairs of opposites that no dharma falls into either side of, before explaining how a teacher can still speak.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 553 — the ninth penetration Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Neither This Nor Not This

Where the earlier penetrations named what a bodhisattva can know or reach, the ninth is built almost entirely out of what cannot be said of any dharma at all.

Chinese Source

「佛子！菩薩摩訶薩以一切法智通，知一切法無有名字、無有種性，無來、無去，非異、非不異，非種種、非不種種，非二、非不二，無我、無比，不生、不滅，不動、不壞，無實、無虛，一相、無相，非無、非有，非法、非非法，不隨於俗、非不隨俗，非業、非非業，非報、非非報，非有為、非無為，非第一義、非不第一義，非道、非非道，非出離、非不出離，非量、非無量，非世間、非出世間，非從因生、非不從因生，非決定、非不決定，非成就、非不成就，非出、非不出，非分別、非不分別，非如理、非不如理。

Ru-Yi Translation

Sons of the Buddha! Through the penetration of the wisdom of all dharmas, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva knows that all dharmas have no name and no inherent nature; no coming and no going; neither different nor not different; neither manifold nor not manifold; neither dual nor non-dual; without self, without comparison; unborn, unceasing; unmoving, indestructible; neither real nor unreal; of a single mark, yet without mark; neither nonexistent nor existent; neither dharma nor non-dharma; not following the conventional, yet not failing to follow it; neither karma nor non-karma; neither karmic result nor non-result; neither conditioned nor unconditioned; neither ultimate truth nor not ultimate truth; neither a path nor not a path; neither release nor non-release; neither measured nor measureless; neither worldly nor transcending the world; neither arisen from causes nor not arisen from causes; neither fixed nor not fixed; neither accomplished nor not accomplished; neither emerging nor not emerging; neither discriminated nor not discriminated; and neither in accord with principle nor not in accord with principle.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

非X、非非X — neither X nor not-X, repeated for nearly twenty pairs. The formula moves from concrete opposites — coming and going, self and comparison — toward the sūtra's own technical vocabulary: conditioned and unconditioned, ultimate truth, dependent origination. No category the tradition has built for talking about reality survives being applied to a single dharma without qualification.

Speaking What Cannot Be Spoken

Having denied every category, the passage turns immediately to a bodhisattva who still teaches — and explains how that is possible without contradiction.

Chinese Source

此菩薩不取世俗諦，不住第一義，不分別諸法，不建立文字，隨順寂滅性，不捨一切願，見義知法，興布法雲，降霔法雨。雖知實相不可言說，而以方便無盡辯才，隨法、隨義次第開演；以於諸法言辭辯說皆得善巧，大慈大悲悉已清淨，能於一切離文字法中出生文字，與法、與義隨順無違，為說諸法悉從緣起，雖有言說而無所著。演一切法辯才無盡，分別安立，開發示導，令諸法性具足明顯，斷眾疑網悉得清淨。雖攝眾生不捨真實，於不二法而無退轉，常能演說無礙法門，以眾妙音，隨眾生心，普雨法雨而不失時。是名：菩薩摩訶薩第九一切法智神通。

Ru-Yi Translation

This bodhisattva does not grasp at conventional truth, does not abide in ultimate truth, does not discriminate among dharmas, does not establish written words; according with the nature of quiescence, he yet does not abandon a single vow — seeing the meaning, knowing the dharma, he raises and spreads clouds of dharma, pouring down the rain of dharma. Though he knows that true reality cannot be spoken of, with skillful means and inexhaustible eloquence he expounds it in sequence, according with the dharma and according with the meaning; attaining skillful mastery in speaking and expounding all dharmas, his great loving-kindness and great compassion already pure, he is able to give rise to written words from within dharmas entirely free of words, according with the dharma and the meaning without contradiction, teaching that all dharmas arise entirely from dependent origination — though there is speech, there is no attachment to it. Expounding all dharmas with inexhaustible eloquence, distinguishing and establishing them, opening, revealing, showing, and guiding, he causes the nature of every dharma to become fully clear and evident, severing every net of doubt, all made pure. Though gathering in beings, he does not abandon the truth; in the non-dual dharma he never regresses; he is ever able to expound unobstructed dharma-gateways, and with manifold wondrous sounds, according with beings' minds, universally rains the rain of dharma, never missing the right time. This is called the bodhisattva-mahāsattva's ninth spiritual penetration, that of the wisdom of all dharmas.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

能於一切離文字法中出生文字 — able to give rise to written words from within dharmas entirely free of words. The same shape as Part 534's forty-one paradoxes, 雖知...而..., "though he knows... yet he...", now aimed squarely at language itself: words are not smuggled in around the emptiness of dharmas, they are produced directly out of it. The next clause states the resolution plainly — 雖有言說而無所著, though there is speech, there is no attachment to it.
