A Hundred Million Dhāraṇī-Gates Begin

Loving-Conduct begins naming what a hundred million asaṃkhyeyas of dhāraṇī-gates actually are — starting with buddha-lands, buddhas, and the dharma itself, moving through karma and samādhi, and closing this first stretch with nine distinct ways of knowing exactly what a being is.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 982 — a hundred million dhāraṇī-gates begin Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Buddha-Lands, Karma, Samādhi, Mind, and Beings

Each named gate stands for its own complete dhāraṇī, and the sequence itself moves outward and inward by turns — from buddha-lands and the three times, to karma's own workings, to samādhi entered and left, to the mind's own dense forest, ending on nine separate ways of knowing a being to its very root.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知眾生所從生 — knowing where beings are born from. This opens nine dhāraṇī-gates in a row devoted entirely to knowing beings, moving from their origin through their affliction, understanding, conduct, nature, desire, and finally their own thoughts — a complete inventory of what there is to know about anyone at all.

Part 982 Complete

This continues chapter thirty-nine, 入法界品, in fascicle sixty-five. Part 983 turns to the next stretch of dhāraṇī-gates, moving from teaching and compassion through every kind of dharma and every kind of world, pure and impure alike.
