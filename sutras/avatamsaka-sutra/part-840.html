A Bodhisattva Arrives from the West

The third arrival brings the most elaborate offering yet: ten distinct kinds of cloud, each one shaped like Mount Sumeru, made variously of incense, fragrant water, blazing light, and maṇi jewels that manifest entire narratives — every buddha's own past causes, and the practices every bodhisattva has carried out.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 840 — a bodhisattva arrives from the west Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Clouds Shaped Like Mount Sumeru

Each of the ten clouds raised here shares the same form — the towering shape of Mount Sumeru itself — while differing entirely in what it is made of, moving from simple incense and fragrant water toward maṇi jewels that manifest the past deeds of every buddha and the practices of every bodhisattva.

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, in the west, beyond an ocean of worlds as numerous as unspeakably many fine motes of dust in every buddha-land, there was a world named Maṇi-Jewel Lamp Sumeru Banner, whose buddha was named Dharma-Realm Wisdom Lamp. In that buddha's own assembly there was a bodhisattva named Universally Victorious Unsurpassed Awesome-Virtue King, who, together with bodhisattvas as numerous as fine motes of dust in an ocean of worlds, came toward the place where the Buddha was, raising up through spiritual power alone: unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of every kind of anointing incense and burning incense; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of every kind of colored fragrant water; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewel kings whose light equals every mote of dust across the whole earth; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of banners adorned with every kind of blazing wheel of light; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, adorned with every kind of colored vajra-treasury maṇi king; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of jambūnada-gold maṇi jewel banners universally illuminating every world; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewels manifesting the entire dharma-realm; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewel kings manifesting the major and minor marks of every buddha; unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewel kings manifesting every tathāgata's own past causes and conditions, proclaiming the practices every bodhisattva has carried out; and unspeakably many clouds shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewel kings manifesting every buddha seated at the site of awakening — filling the entire dharma-realm. Having arrived where the Buddha was, they bowed in reverence at his feet, and then, in the west, conjured a pavilion that was king among every kind of fragrance, covered above with nets of pearls, together with a lion-seat of jeweled lotus-treasury shaped like Śakra's own reflected banner; covered their own bodies with nets of wondrously colored maṇi gems; adorned their own heads with crowns of mind-king jewels; and sat down cross-legged there together with their own retinues.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

現一切如來本事因緣說諸菩薩所行之行摩尼寶王須彌山雲 — a cloud shaped like Mount Sumeru, made of maṇi jewel kings manifesting every tathāgata's own past causes and conditions, proclaiming the practices every bodhisattva has carried out. Ninth among the ten clouds, this one is not made of any physical substance at all, but of narrative itself — an entire, ongoing account of causes and practices held complete within a single jewel.
