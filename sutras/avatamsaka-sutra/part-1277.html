Verse, Stanzas 35–54

Twenty stanzas of five-character lines open on a single mote of dust holding measureless lands, then close with Good-Wealth's own voice breaking through the description for the first time — palms joined, bowing to the one this verse has been describing all along.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1277 — fascicle 77 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Verse, Stanzas 35–54

The meter shortens to five-character lines and the refrain moves to the front — "a buddha-child dwells here" — running through nineteen stanzas of description before the twentieth turns to direct address.

Chinese Source

佛子住於此，
普現眾生前，
猶如日月輪，
遍除生死暗。

佛子住於此，
普順眾生心，
變現無量身，
充滿十方剎。

佛子住於此，
遍遊諸世界，
一切如來所，
無量無數劫。

佛子住於此，
思量諸佛法，
無量無數劫，
其心無厭倦。

佛子住於此，
念念入三昧，
一一三昧門，
闡明諸佛境。

佛子住於此，
悉知一切剎，
無量無數劫，
眾生佛名號。

佛子住於此，
一念攝諸劫，
但隨眾生心，
而無分別想。

佛子住於此，
修習諸三昧，
一一心念中，
了知三世法。

佛子住於此，
結跏身不動，
普現一切剎，
一切諸趣中。

佛子住於此，
飲諸佛法海，
深入智慧海，
具足功德海。

佛子住於此，
悉知諸剎數，
世數眾生數，
佛名數亦然。

佛子住於此，
一念悉能了，
一切三世中，
國土之成壞。

佛子住於此，
普知佛行願，
菩薩所修行，
眾生根性欲。

佛子住於此，
見一微塵中，
無量剎道場，
眾生及諸劫。

如一微塵內，
一切塵亦然，
種種咸具足，
處處皆無礙。

佛子住於此，
普觀一切法，
眾生剎及世，
無起無所有。

觀察眾生等，
法等如來等，
剎等諸願等，
三世悉平等。

佛子住於此，
教化諸群生，
供養諸如來，
思惟諸法性。

無量千萬劫，
所修願智行，
廣大不可量，
稱揚莫能盡。

彼諸大勇猛，
所行無障礙，
安住於此中，
我合掌敬禮。

Ru-Yi Translation

A buddha-child dwells here,
manifesting universally before every sentient being,
just like the wheel of the sun and moon,
dispelling entirely the darkness of birth-and-death.

A buddha-child dwells here,
universally according with the minds of sentient beings,
transforming and manifesting measureless bodies,
filling every land of the ten directions.

A buddha-child dwells here,
traveling universally through every world,
to every place where a tathāgata dwells,
for measureless and countless kalpas.

A buddha-child dwells here,
contemplating every buddha-dharma,
for measureless and countless kalpas,
his mind never once growing weary.

A buddha-child dwells here,
entering samādhi in thought after thought,
and within each and every gate of samādhi,
illuminating clearly the very realm of every buddha.

A buddha-child dwells here,
knowing fully every land,
and for measureless and countless kalpas,
the names of every sentient being and every buddha.

A buddha-child dwells here,
gathering every kalpa within a single thought,
yet only following the minds of sentient beings,
without any notion of discrimination.

A buddha-child dwells here,
cultivating every samādhi,
and within each and every thought,
understanding fully the dharmas of the three times.

A buddha-child dwells here,
seated cross-legged, his body unmoving,
yet manifesting universally within every land,
within every single destiny there is.

A buddha-child dwells here,
drinking from the ocean of every buddha-dharma,
entering deeply into the ocean of wisdom,
fully endowed with the ocean of merit.

A buddha-child dwells here,
knowing fully the number of every land,
the number of every age and every sentient being,
and the number of every buddha's name as well.

A buddha-child dwells here,
able within a single thought to understand fully,
throughout all of the three times,
every land's own arising and dissolving.

A buddha-child dwells here,
universally knowing the practice and vow of every buddha,
the practices a bodhisattva cultivates,
and the faculties, natures, and desires of every sentient being.

A buddha-child dwells here,
seeing within a single mote of dust,
measureless lands and places of practice,
every sentient being, and every kalpa.

Just as within a single mote of dust,
so it is within every mote of dust as well,
every kind of thing fully present within it,
and everywhere, without any obstruction.

A buddha-child dwells here,
universally contemplating every dharma,
every sentient being, every land, and every age,
as without arising and without anything at all.

Contemplating sentient beings as equal,
dharmas as equal, tathāgatas as equal,
lands as equal, and every vow as equal,
with all of the three times equal throughout.

A buddha-child dwells here,
teaching and transforming every being,
making offerings to every tathāgata,
and contemplating the very nature of every dharma.

Throughout measureless millions of kalpas,
the vows, wisdom, and practices he has cultivated
are vast and broad beyond all measure —
praise could never exhaust them.

To that one of such great courage,
whose every practice is without any hindrance,
who abides securely within this very place —
I join my palms and bow in reverence.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

佛子住於此，普現眾生前 — the meter shifts here from seven-character to five-character lines, and the refrain moves from the end of each stanza to its start: 佛子住於此 ("a buddha-child dwells here") opens fourteen of these twenty stanzas. Stanza count and line count both verified directly against the source text.

我合掌敬禮 — the final line of this Part is the first place in the entire verse where Good-Wealth's own voice breaks through the description in the first person. Everything before this point described a figure in the third person; this line turns to direct address, setting up the final stanza still to come.
