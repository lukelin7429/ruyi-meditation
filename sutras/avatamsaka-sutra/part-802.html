Naming the Teaching, the Buddhas' Praise

After everything this chapter has unfolded across two fascicles and hundreds of ten-part lists, it now names itself — first briefly, as the Bodhisattva's Vast and Pure Practice, then in a long compound title tying it to decisive meaning, every dharma, and detachment from the world. Every buddha of the ten directions then appears before Samantabhadra, revealed here as the one who has spoken this entire teaching, to praise him by name.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 802 — naming the teaching, the buddhas' praise Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

The Chapter Names Itself

Naming a teaching within the teaching itself is unusual, and this chapter does it twice: first with a short title, the Bodhisattva's Vast and Pure Practice, and then with a long compound name that folds in decisive meaning, universal entry into every dharma, and transcendence of the world — the very name 離世間品, detachment from the world, spoken by the text describing itself.

Chinese Source

「佛子！此法門名：菩薩廣大清淨行。無量諸佛所共宣說，能令智者了無量義皆生歡喜，令一切菩薩大願、大行皆得相續。佛子！若有眾生得聞此法，聞已信解，解已修行，必得疾成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。何以故？以如說修行故。佛子！若諸菩薩不如說行，當知是人於佛菩提則為永離，是故菩薩應如說行。佛子！此一切菩薩功德行處．決定義華．普入一切法．普生一切智．超諸世間．離二乘道．不與一切諸眾生共．悉能照了一切法門．增長眾生出世善根．離世間法門品，應尊重，應聽受，應誦持，應思惟，應願樂，應修行；若能如是，當知是人疾得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Son of the Buddha! This dharma-gate is named: the Bodhisattva's Vast and Pure Practice. It is proclaimed together by measureless buddhas, able to cause the wise to understand measureless meanings and all give rise to joy, and to cause every bodhisattva's own great vow and great practice to continue without interruption. Son of the Buddha! If there are sentient beings who attain hearing of this dharma, and having heard it, believe and understand it, and having understood it, practice it, they will certainly and swiftly attain unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening. Why is this? Because they practice exactly as it is spoken. Son of the Buddha! If bodhisattvas do not practice exactly as it is spoken, know that such a person is thereby forever separated from the buddha's own awakening; for this reason, bodhisattvas should practice exactly as it is spoken. Son of the Buddha! This chapter — the domain of every bodhisattva's own merit and practice, the flower of decisive meaning, universally entering every dharma, universally giving rise to all-knowing wisdom, transcending every worldly thing, leaving behind the path of the two lesser vehicles, shared with no sentient being at all, entirely able to illuminate every dharma-gate, and increasing sentient beings' own world-transcending roots of goodness — the chapter of detachment from the world, should be revered, should be listened to and received, should be recited and upheld, should be reflected upon, should be delighted in through aspiration, and should be practiced; if one is able to do so, know that such a person swiftly attains unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening.

Chinese Source

」說此品時，佛神力故，及此法門法如是故，十方無量無邊阿僧祇世界皆大震動，大光普照。爾時，十方諸佛皆現普賢菩薩前，讚言：「善哉善哉！佛子！乃能說此諸菩薩摩訶薩功德行處．決定義華．普入一切佛法．出世間法門品。佛子！汝已善學此法，善說此法。汝以威力護持此法，我等諸佛悉皆隨喜；如我等諸佛隨喜於汝，一切諸佛悉亦如是。佛子！我等諸佛悉共同心護持此經，令現在、未來諸菩薩眾未曾聞者皆當得聞。」爾時，普賢菩薩摩訶薩承佛神力，觀察十方一切大眾洎于法界而說頌言：

Ru-Yi Translation

When this chapter was spoken, through the buddha's own spiritual power, and because the nature of this dharma-gate is such, every measureless, boundless, asaṃkhyeya world throughout the ten directions was greatly shaken, and a great light universally shone forth. At that time, every buddha throughout the ten directions appeared before the bodhisattva Samantabhadra, praising him, saying: "Excellent, excellent! Son of the Buddha! You have been able to speak this chapter — the domain of every bodhisattva-mahāsattva's own merit and practice, the flower of decisive meaning, universally entering every buddha-dharma, the dharma-gate of transcending the world. Son of the Buddha! You have already skillfully studied this dharma, and skillfully spoken this dharma. You uphold and protect this dharma through your own awesome power, and we, every buddha, all rejoice in this together; and just as we buddhas rejoice in you, so too does every buddha, all in the very same way. Son of the Buddha! We, every buddha, together with one mind, uphold and protect this sūtra, causing every assembly of bodhisattvas, present and future, who has not yet heard it, to come to hear it." At that time, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva Samantabhadra, sustained by the buddhas' own spiritual power, observed every great assembly throughout the ten directions, together with the entire dharma-realm, and spoke in verse, saying:

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

爾時，十方諸佛皆現普賢菩薩前，讚言 — at that time, every buddha throughout the ten directions appeared before the bodhisattva Samantabhadra, praising him. Only here, near the very close of this immense chapter, does the text name its speaker outright. Everything from the ten pāramitās through the ten kinds of buddha-deeds, spoken across two full fascicles, is retroactively revealed as Samantabhadra's own teaching, now praised in person by every buddha of the ten directions.
