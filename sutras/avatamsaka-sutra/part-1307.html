Only the Vajra Ground Can Hold It

The vajra series continues: vajra that no ordinary person obtains, that a person who does not know jewels cannot recognize or use, that nothing can dissolve, the vajra club that only the strength of Nārāyaṇa can lift, vajra that destroys everything and is itself never diminished, vajra that only the vajra ground can bear, and a flawless vajra vessel from which no water ever leaks.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1307 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Only the Vajra Ground Can Hold It

Seven more paired similes continue the vajra series — who can obtain it, who can recognize it, what can hold it, and what it holds.

Chinese Source

「善男子！譬如金剛，非凡人所得。菩提心金剛亦復如是，非劣意眾生之所能得。「善男子！譬如金剛，不識寶人不知其能、不得其用。菩提心金剛亦復如是，不知法人不了其能、不得其用。「善男子！譬如金剛，無能銷滅。菩提心金剛亦復如是，一切諸法無能銷滅。「善男子！如金剛杵，諸大力人皆不能持，唯除有大那羅延力。菩提之心亦復如是，一切二乘皆不能持，唯除菩薩廣大因緣堅固善力。「善男子！譬如金剛，一切諸物無能壞者，而能普壞一切諸物，然其體性無所損減。菩提之心亦復如是，普於三世無數劫中，教化眾生，修行苦行，聲聞、緣覺所不能者咸能作之，然其畢竟無有疲厭亦無損壞。「善男子！譬如金剛，餘不能持，唯金剛地之所能持。菩提之心亦復如是，聲聞、緣覺皆不能持，唯除趣向薩婆若者。「善男子！如金剛器，無有瑕缺用盛於水，永不滲漏而入於地。菩提心金剛器亦復如是，盛善根水，永不滲漏，令入諸趣。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as vajra is not obtained by ordinary people, the vajra that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: it cannot be obtained by beings of lowly intent. Good son! Just as a person who does not know jewels does not recognize what vajra can do and does not gain its use, the vajra that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: a person who does not know the dharma does not understand what it can do and does not gain its use. Good son! Just as nothing is able to dissolve vajra, the vajra that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: no dharma whatsoever is able to dissolve it. Good son! Just as the vajra club cannot be lifted by any person of great strength, except one who has the great strength of Nārāyaṇa, the mind of bodhi is likewise: no follower of the two vehicles is able to hold it, except a bodhisattva with the firm and wholesome strength of vast and great causes and conditions. Good son! Just as vajra is something no thing whatsoever can destroy, while it is able to destroy everything whatsoever, and yet its own essential nature is in no way diminished, the mind of bodhi is likewise: throughout countless kalpas of the three times it teaches and transforms sentient beings and cultivates ascetic practice, doing everything that śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas cannot, and yet in the end it has no weariness and suffers no damage. Good son! Just as vajra cannot be held by anything else, and only the vajra ground is able to hold it, the mind of bodhi is likewise: no śrāvaka or pratyekabuddha is able to hold it, except those who are heading toward sarvajñā. Good son! Just as a vajra vessel without any flaw or gap, used to hold water, never leaks it away into the ground, the vajra vessel that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: holding the water of wholesome roots, it never leaks them away to run off into the destinies.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

薩婆若 — the sixth simile names the goal as 薩婆若, a transliteration of sarvajñā, where the rest of this chain of similes has used the translated form 一切智, all-knowing wisdom. The transliteration is kept as sarvajñā, the spelling this site already uses for it, so that the one place the passage switches register remains visible. 那羅延 in the fourth simile is Nārāyaṇa, whose strength has already served as a measure earlier in this fascicle's chain.

菩提之心 — the formula on the bodhisattva's side shifts within this part. The first three similes say 菩提心金剛亦復如是, the vajra that is the mind of bodhi is likewise; the fourth, fifth, and sixth drop the vajra and say simply 菩提之心亦復如是, the mind of bodhi is likewise; and the seventh, whose image is a vessel rather than the stone, says 菩提心金剛器, the vajra vessel that is the mind of bodhi. The translation follows each of these exactly rather than levelling them.
