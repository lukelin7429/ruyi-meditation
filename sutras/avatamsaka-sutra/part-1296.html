Sun-Fire, Moon-Water

The paired similes continue through a water-dwelling jewel, a nāga's own pearl, Śakra's bright crown, and a wish-fulfilling pearl, before twin jewels that draw fire from the sun and water from the moon, and a treasury that fulfills every wish without ever diminishing.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1296 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Sun-Fire, Moon-Water

Eight more paired similes continue the pattern, moving from protective jewels through Śakra's own crown to a pair of matched jewels — one drawing fire from sunlight, one drawing water from moonlight — and closing on a treasury that never runs dry.

Chinese Source

「善男子！譬如有人，得住水寶，繫其身上，入大海中，不為水害。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心住水妙寶，入於一切生死海中，終不沈沒。「善男子！譬如有人，得龍寶珠，持入龍宮，一切龍蛇不能為害。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心大龍寶珠，入欲界中，煩惱龍蛇不能為害。「善男子！譬如帝釋，著摩尼冠，映蔽一切諸餘天眾。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，著菩提心大願寶冠，超過一切三界眾生。「善男子！譬如有人，得如意珠，除滅一切貧窮之苦。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心如意寶珠，遠離一切邪命怖畏。「善男子！譬如有人，得日精珠，持向日光而生於火。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心智日寶珠，持向智光而生智火。「善男子！譬如有人，得月精珠，持向月光而生於水。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心月精寶珠，持此心珠，鑒迴向光，而生一切善根願水。「善男子！譬如龍王，首戴如意摩尼寶冠，遠離一切怨敵怖畏。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，著菩提心大悲寶冠，遠離一切惡道諸難。「善男子！如有寶珠，名：一切世間莊嚴藏，若有得者，令其所欲悉得充滿，而此寶珠無所損減。菩提心寶亦復如是，若有得者，令其所願悉得滿足，而菩提心無有損減。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as, when a person obtains the water-dwelling jewel and binds it upon their body, they may enter the great ocean without being harmed by the water, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the wondrous water-dwelling jewel that is the mind of bodhi, they enter into the ocean of every birth-and-death and never sink beneath it. Good son! Just as, when a person obtains a nāga's own jeweled pearl and carries it into a nāga palace, no nāga or serpent whatsoever is able to harm them, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the great nāga's own jeweled pearl that is the mind of bodhi, they enter into the desire realm, and no nāga or serpent of affliction is able to harm them. Good son! Just as Śakra, wearing his maṇi-crown, outshines every other host of devas, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: wearing the jeweled crown of great vow that is the mind of bodhi, they surpass every sentient being of the three realms. Good son! Just as, when a person obtains a wish-fulfilling pearl, every suffering of poverty is entirely dissolved away, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the wish-fulfilling jeweled pearl that is the mind of bodhi, they leave far behind every terror of a deviant livelihood. Good son! Just as, when a person obtains a sun-essence pearl and holds it toward the sunlight, fire is produced, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the jeweled pearl of wisdom's own sun that is the mind of bodhi, they hold it toward the light of wisdom, and the fire of wisdom is produced. Good son! Just as, when a person obtains a moon-essence pearl and holds it toward the moonlight, water is produced, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the jeweled moon-essence pearl that is the mind of bodhi, they hold this mind-pearl, reflecting the light of dedication, and the water of every wholesome root and vow is produced. Good son! Just as a nāga king, wearing upon his head the wish-fulfilling maṇi-jeweled crown, leaves far behind every terror of hostile enemies, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: wearing the jeweled crown of great compassion that is the mind of bodhi, they leave far behind every hardship of every evil destiny. Good son! Just as there is a jeweled pearl named Treasury of Every World's Adornment, and whoever obtains it has their every wish entirely fulfilled, yet the pearl itself suffers no loss or diminishment, the jewel that is the mind of bodhi is likewise: whoever obtains it has their every vow entirely fulfilled, yet the mind of bodhi itself suffers no loss or diminishment.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

持向日光而生於火⋯持向月光而生於水 — a deliberately paired image: the sun-essence pearl draws fire from sunlight just as wisdom draws its own fire from the light of wisdom itself, while the moon-essence pearl draws water from moonlight just as dedication of merit draws forth the water of every wholesome root and vow.
