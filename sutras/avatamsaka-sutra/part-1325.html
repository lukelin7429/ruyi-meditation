Eleven Minds Before the Lion-Throne

With Mañjuśrī withdrawn, Good-Wealth is alone with his own resolve. He wishes to see Mañjuśrī, and to see and serve good spiritual friends as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a trichiliocosm; and from that wish the sūtra unrolls a single long sentence of aspiration, through the ocean of compassion, the quiescent gates of the dharma, the order in which the buddhas of the three times appear, every ocean of dharma and every wheel of dharma, every kalpa of practice, until he stands on the ground of equality and hears the name of Samantabhadra. Thirsting to see him, seated on the jeweled lotus-treasury seat before Vairocana's lion-throne in the site of awakening of Vajra-Treasury, he arouses eleven minds, each as vast as the realm of space.

Eleven Minds Before the Lion-Throne

Good-Wealth's aspiration after Mañjuśrī withdraws, the name of Samantabhadra, and the minds he arouses to see him.

Chinese Source

於是，善財思惟觀察，一心願見文殊師利，及見三千大千世界微塵數諸善知識，悉皆親近，恭敬承事，受行其教，無有違逆；增長趣求一切智慧，廣大悲海，益大慈雲，普觀眾生，生大歡喜，安住菩薩寂靜法門；普緣一切廣大境界，學一切佛廣大功德，入一切佛決定知見，增一切智助道之法，善修一切菩薩深心，知三世佛出興次第；入一切法海，轉一切法輪，生一切世間，入於一切菩薩願海，住一切劫修菩薩行，照明一切如來境界，長養一切菩薩諸根；獲一切智清淨光明，普照十方，除諸暗障，智周法界；於一切佛剎、一切諸有，普現其身，靡不周遍；摧一切障，入無礙法，住於法界平等之地；觀察普賢解脫境界，即聞普賢菩薩摩訶薩名字、行願、助道、正道、諸地、地方便、地入、地勝進、地住、地修習、地境界、地威力、地同住。渴仰欲見普賢菩薩，即於此金剛藏菩提場，毘盧遮那如來師子座前，一切寶蓮華藏座上，起等虛空界廣大心、捨一切剎離一切著無礙心、普行一切無礙法無礙心、遍入一切十方海無礙心、普入一切智境界清淨心、觀道場莊嚴明了心、入一切佛法海廣大心、化一切眾生界周遍心、淨一切國土無量心、住一切劫無盡心、趣如來十力究竟心。

Ru-Yi Translation

Thereupon Good-Wealth reflected and observed, wishing single-mindedly to see Mañjuśrī, and to see good spiritual friends as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a great trichiliocosm, to draw near to every one of them, to reverently serve them, and to receive and practice their teaching without ever going against it; to increase his pursuit of all wisdom, to broaden the ocean of great compassion, to swell the cloud of great loving-kindness, to observe sentient beings universally, to give rise to great joy, and to abide securely in the quiescent gates of the dharma of the bodhisattvas; to take every vast domain as his object, to learn the vast merits of every buddha, to enter the definitive knowledge and vision of every buddha, to increase the dharmas that aid the path to all-knowing wisdom, to cultivate well the profound mind of every bodhisattva, and to know the order in which the buddhas of the three times appear in the world; to enter every ocean of dharma, to turn every wheel of dharma, to be born in every world, to enter every ocean of the bodhisattvas' vows, to abide through every kalpa cultivating the bodhisattva practices, to illuminate every domain of the tathāgatas, and to nurture every faculty of the bodhisattvas; to obtain the pure light of all-knowing wisdom, shining universally through the ten directions, dispelling every darkness and obstruction, his wisdom pervading the dharma-realm; to manifest his body universally in every buddha-land and every state of existence, leaving none unreached; to crush every obstruction, to enter the unobstructed dharma, and to abide on the ground of equality of the dharma-realm. Observing the domain of Samantabhadra's liberation, he at once heard the name of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva-Mahāsattva, his practices and vows, his aids to the path, his right path, his grounds, and the skillful means of the grounds, the entry into the grounds, the advancement in the grounds, the abiding in the grounds, the cultivation of the grounds, the domain of the grounds, the power of the grounds, and the abiding together in the grounds. Thirsting to see Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, right there in the site of awakening of Vajra-Treasury, before the lion-throne of the Tathāgata Vairocana, upon the seat of the treasury of every jeweled lotus, he aroused a vast mind equal to the realm of space, an unobstructed mind that relinquishes every land and leaves every attachment, an unobstructed mind that universally practices every unobstructed dharma, an unobstructed mind that enters everywhere into every ocean of the ten directions, a pure mind that enters universally into the domain of all-knowing wisdom, a clear mind that observes the adornments of the site of awakening, a vast mind that enters every ocean of the buddha-dharma, an all-pervading mind that transforms the whole realm of sentient beings, a measureless mind that purifies every land, an inexhaustible mind that abides through every kalpa, and an ultimate mind that advances toward the ten powers of the Tathāgata.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

一心願見 — from the moment Mañjuśrī withdraws until Good-Wealth hears the name of Samantabhadra, the source is one sentence, and the translation keeps it as one, its clauses grouped by the Chinese semicolons into eight runs. The aspiration moves outward in stages, from the friends he wishes to serve, to the compassion and joy he wishes to increase, to the buddhas' knowledge and the order of their appearance, to every ocean and wheel of dharma, and finally to the ground of equality of the dharma-realm, from which the name of Samantabhadra is heard.

名字、行願……地同住 — what Good-Wealth hears of Samantabhadra is given in thirteen items, of which the last eight are all built on 地, the grounds: their skillful means, entry, advancement, abiding, cultivation, domain, power, and abiding together. The minds he then arouses number eleven, not the ten that such lists usually run to, and each carries its own epithet, vast, unobstructed, pure, clear, all-pervading, measureless, inexhaustible, ultimate; the translation keeps all eleven and their epithets in the source's order. 金剛藏菩提場 names the place as the site of awakening of Vajra-Treasury, and the seat is 寶蓮華藏座, the seat of the treasury of every jeweled lotus, before the lion-throne of Vairocana, the setting in which the whole chapter began.
