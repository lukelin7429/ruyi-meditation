Unrecognized in His Own City

Universal Practice reveals that his own disguise is total — moving through every clan and class of Tosala city in a form identical to whoever he is teaching, so convincingly that not one of them ever learns who he really is, before this same practice scales outward through all ninety-six heterodox groups of Jambudvīpa and beyond.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1040 — unrecognized in his own city Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

A Form No One Can Trace Back

Universal Practice's own disguise is not partial — he takes on a form identical to whoever he teaches, so completely that no being in Tosala city, however closely they listen, ever learns who he truly is or where he came from.

Chinese Source

「又，善男子！此都薩羅城中，一切方所一切族類，若男若女諸人眾中，我皆以方便示同其形，隨其所應而為說法。諸眾生等，悉不能知我是何人、從何而至，唯令聞者如實修行。善男子！如於此城利益眾生，於閻浮提城邑聚落，所有人眾住止之處，悉亦如是而為利益。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Moreover, good son, throughout this city of Tosala, in every place and among every clan, whether men or women, I always employ skillful means to manifest a form identical to their own, teaching them the dharma exactly as is fitting. None of these beings are ever able to know who I really am, or from where I have come — I only cause whoever hears me to practice truthfully according to reality. Good son, just as I benefit beings within this city, so too, in every city, town, and village of Jambudvīpa, wherever people dwell, I benefit them in just the same way.

Ninety-Six Groups, and Every World Beyond

The same anonymous teaching that fills a single city scales outward without limit — first to the ninety-six heterodox groups of Jambudvīpa, then to every continent, then to the entire trichiliocosm, and finally to every world throughout the ten directions.

Chinese Source

「善男子！閻浮提內九十六眾，各起異見而生執著，我悉於中方便調伏，令其捨離所有諸見；如閻浮提，餘四天下亦復如是；如四天下，三千大千世界亦復如是；如三千大千世界，如是十方無量世界諸眾生海，我悉於中，隨諸眾生心之所樂，以種種方便、種種法門，現種種色身，以種種言音而為說法，令得利益。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good son, within Jambudvīpa there are ninety-six different groups, each giving rise to their own differing views and attachments — I tame them all through skillful means, causing them to abandon every one of their views; and just as in Jambudvīpa, so too in the remaining continents of the four; and just as in the four continents, so too throughout the great trichiliocosm; and just as throughout the great trichiliocosm, so too throughout the ocean of beings in measureless worlds in the ten directions — in all of them, according to each being's own wish, I manifest every kind of form through every kind of skillful means and every kind of dharma-gate, teaching the dharma through every kind of speech, causing them all to gain benefit.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

閻浮提內九十六眾 — within Jambudvīpa there are ninety-six different groups. This number appears elsewhere in Buddhist literature as the traditional count of non-Buddhist schools of thought current in ancient India. Universal Practice's own claim is striking precisely because he does not exempt any of them from his teaching — every group, whatever its own view, receives the same disguised visit.
