Sixteen Things to Be

Good-Wealth closes his aspiration with sixteen roles a bodhisattva takes on for every being's sake, each paired with what it accomplishes — father and mother, helmsman, refuge, guide, wondrous flower, and, among them, a Samantabhadra in his own right.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1197 — fascicle 75 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Sixteen Things to Be

Good-Wealth's aspiration closes on a sustained run of sixteen roles, each one a plain metaphor paired with exactly what it does for beings.

Chinese Source

「聖者！菩薩摩訶薩於諸眾生，為莊嚴，令生人天富貴樂故；為父母，為其安立菩提心故；為養育，令其成就菩薩道故；為衛護，令其遠離三惡道故；為船師，令其得度生死海故；為歸依，令捨諸魔煩惱怖故；為究竟，令其永得清涼樂故；為津濟，令入一切諸佛海故；為導師，令至一切法寶洲故；為妙華，開敷諸佛功德心故；為嚴具，常放福德智慧光故；為可樂，凡有所作悉端嚴故；為可尊，遠離一切諸惡業故；為普賢，具足一切端嚴身故；為大明，常放智慧淨光明故；為大雲，常雨一切甘露法故。聖者！菩薩如是修諸行時，令一切眾生皆生愛樂、具足法樂。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Noble one, the bodhisattva-mahāsattva, for the sake of every being, is an adornment, causing them to be born to wealth and joy among gods and humans; is a father and mother, establishing within them the mind of bodhi; is a nurturer, causing them to accomplish the bodhisattva path; is a guardian, causing them to keep far from the three evil destinies; is a helmsman, causing them to cross the ocean of birth-and-death; is a refuge, causing them to abandon every fear of Māra's own afflictions; is an ultimate end, causing them to forever attain the bliss of coolness; is a ford and ferry, causing them to enter the ocean of every buddha; is a guide, causing them to reach the isle of every dharma-treasure; is a wondrous flower, opening and unfolding the mind of every buddha's merit; is an ornament, forever emitting the light of merit and wisdom; is a delight, since whatever he does is entirely upright and fine; is one to be revered, since he keeps far from every evil deed; is a Samantabhadra, fully endowed with a body entirely upright and fine; is a great light, forever emitting the pure radiance of wisdom; and is a great cloud, forever raining down every dharma of ambrosia. Noble one, when the bodhisattva cultivates every practice in just this way, he causes every being to give rise to love and delight, and to be fully endowed with the joy of the dharma."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

為普賢，具足一切端嚴身故 — is a Samantabhadra, fully endowed with a body entirely upright and fine. Counted directly, sixteen roles run through this list, and the fourteenth names the bodhisattva that gives this whole sūtra's opening vision its central figure — not a comparison to Samantabhadra, but a claim that any bodhisattva who cultivates in this way already is one, in exactly this respect.
