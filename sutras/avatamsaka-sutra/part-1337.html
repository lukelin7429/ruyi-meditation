Some See His Birth, Some See His Passing

The great gāthā continues through a series of other buddha-lands and buddhas seen by different beings: the world of Wondrous Joy where Akṣobhya abides with bodhisattvas like Fragrant Elephant; the buddha Moon-Awakening with Vajradhvaja Bodhisattva; the World-Honored Sun Treasury; the buddha of Great Vajra Flame with Wisdom Banner Bodhisattva. On a single hair-tip, ineffable buddhas expound the dharma; within a single pore, a whole adorned land; within a single mote of dust, buddha-lands as numerous as the sands of the Ganges. Then the gāthā turns to the great guide himself, Śākyamuni: some see him only now a bodhisattva, others see him already enlightened through inconceivable kalpas; some see him giving, upholding the precepts, and cultivating every pāramitā to its very end; some see him descending from Tuṣita, living in the palace, leaving the household, and at last seated beneath the bodhi tree, subduing the armies of Māra and accomplishing perfect enlightenment.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1337 — fascicle 80 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Some See His Birth, Some See His Passing

Twenty more stanzas of the great gāthā — other buddha-lands seen within a hair-tip and a mote of dust, and then the many different ways beings see the career of Śākyamuni himself, from bodhisattva to enlightenment beneath the bodhi tree.

Chinese Source

或有見此三千界，
種種莊嚴如妙喜，
阿閦如來住在中，
及如香象諸菩薩。

或見月覺大名稱，
與金剛幢菩薩等，
住如圓鏡妙莊嚴，
普遍十方清淨剎。

或見日藏世所尊，
住善光明清淨土，
及與灌頂諸菩薩，
充遍十方而說法。

或見金剛大焰佛，
而與智幢菩薩俱，
周行一切廣大剎，
說法除滅眾生翳。

一一毛端不可說，
諸佛具相三十二，
菩薩眷屬共圍遶，
種種說法度眾生。

或有觀見一毛孔，
具足莊嚴廣大剎，
無量如來悉在中，
清淨佛子皆充滿。

或有見一微塵內，
具有恒沙佛國土，
無量菩薩悉充滿，
不可說劫修諸行。

或有見一毛端處，
無量塵沙諸剎海，
種種業起各差別，
毘盧遮那轉法輪。

或見世界不清淨，
或見清淨寶所成，
如來住壽無量時，
乃至涅槃諸所現。

普遍十方諸世界，
種種示現不思議，
隨諸眾生心智業，
靡不化度令清淨。

如是無上大導師，
充滿十方諸國土，
示現種種神通力，
我說少分汝當聽。

或見釋迦成佛道，
已經不可思議劫；
或見今始為菩薩，
十方利益諸眾生。

或有見此釋師子，
供養諸佛修行道；
或見人中最勝尊，
現種種力神通事。

或見布施或持戒，
或忍或進或諸禪，
般若方便願力智，
隨眾生心皆示現。

或見究竟波羅蜜，
或見安住於諸地，
總持三昧神通智，
如是悉現無不盡。

或現修行無量劫，
住於菩薩堪忍位；
或現住於不退地，
或現法水灌其頂。

或現梵釋護世身，
或現剎利婆羅門，
種種色相所莊嚴，
猶如幻師現眾像。

或現兜率始降神，
或見宮中受嬪御，
或見棄捨諸榮樂，
出家離俗行學道。

或見始生或見滅，
或見出家學異行，
或見坐於菩提樹，
降伏魔軍成正覺。

或有見佛始涅槃，
或見起塔遍世間，
或見塔中立佛像，
以知時故如是現。

Ru-Yi Translation

Some see this trichiliocosm,
adorned in every way like Wondrous Joy,
where the Tathāgata Akṣobhya abides,
together with bodhisattvas like Fragrant Elephant.

Some see the buddha Moon-Awakening, of great renown,
together with Vajradhvaja Bodhisattva and others,
abiding in a wondrous adornment like a round mirror,
pervading every pure land of the ten directions.

Some see the World-Honored Sun Treasury,
abiding in a pure land of excellent light,
together with bodhisattvas of consecration,
pervading the ten directions to expound the dharma.

Some see the buddha of Great Vajra Flame,
together with Wisdom Banner Bodhisattva,
journeying through every vast land,
expounding the dharma to remove the cataracts of sentient beings.

Upon each and every hair-tip, ineffable buddhas,
fully endowed with the thirty-two marks,
surrounded together by their bodhisattva retinues,
expound every kind of dharma to deliver sentient beings.

Some observe and see, within a single pore,
a vast land fully adorned,
with measureless tathāgatas entirely present within it,
filled everywhere with pure children of the buddha.

Some see, within a single mote of dust,
buddha-lands as numerous as the sands of the Ganges,
filled entirely with measureless bodhisattvas,
cultivating every practice for ineffable kalpas.

Some see, at the place of a single hair-tip,
measureless oceans of lands as numerous as motes of dust and grains of sand,
each arising through its own distinct karma,
with Vairocana turning the wheel of dharma.

Some see a world impure,
some see one formed of pure jewels;
the Tathāgata abides there for a measureless span of life,
and displays even his own parinirvāṇa.

Universally throughout every world of the ten directions,
he displays every inconceivable manifestation,
according to the minds, wisdom, and karma of sentient beings,
and there is none he does not teach and transform to purity.

Such is the unsurpassed great guide,
filling every land of the ten directions,
displaying every kind of spiritual power;
I speak of but a small part of it, and you should listen.

Some see Śākyamuni having accomplished the path of buddhahood
already through inconceivable kalpas;
some see him only now as a bodhisattva,
benefiting sentient beings throughout the ten directions.

Some see this Lion of the Śākyas
making offerings to every buddha and cultivating the path;
some see him as the most supreme and honored one among men,
displaying every kind of power and spiritual deed.

Some see him giving, some see him upholding the precepts,
some patience, some diligence, some the dhyānas,
prajñā, skillful means, vows, power, and wisdom,
each displayed according to the minds of sentient beings.

Some see him bringing every pāramitā to its very end,
some see him abiding securely upon the grounds,
the dhāraṇīs, the samādhis, the spiritual powers, and the wisdom,
all of these he displays entirely, none left unexhausted.

Some see him displaying the cultivation of measureless kalpas,
abiding in the bodhisattva's own station of forbearance;
some see him abiding on the ground of non-retrogression,
some see the water of dharma poured upon his crown.

Some see him displaying the body of Brahmā, of Śakra, of a world-protector,
some see him as a kṣatriya or a brāhmaṇa,
adorned with every kind of form,
just like an illusionist manifesting every kind of image.

Some see him first descending in spirit from Tuṣita,
some see him within the palace receiving his consorts,
some see him casting aside every glory and pleasure,
leaving the household and the world to practice and study the path.

Some see his birth, some see his passing;
some see him leaving the household to practice unusual disciplines;
some see him seated beneath the bodhi tree,
subduing the armies of Māra and accomplishing perfect enlightenment.

Some see the buddha's first entering of nirvāṇa;
some see stūpas raised throughout the world;
some see images of the buddha set up within the stūpas;
and it is because he knows the right time that he displays it in this way.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

妙喜、香象、月覺、日藏、智幢 — five names in these stanzas are semantic compounds rather than transliterations, and each is rendered in the literal English this site has already used for it: 妙喜 as Wondrous Joy (the name of Akṣobhya's own world), 香象 as Fragrant Elephant, 月覺 as Moon-Awakening, 日藏 as Sun Treasury, and 智幢 as Wisdom Banner. 阿閦, by contrast, is a genuine transliteration and is restored as Akṣobhya; 金剛幢 keeps the established Vajradhvaja, since 金剛 (vajra) is kept as a loanword throughout this site rather than translated.

或見……或見 — from the buddha-lands onward, every stanza in this stretch is built on the same refrain, 或見 or 或現, some see, applied again and again to a different aspect of a buddha's career: his giving, his precepts, his pāramitās, his grounds, his bodies as Brahmā or a kṣatriya, his descent from Tuṣita, his birth, his enlightenment, his stūpas. The translation keeps every some see rather than varying the phrase, since the repetition itself is the point: the same buddha is seen entirely differently by each being who sees him.
