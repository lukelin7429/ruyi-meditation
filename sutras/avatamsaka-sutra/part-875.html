The Named Samādhis Conclude

The final twenty-six names in this list of one hundred and one samādhis close on the very samādhi this entire chapter began with — the Lion's Stretching-Forth — before the bodhisattvas who hold all of them enter directly into Vairocana's own boundless ocean of spiritual transformation.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 875 — the named samādhis conclude Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Ending Where It Began

Every name in this list has been distinct, yet the very last one is not new at all: it is the Lion's Stretching-Forth Samādhi itself, the same samādhi the Buddha entered near the start of this chapter, now closing the list of one hundred and one exactly where the whole sequence first opened.

Chinese Source

……法界開敷蓮華三昧、觀諸法如虛空無住處三昧、十方海普入一方三昧、入一切法界無源底三昧、一切法海三昧、以寂靜身放一切光明三昧、一念中現一切神通大願三昧、一切時一切處成正覺三昧、以一莊嚴入一切法界三昧、普現一切諸佛身三昧、知一切眾生廣大殊勝神通智三昧、一念中其身遍法界三昧、現一乘淨法界三昧、入普門法界示現大莊嚴三昧、住持一切佛法輪三昧、以一切法門莊嚴一法門三昧、以因陀羅網願行攝一切眾生界三昧、分別一切世界門三昧、乘蓮華自在遊步三昧、知一切眾生種種差別神通智三昧、令其身恒現一切眾生前三昧、知一切眾生差別音聲言辭海三昧、知一切眾生差別智神通三昧、大悲平等藏三昧、一切佛入如來際三昧、觀察一切如來解脫處師子頻申三昧……。菩薩以如是等不可說佛剎微塵數三昧，入毘盧遮那如來念念充滿一切法界三昧神變海。

……the Samādhi of the Dharma-Realm's Own Lotus Blossoming Open, the Samādhi of Observing Every Dharma as Being Like Empty Space, Abiding Nowhere, the Samādhi of the Ocean of the Ten Directions from a Still, Quiescent Body, the Samādhi of Manifesting Every Great Vow of Spiritual Superknowledge in a Single Thought, the Samādhi of Attaining Perfect Awakening at Every Time and in Every Place, the Samādhi of Entering the Entire Dharma-Realm Through a Single Adornment, the Samādhi of Universally Manifesting the Body of Every Buddha, the Samādhi of Knowing the Vast, Superior Wisdom of Dharma-Gateway Through Every Dharma-Gateway, the Samādhi of Gathering In the Entire Realm of Sentient Beings Through the Vow and Practice of Indra's Own Net, the Samādhi of Distinguishing the Gateway of Every World, the Samādhi of Riding a Lotus Flower and Wandering with Sovereign Freedom, the Samādhi of Knowing Every Kind of Differing Spiritual Superknowledge and Wisdom of Every Sentient Being, the Samādhi of Causing One's Own Body to Constantly Appear Before Every Sentient Being, the Samādhi of Knowing the Ocean of Every Differing Sound and Word of Every Sentient Being, the Samādhi of Through samādhis such as these — as numerous as unspeakably many motes of dust in every buddha-land — the bodhisattvas enter into Vairocana Tathāgata's own ocean of samādhi and spiritual transformation, filling the entire dharma-realm in every single thought.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

觀察一切如來解脫處師子頻申三昧 — the Samādhi of Observing Every Tathāgata's Own Place of Liberation, the Lion's Stretching-Forth. Of one hundred and one names, this final one alone reaches back to the very samādhi that opened this entire sequence, many parts ago, when the Buddha first entered it and the great pavilion began to transform — the list closing the exact circle it began.
