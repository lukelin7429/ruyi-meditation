It Goes Where It Came From

Risen from samādhi, Good-Wealth hears Maitreya name what he has attained and asks the name of the gate of liberation: it is the Adorned Treasury of the Wisdom of Unforgetting Mindfulness That Enters Every Domain of the Three Times, and within it lie ineffably ineffable further gates that only a bodhisattva of a single remaining birth can attain. Then the questions begin. Where have the adornments gone? To where they came from. Where did they come from? From the spiritual power of a bodhisattva's wisdom, with no place of going and no place of abiding, like the rain a nāga king pours from neither body nor mind, and like the works of an illusionist, which come from nowhere and go nowhere and yet are plainly seen.

It Goes Where It Came From

The name of the liberation, and the first questions after the vision — where the adornments went, where they came from, and two similes in answer.

Chinese Source

彌勒告言：「善男子！汝住菩薩不可思議自在解脫，受諸菩薩三昧喜樂，能見菩薩神力所持、助道所流、願智所現種種上妙莊嚴宮殿；見菩薩行，聞菩薩法，知菩薩德，了如來願。」善財白言：「唯然！聖者！是善知識加被憶念威神之力。聖者！此解脫門，其名何等？」彌勒告言：「善男子！此解脫門，名：入三世一切境界不忘念智莊嚴藏。善男子！此解脫門中，有不可說不可說解脫門，一生菩薩之所能得。」善財問言：「此莊嚴事，何處去耶？」彌勒答言：「於來處去。」曰：「從何處來？」曰：「從菩薩智慧神力中來，依菩薩智慧神力而住，無有去處，亦無住處，非集非常，遠離一切。善男子！如龍王降雨，不從身出，不從心出，無有積集，而非不見；但以龍王心念力故，霈然洪霔，周遍天下，如是境界不可思議。善男子！彼莊嚴事亦復如是，不住於內，亦不住外，而非不見；但由菩薩威神之力、汝善根力，見如是事。善男子！譬如幻師作諸幻事，無所從來，無所至去；雖無來去，以幻力故，分明可見。彼莊嚴事亦復如是，無所從來，亦無所去；雖無來去，然以慣習不可思議幻智力故，及由往昔大願力故，如是顯現。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Maitreya said, "Good son! You abide in the inconceivable sovereign liberation of the bodhisattvas, you receive the joy and bliss of the bodhisattvas' samādhi, and you are able to see the manifold supreme and wondrous adorned palaces that are sustained by the spiritual power of the bodhisattvas, that flow from the aids to the path, and that are manifested by the wisdom of vows; you see the bodhisattva's practices, hear the bodhisattva's dharma, know the bodhisattva's virtues, and understand the vows of the tathāgatas." Good-Wealth replied, "So it is, Noble one! This is the majestic spiritual power of the good spiritual friend's blessing and recollection. Noble one, what is the name of this gate of liberation?" Maitreya said, "Good son! This gate of liberation is named the Adorned Treasury of the Wisdom of Unforgetting Mindfulness That Enters Every Domain of the Three Times. Good son! Within this gate of liberation there are ineffably ineffable gates of liberation, which a bodhisattva of a single remaining birth is able to attain." Good-Wealth asked, "These adornments, where have they gone?" Maitreya answered, "They have gone to where they came from." He said, "Where did they come from?" He said, "They came from within the spiritual power of the bodhisattva's wisdom, and they abide in dependence on the spiritual power of the bodhisattva's wisdom; they have no place of going and no place of abiding, they are neither gathered nor permanent, and they are far removed from everything. Good son! It is like the rain a nāga king sends down, which does not come forth from his body and does not come forth from his mind, and has no store from which it is gathered, and yet is not unseen; it is only through the power of the nāga king's thought that it pours down in torrents, everywhere beneath the sky, and such a domain is inconceivable. Good son! Those adornments are likewise: they abide neither within nor without, and yet they are not unseen; it is only through the majestic spiritual power of the bodhisattva and the power of your own wholesome roots that you see such things. Good son! It is just as when an illusionist performs his works of illusion, which come from nowhere and go nowhere; though they neither come nor go, through the power of illusion they are plainly seen. Those adornments are likewise: they come from nowhere and go nowhere; though they neither come nor go, through the power of long-practiced, inconceivable, illusion-like wisdom, and through the power of great vows made in ages past, they appear in this way."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

入三世一切境界不忘念智莊嚴藏 — the name of the liberation is built from the inside out: 不忘念智, the wisdom of unforgetting mindfulness, which 入三世一切境界, enters every domain of the three times, and which is itself a 莊嚴藏, an adorned treasury. The English keeps the order of the Chinese elements as far as the grammar allows, so that the name ends, as the tower's own name does, on Adorned Treasury; the tower Good-Wealth has just left is 毘盧遮那莊嚴藏, and the liberation he has attained within it shares the last two words of that name.

於來處去 — Maitreya's first answer is four characters, and the translation keeps it as one short sentence. The exchange that follows it is the only place in the fascicle where the speakers are reduced to 曰, he said, with neither name given, and the translation likewise drops the names for those two turns. The two similes that close the answer, the nāga king's rain and the illusionist, are the same two images the sūtra has already used for the mind of bodhi and for Good-Wealth's seeing, now turned on the adornments themselves: not from within, not from without, and yet not unseen.
