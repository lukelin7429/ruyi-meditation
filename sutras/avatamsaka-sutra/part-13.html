The Great Lotus, and Ten Hymns on the Buddha's Body

This installment completes fascicle six and the chapter "The Tathāgata's Manifestation." Light from between the Buddha's brows enters through the soles of his feet; a great lotus flower unlike any other appears before him; and from within the very mark of hair between his brows, a bodhisattva named the Supreme Voice of Every Dharma emerges to sit upon its dais, with the rest of the assembly seated on its stamens. Ten bodhisattvas in turn — Supreme Voice among them — sing of the Buddha's single body pervading the whole Dharma-realm, closing the chapter without the Buddha himself ever speaking a word.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 13 — completes fascicle 6 Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Light from the Brow, and the Great Lotus

Chinese Source

爾時，世尊欲令一切菩薩大眾得於如來無邊境界神通力故，放眉間光，此光名：一切菩薩智光明，普照耀十方藏。其狀猶如寶色燈雲，遍照十方一切佛剎，其中國土及以眾生悉令顯現。又普震動諸世界網，一一塵中現無數佛。隨諸眾生性欲不同，普雨三世一切諸佛妙法輪雲，顯示如來波羅蜜海；又雨無量諸出離雲，令諸眾生永度生死；復雨諸佛大願之雲，顯示十方諸世界中。普賢菩薩道場眾會，作是事已，右遶於佛，從足下入。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the World-Honored One, wishing to cause the whole great assembly of bodhisattvas to attain the Tathāgata's own boundless power of spiritual transmutation over every domain, emitted light from between his brows. This light was named: the Wisdom-Light of Every Bodhisattva, Universally Illumining the Treasury of the Ten Directions. Its very form was like a cloud-lamp of every jeweled color, shining universally upon every buddha-land of the ten directions, causing the lands within them, and their living beings as well, to be revealed entirely. It also universally shook the net of every world-system, and within each and every mote of dust manifested countless buddhas. According to the differing natures and desires of living beings, it universally rained down clouds of the wondrous Dharma-wheel of every buddha of the three periods of time, revealing the ocean of the Tathāgata's own pāramitās; and it also rained down immeasurable clouds of going-forth, causing every living being to forever cross beyond birth-and-death; and it further rained down clouds of every buddha's great vow, revealing them within every world of the ten directions. Having accomplished this, before the assembly at Samantabhadra's own awakening-site, it circled the Buddha to the right, and entered in through the very soles of his feet.

Chinese Source

爾時，佛前有大蓮華，忽然出現。其華具有十種莊嚴，一切蓮華所不能及。所謂：眾寶間錯以為其莖；摩尼寶王以為其藏；法界眾寶普作其葉；諸香摩尼而作其鬚；閻浮檀金莊瑩其臺；妙網覆上，光色清淨；於一念中，示現無邊諸佛神變；普能發起一切音聲；摩尼寶王影現佛身；於音聲中，普能演說一切菩薩所修行願。此華生已，一念之間，於如來白毫相中，有菩薩摩訶薩，名：一切法勝音，與世界海微塵數諸菩薩眾，俱時而出，右遶如來，經無量匝，禮佛足已。

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, before the Buddha, a great lotus flower suddenly appeared. This flower was endowed with ten kinds of adornment that no other lotus flower could ever match. These were: every jewel, interspersed, formed its stem; the sovereign maṇi jewel formed its calyx; every jewel of the Dharma-realm formed its leaves entirely; every fragrant maṇi formed its pistils; Jambūnada gold adorned and graced its dais; a wondrous net covered it above, its light and color pure; within a single thought, it revealed the boundless spiritual transformation of every buddha; it was able to give rise universally to every kind of sound; the sovereign maṇi jewel reflected the Buddha's own body within it; and within its very sound it was able to universally proclaim the vow and practice cultivated by every bodhisattva. No sooner had this flower arisen than, within a single thought, from within the very mark of the Tathāgata's white curl of hair, there was a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named the Supreme Voice of Every Dharma, who, together with bodhisattvas as numerous as the fine motes of dust in a world-ocean, emerged all at once, circling the Tathāgata to the right for immeasurable circuits, and, having done so, bowed in homage at the Buddha's very feet.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The brow-light entering "through the very soles of his feet" is a small but deliberate detail: light emitted from the highest point of the body completing its circuit at the lowest closes a figure, suggesting the Buddha's own body as a single, unbroken field rather than a hierarchy of higher and lower parts. The lotus that follows is explicitly said to surpass every other lotus in existence — its stem, calyx, leaves, and fruit are each named individually, a level of specificity the sūtra rarely spends on a single object, marking this flower as the stage for what is about to happen: a bodhisattva born directly from the white ūrṇā between the Buddha's own brows, traditionally counted among the thirty-two marks of a great being.

The Supreme Voice of Every Dharma

Chinese Source

時，勝音菩薩坐蓮華臺，諸菩薩眾坐蓮華鬚，各於其上，次第而住。其一切法勝音菩薩，了深法界，生大歡喜；入佛所行，智無凝滯；入不可測佛法身海，往一切剎諸如來所；身諸毛孔悉現神通，念念普觀一切法界；十方諸佛，共與其力，令普安住一切三昧，盡未來劫，常見諸佛無邊法界功德海身，乃至一切三昧解脫、神通變化。即於眾中，承佛威神，觀察十方而說頌曰：

「佛身充滿於法界，普現一切眾生前，
隨緣赴感靡不周，而恒處此菩提座。
如來一一毛孔中，一切剎塵諸佛坐，
菩薩眾會共圍遶，演說普賢之勝行。

如來安處菩提座，一毛示現多剎海，
一一毛現悉亦然，如是普周於法界。
一一剎中悉安立，一切剎土皆周遍，
十方菩薩如雲集，莫不咸來詣道場。

一切剎土微塵數，功德光明菩薩海，
普在如來眾會中，乃至法界咸充遍。
法界微塵諸剎土，一切眾中皆出現，
如是分身智境界，普賢行中能建立。

一切諸佛眾會中，勝智菩薩僉然坐，
各各聽法生歡喜，處處修行無量劫。
已入普賢廣大願，各各出生眾佛法，
毘盧遮那法海中，修行克證如來地。

普賢菩薩所開覺，一切如來同讚喜，
已獲諸佛大神通，法界周流無不遍。
一切剎土微塵數，常現身雲悉充滿，
普為眾生放大光，各雨法雨稱其心。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, the Bodhisattva Supreme Voice sat upon the lotus's dais, and the assembly of bodhisattvas sat upon the lotus's pistils, each dwelling in order upon it. This Bodhisattva, the Supreme Voice of Every Dharma, thoroughly understood the profound Dharma-realm, giving rise to great joy; he had entered the very conduct of the Buddha, his wisdom never obstructed or delayed; he had entered the immeasurable ocean of the Buddha's own Dharma-body, going to the place of every Tathāgata in every land; every pore of his body manifested spiritual transmutation entirely, and within every single thought he universally beheld the whole Dharma-realm; every buddha of the ten directions together bestowed their power upon him, causing him to abide securely in every samādhi universally, forever beholding, to the very end of future kalpas, the boundless body of every buddha's ocean of merit throughout the Dharma-realm, down to every samādhi, liberation, and spiritual transformation. Then, amid the assembly, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, he contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Buddha's body fills the Dharma-realm entirely, manifesting universally before every living being,
responding to every condition and impulse without exception, and yet forever dwelling upon this very seat of bodhi.
Within each and every pore of the Tathāgata sit buddhas as numerous as the motes of dust in every land,
surrounded together by an assembly of bodhisattvas, expounding the excellent conduct of Samantabhadra.

The Tathāgata dwells at ease upon the seat of bodhi, and within a single hair reveals many an ocean of lands,
and every single hair likewise does the same, pervading the Dharma-realm universally in just this way.
Within each and every land he is established entirely, pervading every land and territory completely,
and bodhisattvas of the ten directions gather like clouds, all coming without exception to this very awakening-site.

The ocean of bodhisattvas of merit and light, as numerous as the fine motes of dust in every land, dwells universally within the Tathāgata's own assembly,
pervading even the whole Dharma-realm entirely.
Within every land, as numerous as the motes of dust in the Dharma-realm, he appears within every single assembly —
such is the domain of wisdom of his own dividing of body, which is established within the conduct of Samantabhadra.

Within the assembly of every buddha, bodhisattvas of supreme wisdom sit together in unison,
each and every one listening to the Dharma and giving rise to joy, practicing in every place across immeasurable kalpas.
Having already entered Samantabhadra's own vast vow, each and every one brings forth every buddha-dharma,
and within Vairocana's own ocean of the Dharma, practices and thereby attains the Tathāgata's own ground.

What Samantabhadra Bodhisattva has awakened to, every Tathāgata together praises with delight;
he has already attained the great spiritual transmutation of every buddha, circulating throughout the Dharma-realm, reaching everywhere without exception.
As numerous as the fine motes of dust in every land, he forever manifests clouds of his own body, filling everything entirely,
universally emitting great light for the sake of living beings, each one raining the rain of the Dharma suited to their own mind."

Nine More Hymns on the Buddha's Body

Chinese Source

爾時，眾中復有菩薩摩訶薩，名：觀察一切勝法蓮華光慧王，承佛威神，觀察十方而說頌曰：

「如來甚深智，普入於法界，
能隨三世轉，與世為明導。
諸佛同法身，無依無差別，
隨諸眾生意，令見佛色形。

具足一切智，遍知一切法，
一切國土中，一切無不現。
佛身及光明，色相不思議，
眾生信樂者，隨應悉令見。

於一佛身上，化為無量佛，
雷音遍眾剎，演法深如海。
一一毛孔中，光網遍十方，
演佛妙音聲，調彼難調者。

如來光明中，常出深妙音，
讚佛功德海，及菩薩所行。
佛轉正法輪，無量無有邊，
所說法無等，淺智不能測。

一切世界中，現身成正覺，
各各起神變，法界悉充滿。
如來一一身，現佛等眾生，
一切微塵剎，普現神通力。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, amid the assembly there was further a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named Observing Every Supreme Dharma, the King of Lotus-Light's Wisdom, who, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Tathāgata's profound wisdom universally enters the Dharma-realm, able to turn according to the three periods of time,
becoming a bright guide for the world. Every buddha shares the same Dharma-body, relying on nothing, without any distinction,
and, according to the intent of every living being, causes them to see the Buddha's own bodily form.

Fully endowed with omniscience, understanding every dharma entirely, within every land, there is nothing he fails to manifest.
The Buddha's body and his light, their forms and marks inconceivable, whoever among living beings has faith and delight in it,
he causes them all to see it according to what is fitting.

From upon a single buddha's body, he transforms into immeasurable buddhas, his thunder-voice pervading every land,
expounding the Dharma as deep as the ocean. Within each and every pore, a net of light pervades the ten directions,
proclaiming the Buddha's wondrous voice, taming those hard to tame.

Within the Tathāgata's own light, he forever sends forth a profound, wondrous voice, praising the ocean of the Buddha's merit and the conduct of the bodhisattvas.
The Buddha turns the wheel of the true Dharma, immeasurable and without any bound,
and the Dharma he teaches has no equal, unfathomable by shallow wisdom.

Within every world, he manifests his body to accomplish true awakening, each and every one rousing spiritual transformation, filling the Dharma-realm entirely.
Within each and every body of the Tathāgata, he manifests buddhas equal to living beings themselves,
and within every land as numerous as motes of dust, he universally manifests the power of spiritual transmutation."

Chinese Source

爾時，眾中復有菩薩摩訶薩，名：法喜慧光明，承佛威神，觀察十方而說頌曰：

「佛身常顯現，法界悉充滿，
恒演廣大音，普震十方國。
如來普現身，遍入於世間，
隨眾生樂欲，顯示神通力。

佛隨眾生心，普現於其前，
眾生所見者，皆是佛神力。
光明無有邊，說法亦無量，
佛子隨其智，能入能觀察。

佛身無有生，而能示出生，
法性如虛空，諸佛於中住。
無住亦無去，處處皆見佛，
光明靡不周，名稱悉遠聞。

無體無住處，亦無生可得，
無相亦無形，所現皆如影。
佛隨眾生心，為興大法雲，
種種方便門，示悟而調伏。

一切世界中，見佛坐道場，
大眾所圍遶，照耀十方國。
一切諸佛身，皆有無盡相，
示現雖無量，色相終不盡。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, amid the assembly there was further a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named Dharma-Joy's Wisdom-Light, who, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Buddha's body constantly reveals itself, filling the Dharma-realm entirely, forever proclaiming a vast voice,
shaking every land of the ten directions universally. The Tathāgata manifests his body universally, entering into the world entirely,
and, according to what living beings delight in and desire, reveals his power of spiritual transmutation.

The Buddha, according to the minds of living beings, manifests universally before them, and whatever living beings behold is entirely the Buddha's own spiritual power.
His light is without any bound, and his teaching of the Dharma is likewise immeasurable,
and the Buddha's children, according to their own wisdom, are able to enter and to contemplate it.

The Buddha's body has no birth at all, and yet is able to reveal itself as being born; the nature of the Dharma is like empty space, and every buddha abides within it.
Without abiding and without going anywhere, he is beheld in every single place, his light reaching everywhere without exception, his renown heard everywhere, far and wide.

Without any fixed substance, without any place of abode, and without any birth that could ever be found, without mark and without form, whatever is manifest is entirely like a reflection.
The Buddha, according to the minds of living beings, raises up the great cloud of the Dharma for their sake,
revealing awakening and taming them through every kind of skillful gateway.

Within every world, he is beheld seated at the awakening-site, surrounded by the great assembly, shining forth upon every land of the ten directions.
Every buddha's body possesses every inexhaustible mark, and though his manifestations are immeasurable, his forms and marks are never exhausted at all."

Chinese Source

爾時，眾中復有菩薩摩訶薩，名：香焰光普明慧，承佛威神，觀察十方而說頌曰：

「此會諸菩薩，入佛難思地，
一一皆能見，一切佛神力。
智身能遍入，一切剎微塵，
見身在彼中，普見於諸佛。

如影現眾剎，一切如來所，
於彼一切中，悉現神通事。
普賢諸行願，修治已明潔，
能於一切剎，普見佛神變。

身住一切處，一切皆平等，
智能如是行，入佛之境界。
已證如來智，等照於法界，
普入佛毛孔，一切諸剎海。

一切佛國土，皆現神通力，
示現種種身，及種種名號。
能於一念頃，普現諸神變，
道場成正覺，及轉妙法輪。

一切廣大剎，億劫不思議，
菩薩三昧中，一念皆能現。
一切諸佛土，一一諸菩薩，
普入於佛身，無邊亦無盡。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, amid the assembly there was further a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named the Fragrant Flame's Light, Universally Bright Wisdom, who, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"Every bodhisattva of this assembly has entered the Buddha's own ground, hard to conceive, and each and every one is able to see the spiritual power of every buddha entirely.
Their body of wisdom is able to enter universally into every mote of dust in every land,
and, seeing their own body dwelling within it, they behold every buddha universally.

Like a reflection appearing in every land, at the place of every Tathāgata, within every one of them entirely, they manifest the very work of spiritual transmutation.
Every conduct and vow of Samantabhadra, already cultivated and made clear and pure,
enables them, within every land, to universally behold the Buddha's own spiritual transformation.

Their body dwells in every place, and everything is equal entirely, and their wisdom, thus able to act, enters the very domain of the Buddha.
Having already realized the Tathāgata's own wisdom, illumining the Dharma-realm equally,
they universally enter the Buddha's very pores, the whole ocean of every land.

Within every buddha-land, they manifest the power of spiritual transmutation entirely, revealing every kind of body, and every kind of name.
Able, within the space of a single thought, to manifest every spiritual transformation universally —
accomplishing true awakening at the awakening-site, and turning the wondrous Dharma-wheel.

Every vast land, across a hundred million inconceivable kalpas, the bodhisattva, within his very samādhi, is able to manifest entirely within a single thought.
Within every buddha-land, each and every bodhisattva universally enters into the Buddha's own body, boundless and inexhaustible."

Chinese Source

爾時，眾中復有菩薩摩訶薩，名：師子奮迅慧光明，承佛威神，遍觀十方而說頌曰：

「毘盧遮那佛，能轉正法輪，
法界諸國土，如雲悉周遍。
十方中所有，諸大世界海，
佛神通願力，處處轉法輪。

一切諸剎土，廣大眾會中，
名號各不同，隨應演妙法。
如來大威力，普賢願所成，
一切國土中，妙音無不至。

佛身等剎塵，普雨於法雨，
無生無差別，現一切世間。
無數諸億劫，一切塵剎中，
往昔所行事，妙音咸具演。

十方塵國土，光網悉周遍，
光中悉有佛，普化諸群生。
佛身無差別，充滿於法界，
能令見色身，隨機善調伏。

三世一切剎，所有眾導師，
種種名號殊，為說皆令見。
過未及現在，一切諸如來，
所轉妙法輪，此會皆得聞。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, amid the assembly there was further a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named the Lion's Vigorous Wisdom-Light, who, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, beheld the ten directions everywhere, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Buddha Vairocana is able to turn the wheel of the true Dharma, and, throughout every land of the Dharma-realm, pervades it entirely like a cloud.
Within all the great world-oceans that exist throughout the ten directions,
through the Buddha's power of spiritual transmutation and vow, he turns the Dharma-wheel in every single place.

Within the vast assembly of every land, his names are each distinct, and, according to what is fitting, he expounds the wondrous Dharma.
The Tathāgata's great, majestic power is accomplished through Samantabhadra's own vow,
and within every land there is nowhere his wondrous voice fails to reach.

The Buddha's body, equal to the motes of dust in every land, universally rains down the rain of the Dharma, without birth, without distinction, manifesting throughout every world.
Across countless hundreds of millions of kalpas, within every land as numerous as motes of dust,
the deeds practiced in ages past, his wondrous voice fully proclaims them all.

Throughout every land of the ten directions, as numerous as motes of dust, a net of light pervades everything entirely, and within that light there is a buddha in every one, universally transforming every multitude of living beings.
The Buddha's body is without any distinction, filling the Dharma-realm entirely,
and is able to cause his own form-body to be seen, skillfully taming beings according to their own capacity.

Every guide that exists in every land of the three periods of time, their every kind of name distinct, teaches the Dharma for their sake and causes it all to be seen.
Past, future, and present — every Tathāgata, the wondrous Dharma-wheel that they turn, this very assembly is able to hear it all."

Chinese Source

爾時，眾中復有菩薩摩訶薩，名：法海慧功德藏，承佛威神，觀察十方而說頌曰：

「此會諸佛子，善修眾智慧，
斯人已能入，如是方便門。
一一國土中，普演廣大音，
說佛所行處，周聞十方剎。

一一心念中，普觀一切法，
安住真如地，了達諸法海。
一一佛身中，億劫不思議，
修習波羅蜜，及嚴淨國土。

一一微塵中，能證一切法，
如是無所礙，周行十方國。
一一佛剎中，往詣悉無餘，
見佛神通力，入佛所行處。

諸佛廣大音，法界靡不聞，
菩薩能了知，善入音聲海。
劫海演妙音，其音等無別，
智周三世者，入彼音聲地。

眾生所有音，及佛自在聲，
獲得音聲智，一切皆能了。
從地而得地，住於力地中，
億劫勤修行，所獲法如是。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, amid the assembly there was further a bodhisattva-mahāsattva named the Ocean of Dharma's Wisdom, Treasury of Merit, who, sustained by the Buddha's majestic spirit, contemplated the ten directions, and spoke a verse, saying:

"The Buddha's children in this assembly have skillfully cultivated every wisdom,
and these very people have already been able to enter such a skillful gateway.
Within each and every land, they universally proclaim a vast voice,
teaching the very place of the Buddha's own conduct, heard throughout every land of the ten directions.

Within each and every thought of mind, they universally contemplate every dharma,
abiding securely in the ground of suchness, thoroughly understanding the whole ocean of every dharma.
Within each and every buddha's body, across a hundred million inconceivable kalpas,
they cultivate the pāramitās, and adorn and purify every land.

Within each and every mote of dust, they are able to realize every dharma,
and thus, without any obstruction, they travel throughout every land of the ten directions.
To each and every buddha-land, they go entirely without exception,
beholding the Buddha's own power of spiritual transmutation, entering the very place of the Buddha's own conduct.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Ten bodhisattvas have now sung in a row — Supreme Voice of Every Dharma, born from the Buddha's own brow-mark, and nine more who simply "further" appear in the assembly with no stated origin — and not one of them has been answered by the Buddha in speech. The chapter's entire content has been light, a lotus, an emergent bodhisattva, and verse upon verse converging on a single claim from every possible angle: that the Buddha's body is not confined to any one place, that it appears differently to different beings without ever being divided, and that this is somehow compatible with him "forever dwelling upon this very seat of bodhi" without moving at all. Chapter three, opening the next fascicle, finally gives this question its formal answer — not through further description, but through Samantabhadra himself entering samādhi.
