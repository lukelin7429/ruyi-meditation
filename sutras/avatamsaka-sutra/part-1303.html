Into the Mouth of the Makara

The paired similes turn to safety and protection: one who enters the great ocean and is not swallowed even in a makara's mouth, one who drinks ambrosia and cannot be harmed, añjana salve that hides its wearer from every māra, the security of relying on a king or a fierce general, water that fears no fire, Śakra's vajra club against the asuras, and a medicine that keeps a person from growing old or thin.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 1303 — fascicle 78 continues Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Into the Mouth of the Makara

Eight more paired similes continue the pattern — every one of them an image of what cannot be harmed, seen, burned, frightened, subdued, or worn down.

Chinese Source

「善男子！譬如有人，善入大海，一切水族無能為害；假使入於摩竭魚口，亦不為彼之所吞噬。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，發菩提心入生死海，諸業煩惱不能為害；假使入於聲聞、緣覺實際法中，亦不為其之所留難。「善男子！譬如有人，飲甘露漿，一切諸物不能為害。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，飲菩提心甘露法漿，不墮聲聞、辟支佛地，以具廣大悲願力故。「善男子！譬如有人，得安繕那藥以塗其目，雖行人間，人所不見。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，得菩提心安繕那藥，能以方便入魔境界，一切眾魔所不能見。「善男子！譬如有人，依附於王，不畏餘人。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，依菩提心大勢力王，不畏障、蓋、惡道之難。「善男子！譬如有人，住於水中，不畏火焚。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，住菩提心善根水中，不畏二乘解脫智火。「善男子！譬如有人，依倚猛將，即不怖畏一切怨敵。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，依菩提心勇猛大將，不畏一切惡行怨敵。「善男子！如釋天王，執金剛杵，摧伏一切阿脩羅眾。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，持菩提心金剛之杵，摧伏一切諸魔外道。「善男子！譬如有人，服延齡藥，長得充健，不老不瘦。菩薩摩訶薩亦復如是，服菩提心延齡之藥，於無數劫修菩薩行，心無疲厭亦無染著。

Ru-Yi Translation

Good son! Just as, when a person is skilled at entering the great ocean, no water-dwelling creature is able to harm them, and even should they enter the mouth of a makara, they are not swallowed by it, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having aroused the mind of bodhi and entered the ocean of birth-and-death, no karma or affliction is able to harm them, and even should they enter the dharmas of the true limit of the śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas, they are not detained or hindered by these. Good son! Just as, when a person drinks a draught of ambrosia, nothing whatsoever is able to harm them, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: drinking the ambrosial draught of dharma that is the mind of bodhi, they do not fall to the stage of the śrāvakas or pratyekabuddhas, because they possess the power of vast and great compassionate vows. Good son! Just as a person who has obtained añjana salve and anointed their eyes with it walks among people unseen by them, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: having obtained the añjana salve that is the mind of bodhi, they are able through skillful means to enter the domain of Māra without any of the hosts of māras being able to see them. Good son! Just as a person who relies upon a king has no fear of anyone else, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: relying upon the king of great power that is the mind of bodhi, they have no fear of the perils of obstructions, hindrances, or the evil destinies. Good son! Just as a person who stays within water has no fear of being burned by fire, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: staying within the water of wholesome roots that is the mind of bodhi, they have no fear of the fire of the liberation-wisdom of the two vehicles. Good son! Just as a person who relies upon a fierce general is not afraid of any enemy at all, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: relying upon the courageous great general that is the mind of bodhi, they do not fear any enemy of evil conduct. Good son! Just as the deva-king Śakra, wielding the vajra club, crushes and subdues every host of asuras, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: holding the vajra club that is the mind of bodhi, they crush and subdue every māra and every follower of non-Buddhist paths. Good son! Just as a person who takes a life-prolonging medicine remains long in vigor and health, growing neither old nor thin, a bodhisattva-mahāsattva is likewise: taking the life-prolonging medicine that is the mind of bodhi, they cultivate the bodhisattva's practices for countless kalpas with a mind free of weariness and free of defiled attachment.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

摩竭魚 and 安繕那 — both names are straightforward transliterations and are restored here: 摩竭 renders makara, the great sea-creature of Indian lore whose mouth swallows whatever enters the deep, and 安繕那 renders añjana, a dark salve applied to the eyes. The añjana simile is a companion to the 翳形藥, the invisibility medicine, that appeared earlier in this same fascicle's chain of similes; there the medicine hid the bodhisattva from every māra in general, while here the salve is what lets the bodhisattva walk into Māra's own domain unseen.

實際 — in the first simile the bodhisattva is said to enter 聲聞、緣覺實際法, the dharmas of the true limit belonging to the śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas, without being detained there. The passage treats the two vehicles' own final attainment as a place the bodhisattva can pass through rather than a place that holds them, which is the point of the makara's mouth: entered, but not swallowed.
