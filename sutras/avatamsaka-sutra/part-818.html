The Three Vehicles Established

The verse moves through moral discipline, the three gates of liberation, and the three vehicles all established through a single thought, before pausing on the bodhisattva's own honest position: the divine eye and recollection of past lives extinguish every delusion, and he knows the buddha's own ten powers — and yet has not yet accomplished them himself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 818 — the three vehicles established Source: Taishō T279 (Śikṣānanda, Tang, 695–699 CE)

Knowing, Yet Not Yet Accomplished

Amid a run of confident attainments, one line names a limit still standing: knowing the buddha's own ten powers is not the same as having accomplished them. The lines that follow describe a mind that understands emptiness while still seeking the wondrous dharma, free of affliction while not yet having exhausted every outflow — attainment and its incompleteness held together without embarrassment.

Chinese Source

能於一念中，普現三世心，
欲色無色界，一切種種事。

隨順三律儀，演說三解脫，
建立三乘道，成就一切智。

了達處非處，諸業及諸根，
界解與禪定，一切至處道。

Ru-Yi Translation

"Within a single thought, he is able to universally manifest the mind of the three times, and every kind of affair throughout the realms of desire, form, and formlessness.

According with the three kinds of moral discipline, expounding the three gates of liberation, establishing the path of the three vehicles, he accomplishes all-knowing wisdom.

Fully understanding what is and is not the case, every karma and every faculty, every element and understanding, and meditative absorption, and every path that reaches every destination.

Chinese Source

宿命念天眼，滅除一切惑，
知佛十種力，而未能成就。

了達諸法空，而常求妙法，
不與煩惱合，而亦不盡漏。

廣知出離道，而以度眾生，
於此得無畏，不捨修諸行。

Ru-Yi Translation

The recollection of past lives and the divine eye extinguish every delusion; knowing the buddha's own ten powers, and yet not yet having accomplished them himself.

Fully understanding the emptiness of every dharma, and yet constantly seeking the wondrous dharma; not joined together with affliction, and yet also not having exhausted every outflow.

Broadly knowing the path of renunciation, and yet using it to deliver sentient beings; within this attaining fearlessness, never abandoning the cultivation of every practice.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

知佛十種力，而未能成就 — knowing the buddha's own ten powers, and yet not yet having accomplished them himself. Set among lines celebrating what has already been mastered, this admission does not read as failure; paired with the next line's emptiness still paired with a continued search for the wondrous dharma, it describes a stage where understanding has outpaced attainment, and the practice continues precisely because of that gap.
