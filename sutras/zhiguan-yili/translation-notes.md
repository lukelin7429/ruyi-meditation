# Guiding Principles of Calming and Contemplation — Translation Notes

These notes record the main translation decisions for the Ru-Yi English translation of the *Zhiguan yili* (止觀義例, Taishō T46, no. 1913 (Zhanran)), so that its terms stay consistent across the whole text and with this site's other Tiantai translations.

## The text

- **Base text:** Taishō T1913, via the CBETA digital edition (new punctuation, 2026 release). Zhanran died in 782; the text is in the public domain.
- **Other English translations:** we have not used any published translation; this translation is made directly from the Chinese.
- **Coverage check:** the Chinese text on each page was compared character by character with the CBETA source to make sure nothing was skipped.

## Terms

| Chinese | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 例 | principle | Each of the seven sections. |
| 十乘 | the ten modes | As in this site's Mohe Zhiguan. |
| 圓頓 | perfect-and-sudden | The kind of calming-and-contemplation the work teaches. |
| 喻曰 | Clarification | Zhanran's replies in the seventh principle. |
