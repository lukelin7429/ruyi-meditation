Wearied of Birth-and-Death, Yet Not Departing

A new sequence of deliberate tensions held together without contradiction: one wearied of birth-and-death who does not realize a śrāvaka's own departure from it; one who knows every dharma as unborn yet raises no view of non-arising; one who contemplates impurity without becoming attached to craving or aversion; one who cultivates great loving-kindness not merely to extinguish anger.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 285 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Held Without Contradiction

Chinese Source

「……超出一切魔境界者；不染一切世間境界者；究竟一切菩薩法門者；隨順一切佛法門者；厭一切生死，而不證聲聞，離生法者；知一切法無生，而亦不起不生見者；觀不淨法，不證離欲法，不染愛者；修習大慈，不為除滅瞋恚法者；觀於緣起，一切法中，無愚癡者；住於四禪，不隨生者；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… of one who transcends every māra's own domain; of one unstained by every worldly domain; of one who consummates every bodhisattva Dharma-gate; of one who accords with every buddha-dharma-gate; of one who is wearied of birth-and-death, yet does not realize the śrāvaka's own dharma of transcending birth; of one who knows every dharma as unborn, yet does not raise even the view of non-arising; of one who contemplates the dharma of impurity, yet does not realize the dharma of detachment, nor become stained by craving; of one who cultivates great loving-kindness, yet does not use it merely to extinguish anger and hatred; of one who contemplates dependent origination, free of ignorance within every dharma; of one who abides in the four dhyānas, yet does not follow their own rebirth … [the reflection continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

This stretch introduces a distinct rhetorical pattern the reflection will sustain for some time: not a single quality named outright, but two things ordinarily bound together — weariness with saṃsāra and śrāvaka departure from it, non-arising and the view of non-arising, loving-kindness and the mere suppression of anger — deliberately held apart. Fazang reads this pattern as the bodhisattva's own signature move throughout this collection: taking the śrāvaka's own correct insight as far as it goes, then refusing the śrāvaka's own further step of exiting the world entirely.
