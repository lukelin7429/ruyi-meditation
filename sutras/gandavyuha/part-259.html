Crossing the Mountain-Torrent of Delusion

What the good friend removes is now named in vivid, concrete images: māra's own hooks and bait severed, the thorns of suffering pulled out, the mountain-torrent of delusion crossed, and the pure, correct path of awakening shown and guided to.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 259 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Māra's Hooks Severed, the Mountain-Torrent Crossed

Chinese Source

「……遠不善法，離惡知識，滅無明闇，諸邪見縛，超出生死一切世間；斷魔鉤餌，拔苦惱刺，出無智險難，邪惑山㵎，越度有流，諸惡邪徑；示導清淨菩提正道，教菩薩法，修習四道明淨慧眼；安立薩婆若，增長菩提心，廣大慈悲；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… far from unwholesome dharmas, separated from evil companions, extinguishing the darkness of ignorance and every bond of wrong view, transcending the entire worldly state of birth and death; severing every hook and bait set by māra, pulling out the thorns of suffering and affliction, escaping the perilous hardship of ignorance and the mountain-torrent of delusion, crossing beyond the flowing stream of existence and every evil, deviant path; showing and guiding one to the pure, correct path of awakening, teaching the bodhisattva's own Dharma, cultivating the bright, pure wisdom-eye of the four paths; establishing one in omniscience, increasing the mind of awakening, and vast loving-kindness and compassion … [the teaching continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

"The mountain-torrent of delusion" (邪惑山㵎) uses the same rare character, 㵎 ("mountain stream" or "torrent"), that named Maitreya's own country, Sea-Torrent — a deliberate echo linking the geography Sudhana is about to travel through with the very obstacles the good friend's own teaching removes. Fazang reads the sequence — darkness dispelled, hooks severed, thorns pulled, a torrent crossed, a path shown — as moving from removing obstruction to actively guiding, the two halves of what a good friend does for anyone under their care.
