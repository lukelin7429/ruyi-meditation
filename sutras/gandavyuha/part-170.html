A Mind That Does Not Rely on Beauty

The mother's verse closes on the girl's own inner life — a stainless mind free of flattery, a voice that turns hearers away from evil karma, and reverence for good and wise friends. Its final line names what has been implicit throughout: for all her sublime, lotus-born form, this girl's own heart relies on none of it, seeking only the unsurpassed path. The whole party then sets out together for the bodhimaṇḍa.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 170 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十三 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Five Stanzas: A Stainless Mind, Seeking Only the Unsurpassed Path

Chinese Source

「『其身淨無垢，　譬如真金像，
若有覩見者，　離害具慈心。

口出微妙音，　無不樂聞者，
若有聽斯音，　遠離諸惡業。

心淨無瑕穢，　質直無諂曲，
隨其所聞法，　如說能修行。

恭敬善知識，　及所尊重者，
遠離貪欲心，　專求於正法。

此女心不恃，　妙色蓮花生，
世間諸榮樂，　唯求無上道。』」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Her body is pure and stainless, like an image of true gold; whoever beholds it departs from harm and becomes endowed with a mind of loving-kindness.

From her mouth issues a sublime, subtle voice, and there is none who does not delight in hearing it; whoever listens to this voice departs far from every evil karma.

Her mind is pure, without flaw or blemish, her nature upright, free of flattery or crookedness; whatever Dharma she hears, she is able to practice exactly as taught.

Reverencing good and wise friends, and those worthy of honor, she departs far from a mind of greed and desire, single-mindedly seeking the true Dharma.

This girl's own mind does not rely upon her sublime form, born from the lotus flower; amid every worldly glory and pleasure, she seeks only the unsurpassed path."

Reading note — after Fazang

The mother's own closing stanza names, at last, the point her entire twenty-four-stanza portrait and this shorter ten-stanza coda have both been circling: her daughter's mind "does not rely upon her own sublime form" at all. Every physical marvel catalogued across both verses — lotus birth, healing touch, sandalwood fragrance — is, by this final line, revealed as beside the point. What actually defines this girl, in her own mother's own words, is a heart that "seeks only the unsurpassed path," regardless of what beauty or fortune surrounds it.
