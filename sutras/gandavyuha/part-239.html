Pure Wisdom-Light

Without leaving the city, Sudhana goes from Firm Liberation to the elder Sublime Moon, whose house is always filled with light. Sublime Moon names his liberation, Pure Wisdom-Light, and refers Sudhana onward once more — south, to a city named Arising, where an elder named Unsurpassed Army awaits.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 239 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

An Elder Whose House Is Always Filled with Light

Chinese Source

「時，善財童子禮堅固足，遶無數匝，辭退而行。向妙月所，禮足圍遶，合掌恭敬，於一面立，白言：「聖者！我已先發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，而未知菩薩云何學菩薩行、修菩薩道？我聞聖者善能誘誨，願為我說。」妙月答言：「善男子！我得菩薩解脫，名淨智光明。善男子！我唯知此智光解脫，如諸菩薩摩訶薩證得無量解脫法門，而我云何能知、能說彼功德行？善男子！於此南方，有城名出生，彼有長者，名無勝軍。汝詣彼問云何菩薩學菩薩行、修菩薩道。」」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Sudhana bowed at Firm Liberation's own feet, circled him countless times, took his leave, and continued on his way. He went to Sublime Moon's own place; he bowed at his feet, circled him, and stood to one side with palms joined in reverence, saying: "Noble one, I have already raised the mind set on unsurpassed, perfect awakening, but I do not yet know how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva's own practice and cultivates the bodhisattva's own path. I have heard that you, noble one, are skilled in guiding and instructing — I ask that you teach me." Sublime Moon answered, saying: "Good man, I have attained the bodhisattva's own liberation named Pure Wisdom-Light. Good man, I know only this liberation of wisdom-light. As for how the great bodhisattvas realize boundless Dharma-gates of liberation — how could I possibly know it, or speak of it? Good man, to the south of here there is a city named Arising. There is an elder there named Unsurpassed Army. Go to him and ask how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva's own practice and cultivates the bodhisattva's own path."

Reading note — after Fazang

Sublime Moon's own episode, the briefest so far among these several elders, is told entirely within a single exchange — arrival, question, answer, and referral, without any extended description of his liberation's own content beyond its name. Fazang reads this compression, following immediately after Firm Liberation's similarly brief episode, as marking a stretch of rapid transitions between the massive Māyā sequence and whatever fuller teaching lies ahead — the pilgrimage's own pacing, not a lesser status for these teachers.
