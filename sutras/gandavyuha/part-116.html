Ten Thousand Buddhas, All Met

After the first buddha's own parinirvāṇa, the prince — now reborn again and again as a wheel-turning king and as the ruling king of one heaven after another — meets eight further buddhas across eight more rebirths, closing out the kalpa of Virtuous Light at a full ten thousand buddhas personally encountered.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 116 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Eight Rebirths, Eight Buddhas, One Kalpa Complete

Chinese Source

「佛子！我於爾時命終之後，即復於彼閻浮提中王宮，受生作轉輪王。彼法輪音聲虛空燈如來滅度之後，我於爾時，守護正法；次值法虛空妙德王佛；次為釋王，即彼道場值天藏佛；次為焰摩天王，即彼世界值大地功德山佛；復值法輪光音聲王佛；次為化樂天王，即彼世界值虛空燈智王佛；次為阿脩羅王，即彼世界值一切法雷震王佛；次為他化自在天王，即彼世界值不可壞力幢佛；次為梵王，即彼世界值法輪化普光音佛。佛子！於彼寶光世界善光劫中，一萬如來出興于世，我悉值遇。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha, at that time, after my life had ended, I was once more reborn within a royal palace in that same Jambudvīpa, and became a wheel-turning king. After that tathāgata, the Empty-Space Lamp of the Sound of the Dharma-Wheel, had entered parinirvāṇa, I, at that time, guarded and upheld the true Dharma; next I met the buddha King of Sublime Virtue in the Dharma's Empty Space; next I became Śakra, Lord of the Devas, and at that same bodhimaṇḍa met the buddha Heavenly Treasury; next I became King of the Devas of the Yāma Heaven, and in that same world met the buddha Mountain of the Great Earth's Merit; and again met the buddha King of the Light and Sound of the Dharma-Wheel; next I became King of the Devas Who Delight in Transformation, and in that same world met the buddha King of the Wisdom of the Empty-Space Lamp; next I became King of the Asuras, and in that same world met the buddha King of the Thunder-Peal of All Dharmas; next I became King of the Devas of Mastery Over Others' Transformations, and in that same world met the buddha Indestructible Banner of Power; next I became Brahmā King, and in that same world met the buddha Universal Light and Sound of the Dharma-Wheel's Own Transformation. Child of the Buddha, within that world Jeweled Light, during the kalpa of Virtuous Light, ten thousand tathāgatas arose in the world, and I met every single one of them."

Reading note — after Fazang

Fazang's own outline for this stretch of the account, already quoted at the close of the previous part, named exactly what this passage delivers: 略舉八佛，通結一萬 — "briefly citing eight buddhas, generally concluding with ten thousand." Eight buddhas are indeed named here, met across a climb through the very heavens of desire and form this collection has traced elsewhere — wheel-turning king, then Śakra, then in turn the kings of the Yāma, Nirmāṇarati, and Paranirmita-vaśavartin heavens, an asura king, and finally Brahmā himself — before the whole sequence is closed with the same round number that has framed this teacher's testimony from its opening: ten thousand buddhas, in a single kalpa, in a single world, every one of them personally met.
