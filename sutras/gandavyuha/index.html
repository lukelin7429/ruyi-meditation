Entering the Dharma-Realm.

The closing chapter of the sixty-fascicle Avataṃsaka Sūtra, and the largest single collection on this site: the youth Sudhana, sent south by Mañjuśrī, visits fifty-three kalyāṇamitra — "good and wise friends" — in turn: monks and kings, physicians and sea-captains, night-devas and a courtesan, the Buddha's own mother, the bodhisattva Maitreya within a tower vast enough to hold the cosmos, and, at the last, Samantabhadra himself. Buddhabhadra's early-5th-century translation, rendered here across 454 parts, with reading notes throughout after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733).

How to read this collection

The Gaṇḍavyūha — "Entering the Dharma-Realm," 入法界品 — is the Avataṃsaka Sūtra's own final chapter, and by itself the single longest sustained narrative in the Buddhist canon: seventeen fascicles (44 through 60) recounting one pilgrimage in full. Mañjuśrī sends the youth Sudhana south to seek a first teacher; every teacher after the first sends him further south still, in a fixed pattern Fazang's commentary names explicitly — a teaching received, a single Dharma-gate or liberation disclosed, and "looking up in reverence and being directed further" to whoever comes next. Fifty-three teachers punctuate this journey, unevenly: some hold Sudhana for a single part, others — Gopā, Māyā, and above all Maitreya — for dozens or, in Maitreya's case, over a hundred and sixty.

Each page carries a continuous run of sūtra and commentary together, matched against Ru-Yi's English translation, with reading notes quoting Fazang's Chinese in full and translating it directly, never paraphrasing without the source in view. The five great stages of the bodhisattva path this pilgrimage embodies — Ten Abodes, Ten Practices, Ten Transferences, Ten Grounds, and the stage beyond them Fazang calls 等覺, Virtual Enlightenment — are traced below, together with a complete link to every part.

What each page includes

  • The Chinese source text — sūtra and commentary together — beside our English translation, verified character-for-character against the base text for full coverage.
  • Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733) throughout, quoting his Chinese in full wherever cited.
  • Each of the fifty-three teachers' own closing transition made visible — the referral, by name and place, to whoever comes next.
  • Terminology matched to this site's other Avataṃsaka pages, most closely the Treatise on the Sūtra of the Ten Grounds.

Sudhana's Fifty-Three Teachers

Grouped by the five stages of the bodhisattva path this pilgrimage traces in sequence — a structure Fazang's own commentary names directly at several points along the way, cited in full below each stage's heading.

The Ten Abodes 十住

Parts 16–30 · Teachers 1–10

Sudhana's first ten teachers, opened by Mañjuśrī's own direction — each closes on a referral south, “looking up in reverence and being directed further” to the next.

Teacher 1 of 53
Meghaśrī
monk · Mount Union — Sudhana's first teacher: twenty-one gates of samādhi for beholding the buddhas, held out even as the monk insists he knows only this one gate among countless others.
Part 16
Teacher 2 of 53
Sāgaramegha
teacher · Sea Gate — Ten qualities of the awakening-mind, then twelve years spent gazing at nothing but the ocean — until a lotus rises from the sea floor bearing a buddha and a sūtra vast enough that all the ocean's water turned to ink could not exhaust a single chapter of it.
Parts 17–18
Teacher 3 of 53
Susthitamati
monk · Sea Shore — A mastery of walking, standing, sitting, and lying down in open space itself, resting, in his own words, on “having nothing at all and doing nothing at all.”
Part 19
Teacher 4 of 53
Megha
physician · Sovereign — The first layperson Sudhana meets: a healer who prostrates before the boy the instant he learns of his awakened mind, and whose medicine reaches every language in three thousand worlds.
Part 20
Teacher 5 of 53
Vimukta
elder · Grove-Dwelling — After twelve years' search, a samādhi revealing buddha-lands beyond counting within his own body — and the teaching beneath the vision: none of it comes to him, he goes to none of it, all of it is mind alone.
Parts 21–22
Teacher 6 of 53
Sāgaradhvaja
monk · Adorning the Peak of Jambudvīpa — Motionless in samādhi for six months, breath and perception stilled, while an entire cosmos of teaching pours from every part of his body — feet, waist, chest, mouth, crown.
Parts 23–24
Teacher 7 of 53
Relinquishment
laywoman · grove Universal Adornment, Sea Tide — A single Dharma-gate: whoever merely sees her, none of it is ever in vain — and a bodhicitta that refuses any bounded number of beings, lands, or buddhas.
Parts 25–26
Teacher 8 of 53
Supreme Voice Without Fear
sage · Sea Tide — A single touch of the hand carries Sudhana through eons at the feet of countless buddhas, his own body never moving and no day ever passing.
Part 27
Teacher 9 of 53
Skillful Life
ascetic · mountain of fire and swords — A teaching that looks, at first, like nothing but self-destruction — until Sudhana repents his doubt, leaps into the blade and flame, and finds only peace.
Parts 28–29
Teacher 10 of 53
Maitrāyaṇī
girl · city Lion's Vigor — In a hall of jewels, every pillar, mirror, bell, and necklace reflects a buddha's entire career, the way the moon appears within clear water. Closes the Ten Abodes.
Part 30

The Ten Practices 十行

Parts 31–41 · Teachers 11–20

Ten more teachers, each embodying one of the ten practices — Fazang's own commentary marks the tenth, According with Every Sentient Being, as this stage's final position.

Teacher 11 of 53
Excellent Manifestation
monk · Rescue and Deliverance — A single walking-path, never left, from which he beholds every buddha in every direction at once — opening the Ten Practices.
Part 31
Teacher 12 of 53
Lord of the Gods
boy · Purity — A science of moles, numbers, and seals, first taught to him by Mañjuśrī, capable of calculating every world, being, and buddha throughout the ten directions.
Part 32
Teacher 13 of 53
Self-Mastery
laywoman · Sea-Dwelling — Plainly robed yet more radiant than any but a buddha, she feeds every being in the ten directions from a single vessel of food that never empties.
Part 33
Teacher 14 of 53
Nectar Crown
elder · Great Flourishing — A Wish-Fulfilling Treasury of Merit that rains every needed thing down from open sky for an entire gathered multitude.
Part 34
Teacher 15 of 53
Dharma-Jewel Crest
elder · Lion's Layered Pavilion — A house ten stories tall, each story filled with a different order of bodhisattvas up to the tathāgatas themselves — the fruit of a single ancient vow.
Part 35
Teacher 16 of 53
Universal Eye, Sublime Fragrance
elder, once an incense-seller · Universal Gate — Heals every illness before teaching the Dharma, and blends an incense whose single burning pellet adorns the whole Dharma-realm.
Part 36
Teacher 17 of 53
King Fulfillment
king · Full Banner — Punishments horrific enough to make Sudhana doubt he can possibly be a bodhisattva at all — until every torment is revealed as illusion, bent only toward turning evildoers to the good.
Part 37
Teacher 18 of 53

king · Good Light — A samādhi of great loving-kindness shakes an entire city in six ways, seen as jeweled by some and defiled by others, depending only on each being's own karma.
Part 38
Teacher 19 of 53
Unmoving
laywoman · Secure Abode — Her palace light alone grants five hundred gates of samādhi at a single touch; ten thousand more follow as she recounts eons spent never once lapsing on a single mind a buddha once taught her.
Parts 39–40
Teacher 20 of 53
According with Every Sentient Being
renunciant of another path — Teaches wrong-view holders and the orthodox alike, without any of them ever knowing who he truly is. Closes the Ten Practices.

The Ten Transferences 十迴向

Parts 42–53 · Teachers 21–30

Ten teachers dedicating merit outward in ten distinct modes, closing at the very bodhimaṇḍa in Magadha where the earth-goddess Secure Abiding reveals treasuries Sudhana himself once planted.

Teacher 21 of 53
Blue Lotus Fragrance
elder · Nectar-Flavor — Thirty kinds of fragrance, each known down to its precise cause, reach, and effect — one pellet turns an entire land's rainwater to gold.
Part 42
Teacher 22 of 53
Self-Mastery the Sea-Captain
sea-captain · Layered Pavilion — Gives away his own labor to the poor, then turns the literal sea — its treasure, danger, tide, and wind — into a figure for the ocean of the Dharma.
Part 43
Teacher 23 of 53
Unsurpassed Victory
elder · Delightful — A single Dharma-gate reaching every destiny of every being, resting on nothing and creating nothing.
Part 44
Teacher 24 of 53
Lion's Vigor
bhikkhunī · Grove of Contention — A garden vast enough for the whole trichiliocosm to enter without crowding, and a samādhi that multiplies her own body to make offering at every stage of every buddha's career at once.
Parts 45–47
Teacher 25 of 53
Vasumitrā
woman — A reputation that makes strangers doubt Sudhana's motives before he even reaches her gate — and a teaching that whoever comes to her bound by desire leaves free of it.
Part 48
Teacher 26 of 53
Secure Abiding
elder — Opens the door of a sandalwood buddha-stūpa each day and beholds every buddha of the three times as never having entered nirvāṇa at all — the briefest episode of the pilgrimage so far.
Part 49
Teacher 27 of 53
Avalokiteśvara
bodhisattva · Mount Light — The Practice of Light Born of Great Compassion: a single vow to free every being from eighteen distinct fears.
Part 50
Teacher 28 of 53
True Course
bodhisattva — Arrives from a distance even devas and humans cannot fathom — a single thought-moment carrying him past worlds beyond telling.
Part 51
Teacher 29 of 53
Mahādeva
deva · Dvāravatī — Washes his own four-armed face with water from the four seas, then piles up mountains of jewels to teach a single Dharma-gate likened to both a cloud that moistens and a net that catches.
Part 52
Teacher 30 of 53
Secure Abiding, Earth-Goddess of the Bodhimaṇḍa
earth-deity · Magadha — Presses her toe into the ground to reveal treasuries of jewels — the fruit of roots of goodness Sudhana himself planted here in ages long past. Closes the Ten Transferences.
Part 53

The Ten Grounds 十地

Parts 54–187 · Teachers 31–40

Ten teachers, one per ground, opened by the night-deva Vāsantī and closed by Gopā — Fazang's own note at Part 54 names this exact span (“from Vāsantī through to Gopā, there are ten spiritual friends, revealing the practice of the Ten Grounds stage”). By far the longest of the five stages: 134 of the collection's 454 parts.

Teacher 31 of 53
Vāsantī
night-deva · Kapilavastu — Rescues sailors, lost travelers, and the poverty-stricken and sick alike, each in a form fitted exactly to their peril. Opens the Ten Grounds.
Parts 54–56
Teacher 32 of 53
Light Free of Defilement
night-deva — Ten dharmas fulfilling every one of a bodhisattva's practices, closing on a vision of Vairocana whose every pore fills the whole Dharma-realm.
Parts 57–59
Teacher 33 of 53
Joyful Eyes Observing Sentient Beings
night-deva — By far the largest episode yet: ten perfections demonstrated through countless transformation-bodies, and an eighty-nine-stanza account tracing one bodhisattva's realization through all Ten Grounds at once.
Parts 60–67
Teacher 34 of 53
Sublime Virtue, Rescuing and Protecting Sentient Beings
night-deva — A brow-light reveals every world inside every mote of dust; one hundred and ten buddhas named in sequence across thirty-eight stanzas.
Parts 68–76
Teacher 35 of 53
Tranquil Sound
night-deva — Nine minds purifying the ocean of every sentient being, and thirty-three kinds of affliction, each answered by the Dharma.
Parts 77–83
Teacher 36 of 53
Sublime Virtue, Guardian of Every City
night-deva — Eleven kinds of body at once, six ways of guiding beings — and, in a rare turn, Sudhana attains her Dharma-gate directly rather than merely witnessing it.
Parts 84–89
Teacher 37 of 53
Blossoming Tree Flower
night-deva — The largest episode to that point — fourteen parts — teaching through a light released as flowers close at sunset, and an exact remedy matched to every affliction.
Parts 90–103
Teacher 38 of 53
Vow-Valor Light, Guardian of Sentient Beings
night-deva — Twenty kinds of body beheld before a single word passes between them; eighteen parts recounting rebirth after sovereign rebirth.
Parts 104–121
Teacher 39 of 53
Sublime Virtue, Perfect and Full
forest-deva · Lumbinī — Ten kinds of a bodhisattva's own rebirth-dharma, taught to twenty thousand nayutas of devas at once.
Parts 122–137
Teacher 40 of 53
Gopā
Śākya woman · Kapilavastu — Fifty parts — the longest single episode of the Ten Grounds — closing on pure and defiled lands beheld with equal composure, and an admission that even after boundless kalpas a bodhisattva's own inner life remains beyond full understanding.
Parts 138–187

Beyond the Ten Grounds — 等覺, Virtual Enlightenment 等覺

Parts 188–414 · Teachers 41–52

Ten further teachers after Lady Māyā, then Maitreya's own vast Tower, then Mañjuśrī's return — the stage Fazang's reading note at Part 72 calls 等覺位, “the practice belonging to the stage of Virtual Enlightenment,” standing between the completed Ten Grounds and buddhahood itself.

Teacher 41 of 53
Lady Māyā
the Buddha's mother — Thirty-three parts: a Dharma-body “neither arising nor ceasing, like lightning, like a dream” — and a vision of one body present, unmultiplied, within every Jambudvīpa of the ten directions at once.
Parts 188–220
Teacher 42 of 53
Light, Lord of the Devas
devī · Trāyastriṃśa Heaven — A kalpa-by-kalpa account of buddhas served since a kalpa named Blue Lotus, attended from their very going-forth.
Parts 221–223
Teacher 43 of 53
Universal Friend
teacher of children · Kapilavastu — A single part: refers Sudhana onward at once to his own most gifted pupil.
Part 224
Teacher 44 of 53
Skilled in Every Art
boy · Kapilavastu — Each root letter he recites opens its own gate of wisdom, extending from forty-two syllable-gates to astrology, agriculture, and statecraft alike.
Parts 225–233
Teacher 45 of 53
Worthy Supreme
upāsikā · Vātana — A liberation named and unfolded before Sudhana is sent onward once more, south.
Parts 234–236
Teacher 46 of 53
Firm Liberation
elder · Fertile Field — Declares his own Dharma-vow already fulfilled the moment he attained it.
Parts 237–238
Teacher 47 of 53
Sublime Moon
elder · same city — A liberation named Pure Wisdom-Light, held within a house always filled with light.
Part 239
Teacher 48 of 53
Unsurpassed Army
elder · city of Arising — Boundless Marks: a liberation through which he has beheld boundless buddhas and attained a boundless treasury.
Parts 240–241
Teacher 49 of 53
Śibi Supreme
brahmin · village of Dharma — True Vow-Speech: through it, no bodhisattva of the three times ever once retrogresses from awakening.
Parts 242–243
Teacher 50 of 53
Born of Virtue and Possessing Virtue
a boy and a girl, taught together — The pilgrimage's first paired teacher: two voices, one liberation, a lesson in itself that the bodhisattva path is not gendered.
Parts 244–247
Teacher 51 of 53
Maitreya
bodhisattva mahāsattva · the Tower Adorned Pure Treasury — By far the largest episode in the entire collection — one hundred sixty-five parts — where a single tower, arisen from a bodhisattva's own vows, holds the entire cosmos within itself.
Parts 248–412
Teacher 52 of 53
Mañjuśrī, Second Appearance
reaching a hand across a hundred and ten yojanas — The pilgrimage's own guide returns to touch Sudhana's crown one final time, establishes him in Samantabhadra's own abiding, then withdraws his power and vanishes.
Parts 413–414

Samantabhadra's Practice 普賢行願

Parts 415–453 · Teachers 53–53

Not a sixth stage added beyond the fifth, but — in Fazang's own words at Part 72 — “the practice and vow of the One Vehicle, unobstructed and pervading all five stages at once.” The fifty-third and final teacher, whose ten vows close the entire pilgrimage.

Teacher 53 of 53
Samantabhadra
bodhisattva · within the assembly itself — The fifty-third and final teacher: ten vows distilling the entire bodhisattva path, closing on the declaration that whoever hears this teaching with joy and undoubting faith swiftly becomes equal to every tathāgata.

Translation Notes

Recurring terms, kept consistent across all 454 parts. See also this site's note on translation method.

善知識 — kalyāṇamitra, "good and wise friend"The fifty-three teachers Sudhana visits in turn — never sought as an authority to submit to, but each holding a single Dharma-gate, disclaimed as one gate among countless others the teacher has not touched.
夜神 — night-devaTen of the Ten Grounds' teachers share this class of being — goddesses who appear and teach chiefly by night, several sharing the very same bodhimaṇḍa in Magadha across dozens of consecutive parts.
善財 — Sudhana"Good Wealth" — the youth whose own name the sūtra explains at his introduction, before Mañjuśrī ever speaks to him directly.
解脫 — liberation / Dharma-gateWhat nearly every teacher discloses in place of a general teaching: one specific, named liberation, disclaimed in the same breath as partial — "I know only this."
等覺 — Virtual (or Equal) EnlightenmentThe stage Fazang's reading note at Part 72 names for the pilgrimage's teachers after the Ten Grounds and before buddhahood itself — Māyā through Maitreya's own tower.
毘盧遮那莊嚴藏 — Vairocana-vyūha-garbha, the Tower Adorned Pure TreasuryMaitreya's tower, arisen from a bodhisattva's own vows and compassion — the setting for the collection's single largest episode, 165 of its 454 parts.
菩提場 — bodhimaṇḍaThe seat, or site, of awakening — literal in Magadha where Sudhana's Ten Grounds teachers gather, and recurring throughout as the place any given teaching is said to unfold.
普賢行願 — Samantabhadra's practice and vowNot a sixth stage beyond the five the pilgrimage otherwise traces, but, in Fazang's own words, the practice "pervading all five stages at once" — the fifty-third and final teacher.
