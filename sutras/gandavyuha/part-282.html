One Buddha as Every Buddha

The paired equivalences continue: one land as every land, one dharma as every dharma, one sentient being as every sentient being, one buddha as every buddha — each held without destroying the mark of the individual — before the three times themselves are folded into a single thought.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 282 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Without Destroying a Single Mark

Chinese Source

「……以一切剎為一剎，以一剎為一切剎，而亦不壞諸剎相者；以一法為一切法，以一切法為一法，而亦不壞諸法相者；以一眾生為一切眾生，以一切眾生為一眾生，而解眾生無差別者；以一佛為一切佛，以一切佛為一佛，而解諸佛無有二者；以三世為一念，以一念為三世者；於一念中，詣一切剎者；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… of one who takes every land as a single land, and a single land as every land, yet without destroying the mark of any land; of one who takes a single dharma as every dharma, and every dharma as a single dharma, yet without destroying the mark of any dharma; of one who takes a single sentient being as every sentient being, and every sentient being as a single sentient being, yet understands sentient beings as without any difference; of one who takes a single buddha as every buddha, and every buddha as a single buddha, yet understands every buddha as without any duality; of one who takes the three times as a single thought, and a single thought as the three times; of one who, within a single thought, goes to every land … [the reflection continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

Each pairing here runs in both directions — one as all, and all as one — carefully qualified each time by "without destroying the mark," a phrase this passage repeats precisely to forestall a natural misreading: this is not a claim that individual lands, dharmas, or beings collapse into a formless unity, but that totality and particularity hold together without either canceling the other. Fazang reads the buddha-pairing's own distinct qualifier — "without any duality" rather than "without destroying marks" — as marking a subtle difference: buddhas are one not merely without erasing distinction but at a level where distinction itself does not properly apply.
