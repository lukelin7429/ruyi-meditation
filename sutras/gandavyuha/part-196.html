A Throne Where Every Ornament Speaks

Within the pavilion, Sudhana beholds a lion-throne of maṇi-jewel, adorned in every kind of way. Its golden bells give forth sublime voices, raining down fragrant flowers; its jeweled bells sing every bodhisattva's own vow; its moon-banner gives forth a buddha's own transformation-body; and its jewels reveal the tathāgata's own successive rebirths, sending forth boundless light across the ten directions.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 196 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Bells, Banners, and Jewels That Reveal a Buddha's Whole Life

Chinese Source

「爾時，善財見樓觀中有摩尼寶師子之座。眾寶莊飾；雜寶欄楯；敷眾妙衣；寶網覆上；建寶幢蓋；於金鈴中，出妙音聲，雨妙香華；諸寶鈴中出諸菩薩願行音聲；寶月幢中出佛化身；淨摩尼中顯現如來次第受生；日摩尼中放無量光，照十方剎；摩尼寶王光明幢中放一切佛，圓滿光明；明淨寶中出眾供具，一切眾生燈佛正法雲；如意寶中念念出生普賢自在，充滿法界；須彌幢中出天妙聲，讚歎如來。」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, Sudhana beheld, within the pavilion-tower, a lion-throne of maṇi-jewel. It was adorned with every kind of jewel; its balustrade of every kind of jewel; spread with every kind of sublime garment; covered over with a net of jewels; raised with jeweled banners and canopies; from its golden bells came forth sublime voices, raining down sublime fragrant flowers; from every jeweled bell came forth the voices of every bodhisattva's own vows and practice; from its jeweled moon-banner emerged a buddha's own transformation-body; within its pure maṇi-jewel appeared the tathāgata's own successive rebirths; within its sun-maṇi-jewel, boundless light was sent forth, illumining the lands of the ten directions; within the banner of light of its king of maṇi-jewels, every buddha's own perfect, full light was sent forth; from its bright, pure jewel came forth every kind of offering, the lamp of every sentient being, the cloud of the buddha's own true Dharma; within its wish-fulfilling jewel, within every single thought-moment, Samantabhadra's own sovereign mastery arose, filling the whole Dharma-realm; from its Sumeru-banner came forth the sublime voices of devas, praising the tathāgata.

Reading note — after Fazang

Every ornament of this throne is itself a small world unto itself, each one giving forth a different register of the Dharma — sound, fragrance, light, a buddha's own transformation-body, even Samantabhadra's own sovereign mastery arising moment by moment. This matches the same principle of containment-without-limit this collection has used for Māyā's own body already, back at teacher 39's account of the Lumbinī birth — here extended outward to the very furniture surrounding her, before Sudhana has even seen her seated upon it.
