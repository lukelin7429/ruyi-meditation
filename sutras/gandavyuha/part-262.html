The Great-Earth Mind

A new teaching begins: what attitude of mind to hold when approaching any good friend. The great-earth mind bears every matter without fatigue; the vajra mind stays firm and upright, indestructible; the vajra-mountain mind cannot be destroyed by any suffering; and five more follow, each with its own reason.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 262 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

From the Great-Earth Mind to the Hired-Laborer's Mind

Chinese Source

「應當如是正念思惟，詣善知識。又，善男子！詣善知識，發大地心，持一切事，無疲倦故；發金剛心，堅固正直，不可壞故；發金剛山心，一切苦患，不能壞故；發無自心，隨彼意故；發弟子心，不違一切教故；發僮僕心，一切苦役，不疲厭故；發養育心，不畏煩惱所污染故；發傭作心，隨所受教，不違逆故；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"You should reflect with right mindfulness in this way as you approach a good friend. Further, good man, in approaching a good friend, raise a great-earth mind, because it bears every matter without fatigue; raise a vajra mind, because it remains firm and upright, indestructible; raise a vajra-mountain mind, because every suffering and affliction cannot destroy it; raise a mind without a self of its own, because it follows the other's own wishes; raise a disciple's mind, because it never goes against any teaching; raise a servant-boy's mind, because it never grows weary or averse to any laborious task; raise a nurturing mind, because it does not fear being defiled by the afflictions; raise a hired-laborer's mind, because it never contradicts whatever teaching it receives … [the list continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

This teaching pivots from what the good friend does to how the seeker must hold their own mind — a shift in perspective that Fazang reads as completing the relationship the ten similes only described from one side. Of particular note is the fourth item, "a mind without a self of its own, because it follows the other's own wishes" — an unusually strong statement of self-effacement even within a list already built on service and endurance, setting the tone for the images of servant, laborer, and disciple that immediately follow.
