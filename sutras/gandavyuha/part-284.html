Without Ever Leaving a Hair's Tip

One whose own body contains every world without destroying the dharma-nature; who cultivates every vow throughout every future kalpa without grasping at any mark of length or brevity; and who, without ever leaving a single hair's-tip of space, manifests in every world, universally proclaiming the true Dharma for sentient beings.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 284 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

A Single Body Containing Every World

Chinese Source

「……自身容受一切世界，而不壞法性者；盡未來劫，修諸願行，而不取劫長短相者；不離一毛端處，而現一切世界，普為眾生說正法者；可尊重者；解甚深法者；達無二者；了無性者；善對治者；體法空者；住慈悲者；遠離一切聲聞、緣覺地者；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… of one whose own body contains every world, yet without destroying the dharma-nature; of one who, throughout every kalpa yet to come, cultivates every vow and practice, yet without grasping at any mark of a kalpa's own length or brevity; of one who, without ever leaving a single hair's-tip of space, manifests in every world, universally proclaiming the true Dharma for the sake of sentient beings; one worthy of reverence; one who understands the most profound Dharma; one who penetrates non-duality; one who fully knows the absence of any nature; one skilled in counteraction; one who embodies the emptiness of every dharma; one who abides in compassion; one far removed from every ground of the śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas … [the reflection continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

"Without ever leaving a single hair's-tip of space, manifests in every world" restates, from the opposite direction, the same principle demonstrated by containing every world within one's own body — presence without displacement, whether the scale runs from small to vast or from a fixed point outward to everywhere. Fazang reads the shorter epithets that follow — worthy of reverence, understanding profound Dharma, penetrating non-duality — as a brief pause in the passage's own rhythm, gathering simpler qualities before the list resumes its longer, more elaborate formulations.
