Revering Every Bodhisattva as the World-Honored One

Sudhana's own contemplation continues: beholding every tathāgata, purifying every buddha-land, supporting every Dharma-teacher, and upholding the true Dharma. Having formed this resolve, he reveres every bodhisattva he meets exactly as though beholding the World-Honored One himself.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 273 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

A Mind Free of Every Worldly Notion

Chinese Source

「……見諸如來，淨一切剎，供養法師，護持正法；成就菩薩諸淨願身，善知緣起，修習不可思議善根。作是念已，淨心信敬一切菩薩，如世尊想；修習諸根，心不顛倒，正念恭敬，離世間想；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… beholding every tathāgata, purifying every buddha-land, making offerings to Dharma-teachers, and upholding the true Dharma; accomplishing the bodhisattva's own every pure, vow-fulfilled body, skillfully knowing dependent origination, and cultivating inconceivable roots of goodness. Having formed this thought, with a pure mind he revered and had faith in every bodhisattva, regarding them exactly as the World-Honored One himself; cultivating his own faculties, his mind free of inversion, with right mindfulness and reverence, apart from every worldly notion … [the contemplation continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

Regarding "every bodhisattva... exactly as the World-Honored One himself" is a striking instruction for Sudhana to hold in mind precisely as he approaches the pilgrimage's most exalted teacher yet — Fazang reads this as pre-empting any temptation to treat lesser teachers as lesser: the reverence due Maitreya is, by this same logic, due every bodhisattva Sudhana has already met, from Lady Māyā down to the paired children who have just spoken.
