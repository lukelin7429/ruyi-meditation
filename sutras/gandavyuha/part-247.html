A Body Made Soft and Radiant

Having spoken of their own liberation, the boy and the girl — through the inconceivable power of their own roots of goodness — cause Sudhana's own body to become soft and radiant, and tell of their own past vow. With this, fascicle 57 of the Avataṃsaka closes, and fascicle 58 opens.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 247 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四—十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Their Own Past Vow, and a Fascicle's Own Close

Chinese Source

「時，童子、童女說自解脫已，諸善根力不思議故，令善財身柔軟、光澤，自說本願。◎」

Ru-Yi Translation

At that time, having spoken of their own liberation, the boy and the girl, because of the inconceivable power of their own roots of goodness, caused Sudhana's own body to become soft and radiant, and told of their own past vow.

Reading note — after Fazang

The text here summarizes rather than quotes their own past vow directly — a narrative compression this collection has occasionally used elsewhere, in contrast to the fuller quoted vow-narratives given by other teachers such as Māyā's own ancient-kalpa account. Fazang notes that a physical transformation of Sudhana's own body — made soft and radiant — recurs at several points across the 53 teachers as a visible sign that a good friend's own teaching has taken root in him, not merely been heard.
