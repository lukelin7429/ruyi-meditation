Beholding Every Buddha with Great Joy

This stretch of teaching closes: cultivating the pāramitās, abiding in the bodhisattva grounds, attaining profound Dharma-forbearance, bestowing every merit on others, beholding every buddha with a mind of great joy, and abiding fully in every buddha's own Dharma.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 260 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Bestowing Merit Upon Others, Abiding in Every Buddha's Own Dharma

Chinese Source

「……修波羅蜜，住菩薩地，得深法忍；淨一切善根，積集一切菩薩功德，施與一切菩薩功德；見一切佛，心大歡喜，護持淨戒，解真實義；出正法門，離諸邪道，現明法門，普照一切，聞持無量諸佛法雲；滅一切煩惱，增益一切智，住一切佛法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… cultivating the pāramitās, abiding in the bodhisattva grounds, attaining profound Dharma-forbearance; purifying every root of goodness, accumulating every bodhisattva merit, and bestowing every bodhisattva merit upon others; beholding every buddha with a mind of great joy, upholding pure precepts, and comprehending the true meaning; bringing forth the gate of the true Dharma, departing from every deviant path, manifesting the bright Dharma-gate, universally illumining all, hearing and upholding boundless clouds of every buddha's own Dharma; extinguishing every affliction, increasing every wisdom, and abiding in every buddha's own Dharma."

Reading note — after Fazang

"Accumulating every bodhisattva merit, and bestowing every bodhisattva merit upon others" names, in miniature, the dedication of merit (迴向) this collection has referenced throughout — merit is gathered not to be kept but to be given away, a movement Fazang reads as inseparable from the good friend's own function: whatever a good friend helps one accumulate is immediately structured for further giving, never treated as private possession.
