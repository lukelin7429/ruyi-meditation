Five Rebirth-Dharmas Explained

Having named all ten kinds of rebirth-dharma, the deva now explains each in turn. This part carries the first five: the vow of offering to every buddha, the branches that fulfill the bodhi-mind, contemplating tranquil cessation, universally illumining the three times with a pure mind, and universally illumining all — each closing on the same formula, being born into the tathāgata's own family.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 123 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

The Vow of Offering, the Branches of the Bodhi-Mind, and Tranquil Cessation

Chinese Source

「佛子！何等為供養一切佛，方便虛空願藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩發如是願：我當恭敬供養一切諸佛，無量喜心見佛無厭，具不壞信，積集功德，供養諸佛，心無厭足。佛子！是為初受生法，薩婆若初門長養善根故。

「佛子！何等為滿足菩提心枝藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心，起大悲心救護一切眾生故；值遇佛心常見佛故；求正法心無所惜故；大莊嚴心向薩婆若故；發大慈心，普覆攝取一切眾生故；不捨一切眾生心，薩婆若莊嚴不可壞故；離諂曲心，得實智故；如說行心，得菩薩道故；不欺一切佛心，滿足諸佛大誓願故；為薩婆若，發大願心教化未來一切眾生故；如是等佛剎微塵等菩提心枝滿足，生如來家。佛子！是為第二受生法。

「佛子！何等為現前方便觀察寂滅虛空藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩，觀察寂滅一切法海心；究竟滿足一切智道不疲倦心；正念善法業海心；一切菩薩諸三昧海清淨心；具一切菩薩諸功德心；出生一切菩薩莊嚴道心；於無量劫勇猛精進不休息心；出生普賢行化一切眾生心；善學威儀，住菩薩德，一切諸有悉非有心。佛子！是為第三受生法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of the Vow of Empty Space, the Skillful Means for Offering to Every Buddha? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva raises a vow such as this: 'I shall reverently make offerings to every buddha, beholding buddhas with a boundlessly joyful mind, never growing weary of it; endowed with unbreakable faith, I shall accumulate merit, making offerings to buddhas, my mind never satisfied nor wearied.' Child of the Buddha, this is the first rebirth-dharma, because it is the very first gate of omniscience by which roots of goodness are nurtured.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of the Branches That Fulfill the Bodhi-Mind? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva raises the mind of unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening: raising the mind of great compassion, because he rescues and protects every sentient being; the mind of meeting buddhas, because he constantly beholds buddhas; the mind of seeking the true Dharma, because he spares nothing for it; the mind of great adornment, because he turns toward omniscience; raising the mind of great loving-kindness, because he universally covers and gathers in every sentient being; the mind that does not abandon any sentient being, because the adornment of omniscience cannot be destroyed; the mind free of flattery and crookedness, because he attains true wisdom; the mind that practices as he speaks, because he attains the bodhisattva's own path; the mind that does not deceive any buddha, because he fulfills every buddha's own great vow; and, for the sake of omniscience, raising the mind of the great vow, because he teaches and transforms every sentient being of the future. Fulfilling branches of the bodhi-mind as numerous as motes of dust in a buddha-land, such as these, he is born into the tathāgata's own family. Child of the Buddha, this is the second rebirth-dharma.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of Empty Space, Directly Contemplating Tranquil Cessation Through Skillful Means? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva has the mind that contemplates the tranquil cessation of the whole ocean of dharmas; the mind, never growing weary, that reaches the very end and fully satisfies the path of omniscience; the mind rightly mindful of the whole ocean of wholesome deeds; the mind pure toward the whole ocean of every bodhisattva's own samādhis; the mind fully endowed with every bodhisattva's own merit; the mind that gives rise to every bodhisattva's own adorned path; the mind of valiant, diligent, unresting effort throughout boundless kalpas; the mind that gives rise to Samantabhadra's own conduct, transforming every sentient being; and the mind that skillfully studies dignified conduct, abides in the bodhisattva's own virtue, and knows every form of existence to be, in every case, without true existence. Child of the Buddha, this is the third rebirth-dharma."

Reading note — after Fazang

Fazang's short-form gloss for these three (T1733: 一供佛願；二覺心支者，依菩提心出生餘行名支也；三觀寂滅) reads: "first, the vow of offering to buddhas; second, the branch of the awakened mind — called a 'branch' because every further practice arises depending on the bodhi-mind; third, contemplating tranquil cessation." His remark on the second name explains a detail easy to miss in translation: the ten minds listed here are called "branches" (枝) of the bodhi-mind specifically because each one is presented as arising because of, or depending on, the bodhi-mind already raised — compassion, meeting buddhas, seeking the Dharma, and the rest are not separate roots but growth issuing from a single trunk.

A Pure, Upright Mind, and Universally Illumining All

Chinese Source

「佛子！何等為以淨直心普照三世藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩，淨直心界照佛菩提，深入菩薩方便法海，深心不壞猶若金剛，背一切有諸生死趣，向一切佛，具自在力趣諸勝道，增益菩薩根，離垢淨心，不可動轉，長養大願，常為諸佛之所護念，壞散一切諸障礙山，悉為眾生而作歸依。佛子！是為第四受生法。

「佛子！何等為普照一切藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩具足方便，教化眾生，不貪財利，以清淨心悉捨一切，持無量淨戒，住佛境界，具足忍法，得一切佛忍，光明法勇猛精進，究竟一切智境界，修習諸禪，具足清淨圓滿普門三昧智慧，以明淨慧日普照法界，得無礙眼，見一切佛海，深入一切諸法原底，智者所讚，令眾生歡喜，修習正法，見真實相。佛子！是為第五受生法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of Universally Illumining the Three Times With a Pure, Upright Mind? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva, with the realm of a pure, upright mind, illumines the buddha's own bodhi, deeply enters the whole ocean of the bodhisattva's own skillful-means dharmas, and, his profound mind unbreakable, like a vajra itself, turns his back on every destiny of birth-and-death that partakes of existence, and turns toward every buddha; endowed with sovereign power, he advances toward every supreme path, increasing the bodhisattva's own faculties, his mind pure and free of every defilement, immovable; he nurtures the great vow, is constantly held in every buddha's own mindful protection, demolishes and scatters the mountain of every obstruction, and becomes, for every sentient being, a refuge. Child of the Buddha, this is the fourth rebirth-dharma.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of Universally Illumining All? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva, fully endowed with skillful means, teaches and transforms sentient beings; not coveting wealth or profit, with a pure mind he relinquishes everything entirely; upholding boundless pure precepts, he abides in the buddha's own state; fully endowed with the dharma of patience, he attains every buddha's own patience; with the radiant dharma of valiant diligence, he reaches the very end of the state of omniscience; cultivating every dhyāna, he becomes fully endowed with the pure, perfect wisdom of the universal-gate samādhi; with the bright, pure sun of wisdom, he universally illumines the Dharma-realm, attains the unobstructed eye, and beholds the ocean of every buddha; deeply entering the very source of every dharma, praised by the wise, he causes sentient beings to rejoice, cultivates the true Dharma, and beholds the true mark of reality. Child of the Buddha, this is the fifth rebirth-dharma."

Reading note — after Fazang

Fazang's gloss continues: 四淨直心；五智普照 — "fourth, the pure, upright mind; fifth, wisdom universally illumining." The fourth rebirth-dharma is unusual among the ten for naming an image rather than only a virtue — a "profound mind unbreakable, like a vajra itself" — and for closing not on the bodhisattva's own attainment but outward, on what that attainment becomes for others: "a refuge for every sentient being." The fifth then widens from a single mind's steadiness to the full range of practice a bodhisattva is fully endowed with — precepts, patience, diligence, meditation, and wisdom together — read here as five separate clauses rather than named as the five pāramitās directly, though the parallel is unmistakable.
