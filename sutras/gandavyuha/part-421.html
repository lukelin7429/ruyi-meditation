A Refuge for Beings Without Refuge

From every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth buddhas of the three times equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filling the whole of space, becoming a refuge for beings who have no refuge; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth pure buddha-lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filled throughout with buddhas and bodhisattvas, teaching and maturing boundless sentient beings; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth pure-and-impure buddha-lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filling the whole of space, causing every defiled being there to become entirely pure; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth impure-and-pure lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, taming every impure being there; and from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth clouds of the bodies of every sentient being equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, according with the world, teaching and transforming sentient beings.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 421 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十七 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Pure Lands, Mixed Lands, and Lands Being Purified — All at Once

Chinese Source

一一毛孔念念中，出一切世界微塵等三世諸佛，充滿虛空，無依眾生為作歸依；一一毛孔念念中，出一切世界微塵等清淨佛剎，諸佛菩薩充滿其中，教化成熟無量眾生；一一毛孔念念中，出一切世界微塵等淨、不淨佛剎，充滿虛空，令染污者，皆悉清淨；一一毛孔念念中，出一切世界微塵等不淨淨剎，調伏不淨眾生；一一毛孔念念中，出一切世界微塵等一切眾生身雲，隨順世間，教化眾生；

Ru-Yi Translation

From every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth buddhas of the three times equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filling the whole of space, becoming a refuge for beings who have no refuge; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth pure buddha-lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filled throughout with buddhas and bodhisattvas, teaching and maturing boundless sentient beings; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth pure-and-impure buddha-lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, filling the whole of space, causing every defiled being there to become entirely pure; from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth impure-and-pure lands equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, taming every impure being there; and from every single pore, in every single thought-moment, there came forth clouds of the bodies of every sentient being equal in number to the motes of dust of every world, according with the world, teaching and transforming sentient beings.

Reading note — after Fazang

The careful distinction here between "pure-and-impure" lands (where defilement is being actively purified) and "impure-and-pure" lands (where impure beings are being tamed toward eventual purity) names two directions of the same transformative process — purification of place and taming of person — occurring simultaneously across countless worlds within every single thought-moment of a single pore's own activity. Fazang reads "becoming a refuge for beings who have no refuge" as this stretch's own emotional center: amid the vision's otherwise near-mechanical repetition of "equal in number to the motes of dust of every world," this single phrase names the concrete, compassionate purpose underlying the entire cosmic display — beings who have nowhere else to turn are precisely what all this boundless activity exists to address.
