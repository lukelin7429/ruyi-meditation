Ten Worlds, One Vow

Gopā's total knowledge of this Sahā world, its buddhas, and its sentient beings closes with a single further claim: the same knowledge holds across nine further categories of world throughout the ten directions. All of it, she says, rests on one source — the buddha Vairocana's own root vow.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 151 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十三 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Nine Further Worlds, and a Single Root Vow

Chinese Source

「知彼菩薩諸佛剎海，究竟法海；知眾生海，修習一切菩薩法門，滿足大願，顯現種種自在神力，如是等事，我悉了知。如此娑婆世界知十方世界世界性、世界海、世界輪、世界圓滿、世界分別、世界旋、世界轉、世界蓮華、世界須彌、世界相中事，亦復如是。盧舍那佛本願力故，我悉深入分別念知，何以故？」

Ru-Yi Translation

"I know the ocean of every buddha-land of those bodhisattvas, and the very end of the ocean of the Dharma; I know the ocean of sentient beings, the cultivation of every bodhisattva's own Dharma-gate, the fulfillment of the great vow, and the manifestation of every kind of sovereign spiritual power — matters such as these, I know them all in full. Just as I know these matters within this Sahā world, so too, within the worlds of the ten directions — every world-nature, every ocean of worlds, every world-wheel, every world-fullness, every world-discernment, every world-whirl, every world-turning, every world-lotus, every world-Sumeru, and every matter within every world-mark — I know them in just the same way. It is by the power of the buddha Vairocana's own root vow that I am able to deeply enter, discern, and recollect all of this. Why is this so?"

Reading note — after Fazang

The ten "world" categories named here — world-nature and ocean of worlds already familiar from earlier teachers in this collection, joined now by world-wheel, world-fullness, world-discernment, world-whirl, world-turning, world-lotus, world-Sumeru, and world-mark — function as this cosmology's own comprehensive taxonomy of every kind of cosmic structure a world can take. Naming all ten in sequence is itself a claim to totality: not one Sahā world known deeply, but every possible register of "world" known equally, throughout every direction. The single cause given for this — Vairocana's own root vow — answers in advance the question Gopā poses to herself at the passage's close, before turning, in what follows, to answer it directly.
