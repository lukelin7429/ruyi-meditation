Boundless Marks

Unsurpassed Army names his liberation, Boundless Marks, through which he has beheld boundless buddhas and attained a boundless treasury. He closes with his self-limitation and refers Sudhana to a village named Dharma, where a brahmin named Śibi Supreme awaits.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 241 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Boundless Buddhas Beheld, a Boundless Treasury Attained

Chinese Source

「長者答言：「善男子！我得菩薩解脫，名無盡相。我以證此菩薩解脫，見無量佛，得無盡藏。善男子！我唯知此無盡相解脫，如諸菩薩摩訶薩得無限智、無礙辯才。而我云何能知、能說彼功德行？善男子！於此城南有一聚落，名之為法。彼聚落中有婆羅門，名尸毘最勝。汝詣彼問云何菩薩學菩薩行、修菩薩道。」」

Ru-Yi Translation

The elder answered, saying: "Good man, I have attained the bodhisattva's own liberation named Boundless Marks. Through realizing this bodhisattva's own liberation, I have beheld boundless buddhas and attained a boundless treasury. Good man, I know only this liberation of Boundless Marks. As for how the great bodhisattvas attain unlimited wisdom and unobstructed eloquence — how could I possibly know it, or speak of it? Good man, to the south of this city there is a village named Dharma. Within that village there is a brahmin named Śibi Supreme. Go to him and ask how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva's own practice and cultivates the bodhisattva's own path."

Reading note — after Fazang

Śibi is a name carried from an older layer of Buddhist story-literature — King Śibi, famous in jātaka tradition for cutting his own flesh to ransom a dove from a hawk, the paradigm of the perfection of giving. A brahmin bearing this name here, at a village literally called Dharma, signals through naming alone what territory this next teacher's own liberation is likely to concern, well before Sudhana ever asks his question.
