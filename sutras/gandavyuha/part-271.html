The Good Friend as the Root of Everything

The children's entire, sweeping teaching closes on its single governing claim: every bodhisattva practice, pāramitā, ground, forbearance, dhāraṇī, samādhi, and vow is consummated through a good friend — and every such dharma takes the good friend as its own root, arising, being born, being grasped, growing, abiding, and being attained all in dependence on this one relationship.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 271 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

"Depending on a Good Friend, One Arises, Is Born, Is Grasped, Grows, Abides, and Attains"

Chinese Source

「善男子！略說菩薩，因善知識究竟一切菩薩行、一切菩薩波羅蜜、一切菩薩地、一切菩薩忍、一切菩薩陀羅尼、一切菩薩三昧門、一切菩薩通明智自在、一切菩薩迴向、一切菩薩大願。善男子！如是等一切法，善知識為本：依善知識起、依善知識生、依善知識取、依善知識發、依善知識長、依善知識住、依善知識得。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good man, to speak briefly: a bodhisattva, through a good friend, consummates every bodhisattva practice, every bodhisattva pāramitā, every bodhisattva ground, every bodhisattva forbearance, every bodhisattva dhāraṇī, every bodhisattva samādhi-gate, every bodhisattva's own sovereign, penetrating wisdom, every bodhisattva dedication, and every bodhisattva's own great vow. Good man, all such dharmas as these take the good friend as their own root: depending on a good friend, one arises; depending on a good friend, one is born; depending on a good friend, one is grasped; depending on a good friend, one is raised up; depending on a good friend, one grows; depending on a good friend, one abides; and depending on a good friend, one attains."

Reading note — after Fazang

Seven verbs in a row — arise, be born, be grasped, be raised, grow, abide, attain — trace a single life-cycle from origin to completion, each stage explicitly dependent on the same one cause. Fazang reads this sevenfold formula as the entire teaching's own final compression: everything said across dozens of parts, from the boundless-qualities list through the ten similes, the twenty-two minds, and the ten paired reflections, reduces here to one claim, stated as plainly as this collection ever states anything — nothing arises, nothing grows, and nothing is attained apart from the good friend.

Part 271 Complete — The Children's Entire Referral Speech Concludes

With this, the two children's enormous teaching — spanning the boundless-qualities list, the ten similes, the twenty-two mind-attitudes, the ten paired reflections, and these closing declarations — is finally complete. Part 272 turns to what happens next: Sudhana, overjoyed, at last sets out on the actual journey toward Sea-Torrent country and the tower where Maitreya himself awaits.
