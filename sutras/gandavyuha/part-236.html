Onward to Fertile Field

Worthy Supreme closes her account, naming the limit of her own knowledge, and refers Sudhana south to a city named Fertile Field, where an elder named Firm Liberation awaits.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 236 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

"How Could I Possibly Know It, or Speak of It in Full?"

Chinese Source

「善男子！我唯知此無依處道場法門，如諸菩薩摩訶薩一切無著功德行。而我云何盡能知說？善男子！南方有城，名為沃田。彼有長者，名堅固解脫。汝可往問云何菩薩學菩薩行、修菩薩道。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Good man, I know only this Dharma-gate, the Bodhimaṇḍa of No Abiding Place. As for how the great bodhisattvas practice every kind of unattached merit and virtue — how could I possibly know it, or speak of it in full? Good man, to the south there is a city named Fertile Field. There is an elder there named Firm Liberation. Go and ask him how a bodhisattva learns the bodhisattva's own practice and cultivates the bodhisattva's own path."

Reading note — after Fazang

What Worthy Supreme names as beyond her own knowledge — "every kind of unattached merit and virtue" — connects directly back to her own liberation's own name, the Bodhimaṇḍa of "No Abiding Place": her own realization is itself a form of non-attachment, yet she is careful to distinguish her own single Dharma-gate from the great bodhisattvas' own comprehensive practice of non-attachment in every form. The referral southward to Firm Liberation, whose very name repeats this same theme, suggests the pilgrimage's own sequence is, for these several teachers, circling a single cluster of related qualities from different angles.
