The Seed of Every Buddha

Maitreya begins the great catalogue: why is this so? Because the mind of bodhi is the seed of every buddha, for it can give rise to every buddha-dharma; it is a good field, for it nurtures the pure white dharmas of sentient beings; it is the great earth, for it upholds every world; it is pure water, for it washes away the defilement of every affliction; it is the great wind, for it moves through every world without obstruction; it is a blazing fire, for it burns away every wrong view and craving; it is a pure sun, for it universally illuminates every class of sentient being; it is a bright moon, for every pure white dharma is fully perfected within it — and so the catalogue continues, one simile flowing into the next without pause.

From the Seed of Every Buddha to a Kind Mother and Nurse

Chinese Source

何以故？菩提心者，則為一切諸佛種子，能生一切諸佛法故；菩提心者，則為良田，長養眾生白淨法故；菩提心者，則為大地，能持一切諸世間故；菩提心者，則為淨水，洗濯一切煩惱垢故；菩提心者，則為大風，一切世間無障礙故；菩提心者，則為盛火，能燒一切邪見愛故；菩提心者，則為淨日，普照一切眾生類故；菩提心者，則為明月，諸白淨法悉圓滿故；菩提心者，則為淨燈，普照一切諸法界故；菩提心者，則為淨眼，悉能覩見邪正道故；菩提心者，則為大道，皆令得入一切智城故；菩提心者，則為正濟，悉令得到出要處故；菩提心者，則為大乘，容載一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，則為門戶，令入一切菩薩行故；菩提心者，則為宮殿，安住修習三昧法故；菩提心者，則為園觀，於中遊戲受法樂故；菩提心者，則為勝宅，一切眾生所歸依故；菩提心者，則為依止，因修一切菩薩行故；菩提心者，則為守護，能滿菩薩諸大願故；菩提心者，則為慈母，增長一切諸菩薩故；菩提心者，則為養育，守護一切諸菩薩故；

Ru-Yi Translation

"Why is this so? The mind of bodhi is the seed of every buddha, for it can give rise to every buddha-dharma; the mind of bodhi is a good field, for it nurtures the pure white dharmas of sentient beings; the mind of bodhi is the great earth, for it upholds every world; the mind of bodhi is pure water, for it washes away the defilement of every affliction; the mind of bodhi is the great wind, for it moves through every world without obstruction; the mind of bodhi is a blazing fire, for it burns away every wrong view and craving; the mind of bodhi is a pure sun, for it universally illuminates every class of sentient being; the mind of bodhi is a bright moon, for every pure white dharma is fully perfected within it; the mind of bodhi is a pure lamp, for it universally illuminates every dharma-realm; the mind of bodhi is a pure eye, for it is able to see clearly the wrong path and the right; the mind of bodhi is the great road, for it causes all to enter the city of all-knowing wisdom; the mind of bodhi is the true crossing, for it brings all to the place of true escape; the mind of bodhi is the great vehicle, for it carries every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is a gateway, for it admits one into every bodhisattva practice; the mind of bodhi is a palace, for one dwells within it cultivating the dharma of samādhi; the mind of bodhi is a garden park, for one plays freely within it, receiving the joy of the Dharma; the mind of bodhi is a supreme dwelling, for every sentient being takes refuge in it; the mind of bodhi is a support, for through it one cultivates every bodhisattva practice; the mind of bodhi is a guardian, for it can fulfill every great vow of the bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is a kind mother, for it nurtures the growth of every bodhisattva; the mind of bodhi is a nurse, for it protects and rears every bodhisattva;"

Reading note — after Fazang

This catalogue, running to well over a hundred similes before it closes, is among the most celebrated single passages in the entire Avataṃsaka corpus — later Chinese commentators frequently excerpt and anthologize it apart from its narrative context here. Fazang groups the similes loosely by register as they unfold: this opening stretch draws chiefly on agriculture and architecture — seed, field, earth, road, vehicle, gateway, palace, garden, house — establishing bodhicitta as at once a point of origin and a place of dwelling, before the catalogue turns to cosmological and then medicinal and martial imagery in the stretches that follow. Ru-Yi renders every simile in full, without exception, in keeping with this site's practice for all such catalogues in the sūtra.
