Like a Seal Producing Its Own Imprint

Sudhana's own extended contemplation closes: extinguishing wrong delusion, he understands every dharma as empty, yet transcends every mark without ever contradicting the dharma of a seed giving rise to a sprout — knowing that everything arises from causes and conditions, exactly like a seal producing its own imprint, a mirror its own image, lightning, a dream, an echo, an illusion.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 279 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Emptiness That Never Contradicts the Seed-and-Sprout Dharma

Chinese Source

「滅邪癡惑，了諸法空，悉無自在；超出諸相，入無相際，而亦不違種生芽法，悉知一切從因緣生，如因印故而生印像，如鏡中像、如電、如夢、如響、如幻，各隨因有。一切諸法，亦復如是，隨業受報，以善方便，潤澤諸法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Extinguishing wrong delusion and confusion, he understood every dharma as empty, all without any sovereign self; transcending every mark, he entered the boundary of marklessness, yet without contradicting the dharma of a seed giving rise to a sprout, fully knowing that everything arises from causes and conditions, just as a seal produces its own imprint, like a reflection in a mirror, like lightning, like a dream, like an echo, like an illusion, each existing according to its own cause. Every dharma is likewise so, according with karma and receiving its own retribution, moistening every dharma through skillful means.

Reading note — after Fazang

The seed-and-sprout dharma named here as never contradicted, even by the deepest emptiness and marklessness, is this passage's own resolution of a tension that could otherwise seem to threaten the entire teaching on karma this collection has built across fifty prior teachers: if all dharmas are empty and markless, does causation still function? Fazang reads the seal's own imprint — produced by the seal yet not identical to it, real in its effect yet without any independent substance of its own — as this whole approach's own final answer, closing Sudhana's contemplation just before he crosses the tower's own threshold.
