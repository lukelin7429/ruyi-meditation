Ten Similes for the Good Friend

Ten images in succession, each with its own stated reason: the good friend as a kind mother, a kind father, a nurturer, a great teacher, a guide, a good physician, a snow mountain, a valiant general, a sturdy boat, and a ferryman.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 261 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Mother, Father, Teacher, Physician, Mountain, General, Boat, Ferryman

Chinese Source

「復次；善男子！善知識者，則為慈母，生佛家故；善知識者，則為慈父，以無量事，益眾生故；善知識者，則為養育守護，不為一切惡故；善知識者，則為大師，教化令學菩薩戒故；善知識者，則為導師，教化令至彼岸道故；善知識者，則為良醫，療治一切煩惱患故；善知識者，則為雪山，長養明淨智慧藥故；善知識者，則為勇將，防護一切諸恐怖故；善知識者，則為牢船，悉令越度生死海故；善知識者，則為船師，令至一切智法寶洲故。是故，善男子！」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Furthermore, good man: a good friend is a kind mother, because through them one is born into the buddha's own family; a good friend is a kind father, because through countless matters they benefit sentient beings; a good friend is one who nurtures and protects, because they never do the slightest evil; a good friend is a great teacher, because they teach one, causing one to learn the bodhisattva precepts; a good friend is a guide, because they teach one, causing one to reach the path of the other shore; a good friend is a good physician, because they heal and treat every affliction and disease of the mind; a good friend is a snow mountain, because they nurture the bright, pure medicine of wisdom; a good friend is a valiant general, because they guard against every fear; a good friend is a sturdy boat, because they cause one to cross entirely over the ocean of birth and death; a good friend is a ferryman, because they bring one to the isle of the Dharma-jewel of omniscience. For this reason, good man —"

Reading note — after Fazang

These ten similes are among the most frequently cited passages from the entire Gaṇḍavyūha in later Buddhist literature, standing on their own as a compact teaching on the good friend independent of the pilgrimage that surrounds them. Fazang notes their own careful arrangement: familial roles first (mother, father, nurturer), then instructional roles (teacher, guide, physician), then roles of protection and passage (mountain, general, boat, ferryman) — the good friend's own function moves from origin, through instruction, to safe arrival.
