Continuing the Buddha-List

The Bhadrakalpa buddha-list continues, thirty more names from Vajra-Mountain Buddha through Valiantly-Advancing-and-Upholding Buddha — among them Upatiṣya Buddha, sharing a name with Śāriputra.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 214 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

From Vajra-Mountain to Valiantly-Advancing-and-Upholding

Chinese Source

「……金剛山佛、智焰盛妙德佛、安隱佛、優波提舍佛、具淨德佛、樂賢德佛、第一義勇佛、百光焰佛、一增上佛、深音聲佛、大地王佛、白淨佛、山音聲佛、殊勝佛、不可壞佛、無上醫佛、功德月佛、不違逆佛、功德聚佛、月出佛、功德天佛、光明盛佛、娑羅陰佛、藥王佛、勝寶佛、金剛慧佛、八十妙德佛、一切無壞佛、大名稱王佛、勇進持佛、……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… Vajra-Mountain Buddha, Wisdom-Flame-Blazing-Sublime-Virtue Buddha, Peace-and-Well-Being Buddha, Upatiṣya Buddha, Complete-Pure-Virtue Buddha, Delighting-in-Worthy-Virtue Buddha, Foremost-Truth-Valiant Buddha, Hundred-Light-Flames Buddha, Singularly-Ascendant Buddha, Profound-Voice Buddha, Great-Earth-King Buddha, White-and-Pure Buddha, Mountain-Voice Buddha, Surpassing Buddha, Indestructible Buddha, Supreme-Physician Buddha, Merit-Moon Buddha, Never-Contrary Buddha, Merit-Accumulation Buddha, Moon-Arising Buddha, Merit-Deva Buddha, Radiance-Blazing Buddha, Sāla-Shade Buddha, Medicine-King Buddha, Supreme-Jewel Buddha, Vajra-Wisdom Buddha, Eighty-Sublime-Virtues Buddha, All-Indestructible Buddha, Great-Renowned-King Buddha, Valiantly-Advancing-and-Upholding Buddha … [the list continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

Upatiṣya (優波提舍) is, elsewhere in the canon, Śāriputra's own given name before his ordination — here it names one of the Bhadrakalpa's buddhas instead, a coincidence the text itself does not remark on. Whether this reuse is deliberate or simply the natural result of a finite stock of auspicious Sanskrit names being drawn on twice across an enormous canon, it is left untranslated here as a proper name rather than glossed, in keeping with this collection's practice for names it cannot verify against any known biography.
