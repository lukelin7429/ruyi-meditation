Awakening Within a Single Thought

The hymn's own refrain returns: attaining correct awakening within a single thought, entering a boundless wisdom-activity that none can measure; unobstructed feet, unattached to anything, understanding every land as without duality; and contemplating every quiescent dharma as entirely like empty space, a domain wholly free of stain.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 295 of 454 complete Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

None Can Measure This Wisdom-Activity

Chinese Source

「一念成正覺，　入無量智業，
莫能測量者，　安住此法堂。

無礙淨慧力，　遊行諸法界，
無垢智觀者，　安住此法堂。

成就無礙足，　一切無所著，
了剎無二者，　安住此法堂。

觀諸寂滅法，　皆悉如虛空，
離垢境界者，　安住此法堂。

大悲觀眾生，　諸苦所逼迫，
拔濟饒益者，　安住此法堂。

Ru-Yi Translation

"In a single thought attaining correct awakening, entering boundless wisdom-activity,
One whom none can measure abides in this Dharma-hall.

The power of unobstructed, pure wisdom, journeying through every dharma-realm,
One whose stainless wisdom contemplates abides in this Dharma-hall.

Accomplishing unobstructed feet, unattached to anything,
One who understands every land as without duality abides in this Dharma-hall.

Contemplating every quiescent dharma as entirely like empty space,
One whose domain is free of stain abides in this Dharma-hall.

With great compassion beholding sentient beings pressed by every suffering,
One who pulls them out and benefits them abides in this Dharma-hall."

Reading note — after Fazang

The refrain's own return after its single break in the previous part reads, in Fazang's reading, as a kind of settling — the wide-angle view of transformation-bodies filling the dharma-realm has now narrowed back to the tower itself, though what fills that narrower frame has only grown more extraordinary: awakening within a single thought, unobstructed feet, a domain wholly free of stain. "Unobstructed feet" (無礙足) recalls the traditional bases of spiritual power (ṛddhipāda) that grant a buddha or advanced bodhisattva the capacity to travel anywhere without hindrance.
