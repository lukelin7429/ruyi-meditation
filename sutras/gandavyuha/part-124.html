Five More Rebirth-Dharmas, and a Closing Vista

The explanation of the ten rebirth-dharmas concludes: being born directly into the tathāgata's own family, the buddha's own power of light, discerning the inexhaustible gates of omniscience, adorning the whole Dharma-realm through transformation, and valiant diligence arriving at the buddha's own ground. A single closing paragraph then widens the frame to what a bodhisattva abiding in all ten makes possible across boundless kalpas.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 124 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Born Into the Family, the Buddha's Own Light, and Inexhaustible Wisdom

Chinese Source

「佛子！何等為生如來家藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩生如來家，隨諸佛教，具足一切甚深法門，同三世一切諸佛大願，同三世一切諸佛善根，同三世一切諸佛法身，遠離世間向離世間趣，長養白淨法，住大功德法門，得佛持定，見諸如來隨所應化，淨諸眾生，不捨大願，聞法受持。佛子！是為第六受生法。

「佛子！何等為佛光明力藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩深入佛力，遍遊十方，供養諸佛，心無疲倦，知一切法如幻如夢，色如電光，成就如化，自在通明；知一切有生趣如影；知一切佛所轉法輪皆悉如響，悉究竟說一切法界。佛子！是為第七受生法。

「佛子！何等為具足分別薩婆若門藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩，以童子身，住菩薩住，觀薩婆若，於無量劫，觀察一一諸智慧門；劫猶可盡，諸智慧門不可窮盡，究竟菩薩自在境界諸三昧門，念念悉詣十方佛所，入不可壞三昧境界，不可壞法、不可壞智無邊境界，得非境界，於少境界悉具足，得不可說地，於無量中，得有量法，知諸世間名假施設，分別一切語言之法。佛子！是為第八受生法。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of Being Born Into the Tathāgata's Own Family? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva, born into the tathāgata's own family, accords with every buddha's own teaching, becomes fully endowed with every profound Dharma-gate, is one with the great vow of every buddha of the three times, one with the roots of goodness of every buddha of the three times, and one with the Dharma-body of every buddha of the three times; departing far from the world, he turns toward the destiny that transcends the world, nurtures pure and stainless dharmas, abides in the Dharma-gate of great merit, attains the buddha's own sustaining samādhi, beholds every tathāgata according to what is fitting to transform beings, purifies every sentient being, does not abandon the great vow, and, hearing the Dharma, upholds it. Child of the Buddha, this is the sixth rebirth-dharma.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of the Power of the Buddha's Own Light? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva deeply enters the buddha's own power, ranges universally through the ten directions, and makes offerings to buddhas, his mind never growing weary; he knows every dharma to be like an illusion, like a dream, form like a flash of lightning, and accomplishes, like a conjured being, sovereign, penetrating clarity; he knows every destiny of existence and birth to be like a shadow; he knows the Dharma-wheel turned by every buddha to be, in every case, like an echo, and thoroughly, to the very end, expounds the whole Dharma-realm. Child of the Buddha, this is the seventh rebirth-dharma.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury Fully Endowed With Discerning the Gate of Omniscience? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva, in the body of a youth, abides in the bodhisattva's own abiding, contemplates omniscience, and throughout boundless kalpas contemplates every single gate of wisdom, one after another; kalpas may yet be exhausted, but the gates of wisdom cannot be exhausted. Reaching the very end of the bodhisattva's own sovereign state, in every samādhi-gate, within every single thought-moment he goes universally to the buddhas of the ten directions, entering the indestructible state of samādhi, the boundless state of indestructible dharma and indestructible wisdom; he attains what is beyond every state, is fully endowed within the smallest state, attains the ground beyond all telling, and, within the boundless, attains the dharma that is bounded; he knows every worldly name to be a mere provisional designation, and discerns the dharma of every kind of language. Child of the Buddha, this is the eighth rebirth-dharma."

Reading note — after Fazang

Fazang's gloss continues its short sequence: 六生佛家；七佛光力；八達佛智 — "sixth, being born into the buddha's own family; seventh, the buddha's own power of light; eighth, penetrating the buddha's own wisdom." The eighth carries an image worth pausing on: kalpas — the very unit this whole collection has used to measure the near-unimaginable — "may yet be exhausted," while the gates of wisdom cannot be. Even the account's largest available scale of time is offered here only to be found insufficient against the scope of what is being described.

Adorning the Dharma-Realm, Arriving at the Buddha-Ground, and a Closing Vista

Chinese Source

「佛子！何等為一切法界化莊嚴藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩種種莊嚴無量佛剎，究竟眾生諸變化身、佛應化身，無所依止，清淨法化，悉行一切無礙法界，應受化者為彼現身，教示種種諸菩薩行，善能出生離諸障礙，一切智門淨智慧藏，教化眾生，未曾失時。佛子！是為第九受生法。

「佛子！何等為勇猛精進至佛地藏受生法？此菩薩摩訶薩悉於三世諸如來所，受灌頂法，一切世界境界無障礙，菩薩悉知三世眾生死此生彼，修菩薩行，知諸眾生心次第起；知三世佛次成正覺，善巧方便知法次第；知一切劫次第成敗，隨應眾生顯現莊嚴，成等正覺顯現次第；轉正法輪，教化無量無邊眾生。佛子！是為第十受生法。菩薩摩訶薩住是法已，種種莊嚴一切佛剎無量億劫，無量法海，無量境界，教化眾生，覺悟無量諸法界流，顯現諸佛不可思議如虛空等深法境界，無量諸行，攝取眾生，現轉法輪，於一切世界，護持佛法，悉於一切境界，以微妙音說不可說佛正法雲，住諸法門趣無礙道，以一切法莊嚴道場，隨所應度成佛興世，教化成熟無邊眾生。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of the Transformative Adornment of the Whole Dharma-Realm? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva adorns boundless buddha-lands in every kind of way, reaches the very end of every transformation-body of sentient beings and every response-body of a buddha, relies on nothing whatsoever, and, through pure, dharmic transformation, universally practices throughout the whole unobstructed Dharma-realm; for whoever is fit to be transformed, he manifests a body before them, and teaches and shows every kind of the bodhisattva's own practice, skillfully able to give rise to the pure treasury of wisdom of every gate of omniscience, free of every obstruction, teaching and transforming sentient beings, never once missing the right moment. Child of the Buddha, this is the ninth rebirth-dharma.

"Child of the Buddha, what is called the bodhisattva's rebirth-dharma of the Treasury of Valiant Diligence, Arriving at the Buddha-Ground? This bodhisattva-mahāsattva receives, at the presence of every tathāgata of the three times, the Dharma of consecration, and, his state unobstructed throughout every world, the bodhisattva fully knows every sentient being of the three times, dying here and being born there, cultivating the bodhisattva's own practice; he knows every sentient being's own mind arising in due order; he knows every buddha of the three times, in due order, attaining the most correct awakening, and, through skillful means, knows the Dharma's own due order; he knows every kalpa's own due order of formation and destruction, and, according to what is fitting for sentient beings, manifests adornment, manifesting, in due order, the attainment of the most correct awakening; he turns the wheel of the true Dharma, teaching and transforming boundless, measureless sentient beings. Child of the Buddha, this is the tenth rebirth-dharma. A bodhisattva-mahāsattva, having abided in these dharmas, adorns every buddha-land in every kind of way throughout boundless koṭis of kalpas, teaching and transforming sentient beings amid a boundless ocean of dharmas and a boundless state, awakening them to the boundless streams of every Dharma-realm, manifesting every buddha's own inconceivable, space-like, profound state of Dharma, and, through boundless practices, gathering in sentient beings, manifesting the turning of the Dharma-wheel, and, throughout every world, upholding and guarding the buddha-dharma; entirely within every state, with a sublime, subtle voice, he expounds a cloud of the buddha's own true Dharma beyond all telling, abides in every Dharma-gate, advances toward the unobstructed path, and, adorning the bodhimaṇḍa with every dharma, according to whoever is fit to be delivered, becomes a buddha and arises in the world, teaching, transforming, and ripening boundless sentient beings."

Reading note — after Fazang

Fazang closes his tenfold gloss: 九嚴法界；十懃至佛。由行此十行令諸菩薩得生佛家故也 — "ninth, adorning the Dharma-realm; tenth, diligently arriving at the buddha's own ground. By practicing these ten practices, bodhisattvas are thereby caused to be born into the buddha's own family." The tenth explanation, alone among the ten, ends by naming the actual endpoint the other nine have been building toward all along — "becomes a buddha and arises in the world" — and the paragraph that follows it, unmarked as a separate rebirth-dharma of its own, functions as Fazang's own fourth prose division: 四結歎益, a closing statement of benefit standing over all ten treasuries at once, not one of them individually.
