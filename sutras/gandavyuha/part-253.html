Boundless Medicine for Boundless Disease

The list continues through every power of wisdom, merit, and Dharma-gate, before turning to a striking image: sentient beings' own afflictions named as diseases, healed through boundless sublime Dharma-medicines and skillful means.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Part 253 in progress Source: Taishō T278, 入法界品第三十四之十五 (Buddhabhadra, Eastern Jin) Reading notes after Fazang's 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733)

Every Power, Every Dharma-Gate, and a Medicine for Every Disease

Chinese Source

「……開發無量諸大慧力；堅固無量諸欲性力；積聚無量諸功德力；長養無量諸淨智力；發起無量菩薩諸力；成滿無量諸如來力；悉分別知無量法門；普入無量諸法方面；淨修無量法門；發起無量法明，照無量法，無量諸根；了知無量諸煩惱病；積集無量諸妙法藥，以善方便，療眾結病；修習無量甘露正法；詣諸佛剎，恭敬供養無量如來；遍入菩薩大眾源底，護持無量如來正法；不譏無量眾生罪咎；……」

Ru-Yi Translation

"… open up boundless power of great wisdom; make firm boundless power of the very nature of aspiration; accumulate boundless power of merit; nurture boundless power of pure wisdom; raise boundless bodhisattva powers; perfectly fulfill boundless tathāgata powers; discern and fully know boundless Dharma-gates; universally enter every direction of boundless dharmas; purely cultivate boundless Dharma-gates; raise boundless light of the Dharma, illumining boundless dharmas and boundless faculties; fully know sentient beings' own boundless afflictive diseases; accumulate boundless sublime Dharma-medicines, and through skillful means heal every one of their bound-up diseases; cultivate boundless ambrosial true Dharma; go to every buddha-land, reverently making offerings to boundless tathāgatas; enter fully into the very wellspring of the bodhisattva's own great assembly, upholding and guarding boundless tathāgatas' own true Dharma; never disparage boundless sentient beings' own faults and transgressions … [the list continues]"

Reading note — after Fazang

The medical metaphor here — afflictions as "diseases" (病), the Dharma as "medicine" (藥) applied through "skillful means" like a physician's own art — recalls a figure this collection has used before for other teachers, but here it is scaled to match the list's own governing word: not one remedy for one ailment, but boundless medicines for boundless diseases. Fazang reads the final item, refusing to "disparage boundless sentient beings' own faults," as a necessary corrective placed immediately after so much talk of disease and affliction — diagnosis is never license for contempt.
