Sixty Koṭis of Nayutas of Buddhas, Named in Full

The list continues through forty-five further names, closing on the buddha Vast Liberation, Light — the very last of sixty hundred thousand koṭis of nayutas of buddhas who arose, one after another, within that single kalpa. At his presence, Gopā attained the pure eye of wisdom.

Reading note — after Fazang

Forty-six named buddhas in unbroken sequence — a list this project translates in full rather than abbreviate, matching the convention already established at earlier teachers' own 98- and 110-buddha lists. What the number "sixty hundred thousand koṭis of nayutas" actually names is beyond ordinary comprehension; what the list itself does is make that unimaginable span concrete, one name at a time, ending on the single buddha whose own teaching finally delivered Gopā "the pure eye of wisdom" — the entry-level clarity this collection has granted its newer bodhisattvas elsewhere, arriving here only after an entire kalpa's worth of buddhas already met.
