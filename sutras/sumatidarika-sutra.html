The Questions of the Girl Sumati

An eight-year-old daughter of an elder in Rājagṛha asks the Buddha six questions in verse — about beauty, wealth, an unbreakable following, rebirth on a lotus, spiritual power, and a peaceful death — and receives ten sets of four causal practices in return, each capped by a verse of its own. She then debates Mañjuśrī on what it means to "abide in" anything at all, and, to prove her own vow true, transforms the entire assembly gold and herself from a woman into a man.

Translator: Ru-Yi Meditation Center Status: Complete Source: Taishō T0336 (tr. Bodhiruci, Tang dynasty) Reading notes: Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Translation Method

The Chinese source is Bodhiruci's translation (菩提流志), an Indian monk active at the Tang court in the early eighth century, who also produced the compiled 120-fascicle Ratnakūṭa Sūtra in which this same text appears as its thirtieth assembly, there titled 妙慧童女會 ("The Assembly of the Girl Sumati"). Two earlier, independent Chinese translations of the same text also survive (T334, T335), along with the Tibetan. No commentary on this sūtra by any premodern figure survives; the modern scholar-monk Yin Shun (印順, 1905–2005) devoted one of his own lecture-records to it, but as a living author's work it remains under Taiwan copyright until 2055 per this site's commentator-vetting rule, and so is not used or cited here. This collection's reading notes are Ru-Yi Meditation Center's own, offered honestly as such rather than attributed to any named source. Classical text is set in the left-hand column below, our English in the right. See also this site's note on translation method.

Sumati's Five Questions

Chinese Source

如是我聞：一時佛在王舍城耆闍崛山中，與比丘眾千二百五十人、菩薩摩訶薩十千人俱。

時王舍城有長者女名為妙慧，年始八歲，面貌端正，容色姝好，諸相具足，見者歡喜——曾於過去無量諸佛親近供養、種諸善根。

時彼女人詣如來所，頂禮佛足，右繞三匝，長跪合掌而說偈言：

「無上等正覺，　為世大明燈。
菩薩之所行，　唯願聽我問。」

佛告妙慧：「今恣汝問，當為解說，令斷疑網。」妙慧白言：「唯然，世尊！願樂欲聞。」

爾時，妙慧即於佛前以偈問曰：

「云何得端正、　大富尊貴身？
復以何因緣，　眷屬難沮壞？
云何見己身，　而受於化生，
千葉蓮花上，　面奉諸世尊？
云何能證得，　自在勝神通，
遍往無量剎，　禮敬於諸佛？
云何得無怨，　所言人信受，
淨除於法障，　永離諸魔業？
云何命終時，　得見於諸佛，
聞說清淨法，　不受於苦惱？
大悲無上尊！　唯願為我說。」

爾時，佛告妙慧童女言：「善哉善哉，善能問此深妙之義。諦聽諦聽，善思念之，當為汝說。」妙慧白言：「唯然，世尊！願樂欲聞。」

Ru-Yi Translation

Thus have I heard. At one time the Buddha was at Rājagṛha, on Vulture Peak, together with an assembly of 1,250 monks and ten thousand bodhisattva-mahāsattvas.

At that time, in Rājagṛha, there was an elder's daughter named Sumati, only eight years of age, of upright countenance and lovely appearance, complete in every mark, delighting all who saw her — for in ages past she had drawn near to and made offerings to immeasurable buddhas, and had planted every root of goodness.

This girl came before the Tathāgata, bowed her head to the Buddha's feet, circled him three times to the right, and, kneeling with palms joined, spoke this verse:

"Unsurpassed, Perfectly and Fully Awakened One,
great lamp illuminating the world —
I ask only that you hear
my questions about the bodhisattva's own practice."

The Buddha said to Sumati: "Ask freely now, whatever you wish, and I will explain it for you, so as to cut through the net of doubt." Sumati said: "Yes indeed, World-Honored One — I long to hear it."

Then Sumati asked the Buddha in verse:

"How does one attain a body upright,
wealthy and noble?
And by what cause does one's own following
become impossible to break apart?
How does one come to see oneself
reborn by transformation,
upon a thousand-petaled lotus,
face to face with every buddha?
How does one attain sovereign,
supreme spiritual powers,
traveling throughout immeasurable buddha-lands,
paying homage to every buddha?
How does one come to have no enemies,
one's every word believed and accepted,
purified of every obstruction to the Dharma,
forever free of every deed of Māra?
How, at the moment of death,
does one come to behold every buddha,
hearing the pure Dharma taught,
without undergoing any suffering?
Great Compassionate, Unsurpassed World-Honored One —
I ask only that you explain this for me."

Then the Buddha said to the girl Sumati: "Excellent, excellent — you ask this deep and wondrous meaning well. Listen carefully, listen carefully, and consider it well; I will explain it for you." Sumati said: "Yes indeed, World-Honored One, I long to hear it."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The sūtra opens by making a deliberate point of Sumati's age and status: eight years old, a daughter rather than a monk or an established teacher, asking the Buddha directly rather than through any intermediary. This site's Śrīmālādevī Sūtra makes the same structural choice with an adult queen instead of a monk; here the sūtra pushes the same move further, choosing a child. Her six couplets of verse compress into fewer questions than the ten sets of practice that answer them — the final couplet alone ("having no enemies... one's word believed... free of every obstruction... free of Māra") bundles four separate concerns into one stanza, which the Buddha's answer will unbundle one at a time.

Ten Vows Answered in Forty Practices

Chinese Source

佛言：「妙慧！菩薩成就四法受端正身。何等為四？一者、於惡友所不起瞋心，二者、住於大慈，三者、深樂正法，四者、造佛形像。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「瞋壞善根勿增長、　慈心、樂法、造佛形，
當獲具相莊嚴身，　一切眾生常樂見。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法得富貴身。何等為四？一者、應時行施，二者、無輕慢心，三者、歡喜而與，四者、不希果報。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「應時行施、無輕慢、　歡喜授與、不希求，
能於此業常勤修，　所生當獲大財位。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法得眷屬不壞。何等為四？一者、善能棄捨離間之語，二者、邪見眾生令住正見，三者、正法將滅護令久住，四者、教諸有情趣佛菩提。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「捨離間言及邪見、　正法將滅能護持、
安住眾生大菩提，　當成不壞諸眷屬。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法，當於佛前得受化生，處蓮花座。何等為四？一者、捧諸花果及細末香散於如來及諸塔廟，二者、終不於他妄加損害，三者、造如來像安處蓮華，四者、於佛菩提深生淨信。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「花香散佛及支提、　不害於他并造像、
於大菩提深信解，　得處蓮花生佛前。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法，從一佛土至一佛土。何等為四？一者、見他修善不為障惱，二者、他說法時未常留礙，三者、然燈供養如來之塔，四者、於諸禪定常勤修習。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「見人修善、說正法，　不生謗毀加留難，
如來塔廟施燈明，　修習諸禪遊佛剎。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法處世無怨。云何為四？一者、以無諂心親近善友，二者、於他勝法無嫉妬心，三者、他獲名譽心常歡喜，四者、於菩薩行無輕毀心。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「不以諛諂親善友、　於人勝法無妬心、
他獲名譽常歡喜、　不謗菩薩得無怨。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法，所言人信。何等為四？一者、發言修行常使相應，二者、於善友所不覆諸惡，三者、於所聞法不求過失，四者、於說法者不生惡心。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「發言修行常相應、　己罪不藏於善友、
聞經不求人法過，　所言一切皆信受。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法能離法障，速得清淨。何等為四？一者、以深意樂攝三律儀，二者、聞甚深經不生誹謗，三者、見新發意菩薩生一切智心，四者、於諸有情大慈平等。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「以深意樂攝律儀、　聞甚深經能信解、
敬初發心如佛想、　慈心普洽障消除。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法能離諸魔。云何為四？一者、了知法性平等，二者、發起精進，三者、常勤念佛，四者、一切善根皆悉迴向。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「能知諸法平等性、　常起精進、念如來、
迴向一切諸善根，　眾魔不能得其便。

「復次，妙慧！菩薩成就四法臨命終時諸佛現前。何等為四？一者、他有所求施令滿足，二者、於諸善法深生信解，三者、於諸菩薩施莊嚴具，四者、於三寶所勤修供養。」爾時，世尊而說偈言：

「他有所求令滿足、　信解深法、捨嚴具、
三寶福田勤供養，　臨命終時佛現前。」

Ru-Yi Translation

The Buddha said: "Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things attains a body upright and fine. What are the four? First, not giving rise to anger toward an evil companion; second, abiding in great loving-kindness; third, deeply delighting in the true Dharma; fourth, making images of the Buddha." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Never let anger, destroyer of good roots, grow;
be loving, delight in Dharma, make the Buddha's image —
you will thereby gain a body adorned with every mark,
one that every being ever delights to see.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things attains a body wealthy and noble. What are the four? First, giving at the right time; second, a mind free of contempt; third, giving joyfully; fourth, not hoping for any reward." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Give at the right time, free of contempt,
giving joyfully, hoping for no reward —
diligently cultivate this practice always,
and wherever you are born you will gain great wealth and rank.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things attains a following that cannot be broken apart. What are the four? First, being well able to cast aside divisive speech; second, establishing beings of wrong view in right view; third, when the true Dharma is about to perish, protecting it so it abides long; fourth, guiding every being toward the Buddha's bodhi." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Cast aside divisive speech and wrong views,
protect the true Dharma when it nears its end,
establish beings in great bodhi —
you will thereby gain a following that can never be broken apart.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things will, before a buddha, be reborn by transformation, seated upon a lotus. What are the four? First, offering flowers, fruit, and fine incense powder, scattering them before the Tathāgata and before stūpas; second, never wrongly causing harm to another; third, making an image of the Tathāgata seated upon a lotus; fourth, giving rise to deep, pure faith in the Buddha's bodhi." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Scatter flowers and incense before the Buddha and his stūpas,
harm no one, and make his image,
hold deep faith and understanding in great bodhi —
you will thereby come to be born on a lotus before a buddha.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things travels from one buddha-land to another. What are the four? First, seeing another cultivate good and not obstructing or troubling them; second, never hindering another when they teach the Dharma; third, lighting lamps as offerings to the Tathāgata's stūpas; fourth, constantly and diligently practicing every meditative absorption." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"See others cultivate good and teach the true Dharma,
and give rise to no slander or hindrance;
offer lamplight to the Tathāgata's stūpas,
and cultivate every meditative absorption —
you will thereby roam freely through every buddha-land.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things dwells in the world without any enemy. What are the four? First, drawing near to good companions with a mind free of flattery; second, a mind free of envy toward another's superior attainment; third, a mind always joyful when another gains renown; fourth, a mind free of contempt toward the bodhisattva path." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Draw near to good companions without flattery,
free of envy toward another's excellence,
always rejoicing at another's renown,
never slandering bodhisattvas —
you will thereby have no enemy at all.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things has every word believed by others. What are the four? First, always matching speech to practice; second, never concealing one's own faults from good companions; third, never seeking fault in the Dharma one has heard; fourth, never giving rise to an ill mind toward one who teaches the Dharma." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Always match speech to practice,
hide no fault of your own from good companions,
seek no fault in the person or the Dharma you hear taught —
every word you speak will then be believed and accepted.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things can be freed from every obstruction to the Dharma, and swiftly attain purity. What are the four? First, upholding the three sets of precepts with deep and heartfelt joy; second, never slandering a profound sūtra one has heard; third, regarding a newly-resolved bodhisattva with the mind of all-knowing wisdom; fourth, having great loving-kindness equally toward every being." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Uphold the precepts with deep, heartfelt joy,
believe and understand every profound sūtra you hear,
revere one newly resolved as you would a buddha,
and let loving-kindness reach all —
every obstruction will thereby dissolve.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things can be freed from every kind of Māra. What are the four? First, understanding the equality of the nature of all dharmas; second, arousing diligence; third, constantly and diligently being mindful of the Buddha; fourth, dedicating every root of goodness to the benefit of all." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Know the equal nature of every dharma,
constantly arouse diligence, be mindful of the Tathāgata,
dedicate every root of goodness —
and every Māra will find no opening at all.

"Furthermore, Sumati! A bodhisattva who accomplishes four things will, at the moment of death, behold every buddha appearing before them. What are the four? First, whatever another asks for, giving it to them in full; second, giving rise to deep faith and understanding in every wholesome dharma; third, giving adornments to bodhisattvas; fourth, diligently making offerings to the Three Jewels." Then the World-Honored One spoke this verse:

"Give in full whatever another asks for,
hold faith and understanding in the profound Dharma, give up your own adornments,
diligently make offerings to the field of merit that is the Three Jewels —
and at the moment of death, a buddha will appear before you."

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Ten sets of four practices, forty items in all, are the Buddha's full answer to Sumati's six couplets of verse — the sūtra itself keeps count, since Sumati's own vow a few lines later refers explicitly to "these forty practices" (是四十行). The first five sets answer Sumati's first five questions directly, one for one: beauty, wealth, an unbreakable following, lotus-rebirth, and free movement between buddha-lands. The last five sets then unbundle her final, four-part couplet — no enemies, believed speech, freedom from obstruction, freedom from Māra — into four separate categories of practice, adding one further category (behind every buddha at the moment of death) that answers her closing question. Every one of the ten sets follows the identical template: a plain causal claim in prose, then the same content compressed into a four-line verse — a teaching style built for memorization rather than argument, closer to a catechism than to a philosophical treatise, and a useful contrast with the register this same sūtra shifts into a few pages later, once Mañjuśrī begins asking his own questions.

Sumati's Great Vow, and the Assembly Turns to Gold

Chinese Source

爾時，妙慧童女聞佛說已，白言：「世尊！如佛所說菩薩諸行，我當奉行。世尊！若我於是四十行中闕於一行而不修者，則違佛教、欺誑如來。」

爾時，尊者大目揵連告妙慧言：「菩薩之行甚難可行。汝今發斯殊勝大願，豈於是願得自在耶？」

爾時，妙慧白言：「尊者！若我弘願真實不虛、能令諸行得圓滿者，願此三千大千世界六種震動。」

是時，妙慧重白目連：「以我如是真實言故，於未來世當得成佛——亦如今日釋迦如來——於我國中無有魔事及以惡趣、女人之名。若我此言非虛妄者，令斯大眾身皆金色。」說是語已，眾皆金色。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the girl Sumati, having heard the Buddha speak, said: "World-Honored One! I shall carry out every bodhisattva practice the Buddha has just described. World-Honored One, if I should lack even one of these forty practices, and fail to cultivate it, then I would be violating the Buddha's own teaching, and deceiving the Tathāgata."

Then the Elder Mahāmaudgalyāyana said to Sumati: "The bodhisattva's practice is exceedingly hard to carry out. Now that you have made this excellent, great vow, can you truly attain mastery over it?"

Then Sumati said: "Elder! If my vast vow is true and not empty, and can bring every one of these practices to completion, then may this trichiliocosm shake in six ways."

Then Sumati said further to Maudgalyāyana: "By the truth of these very words, in a future age I shall attain buddhahood — just as the Tathāgata Śākyamuni has today — and in my own land there will be no deeds of Māra, no evil destinies, and not even the word 'woman.' If these words of mine are not false, may the bodies of this whole assembly all turn the color of gold." The moment she spoke these words, the whole assembly turned the color of gold.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Maudgalyāyana's question is the sūtra's one moment of ordinary skepticism, voiced by a senior monk rather than dismissed as an outsider's doubt — and Sumati answers it not with argument but with an "act of truth" (a well-attested device across early Buddhist and wider Indian literature): stating a claim, then asking reality itself to confirm it through a specific, falsifiable sign. She invokes the device twice in three lines, first for the trembling of the trichiliocosm, then for turning the whole assembly's bodies to gold — and the text confirms the second sign happened exactly as stated, before she has done anything else to demonstrate her attainment. The same device, and the identical sign of turning gold, will recur at the sūtra's climax, when Sumati transforms her own body as well as the assembly's a second time.

A Dialogue With Mañjuśrī on Non-Abiding

Chinese Source

爾時，尊者大目揵連即從坐起，偏袒右肩，頂禮佛足，白言：「世尊！我今先禮初發心菩薩及諸菩薩摩訶薩眾。」

爾時，文殊師利法王子告妙慧言：「汝住何法發斯誠願？」妙慧答言：「文殊師利！非所問也。何以故？於法界中無所住故。」

又問：「云何名為菩提？」答曰：「無分別法，是名菩提。」又問：「云何名為菩薩？」答曰：「一切諸法等虛空相，是名菩薩。」又問：「云何名為菩提之行？」答曰：「猶如陽焰、谷響之行，是菩提行。」又問：「依何密意作如是說？」答曰：「我於此中不見少法密、非密者。」

又問：「若如是者，一切凡夫應即菩提。」答曰：「汝謂菩提異凡夫耶？莫作是見。何以故？此等皆同一法界相，非取、非捨，無成、壞故。」

又問：「於此義中能解了者，其數幾何？」答曰：「如若干幻化心心所量，若干幻化眾生能了斯義。」文殊師利言：「幻化本無，何有如是心心所法？」答曰：「法界亦爾，非有、非無；乃至如來亦復如是。」

爾時，文殊師利白佛言：「世尊！今此妙慧甚為希有，乃能成就如是法忍。」佛言：「如是，如是，誠如所言。然此童女已於過去發菩提心，經三十劫我乃發趣無上菩提，彼亦令汝住無生忍。」

爾時，文殊師利即從坐起，為其作禮，白妙慧言：「我於往昔無量劫前已曾供養，不謂今者還得親近。」妙慧告言：「文殊師利！汝今莫起如是分別。何以故？以無分別得無生忍故。」

又問：「妙慧！汝今猶不轉女身耶？」妙慧答言：「女人之相了不可得，今何所轉？」

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the Elder Mahāmaudgalyāyana rose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, bowed his head to the Buddha's feet, and said: "World-Honored One! From now on I will first bow to any bodhisattva who has newly aroused the bodhi-mind, and to every bodhisattva-mahāsattva."

Then the Dharma-Prince Mañjuśrī said to Sumati: "In what dharma do you abide, that you have made this sincere vow?" Sumati answered: "Mañjuśrī! That is not a question that can be asked. Why is this? Because within the dharma-realm there is nothing to abide in at all."

He asked further: "What is meant by bodhi?" She answered: "The dharma free of discrimination — this is called bodhi." He asked further: "What is meant by a bodhisattva?" She answered: "One for whom every dharma bears the mark of empty space — this is called a bodhisattva." He asked further: "What is meant by the practice of bodhi?" She answered: "A practice like a mirage, like an echo in a valley — this is the practice of bodhi." He asked further: "On what hidden meaning do you base such words?" She answered: "Within this, I see not the slightest dharma that is hidden, nor any that is not hidden."

He asked further: "If this is so, then every ordinary being should already be bodhi itself." She answered: "Do you take bodhi to be something different from an ordinary being? Do not hold such a view. Why is this? Because these are both, equally, marks of the very same dharma-realm — neither to be grasped nor rejected, neither accomplished nor destroyed."

He asked further: "Among those who can truly understand this meaning, how many are there?" She answered: "However many illusion-conjured minds and mental factors there are, that many illusion-conjured beings can understand this meaning." Mañjuśrī said: "An illusion is, at root, nothing at all — how then could there be minds and mental factors of this kind?" She answered: "The dharma-realm is just the same — neither existent nor non-existent; and even the Tathāgata is likewise just the same."

Then Mañjuśrī said to the Buddha: "World-Honored One! This girl Sumati is exceedingly rare indeed, to have accomplished such forbearance of the Dharma." The Buddha said: "So it is, so it is, exactly as you have said. This girl in fact aroused the bodhi-mind in ages past — I myself set out toward unsurpassed bodhi only thirty kalpas afterward, and it was she who first established you, too, in the forbearance of the unborn."

Then Mañjuśrī rose at once from his seat, bowed to her, and said to Sumati: "In ages past, immeasurable kalpas ago, I once made offerings to you — I had not expected that I should have the chance to draw near to you again today." Sumati said: "Mañjuśrī! Do not now give rise to such discrimination. Why is this? Because it is only through freedom from discrimination that the forbearance of the unborn is attained."

He asked further: "Sumati, will you not now transform this female body of yours?" Sumati answered: "The mark of a woman cannot, in the end, be found at all — what, then, is there to transform?"

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Mañjuśrī's opening question, and Sumati's refusal to answer it on its own terms, is a recognizable move to anyone who has read this site's Diamond Sūtra or its Heart Sūtra: a question phrased as "what do you abide in" gets answered by denying there is anything to abide in at all, the same structure the Diamond Sūtra builds an entire argument from. What this passage adds is a genuine reversal of expected seniority. Maudgalyāyana, one of the Buddha's two chief disciples, has just resolved to bow first to any newly-arisen bodhisattva, whatever their age or apparent standing — a resolution the narrative immediately vindicates, since the Buddha reveals that this eight-year-old girl aroused bodhicitta before he himself set out on the path, and that she is in fact responsible for Mañjuśrī's own attainment of the forbearance of the unborn. The child outranks both the monk and the bodhisattva of wisdom in the one currency that actually matters in this sūtra's own terms — how long ago, and how sincerely, bodhicitta was first aroused — while holding no formal rank in the assembly whatsoever.

Sumati Becomes a Man

Chinese Source

「文殊師利！我當為汝除斷疑惑。由我如是真實語故，於當來世得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提時，於我法中諸比丘輩聞命善來出家入道，我國土中所有眾生身皆金色，服用資具如第六天、飲食豐饒隨念而至，無有魔事及諸惡趣，亦復無有女人之名，有七寶林上羅寶網、七寶蓮花覆以寶帳——如文殊師利所成淨剎，裝挍嚴飾等無有異。

「若我此言非虛妄者，令此大眾身皆金色，我之女身變成男子，如三十歲知法比丘。」說此語時，此諸大眾皆作金色，妙慧菩薩轉女成男，如三十歲知法比丘。

是時，地居天眾展轉讚言：「大哉，大哉。妙慧菩薩摩訶薩能於來世得菩提時嚴持佛剎功德如是。」

Ru-Yi Translation

"Mañjuśrī! I shall now dispel your doubt. By the truth of these very words: when, in an age to come, I attain unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening, then within my own teaching, monks will hear the call 'welcome!' and thereby go forth and enter the path; every being in my land will have a body the color of gold, their clothing and requisites like those of the Sixth Heaven, their food abundant and arriving at the very thought of it; there will be no deeds of Māra, no evil destinies, and not even the word 'woman'; there will be groves of the seven treasures hung with jeweled nets, and lotuses of the seven treasures covered by canopies of jewels — adorned and arrayed exactly like the pure land Mañjuśrī himself will accomplish, with no difference at all.

"If these words of mine are not false, may the bodies of this whole assembly turn the color of gold, and may my own female body transform into that of a man, like a thirty-year-old monk versed in the Dharma." The moment she spoke these words, the whole assembly turned the color of gold, and the bodhisattva Sumati transformed from a woman into a man, like a thirty-year-old monk versed in the Dharma.

At that time, the earth-dwelling gods passed word to one another in praise: "Great indeed, great indeed! That the bodhisattva-mahāsattva Sumati, upon attaining bodhi in an age to come, should adorn and uphold a buddha-land with such virtue as this!"

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

The transformation is staged as a second act of truth, using the identical gold-colored sign as before — and it happens immediately after Sumati has just told Mañjuśrī that "the mark of a woman cannot, in the end, be found at all." Read together, these two lines refuse to let the miracle be mistaken for a concession that femaleness was ever a real obstacle her attainment had to overcome. She had already answered every one of Mañjuśrī's questions, already been credited by the Buddha with a bodhi-resolve older than his own, and already secured the forbearance of the unborn — all of this as a woman, before any transformation occurs. The change of body functions here as one more demonstration of her own thesis, that no fixed mark of male or female can ultimately be found, performed for an audience that still needs the demonstration rather than for her own sake.

This makes for a useful contrast with this site's Śrīmālādevī Sūtra, where Queen Śrīmālā receives the Buddha's own prediction of future buddhahood explicitly as a woman, with no transformation of any kind. Two Mahāyāna sūtras, addressing the same underlying claim — that gender poses no real barrier to buddhahood — reach for opposite narrative solutions: one has its heroine become a buddha without ever changing form, the other has its heroine change form as a further proof that form was never the issue to begin with. Neither reading cancels the other; they simply demonstrate the same point from two different angles, and this site does not attempt to rank one as more authoritative than the other.

The Buddha's Prediction, and the Sūtra's Transmission

Chinese Source

爾時，佛告文殊師利：「此妙慧菩薩於當來世成等正覺，號殊勝功德寶藏如來，出現於世。」佛說此經時，三十俱胝眾生於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提住不退轉，八十俱胝眾生遠塵離垢得法眼淨，八千眾生皆獲智證。

五千比丘行菩薩乘心欲退轉，因見妙慧菩薩意樂善根、威德殊勝故，各各脫身所著上服以施如來。如是施已，發弘誓言：「我等以此善根決定願成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」彼諸善男子等以此善根迴向無上菩提故，超九十劫生死之苦，不退轉於阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。

爾時，世尊即記之曰：「汝等於當來世過千劫後，於無垢光明劫中，陽焰世界難忍佛剎，於一劫中相次成佛，皆同一字，號辯才莊嚴如來，出現於世。

「文殊師利！如是法門有大威德，能令菩薩摩訶薩及聲聞乘者獲大利益。文殊師利！或有善男子、善女人為求菩提，無方便善巧行六波羅蜜足滿千劫，若復有人經於半月時一書寫、讀誦此經，所獲福聚比前功德百分、千分、百千俱胝乃至算數譬喻所不能及。

「是故，文殊師利！如是微妙法門即諸菩薩契經之本，我今付囑於汝，汝當來世受持、讀誦、為人解說。譬如轉輪聖王出現於世，所有七寶皆悉在前；王滅之後，寶皆隱沒。如是微妙法門流行於世，即諸如來七菩提分等法明不滅；若不流行，正法當滅。

「是故，文殊師利！若善男子、善女人等為求菩提，應當發起精進，書寫此經、受持讀誦、為人演說。此是我教，勿於後世生悔恨心。」

佛說此經已，妙慧菩薩、文殊師利菩薩，及諸大眾、天、人、阿修羅、乾闥婆等，聞佛所說，皆大歡喜，信受奉行。

Ru-Yi Translation

Then the Buddha said to Mañjuśrī: "This bodhisattva Sumati, in an age to come, will accomplish perfect and complete awakening, and will appear in the world as the Tathāgata Excellent Meritorious Treasure-Store." When the Buddha spoke this sūtra, thirty koṭis of beings became irreversible in unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening; eighty koṭis of beings, far removed from dust and defilement, attained the pure Dharma-eye; and eight thousand beings all attained realization through wisdom.

Five thousand monks practicing the bodhisattva vehicle, whose minds had been on the verge of turning back, on seeing the excellent aspiration, roots of goodness, and majestic virtue of the bodhisattva Sumati, each removed the upper robe they were wearing and offered it to the Tathāgata. Having made this offering, they made this vast vow: "By this root of goodness, we resolve and vow to accomplish unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening." Those good sons, by dedicating this root of goodness to unsurpassed bodhi, thereby leapt over ninety kalpas of the suffering of birth-and-death, and became irreversible in unsurpassed, perfect, complete awakening.

Then the World-Honored One at once gave them this prediction: "You, in an age to come, after a thousand kalpas have passed, in the kalpa called Stainless Radiance, in the world called Mirage, in the buddha-land called Endurance, will, within a single kalpa, attain buddhahood one after another, all under the identical name, appearing in the world as the Tathāgata Eloquence Adorned.

"Mañjuśrī! A dharma-gate such as this carries great majestic virtue, able to bring great benefit to bodhisattva-mahāsattvas and to practitioners of the śrāvaka vehicle alike. Mañjuśrī! Suppose a good son or good daughter, seeking bodhi, practices the six pāramitās without skillful means for a full thousand kalpas; and suppose another person, over the course of half a month, once copies out and recites this sūtra — the mass of merit the latter gains surpasses the former's merit by a hundred parts, a thousand parts, a hundred thousand koṭi parts, indeed by a margin no calculation or simile could ever reach.

"Therefore, Mañjuśrī! A subtle and wondrous dharma-gate such as this is the very root of every bodhisattva sūtra. I now entrust it to you: in ages to come, uphold it, recite it, and explain it to others. It is like a wheel-turning sage-king appearing in the world — every one of the seven treasures then appears before him; but once the king passes away, every treasure vanishes from sight. Just so, when a subtle and wondrous dharma-gate such as this circulates in the world, the light of every tathāgata's seven factors of awakening and the rest of the Dharma is never extinguished; but if it does not circulate, the true Dharma will perish.

"Therefore, Mañjuśrī! If any good son or good daughter, seeking bodhi, should arouse diligence, copy out this sūtra, uphold and recite it, and explain it to others. This is my own teaching — let no one, in ages to come, give rise to regret over it."

When the Buddha had finished speaking this sūtra, the bodhisattva Sumati, the bodhisattva Mañjuśrī, and the whole great assembly — gods, humans, asuras, gandharvas, and the rest — hearing what the Buddha had taught, all rejoiced greatly, and, believing and accepting this teaching, carried it out with reverence.

Reading note — Ru-Yi Meditation Center

Sumati's new name at buddhahood, Excellent Meritorious Treasure-Store, closes a small structural loop the sūtra never comments on directly: a girl who has just finished demonstrating that no mark — of self, of gender, of any fixed identity — can ultimately be found, receives a name built entirely around what she possesses and stores up. The sūtra does not pause to reconcile the two; on its own terms, both the emptiness of every mark and the accumulation of merit and vow are simply true at once, and the closing merit-of-upholding-this-sūtra passage that follows takes the second half of that pair — merit, vow, transmission across generations — as its whole subject, exactly the register this sūtra opened in in with a child's ten sets of causal practice, before Mañjuśrī's questions pushed it somewhere stranger for a while.
