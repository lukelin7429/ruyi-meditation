Saṃyutta Nikāya · Discourse 37.20

Ethical

Susīlasutta — Dutiyapeyyālavagga

Why this discourse

This discourse reverses SN 37.10's own dussīla (unethical, of bad character) into susīla (ethical, of good character) — the exact quality and discourse number this project's own SN 37.10 reading guide named in advance, now confirmed. As a broad character disposition rather than a specific trait, susīla stands as the general ethical counterweight among this series' own nine reversed qualities.

Reading guide

A general disposition, not a specific behavior

Susīla names good moral character broadly, matching dussīla's own general framing at SN 37.10 rather than reversing any single specific act — the same level of generality on both sides of this particular mirror.

A confirmation of an earlier forward reference

This project's own SN 37.10 reading guide named susīla and SN 37.20 specifically as this quality's own positive counterpart and location — this discourse confirms that reference was accurate.

The precepts implicit here, explicit at SN 37.24

Susīla names ethical conduct in general terms; the vagga's own closing discourse (SN 37.24) will make this same underlying commitment explicit and specific, listing the five precepts by name rather than naming a character trait.

The sixth of nine, three from this series' own close

With this discourse, six of the nine bright-side counterparts are now confirmed, leaving three (bahussuta, āraddhavīriya, upaṭṭhitassati) before the vagga's own closing discourse.

Key terms

susīla — ethical, of good moral character — this discourse's own variable fifth quality, directly reversing SN 37.10's own dussīla
dussīla — unethical, of bad character — SN 37.10's own quality, reversed here
saddhā... hirimā... ottāpī... paññavā — the four fixed qualities, unchanged throughout this mirror series
dasamaṁ — “tenth” — this discourse's own untranslated closing marker, matching SN 37.10's own position
pañcasīla — the five precepts — the specific, named form of this same general ethical commitment, met at this vagga's own closing discourse, SN 37.24

The text

The complete translated content of this discourse — a single line, the fivefold clause itself. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).

§1“… They’re faithful, conscientious, prudent, ethical, and wise. …”
