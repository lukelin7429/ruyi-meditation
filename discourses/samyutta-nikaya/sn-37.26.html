Saṃyutta Nikāya · Discourse 37.26

Under Her Thumb

Pasayhasutta — Balavagga

Setting
Not restated in this discourse
Speakers
The Buddha addressing the mendicants
Form
The identical fivefold list of SN 37.25, closing on a second, different outcome
Length
~1 minute to read
Northern parallel
A rough counterpart may exist in the Chinese Saṃyukta-āgama (T99), though this reading guide does not assert a specific matching sutra number
Difficulty
★☆☆☆☆ — a one-word change from SN 37.25's own closing line

Why this discourse

This discourse repeats SN 37.25's own fivefold list word for word — attractiveness, wealth, relatives, children, and ethical behavior — and changes only the closing outcome: a lady equipped with all five, this discourse states, has her husband under her thumb (sāmikaṁ pasayha). Confirms the triptych predicted at SN 37.25: the same condition, restated, now paired with a second, more assertive outcome.

Reading guide

The same five, a different close

Every word of the fivefold list is unchanged from SN 37.25; only the final clause differs, replacing self-assured domestic life with dominance over the husband specifically.

Pasayha: forceful control, named outright

“Pasayha” carries a stronger sense than “visārada” — not mere confidence but active mastery, foreshadowing SN 37.27's own even more explicit “abhibhuyya.”

Key terms

sāmikaṁ pasayha agāraṁ ajjhāvasati — “lives at home having overpowered her husband” — this discourse's own closing clause, replacing SN 37.25's visārado
rūpabalaṁ, bhogabalaṁ, ñātibalaṁ, puttabalaṁ, sīlabalaṁ — the identical fivefold list, unchanged from SN 37.25
bhikkhave — “mendicants” — the Buddha's own address term, opening this discourse and every other discourse in this vagga
dutiyaṁ — “second” — this discourse's own untranslated closing ordinal
sāmikaṁ — “husband” — the party this discourse's own outcome clause turns on

The text

The complete discourse. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).

§1“Mendicants, there are these five powers of a lady. What five? Attractiveness, wealth, relatives, children, and ethical behavior. These are the five powers of a lady. A lady living at home with these five powers has her husband under her thumb.”
