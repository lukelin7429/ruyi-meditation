This Is Mine, Etc.

Why this discourse

The most extreme compression this saṃyutta has shown so far: sixteen sutta-numbers (SN 24.20–35, views 2 through 17 of this vagga's own second round) collapsed into a single bilara-data file holding just one line of editorial instruction, rather than either full elaboration or even SN 24.11–17's own bare-question treatment.

Reading guide

An instruction, not a discourse

"Tell these in full as in discourses 2 through 17 of the previous chapter" (Purimavagge viya aṭṭhārasa veyyākaraṇāni vitthāretabbānīti) — this single sentence is the entirety of this merged file's content: not a restatement of any view, but a direct editorial pointer back to Sotāpattivagga's own second through seventeenth discourses (this-is-mine through the cosmos finite/infinite and soul-body pairs).

Sixteen discourses, one line

Where SN 24.11–17 each still preserved their own bare opening claim, these sixteen sutta-numbers preserve nothing view-specific at all — the compiler trusts the reader to supply not just the aggregate catechism but the sixteen individual claims themselves, purely by cross-reference to the vagga already read.

A different encoding from SN 23's merged files

Unlike SN 23.23–33 or SN 23.35–45, whose merged files preserve each sub-discourse's own individual key prefix internally (sn23.24:, sn23.25:, and so on), this file uses a single unified prefix (sn24.20-35:) for its entire five-key content — the same encoding convention already seen at SN 12.72–81, but taken to a further extreme of compression: only one content key total, rather than one key per sub-discourse.

Confirming this vagga's own eighteen-topic count

This instruction's own wording — "the eighteen expositions" (aṭṭhārasa veyyākaraṇāni) — independently confirms SN 24.18's own closing colophon, verifying from a second, later source that Sotāpattivagga comprises exactly eighteen discourses, not some other figure.

Key terms

purimavagge viya — "as in the previous vagga" — this instruction's own explicit cross-reference back to Sotāpattivagga
aṭṭhārasa veyyākaraṇāni — the eighteen expositions — this instruction's own confirmation of Sotāpattivagga's full discourse count, matching SN 24.18's colophon
vitthāretabbāni — "should be told in full" — the instruction's own directive, trusting the reader to reconstruct sixteen full discourses from one line
peyyāla — an elision mark — here compressing sixteen sutta-numbers to their most extreme point in this saṃyutta so far
dutiyagamana — the second round — the reason these sixteen sutta-numbers exist at all, restating Sotāpattivagga's own second-through-seventeenth discourses

The text

The complete merged file: a single editorial instruction standing in for sixteen sutta-numbers. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).
