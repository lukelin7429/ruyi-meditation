Saṃyutta Nikāya · Discourse 37.19

Not Adulterous

Anaticārīsutta — Dutiyapeyyālavagga

Setting
Not restated in this discourse
Speakers

Why this discourse

This discourse reverses SN 37.9's own aticārī (unfaithfulness, adultery) into anaticārī (marital faithfulness), the specific ethical violation this project already noted would resurface at SN 37.14's own precept-based list — here it appears instead in its character-trait form, reversed rather than reframed.

Reading guide

A specific fidelity, not a general virtue

Like aticārī's own specificity at SN 37.9, anaticārī names a particular ethical commitment within marriage rather than a general character quality — the one discourse in this fixed-template series (on either side of the mirror) naming a specific relational commitment rather than a temperament or capacity.

The same relational territory as SN 37.14 and SN 37.24, differently framed

Sexual misconduct appears three times across this saṃyutta's own first two vaggas: as a character trait reversed here and at SN 37.9, and as a precept violation at SN 37.14 and its own upcoming mirror at SN 37.24 — the same ethical territory addressed through two different structural framings.

Compression matching the series' own settled form

This discourse's own single surviving line matches SN 37.18's own minimal length, continuing the bright-side series' own shorter compression floor.

The fifth of nine, past this series' own midpoint

With akkodhana, anupanāhī, anissukī, amaccharī, and now anaticārī confirmed, this vagga's own series has passed its midpoint, moving toward its own close.

Key terms

anaticārī — not unfaithful, maintaining marital fidelity — this discourse's own variable fifth quality, directly reversing SN 37.9's own aticārī
aticārī — unfaithful, adulterous — SN 37.9's own quality, reversed here
kāmesumicchācārā paṭivirato — “refraining from sexual misconduct” — the precept-based framing of the same ethical territory, met at SN 37.14 and its own upcoming mirror at SN 37.24
navamaṁ — “ninth” — this discourse's own untranslated closing marker, matching SN 37.9's own position
saddhā... hirimā... ottāpī... paññavā — the four fixed qualities, unchanged throughout this mirror series

The text

The complete translated content of this discourse — a single line, the fivefold clause itself. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).

§1“… They’re faithful, conscientious, prudent, not adulterous, and wise. …”
