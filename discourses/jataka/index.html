Jataka

Selected Verses from the Birth Stories

The Jataka ("Birth Stories") is a Khuddaka Nikāya collection of 547 traditional numbered tales recounting the Buddha-to-be's past lives. In every full version the verse is set inside a prose narrative — but that narrative framework is later commentary, not part of the canonical CC0 text this site draws from. Bhikkhu Sujato's CC0 translation covers only the verse portions, and only for 82 of the 547 traditional numbers, scattered non-sequentially (Ja 1 through Ja 79, the entire Book of the Ones, plus three later outliers: Ja 126, Ja 159, and Ja 466).

This collection can therefore never be "complete" the way this site's Dhammapada or Udāna are. Each entry states plainly that it is a bare verse excerpt from a partial selection — often a single aphoristic stanza whose full story exists only in post-canonical commentary, which this reading guide draws on for context but does not present as canonical text. Each entry has the same reading guide, full Sujato translation, and self-check quiz as our other series.

The texts

The Apaṇṇakavagga, Sīlavagga, Kuruṅgavagga, Kulāvakavagga, and Atthakāmavagga, the first five chapters of the Ekakanipāta (50 poems — the "first fifty," per the source text's own division), are complete; the remaining three chapters of the Ekakanipāta, plus three later outlier poems (Ja 126, Ja 159, Ja 466), are being added in order.
