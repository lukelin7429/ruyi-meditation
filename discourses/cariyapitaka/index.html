Cariyapitaka

The Basket of Conduct

The Cariyapitaka ("Basket of Conduct") is a Khuddaka Nikāya collection of thirty-five verse stories recounting past lives in which the Buddha-to-be practiced one of the ten perfections (pāramī) — generosity, ethics, renunciation, wisdom, energy, patience, truthfulness, resolve, love, and equanimity — each first-person narrative closing with an explicit statement of which perfection it illustrates.

This collection is being built toward complete coverage, all thirty-five stories, working in order. Each entry will have the same reading guide, full Sujato translation, and self-check quiz as our other series.
