Another Uttamā

Why this text

This poem's title, ‘Another Uttamā’, names the collection's second same-name pair to sit inside a single book — unlike Thig 2.10 and Thig 3.1's Sāmās, split across a book boundary, this Uttamā follows the first immediately. Where that first Uttamā told a story of a specific teacher and a seven-day practice, this one offers something different: a compact list of attainments, stated without narrative.

Reading guide

A second same-name pair, closer together this time

Thig 3.1's ‘Another Sāmā’ pointed back across a book boundary to Thig 2.10. This poem's ‘Another Uttamā’ points back only one poem, to Thig 3.2 — the collection's second instance of two same-named women placed one after the other.

Seven factors, all of them developed

‘Of the seven awakening factors, the path for attaining extinguishment, I have developed them all, just as the Buddha taught’ — a direct claim to complete, not partial, mastery of a named standard list.

Two meditations, available on demand

‘I attain the meditations on emptiness and signlessness whenever I want’ frames her mastery not as something that occurred once but as available at will — a distinct kind of claim from a single dated breakthrough.

A rightful daughter, and a closing formula reused

‘I am the Buddha's rightful daughter, always delighting in extinguishment’ is a striking self-description, and the poem's final lines — ‘transmigration through births is finished, now there'll be no more future lives’ — use this collection's standard closing formula for the first time in the Book of the Threes.

Key terms

bojjhaṅga — the seven awakening factors, a standard list this poem claims to have developed in full.
suññata — “emptiness” — one of two meditations named as attainable ‘whenever I want’.
animitta — “signlessness” — the second of the two meditations named alongside emptiness.
orasā dhītā buddhassa — “the Buddha's rightful daughter” — this poem's own striking self-description.
punabbhava — “future life” or rebirth — denied outright in this poem's closing line, the collection's standard formula for an ended cycle.

The text

The text in full: three verses. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).

§1Of the seven awakening factors, the path for attaining extinguishment, I have developed them all, just as the Buddha taught.

§2I attain the meditations on emptiness and signlessness whenever I want. I am the Buddha’s rightful daughter, always delighting in extinguishment.

§3All sensual pleasures are cut off, whether human or heavenly. Transmigration through births is finished, now there’ll be no more future lives.
