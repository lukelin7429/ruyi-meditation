Therigatha

Verses of the Senior Nuns

The Therigatha ("Verses of the Senior Nuns") is a Khuddaka Nikāya collection of seventy-three poems attributed to the earliest generation of enlightened Buddhist women — among the oldest surviving spiritual autobiography by women in world literature. Unlike the Cariyapitaka's past-life stories, these are first-person accounts of this life: the hardships that led each woman to renounce the world, her struggle in practice, and the moment of her own awakening.

This collection is being built toward complete coverage, all 73 poems, working in order by book. The books are organized by the number of verses attributed to each poet, from the Book of the Ones through to the Great Book, Sumedhā's single long poem that closes the collection. Each entry will have the same reading guide, full Sujato translation, and self-check quiz as our other series.
