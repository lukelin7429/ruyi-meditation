Theragatha

Verses of the Senior Monks

The Theragatha ("Verses of the Senior Monks") is a Khuddaka Nikāya collection of two hundred and sixty-four poems attributed to the earliest generation of enlightened Buddhist men — the companion collection to the Therigatha. Like its companion, these are first-person accounts of this life: the hardship or insight that led each man to renounce the household, the struggle of training, and, for many poems, the moment of his own awakening, stated directly.

This collection is being built toward complete coverage, all 264 poems, working in order by book, from the Book of the Ones through to the Great Book. Each entry will have the same reading guide, full Sujato translation, and self-check quiz as our other series.

The texts

The Book of the Ones (120 poems), the Book of the Twos (49 poems), the Book of the Threes (16 poems), the Book of the Fours (12 poems), the Book of the Fives (12 poems), and the Book of the Sixes (14 poems) are all complete; fifteen further books remain, built out book by book.
