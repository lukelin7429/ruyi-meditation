Aṅguttara Nikāya · Discourses 5.287–292

A Nun, and So On

Why this discourse

Six discourses stand behind one file. AN 5.286 named a monk alone; this page runs the identical five-precept formula across a nun, a trainee nun, a novice monk, a novice nun, a layman, and a laywoman — covering the entire fourfold assembly of monks and nuns, laymen and laywomen, plus the two intermediate trainee stages specific to women's ordination.

Reading guide

The teaching, run across six categories

The identical five precepts from AN 5.286 — not killing, stealing, engaging in sexual misconduct, lying, or consuming intoxicants — restated once for each of six categories: nun, trainee nun, novice monk, novice nun, layman, and laywoman.

The fourfold assembly, plus two trainee stages

Together with AN 5.286's monk, this page's six categories complete the standard fourfold assembly of the tradition — monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen — and add the two intermediate ordination stages specific to women training toward full ordination: novice nun and probationary trainee nun (sikkhamānā), a stage with no exact male equivalent in this formula.

One term change for lay and female-monastic categories

Where AN 5.286 used abrahmacārī, covering any sexual activity at all under a monk's full celibate vows, this page's categories use kāmesumicchācārinī, ‘sexual misconduct’ specifically — the narrower standard appropriate to those not bound by full monastic celibacy.

The source's own compression, and this page's choice

The Pali source states the formula in full only for the final category, laywoman, eliding the preceding five with its peyyāla mark; this page's English text follows Sujato's translation, which likewise gives the explicit wording only for laywoman while naming all six categories in the discourse's opening line.

What follows

AN 5.293 turns outside the Buddhist community entirely, to an Ājīvaka ascetic — and, notably, states only the hell half of the formula, with no matching heaven statement.

Key terms

bhikkhunī, sikkhamānā — nun, and probationary trainee nun — the intermediate ordination stage specific to women, with no exact male equivalent in this formula.
sāmaṇera, sāmaṇerī — novice monk and novice nun — the junior monastic categories this page adds.
upāsaka, upāsikā — layman and laywoman — completing the fourfold assembly alongside monk and nun.
kāmesumicchācārinī — “sexual misconduct” — the narrower standard used for these six categories, distinct from a fully ordained monk's abrahmacārī.
yathābhataṁ nikkhittā — “placed as if delivered there”, in feminine grammatical form — the same verdict image as AN 5.286, agreeing with the female subjects this page explicitly states in full.

The text

The formula as the source states it: the six categories named, with laywoman given in explicit full wording. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).

Six categories that lead to hell

§1“A nun … trainee nun … novice monk … novice nun … layman … laywoman … with five qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. What five? They kill living creatures, steal, commit sexual misconduct, lie, and consume beer, wine, and liquor intoxicants. With these five qualities they’re placed in hell as if delivered there.

Six categories that lead to heaven

§2A nun … trainee nun … novice monk … novice nun … layman … laywoman … with five qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. What five? They don’t kill living creatures, steal, commit sexual misconduct, lie, or consume beer, wine, and liquor intoxicants. With these five qualities they’re placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
