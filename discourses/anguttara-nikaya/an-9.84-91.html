Aṅguttara Nikāya · Discourses 9.84–91

Hindrances, Etc.

Why this discourse

Standing for eight separate discourses (AN 9.84 through AN 9.91), this page repeats AN 9.74-81's exact instruction — ‘tell in full as in the chapter on mindfulness meditation’ — directing the reciter back to ch.7's eight middle obstacle-lists once more, this time paired with the four bases of psychic power.

Reading guide

The teaching in one sentence

The same eight obstacle-lists given in full at AN 9.64 through AN 9.71 are each given up by developing the four bases of psychic power, exactly as AN 9.83 already showed for the first obstacle-list shared across all three chapters.

The identical instruction, a second time

This page's own surviving text is word-for-word the same instruction already met at AN 9.74-81: ‘tell in full as in the chapter on mindfulness meditation.’ The source doesn't even vary its own pointer between the two occasions, trusting the same substitution logic each time.

Two merged pages, three chapters, one shared source

Both this page and AN 9.74-81 point to the identical eight discourses in ch.7 — neither points to the other, and neither reconstructs the content itself. Ch.7's AN 9.64 through AN 9.71 function as this project's sole on-record version of these eight obstacle-lists, now referenced twice over rather than written out three separate times.

Closing in on the third chapter's own final discourse

With this page, only AN 9.92 remains to close ch.9 — the third and final ‘shackles of the heart’ discourse in this three-chapter series, and the point at which the source's own closing verse explicitly names all three parallel chapters together.

Key terms

tesaṁyeva vitthāro satipaṭṭhānavaggasadisova kātabbo — “tell in full as in the chapter on mindfulness meditation” — the identical instruction already met at AN 9.74-81, repeated here without variation.
cattāro iddhipādā — “the four bases of psychic power” — the one substitution this instruction asks the reciter to make throughout, in place of the four kinds of mindfulness meditation.
nīvaraṇā, kāmaguṇā, upādānakkhandhā — “hindrances, sensual stimulation, grasping aggregates” — the first three of the eight obstacle-lists this page stands for, given in full at AN 9.64-66.
orambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni, gatiyo, macchariyāni, uddhambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni, cetokhilā — “lower fetters, destinations, stinginess, higher fetters, hard-heartedness” — the remaining five obstacle-lists this page stands for, given in full at AN 9.67-71.
iddhipādavaggo — “Iddhipādavagga” — this chapter's own name, naming the third and final remedy in this three-chapter series.

The text

The discourse exactly as it survives in the source: the same instruction already met at AN 9.74-81, pointing back to ch.7's own content once more. Nothing has been added or reconstructed. Translation: Bhikkhu Sujato (CC0, SuttaCentral).
