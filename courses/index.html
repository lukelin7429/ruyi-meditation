Ru-Yi Academy · 如意學院

Study the Dharma the way you'd take a course.

Not a library to browse, but a path to walk — guided courses that take you through the Buddha's teachings one text at a time, with a teacher, reading notes, real questions, and assignments. The way you'd learn anything worth learning.

How a course works

Every session follows the same rhythm — the structure that makes it a course, and not just more reading.

1
LectureThe teacher sets the scene & the key ideas
2
ReadThe primary text itself, with notes in the margin
3
DiscussOpen questions, talked through together
4
AssignmentA short take-home task each week
5
Self-checkA quick quiz to confirm it landed

One theme, several texts

7 short courses

主題短課 — four to seven sessions each, built around a single question and read across several discourses. Take them in any order; none requires another.

The Wheel Set Rolling

四聖諦 · 4 sessions

The four noble truths from four angles — first announced to a skeptical audience, then given their full technical definition, then shown as a deliberate restriction: a handful of leaves against the whole forest.

No prior courseOpen →

No Core Is Found Here

三法印 · 4 sessions

Impermanence, suffering, and not-self read four ways — and closing honestly rather than triumphantly, on the lived gap between understanding an argument and actually being settled by it.

No prior courseOpen →

The Three Fires

三把火 · 6 sessions

Greed, hatred, and delusion read as a mechanism rather than a list of sins — the attention that lets them arise, and the shortest definition of their ending in the whole Pali Canon.

No prior courseOpen →

The Owner of Your Deeds

業與轉世 · 4 sessions

Kamma as causal fit, not fate or a cosmic ledger — with the pattern's own honest limits, and the one deed that ends the whole mechanism.

No prior courseOpen →

Boundless in Every Direction

四梵住 · 4 sessions

Two brahmins argue over whose teacher taught the true path to Brahmā; the answer replaces an unverifiable ritual claim with four states anyone can cultivate and check.

No prior courseOpen →

At the Bedside

死亡與臨終關懷 · 4 sessions

Preparing for your own death, consoling the grieving, counseling the dying in severe pain, and a family present at a death that went well. Named people, not a general philosophy of impermanence.

No prior courseOpen →

Teaching Like the Buddha

像佛陀一樣教書 · 7 sessions

Seven suttas read not for their doctrine but for his method — giving a village a test instead of a belief, refusing a question on principle, reaching a murderer with a single word.
